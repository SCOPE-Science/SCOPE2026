# Scientific review: corank-two Brauer diagram product fibers

**Correctness — PASS.** A rank-\(n-2\) right factor has exactly one uniformly selected upper hook. It loses two propagating strands precisely when both hook endpoints meet propagating strands of the current product, giving probability \(r(r-1)/[n(n-1)]\). The left/right unit action is transitive on each Brauer rank class and preserves the uniform factor measure, so prescribed-product counts equal the rank law divided by the complete stratum cardinality. Solving the elementary finite-state recurrence gives all lengths. Independent exact matching enumeration, not merely a Markov simulation, reproduced all target fibers for \(n=3,4,5\) within tested lengths. The geometric waiting-time decomposition and uniform tail truncation establish the even/odd quadratic absorption limits. **Risk:** the finite checker does not replace the unrestricted combinatorial proof; loop-weighted Brauer algebra products are outside the claim.

**Originality — PASS (historically qualified).** East–Gray (arXiv:1404.2359, Section 8) prove rank-stratum structure and idempotent generating results, including qualitative lower-rank reachability. Dolinka–East (arXiv:1510.08666, Sections 2–3) analyze twisted monoid ideals and Green classes. Wolff (doi:10.1007/s10801-018-0858-7, Sections 3–5) analyzes a different local Metropolis walk on Brauer/BMW diagrams. None of these examined full results supplies the present complete uniform corank-two product fibers, quadratic pure-death absorption distribution, or parity-dependent limiting law. Published-literature and mathematical-priority searches for direct formulas, general double-coset reductions, rank chains and diagram-walk aliases did not find dominating coverage. Kingman’s 1982 coalescent gives the classical pair-merger exponential waiting-time mechanism once the chain is identified; it does not provide the Brauer-monoid multiplication correspondence or target fibers. The older Brauer/Jones commuting-idempotent results describe a distinct invariant, and symmetric inverse-monoid coupon-collector products have a different multiplication mechanism. **Residual risk:** a general unindexed double-coset convolution theorem or probabilistic treatment of diagram monoids could contain the result as a special case; absence from searches cannot prove priority.

**Value — PASS.** The full corank-two stratum is the first singular rank class of the Brauer monoid, whose products generate the singular ideal; prescribed multiplication fibers and time to enter its minimal ideal are therefore intrinsic, motivated algebraic invariants. The exact rank-uniform law holds simultaneously for arbitrary targets and arbitrary lengths. The nondegenerate, parity-sensitive quadratic absorption profile is a structural contrast with the linear-rate coupon-collector behavior of partial permutations, rather than a routine count of elements of a fixed rank. The proof identifies why the two descriptions differ.
