Uniform products of arbitrary corank-two Brauer diagrams admit exact prescribed-target factorizations at every length, because their rank follows a pure-death chain with decrement probability \(r(r-1)/[n(n-1)]\) and their conditional law is uniform on each rank class. Their absorption into the minimal ideal takes quadratic time with different nondegenerate even- and odd-degree limit laws and mean constants \(\log 2\) and \(1-\log 2\), respectively.
