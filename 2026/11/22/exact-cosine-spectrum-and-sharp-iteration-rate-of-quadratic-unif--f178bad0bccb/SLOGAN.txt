The uniform clamped quadratic Schoenberg–Greville operator has explicit nontrivial eigenvalues \(\lambda_j=(3+\cos(j\pi/n))/4\) and half-grid sine B-spline eigenfunctions. Its iterate error decays at the exact spectral rate \(\gamma_n=(3+\cos(\pi/n))/4\), with \(1-\gamma_n\sim\pi^2/(8n^2)\), resolving the fixed-quadratic-degree fine-mesh case of a 2014 spectral conjecture.
