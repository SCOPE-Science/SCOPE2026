In uniform \(q\)-letter circular words, the joint counts of all \(q(q-1)\) nonconstant length-three palindromes have an exact covariance with five explicitly ordered spectral classes for \(q\ge4\) (four for \(q=3\)). Its positive-definite structure yields a fully calibrated multivariate Gaussian limit and a \(\chi^2_{q(q-1)}\) goodness-of-fit statistic for color-resolved consecutive palindromic motifs.
