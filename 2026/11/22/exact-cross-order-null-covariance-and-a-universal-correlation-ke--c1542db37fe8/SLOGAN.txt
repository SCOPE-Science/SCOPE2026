Squared-Gini goodness-of-fit scores built from overlapping spacings of two different orders have an exact finite-sample mixed covariance and an explicit joint Gaussian covariance limit. When the two orders grow only after taking the sample-size limit, their correlation depends solely on their smaller-to-larger ratio \(u\), through \(\sqrt u(3-u)/2\), quantifying how much multiscale spacing tests share under a uniform null.
