In every dimension of the twisted baker map, the first-coordinate stationary process has an exact rational all-lag cubic time arrow: \(\mathbb E[X_0X_n^2]=0\), while \(\mathbb E[X_0^2X_n]\) is strictly negative through lag \(M\) with its first dimension-dependent correction exactly at lag \(M\). The total forward-minus-reverse cubic memory is always \(-2/9\), independent of dimension.
