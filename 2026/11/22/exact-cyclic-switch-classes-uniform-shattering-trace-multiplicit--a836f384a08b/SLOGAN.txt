Binary words with exactly \(k\) cyclic switches, for feasible even \(k\), shatter every vertex support of size at most \(\min\{k+1,n-k+1\}\) and no larger support; a gap-parity product gives every exact trace multiplicity. Their shattering complex is uniform-matroidal, while for fixed positive \(k\) adaptive learning grows like \(k\log_2n\), beginning with a strict static/online gap on the five-cycle when \(k=2\).
