For positive-definite functions supported on \(\{0,\pm1,\pm3\}\) in every cyclic group of order at least seven, the exact maximal counting mass is \(2\) at even orders and a closed trigonometric expression strictly larger than \(2\) at odd orders. Every odd-order real-even optimizer is unique and has a factored cubic Fourier polynomial; its first-neighbor coefficient vanishes exactly when the order is divisible by three.
