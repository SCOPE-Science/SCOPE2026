For binary Fibonacci words (no adjacent ones), the exact all-length generating function of unordered single-deletion-confusable pairs is \(z(1-z+z^3)/((1-z)(1-z-z^2)^3)\). It splits into ordinary Hamming edges \(z/(1-z-z^2)^2\) and additional deletion-only conflicts \(z^2/((1-z)(1-z-z^2)^3)\), exposing a quadratic mean-degree law.
