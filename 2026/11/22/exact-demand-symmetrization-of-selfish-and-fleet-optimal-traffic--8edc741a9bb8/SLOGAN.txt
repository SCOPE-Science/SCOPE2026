For any finite parallel network with positive-slope affine road delays, the unique equilibrium between selfish drivers and a self-optimizing coordinated fleet is exactly the average of the two ordinary Wardrop assignments at demands \((1-\alpha)D\) and \((1+\alpha)D\); the selfish assignment itself equals the lower-demand Wardrop flow. This yields an explicit sharp coordination plateau and quadratic post-threshold inefficiency law on the natural two-road benchmark.
