Every dyadic column truncation of the normalized \(8\times8\) Walsh–Hadamard matrix has squared operator norm at most the largest root of \(32z^3-64z^2+16z-1=0\), with equality at precisely eight xor-translates of a single complete branch. The other \(65{,}528\) truncations have squared norm strictly below \(17/10\), established by a reproducible exhaustive integer certificate.
