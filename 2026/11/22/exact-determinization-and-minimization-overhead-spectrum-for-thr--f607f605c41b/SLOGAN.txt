For all \(2{,}359{,}296\) labeled three-state binary epsilon-free NFAs with singleton initial and final states, the complete joint spectrum of reachable powerset states and minimal-DFA states is determined. Among trim machines the sharp additive overhead is five, attained by exactly \(1{,}716\) machines; even when all eight subsets are reachable, four minimal states are necessary and achievable.
