# Scientific review: two-row rectangular-band dimensionalities

## Correctness
**PASS.** The equality-class version of the dimensionality axiom is shown equivalent to product saturation; multiplication in the rectangular band reduces this to \(R(A)\times C(B)\). The necessity and sufficiency of equal row and column color palettes are proved with both inclusions, yielding two exhaustive, disjoint classification forms. All split choices are counted modulo complement exactly once. Automorphism-orbit and congruence subcounts are separately proved. The standalone enumerator independently confirms all partitions and orbit partitions through five columns, including the strict seven-element example; those finite checks are supplementary, not an infinite proof. The adjoined external identity stays singleton, so the algebraic reduction covers the entire monoid.

## Originality
**PASS, with historical-priority qualification.** Jenča's first publicly posted preprint (November 13, 2026) establishes the general dimensionality property and the orbit construction but not a classification for rectangular-band monoids. Marrow–Misseldine classify semigroup Schur rings in limited dimensions and note rectangular bands; the publicly available full results *All Schur partitions of two-row rectangular bands are automorphic* and *Schur-ring classification of two-row rectangular bands* prove a balanced-strip classification for the coefficientwise Schur condition. Their \(n=3\) count \(13\) agrees with the orbit subcount but is strictly smaller than the present dimensionality count \(16\). The unbalanced three-column example violates Schur coefficient constancy (row coefficients \(2\) and \(1\)), proving noncoverage by the stronger prior condition. The available scientific records and related classifications do not give the full \(D_n\) census. An equivalent classification under older alternative terminology cannot be excluded absolutely.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** The studied invariant is a newly motivated algebraic notion of lax equivalence, whose quotients are relational monoids rather than ordinary congruence quotients. This theorem completely describes its parameter set for an infinite noncommutative family and supplies a closed weighted Stirling formula and labelled exponential generating function. It pinpoints the first strict separation of dimensionalities from all automorphism orbits within this family and distinguishes them from monoid congruences and stronger Schur partitions. These are natural structural boundaries, not an arbitrary finite slice or a recomputation of the preexisting Schur-ring table.

## Limitations and closest work
The result applies only to two-row identity-adjoined rectangular bands with labelled columns. It does not settle higher-row bands or classification up to monoid automorphism. The Schur-ring classification is the closest covering-risk source and was compared through its full publicly available statement and proof, not only by title. General priority remains conditional on discoverable earlier literature. The checking script exhausts partitions only through \(n=5\); universal validity rests on the written proof.
