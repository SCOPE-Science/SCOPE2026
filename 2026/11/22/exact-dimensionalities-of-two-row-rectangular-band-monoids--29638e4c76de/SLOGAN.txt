All dimensionalities of the identity-adjoined two-row rectangular band are classified by separated row partitions or independently two-colored column strips, giving exactly \(B_n+\sum_{k=1}^{n}\left\{\begin{smallmatrix}n\\k\end{smallmatrix}\right\}2^{n-k}\) labelled examples. Unlike the previously classified Schur/orbit subfamily, unequal two-color strips are valid: they first appear at \(n=3\), producing three non-orbit dimensionalities on a seven-element monoid.
