For the Ghosh–Bhuyan–Finkelstein negative-dependence copula, Chatterjee's two directed rank correlations are exactly \(\xi(U\mid V)=2\theta/(3+2\theta)\) and \(\xi(V\mid U)=2\theta^2/[(1+\theta)(1+2\theta)]\). Their strict directional gap has a unique sharp maximum \(0.0837781320\ldots\) at the positive root of \(8\theta^3+12\theta^2-3=0\).
