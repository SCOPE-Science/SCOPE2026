On every sphere, the complete Dirichlet-normalized tangential-gradient concentration spectrum of pure quadratic harmonics on a spherical lune has six explicit symmetry branches. Only on the two-sphere do the sharp best- and worst-observed modes switch symmetry in the interior, at apertures \(\arccos\sqrt{3/8}\) and \(\pi-\arccos\sqrt{3/8}\); in every higher sphere dimension the same planar-radial block controls both extremes.
