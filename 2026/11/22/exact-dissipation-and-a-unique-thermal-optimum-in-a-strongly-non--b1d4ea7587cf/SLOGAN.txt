A strongly directed Glauber–Ising ring has the same stationary Gibbs measure as its reversible counterpart but a strictly positive, exactly computable path-entropy-production rate, recoverable from the initial directional asymmetry of stationary spin-memory correlations. In the thermodynamic limit, dissipation has exactly one temperature optimum, and its dimensionless inverse temperature decreases strictly with directional coupling between two sharp universal bounds.
