Over the two unbounded equilateral base families in the homogeneous \(\{1,2,3\}\)-metric structure omitting triangles \((1,1,1)\) and \((1,2,2)\), every ordered-pair distance channel has an exact closed count. A distance-\(3\) base strictly shifts the entire three-channel type distribution toward longer new-pair distances for every base size at least two, with limiting mass \(4/5\) at distance \(3\) rather than \(3/5\).
