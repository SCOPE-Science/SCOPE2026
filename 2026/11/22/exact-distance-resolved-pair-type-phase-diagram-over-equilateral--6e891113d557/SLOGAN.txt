For complete ordered two-point types over any named equilateral base in the generic \(\{1,2,3\}\)-metric space, all three mutual-distance counts have exact formulas at every base size. As the base grows, the distance laws converge to \((2/9,4/9,1/3)\), \((0,1,0)\), or \((1/9,4/9,4/9)\) according as the old common distance is \(1\), \(2\), or \(3\).
