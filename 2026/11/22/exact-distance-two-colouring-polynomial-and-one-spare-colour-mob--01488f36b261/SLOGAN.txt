The square of the Heawood graph has an exact seven-term nonincidence-matching chromatic polynomial, with \(725760\) optimal seven-colourings and \(70156800\) named eight-colourings. One additional colour changes a completely frozen optimum configuration space into a connected one with exactly \(298045440\) recolouring edges and two exact mobility strata; connectivity itself was already implied by Merkel's general theorem.
