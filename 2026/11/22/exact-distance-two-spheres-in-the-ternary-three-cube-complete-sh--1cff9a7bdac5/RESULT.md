# Exact-distance-two spheres in the ternary three-cube: complete shattering, facets, and mod-two homology

## Finding
Let \(X=\{0,1,2\}^3\), with Hamming distance \(d_H\). For each center \(c\in X\) define the *exact-distance-two sphere* \(R_c=\{x\in X:d_H(x,c)=2\}\). The Boolean concept family is \(\mathcal R=\{R_c:c\in X\}\); it contains 27 distinct concepts, each of size 12. Let \(s_j\) count the unordered \(j\)-element sets \(S\subseteq X\) for which \(\{S\cap R_c:c\in X\}=2^S\). Then the **complete** shattering polynomial is
\[
\boxed{\Phi_{\mathcal R}(z)=\sum_j s_jz^j=1+27z+351z^2+2808z^3+3402z^4.}
\]
In particular \(\operatorname{VCdim}(\mathcal R)=4\), and there are no shattered supports of cardinality five or more.

The shattering complex \(\Delta=\{S\subseteq X:S\text{ is shattered by }\mathcal R\}\) is nonpure: it has precisely 3402 inclusion-maximal four-element faces and 540 inclusion-maximal three-element faces. Let a ternary word such as `012` denote the point with coordinates \((0,1,2)\). Under the full coordinate-and-symbol permutation group \(S_3\wr S_3\) (order 1296), its facets fall into **exactly six orbits**:

| Facet cardinality | Orbit size | Representative |
|---|---:|---|
| 4 | 1296 | `{000,001,012,120}` |
| 4 | 1296 | `{000,001,110,122}` |
| 4 | 648 | `{000,001,012,112}` |
| 4 | 162 | `{000,001,112,122}` |
| 3 | 324 | `{000,001,010}` |
| 3 | 216 | `{000,011,101}` |

Every shattered support is a subset of a group image of one of these six representatives; no additional maximal supports occur. Over \(\mathbb F_2\), its **only nonzero reduced homology ranks** are
\[
\boxed{\dim_{\mathbb F_2}\widetilde H_2(\Delta;\mathbb F_2)=570,\qquad \dim_{\mathbb F_2}\widetilde H_3(\Delta;\mathbb F_2)=1489.}
\]
No assertion is made about integral torsion or integral homotopy type.

## Assumptions and scope
The alphabet has exactly three symbols, the dimension is exactly three, and both support points and sphere centers range over the **entire** 27-word space. Every sphere consists of points at distance **equal to two**, not distance at most two. Supports are unordered subsets, and the empty support is shattered. All finite enumeration and homology statements concern this one fully specified finite incidence structure; none is extrapolated to higher dimensions or other alphabets. The simplicial complex includes the empty face. The group is generated by independent permutations of the symbols in each coordinate and permutations of the three coordinate positions.

## Proof
**1. Finite, exhaustive trace certificate.** Index ternary words lexicographically as \(x_0,\ldots,x_{26}\), and form the 27-by-27 Boolean incidence matrix \(A\), with
\[
A_{c,i}=[d_H(c,x_i)=2].
\]
For a fixed ordered listing \(S=(x_{i_0},\ldots,x_{i_{j-1}})\) of a support, compute the trace mask
\[
T(c,S)=\sum_{r=0}^{j-1}2^r A_{c,i_r}\in\{0,\ldots,2^j-1\}.
\]
The support is shattered if and only if \(\bigl|\{T(c,S):c\in X\}\bigr|=2^j\). This is an equivalence by the definition of shattering, not a heuristic or sampling method. The standalone Python and C++17 programs independently construct the incidence relation, iterate every one-, two-, three-, and four-element support, compute all masks over all 27 centers, and verify the exact counts \(27,351,2808,3402\). The empty support supplies the constant coefficient one. The two programs use independent representations of the ternary words and independent enumeration implementations.

**2. Upper bound and all dimensions.** Because the family contains only 27 concepts, a shattered support of five points would require \(2^5=32\) distinct traces, which is impossible. A support of larger size would contain a shattered five-point subset, which is equally impossible. Thus the exhaustive one-to-four enumeration covers **every possible** shattered support, and the positive fourth coefficient proves the sharp VC dimension four.

**3. Facet and orbit classification.** Every four-face is maximal by Step 2. Compute the set of three-faces that occur as a three-element subset of a four-face; exactly \(2808-540=2268\) three-faces have this property. The remaining 540 are maximal, since every pair is a face and each singleton is contained in a face. The group of 1296 coordinate-and-symbol permutations preserves Hamming distance and hence preserves the trace relation. The Python program enumerates all 1296 permutations explicitly and computes the orbit of each facet; the C++17 program independently generates the same group by eight elementary coordinate/symbol transformations and breadth-first orbit closure. Both find four four-facet orbits of sizes \(162,648,1296,1296\) and two three-facet orbits of sizes \(216,324\). Direct application of the listed permutations to the six displayed representatives gives the respective orbit sizes; the sizes sum exactly to 3402 and 540, so the displayed orbits exhaust **all facets**. Every face is contained in a facet of this finite complex, completing the geometric classification.

**4. Binary simplicial homology.** The nonempty face numbers in dimensions 0 through 3 are \((27,351,2808,3402)\). Over \(\mathbb F_2\), form boundary matrices by giving each face the column whose ones are its codimension-one subfaces. The full one-skeleton is the complete graph on 27 vertices, so \(\operatorname{rank}(\partial_1)=26\). Exact binary Gaussian elimination in **both** supplied programs gives
\[
\operatorname{rank}(\partial_2)=325,\qquad \operatorname{rank}(\partial_3)=1913.
\]
There are no degree-four chains, and the reduced complex has no other nonzero chain degrees. Rank-nullity then yields
\[
\widetilde\beta_0=27-26-1=0,\quad \widetilde\beta_1=351-26-325=0,
\]
\[
\widetilde\beta_2=2808-325-1913=570,\quad \widetilde\beta_3=3402-1913=1489.
\]
The independent Euler check gives reduced Euler characteristic \(27-351+2808-3402-1=-919=570-1489\). This proves exactly the asserted mod-two ranks; exact finite computations, not empirical tests, certify the enumerated ranks.

## Relationship to prior work
Adler, Benediktsson and Macpherson [1] establish bounds and exact thresholds for **distance-one open neighborhoods** of Hamming graphs, explicitly including ternary Hamming graphs; their relation is \(d_H(x,c)=1\), not the exact-distance-two relation considered here. A full coefficientwise census of open neighborhoods [4] gives \(1+27z+243z^2+216z^3\) for \(H(3,3)\); a separate closed-neighborhood census [5] likewise studies radius-one balls. Neither implies the present distance-two coefficients or facet topology. The exact VC theorem for **binary** distance-two spheres [3] makes no nonbinary claim. General pseudorandom-graph VC estimates [2] do not imply an exact 27-point trace enumeration, and the topological Hamming Rips and three-cut complexes in other reports have different face definitions. The six-orbit facet decomposition and the reduced homology refer specifically to the **shattered-support complex**, not a Vietoris–Rips, neighborhood, or cut complex.

## Limitations
This is a complete **finite computer-assisted proof** for exactly \(H(3,3)\). Its numerical face counts and binary boundary ranks rely on correct execution of the supplied exhaustive programs; two independent implementations limit but cannot mathematically eliminate implementation error. The exact mod-two homology ranks do not establish torsion-freeness, integral homology, or a sphere-wedge homotopy type. The literature searches included equivalent terms such as distance-two Hamming relation, second-distance graph and Hamming sphere, and inspected the closest identified full primary texts and prior published research; undiscovered equivalent work remains a historical-priority risk. No independently conducted mathematical audit is claimed.
