Exact Hamming-distance-two spheres on the 27-point ternary three-cube have shattering polynomial \(1+27z+351z^2+2808z^3+3402z^4\), hence VC dimension four. The nonpure shattered-support complex has six symmetry orbits of facets and reduced mod-two Betti ranks \(\widetilde\beta_2=570\) and \(\widetilde\beta_3=1489\), with all other reduced ranks zero.
