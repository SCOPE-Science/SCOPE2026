For every star \(K_{1,n}\) and every \(k\ge n+1\), the exact distance between named proper colourings is the leaf Hamming distance plus one center move and, precisely when a center-colour swap occurs at a leaf, one additional move. This sharp-threshold metric law yields a closed all-pairs distance polynomial and an exact count of diametral pairs; the law fails already at \(k=n\) for every \(n\ge3\).
