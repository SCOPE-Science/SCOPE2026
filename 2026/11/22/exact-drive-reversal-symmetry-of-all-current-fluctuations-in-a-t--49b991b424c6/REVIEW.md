PASS. A three-state tilted exclusion generator has an exact cubic characteristic polynomial whose coefficients are invariant under simultaneous reversal of the field and counting variable. Perron–Frobenius identifies the dominant eigenvalue with the long-time scaled cumulant-generating function. Symbolic verification reconstructs the matrix and checks the polynomial; no finite-time equality is asserted.

## Originality
PASS with residual publication-priority risk. The closest 2025 work concerns mean bias-response and its site-disorder symmetry criteria. The checked literature and records did not state the full all-cumulant tilted-spectrum parity for the two-particle three-site site-trap exclusion process. A generic three-state Markov-cycle identity could imply the result; this is the principal residual risk.

## Value
PASS. Current symmetry is substantially stronger as an identity of the entire long-time fluctuation spectrum than as an identity of mean transport. This smallest site-disordered interacting ring provides an exact structural boundary for diagnostics of rectification: every current cumulant and the large-deviation rate function preserve field reversal even when different fillings have different mean currents. It is not a statement about larger rings.

## Limitations
A finite, irreducible ring is assumed, with exactly two particles and site-dependent departure rates. The claim covers asymptotic current statistics and not finite-time distributions. Equivalent unindexed spectral formulations remain possible.
