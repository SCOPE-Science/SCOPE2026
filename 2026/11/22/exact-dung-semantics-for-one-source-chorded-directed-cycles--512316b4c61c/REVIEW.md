Its endpoint parity condition is both necessary and sufficient; the resulting nonempty complete extensions directly satisfy stable semantics. Counting last-chord positions yields the rational formulas without computational extrapolation. `verify.py` separately checks all 1022 labeled chord subsets for orders 4 through 12 and the chord-set census through order 24.

**Value (PASS).** This is a complete classification over an exponentially large, naturally parametrized perturbation family of a minimal parity obstruction to stable argumentation. The last-chord parity alone decides coherence despite arbitrarily many earlier chords; exact model counts yield a limiting one-third frequency, and nonempty completeness implies stability even when the graph contains both parities of directed cycles. The combination refines the bare parity dichotomy and the broad sufficient conditions without claiming general graph algorithms.
