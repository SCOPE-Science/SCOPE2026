In directed attack cycles with every extra chord departing from a single argument, all nonempty complete Dung extensions are stable and a one-bit parity test on the furthest chord gives the entire preferred/stable semantics. Exactly \((4^{r-1}-1)/3\) of the \(4^{r-1}\) possible chord sets restore stability on an odd cycle of length \(2r+1\), approaching one third.
