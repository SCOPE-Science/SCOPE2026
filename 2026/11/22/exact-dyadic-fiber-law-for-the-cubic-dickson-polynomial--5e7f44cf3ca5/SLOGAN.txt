For the cubic Dickson polynomial \(D_3(X,1)=X^3-3X\), every even residue modulo \(2^k\) occurs and every odd residue is omitted, while a complete four-branch square-root law determines exactly how many preimages each even target has. The largest fiber is \(1+2^{\lceil k/2\rceil}\) for \(k\ge6\), achieved at exactly two targets for odd \(k\ge7\) and eight for even \(k\ge8\).
