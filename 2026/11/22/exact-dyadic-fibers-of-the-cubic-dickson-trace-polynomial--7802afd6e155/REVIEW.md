## Correctness: PASS

The statement fixes \(F(x)=x^3-3x\) and defines all finite and two-adic fibers without normalization ambiguity. For even \(x\), the factor \(x^2+xy+y^2-3\) is odd, yielding a bijective even-to-even branch at every precision. For odd inputs, the exact factorizations \(F(x)+2=(x-1)^2(x+2)\) and \(F(x)-2=(x+1)^2(x-2)\) produce disjoint valuation shells. Their finite images exhaust the specified unit balls, proving two odd roots per noncenter shell and the precise finite fibers, including truncated centers. Exact counts reproduce every target modulo \(2^k\), \(4\le k\le17\), using a separate direct enumeration. The unrestricted theorem follows from the proof, not the finite tests.
