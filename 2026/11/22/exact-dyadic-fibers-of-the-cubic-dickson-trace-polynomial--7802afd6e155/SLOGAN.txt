For the cubic Dickson trace map \(F(x)=x^3-3x\), every dyadic modulus \(2^k\) with \(k\ge4\) has an explicit, target-by-target exact fiber formula, including the singular towers above the critical values \(\pm2\). On \(\mathbb Z_2\), every even target has one even preimage; exactly \(1/48\) of all targets have two additional odd preimages, while \(\pm2\) have one additional odd preimage each.
