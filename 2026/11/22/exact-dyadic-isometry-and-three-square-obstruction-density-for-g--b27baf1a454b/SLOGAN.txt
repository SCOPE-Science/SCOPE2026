The parameter-two generalized derangement sequence is an exact two-adic isometry: \(\nu_2(D_m^{(2)}-D_n^{(2)})=\nu_2(m-n)\) for distinct indices, so it permutes the residues modulo every power of two. Consequently its values fail to be sums of three squares at precisely one disjoint progression for each two-adic obstruction layer, with natural density \(1/6\) and logarithmic counting error.
