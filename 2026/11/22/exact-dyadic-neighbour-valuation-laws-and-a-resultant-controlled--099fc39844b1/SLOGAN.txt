For positive odd \(a,b\) and positive even \(c\), all adjacent pairs of valuations \(\big(\nu_2(an^2+bn+c),\nu_2(a(n+1)^2+b(n+1)+c)\big)\) have an exact one-residue-class frequency law governed by \(d=\nu_2(|a^2-b^2+4ac|)-1\) (infinite when the numerator is zero). The lag-one covariance is exactly \(\frac12-3/2^d\), negative precisely for \(a^2-b^2+4ac\equiv8\pmod{16}\) and positive otherwise.
