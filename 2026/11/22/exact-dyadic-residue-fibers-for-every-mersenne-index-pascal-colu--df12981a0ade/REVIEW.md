The polynomial factorization on each of the \(2^s\) input cosets is exact. For the first \(2^s-1\) branches the extracted power of two is \(2^{s-\nu_2(a+1)}\); the last branch maps onto the odd outputs with exact scale two. In both cases the remaining map has odd difference quotient, proving an isometry and a bijection modulo every power of two. The half-period obstruction and parity boundary imply the least period. Counting branches gives all asserted fibers; integrating them gives the Haar law. An integer verifier independently checks finite cases, but infinite validity rests on the proof.

## Originality — PASS (within inspected evidence)
The 2023 Lane paper concerns complete-row distributions modulo a prime; the full 2001 Barat–Grabner author manuscript concerns all binomial coefficients with both indices varying, and shows prime-free part uniformity in that two-index setting. Neither states or implies the exact Mersenne fixed-column all-power fiber hierarchy. The previously indexed third-column theorem is the already-known \(s=2\) instance, and has not been counted as novel; the family theorem supplies the infinite range \(s\ge3\). Results about the fifth column are not stronger results for this family. A hidden equivalent within general prime-power congruences remains a genuine priority risk. The online third-column source could be inspected only at summary level.
