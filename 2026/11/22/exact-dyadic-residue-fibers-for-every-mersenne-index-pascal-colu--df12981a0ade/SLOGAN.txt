Every Mersenne-index Pascal column \(\binom{n}{2^s-1}\) attains every residue modulo every power of two, with the exact least period \(2^{s+k-1}\) modulo \(2^k\). At sufficiently high precision each odd residue has exactly one index per period, while even-residue fiber sizes form the exact hierarchy \(1,5,21,85,\ldots\), determined by the capped dyadic valuation.
