# Mathematical review

## Correctness
The claim is supported by an all-level matrix-lifting proof, two nonsingular isometry families, and explicit quadratic conjugacies for both critical branches. The root-count factor is elementary and includes the zero and low-exponent boundary cases. Exact independent enumeration through \(2^{14}\) supports, but does not replace, the proof; numerical critical values are not treated as proof of infinite congruences.

## Originality
The published dyadic-image theorem supplies the least period and the first critical branch, and explicitly does not address per-target multiplicities. The earlier \(2\)-adic valuation and recurrence-period literature is narrower in target scope. Both newly analyzed critical and parity branches are required to compute individual fibers. An unindexed equivalent law remains a priority risk; the complete text of Klaška's period paper was not accessible in the present comparison.

## Value
An exact local distribution of all target preimages is a natural stronger invariant than residue coverage or least period. The law identifies every collision multiplicity and explains the nonuniform collision mechanism through two squared-coordinate branches, with finite and unbounded fiber families. This is not a mere rewrite of the known set of attained residues.

## Limitations
The conclusion is for the standard Tribonacci sequence and moduli that are powers of two; arbitrary initial triples and odd primes are open here. Self-review only, with the independent channel still unperformed.
