# Mathematical assessment

## Correctness
PASS. The defining binomial-to-periodic-run identity is explicitly attributed to the inspected 2023 primary paper (2024 open-access edition, Theorem 15). The remaining proof is self-contained: the three acceptance-state sets have exhaustive bit recursion (1), their maxima and boundary distances are exact, and their gap-multiset recursion (2) yields all integer coefficients and excludes every other gap. The smallest simple tribonacci pole proves the stated dyadic-prefix distribution; the geometric lower bound proves infinite mean. The accompanying checker independently enumerates 301 original binomial sums and 121395 adjacent gaps. Finite evidence is not substituted for the universal proof.

## Originality
PASS within investigated sources. The closest primary source gives the periodic run-length transform, not the complete return-gap enumerators. Wu's earlier Fibonacci run transform has precisely the same parity support, a genuine structural equivalence fully acknowledged in RESULT.md, but his inspected theorem does not state or logically imply the explicit gaps and tribonacci frequency generating functions without an additional gap-classification argument. Published Baum–Sweet gap laws are different supports and different spectra. published-finding corpus searches for the exact binomial expression, periodic support, gap list, Fibonacci parity and equivalent formulations found no dominating result; absence from search is not proof of novelty. Residual risk: an unindexed return-gap theorem for the support-equivalent Fibonacci transform.

## Value
PASS. For a natural published binomial-parity sequence, individual support membership and 2-regularity do not provide the distances between successive positive indices. This theorem derives an infinite lacunary list of exactly the possible distances, exact all-level multiplicity series, and a normalized distribution with a nontrivial infinite-mean phase. These constitute an interpretable complete two-point arithmetic invariant rather than an arbitrary parameter slice or simple relabeling.
