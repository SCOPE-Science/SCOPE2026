For the 2023 binomial-parity transform \(T(n)=\sum_k[\binom{-2n+8k}{n+k}\binom nk\bmod2]\), the gaps between consecutive indices where \(T(n)=1\) have precisely the values \(1,2,4,5,9,18,33,65,130,\ldots\), with exact tribonacci-denominator generating functions for every dyadic-prefix multiplicity. The limiting dyadic-prefix gap law has explicit probabilities and infinite mean, even though every finite-prefix mean is finite.
