For odd harmonic sums \(O_n=\sum_{j=1}^{n}(2j-1)^{-1}\), the exact dyadic distance between two values equals that between their squared indices: \(\nu_2(O_b-O_a)=\nu_2(b^2-a^2)\) for \(0\le a<b\). Thus every modular collision is classified by a square congruence, extending the previously known fixed-start valuation to all shifted intervals.
