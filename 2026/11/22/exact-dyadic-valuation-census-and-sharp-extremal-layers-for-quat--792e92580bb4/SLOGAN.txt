The quaternary Fuss–Catalan numbers have an exact rational generating function for their full \(2\)-adic valuation histograms over every binary index block. Their maximum valuation is \(2\lfloor L/2\rfloor\), attained once at even block-depth \(L\) and \(L+1\) times at odd depth, while the valuations have variance asymptotic to \(L/4\) and a Gaussian limit.
