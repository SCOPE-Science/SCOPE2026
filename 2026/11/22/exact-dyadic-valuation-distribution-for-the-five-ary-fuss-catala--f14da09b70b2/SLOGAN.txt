For the five-ary Fuss–Catalan sequence, a single explicit rational generating function determines every two-adic valuation multiplicity on every initial dyadic index block. The valuation has exact mean \(L/2-1+(1+(-1)^{\lfloor L/2\rfloor})/2^{L+1}\) and asymptotic variance \(21L/20\), with a Gaussian limit.
