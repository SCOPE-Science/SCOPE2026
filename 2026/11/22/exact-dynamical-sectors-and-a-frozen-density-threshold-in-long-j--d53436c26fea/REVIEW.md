# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The complete marked-class classification follows from conservation of each gap modulo the hop length, nearest-neighbor transfer connectivity of mobile vacancy units, and a full tour of one vacancy unit translating the marked root by one hop length. The conditional product weights satisfy exact global balance. The sector-current identity is a bijection between weighted compositions of two consecutive totals. Frozen counts follow from bounded compositions; local central limit theory yields their entropy. A standalone checker independently exhausts small directed state graphs and verifies rational stationarity, current and frozen counts. The theorem carefully distinguishes marked sectors from physical unmarked configurations; no finite test is taken as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk

The primary full text (arXiv:2604.01796v1), including theorem, model, current, discussion, and appendices, supplies a Gibbs invariant measure and an ensemble current, not a complete conserved-residue class decomposition or a frozen entropy count. Its own zero-range description is cited as prior work. The related single-step interaction paper, arXiv:2409.05017v2, does not have the fixed-hop residue obstruction. The earlier traffic literature explains the dynamics, but accessible comparisons do not state the current theorem. Specialist unindexed work and an older look-ahead article not fully obtainable may affect bibliographic priority.

## Value — PASS

A fixed-hop system whose Gibbs law is stationary can still have exponentially many blocked preparations and dynamically disjoint active sectors. The sharp threshold and current ratio distinguish Gibbs invariance from ergodicity and establish experimentally meaningful preparation-dependent transport at identical particle density. The result is a full structural classification on the natural marked cover, not a marginal numerical adjustment.

## Limits

All claims concern finite rings with positive two-way hop rates and fixed integer hop length. Only unmarked frozen states, not unmarked moving sectors, are enumerated; the entropy requires density in the open interval. No hydrodynamic evolution or uniqueness across sectors is asserted. Unindexed literature can change publication priority.
