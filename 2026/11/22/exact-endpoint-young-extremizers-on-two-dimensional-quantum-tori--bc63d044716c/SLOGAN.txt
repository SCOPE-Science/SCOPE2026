For finite Fourier polynomials on two quantum tori, equality in the sharp \(L_2\times L_2\to L_\infty\) convolution Young inequality is characterized exactly by matched coefficient magnitudes, a commutator-isotropic support lattice, and an aligned twisted character. Three noncollinear modes can saturate precisely when the rational output-deformation denominator divides their lattice area; irrational output deformation forbids every such triple.
