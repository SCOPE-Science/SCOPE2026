# Scientific comparison

**Correctness.** The coprime primary splitting isolates the nilpotent multiplication by \(Y\). For \(t\le q\), reducing the monic relation modulo \(Y^t\) gives the cokernel with independent coefficients modulo \(p^2\) below degree \(d\) and modulo \(p\) at degrees \(d\) through \(t-1\). This determines the kernel cardinality without asserting preservation of a truncated span under multiplication by a unit. The same relation determines the image ideal after time \(q\). The finite tests complement rather than replace this argument.

**Originality.** The maximum and the leading-order prime-square relation already follow from *Exact maximal transient lengths for cyclic neighbour-sum dynamics*, Proof §§1–2. The additional result uses the relation for an intermediate-cokernel count at each depth, exhibiting the change in growth rate and the complete entrance distribution. Dular's field case and Lewis–Tefft's maximum-time bound are not this census. Unindexed equivalents and inaccessible related work remain possible.

**Value.** In the repeated-primary regime the exact distribution distinguishes the two prime-square growth phases and quantifies how often the known sharp worst case is reached. These counts are not encoded by the nilpotency index alone.
