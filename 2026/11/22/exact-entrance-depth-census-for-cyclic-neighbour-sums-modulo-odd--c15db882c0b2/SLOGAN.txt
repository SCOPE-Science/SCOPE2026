For every odd prime \(p\) and even length \(n\) divisible by \(p\), cyclic neighbour sums modulo \(p^2\) have the exact cumulative first-periodic-entry count \(p^{2(n-p^s)+\min\{2t,t+p^{s-1},2p^s\}}\), where \(s=\nu_p(n)\). The population grows by a factor \(p^2\) per step, then by \(p\) per step, with an exact sharp change at \(t=p^{s-1}\).
