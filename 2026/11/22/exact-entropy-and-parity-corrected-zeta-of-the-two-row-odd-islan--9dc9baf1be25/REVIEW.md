# Scientific comparison: two-row odd-island zeta

**Correctness.** Components spanning the current column are tracked by their parity and visible shape. A periodic label word with a break has a unique period-matched state lift if it is admissible. Without a break the occupied component is infinite and admissible regardless of parity, but the number of period-matched lifts is two or zero according to the parity of the number of singleton columns. The signed no-break trace subtracts exactly the difference and gives the rational determinant quotient. The checker provides finite corroboration, not a proof for all periods.

**Originality.** *Exact two-rail entropy and closed-island counts for the odd-component shift* gives the same parity matrix and entropy, and counts closed finite words rather than periodic configurations. Neither the matrix nor the entropy is claimed as new. The distinction is the explicit periodic-point correction and resulting zeta for the bilateral, open-vertical-boundary strip. Historical priority for the periodic formula is not certified.

**Value.** The periodic census is a benchmark at the first width where rungs affect connectivity. It shows explicitly why a right-resolving parity cover cannot be used without correction to count fixed points of the visible shift. No claim about the full planar entropy or arbitrary strip widths is made.
