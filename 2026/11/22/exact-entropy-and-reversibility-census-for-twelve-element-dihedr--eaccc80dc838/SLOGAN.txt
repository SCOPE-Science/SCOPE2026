Surjective group cellular automata on the twelve-element dihedral full shift have a unique nonabelian automorphism-carrying input and an arbitrary nonzero binary Laurent-polynomial central update. On \(r\) consecutive allowed sites they have exactly \(r^2\) entropy values, with explicit multiplicities and a sharp reversible fraction \(r/(2^r-1)\); local surjectivity alone admits exactly \(6r(2^r-2)\) false positives.
