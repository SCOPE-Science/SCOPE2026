The number of binary linear universal partial words with their unique joker in position three is exactly determined at every order: the exceptional counts are \(0,4,672\) at orders \(3,4,5\), and thereafter the count is \(2^{2^{n-1}-n}\) times an explicit three-periodic correction to \(3/14\). A forced period-three prefix and a rank-six de Bruijn arborescence calculation establish the formula.
