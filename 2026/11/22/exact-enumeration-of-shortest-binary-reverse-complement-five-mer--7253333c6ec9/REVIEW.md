# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The claim separates a previously known length minimum from a new count and equality classification. The sixteen five-mer reverse-complement pairs imply length at least twenty. A displayed twenty-bit cover establishes attainability. The proof derives a graph-theoretic closure condition for any shortest binary odd-order covering trail by balancing reverse-complement vertex pairs and excluding unit endpoint imbalances. A completely specified sixteen-pair orientation search has 52 balanced selections, eighteen strongly connected candidates and canonical Euler-tour multiplicities eight times one, eight times two, and twice four, giving thirty-two circular words. Every such word has sixteen inequivalent linear cuts. The explicit eight four-member symmetry classes account for all thirty-two circular words. A separate literal enumeration of all 1,048,576 length-twenty words reproduces the outcome. Both finite verification methods replayed from the included source.

The precise enumerative invariant is expressly motivated by the open optimal-sequence counting question raised in the 2013 RC-universal-covering work, with present relevance to strand-unoriented sequence assembly.
