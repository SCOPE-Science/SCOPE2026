For an indexed ternary linear word observed through its two endpoints and all nine ordered adjacent-pair counts, the number of uniquely reconstructible length-\(n\) words is exactly \(3n^2+27n-63\) for every \(n\ge6\). A 247-state exact pattern-obstruction automaton gives a rational all-length enumerator and proves that the reconstructible fraction decays as \(3n^2/3^n\).
