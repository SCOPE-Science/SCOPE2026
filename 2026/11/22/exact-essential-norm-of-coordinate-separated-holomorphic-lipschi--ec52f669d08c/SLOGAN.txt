For every coordinate-separated holomorphic self-map of the complex \(\ell_p\) unit ball with relatively compact image strictly inside the ball, the associated holomorphic-Lipschitz-free operator has **exact essential norm** \(\limsup_{j\to\infty}\sup_{z\in\mathbb D}|\varphi_j'(z)|\). Every normalized essential-norm ratio in \([0,1]\) occurs, and for \(1<p<\infty\) all these operators are weakly compact, including the noncompact ones.
