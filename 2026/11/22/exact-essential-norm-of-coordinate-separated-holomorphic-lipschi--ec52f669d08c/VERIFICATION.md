# Verification and limits

The argument is analytic. Its critical checks are the compactness of finite-coordinate linearizations, the derivative estimate for the canonical evaluation embedding on the strict interior ball, and the barycenter lower bound against arbitrary compact perturbations. These are spelled out in `RESULT.md` with exact assumptions.

The optional `check_diag.py` supplies finite numerical consistency checks for the explicit power-family examples; those checks are not presented as proofs of the infinite-dimensional identity. No external proof certificate or independent validation is claimed.
