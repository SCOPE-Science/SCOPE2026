## Correctness
**PASS.** The primary source's matching-to-vertex correspondence makes the four-state transfer exhaustive. Each transition is given explicitly and weighted by the actual Hamming difference; the reduced characteristic polynomial and initial polynomials prove the cubic recurrence. Coefficient extraction proves the two outer-shell counts. The strict Perron spectral gap and analytic moment-generating-function expansion prove the Gaussian limit with explicitly derived positive variance. The independent exact checker reproduced all distances for the stated finite sample; its finite scope is not substituted for the proof.

## Originality
**PASS, subject to bounded historical-priority risk.** The 2020 primary construction establishes vertices, not their chord distribution. An existing general-shard theorem already gives this family's diameter; that diameter is cited as prior coverage, not a novel claim. Existing general-shard enclosing-sphere results do not imply distance multiplicities. A closely related four-state distance-spectrum theorem concerns a larger zero–one linked type-C interval polytope whose top-cardinality face is a translate of the shard polytope; its total enumerator mixes within-face and cross-face chords and no statement in the inspected full result isolates this rank-conditioned recurrence or fluctuation constants. The available primary text and relevant published results were materially inspected, and targeted indexed searches were compared for implication. An equivalent formula under another matroid or language remains possible.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** The entire metric pair distribution in a canonical infinite polytope family is a natural geometric invariant. The exact second shell measures near-diametral geometry beyond a known diameter theorem, and the Gaussian law distinguishes those exponentially sparse extremal chords from typical vertex separations. The result is not a routine reparameterization of the prior all-shard diameter theorem.
