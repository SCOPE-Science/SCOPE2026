For the standard edge-coordinate unit-flow polytope of a length-one/length-two zigzag directed graph, every squared distance between uniformly sampled vertices has an exact rational bivariate generating function. The Euclidean distance, centered at \(\sqrt{2n/\sqrt5}\), converges without further rescaling to a normal law with variance \((9-2\sqrt5)/20\); this metric differs from the known Fibonacci-cube Hamming distance.
