In the standard Euclidean Cayley polytope joining an \(n\)-cube to an \(n\)-simplex, every facet has an exact area and all facets of a fixed nonempty subset rank \(k\) together have area \(\sqrt{k^2-k+1}/k!\), independent of the ambient dimension. Thus exponentially many facets coexist with bounded total boundary area and a factorially tight area-weighted rank law; polynomially many facets capture any prescribed asymptotic proportion of the boundary.
