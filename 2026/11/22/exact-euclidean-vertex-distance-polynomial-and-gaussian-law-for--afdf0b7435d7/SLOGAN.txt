For the canonical cycle graphical zonotope, a three-state transfer matrix gives the **entire** squared Euclidean distance distribution between two uniform vertices in every dimension. Squared distances satisfy \((D_n-n)/\sqrt{2n}\Longrightarrow N(0,1)\), while ordinary Euclidean distances have constant-order Gaussian fluctuations \(\sqrt{D_n}-\sqrt n\Longrightarrow N(0,1/2)\).
