For uniform samples, the covariance between a one-dimensional Euler characteristic curve and unbiased sample variance is exactly \(t(1-t)^n[2+(n+1)t]/12\), strictly positive at every nontrivial radius. Individual rank-specific gap indicators can instead have negative or positive covariance, with an exact one-switch threshold and reversals for the first internal gap precisely at sample sizes \(6\) through \(11\).
