# Review of the explicit reflection-product mixing law

## Correctness — PASS

The normalized domain is all odd prime-power fields and all positive integers \(k\); the sampling set contains exactly the noncentral split involutions, not scalar involutions. The conic \(a^2+b^2-tab=1\) proves all three two-factor fiber cases and nonzero support. The full irreducible character table of the ambient general linear group yields three surviving even-power spectral sectors: trivial determinant characters, Steinberg twists and same-parity principal series. The coefficient functions are determined by direct two-factor counts, so the four-stratum all-time identity follows by convolution powers. Signs of the density deviations are explicitly checked and the exact type multiplicities give the asserted total variation. Orthogonality of the two deviation functions gives chi-square. The checker directly enumerates all matrix products over the fields of orders \(3,5,7,9,11\); it checks pair multiplicities, repeated convolution, all targets, exact total variation and chi-square at the documented lengths. No infinite inference relies on enumeration. Residual correctness risk is dependence on standard, published general-linear character values; those values and parity counts were independently matched to the case \(\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)\).

## Originality — PASS, subject to historical-priority risk

Singh–Thakur (arXiv:0804.1245, Sections 3.1–3.2) discuss strong reality and existence of product decompositions, not target-wise counts or even-time mixing. Digne–Michel (1991, Section 15.9) furnish the full character table; its entries alone are inputs to the new evaluated class-function formula, and no exact total-variation or chi-square identity for this walk was located there. Buell et al. (2017) establish geometric symmetric-space identifications, not iterated random reflections. Ballantyne–Rowley (2016 preprint) treat involutions over even fields and order conditions, not the odd-field reflection class. The more recent double-coset Markov-chain work studies unipotent transvections; Pantangi–Sin (2026, Section 3.1) reproduce the standard character table for a continuous-time graph problem, rather than the discrete reflection walk. Searches of published mathematical records for reflection-product fibers, fixed-target two-involution counts, parity-twisted character decompositions and exact random-walk total variation identified related but not dominating results. Accessible historical claims were inspected for overlap only. An unindexed prior derivation from the classical table remains possible; this is a self-review, not independent priority certification.

## Value — PASS

The uniform conjugacy class of split involutions is a canonical generating measure for the determinant-one degree-two group after two steps. Its all-time pointwise law, exact total variation and chi-square, and positive sharp rate are natural invariants of that walk. The two-factor support is all of the special linear group; after just two reflections its deviation from uniform is asymptotic to \(2/q\), a concrete finite-field mixing phenomenon. The exceptional disappearance of a spectral sector at \(q=3\) changes the exact rate. The formulas are complete for every odd prime power and every positive number of pairs; this is not a selected small numerical example or an unmotivated finite slice.
