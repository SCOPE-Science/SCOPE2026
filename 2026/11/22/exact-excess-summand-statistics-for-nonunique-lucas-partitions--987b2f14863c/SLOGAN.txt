For nonconsecutive Lucas partitions with indices at most \(m\), exactly \(F_{m-2j-2}\) doubly represented integers have \(j\) more summands in the representation containing both indices \(0,2\) than in the canonical one, in the stated admissible range. This exact Fibonacci spectrum has a geometric limiting law with mean \(\varphi^{-1}\) and variance \(1\).
