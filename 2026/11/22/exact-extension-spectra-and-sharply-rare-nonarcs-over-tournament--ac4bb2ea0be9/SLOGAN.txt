Over any named \(n\)-point tournament in the homogeneous digraph forbidding independent triples, every fixed-arity tuple-type count is an exact finite sum of powers of weighted independence-polynomial values. The first three exponential layers are governed by zero, one, or two adjacent nonarcs among the new points, and the probability of any nonarc is asymptotic to \(\tfrac12\binom r2(8/9)^n\) for each fixed arity \(r\ge2\).
