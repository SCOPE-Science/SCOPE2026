At each of the three known minimum degrees for Borwein multiples of Lehmer's square, the complete extremizers are classified: exactly four unrestricted polynomials at degree \(34\), two self-reciprocal polynomials at degree \(38\), and four antireciprocal polynomials at degree \(41\). The degree-\(34\) solutions form one orbit under sign and reversal, while the degree-\(41\) antireciprocal solutions consist of two genuinely distinct sign classes.
