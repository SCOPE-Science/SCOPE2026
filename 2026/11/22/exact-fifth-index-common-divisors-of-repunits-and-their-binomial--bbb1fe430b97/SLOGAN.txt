At index five, a repunit and the binomial transform of its base-repunit sequence share no primes except \(5\) and \(11\), each to exponent at most one; the exact gcd depends only on the base modulo \(55\). For higher-order Mersenne numbers with base \(2^k\), the gcd has exact exponent period \(20\), with the exceptional prime \(11\) arising from a rigid affine coincidence of fifth roots.
