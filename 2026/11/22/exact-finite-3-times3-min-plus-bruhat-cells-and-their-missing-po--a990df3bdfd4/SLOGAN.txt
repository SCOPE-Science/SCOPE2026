For every finite real \(3\times3\) matrix, min-plus upper-triangular Bruhat decomposability is exactly characterized by four nonnegative \(2\times2\) defects together with a fifth inequality or one of three boundary equalities; only the \(321\) and \(231\) cells occur. Explicit factorizations cover every included point, while a small concrete matrix disproves the four-defect sufficiency condition even with all four defects strictly positive printed in arXiv:2611.03896v1.
