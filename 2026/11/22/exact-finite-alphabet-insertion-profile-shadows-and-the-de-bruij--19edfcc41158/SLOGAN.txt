For every alphabet of size \(q\ge3\), the number of ordered-digram frequency profiles reachable by inserting one symbol into a word has an exact pointwise formula and sharp maximum \(q(q-1)^2+3q-2\). The first maximizers have length \(q^2+1\), are exactly the linearizations of order-two de Bruijn cycles, and number \((q!)^q\); the maximum is attained by a proportion tending to \(1/q\) of uniformly random long words.
