In a finite logistic birth–death population with immigration, two separated stationary count peaks appear exactly beyond an explicit sharp growth-to-death threshold, provided the per-empty-site immigration rate is below \(\mu/N\). The threshold is an integer-lattice correction to a closed square-root curve, and under immigration \(\alpha=\eta\mu/N\) it equals \(1+2\sqrt{1-\eta}/\sqrt N+(1-\eta)/N+O_\eta(N^{-3/2})\).
