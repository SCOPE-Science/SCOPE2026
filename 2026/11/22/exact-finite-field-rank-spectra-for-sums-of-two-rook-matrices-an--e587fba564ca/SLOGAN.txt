The complete finite-field rank spectrum of a sum of two partial permutation matrices is an explicit exponential generating function, with characteristic-two cycles producing precisely one additional rank defect. Among independent uniform partial permutations, the probability of an invertible sum has stretched-exponential scale \(\exp(-(4-2\sqrt2)\sqrt n)\), multiplied by \(n^{-1/4}\) in characteristic two but by a constant in odd characteristic.
