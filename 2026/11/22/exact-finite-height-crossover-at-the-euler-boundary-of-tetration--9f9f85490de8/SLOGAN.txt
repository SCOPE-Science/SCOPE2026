Odd-height tetration CDFs have an exact, two-sided boundary-layer crossover at the Euler threshold: when \(-\log x=e(1+a/k)\), the scaled height-\(2k+1\) CDF deficit converges locally uniformly to \(e^{-1}\sqrt{6a/(1-e^{-2a})}\), with its continuous value \(\sqrt3/e\) at \(a=0\). The same theorem gives the limiting CDF's one-sided square-root branch and the exact asymptotic probability mass in every finite critical-window interval.
