The convex hull of every outward logarithmic-spiral arc \(\gamma(t)=e^{at}(\cos t,\sin t)\) remembers only its last \(\ell(a)\in(\pi,2\pi)\) radians, joined by one chord; its normalized perimeter and area become exactly constant after that lag. At the contact threshold, perimeter has a cubic one-sided deficit while area has a quadratic one-sided deficit.
