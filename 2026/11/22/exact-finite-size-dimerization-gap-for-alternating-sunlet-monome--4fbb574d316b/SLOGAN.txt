For an alternating positive-weight monomer–dimer sunlet, the exact gap between its two positive matching-zero bands satisfies \(g_m^2=(\sqrt a-\sqrt b)^2+4\sin^2(\pi/(4m))\). The formula separates persistent weight staggering from finite-cycle quantization and yields the critical scaling \(m g_m\to\sqrt{\kappa^2+\pi^2/4}\) when \(m(\sqrt a-\sqrt b)\to\kappa\).
