For every cyclic real-spectrum drift with rank-one forcing, the integrating-factor SSPRK2 and SSPRK3 Lyapunov covariances attain full rank after exactly \(d-1\) and \(\lceil(d-1)/2\rceil\) time steps, independent of step length. On the controllable three-state nilpotent chain, one Simpson step is already positive definite but overestimates the exact covariance determinant by the constant factor \(5/2\) at every positive step length.
