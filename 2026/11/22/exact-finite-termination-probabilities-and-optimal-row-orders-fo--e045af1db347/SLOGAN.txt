For equispaced planar Kaczmarz equations, a complete randomly reshuffled sweep solves every initial error exactly with probability tending to \(1-e^{-1}\) when the number of equations is even, but never does so when it is odd. The exact finite permutation law, the sharp \(e^{-1}/n\) asymptotic advantage over independent row draws, and all globally best deterministic row orders follow from a geometric perfect-matching structure.
