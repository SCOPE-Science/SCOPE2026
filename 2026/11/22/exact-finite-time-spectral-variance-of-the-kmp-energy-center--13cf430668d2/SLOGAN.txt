For a localized energy release in the microscopic one-dimensional KMP chain, the center-of-energy variance has an exact positive finite-time spectral density supported on \((0,4)\). It is strictly increasing and concave, and equals \(\sqrt{t/\pi}+1/4-11/(16\sqrt{\pi t})+O(t^{-3/2})\) at long times for unit-rate bonds; the nonzero constant is a lattice correction beyond the known continuum leading term.
