For an independent complex-Gaussian Fourier polynomial, the fourth power of the normalized \(L^4/L^2\) factor has exact mean \(2n/(n+1)\), exact variance \(4(n-1)(2n^2-2n-1)/(3(n+1)^2(n+2)(n+3))\), and Gaussian fluctuations with limiting variance \(8/3\) after \(\sqrt n\) scaling. Exact self-normalization cancels the quadratic Gaussian chaos and leaves a fourth-chaos central limit theorem.
