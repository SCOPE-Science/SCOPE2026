# Review of the finite-window diagonal-semigroup frame theorem

## Correctness — PASS

The real exponential system is a Chebyshev system at any distinct positive rates. Andréief integration proves strict total positivity of its finite-window moment Gram matrix, so the inverse has exactly the alternating sign pattern needed for a universal Rayleigh lower bound on inverse frame norm. Positive normalized row sums produce an attaining Perron eigenvector; equality forces those same modal magnitudes. Vandermonde rescaling and the shifted-Legendre squared-norm formula prove the sharp short-window coefficient; the Cauchy limit is continuous. The embedded reproducibility program passed 1,013 numerical/exact checks, which are not a substitute for these analytical arguments.

## Originality — PASS, with bibliographic qualification

Bailey–Gallardo-Gutiérrez–Partington (arXiv:2611.13867v1) provide qualitative continuous-frame and cogenerator criteria. Chen–Belabbas (arXiv:1610.08885) already prove the *infinite-horizon* row-sum optimum. Trélat–Zuazua (arXiv:2609.37081) already give the first small-window scalar-design terms and leading modal weights, as well as horizon-global covariance relaxation statements. These pre-existing results are credited, rather than claimed again. The newly derived all-finite-horizon formula and complete maximizing vectors are not supplied by the compared primary results, nor located by the mathematical-record or literature searches. An older total-positivity equivalent remains possible and is a residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Finite-window lower bounds determine worst-case recovery stability for orbit measurements when sensing must stop at a prescribed time. An explicit global optimization for any number of distinct decay modes and every horizon is a natural complete classification, not an arbitrary finite example. The optimizers change nontrivially with the observation window; the theorem quantifies the interpolation between the earlier-known short-time and infinite-time extremes.

## Limits

The claim covers one generator vector, real finite-dimensional self-adjoint contraction semigroups with simple spectrum and Lebesgue time windows. It does not cover arbitrary generators, repeated rates, nonnormal operators, multiple vectors, or nonuniform sampling measures. The possibility of an older equivalent general-purpose design theorem remains unresolved. No independent review has taken place.
