## Correctness — PASS
Kummer's theorem identifies the valuation of the integral five-ary Fuss–Catalan coefficient with the number of carries of \(n+4n\). The bit transition and terminal flushing vector produce an exact eight-state weighted enumerator. Two displayed integer-polynomial vector identities resolve the coefficient of valuation one as \(t^3(1+t)/(1-t-t^3-t^4)^2\); the standalone checker reconstructs the transition and tests these identities coefficientwise without approximate arithmetic. Singularity analysis at the golden-ratio pole gives \((3+\sqrt5)L\varphi^L/50\) and the ratio to the known parity shell. Independent digit-sum comparisons cover all indices below \(2^{16}\), but the infinite assertions come from the exact algebraic identities. Remaining risk: an error in the general link between carry-outs and valuations would invalidate the argument; this is Kummer's standard theorem, and both matrix construction and independent digit-sum tests agree.

## Originality — PASS (bounded-search assessment)
Full text of the published five-ary Fuss–Catalan parity/zero-run result was inspected. It gives the zero-valuation shell and the run lengths of even coefficients, not the first positive valuation shell. The full text for the published \(\binom{5n}{2n}\) valuation distribution describes addition \(2n+3n\), not \(n+4n\); its marginal distribution, even combined with the identity involving \(\binom{2n}{n}\) and \(\binom{4n}{n}\), does not supply the missing joint valuation. The published complete ternary Fuss–Catalan distribution and the \(\binom{3n}{n}\) first-shell result both have no valuation-one terms; neither determines the quintic first shell. The motivating Eu–Huang–Kao Raney paper studies a different parameter, and Bobrowski–He–Shiue prove specific divisibilities rather than the all-length modulo-four census. No exact earlier valuation-one enumerator or dominating general result was located in searched mathematical records, OEIS, and literature. Residual risk: an unindexed equivalent or a specialization of general binomial digital methods could exist; the search does not prove worldwide priority. Zero-shell formulas and the generic transfer method are explicitly not claimed as novel.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The first positive \(2\)-adic valuation shell is a natural refinement of the published binary parity and zero-run classification for a canonical Fuss–Catalan combinatorial sequence. Unlike a bare finite table, its exact all-length rational count settles the population of coefficients congruent to two modulo four, gives a polynomially enhanced golden-ratio growth rate, and quantifies how near-odd terms outnumber odd terms on long dyadic blocks. This is a meaningful arithmetic boundary: the related \(\binom{3n}{n}\) shell is identically empty, so this is not a formal substitution in a known uniform statement. The finding does not claim a general slope classification or higher valuation layers.
