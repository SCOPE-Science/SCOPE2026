For the five-ary Fuss–Catalan numbers \(C_5(n)=\binom{5n}{n}/(4n+1)\), the exact number of values congruent to \(2\pmod4\) at indices \(0\le n<2^L\) has generating function \(t^3(1+t)/(1-t-t^3-t^4)^2\). This first positive binary-valuation shell grows as \((3+\sqrt5)L\varphi^L/50\), where \(\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\), and its ratio to the already-known odd shell grows as \((3-\sqrt5)L/10\).
