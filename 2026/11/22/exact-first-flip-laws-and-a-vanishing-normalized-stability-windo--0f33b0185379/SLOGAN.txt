For the generalized rational logistic family \(G_{\rho,\beta}(x)=\rho x(1-x)^\beta/(1+x)\), every real \(\beta\ge2\) has an exact supercritical first-flip threshold, an explicit square-root two-cycle coefficient, and a sharp critical \(n^{-1/2}\) relaxation constant. The admissible fixed-point-stable parameter fraction shrinks as \(e/\beta\), while rescaling by \(\beta\) reveals the Ricker-map universal first-flip amplitude \(\sqrt6\).
