The exact local Frobenius sensitivity of a rank-one two-sided sampled Galerkin update is \(\sqrt{1+\tan^2\theta_L+\tan^2\theta_R}\), and its entire derivative singular spectrum is explicit for every matrix dimension. Normal increment errors feed into two tangent channels according to the old-basis alignment angles, sharpening the coefficient in a published finite-error bound without claiming a stronger global estimate.
