The initial two-term monomial projection on homogeneous quadratics of the complex bidisk has the same exact norm \(2/\sqrt3\) for **every nonzero complex Banach-valued coefficient space**. A two-point interpolation certificate proves the universal bound, and in the scalar case every extremizer is a phase rotation and scalar multiple of \(\tfrac12w^2+zw-\tfrac14z^2\).
