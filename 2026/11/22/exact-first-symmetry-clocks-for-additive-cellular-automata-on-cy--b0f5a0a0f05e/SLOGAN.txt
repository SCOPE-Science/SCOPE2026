For the difference cellular automaton \(F=I-S\) on every cyclic ring over \(\mathbb F_q\), the exact number of initially asymmetric configurations first acquiring translation symmetry at update \(t\) is \((q-1)q^{t-1}\sum_{r\mid\operatorname{rad}(m)}\mu(r)q^{n/(pr)}\) for \(1\le t\le(p-1)p^{a-1}\), where \(n=p^a m\) and \(p\nmid m\). Conditioned on symmetry appearing after time zero, its lag behind the sharp cutoff has a universal reversed-geometric distribution independent of \(m\); no new symmetry ever appears when \(p\nmid n\).
