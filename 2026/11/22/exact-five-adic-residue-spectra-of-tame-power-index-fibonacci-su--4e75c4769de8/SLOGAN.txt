For every exponent \(d\ge1\) not divisible by five, the Fibonacci numbers indexed by \(n^d\) have an exact five-adic value spectrum: the nonzero valuations are exactly multiples of \(d\), with a two-class leading-unit restriction precisely when \(4\mid d\). Every represented residue has an explicit fiber multiplicity, and the square-index image has Haar measure \(5/6\).
