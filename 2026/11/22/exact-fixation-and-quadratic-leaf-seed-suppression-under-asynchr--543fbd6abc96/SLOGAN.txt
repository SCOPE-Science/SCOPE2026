On a star network with pure closed-neighborhood conformity and asynchronous imitation, a lone cooperative hub succeeds with probability approaching \(0.4148196588\ldots\), but a lone cooperative leaf succeeds with probability asymptotic to \(0.8296393177\ldots/m^2\). An exact finite-product formula gives consensus probabilities from every initial hub state and number of cooperating leaves; the quadratic leaf disadvantage contrasts with the linear leaf disadvantage in the ordinary voter model.
