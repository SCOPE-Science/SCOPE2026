For fixed low-rank spatial and velocity bases, an entrywise nonnegative kinetic correction with prescribed positive mass and momentum exists exactly when a nonnegative spatial factor exists and the target mean velocity lies in an explicit reduced velocity-moment polytope. Every feasible case has a rank-one witness; when the velocity basis has codimension one, that polytope has an exact two-node formula, and one added velocity direction is necessary and sufficient to repair an excluded physically admissible mean.
