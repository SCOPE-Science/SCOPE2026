# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The proof determines the exact periodic Rule 150 kernel for every ring length, derives each binomial moment of the fixed-weight source multiplicities and inverts the finite triangular system. Special cases at lengths one, two, three and at weight zero or full weight are included. Direct enumeration of 524286 inputs over 189 slices and exact parameter sweeps through length 240 match the formulas. The finite checks corroborate but do not establish the all-length identities.

## Originality

**PASS, with residual historical risk.** Earlier finite-automaton reversibility results determine when the unrestricted map is bijective; published inverse-transition expressions assume reversibility and therefore exclude the singular nontrivial kernel cases. The closest unrestricted necklace-degree result works after quotienting rotations and does not classify fixed-weight indexed preimages. The new spectrum supplies exact multiplicity one through four for every weight and length. Standard general coding-theoretic coset-weight identities might yield this result by specialization; an unindexed equivalent formulation cannot be ruled out.

## Value

**PASS.** Fixing source composition is a standard natural information constraint. Its zero-error output capacity and every ambiguity-list size determine what can be recovered from a parity cellular-automaton read when the number of marked sites is known. The sharp one-third/two-thirds injectivity window and the central fourfold ambiguity are structural facts not supplied by the unrestricted 4-to-1 bound alone. This is an exact characterization of a motivated invariant, not a complexity or general coding theorem.

## Relevant prior literature and limitations

Bhattacharjee and Das (arXiv:1903.06011, 2019) establish Rule 150's length-dependent reversibility. Hernández Serrano and Martín del Rey (doi:10.1016/j.amc.2019.03.060, 2019) treat inverse formulas only at reversible lengths. Dennunzio et al. (doi:10.4230/LIPIcs.ICALP.2020.125, 2020) study additive-cellular-automaton dynamics at a broader level. The spectrum concerns indexed finite rings, one synchronous update and fixed-weight inputs, not necklaces, noisy outputs or multiple steps. This is a self-review, not external scientific validation.
