# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The population generating function is an exact telescoping product of independent immigrant-family generating functions. Differentiating the youngest-family product yields the population-weighted marked coefficients; division by the geometric population probability proves the finite conditional law. A uniform-on-compacts geometric-power limit, together with exponential tightness of the rescaled population, proves the joint limit. The marginal age density and mean follow by a positive logarithmic integral. Separate exact-rational family-cohort enumeration from the model and numerical mixture tests are supplied in `check.py`. No finite calculation is used as a substitute for the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS against available evidence

The nearest 2026 local-limit paper concerns the population count and the geometric approximation rather than immigrant-founder labels. Klebaner–Sagitov (2002) give a backward process and a distinct definition of population age as a hitting time; Cardona-Tobón–Palau (2025) study coalescence within one family without immigration; Smadi–Vatutin (2021) study the *only surviving family* conditional event, not the genealogy of a uniformly chosen individual in an unconditioned critical population. Available published findings on polynomial-immigration family extremes concern the largest founder family under growing immigration rather than the ancestry of an individually sampled member with one immigrant per generation. Searches for uniformly sampled immigrant ancestry, its exact conditional law and equivalent formulations did not identify the statement. The full text of a broad 1974 branching-with-immigration age-structure paper remained inaccessible; it is an explicit priority risk, and lack of access is not represented as proof of novelty.

## Value — PASS

Choosing an individual uniformly *within a realized population* differs from first selecting a size-biased population, and the exact contrast is mathematically consequential: a uniform founder-age law is replaced by a nonuniform logarithmic limiting density and strict population-dependent conditional age ordering. The finite joint formula identifies a natural genealogical statistic invisible to the population-only local limit, while the joint scaling law characterizes its dependence on realized abundance. These are motivated, nontrivial distributional facts rather than merely restatements of the geometric population marginal.

## Risks and boundaries

No extension to arbitrary offspring distributions is proved. A historical source that discusses age structure was available only by abstract. The explicit law may have appeared under another genealogical formulation and deserves specialist priority review. Neither correctness nor priority has been independently assessed.
