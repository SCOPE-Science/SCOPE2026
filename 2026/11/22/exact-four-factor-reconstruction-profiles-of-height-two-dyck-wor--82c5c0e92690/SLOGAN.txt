The complete oriented four-factor readout of height-two Dyck words has exactly the realizable tuples of a six-parameter primitive-component classification; its distinct-profile series is \(z(1+z^2+z^3-z^4+3z^5-z^6+2z^7-2z^8)/((1-z)^2(1-z^3)(1-z^4))\). Consequently the sharp zero-error readout capacity is \(R_n=n^3/18+O(n^2)\), with a least-period-twelve cubic quasipolynomial and the first ambiguity at semilength five.
