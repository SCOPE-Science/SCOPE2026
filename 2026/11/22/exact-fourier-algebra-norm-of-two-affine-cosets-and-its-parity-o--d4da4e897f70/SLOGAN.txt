The Fourier-algebra norm of the union of any two affine cosets in a finite abelian group depends exactly on subgroup-intersection ratios and, when disjoint, one quotient-element order. That order obeys a sharp odd–even hierarchy: order two minimizes the norm, order three maximizes it, and the transition to the continuous average is exponentially fast for unequal coset ratios but quadratically slow at equal ratios.
