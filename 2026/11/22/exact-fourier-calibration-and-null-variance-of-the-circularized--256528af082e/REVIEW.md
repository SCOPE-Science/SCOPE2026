# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Dirichlet spacings represented by normalized independent exponentials yield the periodic bridge via the functional CLT; at grid points the centered pooled spacing sum is exactly the corresponding bridge increment divided by sqrt(m). Interior Taylor errors are O_P(m^−3/2) per term and vanish after the prescribed averaging. The endpoint argument applies to the nonlinear score itself: beta log expectations, Jensen nonnegativity and 0<log(j)−ψ(j)≤1/j give E h≤4/m for all i,c. The omitted ranks therefore contribute O(δ)+O(1/m) in expectation, and the limiting increment variance t(1−t) gives expectation 2δ for the omitted continuum strip. Markov and convergence together justify the full weak limit without uniform relative linearization near 0 or 1. In the periodic bridge derivative, each nonconstant Fourier mode is white noise and the real and imaginary variances are 1/2; integrating the squared increment and pairing conjugate modes gives a_k=[γ+log(2πk)−Ci(2πk)]/(π²k²) times χ²_2, with independent pairs. The mode-ordering inequality follows from G(2π)>1 and x³(G(x)/x²)'=1−cos x−2G(x). The increment integral has mean one. I independently recomputed its variance by Wick's identity and circular arc overlap: for 0<p≤q≤1/2, the squared overlap integrated over relative starts is p²q−p³/3, so the squared covariance integral is p²q(1−q)−p³/3. Complement and exchange symmetries reduce the variance to 16 times the integral on [0,1/2] of p(1/2−p)/(1−p)+p² log(p/(1−p))/(3(1−p)). The substitution y=p/(1−p) yields the log integral ∫_0^1 log(y)/(1+y) dy=−π²/12 and the resulting variance is (42−4π²)/9; independent quadrature agreed at 38 digits. Positive third cumulant proves non-Gaussianity. Ci(2πk)=O(k^−2) and integral comparison give the two asserted tail asymptotics; nonnegativity gives the Markov cutoff.

## originality

PASS

Pass for the proof of the weak limit of the actual nonlinear score with endpoint control, and its explicit Fourier calibration (including closed variance and cutoff). Liu already defined the exact same statistic, derived the same continuum entropy kernel, and in a companion preprint announced the broad infinite weighted-chi-square asymptotic form. That representation and the kernel are not discoveries of this record, and the Fourier spectrum is determined by the prior kernel; the new work is its explicit evaluation and rigorous identification as the limit of the original nonlinear statistic, rather than solely of a Gaussian approximation.

## value

PASS

Explicit, ordered spectral weights together with a proved nonlinear limit let a researcher compute limiting critical values for the existing circularized test without treating a discretized Gaussian heuristic as the theorem. The sharp tail law quantifies spectral truncation, and the exact variance is an interpretable comparison to unpooled Anderson–Darling dispersion. The contribution is not a claim of stronger testing power.
