# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The Walsh character at weight k assigns a factor −1 to each chosen label that is a singleton; expansion of the product gives the binomial Bell transform by deleting the prescribed singletons. Dobiński's identity gives E[X^(n−k)(X−2)^k] with X Poisson(1), and there are binom(n,k) such characters. Even k is strictly positive from r≥3. For k odd and d=n−k>0, only r=1 is negative; for k≥3 the r=3 and 4 terms exceed it after multiplication by e, and for k=1,d≥2 the r=3 term suffices. The outstanding k=1,d=1 is zero. For odd k=n, the negative terms total −2^n−1 after multiplying by e. At n=9, r=5,6 exceed this magnitude by exactly 5441/360; division by 2^n gives a strictly improving inequality at larger n. An independent labelled-block exponential generating function exp(exp(x+y)−1−2y), with y marking the k labels, gives the recurrence A(d,k+1)=Σ_{a≤d,b≤k} binom(d,a)binom(k,b)A(a,b)−2A(d,k), starting with A(d,0)=B_d. Exact integer evaluation through n=12 reproduced the sole weight-zero class at (n,k)=(2,1) and the four negative diagonal values −1,−3,−10,−21. The sign estimates, not that finite range, prove the all-n theorem. Multiplicities then give the original exact inertia and sharp threshold.
