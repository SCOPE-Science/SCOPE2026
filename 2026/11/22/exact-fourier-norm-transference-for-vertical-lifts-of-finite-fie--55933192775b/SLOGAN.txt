A quadratic Gauss-factorization transfers the complete Fourier-magnitude spectrum, sharp endpoint projection norm, and additive energy of every vertically thickened finite-field parabola to the one-dimensional Fourier data of its vertical fiber. Two disjoint translated parabolas have a separation-independent, characteristic-sensitive exact norm whose amplification relative to one parabola tends to \(4/\pi\) along prime fields.
