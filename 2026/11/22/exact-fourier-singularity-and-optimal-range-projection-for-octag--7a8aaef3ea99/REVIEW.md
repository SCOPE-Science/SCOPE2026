# Review of the octagon quadrangulation Fourier claim

## Correctness — PASS
The objects are quadrangulations of a convex even polygon, embedded by exactly the admissible opposite-parity *interior* diagonals, with unnormalized binary Walsh multipliers. The geometric parity argument and the polygon side-count equality establish the noncrossing-subset equivalence. At eight vertices the admissible diagonals form an explicit eight-vertex Wagner compatibility graph, with one edge per quadrangulation. The exact cut identity \(W(S)=12-2|\delta(S)|\) reduces the complete spectrum to 256 finite cuts. The seven-file package contains a standalone integer verifier that independently evaluates all 256 signed quadrangulation sums and cut sums, all 256 projection kernel coefficients, and all 32,768 decagon multipliers. The ranks, cancellation weights, sharp nonzero gap, and norm \(59/16\) are thereby exact, not statistical. Averaging all projections over group translations establishes optimality without a translation-symmetry premise. The 55 decagon quadrangulations enforce odd Walsh multipliers; the explicitly checked multiplier one establishes the sharp nonzero gap. No infinite-order empirical extrapolation is made.

## Originality — PASS (bounded comparison)
The earliest archived source is Schuetz–Whieldon, arXiv:1401.7194 (2014-01-28), which proves classical polygonal/Fuss–Catalan enumeration but not octagon signed Walsh or recovery projections. Chapoton, arXiv:1505.05990 (2015-05-22), studies quadrangulation flip/Stokes structures, not Walsh transforms or the diagonal-pair compatibility cut polynomial. A close published-finding corpus record on convex polygon triangulation Fourier cancellation treats triangulations rather than quadrangulations; the Wagner-graph four-cut *complex* record studies topology, not this complete graph-cut-size distribution or optimal projection. The result's geometric reduction, all 256 multipliers, first crossing cancellation, and decagon recovery are not consequences of the cited enumerative identities alone. General Ising/Tutte techniques can evaluate cut polynomials, however, and a previously tabulated full Wagner cut polynomial might imply the octagon frequencies; this unindexed-evaluation priority risk remains explicitly open. No uniqueness proof is claimed.

## Value — PASS
Quadrangulations are the classical Fuss–Catalan analogue of triangulations and their binary diagonal-incidence representation is intrinsic. The first noninvertible case (octagon), exact characterization of its least-support Fourier blind spots by *crossing* diagonals, complete rank and sharp optimal \(\ell^1\)/\(\ell^\infty\) recovery projection constant reveal recoverability properties unavailable from ordinary enumeration. Immediate zero-free decagon recovery shows singularity is not monotone in polygon order. The connected cut-spectral interpretation provides a natural bridge between Catalan objects and finite harmonic analysis; the claim is finite, self-contained and mathematically motivated, not a routine parameter rescaling.
