Octagon quadrangulations are the edges of the eight-vertex Wagner graph in diagonal-incidence coordinates, so their Walsh transform is \(12-2\) times a graph-cut size: exactly \(72\) of \(256\) frequencies vanish, first at the \(16\) crossing-diagonal pairs. The recoverable Fourier range has the sharp unrestricted \(\ell^1\) and \(\ell^\infty\) projection norm \(59/16\), while quadrangulation convolution becomes invertible again on the decagon.
