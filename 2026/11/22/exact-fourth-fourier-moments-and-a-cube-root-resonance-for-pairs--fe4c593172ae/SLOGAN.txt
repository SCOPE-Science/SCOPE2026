For two distinct nonzero curvatures, the fourth Fourier moment of the union of finite-field parabolas has a two-character formula, whereas the fourth moment counting their common origin twice is curvature-independent. The large-field sharp lower envelope is first attained at field order \(17\); for odd field orders \(q\ge17\), the opposite-curvature pair is the unique energy maximizer if and only if \(q\equiv2\pmod3\).
