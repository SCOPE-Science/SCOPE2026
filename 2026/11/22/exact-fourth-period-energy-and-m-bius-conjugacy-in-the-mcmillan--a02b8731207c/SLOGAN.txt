For the real McMillan sextupole map, every nonexceptional point on the exact invariant level \(K_a=-a\) has least period four, and there are no other primitive four-cycles. This one-dimensional family exists for every \(a\ne-1\), where its dynamics reduces to an explicit Möbius involution, but disappears completely at the linear third-order resonance \(a=-1\).
