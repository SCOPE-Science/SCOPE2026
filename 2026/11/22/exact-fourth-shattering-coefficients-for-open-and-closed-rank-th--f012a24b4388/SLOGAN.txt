For every order \(n\), the numbers of shattered four-vertex supports of the open and closed neighborhoods of \(\operatorname{KG}(n,3)\) have explicit, exact finite binomial-coefficient formulas. The first nonzero layer occurs at \(n=9\), where both counts are \(1260\) and all supports share a two-point core; the two counts differ from \(n=10\) onward but have the same leading asymptotic term.
