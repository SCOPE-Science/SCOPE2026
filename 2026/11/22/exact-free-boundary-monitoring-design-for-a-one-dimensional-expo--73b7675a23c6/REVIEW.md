# Scientific summary

The noiseless, known-mean exponential-covariance model reduces the integrated pointwise conditional variance to a strictly convex sum of two terminal costs and one bridge cost per interior gap. The unique minimum on the gap simplex is interior, with two equal positive margins shorter than the common interior gap. The margin-to-interior ratio tends to \(1:3\) in the strong-correlation regime \(\theta L\downarrow0\). For two stations with \(\theta L=1\), the normalized loss is about \(0.18645\), versus \(0.31304\) with both endpoints compulsory. This model comparison does not assert an improvement for an empirical field with noise, drift or covariance uncertainty.

Dasgupta, Mukhopadhyay and Keith, arXiv:2004.13967, Section 5.1 and Theorem 5.2, impose both endpoints. The earlier free-node Ornstein–Uhlenbeck quadrature result (record [971795799fba](https://github.com/Resultary/2026/tree/main/2026/11/8/SCOPE-exact-optimal-nodes-and-stratification-penalty-for-ornstein-uhle--971795799fba)) also gives shorter positive margins, but with a different equation for a different criterion: posterior variance of the integral. The available comparisons do not establish absolute historical priority; in particular, the full Antognini–Zagoraiou (2010) article was not available for a statement-level comparison.
