# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The exact squared-distance polynomial was reconstructed from the conditional matching-pair shared-entry factorial moments and the Frobenius identity. The conditional pgf converges uniformly to a mean-one Poisson law when both ranks approach the full rank, independently of the Gaussian deficiency scales established by the classical Laguerre expansion. The ordinary-distance variance is \(1/8\) after the stated normalization, by the delta method. The standalone exact checker exhausts all matching pairs through order four and verifies polynomial normalization in larger orders. These calculations supplement, not replace, the infinite-dimensional argument.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The result answers a natural complete metric-statistics question on a high-dimensional polytope. It separates the order-\(n^{1/4}\) rank fluctuations from the unscaled Poisson shared-edge correction and determines the sharp order-one variance of the rescaled ordinary distance. The full polynomial determines every finite pair-distance multiplicity, not only a first or second moment.

## Limitations
The theorem concerns independent uniform vertices of square partial-permutation polytopes in their canonical Frobenius coordinates. No extension to arbitrary graphs, fixed matching rank, rectangular matrices, or uniform interior volume is asserted. Historical priority and the proof have not received independent mathematical audit.
