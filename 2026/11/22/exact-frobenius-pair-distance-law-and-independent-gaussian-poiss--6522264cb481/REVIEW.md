# Scientific summary

The main result is the joint rank-deficiency and shared-cell limit for two independent uniform partial permutation matrices. The proof combines classical Laguerre asymptotics with an exact rank-conditioned common-cell generating function; it proves independence of the Poisson limit from both normalized deficiencies. In squared Frobenius distance the unscaled correction is twice the common-cell count, so its limiting support is even. An exact finite distance formula is included as a tool, not as a separate claim of originality: the component counts in record [e587fba564ca](https://github.com/Resultary/2026/tree/main/2026/11/22/SCOPE-exact-finite-field-rank-spectra-for-sums-of-two-rook-matrices-an--e587fba564ca) give the same finite law by marking singly occupied cells. The closest related product-rank joint law counts coincidences of vacancies rather than occupied cells; it does not identify this distance correction. The result applies to independent uniform vertices of the square polytope and makes no claim of absolute historical priority.
