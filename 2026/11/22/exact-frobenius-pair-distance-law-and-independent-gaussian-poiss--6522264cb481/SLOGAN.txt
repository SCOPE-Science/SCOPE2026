For two uniformly sampled vertices of the doubly substochastic matrix polytope, the entire Frobenius squared-distance distribution has an explicit finite factorial-moment polynomial. Their two rank deficiencies exhibit independent \(n^{1/4}\)-scale Gaussian fluctuations while the number of shared occupied entries tends independently to \(\operatorname{Pois}(1)\), yielding a sharp ordinary-distance Gaussian variance of \(1/8\).
