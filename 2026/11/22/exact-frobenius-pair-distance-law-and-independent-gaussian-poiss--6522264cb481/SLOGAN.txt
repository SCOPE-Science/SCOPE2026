For two independent uniform partial permutations, the rank deficiencies fluctuate on the \(n^{1/4}\) scale as two independent Gaussians, while the number of shared occupied cells converges independently to \(\mathrm{Pois}(1)\). The ordinary Frobenius distance has a centered Gaussian limit of variance \(1/8\) after scaling by \(n^{1/4}\); the unscaled squared-distance residual after removing both rank contributions converges to twice the Poisson variable.
