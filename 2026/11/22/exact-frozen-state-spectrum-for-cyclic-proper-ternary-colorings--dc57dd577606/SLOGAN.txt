Proper three-colorings of an indexed cycle are frozen under legal adjacent-color swaps exactly when their signed cyclic color increments avoid three equal successive signs. Their complete all-length count is \(F_n=L_n+2+2\cos(2\pi n/3)+2r_n\), with the explicitly defined cubic recurrence for \(r_n\); frozen colorings occur at all cycle lengths except \(3,5,7\), and their fraction decays asymptotically as \((\varphi/2)^n\).
