# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The full eight-state generator is decomposed into even and odd monomial sectors, producing an exact characteristic polynomial and the least nonzero decay rate. The derivative argument establishes strict monotonicity on both sides of the signed-bond matching point for every positive inverse temperature, and direct substitution proves the universal low-temperature boundary-layer limit. An independent exact-rational finite-state checker verifies 4096 monomial action equalities over 64 rational activity pairs. These checks corroborate, but do not replace, the general algebraic proof. No numerical extrapolation or finite enumeration is used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS on examined evidence
The closest primary precursor, Nacu's 2003 theorem for ferromagnetic cycles, presupposes nonnegative bonds and establishes monotonic *slowing*, whereas the signed triangle has a uniquely maximal gap at an interior antiferromagnetic strength. Pini–Rettori examine antiferromagnetic spin chains and competing next-neighbor couplings in different driven or approximate settings; neither inspected analysis supplies the full finite triangle Markov gap or its singular limiting logistic profile. Ismail–Salem concern a six-spin frustrated triangular cluster; only the abstract was accessible, making hidden overlap a residual risk rather than a verified negative full-text result. Searches of indexed mathematical findings for the signed-triangle exact gap, opposite-sign bond, and low-temperature parameter window did not surface an equivalent claim. Absence from an index is not uniqueness proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The first simple cycle is the natural minimum graph supporting bond-sign frustration. Its sharp, all-temperature optimal relaxation rate is a substantive boundary to the classical ferromagnetic monotonicity principle: the frustrating exchange first *accelerates* mixing before suppressing it. The noncommuting zero-temperature limits and exact logistic width describe a real dynamical mechanism not contained in a mere tabulation of eigenvalues. The result makes no assertion about larger rings or physical thermodynamic transitions.

## Prior literature and residual limitations
Nacu (2003), arXiv:math/0305056; Pini–Rettori (2007), arXiv:0707.1953; Ismail–Salem (2003), doi:10.1002/pssb.200301657. Exact three-spin results could have appeared under other terminology or as an unstated specialization. The Ismail–Salem full text was inaccessible; its abstract describes a six-spin model and cannot settle equivalence. Independent evaluation and historical-priority verification remain outstanding.
