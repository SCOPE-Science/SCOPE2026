For the separately profiled scale likelihood ratio comparing two fixed inverse-Weibull shapes from \(m\ge2\) lower records, the log ratio is exactly a shifted \(\operatorname{Gamma}(m-1,1)\) variable, with nuisance-scale-free selection probabilities. Both wrong-selection probabilities have the same sharp exponential rate, and explicit Gamma tails replace simulation-based calibration of the inverse-Rayleigh versus inverted-exponential decision.
