For every \(n\ge2\), the maximal gaps of \(\langle n^2,n(n+1),(n+1)^2\rangle\) start in exactly \(2n-2\) explicit arithmetic progressions, each of length \(n\) and step \(n^2\). The corresponding semigroup polynomial has exactly \(4n(n-1)+1\) nonzero coefficients, and its negative exponents are precisely these starts.
