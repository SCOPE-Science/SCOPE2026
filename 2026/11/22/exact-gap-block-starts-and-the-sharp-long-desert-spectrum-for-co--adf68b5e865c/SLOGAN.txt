For \(\langle T_n,T_{n+1},T_{n+2}\rangle\), every maximal missing-integer block starts at an explicit Apéry-residue position, and their number is exactly \(n^2\) for even \(n\) or \(n^2-1\) for odd \(n\). The blocks longer than \(n+1\) are exactly the \(\lfloor(n+1)/4\rfloor\) gaps between consecutive coin-count layers, with lengths decreasing by \(2n+3\).
