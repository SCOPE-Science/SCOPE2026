For the numerical semigroups \(\langle a,2a+2,4a+6\rangle\) with odd \(a\ge3\), every gap run longer than one has even length and occurs either twice or never, according to a sharp period-three rule. The number of isolated gaps is an explicit quadratic quasipolynomial of period three in \((a-1)/2\), completing the entire gap-run spectrum for this infinite family.
