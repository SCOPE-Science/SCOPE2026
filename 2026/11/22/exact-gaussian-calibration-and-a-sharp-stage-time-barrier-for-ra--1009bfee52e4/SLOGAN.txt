A shared-stage explicit randomized-midpoint Langevin step can exactly preserve a Gaussian equilibrium at positive finite step size only for isotropic curvature: \(0<h\lambda<1\) and a uniquely calibrated **deterministic** stage \(U=(1+\sqrt{1-(h\lambda)^2})^{-1}\) yield the exact law, while any nonconstant covariance-calibrated stage law has positive fourth cumulant. The fastest such exactly Gaussian-calibrated step occurs uniquely at \(h\lambda=1/\sqrt2\), with contraction \(2-\sqrt2\); anisotropic Gaussian targets admit no exact target covariance with any common stage law.
