For every row of the rencontres triangle, the gcd over odd moved-support and positive fixed-point entries is exactly \(2^{\nu_2(n(n-1)(n-2))}\), multiplied by the unique odd prime \(p\) if and only if \(n\) is a power of \(p\). An exact binary-digit criterion also determines the two-adic valuation of every retained entry and counts every minimizing column.
