For every irrational-coefficient polynomial \(f\), the entire natural-density gcd spectrum of \(n\), \(\lfloor f(n)\rfloor\), and \(\lfloor f(n)+n/b\rfloor\) has an explicit universal finite-residue formula, including an exact \(1/(b\zeta(2)d^2)\) tail for \(d\ge b\). As \(b\to\infty\), these laws converge in total variation to \(1/(\zeta(3)d^3)\) at rate \(O((\log(2b))^2/b^2)\), despite the exact rational dependence between the two floor arguments.
