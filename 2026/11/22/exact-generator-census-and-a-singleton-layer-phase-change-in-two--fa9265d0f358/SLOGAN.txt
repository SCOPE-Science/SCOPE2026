For the Heyting algebra of upsets of a complete two-level poset, generating predicates are exactly colourings injective within each level, with an extra cross-level exclusion only when the upper level is a singleton. This yields an exact signed-Stirling count of all ordered generating tuples and a sharp generator rank of \(\lceil\log_2 a\rceil+\lceil\log_2 b\rceil\) for \(b\ge2\), versus \(\lceil\log_2(a+1)\rceil\) when \(b=1\).
