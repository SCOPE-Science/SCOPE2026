For every three-variable generic-phase Lissajous hypersurface with pairwise coprime positive circuit weights \(p_1,p_2,p_3\), its normalization is a smooth two-torus and its singular locus has exactly \(3(p_1+p_2+p_3)-3\) irreducible curves, each generically an ordinary double crossing. At the two inversion-symmetric phases the normalization changes to a torus involution quotient with exactly four \(A_1\) points.
