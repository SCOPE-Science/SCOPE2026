# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The precise objects are bilateral binary zero-run gap shifts with lower mechanical allowed-gap indicators of any irrational slope, and their canonical length-\((N+1)\) covers. Every forbidden word and cutoff index is derived explicitly. The standard renewal equation gives the two entropy roots. The tail at the limiting root is an *exact* complementary Sturmian generating series, and the derivative is bounded below by a positive mean return-time constant, yielding a uniform normalized Taylor estimate. A telescoping rotation discrepancy identity proves strict monotonicity of the discounted mechanical-word map; each rotation breakpoint swaps the adjacent digits \((0,1)\) to \((1,0)\) and removes a gap of width \((1-r)r^{n-1}\). The widths sum to the full endpoint span, proving there are no other complementary gaps. Sturmian factor complexity gives a zero Hausdorff-dimension cover, and the dense breakpoints plus strict monotonicity give compact perfect nowhere-dense range. Aperiodic and periodic boundary cases for the finite covers are addressed. The executed standard-library checker passed 18,414 language comparisons, 23 plateau witnesses, 18 jump comparisons, the endpoint normalization, and eight entropy-root comparisons. All infinite assertions are proved analytically; the finite tests only corroborate indexing and calculations.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence
An exact canonical-cover entropy expansion and a *complete geometric gap-size census* for the Sturmian-indexed binary gap shift was not found in the source and claim comparisons. Coles–Cyr–Kra–Pavlov's 2026 paper treats cover stability qualitatively, without this object-specific cluster-set geometry. Classical S-gap work gives the base renewal equation, not the phase-range discontinuity classification. A published geometric-return-distance theorem gives a countable cluster set, whereas the new theorem has an uncountable zero-dimensional Cantor set with **every gap width** enumerated. The published decorated-Sturmian gap shift instead has Sturmian words *inside* marker gaps and studies periodic zeta data, not which zero-run lengths are allowed. The published Fibonacci Sturmian language-cover staircase has limiting zero entropy, a different system. Bugeaud–Laurent's abstract concerns continued fractions of Sturmian digit expansions and does not state a finite-cover entropy gap census. Relevant full published result texts and the motivating PDF have been examined; older broad Sturmian-expansion treatments remain a bibliographic priority risk. The distinctive novelty claim is the coupled entropy-asymptotic and all-gap classification, not the previously known renewal equation, Sturmian complexity, or elementary mechanical-word identity.

## Value — PASS
Canonical finite-type approximations measure how quickly finite forbidden-word information recovers a symbolic system's entropy. Even a quantified pointwise error does not describe the possible normalized limiting values. This exact structural result identifies a genuinely fractal arithmetic obstruction to any single limiting coefficient and gives the full shape of that obstruction—one complementary interval of each exact geometric size, its total hull length, Lebesgue nullity, Hausdorff dimension, and exact cover plateau stages. The invariant is natural for finite-resolution entropy approximation; its exact all-slope classification sharply contrasts with countable phase sets from geometric gaps and finite phase sets from eventually periodic gap patterns.
