Canonical finite-type cover entropy errors for a binary gap shift with Sturmian-indexed allowed lengths have a compact, uncountable, zero-dimensional Cantor cluster set. All of its complementary gaps have the **exact** geometric lengths \( (1-r)r^{n-1}/T\) for \(n\ge1\), with no other gaps, where \(r\) and \(T\) arise from the classical renewal entropy equation.
