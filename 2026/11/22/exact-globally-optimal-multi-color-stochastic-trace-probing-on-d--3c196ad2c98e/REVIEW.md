# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The Green kernel solves the shifted cyclic difference equation, the class-supported Rademacher variance identity follows from independent sign pairing, and the strict Jensen lower bound applies to every color class including nonproper and unbalanced partitions. The exact geometric-series class lower bound has strictly positive second derivative in real class size, forcing equal class sizes and then equally spaced vertices. This proves the full quantifier range; the standalone checker corroborates the formulas and uniqueness with 1,358,283 exact partition comparisons, without being substituted for an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS on inspected evidence

The focal original source (Stathopoulos–Laeuchli–Orginos, arXiv:1302.4018v1) develops distance-coloring and hierarchical probing, but its inspected sections do not prove global fixed-budget optimality among all partitions for the specified independent stochastic estimator. The closest exact two-color cycle result states a checkerboard theorem only for two colors, whereas the present statement concerns \(k\ge3\) and all equal-size multi-color partitions. Another source on equal spacing maximizes a continuous circle's minimum potential rather than the within-class pair-energy sum. A later paper on displaced lattice probing was available in abstract only. Missing full text of the closely relevant exact two-color record and potentially unindexed equivalent Potts/energy-minimization literature remain priority risks.

## Value — PASS

The result settles a natural computational-design question: the exact globally optimal partition at any fixed supported-probe budget dividing the cycle size. It establishes uniqueness against unbalanced and improper allocations, produces an evaluable minimum variance, and quantifies its high-screening savings relative to equal-cost full-support Hutchinson probing. Such a universal certification goes beyond a constructive graph-coloring heuristic.

## Boundaries and evidence

All claims concern finite positive shifts on unit cycles and independent probes. No result for nondivisible sizes or arbitrary correlated probe mechanisms is asserted. Exact finite tests are supplemental to, not substitutes for, the analytic proof.
