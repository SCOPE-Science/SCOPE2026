For every divisible unweighted cycle with \(n=km\), \(k\ge3\), \(m\ge2\), and every positive screening strength, the unique optimal unlabeled partition for \(k\) independent color-supported Rademacher trace probes is the set of residue classes modulo \(k\). An exact variance formula and a strict cyclic-spacing proof show that this remains globally optimal even against unbalanced and improper colorings.
