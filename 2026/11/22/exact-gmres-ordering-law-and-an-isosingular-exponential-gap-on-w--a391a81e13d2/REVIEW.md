# Review of exact GMRES ordering and isosingular growth on weighted chains

## Correctness — PASS

The positive-weight hypotheses guarantee a full Krylov flag generated by the first coordinate and a one-dimensional residual complement at every nonterminal step. The reciprocal-prefix residual identity follows by orthogonal projection, not by experiments. Termwise product rearrangement establishes the exact simultaneous minimax ordering; two finite geometric sums and a reversal-transpose identity establish the isosingular exponential ratio. Exact-rational verification independently reconstructs the finite least-squares problems and checks the resulting identities. Universal statements are proved symbolically.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

The general GMRES projection characterization, constant-coupling Jordan formulas, qualitative arbitrary-curve constructions with fixed eigenvalues, and qualitative ILU ordering effects are acknowledged prior results. The assessed contribution is specifically **all-horizon extremization under a fixed multiset of freely ordered positive edge couplings**, together with a **fixed-right-hand-side, isosingular** family whose penultimate residual ratio is an exactly computed exponential. The motivating ILU paper and 2026 Toeplitz analysis have been compared by their objects and claims. A directly related published result on constant-coupling Jordan blocks is **not** being relabeled as new. Existing literature or records not available in full may contain an equivalent weighted-chain sorting result, so historical priority remains qualified.

## Value — PASS

This is a motivated boundary example for short-horizon approximate triangular solves: neither eigenvalues nor singular values determine how quickly a fixed right-hand side is reduced. The same two multisets of couplings, arranged in opposite directions, have equal full singular spectra yet exponentially different penultimate GMRES residuals. In addition to the counterexample, the result solves the exact finite-horizon coupling-arrangement problem for every size and every positive multiset, making it a transparent reproducible numerical benchmark. It does not infer performance on arbitrary ILU factors or superiority of a practical reordering strategy.

## Closest literature and limitations

Dargaville–Pain (2026) compare several iterative approximate triangular solvers and demonstrate ordering dependence without giving this exact chain theorem. Liesen–Strakoš (2004) and Ipsen (2000) establish constant-Jordan residual results, which cover the homogeneous special case but not the free-order multiset extremization. Chen–Soodhalter (2026) constrain behavior of tridiagonal Toeplitz systems with constant diagonals. Greenbaum–Pták–Strakoš (1996) construct general systems with prescribed GMRES curves and eigenvalues, without fixing singular values or a bidiagonal chain. A mathematical-record abstract about two-step three-site bidiagonal stagnation is related but concerns a distinct question, and its repository full text was inaccessible. The present theorem is exact-arithmetic and restricted to a single start vector.
