For positive weighted unit-bidiagonal chains with a fixed first-coordinate right-hand side, the exact GMRES residual is governed by reciprocal prefix products: sorting couplings upward is simultaneously optimal at every step, and sorting downward is worst. Reversing a two-block chain preserves **all eigenvalues and singular values** yet gives a penultimate-step residual ratio asymptotic to \(\sqrt{5/8}\,2^m\), quantifying a sharp orientation dependence invisible to either spectrum.
