For exact unrestarted GMRES with a root point source on any radially weighted, leaf-grounded finite tree, the iteration-\(k\) residual is uniformly \(1/B_k\) on the radius-\(k\) ball and zero outside, where \(B_k\) is that ball's vertex count. Every preterminal residual is independent of all positive conductance choices, even when varying leaf grounding makes the system arbitrarily ill-conditioned.
