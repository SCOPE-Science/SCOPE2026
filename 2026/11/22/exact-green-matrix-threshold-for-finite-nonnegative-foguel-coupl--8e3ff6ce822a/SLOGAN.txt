For every finite-support, entrywise nonnegative (possibly nonsymmetric) Foguel coupling, a finite Green covariance matrix gives the exact threshold for numerical radius one and a unique singular-value equation for the radius above that threshold. Matrix-unit couplings exhibit an explicit inverse-geometric-depth threshold and a sharp quadratic onset, while two-way off-diagonal couplings obey a nontrivial exact quartic boundary.
