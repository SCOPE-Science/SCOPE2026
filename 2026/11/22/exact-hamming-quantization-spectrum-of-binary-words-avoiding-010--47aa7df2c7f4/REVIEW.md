# Scientific review: exact no-010 Hamming quantization spectrum

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof identifies disjoint occurrence intervals as a lower bound, and provides explicit mutually nonadjacent substitutions attaining it. A separate equal-length interval-greedy argument justifies the three-state weighted automaton. Exact rational inversion yields the entire distance spectrum, the degree-maximal coefficients and the uniform-source moments. The self-contained `verify.py` compares an independent optimal-substitution dynamic program against both the packing algorithm and constructive substitutions for all binary inputs through length fifteen, and validates coefficient identities through length 180. This does not confuse finite checks with a universal proof.

**Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk.** Elimelech, Meyerovitch and Schwartz, arXiv:2302.02363 (first public 2023-02-05), introduce the general quantization framework and solve homogeneous zero-run cases, but the inspected primary full-text passages do not provide the mixed-factor `010` distribution. The same-language publication *Exact cyclic-cover lengths for binary words avoiding `010`* determines minimum cyclic superstring lengths, a different optimization. Its avoidance-counting series is the zero-distance specialization only and does not imply the positive-distance coefficients. Actual search comparisons included Hamming-distance enumerator, deep-hole, nearest-language and equivalent-formulation phrases, plus publication-level source inspection. An unindexed general theorem remains possible; no independence or historical-exhaustiveness certificate is claimed.

**Value — PASS.** Beyond a codebook count or a fixed covering-radius bound, the theorem calculates an exact all-length nearest-codeword distortion spectrum and constructs an optimal nearest-word quantizer. It gives polynomially many exact worst inputs even though the input space is exponential, and determines the contrasting typical error density \(1/10\) and worst-case density \(1/3\). These are motivated invariants for constrained quantization rather than a gratuitous finite sample.

**Limitations.** Binary alphabet, the one linear forbidden factor `010`, substitution metric, and uniform independent source assumption for the moments. No extension to cyclic strings, deletion distance, or other forbidden factors. Independent validation and independent publication-priority review have not been performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
