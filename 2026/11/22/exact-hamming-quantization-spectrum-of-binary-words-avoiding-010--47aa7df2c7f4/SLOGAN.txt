For binary words, the minimum number of substitutions needed to eliminate every contiguous `010` equals the maximum packing of disjoint `010` occurrences, giving the complete Hamming-quantization series \(\sum_{n\ge0}P_n(u)z^n=(1+z^2)/(1-2z+z^2-(1+u)z^3)\). The sharp radius is \(\lfloor n/3\rfloor\), with only polynomially many deepest words, while the uniform-source average correction cost grows as \(n/10\).
