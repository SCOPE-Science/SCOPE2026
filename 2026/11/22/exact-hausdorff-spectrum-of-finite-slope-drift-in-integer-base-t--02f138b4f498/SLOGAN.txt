For every integer-base Takagi–van der Waerden function, the set where finite-approximant slopes have prescribed average \(u\in[-1,1]\) has an exact Hausdorff dimension obtained from an explicit two-state pressure. The endpoint dimension is \(\log\lceil b/2\rceil/\log b\), and odd bases have a genuine correlated-sign spectrum rather than the independent-sign entropy formula.
