For discrete Rayleigh–Weibull lifetimes with survival \(\Pr(X\ge n)=q^{(e^{t n^\beta}-1)^2}\), the hazard is always increasing when \(\beta\ge1/2\), while for \(0<\beta<1/2\) it has an exact, strictly decreasing sequence of scale thresholds marking its only possible decline-to-increase transition. The threshold for all-age increasing hazard is the unique positive root of \((e^{2^\beta t}-1)/(e^t-1)=\sqrt2\), and the agewise thresholds satisfy \(n^\beta t_n\to z_\beta\) for an explicitly defined positive constant.
