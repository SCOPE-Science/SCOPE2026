The twenty-four hexagons of the Möbius–Kantor graph yield a Boolean convolution with exactly \(11{,}534{,}336\) surviving Walsh frequencies and a sharp nonzero spectral condition number of \(6\). The smallest possible \(\ell^1\)- or \(\ell^\infty\)-norm of any projection onto its recoverable range is exactly \(123/16\), without requiring translation symmetry.
