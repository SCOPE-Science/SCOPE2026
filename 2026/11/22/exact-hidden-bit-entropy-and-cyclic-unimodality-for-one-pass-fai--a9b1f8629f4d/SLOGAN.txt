For a single fair switching pass using each edge of a simple \(k\)-cycle exactly once, the private switch bits are fully recoverable from the final permutation unless the public edge order is cyclically unimodal. Exactly \(k2^{k-2}\) of the \(k!\) orders have this defect, and a uniformly ordered cycle loses exactly \(1/(k-1)!\) bits of conditional Shannon entropy on average.
