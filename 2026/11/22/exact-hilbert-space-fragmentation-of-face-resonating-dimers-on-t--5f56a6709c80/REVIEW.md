# Scientific review: exact face-flip sectors on truncated prisms

## Correctness — PASS

The claim concerns the \(6n\)-vertex truncation of the cubic prism for every integer \(n\ge3\). Odd external-edge parity at each original degree-three vertex gives precisely \(2^{n+1}\) perfect matchings. An explicit two-row binary representation reduces the physical octagonal-face move to a single-bit toggle only when its two cyclic neighbors are one and the row parity is zero; the two long end faces form one additional four-cycle. A permanent adjacent-zero core labels all other dynamic components, each isomorphic to an independent-set toggle graph on an induced subgraph of a cycle. This proves completeness, including the multiplicity recurrence and the parity-dependent frozen counts. The Rokhsar--Kivelson Hamiltonian is exactly the graph Laplacian at equal kinetic and potential couplings, with one kernel dimension per component. The \(v>t\) and \(v<t\) claims follow from nonnegative quadratic forms and a negative variational trial state, respectively. Both standalone computational checks were executed successfully on their actual source paths: the binary-word checker for \(3\le n\le11\), and independent graph-level dimer enumeration for \(3\le n\le7\). These finite checks are independent support, not a substitute for the all-\(n\) proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Close-packed quantum-dimer dynamics is a canonical test case for constrained many-body kinetic fragmentation. Vertex truncation of the natural circular-prism family creates a nonbipartite polyhedral host with genuine octagonal/end-face resonance terms. The finding completely resolves every connected Hilbert-space sector, its size and its Laplacian zero mode for all circumferences, rather than reporting a sample enumeration. The value is this natural complete classification and kinetic mechanism, not a claim of a thermodynamic phase or a new general Rokhsar--Kivelson principle.
