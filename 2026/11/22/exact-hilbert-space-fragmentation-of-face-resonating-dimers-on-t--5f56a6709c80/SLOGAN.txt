For all truncated cyclic prisms, the face-resonating quantum-dimer Hilbert space splits into exactly \(2^n+b_n-3\) sectors, where \(b_n\) counts cyclic binary strings with no isolated marked site. A single \(L_n+3\)-state component joins a Lucas cube to a four-cycle, while the fraction of exactly frozen dimer coverings tends to one half; these same sectors give the exact zero-mode multiplicity at the Rokhsar--Kivelson point.
