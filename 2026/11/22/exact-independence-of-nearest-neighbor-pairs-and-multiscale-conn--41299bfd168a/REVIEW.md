## Correctness — PASS

The proof identifies reciprocal nearest-neighbor pairs with cyclic local minima of uniform Dirichlet spacings and all-radius component counts with a symmetric long-gap count. For continuous exchangeable spacings, the entire cyclic rank permutation is independent of the sorted values. Their measurable functions are therefore independent, even as random processes. Exact mean and variance follow by local comparisons in uniform permutations; the exceptional sizes were checked separately. Standalone exact enumeration and analytical arguments cover complementary roles; no finite experiment is presented as infinite proof.

Rank-versus-order-statistic independence and the local-minimum interpretation are standard ingredients and are credited. ## Value — PASS

The result separates two fundamental classes of features in the same random spatial network: the local nearest-neighbor matching pattern, and the global connectivity response at every communication range. The exact independence is stronger than a covariance cancellation at one radius, and supports exact conditional reliability statements: observing any symmetric spacing or connectivity statistic conveys no information about the total number of reciprocal nearest-neighbor pairs. It also pinpoints why the superficially related isolated-vertex statistic does not share this separation. The structural generality within this natural model, rather than computational novelty, is the mathematical value.
