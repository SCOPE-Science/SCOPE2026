The exact sharp \(BV\)-to-\(L^1\) error norm of the degree-\(n\) Bernstein approximation is a finite maximum of beta order-statistic absolute deviations, attained by a single grid jump. Its decay has sharp constant \(\lim_{n\to\infty}\sqrt n\,C_n=1/\sqrt{2\pi}\), and all maximizing jump indices approach the grid midpoint.
