For classical Bernstein–Durrmeyer approximation, the exact \(L^1\) error divided by input total variation is at most \(\binom{2n+2}{n+1}/4^{n+1}\), and equality holds exactly for a nonzero centered single jump up to an additive constant. The sharp error decays as \(1/\sqrt{\pi n}\), revealing a factor-\(\sqrt2\) asymptotic penalty relative to point-sampled Bernstein approximation.
