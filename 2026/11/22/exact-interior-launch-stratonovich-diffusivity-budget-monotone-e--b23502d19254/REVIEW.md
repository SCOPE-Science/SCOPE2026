# Scientific scope of the gain theorem

The corrected result concerns strict monotonicity of the optimal fractional time gain and its precise small- and large-launch-position laws. The Stratonovich intrinsic time is minimized by the two levels from the established variational condition. The cubic determining the contrast follows by equating the two stationarity values in Menon, arXiv:2611.17470v1, Eq. (50); it is not an originality claim. The cited figure reports numerical gain values. The signed derivative of the optimized ratio and its cubic and square-root limits give the analytic result. The optimizer is admitted in the positive measurable class; smooth profiles approximate but do not attain it. Comparisons with the prior sources do not certify priority.
