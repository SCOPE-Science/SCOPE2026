For a fixed average diffusivity, the exact optimum for Stratonovich escape launched inside a reflecting–absorbing interval has a uniquely determined two-layer contrast governed by \(b t^3-a t-b=0\). The optimal escape-time gain rises strictly with launch position; as the target-side interval shrinks, it consumes exactly half the diffusivity budget to leading order and leaves a sharp \(2\sqrt{1-a}\) relative time deficit.
