# Scientific scope of the odd-cycle inball theorem

The corrected finding is the exact inradius in the odd-cycle embedding, with centroid uniqueness and all-facet tangency. The known combinatorial fact that the odd-cycle edge polytope is a simplex does not fix its Euclidean facet heights. The odd alternating closure determines the normalized facet-normal length, and the sum of those normals proves the global bound and uniqueness. Even cycles are excluded: the bipartition sign change, translation and scaling carry the known bipartite root-polytope inradius and center-dimension law to the even-cycle midpoint problem. A full version of that prior record was not available for comparison of additional center-locus descriptions. The exterior chord-distance result for the same hull computes a different invariant.
