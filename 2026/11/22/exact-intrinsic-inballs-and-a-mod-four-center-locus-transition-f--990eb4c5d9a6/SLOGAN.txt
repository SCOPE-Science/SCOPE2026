Selecting the edges of a Hamiltonian cycle from a regular simplex produces a midpoint polytope with an exact intrinsic Euclidean inradius and complete facet-distance spectrum. For even cycle length, the set of centers of largest inscribed balls switches from a unique point when \(4\mid n\) to a genuine \((n/2-1)\)-dimensional difference-constrained polytope when \(n\equiv2\pmod4\).
