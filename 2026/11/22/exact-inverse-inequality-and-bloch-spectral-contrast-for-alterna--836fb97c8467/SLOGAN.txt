On a periodic piecewise-linear spline grid with alternating cell lengths \(a,b>0\), the exact sharp derivative-to-mass constant is \(\sqrt{12/(ab)}\), attained only by alternating nodal signs. The full two-band generalized spectrum opens a gap precisely when the cell lengths differ, and its normalized width \(\delta/(4-\delta^2)\) uniquely identifies their relative contrast \(\delta=|a-b|/(a+b)\).
