For the published incommensurate two-sine map, every interior output has infinitely many unrestricted real one-step predecessors, with an exact density given by a one-dimensional arcsine-max integral. The density is \(\max(\alpha,\beta)/\pi\) at the zero output and vanishes toward either unattained amplitude endpoint at the sharp rate \(\sqrt{2(\alpha^2+\beta^2)}\sqrt\delta/\pi^2\).
