For binary sources forbidding adjacent `11`, the complete number of length-\(m+d\) deletion parents of any received word depends only on its length, Hamming weight, and adjacent `11` count, through one exact rational generating function. A received word has a unique admissible parent exactly when its number of adjacent `11` pairs equals the number of deletions; for every fixed positive deletion count, almost all feasible received words are uniquely invertible, with an explicit first-order error term.
