For indexed ternary words with no equal adjacent symbols, two-site maximum reads have exactly \(F_{m+3}-1\) possible outputs at read length \(m\); every individual inverse-list size is given by paired runs of the read symbol \(2\), and a rational bivariate series counts the full spectrum. The all-\(2\) observation uniquely maximizes ambiguity, while precisely four outputs have only two predecessors at every length \(m\ge3\).
