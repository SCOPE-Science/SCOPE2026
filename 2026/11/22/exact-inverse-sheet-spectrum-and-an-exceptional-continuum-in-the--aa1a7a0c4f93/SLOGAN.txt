A three-dimensional modular map used to generate pseudorandom sequences has an exact full-cube inverse-fiber count on every integer-gain slice: every gain \(m\ge2\) is onto, and one explicitly parameterized exceptional locus has continuum-sized fibers. At the natural gain \(m=64\), every other output has between \(106848\) and \(114400\) distinct one-step real predecessors.
