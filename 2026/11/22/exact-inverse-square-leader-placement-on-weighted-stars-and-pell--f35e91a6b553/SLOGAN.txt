Every fixed-budget inverse-square grounded-Laplacian leader optimum on an arbitrarily weighted star chooses either the hub plus the weakest \(k-1\) leaves or just the weakest \(k\) leaves, with an explicit quadratic comparison of the two costs. On equal-weight stars the hub-free choice begins near leader fraction \(1/\sqrt2\), and exact ties form an infinite Pell-equation sequence.
