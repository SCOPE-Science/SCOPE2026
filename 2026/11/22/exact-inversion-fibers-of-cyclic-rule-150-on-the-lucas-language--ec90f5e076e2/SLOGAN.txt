On the cyclic binary Lucas language, synchronous Rule 150 is uniquely invertible exactly when the ring length is not divisible by three; at lengths \(n=3m\), the entire nonempty one-step inverse-fiber spectrum is \(N_3=1\), \(N_2=3(2^{m-1}-1)\), and \(N_1=L_n-3\cdot2^m+3\). The same spectrum persists at every positive time on odd rings, whereas on multiples of six additional information is already lost after the second update.
