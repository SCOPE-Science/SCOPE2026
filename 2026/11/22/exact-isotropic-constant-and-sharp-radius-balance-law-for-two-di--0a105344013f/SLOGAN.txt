For the Minkowski sum of two noncoplanar circular disks of radii \(a,b>0\), the affine-invariant isotropic constant obeys \(L^6=(49-u^2)(11+u^2)/(110592\pi^2)\), with \(u=(a-b)/(a+b)\), independently of plane angle. Equal radii uniquely minimize it, and the exact deficit is \(u^2(38-u^2)/(110592\pi^2)\).
