For numerical semigroups with \(F=m+r<2m\), the exact genus-conditioned mean of embedding dimension is a finite matching-polynomial coefficient, while its unconditional mean has a universal \(5/2\) correction. Although embedding dimension alone obeys a Gaussian limit, the joint defect \(e+g-2m\) converges exponentially fast in total variation to a nondegenerate integer-valued law of mean \(2\).
