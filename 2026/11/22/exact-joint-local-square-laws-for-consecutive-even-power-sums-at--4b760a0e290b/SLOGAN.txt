For every odd prime \(p\), consecutive power sums \(\sum_{j=1}^n j^{p-1}\) have an exact four-cell joint square-status law modulo each \(p^k\). Their \(p\)-adic square-membership indicators are positively correlated when \(p\equiv3\pmod4\), but negatively correlated when \(p\equiv1\pmod4\), with explicit rational densities and logarithmic index-counting error.
