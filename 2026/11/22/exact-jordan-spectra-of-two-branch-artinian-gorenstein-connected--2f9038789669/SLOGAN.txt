Every multiplication Jordan partition in the two-branch Gorenstein algebra \(\mathbb F_q[x,y]/(xy,x^a-y^b)\) has an exact finite-field count from a two-valuation sum, with one possible root-of-minus-one resonance. In the balanced family the types \((n,n)\) and \((n+1,n-1)\) exchange precise masses governed by \(\gcd(n,q-1)\) and the existence of an \(n\)-th root of \(-1\).
