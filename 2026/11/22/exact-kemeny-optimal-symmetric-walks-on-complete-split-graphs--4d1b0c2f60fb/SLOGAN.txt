For every complete split graph \(K_p\vee\overline{K_q}\) with \(p,q\ge2\), the unique symmetric stochastic walk minimizing Kemeny's constant sets every crossing probability to \(1/\max\{p,q\}\) and every clique-edge probability to \((p-q)_+/[p(p-1)]\), even among non-invariant symmetric competitors. Clique edges vanish exactly when \(q\ge p\); forcing them to vanish when \(p>q\) has an exact positive cost and can increase Kemeny's constant by an unbounded factor.
