For every equal-arm spider with at least three arms and at least three edges per arm, the unique symmetric-stochastic Kemeny-optimal walk saturates the hub, alternates two exactly computable edge probabilities down each arm, and leaves positive holding mass at exactly one nonterminal junction per arm. An equal-edge maximum-degree walk has asymptotic Kemeny cost \(k/2\) times the optimum as arm length grows with arm count \(k\) fixed.
