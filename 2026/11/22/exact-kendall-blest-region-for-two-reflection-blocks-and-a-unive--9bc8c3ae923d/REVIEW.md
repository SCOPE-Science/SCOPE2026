**PASS.** The proof defines the rank coefficients, the graph-copula class and the entire parameter range, including endpoints. Direct block integration gives the exact coefficients; right-packing and an adjacent swap determine placement and block order; a monotone scalar comparison proves the sharp lower bound for any finite or countable block collection; a strictly decreasing cubic with a unique root determines the upper ratio optimum; the total-length constraint gives the explicit transition.
