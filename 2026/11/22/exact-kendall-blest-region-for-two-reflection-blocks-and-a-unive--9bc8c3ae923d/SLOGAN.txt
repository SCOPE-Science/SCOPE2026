The complete Kendall \(\tau\)–symmetrized Blest \(\eta\) region for copulas with at most two reflected intervals has explicit lower and piecewise algebraic upper boundaries, with a sharp saturation transition at \(\tau\approx-0.09472465\). The lower boundary remains sharp even across any finite or countable number of reflection intervals, while every unsaturated finite reflection-block configuration admits a strict same-Kendall improvement by refinement.
