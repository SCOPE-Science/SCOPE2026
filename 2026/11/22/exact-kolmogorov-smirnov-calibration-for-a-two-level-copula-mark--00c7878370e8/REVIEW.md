# Scientific review: two-level copula Kolmogorov–Smirnov calibration

## Correctness — PASS

The complete probability model and the admissible parameter region are specified. The proof recovers the exact two-state transition matrix, uses its bounded martingale CLT to identify the knot variance, and obtains a functional limit from independent conditional-uniform empirical bridges. Conditioning on the Gaussian knot, using Dirichlet Brownian heat kernels for both bridge segments, and integrating proves the two-sided supremum CDF for every positive threshold. Anderson's inequality supplies the *strict*, not merely weak, stochastic ordering. The periodic and absorbing endpoints are analyzed separately. The included code checks the block transition algebra, equivalent covariance normalization, iid Kolmogorov law, ordering in several parameter regimes, periodic-endpoint limit and an independent finite-sample simulation. Analytic steps, not these finite checks, justify the full-domain assertions. A remaining scientific risk is whether the same closed-form null CDF occurs under an unlocated alternative parametrization.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge, provisional)

The copula, its eigenfunction, the two-level block structure and spectral mixing results are prior work of Longla (2023/2024); they are not claimed as new. The general weak-dependence empirical-process CLT of Doukhan–Fermanian–Lang (2009) already gives the existence of a Gaussian limiting process under appropriate conditions. Longla–Hamadou (2025) and Longla–Siddiqua (2025) give related copula estimation CLTs and independence tests; Janczura–Weron (2013) derive a weighted residual KS procedure for regime-switching models. These results do not state or algebraically evaluate the ordinary unweighted marginal KS limit as the single killed-heat-kernel integral, nor establish its strict persistence ordering and the two extreme calibration regimes. Relevant published findings on Gaussian endpoint bridges concern one-sided persistence with random endpoints, not a two-sided Brownian bridge with a single random interior knot and a Markov-copula source. The closest own recorded copula findings concern exact parameter admissibility or mixing spectra, not this empirical-CDF limit. The 2025 handbook chapter was available as an extensive public overview, but full text could not be obtained through accessible authorized sources; its generic CLT material remains a priority risk. General theories are credited as the foundation rather than displaced. No claim of unique historical priority is made.

## Value — PASS

A dependent sample's ordinary Kolmogorov–Smirnov null law need not be Kolmogorov's universal iid law, even with a correctly specified uniform marginal. This model admits an explicit one-dimensional calibration rather than general simulation or an abstract Gaussian covariance specification. The strict ordering identifies which conventional test thresholds become anticonservative and conservative, and the periodic-versus-absorbing endpoints mark a sharp change from a pinned Gaussian bridge to inconsistency of the empirical marginal. These are natural questions for goodness-of-fit under Markov dependence, not arbitrary parameter slices or an isolated numerical improvement.
