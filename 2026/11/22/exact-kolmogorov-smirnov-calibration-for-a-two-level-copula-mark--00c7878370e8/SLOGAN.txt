For the two-level Longla copula Markov chain, the ordinary marginal Kolmogorov–Smirnov statistic has an exact asymptotic null CDF given by a single Gaussian integral of two killed Brownian-bridge probabilities, strictly ordered by the dependence parameter. At the periodic endpoint it becomes the square of a rescaled classical Kolmogorov CDF; at the absorbing endpoint the empirical marginal CDF is inconsistent.
