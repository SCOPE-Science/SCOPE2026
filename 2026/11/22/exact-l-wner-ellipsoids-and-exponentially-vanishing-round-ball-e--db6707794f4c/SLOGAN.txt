The ordinary edge polytope of every even cycle has an explicit, unique Löwner ellipsoid touching all vertices. In its intrinsic Euclidean embedding, the ellipsoid's volume is exactly \(m/2^{m-1}\) times that of the smallest enclosing ball on \(2m\) cycle vertices, even though every vertex touches both surfaces; its semiaxis ratio is \(\cot(\pi/(2m))\).
