For every differentiably moving finite barcode, the full persistence landscape has an exact metric speed determined only by the lifetimes and velocities of the two endpoints of each bar, even when bars overlap or coincide. For \(p=2\), its squared speed is \(\frac12\sum_i(d_i-b_i)(|b_i'|^2+|d_i'|^2)\), so no landscape construction or sorting is needed to evaluate it.
