The rotating-memory Fibonacci sequence has an exact least eventual period and a sharp least preperiod modulo every integer \(M\ge2\): away from primes 2 and 3 the period is \(k\operatorname{ord}_m(3\cdot2^{k-2})\), while all 2- and 3-power transients have explicit cutoff formulas. The exceptional rotation length \(k=2\) retains its 2-adic component, giving period \(2\operatorname{ord}_{M/3^{\nu_3(M)}}(3)\) whenever that reduced modulus exceeds one.
