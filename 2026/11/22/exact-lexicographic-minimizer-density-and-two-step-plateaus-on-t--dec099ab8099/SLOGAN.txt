On the stationary binary no-`11` source, ordinary lexicographic three-window minimizers have exact density \(\rho_k=(2+\varphi^{-(2\lfloor k/2\rfloor+1)})/(\varphi+2)\) at every \(k\ge1\). The sampling density has strict two-step plateaus and converges to \(1-1/\sqrt5\); exact endpoint-refined Fibonacci counts explain the phenomenon.
