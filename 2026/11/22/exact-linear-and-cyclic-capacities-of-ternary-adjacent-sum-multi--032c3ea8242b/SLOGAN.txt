For an unordered multiset of consecutive integer sums over the ternary alphabet \(\{0,1,2\}\), the exact number of possible outputs from \(m\) linear reads is \(\binom{m+4}{4}-m^2+1\), while \(m\) cyclic reads admit exactly \((m^4+8m^3-28m^2+136m+27\mathbf1_{2\nmid m})/48\) outputs. Closing the word into a circle therefore halves the leading zero-error codebook capacity under this specific readout.
