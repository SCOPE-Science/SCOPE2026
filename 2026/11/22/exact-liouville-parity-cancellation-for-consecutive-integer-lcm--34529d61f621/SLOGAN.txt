For Farhi's consecutive-integer LCM quotient \(Q_k(n)\), even and odd values of the total prime-factor count \(\Omega(Q_k(n))\) occur with exactly equal frequency if and only if \(k+1=3\cdot2^a\) for an integer \(a\ge0\). All other window lengths have a strict Liouville-parity imbalance, determined by a unique dyadic cancellation among the independent prime-local factors.
