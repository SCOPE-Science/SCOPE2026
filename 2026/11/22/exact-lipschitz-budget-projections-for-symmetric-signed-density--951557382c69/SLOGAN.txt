For a real continuous-function hyperplane defined by any symmetric, almost-everywhere positive signed density, the least projection norm under a Lipschitz correction budget has an exact scalar-integral formula and a unique clipped-ramp minimizer. If the density is asymptotic to \(ct^\beta\) at the sign-change point, the excess over the unattained constant \(2\) is exactly asymptotic to \(2cL^{-(\beta+1)}/((\beta+1)(\beta+2))\), realizing every positive polynomial decay exponent.
