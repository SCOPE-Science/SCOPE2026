For zigzag order polytopes, every vertex degree is \(n+\sum_i\binom{k_i+a_i+b_i-1}{2}\), determined by clusters of alternating ones in a corresponding path-independent-set word. Exactly four vertices are simple in every dimension \(n\ge3\), whereas the maximum degree is \(n+\binom{\lceil n/2\rceil}{2}\), attained once in odd dimension and four times in even dimension.
