# Scientific review: exact raw Jordan-power filtration

## Correctness — PASS

The central-coset proof yields the stronger identity \(L_n\cap Kh_v=I_{S(v)}I^{n-2}h_v\) for every \(n\ge2\). Its reverse inclusion constructs one commutator coordinate at a time and proves all required monomials occur. Summing squarefree monomials gives the all-rank dimensions, with no reliance on finite enumeration for the infinite theorem. Exact, independent binary checks for \(D_8\), \(Q_8\), mixed rank-two products, and a rank-three polynomial recurrence agree with the proof.

Bhandari–Passi and GAP LAGUNA use associative ideals generated by commutators, not the raw left-normed product spans whose dimensions are determined here. No covering full dimension spectrum was identified in accessible literature and indexed records; the inaccessible full text of Gildea and other specialist work remain material priority risks.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This classifies the full homogeneous and numerical Jordan/Lie lower central filtration for direct products of order-eight nonabelian groups in characteristic two. It identifies a structural top-layer defect and shows exact dihedral/quaternion insensitivity across all ranks, beyond a mere index or low-order census.

## Limits

Only raw left-normed Jordan-power vector spaces in characteristic two, for stated direct products and finite abelian factors, are covered. The claim does not classify arbitrary class-two groups or the conventional two-sided lower Lie-power ideals. Independent specialist review remains necessary.
