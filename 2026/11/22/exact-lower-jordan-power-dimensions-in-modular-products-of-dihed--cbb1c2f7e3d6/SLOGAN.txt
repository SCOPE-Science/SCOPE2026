In characteristic two, the complete raw Jordan-power filtration of \(k[A\times H_1\times\cdots\times H_r]\), with each \(H_i\) dihedral or quaternion of order eight and \(A\) finite abelian, has dimension \(|A|\sum_{j=n-1}^{r}\binom rj(4^r-4^{r-j})\) in degree \(n\ge2\). The filtration is independent of every dihedral/quaternion choice and has last nonzero layer of dimension \(|A|(4^r-1)\), despite being strictly smaller than the corresponding associative commutator ideals.
