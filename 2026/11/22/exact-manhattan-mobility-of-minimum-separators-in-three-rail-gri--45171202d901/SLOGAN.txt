For a three-row rectangular grid with terminals at the two middle-row endpoints, every minimum separating triple moves optimally by coordinatewise Manhattan transport: preserving a minimum cut never costs an extra adjacent slide. All \(9(n-2)\) minimum cuts form a connected reconfiguration graph with exactly \(16n-36\) edges, radius \(2+\lceil3(n-3)/2\rceil\), and diameter \(3n-5\), for every \(n\ge3\).
