In Brownian search with independently sampled reset positions, the reset site responsible for the first excursion above any threshold outside the reset region is biased toward the far endpoint, whereas the site responsible for eventual capture is biased toward the target. Their exact conditional joint law is a diagonal/product mixture with dependence weight \(e^{-m\sqrt{r/D}}\), and at rapid resetting the two origins approach opposite endpoints with independent Gamma boundary layers.
