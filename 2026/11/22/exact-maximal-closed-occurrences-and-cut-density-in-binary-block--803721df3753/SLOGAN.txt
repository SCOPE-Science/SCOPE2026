For every binary block power \((0^a1^b)^k\) with \(a,b\ge1\) and \(k\ge3\), all maximal closed substring occurrences consist exactly of the maximal constant runs, bridges between successive same-symbol runs and the whole word. Their number is \((k-1)(a+b)+3\), and the exact largest number crossing one cut is \(a+b-1\), attained at the boundaries between complete periods.
