For every prime \(p\ge5\) with at least one divisible single-digit central trinomial coefficient, maximal divisible runs below \(p^L\) have lengths exactly \(p^j\), occurring \(z(p-z)^{L-j-1}\) times, where \(z\) counts the divisible single-digit coefficients. All nondivisible runs then have a sharp, explicitly computed bound smaller than \(p\), and their complete length-frequency distribution is determined at every block depth. If \(z=0\), no central trinomial coefficient is divisible by \(p\).
