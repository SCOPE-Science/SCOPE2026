The indices at which \(\binom{3n}{n}\) is odd contain no four-term arithmetic progressions, and the exact number of their three-term progressions below \(2^r\), across all positive spacings, has generating function \(z^2/((1-z-z^2)(1-z-3z^2+2z^4))\). This classifies the full dyadic census of their longest possible progressions.
