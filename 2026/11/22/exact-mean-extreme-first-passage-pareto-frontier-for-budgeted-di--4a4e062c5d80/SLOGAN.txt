A fixed-budget diffusion profile that minimizes mean first-passage time is not optimal for the fastest of many independent searchers: it improves the single-searcher mean by \(1/9\) but worsens the leading collective-arrival time by a factor \(32/27\). Every nondominated compromise between these objectives has a unique explicitly parametrized spatial diffusivity profile, with exact costs involving a quartic relation and logarithms.
