The complete ordered Hamming-weight readout of every length-\(h\) window of a binary length-\(n\) word has exactly \(\prod_j(2^{\ell_j}-1)+\sum_j\prod_{i\ne j}(2^{\ell_i}-1)\) distinct values, where \(\ell_j\) are the lengths of the \(h\) residue columns. Every inverse fiber is binomial, its size determined by how many residue columns are all-zero or all-one; the formulas give the exact number of nontrivial metameric classes for every \(n\ge h\ge2\), resolving a previously posed local-histogram counting question.
