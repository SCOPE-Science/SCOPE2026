For rate-one zero-temperature edge-exchange Kawasaki dynamics with five positive spins on the Petersen ferromagnet, only \(8/63\) of uniformly chosen initial configurations ever reach a ground state. The other \(55/63\) enter one irreducible, permanently active 120-state excited class; the complete 252-state recurrent decomposition and mean terminal-sector entrance time \(5/42\) are exact.
