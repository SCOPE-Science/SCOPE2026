The canonical vertex-incidence perfectly matchable-subgraph polytope of an \(n\)-vertex path has an explicit parity-dependent Euclidean inradius and a full incenter locus of dimension \(n-3\) for odd \(n\) and \(n-4\) for even \(n\). Unlike its unique-incenter ordinary matching-edge realization with constant inradius, its natural ambient realization has radius asymptotic to \(n^{-1/2}\) and increasingly many optimal-center degrees of freedom.
