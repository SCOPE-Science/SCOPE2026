For the quadratic cardinal-spline \(L^2\) projector, the exact norm of evaluation at the midpoint of a unit cell is an explicit geometrically convergent quadratic-root series lying strictly between \(1.75676378\) and \(1.75676379\). Its midpoint positivity-loss defect on data between zero and one is exactly half the norm excess above one; no claim is made about the full projector norm.
