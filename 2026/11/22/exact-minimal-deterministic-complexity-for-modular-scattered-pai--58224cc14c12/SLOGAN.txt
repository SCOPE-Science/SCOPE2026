The minimal complete deterministic automaton for binary words avoiding the contiguous factor \(0^r1\) and having a number of scattered \(01\) occurrences divisible by \(m\) has exactly \(rm^2+2\) states for all \(r,m\ge2\). Every active modular/run signature is necessary, but the entire terminal run collapses to one accepting class and one rejecting class; at \(r=1\), the complexity is exactly three.
