# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof derives automorphisms as a simultaneous permutation centralizer and uses the full wreath-product decomposition of equivariant permutations of a finite abelian group-set. Restricting the centralizer to exponent \(p\) leaves exactly the counted orbit types. The faithful-degree inequality, the forced odd-prime free orbit and explicit extremizers cover every stated prime, rank and boundary. Exact finite tests corroborate selected constructions but do not certify the general theorem by themselves.

## Originality — PASS (historical priority provisional)
The closest motivating source, Militaru arXiv:2611.03380v1, proves a broader arbitrary-map centralizer realization and asks about minimum-size realizations. Agore--Chirvasitu--Militaru arXiv:2303.06700 gives arbitrary-map universal centralizers and one-function wreath theory. Militaru arXiv:2610.39228 gives other broad Yang--Baxter realization families; Jedlička--Pilitowska--Zamojska-Dzienio arXiv:2011.00229 classifies a different involutive indecomposable class. No inspected primary statement, relevant mathematical-result search, or accessible related prior claim supplied the new restricted exact degree function or sharp rank cutoffs. Wreath decompositions and the commuting-permutation ansatz are known inputs; an unindexed equivalent remains a priority risk.

## Value — PASS
An explicit open direction concerns minimization of automorphism realizations. This result determines all attainable elementary-abelian ranks and minimal underlying sizes within a canonical nondegenerate solution class. Its obstruction is global in cardinality and exhibits a binary exception, so it gives structural restrictions instead of merely small computations.

## Limitations
This result does not bound automorphism ranks in all Yang--Baxter solutions or those built from arbitrary maps. The finite checker is supplementary, and no independent scientific assessment has occurred.
