For braid solutions built from two commuting permutations, the elementary-abelian group \((C_p)^r\) occurs as the full automorphism group only up to rank \(p+3\) for odd \(p\), or rank six for \(p=2\). Its exact smallest underlying set has \(pr\) points until the odd-prime threshold \(r=p+1\), after which the two remaining realizable ranks require \(p^2+p(r-2)\) points.
