Every depth-\(h\) ordinary modal type on finite outward rooted trees has a unique smallest tree witness; their minimum-order spectrum is the full interval from \(1\) to a recursively determined maximum, with exact weighted generating polynomial \(P_{h+1}(z)=qz\prod_j(1+z^j)^{[z^j]P_h(z)}\). Among uniformly chosen depth-\(h\) types, minimum witness order obeys an explicit Berry--Esseen bound and exponential concentration around exactly half its maximum.
