For every strictly positive one-spike spectrum, the worst possible eigenvector orientation makes all interior \(k\)-column Nyström choices equally good, so even the optimal deterministic choice cannot beat the \(k\)-DPP mean trace error. The equality holds for every \(n\ge4\), \(2\le k\le n-2\), and the worst orientations are exactly those with a flat-magnitude spike eigenvector.
