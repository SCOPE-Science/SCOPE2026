For undirected length-two Kautz networks with \(q=3,4,5,6\) symbols, the exact minimum sizes of radius-one identifying codes are \(4,4,5,7\), attained by respectively \(9,168,24,4320\) labeled codes. Every minimum code is classified into \(3,10,1,6\) alphabet-relabeling orbits, demonstrating a sharp change between the five- and six-symbol cases.
