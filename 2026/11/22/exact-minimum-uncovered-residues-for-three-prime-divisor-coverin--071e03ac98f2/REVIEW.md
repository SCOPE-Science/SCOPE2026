# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The first three classes have exact CRT inclusion–exclusion count. Each pair-divisor class has an unavoidable one-point intersection with the previously selected class modulo its omitted prime, imposing the three stated incremental caps. A final point cap yields the global bound, and an explicit seven-coordinate CRT witness attains every cap. This proves the formula uniformly, not merely in the checked examples. `verify.py` independently enumerates witness coverage and tests selected additional choices.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** The identical identity appears as **Conjecture 1** in Öztürk's August 2026 paper, accompanied by 25 finite instances and an explicit statement that a proof was unknown. The nearest general covering-system literature, including Dai et al. (2026), gives restrictions on entire covering systems but not the exact three-prime optimum. Relevant indexed mathematical results and accessible prior claims have been compared. An unindexed later or independent solution cannot be excluded.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The theorem resolves a directly motivated published conjecture over every squarefree integer with exactly three odd prime factors, and provides both an exact minimum and constructive maximizers. It does not resolve the unrestricted odd-modulus covering problem.

**Closest primary literature.** Öztürk, *Closed-form nonexistence certificates for odd covering systems* (2026), §6, Conjecture 1, and Dai–Li–Li–Yi, *On the moduli of distinct covering systems* (2026), §6.2. This review is not independent mathematical validation.
