# Scientific review: exact mixed-composite rational quadratic-floor coprimality densities

## Correctness — PASS
The proof reduces each residue class to a two-linear-argument gcd. All possible shared primes divide an explicit bounded remainder. Prime factors of the denominator either forbid the entire fiber or have no effect; other primes exclude exactly one class each. CRT proves the full rational density and a period. The zero-remainder case yields the uniquely located square-denominator spike. The exact checked model agrees with 309466 direct gcd/floor comparisons in 1102 parameter cases. The code is finite supporting evidence, not the proof.

## Originality — PASS, with stated residual risks
The nearby arXiv:2611.04345v1 theorem requires irrational quadratic coefficients. The published rational-floor prime-denominator theorem and an accessible prior prime-square finding do not cover denominators with multiple distinct prime factors; the linear rational-floor gcd histogram uses degree one. These are comparisons of mathematical implication, not title matching. Full available primary statements and the two relevant published records were inspected. No mixed-composite classification was located, but search cannot certify historical uniqueness and some older-week history was unavailable.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The result resolves an entire natural arithmetic regime: all mixed-composite denominators and every reduced rational numerator. Its explicit density, periodic-plus-spike classification and squareclass symmetry reveal local obstructions unseen in the prime denominator formula. The work does not claim the CRT itself is novel; it gives a motivated exact structural classification.
