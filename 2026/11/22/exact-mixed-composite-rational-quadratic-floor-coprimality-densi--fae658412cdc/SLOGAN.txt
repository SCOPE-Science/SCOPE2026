For every reduced rational quadratic coefficient with a denominator having at least two distinct prime factors, the coprimality indicator \(\mathbf1_{\gcd(n,\lfloor an^2/b\rfloor)=1}\) is explicitly periodic, except for a single initial success \(n=\sqrt b\) precisely when the denominator is square. Its rational density is an exact finite Euler-factor sum with a numerator square-class symmetry.
