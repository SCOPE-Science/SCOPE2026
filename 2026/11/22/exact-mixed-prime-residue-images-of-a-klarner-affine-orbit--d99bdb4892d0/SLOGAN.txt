For the Klarner orbit generated from zero by \(2x\), \(3x+2\), and \(6x+3\), every modulus \(m=2\cdot3^bu\) with \(b\geq1\) and \(\gcd(u,6)=1\) has an explicit complete reachable-residue classification: exactly \(u(3^{b+1}+1)/2\) classes occur. Its compatible parity–ternary closure has Haar measure exactly \(3/4\), although the orbit is not claimed to have ordinary density \(3/4\).
