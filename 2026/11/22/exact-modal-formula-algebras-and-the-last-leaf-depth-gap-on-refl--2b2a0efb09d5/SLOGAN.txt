On a finite reflexive rooted star with \(k\) leaves and \(s\) Boolean letters, the entire ordinary modal formula algebra has exactly \(2^{q\sum_{j=1}^{\min(k,q)}\binom qj}\) operations when \(k\geq1\), with \(q=2^s\); no modal depth above two adds anything. Its last branching distinction occurs at \(k=q\), one leaf after depth-one formulas have already saturated, and the depth-two gap is sharp whenever \(s\geq1\) and \(k\geq2\).
