# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The invariant measure is the exact Poisson-age pushforward of the Adler orbit, with no fixed-point atoms. The rotating formula is a geometric sum of age preimages, while the locked formula has one age preimage. On every support interval \(p'/p=(K\cos\phi-r)/(\Delta-K\sin\phi)\), giving all mode counts and equality cases without numerical root finding. The stable-root expansion establishes the power-law exponent \(r/\sqrt{K^2-\Delta^2}-1\) and a strictly positive finite leading coefficient. At the double zero \(\Delta=K\), the exact antiderivative \(A=(\tan\phi+\sec\phi-1)/K\) proves the sharp essential edge and its prefactor. The standalone numerical diagnostics passed 97 checks; they are supplementary.

**Originality — PASS, subject to the stated literature risk.** Sarkar–Gupta (arxiv:2203.00339, §§II–III) derive stationary distributions of a *macroscopic Lorentzian Ott–Antonsen order parameter*, whose cubic drift and criticality differ from a finite detuned two-oscillator relative phase. Majumder–Chattopadhyay–Gupta (arxiv:2402.14921) study subsystem resetting, which does not fully renew the two-body relative phase. Grange (arxiv:2111.15241, §§3 and 5) treats Brownian circular windings with shortest-arc returns. General resetting-moment theory (Olsen, 2023) motivates the age-mixture method but does not state the present sharp two-rate Adler mode/edge thresholds and essential saddle-node coefficient. Published indexed notes on Lorentzian order-parameter spectral and renewal-tail edges have different state variables, reset clocks, and conclusions. Prior general transport identities remain genuinely relevant; they are not claimed as new. Unindexed or incompletely accessible close work is a residual publication-priority risk.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The mode and locking-edge behavior of the stationary relative-phase density are natural experimental observables of a minimal synchronizing pair. Their exact, complete classification reveals different effects of the reset rate relative to the coupling and to the deterministic locking relaxation rate, and it distinguishes algebraic edge accumulation from a super-algebraically depleted saddle-node edge. The mode location \(\arccos(r/K)\) in the interior-mode regimes is independent of detuning, while the locked-edge exponent separately records \(\sqrt{K^2-\Delta^2}\). This provides a useful geometric and inverse-model diagnostic rather than an arbitrary finite parameter slice.
