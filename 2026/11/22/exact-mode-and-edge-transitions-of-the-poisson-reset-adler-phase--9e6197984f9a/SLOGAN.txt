For a Poisson-reset detuned two-oscillator Adler phase, the stationary angular density has sharp morphology thresholds at the coupling \(K\) and, in the locked phase, the relaxation rate \(\sqrt{K^2-\Delta^2}\). At the locking saddle-node \(\Delta=K\), its stationary endpoint changes from algebraic behavior to an explicit super-algebraically flat essential edge, while the rotating phase has two interior extrema precisely when the reset rate is below \(K\).
