For Dombos partitions, the entire progression \(d(6n+4)\pmod4\) is determined by the quadratic-character divisor sum of \(8n+5\), and its nonzero residue has an exact prime-times-square classification. The exceptional coefficients occur with prime-like frequency \(\zeta(2)X/\log X\) through index \(X\), rather than positive density.
