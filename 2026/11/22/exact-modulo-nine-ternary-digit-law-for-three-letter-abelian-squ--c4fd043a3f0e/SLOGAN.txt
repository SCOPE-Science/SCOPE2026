For the three-letter Abelian-square counting sequence \(A(n)\), every ternary digit factors independently modulo nine: digits \(0,1,2\) contribute \(1,3,6\) respectively. Hence \(A(n)\) is divisible by nine exactly when its positive index has at least two nonzero ternary digits; the only valuation-one indices are \(3^j\) and \(2\cdot3^j\).
