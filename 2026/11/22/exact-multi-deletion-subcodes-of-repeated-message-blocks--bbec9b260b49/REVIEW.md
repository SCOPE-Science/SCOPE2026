# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For common exactly-t descendants in a positional alignment, source deletions spoil at most t blocks and target deletions in internal matched gaps spoil at most t additional blocks. Thus one full source block occurs consecutively in the other power when r≥2t+1. Its first matched positions differ by the difference of the preceding deletion counts and hence by at most t, giving the claimed signed phase shift. Conversely, the common unchanged overlap at a phase shift s has length rm−|s| and can be shortened to rm−t. The observation remains a linear word. At least period d each orbit is a labeled cycle-power conflict graph: the maximum independent set has gaps at least t+1 for two or more selected points, yielding the floor when feasible and one otherwise. For d>t, marking a chosen vertex, counting the ordered excess gaps and dividing by k counts each maximal-size set exactly once; for d≤t the only choices are singletons. Primitive-period Möbius inversion supplies the orbit counts. Proper periods contribute at most Σ_{d|m,d<m}q^d=O(mq^(m/2)) messages, so the full-length term proves the claimed limit. The up-to-t consequence uses further deletion of a common descendant of length at least rm−t.

## originality

PASS

The distinct result is the signed phase criterion for repeated blocks at general deletion budget and its resulting exact restricted-code optimum and enumerator. Abstract cycle-power counts are known, and there is closely related single-insertion work; neither establishes the full multiple-deletion conflict graph.

## value

PASS

For a natural redundancy encoder, the theorem resolves exactly which messages stay confusable despite extra repetitions. The graph result gives constructive optimum selection and the count of optimum labeled subcodes; the exact penalty tends to a nontrivial fraction determined by the deletion budget. The value is the channel-specific structure, not the known cycle-power enumerator by itself.
