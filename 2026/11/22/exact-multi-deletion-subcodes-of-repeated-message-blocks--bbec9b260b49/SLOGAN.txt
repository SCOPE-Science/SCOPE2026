Repeating every message block at least \(2t+1\) times does not remove cyclic-phase ambiguity under \(t\ge2\) adversarial symbol deletions: two repeated blocks are confusable exactly when their messages are within \(t\) cyclic shifts. This gives an all-alphabet exact capacity, a closed count of every maximizing subcode, and limiting retained-message fraction \(1/(t+1)\).
