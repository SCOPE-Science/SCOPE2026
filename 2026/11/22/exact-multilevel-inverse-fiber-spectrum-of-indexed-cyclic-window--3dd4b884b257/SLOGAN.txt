Complete integer-sum readout of a \(q\)-ary circle has inverse lists given exactly by coefficients of bounded-composition polynomials across residue classes. Their total number is \(\bigl(q^m-(q-1)^m\bigr)^{d-1}\bigl(q^m+(d-1)(q-1)^m\bigr)\), and ternary three-residue channels have seven explicitly enumerated list sizes.
