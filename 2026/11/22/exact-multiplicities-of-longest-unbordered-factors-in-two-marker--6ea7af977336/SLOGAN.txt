For every binary word containing exactly two ones, the number of occurrences of a longest unbordered factor is at most four; all cases have an exact rational all-length generating function, and the fourfold case is the unique equal-gap word at each eligible length. Under uniform fixed-weight sampling, the count converges to one with probability \(2/3\) and two with probability \(1/3\), with an explicit joint limit law for the maximizing length.
