For the ordinary Anderson–Darling test and every higher-order generalized Watson test computed on the same continuous-null sample, the finite-sample covariance is exactly \((1-1/n)\kappa_m\), with an explicit positive rational \(\kappa_m\). Their correlation rises strictly with sample size and falls strictly with Watson integration order, but retains a nonzero limit of about \(0.6487643381\) when order and then sample size tend to infinity.
