The exact worst norm of every iterate of a nearly non-contractive permutation on a normalized finite Lipschitz-free space is governed by distances to the next multiples of attainable two-point orbit lengths: \(\max\|U_f^k\|=1+\min\{1,\varepsilon B_n(k)\}\). For positive tolerance the sharp envelope has fundamental temporal period \(\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,n)\), despite each individual permutation having a much shorter period.
