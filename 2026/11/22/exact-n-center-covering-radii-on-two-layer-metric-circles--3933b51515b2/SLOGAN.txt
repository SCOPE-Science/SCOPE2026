A metric circle crossed with two points has a complete, exact \(N\)-center covering-radius formula: \(R_N=\min\{L/(2\lfloor N/2\rfloor),(L+2s\lceil N/2\rceil)/(2N)\}\), with the first entry infinite for \(N=1\). Alternating centers are optimal until the two-point separation reaches \(L/(2\lfloor N/2\rfloor)\); beyond it, separate-sheet coverings become optimal, producing a sharp parity-dependent transition.
