# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The exact formula follows from unitary cyclic Fourier transforms on each ring, descending azimuthal-order alias elimination, and leading coefficients of associated Legendre polynomials. Each positive power in the determinant is counted: ring Fourier normalizations, latitudinal half-powers, and repeated Vandermonde differences. Strict log-concavity on each order chamber proves uniqueness there and boundary decay. At nine samples the two stationary branches are exactly solved, and their global comparison has a positive rational difference. An independent standard-library direct matrix checker tests varying offsets, latitudes and bandwidths; rational quadratic-field assertions test the stationary equations and comparison. These finite tests supplement, but do not prove, the all-bandwidth theorem.

## Originality — PASS relative to accessible evidence
Khalid (arXiv:2611.16441, source submitted 2026-11-12) proves exact nested-ring sampling and optimizes error amplification, which neither states nor implies a closed determinant expression or global D-optimal nine-sample latitude pair. Earlier sampling schemes by Khalid–Kennedy–McEwen (arXiv:1403.4661) and Nafees et al. (arXiv:1709.02497) optimize interpolation condition numbers, a distinct objective. The historical one/three/five layout in NASA report 19730008792 is distinct from an exact unrestricted latitude maximization. Nearby published problems optimize spherical-harmonic conditioning for other support geometries, or a two-ring phase offset. The known Legendre/Vandermonde identities themselves are expressly not asserted as new. A pre-existing implicit determinant/fekete-design computation remains possible; database silence does not prove novelty.

## Value — PASS
The new invariant is the full information-volume determinant for the sample-minimal spherical-harmonic transform, a natural experimental-design criterion with direct relevance to invertibility and spectral sampling. The theorem gives an exact all-bandwidth factor, offset invariance, a rigorous chamber concavity principle, and the first nontrivial full nine-sample global latitude optimization in radicals. The equiangular nine-sample placement is improved by about six percent in absolute determinant, under the exact same number of samples and ring architecture, without claiming improvements for other stability metrics.

## Related literature and limitations
Primary relevant material read: Khalid 2026 Sections II–VI and key interpolation formulas; Khalid et al. 2014 ring placement discussion; Nafees et al. 2017 placement/conditioning sections; Giacaglia–Lundquist 1972 one/three/five construction. Closest published mathematical-result summaries include a two-ring angular-offset determinant theorem and single-ring exact-degree quartic harmonic conditioning, neither of which yields the present multiring determinant formula. Global Fekete optimality among unconstrained sphere nodes, stochastic noise performance, and full bibliographic priority remain unproved. Independent audit has not been performed.
