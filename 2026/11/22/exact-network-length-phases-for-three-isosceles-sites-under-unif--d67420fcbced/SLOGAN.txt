For the three Euclidean target sites \((-1,0),(1,0),(0,h)\), the shortest connected network lying within distance \(r\) of all three has an exact all-height, all-radius length formula and an explicit minimizer. Its canonical tripod, two-arm and horizontal/vertical single-segment phases meet at the sharp height thresholds \(h=1/\sqrt3,1,\sqrt3\), with a direct tripod-to-point collapse only at \(h=\sqrt3\).
