The integral symmetric torus sextics \((XY-Z^2)^3+s(X^3+Y^3+\lambda Z^3)^2=0\), for \(\lambda s\ne0\) and \(\lambda^2\ne4\), have precisely six permanent cusps and three explicit outer-node walls; the crossing \(\lambda^2=-4,\ s=-1/4\) yields a rational curve with four nodes. Away from those walls, their genus-four normalizations admit an explicit smooth quadric-cubic canonical model and a double covering of an elliptic cubic branched at six points.
