**Correctness — PASS.** The local rule, two-step de Bruijn graph, first-letter/prefix/suffix minimal-forbidden recognizer, productive transfer matrix, and exact polynomial identity are reconstructed from complete definitions. The machine has 29 reachable and 16 productive states; its determinant has factors \((1+z^4)(1-2z+z^2-z^4-z^8)\). Exact first eighteen coefficient identities, combined with the adjugate degree bound, establish the rational series for every length. The unique primitive productive recurrent component and exact Sturm root brackets support the positive exponential asymptotic. The independently computed 13-state image-language matrix has Perron root in \( (1.732101,1.732102) \), while the obstruction matrix root is in \( (1.647072,1.647073) \), proving a strict gap; Wu–Zhang (2026) supplies the measure-theoretic corollary. Three complete packaged checks passed, including independent input-block enumeration through length fourteen. Residual risk is implementation error common to finite-state constructions, mitigated by direct enumeration and two separately organized derivations; independent review has not occurred.

**Originality — PASS within inspected evidence.** The closest published results give (i) a complete one-step first-offender classification, (ii) the first time-one/time-two language difference and length-twelve Rule 126 witness, (iii) one-step cyclic image counts, and (iv) a published Rule 126 time-two process graph. Wu and Zhang (2026-11-02) give the general criterion for strict minimal-obstruction entropy gaps, but not Rule 126 computations. None of the inspected full results evaluates the all-length time-two minimal-forbidden series, its exponential growth, or the explicit entropy gap. The published process graph contains enough information to derive further invariants in principle, so the present claim is deliberately limited to the new exact invariant and the non-finite-type transition, not the underlying regular-language construction. Residual risk is an unindexed prior evaluation of the same generating series.

**Value — PASS.** Minimal forbidden words are a natural measure of image-language complexity. Rule 126 is singled out by the previously proved sharp twelve-letter first discrepancy. The result shows that this delayed first discrepancy conceals an actual transition from a one-obstruction finite-type image to a proper sofic image with exponentially many minimal obstructions, and quantifies the whole sequence and exponential rate rather than merely exhibiting a witness. The exact rate is strictly below the independently calculated image entropy (gap about \(0.05033578\) nats/site), providing an explicit nontrivial example to which the newly published maximal-entropy-measure criterion applies. Its consequence is credited to Wu–Zhang rather than claimed as a new ergodic theorem.
