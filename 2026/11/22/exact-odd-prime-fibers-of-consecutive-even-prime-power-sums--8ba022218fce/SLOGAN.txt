For every odd prime \(p\) and exponent \(2p^s\), the consecutive-input polynomial \(x^{2p^s}+(x+1)^{2p^s}\) has exactly \((p-1)/2\) regular full balls and one disjoint scaled-square component in \(\mathbb Z_p\). Every residue fiber at every prime-power level is explicit, and the image has exact Haar measure \(p^{1-s}/(2(p+1))\).
