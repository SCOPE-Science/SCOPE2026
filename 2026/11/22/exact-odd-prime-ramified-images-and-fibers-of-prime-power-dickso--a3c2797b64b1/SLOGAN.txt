For every odd prime \(p\ge5\), each iteration of the degree-\(p\) first-kind Dickson polynomial contracts its noncritical \(p\)-adic input classes by exactly one valuation step, but its two critical classes by exactly two. This determines every image residue and fiber modulo \(p^k\) for each iterate \(D_{p^r}\), with exact image Haar measure \((p-2)/p^{r+1}+2/p^{2r+1}\).
