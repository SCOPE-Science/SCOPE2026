For every odd prime \(p\), the complete \(p\)-adic valuation histogram of the two-index super Catalan factorial quotient on \(0\le m,n<p^r\) has the universal rational generating function \(\frac{1-ptz}{1-p(p+1)(1+t)z/2+p^3tz^2}\). The valuation is the occupation time of an exact two-state carry chain, giving all possible valuations, exact nondivisibility counts, and a Gaussian law.
