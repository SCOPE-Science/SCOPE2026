On a periodic compact \(U(1)\) gauge lattice with negative plaquette coupling, odd transverse plaquette area forces a sharp, uniformly distributed frustration defect: the exact optimized plaquette value is \(-\cos(\pi/A)\), rather than \(-1\). In three dimensions every optimal plaquette pattern is spatially constant, and an odd cubic torus pays only an \(O(L^{-1})\) total excess energy, with eight discrete plaquette branches.
