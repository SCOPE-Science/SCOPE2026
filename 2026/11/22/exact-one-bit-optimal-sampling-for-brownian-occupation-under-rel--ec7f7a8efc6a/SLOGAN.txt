One Gaussian-noisy **sign bit** of Brownian motion has a unique optimal time for estimating its positive occupation fraction at every finite relative noise level: \(x=1/(a^2+k(a)^2)\), with \(a=(1+\eta)^{-1/2}\) and explicit \(k\). In the noiseless limit, this occurs at \(16/(16+\pi^2)\), and that single bit explains exactly \(8\arctan^2(4/\pi)/\pi^2\) of the occupation fraction's variance.
