For \(n\ge4\) independent uniform directions in the plane, at most two vertices can be individually pivotal for the origin's containment in their convex hull. The probabilities of exactly zero, one and two pivotal vertices are \(1-n(n-3)/2^{n-1}\), \(n(n-4)/2^{n-1}\) and \(n/2^{n-1}\), respectively.
