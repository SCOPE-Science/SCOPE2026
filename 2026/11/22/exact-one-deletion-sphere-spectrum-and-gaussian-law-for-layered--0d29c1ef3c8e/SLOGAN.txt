The complete distribution of distinct rank-standardized single-deletion outputs from uniform layered permutations has an explicit rational generating function, with sharp possible volumes \(1\) through \(\lfloor(2n+1)/3\rfloor\). Its mean is \(3(n+1)/8\), its exact variance is \(7(n-1)/64\) for \(n\ge5\), and its fluctuations are Gaussian on the \(\sqrt n\) scale.
