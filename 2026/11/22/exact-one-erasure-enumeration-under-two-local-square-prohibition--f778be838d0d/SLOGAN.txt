For binary partial words containing exactly one hole and avoiding all compatible tandem squares of half-length two or three, the complete length enumerator is \(N(z)/(1-z^2-z^3-z^4+z^6)^2\) with the explicit polynomial \(N\) given in RESULT.md. The number of admissible length-\(n\) words grows as \(0.5019696145\ldots\,n(1.4012683679\ldots)^n\), showing a linear prefactor from allowing one erasure without changing the exponential base.
