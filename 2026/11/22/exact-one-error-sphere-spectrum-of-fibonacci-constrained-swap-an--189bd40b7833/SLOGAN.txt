For every binary length-\(n\) word avoiding `11`, the number of distinct allowed one-replacement or adjacent-swap outputs that also avoid `11` is exactly \(n\) minus its number of occurrences of `101`. The complete all-length sphere-size histogram is a rational bivariate generating function, with sharp extremizers and average sphere size asymptotic to \(2n/\sqrt5\) (plus the unchanged word).
