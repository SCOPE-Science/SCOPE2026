The multilinear polytope whose coordinates are all squarefree monomials of one fixed degree \(r\) has a universal clique of vertices indexed by supports of size at least \(r\); its remaining graph is exactly that of a uniform-matroid independence polytope. The full degree distribution and edge count are explicit, and the diameter is \(1\) for \(r=2\) and exactly \(2\) for every \(r\ge3\).
