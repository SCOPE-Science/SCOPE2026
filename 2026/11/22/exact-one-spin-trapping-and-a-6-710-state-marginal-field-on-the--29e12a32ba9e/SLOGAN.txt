On the antiferromagnetic Ising dodecahedron, the same five configurations minimize energy for every \(0<h<3J\), but the number of excited absorbing one-spin traps changes from 245 to 290 across \(h=J\). Exactly 6,710 configurations are weakly stable at that interior field, while a face-dual matching argument proves that all 250 zero-field strictly stable configurations already minimize the exchange energy.
