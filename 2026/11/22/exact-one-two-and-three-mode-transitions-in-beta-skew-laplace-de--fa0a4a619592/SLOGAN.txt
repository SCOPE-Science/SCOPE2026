The 2022 beta-skew-Laplace density has exactly one local mode for \(|\beta|\le0.007530403094912\ldots\), two for intermediate tilt through \(0.039483567844106\ldots\), and three for larger tilt, with both exact boundaries specified by unique cubic roots. At every finite tilt the origin remains a strict cusp mode, but the large-tilt total-variation limit has just the two modes \(\pm6\).
