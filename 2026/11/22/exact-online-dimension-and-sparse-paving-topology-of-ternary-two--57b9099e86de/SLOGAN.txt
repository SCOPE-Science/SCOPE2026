Unions of two distinct smooth conics in the ternary projective plane have Littlestone dimension eight but VC dimension seven, certified by a complete depth-eight decision tree and an exhaustive recurrence over ternary partial assignments. Their shattered supports form a rank-seven sparse-paving matroid whose 78 circuit-hyperplanes are exactly pairs of projective lines, yielding an integral wedge of 714 six-spheres.
