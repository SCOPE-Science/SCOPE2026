In a finite weak order with \(h\) antichain levels each of width \(w\), ideal membership has VC dimension \(w\) but exact Littlestone dimension \(w-1+\lfloor\log_2(h+1)\rfloor\). The generic information bound is never more than one bit higher, and for every width \(w\ge2\) it first becomes strictly higher at exactly five levels.
