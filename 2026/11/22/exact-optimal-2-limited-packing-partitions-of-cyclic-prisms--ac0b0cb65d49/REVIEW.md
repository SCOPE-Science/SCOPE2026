# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The two-color obstruction follows from a pair of linear recurrences on the rung sums and differences, including the odd and \(4\)-divisible boundary cases. Every three-color assignment is captured by an explicitly defined 81-state finite-memory transfer. The characteristic polynomial is checked coefficient-by-coefficient using Newton identities on exact integer traces through order 81, and brute graph enumeration independently agrees for widths 3–7. Formal log-determinant extraction proves the formula for every circumference, not only checked examples.

**Originality — PASS (qualified).** Ahmadi–Soltankhah–Samadi (arXiv:2308.16837, 2023-08-31) prove the exact tree result and general inequalities rather than this prism enumeration. The previously studied perfect prism colorings (Lisitsyna 2013; Lisitsyna–Avgustinovich 2016) form a strict subclass of these capacity-two partitions, with an explicit nonperfect valid coloring of \(Y_3\) supplied in the result. Searches for prism/circular-ladder 2-limited packing partitions, equivalent closed-neighborhood color capacities, broader coloring classifications, and research-record implications found no source giving the same counts. The full text of the 2016 paper was not recovered, and unindexed computations remain a priority risk.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The exact optimal-coloring census for every circular ladder sharpens an established partition invariant on a natural cubic graph family: it exhibits a parity jump in the optimum, a mod-four rigidity in two-color solutions, and an exponentially growing class of three-color optima with an explicit factored rational generating function. The determinant certificate makes the nontrivial all-size enumeration reproducible and useful as a benchmark for local-packing coloring algorithms. The result does not assert analogous formulas for other products.
