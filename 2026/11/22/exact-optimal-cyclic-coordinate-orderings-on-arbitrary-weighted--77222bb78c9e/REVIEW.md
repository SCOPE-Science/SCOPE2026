# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS
The final claim is a complete odd-cycle permutation classification for ordinary exact Gauss–Seidel sweeps, not a statement about finite-time norms. The determinant identity follows by a full Leibniz-expansion partition: in a chordless cycle, each nontrivial determinant permutation component is an edge transposition or one of two full directed cycles. This makes every matching term independent of update order and leaves only the two complete-cycle exponents. The same determinant identity can be checked directly after each possible graph permutation. Positive definiteness and the Perron eigenvalue of the normalized weighted adjacency establish a strictly increasing determinant on the relevant interval; the opposite scalar term is strictly decreasing. Nonnegativity of the Gauss–Seidel matrix then identifies the unique scalar crossing as its spectral radius. A circular-ascents/Eulerian-number argument counts exactly all minimizers. The exact symbolic verifier and independent finite sweep enumeration passed. The finite enumeration is supporting evidence, not the proof for arbitrary dimension.

## Originality — PASS on inspected sources, with residual priority risk
Varga (1959, *Orderings of the successive overrelaxation scheme*, Theorems 2–4 and corollary following Theorem 3) derives a general Jacobi-squared spectral-radius lower bound and characterizes its attainment by consistent orders. In nonbipartite weighted odd rings this bound is not attained, so that theorem alone does not rank the nonattaining orders. The new finding gives every rate, a strict total ranking by circular imbalance, and all optimizer multiplicities for arbitrary ring weights and diagonal strengths. Varga's 1959 p-cyclic paper derives specific spectra for consistently ordered p-cyclic matrices, whereas symmetric odd rings do not satisfy the relevant index-two condition. Oswald–Zhou (arXiv:1510.04727, publicly posted October 15, 2015) studies random reordering and contraction inequalities rather than exact weighted-ring deterministic permutation optima. A historical Heller (1957) article specifically concerns equal-spectrum orderings for multidigonal systems; its full text was unavailable, though Varga's 1959 introduction describes its scope. Consequently the full characteristic-polynomial partition has a stated residual priority risk; the independent, parameter-uniform strict ranking has not been found in the inspected material. A negative search result does not establish publication priority.

## Value — PASS
Odd cycles are the smallest topological obstruction to bipartite red–black ordering. A full classification of all cyclic coordinate-descent orders under *arbitrary* positive heterogeneity yields a robust design rule without estimating weights: nearly balance forward and backward update precedences around the unique cycle. The exact root equation quantifies the unavoidable odd-cycle penalty beyond the classical Jacobi-squared benchmark; Eulerian counts quantify how many schedules achieve that best possible convergence rate. This is a structural, all-dimensional optimization result rather than an isolated numerical comparison or unmotivated small graph census. The result does not select best finite-horizon norms or compute hardware-aware schedules.
