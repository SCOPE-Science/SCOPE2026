The number of optimal one-edge-at-a-time transformations between opposite acyclic orientations of labelled \(K_{2,n}\) has the exact formal generating function \(\sum_{n\ge0}g_nz^n/n!=S(z)/(2-S(z))\), with \(S(z)=\sum_{n\ge0}(2n-1)!!z^n\). Equivalently, optimal flip orders are asymptotically a proportion \(2^{1-n}\) of all orders of the \(2n\) edges.
