For every \(m\ge3\) and \(n\ge5\), all maximum open packings of the labelled cyclic clique cylinder \(C_n\square K_m\) admit an exact three-residue multiplicity formula, refining an already-known maximum-cardinality bound. A color-symmetric transfer determinant also gives every nonmaximum packing coefficient, with the cycle-closing obstruction producing alternating exponential terms in the optimum census.
