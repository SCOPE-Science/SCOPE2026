For exact observations of a Brownian bridge independently erased with delivery probability \(p>0\), the unique optimal \(n\)-point grid for integrated mean-square path reconstruction has boundary margins \(1/[2+(n-1)p]\) and interior gaps \(p/[2+(n-1)p]\). Its sharp risk is \((2-p)/[6(2+(n-1)p)]\): unreliable samples optimally cluster toward the midpoint rather than remaining uniformly spaced.
