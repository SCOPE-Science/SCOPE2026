For every Euclidean stadium, the maximum-area inscribed triangle and the optimal translative lattice-covering density admit explicit, sharp algebraic formulas; a noncircular stadium has exactly two maximizing triangles. As the stadium elongates, its largest-triangle area fraction rises strictly from \(3\sqrt3/(4\pi)\) toward \(1/2\), while the optimal lattice-covering density falls strictly from \(2\pi/(3\sqrt3)\) toward \(1\), yielding unique aspect-ratio reconstruction.
