For any finite convolution of biased Bernoulli point pairs at commensurable lengths, the exact maximum number of mutually orthogonal exponential characters is \(2^r\), where \(r\) counts the distinct dyadic valuations of the steps whose probabilities are exactly one half. An explicit binary frequency cube attains the bound, showing that neither a repeated dyadic step scale nor an arbitrarily biased extra factor adds another orthogonal dimension.
