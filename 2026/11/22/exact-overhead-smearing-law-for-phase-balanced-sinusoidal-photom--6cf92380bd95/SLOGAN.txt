For a photon-limited, phase-balanced sinusoidal light curve with positive per-exposure overhead, information per charged observing time has a unique globally optimal exposure, strictly increasing with overhead but always below \(0.371010\) of the signal period. The optimum obeys the sharp cube-root law \(t_*(h)\sim(6h/\omega^2)^{1/3}\) at small overhead, with information-efficiency loss proportional to \(h^{2/3}\); calendar availability and correlated noise are excluded.
