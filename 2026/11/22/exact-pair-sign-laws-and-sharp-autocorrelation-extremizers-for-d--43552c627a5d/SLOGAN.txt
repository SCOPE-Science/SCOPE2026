The quadratic character of the Domb numbers modulo five has exactly balanced limiting signs and asymptotically independent consecutive signs, but its complete-block adjacent-pair counts retain an explicit \(3^r\)-scale finite bias. Every shifted two-point correlation follows a five-adic carry recursion, with sharp extrema \(-1/5\) only at shifts \(2\cdot5^m\) and \(2/5\) only at shifts \(3\cdot5^m\).
