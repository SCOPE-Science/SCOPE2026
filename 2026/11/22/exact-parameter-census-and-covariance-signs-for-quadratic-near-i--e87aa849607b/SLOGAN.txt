For the natural quadratic near-identity permutations \(x\mapsto x+p(x^2-a)\) modulo odd prime powers, the complete parameter-averaged cycle census has explicit type frequencies and an exact covariance matrix. Longest-cycle counts are negatively correlated with every shorter count, while all other distinct cycle counts are positively correlated, and the small-index counts converge jointly to one heavy-tailed law with infinite second moment.
