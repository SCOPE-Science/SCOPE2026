For every integer evaluation parameter \(t\) and every \(r\ge2\), the gcd of the two Stern \((0,1)\)-polynomials at indices \(2^r+1\) and \(2^r+3\) equals \(\gcd(2t+1,R_r)\), where \(R_r=\sum_{j=0}^{r-1}2^{2^{r-1}-2^j}\). This gives the complete divisor spectrum, exact totient frequencies and least parameter period; the same sparse odd integer is the exact polynomial resultant.
