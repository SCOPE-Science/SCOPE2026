The uniquely extremal 21-neighbor convex-triangle tiling requires exactly 12 mutually noncontacting color classes in the plane, but its rectangular flat-torus quotients need 12 colors when their original lattice-vertex count is even and exactly 13 when it is odd, for every pair of periods at least five. This parity-sharp transition follows from an exact doubled-edge contact model, abelian Cayley 1-factorization and the Goldberg–Seymour edge-coloring theorem.
