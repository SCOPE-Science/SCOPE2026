Odd values of the distinct-Fibonacci partition function have an exact rational counting series over Fibonacci windows: \(\sum_{k\ge0}B_kz^k=(1-2z^4)/(1-2z+z^2-2z^4+2z^5)\). Their counting function grows as \(\Theta(X^{0.9022534195\ldots})\), so they have natural density zero despite every integer admitting a Fibonacci partition.
