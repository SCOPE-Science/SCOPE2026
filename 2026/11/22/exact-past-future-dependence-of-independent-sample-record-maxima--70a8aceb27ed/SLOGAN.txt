For the running maximum of any i.i.d. continuous sample, total-variation dependence between the entire past through time \(n\) and the infinite future from time \(m>n\) is exactly \(r+r(1-r)(1-r)^{1/r}/(1+r)\), where \(r=n/m\). This approaches zero at the sharp rate \((1+e^{-1})n/m\) when \(m/n\to\infty\), even though the uniform mixing coefficient remains one and the past–future mutual information is infinite at every finite separation.
