For four independent Haar-phase Fourier taps, the entire aperiodic peak-sidelobe distribution follows from a two-variable arcsine law, and exactly \(5/36\) of random codes satisfy the generalized Barker bound. Immediately above that positive atom the success probability grows by \(\varepsilon\log(1/\varepsilon)/\pi^2+O(\varepsilon)\), giving a sharp logarithmic random-search benchmark.
