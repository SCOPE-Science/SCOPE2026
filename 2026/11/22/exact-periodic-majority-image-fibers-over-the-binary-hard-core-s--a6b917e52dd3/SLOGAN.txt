For the majority cellular automaton restricted to cyclic binary words avoiding adjacent ones, every nonzero output has an exact inverse-fiber product over its cyclic gaps of ones; possible gaps are precisely \(2\) and the integers at least \(5\), and unique inversion occurs exactly when every gap belongs to \(\{2,5,6,7\}\). Exact rational series count all output words and all singleton fibers at every ring length; the fraction of uniquely invertible outputs decays asymptotically like \((0.9496455623\ldots)^n\).
