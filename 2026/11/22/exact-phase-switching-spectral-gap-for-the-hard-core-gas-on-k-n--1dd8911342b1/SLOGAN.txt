On the balanced complete bipartite hard-core gas, the full continuous-time Glauber spectral gap equals a single killed Ehrenfest-urn eigenvalue, uniquely specified by an explicit binomial resolvent equation. It decreases strictly with system size and has the sharp fixed-activity asymptotic \(g_n(\lambda)=n\lambda(1+\lambda)^{-n}[1-(\lambda n)^{-1}-(1+\lambda)/(\lambda^2n^2)+O_\lambda(n^{-3})]\).
