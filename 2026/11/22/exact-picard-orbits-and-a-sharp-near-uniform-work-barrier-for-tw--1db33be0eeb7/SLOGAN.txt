For simultaneous linear-mass and quadratic-energy conservation, the published predictor-initialized multi-invariant discrete-gradient Picard solver has an exact fractional-linear orbit, two possible nonsingular correction branches, and a sharp iteration cost growing like the reciprocal of the predictor's distance from the uniform state. A concrete three-stage explicit predictor for a conservative oscillator reaches the singular uniform predictor at a finite time step, while nearby steps have the precisely quantified work blow-up.
