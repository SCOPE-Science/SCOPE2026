# Scientific review: Exact planar contact law and a dimension ladder for proportional Brownian resetting

## Correctness — PASS
The claim specifies a fixed finite Euclidean dimension, isotropic Brownian generator \(D\Delta\), constant Poisson rate \(r>0\), and one synchronous scalar multiplier \(a\) with \(0<|a|<1\). Stationarity and uniqueness follow from an explicitly convergent perpetuity and synchronous coupling. Its characteristic function satisfies the exact radial identity \(\Phi_b(bu)-\Phi_b(u)=u^2\Phi_b(u)\). Since the Fourier product decreases faster than every polynomial for every fixed \(0<b<1\), radial Fourier inversion and all algebraic integrations are justified. The planar formula follows from an exact Frullani/Tonelli calculation. Radial Fourier integrals in dimensions differing by two give an exact recursion. Odd-dimensional endpoint constants follow by an explicitly bounded integrand and dominated convergence; even-dimensional constants follow from a finite product. Finite numerical tests corroborate, but do not replace, the proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The principal precedent is Biroli and collaborators, arXiv:2406.08387 (2024), whose full one-dimensional analysis gives the proportional-reset process and explicitly names extension to higher dimensions as an open direction. Pierce, arXiv:2204.07215 (2022), analyzes the one-dimensional pantograph density. Existing published work on three-dimensional Bessel partial resetting yields a related small-contraction radial contact asymptotic when the reset rate is tied to the contraction; **this special case is not claimed as a new discovery**. Existing one-dimensional high-order smoothness results and a multivariate random-contraction variance-gamma characterization do not state the all-dimensional exact contact-density recursion or the exact planar logarithm. The novelty claim is confined to this combined dimensional identity and its exact even/odd hierarchy. The fact that the underlying Fourier product is known means an equivalent derivation may exist in unindexed mathematical physics or probability literature.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The previously established one-dimensional density becomes singular at a completely resetting point in higher dimensions. The exact two-dimensional contact law identifies the contraction strength directly from a stationary point-density measurement, while the all-dimensional recursion computes otherwise inaccessible high-dimensional local concentrations without solving a new spatial integro-differential equation. The parity-dependent hierarchy gives sharp nonuniform limits: powers with an additional logarithm in even dimensions, different powers in odd dimensions, and an explicit coefficient in each case. This is a natural, entire-dimensional classification of a model extension expressly motivated in the primary literature, not an arbitrary finite parameter slice or a claim of a new Fourier product.

## Prior literature, scope and residual risk
The 2024 primary preprint concerns one-dimensional steady states and search; its higher-dimensional extension was not solved there. The 2022 precursor also concerns one spatial dimension. A three-dimensional Bessel result and published findings about one-dimensional nonanalytic regularity represent genuine neighboring work; their overlap is identified, not concealed. Wider equivalent results and implicit consequences of pre-existing product formulas remain possible. No analysis of MFPT, non-scalar jumps, non-Poisson clocks, or dimension-uniform asymptotics is claimed. Independent mathematical validation remains outstanding.
