## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence
The closest primary source, Đorić (arXiv:2507.10126), proves the same formal symmetric-product/suspension formula under a **finite chain-recurrent set** or supplies a wandering-point lower bound; neither hypothesis holds for minimal Sturmian dynamics. The 2023 symmetric-product lower bound requires wandering points, also absent. A published Sturmian parity-factor result identifies the singleton-collapsed symmetric square with a parity factor but computes zero **ordinary topological** entropy and ergodic measures, not polynomial entropy. A published exact finite-hyperspace hierarchy for rotating-disk continua has a different nonminimal, pointwise periodic state space and relies on radial packing rather than Sturmian phase labels. A finite-atomic measure-space entropy theorem concerns ordinary topological entropy, not the invariant here. Searches of relevant records, accessible prior claims, and literature revealed no stronger theorem implying all stated finite-layer equalities in the minimal Sturmian setting. ## Value — PASS
The new result determines a natural exact complexity invariant for *all* finite-subset levels of an iconic aperiodic, minimal, low-complexity system, including a quotient where factor complexity can in principle be lost. It shows precise polynomial amplification without wandering points or finite chain recurrence and distinguishes it from zero ordinary topological entropy. The full hyperspace consequence gives a natural finite-to-infinite transition; it is not an arbitrary numerical slice. It leaves open whether similar sharp equalities hold for wider minimal symbolic classes and does not claim sharp leading constants.
