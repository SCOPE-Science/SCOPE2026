For a nonlinear opinion model with a unit attractive path and two repulsive ties—one joining the endpoints and one located anywhere on the path—the exact consensus-loss threshold is \(2/(K+\sqrt{(K-2)^2+4})\), independent of where the second tie lies. Above it there are exactly two attracting global-potential-minimum polarization profiles, and moving the hostile path tie merely moves the enlarged opinion step without changing the total opinion range.
