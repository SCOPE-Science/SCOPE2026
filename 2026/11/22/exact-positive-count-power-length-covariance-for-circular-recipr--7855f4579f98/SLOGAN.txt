For uniform points on a circle, the number of reciprocal nearest-neighbor pairs is **strictly positively correlated with their total power-weighted length for every real exponent \(\alpha>0\)**, with an exact gamma-function covariance valid at every size \(n\ge5\). Its ordinary-length covariance is the sample-size-independent constant \(16/675\), in contrast to the previously known negative covariance with the total *directed* nearest-neighbor length.
