For the Heawood graph, the unsuccessful ordinary power-dominating sets are exactly the subsets of its fourteen four-vertex closed neighborhoods, giving \(\mathcal P_H(z)=(1+z)^{14}-1-14z-63z^2-56z^3-14z^4\). Its \(28\) minimum two-sensor sets are the Fano nonincidence flags and form a connected six-regular exchange graph of diameter three.
