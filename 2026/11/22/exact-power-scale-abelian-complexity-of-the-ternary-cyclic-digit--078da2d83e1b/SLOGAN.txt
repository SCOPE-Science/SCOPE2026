For the ternary cyclic morphism \(0\mapsto001\), \(1\mapsto112\), \(2\mapsto220\), coding the zero letter to a binary one gives exactly \(3^{h+1}-1\) Abelian types at length \(3^{2h}\) and \(5\cdot3^h-1\) at length \(3^{2h+1}\), for every \(h\ge0\). The different even- and odd-scale constants arise from a two-step spectral collapse of the substitution matrix.
