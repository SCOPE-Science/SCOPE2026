For finite Alexandrov chains, two-sided commutation of next with interior has an exact joint census by image defect and the least power at which all temporal observations stabilize: its counts obey a terminating binomial restriction recurrence. At defect \(r>0\), every stabilization time from \(1\) to \(n-r\) occurs, and the sharp upper time is attained by exactly \(r\) maps when the image has at least two points (one map when the image is a singleton).
