For the unit-seeded recurrence \(x_{n+2}=a x_{n+1}+x_n\), every prime dividing \(a\) has a completely determined local quotient closure: exactly \(1+p^{\nu_p(a)}\mathbb Z_p\), with full \(2\)-adic unit coverage when \(\nu_2(a)=1\). Except at that dyadic boundary, every allowed value modulo any prime power occurs exactly twice per least period, once at each index parity.
