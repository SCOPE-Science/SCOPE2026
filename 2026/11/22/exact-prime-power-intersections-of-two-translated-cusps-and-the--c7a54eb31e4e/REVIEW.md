# Scientific review of the translated-cusp intersection law

## Correctness — PASS

Both polynomial equations are reduced, for odd primes, to one factored quartic. At primes at least five its two transverse roots contribute zero or two Hensel lifts and its double root contributes \(p^{\lfloor k/2\rfloor}\). A full 3-adic analysis uses the unique root of \(9u^2-14u+9\) of exact valuation two and separately counts the tangent and double-root branches. At two the linear difference gives two candidate \(x\)-classes, and the even/odd valuation classification gives every binary count. All boundaries \(k=1,2,3,4,5,6,7\) are covered. Chinese remainder theory gives composite moduli. The general distribution convergence is taken from [1], with its hypotheses checked explicitly. The Euler moments use the proved \(p^{k/2}\) growth and prime-series comparison. The packaged checker passed 71 prime-power formula cases, 29 full brute prime-power checks and 19 composite checks; tests are not infinite proofs.

## Originality — PASS on inspected accessible evidence

A full reading of [1, Theorem 3.1, Section 4, Remark 4.4] shows the general convergence theorem and generic local-factor expression, not the exact translated-cusp local counts or moment threshold. A full reading of [2, Sections 1, 5 and 6] shows structural gcd/lcm stability and coprimality densities, not this pair's prime-power root spectrum. Relevant related mathematical records on cusp local densities, binary resultant gcd factors and cubic collision counts describe different objects or invariants. The exact quartic factorization might be implicitly subsumed by an unindexed general local-zeta or resultant calculation; that remains the substantive priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The nontransverse intersection of two natural integer cuspidal curves creates an infinite family of exact prime-power arithmetic invariants. The theorem classifies every local multiplicity, distinguishes wild primes two and three from the generic quadratic-character branch, and identifies different sharp local and global moment boundaries. This is a complete natural classification, not merely a finite experiment, a reformulation of general existence, or a routine change of notation.

## Limits and source relationship

The finite-box limiting law and generic Euler-factor framework are inherited from the published result [1]. The precise local factors and the two sharp moment boundaries are the new claim. The numerical density is not a certified approximation; the literature check is not a mathematical proof of priority. No independent examination has been performed.
