# Mathematical verification and boundaries

The exact identities are derived from factorial valuations and a base-digit block partition, not inferred from finite experiments. The sharpness proof uses two universal witnesses for every prime-power modulus of exponent at least two, plus the modulus-\(p\) base case. The residue density argument explicitly proves Fourier cancellation for all integer cutoffs, avoiding a gap between prime-power block frequencies and natural density.

Run `python check.py` with Python 3 and no external dependencies. It cross-checks multiple independent formulations, complete small row products and modular histograms. Its diagnostic ranges are finite and cannot certify statements for arbitrarily large primes, exponents or indices. Historical originality remains subject to independent literature review. The independent audit status in the front matter is not a proof or certification.
