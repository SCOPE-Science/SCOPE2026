For every prime \(p\ge5\), the prime-index Dickson polynomial with a unit parameter has a complete prime-power image and fiber classification: a nonsquare parameter gives \(p\) full \(p^{-2}\)-balls, while a square parameter replaces two of these by narrower \(p^{-3}\)-balls. The exact image densities are \(1/p\) and \((p^2-2p+2)/p^3\), respectively.
