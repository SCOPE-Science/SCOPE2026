# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
A complete proof uses pointwise stabilizers in the dihedral permutation action, expands a weighted restriction-pair convolution, and computes every coefficient via the empty, singleton and antipodal correction. The exact independent checker enumerates every distinct partial map and all ordered products for cycle sizes three through seven. These finite computations corroborate but do not replace the universal proof.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)
The original cycle partial-isometry structure and nonunique extension classes belong to Fernandes–Paulista (2022). The archived chain-monoid antecedent is Fernandes–Quinteiro (2015), but that work is not misrepresented as a cycle-product theorem. Closest public results count one-element involutions and nilpotents, all products in a different chain monoid, or rank-uniform symmetric inverse-monoid convolutions. None of the inspected complete results provides the exact two-factor rank polynomial for *uniform distinct* dihedral restrictions. A stronger unpublished or unlocated group-action restriction theorem remains a priority risk; novelty is not independently certified.

## Value — PASS
The exact natural rank polynomial applies to every cycle length, incorporates small-domain extension nonuniqueness, and provides an exponentially accurate binomial product-rank law. The statistic is not determined by a single-element rank count and differs from previously computed chain product laws.

## Limits
No statement about all factor lengths, fixed factor ranks, full product-map fibers or historical priority beyond the inspected sources. The 2015 archival antecedent concerns a different metric space, while direct cycle structural input is published from 2022 onward.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
