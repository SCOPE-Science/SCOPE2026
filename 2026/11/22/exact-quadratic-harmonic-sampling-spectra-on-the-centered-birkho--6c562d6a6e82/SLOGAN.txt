The centered permutation-matrix vertices of every Birkhoff polytope form a spherical \(2\)-design, yet exactly \(n(n-1)(n-2)-2\) independent quadratic harmonics vanish on all of them. The entire nonzero squared sampling spectrum has just three Specht-character levels, and its sharp positive condition number grows as \((n-2)/2\) from dimension parameter \(n\ge5\).
