For each n ≥ 4, the normalized centered Birkhoff vertices form a spherical 2-design, but exactly n(n−1)(n−2)−2 independent quadratic harmonics vanish on them. Three Specht sectors give the positive squared sampling spectrum; two eigenvalues coincide at n = 4. The ratio of the largest to the smallest positive squared singular value is (n−2)/2 for n ≥ 5.
