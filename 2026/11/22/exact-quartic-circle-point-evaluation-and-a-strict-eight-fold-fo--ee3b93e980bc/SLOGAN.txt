For all complex circle polynomials of degree at most four, the exact sharp fourth-power point-evaluation constant is the algebraic number \(C_{4,4}=7.850841363699946\ldots<8\), with a unique normalized palindromic quartic extremizer determined by a degree-nine equation. The same invariant gives the sharp norm \((C_{4,4}/9)^{1/4}\) of diagonal Hardy-to-Bergman restriction on homogeneous bidisc quartics.
