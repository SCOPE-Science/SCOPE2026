For a uniform point in the body-centered-cubic Voronoi truncated octahedron, the near-covering-radius squared-error tail has an exact two-dimensional integral and an all-orders positive rational expansion beginning \(128\varepsilon^3+1024\varepsilon^4+\frac{53248}{5}\varepsilon^5\). Its independent-sample worst-error deficit has a cubic Weibull endpoint law.
