# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The group-theoretic core follows from the irreducibility of the elementary-abelian kernel: the only normal subgroups are trivial, the kernel, and the whole group; every proper nontrivial endomorphism consequently factors through a cyclic prime-order quotient. Distinct order-prime complement subgroups cannot have commuting nontrivial images. This gives the exhaustive two-branch local form. The automorphism normalizer is semilinear of order \(d(p^d-1)\), and there are \(p^d\) possible images of the complement generator. One-dimensional scalar Laurent entropy is given by the explicitly cited Theorem 4 of Castronuovo et al. (2026). Direct coefficient-support counting then proves all multiplicities. Independent finite-group enumeration and finite Laurent-vector tests passed for the cases described in `VERIFICATION.md`; no infinite claim is inferred from enumeration.

**Originality — PASS on the inspected comparisons, with historical priority risk.** The 2026 general entropy paper supplies computability and the scalar formula but not a uniform classification over all irreducible Frobenius groups, or the closed full entropy multiplicity distribution at each radius. Dennunzio, Formenti and Margara (2024) treat \(A_4\) and odd dihedral groups for dynamical decision properties, not these count distributions; these named groups are acknowledged special cases. The 2025 divide-and-conquer preprint offers a general structural algorithm; its full text was not inspected, so the abstract alone cannot exclude an equivalent theorem. Searches for equivalent finite-group-semigroup and radius-entropy formulations found no direct matching statement, which is evidence of limited scope, not a proof of priority. The most material residual risk is that earlier group-endomorphism counts plus later scalar entropy formula make the classification a straightforward combination.

**Value — PASS.** The result is a complete parameter-uniform entropy spectrum and frequency table over an infinite natural family of nonsimple nonabelian group alphabets. The two entropy logarithmic scales, the exact maximum-radius range, and the number of rules at every entropy are meaningful structural measurements not supplied by individual entropy computability. It distinguishes positive entropy carried by the cyclic quotient from shift entropy carried by the full nonabelian alphabet and includes tetrahedral, odd dihedral-prime and larger irreducible Frobenius examples.

**Scope and risk.** All claim components refer to one family and one local-rule census. No result for composite complements, reducible actions, arbitrary cellular automata or conjugacy classes is asserted. The priority comparison is based on the actual inspected source sections and the stated inaccessible-material limit.
