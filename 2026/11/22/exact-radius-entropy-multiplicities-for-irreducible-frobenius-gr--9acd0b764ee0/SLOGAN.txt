For every irreducible elementary-abelian-by-prime Frobenius group \(G=\mathbf F_p^d\rtimes C_q\), all one-dimensional group cellular automata are either automorphism-shifts or scalar quotient rules. At centered radius \(R\), their only entropies are \(k\log q\) for \(0\le k\le2R\) and \(k\log|G|\) for \(0\le k\le R\), with exact closed multiplicities for every level.
