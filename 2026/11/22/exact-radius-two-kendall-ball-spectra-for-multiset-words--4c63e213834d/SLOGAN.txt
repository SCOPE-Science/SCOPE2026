Every radius-two adjacent-transposition ball around a word with repeated symbols has an exact three-statistic formula, \(|B_2(x)|=\binom{n-1-K}{2}+2(n-1)-K-C-2A\), and a two-state transfer computes the complete distribution at every length and alphabet size. The sharp binary and nonbinary maxima differ, while random ball volumes exhibit Gaussian fluctuations for fixed alphabets and Poisson-scaled deficits when the alphabet grows proportionally to word length.
