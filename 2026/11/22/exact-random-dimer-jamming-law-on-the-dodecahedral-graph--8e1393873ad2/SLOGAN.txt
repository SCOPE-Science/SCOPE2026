On the dodecahedral twenty-site network, irreversible random dimers jam at exactly six, seven, eight, nine or ten bonds with a complete exact rational distribution, even though perfect coverage occurs only about 7.39% of the time. The rare six-dimer event has probability exactly \(5(1/5)^6=1/3125\): its five possible vacant configurations are the five eight-vertex inscribed cubes, and each requires six independent five-way arrival races.
