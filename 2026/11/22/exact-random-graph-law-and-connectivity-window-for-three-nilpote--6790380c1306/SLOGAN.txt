Uniform labeled degree-three nilpotent semigroups with a prescribed product-ideal size have an exact inclusion–exclusion law for their generator commuting graphs, exponentially close to an Erdős–Rényi graph. When the product ideal has size \(\lfloor r/(\log r+c)\rfloor\) for \(r\) generators, the ordinary noncentral commuting graph is connected with limiting probability \(\exp(-e^{-c})\).
