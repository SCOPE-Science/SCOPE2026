For uniformly oriented orthogonal shadows of the regular rhombic dodecahedron, the whole projected-area law reduces to one explicit algebraic quadrature, and its projection body is a scaled truncated octahedron. The upper tail stays exactly linear down to the mean shadow area \(6\sqrt2\), where first spherical-cap overlaps create a \(3/2\)-power correction, while the lower tail has sharp quadratic coefficient \(\sqrt3/(8\pi)\).
