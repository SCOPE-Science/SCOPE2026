The sharp two-input path-variation sensitivity of a centered, endpoint-clamped rank filter with odd window width \(w\) and ascending rank \(q\) is exactly \(2\min\{q-1,w-q\}+1\) as soon as there are at least \(w+1\) samples. Every coefficient is attained by an arbitrarily small single-jump perturbation: extrema are nonexpansive, the median amplifies by the full window width, and all intermediate ranks form a complete sharp ladder.
