Under a fully specified continuous null, the Ebner–Liebenberg–Visagie uniformity statistic has exact finite-sample covariance \( (26n-17)/(1260n) \) with Cramér–von Mises and \( (n-1)/(210n) \) with Watson. Both null correlations increase strictly with sample size, while the squared empirical-mean mode contributes more than \(10/13\) of the Cramér–von Mises covariance for every \(n\ge2\).
