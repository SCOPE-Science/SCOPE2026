When the second circular sample has at most as many observations as the first, its two-sample Rao spacing-frequency statistic is exactly an affine transform of the circular runs statistic, even away from the null. Under a common continuous null, the exact squared correlation of Rao with Dixon's quadratic statistic is \(\frac{(m+2)(m+3)(m+n-2)}{m(m+n)(m+n+1)}\), and at equal sample sizes it decreases strictly from \(1\) to the limit \(1/2\).
