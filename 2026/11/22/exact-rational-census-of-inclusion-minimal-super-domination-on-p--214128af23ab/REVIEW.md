**Correctness: PASS.** The complement of a super dominating set is open irredundant by direct witness correspondence, and inclusion-minimality is equivalent to maximal open irredundance by its hereditary definition. The nine-symbol local test correctly evaluates witnesses and every possible one-vertex extension. An exact integer equality of all suffix-state vectors at index 18, not merely of scalar output counts, is propagated by the fixed linear transition and proves a recurrence for all indices at least 18; indices 12–17 and the initial numerator coefficients are checked explicitly. The attached script reproduces the certificate and separately checks the original graph definition for all subsets of paths up to length 15. Risk: the proof depends on a computationally checkable finite integer certificate rather than a handwritten reduction of the 111-state calculation.

The 2008 maximal open-irredundance criteria supply general structural definitions but, in the inspected material, do not give this rational path census. The 2026 super-coalition paper concerns partition maxima rather than counts of minimal super-dominating sets. The later 17 November 2026 preprint deals with extremal cardinalities and was read for relevant implications, not used to establish the first-public source date. Searches did not reveal a dominating equivalent enumerator, but historical literature under another name may exist.

**Value: PASS.** Exact enumeration of all minimal feasible vertex sets is a stronger and different invariant than either minimum or maximum size. The rational generating function yields a practical linear recurrence of order ten for the entire infinite path family and determines, in particular, how many irredundant maximal boundary patterns occur at every size.
