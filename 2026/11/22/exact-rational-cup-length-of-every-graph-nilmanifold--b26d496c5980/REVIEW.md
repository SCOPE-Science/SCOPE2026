# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** The graphical Chevalley–Eilenberg model is explicitly fixed, including characteristic zero, graph simplicity, and exterior degrees. Distinct edge-pair replacements make the differential injective on nonconstant pure-edge monomials; the vertex ideal catches every positive-degree cocycle and has \((n+1)\)-st power zero. A choice of endpoints yields \(n\) individually closed cochains whose product is the non-boundary top form. The proof covers empty edge sets and isolated vertices. Rational cup-length agrees by scalar extension of finite-dimensional cohomology rings; explicit invariant forms are only claimed real for arbitrary lattices. An independently written exact-sign checker exhaustively confirms the constructive steps for all 1,099 simple graphs on one through five labelled vertices; the general statement does not rely on finite tests.

Pouseele–Tirao (2009) focus on degree-two cohomology and symplectic/contact structures. Actual searches for the graph-nilmanifold cup-length, its first-Betti-number formulation, and cohomology-ring aliases in mathematical literature and indexed research records returned no covering result. The closest indexed work on graphical nilpotent Lie algebras concerns finite-field abelian-subalgebra counts, whereas generic graph cohomology cup-length concerns a graph's simplicial complex, not the associated manifold. This is evidence of a precise gap, not a uniqueness proof. Inaccessible full text of the 2009 article and unindexed equivalent formulations remain residual risks.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Cup-length is a natural multiplicative manifold invariant that cannot be recovered from total cohomology dimension alone. The theorem gives an exact invariant for every finite simple graph and an explicit factorization of the top fundamental cohomology class. It explains why adding arbitrarily many edge generators (and changing the rational Betti profile) cannot increase this cup-length, and makes the cohomological lower bound on Lusternik–Schnirelmann category explicit. It is a complete structural result, not a tiny finite census.

The general claim does not extend to multigraphs, arbitrary nilpotent algebras, characteristic-two coefficients, or a determination of LS category. Residual priority risk remains, and exact certificate checks do not substitute for independent mathematical audit.
