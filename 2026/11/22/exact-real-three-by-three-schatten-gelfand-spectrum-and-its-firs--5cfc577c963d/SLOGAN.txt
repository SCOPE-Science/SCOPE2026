For real three-by-three Schatten embeddings with \(0<p\le1\) and \(2\le q\le\infty\), the nine Gelfand numbers form exactly three plateaux: \(1\) at indices one through four, \(2^{1/q-1/p}\) at five through eight, and \(3^{1/q-1/p}\) at nine. The first strict drop occurs at codimension four, attained by the traceless symmetric five-plane; previously known penultimate and final-index values are credited.
