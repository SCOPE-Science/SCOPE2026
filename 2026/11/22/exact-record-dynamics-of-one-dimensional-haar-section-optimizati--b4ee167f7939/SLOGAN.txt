For exact-line-search one-dimensional Haar-section Frank--Wolfe on a disk with a nonconstant affine objective, each chord produces a fresh uniform boundary proposal: the iterates are exactly the lower-record process of independent circle samples. The sharp objective error has mean \(\pi^2/N^2+o(N^{-2})\), a Weibull limit, and geometric target-hitting times, despite only \(\log N+o(\log N)\) accepted movements almost surely.
