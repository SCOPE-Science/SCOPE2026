# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The precise update convention leads to a deterministic, collision-free transport of each downstream bit. The first-erase expression indexes its gate times as \(s\equiv b-1-j\pmod b\), with source position \(-s\bmod a\). Those visits exhaust the appropriate \(a/\gcd(a,b)\) source residues over \(\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)\) rounds. Erasure is irreversible, so the latest doomed present bit determines first entry; this supplies the exact per-source unrestricted count. Independent enumeration of all initial states for 25 cycle-length pairs confirms every first-entry shell and the late products. The terminal counting uses a unique last zero, not an unjustified extension of an empirical table.

## Originality
**PASS, with residual historical priority risk.** The closest primary source, Chen–Gao–Başar's elementary-digraph calculation, already gives the eventual projection by the least-common-multiple clock; that prior fact is credited rather than claimed. Jarrah–Laubenbacher–Veliz-Cuba supply periodic-orbit examples and broad transient bounds, and Colón-Reyes–Laubenbacher–Pareigis supply general monomial dynamics. The new assertion is the full first-periodic-entry census and the closed arithmetic late shells, including the sharp terminal probability. A related double-bridge count addresses periodic orbits and basins but not entry-depth strata. Exact aliases, wider coverage and potential stronger implications were searched; no explicit covering full shell result was found. General symbolic methods might recover these formulas, and unindexed equivalent results remain possible.

## Value
**PASS.** Transient depths measure how rapidly feedforward regulatory feedback erases incompatible activity and are distinct from periodic-point counts. The time-resolved product is valid for all sizes and all entry times; its last-shell specialization exposes a qualitative arithmetic dichotomy: equal-size pairs have a macroscopic terminal layer, whereas consecutive-size pairs have an exponentially small layer despite a quadratic maximal clock. These are natural scale comparisons for coupled oscillatory networks, not arbitrary finite exceptions or a normalization check.

## Limits
Two simple directed cycles joined by one AND input; not arbitrary digraphs, multi-bridge systems or stochastic updates. The last-shell population does not imply typical convergence time. No independent mathematical audit has been performed.
