For two independently erased exact observations of a Brownian bridge, the unique best locations for estimating its integrated squared path are \(1/2\pm d_p\), where \(d_p=[3p+\sqrt{33p^2-8p+16}-4]/[8(3p+2)]\). The optimal sites are always distinct at positive reliability, move apart strictly as reliability grows, and equal \(3/7,4/7\) when \(p=1/2\).
