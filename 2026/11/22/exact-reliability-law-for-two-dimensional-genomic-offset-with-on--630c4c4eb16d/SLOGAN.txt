In a two-dimensional genomic-offset model with one Gaussian latent adaptive trait, the squared predictive correlation across possible genetic architectures has an exact Cauchy-transform distribution controlled only by the GEA spectral ratio \(\kappa\). As anisotropy grows, its median deficit is of order \(\kappa^{-1}\), but its mean deficit is of order \(\kappa^{-1/2}\): rare poorly aligned architectures dominate average prediction error.
