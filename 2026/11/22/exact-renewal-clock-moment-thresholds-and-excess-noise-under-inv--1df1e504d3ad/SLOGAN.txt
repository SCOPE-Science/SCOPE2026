For Brownian adaptive resetting at rate \(a/(b+|x|)^2\), every reset-clock moment has a sharp strength threshold: the \(m\)-th moment is finite exactly when \(a/D>2m(2m-1)\). When the variance is finite, reset counts are strictly overdispersed, with exact limiting Fano factor \(1+4\eta/[(\eta-3)(2\eta-1)]>1\), where \(\eta=(\sqrt{1+4a/D}-1)/2\).
