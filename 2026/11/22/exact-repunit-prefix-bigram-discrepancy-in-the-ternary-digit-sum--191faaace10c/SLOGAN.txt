The ternary base-three digit-sum word is sharply \(2\)-letter-balanced but has unbounded contiguous \(01\)-factor discrepancy. For every \(r\ge1\), the \(01\) count before the all-ones ternary index \(N_r=(3^r-1)/2\) has an exact three-residue-class deviation from its known frequency \(3/13\), equal to \(-r/3\) when \(3\mid r\).
