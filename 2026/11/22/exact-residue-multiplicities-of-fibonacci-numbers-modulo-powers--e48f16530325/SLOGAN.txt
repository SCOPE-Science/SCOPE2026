Every Fibonacci residue modulo \(11^k\) has an exact count of occurrences in the least period: six full residue disks have constant frequencies \(1,2,3\), while the exceptional disk has frequencies \(11\) times the number of roots of a precise eleven-adic square congruence. This strengthens the known value-set density \(145/264\) to an all-exponent, target-by-target pushforward distribution.
