Counting all distinct binary subsequences modulo \(m\) yields a residue-sensitive automaton split: the minimum DFA for total count zero has \(J_2(m)\) states, but for total count minus one it has only \(J_2(m)/\varphi(m)\). The exact collapse to projective residue pairs produces an unbounded factor of \(\varphi(m)\) between two adjacent acceptance residues.
