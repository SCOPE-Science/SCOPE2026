Restricting cyclic binary Rule 150 to inputs with no adjacent ones changes its otherwise fourfold inverse fibers into a sharp three-level spectrum: for \(n=3k\), exactly one output has three admissible sources, exactly \(3\cdot2^{k-1}-3\) have two, and all remaining attainable outputs have one. When \(3\nmid n\), every permitted source is uniquely recoverable, and across all lengths the recoverable fraction approaches one exponentially.
