For every binary four-run word \(0^a1^b0^c1^d\), the overlapping greedy Lempel–Ziv phrase count has a closed five-indicator formula, and reversal changes it by at most three phrases, sharply starting at length 12. Under uniform positive run-length compositions, the exact seven-point limiting reversal-defect law is \((1/6,1/12,1/18,7/18,1/18,1/12,1/6)\).
