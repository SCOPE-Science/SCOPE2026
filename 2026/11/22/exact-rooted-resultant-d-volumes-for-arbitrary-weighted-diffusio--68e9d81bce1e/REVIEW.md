# Scientific review: rooted-resultant volumes on weighted paths

## Correctness — PASS
The claim is for arbitrary positive weighted paths, every vertex, every finite positive horizon and homogeneous nonnegative damping; the infinite-horizon formula separately assumes positive damping. The critical nonstandard identity is \(|\det C_j|=E_j|\operatorname{Res}(p_j^-,p_j^+)|\), proved through the spectral cofactor–resultant identity, Schur expansion, and consecutive tridiagonal principal-minor resultant recurrence. It remains valid when the two side polynomials have a common root, because both the resultant and the controllability determinant vanish. The Gramian factorization follows from a positive definite exponential kernel, and the infinite-time statement from the Cauchy determinant. For the five-site mirror-symmetric path, exact symbolic substitution gives factors \(a^5b^5\), \(a^4b^5(a-2b)\), \(0\), and their reflected equals, yielding a strict endpoint/interior threshold \(b=a\) with all ties accounted for. The companion verifier ran 200 exact and numerical checks, including five symbolic parametric identities and three distinct design regimes. Numerical checks supplement but never replace the complete proof.

## Originality — PASS on inspected evidence, with publication-priority uncertainty
Parlangeli and Notarstefano (2011; source inspected) establish reachability ranks for uniform paths. The exact all-order unweighted coprimality, unimodular Krylov determinant and equal-volume tie are **previously known and not accepted as new**. Dilip (2019; primary full text inspected) establishes the general symmetric spectral-Gramian determinant reduction and a six-site unweighted example; neither general method nor that example is new. Liu and Shan (2026; relevant sections and proof inspected) use root cofactors and resultants and evaluate central-root terms for a uniformly parameterized family of path-rooted products, not arbitrary independent positive conductances. The complete publicly available four-site weighted-path determinant phase diagram was read in full; its order-four factors are prior special cases. The proposed novelty is specifically the **all-length, all-root, all-positive-edge monomial-times-two-block-resultant factorization** and its distinct **five-site reflection-symmetric sharp phase diagram**. No full-text prior result inspected states this combined weighted theorem. General Jacobi/orthogonal-polynomial literature might contain an equivalent factorization in another notation; novelty is provisional, not independently established.

## Value — PASS
The determinant quantifies full-state reachable volume from a unit coordinate actuator. The weighted formula turns all candidate actuator comparisons, singular placements and finite/infinite horizon determinant ratios into exact weighted cut-polynomial computations without eigenvector normalization or numerical time integration. It works for every finite path size and every independent positive edge conductance and thus is not a fixed small-parameter substitution. The five-site family illustrates a meaningful structural boundary: endpoint actuation is optimal when the outer conductance dominates, adjacent interior actuation is optimal when the inner conductance dominates, and the center is always uncontrollable. The exact transition is invariant under damping and horizon. The result goes beyond the prior uniform equal-volume conclusion and the earlier four-vertex phase diagram.

## Scope and remaining limitations
A single coordinate actuator and symmetric positive-weight path are essential. There is no classification for multiple inputs, other topologies, heterogeneous damping, or unequal input strengths. The arbitrary-weight formula is an exact algebraic ranking method rather than a universal inequality phase partition. The numerical verifier cannot establish publication priority; equivalent historic weighted-Jacobi formulas remain a risk.
