For every \(0<p<2\), the area shared by two concentric rotated Lamé regions has an exact strictly angle-monotone formula; the first nonlinear loss correction at alignment has sharp order \(d^{p+1}\). For the four-cusped astroid \(p=2/3\), the complete rotation-overlap function is elementary and has a \(d^{5/3}\) cusp correction.
