The theorem is proved symbolically for all positive integers by an exact seven-state integer transition matrix. Run `python CHECK.py`, requiring only Python's standard library. The checker reconstructs the complete transition states from the local tiling criterion, checks the displayed matrix, the accepting vector, the period identity and base terms, then independently enumerates tilings and invariant simplex-orbits for each even width from two through sixteen.

The direct enumeration is a bounded cross-check, not the infinite proof. The infinite claim relies on the finite, explicit state-exhaustion and exact integer identity. The published nonequivariant homotopy theorem supplies the Fibonacci Betti numbers and concentration in one degree, not the new symmetry character. No integral homology description for quotient spaces is claimed.
