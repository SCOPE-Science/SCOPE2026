For the source two-station electric-vehicle charging game, near the exact capacity threshold \(\lambda=24\), station 0 attracts \(13/24\) of vehicles but half the total energy load; the two stations split their scarce unused power in the ratio \(23:25\). Both waiting costs diverge like \(40/(24-\lambda)\) euros, yet their difference converges to minus five euros with an exact first correction.
