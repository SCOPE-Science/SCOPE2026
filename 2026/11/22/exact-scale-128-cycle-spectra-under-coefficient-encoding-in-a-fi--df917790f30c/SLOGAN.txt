On the full \(6401\)-state, seven-fractional-bit grid of a published fixed-point-free four-branch chaotic map, rounding the exact outer slope \(4/5\) produces only cycles of periods \(34\) and \(40\), whereas first encoding that slope as \(51/64\) produces six cycles of periods \(8,20,24,24,28,28\). The complete basins and transient lengths are certified by exact arithmetic, and \(5122\) immediate edges change; neither model is claimed to reproduce a particular FPGA implementation.
