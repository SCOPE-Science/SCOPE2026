For two arbitrary finite ultrametrics, covering their finite-\(p\) metric product by radius-\(r\) balls achieves the product of factor covering numbers exactly when the largest factor radius-\(r\) ball diameters satisfy \(\Delta_X^p+\Delta_Y^p\le r^p\). If one factor has equilateral radius-\(r\) classes, every product covering number is given by an explicit sum of single-factor covering numbers at transferred scales.
