# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The proof reconstructs a unique bounded partition from every binary word and proves the crucial equivalence: a scattered \(\mathtt{abbab}\) embedding exists exactly when two positive rows differ by at least two. Fixing the number of positive rows then yields a unique balanced partition for each feasible area, proving the integer formula without recourse to finite enumeration. All zero-count boundaries, polynomial normalization, extrema, and the Gaussian denominator follow by explicit finite sums. Two independently structured exact scripts reproduce all finite claims in their stated domains.

## Originality — PASS against inspected evidence

Fazekas–Huang–Mercaş (arXiv:2610.06717, Section 5, read in full HTML) define fixed-pattern probabilities and give an additive approximation scheme; the inspected treatment of \(\mathtt{abab}\) is factoring-related and does not furnish the balanced-row count for \(\mathtt{abbab}\). Richomme (arXiv:2510.07159) establishes the already-known partition correspondence, not the particular length-five avoidance enumerator. Three closely related complete published results were read: all balanced four-letter avoidance spectra, sandwich patterns \(ab^t a\), and the special \(\mathtt{abbba}\) five-letter bound. Their explicit theorems fail to cover the trailing-\(b\) positive-part criterion or the complete numerator and sharp maximum derived here. An exact repository text search for both `abbab` and its binary encoding `01101`, as well as semantic and primary-literature searches, found no directly equivalent statement. This is not a proof of global priority; an unindexed or implicit equivalent remains possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The 2026 fixed-parameter probability problem motivates exact tractable pattern families beyond the four-letter results. For the natural first five-letter pattern with two separated \(b\)-blocks, a closed all-parameter answer is unusually small despite a generally nontrivial Gaussian class denominator. The balanced-positive-row characterization provides a structural explanation, a complete length-and-inversion generating polynomial, a sharp maximum, and the stronger statement that no class can force the pattern. This is a natural complete infinite family of exact conditional counts, not a finite unmotivated table.

## Scope and risks

No theorem about arbitrary five-letter patterns or efficient denominator evaluation is claimed. The occurrence criterion must keep the requirement of at least one \(b\) **after** the second \(a\); the proof explicitly includes this requirement. Literature novelty is relative to inspected sources and indexed records. Computational checks are finite corroboration, not a replacement for the all-parameter proof. No external independent audit is claimed.
