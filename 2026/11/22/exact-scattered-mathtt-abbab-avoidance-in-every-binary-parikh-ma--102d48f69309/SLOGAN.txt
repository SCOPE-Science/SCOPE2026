For every nonempty binary Parikh-matrix class with \(A\) copies of \(a\), \(B\) copies of \(b\), and \(C\) scattered \(ab\) pairs, the exact number of words avoiding scattered \(\mathtt{abbab}\) is \(1\) for \(C=0\) and \(\min(A,C)-\lceil C/B\rceil+1\) otherwise. Avoidance corresponds exactly to bounded Ferrers partitions whose positive rows differ by at most one, giving a closed inversion-refined generating polynomial and proving that this pattern is avoidable in every class.
