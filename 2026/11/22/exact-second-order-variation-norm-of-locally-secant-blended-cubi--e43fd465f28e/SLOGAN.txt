For cubic Hermite interpolation with knot derivatives given by arbitrary fixed blends of neighboring secants, the exact gain from discrete slope-jump variation to the total variation of the continuous derivative is \(\tfrac53\sup_j(|\theta_j|+|1-\theta_j|)\), independent of every knot-length ratio. Exactly the convex blends minimize this gain to the sharp universal value \(5/3\), with a precise penalty for extrapolating weights.
