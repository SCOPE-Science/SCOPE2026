Repeated circular von Mises smoothing has the exact sharp bounded-variation coefficient \(K_r(\beta)=\frac4\pi\sum_{j\ge0}\frac{(-1)^j}{2j+1}(I_{2j+1}(\beta)/I_0(\beta))^r\), attained only by half-circle jumps. Every extra block of iterations improves variation contraction strictly beyond multiplication of the separate sharp bounds, with decay governed by the first Bessel Fourier mode.
