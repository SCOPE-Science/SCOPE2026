For the total tagged-part count of odd-part designated partitions, the exact \(3\)-adic obstruction to proportionality between consecutive \(12\cdot3^m\) progressions is \(\nu_3(D_m)=2m+7\), with a leading unit alternating modulo three. Consequently Li–Tang's congruence modulo \(3^{m+5}\) is optimal for **every** depth, even when the multiplier may be any integer.
