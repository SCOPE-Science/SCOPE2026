One of the two half-spin families of maximal generators of a finite hyperbolic quadric shatters precisely the linearly independent, pairwise orthogonal singular-point supports of size at most \(n-1\). For every prime power \(q\), both the VC and Littlestone dimensions are exactly \(n-1\), with an explicit all-rank face polynomial and a nonmatroidal, nonflag shattering complex in ranks at least three.
