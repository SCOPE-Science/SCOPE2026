For convex equivalence relations with exactly \(b\) interval blocks on \([n]\), the pair-equality queries are shattered precisely when their cut-site intervals each have a private site, can all be pierced by at most \(b-1\) sites, and leave at least \(b-1\) unused sites. Their exact VC dimension is \(\min\{2(b-1),\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor,n-b\}\), revealing three sharp competing constraints absent when the number of blocks is unrestricted.
