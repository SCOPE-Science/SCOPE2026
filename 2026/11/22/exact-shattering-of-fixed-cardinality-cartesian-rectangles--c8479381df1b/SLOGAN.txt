Fixed-cardinality Cartesian rectangles have an exact all-grid star-component quota test for Boolean shattering, and a closed shattering polynomial when both sides have enough unused coordinates. In that padded range the VC dimension is \(r+s-2\) for quotas \(r,s\ge2\), with exactly \(rs\binom mr\binom ns\) largest shattered supports when \(r,s\ge3\).
