All maximal independent sets of the path \(P_n\) have the exact vertex-query shattering series \(\sum_{n\ge0}\Phi_n(z)t^n=(1+2t^2z-t^3z)/(1-t-2t^3z+t^4z)\), with \(\operatorname{VCdim}=\lfloor(n+1)/3\rfloor\). Individual shattered supports admit a pairwise directed-reachability criterion, and exactly \(2^k\) maximal supports occur at length \(3k-1\).
