For Dyck words of semilength 1 through 6, the exact single-deletion code capacities are (1, 1, 2, 4, 9, 24), and the fractional packing optima are (1, 1, 2, 4, 29/3, 3269/131). The first strict objective gap is 2/3 at semilength 5. This is not the onset of polyhedral nonintegrality: a fractional vertex already exists at semilength 4.
