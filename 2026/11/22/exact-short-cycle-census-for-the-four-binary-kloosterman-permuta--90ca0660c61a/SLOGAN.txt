The four basic binary Kloosterman permutations on the trace-one fiber have an exact, uniform fixed-point and primitive-two-cycle census in every extension degree. The maximum number of two-cycles is ten, attained precisely by the index-ten map in degrees congruent to ten modulo twenty; additional five-cycle and ten-cycle-count resonances follow from degree-five and degree-ten irreducible factors of the second-iterate numerator.
