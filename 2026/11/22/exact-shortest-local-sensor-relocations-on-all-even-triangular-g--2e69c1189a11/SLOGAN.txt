For every even triangular graph \(L(K_{2k+2})\), the exact shortest adjacent-slide distance between **any** two minimum power-dominating placements is the number of newly required matching edges plus one per alternating cycle, plus one precisely when both initial holes lie on the same alternating path. The full relocation graph is connected, vertex-transitive, \(4k\)-regular, and has sharp radius and diameter \(\lceil3k/2\rceil\); larger complete-graph adjacency creates no shortcuts beyond suitable complete-bipartite routes.
