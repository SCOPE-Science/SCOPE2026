Every dyadic regular-paperfolding prefix of length \(2^k\), for \(k\ge6\), has exactly two globally shortest unique contiguous substrings; their common length is \(2^{k-4}+1\) and their zero-based starts are \(11\cdot2^{k-4}-1\) and \(12\cdot2^{k-4}-1\). A length-seven parity delimiter and a finite occurrence certificate reduce this all-length theorem to a boundary-aware induction.
