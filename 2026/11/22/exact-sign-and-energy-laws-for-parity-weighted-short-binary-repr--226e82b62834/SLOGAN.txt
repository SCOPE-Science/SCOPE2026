For short signed-binary representations, the even-minus-odd negative-digit count is positive at exactly \(2^{L-1}\) positive indices below \(2^L\), although the zero indices form a known sparse Fibonacci family. The signed total is exactly \(L\) while the sum of squared imbalances is exactly \((3^{L-1}+1)/2\), revealing exponential energy beneath almost perfect aggregate cancellation.
