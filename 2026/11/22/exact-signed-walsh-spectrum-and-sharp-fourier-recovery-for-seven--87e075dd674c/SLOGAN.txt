For the Eulerian edge orientations of the seven-vertex squared cycle \(C_7^2\), binary counting convolution has exactly \(6,336\) recoverable Walsh modes and \(10,048\) lost modes, with a sharp surviving multiplier gap of \(4\). The least possible \(\ell^1\)- or \(\ell^\infty\)-norm of any projection onto its recoverable range is exactly \(281/64\), and its first even-weight cancellations occur on fourteen explicit pairs of edges.
