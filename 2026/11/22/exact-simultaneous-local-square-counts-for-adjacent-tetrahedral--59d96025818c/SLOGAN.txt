For every prime \(p\ge5\) and depth \(k\), adjacent tetrahedral values \(\binom n3\) and \(\binom{n+1}3\) have an exact simultaneous local-square count with an elliptic Frobenius-trace term and four explicitly controlled singular residue balls. Among primes \(p\equiv3\pmod4\), the covariance of their \(p\)-adic square indicators is strictly negative for \(p\equiv3\pmod8\) and strictly positive for \(p\equiv7\pmod8\).
