The finite three-bit majority map has exactly \(U_m\) length-\(m\) outputs with a unique length-\(m+2\) binary predecessor, where the all-length generating function is \(\frac{2z^3(1+3z-z^2+z^4-z^5-z^6)}{1-z-z^2+z^3-2z^5-z^6-z^8-z^9}\). A thirty-state saturated-preimage automaton proves this exact singleton spectrum for all positive lengths.
