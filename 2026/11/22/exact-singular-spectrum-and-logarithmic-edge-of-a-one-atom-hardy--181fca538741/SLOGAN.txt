A single positive endpoint atom turns Hardy averaging into a compact operator with a completely explicit simple singular spectrum, quantized by \(\kappa\log(1+\beta^{-1})+3\arctan(2\kappa)\in\pi\mathbb N\). Its exact Hilbert–Schmidt mass is \(1+\log(1+\beta^{-1})\), while every fixed singular rank approaches the noncompact Hardy edge at the sharp inverse-logarithmic-square rate.
