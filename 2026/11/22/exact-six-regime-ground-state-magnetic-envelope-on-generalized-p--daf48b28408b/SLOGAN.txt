On the full cubic generalized Petersen family \(GP(n,2)\), the zero-temperature three-color Potts antiferromagnet has a six-branch limiting magnetic ground-energy envelope with exactly five critical fields \(-1,0,5/4,7/4,3\). The envelope is attained exactly whenever the number of two-spin cells is divisible by 20, and a complete 27-state integer potential certificate proves its global lower bound for every finite ring.
