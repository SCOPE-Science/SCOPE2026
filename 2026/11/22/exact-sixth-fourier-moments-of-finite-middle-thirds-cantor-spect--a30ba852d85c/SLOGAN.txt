The ordered six-tuple collision energy of the finite middle-thirds Cantor digit set obeys the exact recurrence \(E_{k+2}=26E_{k+1}-90E_k\), with \(E_0=1\) and \(E_1=20\). Although every integer in its triple-sum range is represented, the ratio of its collision energy to that of a uniform triple-sum distribution grows at the sharp exponential rate \((3(13+\sqrt{79})/64)^k\).
