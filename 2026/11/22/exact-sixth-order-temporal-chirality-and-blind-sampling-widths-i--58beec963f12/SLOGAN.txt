A Brownian particle with three-state cyclic switching diffusivity can hide its nonequilibrium circulation from all mean-square displacements and two-time squared-increment correlations while revealing its reversal, at known diffusivity ordering, through an exact three-window sixth-order contrast. This contrast vanishes identically at the infinite sampling-width lattice \(h=2n\pi/(\sqrt3|\alpha-\beta|)\), even when the hidden cycle remains irreversible.
