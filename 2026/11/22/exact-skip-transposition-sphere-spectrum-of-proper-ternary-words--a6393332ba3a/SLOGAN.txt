For proper ternary words, legal transpositions of symbols exactly two positions apart occur precisely at maximal length-two runs of the binary difference-sign sequence. This yields the exact all-length degree generating function \(6A/(1-A)\) with \(A=z+uz^2+z^3/(1-z)\), a gap-free sharp degree spectrum, a frozen-word enumerator, and Gaussian degree fluctuations.
