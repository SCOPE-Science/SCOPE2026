The exact side slimness of every compact regular hyperbolic \(n\)-gon is controlled by its side midpoint and undergoes a sharp nearest-contact transition: for \(n\ge5\), the closest other-side points change from vertices to interior perpendicular feet at \(R=\operatorname{arcosh}(\cot(\pi/n))\). The profile is strictly increasing, is \(C^1\) but not \(C^2\) at this transition, and converges exponentially to the regular ideal-polygon value.
