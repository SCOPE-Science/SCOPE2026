For the harmonic-cubic quadratic gradient flow, the planar set of initial points surviving beyond time \(t\) has exactly \(\operatorname{B}(1/2,1/6)^3/(27t^2)\) area, yielding an exact conditional Pareto law. Smooth positive-density initialization gives a \(t^{-2}\) lifespan tail, but uniformly sampling a fixed circle gives the sharper \(t^{-3}\) tail, both with explicit constants and moment thresholds.
