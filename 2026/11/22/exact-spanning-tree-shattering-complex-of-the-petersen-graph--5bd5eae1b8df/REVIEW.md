# Scientific review: Petersen spanning-tree shattering complex

## Correctness — PASS

The finite claim has a complete certificate: the universal spanning-tree trace criterion is proved by forest extension, then all \(32768\) possible fifteen-coordinate supports are checked by exact finite union-find tests. The shattering face vector, purity, nineteen \(S_5\)-facet orbits, ten six-cycle and sixty five-cycle obstructions, and the explicit matroid-exchange counterexample are separately asserted in the source code. Independently, a C++ implementation enumerates all \(2000\) actual trees and tests every one of their support traces; it reproduces the same polynomial and maximality distribution. The classification of the seventy excluded cotree bases has a direct five-cycle decomposition explanation. Limits: this does not establish any statement for larger Kneser graphs.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence

The general graphic/cographic independence characterization, matroid-intersection framework, and Petersen spanning-tree count are known and are excluded from the novelty claim. The inspected full literature on graph neighborhood VC dimension, matroid enumeration, Petersen tree pivot exchange, and other graph-family spanning-tree shattering does not imply the specific complete Petersen face vector, nineteen facet orbits, sixty-plus-ten exclusion classification, or exchange witness. In particular, a theorem computing a VC dimension or enumerating maximal common independent sets algorithmically does not provide these numerical invariants. An unindexed preexisting finite census remains a real priority risk; negative searches cannot prove novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The Petersen graph is a canonical highly symmetric, nonplanar cubic test case, and the complete trace complex is a natural exact invariant of edge membership under spanning-tree uncertainty. The six-edge top coefficient is **not** just the classical spanning-tree count: it excludes exactly seventy cyclic coforest bases. The full degree-by-degree counts and nineteen automorphism orbits expose internal geometry invisible to VC dimension alone, while the explicit exchange obstruction separates purity from matroid structure. This is a complete motivated finite classification, not an arbitrary numerical slice or a repackaged known table.

## Closest sources and limitations

Benediktsson–Macpherson–Adler (2020, arXiv:2007.16042) studies neighborhood concept classes on Johnson and Hamming graphs, not spanning-tree edge incidences. Kobayashi–Kurita–Wasa (2023, arXiv:2307.08948) studies algorithmic enumeration of common independent sets, not this particular graph's f-vector. The previously available Petersen pivot-exchange research treats a tree-reconfiguration metric, and the corresponding complete \(K_{3,n}\) shattering work treats a different graph family. The exhaustive verification is finite and transparent, but historical priority and independent external validation remain open.
