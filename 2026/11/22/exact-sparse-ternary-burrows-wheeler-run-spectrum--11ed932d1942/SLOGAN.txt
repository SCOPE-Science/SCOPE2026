For ternary necklaces with exactly one \(1\), two \(2\)s and all other symbols \(0\), the complete all-length sentinel-free Burrows–Wheeler **linear run** spectrum has five explicit rational generating functions, with attainable run counts \(2,3,4,5,6\). The binary three-identical-marker parity restriction disappears: all four counts \(3,4,5,6\) occur at every length \(n\ge6\), and almost all such necklaces attain six runs as their length grows.
