In the universal half-flux quantum torus, every three-term element \(a\mathbf1+bU+cV\) has an exact spectral annulus and an exact full singular-value interval. Its \(\varepsilon\)-pseudospectral hole collapses at the sharp threshold \(\varepsilon=||b|-|c||\), which can be much smaller than the ordinary spectral-hole radius, and its norm and inverse norm have closed formulas for all complex coefficients.
