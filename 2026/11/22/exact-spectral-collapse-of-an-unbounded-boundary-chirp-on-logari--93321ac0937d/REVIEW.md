# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS

The measure normalization yields monomial norms \(\|z^n\|^2=(n+1)^{-a}\). With \(\gamma=a-1/2\), the symbol belongs to the underlying \(L^2\) space, so polynomial Toeplitz compression is well defined. The exact Gaussian-Laplace transform gives all eigenvalues; a convergent Schatten sum and precise Riemann-sum and maximum arguments give both sharp singular-parameter constants. The modulus has explicit divergent diagonal entries. The finite checker corroborates numerical normalizations but does not substitute for proof.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge)

General locally integrable oscillatory-symbol cancellation and radial Toeplitz moment formulas predate this result and are explicitly credited to Hagger–Liu–Taskinen–Virtanen (2020 preprint) and the broader literature. The exact logarithmic-weight spectrum, all-positive Schatten orders, and **sharp** \(c\downarrow0\) norm-blow-up constants are not implied by the materially inspected general conditions. Indexed related results use either ordinary weighted Bergman chirps, Cesàro transforms, or Gaussian Fock kernels. An inaccessible general-radial 2022 manuscript and older specialized papers remain residual historical-priority risks, not claims of exhaustive novelty.

## Value — PASS

The exact model quantifies cancellation-induced regularization of a genuinely unbounded positive-symbol Toeplitz operator by an arbitrarily small phase oscillation. It supplies both the complete spectrum and optimal operator/Schatten blow-up constants as regularization is removed, useful as a rigorous benchmark for singular-symbol quantization. The coupled exponents arise naturally from the half-order Laplace kernel, not from an arbitrary finite table computation.

## Scope and limits

The proof does not assert a general necessary/sufficient compactness criterion for all unbounded radial symbols; independent mathematical audit and complete historical-priority certification remain absent.
