For \(1/2<a<1\), an unbounded inverse-logarithmic radial chirp on a polylogarithmic Bergman space has an **exact** root-exponential Toeplitz spectrum and belongs to every Schatten class for each nonzero chirp strength. As the chirp vanishes, all Schatten quasi-norms diverge with explicit sharp constants, while the zero-chirp positive-symbol Toeplitz operator is unbounded.
