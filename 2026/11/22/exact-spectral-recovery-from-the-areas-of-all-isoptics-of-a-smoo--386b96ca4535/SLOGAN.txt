The full family of exterior isoptic enclosed areas of any smooth positively curved planar oval determines its perimeter and every nontranslational Fourier power \(a_m^2+b_m^2\) of its support function through explicit convergent parity-telescoping formulas. This inverse data does not determine the oval up to congruence: two explicit noncongruent analytic ovals have exactly the same area at every isoptic angle.
