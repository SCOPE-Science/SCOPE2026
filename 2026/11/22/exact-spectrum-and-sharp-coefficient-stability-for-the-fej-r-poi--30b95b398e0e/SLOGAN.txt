For every degree \(n\), the pointwise Fejér inequality has an exact sharp coefficient-distance stability constant \(1/[n\cos^2(\pi/(2n))]\) for even \(n\), and \(1/[n\cos^2(\pi/(2(n+1)))]\) for odd \(n\). The full slack-matrix spectrum and its unique least-stable parity mode are explicit, sharpening the known equality-only classification.
