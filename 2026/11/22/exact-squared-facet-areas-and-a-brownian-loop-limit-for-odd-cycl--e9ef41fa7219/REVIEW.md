# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The result has one precisely specified domain: canonical Euclidean symmetric-edge polytopes of odd simple cycles of length at least three. The published facet-potential criterion forces exactly one missing cycle edge, and a balanced sign bridge is necessary and sufficient; these facts yield a complete bijection and exact facet count. The Euclidean facet-area normalization follows from a signed tree-incidence determinant \(n\) and the pyramid base-area identity. The exchangeable sign covariance proves the squared-area formula without approximation. The conditioned-walk functional invariance principle and periodic Green-kernel spectrum prove the limiting law, the moment values and the surface asymptotic; path results are independently reconstructed at second-moment level. The supplied standard-library program verifies all signed-root Gram determinants for \(n=3,5,7,9\) using exact integer arithmetic. Infinite claims rely on the analytic argument, not finite checks.

## Originality — PASS, historical priority provisional
The closely matching 2026 tree-root facet-area result already proves a Wiener-index squared-area identity and centered Brownian-motion limit for trees, including paths; it was inspected through its complete original RESULT.md and is credited, with no assertion that the path benchmark is new. Tree-root polytopes have independent edge-root vectors and unconstrained facet signs, while odd-cycle root vectors have one circuit and their facet signs must sum to zero. Thus the tree result does not imply the balanced-bridge metric, exact universal half-factor, periodic Brownian-bridge limit, or cycle/path surface comparison. Full public texts of the 2018, 2019 and 2021 primary symmetric-edge-polytope papers supply facet descriptions, combinatorial/Ehrhart invariants, and simpliciality characterizations but not the claimed Euclidean facet-area law. Indexed cycle-Löwner/Mahler and cycle graphical-zonotope results concern other invariants or other polytopes. Unindexed equivalent work remains possible, and an independent priority search is advisable.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The exact half-factor isolates a structural and measurable Euclidean effect of closing a path into an odd cycle; its proof is not an arbitrary finite invariant recomputation. A sharp surface-area comparison exhibits growing geometric separation under a minimal graph modification, while the Brownian-loop limit gives a nondegenerate full asymptotic random-facet metric profile with computable transform and variance. These are natural metric refinements of an established graph-polytope family, not merely restated facet counts.
