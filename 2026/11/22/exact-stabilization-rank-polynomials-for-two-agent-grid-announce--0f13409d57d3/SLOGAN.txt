For the two-agent epistemic announcement \(\Diamond_1p\wedge\Diamond_2\neg p\) on any full finite rectangular grid, a triangular polynomial recurrence counts **every** stabilization rank exactly, including cycles and extinction. The maximum rank is \(2\min(m,n)-1\) on square grids and \(2\min(m,n)\) otherwise, with explicit extremal counts and a complete five-degree distribution when one shore has two worlds.
