For a uniform random function \(f:[n]\to[n]\), viewed as an attack graph \(v\to f(v)\), the number of stable extensions is either zero or a power of two, with an exact all-order exponential generating function \(\frac{1-U}{1-R}(1-U^2)^{-y/2}\). The probability that preferred and stable semantics agree tends to \(\sqrt{(1-w)/(1+w)}\approx0.5255544565\), where \(w e^w=1\); conditional on agreement, the stable-extension exponent tends to a Poisson law.
