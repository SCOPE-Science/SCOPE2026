For every \(m\ge2\), recognizing binary words without adjacent ones whose number of distinct scattered subsequences (including empty) is divisible by \(m\) requires exactly \(2m^2\prod_{p\mid m}(1-p^{-2})+1\) states in a minimal complete DFA. Legal run-constrained blocks still generate every primitive modular counting state, and a final-one flag doubles the necessary state classes before the rejecting sink.
