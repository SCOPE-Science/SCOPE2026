For the real square Banach plane (equivalently real two-atom \(\ell_1^2\)), the exact norm-difference Steinhaus modulus is \(U(d;a)=\min\{d,\max\{0,2a+d/2-1\}\}\). Perturbations are completely invisible below radius \((2-d)/4\), then detect linearly, and guarantee the full separation \(d\) from radius \((2+d)/4\).
