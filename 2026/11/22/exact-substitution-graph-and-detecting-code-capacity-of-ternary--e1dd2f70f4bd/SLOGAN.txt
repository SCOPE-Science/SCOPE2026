Every ternary word avoiding the short tandem repeats \(aa\) and \(abab\) belongs to a Hamming-one mutation component of size at most four; for lengths at least six the components are exactly \(6F_{n-6}\) isolated vertices, \(12F_{n-5}\) edges, and \(6F_{n-4}\) four-cycles. This gives the complete polynomial counting one-substitution-detecting codebooks and the exact maximum size \(6F_{n-6}+12F_{n-5}+12F_{n-4}\).
