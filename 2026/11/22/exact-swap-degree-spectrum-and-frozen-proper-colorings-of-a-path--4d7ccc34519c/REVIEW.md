# Mathematical review

## Correctness
PASS. For every named palette and path length in scope, the proof establishes a necessary-and-sufficient local swap criterion. Generating a uniformly proper coloring by independent nonzero modular increments proves the claimed independence of the distance-two inequality indicators, even in the ternary case. The weighted two-state transfer calculation has independently checked initial values and characteristic polynomial. The all-length frozen recurrence and sharp maximum-degree count follow from exact indicator pattern restrictions. The variance includes both boundary-product covariances, and the Gaussian limit follows from a one-dependent sum with strictly positive asymptotic variance. Direct enumeration and long-word crosschecks support these derivations without being used as infinite certificates.

## Originality
PASS, qualified. Yamanaka et al. (2015) treat colored token swapping without properness of intermediate configurations. Fuchs et al. (2025), inspected at its path algorithms and local swappability results, cover the ternary swap condition and reachability, so these are explicitly credited. Their stated results do not contain the all-palette degree enumerator, frozen-state count, or moment/CLT laws. Relevant indexed research on deletion-reconstruction degrees and binary run-limited swaps studies other adjacency relations and does not imply this statement. Searches cannot exclude an unindexed quantitatively equivalent enumeration; that remains a residual originality risk. The 2015 work supplies the archive-dated adjacent-swap antecedent while the properness-preserving formulation is explicitly more recent.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The complete degree histogram is a natural quantitative invariant of a graph-reconfiguration system, capturing local branching availability across **every** named proper coloring rather than only the existence of one solution or a reconfiguration path. The closed isolated-vertex count and full-degree count describe two extremal populations, while explicit dispersion and Gaussian fluctuation identify typical local mobility. This is a structural all-parameter refinement of an algorithmically studied problem, not an isolated parameter substitution or known-table lookup. It does not establish component sizes or code capacities.

## Closest literature, limits, and risks
- Yamanaka et al., *Swapping Colored Tokens on Graphs*, WADS 2015, DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-21840-3_51 — unrestricted token swapping; earliest identified source about this local operation.
- Fuchs et al., *Coloring Reconfiguration under Color Swapping*, ISAAC 2025 / arXiv:2511.06473 — defining proper-preserving rule, ternary local criterion, path reachability; the closest directly applicable predecessor.
- Indexed work on run-limited swaps, two-read deletion reconstruction degrees, and equality indicators does not supply the same graph-degree distribution. A differently named local branching statistic in unindexed literature remains possible.
