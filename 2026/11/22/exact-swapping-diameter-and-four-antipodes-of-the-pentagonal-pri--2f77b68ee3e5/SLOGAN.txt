On the ten-vertex pentagonal prism, sequential swaps of distinct tokens have exact worst-case cost \(17\); every placement has precisely four farthest placements, obtained by exchanging the two rails while independently rotating each by two steps in either direction. An exhaustive distance classification also shows that the half-total-distance transport bound can underestimate the optimum by four swaps, in exactly \(1{,}440\) placements per starting state.
