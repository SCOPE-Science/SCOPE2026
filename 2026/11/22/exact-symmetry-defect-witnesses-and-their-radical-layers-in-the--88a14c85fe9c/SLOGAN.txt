The quaternion group algebra \(\mathbb F_2[Q_8]\), a canonical smallest unital reversible nonsymmetric ring, has exactly \(147456\) ordered triples witnessing failure of symmetry, with probability \(9/1024\). All witnesses occur in precisely the three radical-valuation patterns \((1,1,2)\), \((1,2,1)\), and \((2,1,1)\), equally often, and a complete joint multiplication-rank spectrum explains their exact count.
