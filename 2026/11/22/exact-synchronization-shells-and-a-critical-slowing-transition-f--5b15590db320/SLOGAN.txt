Synchronous incoming NOR on a \(q\)-ary depth-\(d\) de Bruijn graph always enters its unique uniform two-cycle within exactly the sharp bound of \(d\) updates, with an explicit first-entry distribution at every initial Bernoulli bias. Its typical synchronization clock is \(2\log_q d+O_{\mathbb P}(1)\) off a single critical bias but grows to \(d-O_{\mathbb P}(1)\) at criticality.
