On complete rooted \(b\)-ary trees, three-state Greenberg–Hastings excitation with threshold \(b\) reaches its sharp extinction time \(h+2\) in exactly \(2^{b^{h-2}}3^{(b^{h-2}-1)/(b-1)}\) initial colorings for \(h\ge2\). These maximizing states are characterized by two pinned bottom layers, a binary-choice third layer and freely colored interior vertices, so their uniform probability is exactly \((2/3^{b^2+b+1})^{b^{h-2}}\), decaying doubly exponentially with height.
