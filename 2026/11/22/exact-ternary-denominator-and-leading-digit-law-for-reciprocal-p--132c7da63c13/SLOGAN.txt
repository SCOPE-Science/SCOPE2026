For generalized Jacobsthal numbers \(U_n(a)=(a^n-(-1)^n)/(a+1)\) with \(a\equiv-1\pmod3\), every finite reciprocal-power prefix \(\sum_{n=1}^{N}U_n(a)^{-h}\) has exact ternary denominator exponent \(h\lfloor\log_3N\rfloor\). After multiplying by the corresponding power of \(3\), its leading unit digit is precisely the leading ternary digit of \(N\).
