For proper ternary colourings of every complete bipartite graph \(K_{m,n}\), the entire additive Fourier convolution spectrum is integer-valued and has an explicit two-shore formula. Exactly when both shores have even size, additional Fourier modes disappear beyond the unavoidable global-translation kernel; their number is an exact polynomial in powers of two, and the sharp surviving spectral gap jumps from \(3\) to \(9\).
