For ternary words forbidding equal adjacent symbols, the exact one-unknown-deletion correcting code capacities at lengths \(1\) through \(7\) are \(1,1,3,4,8,12,22\); hence seven symbols are necessary and sufficient to represent sixteen messages. At length \(7\), the natural fractional deletion-shadow packing optimum is \(99/4\), strictly larger than the integer optimum \(22\), as witnessed by exact finite search and rational primal-dual certificates.
