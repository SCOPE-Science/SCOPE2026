For every nonunit ternary parameter \(a\), the image of the cubic Dickson map \(x\mapsto x^3-3ax\) is a disjoint union of two fixed unit cylinders and a recursively classified zero cylinder, yielding exact value counts modulo every power of three. At parameter valuation one its two density phases are \(19/81\) and \(7/27\); at increasing valuations the density converges geometrically to the cube-map value \(3/13\).
