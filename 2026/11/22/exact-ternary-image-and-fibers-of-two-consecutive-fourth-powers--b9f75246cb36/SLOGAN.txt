The three-adic values of two consecutive fourth powers form exactly the disjoint union \(1+3\mathbb Z_3\) and \(\frac18+\frac{27}{4}(\mathbb Z_3)^2\). This gives every prime-power fiber and the exact limiting attainable-residue proportion \(25/72\), with a singular scaled-square branch only visible beyond modulus \(27\).
