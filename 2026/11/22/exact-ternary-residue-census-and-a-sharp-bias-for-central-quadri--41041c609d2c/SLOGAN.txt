The residues modulo three of every central quadrinomial coefficient have a complete Fibonacci census over all ternary-power index blocks: the nonzero count is \(F_{2L+2}\), and the imbalance of residues one and two has rational generating function \((1+3z+2z^2)/(1-2z^2-3z^3)\). The imbalance is positive with sharp exponential growth base \(\rho=1.893289\ldots\), but is asymptotically negligible relative to the nonzero support.
