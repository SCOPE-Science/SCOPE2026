The full \(3\)-adic valuation histogram of the Fibonomial triangle through row \(4\cdot3^L-1\) has rational generating function \(\sum_{L\ge0}H_L(u)z^L=(10-42uz)/(1-6(1+u)z+27uz^2)\). Exactly \(10\cdot6^L\) entries are nonmultiples of three, while exactly \(18\cdot6^{L-1}\) entries attain the highest valuation \(L\) for \(L\ge1\).
