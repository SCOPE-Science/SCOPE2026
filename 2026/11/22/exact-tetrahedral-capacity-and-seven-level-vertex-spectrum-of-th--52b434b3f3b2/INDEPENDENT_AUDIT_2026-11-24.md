# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The truncated body is the standard simplex in barycentric coordinates with each lambda_i<=2/3. Its vertices are exactly the permutations of (2,1,0,0)/3: two caps cannot be tight, and without a tight cap an original simplex vertex would be required. The signed determinant is affine in each point separately; at least one vertex in a convex decomposition has at least the current absolute determinant. Iterating proves a vertex-supported global maximum, not vertex-only optimality. Independent determinants in tripled barycentric coordinates with one coordinate dropped give levels 0–6 with multiplicities 78,48,192,27,120,6,24. Each determinant unit is 1/27 of the full regular tetrahedron volume, which is 72 in the given normalization. Hence the maximum is 16, and removing four disjoint 1/27-volume corners gives 16/(184/3)=6/23. Permuting the four original vertices acts transitively on the 24 maximizing four-sets. Checking fixed bases on each opposite hexagonal face gives the eight listed base–face pairs; the signed determinant is constant on the three apex vertices in each pair, hence by affinity on the full triangular face. Every maximizing vertex four-set occurs exactly once in these families.

Nearby published volume spectra concern other Archimedean bodies, and the same-solid primary literature inspected concerns particle packings, not maximum inscribed simplices. No examined statement or applicable stronger theorem supplies the exact target capacity, spectrum and optimizer construction. Historical priority is not certified.

## value

PASS

Largest simplex volume relative to the body's volume is a natural affine-geometric benchmark on this canonical solid. The complete vertex spectrum provides the strict next-volume gap, and the eight positive-area constructions show why the existence of a vertex-supported optimum is not a rigidity statement about all optima. These precise invariants are useful for comparing candidate contained simplices and are not supplied by the general reduction alone.
