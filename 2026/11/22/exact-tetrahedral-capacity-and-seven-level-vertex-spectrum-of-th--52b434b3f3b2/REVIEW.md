# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The affine determinant vertex-reduction proof covers arbitrary contained tetrahedra. A complete independent integer-permutation determinant enumeration covers all \(495\) vertex quadruples and yields the seven multiplicities, maximum determinant \(96\), unique maximal tetrahedral symmetry orbit, and all eight signed-affine face families. The exact original/corner volume ratio gives occupancy \(6/23\). The checker is finite but exhaustive for this finite vertex list and is supplemented by analytic reduction; no unsupported infinite extrapolation is needed.

## Originality — PASS (provisional historical priority)
Jiao–Torquato (2011) and Chen–Jiao–Torquato (2014) treat packing/phase behavior of this same shape; the inspected 2011 full text does not calculate the internal largest simplex or enumerate vertex determinant volumes. Closest published records concerning maximum simplices in truncated octahedra, rhombicuboctahedra, and other polyhedra have different vertex sets and do not entail these seven multiplicities, eight families, or \(6/23\). Web and semantic searches did not locate a covering truncated-tetrahedron result. Negative search is not a proof of priority; some older geometrical literature has not been read in full.

## Value — PASS
The truncated tetrahedron is the smallest Archimedean truncation of a Platonic solid and a natural benchmark for contained-simplex capacity. The result provides a sharp scale-free occupancy fraction, the entire exact vertex simplex-volume spectrum rather than just its maximum, symmetry rigidity of all 24 extreme vertex sets, a strict next-volume gap, and eight positive-dimensional optimizer families. These are geometric classifications of a canonical solid, not arbitrary parameter substitutions.

## Limitations
Neither all possible nonvertex maximal families nor uneven-truncation extensions are classified; external priority and independent mathematical audit remain open.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
