# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The companion determinant is one, hence the state map is a permutation. The exact factorization of its characteristic polynomial modulo three has one simple scalar branch of order two and an unramified quadratic branch of order eight. The scalar root has exact first-order three-adic displacement since the defining polynomial at minus one is minus three and its derivative is a unit. The integral matrix identity \(M^8-I=3B\), with \(\det B=1\), gives exact order-one lifting on both branches. The resulting fixed-point counts and Möbius-type subtraction yield the claimed primitive cycles, including exclusion of periods divisible by four but not eight. Full enumeration at levels one through four matches exactly. Risk: the proof uses standard Hensel factorization and odd-prime lifting, stated with hypotheses; neither finite computation nor a literature assertion is used as a replacement for the infinite proof.
