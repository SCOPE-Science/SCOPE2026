For the Narayana recurrence modulo \(3^k\), all \(3^{3k}\) initial triples partition into one fixed point, one cycle of each length \(2\cdot3^r\), and exactly \((13\cdot9^r-1)/4\) cycles of each length \(8\cdot3^r\), for \(0\le r<k\). The complete cycle count is \(1+3k/4+13(9^k-1)/32\), with no other periods or transients.
