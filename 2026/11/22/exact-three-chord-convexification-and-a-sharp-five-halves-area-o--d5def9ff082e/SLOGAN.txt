For the orthoptic of a strictly convex oval with support \(p(t)=R+\varepsilon\cos3t\), convexification creates exactly three supporting chords when \(\varepsilon>R/(8\sqrt2)\), and the exact missing area has a sharp positive \((\varepsilon-R/(8\sqrt2))^{5/2}\) onset. The result computes the full chord geometry and area defect, beyond the previously studied curvature-sign transition.
