For every \(n\ge1\), the minimum number of three-fold DP colourings of \(K_{3,n}\) is exactly \(24,18,14,6,5,0\) as \(n=1,2,3,4,5,\ge6\), respectively. The positive pre-threshold counts come from a complete 36-type finite normal-form census; the previously known six-vertex noncolourability threshold has an independent simple constructive proof.
