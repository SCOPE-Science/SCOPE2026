# Review of the exact four-one palindromic three-deck fiber classification

**Correctness — PASS.** The full eight-entry deck is recovered from two coordinates, \(t\) and \(H\), using the four positions of the ones and explicit combinatorial counts. Equality of those coordinates leads to one quadratic factorization, showing a fiber has at most two words. Its nontrivial root gives an integral partner if and only if the stated oriented gap difference is a positive multiple of five. Unique orientation gives the exact collision census and its rational generating function. The uniform-source limit follows from counting solutions of \(2a+3c+10d=N\). The standalone dynamic-deck verifier, literal triple enumeration and fiber grouping all agree within the declared finite ranges.

**Originality — PASS against inspected sources.** Angal et al. explicitly provide the first length-14 collision pair, so that pair is not claimed as new. Their primary full text establishes general restricted reconstruction bounds and its Section 3.1 has no complete all-length classification of these palindromic weight-four three-deck fibers, no rational collision-pair generating series and no limiting \(2/15\) law. The full text of Chrisnata et al. provides tables and global bounds, not the restricted fiber result. Related records on weight-three three-decks and bounded-run families have different hypotheses. The dimension-bound paper of Nilforoushan and Parvaresh was compared at abstract level only; an unindexed equivalent remains a historical priority risk.

**Value — PASS.** The claim supplies a complete decoding-list classification and exact zero-error deck count for a natural structurally constrained family at the first weight at which unrestricted three-deck ambiguity can become unbounded. The nontrivial limiting ambiguous proportion quantifies the information loss of this observation channel under a natural fixed-content symmetric prior. Its exact closed-form all-length census is substantially more informative than the previously known example.

Scope limits: equivalence only among four-one binary palindromes, not arbitrary competitors; finite computation is a corroboration, not a substitute for proof. Bibliographic incompleteness remains possible.
