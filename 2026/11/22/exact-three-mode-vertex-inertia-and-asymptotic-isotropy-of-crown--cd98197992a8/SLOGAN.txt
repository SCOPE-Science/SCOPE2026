For the fixed-size three-matching polytope of every crown graph \(\mathrm{Cr}_n\) with \(n\ge4\), the entire Euclidean uniform-vertex covariance has exactly three positive eigenvalue levels, with a uniquely forced null direction and explicit multiplicities. Its sharp intrinsic condition number is \(1+2n/((n-3)(n-2))\), decreasing from \(5\) to \(1\): fixed-cardinality rook placements become asymptotically isotropic in their affine span.
