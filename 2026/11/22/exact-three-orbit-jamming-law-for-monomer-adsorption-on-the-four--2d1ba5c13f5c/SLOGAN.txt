Nearest-neighbor irreversible monomer adsorption on the sixteen-vertex four-cube jams at exactly four, five, or eight occupied sites, with probabilities \(1/6\), \(64/165\), and \(49/110\). Despite only two of forty-two maximal independent sets being fully packed, the random greedy dynamics reaches one of these with probability \(49/110\), and every terminal-state orbit has an explicit individual probability.
