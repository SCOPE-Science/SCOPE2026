The three-row odd-island shift has exact entropy \(\log\Lambda_3\), with \(\Lambda_3\approx6.88017738\) determined by an explicit degree-eleven polynomial and a 19-state frontier transfer. Independent three-row strips separated by vacant rows give the quantitative planar lower bound \(h_{\mathrm{top}}\ge\frac14\log\Lambda_3>0.48216\), strictly stronger than the two-row bound.
