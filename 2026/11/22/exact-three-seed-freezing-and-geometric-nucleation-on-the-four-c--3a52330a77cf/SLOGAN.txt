On the sixteen-spin four-cube, standard zero-temperature fair-tie Glauber dynamics can freeze from just three minority spins, but never from one or two. Exactly four of the six three-spin Hamming-distance geometries can nucleate a frozen half-cube, with a sharp maximum probability \(12218/844401\) for the equidistant \((2,2,2)\) triple; the full six-class law follows from an exact absorbing-chain certificate.
