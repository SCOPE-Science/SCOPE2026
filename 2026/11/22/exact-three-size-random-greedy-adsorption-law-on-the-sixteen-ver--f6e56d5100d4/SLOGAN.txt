On the sixteen-vertex Boolean four-cube, random greedy adsorption jams at exactly \(4\), \(5\), or \(8\) sites with probabilities \(1/6\), \(64/165\), and \(49/110\). Its probability of producing an optimal eight-site packing is \(1029/110\) times that under a uniform choice of maximal independent set, and the complete labeled output law is uniform within each of three cube-automorphism orbits.
