For every even generalized-derangement parameter \(v\ge4\), the odd-index terms form an exact \(2\)-adic isometry onto the even residues, giving a unique root \(\xi_v\) with \(\nu_2(D_n^{(v)})=\nu_2(n-\xi_v)\) for all odd \(n\). The three-square obstructions form one computable odd-index residue class on each even valuation shell, together with one even-index class precisely when \(v\equiv2\pmod4\); their natural density is exactly \(1/24\) or \(1/6\), respectively.
