For the stationary orthogonal tents map, horizontal displacements after one and two steps are exactly Haar-uniform, but the third-step displacement has an explicit nonconstant, strictly positive piecewise-linear density with twelve rational knots. Its nonzero Fourier harmonics vanish **exactly** at frequencies divisible by \(60\), making \(\{60\Delta_3\}\) uniform with the smallest possible integer multiplier \(60\).
