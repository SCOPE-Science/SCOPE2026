Three unequal-temperature heat-bath Glauber spins on a ferromagnetic triangle admit an exact positive-conductance thermal network, including a unique temperature at which the middle-temperature reservoir changes heat-current direction. Exchanging one-hot/two-cold with two-hot/one-cold baths yields a sharp, strictly unattained heat-current rectification factor of \(4/3\) in the infinite-hot/zero-cold limit.
