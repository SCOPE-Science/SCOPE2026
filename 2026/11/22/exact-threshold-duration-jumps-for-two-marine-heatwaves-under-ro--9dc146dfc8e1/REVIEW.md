# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In the stated continuous boxcar model, any window receiving positive dose from both pulses contains the intervening gap, so its exposure is ≤ W−g. Thus for g>W−q the mixed response never meets q, leaving exactly the two separate one-pulse superlevels when q≤d and no superlevel otherwise. For q≤d and g≤W−q, if the separate superlevels leave a hole, throughout that hole the first pulse's response falls at unit slope and the second rises at unit slope; their sum is W−g≥q. This yields the claimed outer endpoints. When q>d the two responses are needed; the rising and falling sides imply t≥g+q and t≤W+2d−q by the upper bound d on each single response. On that interval with g≤W−q the sum equals min(2d, W+2d−t, t−g, W−g), hence is at least q. At the critical gap the weak-threshold bridge has equality length q, and the entire strong-threshold superlevel has equality length 2d−q. Inclusion of the equality sets gives both right jumps and both component transitions, including the weak case q=d. Independent exact rational endpoint interpolation agreed in 660 cases near and at the critical gap, retaining even isolated equality points.
