Two identical rectangular marine heatwaves monitored with a long rolling thermal-stress window change from merged to separate threshold episodes—or lose their combined high-threshold episode—at exactly the clean gap \(g_*=W-q\). At that boundary the modeled total threshold-exceedance time drops discontinuously by \(q\) for a threshold no greater than one pulse's dose and by \(2d-q\) for a higher threshold, even though total thermal exposure is unchanged.
