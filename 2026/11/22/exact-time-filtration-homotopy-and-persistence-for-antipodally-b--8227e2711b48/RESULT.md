# Exact time-filtration homotopy and persistence for antipodally balanced four-grain avalanches

## Finding
For every integer \(m\ge4\), place one chip at each vertex of \(\{0,1,m-1,m\}\) of the consistently forward-directed cycle on \(2m\) vertices, and no chips elsewhere. At parallel-firing time \(t\), let \(F_t\) be the set of firing vertices, and let \(K_t\) be the simplicial complex generated by \(F_0,\ldots,F_t\). For \(0\le t\le2m-1\), there are integral homotopy equivalences
\[
|K_t|\simeq
\begin{cases}
\{\mathrm{point}\},&0\le t\le m-3,\\
S^1,&m-2\le t\le m,\\
S^1\vee\bigvee^{t-m}S^2,&m+1\le t\le2m-2,\\
S^1\vee\bigvee^m S^2,&t=2m-1.
\end{cases}
\]
The persistent integral homology modules of the natural inclusions \(K_0\subset K_1\subset\cdots\subset K_{2m-1}\) are free interval modules with **only essential bars**. There is one degree-zero bar born at \(t=0\), one degree-one bar born at \(t=m-2\), one degree-two bar born at *each* \(t=m+1,m+2,\ldots,2m-2\), and **two** additional degree-two bars born simultaneously at \(t=2m-1\). All bars persist under the stationary continuation \(K_t=K_{2m-1}\) for \(t\ge2m-1\). All other persistent homology groups vanish; the conclusion holds integrally and hence over every field.

## Assumptions and scope
The graph has vertex set \(\mathbb Z/(2m)\) and one directed arc \(i\to i+1\). A vertex holding one chip fires, sends its chip along its unique outgoing arc, and becomes empty in that simultaneous round. All firing sets are binary and the configuration shifts forward one vertex per round. The avalanche complex at time \(t\) is the downward closure of all firing sets from times zero through \(t\), not a Vietoris–Rips or clique filtration. Wedges and homology are unreduced where appropriate; an empty wedge of two-spheres means a point. Homotopy statements concern geometric realizations. The filtration is extended constantly after its first period, at time \(2m-1\).

## Proof
**1. Exact firing facets.** Because all chips move forward simultaneously and there is at most one chip per vertex, direct induction gives
\[
F_t=\{t,t+1,t+m-1,t+m\}\pmod{2m}.
\]
Thus \(K_t=\bigcup_{s=0}^t\Delta(F_s)\). These are tetrahedra, and the displayed sets are distinct for \(0\le s<2m\), by inspecting their cyclic gap pattern \((1,m-2,1,m)\).

**2. Complete attachment-intersection table.** Write \(I_t=\Delta(F_t)\cap K_{t-1}\) for \(t\ge1\). Each \(I_t\) has dimension at most one. Its maximal edges and any isolated vertex are exactly the following, where every unordered pair is an edge:

| Round | Edges of \(I_t\) | Additional isolated vertex |
|---|---|---|
| \(1\le t\le m-3\) | \(\{t,t+m-1\}\) | none |
| \(t=m-2\) | \(\{m-2,2m-3\}\) | \(m-1\) |
| \(t=m-1\) | \(\{m-1,m\},\{m-1,2m-2\}\) | none |
| \(t=m\) | \(\{0,m\},\{m,m+1\},\{m,2m-1\}\) | none |
| \(t=m+j,\ 1\le j\le m-2\) | \(\{j-1,j\},\{j-1,m+j\},\{j,m+j\},\{m+j,m+j+1\}\) | none |
| \(t=2m-1\) | \(\{m-2,m-1\},\{m-2,2m-1\},\{m-1,2m-1\},\{0,m-1\},\{0,2m-1\}\) | none |

For completeness, this table is a direct finite residue calculation, not an empirical pattern. Let \(S=\{0,1,m-1,m\}\). A nonempty subset \(E\subset F_t\) lies in an earlier tetrahedron exactly when
\[
\bigcap_{v\in E}\{v-s:s\in S\}\ \cap\ \{0,1,\ldots,t-1\}\ne\varnothing
\quad\text{in }\mathbb Z/(2m).
\]
For the four vertices of \(F_t\), test their six pairs by this displayed criterion, with \(0\le t\le2m-1\); the resulting edge set is precisely the table. Testing the four triples gives the empty set in every case. For singleton vertices the same test gives the listed edge endpoints together with the sole isolated vertex at \(t=m-2\). The only order changes in the cyclic residues occur at the displayed thresholds. This supplies an exact all-parameter method to verify each entry using at most four candidate earlier centers per vertex.

**3. Early stages.** Every new tetrahedron \(\Delta(F_t)\) is contractible. Its inclusion into \(K_t\) is a simplicial cofibration, so the union is modeled by the homotopy pushout of \(I_t\to K_{t-1}\) and \(I_t\to\Delta(F_t)\). The first stage \(K_0\) is a tetrahedron. For \(1\le t\le m-3\), \(I_t\) is an edge, and the union of two contractible spaces over a contractible edge remains contractible. At \(t=m-2\), \(I_t\) is an edge plus a disjoint point; the preceding space is connected and contractible, so its homotopy pushout with a contractible tetrahedron has type \(S^1\). At \(t=m-1\) and \(t=m\), the intersection is a tree, so the homotopy type remains \(S^1\).

**4. Explicit nullhomotopies of all later attachments.** For \(t=m+j\) with \(1\le j\le m-2\), the graph \(I_t\) is the triangular loop on \(\{j-1,j,m+j\}\), with one pendant edge. Its triangle loop is nullhomotopic in \(K_{t-1}\): cone it from vertex \(m+j-1\). Its three cone triangles lie respectively in the *earlier* facets \(F_{j-1},F_j,F_{m+j-1}\), because these facets contain the vertex triples
\[
\{j-1,j,m+j-1\},\quad
\{j,m+j-1,m+j\},\quad
\{j-1,m+j-1,m+j\}.
\]
All their indices are strictly less than \(t\). Thus the map \(I_t\to K_{t-1}\) is nullhomotopic; adjoining the contractible tetrahedron wedges on \(\Sigma I_t\simeq S^2\).

At \(t=2m-1\), the intersection graph is two triangles sharing the edge \(\{m-1,2m-1\}\), so its homotopy type is \(S^1\vee S^1\). The first loop, \(\{m-2,m-1,2m-1\}\), is coned from \(2m-2\) using the preceding argument with \(j=m-1\). The other loop, \(\{0,m-1,2m-1\}\), is coned from vertex \(m\), using triangles contained in \(F_0,F_{m-1},F_m\). All these tetrahedra were present before the final round. Consequently both generators of \(\pi_1(I_{2m-1})\) die in the earlier complex, so the final attachment wedges on two further two-spheres. This completes the homotopy classification at every stage.

**5. Persistent integral maps.** At each early contractible-intersection stage, the inclusion induces an isomorphism on existing positive-degree homology. At the disconnected-intersection stage it adds a free degree-one generator. At each later connected-intersection stage, the inclusion of the intersection is nullhomotopic, so the integral Mayer–Vietoris sequence (or the homotopy pushout splitting above) makes \(H_k(K_{t-1};\mathbb Z)\to H_k(K_t;\mathbb Z)\) a split injection for all \(k\), and supplies one or two new free degree-two generators precisely as stated. Compatible free bases may be chosen successively, yielding the explicit essential-only interval decomposition and excluding all finite positive-duration bars, integrally.

## Relationship to prior work
Riihimäki and Smith (2026, arXiv:2606.26786, first public 2026-06-25) define the avalanche time filtration and motivate its topology. Their classifications of other binary configurations do not give the stages for this four-grain antipodal boundary.

The published result *A torus phase for two-pair avalanches on directed cycles* establishes a global \(2n\)-collapse for the same four-grain support in a wider parameter regime and gives the terminal Euler characteristic at \(2m\) vertices. It does **not** determine the time-filtration inclusions, intermediate homotopy types, or birth rounds. Separately, *A sphere necklace and integral dihedral homology for closed neighborhoods of Möbius ladders* classifies a simplicial complex isomorphic to the **terminal collapsed complex**, giving \(S^1\vee\bigvee^mS^2\). Thus **the terminal homotopy type itself is already covered by the combination of those published results and is not presented as a novelty**. The new assertion is the entire original firing-time filtration, its explicit stagewise nullhomotopies, and its integral persistent-homology maps, which cannot be recovered merely from a collapse of the terminal complex. *Equivariant persistence reduction for periodically lifted avalanche complexes* concerns repeating a fixed smaller cyclic pattern; the present pattern has no nontrivial translational repetition. The one-bar directed-path two-pair classification concerns a graph with a terminal sink rather than a directed cycle and contains no terminal double birth.

## Limitations
The result applies to binary threshold-one synchronous firing on a consistently directed cycle of length exactly \(2m\), with the four grains at precisely \(\{0,1,m-1,m\}\) and \(m\ge4\). It does not classify arbitrary four-grain positions, transient nonbinary firing, other directions or graphs, or different filtration definitions. A full independent mathematical audit has not been performed; a potentially unindexed equivalent persistence calculation remains a priority risk.
