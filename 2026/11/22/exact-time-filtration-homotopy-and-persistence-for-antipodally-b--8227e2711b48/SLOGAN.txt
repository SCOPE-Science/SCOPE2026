For four antipodally balanced chips on a forward directed cycle of \(2m\) vertices, \(m\ge4\), the firing-time avalanche complex is initially contractible, becomes a circle at round \(m-2\), and acquires two-spheres in exactly the rounds \(m+1,\ldots,2m-2\), with a simultaneous double birth at the final round. Every positive-degree persistent integral homology class survives; this full time-filtration classification refines the previously known terminal homotopy type.
