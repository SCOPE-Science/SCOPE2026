For the binary word marked at positions \(2^j-1\), one Defant--Kravitz tortoise step loses exactly \(n-1\) distinct length-\(n\) factors, while the exact tortoise complexity is \(2+\min\{n-2^{\lfloor\log_2 n\rfloor-1},2^{\lfloor\log_2 n\rfloor}\}\) for \(n\ge2\). This gives a linear-complexity word with a linearly unbounded tortoise defect, sharply illustrating why the bounded-gap assumption in the known uniform-defect theorem matters.
