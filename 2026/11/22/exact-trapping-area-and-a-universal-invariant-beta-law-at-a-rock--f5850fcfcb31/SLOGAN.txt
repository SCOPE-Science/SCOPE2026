The conservative radial rocking well loses its entire bounded phase-space capacity strictly and continuously, with exact quadrature and sharp collapse \(\mathcal A(\beta)\sim(256\,2^{3/4}/45)(27/256-\beta)^{5/4}\). Uniform invariant Liouville area on that vanishing well, under natural position–velocity scaling, converges in total variation to a cubic lobe whose radial fraction has the \(\operatorname{Beta}(3/2,2)\) law.
