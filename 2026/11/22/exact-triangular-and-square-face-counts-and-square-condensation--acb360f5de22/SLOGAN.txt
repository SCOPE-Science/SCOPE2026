For panhandle matroid base polytopes, the exact numbers of Euclidean two-faces of area \(\sqrt3/2\) and \(2\) have simple binomial formulas. At each fixed rank \(r\), their limiting square frequency is uniquely maximized by an explicit head fraction; as \(r\to\infty\), this optimum approaches one with sharp deficit \(4/(3\sqrt{r-1})\).
