The complete facet-coplanarity Jacobian of every cube in dimension \(n\ge3\), restricted to antipodally even radial vertex motions, has a two-band squared singular spectrum for \(n\ge4\): \((n-2)2^{n^2-n-1}\) and \(n2^{n^2-n-1}\). The sharp singular-value condition number is \(\sqrt{n/(n-2)}\) for \(n\ge4\), approaching perfect conditioning in high dimension; in dimension three only the lower positive band occurs.
