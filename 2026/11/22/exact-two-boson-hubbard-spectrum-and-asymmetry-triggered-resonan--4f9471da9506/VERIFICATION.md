# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In normalized Fock coordinates the doublon-to-cross-shore map contributes squared coupling 2n (2m after exchange); the distinct-pair map contributes n times the incidence Gram matrix (m−2)I+J. Splitting that Gram matrix into constants and sum-zero vectors gives the factors, with separate incidence kernels and the cross-shore sum-zero tensor giving the dark blocks. In the trivial block the two U leaves and two zero leaves can each be rotated into one dark and one coupled leaf; the squared combined couplings are 2(m+n) and 4mn−2(m+n), yielding (x−U)xF0. For size two the incidence map has rank one with no distinct-pair standard leaf; size three has no incidence kernel. These blocks account for the full symmetric-square dimension. Cubic signs at 0 and U and the asymptotic signs give simple roots in the three disjoint intervals; they do not alone exclude coincidence of two outer roots. The subtraction identity (including x times the standard quadratic when needed) supplies that exclusion: any common nonzero root has x=(3n−2)U/(3n) for the A family and then x²=n(m−3n). At m>3n only this standard family can match; its m−1 copies plus one trivial root give dimension m. Near zero the implicit slopes differ with sign 3n−m for the trivial minus A branch; at large U the middle-root arrowhead equation x²=b−ax/(U−x) bounds x and gives limit √b, larger for the trivial branch. Uniqueness of the coincidence therefore implies order exchange. An independent ordered-tensor construction gave exact characteristic polynomials in eight cases and rational nullity eight in both orientations of K(8,2) at U=3, E=2.
