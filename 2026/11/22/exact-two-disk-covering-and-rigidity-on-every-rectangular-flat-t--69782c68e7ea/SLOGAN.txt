Every Euclidean rectangular flat torus with periods \(a\ge b>0\) has exact unrestricted two-disk covering radius \((a^2+b^2)/(4a)\), attained by a unique half-diagonal pair up to translation; its complete deepest-hole set consists of four points, collapsing to two at the square. At fixed area the best rectangular aspect ratio is \(\sqrt3\), where the two-site lift is a triangular covering lattice.
