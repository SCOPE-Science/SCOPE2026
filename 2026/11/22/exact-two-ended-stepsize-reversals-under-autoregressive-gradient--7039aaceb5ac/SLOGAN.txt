For scalar finite-horizon SGD with equal-marginal-variance AR(1) gradient noise, the globally optimal deterministic learning rates have a complete two-ended phase diagram: negative correlation permits only an **initial** upturn when \(D^2/\sigma^2<-\rho(1+\rho)\), while positive correlation permits only a **terminal** upturn at a sharp horizon-dependent threshold. The exact schedule and terminal risk are available in closed form for every \(N\ge3\), \(-1<\rho<1\), and positive signal-to-noise ratio.
