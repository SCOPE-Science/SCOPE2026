For an odd \(n\)-point Fourier grid with two erased observations and the largest exactly recoverable trigonometric space, the unique quadrature rule has exactly \(\delta-1\) negative weights, where \(\delta\) is the adjusted cyclic erasure separation. Uniform random erasure pairs yield positivity with probability \(2/(n-1)\), but make the mean independent-noise variance \(\sigma^2(n+3)/(2n)\to\sigma^2/2\), instead of vanishing as on the intact grid.
