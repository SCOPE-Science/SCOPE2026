The two-hard-core-boson hopping Hamiltonian on every friendship graph with \(m\ge3\) triangles has an explicit complete characteristic polynomial. Its localized spectral sectors at energies \(-2\), \(0\), and (for \(m\ge4\)) \(2\) account asymptotically for fractions \(1/4\), \(1/2\), and \(1/4\) of the spectrum, while all complementary blocks together have only \(5m\) dimensions.
