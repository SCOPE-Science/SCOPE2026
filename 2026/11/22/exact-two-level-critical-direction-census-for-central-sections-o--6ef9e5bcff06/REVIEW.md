## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge comparison)

The direct 2023–2024 full text by Ambrus and Gárgyán, arXiv:2307.03792, proves existence of a \((2,3)\) two-level critical normal for dimension five but explicitly says uniqueness is suspected rather than proved and provides only a numerical value (Section 5). Its Section 6 already settles saddle status, which is not claimed as new. Ambrus's 2021 treatment, arXiv:2107.14778, classifies dimensions up to four, not the new five-dimensional case. Brandenburg–De Loera–Meroni's 2023 general polytope-section optimization methods furnish an algorithmic framework but do not state the explicit 5-dimensional two-level census in their inspected abstract and metadata.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This answers a natural finite-dimensional instance of an explicit uniqueness question in the directly motivating paper. It strengthens the existence and numerical table to one algebraic, uniquely classified two-level full-support stationary orbit, gives all four multiplicity-case exclusions, and counts the resulting unoriented hyperplanes. These are geometry-of-sections statements rather than arbitrary discretizations or small numerical gains. The finite-dimensional answer is valuable as a sharp test case of the general uniqueness problem, without claiming a higher-dimensional theorem.
