The five-cube has exactly 160 unoriented central-section stationary hyperplanes whose normals have five nonzero coordinates and exactly two absolute-coordinate levels. They form a single symmetry orbit with the unique large-to-small pattern \((1,1,\tau,\tau,\tau)\), where \(\tau=(24-\sqrt{69})/39\); this supplies the exact first higher-dimensional two-level stationarity census and resolves a previously numerically conjectured uniqueness case.
