On a noiseless rotating orbit, the complete population solution of linear chord-velocity matching depends only on the first two moments of the collocation offset, yielding exact residual, damping and frequency-fold formulas. Fourth-order recovery of the true generator occurs exactly at mean \(1/2\) and variance \(1/12\), yet every such distribution has a sharp pre-Nyquist frequency fold at \(2\pi/3\), while its optimized training residual remains of order \(h^2\).
