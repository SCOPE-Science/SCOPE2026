For even integers \(2n\ge26\), restricted coprime representations can be annihilated using at most two prime factors not dividing \(n\) exactly at \(2n\in\{26,28,34,40,46\}\); no one such prime suffices. In particular the exact first nontrivial support-budget endpoint is \(J(2)=46\), obtained by combining a published infinite cutoff with an exhaustive finite prime-cover certificate.
