For every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\) of characteristic at least five with \(q\equiv2\pmod3\), the smooth quartics \(X^3Z+Y^4+rY^2Z^2+Z^4=0\) have exactly two or four rational bitangent lines: among the \(q-2\) allowable parameters, precisely \((q+1)/2\) have two and \((q-5)/2\) have four. An explicit bitangent radical tower and an elliptic doubling argument explain both the arithmetic dichotomy and its sharp frequency distribution.
