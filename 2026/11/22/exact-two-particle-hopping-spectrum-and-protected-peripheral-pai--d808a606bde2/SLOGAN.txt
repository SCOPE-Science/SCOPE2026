For two hard-core bosons on any complete-split graph \(K_r\vee\overline{K_m}\) with \(r,m\ge3\), the *entire* hopping characteristic polynomial factors into two universal linear factors, two quadratic families, and one cubic, with exact multiplicities. Its peripheral zero-energy flat band occupies the fraction \((m-3)/(m-1)\) of any initially localized pair of distinct peripheral sites, protecting that probability against escape for every time, irrespective of clique-core size.
