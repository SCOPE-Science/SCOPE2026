For every odd modulus, the full shifted two-point census of square residue classes—including nonunits—is an explicit product of prime-power character and valuation formulas. For primes \(p\equiv1\pmod4\), square unit shifts beat nonsquare shifts modulo \(p\), but **lose at every exponent \(m\ge2\)**; for \(p\equiv3\pmod4\), all unit shifts tie.
