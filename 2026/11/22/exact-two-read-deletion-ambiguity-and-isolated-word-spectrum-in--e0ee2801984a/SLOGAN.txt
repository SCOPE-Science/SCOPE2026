For binary words avoiding the contiguous factor `010`, two distinct single-deletion reads confuse precisely the pairs joined by an admissible adjacent swap, and the complete edge enumerator is \(z^2(1-z)^2/(1-2z+z^2-z^3)^2\). The words with no confusable partner are exactly those whose `11`-padded forms avoid `110111` and `111011`; their number has generating function \((1+z+z^3+2z^4+2z^5+z^6)/(1-z-z^3-z^5-z^6-z^7)\).
