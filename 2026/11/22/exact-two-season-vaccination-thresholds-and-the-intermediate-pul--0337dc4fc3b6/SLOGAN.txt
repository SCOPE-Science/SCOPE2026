For a two-season epidemic with replenishing susceptibility, the exact largest hazard budget for which the globally optimal periodic vaccination policy is a single pulse is \(M_0=\log\min\{H/x,x/L\}\), where \(x\) is the exponentially discounted seasonal transmission average. In an explicit parameter regime, every budget immediately above this threshold has a uniquely determined optimal combination of an annual pulse and constant vaccination during an initial interval of the high-transmission season, with the pulse mass given exactly by a monotone parametric formula.
