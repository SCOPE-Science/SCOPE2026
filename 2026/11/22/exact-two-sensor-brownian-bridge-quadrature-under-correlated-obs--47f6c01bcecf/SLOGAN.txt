For two predetermined Brownian-bridge integral observations with equal delivery probability \(p\) and joint delivery probability \(q\), the unique optimal symmetric sites lie at \(\frac12\pm\frac{q}{2(2p-q+\sqrt{(2p-q)^2+3q^2})}\). More correlated delivery pushes the sites farther apart but strictly **increases** the optimally achievable integral-estimation risk at fixed individual reliability.
