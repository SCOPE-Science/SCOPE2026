For the union of any two distinct nonzero quadratic spheres in every even-dimensional vector space over an odd finite field, the complete two-sum convolution and fourth Fourier moment have exact character-sum formulas. Averaging over the second radius produces a three-valued cancellation of the associated elliptic trace corrections: \(\sum(\tau_a+\tau_b)=-4-2\chi(-3)\), including the singular-ratio conventions, and therefore an explicit closed formula for the mean four-step return probability.
