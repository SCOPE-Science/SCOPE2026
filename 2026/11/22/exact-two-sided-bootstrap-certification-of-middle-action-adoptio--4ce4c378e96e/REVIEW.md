# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The coordinatewise monotone outsider map admits a lower trajectory from all \(-1\) and an upper trajectory from all \(+1\). These extremal trajectories are monotone, remain on opposite sides of the middle action, and their exact permanent middle-action sets are the two strict bootstrap closures. Comparison proves the intersection theorem, including the equality boundary, for all finite networks under the stated conditions. Seed preservation at the threshold endpoints and finite-time threshold crossing are proved directly. The four-agent witness, minimality argument, and all rational certificate data were checked by the supplied executable.

## Originality — PASS, with access limitations

The July 2026 quantized-communication model motivates the question but, in accessible descriptions, does not assert this exact guaranteed-middle-adopter theorem. The most directly relevant November 2026 preprint, read in its substantive full text, establishes a sufficient combined-threshold cascade; its own two-action exactness statement does not imply exactness for three actions, and its multipolar discussion explicitly leaves intermediate-action propagation unresolved. The exact equivalence through intersection of separate closures and the minimal four-agent strict gap are not implied by that sufficient condition. Binary linear-threshold contagion and continuous-time quantized-consensus results have different hypotheses and conclusions. Dedicated semantic record searches revealed no covering claim. The July manuscript was not fully accessible and possible unindexed equivalents remain priority risks.

## Value — PASS

The result turns a merely sufficient cascade estimate into an exact universal-certification procedure for permanent moderate-action adoption on directed heterogeneous networks. It proves that two different polarity-specific routes can cooperate to guarantee middle-action consensus while each individual outsider fails the usual combined-threshold eligibility test; the smallest realizing network makes the limitation concrete. It is a structural theorem rather than an arbitrary numeric exercise, and it answers an expressly motivated multi-action propagation gap. It does not assert an empirical outcome or a bound on all transient dynamics.

## Main limitations

The seed must be robust, the quantizers monotone and right-continuous, and the network/static inertia hypotheses are indispensable to the proof. The three-bin extension need not coincide with strategic cheap-talk equilibrium quantizers for every parameter choice. Independent historical priority and independent theorem audit are still outstanding.
