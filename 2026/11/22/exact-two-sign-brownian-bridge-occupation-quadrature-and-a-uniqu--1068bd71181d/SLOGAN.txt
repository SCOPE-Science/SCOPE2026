Two noiseless sign measurements give an exact closed-form Bayes estimator and risk for the positive occupation proportion of a Brownian bridge. Within reflection-symmetric two-site designs, the unique optimum lies at \(s=0.336784966762\ldots\) and \(1-s\), recovering \(69.8076\ldots\%\) of occupation variance; at trisection sites the optimal decoder instead has three equally probable reconstruction values.
