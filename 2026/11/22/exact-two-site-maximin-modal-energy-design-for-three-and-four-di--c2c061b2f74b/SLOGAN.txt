For independently resolved Dirichlet sine channels, two sensor locations maximize the least modal information over either the first three or first four modes at exactly \(5/4\). Precisely four unordered designs attain the bound, choosing one site from \(\{1/5,4/5\}\) and one from \(\{2/5,3/5\}\); each equalizes all four modes and strictly outperforms any repeated-site design.
