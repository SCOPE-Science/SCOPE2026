Two sites maximize the least summed Dirichlet sine energy over either the first three or first four modes at exactly \(5/4\). Precisely four unordered designs attain the bound, choosing one site from \(\{1/5,4/5\}\) and one from \(\{2/5,3/5\}\); each equalizes all four modes. The bound is also a scaled Fisher-information optimum for independent Gaussian resolved channels of common variance.
