Over a pointwise named transitive \(n\)-vertex tournament in the countable homogeneous oriented graph omitting directed three-cycles, the two distinct-new-point channels have exact counts \(a_n=n(n+9)4^n/8+3^n\) for each directed edge and \(b_n=(n+2)^2 4^{n-1}\) for nonadjacency. The fraction of adjacent ordered types decreases strictly from \(2/3\) to the limit \(1/2\), with leading deviation \(5/(4n)\).
