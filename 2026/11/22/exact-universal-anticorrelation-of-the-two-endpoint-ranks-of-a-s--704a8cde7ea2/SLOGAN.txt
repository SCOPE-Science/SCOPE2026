For every symmetric atomless i.i.d.-increment random walk, the ranks of its first and last levels have exact covariance \(- (m+1)(m+2)/24\) and correlation \(-(m+2)/(3m)\), regardless of the step distribution or even the existence of moments. The endpoint ranks remain negatively correlated at limiting strength \(-1/3\), despite having identical discrete-arcsine marginal laws.
