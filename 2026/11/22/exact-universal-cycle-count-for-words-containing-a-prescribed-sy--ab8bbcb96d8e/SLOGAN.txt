For each labeled alphabet of size \(q\ge2\), the number of circular words listing every length-\(n\) word containing one prescribed symbol exactly once is \(q^{q^{n-1}-(q-1)^{n-1}-n+1}((q-1)!)^{q^{n-1}-(q-1)^{n-1}}\) for every \(n\ge2\). The exact count follows from a three-eigenvalue factorization of the restricted de Bruijn Laplacian; existence of these cycles was previously known.
