The sharp probability that two uniform marginals land exactly on an axis-aligned \(L^p\) superellipse contour has an explicit three-branch formula for every \(p>0\), and an attaining copula can be constructed by saturating both folded marginal densities. For fixed semiaxes the diamond \(p=1\) is the unique shape attaining the full projection bound; every curved contour loses strictly positive copula capacity.
