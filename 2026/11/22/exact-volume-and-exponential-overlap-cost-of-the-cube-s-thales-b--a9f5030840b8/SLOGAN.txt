The \(2^d\) Thales balls joining the center of a \(d\)-cube to its vertices cover the cube, but their ambient union has an exact half-normal moment formula and grows in volume relative to the cube like \(C\,\Lambda^d/\sqrt d\), where \(\Lambda\approx1.69763020222053\). The exponential base and prefactor are determined uniquely by a one-variable Gaussian saddle equation.
