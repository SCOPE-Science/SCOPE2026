For the real norms \(\|(u,v)\|_t=\max\{|u|,|v|,t|u-v|\}\), the unrestricted von Neumann–Jordan constant has a closed sharp formula for every \(t>0\): a flat square phase, two strictly monotone hexagonal branches, and a unique golden-ratio minimum at the affine-regular hexagon \(t=1\). The formula quantifies the exact onset of uniform non-squareness at \(t=1/2\), with explicit extremizers and sharp first-order transition laws.
