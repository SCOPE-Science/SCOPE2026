## Mathematical value: PASS

The dodecahedron is the smallest nontrivial fullerene skeleton and a canonical cubic symmetric graph with nontrivial perfect matching classes. A complete new sign spectrum and the least unrestricted endpoint norm give natural exact recovery invariants for its dimer-incidence dictionary; the 60 first cancellations at support size two give a concrete sparse identifiability boundary. This is a graph-specific finite classification, not a claim to a general recovery theorem or a result deducible from the 36-count alone.
