The thirty-six perfect matchings of the dodecahedral graph have exactly 700 vanishing Walsh sign classes out of 2048, with the first cancellations at sixty two-edge supports and a sharp surviving multiplier gap of four. The globally optimal \(\ell^1\)- and \(\ell^\infty\)-projection norm for their recoverable Fourier range is exactly \(83/8\), even without assuming translation invariance.
