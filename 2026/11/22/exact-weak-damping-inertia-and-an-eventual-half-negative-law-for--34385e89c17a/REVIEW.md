## Correctness — PASS
For the circle Fourier coefficients \(b_m=\pi^{-1}\int_0^\pi e^{-t\theta^\alpha}\cos(m\theta)d\theta\), the second derivative is integrable for all real \(\alpha>1\), permitting twice integration by parts and giving the nonzero signed coefficient \((-1)^{m+1}t\alpha\pi^{\alpha-2}e^{-t\pi^\alpha}/m^2\) plus a smaller term. Absolute Fourier summability justifies exact finite cyclic aliasing. All aliases preserve parity when the grid size is even. At high frequencies the alias terms share a strict sign; low nonzero coefficients determine two-mode corrections; low zero coefficients inherit strict baseline sign from nonzero aliases. At the sufficient weak-damping bound, concavity gives the strict Fourier sign by an integral with nonnegative factors for every positive index. The zero Fourier mode is positive directly. These establish both count formulas, including endpoints and small grids. A standalone test passed 492 selected cases, not used as a substitute for the infinite proof.
