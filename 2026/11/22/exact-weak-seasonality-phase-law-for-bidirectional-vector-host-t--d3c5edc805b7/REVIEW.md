# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The positive Perron solution yields an analytic periodic Riccati ratio, with stable zero-forcing derivative \(-2s\). Its first two Fourier perturbation equations give the explicit coefficient, and the positive square identity \(H^2-s^2(1-r^2)^2=r^2(4s^2+\omega^2)\) proves phase uniqueness and the exact enhancement/suppression partition. Half-period conjugacy gives evenness, and commuting matrices prove the equal-removal aligned exact identity. The extracted standard-library checker passed 202 assertions. All positivity, frequency, phase, amplitude and asymptotic quantifiers were checked; at coefficient-zero phases, the fourth-order sign is not claimed.

**Originality — PASS on inspected evidence.** Primary full text of Khong–Carmona–Gandon (2023) proves a related second-order seasonal Floquet response, but models a common biting-rate cycle and/or vector-density fluctuation rather than two independently phased directional transmission coefficients. Nipa–Allen (2021) uses precisely the two independently varying directions and compares fixed aligned/quarter-period phasings, providing the closest model; its inspected numerical and branching-process sections do not give the closed phase law, unique optimum or all-parameter sign boundary. Wang–Zhao (2008) provides general periodic epidemic thresholds without the specialized phase optimizer. Published semantic-research results on four-season vector–host matching, a vaccine-pulse SEIRS optimum and two-layer switched SIS were compared by hypothesis and implication: none has a continuous two-direction sinusoidal phase variable with this Floquet coefficient. Historical priority remains provisional, and generic perturbation machinery is acknowledged as pre-existing.

**Value — PASS.** Distinct timing of the two directional infection opportunities is already present in published stochastic vector–host models, so their optimal and worst relative lag is a motivated, non-arbitrary problem. The exact removal-rate-dependent optimizer and critical-baseline phase-only invasion reversal explain a potentially consequential modeling distinction without claiming clinical or ecological implementation. An explicit all-parameter classification is more than a numerical special case.
