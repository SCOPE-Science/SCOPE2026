For complex weighted Wiener balls, the exact \(L^2\) ambiguity of every translated rectangular sampling grid is achieved by two cancelling Fourier modes and equals \(\max_j\sqrt2/F_{m_j}(r_j)\). The worst hidden alias changes from zero-versus-high frequency to a centered Nyquist pair at smoothness \(r=1\), and fixed uniform grids lose a polynomial factor relative to optimized nonlinear sampling in one dimension.
