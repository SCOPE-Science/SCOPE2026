For two identical two-reservoir flood pulses, the arrival lag maximizing total time above a discharge threshold is never an arbitrary intermediate delay: it is either simultaneous arrival or the instant the first pulse falls back to the threshold. At a threshold of \(1/4\) of the normalized discharge scale, the optimally staggered pulses remain above threshold about \(37.72\%\) longer than simultaneous pulses, even though the latter maximize peak discharge.
