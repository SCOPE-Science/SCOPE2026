For rate-one zero-temperature Glauber dynamics on the ten-spin Petersen ferromagnet, the exact probability of freezing without consensus from independent Bernoulli-\(p\) spins is \(\frac{2}{189}[p(1-p)]^3(924+425p(1-p))\), maximized at \(p=1/2\) with value \(4121/24192\). Exactly twelve nonconstant absorbing states are complementary five-cycle partitions, and each has exactly one twelfth of the total nonconsensus absorption probability.
