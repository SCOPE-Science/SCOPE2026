For every dimension \(d\ge2\), the intersection body of the centered \(d\)-cube has exactly \(2d\) linear algebraic-boundary components: its only facets are coordinate \((d-1)\)-crosspolytopes at \(x_i=\pm2^{d-1}\). A truncated-power density factorization rules out all slanted facets, resolving an explicit published all-dimensional conjecture; the result also holds for centered parallelotopes by affine covariance.
