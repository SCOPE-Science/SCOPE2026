The complete 28-line bitangent arrangement of the order-nine quartic \(X^4+XY^3+YZ^3=0\) has exactly 18 ordinary triple intersections and 324 ordinary nodes, with no higher crossing. The nine finite triples are explicitly \([-1/(3d^2):d:1]\) for \(27d^9=4\), and the nine infinite triples are determined by the classical degree-nine slope polynomial.
