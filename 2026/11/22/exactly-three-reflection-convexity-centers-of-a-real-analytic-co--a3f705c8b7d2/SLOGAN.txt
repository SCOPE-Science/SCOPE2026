The smooth real-analytic constant-width oval with support \(R+\varepsilon\cos3\theta\), where \(R>8\varepsilon>0\), has exactly three points about which reflection makes a convex union. Each such union has centered support \(R+4\varepsilon|\cos\theta|^3\), yielding exact perimeter \(2\pi R+32\varepsilon/3\) and area \(\pi R^2+32R\varepsilon/3-4\pi\varepsilon^2\).
