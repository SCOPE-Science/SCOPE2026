For every positive Gaussian scale, the Zak transform of the **unchirped first Hermite window** has precisely the three parity zeros \((0,0)\), \((0,1/2)\), and \((1/2,0)\) in \([0,1)^2\). This settles the arbitrary-dilation, zero-chirp subcase of a 2025 conjecture, while leaving nonzero chirps open.
