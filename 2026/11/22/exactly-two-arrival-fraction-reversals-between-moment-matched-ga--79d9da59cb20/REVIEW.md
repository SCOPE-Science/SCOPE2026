# Review: moment-matched residence-time crossings

**Correctness — PASS.** With the explicit normalization and parameter conventions in `RESULT.md`, the derivative of the logarithmic density ratio is a rational function with exactly two positive stationary points. Three equal raw moments force three sign changes in the density difference; the equal-mean integral of the CDF difference then forces exactly two CDF sign reversals. A concentration expansion yields the asymptotic \(1\pm\sqrt v\) cutoffs. The 128 diagnostic assertions corroborate selected parameters, not the infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS, subject to historical priority.** McGuire et al. (2005) describe Gamma and dispersion residence-time transfer kernels, Starn and Belitz (2018) fit Gamma and inverse Gaussian alternatives, and Giner and Smyth (2016) describe the inverse-Gaussian CDF. Colonius's probability-chapter figure already plots an equal-moment Gamma/IG density comparison, so that qualitative fact is excluded from the claimed novelty. The all-variance uniqueness/sign structure for cumulative crossings and their concentrated-law limit were not established by the inspected source passages. An unexamined stronger stochastic-order source might supersede the claim; historical priority is not independently certified.

**Value — PASS.** Matching mean and variance, a practical calibration constraint, is insufficient even to rank early-arrival fractions globally. The two crossover times precisely partition delivery-deadline regimes for a physically motivated pair of residence kernels. This is a mathematical transfer-function result, not an observational validation of hydrologic model selection.
