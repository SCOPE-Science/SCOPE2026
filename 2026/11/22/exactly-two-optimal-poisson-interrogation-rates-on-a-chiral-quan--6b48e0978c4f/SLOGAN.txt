For a three-site chiral quantum ring with an ideal Poisson-time projective detector and complete source re-preparation after every missed probe, the expected first-detection time has exactly two distinct, globally optimal interrogation rates \(r_\pm=(3+2\sqrt3\pm\sqrt{12\sqrt3-3})/2\). Reversing only the magnetic circulation removes this degeneracy, leaving one optimal rate \(r=\sqrt6\).
