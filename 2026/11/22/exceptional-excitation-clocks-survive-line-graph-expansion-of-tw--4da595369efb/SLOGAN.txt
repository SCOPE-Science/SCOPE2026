For one-contact three-state excitation, passing from the pentagonal prism or the Petersen graph to its fifteen-vertex line graph preserves every previously counted non-three primitive temporal-orbit population, while creating vastly more three-cycles and longer transients. The exceptional waves form five spatial classes at period seven on the prism line graph but a single spatial class at period eight on the Petersen line graph; the result is proved for these two finite graphs by two exhaustive algorithms and exact symmetry reduction.
