A factorially damped compact shift plus one square-zero rank-one perturbation has a complete nonzero spectral lattice with sharp counting \(N_\alpha(r)=1/(\pi r)+O_\alpha(1)\) and an exact infinite set of defective-eigenvalue couplings \(\alpha_k=i/(2\pi k)\). For positive real coupling, its unique spectral-radius eigenvalue decays only as \(1/\log(1/\alpha)\), while its sole negative real eigenvalue decays as \(-\alpha\).
