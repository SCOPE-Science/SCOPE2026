For odd square rook graphs \(K_{2m+1}\square K_{2m+1}\), \(m\ge2\), the exchange graph of minimum identifying codes is vertex-transitive and exactly \(7m\)-regular, with a closed all-order vertex and edge count. At the first case \(K_5\square K_5\), all \(7200\) optima form a connected exchange graph of diameter \(9\), with the exact distance-shell distribution independently checked by two exhaustive programs; the underlying optimum classification is prior work.
