# Scientific scope

The seven field classes cover all nonnegative fields because local neighbor sums are odd and have absolute value at most five. The numerical census is unchanged by the geometry correction: the low-field family has a hexagon on one sign class and two triangles on the other. The intermediate two-edge description requires an external-neighbor constraint. Neutral flips at threshold fields are not excluded by weak stability.

The prior equilibrium ground-state analysis and induced-subgraph energy catalogue are credited to Strečka, Karlová and Madaras. The distinct question is one-spin stability rather than the equilibrium envelope or the configuration-energy census. The all-field flip table is a finite benchmark; it does not give basin sizes, thermal lifetimes or a historical-priority guarantee.
