On the antiferromagnetic Ising icosahedron, all three sub-saturation magnetic plateaux harbor extra strict one-spin traps above the thermodynamic ground states, in geometric families of 20 complementary hexagon partitions, 15 two-edge matchings, and 6 antipodal down-spin pairs. Exactly at \(h/J=1\) and \(h/J=3\), the weak-minimum counts spike to 407 and 191, but neutral flips at those fields prevent interpreting all weak minima as absorbing configurations.
