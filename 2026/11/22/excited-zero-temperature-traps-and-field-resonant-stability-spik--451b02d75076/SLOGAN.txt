On the antiferromagnetic Ising icosahedron, the three sub-saturation field intervals have 20, 15, and 6 excited strict one-spin minima. At low field a chordless hexagon and two disjoint triangles carry opposite spin signs; at intermediate field the four down spins induce two disjoint edges and give every up spin two down neighbors; at high field the two down spins are antipodal. Weak-minimum counts peak at 407 and 191 at the two internal transition fields, where neutral flips are possible.
