A connected star can retain exponentially many distinct locally stable belief profiles after its epidemic has been provably eradicated: for \(m\) leaves and sufficiently strong opinion edges, the exact number is \(2^m\) when \(m\) is even and \(2^m-\binom m{(m-1)/2}\) when it is odd. The result is an exact sign-pattern census for the coupled higher-order SIS–opinion model, not a claim of global convergence of beliefs.
