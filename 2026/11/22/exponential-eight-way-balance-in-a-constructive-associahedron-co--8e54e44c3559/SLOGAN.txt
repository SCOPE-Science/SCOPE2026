Wood's explicit eight-colouring of associahedra is asymptotically equitable: on the \(C_{n-1}\) ordered full binary trees with \(n\) leaves, each colour occupies \(C_{n-1}/8\) states up to relative error \(O(n^{3/2}(250/251)^n)\). Five-leaf fringe-subtree substitutions establish both four-way root-label balance and a parity-reversing involution, without improving or claiming optimality of the underlying chromatic upper bound.
