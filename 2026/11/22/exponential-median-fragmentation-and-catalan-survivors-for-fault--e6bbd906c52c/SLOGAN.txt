Removing any \(k\) vertices from an opposite-corner rectangular grid splits its surviving original-length routes into at most \(2^k\) convex median rerouting components, exactly indexed by which side of each failed vertex the route takes; connected pairs retain the undamaged area's exact flip distance. The bound is attained by \(k\) alternating diagonal failures, for which exactly the Catalan number \(C_{2k}=\frac{1}{2k+1}\binom{4k}{2k}\) original-length routes survive.
