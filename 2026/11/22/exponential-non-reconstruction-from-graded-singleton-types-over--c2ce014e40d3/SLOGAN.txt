In the homogeneous three-distance ultrametric, even the complete polynomial of singleton types graded by nearest distance and number of nearest parameters forgets the nesting of metric balls. For some \(n\)-point parameter bases, that same polynomial is shared by at least \(\exp(\Omega(n/\log n))\) pairwise nonisometric bases; a distinct-refinement construction and a partition-count pigeonhole argument prove the lower bound.
