For every number of one-sided Hardy-average iterations, the exact real \(BV\)-to-\(L^2\) identity-error norm is attained only by a single jump at a uniquely determined position. As the iteration count \(m\) grows, this jump moves toward zero at scale \(e^{-m/e}m^{-1/(2e)}\), while the squared sharp-norm deficit has the exact leading coefficient \(\frac{e}{e-1}(2/\sqrt{2\pi m})^{1/e}e^{-m/e}\).
