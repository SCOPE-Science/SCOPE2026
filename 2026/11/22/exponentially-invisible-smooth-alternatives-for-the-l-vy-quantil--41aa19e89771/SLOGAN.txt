For the published two-band Lévy quantile-conditional-mean test, a smooth, strictly positive non-Lévy alternative can have a test-statistic law exponentially close in total variation to the null: every test using only that statistic then has limiting power equal to its null rejection level. An explicit quantile deformation gives a sharp common-uniform coupling disagreement exponent of \(D(0.3\|0.4)=0.0216008541435465\ldots\), while the full sample remains consistently distinguishable.
