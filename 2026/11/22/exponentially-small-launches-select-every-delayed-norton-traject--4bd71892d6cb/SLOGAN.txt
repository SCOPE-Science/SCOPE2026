A finite sphere cannot leave the apex of a generalized Norton dome from exact rest, but outward launch velocities tending to zero can select any delayed point-particle departure when the radius vanishes. The flight-time delay is uniformly \(\sqrt{\rho/g}\log(v_\rho^*/v_\rho)+O(\sqrt{\rho/g})\), exhibiting a universal exponentially small velocity threshold across all fixed dome exponents \(a>1\).
