For a globally Poisson-reset \(n\)-qubit W state exposed to independent local depolarization, the unique one-qubit-cut PPT-to-NPT boundary is exponentially small: \(r_{\mathrm{PPT}}(n)\sim\gamma n^{3/2}2^{-n}/2\). At the critical scale \(r=\gamma c n^{3/2}2^{-n}\), the smallest partial-transpose eigenvalue satisfies \(2^n\lambda_{\min}\to1-2c\); this is a bipartite NPT criterion, not a claim of genuine multipartite entanglement.
