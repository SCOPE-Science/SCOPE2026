# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The Dirichlet ground-state transform with u_n=√n g_n gives conductance √(n(n+1)) and mass n, without an extra boundary edge. For λ↓0 and N=⌊λ^(-1/2)⌋ the resistance from a fixed site to N is log N+O(1). The resistance-linear test has energy 1/(log N+O(1)) and massive cost O((log N)^(-2)), using ∑_{n≤N} n(1+log(N/n))²=O(N²). Conversely, at that energy the mass on [N,2N] forces some |g_k|=O((log N)^(-1/2)); Cauchy–Schwarz along the path supplies the matching lower bound. In the finite-support variational quotient, small energy makes the g-values on the support differ by o(1), giving |g_m|²→1/S_V under unit defect normalization. Thus the reciprocal top Birman–Schwinger eigenvalue is asymptotic to 2/(S_V log(1/λ)); it increases continuously from zero to infinity. Finite-rank perturbation preserves the essential bottom zero, giving a negative isolated eigenvalue for every t>0. On the codimension-one hyperplane g_m=0 the defect is bounded by a fixed multiple of the gradient energy, so small t allows at most one negative eigenvalue counting multiplicity. Inverting the top-eigenvalue equation gives the claimed limit.
