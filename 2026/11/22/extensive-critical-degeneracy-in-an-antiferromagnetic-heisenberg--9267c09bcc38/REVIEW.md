# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The Hamiltonian is reconstructed as a coupling between one spin-\(1/2\) and the sum of conserved spin-zero/spin-one outer pairs. The spin Casimirs produce an exhaustive finite-dimensional decomposition with energies \(K(k-3m/4)+Js/2\) and \(K(k-3m/4)-J(s+1)/2\), including the correct \(2s+2\) and \(2s\) magnetic multiplicities. The full Hilbert-space dimension sum is \(2^{2m+1}\). Monotonicity in \(s\) and the affine dependence on the number of triplet pairs \(k\) yield both exact critical ratios and ground-state degeneracies; comparing adjacent sectors yields the gap. An independently constructed computational-basis matrix agrees across all eigenvalues and multiplicities in 15 finite cases for \(m=2,3,4\), with a maximum absolute discrepancy of \(2.31\times10^{-14}\). The analytical proof, not numerical sampling, covers all integers \(m\ge2\).

## Originality — PASS on examined evidence, provisional

The closest accessible source on triangular frustrated clusters, Antonosyan–Bellucci–Ohanyan (arXiv:0807.4300, 2008), couples quantum plaquettes through *Ising* variables in a chain rather than through one common Heisenberg spin. The full Karl'ová–Strečka article (2023) concerns a four-spin star with a triangle on its three outer vertices; it does not contain a family with \(m\) edge-disjoint outer pairs and one shared central vertex. Xian (1995) treats ladder/rung Heisenberg chains. The 2014 classical windmill paper concerns an interpenetrating two-dimensional lattice. Searches by graph alias, spin Hamiltonian, coupling-ratio crossings, and degeneracy formula found no theorem implying the full present classification. General SU(2) spin coupling is foundational prior methodology and is expressly credited, not claimed novel. Independent historical-priority checking of all literature is outstanding; unindexed or earlier calculations could cover the result.

## Value — PASS

Unlike a direct evaluation of a small spin cluster, this is a complete all-size ground-state and energy-gap phase diagram of a natural graph family with extensive quantum frustration. The exact lower-ratio crossing simultaneously changes the total ground spin from order \(m\) to \(1/2\), and raises ground-state entropy per spin to \((\log2)/2\), while the neighboring coupling regimes have zero residual entropy density. An exact finite-temperature spectral decomposition is available, and the all-size magnetic-sector selection provides a reproducible benchmark for frustrated star-cluster calculations. These statements are narrower than any thermodynamic phase transition claim and require exact equal-coupling symmetry.
