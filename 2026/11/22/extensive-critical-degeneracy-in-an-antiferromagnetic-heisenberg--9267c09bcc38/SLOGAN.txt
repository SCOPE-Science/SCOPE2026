For the antiferromagnetic spin-\(1/2\) Heisenberg model on \(m\) triangles sharing one vertex, the exact coupling thresholds are \(K/J=1/2\) and \(K/J=1\). At the first threshold, the ground space contains exactly \(m2^m\) states and has residual entropy density \((\log2)/2\), while every other fixed positive coupling ratio has zero residual entropy density.
