# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The definition \(C_t=D(I-tS)^{-1}\) fixes the normalization and gives compactness for each fixed \(0\le t<1\). Every compound minor is either zero or a nonnegative geometric monomial times row denominators; the exact interlacing criterion is proved by a disjoint-interval column transformation. Entrywise order of the exterior-power operators implies order of products of the largest \(k\) singular values for all \(k\). Positivity of the Gram matrices gives strict first-rank increase; the Poisson average gives additive Ky Fan order and strict finite \(\mathcal S_p\) norms for \(p>1\). A weighted Toeplitz counting argument proves the exact fixed-parameter ordered singular-value constant, not merely a finite-section bulk law. Its block-freezing, boundary-rank and truncation-error estimates justify passage from finite-band symbols to the geometric symbol. The elliptic-integral normalization and endpoint follow by elementary substitutions and the standard \(K\)-asymptotic. The standalone finite diagnostic checker passed 2,182 tests from the actual included file; it is not an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)
Rhaly (1984) treats the identical geometrically weighted generalized Cesàro matrices and proves their compactness, Hilbert–Schmidt property, ordinary spectrum, numerical-range facts and norm-related inequalities, but not the parameterwise compound-matrix inequality or ordered singular-value coefficient. Albanese–Bonet–Ricker (2023, 2024) study this family on sequence and Hardy spaces, respectively, without the stated exterior-power or singular Weyl classification. Published full-text published-finding corpus studies on finite classical Cesàro matrices, the power-diagonal commutator of the generalized family, and power-decaying Rhaly rows have explicitly different parameter objects and conclusions. The special symbol integral alone may be implied by general Toeplitz/pseudodifferential spectral theory; claimed novelty is chiefly the exact all-rank exterior order combined with its explicit singular-profile comparison. Unindexed generalized total-positivity or majorization literature could cover the order; independent source reconciliation is not performed.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The compact family is the canonical geometric deformation of the noncompact Cesàro operator while every nonzero ordinary eigenvalue remains fixed. Determining an all-rank multiplicative singular ordering is a structural statement controlling every finite-dimensional volume expansion, not just a norm bound, and it is stronger than the known Hilbert–Schmidt monotonicity. The explicit elliptic coefficient characterizes approximation-number tails, the sharp trace-class boundary, and its logarithmically diverging approach to the noncompact endpoint, thereby separating singular and ordinary spectral behavior. These features form a coherent natural classification rather than an arbitrary parameter example.
