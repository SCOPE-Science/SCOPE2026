For the canonical \(\mathbb F_Q\)-conic family of \(Q(Q+1)\) Artin–Schreier points on \(Q+1\) concurrent lines, the exceptional complete-intersection projection centers are exactly the \(Q(Q-1)/2\) planes indexed by irreducible quadratic polynomials over \(\mathbb F_Q\), minus the forbidden secant planes. Their good locus is a disjoint union of punctured affine-plane products and has exactly \(\tfrac{Q(Q-1)}2 Q^r(Q^r-Q)\) points over \(\mathbb F_{Q^r}\).
