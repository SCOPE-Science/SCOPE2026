For the anticanonical dense-torus code of the smooth degree-seven toric del Pezzo surface over every \(\mathbb F_q\) with \(q\ge5\), the only minimizing four-dimensional subcodes vanish on a full coordinate ruling fiber plus one off-fiber point, while the only minimizing five-dimensional subcodes vanish on a full ruling fiber. Their exact numbers are \(2(q-1)^2(q-2)\) and \(2(q-1)\), respectively, and the result supplies the complete eight-term generalized-weight hierarchy.
