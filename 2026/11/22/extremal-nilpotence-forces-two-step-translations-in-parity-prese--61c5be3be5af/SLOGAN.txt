In the parity-preserving fence inverse monoid, attaining the largest possible nilpotency index forces a partial map to become a restriction of translation by \(2\) or \(-2\), even though generic elements are not translations. All extremizers form Boolean families with exact rank polynomials \(E_{2m+1}(z)=2z^m(1+z)^{m-1}\) and \(E_{2m}(z)=4z^{m-1}(1+z)^{m-1}-2z^{2m-2}\).
