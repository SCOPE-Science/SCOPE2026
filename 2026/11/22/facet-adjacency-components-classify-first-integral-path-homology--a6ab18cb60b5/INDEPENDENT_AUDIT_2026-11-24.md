# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In degree two every shortcut crossed by deleting the middle vertex is a non-arrow distinct from loops, by the rank cycle V→E→F→V. The coefficients must sum to zero separately for each ordered endpoint pair; subtracting a reference path in each group gives a basis over the integers. Thus a cocycle equates all two-path weight sums with common endpoints. Normalize all return weights from one reference facet to zero by vertex potentials. Comparing its two paths to an edge identifies its two vertex-to-edge weights as a_e. Doing the same for any facet, and using one-skeleton connectedness, makes all returns from that facet have one weight t_f. The two choices of intermediate edge for a vertex of a triangle force e→f weights to be q_f−a_e. Comparing e→f→v with e→g→v for facets on the same edge gives q_f+t_f=q_g+t_g. Potentials −a_e on edges and t_f on facets kill nonzero weights outside the e→f arcs and leave exactly these component constants. Conversely the constants satisfy all three types of relation; a coboundary preserving zero on the other two arc types has constant potential, proving uniqueness. The construction uses no division and is natural in A. The integral complex is free in each degree and H_0=Z by weak connectedness. Universal coefficients now give Hom(H_1,A)=A^c; taking A=Q fixes rank c, and taking A=Z/p excludes every primary torsion summand. The r-triangle example contracts to the common vertex while its facet graph has r isolated vertices, giving the claimed separation.

## originality

PASS

The digraph construction is established, and the field-coefficient result for connected closed oriented surfaces is covered by Ivanov–Zhang. The supported distinct result is the component-indexed computation for arbitrary connected pure two-complexes with all returns, over arbitrary abelian coefficients in degree one and integrally in homology. The best matching primary theorem has a duality hypothesis which fails for the contractible vertex wedges. The component-count analogues in the index use different digraph classes or compute a different group. This is a limited comparison of inspected statements, not a priority claim.

## value

PASS

This answers a natural extension of the cyclic Hasse manifold calculation without assuming duality. It identifies the invariant controlling the entire first-degree group, not just a bound on Betti number, and shows that path homology can have unbounded rank in a natural family with contractible simplicial realizations. The arbitrary-coefficient normalization explains both the relevant adjacency notion and the absence of integral torsion.
