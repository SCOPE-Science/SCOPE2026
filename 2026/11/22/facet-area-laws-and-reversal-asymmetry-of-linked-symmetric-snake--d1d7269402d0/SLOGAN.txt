Every linked type-\(C\) snake-interval polytope has a complete standard-Euclidean facet-area measure given by linear-extension counts of contracted or deleted elements of its oriented-path poset, with precisely computed normal-length weights. Reversing a single endpoint turn gives equal-volume, unimodularly equivalent polytopes whose Euclidean surface areas are strictly different in every dimension \(n\ge4\), with an explicit positive gap.
