# Self-review

## Correctness (PASS)
For \(n\ge2\), \(s\ge2\), and \(1\le k<ns\), a clique of size at least two changes exactly one token coordinate. Distinct maximal coordinate fibres intersect in at most one vertex, the bounded-occupancy coefficient calculation counts each fibre exactly once, and all feasible saturated-site counts occur. Connectivity follows vacancy-assisted moves and exchanges. A simplicial collapse to the incidence graph gives the exact integral Betti rank. Algebraic comparison of \(L\) and \(U\) proves the sharp purity classification. The supplied checker tested 45 distinct admissible and full-capacity boundary instances, with finite corroboration not presented as an unrestricted proof.

## Originality (PASS; provisional historical priority)
Song et al., arXiv:2604.04845, introduce the model. Cai et al., arXiv:2611.06275, already provide the common-coordinate clique lemma, clique number and connectivity, so these ingredients are not claimed as new. The coloured-token model in Dalfó et al., arXiv:2611.08186, treats a different capacity regime. The complete capacity-dependent facet distribution, its exact purity threshold, and integral circle-wedge count were not found in the checked materially relevant primary texts or mathematical-record comparisons; related Hamming occupancy complexes may be described under other names. Missing indexed matches do not certify global priority.

## Value (PASS)
The exact facet spectrum measures every maximal single-token reassignment opportunity, not just the largest clique. Non-purity occurs throughout the nontrivial intermediate capacity regime, with no gaps in possible facet dimensions. The explicit Betti rank determines the entire integral homology of a natural topological complex for all admissible parameter triples. This constitutes a complete, motivated classification of structurally distinct capacity regimes.

## Limits and sources
Full-capacity placements are excluded and have isolated configurations. The theorem does not extend to arbitrary host graphs or capacities below two. Finite computation does not replace proof, and independent validation is outstanding. Primary sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04845 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06275 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08186 .
