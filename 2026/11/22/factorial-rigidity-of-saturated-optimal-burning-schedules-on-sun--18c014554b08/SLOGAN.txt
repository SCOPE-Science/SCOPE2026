At the sharp pendant-coverage boundary \(n=(k-1)^2+1\), the labelled sunlet has exactly \(2n(k-1)!\) ordered optimal strict burning schedules, each encoded by a cyclic tiling of its pendant positions. Changing just one ignition while retaining optimality can only switch the penultimate source between a cycle vertex and its own pendant, so the full exchange graph is \(n(k-1)!\) disjoint edges.
