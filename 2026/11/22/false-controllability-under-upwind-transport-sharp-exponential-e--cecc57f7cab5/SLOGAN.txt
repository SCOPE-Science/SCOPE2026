Uniform upwind transport makes two physically separated interval actuators and targets falsely controllable before any signal can arrive: their discrete Gramian is positive but has a sharp \(h^4e^{-2I/h}\) asymptotic, so its implied minimum control energy explodes exponentially. Exactly at arrival the Gramian instead has a universal \(h^{3/2}\) Gaussian boundary layer, with explicit sharp constant \((1-1/\sqrt2)/(3\sqrt\pi)\) after physical scaling.
