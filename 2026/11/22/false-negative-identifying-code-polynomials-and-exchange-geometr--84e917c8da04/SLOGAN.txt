The cospectral Shrikhande and four-by-four rook graphs both require exactly \(10\) detectors to identify vertices despite one false-negative report, yet admit only \(256\) and \(720\) optimal placements, respectively. The rook optimum exchange graph is connected, while the Shrikhande optimum exchange graph splits into \(23\) components, including six isomorphic copies of the rook graph and sixteen isolated placements.
