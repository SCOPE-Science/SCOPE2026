For any two noncommuting rank-one orthogonal dissipation generators with unequal positive rates, placing the larger rate on the outside half-steps of the symmetric three-exponential Strang product strictly minimizes one-step Frobenius error against the exact semigroup among the two reversed Strang orderings, at every positive timestep. The signed error difference admits an exact positive-factor formula times \(b\coth(bh/2)-a\coth(ah/2)\), yielding an unconditional faster-edge-outer rule for reversible three-site diffusion.
