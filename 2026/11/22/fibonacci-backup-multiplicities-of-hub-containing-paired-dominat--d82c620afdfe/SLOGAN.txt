On each labelled wheel with \(n\ge3\) rim vertices, the entire joint distribution of the selected-set size and the number of backup perfect matchings is explicit for hub-containing paired-dominating sets. Such sets number \(nF_{n-1}+\mathbf1_{\{n\text{ odd}\}}\), exactly \(nF_{n-2}\) have a unique possible pairing, and their total pairing-witness count is \(nF_n\), yielding a limiting unique-pairing fraction \(\varphi^{-1}\).
