# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

Every permitted one-line adjacent swap is multiplication by a simple transposition; because both factors are self-inverse, it preserves the self-inverse condition exactly if they commute. That happens only when the two adjacent points are both fixed or form their own two-cycle, proving the local toggle criterion. This yields exact long-cycle invariance, a component-to-path-matching bijection, product Fibonacci-cube geometry, and the shortest-path formula. The inclusion-exclusion component count and edge double-counting follow independently. The Poisson claims use factorial moments and elementary inductive bounds on ratios of consecutive involution counts; overlap contributions vanish at strictly smaller order. Finite checks by exhaustive enumeration through nine positions agree but do not substitute for the proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

The inspected 2022 Fibonacci-cube study analyzes diametral paths of already-defined Fibonacci cubes, not the involution-induced subgraph of the permutahedron. The 2022 independent-set token-sliding paper preserves token number, whereas legal swaps here toggle disjoint adjacent two-cycles. Published research search results for permutations avoiding fixed points and adjacent-swap derangements concern different vertex sets. The local combinatorial observation alone is elementary; claimed originality resides in the exact all-component geometric decomposition together with sharp extremal components, enumerative formulas and the Poisson law. Search cannot rule out an unindexed equivalent group-theoretic result.
