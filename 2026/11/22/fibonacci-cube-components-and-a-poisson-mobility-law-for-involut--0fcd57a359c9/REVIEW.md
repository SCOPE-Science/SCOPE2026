# Scope of the revised finding

The theorem concerns component and metric geometry, extremal component sizes, and exact graph counts for adjacent-position swaps of involutions. The proof uses the commuting criterion and residual-path matchings. For the extremum, joining two nonempty residual runs strictly increases the number of matchings, because the joining edge alone is a new matching. The exact enumeration supplied for small sizes tests consequences of the analytic proof.

The adjacent fixed–fixed count and adjacent partner count are the two relevant motifs of the separately indexed deletion-diversity theorem. Their limits imply the degree law, the isolated fraction, and, together with the component enumeration, the limiting mean component size. They are retained as context, not as independent originality claims. The geometry supplies information not given by those statistics: which other states are reachable and the exact distance to each reachable state.
