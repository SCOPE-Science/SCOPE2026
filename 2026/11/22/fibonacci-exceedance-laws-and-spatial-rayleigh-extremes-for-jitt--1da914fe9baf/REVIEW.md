# Scope of the revised finding

The finding is the joint location–deficit process in the equal-stratum model, not a novelty claim for the general hard-core counting law or for the univariate maximum. The proof uses the exact local triangular density and exclusion of adjacent bounded marks, with an explicit bound on the omitted terms in the marked Laplace functional. The Poisson intensity determines the largest gaps jointly with their locations; the endpoint convention does not affect the limiting maximum.

The finite count law is retained as a useful specialization of the known one-dependent hard-core process identities. The univariate Rayleigh limit alone also follows from Watson's theorem. The finite verification script checks several unmarked consequences; the spatial finding is proved analytically.
