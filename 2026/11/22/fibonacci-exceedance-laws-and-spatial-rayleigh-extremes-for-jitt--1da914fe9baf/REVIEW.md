# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The event that a neighboring jittered gap exceeds \((2-\delta)/n\) is a triangle of area \(\delta^2/2\); at \(\delta\le1/2\), neighboring exceedances are mutually exclusive and nonadjacent ones are independent. Expanding the generating function over nonneighboring index subsets proves the entire finite formula. Mixed factorial moments for rare, position-marked gap deficits show convergence to a Poisson process with intensity \(ds\,t\,dt\). This directly yields the Rayleigh extreme and uniform independent location. A separate numerical integration and 316 reproducibility checks support the analytic argument; tests are not treated as proof of infinite statements.

## Originality — PASS (historical qualification)

The 2015 source of the jittered-sampling construction (Pausinger–Steinerberger, arXiv:1510.00251) studies discrepancy and includes no corresponding finite gap-tail law. Mijatović–Vysotsky (arXiv:1508.00944) study unstratified independent uniform spacings and prove a distinct Gumbel law, rather than the bounded, stratified Rayleigh deficit. Watson's 1954 framework for dependent maxima is relevant general theory but does not explicitly identify this model's exact independent-set polynomial or spatial marked process. The possibility of an older equivalent result in perturbed-lattice or scan-statistic literature remains an identified originality risk, not a claim of exhaustive historical uniqueness.

## Value — PASS

The exact finite-sample tail answers a natural covering-resolution question for the widely used one-per-cell sampling design. The marked limit identifies both the sharp size of the largest unobserved gap and its location, and gives all fixed extreme ranks. This contrasts sharply with the classical independent-uniform Gumbel regime and goes beyond another numerical estimate of average discrepancy.

## Limitations

The exact formula requires equal strata, independent uniform jitter, and the stated deficit interval. The limiting theorem is local at the maximum and does not cover arbitrary nonuniform strata. Independent external audit and complete historical priority determination are not performed.
