One point per equal interval gives an exact Fibonacci-type polynomial for the number of gaps exceeding \((2-\delta)/n\), valid for every \(n\ge2\) and \(0\le\delta\le1/2\). The near-largest gaps form a spatial Poisson process, so their maximum has a Rayleigh deficit of order \(n^{-3/2}\) and its location becomes uniform.
