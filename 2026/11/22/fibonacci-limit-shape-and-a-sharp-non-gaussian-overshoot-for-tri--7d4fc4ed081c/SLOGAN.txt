Exponential stick samples conditioned to contain no triangle have a deterministic Fibonacci-shaped profile of their largest normalized lengths: the \((j+1)\)-st largest fraction approaches \(\varphi^{-j-2}\). Its order-\(1/n\) fluctuations form a coupled non-Gaussian exponential series, whose largest-stick overshoot has the sharp tail \(\mathbb P(R_0>t)\sim\varphi^3 e^{-\varphi t}/\sqrt5\).
