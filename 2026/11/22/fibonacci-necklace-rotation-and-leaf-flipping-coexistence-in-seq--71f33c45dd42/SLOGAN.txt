Sequential \((1,3)\) bi-threshold dynamics on a sunlet contains an explicitly conjugate cyclic golden-mean rotation together with \(2^{n-1}\) independent leaf-flipping two-cycles. This constructed invariant family has exactly \(2^n+\mathcal L_{n-1}-2\mathbf1_{\{n\text{ odd}\}}\) periodic configurations and an exact Lucas–Möbius census of its longer primitive orbits; completeness beyond the constructed family is not claimed.
