# Scientific assessment: Fine-number spectral floor for Dyck-return convolution

## Correctness — PASS

For every semilength, a unique primitive-excursion decomposition establishes the signed character polynomial. Splitting at a chosen interior return makes each coordinate derivative a product of shorter signed polynomials. The all-negative generating equation has coefficients 1, 0, then strictly positive; induction yields every Walsh multiplier at least the corresponding Fine number. The upper bound is the Catalan total, and a square-root singularity gives the asymptotic condition number nine. The independent integer verifier completed 69,432 assertions through the stated finite ranges. Exhaustive tests do not replace the analytic proof.

## Originality — PASS under bounded comparison

The classical Fine-number values and Catalan excursion decomposition are credited to Deutsch–Shapiro and Cameron–McLeod. Aggregate return/hill generating functions do not yield time-resolved signed Fourier positivity. The fully inspected published height-three Dyck step-vector Fourier result concerns an entirely different coding and modulo-three zero phenomenon. Searches for return-set convolution, Fine extrema, and signed excursion composition did not find a covering theorem; absence of matches is not a uniqueness proof. The Fine-number survey's full text was not inspected, and historical priority remains conditional on unindexed sources.

## Value — PASS

The result gives a global, sharp, strictly positive Fourier floor and exact deconvolution stability for a natural distribution with dependent return positions. Unlike the known all-negative Fine enumeration alone, it controls every sign pattern, proving invertibility throughout an infinite family except one explicitly classified order.

## Limitations

Only unrestricted Dyck paths, with interior ground-level returns recorded with multiplicity, are covered. No analogous result for bounded-height excursions is asserted. This is a self-review, not independent mathematical validation.
