For the countable homogeneous ultrametric space with distances \(1,\ldots,h\), all injective finite-base tuple-type counts depend only on the numbers of old balls at each level, and the first \(h-1\) arities recover precisely those numbers. At three levels, two nonisometric five-point bases already have identical type counts at every arity and identical multisets of old ball sizes at every level; five is the sharp threshold for that strengthened collision.
