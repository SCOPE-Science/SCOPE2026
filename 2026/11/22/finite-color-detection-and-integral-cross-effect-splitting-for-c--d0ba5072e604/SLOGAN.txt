For every finite simple graph, fixed-degree integral homology of the independence complexes \(\operatorname{Ind}(G\times K_n)\) across all color counts splits into named color cross-effects of sizes at most the homological degree. In degree \(d\), torsion can occur at any palette size exactly when it occurs already at \(d+1\) colors, and rational Betti numbers are nonnegative binomial polynomials of degree at most \(d\).
