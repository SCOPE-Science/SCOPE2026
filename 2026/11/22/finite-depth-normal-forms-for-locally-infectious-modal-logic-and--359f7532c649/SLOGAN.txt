Every finite Bochvar-Kripke frame has an exact depth-graded normal form: a formula's undefinedness is determined by a subset of locally undefined letters, while its remaining outputs are arbitrary Boolean functions of finite-depth three-color modal signatures. On an \(n\)-world directed cycle with \(k\) letters, the number of depth-at-most-\(d\) formula functions is \(\sum_{s=0}^{k}\binom{k}{s}2^{2^s3^{k\min(d+1,n)-s}}\), and the sharp stabilization depth is \(n-1\) for \(k\ge1\).
