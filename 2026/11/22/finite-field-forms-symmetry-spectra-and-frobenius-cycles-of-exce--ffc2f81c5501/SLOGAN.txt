For every exceptional Chebyshev root divisor with \(2d=p^r+1\), all of its finite-field projective forms, rational symmetry groups, and Frobenius cycle structures admit explicit gcd-and-congruence formulas. Already for \(d=6\) in characteristic \(11\), six nonisomorphic arithmetic forms have only five distinct zeta functions, ranging from six rational points to a single six-cycle.
