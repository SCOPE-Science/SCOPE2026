For the Borel representation fourfold of \(\langle x,y,z\mid x^2y^3=z^{2m+1}\rangle\), all finite-field point counts in good characteristic are controlled by the explicit correction \(8(\gcd(2m+1,q^r-1)-1)\) when \(q^r\equiv1\pmod{12}\), and zero otherwise. This determines the complete Hasse–Weil zeta function from the Frobenius splitting of a finite étale coefficient-base scheme.
