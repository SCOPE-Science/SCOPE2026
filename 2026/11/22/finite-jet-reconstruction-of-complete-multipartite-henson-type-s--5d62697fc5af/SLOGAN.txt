For complete \(r\)-partite named bases in the homogeneous \(K_{r+1}\)-free graph, the first \(3r-1\) neighborhood-grade counts recover every shore size, regardless of the base's total order. For three shores, grades one, three, and five already suffice, and grade four first fails at thirteen parameters: \(K_{1,6,6}\) and \(K_{2,2,9}\) agree through grade four but differ at grade five.
