At the full-height endomorphism point of a delayed Lozi map, an initially uniform \(d\)-dimensional ensemble becomes an exactly stationary, singular \(k\)-dimensional probability after \(d-k\) steps. Although all positive-lag linear correlations vanish, squared-coordinate correlations survive precisely at multiples of \(k\), and their stationary central-limit variance is the dimension-independent value \(4/27\).
