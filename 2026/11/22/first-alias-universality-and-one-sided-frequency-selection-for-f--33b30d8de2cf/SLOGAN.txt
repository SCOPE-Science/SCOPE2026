For every continuous two-sided fractional damping law with asymptotic coefficients \(c_+,c_->0\), the corrected sampled resolvent has the sharp norm error \(\|E_T\|\sim T^\alpha/[(2\pi)^\alpha\min(c_+,c_-)]\), not merely the known order \(O(T^\alpha)\). Each alias has an explicit complex Lorentzian profile, and asymptotically worst frequencies lie in a narrow window around the first alias on the less-damped side.
