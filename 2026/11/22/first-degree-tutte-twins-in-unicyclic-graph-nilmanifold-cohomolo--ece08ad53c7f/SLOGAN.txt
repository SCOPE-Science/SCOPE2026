Among connected unicyclic graphs, six vertices are the first size at which distinct graphs can share both their degree multiset and Tutte polynomial; there are exactly two such pairs up to isomorphism. Their associated twelve-dimensional graph nilmanifolds nevertheless have different full rational Poincaré polynomials, with total Betti ranks \(1280\) versus \(1320\) for the triangular pair and \(1280\) versus \(1316\) for the quadrangular pair.
