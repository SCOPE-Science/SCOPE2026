The first four embedded prolongation degrees of \(x_1'=x_2^n,\ x_2'=x_1^n\) are exactly \((m^2+1)n^2-m^2\) for all integers \(n\ge3\) and \(0\le m\le3\). A uniform weighted-initial-form proof computes both nontransverse intersections at infinity and verifies this initial range of a proposed two-parameter degree formula.
