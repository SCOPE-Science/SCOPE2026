Among Dyck words of semilength \(n\le6\), exact single-bit-substitution-correcting code sizes are \((1,1,2,4,10,22)\), while the natural fractional Hamming-ball packing optima are \((1,1,2,4,10,26)\). Thus the first strict fractional gap occurs at semilength six, with a rigorously certified four-codeword shortfall.
