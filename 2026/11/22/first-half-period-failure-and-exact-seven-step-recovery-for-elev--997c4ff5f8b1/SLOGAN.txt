For ordered prime cycles of lengths \(3,5,7,11\), the shortest distinguishing left-right observation horizons are exactly \(1,3,4,7\). Eleven is the first prime where observing all orbit points for only half the cycle (rounded up) fails: six observations leave precisely \(77,760\) pairs of two-element ambiguity fibers and \(720\) four-element fibers, but seven observations distinguish every cycle.
