For one-error binary \(1\to0\) coding on words avoiding adjacent ones, the first seven maximum code sizes are \(1,1,2,3,4,6,8\), whereas the corresponding fractional packing optima are \(1,1,2,3,4,6,9\). Thus the first gap occurs at length seven even though its fractional bound is integral; precisely ten eight-word codebooks exist, sharing six forced words.
