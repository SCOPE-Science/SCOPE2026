The ordinary cycle general-position complexes first acquire integral second homology at \(C_9\), with rank three, and among even cycles at \(C_{12}\), with rank four; the complete integral homology through twelve vertices is torsion-free and explicit. At nine vertices the dihedral action has a unique rational invariant two-class and no invariant one-class, so the ordinary symmetry quotient has the rational homology of a two-sphere.
