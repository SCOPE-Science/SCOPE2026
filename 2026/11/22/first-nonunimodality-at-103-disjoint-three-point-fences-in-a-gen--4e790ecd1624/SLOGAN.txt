For one-point types in the homogeneous generic poset over \(k\) disjoint three-point fences, the exact comparable-parameter generating polynomial is \((1+2z+z^2+z^3)^k+(1+z+2z^2+z^3)^k-1+k(2z^2+z^3)\). Its coefficients are unimodal for every \(1\le k\le102\) but first develop a strict two-sided valley at \(k=103\); a general asymmetric-ideal-mean principle proves that such valleys eventually persist in disjoint-copy families.
