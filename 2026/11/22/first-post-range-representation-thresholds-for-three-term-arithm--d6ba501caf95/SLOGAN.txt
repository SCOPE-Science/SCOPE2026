The first threshold beyond the known \(p\le\lfloor a/2\rfloor\) range for three-term arithmetic progressions has an exact, parity-dependent \(p\)-Apéry set for every coprime step \(d\ge3\). For \(p=\lfloor a/2\rfloor+1\), this gives complete Frobenius and genus formulas, with a one-row parity jump at even \(a\).
