In the natural \(\mathrm{PGL}_2(23)\)-invariant genus-\(505\) curve pencil, every one of the \(21\) smooth prime-field marked members is nonordinary, although exactly \(490\) of \(527\) quadratic-field marked members are ordinary. This is the first such prime-field ordinarity gap for primes \(p\ge5\), with complete \(23\)-rank strata certified by an explicit Legendre-polynomial divisibility identity.
