For six fixed equispaced interpolation nodes, the exact positivity region of degree-five quadrature against any symmetric probability law is a three-inequality positivity region in its centered second and fourth moments. Along the symmetric \(\operatorname{Beta}(a,a)\) family, positivity occurs on exactly two disconnected intervals, \(0<a\le(107-\sqrt{1081})/18\) and \((107+\sqrt{1081})/18\le a\le11\); six nodes are the first closed equispaced rule for which this reentrant phenomenon occurs.
