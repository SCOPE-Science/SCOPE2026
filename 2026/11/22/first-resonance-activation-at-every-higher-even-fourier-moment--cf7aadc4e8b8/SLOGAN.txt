For every integer even-moment order \(2k\ge6\), analytic Fourier trinomials have a sharp resonance transition immediately above the Hardy hypercontractivity radius: if the high frequency exceeds \(k\) times the low frequency it is completely suppressed, but exactly at that collision every optimizer activates it at scale \(\delta^{k/2}\). Resonance raises the optimal \(2k\)-th moment by the sharp positive amount \(C_k\delta^k+o(\delta^k)\), with \(\delta=\rho^{2m}-1/k\).
