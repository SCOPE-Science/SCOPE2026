# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof gives a bijective finite recurrence for all alternating-sign matrices by column-prefix states and an independently implemented monotone-triangle enumeration. Complete exact transforms on every frequency, inversion/Parseval, direct orbit-representative sums, and the nonzero-frequency projection kernel jointly support all finite counts and the endpoint optimum. The all-projections bound follows by translation averaging. The checked standalone code reconstructs every figure without input datasets.

## Originality — PASS under bounded comparison

Closest prior mathematics: Lalonde (2004) treats the one-negative-entry subclass; Behrend (2012) supplies global negative-entry and boundary refined generating functions, not pointwise \(16\)-cell Fourier characters. The related order-four published Walsh result uses the binary *nonzero* support of all entries; the order-five interpolation result measures low-degree ternary marginals on probability laws. None of their inspected results determines the order-six negative-site XOR spectrum or its exact projection norm. Historical priority cannot be certified exhaustively; earlier unindexed site-weighted enumeration is a concrete residual risk.

## Value — PASS

The first failure of spectral recovery occurs at a nontrivial natural size, with a sharply classified nullspace and a sizeable exact optimal recovery projection norm. It quantifies the information loss caused by mixing the spatial negative-entry masks of a classical combinatorial family, not just the total number of matrices. The finite cutoff and the full exact invariant have a clear interpretation, without claiming a result for larger orders.

## Scope and limits

This is a finite exact result for \(n\le6\), with a sharp projection calculation only at \(n=6\). No claim is made about size seven or above, no priority inference is drawn from unavailable sources, and no independent audit has been conducted.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
