For positive symmetric weights on the five consecutive integers, the maximum number of mutually orthogonal Fourier characters is completely classified into exact one-, two-, three-, four- and five-character regions. A curved resonance family has maximal triples with irrational frequency differences even at rational weights, whereas an exact maximum of four occurs only at the \(1:2:2\) outer–inner–central ratio and an exact maximum of five requires uniform weights.
