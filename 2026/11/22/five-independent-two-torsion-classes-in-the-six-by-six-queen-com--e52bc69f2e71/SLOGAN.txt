The full square-dihedral geometric quotient of the ordinary six-by-six nonattacking-queen complex has reduced integral homology \(\widetilde H_2\cong(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^5\) and \(\widetilde H_3\cong\mathbb Z\), with all other reduced groups zero. The five independent two-torsion classes are invisible in the previously known rational symmetry character.
