The ordinary five-cut complexes of the first five nondegenerate Möbius ladders have exact integral homotopy types \(S^1\), \(\bigvee^{41}S^4\), \(\bigvee^{216}S^6\), \(\bigvee^{582}S^8\), and \(\bigvee^{1213}S^{10}\), respectively. An exhaustive acyclic discrete-Morse certificate establishes all five classifications, with a dimension-dropping circle in the eight-vertex case; nothing is claimed for larger ladders.
