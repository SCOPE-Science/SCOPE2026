The fourteen-point finite \(T_0\) spaces whose inseparability graphs contain an induced seven-antihole \(\overline{C_7}\) have exactly five homeomorphism types, parameterized by dihedral orbits of vertex covers of a step-two seven-cycle. Their order complexes are wedges of \(5,6,6,7,8\) circles respectively; the known seven-clique-cover bound explains minimality, while the equality classification and weak homotopy types are the substantive refinement.
