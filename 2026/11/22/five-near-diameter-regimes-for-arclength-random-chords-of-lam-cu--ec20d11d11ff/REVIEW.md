## Mathematical value — PASS

The Lamé curves interpolate naturally between nonconvex cuspidal astroids, a cornered diamond, singular-curvature convex ovals, the circle, and smooth rounded-square ovals. Their Euclidean diameter contact changes from finitely many linear tips through fractional-power contacts to a continuum and then nondegenerate quadratic contacts. The resulting *exact* regime exponents, gamma-function amplitudes, exceptional doubled diamond constant, and explicit conditioned rare-shape domains characterize a natural geometric probability functional throughout that family. They provide normalization checks for future studies of extreme sample diameters and singular-boundary chord models; no general downstream theorem is asserted.
