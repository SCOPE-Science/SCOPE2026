For two independent Euclidean-arclength-uniform points on \(|x|^p+|y|^p=1\), the chance their chord is within \(\varepsilon\) of the diameter has a complete five-case sharp power law across \(p=1\) and \(p=2\). The diamond at \(p=1\) has an isolated doubling of its quadratic coefficient, while all regimes admit explicit conditional limiting shapes for near-diametral pairs.
