For any pure three-complex, a tetrahedral facet subdivision merges the \(k\) components of its simplicial double meeting that facet and changes its first Betti rank by exactly \(6-k\). In particular, the double of every stacked simplicial four-polytope with \(n\ge5\) vertices is \(\bigvee^{5n-19}S^1\), gaining five circles at each stacking.
