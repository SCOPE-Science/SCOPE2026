The exact five-edge rank-restoration law was not located in the inspected full primary literature or targeted searches; historical priority cannot be guaranteed, and a hidden equivalent matrix-minor/coding result remains the principal risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The Tutte–Coxeter graph is a canonical, highly symmetric smallest cubic girth-eight graph and a standard finite generalized-quadrangle incidence network. The theorem supplies the *minimum* number and an explicit fully robust set of gate omissions that unlock a maximally entangled balanced cut, with one-entanglement-unit gain for each subset and dimension-uniform integral behavior. This sharp and physically counterintuitive preparation-fault phenomenon is a motivated finite quantum-resource benchmark; no noise tolerance, experimental advantage or general larger-geometry conjecture is claimed.
