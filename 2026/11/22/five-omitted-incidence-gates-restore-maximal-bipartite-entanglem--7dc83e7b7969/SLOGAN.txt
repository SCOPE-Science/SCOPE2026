Five carefully selected omitted controlled-phase gates raise the Tutte–Coxeter graph state’s duad-versus-syntheme entanglement from \(10\log_2 d\) to the maximum \(15\log_2 d\) bits for every local qudit dimension \(d\ge2\). Every subset of those five omissions gains exactly one \(\log_2 d\) unit, and fewer than five omissions cannot reach maximal entanglement.
