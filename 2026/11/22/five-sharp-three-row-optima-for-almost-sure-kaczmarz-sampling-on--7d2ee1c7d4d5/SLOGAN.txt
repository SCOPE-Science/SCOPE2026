On five equally spaced planar Kaczmarz row directions, the fastest almost-sure operator contraction among all fixed iid row-sampling laws has exactly five optimizers, each sampling three rows with probabilities proportional to \(2b-a,b,b\), where \(a=-\log\cos^2(\pi/5)\) and \(b=-\log\cos^2(2\pi/5)\). All five beat every two-row and uniform sampler in typical exponent but lose to uniform sampling on the one-step worst-direction mean-square objective.
