On every two-subset Kneser graph \(\operatorname{KG}(n,2)\) with \(n\ge8\), optimal three-sensor placements form a connected adjacent-slide graph of radius \(4\) and diameter \(5\). Exactly the disjoint source-target configurations whose direct-slide bipartite graph has matching number at most \(1\) require the full five moves; triangle-shaped placements uniquely have eccentricity \(4\).
