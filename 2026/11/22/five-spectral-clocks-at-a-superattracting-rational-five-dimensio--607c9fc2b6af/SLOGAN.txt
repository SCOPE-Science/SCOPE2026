For the positive five-dimensional rational map studied by Zhang and Shen, the logarithm of the distance to its superattracting equilibrium has an exact five-eigenmode expansion, with Perron exponent \(1.429108319838\ldots\) and a *growing oscillatory subleading mode* of modulus \(1.125407111123\ldots\). Explicit convergent amplitude series expose a strictly positive first-order response to the nonlinear offset, while global convergence itself is already known from earlier literature.
