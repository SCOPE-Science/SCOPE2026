# Five three-spheres in the \(A_5\)-quotient of a directed-forest complex

## Finding

Let \(D_5\) be the loopless complete bidirected graph on the set \(\{1,2,3,4,5\}\). Let \(\Delta(D_5)\) be its directed-forest simplicial complex: the simplicial vertices are ordered arcs \(i\to j\) for \(i\ne j\), and a set of arcs is a face if each graph vertex has indegree at most one and there is no directed cycle. Simultaneous relabeling acts on \(\Delta(D_5)\). Its index-two alternating subgroup has the following **ordinary geometric orbit space**:

\[
\boxed{|\Delta(D_5)|/A_5\simeq\bigvee^{5}S^3.}
\]

This is an integral homotopy equivalence. In particular, the quotient is simply connected, \(\widetilde H_3\cong\mathbb Z^5\), and every other reduced integral homology group vanishes. The residual involution from \(S_5/A_5\) fixes a two-dimensional subspace of rational third homology and negates a three-dimensional subspace; its ordinary further orbit quotient is the previously classified \(S_5\)-quotient \(S^3\vee S^3\).

## Assumptions and scope

The vertices \(1,\dots,5\) label the underlying directed graph, and a directed forest means a disjoint union of outward-rooted directed trees, possibly with isolated graph vertices. This is *not* a complex of undirected graph forests or a directed-linear-forest/multipath complex. A set of \(k\) arcs is a simplex of dimension \(k-1\); a directed forest has at most four arcs, so \(\Delta(D_5)\) has dimension three. The quotient is \(|\Delta(D_5)|/A_5\), not a homotopy-orbit space, not the naive face-orbit simplicial complex, and not the quotient by all of \(S_5\).

## Proof

**1. Stabilizer-correct cellular model.** Let \(P\) denote the nonempty face poset of \(\Delta(D_5)\). The barycentric subdivision has simplices indexed by strict flags of nonempty faces \(F_0\subsetneq\cdots\subsetneq F_k\). Each relabeling preserves the cardinalities of the faces. Consequently, an element stabilizing a flag setwise fixes every face in the flag and therefore fixes its barycentric simplex pointwise. The orbit space has a regular \(\Delta\)-complex cell structure with a distinct oriented cell for each \(A_5\)-orbit of **whole flags**; its integral boundary is

\[
\partial[F_0,\ldots,F_k]=\sum_{j=0}^{k}(-1)^j[F_0,\ldots,\widehat F_j,\ldots,F_k].
\]

Crucially, the bracket denotes the orbit of the entire shortened flag, not a tuple of separate face-orbit classes. This handles nonfree stabilizers without rationalizing the chain complex.

**2. Exact integral chain computation.** Enumerating the directed forests by increasing number of arcs gives \((20,150,500,625)\) faces of sizes one through four, respectively. Strict-chain enumeration gives \((1295,12050,25500,15000)\) subdivision simplices in dimensions zero through three. Under the 60 even permutations, their flag orbits give cellular ranks

\[
(\operatorname{rank}C_0,\operatorname{rank}C_1,\operatorname{rank}C_2,\operatorname{rank}C_3)=(27,206,425,250).
\]

All consecutive signed boundary products are exactly zero. The standalone verifier performs **451 integral unit-pivot chain cancellations**. Each cancellation uses a differential entry \(\pm1\), makes an integer unimodular column change, deletes an acyclic two-generator summand, and removes the deleted generators from adjacent maps. The remaining chain complex has one zero-dimensional generator and five three-dimensional generators, with zero differentials. Thus no nonunit Smith factors or hidden integral torsion remain:

\[
H_0\cong\mathbb Z,\qquad H_1=H_2=0,\qquad H_3\cong\mathbb Z^5.
\]

As an independent enumeration check, the signed Burnside–Lefschetz sum over the four even-permutation cycle types \(1^5,1^2 3,1\,2^2,5\) has respectively \((1,20,15,24)\) elements and Lefschetz numbers \((-255,0,1,0)\). Dividing their weighted sum by 60 yields \(\chi=-4\), identical to \(27-206+425-250=-4\).

**3. Fundamental group.** The complete directed graph has a complete source; by the complete-source shellability theorem of Kozlov, as described by Jojić, \(\Delta(D_5)\simeq\bigvee^{256}S^3\). Hence \(|\Delta(D_5)|\) is connected and simply connected. Every three-cycle in \(A_5\) fixes both labels outside its support, hence fixes the simplicial vertex representing the arc between those two labels. Since the three-cycles generate \(A_5\), the normal subgroup generated by fixed-point elements is the whole group. Armstrong's orbit-space fundamental-group theorem gives \(\pi_1(|\Delta(D_5)|/A_5)=1\).

**4. Homotopy type.** The quotient is a simply connected three-dimensional CW complex with \(H_2=0\) and \(H_3\cong\mathbb Z^5\). By the degree-two Hurewicz theorem, \(\pi_2=0\); by the degree-three Hurewicz theorem, \(\pi_3\to H_3\) is an isomorphism. Choosing five maps \(S^3\to|\Delta(D_5)|/A_5\) whose Hurewicz images form an integral basis produces a homology equivalence from \(\bigvee^5S^3\). The homological Whitehead theorem upgrades this to the asserted homotopy equivalence. Because the quotient by the residual \(S_5/A_5\cong C_2\) is the full symmetric orbit space \(\bigvee^2S^3\), rational transfer identifies the fixed eigenspace of the involution on \(H_3\) with a two-dimensional space; the remaining three dimensions have eigenvalue \(-1\).

As an independent test of the action convention, it also reconstructs the full \(S_5\)-quotient cell counts \((19,121,226,125)\) and its reduced chain ranks \((1,0,0,2)\) from the same definitions. `verification_output.txt` records the actual output. The homotopy upgrade requires the cited shellability, Armstrong and Whitehead theorems, not merely the finite calculation.

## Relationship to prior work

Kozlov (2001, Section 6) studies exactly the full symmetric quotient \(|\Delta(D_n)|/S_n\), gives a stabilizer-correct flag description, computes rational top Betti numbers, and discusses possible integral torsion; it does not identify the five-label alternating-group quotient. Engström's publicly posted 2005 treatment gives directed-tree complexes and their topology, but not this \(A_5\) orbit classification. Jojić (2011 first preprint, 2012 revised) gives the labeled complete-digraph shellability and integral sphere count; these do not determine the homotopy type of its nonfree \(A_5\)-orbit space. The recent exact full symmetric five-label calculation gives \(\bigvee^2S^3\) and is used solely as a comparison: a full \(S_5\)-quotient homotopy type cannot recover the extra three alternating-quotient classes or rule out integral torsion in its index-two intermediate quotient. Note that the alternating groups \(A_k\) appearing in Kozlov's edge-label spectral sequence act on **edge orderings**, not by even permutations of the five graph-vertex labels, and therefore do not already assert this result.

## Limitations

The conclusion is specific to the complete directed graph on five labels and its even-label group; no all-size formula is claimed. The unit cancellations prove integral chain homotopy, not an explicit geometric collapse or homeomorphism. The topological conclusion additionally uses established external theorems. Unindexed equivalent work or a stronger unpublished general theorem remains a scientific-priority risk.
