The full standalone checker enumerates 95 perfect-matching facets and 50,462 nonempty faces, checks an acyclic Morse field on the entire face poset, and computes the exact integral six-to-one boundary matrix \((1,0,-1,-1,0,0)\). Critical hypotheses and every finite incidence are checked from the included program. The result is not extrapolated to other sizes.

Search nonappearance is not a proof of historical uniqueness; an unindexed equivalent calculation is a residual risk.

The rectangular-grid sphere-wedge question is a specific open-ended program in the motivating literature. The four-by-five case is a natural first odd-length example at four rows, and its complete integral homotopy type, distinct from the published four-by-four mixed-dimensional answer and the three-row family, supplies a meaningful finite benchmark. The proof identifies both the count of three-spheres and their integral attaching data, not merely a numerical Euler characteristic or an arbitrary sample.
