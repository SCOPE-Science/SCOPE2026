# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The positive-definite denominator argument, exact cofactor identities, complete classification of simple connected graphs through four vertices, and algebraic sign covering on the path of order five jointly prove the claimed minimality and exact positivity window. The independent exact polynomial reconstruction in `checker.py` is supplementary and uses no floating-point test. No statement about all larger graphs is inferred.

## Originality
PASS, with historical priority risk. Gregorio–Mugnolo, arXiv:1712.07370 (especially Sections 2 and 6), studies the same graph bi-Laplacian but its evolution semigroup rather than this positive-parameter rational resolvent; its qualitative results do not imply the two roots, full P5 nonnegativity set or minimum graph order. Benzi–Simunec (arXiv:2012.08389) develops rational graph filters, not the exact sign phase. Ulm's 1999 continuum fourth-order resolvent positivity study is relevant but addresses another operator; its full paper was not accessible. Searches in the published mathematical-record corpus, exact graph-filter aliases, and standard web indexing identified no equivalent finite-graph result. Exhaustive search cannot establish uniqueness of this result and earlier publication remains possible.

## Value
PASS. Entrywise positivity, rather than spectral contractivity, determines whether a biharmonic resolvent maps positive signals to positive outputs. The sharp first-size obstruction and explicit recovery window identify a nonmonotone sign phenomenon on the smallest natural path witness. The entire-matrix cutoff is more informative than a one-off negative entry and supplies a compact rigorous benchmark for higher-order graph smoothing.

## Limitations
Only unweighted simple connected graphs are considered. The result does not determine all five-vertex graph phase diagrams or arbitrary path lengths. The manuscript is self-reviewed only; no independent audit or historical-priority certification exists.
