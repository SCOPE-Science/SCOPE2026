The smooth characteristic-eleven dihedral genus-four pencil \(y^2=x^{10}+ax^5+1\) has exactly five marked Weil-isogeny types, with the unique elliptic fourth-power Jacobian at \(a=\pm5\). Its geometric Hasse divisor is the double of six distinct points of Frobenius types \((1,1,4)\); the exceptional fibers have Jacobian \(11\)-rank two, never zero.
