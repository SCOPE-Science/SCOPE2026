The fourteen smooth cyclic-six quartics \(X^3Z+Y^4+rY^2Z^2+Z^4=0\) over \(\mathbb F_{17}\) all have eighteen rational points but realize five distinct Jacobian Weil polynomials. Their seven geometric classes include two pairs with isogenous simple Pryms, while precisely the class \(r=\pm5\) has a superspecial cube Jacobian and is maximal over \(\mathbb F_{289}\).
