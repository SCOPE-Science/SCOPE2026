The result includes all radial weights, all positive correlation parameters, all equality planes inside the span, and a sharp fixed-angle complexity statement; it does not rely on an arbitrary sample or a marginal numerical gain.

The result concerns linear, not affine, approximation and real, not complex, Hilbert geometry. It does not claim novel Gram inversion, strong hardness, or a general attractor-width theorem. The unresolved risk is whether the special equiangular reduction was already observed as a corollary of broader classical matrix approximation theory.
