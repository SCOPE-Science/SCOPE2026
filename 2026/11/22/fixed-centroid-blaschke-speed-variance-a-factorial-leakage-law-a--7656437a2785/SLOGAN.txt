For every fixed degree \(n\ge2\), prescribing a sufficiently small nonzero complex sum \(s\) of Blaschke zeros forces a unique global minimum of boundary angular-speed variance, with sharp value \(2|s|^2+2|s|^{2n+2}/(n!)^2+O_n(|s|^{2n+4})\). Its simple zeros converge, after division by \(s\), to the roots of the reverse degree-\(n\) truncated exponential polynomial.
