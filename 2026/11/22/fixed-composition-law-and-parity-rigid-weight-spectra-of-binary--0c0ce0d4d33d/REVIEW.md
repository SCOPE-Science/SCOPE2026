# Scientific review: binary Abelian-unbordered fixed-composition law

## Correctness — PASS

The proof defines Abelian-border equivalence precisely and reduces every proper border to an inward paired difference walk. Reflection preserves the two zero-step weights individually, yielding an all-length coefficient identity. The binomial-product coefficients imply the alternating equality and strictness pattern; an explicit falling-factorial Vandermonde identity produces the variance. Stirling's formula gives the interior-density probability, with endpoint densities excluded. Direct enumeration of all words through length eighteen and independent exact killed-walk dynamic programming through forty inward pairs passed. The experiments are checks, not substitutes for the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

The 2013 Rampersad–Rigo–Salimov preprint and 2014 Goč–Rampersad–Rigo–Salimov extension were inspected in the sections on nonreturning walks and two-colored Motzkin paths. Those results derive *unweighted* enumeration and provide foundational bijections, but not the composition-specific polynomial and its shape and microcanonical consequences. The accessible 2017 Blanchet-Sadri–Chen–Hawes full text (especially Theorems 1–3) treats minimum border length and total border count, using Narayana numbers for first returns; its displayed statements do not give the fixed-weight *unbordered* distribution, although its path framework is closely related. The 2005 Harju–Nowotka work concerns ordinary rather than Abelian borders. This is a refinement of existing methods rather than a new enumeration paradigm. Possible uncatalogued equivalents and consequences hidden in stronger generating-function treatments are residual priority risks.

## Value — PASS

Fixing the number of ones is a natural source constraint for combinatorics on words and constrained binary coding. The exact polynomial supplies the complete conditional Abelian-border-free probability across every Hamming layer, not just the previously known total. Its alternating strict/equality log-concavity pattern and the density-dependent sharp constant distinguish balanced from imbalanced sources quantitatively. It is a complete natural composition classification, not an arbitrary finite slice or a numerical gain claim.

## Limits

The result is restricted to indexed binary linear words, with no claim about ordinary borders, larger alphabets, or extreme composition density asymptotics. The fixed-content polynomial is obtained using established reflection and path methods; priority remains qualified and independent validation has not occurred.
