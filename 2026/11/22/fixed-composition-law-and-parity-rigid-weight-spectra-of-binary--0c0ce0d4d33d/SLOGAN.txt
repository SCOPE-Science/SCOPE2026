The exact fixed-Hamming-weight spectrum of binary Abelian-unbordered words alternates between squared binomial coefficients and consecutive-binomial products, producing a precise parity-dependent log-concavity rigidity. Its composition-conditioned probability has the sharp asymptotic \(2\sqrt{p(1-p)}/\sqrt{\pi m}\) at length \(2m\), and its entire weight distribution has explicit finite moments.
