Deleting a prescribed number of copies of one symbol from fixed-composition words has an exact conditional first- and second-moment spectrum, determined by a rational three-variable generating function. When exactly half the source positions hold that symbol, the mean total number of distinct residual words is asymptotically \(\frac{3\sqrt3}{4}(27/32)^m\) times its sharp maximum; the maximum itself is reached by only \((m+1)/\binom{2m}{m}\) of the words.
