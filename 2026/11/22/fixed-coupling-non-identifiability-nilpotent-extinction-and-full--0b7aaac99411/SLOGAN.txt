For the matrix logistic map \(H\mapsto BH(I-H)B^*\), every rank-one coupling \(B=\sigma uv^*\) collapses all states to a one-dimensional logistic map with gain \(\sigma^2|v^*u|^2\). The *same* positive coupling matrix \(BB^*=4uu^*\) can therefore produce either complete extinction in two steps or full logistic chaos with topological entropy \(\log2\), depending only on the choice of its factor \(B\).
