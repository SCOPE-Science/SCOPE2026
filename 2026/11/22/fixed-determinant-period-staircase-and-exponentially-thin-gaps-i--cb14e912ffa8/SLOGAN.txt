For the generalized max-plus oscillator with any fixed determinant \(D=r^2>1\), every sufficiently large period has a superattracting reset window of width asymptotic to \(2r\pi^2/n^3\). Its intervening parameter gap is exponentially thinner, asymptotic to \(2\pi^2(r-1)r^{-n}/(r^2n^3)\), establishing a universal fixed-determinant period staircase near \(T=2r\).
