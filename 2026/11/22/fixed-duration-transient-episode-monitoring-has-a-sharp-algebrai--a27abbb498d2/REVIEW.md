# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The one-window interception law is an exact sum of (i) arrivals before activation which survive until activation and (ii) arrivals during activation. First-detection classes are disjoint, providing the exact total probability. The Bellman maximand is \(e^{-y}(y+1-x+xv)\); its derivative changes sign only at \(y=x(1-v)\), establishing the global optimum and uniqueness for every finite number of prescribed equal-length windows. The positive-window exponential error constant follows from a positive convergent infinite product; the zero-window algebraic boundary follows from the reciprocal-increment expansion. The proof specifies all quantifiers and endpoint behavior. An independently executable standard-library checker completed 16,596 assertions; these numerical tests supplement rather than establish the all-size result.

## Originality — PASS on inspected evidence

The closest directly related public record, *Optimal snapshot calendars for a single transient ecological presence episode* (2026), provides the zero-length snapshot recurrence and strictly increasing gaps, not the positive-window recurrence, finite cumulative idle-time limit or exponential miss law. Its complete result and proof were read and compared by implication. Yau et al. (2010) already analyze exponential stochastic events, step detection and intermittent continuous monitoring; their full text addresses stationary recurring events and periodic resource sharing, rather than a single transient presence episode and an unrestricted finite nonperiodic window calendar. Chen et al. (2024) supply the ecological motivation rather than an all-size scheduling theorem; Guillera-Arroita et al. (2014) solve a different occupied-site Poisson detection and management-cost problem. The current result is a finite-duration, one-episode claim only and makes no first-in-history guarantee. General inspection-scheduling work may contain an equivalent special case; this remains the chief historical-priority risk.

## Value — PASS

The exact optimal gap recursion gives a complete nonperiodic deployment schedule, not merely a simulation. The sharp distinction between algebraic misses for instantaneous snapshots and exponential misses for any fixed positive window duration is a structural sampling-design boundary. Its strictly positive infinite-product constant quantifies the best advantage of deliberately separated windows over uninterrupted coverage starting at time zero with identical total active time. Fixed equal window lengths and zero-horizon-penalty assumptions are important: this is a mathematical benchmark for intermittent observation of ephemeral taxa, not a calibrated passive-molecular sampler design.
