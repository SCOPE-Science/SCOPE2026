For a prescribed maximum jump \(M\), the primitive jump sets producing a given numerical semigroup form either no fiber or an explicit Boolean lattice determined by reflected minimal generators. The ordinary semigroup \(H_c=\{0\}\cup[c,\infty)\) has a sharp first-realization threshold and exactly \(2^{M-2c}\) eventually spectrally indistinguishable jump sets when \(M\ge2c\).
