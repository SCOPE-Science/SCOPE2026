For self-inverse permutations queried at individual ordered matrix cells, shattered supports are disjoint fixed-point and two-cycle queries, except that a support covering all vertices cannot include a fixed-point query. This gives exact face counts, VC and Littlestone dimensions \(n-1\) for \(n\ge2\), a rank-one top integral homology group for \(n\ge3\), and a random-support threshold \(e^{-2c^2}\).
