For self-inverse permutations queried by individual ordered matrix cells, the shattered supports are precisely vertex-disjoint fixed-point/two-cycle queries, except that a support covering every vertex cannot include any fixed-point query. This yields an exact exponential shattering series, equal VC and Littlestone dimensions \(n-1\), every facet size from \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\) through \(n-1\), a unique top integral homology generator, and the random-support threshold \(e^{-2c^2}\).
