For every fixed first coordinate \(a\geq0\), the smallest label reaching \((a,b)\) in ordered quadrant-lattice chip-firing grows as \(a!2^{b+a}/b^a\), with relative error \(O_a((\log b)/b)\). The same proof gives a nonintegral limiting staircase for the complete small-label entry count, extending the previously known boundary rows to all fixed higher rows.
