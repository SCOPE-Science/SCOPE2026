For the published studentized cross-MMD with a characteristic Gaussian kernel, fixed samples of size \(2s\) per group and growing independent-coordinate dimension have the universal null limit \(\sqrt{s/(s-1)}\,t_{2s-2}\), rather than standard normal. With four observations per group, a nominal one-sided 5% Gaussian cutoff actually rejects 18.2400% of the time in the high-dimensional limit, while a scaled-Student cutoff restores asymptotically correct size.
