For the minimal reducible three-sheet covering (one global sheet plus a connected double cover), the optimal error of an \(m\)-term arbitrary-weight Markov approximation is exactly \(4/[3(m-1)]\) for every \(m\ge2\), while the equal-weight error retains the connected three-sheet formula. This gives sharp limiting equal-weight penalties of \(3/2\) or \(2\), depending on \(m\bmod3\), despite the presence of a global section.
