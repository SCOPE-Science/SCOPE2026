For tempered fractional Laplace transport with independent gamma diffusivity and a gamma clock, the fixed-time ultimate displacement tail is proportional to a power of \(|x|\) times \(\exp[-3(\lambda x^2/(4a\beta))^{1/3}]\), with an exact finite-tempering amplitude correction \(e^{-\lambda(2H-1)\tau}\). A large displacement simultaneously selects high diffusivity and long operational time, whose standardized latent fluctuations become Gaussian with correlation \(-1/2\).
