The all-size theorem explains a counterintuitive quantum transport contrast: a localized opposite-shore pair is almost completely confined even as site degrees grow, yet balanced complete bipartite graphs transfer a uniform pair state perfectly to its opposite shore. The flat-band multiplicity, projection weight, full propagator frequency content, sharp transfer criterion, and local-transfer ceiling have substantive physical motivation. It does not infer a thermodynamic transition or physical implementation.
