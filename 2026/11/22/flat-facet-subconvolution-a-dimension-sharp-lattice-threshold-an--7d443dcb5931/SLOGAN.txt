For lattice Fourier weights \(e^{-a\|k\|_\infty}(1+\|k\|_\infty)^{-p}\) in dimension \(d\ge2\), subconvolutivity holds exactly when \(p>d\). The coordinate-axis quotient has sharp growth \(C_{d,p}N^{d-p}\) below the boundary and the universal, decay-rate-independent critical law \(2^d\log N+O(1)\), caused by the cube norm's flat equality region.
