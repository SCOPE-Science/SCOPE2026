On \(K_{2m}\) with any \(k\) independent missing edges, strict synchronous majority has an explicit binomial-sum census of two-cycle configurations by their exact number of flipping vertices. As the deleted-matching density tends to \(\alpha\), a uniformly sampled balanced configuration flips a fraction tending to \(1-\alpha/2\); only deletion of the final matching edge creates the \(2^m\) additional fixed configurations.
