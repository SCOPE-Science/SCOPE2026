A magnetic bouquet of \(m\) triangles attached at one contact to a quantum wire has exactly one in-band perfect-reflection energy for every nontrivial flux precisely when \(m\geq3\), even though it retains \(2m-2\) compact embedded modes. At weak flux its surviving narrow Fano notch has an exact, asymmetric \(O(\phi^2)\) line shape, with local full-width coefficient asymptotic to \(1/(\sqrt3m)\).
