Palindromic words form a forest under two-read single-deletion confusability: for odd length \(2m+1\), its nontrivial components are explicitly counted trees with power-of-two sizes. The exact largest two-read code has \(q^m(q+1)/2\) words for every alphabet size \(q\ge2\) and \(m\ge1\), while every even-length palindrome can be retained.
