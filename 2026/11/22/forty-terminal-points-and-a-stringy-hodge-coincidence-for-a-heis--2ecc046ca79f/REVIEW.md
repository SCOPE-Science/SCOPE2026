# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS
The fixed-set theorem and cyclic stabilizers are read from Giovenzana–Rojas–Song (2026), §5. Each of the 40 order-three subgroups accounts for a 27-point fixed set and one 27-point orbit. Linear symplectic representation theory gives the isolated type \(\frac13(1,1,2,2)\), and Reid–Tai and Verbitsky exclude a crepant resolution. Kapfer–Menet's exact fourfold cohomology and the deformation-invariant translation action combine with Lefschetz traces to give 28 invariant degree-four classes. Batyrev's formula gives the 80 age-two contributions. The exact finite-group checker agrees but does not replace the cited geometric theorems.

## Originality — PASS, with priority risk
The initiating paper establishes the 1080-point fixed locus, not the entire quotient's singular basket and stringy Hodge polynomial. Kapfer–Menet establish cohomology of the smooth covering hyperkähler variety, not of the coarse order-81 quotient. Bertini–Grossi–Mauri–Mazzon give broader terminalization theory and some relevant partial cohomological consequences; no dominating explicit complete quotient calculation was identified. The individual deductions from prior theorems are credited. Unindexed earlier quotient computations cannot be ruled out.

## Value — PASS
The canonical full-level translation quotient is a natural algebraic variety attached to a newly constructed family. Its exact singularity, Betti and Hodge invariants and the coincidence of stringy Hodge polynomials despite nonexistence of a crepant resolution provide a complete, structurally motivated geometric account. It is not a statement about arbitrary groups or non-smooth members.

## Limitations
The decomposition is rational, not integral. Completeness relies on the fixed-set and translation-cohomology theorems; finite checking alone cannot establish those.
