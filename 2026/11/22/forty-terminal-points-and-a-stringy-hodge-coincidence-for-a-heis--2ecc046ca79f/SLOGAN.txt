The natural order-\(81\) translation quotient of each smooth Heisenberg Kummer fourfold has exactly \(40\) isolated terminal singularities of type \(\frac13(1,1,2,2)\), Betti polynomial \(1+7t^2+8t^3+28t^4+8t^5+7t^6+t^8\), and no crepant resolution. Nonetheless its stringy Hodge polynomial equals that of its smooth covering fourfold: \(E_{\mathrm{st}}(X/G;u,v)=E(X;u,v)\), with \(80\) age-two twisted classes restoring the missing middle cohomology.
