The \(2295\) maximal two-isogeny kernels of a genus-four hyperelliptic Jacobian have a complete Frobenius classification controlled by the \(42\) branch-point cycle types, producing exactly \(29\) distinct zeta spectra. Two explicit curves over \(\mathbb F_5\) have equally many rational Jacobian two-torsion points (\(16\)) but respectively \(151\) and \(39\) rational maximal isotropic kernels.
