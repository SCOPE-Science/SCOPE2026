The central trinomial residues modulo five admit exactly forty of the sixty-four possible consecutive three-letter words; twenty-four words are forbidden for every index. Their complete limiting joint law has twelve words of frequency \(1/20\), four of frequency \(1/40\), and twenty-four of frequency \(1/80\), with exact finite base-five block counts and a logarithmic-squared all-cutoff discrepancy bound.
