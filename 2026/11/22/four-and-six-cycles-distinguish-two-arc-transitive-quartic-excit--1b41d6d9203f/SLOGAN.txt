For three-state, two-contact excitable dynamics, two arc-transitive four-regular graphs on twelve sites have radically different attraction spectra: the cuboctahedron has only eight isolated primitive three-cycles, while the circulant \(C_{12}(1,5)\) has nontrivial periods three, four, and six whose cycles together attract \(31386\) initial states. Both have the same sharp six-update transient cutoff, and every one of their \(3^{12}\) labelled initial configurations is counted exactly.
