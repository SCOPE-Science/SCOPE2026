The four resulting zeta numerators separate the four projective orbits even as abstract curves.

Kudo's 2024 article constructs the genus-five models and explicitly leaves isomorphism classification to later work. Kawakita's subsequent point-bound and isogeny discussion has different branch configurations and does not tabulate these 140 presentations. Nearby finite-field work computes arithmetic spectra and marked-model frequencies but stops short of classifying abstract isomorphism types. This result adds complete orbit transitivity including square-class twists, and proves the four spectra are the four exact abstract classes. No claim is made that the orbit method or the previously known point counts are novel. An unindexed equivalent orbit enumeration remains possible.
