The 140 two-twist presentations of genus-five Howe covers with all eight branch points on \(\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_7)\) collapse to exactly four \(\mathbb F_7\)-isomorphism classes, represented by projectivity orbits of sizes 14, 14, 28, and 84. Their four distinct Weil numerators completely distinguish these classes; the 84-presentation maximal stratum is a single isomorphism class.
