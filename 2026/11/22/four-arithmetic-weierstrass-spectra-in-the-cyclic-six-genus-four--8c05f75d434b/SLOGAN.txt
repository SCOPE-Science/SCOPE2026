For every odd prime power \(q\equiv2\pmod3\), the ten Weierstrass points of the smooth cyclic-six genus-four family \(y^2=x^{10}+x^7+ax^4+bx\) have exactly four Frobenius types, with explicit positive polynomial frequencies across its coefficient-marked parameter space. The same classification determines all rational branch-point and Jacobian \(2\)-torsion counts, with the latter exactly \(32,16,4,2\) in the four types.
