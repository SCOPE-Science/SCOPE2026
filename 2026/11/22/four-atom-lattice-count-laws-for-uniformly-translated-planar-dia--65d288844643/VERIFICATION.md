## Methods and limits
The algebraic proof rests on the same-parity lattice transform, two independent uniform interval-centre phases, exact joint-parity torus areas, and a uniformly bounded residual term for the binomial limit. Each probability is nonnegative and the four probabilities sum to one. The mean is checked against the area \(2R^2\).

Run `python3 checker.py` with ordinary Python 3. It checks all exact probability sums and means for many integer radii and rational phases and verifies the predicted mass function by direct rational-coordinate lattice counts at many centres. Grid comparisons are finite corroboration only; they are not an exhaustive proof for all translations or radii. No independent mathematical audit or external validation has been performed.
