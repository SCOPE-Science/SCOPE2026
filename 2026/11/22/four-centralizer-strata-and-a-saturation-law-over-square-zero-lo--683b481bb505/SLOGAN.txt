For every finite commutative local ring with a square-zero maximal ideal of residue-field dimension \(d\ge2\), the full \(2\times2\) matrix ring has exactly four centralizer cardinalities, with explicit multiplicities depending only on \(q,d\). Its commuting probability is exactly \(q^{-6}+(q^6+q^5-q^3-q^2)q^{-2d-8}\), so at fixed \(q\) it approaches \(q^{-6}\) and almost all commuting pairs have scalar reductions.
