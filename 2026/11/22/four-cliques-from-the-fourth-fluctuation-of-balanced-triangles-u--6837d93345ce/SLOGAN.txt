When the edges of any fixed simple graph receive independent fair signs, the variance of its balanced-triangle count gives the number of triangles, and its fourth cumulant additionally reveals the exact number of four-cliques. The recovery law is \(q(G)=[2\kappa_4(B_G)+\operatorname{Var}(B_G)]/3\), because four distinct balanced-triangle sign characters interact only across tetrahedral faces.
