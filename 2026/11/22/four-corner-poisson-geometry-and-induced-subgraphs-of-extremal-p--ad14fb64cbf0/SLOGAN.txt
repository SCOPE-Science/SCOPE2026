For a uniform permutation graph, the low- and high-degree extremal vertices converge jointly to a finite graph generated by four independent Poisson corner clouds, with every limiting edge determined by the relative corner coordinates. At the same degree threshold, the low and high vertex counts both approach \(\operatorname{Poisson}(t^2)\), yet their expected induced-edge counts approach \(t^4/6\) and \(t^4/3\), respectively.
