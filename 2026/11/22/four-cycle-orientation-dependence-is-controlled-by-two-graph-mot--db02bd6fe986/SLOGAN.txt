In a fair random orientation of any finite simple graph, two distinct directed-four-cycle indicators have covariance \(-1/64\) when their union is \(K_4\), \(+1/64\) when it is \(K_{2,3}\), and zero otherwise. Thus their pairwise independence has an exact forbidden-subgraph criterion and their count has an explicit two-motif variance formula; the cube nevertheless shows that pairwise independence need not extend to three-wise independence.
