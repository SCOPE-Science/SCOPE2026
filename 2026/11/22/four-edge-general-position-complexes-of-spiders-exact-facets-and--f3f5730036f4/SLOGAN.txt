For every spider, forbidding four selected edges on a geodesic gives explicit complete face and facet polynomials, with purity failing exactly when at least three arms are present and one arm has length at least three. On the equal two-edge-arm spider with \(d\ge2\) arms, the resulting complex is \(\bigvee^{d-1}S^d\), and its integral top homology is the sign-twisted arm-permutation augmentation lattice.
