# Mathematical review

## Correctness
PASS. Every concept is generated by XOR of two distinct perfect matchings on six labelled vertices. The structural proof reduces the shattering criterion to four graph types, each of which has a sixteen-pattern witness table. An exhaustive direct scan of all 32,768 supports in C++ reproduces the Python incremental enumeration and face polynomial. Two independent exact binary boundary eliminations give ranks \(1,14,91,284\), yielding the sole nonzero reduced mod-two Betti number \(391\). The complete finite verification is reproducible from the included files without network access.

## Originality
PASS within the inspected source coverage. The earliest verified public motivating work, Cambie–Girão–Kang, arXiv:1808.02352 (2018-08-07), proves general VC bounds for symmetric differences but no complete shattering support polynomial or topology for the matching family of \(K_6\). A separate published Hamiltonian-cycle shattering record concerns the restricted six-cycle family, and its conclusion does not imply the traces when all four-cycles are added. Searches for the graph-shape criterion, coefficient sequence, and homology number produced no covering result. The indexed Hamiltonian-cycle full text was not accessible; unindexed equivalent earlier results remain possible.

## Value
PASS. Pairwise symmetric differences of one-factors are canonical alternating-cycle objects, and the six-vertex complete graph is the first instance with two possible positive even-cycle lengths. The result gives a sharp structural iff criterion, a complete finite shattering complex, four natural facet types, and a nontrivial exact homology invariant beyond scalar VC dimension. These are mathematically motivated by the interaction between matching constraints, Boolean traces, and simplicial topology, rather than an arbitrary numerical slice.

## Scope and source limitations
The proof is finite, with independently executable checks for the boundary ranks. It does not establish an all-order formula or integral homotopy type. Earlier sources about general symmetric-difference VC bounds and the six-cycle subfamily are credited, and historical priority is not independently certified.
