For the Parseval harmonic frame obtained from a hyperbolic quadratic bent function on \(\mathbb F_2^{2m}\), every four-erasure Gram spectrum belongs to one of three explicitly counted classes, for all integers \(m\ge2\). The sharp retained lower frame bound is \(1/2-2^{-m}\), attained on exactly \(n^3(n-1)/192\) masks where \(n=2^{2m}\); the classical sixty dependent quadruples at \(m=2\) form only one of the three classes.
