# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof starts from the antiflag count \(A^3=cA+aK\) and its regularity companion \(A^2K=dK\). The case split derives the longest possible zero- and one-primary adjacency Jordan blocks, explicitly proves their existence in every sharp branch, and characterizes the projective exception by the matrix equality \(A^2=\operatorname{diag}(J,J)\). The polynomial transfer from adjacency blocks to reversible temporal blocks gives the exact all-iterate fixed-space expression. One executable independently constructs and checks thirteen finite incidences and 312 exact rank comparisons; a second, update-level program checks all 69,632 states on two small models. The finite checks corroborate, but are not substituted for, the proof.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** Bose's 1963 paper supplies the geometry and underlying antiflag axiom, while Vlasov (2013) supplies the general reversible construction. The closest published projective-plane wave study covers only the symmetric plane subfamily; the strongly regular graph wave theorem concerns point graphs, not bipartite incidence graphs. Prior generalized-quadrangle work treats the special case \(\alpha=1\). None of the compared statements implies the present full arbitrary-\(\alpha\) theorem, especially the mixed-parity and even-projective exceptional split. Search coverage is not a proof of absolute priority; unindexed equivalent work remains possible.

**Value — PASS.** Partial geometries form a canonical bridge between projective planes, affine nets, generalized quadrangles and geometric strongly regular graphs. The classification unifies previously separate incidence-wave clocks while detecting an exceptional 12-step regime not visible from the parity of \(\alpha,s,t\) alone. It supplies a uniform, finite-rank route to exact temporal orbit counts across potentially nonisomorphic realized geometries, without conflating geometry-parameter data with code ranks.

**Limits.** Only characteristic-two finite fields, realized partial geometries and the stated linear reversible wave are covered. Some parameter triples may have no realization. Incidence-specific binary ranks are needed for numerical orbit multiplicities. No independent mathematical validation has occurred; historical-priority risk remains.
