For any union of three positive-length intervals with two equal positive gaps and known total span \(L\), four Fourier-intensity measurements—at \(0\), at \(2\pi/L\), and the second and fourth derivatives at \(0\)—uniquely determine the union up to translation and reflection. The result isolates a rigid nonconvex family despite the known homometric ambiguities for unrestricted three-interval unions.
