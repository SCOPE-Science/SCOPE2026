# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

**Evidence and reasoning.** The exact ordered gaps in each length-\(14\) block are \((2,3,4,5)\) and \((2,4,3,5)\), ensuring identical opportunity density and gap histograms irrespective of the block word. The exhaustive eligible-offset table proves renewal at each boundary and the four threshold-band spike counts. Superexponentially growing alternating letter blocks have extreme empirical letter densities \(0\) and \(1\), and their Cesàro increments vanish, proving the full interval of asymptotic spike-density limit points. The minimal-four assertion uses a rigorous greedy exchange argument plus finite-window reflection; every permutation of at most three ordered gaps is generated by rotation and reflection. Exact rational script checks are independent consistency tests, not the infinite proof.

**Limit.** Physical threshold crossing is modeled by the on-time-scale forward projection with the first event at the origin. No assertion about all possible resets/phases or stochastic inputs is needed.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence

**Nearest evidence.** Kaymakçalan–Selçuk (2026, arXiv:2611.17219v1, Sections 5–8) establishes the arbitrary-time-scale projection recursion and homogeneous-lattice firing rate, but not failure of firing-rate convergence under perfectly convergent event density and individual gap frequencies. Peterson (2021, DOI 10.1007/s00422-021-00868-8, Introduction, Methods, Results and Discussion) studies numerically computed detection-interval distributions for inhomogeneous Poisson arrivals and random dead times, not exact deterministic gap rearrangement or aperiodic gluing. Related results on two-phase periodic and Sturmian opportunity clocks identify convergent rates under more structured clocks; their conclusions neither imply nor subsume this fixed-marginal counterexample.

**Method of comparison.** Tested equivalent descriptions as fixed refractory/nonparalyzable dead-time thinning, earliest-event greedy selection, reorderings of periodic gap lists, and first-order gap statistics versus detected-event rate. No located primary source explicitly gives the four-gap minimality, the exact \((3,4]\) threshold band and complete limit interval together. This is not a claim of exhaustive priority clearance; general combinatorial greedy optimality is pre-existing.

**Risks.** Unindexed older deterministic dead-time or scheduling literature may contain an equivalent construction; current review is same-model only.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The question is whether robust regularity of the external event clock—uniformly bounded gaps, a fixed average event rate, and a complete convergent empirical distribution of consecutive waiting times—suffices to define the long-run firing rate of a time-scale threshold neuron. The exact counterexample shows it does not. Its four-gap minimality shows the obstruction is genuinely ordering-dependent rather than a one- or two-gap artifact, and the sharp interval of reset times isolates where nonconvergence is possible within the constructed family. This is a motivated dynamical boundary result and not a tiny numerical gain, an arbitrary finite table, or a reformulation of the source's homogeneous-lattice formula.

The proof does not establish genericity, sufficiency of higher-order correlation assumptions, or behavior outside the specified reset-time interval. External mathematical validation remains outstanding.
