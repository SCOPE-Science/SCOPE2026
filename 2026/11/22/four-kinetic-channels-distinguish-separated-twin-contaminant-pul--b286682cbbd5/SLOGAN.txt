Four exactly calibrated first-order passive samplers with clearance rates \(\lambda,2\lambda,3\lambda,4\lambda\) always separate a single rectangular pollution pulse from two equal-height, equal-duration pulses separated by a positive gap: one exact cubic contrast is zero in the single case and strictly negative in the twin case. Three channels cannot guarantee that distinction, and the four-channel algebraic gap contrast has a unique maximum at an intermediate separation.
