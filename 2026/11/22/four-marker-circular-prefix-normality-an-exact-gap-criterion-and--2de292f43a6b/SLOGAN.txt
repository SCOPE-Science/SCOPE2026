For every binary necklace with exactly four ones, a periodic prefix-maximal rotation exists exactly when a longest cyclic zero gap immediately precedes a shortest one; the forbidden-necklace generating function is \(z^7/[(1-z)^4(1+z^2)(1+z+z^2)]\), and their asymptotic proportion is \(2/3\). Four markers are the sharp universal boundary for this extremal-gap criterion: it fails for a five-marker example.
