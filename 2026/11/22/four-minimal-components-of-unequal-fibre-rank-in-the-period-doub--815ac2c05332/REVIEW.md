The six-bit fibre reconstruction holds over every bilateral base point because the difference equation is a reversible recurrence in each residue class modulo six. The substitution square has four disjoint clopen phases, furnishing a globally defined invariant orbit label for the three even-prefix bits; the corresponding fibre sizes are directly \(8|\mathcal O|\). The classification resolves the first non-dyadic even binary difference offset after the known two and four-step regimes and exhibits a new qualitative phenomenon: components over the same minimal substitution base have different fibre cardinalities. It supplies a structural finite-group mechanism for nonuniform ranks, not merely a routine small finite count, and provides a concrete benchmark for a natural open-ended classification problem for odd-length permutative lifts. No generalization to other offsets or invariant measures is claimed.
