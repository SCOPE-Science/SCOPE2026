For every even square rook graph \(K_{2k}\square K_{2k}\) with \(k\ge3\), minimum identifying codes fall into exactly four full-automorphism orbits (five under permutations preserving rows versus columns) and number \(((2k)!)^2(1/k!+2/(k-2)!+1/[4(k-3)!])\). As the side length grows, almost every optimal code has one isolated edge and two oppositely oriented five-vertex path components.
