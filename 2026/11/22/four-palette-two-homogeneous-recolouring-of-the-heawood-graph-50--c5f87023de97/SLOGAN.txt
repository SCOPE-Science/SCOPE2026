The two-homogeneous named four-palette recolouring graph of the Heawood graph is exactly \(2520K_1\sqcup504T_7\), where \(T_7\) is a seven-vertex tree with degrees \(3,3,2,1,1,1,1\). Every nontrivial component has one fall three-colouring at its centre, yet \(2520\) genuine four-colour states are isolated.
