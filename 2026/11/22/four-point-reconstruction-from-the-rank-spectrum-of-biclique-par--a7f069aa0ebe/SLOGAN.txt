Within complete-bipartite graphs with both parts of size at least two, the numbers of rank-one, rank-two and rank-four elements of the partial-automorphism inverse monoid determine the graph up to isomorphism. Rank three adds no information to the first two ranks, and the infinite family \(K_{h^2,h^2}\) versus \(K_{h^2-h,h^2+h}\) proves that rank four is indispensable.
