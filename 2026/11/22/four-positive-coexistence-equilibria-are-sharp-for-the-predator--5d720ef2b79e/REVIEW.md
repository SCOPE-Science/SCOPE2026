# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The model uses strictly positive 2-by-2 reproduction and capture payoff matrices. Each strictly positive equilibrium produces a root of \(Q=K\delta(rh-\delta g)-h\) without any division by the possibly vanishing affine function \(h\). The exact identity \(rh-\delta g=u_Bh-v_Bg\) reduces the degree to at most four. For each possible root, positive predator/prey density is unique. The rational example has a nonzero quartic with four disjoint strict sign-changing intervals, and \(g/h>0\) throughout all of them. The exact checker was executed successfully. The upper bound is explicitly restricted to \(Q\not\equiv0\).

## Originality — PASS, literature-bounded

The 2026 source paper provides the model and the same equilibrium equations, describes a fifth-degree elimination, and displays up to three simultaneous positive equilibria; the sharp quartic cancellation and robust four-state rational witness are not derived there. The original 2022 model paper studies coexistence criteria. Other accessible research concerns boundary stability exchange, particular Hopf bifurcations or balanced-payoff global selection, none of which entails the present all-payoff nondegenerate maximum. A complete search of unpublished or inaccessible literature is not possible, so earlier equivalent results remain a priority risk.

## Value — PASS

Determining the maximum number of fully coexisting stationary states is a natural qualitative question for this three-population eco-evolutionary model. The exact algebraic reduction improves the equilibrium-computation problem, and the open four-coexistence regime shows a stronger multiplicity than previously displayed examples. This is not an arbitrary parameter slice: the bound holds across the nondegenerate positive-payoff family. No stability claim is made.

## Limitations

Only positive steady-state counts, not local stability or global dynamics, are established. The zero-polynomial family is outside the finite-count claim; no independent audit or exhaustive historical-priority determination has been performed.
