One stationary one-dimensional view of a four-direction harmonic swimmer has four distinct integrable caustics for \(0<\gamma/\mu<1/2\); their inner-to-outer leading-amplitude ratio is exactly \(1/[2\cos(\pi\gamma/\mu)]\). Their positions and that ratio reconstruct persistence, propulsion-to-trap length, and the view angle up to square symmetry, without assuming a two-dimensional observation.
