For two real circle Fourier frequencies, sixth-moment Poisson smoothing has four exact mode-selection regimes: the upper tone disappears sharply at relative damping \(t=1/3\) generically and \(t=2/7\) at the octave, but persists at every positive damping for third- and fifth-harmonic resonances. The complete sharp operator norm and each uniquely optimizing amplitude are given by explicit one-variable polynomials.
