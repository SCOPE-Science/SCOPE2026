For three identically fallible smoke-plume monitors constrained to symmetric sites \((-d,0,d)\), the exactly optimal spacing has four reliability regimes: widening, an unexpected fixed-spacing plateau at \(d=r\), widening again, and final contact-free spacing \(d=2r\). The plateau is a sharp effect of competing double- and triple-coverage probabilities under Gaussian uncertainty, not a field-calibrated sensor recommendation.
