The complete integral vertex-metric Rips filtrations of all eighteen single-edge faults of the Tietze graph fall into four structural edge orbits: at radius two they yield wedges of respectively two, one, four and four two-spheres, while radius three is always contractible. The orbit of an edge is determined by its two endpoint distances to the graph's unique triangle; explicit simplicial collapses and integral group-presentation certificates prove every homotopy type.
