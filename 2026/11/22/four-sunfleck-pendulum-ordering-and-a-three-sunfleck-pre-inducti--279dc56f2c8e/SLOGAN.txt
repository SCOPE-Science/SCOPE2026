For a one-state photosynthetic-induction model with equal dark gaps, the exact globally optimal order of three unequal lightflecks switches at initial induction \(s=q a\), where \(a\) is the shortest lightfleck's activation increment and \(q\) is shade retention. From zero induction, four unequal lightflecks have exactly two optimal orders for every positive induction and relaxation rate: the pendulum orders with the two shortest at the ends and the two longest in the middle.
