Four is the sharp minimum number of vertices for a planar convex polygon whose area centroid and arclength boundary centroid coincide at exactly one positive Euclidean rounding radius. Every nonrectangular isosceles-trapezoid base ratio admits an open family of such reversals; the critical height and the strictly positive linear onset slope \(z\sqrt{1-z^2}/(\pi z-3\arcsin z)\) are explicit.
