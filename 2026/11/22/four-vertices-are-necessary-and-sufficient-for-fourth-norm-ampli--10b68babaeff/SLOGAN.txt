Among all connected simple unweighted graphs, four vertices are the first size where the heat-derivative Littlewood–Paley square function can amplify an \(L^4\) norm beyond the universal \(L^2\) factor \(1/2\). The path on four vertices has an explicit witness of fourth-power ratio \(17/256\), whereas every graph on at most three vertices obeys the exact \(1/2\) bound.
