For the symmetrically filtered Euler heat step, every connected unweighted graph on at most three vertices admits an all-step stochasticity guarantee, but the four-vertex path loses a transition sign sharply at \(h=2\rho/s\), with \(2\rho^3-6\rho^2-5\rho-1=0\). The loss persists for every positive splitting strength even while the update is an unconditional Euclidean contraction for \(s\ge1/4\).
