# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The result is conditional on independent annual environments, a four-point positive yield alphabet, fixed dormant-seed survival, a strictly positive mean advantage over dormant survival, and two fixed moments. Strict log-growth concavity yields the unique optimizer and endpoint test. Vandermonde interpolation proves the exact positive derivative sensitivity to the third moment for all \(g>0\), which in turn proves the robust worst-law ordering and optimizer monotonicity, including endpoint equality cases. The quartic support polynomial proves the exact moment threshold. The complete rational witness and 5,615 exact supplementary assertions were verified using the standalone checker. Finite checks are not relied on as universal proofs.

## Originality

PASS on inspected evidence, with residual historical priority risk. Cohen (1966) already supplies the basic seed-bank log-growth model and harmonic-mean criterion, so those are excluded from the contribution. Siegmund et al. (2022 preprint) study observed and optimized germination with environmental reproductive variation; ten Brink et al. (2020) model seasonal dormancy; Sun and Boyd (2018) formulate general robust log-growth optimization. None of the inspected relevant passages states the four-support fixed-mean-and-variance exact third-moment derivative law, the all-policy same least-favorable distribution, and its support-minimal opposite-policy witness. Related mathematical research records on correlated environmental seed reserves and age-limited seed banks consider distinct stochastic objects or schedules. General divided-difference, stochastic-dominance, and robust optimization literature may imply parts of this exact classification; exhaustive global priority has not been established.

## Value

PASS. The exact moment-matched pair proves that a common ecological summary (mean reproductive success plus variance) cannot determine even whether complete versus partial germination is optimal; the sharp third-moment boundary identifies the missing information. The shared least-favorable law yields a tractable robust decision over an entire natural moment ambiguity set and the four-level minimality shows where this ambiguity first becomes possible. The theorem is a mathematical benchmark, not evidence of empirical policy improvement.

## Limits

The yields and survival are assumed known and positive, the environment is independent by year, the within-year biology is compressed to one scalar multiplier, and the robust adversary ranges only over laws on the specified four levels. No external independent validation is asserted.
