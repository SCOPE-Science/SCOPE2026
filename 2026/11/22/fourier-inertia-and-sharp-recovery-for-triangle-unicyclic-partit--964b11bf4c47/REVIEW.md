# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof is complete for every connected triangle-unicyclic simple graph and every Fourier character. The contraction step is a bijective component-counting identity; Poisson moment algebra and two recurrences reduce all infinite cases to the proved dominant positive summand bound and the explicitly computed base row. The two independent graph topologies and full set-partition transforms through nine vertices passed 34,286 exact checks, and the finite transitional inertia counts were obtained from binomial character multiplicities. The finite checker does not replace the infinite proof.

Historical priority remains subject to unindexed specializations; neither term matching nor unsuccessful search proves uniqueness.

## Value — PASS
A single triangular cycle is the smallest dependency beyond a forest in natural same-block edge profiles. The theorem completely classifies recoverability and positive definiteness over this entire first cyclic graph family and provides exact sharp stability constants, including a nontrivial transition and shape independence. Its value does not depend on extending the theorem to all unicyclic cycle lengths.
