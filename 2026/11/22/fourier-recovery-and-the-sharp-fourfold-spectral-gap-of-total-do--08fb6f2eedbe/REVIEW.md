A second 2016 paper studies graph assemblies through primary subgraphs. Full published statements for cyclic-window Fourier transforms and cycle-independent-set Fourier transforms were inspected; their cyclic constraints and single cyclic transfer factors differ materially from two independent open-path factors here. The proposed contribution is the all-character path-total-domination invertibility classification plus sharp fourfold minimal nonzero Fourier magnitude. No inspected source proved or logically implied that complete statement, but the literature inspection is bounded.

A plausible trivariate total-domination-polynomial paper by Markus Dod (Discrete Applied Mathematics 209, 2016, 92–101; DOI 10.1016/j.dam.2015.10.008) was identified. Only bibliographic information was accessible; institutional full-text access was unavailable. Therefore it is not classified as NOT_COVERING. An earlier equivalent multivariate specialization remains the leading priority risk. The close inference from classical path polynomials is an additional originality risk, explicitly assessed against the distinct natural path-total-domination object and the two-factor sharp gap.
