For the fixed-point incidence profiles of all labelled involutions, Boolean counting convolution is invertible in every order except two and three: even-order Walsh multipliers are strictly positive, while odd-order multipliers have a sharp central sign change. In every invertible order at least four, the exact least multiplier magnitude is the central moment \(\mathbb E[(Z^2-1)^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}]\) of a squared standard Gaussian, yielding a sharp \(\ell^2\) condition number.
