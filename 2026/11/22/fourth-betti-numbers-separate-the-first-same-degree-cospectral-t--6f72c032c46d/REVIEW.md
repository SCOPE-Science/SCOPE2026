# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. Leaf attachment with canonical tree isomorphism codes exhausts every unlabeled tree of size at most nine; the standard tree matching-polynomial recurrence computes the adjacency characteristic polynomials. The Chevalley–Eilenberg differential is given on generators, its square vanishes, and support decomposition makes the exact rational rank computation complete. The separate modular calculation and degree-by-degree chain checks corroborate the result, without replacing rational proof. The output records all relevant ranks and dimensions; Nomizu's theorem supplies the de Rham interpretation.

## Originality
PASS with residual priority uncertainty. The motivating 2026 primary work (arXiv:2611.17014) concerns path graphs and cannot directly cover the branching cospectral tree pairs. Aldi et al. (arXiv:2402.14188) gives general structural methods, third-degree formulas and all-degree star formulas, but the accessible material does not state or imply the specific fourth-degree separation at the minimal same-degree cospectral tree size. A separate indexed tree-spectrum census concerns **generalized** spectral equivalence rather than a cohomological distinguishing invariant; its implication does not include fourth Betti numbers. The closest related paper's entire revised full text could not be inspected, and equivalent unindexed computations could exist.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The object and invariant are natural: nilmanifolds arising functorially from graphs and their de Rham Betti numbers. The theorem gives both a sharp finite minimality threshold and all colliding pairs, then shows that fourth cohomology detects topology invisible to two standard graph invariants even though lower Betti numbers agree. This is a structural counterexample and a reproducible benchmark for graph-derived nilmanifold cohomology, not an arbitrary isolated arithmetic number.

## Limits
The result is an exact bounded tree census, not a classification of larger graphs, a complete Poincaré polynomial calculation, or an integral-cohomology statement. Priority and generalization beyond the specified four graphs remain open to external inspection.
