# Mathematical review

## Correctness
**PASS (same-model).** Under the explicitly stated graph class and Euclidean normalization, the fourth intrinsic volume is an exact weighted forest sum. Incidence-column Gram determinants have the tree-component-size product by the matrix-tree theorem. All possible four-edge subgraphs are partitioned into five forest types and four-cycles. Five separately proved finite combinatorial counts yield the coefficient \(-\alpha\) of the four-cycle number, with \(\alpha=8\sqrt3+4\sqrt5-10-8\sqrt2>0\) established by rational radical bounds. The first three volume formulas follow from the one-, two-, and three-edge census; the proof does not extrapolate from numerical cases. The self-contained checker directly verifies integer Gram determinants and forest-type counts for ten graphs, including a matched cubic pair with distinct four-cycle counts.

## Originality
**PASS (best-knowledge, with priority risks).** In *Deformed Graphical Zonotopes* (Padrol–Pilaud–Poullot; arXiv:2111.12422; first version 2021-11-24), the underlying graph zonotopes and triangle-free deformation cones are studied, including a four-cycle example, but no fourth Steiner intrinsic-volume formula is stated. In *Isoperimetric Problems for Zonotopes* (Joós–Lángi; arXiv:2206.03204; first version 2022-06-07), Corollary 1 supplies the established exterior-product intrinsic-volume identity; the graph-specific regular-four-cycle recovery is not given there. The closest recorded result on **third** intrinsic volumes of nearly complete graphical zonotopes uses three-edge forests and a different graph class. A recorded **all-ranks** formula for \(K_{2,q}\) is confined to a family outside the present dimension-at-least-four regular class, except for its nonqualifying four-cycle member. A recorded cubic **directional-width moment** theorem detects triangles, not the claimed fourth Steiner-volume four-cycle statistic. Actual full mathematical statements, rather than titles alone, were compared. A broader weighted-forest formula could contain the result implicitly, so historical priority is provisional rather than certified.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** The fourth Euclidean Steiner intrinsic volume is a natural, rotation-invariant polyhedral measurement, not an arbitrary assigned graph statistic. The result gives a strict *first-detectable* hierarchy within all fixed-order, fixed-degree connected triangle-free regular graphs: three successive intrinsic volumes are geometrically indistinguishable, whereas the fourth measures the number of squares with a universal nonzero step. The square-count distinction is realized by natural 14-vertex bipartite cubic graphs. The nontrivial five-class forest census supplies an exact infinite-family metric statement, not a finite table or a restatement of the forest determinant formula.
