For the natural non-isometric \(2\)-isometric unilateral shifts, the difference between \(k\)-fold arithmetic-mean and Aluthge iterations belongs exactly to the Schatten classes \(\mathcal S_p\) with \(p>1/4\). Its entire singular-weight profile is asymptotic to \(k^{-3}/[32(t+1/2)^4]\), giving a universal \(k^{-2}/12\) trace-norm law and \(k^{-3}/2\) operator-norm law.
