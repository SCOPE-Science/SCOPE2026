On the conservative boundary of a studied sine-driven three-dimensional flow, exact invariant cylinders carry a nontrivial equilibrium diagram: at \(g=kb^2/3\), two off-axis saddles emerge with the sharp fourth-root law \(|x|\sim(45(R^2-kb^2)/(2kb^2))^{1/4}\). On the neighboring lower-\(g\) side, a curved fold creates two symmetric equilibrium pairs, one elliptic and one hyperbolic, while the full cylinder dynamics admits a second exact first integral.
