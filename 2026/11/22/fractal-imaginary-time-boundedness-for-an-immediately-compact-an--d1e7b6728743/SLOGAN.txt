An immediately compact analytic Hilbert-space semigroup can have a dense, nonclosed group of bounded imaginary times whose Hausdorff dimension is exactly \(1-\gamma\) for \(0<\gamma<1\), zero but uncountable at \(\gamma=1\), and countable above that threshold. An explicit factorial-spectrum block construction identifies every complex boundedness point, not merely the open holomorphy region.
