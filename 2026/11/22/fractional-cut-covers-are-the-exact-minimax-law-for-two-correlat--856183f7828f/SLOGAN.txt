For any known sparsity graph, the minimum possible worst-case variance of two arbitrarily coupled full Rademacher trace probes is exactly \(4(1-1/\operatorname{fcc}(G))\) per unit unordered off-diagonal edge energy, where \(\operatorname{fcc}\) is the classical fractional cut-covering number. An optimal randomized cut attains this bound against every matrix in the graph class, while deterministic sign flips are robustly suboptimal on every nonbipartite graph.
