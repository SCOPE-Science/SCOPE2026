With uniform point weights, **every** block-weight maximizer for the Schur multiplier of a balanced \(2\)-design is exactly a fractional \(2\)-design preserving all pair marginals. For the complete \(k\)-uniform design, the exact optimizer-face dimension is \(\binom vk-\binom v2\) when \(2\le k\le v-2\), and zero when \(k=v-1\).
