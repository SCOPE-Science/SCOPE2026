For a positive-semidefinite signed network with repulsive cliques, fractional powers \(L^\alpha\) become genuine Markov generators exactly below an explicit, generally subunit critical exponent \(\alpha_*\). Above that threshold the heat kernel has a unique sharp positivity-recovery horizon, whose onset is linear in \(\alpha-\alpha_*\) while the worst \(\ell^\infty\)-norm excess is quadratic; both constants are explicit.
