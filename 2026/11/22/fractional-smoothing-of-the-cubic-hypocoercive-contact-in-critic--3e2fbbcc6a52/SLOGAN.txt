At the exact dissipativity threshold of every finite Jordan chain, fractional Mittag--Leffler propagation has a sharp \(t^{2\alpha}\) norm deficit governed by the variance of the random subordination clock, while ordinary semigroup propagation instead has a sharp cubic deficit. A uniform two-parameter expansion gives the exact crossover at \(1-\alpha\asymp t^{\alpha}\), with dimension-dependent coefficients in closed form.
