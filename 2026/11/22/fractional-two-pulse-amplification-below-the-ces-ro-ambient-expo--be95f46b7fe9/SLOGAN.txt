For every \(1\le p<\infty\), \(0<q<p\), and \(0<\alpha<1/p\), the polynomially weighted backward shift is \(q\)-absolutely Cesàro bounded, but its optimal averaging constant is **strictly larger** than the best single-coordinate test \(1/(1-\alpha q)\). The strict amplification is generated by just two nearby coordinates and has an exact positive beta-function boundary-layer coefficient with exponent \(1-\alpha q\).
