# Review of the free-algebra growth and basis-rigidity theorem

## Correctness

PASS. For any positive finite rank, the multiplication table yields the first-outside-\(\{2,3\}\) product rule, hence an exact and injective normal form for multiplicative words. The content-detecting evaluation at \(2\) versus \(3\) proves the all-rank generating-tuple rigidity without a finite-rank extrapolation. The explicit rank-three certificate gives \(778\) distinct witnessed functions, including generators, and checks closure under both operations on every assignment. Rank-one and rank-two enumerations are exhaustive.

## Originality

PASS, with residual historical-priority risk. Ma--Yue's primary paper [1] supplies the semiring, its word-evaluation rule, and relevant subvariety theory, but the inspected material does not give the exact free-rank spectrum, full-support counts, or all-rank automorphism classification. Its earlier six-element analogues [2,3] involve different algebras. The most similar available mathematical-research record concerns a multiplicatively idempotent free semiring, whereas this one is not multiplicatively idempotent. Word normal-form enumeration is a close deduction from [1] and is not advertised as an independent invention. Unlocated prior general theorems or unpublished calculations remain possible.

## Mathematical value

PASS. This is a natural, uniquely motivated free-object census for the then least-order finite generator of a nonfinitely based limit variety. Three-generated free algebras occur explicitly in [1]'s structural proofs, so their exact \(778\)-element size and \(721\) full-support classes provide usable, reproducible information about the equational complexity at that rank. All-rank rigidity additionally gives a structural classification of generating tuples and automorphisms rather than an isolated small-number count.

## Limitations and sources

The finite free-algebra cardinalities stop at rank three; there is no conjectured closed all-rank formula. Primary text [1], including its tables and the most relevant sections, was inspected. Sources [2] and [3] were examined in relevant introductory/material sections. Related free-semimodule work [4] concerns different free objects. Literature and database searches may miss unindexed or unpublished results. Independent verification is not claimed.
