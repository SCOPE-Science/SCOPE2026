For every finite graph whose deletion of any vertex remains connected, the geodesic general-position complex of its one-leaf corona has a **free fundamental group** of rank equal to the number of original vertices plus the number of edges with no equidistant third vertex. The rank doubles from \(n\) to \(2n\) between odd and even sunlet cycles and becomes \(|V(G)|+|E(G)|\) for two-connected bipartite base graphs.
