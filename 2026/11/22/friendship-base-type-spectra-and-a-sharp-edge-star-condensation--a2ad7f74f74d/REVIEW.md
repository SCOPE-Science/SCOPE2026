## Originality — PASS, provisional
The Henson and Conant primary articles supply homogeneous extension theory and the logical classification framework, but not the friendship-base all-arity edge-threshold concentration theorem. A previous computation of the friendship-base unary compatibility matrix covers unary and pair-feasibility facts, not three-or-more-point star condensation. Published work on disjoint maximal parameter cliques gives a different shared-neighborhood geometry and only the first three exponential layers. The classical Kruskal–Katona theorem implies the weighted small-star bound, but does not alone supply the Henson transfer, its exponential bases, or the conditional type distribution. Search comparisons across aliases and stronger coverage found no prior implication of the full claim. Historical priority remains provisional, particularly for unindexed enumeration literature.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Conditional rare-event structure is a motivated refinement of finite-base orbit spectra: observing at least \(k\) internal adjacency relations does not merely force a sparsity threshold, but almost surely forces the unique maximal-overlap motif, a \(k\)-edge star. This holds for an entire hierarchy \(1\le k\le r-1\), with sharp exponential rates and multiplicities, and depends on the shared-hub geometry of a natural homogeneous-structure parameter family. It supplies higher-order information not recoverable from a unary compatibility matrix alone and contrasts with the disjoint-clique parameter regime. The result is not claimed as a general new Kruskal–Katona theorem.
