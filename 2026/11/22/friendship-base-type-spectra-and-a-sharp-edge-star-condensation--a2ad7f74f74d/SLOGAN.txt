In the \(K_4\)-free Henson graph, ordered tuple types over a named friendship graph have an exact finite transfer formula. As the number of triangular petals grows, conditioning a uniformly chosen \(r\)-tuple type to have at least \(k\) internal edges forces those edges onto a \(k\)-edge star, with sharp exponential probability \(L_{r,k}(3/4+(1/4)(3/4)^k)^m\) for every fixed \(1\le k\le r-1\).
