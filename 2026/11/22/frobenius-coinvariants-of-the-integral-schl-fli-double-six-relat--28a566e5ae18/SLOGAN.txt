For a cubic surface obtained by blowing up a Frobenius-stable sextuple of plane points, the integral Schläfli double-six relation quotient reduces modulo two to the permutation module on the fifteen pairs of the sextuple. Its Frobenius coinvariants are exactly \(\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z\oplus(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^{o_2-1}\), where \(o_2\) counts Frobenius orbits on those pairs.
