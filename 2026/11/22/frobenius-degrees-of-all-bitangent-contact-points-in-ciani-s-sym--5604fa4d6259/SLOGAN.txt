For every smooth Ciani symmetric quartic pencil member over a finite field of characteristic at least five, the reduced support of all bitangent contacts has an explicit Frobenius cycle decomposition with degrees only \(1\), \(2\), and \(4\). Exactly \((q-\chi(-1))/4-\mathbf1_{q\equiv3\pmod8}\) marked parameters require degree-four contacts, although all the bitangent lines themselves split over the quadratic extension.
