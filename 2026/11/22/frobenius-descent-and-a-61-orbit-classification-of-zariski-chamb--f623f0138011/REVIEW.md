# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The claim is about five setwise Frobenius-invariant negative-curve supports and one full-group orbit count. The full set of thirteen negative curves follows from anticanonical nefness, adjunction, multiplicity identities, Cauchy–Schwarz and Bézout. The exact Schur-complement criterion provides a separate classification of permissible negative supports. Two independent standalone programs reconstruct the complete finite universe by different criteria, agree on the published geometric polynomial of 726 supports, then agree on all five fixed-support polynomials and the 61-orbit Burnside/direct enumeration results. The passage from invariant support to an arithmetic chamber uses a descended ample divisor plus a large multiple of the invariant support. The proof does not infer an infinite statement from unproved finite sampling.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The classification is complete for the natural five arithmetic forms of the maximally nodal cubic. It refines the geometric Zariski-chamber invariant by an explicit arithmetic descent invariant for big divisor classes, and distinguishes two Frobenius actions with equal cycle partitions on all thirteen negative curves (66 versus 74 invariant chambers). Each form occurs over every finite field in the stated characteristic range. This is a geometrically intrinsic and motivated arithmetic question, not an arbitrary parameter slice.
