For the characteristic-five smooth proper crepant resolution of the Burek–Chmiel–Cynk–Oczko elliptic self-product, the entire Hasse–Weil zeta function is determined by \(24\) positive and \(18\) negative degree-two Frobenius eigenvalues \(\pm5\). The resulting exact formula counts its points over every extension \(\mathbb F_{5^r}\), distinguishing the self-product from the further projective Kummer quotient.
