The 76 geometric Zariski chambers of every smooth degree-five del Pezzo surface carry exactly seven possible Frobenius orbit profiles, with respectively 76, 24, 8, 7, 3, 2, or 1 chambers descending to the ground finite field. The complete profiles determine the zeta functions of the finite étale chamber schemes; notably, a four-cycle supports a rational non-nef chamber on four conjugate exceptional curves despite having no individually rational exceptional curve.
