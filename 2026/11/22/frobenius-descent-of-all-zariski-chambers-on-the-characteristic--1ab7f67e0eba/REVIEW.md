# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The special plane blowup has precisely fourteen negative curves: anticanonical nefness, adjunction and the finite Fano incidence rule exclude all other possible \((-1)\) and \((-2)\) curves. Every selected curve intersection submatrix is checked exactly against the degree-at-most-one support criterion, and the Galois-invariant-support implication is proved using a ground-field ample divisor and an invariant reduced support. The 168 collineations and 16,384 subsets are exhaustively enumerated; direct fixed-set counts agree with independent vertex-orbit-union counts and with Burnside's lemma. The 7-cycle rigidity follows separately from two transitive vertex orbits.

## Originality — PASS (historical priority provisional)
Bauer–Funke–Neumann (arXiv:0912.0958) and Bauer–Schmitz (arXiv:1204.4373) give the general Zariski-chamber correspondence and algorithms; their inspected proofs do not include the characteristic-two Fano-plane Frobenius tables. The earlier published result *Zariski chambers of the characteristic-two Fano-plane blowup* already proves the entire 2,257-chamber geometric count and induced-matching characterization. Those are acknowledged, not reclaimed. The current new part classifies all collineation-twist rational chamber polynomials and the global 54-orbit partition. Accessible historical findings on the same graph's Picard tori and critical group do not imply this chamber classification,. Search coverage is finite; possible earlier unindexed equivariant enumerations remain a risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This is a natural complete arithmetic classification of negative-support strata on a distinguished weak del Pezzo surface. The order-seven three-chamber collapse and complete support-size distributions describe rationally defined regimes of big linear series that the previously known geometric total does not reveal. The 54-orbit partition provides a global symmetry invariant; none of these quantities is chosen as an arbitrary graph slice.

## Limitations
Only \(\operatorname{GL}_3(\mathbb F_2)\)-twists over fields of characteristic two are classified. This is not a classification of all forms or individual divisor classes. The polynomial of the identity twist and the fourteen-curve geometry are prior work. The exact finite calculation supports but does not replace the geometric proof, and historical priority remains provisional.
