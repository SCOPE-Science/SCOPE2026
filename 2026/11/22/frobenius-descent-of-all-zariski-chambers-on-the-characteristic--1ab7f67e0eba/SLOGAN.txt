For the characteristic-two Fano-plane seven-point blowup, the rational Zariski chambers of all \(\operatorname{GL}_3(\mathbb F_2)\) twists have exact counts \(2257,209,25,21,3\) according to Frobenius order \(1,2,3,4,7\). In the seven-cycle case only the nef chamber and the chambers supported on all seven curves of one bipartition descend; the \(2257\) geometric chambers form precisely \(54\) collineation orbits.
