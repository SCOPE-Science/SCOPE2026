For any ordered pair of smooth finite-field conics with two distinct double contacts, the \(n\)-vertex Poncelet cycles form a split or nonsplit one-dimensional torus with exactly \(q^m-\epsilon^m\) cycles defined as sets over \(\mathbb F_{q^m}\). Frobenius rotates their vertices when the tangent multiplier and contact divisor have matching quadratic types, but reflects them otherwise, yielding a complete explicit distribution of cycles with \(0,1,2\), or \(n\) rational vertices.
