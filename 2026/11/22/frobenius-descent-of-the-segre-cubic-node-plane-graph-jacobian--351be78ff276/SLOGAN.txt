The 25-vertex node–plane graph of the Segre cubic has integral graph Jacobian \((\mathbb Z/2)^6\oplus(\mathbb Z/10)^2\oplus\mathbb Z/20\oplus\mathbb Z/40\oplus(\mathbb Z/80)^4\), and all eleven finite-field Frobenius types have explicitly classified integral fixed and coinvariant groups. The number of rational graph-Jacobian classes ranges from \(160\) for type \((3,2,1)\) to \(2^{29}5^8\) in the split case.
