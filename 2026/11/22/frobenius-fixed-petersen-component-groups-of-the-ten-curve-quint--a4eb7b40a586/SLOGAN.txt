For the complete ten-curve stable boundary of every quintic del Pezzo surface over a finite field, the Frobenius-fixed Petersen critical group is classified by all seven \(S_5\) cycle types: \(C_2\oplus C_{10}^3\), \(C_{10}^2\), \(C_2\oplus C_{10}\), \(C_5\), \(0\), \(C_2\), and \(C_5\), respectively. In particular, the \((3,2)\)-twist has trivial rational Néron component group whenever a proper regular semistable smoothing with unit node thicknesses exists.
