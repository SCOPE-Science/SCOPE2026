The nine-line \(2A_1\) quartic del Pezzo resolution has five Frobenius types fixing exactly \(221,61,17,23,5\) Zariski chambers, and its full eight-element incidence group has \(52\) chamber orbits. Two realizable involutions have identical splitting cycle partitions on the eleven negative curves but fix \(17\) versus \(23\) chambers, so curve splitting alone does not determine arithmetic chamber descent.
