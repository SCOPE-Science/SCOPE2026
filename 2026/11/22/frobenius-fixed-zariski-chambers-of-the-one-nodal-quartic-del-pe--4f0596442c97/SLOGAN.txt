The unique-root stabilizer \(S_4\times C_2\) of the one-nodal quartic del Pezzo resolution has ten Frobenius types, with an exact fixed-Zariski-chamber distribution for each type and precisely \(30\) geometric chamber orbits. In the most restrictive type, only three of the \(285\) geometric chambers descend: the nef chamber, the exceptional \((-2)\)-support chamber, and one four-curve support chamber.
