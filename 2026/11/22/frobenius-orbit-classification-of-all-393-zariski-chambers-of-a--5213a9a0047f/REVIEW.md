# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The exceptional-curve classes and intersection form yield exactly the negative supports as pairwise disjoint sets. For every invariant support, the divisor \(-K_S+2B\) has a direct nef-positive and negative Zariski decomposition, proving it represents a genuine rational chamber. Two completely different integer representations (Hamming-bit affine action and Picard-lattice reflections) each exhaustively generated \(1920\) Weyl elements, identified eighteen signed types and obtained the same cycle profiles on \(393\) supports. The finite computation is exhaustive for the finite group; the all-field theorem rests on the geometric chamber and Frobenius argument.

## Originality
PASS, provisional. Bauer--Funke--Neumann already supply the geometric chamber count and algorithm; the signed Weyl action and its arithmetic meaning are also known. The nearest published quartic del Pezzo results tabulate complement point counts and line/node/branch incidence, not Frobenius cycles on all negative-definite supports. An earlier unindexed induced-permutation-character table remains a priority risk, and some prior text was only partially accessible.

## Mathematical value
PASS. A complete eighteen-type classification determines which stable-base-locus strata descend and all their extension-field orbit statistics. Some nonsplit types admit non-nef rational chambers although no geometric exceptional curve is individually rational. This is a natural surface-geometric arithmetic invariant, not merely the known 393 count.

## Limits
The Weyl table is conditional on the arithmetic type realized by the surface; no universal realization claim for small fields. The geometric chamber count and known representations are not claimed as new; priority remains provisional, and this is not independent validation.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
