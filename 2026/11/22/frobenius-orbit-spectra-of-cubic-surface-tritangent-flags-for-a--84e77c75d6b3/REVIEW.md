# Review: Frobenius orbit spectra of cubic-surface tritangent flags

## Correctness — PASS
For a smooth marked cubic obtained by blowing up a Frobenius-stable sextuple in general position, the classical exceptional/conic/pair-line labelling and the anticanonical intersection form give all 27 lines and the exactly 30 ordered-pair and 15 perfect-matching tritangent planes. These lists are equivariant under a permutation of the six centres. The flag set therefore has 135 elements. The fixed-centre, fixed-pair and invariant-matching formulas derive fixed-point counts on all three sets, while Möbius inversion determines the complete cycle profiles. The independent direct orbit construction and all 720 labelled permutations agree with every displayed row. The line-free/rational-plane boundary follows from the absence of fixed centres and fixed unordered pairs. A finite computer calculation is not used as a substitute for the geometric identification.

## Originality — PASS (historical-priority limitation)
Polo-Blanco–Top (2008), Lemma 2.2, gives the 30-plus-15 classical list; neither that list nor the standard cubic-surface line geometry is asserted as new. Elsenhans–Jahnel (2010) treats Galois-stable cubic-surface structures and includes particular Frobenius/involution comparisons. Das (2020), *Arithmetic statistics on cubic surfaces*, gives a more general Galois-conjugacy framework and aggregate rational-tritangent statistics. These sources could make the finite table derivable in principle, but the inspected material did not supply the simultaneous all-eleven cycle census of 27 lines, 45 planes and 135 incidence flags together with the rational-plane constituent-line distribution. Other recorded finite-configuration computations concern different incidence schemes. Historical exhaustive priority is not claimed; general character tables or unindexed work remain a specific risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The tritangent-plane and line–plane incidence schemes are canonical finite geometric objects associated to a marked cubic surface. Their exact Frobenius spectra determine every extension-field count and finite étale zeta function. The explicit descent separation for cycle types \((6)\) and \((3,3)\) detects rational tritangent planes with no individually rational lines and refines the previously studied number of rational tritangent planes. These are complete natural arithmetic invariants, not an arbitrary numerical slice.

## Scope and remaining risks
The result is confined to good-characteristic smooth six-point blowups. It does not classify arbitrary \(W(E_6)\) arithmetic Frobenius classes, does not establish per-field realizability for every row, and does not claim the published broader framework is insufficient to derive these counts. Relevance of historically inaccessible or unindexed tables cannot be excluded. The source comparisons establish best-knowledge originality rather than independent certification.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
