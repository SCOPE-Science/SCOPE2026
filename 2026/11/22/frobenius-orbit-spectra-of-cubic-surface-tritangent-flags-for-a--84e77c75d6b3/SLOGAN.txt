For cubic surfaces obtained by blowing up a Frobenius-stable sextuple of points, the complete Frobenius cycle spectra of the 27 lines, 45 tritangent planes, and 135 line–plane flags are classified by the eleven partitions of six. A surface in this class has no rational lines yet has rational tritangent planes exactly for sextuple cycle types \((6)\) and \((3,3)\), yielding respectively one and three such planes, none containing a rational geometric line.
