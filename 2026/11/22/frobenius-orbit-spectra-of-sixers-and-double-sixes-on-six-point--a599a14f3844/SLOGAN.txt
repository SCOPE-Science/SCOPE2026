For a smooth cubic surface obtained by blowing up a Frobenius-stable six-point configuration in a plane, the 72 geometric sixers and 36 double-sixes have explicit eleven-case Frobenius orbit spectra determined only by the six-point cycle type. This classifies all rational plane blowdowns and the rational double-sixes whose halves are exchanged by Frobenius, including examples with two rational blowdowns but no individually rational exceptional lines.
