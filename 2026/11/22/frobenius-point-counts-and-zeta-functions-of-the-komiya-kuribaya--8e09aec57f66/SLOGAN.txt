The reduced union of all 28 bitangents to the Komiya–Kuribayashi plane quartic has an explicit zeta function over every finite field of characteristic at least seven, with seven geometric incidence-reduction primes but only a small number of Frobenius point-count regimes. At characteristic seven 24 additional geometric triple crossings appear, yet the ground-field union still has exactly \(4q+1\) rational points; the change is detected over the quadratic extension.
