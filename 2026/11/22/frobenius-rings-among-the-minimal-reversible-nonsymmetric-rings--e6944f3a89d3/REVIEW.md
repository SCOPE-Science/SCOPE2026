The filtered graded lemma follows by taking a nonzero lowest radical term and a complementary-degree factor with nonzero top product. The 186-class partition and the names of the exceptions are taken from the 2026 primary source; the complete 17-model graded pairing computation is independently executable in the included standalone script. Direct computation of all 19 characteristic-two quotient socles agrees, with only ring 7 having dimension two. The characteristic-four exception follows from its two-dimensional terminal radical layer. This proves exactly the claims stated, contingent on the source classification.

## Originality — PASS, historically qualified

The closest full-text source, *All unital reversible nonsymmetric rings of order 256* (arXiv:2611.19962v1, 2026-11-15), provides the isomorphism classification, Hilbert vectors, and two nonduo indices, but does not establish their Frobenius structure or generating-character counts. The earlier *Minimal Reversible Nonsymmetric Rings* by Szabo (arXiv:1710.07338) proves the order lower bound and gives examples, without a complete Frobenius census. The present statement is not a simple rephrasing of the duo result: in general duo and Frobenius are distinct, and the uniform graded-perfect-pairing calculation supplies the missing implication. Comparisons of the precise object and invariant were made through the cited full texts, web searches for Frobenius, quasi-Frobenius, self-injective, and socle aliases, and published mathematical-result records. No matching coverage was identified. Residual risk: unindexed or implicit older ring-character statements.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Frobenius duality is a fundamental structural property of finite rings, controlling generating characters, nondegenerate bilinear forms and code-theoretic duality. The determination of this property for the complete newly discovered 186-ring family has a natural interpretation and identifies exactly two obstructions, not an arbitrary micro-enumeration. The proof establishes a reusable bridge from graded top-degree pairings to Frobenius duality in mixed-characteristic local rings. The numerical coincidence with the independently classified duo exceptions is structurally informative. It does not rely on unsupported future applications.
