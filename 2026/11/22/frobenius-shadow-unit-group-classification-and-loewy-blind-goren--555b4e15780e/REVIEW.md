# Scientific scope and comparison

The proof computes the identical group structures directly for the specified exponent pairs and distinguishes the rings by the highest total degree of a standard monomial. The general monomial unit-group calculation is not asserted as a contribution: it also follows from principal-unit factorization along primitive rays and classical Witt-vector decomposition. The inspected multirelative K-theory and elementary abelian unit-group sources do not give the simultaneous constraints demonstrated by the pair: two generators, equal dimension, Gorenstein, same non-elementary principal-unit group, and distinct Loewy lengths over each odd-characteristic finite field. This is a reconstruction counterexample, not a classification of collisions. Unindexed or differently formulated examples remain a priority risk.
