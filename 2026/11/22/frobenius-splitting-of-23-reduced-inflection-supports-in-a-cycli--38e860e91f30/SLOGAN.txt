For every finite field of characteristic at least five, the 23 reduced flex supports of a generic cyclic-six genus-three quartic have their complete Frobenius orbit partition determined by a quartic factorization, a sextic factorization, and explicit cubic-residue tests. Over \(\mathbb F_7\), the quadratic-twist parameters \(r=3\) and \(r=4\) have seven rational flexes each but different reduced-flex zeta functions.
