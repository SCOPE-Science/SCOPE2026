For the standard Bring curve in characteristic at least eleven, the 120 tritangent planes and 60 stalls all have Frobenius orbit length equal to the order of Frobenius on the roots of \(t^3+2t^2+3t+4\). Its 120 other tritangent contact points have orbit length equal to the least common multiple of that order and the quadratic splitting degree of \(-15\), yielding all three finite-configuration zeta functions and a sharp extra obstruction to fully rational contacts.
