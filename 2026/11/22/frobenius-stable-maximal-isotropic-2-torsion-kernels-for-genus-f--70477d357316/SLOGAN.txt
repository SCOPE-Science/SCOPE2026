The \(2295\) maximal isotropic order-\(16\) two-torsion kernels of a genus-four hyperelliptic Jacobian have an explicit, complete Frobenius-descent table indexed by all \(42\) cycle partitions of its ten branch points. A ten-cycle descends exactly one polarized degree-\(16\) kernel despite having no nonzero rational two-torsion, whereas a nine-cycle with one fixed branch point descends none.
