For every prime power \(q\), a normal singular degree-\(q+1\) space-filling hypersurface with alternating-matrix rank \(2r\ge4\) has an exact \(q\)-Frobenius-thickened linear Jacobian vertex, a one-blow-up resolution of discrepancy \(2r-q-2\), and a closed rank-stratified census. Its log-canonical and canonical thresholds are sharp, yet it is never \(F\)-pure along that vertex.
