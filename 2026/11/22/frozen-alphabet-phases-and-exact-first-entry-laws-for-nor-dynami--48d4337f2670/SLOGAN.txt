Incoming-neighbor NOR on every directed Kautz word graph has exactly \(q+1\) frozen alphabet phases and one global two-cycle, with a sharp word-length entrance clock and an explicit first-entry distribution. Under independent Bernoulli initialization, a single critical bias retains frozen-phase probability \((q+1)p_*^2\) at arbitrarily large depth, whereas every other fixed bias yields vanishing frozen-phase probability.
