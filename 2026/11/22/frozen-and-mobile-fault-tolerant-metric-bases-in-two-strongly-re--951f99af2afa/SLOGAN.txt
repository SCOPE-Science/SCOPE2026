The Shrikhande and \(4\times4\) rook graphs have identical strongly regular parameters and both require six landmarks to distinguish every pair of vertices after any one landmark fails, but have respectively \(448\) and \(1600\) optimal configurations. All Shrikhande optima are frozen under one-landmark replacements yet become connected under exactly two simultaneous replacements (diameter \(3\)), while the rook optima already form a connected one-replacement graph (diameter \(6\)).
