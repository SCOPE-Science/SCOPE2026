# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The exact graph has fourteen labelled vertices and twenty-one edges, with closed-neighbourhood separation tested on every incidence. The Fano-pair argument rules out three colours, agreeing with the existing property-B criterion. The four-colour statement is a finite exhaustive theorem: the two included programs generate all named candidate assignments using distinct encodings and enumeration strategies, compute admissibility from the defining predicate, and independently determine the full reconfiguration graph. The first checks connected-component sizes by disjoint-set union; the second checks one-vertex components by breadth-first search. Both independently find 54,264 states, 185,808 single-coordinate edges, 6,048 singleton components, eight components of size 882, six of size 6,860, and connectivity of the exactly-two-coordinate graph with 897,456 edges. An explicit checked vertex bijection proves that the two encodings describe the same graph. The result is not inferred from a partial sample.

## Originality — PASS, with historical uncertainty
The published minimum four is **not** counted as a new result. The original 2010/2012 paper covers bipartite upper bounds and a three-colour hypergraph test but does not classify the four-colour Heawood state graph. The 2011/2012 recolouring lemma applies only at palettes strictly larger than twelve for cubic graphs, not at four. The 2023 product-colouring paper treats other graphs and chromatic thresholds. Published results concerning ordinary proper three-colour Heawood reconfiguration or dominated colouring in Fano incidence graphs do not imply the more restrictive four-colour locally identifying configuration or the two-change connectivity. Searches of indexed mathematical records and the accessible literature located no exact matching census. An unindexed thesis, program output, or paper could still establish historical precedence; no negative search alone is taken as proof of priority.

## Value — PASS
At the established four-colour minimum the allowed assignment space is sharply fragmented: 6,048 optima cannot be repaired by even a single legal vertex recolouring, while permitting simultaneous changes of just two vertices restores connectivity. The six support-core sectors and eight monochromatic-shore sectors connect this mobility phenomenon to the non-two-colourable Fano incidence structure. Exact classification of feasible states and their constrained update geometry is a natural and specific refinement of the classical chromatic minimum, rather than an arbitrary numerical instance.

## Closest literature and scientific limits
Relevant full texts inspected include Esperet et al. (2012) especially Section 2 and Section 9, and Foucaud et al. (2012) especially the recolouring lemma. The 2023 product paper was compared for scope. The available mathematical index includes several different Heawood colouring problems but no matching four-colour lid reconfiguration claim. The claim is finite, not a general theorem for arbitrary projective planes. Independent scholarly audit and exhaustive historical priority determination have not been performed.
