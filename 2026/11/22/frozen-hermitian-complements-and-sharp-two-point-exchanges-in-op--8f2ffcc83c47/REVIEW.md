The certificates test every one of the \(2^{21}\) point masks, all permitted exchanges of every optimal mask, and one BFS origin from each provably transitive profile. They establish the exact radius, diameter and component sizes, not merely sample lower bounds. The incidence-graph extension is a proved Cartesian-product consequence.
