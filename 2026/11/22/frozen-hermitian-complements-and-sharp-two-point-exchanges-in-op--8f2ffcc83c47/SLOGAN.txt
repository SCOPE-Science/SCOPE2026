Among the 11480 minimum two-fold blocking sets in \(\operatorname{PG}(2,4)\), the 280 Hermitian-unital complements are frozen under one-point replacement, while every other optimum belongs to a single component of diameter eight. Allowing two simultaneous replacements connects every optimum and gives diameter five; the analogous minimum double-total-domination network has 78961 one-replacement components.
