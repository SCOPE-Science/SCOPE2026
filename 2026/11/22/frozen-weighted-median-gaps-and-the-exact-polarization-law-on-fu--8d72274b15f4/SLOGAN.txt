On a fully connected, common-influence weighted-median social network, the initial weighted-median interval never moves: every opinion has an exact geometric trajectory toward its nearest endpoint. For independent uniform initial opinions, the lasting polarization gap is zero or has an exact \(\operatorname{Beta}(1,n)\) distribution, with the nonzero probability determined by balanced weight subsets; uniform weights polarize almost surely at even population sizes and reach consensus at odd sizes.
