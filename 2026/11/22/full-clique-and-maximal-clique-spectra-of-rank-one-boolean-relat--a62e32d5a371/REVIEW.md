# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
Nonzero rank-one Boolean relations have unique nonempty rectangular supports. For distinct rectangles commutation forces both mixed products to vanish, so each coordinate is either left-only, right-only, absent, or an idempotent's unique private overlap. Three inclusion–exclusion sets impose root and nonzero requirements; a signed first-kind Stirling inversion removes duplicate square-zero labels. For maximal families, the union of supports must cover the ground set and all nonempty rectangles between the exclusive left and exclusive right supports must be present. The latter classification yields the independent Stirling-second-kind maximal polynomial. Direct Boolean-matrix computation checks every bivariate clique and maximal-clique coefficient for each set size one through four (225 vertices at size four). Those finite checks are not used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, subject to literature-priority risk
The closest published rank-one Boolean-relation enumerator covers only **idempotent** vertices. It supplies the \(j=0\) specialization of the present all-clique formula; maximality in that smaller induced graph does not supply maximality among all rank-one rectangles. Kim–Roush establish the underlying binary-relation algebra; Dolžan–Kokol Bukovšek–Oblak analyze the full Boolean commuting-graph diameter. Paulista's completely \(0\)-simple semigroup work includes general commutation and clique-number results, which are prior coverage of those individual claims, but the inspected clique-number treatment does not supply the full multivariate clique or inclusion-maximal-size enumerators. The main new claim is those two complete enumerators for all rank-one rectangles, including the square-zero stratum. A general earlier Rees \(0\)-matrix enumeration formula remains a plausible priority risk; novelty here is best-knowledge, not a historical uniqueness certificate.

## Value — PASS
Commutative subsemigroups and maximal commutative subsemigroups are structural invariants of finite semigroups. Instead of a single extremal size, the result gives complete coefficientwise and maximality distributions for all dimensions and separates idempotent versus square-zero generators. The four-point example already exhibits holes in maximal-family sizes while the idempotent-only induced graph has a different classification. This is a natural complete enumeration of a canonical semigroup rather than an arbitrary parameter slice.

## Limits
The all-rank-one semigroup is a strict subset of the full binary-relation monoid. Earlier equivalent general Rees formulas are possible. Exhaustive computational tests end at four ground points, while the proof is combinatorial for arbitrary size. No independent proof or priority audit has been performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
