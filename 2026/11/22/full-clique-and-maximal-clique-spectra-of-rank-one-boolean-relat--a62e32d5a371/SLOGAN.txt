Every unordered commuting family of nonzero Boolean-rank-one relations has an explicit two-variable enumeration by its idempotent and square-zero members, and every inclusion-maximal such family has a separate exact size polynomial. These formulas enumerate all commutative subsemigroups containing zero in the rank-at-most-one relation semigroup; for four points the maximal-clique polynomial is \(29z+235z^2+254z^3+193z^4+8z^7+6z^9\).
