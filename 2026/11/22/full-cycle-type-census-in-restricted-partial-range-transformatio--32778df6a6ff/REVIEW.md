# Scientific assessment: full cycle-type census in restricted-partial-range transformation semigroups

## Correctness — PASS
The claim counts maps \(f:X\to X\) satisfying \(f(Y)\subseteq Z\), for \(1\le |Z|\le |Y|\le |X|\), by the complete directed-cycle partitions inside \(Z\) and \(X\setminus Y\). The directed-cycle location argument excludes cycles meeting \(Y\setminus Z\). Prescribing the two cycle permutations leaves exactly two independent parent forests: a complete rooted forest on \(Z\), with the intervening \(Y\setminus Z\) vertices attaching directly to \(Z\); and a complete rooted forest on \(X\) with root set \(Y\cup C_W\). The directed matrix-tree determinant proves both forest factors. All zero-width and all-root boundary cases have explicit conventions. The derivation of the factorized stable-rank polynomial, both probabilities, and parameter recovery follows without hidden genericity assumptions. The standalone exhaustive checker independently enumerated all maps in all 56 size triples with \(1\le k\le m\le n\le6\), checked every occurring pair of cycle types and periodic-vertex counts, and checked total cardinality and probability identities. Finite enumeration is supporting evidence, not the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS relative to checked evidence
Jin (2021) introduces the nested restricted-range semigroup and treats its rank and isomorphism. Jin–You (2023) explicitly enumerate all, regular and idempotent transformations, but do not establish a two-region cycle-type distribution or eventual-period census in the sections examined. Hansen–Jaworski (2010) give the established forest-plus-cycle viewpoint for random mappings; that method is credited rather than claimed. The new precise statement is the evaluation of that mechanism for the nested semigroup to obtain the exact independent two-zone cycle-type counts and the bivariate reconstruction consequence. Searches of related mapping and semigroup literature did not identify the same statement. The assessment is qualified: broader implicit enumeration in random-mapping theory may exist, and the inspected pages of the 2010 work do not exhaust all possible related generalizations.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This is a complete, nontrivial classification of eventual cycle structures and periods for a natural and previously investigated infinite family of transformation semigroups, across all nested-set cardinalities. It distinguishes elements of the same semigroup by their actual long-term dynamics, not only by cardinality, image rank or idempotence. The independent factorization gives an exact \(m/n\) absorption probability, while the bivariate polynomial reconstructs the three size parameters outside an identified sharp degeneracy. The full type formula makes exact counts of every eventual semigroup period directly computable by partition sums and Möbius inversion. No asymptotic law, arbitrary functional-graph isomorphism classification, or independent validation is claimed.
