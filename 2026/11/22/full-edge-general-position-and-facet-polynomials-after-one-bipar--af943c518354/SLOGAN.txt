Deleting a single edge from \(K_{m,n}\), for \(m,n\ge3\), turns its one-facet edge-general-position complex into one with exactly \(2+(2^{m-1}-1)(2^{n-1}-1)\) facets, counted alongside all legal edge sets by two explicit polynomials. In balanced graphs the maximum drops by the shore size, and a gap of unbounded length separates the two largest facet-size levels.
