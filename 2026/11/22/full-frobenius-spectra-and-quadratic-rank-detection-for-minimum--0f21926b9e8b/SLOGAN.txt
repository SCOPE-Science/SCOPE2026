Every degree-\(q+1\) hypersurface filling projective space over \(\mathbb F_q\) has an explicit complete Hasse–Weil zeta function governed only by its alternating-matrix rank. Although all such hypersurfaces have the same base-field points, a single quadratic-extension count distinguishes their rank and thus their projective-equivalence class; the quadratic count itself follows from classical Hermitian formulas.
