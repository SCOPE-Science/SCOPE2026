# Full graded dihedral cohomology of the hexagonal graph nilmanifold

## Finding
Let \(N(C_6)\) be the compact 12-dimensional Dani–Mainkar nilmanifold of the cycle on six vertices, with its natural action of the dihedral group \(D_6=\langle r,s\mid r^6=s^2=1,\ srs=r^{-1}\rangle\). The **complete graded rational cohomology character** \(\chi_k(g)=\operatorname{tr}(g\mid H^k(N(C_6);\mathbb Q))\) is given by the following table. A mirror \(s\) fixes two opposite vertices, while \(rs\) fixes no vertices. All powers of \(r\) and mirrors are specified up to the six conjugacy classes; each character row applies throughout the indicated class.

| Degree \(k\) | \(1\) | \(r,r^5\) | \(r^2,r^4\) | \(r^3\) | \(s,r^2s,r^4s\) | \(rs,r^3s,r^5s\) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| 1 | 6 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 2 | 0 |
| 2 | 27 | 0 | 0 | -3 | 1 | -1 |
| 3 | 68 | 0 | 2 | 0 | 0 | 4 |
| 4 | 146 | -1 | -1 | 8 | 6 | 4 |
| 5 | 219 | 0 | 0 | 3 | 7 | -5 |
| 6 | 238 | 0 | 4 | -6 | 0 | 0 |
| 7 | 219 | 0 | 0 | 3 | -7 | 5 |
| 8 | 146 | -1 | -1 | 8 | -6 | -4 |
| 9 | 68 | 0 | 2 | 0 | 0 | -4 |
| 10 | 27 | 0 | 0 | -3 | -1 | 1 |
| 11 | 6 | 0 | 0 | 0 | -2 | 0 |
| 12 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | -1 | -1 |

Consequently, the rational Betti vector of the ordinary geometric quotient \(N(C_6)/D_6\) is
\[
(1,1,2,7,15,19,20,18,10,5,2,0,0).
\]
The total rational cohomology ranks of the covering nilmanifold and the full quotient are respectively \(1172\) and \(100\). The sixfold rotation quotient has Betti vector
\[
(1,1,4,12,25,37,40,37,25,12,4,1,1).
\]
The two nonconjugate reflection quotients have **different** Betti vectors:
\[
\begin{aligned}
N(C_6)/\langle s\rangle &: (1,4,14,34,76,113,119,106,70,34,13,2,0),\\
N(C_6)/\langle rs\rangle &: (1,3,13,36,75,107,119,112,71,32,14,3,0).
\end{aligned}
\]
These are rational, not integral, cohomology statements.

## Assumptions and scope
Use vertex indices modulo six. Write the rational Lie algebra with generators \(v_0,\ldots,v_5\), central generators \(z_0,\ldots,z_5\), and brackets \([v_i,v_{i+1}]=z_i\); all other independent brackets vanish. The corresponding Chevalley–Eilenberg algebra is the exterior algebra on degree-one generators \(x_i,y_i\), with \(dx_i=0\) and \(dy_i=x_i\wedge x_{i+1}\). The cyclic edge from 5 to 0 is oriented in that order. These choices differ from the increasing-label convention only by a sign change of one central generator.

A compatible compact nilmanifold exists by the integral rational structure, choosing a uniform lattice invariant under the finite graph automorphism group. For example, use the subgroup generated by the exponentials of all twelve integer basis generators in the simply connected group; signed permutations of these generators preserve this subgroup. The induced dihedral maps act by \(r:i\mapsto i+1\) and \(s:i\mapsto-i\) on vertices, and by the induced signed permutation of oriented edge generators. The statements are invariant under choosing any equivariantly compatible such compact quotient. By Nomizu's theorem, its rational cohomology is computed by this Chevalley–Eilenberg algebra, equivariantly.

## Proof
**1. Differential and dihedral action.** A wedge monomial is encoded by a subset of the twelve ordered generators. Apply the graded Leibniz rule to obtain its differential as a signed sum of wedge monomials. For an element \((a,\epsilon)\) of the dihedral group, map each vertex index \(i\) to \(a+\epsilon i\pmod6\); a reversed oriented edge receives coefficient \(-1\). In addition, reorder the resulting exterior monomial with its permutation sign. This produces explicit rational cochain matrices \(D_k\) and signed permutation matrices \(G_k\). All \(4096\) monomials satisfy \(d^2=0\) and \(dG_g=G_gd\) for all twelve group elements; these finite checks are repeated in the second program.

**2. Exact finite cohomology ranks and character formula.** Assign multidegree \(e_i\) to \(x_i\), and \(e_i+e_{i+1}\) to \(y_i\). The differential preserves this multidegree, partitioning the full exterior basis into \(1582\) independent weight blocks of maximal size \(18\). Within each block, rational Gaussian elimination finds an exact basis of each boundary space \(B^k=\operatorname{im}(D_{k-1})\). For each dihedral element that preserves a weight block, compute the trace of its signed action on \(C^k\), \(B^k\), and \(B^{k+1}\). The two short exact sequences from \(B^k\) to cocycles and cohomology imply
\[
\operatorname{tr}(g\mid H^k)=\operatorname{tr}(g\mid C^k)-\operatorname{tr}(g\mid B^k)-\operatorname{tr}(g\mid B^{k+1}).
\]
An element moving a weight block contributes zero to the full trace. Exact elimination and summation over fixed blocks give the displayed six character columns in degrees zero through twelve. In particular the identity column is the Betti vector \((1,6,27,68,146,219,238,219,146,68,27,6,1)\), with sum \(1172\), and Euler characteristic zero.

**3. Independent verification of all character classes.** Separately construct invariant cochains by signed group-orbit sums, rather than by traces on boundary bases. Integral signs and exact rational ranks determine \(H^k(C^\bullet)^H\) for six subgroups \(H\): the identity group, all rotations, rotations by two vertices, the half-turn group, either of the two reflection classes, and the full dihedral group. The outputs are stored as reproducible data. Character averaging over these subgroups solves for the traces in all six conjugacy classes, giving exactly the same table. A further direct invariant-complex computation for the entire dihedral group independently gives the quotient ranks shown above.

**4. From invariants to quotients.** For a finite group acting smoothly on a compact manifold, rational transfer (or an equivariant finite triangulation) identifies \(H^*(N(C_6)/H;\mathbb Q)\) with \(H^*(N(C_6);\mathbb Q)^H\). Applying the character projector, \(\dim H^k(N(C_6)/H;\mathbb Q)=|H|^{-1}\sum_{h\in H}\chi_k(h)\), gives all asserted quotient vectors. This holds even when the finite action has fixed points, and does not assert that the quotients are manifolds.

## Verification
The standalone scripts `character_exact.py`, `invariant_exact.py`, and `subgroup_exact.py` use only the Python standard library and exact rational arithmetic. Run `python character_exact.py`, `python invariant_exact.py`, and `SUBGROUP=0 python subgroup_exact.py`; to reproduce each subgroup data record, run `SUBGROUP=0,1,2,3,4,5`, `SUBGROUP=0,2,4`, `SUBGROUP=0,3`, `SUBGROUP=0,6`, and `SUBGROUP=0,7` with `python subgroup_exact.py`. Each record must coincide with the included corresponding JSON result. Run `python crosscheck.py` after generating or using the included data; it asserts character recovery from invariant ranks, both quotient computations, Poincaré duality, and all degreewise character-averaging identities. Recorded results were generated from the actual scripts and independently replayed. The entire cochain basis and every degree are covered, not a statistical sample.

## Relationship to prior work
Aldi, Bevins, Chatzitheodoridis, Da Silva, Frias, and Mejía Gómez [1] give formulas for **path-graph** nilmanifolds and a general total cohomology lower-bound framework, but do not classify the six-cycle's entire graded dihedral character or its two reflection quotients. Aldi, Butler, Gardiner, Grandini, Lichtenwalner, and Pan [2] give the general graph Lie-algebra cochain decomposition, low-degree cohomology and all-degree results for stars; their general method allows computation but their statements do not supply the six-cycle's degreewise character. The value \(1172\) coincides with a previously available six-cycle total-cohomology lower bound, so that numerical coincidence is **not** claimed as an independent new theorem. A previously recorded five-cycle dihedral-character computation concerns an odd cycle with just one reflection conjugacy class; it does not imply this hexagonal result. This note's contribution is the explicitly determined six-class representation in all thirteen degrees and its differential reflection quotients.

## Limitations
The result is specific to \(C_6\); no uniform recurrence for arbitrary cycles is proved. It is a classification of rational graded *vector-space representations*, not of the cup-product algebra, integral cohomology or integral torsion. It does not determine orbifold isotropy or the smooth type of quotient spaces. No independent mathematical audit or definitive exhaustive priority certification has been performed. Source-index incompleteness presents a residual priority risk.
