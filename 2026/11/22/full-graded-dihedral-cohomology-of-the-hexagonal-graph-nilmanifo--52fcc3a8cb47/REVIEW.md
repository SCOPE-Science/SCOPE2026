# Scientific review: hexagonal graph nilmanifold

**Correctness — PASS.** The object and differential are specified in an oriented twelve-generator Chevalley–Eilenberg algebra. Two independently implemented exact elimination pathways agree on all thirteen ordinary Betti numbers and the full-dihedral invariant Betti numbers. A second implementation exhaustively verifies \(d^2=0\) and all twelve differential-equivariance identities on each of the 4096 exterior monomials. Five further subgroup-invariant computations recover every one of the six dihedral character columns and both reflection quotient Betti vectors. Nomizu's theorem identifies Lie-algebra and nilmanifold rational cohomology, while finite-group transfer identifies quotient rational cohomology with invariants. The theorem does not infer integral or multiplicative structure from these computations.

**Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk.** The recent path-graph paper arXiv:2611.17014v1 discusses all-degree path formulas and a total cohomology bound, not the hexagonal cycle's thirteen-degree symmetry character. The complete 2024/2025 paper arXiv:2402.14188 develops a general graph Lie-algebra cohomology decomposition and low-degree results plus all-degree stars, but gives no six-cycle dihedral table. The nearest accessible prior calculation of a pentagonal graph nilmanifold's rational dihedral character has a different graph and only one class of reflections; neither it nor the earlier numerical lower bound implies the six conjugacy-class character values or the two distinct reflection quotients. Targeted mathematical-index and literature searches did not identify a covering result; this is comparison-based originality, not a claim that absence of a search hit proves uniqueness. Inaccessible or unindexed work is a residual risk.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The six-cycle is a canonical first even hexagon with two geometrically distinct reflection conjugacy classes, so its complete graded equivariant cohomology is a naturally motivated invariant. The exact character yields several quotient Betti classifications, including a detectable difference between mirror types. That equivariant distinction is not supplied by ordinary Betti numbers or by the previously known total-cohomology lower bound; it is useful for subsequent study of symmetry quotients of graph nilmanifolds. No general result for longer cycles or cup-product description is asserted.

The closest literature is Aldi et al., *Poincaré polynomials of nilmanifolds associated with path graphs* (2026), and Aldi et al., *On the cohomology of Lie algebras associated with graphs* (2024 preprint, 2025 journal publication). Limitations include unindexed publication-priority risk, the twelve-dimensional hexagonal case only, rational coefficients only, and no independent verification by a separate mathematical reviewer.
