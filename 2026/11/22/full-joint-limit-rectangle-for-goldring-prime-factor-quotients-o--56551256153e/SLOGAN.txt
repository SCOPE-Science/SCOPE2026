For Goldring's arithmetic map on products of three distinct primes, the two normalized largest-prime-factor and size quotients have **every** simultaneous finite limit in \([0,1]\times[0,\infty)\), even when the image is also squarefree. Each neighborhood contains \(\gg X/(\log X)^6\) such inputs below \(X\), in sharp contrast to the sparse joint limit set on repeated-prime inputs.
