# Scientific assessment

## Correctness (C): PASS

At full combined length, minimality forces a disjoint shuffle and unique embeddings of both inputs. Coordinatewise minimum/maximum of embeddings proves that nonuniqueness always has a single-coordinate replacement; the permitted path-step tests are therefore necessary and sufficient. Congruence reductions yield three closed live-offset graphs, and direct induction yields the constant, binary-branching and Fibonacci counts. Boundaries and every positive repetition count are included. A separate direct deletion-minimality enumerator and a grid dynamic program reproduce the claimed values on independently chosen finite test sets. The computer checks are confirmations, not replacements for the infinite proof.

## Value (V): PASS

Maximum-length minimal common supersequences are a natural extremal layer of the well-studied minimal-supersequence problem. Cyclically shifted permutation powers are a canonical family with strong periodic structure but nontrivial subsequence ambiguity. The first three nonbinary alphabet sizes display three mathematically distinct exact laws: one output, power-of-two branching, and Fibonacci branching. The path criterion also exposes the mechanism underlying these changes and provides benchmark counts for enumeration algorithms. The work does not claim a closed form for every alphabet size or every output length.
