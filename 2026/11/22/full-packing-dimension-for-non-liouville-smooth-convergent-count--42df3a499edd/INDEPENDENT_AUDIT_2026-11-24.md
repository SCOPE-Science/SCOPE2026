## correctness

PASS

The exact cited input is Marques Proposition 2.1, a limsup along convergent denominators N for log(3)/log(2), with absolute constant for 4<ν≤5 and D≥2. Its inequality excludes the fraction R(b) itself. For a fixed large parent t take ν=4+1/t and D=3^((τ−1)t)/6. Lemma 2.2 and the proof of Lemma 2.3 give lower density at least ρ_t−CD^(2−ν)≥ρ_t/2, where ρ_t is comparable to 3^(−τt); the relative error is O(3^((2−τ)t)). Thus this uses the density inequality actually proved, not just the stated existence of two successors or uniform thresholds in t. The same arc estimate bounds multiplicity in any interval of length O(1/N), giving c_tN separated admissible centers. Fixing finitely many parents before choosing arbitrarily large common N_j makes nesting and same-level separation compatible with log floor(c_jN_j)/log N_j→1. The child intervals are exponentially small in N_j while gaps exceed 5/N_j. A consistent equal-branching measure then gives mass at most 1/B_j to balls of radius 1/N_j, which forces zero outer mass for every set of upper box dimension <1 and implies packing dimension 1 by the countable-upper-box characterization. Along each branch, distance from R(t_j) is between Q_j^(−τ) and 3Q_j^(−τ), with Q_j=3^t_j. Rational separation rules out a rational limit and Legendre makes the rationals convergents. For Q_j≤v<Q_{j+1}, if v≤D_t and the rational is distinct from R(t_j), subtraction of 3Q_j^(−τ) from 1/(vQ_j) leaves at least 1/(2v²). If v>D_t, use admissibility at the next center; v<Q_{j+1} gives separation ≥1/(vQ_{j+1}), so the offset from the next center is less than half that separation for large j and the residual error is at least 1/(32v^(4+1/t_j)). The center case is bounded below by v^(−τ). Rationals outside [1,2] are irrelevant at large denominators for a fixed interior limit. These bounds and the smooth convergents give exact finite τ, including τ=4. Finally there is exactly one eligible center inside (1,2) for each b, and eventually any convergent in question lies there. The summable diameter powers of the radius-3^(−2b) limsup cover give Hausdorff dimension zero for the entire F_τ. The verification notes claim no numerical or formal proof; the exceptional-approximation estimate is used as the stated primary-source theorem, not re-certified in full here.

## originality

PASS

Marques already constructs exact-exponent smooth-convergent Cantor sets and derives the density needed to choose successors; neither existence nor the density lemma is a new finding. The supported increment is the simultaneous sharp dimension statement on the thin exact-convergent locus, especially the compact full-packing subset obtained by exploiting common arbitrarily distant blocks with linear branching. The retrieved exact-order packing theorems do not impose the required restriction on approximants.

## value

PASS

This is a metric strengthening of an existence theorem for counterexamples with prescribed finite irrationality exponent. The entire arithmetic locus has zero Hausdorff dimension by a lacunary cover, yet a compact part already has maximal packing dimension at every stated exponent. Producing a compact witness, rather than inferring largeness from density or an unrestricted exponent set, makes the contrast substantive. The value rests on full packing dimension and its simultaneous restrictions, not on the elementary Hausdorff cover by itself.
