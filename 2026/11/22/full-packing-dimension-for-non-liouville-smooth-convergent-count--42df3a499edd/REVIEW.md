Schleischitz's packing results concern other Diophantine exponent sets without exact simultaneous two-power numerator, three-power denominator and convergent constraints; they do not imply the claim. Similar-sounding published dimension results for dynamically defined Cantor sets and unrelated arithmetic loci have different objects. The relevant indexed literature and recoverable historical claims were compared on implications and no covering result identified. Residual risk: an unindexed general smooth-convergent packing theorem, or a hidden equivalent formulation, might cover this case.

The key many-branch selection is nontrivial, uses the recent theorem's positive-density input in a way the original construction did not, and applies uniformly for every finite exponent in the known counterexample range. No stronger dimensional, effectiveness, or prime-base-generalization assertion is made.
