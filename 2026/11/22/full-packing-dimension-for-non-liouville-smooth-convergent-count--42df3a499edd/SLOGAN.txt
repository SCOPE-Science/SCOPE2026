For every fixed finite irrationality exponent \(\tau\ge4\), the real numbers having infinitely many regular continued-fraction convergents \(2^a/3^b\) have Hausdorff dimension \(0\) but maximal packing dimension \(1\). A multibranch refinement of the 2026 Erdős–Mahler counterexample produces a compact Cantor subset realizing both dimensions.
