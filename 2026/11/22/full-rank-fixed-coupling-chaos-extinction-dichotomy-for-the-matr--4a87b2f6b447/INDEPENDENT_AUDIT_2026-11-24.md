The first inequality proves forward invariance for both factors. The diagonal factor restricts to logistic 4, which is conjugate to the full tent map via x ↦ sin²(πx/2); the same change of variable pushes the tent map's invariant Lebesgue law to the arcsine law. This proves the entropy lower bound and invariant probability, not chaos on all states. Induction gives the claimed even and odd bounds and uniform convergence; invariance against continuous tests forces δ₀, and separated orbit segments are uniformly bounded in number at each positive scale because all sufficiently late images are close to zero. The proof covers real t in (0,1/2) and all complex Hermitian states. The supplementary checker samples real rational matrices only; it was inspected, not executed, and is not needed for the proof.
