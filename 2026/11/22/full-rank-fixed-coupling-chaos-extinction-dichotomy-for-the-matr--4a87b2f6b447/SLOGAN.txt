In the matrix logistic map, fixing a strictly positive-definite coupling \(A=BB^*\) does not even determine whether the system supports logistic chaos or whether every state decays uniformly to zero. Two invertible factors of the same \(A=\operatorname{diag}(4,t^2)\), for every \(0<t<1/2\), exhibit these opposite behaviors, and the extinction factor obeys an exact two-step contraction.
