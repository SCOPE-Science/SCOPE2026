For each fixed integer \(k\ge0\) and ratio interval \(0<a<b<\infty\), \(\Phi_k\) gives a well-defined positive probability law with all exponential moments, and its consecutive weighted ratios recover the input sequence exactly. Multiplying its mass at index \(n+1\) by the centered quadratic-bias weight cancels the two factorial increments and shifts the sequence by one. Uniform factorial domination gives continuity in total variation, and coordinatewise positive quotients give continuity of the inverse. This establishes a topological conjugacy, not merely numerical support. Symbolic-shift dynamics rigorously imply every period, infinite entropy and a continuum of limit distributions; iid full-support ratio sequences yield dense orbits almost surely by disjoint-block Borel–Cantelli. Constant/periodic ratio-tail equivalences and the explicit \(O(\varepsilon)\) TV envelope follow by continuity and product inequalities. The finite checker passed 2,209 diagnostic assertions; the proof supplies the infinite-dimensional argument.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A probabilistic bias map known to single out a classical Bessel fixed-point family need not approach that family under iteration. The theorem completely resolves its dynamics on a natural compact, full-support, all-exponential-moments-finite class: every period occurs, periodic laws are dense, entropy is infinite, and an iid full-support ratio model gives almost-sure density in the whole invariant class, not just one exceptional uncountable omega-limit set. A sharp qualitative diagnostic—tail-ratio convergence if and only if convergence to a Bessel law—comes with explicit cyclic limits and a computable quantitative TV bound. These consequences distinguish convergence, periodicity and nonconvergent chaotic behavior for a motivated transformation.
