The centered quadratic-Bessel bias transform on a compact class of strictly positive, all-moments-finite probability laws is exactly conjugate to a full one-sided shift: it has dense cycles of every length, infinite topological entropy, and even uncountable orbit-limit sets; for iid full-support tail ratios, almost every orbit is dense in the entire invariant class. Convergence to a Bessel fixed point occurs exactly when the factorially weighted consecutive probability ratios converge to a constant; asymptotically periodic ratios give explicit periodic limiting laws with quantitative total-variation bounds.
