The \(24\) nodal fibers of a general bidegree-\((2,3)\) K3 genus-one pencil have full geometric permutation monodromy \(S_{24}\), although a very general surface's fibration has multisection index \(3\) and no section. Its conjugate genus-one pencil has the same monodromy, and the generic ternary-cubic discriminant under the quadratic parameterization has Galois group \(S_{24}\).
