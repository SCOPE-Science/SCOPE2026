For ordinary cycle independence complexes with at least eight vertices, every dihedral-subgroup geometric quotient is simply connected except a full rotational quotient at a prime-power cycle length; its fundamental group is then the cyclic group of order that prime. For every prime cycle length at least eleven, the free rotational quotient has the integral cohomology ring of an odd-dimensional lens space and exhibits prime torsion in an unbounded number of homology degrees.
