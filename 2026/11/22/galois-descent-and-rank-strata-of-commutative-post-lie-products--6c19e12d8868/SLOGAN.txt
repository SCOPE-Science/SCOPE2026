For the irreducible almost-abelian Lie algebra \(\mathbb F_qt\ltimes\mathbb F_{q^d}\), commutative post-Lie products form a smooth \(d\)-dimensional scheme with \(2^d\) geometric affine components, but only \(2q^d\) base-field products. Its scalar-extension counts are \(q^{ds}2^{\gcd(d,s)}\), with an explicit necklace zeta function and a complete rank polynomial revealing new ranks after extension.
