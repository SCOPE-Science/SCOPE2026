The four exceptional cubic symmetric graphs of girth six have exact Dominator-first and Staller-first domination-game lengths \((7,6)\), \((7,6)\), \((8,7)\), and \((9,9)\), respectively. Every optimal and suboptimal second reply is classified by graph distance: Pappus requires a distance-four response to an S opening, whereas every legal response is optimal on Heawood and Desargues.
