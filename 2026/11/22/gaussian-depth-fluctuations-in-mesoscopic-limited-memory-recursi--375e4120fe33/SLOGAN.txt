The newest-vertex depth in a polynomial recent-window recursive tree obeys an exact-centering Gaussian limit: for every fixed \(0<\beta<1\), its fluctuation scale is \(n^{(1-\beta)/2}\) and its limiting variance constant is \(2/[3(1-\beta)]\). A bounded-increment ancestral martingale and a pathwise \(O(\log n)\) potential-error estimate prove the result for all such exponents.
