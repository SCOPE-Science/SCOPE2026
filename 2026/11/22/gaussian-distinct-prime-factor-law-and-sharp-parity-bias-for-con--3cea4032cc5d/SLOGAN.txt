The number \(\omega(Q_k(n))\) of distinct prime divisors of the consecutive product-to-LCM quotient has an exact Bernoulli prime-window law and a Gaussian growing-window limit with mean \((\log2)k/\log k\) and variance \((3\log2-2)k/\log k\). Its unitary-Möbius sign is balanced only for \(k=2\), and its nonzero bias otherwise has sharp logarithmic magnitude \(-(2\log2/3+o(1))k/\log k\).
