Gaussian directions expose the half-indicator vertices of the clique fractional stable-set polytope with exactly computable normal-cone probabilities, and this law becomes exponentially close to uniform on those vertices. Two independent directions expose the same vertex with probability \(2^{-n}(1+O(n(0.9)^n))\), and the number of half-support coordinates in a collision is asymptotically binomial with parameter \(1/2\).
