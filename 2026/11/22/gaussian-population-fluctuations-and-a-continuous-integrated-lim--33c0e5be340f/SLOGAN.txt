In singular Brownian-excursion immigration, the centered live-particle count has a globally nonstationary, one-quarter-self-similar Gaussian multitime limit whose local tangent at positive times is fractional Brownian motion with Hurst index \(1/4\). Although the count is infinite at a dense set of random times, its centered integrated population-time has a genuine continuous functional Gaussian limit with the exact variance \(4\kappa t^{5/2}/15\), where \(\kappa=2\rho\sqrt{2/\pi}\).
