## Value — PASS
The motivating paper identifies two thin-shell radii but cannot determine the distribution of the shell fluctuations. The new theorem resolves their Gaussian fluctuations and discovers a common dimensionless variance \(22/5\), compared with variance \(2\) for the squared radius of the Gaussian path-average. This strict factor \(11/5\) distinguishes volume-weighted hull geometry from a Gaussian proxy despite identical leading radii. It is a natural second-order invariant for the same annealed measure introduced in the source, not an arbitrary parameter slice or mere restatement of its first two moments. It is specific to expected-volume sampling and should not be extrapolated to typical realized hulls.
