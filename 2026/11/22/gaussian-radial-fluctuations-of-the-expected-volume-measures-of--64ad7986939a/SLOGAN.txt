A volume-biased random interior point of a high-dimensional Brownian-motion hull or Brownian-bridge hull has Gaussian squared-radius fluctuations on scale \(\sqrt d\), with variances \(22/45\) and \(11/360\), respectively. Despite different radial scales, both have the same normalized variance \(22/5\), which is \(11/5\) times the squared-radius fluctuation variance of the corresponding Gaussian path time-average.
