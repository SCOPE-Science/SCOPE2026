Gaussian regulation of \(z^m\) on Fock space creates a sharp Hankel singular-channel profile \(u^{m+1}e^{-2u}\), while its conjugate develops a cancellation notch \(u^{m-1}(u-m)^2e^{-2u}\). In the first angular mode, the analytic symbol has larger trace norm but smaller operator norm than its conjugate as the regulator vanishes, even though their Hilbert–Schmidt norms agree exactly at every positive regulator.
