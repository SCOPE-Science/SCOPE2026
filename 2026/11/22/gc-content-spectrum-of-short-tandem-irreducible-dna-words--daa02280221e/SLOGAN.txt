The complete GC-composition enumerator of four-letter words forbidding tandem squares of lengths one and two is rational with an explicit degree-eight denominator. Exactly balanced words of even length \(n\) number asymptotically \(\frac{6\sqrt3}{\sqrt{\pi(7\sqrt3-6)}}(1+\sqrt3)^n/\sqrt n\), refining the previously known exponential rate with an exact prefactor.
