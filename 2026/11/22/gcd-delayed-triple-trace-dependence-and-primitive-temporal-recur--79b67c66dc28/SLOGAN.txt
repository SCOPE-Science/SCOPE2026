For additive Rule 90 over any finite field, any two stationary temporal sensors are perfectly independent, but three at sites \(0<h<k\) have a complete primitive polynomial recurrence and exactly \(\max\{0,t-k+\gcd(h,k)\}\) independent constraints in a \(t\)-frame observation. Thus the first dependence occurs sharply at \(t=k-\gcd(h,k)+1\), with a nonconvex trace law controlled by a Chebyshev strong-divisibility identity.
