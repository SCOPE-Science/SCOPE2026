# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The cut-rank reduction follows from finite-field Fourier orthogonality and the block bipartition of a graph-state adjacency matrix. The critical proof separately analyzes the group algebra for one dyadic cycle and two twice-odd cycles, identifies the zero-resonant common roots by \((\zeta-\eta)(\zeta\eta-1)=0\), counts the \(J_1\) and \(J_2\) multiplicities including the repeated inverse-root labels, and fuses them by a valid characteristic-two power-of-two Jordan lemma. The result is exactly \(16\gcd(s,t)-8\) zero Jordan blocks. Two separately implemented direct finite computations checked 17 physical biadjacency ranks and 10 full-adjacency power-rank profiles; they are corroboration, not a substitute for the all-size proof. Boundary assumptions \(a\ge2\) and odd \(s,t\ge3\) ensure simple cycles and no accidental characteristic-two exceptional roots.

## Originality — PASS on examined evidence, with priority risk

The foundational graph-state cut-rank result (Hein, Eisert and Briegel, arXiv:quant-ph/0307130) and the Cartesian-sum algebra (Zaidenberg, arXiv:math-ph/0608027 and math-ph/0606070; Norman 2008) are credited. The previously published even-by-even two-torus formula \(mn/2-\gcd(m,n)\), the separate \(C_4\square C_4\square C_{2^a}\) omitted-bond theorem, and the two-dimensional dyadic Jordan classification have been compared by implication after reading their actual statements and proofs. The new mixed-dyadic, two-odd-core **three-dimensional** resonance is not one of those formulas nor a corollary of their stated restrictions. Zaidenberg's general higher-dimensional spectrum-of-products statement gives possible eigenvalues but not the exact nonsemisimple zero-Jordan multiplicity. The nearest other automata preprint uses null-boundary path grids, not the present periodic cycles. No equivalent exact three-torus law was found in searched records; undiscovered equivalences remain possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result gives an exact all-size entanglement resource measure on a natural three-dimensional periodic quantum cluster family. Its gcd-dependent deficit records a nontrivial commensurability resonance between two odd-core directions. Two 720-qubit, degree-six tori can differ by 16 entanglement bits purely from the arithmetic arrangement of their circumferences. Unlike an arbitrary slice, the restriction captures the first tractable case of two distinct odd-core spectral families and supplies a structural Jordan theorem enabling reuse in characteristic-two lattice dynamics.

## Limitations and context

This is a fixed checkerboard bipartition of ideal, unweighted qubit graph states. It does not assert global maximum bipartite entanglement, a generic three-torus formula, or a quantum Hamiltonian ground-state theorem. The general literature on modular convolution and lattice parity dynamics may contain a differently presented equivalence, and an independent historical-priority audit remains necessary.
