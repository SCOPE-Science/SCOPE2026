On \(C_{2^a}\square C_{2s}\square C_{2t}\) qubit cluster states with odd \(s,t\ge3\), checkerboard entanglement is exactly \(2^{a+1}st-8\gcd(s,t)+4\) bits, and the Schmidt spectrum is completely flat. The fixed four-bit deficit for coprime odd side cores jumps by eight bits per additional unit of their greatest common divisor, even at fixed total qubit count.
