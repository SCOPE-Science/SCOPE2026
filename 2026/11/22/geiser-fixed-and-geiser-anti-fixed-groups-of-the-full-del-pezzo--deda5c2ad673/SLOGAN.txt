For the natural Geiser involution on the critical group of all 56 exceptional curves of a complex degree-two del Pezzo surface, the fixed subgroup is \(\mathbb Z/2\oplus(\mathbb Z/28)^{26}\), while the anti-fixed subgroup is \((\mathbb Z/2)^6\oplus(\mathbb Z/28)^{14}\oplus(\mathbb Z/560)^7\). All five-primary torsion is anti-invariant, and an extra factor of two in the product of subgroup orders prevents an integral splitting into the two groups.
