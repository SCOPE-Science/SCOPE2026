For every regular Euclidean simplex of dimension \(d\ge3\), moving the fixed pedal-projection point along an altitude yields no return to the original regular shape at **any** positive iterate except at most countably many positions. An exact two-variable rational map explains the obstruction: its centred fixed point has non-root-of-unity multipliers \((-1\pm i\sqrt{d^2-1})/d\).
