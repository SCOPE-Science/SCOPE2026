The smooth curves underlying the integral Fibonacci exponentials \(G_k(x)=\exp(\sum_{n\ge1}5F_n^{2k}x^n/n)\) have explicit geometric genus and all ramification indices determined by the base-five valuations of \(\binom{2k}{j}\). Every branch point is totally ramified exactly when \(2k+1\) is a power of five or three times a power of five, and the arithmetic normal closure has a cyclotomic semidirect-product Galois group.
