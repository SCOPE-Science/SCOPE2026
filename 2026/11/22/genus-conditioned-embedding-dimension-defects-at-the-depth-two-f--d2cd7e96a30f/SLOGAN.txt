For numerical semigroups with Frobenius number \(2m-c\), the difference between embedding dimension and its genus-determined baseline is exactly the number of missing small two-sums of a binary set. At fixed multiplicity, Frobenius number and genus, this defect has a nondegenerate limiting fringe law of mean \((1-p)(2-p)/p^2\), with a finite hypergeometric coefficient formula for its exact mean.
