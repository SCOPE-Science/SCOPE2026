On every connected bipartite graph of diameter at most three and without four-cycles, ordinary geodetic vertex sets coincide **set by set** with edge-geodetic vertex sets. For the Heawood graph this yields a complete projective classification of 65 inclusion-minimal edge-geodetic sets, with cardinality polynomial \(21z^4+14z^5+28z^6+2z^7\); the already-known 21 minimum flag sets are included but not claimed as new.
