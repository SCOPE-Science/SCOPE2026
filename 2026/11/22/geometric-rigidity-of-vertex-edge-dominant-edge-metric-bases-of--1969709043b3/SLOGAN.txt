For the binary four-cube and five-cube, joint vertex-edge coverage raises the minimum number of edge-resolving landmarks by exactly one: \(\gamma_{emd}(Q_4)=4\) and \(\gamma_{emd}(Q_5)=5\). Every optimum is geometrically classified: the four-cube has \(176\) placements in three symmetry orbits, whereas the five-cube has exactly \(160\) placements, all consisting of an antipode together with four neighbors of one vertex.
