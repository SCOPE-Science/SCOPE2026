For uniform random dimer parking on a path, the exact probability of \(k+1\) vacancies at successive distance three is \(3^{-k}a_s a_{n-s-3k+1}\), where \(a_m=\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}(-1)^j/j!\). The longest run of overlapping local augmenting paths is \(\log_3 n\) to first order, and its rare-event count has a Poisson limit with clustering factor \(2/3\).
