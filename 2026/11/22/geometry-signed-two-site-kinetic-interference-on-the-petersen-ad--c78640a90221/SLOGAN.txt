On the Petersen graph, two modified hard-core monomer arrival rates have opposite effects on the probability of four-particle jamming depending solely on whether the modified sites are adjacent or nonadjacent. The exact changes are \(+4H(a,b)\) and \(-2H(a,b)\), with \(H(a,b)=(a-1)(b-1)/[3(a+2)(b+2)(a+b+1)(a+b+8)]\), for every \(a,b>0\).
