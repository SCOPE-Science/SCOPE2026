For every infinite binary support with arbitrarily long gaps, the first arity where Abelian maximal pattern complexity exceeds its aperiodic minimum determines exactly the girth of the associated additive incidence graph. If that graph is unicyclic with cycle length \(2r\), the first excess occurs exactly at \(\binom{r+1}{2}\) and equals \(r+2\); explicit infinite supports realize every \(r\ge2\).
