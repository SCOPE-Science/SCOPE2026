At the exact cubic cusp of a balanced four-compartment car–bus–train congestion flow, every physical trajectory converges to the unique nonhyperbolic equilibrium. Except for its strong-stable set, convergence has the universal signed vector law \(\sqrt t(X(t)-E)\to\pm\sqrt{53/768}(-1/3,1,1,-1/3)\); the extra transport compartments change the critical amplitude despite the same scalar stationary equation.
