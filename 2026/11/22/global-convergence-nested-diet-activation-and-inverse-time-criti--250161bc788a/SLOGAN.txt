For a fixed pollinator guild and linearly renewing floral rewards, adaptive foraging converges globally to a unique equal-reward diet at every positive adaptation speed, and additional flower types enter that diet at exact, ordered pollinator-density thresholds. At the first two-source threshold, the newly recruited preference decays toward zero as \(1/(Kt)\), with \(K\) explicitly determined by nectar production, loss, consumption and behavioral adjustment rates.
