The claim is about the reduced affine union of two specific composition components over the complex numbers. The critical nonstandard step is not simply their known intersection parameterization: high coefficients in the degree-\(2m\) polynomial reconstruct the right factor \(H\) and outer coefficient \(b\) **polynomially**, making one component a graph. The graph is equivariant for the involution \(P(t)\mapsto P(-t)\). A single triangular polynomial automorphism therefore straightens the graph while preserving linearity of the even-polynomial component. This gives an exact global coordinate-ring isomorphism; the displayed conductor, Hilbert–Samuel series and depth follow algebraically. The prime-index statement uses Fried's invariant reducibility criterion through the initiating paper.
