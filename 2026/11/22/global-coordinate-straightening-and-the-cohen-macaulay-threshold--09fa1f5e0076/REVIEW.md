# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Normalize the inner factor by making it monic and subtracting its constant, absorbing the affine change in the outer factor. Centering the composite forces the next-to-leading inner coefficient to vanish (its coefficient in the composite is the outer degree times that inner coefficient). For inner degree 2 this gives t² and the even coefficient plane. For outer degree 2 the composite is H²+bH with the stated H. Descending from a_{2m−2} to a_{m+1} gives each h_j as half a high coefficient minus an expression in already recovered h's, and a_m then gives b. Every high vector therefore occurs and uniquely determines the low vector F(x). Since m is odd, P(t)→P(−t) acts on (H,b) by (−H(−t),−b); graph uniqueness implies parity equivariance. The shear y→y−F(x) is polynomially invertible and preserves the equations for the even plane because the odd part of F vanishes on even high vectors. The two resulting ideals (u,w) and (v,w) intersect in (w,u_i v_j) and sum to (u,v,w). Fixed graph points are precisely b=0 with H odd, giving H=tW(t²). The coordinate ring is the fiber product of the two polynomial rings over the shared z-ring. Its finite integral closure is their product; multiplying by the component idempotents shows that the conductor consists exactly of pairs with both restrictions zero. Off the intersection there is one regular branch; on it there are two local minimal primes. Translation at any closed intersection point followed by completion gives the stated formal monomial ring. Counting monomials in two 2d-variable rings with their d-variable overlap subtracted proves the series, dimension, embedding dimension and multiplicity. In the fiber-product exact sequence, the middle and last depths are 2d and d; local cohomology vanishes below d+1 for the first term and is nonzero in degree d+1. For prime p≥5 the invariant-Bezoutian criterion in the cited Theorem 5.3 applies in composite degree 2p, with exactly the two listed factorizations and no prime-degree Dickson exception. Non-Cohen–Macaulayness on the intersection also holds at nonclosed points by the same depth argument after localizing along the shared z-plane.
