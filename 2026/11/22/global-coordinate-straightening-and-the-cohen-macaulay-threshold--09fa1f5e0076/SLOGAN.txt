For every odd \(m=2d+1\geq5\), the reduced union of the two principal degree-\(2m\) polynomial-composition loci is globally polynomially equivalent to two \(2d\)-planes meeting along a \(d\)-plane. Its completed local depth along the intersection is \(d+1\), so the full reduced invariant-Bezoutian reducibility locus is non-Cohen–Macaulay whenever \(m\geq5\) is prime.
