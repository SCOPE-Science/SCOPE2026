With inverse-linear prey escape and zero adult-prey carryover, the adult-stage predator is globally eliminated whenever its replacement potential does not exceed the juvenile-stage predator's, even if the resident populations oscillate. Under Beverton–Holt recruitment, a strictly positive three-species equilibrium exists uniquely in the sharp wedge \(R_J>1\) and \(R_J<R_A<1+r(R_J-1)\).
