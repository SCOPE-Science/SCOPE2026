In the cubic Lorenz-like family with \(c=2a\), \(b_1=0\), and \(b_2=-2ab_4-b_3\), every real trajectory converges exponentially to one equilibrium of its parabola whenever \(a,b_4>0\) and \(b_3\ge0\), with an explicit negative-height transient bound. Every invariant probability is supported on this parabola, whereas every \(b_3<0\) makes some of its equilibria transversely unstable.
