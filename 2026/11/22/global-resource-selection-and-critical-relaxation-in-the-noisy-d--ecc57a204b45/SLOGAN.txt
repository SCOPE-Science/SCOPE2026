For the noise-corrected environmental donation game, a single explicit entropy function proves that **every interior trajectory** converges to coexistence at low positive noise but to a fully depleted or fully restored resource state above the sharp threshold \(k_c^2=(1+\theta)/(2sr|\theta-1|)\), with the latter transition absent when \(\theta=1\). Exactly at the threshold the resource distance decays universally as \(1/[\varepsilon|\theta-1|t]\), whereas zero noise produces neutral closed orbits.
