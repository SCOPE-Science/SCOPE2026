# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The statement fixes a linear indexed doubled-label source and oriented multiplicity-preserving digram observation, and the proof reconstructs **every** noncrossing word against unrestricted competitors. The essential structural claims are independently justified: a noncrossing pairing has an innermost adjacent doublet; the full profile determines deletion of that doublet and its former neighbors; every directed bigram in a noncrossing doubled-label word is unique, ensuring reinsertion. Induction on label count gives an all-order proof. The Catalan-matching labeling count gives \(n!C_n\) exactly. A distinct concrete crossing collision at order three and direct inspection at orders one and two establish the sharp boundary; appending fresh doublets propagates it. Direct finite enumeration through order five checks global fibers, and a second Catalan-skeleton enumeration through order six checks the constructive decoder. The finite computations are supplementary and not used to infer the all-order theorem.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)
The first antecedent, the general \(2\)-Abelian invariant from Karhumäki–Saarela–Zamboni (2013), does not supply singleton fibers for all labeled noncrossing double-occurrence words. The primary 2024 Brualdi–Dahl text studies Stirling and quasi-Stirling words, and has an inversion-multiset rigidity statement for a different readout; an inversion multiset is not derivable from directed bigram counts on all double-occurrence words. The primary 2018 Cruz et al. preprint describes equivalence under relabeling/insertions, not this fixed-alphabet directed digram problem. Queries on both general equivalence and double-occurrence subclasses and comparison with nearby retrieved records yielded no dominating theorem. Residual risk: a proof of the same implication in older unindexed work or specialist genome-assembly literature could escape these sources. This is not proof of priority.

## Value — PASS
The noncrossing subclass is a canonical Catalan family of paired-symbol sequences rather than an arbitrary finite slice. Knowing that a two-gram multiset has exactly one source even among **all** double-occurrence words is stronger than injectivity restricted to that class: it gives a constructive zero-error decoder and the exact attainable source capacity \(n!C_n\) at every order. The sharp three-label failure on the unrestricted family identifies an informative structural boundary. This is not a statement about noisy, cyclic or unlabeled reconstruction.
