# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Condition on the common environment's past: independent thermal noises give independent centered Gaussians with common variance V. Its normalized value U has the two drift fields −2aU and 2b(1−U); stationary zero flux and switching balance give the positive tilted beta density on (0,1), with shapes A=r1/(2a), B=r2/(2b). Both boundary atoms are absent because each past realizes both stiffnesses over intervals of positive length. Conditional Gaussian moments give S=E[V], K=Var(V), and κ4=3K, and exact generator identities agree with the displayed moment formulas. Set t=A+B and z=A/t. For fixed t the likelihood ratio for increasing z is strictly increasing, so the mean varies continuously and strictly from 0 to 1. At fixed mean, increasing t forces both shapes to increase: if one shape were unchanged or decreased while the other increased, the likelihood ratio would be strictly monotone and the mean would change. With both shape increments positive the new-to-old density ratio vanishes at both endpoints, has a single interior maximum, and by normalization crosses 1 exactly twice. Integrating the signed density difference against the monic quadratic with those roots gives a strictly negative integral; cancellation of the mass and linear terms makes this exactly the second-moment difference. At fixed mean the variance therefore decreases strictly and continuously as t increases. The reported moment formulas give normalized second moment tending to m at t↓0 (the endpoint mixture) and m² at t→∞ (concentration). This proves the full open wedge, absence of finite positive boundary rates and the unique nested monotone inversion.

## originality

PASS

The exact tilted-beta mixture and both forward observables, including the general unequal-rate covariance, are already in Biroli et al. (2023) and are not original here; the single-particle fourth-cumulant substitution is immediate from the mixture. The supported distinct result is global identification for every positive ordered pair, with the sharp image and a constructive monotone inverse. The closest distinct catalog result gives an inverse only in a restricted family defined by the full stationary autocovariance having one exponential. A forward map, even with exact rational formulas, does not by itself establish either the absence of additional positive solutions or attainment of the whole open wedge.
