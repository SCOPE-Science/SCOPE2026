For Brownian particles sharing a two-state harmonic trap with known stiffnesses and diffusivity, stationary position variance and cross-particle squared-position covariance determine both unequal switching rates uniquely. Their complete feasible region is \(D/a<S<D/b\) and \(0<K<(S-D/a)(D/b-S)\); a single particle's fourth cumulant can replace the cross-particle measurement through \(\kappa_4=3K\).
