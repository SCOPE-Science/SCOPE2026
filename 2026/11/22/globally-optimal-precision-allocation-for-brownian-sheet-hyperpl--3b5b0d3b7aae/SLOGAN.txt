For a Brownian sheet in every dimension \(d\ge2\), estimating its volume integral from one noisy complete hyperplane integral in each coordinate direction has a unique global optimal design: split measurement precision equally and put every slice at the same explicitly determined coordinate. The optimum exhibits an exact exponential-in-dimension precision scale \(P\asymp d3^{d-1}\) for the transition from boundary-near to interior slices, and its noiseless residual fraction is \(1/(8d+1)\).
