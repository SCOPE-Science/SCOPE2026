For stationary exponential-covariance Gaussian quadrature with \(n\) noisy point sensors and freely divisible total measurement precision \(P\), the globally optimal design uniquely uses equal precision, equal quadrature weights, and symmetric margins around equally spaced interior points. All \(n+1\) gaps become equal exactly at the universal transition \(P=n\), independently of the covariance length scale; below that budget the margins are wider than the internal gaps, and above it they are narrower.
