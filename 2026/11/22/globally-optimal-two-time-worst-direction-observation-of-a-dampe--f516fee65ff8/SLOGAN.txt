For two equal-noise scalar observations of a known damped planar rotation, the unique globally worst-direction-information-optimal design observes immediately and then at a sharply determined phase \(z_r\), clipped by the horizon. This phase lies strictly after the determinant-optimal phase and moves from \(\pi/2-\pi r^2+O(r^3)\) under weak damping to \([1+W(2e^{-2})/2]/r+O(r^{-3})\) under strong damping, where \(r\) is decay divided by frequency.
