Holub–Shallit (2016, Section 4.1) and Régnier (1992) enumerate unbordered **unconstrained words**, which cannot by numerical or logical implication determine the Fibonacci-restricted count or its phase corrections. The published finite-type closure study (2026) gives exponentially sharp *bounds* and the expected *number of short closing periods*, and expressly leaves the complete deficit law uncomputed. A separate paper on unbordered factors of the **single infinite morphic Fibonacci word** does not address all words of the golden-mean shift. Actual targeted database and bibliographic searches did not identify an exact prior result for the constrained Mahler equation and two coefficients; failure of a search is not a proof of novelty. Residual risks include unindexed automata-enumeration literature, broader border-generating-function specializations, and possible previously implicit asymptotic consequences of a general constrained-word theorem.

**Value (PASS).** The maximal-period atom is a natural unresolved finite-scale observable in a canonical primitive SFT, beyond first-order normalized time and variance. The exact law is computationally efficient, gives a new nontrivial probability constant, explains the parity split quantitatively, and exhibits a dyadic cascade of complex singularities. This is an exact natural special-case computation rather than a merely tiny numerical gain or a claim about all finite-type shifts.

**Limits.** Only a single atom is evaluated; no complete penetration-time law, general SFT extension, or independent audit is claimed. The formulas rely on uniform finite-word sampling and adjacency-forbidden binary words.
