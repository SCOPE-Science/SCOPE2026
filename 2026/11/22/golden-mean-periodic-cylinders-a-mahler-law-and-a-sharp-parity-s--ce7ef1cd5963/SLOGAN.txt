In the binary golden-mean shift, the probability that a uniformly selected admissible length-\(n\) cylinder needs a periodic orbit of full period \(n\) approaches \(0.1556298471\ldots\), with two distinct sharp even/odd corrections of order \(\varphi^{-n/2}\). An exact Fibonacci-weighted shortest-border decomposition gives a Mahler functional equation whose square-root poles also force the unit circle to be a natural boundary.
