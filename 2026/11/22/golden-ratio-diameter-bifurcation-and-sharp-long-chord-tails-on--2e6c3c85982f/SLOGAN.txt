For parameter-uniform random chords of the symmetric parabola arc \(P(t)=(t,t^2)\), the exact golden-ratio arc cutoff \(a_*=\sqrt{(11+5\sqrt5)/8}\) changes the diameter contacts from the two extreme endpoints to two endpoint–interior pairs. The near-diameter probability consequently changes sharply from order \(\varepsilon^2\) to order \(\varepsilon^{3/2}\), with explicit constants and conditional limiting shapes.
