The proof reduces every edge projection inequality to an interval for a Gram-dual coordinate, then uses a maximum-principle argument on the normal equations of every higher-dimensional face. Strict row diagonal dominance follows exactly from interval feasibility, and also ensures positivity of the coefficients used in the contradiction. The full-dimensional face yields interior membership without circular assumptions. The determinant Jacobian supplies the exact volume formula, and the eigenvalue calculation supplies the three-dimensional equiangular cases. The standalone exact-rational checker verifies 31,500 finite face projections but is corroborative, not a substitute for the proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The all-face orthogonal kernel was introduced to control geometrically admissible polyhedral cutouts. Determining whether it exists for a natural class is not automatic, and the theorem sharply distinguishes modest shear from collapse of all admissible points. It compresses exponentially many face constraints to the edge conditions, gives the exact admissible-point probability in every dimension and supplies sharp equiangular thresholds. The statement is a structural classification of a naturally defined object, not just a small numerical refinement.
