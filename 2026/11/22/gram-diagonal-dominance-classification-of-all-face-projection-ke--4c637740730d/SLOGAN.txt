For every Euclidean parallelotope, orthogonal projections to all faces land in their relative interiors exactly when the projections to all edges do. Its all-face orthogonal kernel exists precisely for strictly diagonally dominant generator Gram matrices; when nonempty, it is an explicitly described parallelotope with exact volume ratio \(\prod_i(g_{ii}-\sum_{j\ne i}|g_{ij}|)/\det G\).
