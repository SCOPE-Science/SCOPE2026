The proof identifies the deleted-product cells with a precise level of the red–blue affine height on a Bier sphere, then identifies its upper half as the PL regular neighborhood of the induced connected graph. Handle decomposition and spanning-tree cancellation give the orientable connected-sum boundary for all \(N\ge5\). The classic Bier sphere and PL regular-neighborhood theorems are stated as inputs, not new lemmas. Separate signed-chain calculations on six small graphs and two finite fields corroborate indexing and homological consequences, not the universal PL classification.

The previously determined single three-dimensional mixed example and general fundamental-group formula are proper partial consequences. Search of mathematical records and relevant primary full texts found no general graph–Bier equator PL classification, but cannot exclude unindexed equivalents.

A precise global PL type becomes a function of the first cycle rank alone, making it a structural classification rather than a routine small-case exercise.
