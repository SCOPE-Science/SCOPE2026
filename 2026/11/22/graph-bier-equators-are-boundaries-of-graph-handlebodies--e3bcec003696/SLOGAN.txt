The color-midsection of the Bier sphere of every connected graph on \(N\ge5\) vertices is PL homeomorphic to \(\#^{|E|-N+1}(S^1\times S^{N-4})\), because it is the boundary of a regular neighborhood of that graph. Consequently, the mixed ordered simplex-skeleton deleted product with one-dimensional first factor is a connected sum of \(\binom{q+3}{2}\) copies of \(S^1\times S^q\) for every \(q\ge1\).
