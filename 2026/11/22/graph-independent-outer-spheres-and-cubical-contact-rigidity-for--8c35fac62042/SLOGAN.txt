For the unit coordinate-disc Minkowski sum of a graph cone over **any** noncomplete regular rim graph, the exact enclosing Euclidean sphere and all \(2^{n+1}\) contact points depend only on the rim order and degree—not on its edge arrangement. Its diameter graph is a perfect antipodal matching on those contacts; conversely, any flat-rim interior outer-support maximizer forces regularity of the rim.
