Under independent rate-one vertex clocks, the elapsed time to repair a fixed conflict graph with \(m\) edges and squared-degree overlap \(D_2=o(m^{3/2})\) obeys the universal limit \(2T-\log m\Rightarrow\mathrm{Gumbel}\). A quantitative surviving-edge Poisson bound proves this half-Gumbel law; stars and complete graphs exhibit different high-degree clocks.
