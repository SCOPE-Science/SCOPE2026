For every fixed \(k\ge2\), the optimal no-feedback score after \(k\) GSR riffle shuffles, divided by \(\sqrt n\), converges to the sum of two independent half-normal random variables divided by \(\sqrt{2(2^k-1)}\). In particular, its limiting distribution function is the square of an error function, and the sharp variance scale is \((1-2/\pi)n/(2^k-1)\).
