# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The unrestricted minimization is strictly convex in independent edge weights on every connected feasible Laplacian: positivity of scalar second divided differences and injectivity of the edge-to-Laplacian map ensure uniqueness. The rotational symmetry is therefore inherited by the global solution, rather than postulated. Fourier eigenvalues distinguish odd antipodal modes from even cycle modes. An exact first-order condition on the *affine rung-budget coordinate* proves the threshold including equality, and endpoint-sum estimates establish the three asymptotic regimes. Exact rational cycle pseudoinverses and finite weighted Fourier calculations were reproduced by the standalone checker (15,506 finite assertions). Finite tests do not prove the universal result.

## Originality — PASS on inspected evidence, with residual priority risk

The closest directly matching prior result is the published determinant-optimal Möbius-ladder price theorem, which proves the \(p=0\) threshold \(n^2/(2n-1)\); that result is explicitly treated as previously known. Bailey–Cameron analyze fixed unweighted Möbius-ladder designs and general spectral criteria, Azimi–Farrokhi calculate fixed-weight resistance structures, and Ghosh–Boyd–Saberi provide the broad convex resistance-design framework. The inspected statements and claims do not give a positive real-order rung-deletion price rule or the logarithmic critical law. A specialized unindexed equivalent could exist, and not all cited full texts were reviewed cover to cover.

## Value — PASS

The precise budget price needed to eliminate a whole class of long-range edges is a motivated network-design invariant. The result supplies its exact all-size criterion, two useful rational endpoints, and a three-regime phase boundary with a logarithmic critical scale. This is a structural support-selection phenomenon, not merely a recomputation of a fixed-graph trace or the already-known determinant optimum.
