On Möbius ladders with individually priced antipodal rungs, the unique inverse-spectral-moment-optimal conductance design deletes all rungs at an explicit price threshold for every real order \(p>0\). The threshold scales as \(n^{2p+1}\) below order \(1/2\), as \(n^2/\log n\) at order \(1/2\), and as \(n^2\) above it; the known determinant-order law is recovered without claiming it as new.
