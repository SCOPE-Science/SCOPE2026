For every regular intersection of \(m\ge3\) unit disks, the area excess over the equal-perimeter two-disk lens has its unique maximum at perimeter \(\pi\), regardless of \(m\); that maximum is \(G_m=1-(m/2)\tan(\pi/(2m))\). The deficit from this maximum satisfies the globally sharp inequality \(G_m-\Delta_m(P)\ge(G_m/\pi^2)(P-\pi)^2\), with equality only at the midpoint and degenerate perimeter endpoints; the \(m=3\) maximizer is the Reuleaux triangle.
