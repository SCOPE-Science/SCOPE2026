The Euclidean Laplacian simplex of the binary hypercube has exactly \(d\) strictly ordered interior dihedral-angle levels, indexed by the Hamming distance between opposite facet labels. In dimension \(d\to\infty\), adjacent cube labels account for asymptotically all squared non-right-angle energy, even though these ridges are an exponentially small fraction of all ridges; obtuse and acute extreme angle deviations scale as \(2/d\) and \(d^2(\log d)^2/(8\,2^d)\), respectively.
