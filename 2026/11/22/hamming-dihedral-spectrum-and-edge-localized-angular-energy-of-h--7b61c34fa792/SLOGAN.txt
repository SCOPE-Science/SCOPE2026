An exponentially small fraction of ridges of the Euclidean hypercube Laplacian simplex—the ones with adjacent opposite labels—carries all but O(1/d) of the squared dihedral-cosine energy. The obtuse and acute extreme deviations from a right angle occur at distances one and d, and have respective scales 2/d and d²(log d)²/(8·2^d) as d grows.
