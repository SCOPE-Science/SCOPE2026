# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

For each \(n\ge5\) and strict-leader-tie population \((L,L,r)\), the local open-neighborhood count is \(N_D-s_D(i)\). The proof rules out adjacent or distance-two minority sites, then excludes high-color repetitions; the delicate boundary \(L=r+1\) is handled with an even-length cyclic parity argument. The converse is checked at all three permitted local triple types. The selected-sites count follows by cyclic compositions, and the transfer matrix has characteristic polynomial \(t^3-t^2-z\). Two independent finite checkers agreed with the proof's exact formulas; they are corroboration rather than substitutes for the all-size argument. Boundary cases exclude an absent minority and equal three-way populations.

## Originality — PASS, with historical qualification

The full relevant sections of Mossel–Neeman–Tamuz (arXiv:1207.0893) cover aggregation and an untied plurality variant on expanders rather than the fixed-state class considered here. Becchetti and collaborators (arXiv:1310.2858) use randomly sampled neighbor opinions and randomized ties on cliques. The closest publicly available exact cycle-complement calculation concerns binary majority and has no third-color stratum. Searches for the graph, three opinion labels, tied leading histograms, gap-two exclusions and hard-core transfer formulas found no covering statement. An unindexed equivalent remains a residual risk.

## Value — PASS

The result isolates a natural diversity-preserving equilibrium sector under full-neighborhood social updating, gives its complete structural description for every graph size and admissible minority population, and derives an exact generating expression and exponential entropy. The defect-placement condition is a genuinely restrictive dynamical consequence of the tie convention, not an imposed combinatorial model. The coefficient formula and growth rate characterize an infinite family of exact invariants that would be unavailable from the prior binary classification.
