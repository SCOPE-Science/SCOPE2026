For three-opinion tie-retaining plurality on a complete graph with the edges of one cycle deleted, every equilibrium having two equally frequent leaders and a nonempty strict minority is an alternating two-opinion word with minority sites at cyclic mutual distance at least three. These minority-defect equilibria have an exact hard-core counting formula and grow as \(3\lambda^n\), where \(\lambda\) is the root greater than one of \(\lambda^3-\lambda^2-1=0\).
