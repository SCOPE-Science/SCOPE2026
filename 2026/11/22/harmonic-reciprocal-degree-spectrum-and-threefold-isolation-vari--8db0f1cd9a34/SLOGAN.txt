In a two-coordinate random threshold digraph, the undirected graph of **mutual** arcs has an exact harmonic reciprocal-degree spectrum: \(\Pr(D_i=k)=(H_n-H_k)/n\). Although its isolated-vertex count has expectation \(H_n\sim\log n\), its variance is \(3\log n+o(\log n)\), revealing a threefold inflation from shared reciprocal-neighborhood fluctuations.
