# Self-review

**Correctness (C): PASS.** The full theorem holds for every integer \(n\ge1\) with distinct source symbols \(U,D,F\), arbitrary one-symbol deletions, and distinct common outputs. Each path of height at most one is uniquely determined by an even subset of nonflat positions. Cai et al. Proposition 9 reduces all edges to opposite alternating intervals. Height admissibility excludes both \(U,D\) phases and all \(U,F\) or \(D,F\) intervals longer than two. Hence only adjacent flat swaps survive, giving exactly the even-token path graphs. A disjoint matching constructed by the first mixed adjacent-index pair proves the exact independence number, with parity as an explicit attaining code. Unique boundary crossings count edges; sorted token motion proves distances; rightward extreme geodesics are rectangular tableau extensions. Direct exhaustive read-set tests for length \(1\) through \(10\), matching tests through \(18\), and independent BFS/geodesic checks through \(9\) all passed from the package. These finite checks supplement rather than replace the proof.

(2020), Corollary 3.10; the exact numerical values and classical tableau counts are not themselves claimed as new. Cai et al. (2020), Proposition 9, supplies the alternating-interval deletion characterization. The 2016 synchronization-channel work establishes foundational context. The contribution is the explicit isomorphism of the *height-one Motzkin two-distinct-read confusability graph* to the disjoint union of even-token path graphs, and its concrete optimal parity reconstruction codes and read-channel geometry. Claim and implication searches, including source, aliases, token graph, constrained Motzkin, and direct two-read deletion terminology, did not locate this bridge. Closest known graph and reconstruction sources are scientific antecedents and do not state it. Residual risk: a mathematically equivalent unindexed or differently described prior Motzkin-channel result may exist, or channel-specific equivalence may be viewed as a relatively direct application by specialists.

**Value (V): PASS.** A natural constrained synchronization source receives an exact finite-blocklength two-read zero-error capacity and an explicit optimal encoder-side code; the result also classifies connected confusability families, edge counts, distances and a meaningful path multiplicity interpretation. Unlike computing arbitrary examples, the theorem identifies a structural correspondence that provides constructive reconstruction-code designs at *all lengths*. It does not calculate higher-height Motzkin capacities, standard one-read deletion codes or unique decoding from a single trace.
