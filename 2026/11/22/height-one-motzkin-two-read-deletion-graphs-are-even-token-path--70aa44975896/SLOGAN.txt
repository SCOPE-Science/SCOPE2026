The two-read one-deletion confusability graph of length-\(n\) height-one Motzkin excursions is precisely the disjoint union of even-token graphs of a path. Consequently an explicit parity construction gives the sharp code size \(\frac12(2^{n-1}+2^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor})\), with exact component, edge, distance and shortest-chain formulas.
