For an Ornstein–Uhlenbeck reset excursion surviving without a reset, the probability of lying above its initial location approaches its quasi-stationary limit from below precisely when \(0<\kappa a^2/D<3\), and from above when \(\kappa a^2/D\ge3\). At the sharp Hermite-node threshold \(\kappa a^2/D=3\), the leading relaxation mode vanishes and convergence accelerates from \(e^{-2\kappa t}\) to \(e^{-4\kappa t}\); below that threshold the probability must have an interior minimum.
