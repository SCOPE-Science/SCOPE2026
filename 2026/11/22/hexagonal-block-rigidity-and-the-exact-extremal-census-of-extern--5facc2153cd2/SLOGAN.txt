For an odd-size labelled parameter set in a finite outerplanar graph, every maximum family of external open-neighbourhood one-types is assembled from independent four-choice packets on the triples of a three-uniform hypertree. The number of distinct maximum type families on \(m=2t+1\geq3\) parameters is exactly \(2^t(2t)!\,(2t+1)^{t-1}/t!\); this classifies equality beyond the previously known sharp numerical bound.
