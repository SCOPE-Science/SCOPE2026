In a simultaneously Poisson-reset Brownian gas, one hidden exponential reset age controls every particle's variance, but a snapshot of \(N\) positions reveals only \(\tfrac12\log N\) nats of that clock at leading order. The exact latent-age mutual information is strictly increasing in \(N\) and has the model-specific positive correction \(2/(3N)\) beyond the established leading logarithmic law.
