A hidden linear coordinate of a three-dimensional sine map advances by exactly \(\alpha\) on a neutral-feedback surface; when \(2\beta+\gamma<1\), every orbit synchronizes exponentially to a uniquely forced circle graph, with an explicit first-order statistical bias and no stationary probability in the full space for nonzero drift. At zero drift, the sharp boundary \(2\beta+\gamma=1\) produces a supercritical pitchfork on a distinguished invariant leaf.
