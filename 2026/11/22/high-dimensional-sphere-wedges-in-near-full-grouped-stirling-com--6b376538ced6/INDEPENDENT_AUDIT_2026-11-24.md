# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In a near-full color factor the missing vertex gives a 0-cell and a single moving robot gives an edge, with unavailable vertices exactly the endpoints. With r groups the covering condition is empty intersection of these supports. The resulting product subcomplex is a deformation retract of the thin-diagonal complement: on a disallowed cube project from its diagonal corner, or from the center if the whole cube has a main diagonal. In either case nondiagonal rays stay nondiagonal and boundary points stay fixed, so descending-dimensional retractions glue along the boundaries. Normalize the sum of radial coordinates on the product star to one; the target is the join of r m-point sets, and the removal is exactly one barycenter in each of the m all-equal facets. Projecting each punctured facet to its boundary deletes just those open facets. The full join is (r−2)-connected with free top homology of rank (m−1)^r. For each deleted facet j the join of differences between label j and alternatives in each factor, with two distinct alternatives in the first two factors, is an integral cycle supported on exactly that one deleted facet among the m deleted facets, with coefficient ±1 there. The relative map is surjective over Z, so only the asserted top homology remains. Since r≥4 leaves the two-skeleton unchanged, the punctured join is simply connected, and Hurewicz and homological Whitehead give the integral wedge description. The supplied finite checker was inspected, not executed; its recorded mod-two ranks do not establish the integral or homotopy conclusions.

## originality

The cubical/continuous equivalence in this regime is covered by prior stabilization, and the cubical counts are established in the initiating paper.
