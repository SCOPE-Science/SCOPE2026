# Scientific review

## Correctness
The explicit color-to-cube bijection and finite descending radial deformation prove the diagonal-complement identification. Radial normalization of a product of star cones reduces the problem to the join of discrete arm sets with \(m\) diagonal top facets removed. Cross-polytope cycles with two unequal alternative labels split the relative top-cell map; the resulting homology is free of rank \((m-1)^r-m\). For \(r\ge4\), deletion of top facets preserves the two-skeleton, hence simple connectivity, and Hurewicz plus Whitehead gives the claimed wedge. The independent cubical/simplicial chain enumerations match for fifteen cases, including the five four-color examples.

## Originality
Revelli–Scheirer's 2026 near-full-color calculation supplies cell counts and explicitly leaves higher-dimensional star homotopy unsettled. The 2024 no-\(k\)-equal stabilization theorem provides a broader cubical model, but does not imply the specific sphere rank or attaching-map classification without the new join and facet cycles. Chettih's 2016 calculated examples use three points, not the stated four-or-more-group range. No matching high-dimensional uniform classification was identified in the checked sources; unpublished or unindexed equivalent calculations remain possible.

## Value
This is a natural complete classification for an infinite family specifically singled out as a higher-dimensional gap in the 2026 introduction, with exponentially growing integral sphere multiplicity, not an arbitrary small-parameter census. The topological proof supplies both connectivity and the absence of hidden attaching obstructions, which cell enumeration alone does not establish.

## Limitations and risks
This does not cover \(r<4\), other tree shapes, or symmetry actions. The general discrete configuration equivalence predates this result; that background is not claimed as original. The literature comparison is not an independent priority certification; expert review of the topological model would be useful.
