# High-girth near-ties and vanishing essential energy in harmonic one-bar representatives

## Finding

There are generic persistent first-homology bars on unweighted, two-vertex-connected graphs of maximum degree three and arbitrarily large girth for which exactly one edge is essential, yet arbitrarily many nonessential edges have harmonic coefficient arbitrarily close in magnitude to that of the essential edge. In the same examples, the essential edge carries an arbitrarily small fraction of the squared harmonic norm.

More precisely, for any integer \(g\ge 3\), integer \(N\ge 1\), and real \(0<\varepsilon,\eta<1\), there is a two-stage filtration of finite one-dimensional simplicial complexes \(K_0\subset K_1\) such that: (i) \(K_1\) is an unweighted simple, two-vertex-connected, subcubic graph of girth at least \(g\); (ii) a single edge is attached at the second stage, creating exactly one new, generic, infinite first-homology bar; (iii) its essential edge set has cardinality one; (iv) at least \(N\) nonessential edges have harmonic coefficient magnitude exceeding \((1-\varepsilon)\) times the essential coefficient magnitude; and (v) the squared norm on the essential edge divided by the total squared norm of the harmonic representative is less than \(\eta\).

The construction gives the complete exact formula. Let \(P\) and \(Q\) be internally vertex-disjoint paths from \(u\) to \(v\) with respective edge counts \(a\ge2\) and \(b\ge a\), and let \(e\) be a new edge from \(u\) to \(v\). Orient all edges toward \(v\), write \(P,Q\) for the signed sums of the oriented edges of the two paths, and take \(K_0=P\cup Q\) and \(K_1=K_0\cup\{e\}\). The new harmonic bar representative, uniquely normalized to have coefficient one on \(e\), is

\[
h_{a,b}=e-\frac{b}{a+b}P-\frac{a}{a+b}Q.
\]

Its essential set is exactly \(\{e\}\), its nonessential maximum-to-essential coefficient ratio is \(b/(a+b)\), and the squared-energy fraction of its essential edge is

\[
E_{a,b}=\frac{\|h_{a,b}|_{\{e\}}\|^2}{\|h_{a,b}\|^2}
=\frac{a+b}{a+b+ab}.
\]

## Assumptions and scope

Homology and harmonic chains are taken with real coefficients and the standard edge-orthonormal inner product. The complexes are regarded as one-dimensional simplicial complexes, **not** their clique complexes: even when the graph has a three-cycle, no two-simplices are attached. A generic bar means one of multiplicity one with no other bar born at its birth parameter. An edge is essential to this bar when it occurs in every cycle representing a nonzero class in the new one-dimensional birth quotient. The squared-energy fraction above is simply the squared Euclidean norm on the essential support divided by the full squared norm; it is not a new definition of persistence or a claim about every possible support statistic.

The filtration is constant at \(K_0\) for parameters \(0\le t<1\) and at \(K_1\) for \(t\ge1\). It may be preceded by any simplex-wise construction of \(K_0\) completed before parameter one. At parameter one exactly the edge \(e\) is added. Its degree-one barcode has one older essential interval and the unique new interval \([1,\infty)\). No death phenomena or higher-dimensional simplices are involved.

## Proof

**Cycles and the generic bar.** The graph \(K_0\) is a simple cycle of length \(a+b\), with real cycle space generated by \(z=P-Q\). Adding \(e\) increases the graph cycle rank from one to two. Hence the new birth quotient \(Z_1(K_1)/Z_1(K_0)\) is one-dimensional and has one bar, of multiplicity one, born at the sole new-edge event. Every cycle in that quotient with nonzero class has a nonzero coefficient on \(e\). Conversely, the two cycles \(e-P\) and \(e-Q\) represent nonzero classes in the same one-dimensional quotient. The former avoids every edge of \(Q\), and the latter avoids every edge of \(P\). Therefore \(\{e\}\) is precisely the intersection of supports of all representatives.

**Harmonic normalization.** Since there are no two-simplices, the degree-one harmonic space is exactly the graph cycle space. The harmonic representative for the newly born simple bar lies in the orthogonal complement of the earlier cycle space in \(Z_1(K_1)\). Every cycle with coefficient one on \(e\) has the form \(e-cP-dQ\) with \(c+d=1\), because \(\partial e=\partial P=\partial Q=v-u\). Orthogonality to \(z=P-Q\) reads \(-ac+bd=0\), since the path supports are disjoint and each oriented path has squared norm equal to its edge count. Solving these two equations gives \(c=b/(a+b)\) and \(d=a/(a+b)\), proving the displayed formula and its uniqueness. These equations are also the strictly convex minimum-energy conditions for the affine coset of cycles with coefficient one on \(e\).

**Coefficient and energy laws.** All \(a\) edges of \(P\) are nonessential and carry coefficient magnitude \(b/(a+b)\), and all \(b\) edges of \(Q\) are nonessential and carry magnitude \(a/(a+b)\). Both magnitudes are strictly less than one for finite positive \(a,b\), agreeing with strict essential-edge dominance. Because edge supports are disjoint,

\[
\|h_{a,b}\|^2=1+a\frac{b^2}{(a+b)^2}+b\frac{a^2}{(a+b)^2}
=1+\frac{ab}{a+b},
\]

which yields the claimed essential energy fraction.

**Simultaneous arbitrarily poor margins.** First choose an integer \(a\ge\max\{2,g-1,N\}\) sufficiently large that \(1/(1+a/2)<\eta\). Then choose an integer \(b\ge a\) so large that \(b/(a+b)>1-\varepsilon\). Since \(ab/(a+b)\ge a/2\), the essential energy fraction is at most \(1/(1+a/2)<\eta\). At least the \(a\ge N\) nonessential \(P\)-edges exceed the proposed coefficient threshold. Finally, \(K_1\) has three internally disjoint \(u\)-to-\(v\) paths of lengths \(1,a,b\), so its maximum degree is three, it has no cut vertex, and its girth is \(\min\{a+1,b+1,a+b\}=a+1\ge g\). This establishes all quantifiers.

## Verification

The standalone `check_harmonic.py` script independently constructs incidence boundaries and exact rational coefficient vectors, checks vanishing boundary and orthogonality to the older cycle, checks the norm identities, independently solves the energy-minimization parameter, and checks degree and coefficient conditions for eight representative pairs: \((2,2),(2,3),(2,7),(3,20),(4,9),(7,91),(15,300),(47,4700)\). All eight cases passed with exact fractions. The general quantifier statement follows from the preceding algebra, not from the finite tests.

Run using `python3 check_harmonic.py`; only the Python standard library is required.

## Relationship to prior work

Basu, Guzmán-Sáenz, and Parida (2026), Theorem 1 and the proof in Section 2, establish strict coefficient-wise dominance of essential edges for generic degree-one bars; their Lemma 2.7 gives the minimum-energy/electrical-flow background. That strict qualitative inequality is **respected** here. The contribution is the simultaneous quantified failure of a uniform multiplicative margin, a uniform bound on near-tied nonessential edges, and a uniform positive essential squared-energy fraction, even under subcubic two-vertex-connectivity and arbitrary girth. These negative uniform conclusions are not implied just by the published strict inequality. The example also specializes the established parallel-network electrical-energy principle, so no novelty is claimed for the underlying two-resistor current split itself.

Basu and Cox (2021/2024) introduce harmonic persistent homology and essential-content maximality for simple bars; their global maximality theorem is compatible with arbitrarily small numerical essential energy. Hou, Parsa, and Wang (2025) study a distinct harmonic-chain barcode and its stability, rather than uniform edge-identification margins for a specified generic bar.

## Limitations

This is an explicit sharpness/boundary family, not a replacement for the strict-dominance theorem. It uses real, edge-orthonormal unweighted graph chains, a simple infinite bar, and the particular squared Euclidean norm fraction. It does not establish analogous conclusions for weighted inner products with additional constraints, for finite bars with death simplices, for higher-degree homology, or for statistical noise recovery under a specified stochastic model. The formula is elementary after reducing to two parallel paths; the mathematical content is the simultaneous strong-graph-class and arbitrary-false-positive threshold failure. Equivalent previously unindexed formulations may exist.
