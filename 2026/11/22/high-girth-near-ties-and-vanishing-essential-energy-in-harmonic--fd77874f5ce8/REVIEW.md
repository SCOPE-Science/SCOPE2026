# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

For the three-arm graph consisting of one direct edge and two internally disjoint paths, graph cycle rank rises from one to two under the single edge insertion. The new bar is simple, and the edge is essential while all path edges are avoidable via the two alternative cycles. Orthogonality to the old cycle forces the two exact rational path coefficients; the norm and arbitrary-girth limits follow by direct algebra. The exact rational standalone checker passed eight cases, including an independent energy-minimization calculation. Finite checking is corroborative; the proof covers every allowable parameter.

## Originality — PASS (best knowledge, with residual risk)

The closest primary result is Basu–Guzmán-Sáenz–Parida (2026), which establishes strict essential/nonessential coefficient inequality for generic degree-one bars. Its proof contains generic electrical-minimization machinery; however, it does not state or imply from strictness alone a uniform positive coefficient gap, a universal bound on near-ties, or a positive lower bound on essential squared energy. This result provides an explicit simultaneous counterfamily under the additional natural constraints of two-connectivity, maximum degree three, and growing girth. Basu–Cox establishes essential-content maximality; Hou–Parsa–Wang studies a different barcode. Prior electrical-network work contains the basic current split, and the residual risk of an equivalent already-published sharpness family remains. No independent priority certification is asserted.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The family gives a precise negative boundary to quantitative interpretations of a recently established qualitative topological identification theorem. A coefficient-only classifier with any fixed relative cutoff below the essential maximum can return arbitrarily many false positives, while the exact essential squared-energy share is arbitrarily small even without hubs, bridges, low girth, or weighted edges. This is a motivated structural limit, not an arbitrary finite-instance recalculation.

## Scope and unresolved issues

No probabilistic reconstruction claim, finite-death extension, higher-degree analog, or uniform weighted-edge statement is established. The algebraic family is deliberately simple; unindexed equivalents are a residual publication-priority risk. The exact checker is not a replacement for the proof.
