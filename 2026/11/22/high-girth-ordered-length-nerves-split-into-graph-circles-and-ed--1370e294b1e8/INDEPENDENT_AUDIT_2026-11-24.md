# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For the ordered inclusive total-length simplicial set, the exact-length tree premise was checked in Tajima and can also be seen directly: expand each tuple to its tree walk and mark its cuts. Cuts at immediate backtracks are mandatory; the length-preserving boundary deletes only optional cuts. If any optional position exists, insertion at that fixed position with the corresponding deletion sign contracts that walk summand. The only surviving positive-length walks alternate along one edge. On this diagonal the first filtration differential deletes endpoints; for each edge above length one it has rank one with primitive image, and its image is the next kernel. At length one the incidence boundary in a tree has kernel generated by the sums of opposite arcs. The cumulative homology is therefore just H₀ and the primitive alternating top cycles; higher filtration differentials cannot connect occupied diagonal bidegrees. Geodesic triangles and backtracking triangles reduce loops to ordinary tree loops. These arguments extend to the locally finite covering tree by finite subtrees. For 2L<g the consecutive shortest paths both are unique and lift consistently under faces: replacing a concatenation of length ≤L by its geodesic cannot change the endpoint lift, since the comparison closed walk has length ≤2L and hence reduces to the trivial walk. The free deck action on the lifted nerve is properly discontinuous: bounded L and local finiteness give finite closed cell neighborhoods, and only finitely many translates can intersect such a neighborhood, since an intersection entails a common face vertex. Thus its quotient is indeed the universal cover. The embedded graph gives the fundamental group isomorphism, and each two-vertex nerve contributes a sphere whose lifts give one full edge orbit of the integral basis. The map from the graph with spheres is a homology isomorphism upstairs and a homotopy equivalence by Whitehead. Swapping endpoints multiplies the explicit top cycle by (−1)^{L+1}. The supplied checker and output were inspected, not rerun; field Betti numbers are not evidence of integral primitivity or the general homotopy type.

The exact-length magnitude input, cumulative tree case, and cutoff-two theorem are covered material and should not be claimed as new.
