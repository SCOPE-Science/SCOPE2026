For every finite connected graph with a cycle and girth greater than twice an integer cutoff \(L\ge3\), its ordered total-length enriched nerve has the exact integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^{|E|-|V|+1}S^1\vee\bigvee^{|E|}S^L\). The top integral homology is the signed edge-permutation lattice, with endpoint reversal changing sign precisely in even dimensions.
