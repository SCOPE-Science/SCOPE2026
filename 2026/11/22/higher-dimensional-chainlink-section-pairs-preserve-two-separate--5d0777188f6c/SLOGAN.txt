Complementary sections of every admissible chainlink polytope in Euclidean dimension at least three preserve not just intrinsic volume, but the two separate Euclidean facet-area totals for coordinate and cyclic-difference facets. The sections can nevertheless have different vertex counts: the four-coordinate example at heights \(9\) and \(13\) has \(16\) and \(15\) vertices while both have surface area \(43\sqrt3+49\sqrt2\).
