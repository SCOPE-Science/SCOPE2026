The dense-torus anticanonical code of the degree-six toric del Pezzo surface has higher generalized weights \((d_3,d_4,d_5,d_6,d_7)=((q-1)^2-q,(q-1)^2-q+1,(q-1)^2-2,(q-1)^2-1,(q-1)^2)\) for every prime power \(q\ge5\). The third optimum is exactly one rational ruling fiber plus one off-fiber point, while the fourth optimum is a ruling fiber alone.
