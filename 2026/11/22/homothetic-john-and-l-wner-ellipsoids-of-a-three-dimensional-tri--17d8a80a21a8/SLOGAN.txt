The convex hull of \((\cos t,\sin t,\cos2t)\) has exactly computable unrestricted John and Löwner ellipsoids, each supported by four regular-tetrahedron-normalized contacts; remarkably, the outer ellipsoid is precisely twice the inner. This gives exact affine ellipsoidal containment factor \(2\), with ellipsoid volume ratio \(8\), despite the body's nonsymmetric shape.
