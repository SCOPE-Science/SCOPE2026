The canonical five-dimensional generalized symmetric-edge polytope of the exceptional regular matroid \(R_{10}\) has 20 vertices and 12 facets, and its unique John and Löwner ellipsoids are exact homothetic copies with ratio \(\sqrt5\), saturating the sharp symmetric John factor. All 20 vertices and all 12 facets are contacts of the respective ellipsoids, whose common principal-axis anisotropy is \((3+\sqrt5)/2\).
