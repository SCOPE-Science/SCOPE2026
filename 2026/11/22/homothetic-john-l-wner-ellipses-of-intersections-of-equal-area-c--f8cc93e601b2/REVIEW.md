## Correctness — PASS
The equal-determinant identity forces the trace of the normalized matrix difference to vanish. Its eigenvalues are consequently exactly opposite, giving the square-symmetric intersection \(\|z\|^2+s|z_1^2-z_2^2|\le1\). Uniqueness of the John and Löwner optimizers plus quarter-turn symmetry proves global circularity in those normalized coordinates, not just among centered candidates. The exact inner and outer radii follow from the radial function; explicit four-point contact certificates independently confirm the extremality mechanism. Matrix determinants prove the mismatch expression, area ratio, equality cases and sharp limiting family. The standalone checker checked 187 matrix pairs, but the analytic proof is the completeness argument. Nonstandard step checked: trace zero follows from the special 2-by-2 determinant identity, not from higher-dimensional determinant equality.

## Value — PASS
The object—intersection of two equal-area centered ellipses—is a natural affine-invariant lens. Its two canonical optimal ellipses are ordinarily separate variational problems. The general symmetric planar factor-two upper bound is classical; what is specific here is the exact all-pair sharp profile and inverse geometry. Their exact homothety for arbitrary nonaligned, noncongruent input ellipses is a structural theorem; the area ratio is a complete, sharply bounded mismatch statistic with a precise inverse formula and equality/degeneration classification. This is more than a sample computation or an arbitrary parameter slice, and is directly relevant to best inscribed/circumscribed elliptical approximations.

## Limits and relevant literature
Equal area and concentricity are essential; the theorem does not cover translated inputs or higher dimensions. Closest source is W.
