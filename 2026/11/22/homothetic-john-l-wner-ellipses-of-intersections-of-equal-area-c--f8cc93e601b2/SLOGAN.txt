For intersections of any two centered, equal-area ellipses, the unique John and Löwner ellipses are always homothetic with the same shape matrix \(M=(A+B)/2\), and their exact area ratio is \(1+\delta/\sqrt{4+\delta^2}<2\). This sharp ratio uniquely reconstructs the affine spectral mismatch \(\delta=\sqrt{\operatorname{tr}(A^{-1}B)-2}\) of the input ellipses.
