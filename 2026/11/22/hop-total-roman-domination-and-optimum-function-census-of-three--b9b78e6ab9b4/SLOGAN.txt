For every \(n\ge8\), the hop-total Roman domination number of the three-subset Kneser graph is \(2\lfloor n/3\rfloor-\mathbf{1}_{\{3\mid n\}}\). All minimum assignments are partial triple packings: at multiples of three they require a unique labelled-one complementary triple, while the residue-one case also admits exactly one overlapping defender pair; explicit factorial formulas count every optimum.
