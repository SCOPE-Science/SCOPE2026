For Kirchhoff heat smoothing on any equal-arm metric star of degree at least three, the worst short-time squared \(L^2\) approximation error per unit hub-penalized variation is uniquely attained by a jump separating one entire arm from the others. Its exact small-time coefficient is \(2(1-1/m)(2-\sqrt2)/\sqrt\pi\), and the sharp finite-time norm is an explicit Dirichlet–Neumann eigenvalue series throughout a nonempty initial time interval.
