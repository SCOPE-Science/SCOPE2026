Every maximum edge \((t+1)\)-general-position set of the Boolean cube \(Q_d\), for \(d\ge2\) and \(2\le t\le d\), is exactly a union of \(t\) whole coordinate edge classes, so there are \(\binom dt\) optima. More strongly, the full optimal face of the box-constrained fractional geodesic-packing relaxation is the hypersimplex \(\Delta(d,t)\); the previously known extremal cardinality is \(t2^{d-1}\).
