The theorem identifies the precise non-Gaussian random residual after the cancellation of all interior fluctuations, with an error uniform in the two independently growing scales (age of the block and block length). Its explicit two-boundary distribution is useful for finite-window uncertainty quantification in recent-neighbor growth networks and distinguishes these networks from Poisson/central-limit degree-count heuristics. A general independent-displacement theorem would already guarantee qualitative hyperuniformity; the value of the present result is its quantitative model-specific distribution and uniform convergence estimate.
