In exponentially age-preferential multigraphs, the centered incoming-edge count of a long block far from the initial vertex converges in total variation to the difference of two independent, explicitly specified Poisson-binomial boundary fluxes, with error at most \(m(q^{k+1}+2q^L)/(1-q)\). Its fluctuations stay bounded as block length grows, and the stationary indegree field has an exact quadratic spectral zero: a non-Gaussian hyperuniform degree law rather than a central limit theorem.
