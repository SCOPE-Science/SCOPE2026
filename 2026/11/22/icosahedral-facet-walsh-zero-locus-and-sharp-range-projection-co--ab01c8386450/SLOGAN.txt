Convolution by the twenty triangular-face incidence vectors of the regular icosahedron has exactly \(796\) vanishing Walsh modes, characterized by nonedge pairs, their complements, and \(724\) six-sets with \(e(S)-t(S)=5\). The recoverable range has dimension \(3300\), and its globally sharp \(\ell^1\)- and \(\ell^\infty\)-projection constant is \(7029/512\).
