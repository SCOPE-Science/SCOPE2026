The syntactic monoid of the binary factor language \(\{0,1\}^*0^r1^s\{0,1\}^*\) has exactly \(\binom r2+\binom s2+2rs+3\) idempotents when \(r,s\ge2\), with the sharp one-run exception \(\binom{r+s}{2}+3\) when \(\min(r,s)=1\). Every nonidentity idempotent is a retraction of rank at most two, yielding limiting idempotent densities \(3/5\) for balanced long runs and \(1/2\) for one fixed-length run.
