The Shrikhande and \(4\times4\) rook graphs have exactly the same skew-zero-forcing polynomial, with \(4032\) optimal ten-vertex sets each, and each has \(60\) four-vertex plus \(256\) six-vertex inclusion-minimal skew forts. Their smallest obstruction geometries nevertheless differ: the Shrikhande graph has \(32\) triangle-plus-isolate skew forts, whereas the rook graph has none.
