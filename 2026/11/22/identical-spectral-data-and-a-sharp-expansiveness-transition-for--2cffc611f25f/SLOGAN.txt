For bounded two-scale Jordan-block semigroups with identical full, point, and approximate spectra for every coupling exponent, Bowen–Walters positive expansiveness holds exactly when the nilpotent decay exponent \(\gamma\) is at least the diagonal growth exponent \(\beta\). Below this threshold the sharp lower-modulus collapse is \(m(t)\sim a^q e^q(cq^q)^{-1}t^{-(1-q)}\), where \(q=\gamma/\beta\), even though every nonzero orbit escapes and the generator stays bounded below.
