# Review of image-rank-refined dicyclic endomorphism orbits

## Correctness

PASS. The normal-form exhaustion uses only the exact presentation relations, including all coset, central and quaternion-image cases, with no assumption that endomorphisms are invertible or descending. Automorphism conjugation is computed on both generators. The resulting unit-group quotient yields explicit orbit labels and exact sizes, while stabilizers of a quaternion-type generator yield the exceptional three orbits. A direct \(Q_8\) argument treats the small-group discontinuity. A self-contained exact checker reconstructs all homomorphisms and all automorphism orbits for \(2\le m\le12\); these finite checks do not replace the proof.

## Originality

PASS relative to accessible evidence, with historical priority provisional. Hu–Ruan (2022) concern bijective skew morphisms, not arbitrary nonbijective homomorphisms. Madhusudanan–Sudhakara–Seth (2023), §6, restrict to descending endomorphisms and induced graphs, which cannot recover the orbit structure of all endomorphisms, especially the quaternion-type family. Malone (1973), Theorem 2, already counts all endomorphisms for generalized quaternion groups of 2-power order and is credited for that unrefined count; an orbit-refined uniform theorem is not present in the inspected material. Puusemp (2006) concerns determination by the endomorphism semigroup, a different implication. A possible older general conjugacy-class theorem and the inaccessible full Hu–Ruan subscription text remain residual risks.

## Value

PASS. The result completely classifies the symmetry types of *all* self-homomorphisms of an infinite group family, not just automorphisms or retractions. It gives every image-rank orbit count, each orbit size and a compact arithmetic total governed by the odd divisor sum and the 2-adic valuation. The exceptional \(Q_8\)-image stratum explains a genuine group-structural transition rather than a parameter-specific numerical increment.

## Scientific limits

The enumeration covers finitely many parameters and therefore verifies examples, not the universal theorem. Originality is assessed against inspected mathematical publications, not certified against the full historical literature. No independent expert or formal audit has occurred.
