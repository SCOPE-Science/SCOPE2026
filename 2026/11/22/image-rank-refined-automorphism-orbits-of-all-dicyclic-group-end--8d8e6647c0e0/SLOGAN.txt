The complete image-rank-refined automorphism-orbit distribution of all endomorphisms of each dicyclic group has an explicit divisor formula, with three additional quaternion-image orbits precisely when \(m\equiv2\pmod4\). For \(m=2^e h\ge3\) with odd \(h\), the total number of classes is \((3\cdot2^e-1)\sigma(h)+2+\mathbf1_{e\ge1}+3\mathbf1_{e=1}\); the quaternion group of order eight has exactly seven classes.
