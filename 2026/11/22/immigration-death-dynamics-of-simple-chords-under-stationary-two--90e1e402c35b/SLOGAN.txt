In a stationary uniform random perfect matching, repeatedly rewiring two matched pairs turns the number of cyclically adjacent matched pairs into a stationary immigration–death process with birth rate \(2\) and per-pair death rate \(2\), on the time scale of \(n\) rewires. The exact finite-size lag covariance is a geometric eigenmode, and the limiting probability of no adjacent pairs at both times \(0\) and \(t\) is \(\exp(-2+e^{-2t})\).
