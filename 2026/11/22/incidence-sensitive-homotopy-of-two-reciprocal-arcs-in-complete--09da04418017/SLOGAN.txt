For the one-way complete bipartite digraph with shores of sizes \(2\) and \(n\ge2\), adding exactly two reciprocal arcs gives a complete three-orbit integral homotopy classification: disjoint arcs always produce \(S^1\vee S^2\), whereas incident arcs give \(S^1\) for \(n=2,3\) and \(\bigvee^{n^2-5n+5}S^2\) for \(n\ge4\). The disjoint case stabilizes by explicit simplicial collapses when more sink vertices are added.
