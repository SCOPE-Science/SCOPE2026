In a one-leaf corona, all inclusion-minimal super dominating sets correspond exactly to three-colour partitions whose first two colours do not share edges and whose third-colour vertices see both of the first two. For the labelled sunlet \(C_n\circ K_1\), their possible sizes are exactly \(n+2j\) for \(0\leq j\leq\lfloor n/4\rfloor\), with coefficient \(\frac nj\binom{n-2j-1}{2j-1}\) for \(j\geq1\) and two sets at \(j=0\).
