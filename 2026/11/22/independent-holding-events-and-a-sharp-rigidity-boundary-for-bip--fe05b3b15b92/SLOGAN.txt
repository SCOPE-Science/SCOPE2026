For the delete-first, uniform-crossing-edge spanning-tree exchange on \(K_{a,b}\), the indicators of an unchanged update are i.i.d. from every initial tree **exactly** when \(\min(a,b)\le2\); with both sides at least three, stationary successive holding indicators have strictly positive covariance.
