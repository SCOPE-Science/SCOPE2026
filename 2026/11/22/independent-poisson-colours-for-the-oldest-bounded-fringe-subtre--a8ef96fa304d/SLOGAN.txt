In a uniform random recursive tree, the earliest vertices whose entire descendant subtrees have each of several fixed sizes form independent birth-rank Poisson point processes, all with intensity \(x\,dx\) on the \(\sqrt n\) scale. Consequently the size of the very first subtree of size at most \(K\) is asymptotically uniform on \(\{1,\ldots,K\}\), independently of its Rayleigh-scaled birth label; the finer increasing tree shape has its uniform recursive-tree mark law.
