Every independent secure dominating placement on a labelled cycle is a unique circular tiling by two-vertex/one-guard blocks and seven-vertex/three-guard blocks. Consequently its complete size polynomial is \(n[x^n]\log\!\bigl(1/(1-zx^2-z^3x^7)\bigr)\) for every simple cycle of length \(n\ge4\), and these robust placements are exponentially rare among independent dominating sets.
