# Scientific review of the subdivided multi-theta induced-forest classification

**Correctness: PASS.** The critical cycle claim follows because all internal vertices have graph degree two, so each simple cycle uses exactly two whole pole-to-pole arms. Complementing this complete list gives the maximal Alexander-dual faces. In each nonempty arm block the extra vertices are dominated by a fixed representative, reducing to the \((m-3)\)-skeleton of a simplex. That skeleton has free top reduced homology of rank \(m-1\). Combinatorial Alexander duality shifts the unique nonzero integral group to degree \(N-m\). Triangle-freeness gives the entire two-skeleton and trivial fundamental group, while \(N-m\ge2\) permits Hurewicz and Whitehead to establish the wedge rather than only its homology. Exhaustive finite checks are corroborative and do not substitute for these deductions.

**Originality: PASS against inspected evidence.** The primary 2023 forest-filtration work covers the non-subdivided bipartite \(K_{2,m}\) specialization, chorded cycles with a direct edge, and cactus graphs, but its discussed claims do not imply arbitrary internally vertex-disjoint paths with different subdivision lengths. The 2024 theta independence-complex calculation studies independent vertex sets, a different invariant. Semantic record searches for aliases and variable-length theta forest complexes did not identify a stronger covering result; known indexed work on multipath complexes or graph configuration spaces concerns different objects. This is a best-knowledge comparison, not a uniqueness certificate. Potential unindexed variants or general homological results imply a residual bibliographic risk.

**Value: PASS.** The classification treats an entire natural series-parallel family that lies just beyond the cactus-graph theorem: all cycles overlap in two poles, yet the homotopy type remains a single-degree bouquet. The result distinguishes the number of independent pole-to-pole paths (sphere rank) from arbitrary internal subdivisions (dimension), yielding an exact integral invariant, not simply a restatement of the known complete-bipartite case. It has a complete proof and a sharp stated domain; no claim is made for finite-degree filtration stages or extra chords.

**Closest literature and limitations.** A. Carnero Bravo (arXiv:2307.13271), Theorem 31 and Theorem 34, is the principal comparison. Przytycki–Silvero (2024), Example 3.8, covers ordinary independence complexes of three-arm theta graphs, not the induced-forest condition. Only the ordinary integral homotopy type is established; the all-length symmetry module and persistence maps are not assessed.
