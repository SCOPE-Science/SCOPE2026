A detector placement on the sixteen-vertex Clebsch graph power dominates precisely when it contains an independent triple or an induced three-vertex path: this yields exactly \(320\) minimum three-sensor placements and sixty maximal failed four-sensor placements, with unique completion of each failed triple. The optimal placements form a connected \(21\)-regular exchange graph of diameter three, with every placement having the same distance profile \((1,21,126,172)\).
