In the published three-dimensional symmetric sine map, the positive net gain produces a complete fixed-point staircase with asymptotically \(2k/\pi\) stationary states. Along pure sine feedback, infinitely many *disjoint* gain windows contain a pair of attracting nonzero fixed points, their widths decay sharply as \(2/((2m+1/2)\pi)\), and every such window persists as multistability in an open three-parameter region.
