For every fixed \(r\ge2\), the starting positions of \(r\) consecutive missing part sizes in a uniformly random partition of \(n\), scaled by \(n^{r/[2(r+1)]}\), converge to an inhomogeneous Poisson point process of intensity \((\pi/\sqrt6)^r x^r\,dx\). Thus the first \(r\)-chain minimal excludant has a sharp Weibull-type survival law \(\exp[-(\pi/\sqrt6)^r y^{r+1}/(r+1)]\), with all subsequent onset locations governed by the same process.
