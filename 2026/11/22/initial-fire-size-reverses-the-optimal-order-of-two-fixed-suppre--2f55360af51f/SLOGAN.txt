For a two-active-site birth–death–suppression model, swapping identical prescribed high- and low-intensity episodes reverses the finite-deadline extinction ranking according to initial fire size, not episode duration: the exact initial-state mixing threshold is \(\alpha_*=(d-m)/(d-m+\lambda)\) when \(d>m\). With natural per-site losses \(d=2m\), strong-first always favors extinction from one active site, while strong-last always favors extinction from two; a one-site capacity has no ordering effect.
