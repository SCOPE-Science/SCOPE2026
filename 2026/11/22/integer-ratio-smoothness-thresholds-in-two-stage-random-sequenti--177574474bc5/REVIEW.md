# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof reconstructs the exact finite-rod gap decomposition and the finite-interval RSA recurrence, shows their continuum limits uniformly where needed, and derives the all-ratio integral. The method-of-steps identity gives the exact unique first discontinuous derivative of the finite-interval count at each integer, and differentiation under the smooth kernel transfers it to the coverage. The positive integral rules out cancellation. The included numerical checker independently replays both the discrete and continuum formulas for small and moderate rod sizes; no finite test is used as an infinite proof. Main assumptions: first thermodynamic limit, then fixed-ratio large-rod limit, large-first staged protocol.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Pinsky's exact discrete staged model and gap formulas are prior work, and his continuum theorem explicitly stops at ratios at most two. The new all-ratio asymptotic and infinite differentiability-jump hierarchy are not implied by his stated continuum result without a separate scaling and regularity argument. Earlier Hassan et al. and Reeve–Wattis competitive binary adsorption use continuously mixed arrivals (or an asymptotic small-short-species-arrival regime), and their inspected formulas do not settle this fixed-ratio staged limit. Related indexed discrete gap-monotonicity results concern a different statistic. Full-text primary comparisons and semantic searches found no covering theorem. Residual publication-priority risk: equivalent formulas may exist in literature about staged deposition or parking with postprocessing under different terminology.

## Value — PASS

The full fixed-ratio limit answers an explicit mathematical-physics extension raised in a recent primary article. More substantially, the positive jump coefficients classify an entire hierarchy of otherwise invisible nonanalytic onset thresholds when successively larger numbers of short rods fit into a first-stage vacancy. A complete natural parameter-axis classification supports analysis of coverage response to relative rod size without requiring a downstream theorem.

## Limits

No result is claimed for simultaneous mixed arrivals, joint scaling of substrate length, finite-time kinetics, or concentration of coverage. Exact jump coefficients are one-sided continuum-limit derivatives. Bibliographic priority is not established by the absence of search hits alone.
