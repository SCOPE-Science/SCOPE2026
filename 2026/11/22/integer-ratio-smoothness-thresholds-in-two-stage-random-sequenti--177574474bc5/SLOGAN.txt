Saturating an infinite line first with long rods and then with short rods has an exact continuum expected-coverage law for every fixed length ratio \(L>1\). Every integer ratio \(m\ge2\) creates a new, progressively weaker singularity: the coverage is \(C^{m-1}\) across \(m\), but its \(m\)-th derivative jumps upward by \(2^m I/[m^2(m-1)!]\), with an explicit universal positive constant \(I\).
