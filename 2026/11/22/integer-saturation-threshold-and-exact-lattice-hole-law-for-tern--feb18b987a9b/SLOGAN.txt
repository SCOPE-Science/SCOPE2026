For ternary words of fixed positive content, the three independent scattered two-deck coordinates fill every lattice point of their exact convex hull if and only if no more than one letter appears repeatedly. With one singleton and other multiplicities \(b,c\), the exact number of missing lattice points is \(\big((b^2-1)(c^2-1)+(\gcd(b,c)^2-1)\big)/6\), asymptotically one-quarter of the hull lattice points when both multiplicities grow.
