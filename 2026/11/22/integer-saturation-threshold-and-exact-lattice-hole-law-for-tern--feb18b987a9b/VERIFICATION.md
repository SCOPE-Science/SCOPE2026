# Verification and reproducibility

The computation programs use Python 3 standard-library arithmetic. They implement the complete multiset-word deck scan, integer point enumeration in the proposed polytope, two independent calculations of exact singleton holes and a separate binary interleaving construction. All required source files are embedded alongside this document.

Run `python3 verify_deck_polytope.py`. Its captured output is `verify_deck_polytope.txt`: 60 positive content triples through total length ten were directly compared with their entire integer hull; the singleton hole formula was also checked against 625 distinct double sums for \(1\le b,c\le25\).

Run `python3 verify_deck_gap_slices.py`. Its captured output is `verify_deck_gap_slices.txt`: 1,301 singleton gap slices were generated by independent binary interleavings and checked against the claimed attainable interval and exact lattice-hole count.

The six-point hull proof, explicit hole obstruction, and exact fractional-part summation are the unrestricted mathematical certificates. Exhaustive finite output is not proof for all multiplicities, and no independent expert certification is claimed.
