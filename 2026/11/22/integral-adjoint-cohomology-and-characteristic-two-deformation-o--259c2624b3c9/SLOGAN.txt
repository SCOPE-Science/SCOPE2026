The canonical six-dimensional toral extension of the defining \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\)-module has only \(2\)-primary integral adjoint cohomology, with degrees \(1\) through \(6\) of ranks \((1,3,6,9,8,3)\), and none in other characteristics. Its characteristic-two cohomology has Betti vector \((1,4,9,15,17,11,3)\), and the new bracket \([x,y]=\lambda(h+t)\) gives a nontrivial integrable deformation.
