For every one-edge-spined book of \(h\ge2\) hexagons, the graph-metric scale-two Vietoris–Rips complex has an integral top-homology basis freely permuted by the arms, while pole reversal preserves every basis class. Every subgroup of the full automorphism group has genuine geometric orbit space homotopy equivalent to one two-sphere per orbit of arms; in particular the full quotient is \(S^2\), unlike the contractible full quotient of two-edge-spined books.
