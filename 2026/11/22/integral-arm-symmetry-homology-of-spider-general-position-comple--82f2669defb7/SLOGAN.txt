For the geodesic general-position complex of a spider with \(r\ge3\) equal-length arms, the full arm-permutation action on integral homology is an explicit augmentation-plus-edge lattice in degree one and a sign-twisted tensor augmentation lattice in degree \(r-1\). The geometric quotient has only \(\binom{\ell}{2}+\ell-1\) rational circle classes and \(\binom{\ell-1}{r}\) high-dimensional classes, so the latter vanish sharply when the arm length \(\ell\le r\).
