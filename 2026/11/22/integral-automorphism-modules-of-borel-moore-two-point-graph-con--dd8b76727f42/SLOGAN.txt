For every finite connected graph, the untwisted integral Borel–Moore homology of its unordered two-point configuration space is naturally \(\bigwedge^2H_1(G;\mathbb Z)\) in degree two and \(H_1(G;\mathbb Z/2)\) in degree one, with all other degrees zero. This identifies the entire integral graph-automorphism action, rather than only the previously known homology-group ranks.
