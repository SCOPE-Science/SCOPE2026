The three realizable complex nine-line \((3,3)\)-nets have three distinct integral graph Jacobians after blowing up every triple point: \(C_5\oplus C_{15}^{2}\oplus C_{45}^{2}\oplus C_{135}\), \(C_3\oplus C_{45}^{2}\oplus C_{135}\oplus C_{405}\), and \(C_3\oplus C_9^4\oplus C_{27}^3\). The five-primary torsion disappears completely in the Ceva twelve-triple case, even though all three reduced boundaries have exactly 36 nodes.
