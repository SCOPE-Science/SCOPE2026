The canonical \(316\)-vertex graph joining the \(28\) bitangents of a smooth plane quartic to its \(288\) Aronhold heptads has critical group \(\mathbb Z/16\oplus(\mathbb Z/64)^{26}\oplus(\mathbb Z/7)^{235}\oplus(\mathbb Z/49)^{26}\). Its spanning-tree count is \(2^{160}7^{287}\), while the previously known rectangular incidence cokernel is a different invariant.
