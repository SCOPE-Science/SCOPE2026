For the canonical incidence graph joining the 27 lines of a smooth complex cubic surface to its 45 tritangent planes, \(K(\Gamma)\cong(\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z)^{10}\oplus(\mathbb Z/9\mathbb Z)^5\oplus(\mathbb Z/27\mathbb Z)^8\oplus(\mathbb Z/135\mathbb Z)^6\). It has exactly \(3^{62}5^6\) spanning trees, while the integral group gives strictly finer information than the spectrum.
