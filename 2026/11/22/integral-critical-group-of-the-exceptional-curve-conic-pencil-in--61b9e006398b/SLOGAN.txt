The canonical 16-by-10 exceptional-curve/conic-pencil incidence graph of a smooth complex quartic del Pezzo surface has critical group \(\mathbb Z/5\oplus(\mathbb Z/20)^4\oplus\mathbb Z/80\oplus(\mathbb Z/320)^4\). Its 80 edges encode the components of forty reducible conic fibers, while the exact spanning-tree count is \(2^{36}5^{10}\).
