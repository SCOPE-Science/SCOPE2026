The full intersection-multiplicity multigraph on the 240 exceptional curves of a smooth degree-one del Pezzo surface has exact critical group \((\mathbb Z/240\mathbb Z)^{222}\oplus(\mathbb Z/72000\mathbb Z)^8\). The eight enlarged factors reflect the rank-eight \(E_8\) root correction; unlike the simple intersection-one graph, the complete weighted group has no seven-primary torsion.
