The intersection-one graph of the 56 exceptional curves on any smooth complex degree-two del Pezzo surface has critical group \((\mathbb Z/2)^{14}\oplus(\mathbb Z/4)^7\oplus(\mathbb Z/28)^5\oplus(\mathbb Z/336)^{14}\oplus\mathbb Z/3024\oplus(\mathbb Z/6048)^6\). Unlike the usual Gosset graph, its edges connect curves meeting once rather than disjoint curves, and its exact spanning-tree count is \(2^{128}3^{35}7^{26}\).
