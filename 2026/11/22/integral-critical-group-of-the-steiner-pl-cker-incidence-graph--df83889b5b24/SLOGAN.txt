The complete integral graph Jacobian of the classical twenty-Steiner-point/fifteen-Plücker-line configuration is \((\mathbb Z/2)^2\oplus(\mathbb Z/10)^2\oplus(\mathbb Z/30)^2\oplus(\mathbb Z/180)^4\). Its 35-vertex Levi graph has \(2^{14}3^{10}5^8\) spanning trees, and the same group occurs as the geometric component group for regular unit-thickness semistable genus-26 smoothings with that dual graph.
