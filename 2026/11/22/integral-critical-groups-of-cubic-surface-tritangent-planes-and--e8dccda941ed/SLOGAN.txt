The graph of the \(45\) tritangent planes of a smooth cubic surface has critical group \((\mathbb Z/3)^6\oplus(\mathbb Z/15)^4\oplus(\mathbb Z/45)^5\oplus(\mathbb Z/135)^{14}\). The second graph in Spence's \((45,12,3,3)\) catalogue has exactly the same eigenvalues and tree count but a different critical group, proving that this natural integral geometric invariant is invisible to the ordinary spectrum.
