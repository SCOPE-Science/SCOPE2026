# Integral first off-diagonal magnitude homology of the Pappus graph

## Finding
Let \(P\) be the eighteen-vertex Pappus graph in LCF notation \([5,7,-7,7,-7,-5]^3\), with the shortest-path metric \(d\). For every ordered pair of vertices \((a,b)\),
\[
\operatorname{MH}_{3,4}^{a,b}(P;\mathbb Z)\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z^2,&d(a,b)=2,\\
\mathbb Z,&d(a,b)=4,\\
0,&d(a,b)\in\{0,1,3\}.
\end{cases}
\]
The distance-shell populations are \((1,3,6,6,2)\) from every vertex, so
\[
\operatorname{MH}_{3,4}(P;\mathbb Z)\cong \mathbb Z^{108\cdot 2+36}=\mathbb Z^{252}.
\]
All endpoint summands are torsion-free. The numerical mod-two ranks were previously known; the new statement lifts the classification integrally and excludes torsion of **every prime**.

## Assumptions and scope
The graph has vertices \(0,\ldots,17\), a cycle edge \(\{i,i+1\pmod{18}\}\) for each \(i\), and a chord \(\{i,i+s_i\pmod{18}\}\), where the sequence \((s_i)\) repeats \((5,7,-7,7,-7,-5)\) three times. All graph distances and all magnitude chains use this exact graph, without edge weights. In degree \(k\), the length-four magnitude chain group is freely generated by tuples of \(k+1\) vertices with neighboring entries distinct and total distance four. Nonconsecutive repeats are allowed. The signed boundary deletes an internal vertex at index \(i\) with sign \((-1)^i\), only when metric length is preserved. Fixing the two endpoints gives a direct-sum decomposition of the chain complex over \(\mathbb Z\).

## Proof
Construct all chains in degrees two, three and four for each of the \(18^2=324\) ordered endpoint pairs. Every chain of total metric length four has at most four edges, so these are all the chain degrees needed to compute third homology: fifth chains are impossible. Let \(B_3(a,b)\) and \(B_4(a,b)\) be the exact signed integral boundary matrices. The exhaustive enumeration gives the following complete profiles, uniform for every pair at the indicated distance:

| \(d(a,b)\) | Number of pairs | \(\operatorname{rank} C_2\) | \(\operatorname{rank} C_3\) | \(\operatorname{rank} C_4\) | \(\operatorname{rank}B_3\) | \(\operatorname{rank}B_4\) | \(\operatorname{rank} H_3\) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 18 | 6 | 18 | 15 | 6 | 12 | 0 |
| 1 | 54 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 2 | 108 | 7 | 18 | 9 | 7 | 9 | 2 |
| 3 | 108 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 4 | 36 | 12 | 18 | 6 | 11 | 6 | 1 |

For each of all 324 pairs, direct exact multiplication gives \(B_3B_4=0\). Every nonzero Smith diagonal factor of both signed matrices is exactly \(1\), and in particular every image \(\operatorname{im} B_4\) is a **primitive** sublattice of the free group \(C_3\). Thus \(C_3/\operatorname{im}B_4\) is free abelian. Since \(H_3=\ker B_3/\operatorname{im} B_4\) is a subgroup of this free abelian quotient, it has no torsion. Its free rank is \(\operatorname{rank}C_3-\operatorname{rank}B_3-\operatorname{rank}B_4\), yielding the table and the stated decomposition. This is a complete finite exact integer proof, not an inference from finite sampling or a field calculation.

## Verification
Run `python3 pappus_exact.py` with Python 3 and SymPy. The script reconstructs the LCF graph, computes every all-pairs shortest-path distance by breadth-first search, enumerates all possible degree-two, -three and -four length-four chains with proper consecutive vertices, builds signed boundary matrices, checks every \(B_3B_4=0\), and computes all 648 Smith normal forms over \(\mathbb Z\). See `pappus_exact_output.txt` for the actual replay. The chain profiles, the unit elementary divisors and the total free rank are reproducible from the included code.

## Relationship to prior work
The existing Pappus graph calculation identifies exactly the same endpoint ranks over \(\mathbb F_2\), but expressly makes **no** claim about integral torsion or other coefficients [4]. Its ranks do not imply torsion-freeness over \(\mathbb Z\), because odd-primary torsion is invisible over \(\mathbb F_2\). The new signed Smith computation rules out every such possibility and provides the integral endpoint classification. The published magnitude-chain and geometry frameworks [1–3] do not tabulate this integral Pappus calculation; the girth-based vanishing theorem applies at girth at least seven, whereas this graph has girth six.

## Limitations
This is a complete calculation for one named graph and the one bidegree \((3,4)\), not an infinite-family formula, a determination of other bidegrees, or a new mod-two rank statement. The computation proves group structure but does not specify the full integral automorphism representation or a canonical integral geometric basis. Literature comparisons cannot exclude every unindexed equivalent calculation; the most relevant indexed characteristic-two predecessor is directly acknowledged.
