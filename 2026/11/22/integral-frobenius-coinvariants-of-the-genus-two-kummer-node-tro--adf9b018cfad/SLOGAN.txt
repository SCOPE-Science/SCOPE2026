For every smooth genus-two curve over an odd finite field, the formal Kummer node–trope incidence cokernel has an exact eleven-type Frobenius coinvariant classification, and every resulting group contains a cyclic element of order twelve. Two Weierstrass splitting patterns with identical Frobenius orbit profiles on the sixteen nodes and sixteen tropes yield nonisomorphic coinvariant groups of the same order \(3072\).
