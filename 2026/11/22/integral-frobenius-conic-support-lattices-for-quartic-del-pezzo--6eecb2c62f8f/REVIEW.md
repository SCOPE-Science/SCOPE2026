# Review of the integral Frobenius conic-support classification

## Correctness (C): PASS
The result concerns the exact cokernel of a specified map between Frobenius-fixed **free permutation lattices**, not the Picard lattice. On the geometric blow-up of five general points, the sixteen exceptional-line classes and ten conic classes are explicit. For each conic class the four reducible fibers give exactly eight distinct components. The resulting \(16\)-by-\(10\) binary map is reconstructed directly, not read from a database. Its row words identify the standard even-signed \(D_5\) action on the sixteen lines and ten pencils. Frobenius invariants have integral orbit-sum bases, and the matrix entry is the number of pencil-orbit members incident to any fixed line in a line orbit. All nonzero entries are integers; no rational averaging is used. The verifier enumerates all 1920 Weyl elements, checks each of the 18 classes under conjugation, and performs exact unimodular Smith reductions of the corresponding matrices. An independent SymPy Smith implementation agreed for one representative of every type. The positive five-cycle has an additional hand-checkable \(4\)-by-\(2\) matrix of Smith factors \(1,10\). This finite exhaustive calculation is a valid certificate once the standard del Pezzo/Weyl construction is established. It makes no claim about geometrically impossible Frobenius realizations over a given base field.

## Mathematical value (V): PASS
The ten conic fibrations and sixteen exceptional curves are intrinsic finite geometric configurations of a quartic del Pezzo surface. When Galois action is nontrivial, determining whether integer orbit-summed conic supports saturate the lattice of rational formal line-orbit combinations is a natural integral question, qualitatively different from rank or point counts. The complete result identifies a previously unreported **five-primary obstruction** in the basic positive five-cycle class and classifies the two-primary/free defects in every other arithmetic Weyl type. It is an exact natural invariant with a concrete reconstruction interpretation, not a random small-prime computation or a specialization of the geometric Smith form.
