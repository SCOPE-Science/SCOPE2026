The ten conic pencils on a smooth quartic del Pezzo surface determine a canonical integral support map to its sixteen exceptional-line labels. Across all eighteen Frobenius types, its invariant-lattice cokernel is torsion-free in eleven types, has \(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\) torsion in six, and has \(\mathbb Z/10\mathbb Z\) torsion for the positive five-cycle, even though the geometric map has only a factor of two.
