The reduced twelve-line Hesse boundary has a complete, characteristic-uniform arithmetic zeta formula and covers every projective point precisely over \(\mathbb F_2\), \(\mathbb F_4\), and \(\mathbb F_7\). More strongly, when \(q\equiv2\pmod3\), its \(28\)-dimensional Picard torus splits **integrally** as \(\mathbb G_m^3\times R^1_{\mathbb F_{q^2}/\mathbb F_q}\mathbb G_m\times(\operatorname{Res}_{\mathbb F_{q^2}/\mathbb F_q}\mathbb G_m)^{12}\), with a \(27\)-dimensional unipotent part.
