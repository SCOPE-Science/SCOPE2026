For odd prime degree \(\ell\ge5\), the connected Picard torus of the \(3\ell^2\) standard lines on a Fermat surface is integrally \(\mathbb G_m\times(\operatorname{Res}_{\mathbb F_{q^d}/\mathbb F_q}\mathbb G_m)^{(3\ell^3+3\ell^2-6\ell)/d}\), where \(d\) is the order of \(q\) modulo \(\ell\). This determines the full finite abelian group of rational points after adding a split unipotent factor of dimension \(3\ell(\ell-1)(\ell-2)\).
