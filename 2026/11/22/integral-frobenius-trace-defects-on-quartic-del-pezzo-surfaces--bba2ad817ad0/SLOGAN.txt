The sixteen exceptional curves on a degree-four del Pezzo surface generate the Frobenius-invariant Picard lattice only up to integral index \(1,2,4,\) or \(16\), with exact frequencies \(12,3,2,1\) across the eighteen Weyl types. Four types have identical curve-orbit splitting \(4^4\) yet have all four different indices, so even full exceptional-curve zeta data cannot reconstruct the integral trace lattice.
