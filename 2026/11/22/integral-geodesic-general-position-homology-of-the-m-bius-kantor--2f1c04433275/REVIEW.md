# Scientific review: integral geodesic general-position homology of the Möbius–Kantor graph

## Correctness — PASS

The claim is restricted to the finite graph \(\operatorname{GP}(8,3)\), the given shortest-path betweenness definition, and integral homology. An exhaustive check of 65,536 subsets produces face counts 16, 120, 224, 220 and 80, from 336 prohibited triples. The appended certificate supplies 227 valid elementary collapse pairs and replays each free-face condition. Exact signed integral boundaries of the resulting 16/85/77/28-cell simplicial complex have ranks 15/46/27 and exclusively unit Smith factors; hence the claimed ranks and absence of torsion. The original and collapsed Euler characteristics agree at −20. No homotopy wedge is claimed.

Full result texts of the closest same-graph two-independence result, the bipartite-corona general-position theorem, and the path-ladder general-position homology theorem were inspected and found inapplicable to this finite graph-metric complex; unindexed equivalent literature remains a risk.

## Value — PASS

The Möbius–Kantor graph is the canonical sixteen-vertex cubic symmetric incidence graph of the \((8_3)\) configuration. Its geodesic general-position complex has nontrivial **integral** homology simultaneously in three positive dimensions, a structural property not fixed by the maximum permissible vertex-set size or face vector. The finite classification gives an exact benchmark against homology of other natural complexes on the same graph and for geodesic topology of incidence graphs; in particular the integral torsion question is settled for this concrete object. Its scope and prospective use are finite and explicit, rather than an unproved family theorem.
