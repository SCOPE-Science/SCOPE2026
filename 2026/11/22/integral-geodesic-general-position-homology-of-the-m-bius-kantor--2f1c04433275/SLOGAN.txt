The geodesic general-position complex of the Möbius–Kantor graph has reduced integral homology \(\mathbb Z^{24}\), \(\mathbb Z^4\), and \(\mathbb Z\) in degrees one, two, and three, respectively, and no other reduced homology. A complete face census, 227 elementary collapses and exact unimodular boundary reductions establish the torsion-free, three-degree result without asserting a homotopy classification.
