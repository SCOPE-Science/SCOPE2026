For every \(n\ge13\), deleting any single edge from the third power of the \(n\)-cycle changes its open neighborhood complex to a space with reduced integral homology \(\mathbb Z\) in degree one and \(\mathbb Z^2\) in degree two, with no torsion or other reduced homology. The relative fault response is uniformly \(\mathbb Z^2\) in degree three, for all three edge-distance orbits, even though the intact complexes alternate between orientable and twisted sphere bundles.
