The ordinary fivefold column-rotation quotient of the hard-square independence complex on \(C_4\square C_5\) has \(\pi_1\cong C_5\), integral homology \(H_1\cong\mathbb Z/5\), \(H_3\cong\mathbb Z^2\), and \(H_4\cong\mathbb Z\), with other positive homology zero. Its canonical mod-five degree-one class has a nonzero product with its Bockstein in degree three, so the quotient is not a wedge of the corresponding degree-one Moore complex and spheres.
