For the simplicial complex of subsets of the Boolean \(n\)-cube containing no three corners of a coordinate square, complete reduced integral homology is computed for \(2\le n\le5\): at \(n=4\) it occupies two dimensions, while at \(n=5\) it is precisely \(\mathbb Z^{681}\) in degree seven. All four cases are torsion-free, certified by exhaustive signed Morse reductions and integral Smith operations.
