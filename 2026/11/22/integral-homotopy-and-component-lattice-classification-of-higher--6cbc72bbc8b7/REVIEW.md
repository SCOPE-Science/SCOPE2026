# Review of the higher-tolerance graph homotopy theorem

## Correctness
PASS. The input is explicitly the unfilled 1-dimensional graph complex, so the tolerance complex has complete lower skeleton and precisely edge-containing top simplices. In the integral augmented exterior-chain model, top boundaries span exactly the exterior ideal generated by graph-edge differences. Those differences form a saturated graph-component augmentation sublattice, making the quotient torsion-free with the stated ranks and naturality. For tolerance at least two, Hurewicz identifies all top-cell attaching classes with the cellular boundary matrix, and its primitive image permits integral unit cancellations. The independent checker passed 104,416 finite tests, but no claim of an infinite proof is based on those tests.

## Originality
PASS under the investigated sources, with residual unindexed-priority risk. The 2026 tolerance-complex paper treats collapsibility, Lerayness and symbolic powers, not the exact all-graph homotopy or integral exterior-lattice structure. Kim–Lew's earlier tolerance bounds likewise do not imply the decomposition; their bounded-independence graph complex is generally larger than the specified tolerance complex. Prior exact one-tolerance results concern only tolerance one. The present claim covers every integer tolerance at least two and arbitrary graphs. Searches for the exact statement, its component-augmentation equivalence, higher-tolerance variants and broader covering theorems found no decisive coverage. This is a best-knowledge comparison, not an exhaustive priority guarantee.

## Value
PASS. This completes a natural infinite tolerance ladder above the previously known one-tolerance boundary, detects mixed-dimensional homotopy and supplies an integral automorphism-equivariant description of the lower homology. Its all-graph formula depends on two familiar, independently meaningful invariants (components and independent sets), while the primitive boundary argument rules out torsion and nontrivial attaching obstructions. This is more than recomputing a fixed graph table or restating a Leray bound.

## Closest sources and limitations
Selvaraja (arXiv:2611.14384, 2026-11-10) motivates higher tolerance but only provides numerical bounds for the pertinent comparisons. Kim and Lew (arXiv:2109.03030) introduce the construction and develop general bounds. Kim and Lew (arXiv:1912.12605) analyze a different, less truncated bounded-independence complex. Preexisting exact tolerance-one graph results do not include tolerance two or higher. The input excludes filled graph triangles; top-degree integral representation structure and higher-dimensional input complexes are outside the claim. Unindexed work and independent validation remain residual risks.
