For every finite graph regarded as an unfilled one-dimensional complex, all tolerance levels \(t\ge2\) have integral homotopy type a wedge of spheres in only dimensions \(t\) and \(t+1\), with exact ranks determined by graph components and independent sets. The lower-degree integral homology is naturally the \((t+1)\)-st exterior power of the component augmentation lattice, and the higher attaching maps can be canceled using a primitive integral boundary image.
