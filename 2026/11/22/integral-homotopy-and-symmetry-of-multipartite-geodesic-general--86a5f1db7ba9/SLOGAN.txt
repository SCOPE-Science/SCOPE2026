For every connected complete multipartite graph \(K_{a_1,\ldots,a_t}\) with \(t\ge2\), the simplicial complex of geodesic general-position vertex sets is exactly \(\bigvee^{\sum_i(a_i-1)}S^1\vee\bigvee^{\prod_i(a_i-1)}S^{t-1}\) up to integral homotopy. For \(t\ge3\), its integral first-homology module is the direct sum of the within-part augmentation lattices, while its top homology is their tensor product with a sign on part exchanges.
