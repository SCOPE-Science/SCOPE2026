Deleting \(t\) disjoint edges from \(K_{p,q}\) gives a complete integral induced-forest homotopy classification: after the first deletion, the complex is a wedge of \(\beta_{p,q,t}\) three-spheres and \(\binom t3\) four-spheres, with \(\beta_{p,q,t}=(t-1)(p-1)(q-1)-(p+q-3)\binom t2+\binom t3\). The second deleted edge creates \((p-2)(q-2)\) three-spheres, and the third initiates a new four-dimensional homology family.
