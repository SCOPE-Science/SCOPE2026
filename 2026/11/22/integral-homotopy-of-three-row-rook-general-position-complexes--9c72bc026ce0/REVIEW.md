# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The face condition in the three-row rook graph is exactly a forbidden three-edge path in the corresponding bipartite graph. Any selected column with two rows forces the other chosen cells into the sole unused row and excludes a second repeated column. This gives an exact union of a join of three-point sets, contractible thickening simplices, and one full triangle per column. Distinct thickening simplices intersect within the join; the intersection of each with the join is contractible, hence the join inclusion is a homotopy equivalence. Its known sphere-wedge homotopy type is simply connected for \(n\ge3\), so the remaining column triangles attach along nullhomotopic loops. These steps prove the complete integral homotopy claim. Independent finite geodesic enumeration and mod-two chain ranks agree for \(3\le n\le6\). No inference beyond those numerical cases is made from computation.

## Originality — PASS with residual priority risk

A complete rectangular-rook general-position enumerator and a separate maximal-set enumerator provide the star-forest face characterization and all face counts, but neither their inspected full RESULT texts nor their formal claims contain a homotopy calculation. The new result is not claimed to discover that face characterization; it proves a uniform homotopy splitting by contractible simplex attachments and simultaneous null circle maps. The inspected recent arXiv papers on geodesic-independence invariants and general-position polynomials concern scalar invariants and enumerators, not this exact topological classification. Targeted semantic searches under rook-graph, \(P_4\)-free edge-set, star-forest complex, line-graph, and sphere-wedge aliases did not reveal a theorem implying the present homotopy type. Unindexed or differently formulated work is an unresolved bibliographic possibility; publication priority is not certified.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This is a complete classification for a canonical infinite graph-product family with a natural simplicial structure. For \(n\ge4\) the complex has simultaneously \(n\) low-dimensional two-spheres and \(2^n\) spheres in growing dimension \(n-1\), yet has simplices of dimension \(n\). The split inclusion of the join core distinguishes the old counting information from genuinely new homotopy information, including integral torsion-freeness and null attaching maps. The exponential-versus-linear growth and nonpure homotopy dimensions supply structural, not arbitrarily sliced, motivation.

## Scope and limits

The restriction \(n\ge3\) is essential to the simply-connected-core proof; the two-column case is not included. No general result for four or more rows, no equivariant equivalence, and no independent validation is asserted. The proof relies on established homotopy gluing and join facts, and on the exact face classification reconstructed explicitly in the manuscript.
