For the perfect-matching extendibility complexes of the hexagonal, octagonal, and decagonal bipyramid graphs, the integral homotopy types are respectively \(\bigvee^4 S^1\), \(\bigvee^3 S^2\), and \(S^2\vee S^3\vee S^3\). The decagonal bipyramid is the first mixed-dimensional homology case among these consecutive even bipyramids.
