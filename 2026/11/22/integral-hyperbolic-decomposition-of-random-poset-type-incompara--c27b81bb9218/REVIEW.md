# Review of the integral incomparability type form

## Correctness — PASS
The full claim is proved for each finite named chain by a five-step construction. Every one-type corresponds to an interval of cuts. Two such types can be realized incomparably exactly when their intervals meet, with necessity and sufficiency justified by transitivity and the random poset extension property. All interior rows are explicit integer combinations of boundary rows; a unimodular basis transform reduces the boundary Gram form to one unit and hyperbolic planes. Integer-exact code independently checked sizes through nine and all two-point transitivity cases through five. Finite checks corroborate but do not replace the universal proof.

## Originality — PASS (provisional historical priority)
The closest fully read published findings are *Order-polynomial spectra of tuple types over chains in the generic partial order* and *Six rank-three theories of complete discrete-interval intersection graphs*. The former has scalar tuple enumerators, not the integer form; the latter studies FO rank-three equivalence of loopless interval graphs, not the shifted looped matrix. The arXiv random-poset reduct and interval-graph model-checking papers, and the 1986 matrix-profile paper, were checked in relevant sections and do not give this all-size unimodular congruence. An unindexed equivalent interval-matrix identity is a substantive residual risk; search absence is not novelty proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The exact integral hyperbolic form supplies much more than a rank table: an explicit primitive kernel basis, full rank stability in every characteristic, no Smith torsion and real signature one, for a natural existential relation on exponentially many ordered pairs of a quadratic type set. The entire arithmetic core is concentrated on boundary types, exposing a uniform redundancy principle.

## Limitations
The parameter base must be a chain, and the matrix encodes existential pairwise incomparability, not all two-point types or arbitrary finite parameter posets. An independent expert validation and exhaustive historical priority search have not been performed.
