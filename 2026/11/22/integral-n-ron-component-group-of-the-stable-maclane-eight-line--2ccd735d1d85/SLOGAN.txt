The natural sixteen-component stable boundary obtained by blowing up the eight triple points of either MacLane eight-line arrangement has genus \(13\) and graph/Néron component group \(\mathbb Z/2\oplus\mathbb Z/6\oplus(\mathbb Z/12)^2\oplus(\mathbb Z/48)^2\). An exact integral Smith certificate gives its full invariant factors, beyond the classical incidence counts.
