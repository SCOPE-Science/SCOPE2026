# Mathematical review: primitive-position Ramanujan Gram matrices

## Correctness — PASS

The local Smith computations are reconstructible from the exact prime-power Ramanujan identity and the determinantal divisors of the complete-graph Laplacian. For a prime power, the unit restriction changes both local block counts and rank; the proof explicitly handles the exponent-one, higher-exponent, and prime-two cases. The Chinese-remainder step is integral (permutation followed by tensoring integer unimodular transformations). Primewise sorting gives canonical invariant factors; counts and the squarefree criterion follow. The packaged checker independently computed 39 exact integer Smith forms. This is finite corroboration, not a substitute for the all-modulus proof.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge)

Fowler–Garcia–Karaali (2012) establishes the prime-power Ramanujan identities and divisor-indexed supercharacter machinery. The published *Complete Smith form of Ramanujan-sum convolution circulants* treats all residues, not only units, and has a different nonsquarefree rank. The published *Smith normal form of the reduced divisor-indexed Ramanujan matrix for every modulus* and *Smith invariant factors of the divisor-indexed Ramanujan Gram matrix* use divisor indexing; their forms do not imply this specific unit-principal-submatrix classification. Schlage-Puchta's determinant concerns a divisor-indexed matrix. The direct unit-indexed equivalence and stronger general-covering searches revealed no matching all-modulus statement. An unindexed more general theorem may still imply this result; a failed search is not a novelty certificate.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Primitive positions are a canonical arithmetic Fourier sampling set. The result sharply characterizes when primitive-position sampling loses information, proving that the Gram matrix is nonsingular if and only if the modulus is squarefree. It also identifies the full integral cokernel for every modulus, including its free and torsion components. These are natural structural invariants of the exact Fourier sampling matrix, not an arbitrary finite enumeration or merely a restatement of full-circulant data.

## Limits

Classical Ramanujan identities and integer Laplacian Smith forms are inputs, not claimed new. The proof concerns this matrix only. Historical equivalent coverage remains a risk, and no external mathematical validation is asserted.
