For every prime \(p\), the matrix \((\gcd(a-b,p^2))\) restricted to invertible residues has an explicit complete integer Smith form; for odd \(p\) its largest invariant factor is \(6p^2(p-1)^2\). The prime-square restriction produces a distinguished cyclic \(3\)-torsion factor even when \(p\equiv2\pmod3\), a feature invisible from the entrywise prime divisibility pattern.
