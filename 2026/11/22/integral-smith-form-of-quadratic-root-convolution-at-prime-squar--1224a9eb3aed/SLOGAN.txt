For every odd prime \(p\), the integral square-root-count circulant modulo \(p^2\) has exactly \(p(p-2)\) Smith factors \(p\), \((p+1)/2\) factors \(p^2\), \((p-3)/2\) factors \(p^3\), one factor \(p^4\), and \(p\) unit factors. The resulting cokernel exponent is exactly \(p^4\) for all odd primes, and the proof reduces the prime-square kernel to a base-field quadratic-character circulant by an integral coset decomposition.
