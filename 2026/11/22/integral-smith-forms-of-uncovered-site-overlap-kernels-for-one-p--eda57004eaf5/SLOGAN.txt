For any finite partial Steiner triple system whose leave is connected, the integral Smith form of the old-site vacancy-overlap matrix of all labelled one-point extension diagrams depends only on nonbipartiteness, order parity, or the sizes of the two bipartition classes. In particular, a leave \(K_{3,3}\) produces a nonzero factor \(6\), whereas the star \(K_{1,7}\) produces factors \(2\) and \(18\); these are overlap invariants of separate diagrams, not assertions of joint realizability.
