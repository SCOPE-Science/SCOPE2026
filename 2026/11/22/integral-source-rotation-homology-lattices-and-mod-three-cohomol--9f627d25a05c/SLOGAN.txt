Cyclically permuting the three source vertices of \(\operatorname{Hom}(K_3,K_n)\) yields an integral top-homology lattice with regular \(C_3\)-blocks and exactly one parity-dependent exceptional block. For \(n\ge5\), the quotient has periodic order-three lower homology and a complete mod-three cup-product ring containing a truncated \(H^*(BC_3;\mathbb F_3)\), with cup-length \(\lceil(n-3)/2\rceil\).
