# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The Babson–Kozlov wedge theorem gives concentration in degree d=n-3, with reduced rank 2^n-3 by signed ordered-partition enumeration (including the six-point case n=3). No relative interior can be stabilized by rotating the three nonempty disjoint source sets, so oriented cells in each orbit supply a free group-ring summand by orienting one representative and transporting. The augmented cellular exact sequence ends in the trivial module and gives H_d stably the (d+1)-st syzygy by Schanuel; the alternating g-1 and norm resolution gives I for even d, Z for odd d. The integral C_3 lattice classification gives the three indecomposable types Z,I,R; mod three their blocks have sizes 1,2,3. Free stabilization changes only the size-three count, so this classification and the integral rank determine the given actual lattice. Independently built signed cellular matrices confirm the Jordan data for n=4–7. For n>=5, the free cover is universal; the classifying map has the (d-1)-connected fiber X_n. Its integral homology agrees with that of BC_3 below d; the quotient has free top homology because there are no higher cells, and its Euler characteristic is chi(X_n)/3. The mod-three classifying map is an isomorphism below d and injective in d, since there is no lower nonzero fiber row that could alter these degrees. UCT gives the claimed dimension of the top complement; products involving it in positive degree vanish. For the cup-length upper bound, use that H^1 is one-dimensional and its generator squares to zero: an L-fold nonzero product needs degree at least 2L-1. The stated polynomial and polynomial-exterior monomials attain the bound. These checks preserve the scope n>=3 for the lattice and n>=5 for the quotient.
