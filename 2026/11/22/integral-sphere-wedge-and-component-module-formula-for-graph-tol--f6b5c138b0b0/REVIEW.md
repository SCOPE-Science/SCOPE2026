# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The tolerance-face rule is exact for strictly one-dimensional complexes: all faces through cardinality \(t+1\), and exactly edge-containing faces of cardinality \(t+2\). The full skeleton's integral cycle lattice is the exterior power of the vertex augmentation lattice. Top boundaries are the exterior ideal generated by graph-edge differences; this is an equality established using the explicit edge-containing simplex basis, not merely a one-sided containment. The quotient by those differences is the free component augmentation lattice, proving integral torsion-freeness and the two displayed ranks. For \(t\ge2\), Hurewicz and Smith transformations determine the complete attaching-map homotopy type. Boundary cases, disconnected graphs, and empty-edge graphs are included. Independent finite computations tested every labeled graph on up to six vertices (100,416 graph/tolerance cases) and five integral Smith instances. Risk: the homotopy-category basis-change step relies on the standard homotopy classification of maps between wedges of spheres, explicitly requiring \(t\ge2\).

## Originality — PASS, with residual literature risk

The general tolerance definition and Leray bounds of Kim–Lew (2023) and the November 2026 developments of Selvaraja are established prior work. The published statements and relevant full-text sections concern Leray/collapsibility, squarefree symbolic powers and special input classes, rather than the exact arbitrary-graph integral homotopy formula proved here. An earlier bounded-independence graph construction is not equivalent to this truncated-tolerance complex; it can have arbitrarily higher-dimensional faces. The excluded tolerance-one parameter does not imply the arbitrary \(t\ge2\) exterior-power calculation. Searches for aliases, stronger coverage, exact tables and claim implication were performed against indexed research records and the primary papers. No covering statement was found. Risk: an unindexed, newly published, differently phrased algebraic topology result could imply the theorem. This review is not proof of publication priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The motivating gap is a global, integral topological classification rather than an upper bound on a Leray number. The theorem simultaneously determines homotopy types for all finite graphs and all \(t\ge2\), establishes integral torsion-freeness, and identifies the natural lower graph-automorphism module. It distinguishes connected, disconnected, and edgeless cases and realizes genuinely mixed-dimensional wedges (for example, two disjoint edges with three isolated vertices yield four two-spheres and three three-spheres at tolerance two). The arbitrary-t exterior-ideal argument and integral homotopy upgrade are the substantive structural content; the numerical example alone is not the contribution.

## Scope and remaining risk

Only the strict graph one-skeleton is covered; graph clique complexes and higher-dimensional inputs are excluded. Equivariant homotopy is not asserted. Independently certified mathematical validation and complete publication-priority checking have not been conducted.
