For every \(n\ge6\), the Alexander dual of the ordinary four-cut complex of the thin torus graph \(C_4\square C_n\) is an integral wedge of \(3n\) three-spheres and \(\binom{4n-1}{3}-70n\) two-spheres. A cyclic collapse reveals three local cube spheres per column and gives the complete torsion-free integral homology of the original nonshellable cut complex.
