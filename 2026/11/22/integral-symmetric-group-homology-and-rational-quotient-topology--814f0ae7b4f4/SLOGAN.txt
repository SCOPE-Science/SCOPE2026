The entire integral \(S_n\)-homology of the containment-order partial-partition complex is a direct sum of signed induced modules indexed by block-size partitions with no singleton parts. Rational homology of its full symmetric quotient is counted instead by **distinct** block sizes, with generating series \(\prod_{r\ge2}(1+ut^r)-1\) and a sharp degree threshold \(n\ge j(j+3)/2\).
