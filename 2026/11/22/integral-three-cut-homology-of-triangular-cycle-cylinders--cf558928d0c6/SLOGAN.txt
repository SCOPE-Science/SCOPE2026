The ordinary three-cut complex of \(K_3\square C_n\), for every \(n\ge5\), has precisely \(n\) integral classes in degree \(3n-5\) and \(\binom{3n}{2}-15n+1\) classes in degree \(3n-4\), with no torsion. Its Alexander dual is a wedge of \(n\) two-spheres and \(\binom{3n}{2}-15n+1\) circles, giving a non-shellable infinite family.
