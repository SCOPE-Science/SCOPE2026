The nine-row staircase rook complex \(\operatorname{St}(9)\), singled out in Clark–Zeckner's 2010 torsion question, has complete reduced integral homology \(\widetilde H_4\cong\mathbb Z^{402}\) and \(\widetilde H_5\cong\mathbb Z^{815}\), with every other reduced group zero. Exact acyclic Morse reduction and unimodular Smith elimination prove that this first uncomputed case has no integral torsion, without claiming a full homotopy decomposition.
