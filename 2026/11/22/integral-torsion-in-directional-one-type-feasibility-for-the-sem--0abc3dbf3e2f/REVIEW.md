# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof derives all one-point types and exact arc-feasibility entries from the rectangle law, then performs a unimodular Boolean-difference reduction to a \(k^2+1\) core. Primitive cokernel relations reduce to a single cyclic factor of order \(2(k+1)\) and the stated independent order-two factors, with separate derivations for \(k=1,2\). Eight independently enumerated examples (\(4130\) entry comparisons) and exact Smith computations through five classes corroborate the algebra.

**Originality — PASS, provisional.** Jasiński–Laflamme–Nguyen Van Thé–Woodrow, Section 10, gives the semigeneric bit-pattern classes and the forced-orientation mechanism; Coskey–Ellis, Lemma 4.1, gives the known two-class parity factorization. Previously indexed numerical orbit profiles, named-base type counts and the two-part symmetry character address different invariants and do not imply these integer invariant factors. Targeted literature, indexed-result and earlier mathematical-claim comparisons did not locate the new directed feasibility Smith classification. The residual risk is an unindexed matrix-theoretic equivalent.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The result classifies the full integral cokernel, including its prime-dependent rank defects, for an exponential-size family of natural logical feasibility matrices over *arbitrary* finite named bases. The fact that all arithmetic factors depend only on the number of old classes, not class sizes or orientations, is a meaningful structural rigidity and provides new information not present in tuple-orbit enumeration.

**Closest literature and limits.** The original parity criterion is classical; all local two-class bit factorizations are prior. The new content is the integral all-size reduction and Smith factors, not the ambient Fraïssé construction. This classification does not address eigenvalues, individual two-point type multiplicities or higher-point simultaneous realization. Historical priority is provisional, and independent mathematical validation is pending.
