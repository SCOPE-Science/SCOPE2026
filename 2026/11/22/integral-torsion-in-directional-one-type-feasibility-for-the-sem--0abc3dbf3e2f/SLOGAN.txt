The integer directed arc-feasibility matrix on nonalgebraic one-point types of the semigeneric homogeneous multipartite digraph has invariant factors determined solely by the number \(k\) of named nonadjacency classes. For \(k\ge3\), its finite cokernel is \((\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^{k^2-2k-1}\oplus\mathbb Z/[2(k+1)]\mathbb Z\), regardless of parameter-class sizes or original orientations.
