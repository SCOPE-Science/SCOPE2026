# Scientific review: arbitrary Latin-square graph clique homotopy

## Correctness — PASS
Every term of the theorem is fixed by row/column/symbol graph adjacency and the exact definition of intercalates. The exhaustive clique-family classification confines every dimension-greater-than-two face to a line simplex or a unique intercalate tetrahedron. Free-face collapse is justified by descending-size order, intersecting lines having at most one common vertex, and the uniqueness of mixed-tetrahedron triangles. The resulting two-complex has explicit integral cell counts. An independent nonabelian and elementary-abelian finite test actually executes all free-face pairs. Simple connectivity is not inferred from homology: symbol edges are homotoped into the rook graph and every rook square has an explicit two-triangle or six-triangle disk. The product-of-simplices two-skeleton generates the rook graph's fundamental group relations. Integral rank follows from Euler characteristic and freeness of degree-two cellular cycles; Hurewicz and Whitehead then give the precise wedge homotopy type. Risks: finite checks are not a universal proof, and integer homotopy does not assert equivariant splitting.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk
Cioabă–Mim (arXiv:2606.27328, initial June 25, 2026; full Latin-square section inspected) prove the precise field-homology ranks and intercalate counts, not the full homotopy equivalence. Their integral Smith-form result also does not imply simple connectivity. The closest published same-object homotopy theorem, *Integral homotopy type of cyclic Latin-square clique complexes of odd order* (full RESULT.md inspected), explicitly restricts its proof and theorem to cyclic odd-order Latin squares and excludes arbitrary squares and even orders. Published partial-transversal classifications concern a different simplicial complex. Actual semantic and literature queries found no all-Latin-square homotopy classification. The novelty is not the numerical rank: it is the all-squares fundamental-group triviality and uniform integral wedge decomposition, including intercalates in even and nonassociative tables. Unindexed equivalent work is possible, so no exhaustive priority certification is asserted.

## Value — PASS
A complete integral homotopy classification for every Latin-square graph is stronger than known field and integral homology and covers infinitely many previously unclassified even-order and noncyclic examples. The natural intercalate invariant exactly controls the number of two-sphere summands, while no hidden fundamental-group or attachment ambiguity survives. Explicit contrasting order-six multiplication tables give \(116\) and \(98\) spheres, not a cosmetic relabeling. The constructive two-dimensional collapse and square fillings are reusable topological mechanisms for clique complexes with several parallel classes.

## Limitations and literature
The theorem is restricted to order at least five and to the ordinary clique complex of the three-class Latin-square graph. No group-equivariant homotopy, wider orthogonal-array generalization, or independent validation is claimed. Main literature: Cioabă and Mim, arXiv:2606.27328, sections 3.2–3.6; published cyclic odd-order Latin-square graph homotopy theorem, October 25, 2026. Same-object prior content was compared at the implication level, not merely by title.
