For every alphabet size \(q\ge2\), the clique complex of the loopless simple undirected two-letter de Bruijn overlap graph is a wedge of exactly \((q-1)(q-2)(2q-3)/6\) two-spheres. The proof identifies a complete-graph nerve and precisely two triangle fillings per three-letter subset, giving a full integral homotopy classification rather than a graph-cycle rank.
