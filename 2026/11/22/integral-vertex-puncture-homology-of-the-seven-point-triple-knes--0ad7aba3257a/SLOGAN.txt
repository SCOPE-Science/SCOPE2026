Deleting one triple-vertex from the seven-point triple-Kneser independence complex leaves integral homology of ranks \(23\) and \(3\) in degrees \(6\) and \(9\); its link has ranks \(6\) and \(4\) in degrees \(5\) and \(8\). Exhaustive acyclic Morse matchings show these groups are torsion-free and the deletion-to-ambient maps are primitive, without asserting a wedge-of-spheres homotopy type.
