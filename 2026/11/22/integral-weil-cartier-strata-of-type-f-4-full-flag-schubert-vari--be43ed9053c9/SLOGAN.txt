Among all \(1,152\) type-\(F_4\) full-flag Schubert varieties, exactly \(233\) are \(\mathbb Q\)-factorial: \(171\) are factorial, \(49\) have global Weil–Cartier quotient \(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\), and \(13\) have \((\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^2\). Thus the maximal finite global defect has order \(4\) but exponent only \(2\), an exact integral refinement of the general divisor criterion.
