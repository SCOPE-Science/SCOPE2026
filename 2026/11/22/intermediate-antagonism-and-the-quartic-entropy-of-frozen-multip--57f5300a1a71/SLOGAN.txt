For identical connected regular networks coupled by intermediate antagonism \(d-2<a<d\), the frozen multiplex states are exactly two uniform aligned states plus sitewise opposed states whose two-coloring has no isolated-color vertex. On cubic prisms, their count grows as \(\alpha^n\), where \(\alpha\) is the unique root in \((3,4)\) of \(z^4-3z^3-z^2+2=0\).
