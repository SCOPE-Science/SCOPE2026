Iterating perpendicular feet from a fixed interior point on the symmetry axis of an equilateral-base tetrahedron yields an exactly integrable shape map, with first integral \(H=(2uv-1)/((1+u^2)(1+v^2))\). Except for the regular centered tetrahedron, every normalized orbit lies on a compact invariant oval on which the map is conjugate to a rigid circle rotation; there are dense nonperiodic shape orbits arbitrarily close to the regular case.
