For a binary source with maximal constant-run length \(h\), the number of possible parents after one deletion is determined exactly by the receiver's run lengths, and the complete all-length inverse-list spectrum is rational. A receiver uniquely determines its source exactly when it has one run of length \(2h\) and every other run is of length at most \(h\); among feasible long receivers the bounded list sizes converge to an explicit truncated geometric law.
