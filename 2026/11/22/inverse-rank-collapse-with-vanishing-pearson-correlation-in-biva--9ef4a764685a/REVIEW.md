# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof specifies the full strictly positive parameter domain, explicit transformed joint density, all integrability requirements, and every limit. Normalization gives \(Z_\kappa\sim(\log\kappa)/\kappa\), dominated moment integrals yield \(\operatorname{Var}(X_i)\sim B_i/\log\kappa\), and the cross moment is \(O(\kappa^{-1})\). The exact covariance normalization then produces the displayed negative Pearson coefficient. Separately, logarithmic coordinates converge jointly to \((U,1-U)\), with uniform marginals, giving limiting copula \(W\) and both limiting rank correlations \(-1\). Conditional monotonicity proves negative covariance at every finite positive coupling. The finite diagnostic script passes; it does not replace the proof.

## Originality — PASS, with documented residual risk

The 1988 exponential-conditionals parent density and its basic negative correlation are prior knowledge, as are general proportional-hazards transformations (2022) and the 2024 power-Lindley family, including numerical correlation tables. The entire originating 2024 article and relevant 2022 and 2009 primary materials were read. None of their inspected results states or directly dominates the combined strong-coupling countermonotone copula and exact Lindley-dependent logarithmic Pearson asymptotics. The 1988 source's full text was unavailable, so priority remains provisional; its abstract cannot justify a whole-document negative claim. The new statement is not presented as a new distribution or a restatement of negative correlation.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This is a parameter-natural regime of a published two-component survival model. In that regime essentially maximal negative *rank* dependence coexists with arbitrarily small absolute *linear* correlation, which is a substantive interpretation hazard when assessing paired lifetimes. A sharp, nonzero coefficient accounts for the precise logarithmic decay and gives a quantitative benchmark for fitting and diagnostics. The sign-changing interpretation is not a routine reparameterization of an already known rank result.
