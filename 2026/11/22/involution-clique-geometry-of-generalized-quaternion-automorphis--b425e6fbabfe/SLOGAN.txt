For each generalized quaternion group of order \(2^{k+1}\) with \(k\ge3\), the commuting graph of nonidentity involutions in its automorphism group is exactly \(K_1\vee[(K_2\vee(2^{k-2}K_4))\sqcup(2^{k-2}K_2)]\). Consequently, its maximal elementary abelian \(2\)-subgroups comprise exactly \(2^{k-2}\) groups of order eight and the same number of order four.
