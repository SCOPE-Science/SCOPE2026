For every even finite crown, an explicit two-state cyclic run formula counts all self-inverse partial automorphisms jointly by fixed points and transposed pairs, with sharp corank-one and corank-two coefficients. Their total grows on the quarter-factorial logarithmic scale \(\log H_N=\tfrac14N\log N+O(N)\), whereas the full partial-automorphism monoid grows on the half-factorial scale.
