The involutions of each odd-characteristic multivariable truncated-polynomial automorphism group have an exact class-size and fixed-subalgebra spectrum indexed by their negative tangent eigenvalue count. For fixed field and embedding dimension, almost every involution has tangent map \(-I\) at large odd jet orders, but the uniquely dominant tangent map at large even jet orders has exactly \(\lceil d/2\rceil\) negative eigenvalues.
