On the free cube-zero associative algebra \(V\oplus(V\otimes V)\), the complete finite-field census of involutive automorphisms is controlled by the Jordan type of the action on \(V\). In characteristic two, each fixed linear action with \(s\) singleton Jordan blocks has exactly \(q^{s^3}\) identity-linear conjugacy classes of lifts: an explicit cubic obstruction to linearization, absent in odd characteristic.
