Two twelve-vertex Archimedean graphs have opposite majority-oscillation basin behavior: on the cuboctahedron all 20 primitive two-cycles have no transient predecessors, while on the truncated tetrahedron 239 two-cycles attract 2,366 of the 4,096 binary states. Exact exhaustive certificates also determine sharp settling times of four and five updates, respectively, and the full spatial-symmetry class counts.
