For every rectangular rook graph, locating-total codes are exactly the selected-cell bipartite graphs with at most one isolated row-or-column vertex and no isolated-edge component; a labelled component sieve determines every coefficient of their counting polynomial. Every optimum is a bipartite forest, with \(\gamma_t^L(K_m\square K_n)=\max\{n-1,\lceil2(m+n-1)/3\rceil\}\) for \(2\le m\le n\), and ordinary locating-domination differs by one precisely on a sharp modular aspect-ratio locus.
