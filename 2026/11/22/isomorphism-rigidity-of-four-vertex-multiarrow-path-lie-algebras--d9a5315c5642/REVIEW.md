## Correctness: PASS

The path-algebra construction is associative before taking commutators. Explicit degree-two and degree-three brackets identify the lower central series. The decisive centralizer computation uses nonzero tensor products to force both outer degree-one components to vanish, in every characteristic. Intrinsic dimensions recover the middle arrow count, outer sum and product, and hence the complete unordered outer pair; reversing a path supplies sufficiency. The independent finite implementation checks 81 characteristic/parameter combinations, and the unbounded collision family follows by direct substitution. The finite tests are supplemental, not the all-field proof.

This gives a necessary-and-sufficient isomorphism classification in a natural infinite three-step nilpotent Lie family, valid in every characteristic.
