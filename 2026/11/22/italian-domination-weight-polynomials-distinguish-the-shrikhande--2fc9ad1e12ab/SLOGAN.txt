The strongly regular Shrikhande and four-by-four rook graphs have distinct complete Italian-domination weight polynomials: the rook polynomial exceeds the Shrikhande polynomial by \(8z^4+224z^5+1184z^6+1408z^7+1176z^8+832z^9+640z^{10}+256z^{11}\), while every higher coefficient agrees. Both minima equal \(4\), with exactly \(16\) Shrikhande and \(24\) rook optimal labelings.
