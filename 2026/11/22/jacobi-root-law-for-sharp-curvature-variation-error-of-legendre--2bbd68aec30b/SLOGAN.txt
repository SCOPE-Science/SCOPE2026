The sharp unweighted \(L^2\) Legendre projection error for functions with bounded variation of the first derivative reduces to single hinges, and its stationary knots at *every degree* are exactly the interlacing zeros of two consecutive \(P_j^{(1,1)}\) Jacobi polynomials. The midpoint alternates between strict local maximum (odd degree) and strict local minimum (even degree); at degree two the unique worst knots are \((5\pm\sqrt5)/10\), with sharp squared constant \(1/1500\).
