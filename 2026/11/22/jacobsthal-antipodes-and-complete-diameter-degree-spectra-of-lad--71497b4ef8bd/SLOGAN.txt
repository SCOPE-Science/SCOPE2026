For the canonical Euclidean perfect-matching polytope of the \(2\times n\) open ladder, the sharp squared diameter is \(2n\), with exactly \((2^{n-1}+(-1)^n)/3\) unordered diametral pairs. A tiling using \(k\ge1\) horizontal domino pairs has exactly \(2^{k-1}\) farthest tilings regardless of placement, producing a complete Fibonacci-to-Jacobsthal diameter-degree law.
