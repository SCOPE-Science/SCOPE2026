For every prime \(p\ge5\), two exponent-\(p\) groups of equal order have exactly the same conjugacy-class-size and irreducible-character-degree multiplicities and all orientable surface-word distributions, yet have nilpotency classes \(p-1\) and \(2\). Their gap is unbounded because these invariants depend only on the rank of a unipotent cyclic action, while its nilpotency index varies.
