All ordered pairwise-commuting tuples of self-inverse partial permutations have an exact Gaussian-binomial exponential generating function, simultaneously recording each domain rank and their product rank. In particular, the ordered commuting-pair counts have generating function \(\exp(4u+5u^2/2+u^4/4)\), with coefficients \(1,4,21,124,817,5844,45061,\ldots\) after multiplication by factorials.
