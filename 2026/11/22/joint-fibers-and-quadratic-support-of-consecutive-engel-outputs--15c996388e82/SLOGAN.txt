For every odd finite field, the *joint* prescribed-target fibers of the consecutive Lie Engel outputs \(([X,[X,Y]],[X,[X,[X,Y]]])\) on \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\) have an exact three-stratum quadratic-and-orthogonality classification. The image comprises exactly \(1+q(q^2-1)(1+(q-1)^2)\) output pairs; this refines the known marginal two-Engel count and gives conditional laws that marginals do not determine.
