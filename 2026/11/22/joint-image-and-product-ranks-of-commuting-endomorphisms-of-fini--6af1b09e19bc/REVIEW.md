# Scientific review

## Correctness
The affine presentation follows from an integer recurrence valid even at even modulus. Pairwise commutation is exactly the determinantal system, and the valuation shell with a primitive coordinate yields the translation fiber \(p^{e+(t-1)h}\). When one coefficient is nonunit the fiber is exactly \(p^e\). The unweighted normalization and rank of every product are then forced. The standalone checker replays direct composition and independently computes every coefficient in seven small configurations. Risk: test dimensions are finite; the unbounded domain depends on the written fiber bijection, not on brute force.

## Originality
The 2010 quandle automorphism result is a model source, not a rank-census precedent; the 2020 affine-monoid paper studies Gabriel quivers. A published triangular-algebra result **already proves** the entire unweighted determinantal tuple sequence and clique polynomial, so those statements are expressly excluded from originality. It does not give the shifted-multiplier image and product-rank coefficients. The closest affine-monoid centralizer result gives single-element cardinalities and ordered pair totals, not the all-arity multivariate coefficients. Primary full-text sources and the relevant published complete results were read. A stronger unlocated rank-weighted determinantal theorem remains the principal historical-priority risk.

## Value
Joint image sizes and composite image size are intrinsic invariants of a commuting family of endomorphisms. The exact statement resolves every feasible prime-power stratum simultaneously for all tuple lengths; more notably it proves a sharp structural decoupling outside the all-automorphism stratum, where the translation fiber is exactly one cyclic factor independently of the remaining ranks. This is a complete natural statistic with an identifiable singular/regular boundary, not a recomputation of the known unweighted count.

## Limits
Cyclic rather than general Alexander quandles; all-arity unweighted counts are existing results; no independent publication-priority determination or independent mathematical validation.
