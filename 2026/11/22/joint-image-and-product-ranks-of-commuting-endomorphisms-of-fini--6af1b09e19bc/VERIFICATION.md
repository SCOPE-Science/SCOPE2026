The proof uses exact affine presentation, a fixed-multiplier translation-fiber bijection, and valuation-based image cardinalities. Run `python3 rankcheck.py` and `python3 verify.py` from the same directory as the scripts. The former independently tests all joint-rank and composition-rank coefficients in seven cases; the latter tests unweighted tuple and clique counts and quandle-endomorphism parametrization. Each test set is finite and does not substitute for the general proof. No independent audit is claimed.
