# Scientific review: joint rank–stabilization-depth census

## Correctness — PASS
Every monotone regressive map corresponds to an ordered plane forest in breadth-first order. The exact image-rank weight is one per fixed root plus one per nonroot internal vertex; the stopping index is maximal component height. The tree and forest recurrences follow by disjoint sequence decomposition. The sharp boundary is counted by one-spine trees and the binomial coefficient \(\binom{n+r-3}{2r-2}\). Direct exhaustive enumeration of 6,917 maps in sizes one through nine agrees with the separately implemented coefficient recurrence. Finite testing is supporting evidence and not a substitute for the complete combinatorial proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The individual Catalan/Narayana rank marginal, the fixed-point and potency distributions, and the plane-forest bijection are known and expressly credited. The closest same-monoid primary full text on potency, Yağcı–Korkmaz (2020, DOI:10.1007/s40840-020-00899-7), does not mark original image size, while the 2026 harmonic power-rank study proves fixed-power asymptotic marginal behavior, not an exact joint distribution. Ganyushkin–Mazorchuk (2010/2011, arXiv:1006.0316) supplies the classic monoid realization. Sönmez–Ünlü (2013/2014, DOI:10.1080/00927872.2012.716115) concerns ordered-tree potency counts and is a **significant priority risk** because only its abstract was accessible; Laradji–Umar (2004) treats image-rank strata but its full text was also not examined. No independent historical uniqueness verification is asserted.

## Value — PASS
A complete exact joint image-rank versus stabilization-depth distribution is a natural refinement of two separately studied monoid invariants. The universally sharp bound \(d\le r\) and explicit positive boundary multiplicity show which deepest dynamics are possible at each image rank, and the rational recursion computes all interior strata, without arbitrary numerical cutoffs.

## Limits
The result uses standard tree-series methods and is not claimed to give a closed elementary expression for every interior cell. Independent audit, full priority confirmation, and general asymptotic fluctuation theory have not been performed.
