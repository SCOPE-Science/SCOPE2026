The complete ordered three-way pairwise-trace spectrum of rank-one \(2\times2\) finite-field projectors has at most three positive fiber levels, with an explicit quadratic-root criterion for each target. Exactly \((q^3+4q^2-4q+\epsilon_q)/2\) of the \(q^3\) prescribed trace triples are attainable, asymptotically one half.
