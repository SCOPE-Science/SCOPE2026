For any fixed number of independent uniform rooted PDA phylogenetic trees, the entire array of pairwise common-clade counts converges jointly to independent \(\operatorname{Poisson}(1/8)\) variables, even though common-cherry overlaps sharing a tree have exact strictly positive covariance asymptotic to \(1/(64n)\). This supplies a simultaneous null calibration for rooted Robinson–Foulds distance matrices, beyond the known two-tree marginal law.
