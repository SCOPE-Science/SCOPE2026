For uniform \(\ell\)-regular overpartitions, the first gap in the nonoverlined support and the first gap in the entire support have a singular joint Rayleigh limit with exactly half its mass on the diagonal. Their *exact* integer coincidence probability has the sharper expansion \(1/2+\pi n^{-1/4}/[8(1-1/\ell)^{1/4}]+o_\ell(n^{-1/4})\).
