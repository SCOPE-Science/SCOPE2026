The Jordan partition of every noninvertible substitution endomorphism of \(\mathbb F_q[t]/(t^N)\), restricted to its maximal ideal, depends only on the first nonzero degree \(d\): its power ranks are \(\lfloor(N-1)/d^r\rfloor\), with an exact finite-field census of every partition. For uniformly random such endomorphisms, nilpotency depth divided by \(\log N\) converges to an infinite pure-point law instead of concentrating.
