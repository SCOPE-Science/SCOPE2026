# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The unique-path formula for powers on a rooted-tree incidence representation is proved for arbitrary fields. Its disjoint row-support property gives the full rank sequence, which gives the Jordan partition. The integer polynomial recurrence counts all edge labels with exact multiplicity, and direct exhaustive matrix tests agree in all enumerated configurations. The maximal-block asymptotics follow from a critical quadratic recurrence, a strict supercritical fixed-point argument, and matching union/independent-subtree bounds. The full-radical statement is limited explicitly to the highest nonzero power.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** The closest complete-tree percolation literature already proves reliability identities and the classical threshold, and those are credited. The distinct statement is an exact multivariate Jordan-partition census for the entire natural cover-supported incidence-algebra subspace, using per-vertex path depths and an integer polynomial recurrence. The closest located layered-incidence radical result concerns dense all-to-all level blocks and maximal powers only; it does not imply these branching-tree all-power rank spectra. We found no equivalent among the searched literature, accessible historical titles/claims and mathematical research records. It remains possible that a more general weighted-tree-shift/Jordan theorem subsumes the result, and novelty is not independently certified.

**Value — PASS.** Nilpotent Jordan type is a natural algebraic invariant, and the result gives its complete finite-field distribution in an unbounded family rather than a single numerical probability. The rank lemma uncovers a characteristic-independent structural simplification, while the binary critical versus supercritical regimes give a sharp qualitative distinction in the largest Jordan block. The percolation recurrence is pre-existing, but its all-powers Jordan reconstruction and exact frequency census are not the same as scalar connectivity reliability.

**Closest literature and limits.** Khripchenko–Novikov establish incidence-algebra structure; Quinn studies path-algebra presentations; Mohammadi–Sáenz-de-Cabezón–Wynn prove complete-tree reliability/criticality; the relevant published layered-incidence radical census handles non-tree ordinal-sum posets. No claim of a new percolation critical threshold, complete Jordan census for all of \(J\), or an independent priority judgment is made.
