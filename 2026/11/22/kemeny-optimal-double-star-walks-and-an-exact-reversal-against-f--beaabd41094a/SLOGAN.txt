On every double-star tree with at least two leaves at each hub, the globally Kemeny-optimal symmetric stochastic walk is unique and has an explicit square-root bridge allocation; balancing the numbers of leaves strictly decreases the optimum. On balanced double stars with at least two leaves per hub, the independently fastest-mixing chain has **worse** Kemeny cost than even maximum-degree weighting, by an exact positive radical certificate, and its Kemeny cost is asymptotically \(1+1/\sqrt2\) times optimal.
