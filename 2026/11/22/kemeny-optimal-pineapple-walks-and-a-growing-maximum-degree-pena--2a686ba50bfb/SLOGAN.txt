For a complete graph with \(r\) private leaves attached to one gateway, the unique Kemeny-optimal symmetric stochastic walk saturates all clique rows and uses every allowed edge, even when arbitrary non-invariant weights and self-loops are permitted. As the clique grows with \(r\) fixed, the usual maximum-degree walk costs exactly \(r+1\) times as much in the limit; the optimal pendant probability tends to \(1/(r+\sqrt{r+1})\).
