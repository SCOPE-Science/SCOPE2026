On two equal cliques joined by a single bridge, the unique Kemeny-optimal symmetric stochastic walk devotes asymptotically \(\sqrt2-1\) of each gateway's transition probability to that bridge, while every vertex has zero holding probability. The optimal Kemeny constant is asymptotically \((7/2+\sqrt2)s\) for clique size \(s\), whereas the maximum-degree walk incurs a factor asymptotic to \(s/(7+2\sqrt2)\).
