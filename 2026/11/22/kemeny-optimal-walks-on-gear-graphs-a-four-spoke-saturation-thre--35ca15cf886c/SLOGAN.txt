On a gear graph with \(n\) rim vertices, the unique Kemeny-optimal symmetric walk stops holding at the hub exactly when \(n\ge4\), assigns spoke probability \(1/n\), and makes each inserted rim vertex hold with probability \(1/n\). Its minimum Kemeny sum is asymptotic to \(2n^{3/2}\), giving a \(\frac{5}{4\sqrt3}\sqrt n\) improvement over maximum-degree weighting.
