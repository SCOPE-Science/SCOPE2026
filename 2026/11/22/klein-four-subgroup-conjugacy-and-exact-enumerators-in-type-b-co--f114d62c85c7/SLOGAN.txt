For every type-\(B_n\) Coxeter group, the number of conjugacy classes of Klein-four subgroups has an explicit rational generating function and is a degree-ten quasipolynomial of period dividing twelve, while the total number of embedded Klein-four subgroups has an explicit exponential generating function. The classification uses signed transitive \(C_2^2\)-sets and removes nonfaithful actions, yielding precisely \(2,7,23\) conjugacy classes in dimensions \(2,3,4\).
