## Reproduction

Run `python verify.py` with Python 3.10 or later. The script expands the ordinary generating series through degree ten using integer arithmetic. For each rank through five, it also constructs all signed permutations, forms every subgroup generated by a pair of distinct commuting nonidentity involutions, and finds the conjugacy classes by applying generators of the signed permutation group. The two class counts agree for every rank in that finite range.

The independent enumeration is exhaustive only in the indicated finite range. The proof in `RESULT.md` supplies the full generating series and its asymptotic consequences.
