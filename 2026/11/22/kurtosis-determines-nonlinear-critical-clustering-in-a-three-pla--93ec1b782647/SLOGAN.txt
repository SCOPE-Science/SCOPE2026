In a three-player Gaussian resource-allocation game at the exact clustering threshold, resource variance alone does not decide nonlinear stability: kurtosis above \(13/12\) makes the symmetric state locally attracting, while kurtosis below \(13/12\) makes it Lyapunov unstable. For the attracting case, generic critical disagreements decay with the sharp \(t^{-1/2}\) law, and two finite resource distributions with the same variance exhibit opposite critical behavior.
