Boolean point-incidence queries against all Lagrangian subspaces of a finite symplectic space shatter exactly the linearly independent, pairwise orthogonal vectors, yielding a complete all-rank shattering polynomial and \(\operatorname{VCdim}=\operatorname{Ldim}=n\). The resulting pure complex is neither matroidal nor flag in ranks at least two, and at binary symplectic rank three its only nonzero reduced mod-two Betti number is \(\widetilde\beta_2=2897\).
