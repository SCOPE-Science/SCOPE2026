For words with \(r\) copies of one delimiter and \(s\) distinct singleton symbols, complete contiguous one- and two-factor observations are classified by sets of at most \(r\) ordered lists on \(s+1\) labeled tokens; their exact stratum counts are unsigned Lah numbers and their fibers have sizes \((r-1)!/(r-1-k)!\). Once \(r\ge s+1\), the number of distinguishable profiles saturates, and a single profile captures probability \(1-s(s+1)/r+O_s(r^{-2})\) of uniformly sampled words.
