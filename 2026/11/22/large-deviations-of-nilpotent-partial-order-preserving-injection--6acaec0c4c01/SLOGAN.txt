For a uniformly chosen nilpotent increasing partial injection of an \(n\)-point chain, its domain rank has a Gaussian limit with mean \(n/2\) and variance \(n/8\) to leading order, and its proportion of occupied points satisfies a full large-deviation principle with rate \(2[\log 2+s\log s+(1-s)\log(1-s)]\). Previously known finite enumeration serves as input; the claim concerns the conditional-rank limit laws.
