In the MLE-standardized Weibull min-characteristic-function test, increasing the exponential weight \(\gamma\) makes the statistic's leading term deterministic at the explicit saddle-point scale \(\gamma^{-7/4}e^{-2\sqrt{2\gamma}}\). The first sample-dependent correction is exponentially smaller and controlled by the largest standardized observation, so the test eventually ranks any two fixed samples with unequal maxima in reverse order of those maxima.
