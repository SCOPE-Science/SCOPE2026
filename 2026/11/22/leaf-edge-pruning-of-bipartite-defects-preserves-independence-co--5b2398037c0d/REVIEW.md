# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
For an arbitrary bipartite defect graph \(F\), a degree-one vertex \(v\) with neighbor \(w\) forces every face containing the mixed edge \(vw\) in the ordinary independence complex of the bipartite complement to lie in the simplex on \(\{v,w\}\cup(N_F(w)\setminus\{v\})\). Taking another neighbor \(x\) of \(w\) and ordering subsets of the other \(d-2\) neighbors in descending size pairs each lower face with the one obtained by adding \(x\). At each step this lower face has exactly that unique remaining coface. The pairs partition precisely the faces lost on deleting the defect edge, proving the exact \(2^{d-2}\) elementary collapses. Forest pruning preserves the number of nontrivial components and ends at a matching. The final complex is two contractible shore simplices joined by one edge per component, giving the asserted \(S^0\) or circle wedge homotopy type. No computation is needed for the general proof; the attached checker independently tested 1,399 free-face pairs and 440 mod-two forest homology instances.

## Originality — PASS against checked evidence
Primary literature on independence-complex homology includes Berghoff (arXiv:2008.06267, Theorem 1.1), Barmak (arXiv:1007.0418, Section 3), and Abdelmalek et al. (arXiv:2106.10863). They establish respectively a general homology spectral sequence, suspension results for triangle-free graphs, and higher-independence properties of chordal graphs. A relevant later circle-graph study (Bakshi et al., arXiv:2412.01125, Section 3) discusses the complementary bipartite graph underlying circle-wedge homology, but neither that paper nor the other inspected statements supplies the explicit universal leaf-edge simplicial-collapse equivalence or its exact count. Published-record searches for bipartite defect pruning, graph complementation, and forest component formulas did not identify the same statement. An unindexed equivalent or a reduction buried in general graph-folding literature remains possible; no exhaustive novelty guarantee is asserted.

## Value — PASS
The result is a structural all-size simple-homotopy invariance principle for arbitrary bipartite error patterns, preserving torsion and nonlinear homotopy information across removal of pendant defect edges. It also yields an exact complete classification for all forest defect patterns with arbitrary branching and number of vertices. The degree-one boundary is sharp: a lone defect edge cannot be removed without changing connectivity. Thus the theorem separates cyclic-core topology from pendant branching, rather than reporting a small parameter value or a routine Betti-number recomputation.
