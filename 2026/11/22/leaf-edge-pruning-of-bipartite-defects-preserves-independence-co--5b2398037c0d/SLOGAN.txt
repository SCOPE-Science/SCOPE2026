For the independence complex of a complete bipartite graph with deleted-edge defect graph \(F\), pruning a defect leaf whose neighbor has degree \(d\ge2\) is realized by exactly \(2^{d-2}\) elementary collapses, even when \(F\) contains cycles. If \(F\) is a forest with \(c\) edge-containing components, the complex is \(S^0\) when \(c=0\) and a wedge of \(c-1\) circles otherwise.
