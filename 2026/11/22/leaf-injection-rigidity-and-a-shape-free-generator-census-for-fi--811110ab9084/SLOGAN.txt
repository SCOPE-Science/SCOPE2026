For finite outward-directed forests under the immediate-child modality, a Boolean predicate tuple generates every vertex singleton **if and only if its leaf color words are all different**; internal-vertex colors are unconstrained. On an \(n\)-vertex forest with \(L\) leaves, the exact generating-tuple census is \((2^k)_{\underline L}(2^k)^{n-L}\), with sharp formula depth at most the forest height and the classical birthday window for random generation.
