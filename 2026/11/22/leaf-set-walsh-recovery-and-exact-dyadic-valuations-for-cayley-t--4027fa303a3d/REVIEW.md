# Review of leaf-set Walsh recovery

**Correctness: PASS.** The convolution kernel counts every labelled tree exactly once before pushing forward to its degree-one vertex set. Prüfer strings give the exact character formula by signed inclusion-exclusion. In even order the \(j=1\) term has exactly \(1+\nu_2(k)\) factors of two, while all other terms lie one power deeper in the dyadic filtration; the zero-character and two-vertex boundaries are handled separately. This proves every multiplier nonzero, the valuation multiplicities, the exact spectral gcd and the mod-two invertibility statement. The included executable `verify.py` passed 18,541 exact checks, combining direct code enumeration, independent Stirling-number counts and exact Walsh transforms. Finite verification is corroborating evidence, not an infinite proof.

**Originality: PASS subject to bounded historical risk.** The closest exact same-object source, Remmel and Williamson (2010), already gives \((n-r)!S(n-2,n-r)\) trees for each prescribed \(r\)-vertex leaf set. It therefore determines the full unsigned counting kernel and allows one to compute finite Fourier transforms, but it does not establish the universal dyadic valuation formula or zero-free convolution theorem. Lennon (2008) gives the coding framework but addresses mutation locality. Nearby edge-incidence spanning-tree Walsh results use different frequencies and neither imply the present leaf-set character valuations nor contradict their all-order nonvanishing. Searches considered aliases such as empty-box occupancy, Prüfer leaf sets, signed leaf statistics, and Boolean convolution. The main priority risk is an unindexed signed occupancy or exact leaf-enumerator specialization already proving the congruences, particularly because the unsigned leaf-set distribution is classical. No assertion of comprehensive historical priority is made.

**Value: PASS.** The leaf profile is a natural dependent-set summary of a uniform Cayley tree, and a given leaf set occurs with nontrivial Stirling multiplicity while many binary profiles are forbidden. Full Fourier recoverability of the multiplicity-weighted kernel therefore does not follow from a simple product law or from the number of attainable profiles. The exact dyadic stratification determines an arithmetic stability feature at every character weight, including half of all modes with valuation exactly one at even orders and full local integral invertibility at odd orders. The result is a class-wide quantitative classification, rather than a recomputation of individual tree counts.

**Limitations and closest literature.** The claim is specific to counting leaf indicators, not to edge-incidence spanning-tree indicators or unweighted attainable-leaf-set supports. No dyadic Smith normal form, real inverse norm, or result for other primes is claimed. The known full leaf-set enumeration is acknowledged rather than presented as new; the novelty assessment is bounded to the listed inspections and indexed searches. The supporting references are Craig Lennon, *On the locality of the Prüfer code* (arXiv:0802.3514), and Jeffrey B. Remmel and S. Gill Williamson, *Ranking and unranking trees with a given number or a given set of leaves* (arXiv:1009.2060).
