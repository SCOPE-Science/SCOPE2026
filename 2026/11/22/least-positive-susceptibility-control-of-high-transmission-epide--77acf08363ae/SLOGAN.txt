In a heterogeneous SIR epidemic, the last *positive* susceptibility class sets the high-transmission prevalence-peak time: \(Rt_{\rm peak}/\log R\to[\lambda_*(1-sp_0)]^{-1}\). For a two-class law with fixed mean susceptibility, letting its smaller susceptibility approach zero makes this coefficient diverge, but setting it to zero exactly gives a finite smaller coefficient and changes the large-transmission peak-timing order.
