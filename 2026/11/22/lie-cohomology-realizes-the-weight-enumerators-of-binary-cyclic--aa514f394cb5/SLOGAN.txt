The trivial-coefficient Betti polynomial of an irreducible binary almost-abelian Lie algebra is \(P_L(z)=(1+z)W_C(z)\), where \(C\) is its Frobenius-relation cyclic code. Conversely every length-\(d\) binary cyclic code of minimum distance at least three arises, so higher Lie cohomology detects exactly the normal-basis defect and the code's Hamming-weight distribution.
