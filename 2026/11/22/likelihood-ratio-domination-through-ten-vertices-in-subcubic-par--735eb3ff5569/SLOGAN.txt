For every connected graph of maximum degree three on at most ten vertices, the size of a uniformly random greedy maximal independent set dominates the size of a uniformly sampled maximal independent set in **likelihood-ratio order**, strictly stronger than stochastic dominance. An exhaustive exact-rational, isomorphism-complete census checks all \(2,571\) connected graph types, including \(2,458\) types at orders eight through ten.
