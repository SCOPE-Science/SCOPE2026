Near every integer ratio \(m\ge2\) of two nonreal pole frequencies in a quintic Hausdorff-moment problem, the smallest real-pole separation restoring Cauchy-dual subnormality is \(\kappa_m|r-m|+o(|r-m|)\). The exact positive constant \(\kappa_m\) is a two-phase variational maximum, reflecting a long-time oscillatory beat that is invisible at fixed logarithmic times.
