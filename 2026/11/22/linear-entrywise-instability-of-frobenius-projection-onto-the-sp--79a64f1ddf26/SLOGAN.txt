Frobenius projection onto the spectral-norm unit ball can magnify a matrix's maximum-entry perturbation by an amount proportional to its order, despite being nonexpansive in Frobenius norm. Rank-one boundary tangents have a sharp asymptotic amplification of \(\sqrt{mn}/4\), while the complete real \(2\times2\) projector has exact maximum-entry Lipschitz constant \(3/2\).
