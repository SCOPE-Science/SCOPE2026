# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof derives the full ordinary derivation matrix from the Leibniz identity. It establishes the central restriction of any local map, reconstructs the pointwise tensor-square action from decomposable tensors and flip-symmetry on squares, and explicitly constructs interpolation derivations for every noncentral input. For two-locality, pairing an invertible diagonal quadratic tensor with a shifted generator vector gives the skew-adjoint equation and forces \(A=S\) by varying two diagonal entries. The proof holds for all fields of characteristic different from two and all \(m\ge2\). The standalone exact-arithmetic checker covers reflexive-hull dimensions in ranks two and three over the specified small fields and excludes all nonzero two-point mismatches for rank two over two prime fields; it does not prove the infinite theorem by sampling.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Local derivations and two-local derivations are established invariants in nilpotent algebra research. The theorem completely computes both for a natural, universal infinite family, with exact dimensions and a qualitative difference from the corresponding Lie case. The nonstandard rank-one tangent-preserver lemma and diagonal-pair obstruction give a structural explanation that is useful beyond enumerated small examples. The paper does not claim that nonlinear 2-local derivations are ordinary.
