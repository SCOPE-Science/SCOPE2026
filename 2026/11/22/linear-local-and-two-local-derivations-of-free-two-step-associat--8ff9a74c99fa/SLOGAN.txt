In the nonunital free two-step associative algebra \(V\oplus(V\otimes V)\), every linear local derivation independently chooses two generator-space endomorphisms and an arbitrary quadratic shear, giving an \(m^2\)-dimensional defect beyond ordinary derivations. Over every field of characteristic different from two and for every generator rank \(m\ge2\), simultaneous interpolation at two points removes that entire defect: every **linear** two-local derivation is ordinary.
