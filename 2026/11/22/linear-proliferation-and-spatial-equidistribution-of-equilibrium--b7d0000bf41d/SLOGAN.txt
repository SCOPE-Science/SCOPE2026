In the three-dimensional cosine–memristor map, the number of distinct equilibrium lines grows as \(4\mu\gamma[b_\mu(1-b_\mu)-a_\mu(1-a_\mu)]+O(1)\) when amplification grows, and their transverse coordinates have an explicit asymptotic density. Outside a bounded number of exceptional lines, both transverse multipliers are uniformly expanding, even though every line retains one neutral tangent multiplier.
