Every undirected two-letter de Bruijn network on \(q^2\) vertices, \(q\ge3\), has an explicit radius-one identifying code with only \(q+\lfloor q/3\rfloor\) sensors, at most \(4/3\) times optimal. A universal lower bound of \(q\) sensors accompanies the construction, with exact optima \(4,5,6\) for alphabet sizes \(3,4,5\).
