For the Liouville sign of a product-to-LCM quotient over any arithmetic window \(n,n+c,\ldots,n+kc\), exactly three stride-valuation regimes allow perfect positive/negative density balance. Odd strides balance at \(k+1=3\cdot2^r\); strides with \(\nu_2(c)=1\) balance at odd powers of two, those with \(\nu_2(c)=2\) at even powers of two, and no stride divisible by eight balances.
