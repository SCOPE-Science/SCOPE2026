# Scientific review: local clique factorization

**Correctness — PASS.** For terminal distance at most two, every terminal-terminal edge must be deleted; every length-two terminal path through a terminal is then automatically hit. At each nonterminal vertex, the remaining adjacent terminals must form a clique in their original induced graph, and different nonterminal choices act on disjoint edges. The proof derives the complete product, the exact minimum, inclusion-minimal configurations and the complete multipartite factorization without asymptotic assumptions. An independently implemented graph-distance checker agrees for every labelled graph of order at most five and every eligible terminal set (17,804 instances). A second checker independently covers 1,905 complete-multipartite instances. The universal statements are proved, not extrapolated.

**Originality — PASS, with historical-priority risk.** Bosek–Serra–Vena (arXiv:2611.20287v1) supply the definition, shortest-path transversal reformulation, and cardinality lower bound but not the neighborhood-clique factorization, full spectra, or the general maximal-clique minimality criterion. Prabha–Sandhiya–Manuel (arXiv:2610.36821v1) study edge transversals of globally maximal geodesics, a different condition. The most relevant indexed geodesic-transversal records also concern globally maximal paths or vertex transversals. Targeted mathematical-record searches and inspected earlier literature found no dominating formula, though retrieval and indexing are not exhaustive. The graph-universal clique-polynomial realization and exact sharp multipartite rigidity exceed the earlier scalar bound.

**Value — PASS.** This is a broad structural reduction for an emerging terminal-distance interdiction invariant, not an isolated numerical computation. It transforms a global shortest-path edge-hitting requirement into independent local clique choices on every graph with terminal diameter two; determines complete cardinality and inclusion-minimal spectra; exposes a universal nonreal-root obstruction; and gives a natural, fully classified real-rooted subclass with a sharp equality boundary and explicit optimum reconfiguration geometry. No algorithmic tractability for arbitrary clique factors is asserted.

**Closest sources and limits.** The primary paper's full seven-page content was inspected, notably Lemma 2.6, Theorem 4.1 and the concluding equality-case question. The earlier edge-geodesic-transversal paper was only available by abstract here. Related indexed complete-multipartite geodesic-transversal summaries could not be verified in full text; they address different definitions. Broader historical search and independent validation remain open.
