For every weighted two-digit Cantor Fourier tower, each atom's local inverse-Gram variance is exactly its inverse mass times one common arithmetic factor, \(\bigl(2^n w_{\boldsymbol\delta}\bigr)^{-1}\prod_j\csc^2(\pi d t_j/b)\). This yields an exact all-depth inverse-trace/determinant identity and proves that uniform spatial masses and sine-maximizing digit frequencies jointly minimize both average and worst-coordinate reconstruction noise; at odd quotient base \(q\), the sharp mean inverse-variance barrier is \(\cos^{-2n}(\pi/(2q))\).
