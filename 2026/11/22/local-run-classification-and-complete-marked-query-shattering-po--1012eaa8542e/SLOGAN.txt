For proper three-colourings of every finite cycle, the *entire* vertex–colour equality shattering complex is a cyclic collection of selected runs of lengths at most three: singleton runs have three target colours, while two- and three-vertex runs have six allowable colour markings each. A single logarithmic coefficient formula counts every shattered marked-query support and refines the previously known \(\lfloor3n/4\rfloor\) VC-dimension bound. The resulting complex is nonpure on every even cycle of length at least six, with an exact family of lower-dimensional maximal faces.
