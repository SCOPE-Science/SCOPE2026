# Scientific review — locally constant suspension roofs

## Correctness (C): PASS
The proof treats an arbitrary finite-window positive real-valued roof by higher-block recoding. A first-different-letter argument constructs a uniform separated family of size at least \(p(\lfloor tn/(2M)\rfloor)\); compactness of the cross-section supplies the separation constant, and uniform continuity of the finite interval of flow times transfers it to discrete samples. For each target scale a uniformly finite phase mesh and a finite coordinate cylinder produce a spanning bound \(K_\varepsilon p(\lceil tn/m\rceil+O_\varepsilon(1))\). Bounded doubling equates the resulting fixed rescalings. The treatment of negative times follows inversion symmetry. In the explicit substitution, the incidence matrix yields an unbounded \(2^k\) letter-count discrepancy, proving non-cohomology; its factor complexity is linearly bounded by a supertile decomposition and below by Morse–Hedlund. The finite checker supports only these finite identities, not the general proof. Remaining scientific risks include unnoticed boundary conventions in the quotient-chart argument; those are addressed by the extended-flow map rather than identifying phases discontinuously.

## Mathematical value (V): PASS
This gives a natural, clearly delimited positive answer to a variable-roof question left open in the motivating paper. It preserves a growth-order invariant finer than polynomial and topological entropy, for a broad class that includes highly nonuniform return sums and irrational roof ratios. The explicit primitive-substitution example confirms the theorem is not merely the already noted cohomologous-to-constant case. The statement deliberately excludes arbitrary continuous roofs, where a fixed symbolic block need not control all accumulated timing errors.
