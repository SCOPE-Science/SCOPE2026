For the three-branch quadratic stochastic interval map with two discontinuities, every nonconstant periodic orbit in the smooth interior is hyperbolically repelling, of any prime period and for all admissible parameters. The exact multiplier is \(\prod_j[1+(x_{j+1}-x_j)^2/(x_{j+1}(1-x_{j+1}))]\), even though individual Euclidean branch derivatives can be below one.
