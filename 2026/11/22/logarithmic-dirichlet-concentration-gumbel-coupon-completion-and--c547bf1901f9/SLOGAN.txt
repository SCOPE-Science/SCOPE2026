With \(q\) symmetric Pólya colors each starting at concentration asymptotic to \(\kappa\log q\), the complete-coverage time has a Gumbel limit centered at \(qa_q(q^{1/a_q}-1)\), rather than the fixed-concentration Fréchet regime. The last unseen color's latent probability is asymptotic to \(e^{-1/\kappa}/q\), despite a typical color having probability asymptotic to \(1/q\).
