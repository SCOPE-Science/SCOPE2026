# Verification
The mathematical limit follows from the exact Dirichlet--multinomial zero-count factorial-moment identity, a logarithmic product estimate with error vanishing for positive shapes of order log q, and the conjugate Beta posterior for labels unseen at a fixed time. The last label belongs to this unseen population with probability approaching one after taking an arbitrarily early fixed Gumbel-window cutoff.

`verify.py` uses standard-library exact fractions to compare the closed factorial moments with independent composition enumeration for small q and to check the conditional Beta variance formula. It also performs explicit finite q logarithmic factorial-moment diagnostics for three shape growth rates; discrepancies decrease over the two largest tested sizes. These are finite consistency checks, not certificates of the infinite theorem.

The source comparison is qualified because several close public records were only accessible at title/abstract level, and a more general triangular-array theorem might have implications not recognized here. No independent mathematical audit has been performed.
