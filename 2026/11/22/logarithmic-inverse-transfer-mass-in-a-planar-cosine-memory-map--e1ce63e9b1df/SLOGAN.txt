For the full-plane cosine-memory map \(F(x,y)=(a+bc\,x\cos(dy),y+ex)\) with \(bcde\ne0\), each target has \(2|de|R/\pi+O(1)\) predecessors in the strip \(|x|\le R\), while its regular inverse-Jacobian mass is \(2\log R/(\pi|bc|)+O(1)\). This yields an exact logarithmic area law and a non-sigma-finite pushforward of planar Lebesgue measure, despite the absence of singular mass over null target sets.
