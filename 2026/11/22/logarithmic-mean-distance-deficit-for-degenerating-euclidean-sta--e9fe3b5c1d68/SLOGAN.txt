For an arbitrarily thin stadium of segment length \(L\) and cap radius \(r\), the perimeter-normalized average distance of two uniformly sampled boundary points exceeds \(1/6\) by precisely \((r/L)^2\log(L/r)+O((r/L)^2)\). The leading logarithmic coefficient is exactly one, quantifying sharp saturation of the perimeter inequality along a naturally rounded degeneration.\n
