Uniform extreme points of the normalized \(n\times n\) Monge polytope have an exact squared-Frobenius-norm Mellin spectrum and a Pareto–logarithmic radial limit. Biasing vertices by the \(s\)-th power of their collision probability yields a sharp transition at \(s=1\): macroscopic rectangle sides below the threshold, independent uniform logarithmic scales at the threshold, and atomic zeta-distributed sides above it, with an explicit \(1/\log n\) crossover window.
