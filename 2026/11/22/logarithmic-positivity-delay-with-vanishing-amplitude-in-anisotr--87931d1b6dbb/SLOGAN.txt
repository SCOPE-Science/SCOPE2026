On a four-cycle with alternating positive conductances, the fractional heat operator \(e^{-tL^\alpha}\) is entrywise positive for every \(t>0\) exactly when \(0<\alpha\leq1\); for \(\alpha>1\), only opposite-corner entries are initially negative and each recovers at a single sharp time. As the weaker-to-stronger conductance ratio \(r\downarrow0\), the recovery time grows as \((\alpha-1)\log(1/r)/(2b)^\alpha\) while the largest \(\ell^\infty\)-norm amplification falls to \(1+\alpha r/(2e)+o(r)\).
