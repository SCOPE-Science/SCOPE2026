## Mathematical value — PASS

The Roman surface is a canonical singular algebraic surface with a nontrivial semidefinite convex hull. The full random-width distribution is an intrinsic integral-geometric descriptor. Its maximum occurs along three great circles, but logarithmically rare almost-maximal views concentrate at their six intersections; this distinction has an exact two-term probability coefficient.
