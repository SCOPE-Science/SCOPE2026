In a vertex-growing, uniformly sampled random \(k\)-hypergraph, repeat attempts have a sharp rank dichotomy: for \(k\ge3\) they occur only finitely often almost surely, whereas for \(k=2\) their count has a logarithmic strong law and Gaussian fluctuations with coefficient \(2(1-p)^2/p^2\). In the graph case this count is exactly the excess first Betti number caused by retaining parallel edges rather than collapsing them.
