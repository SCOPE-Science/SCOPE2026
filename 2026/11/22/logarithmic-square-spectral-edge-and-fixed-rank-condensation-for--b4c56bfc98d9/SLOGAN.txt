For compact radially damped cross-products of distinct coprime monomial Hardy compositions, every fixed positive eigenvalue of the Hermitian part approaches the spectral edge \(2\) with the same exact gap \(\pi^2(\log q)^2/\log^2(1/(1-\rho))\), where \(q\) is the larger degree. This gives a sharp quantitative radial counterpart of the known undamped polynomial-cutoff numerical-radius law, now including every fixed eigenvalue rank.
