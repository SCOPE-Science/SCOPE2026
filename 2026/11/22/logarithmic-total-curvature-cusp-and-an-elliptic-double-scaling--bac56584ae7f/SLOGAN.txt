At the classical inflection radius of a standard torus knot, total absolute curvature has a sharp positive \(\delta^2\log(1/|\delta|)\) cusp with an explicit winding-dependent coefficient. When the meridional winding grows and the tube radius scales like \(p^2/q^2\), the normalized total curvature converges uniformly to a strictly increasing elliptic-integral profile with its own universal logarithmic cusp.
