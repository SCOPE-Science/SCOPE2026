In a uniform random recursive tree, vertices with exactly \(r\) children first appear on the logarithmically shifted birth scale \(\sqrt{n/(\log n)^r}\): for each fixed finite set of degrees, the rescaled birth-label point processes converge to independent Poisson processes with intensity \(x/(2^r r!)\,dx\). Among vertices with at most \(K\) children, the oldest has exactly \(K\) children with probability tending to one.
