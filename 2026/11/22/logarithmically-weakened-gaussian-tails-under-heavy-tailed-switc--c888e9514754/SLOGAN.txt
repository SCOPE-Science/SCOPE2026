Alternating strictly positive harmonic traps with soft-sojourn survival \(\Pr(W>t)\sim c t^{-\beta}\), \(\beta>1\), produces a stationary Brownian density equal asymptotically to the soft-equilibrium Gaussian multiplied by an exact factor proportional to \([\log(|x|/\sqrt{D/a})]^{1-\beta}\). The coefficient depends only on the residence tail amplitude, the mean cycle time, and the soft trap rate—not the stiff trap rate—when the stiff interval is fixed and positive.
