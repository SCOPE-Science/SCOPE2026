Possible unpublished exact enumerators or unrecognized equivalent consequences of more general partition identities remain priority risks.

**Mathematical value: PASS.** The rare event that one of \(n\) arms bridges the hubs generates a nonlocal, strictly positive order-\(n^{-1}\) covariance between two vertex-disjoint connection events. The activity maximizing that finite-size signal is determined uniquely for every \(n\ge4\), with a nontrivial \(22/(3n)\) shift and universal \(4/27\) peak coefficient. This is an all-size quantitative correlation law for a natural infinite family, not a fixed-graph table entry.

The result is limited to homogeneous fugacity and disjoint pairs of leaves. It does not establish the general conjecture of connectivity association on arbitrary graphs, and it has not been independently validated. Historical priority remains provisional.
