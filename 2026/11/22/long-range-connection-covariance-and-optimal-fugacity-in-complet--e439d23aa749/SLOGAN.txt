In the unrooted arboreal gas on \(K_{2,n}\), connections between two disjoint pairs of leaves are strictly positively correlated at every positive activity, with an exact covariance decaying as \(n^{-1}\). The unique covariance-maximizing activity lies above one and approaches it as \(1+22/(3n)\), while the sharp rescaled peak converges to \(4/27\).
