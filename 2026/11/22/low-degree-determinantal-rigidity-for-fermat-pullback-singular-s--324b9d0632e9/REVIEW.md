# Scientific review: initial Jacobian syzygies of Fermat-pullback surfaces

**Correctness — PASS.** The first-syzygy module of the powers of the three regular forms is Koszul. Below the common generator degree \((m-1)a\), the intermediary syzygy coefficients must vanish. The resulting kernel of the rank-three Jacobian matrix is generated over the polynomial ring by its primitive cofactor vector because the maximal minors are coprime. This proves all graded pieces through the stated endpoint, not just minimal degree. First-jet incidence gives the generic gcd condition and eliminates off-base singularities, while transversality and Bézout give the \(a^3\) ordinary multiple points. Local analytic coordinates give \(\mu=\tau=(m-1)^3\); isolated singularities force integrality. A separate exact checker confirms a concrete instance and the seven kernel dimensions but is not used to extrapolate the theorem.

**Originality — PASS, with priority risk.** The closest initiating study, Dimca–Sticlaru (arXiv:2604.24308), relates the complete graded Betti numbers and singular-scheme degree abstractly but does not supply this family's initial Jacobian-syzygy module. Dimca's lower bound (arXiv:1408.2244) plus the obvious determinant vector already imply minimal degree \(3a-3\); that equality is explicitly treated here as previously implied. The nontrivial claimed addition is that *every* relation through \((m-2)a\) is a polynomial multiple of the single determinant vector, giving a complete Hilbert-function segment and uniqueness of the minimal generator. Full texts of the nearby 2014, 2016, 2025 and 2026 works were compared for implication as well as terminology. A differently phrased unindexed commutative-algebra result might entail the statement.

**Value — PASS.** The initial graded syzygy module, not merely its first nonzero degree, is a natural invariant in the current study of Jacobian algebras of singular hypersurfaces. The result specifies the exact number of relations at every early degree for an infinite family of integral surfaces with explicit controlled singularities. It tests a natural three-form analogue of the two-form gradient-cross-product construction and gives data that the general degree and singularity Betti identities do not determine.

**Limitations and risks.** Restricted to the stated range and hypotheses over characteristic zero. No full minimal free resolution or first new relation after the cutoff is claimed. Genericity is proved by incidence arguments; the small exact example checks rather than replaces this proof. Equivalent unindexed literature could affect publication priority. Independent review or validation has not been performed.
