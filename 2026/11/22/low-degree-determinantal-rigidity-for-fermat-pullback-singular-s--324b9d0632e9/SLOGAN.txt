For a three-form Fermat pullback \(F=g_1^m+g_2^m+g_3^m\) in four variables with a regular base and primitive Jacobian minors, every Jacobian syzygy of coefficient degree at most \((m-2)a\) is a multiple of one determinantal relation of degree \(3a-3\). General degree-\(a\) triples produce integral surfaces with exactly \(a^3\) isolated ordinary \(m\)-fold points, providing a fully quantified initial syzygy module for singular projective surfaces.
