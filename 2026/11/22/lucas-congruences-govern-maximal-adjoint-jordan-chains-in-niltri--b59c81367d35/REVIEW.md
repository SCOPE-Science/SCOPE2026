# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
For each matrix-unit generator, the top adjoint power is a single central coefficient. The binomial commutator expansion and the exact superdiagonal path lengths force the coefficient \((-1)^{n-i-1}\binom{n-2}{i-1}\prod_{j\ne i} a_j\). The image is at most one dimensional and the operator's next power vanishes, so a nonzero top power is equivalent to one longest possible Jordan block. Counting full-support tuples plus permitted exactly-one-zero tuples gives the formula. The defining ideal consists of squarefree monomials; its minimal primes are precisely the stated hyperplanes and coordinate pairs, proving the scheme claim without an unproved reducedness assumption. Lucas's theorem supplies the digit criterion. Eighteen direct finite-field checks, with random higher-diagonal perturbations and extension fields, corroborate special instances only.

The exact \(n=5\) case, including its characteristic-three component resonance, is already established by a prior calculation and is **not claimed new**. Its exact \(n=5\) rank-three / rank-two formula agrees with the present specialization. The new claim is the universal \(n\ge3\) maximal-adjoint-block count and all-dimensional reduced scheme/component decomposition, together with the infinite base-\(p\) codimension transition. Searches of mathematical finding records for adjoint powers, longest Jordan chains, Lucas congruences, niltriangular Lie algebras and degeneracy ideals found nearby results on the Witt nilradical, matrix powers of incidence radicals, and first adjoint rank, none of which implies the all-\(n\) statement. Accessible primary papers on Jordan–Kronecker coadjoint invariants, triangular Jordan matrix types, solvable Jordan–Chevalley theory, and strong ad-nilpotence were materially inspected. Residual risks: a nonindexed classical binomial-adjoint calculation or a previously published all-dimensional component formula could cover the result; no claim of exhaustive historical priority is made.

## Value — PASS
For every \(n\), maximal adjoint nilpotency index is a canonical conjugacy-invariant measure of the generic Lie action, and the nonmaximal locus controls its degeneration geometry. The answer exhibits a characteristic-dependent and infinitely recurring codimension-one versus codimension-two transition, and counts all maximal-index elements exactly over every extension field. This goes beyond the single previously established \(n=5\) case and beyond ordinary matrix Jordan partitions. The value is the natural all-dimensional invariant and its geometric classification, not a claim of a general theorem about all nilpotent Lie algebras.

## Checked literature and limits
Bolsinov–Zhang (arXiv:1211.0579) treat coadjoint Jordan–Kronecker pencils, not the top power of a niltriangular adjoint map. Cagliero–Szechtman (arXiv:1512.09096) study solvable matrix Lie algebras in characteristic zero without the modular counting theorem. Fuchs–Kirillov (arXiv:2204.00066) count Jordan types of triangular matrices rather than their adjoint operators. The 2022 strong ad-nilpotence paper treats different criteria and setting. The proof does not determine all other Jordan blocks; exhaustive equivalence search in earlier unindexed literature has not been established. Finite checks cannot replace the all-dimensional proof.
