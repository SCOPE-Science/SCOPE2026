In strictly upper-triangular Lie algebras over finite fields, the number of elements whose adjoint action has a longest possible Jordan block is exactly \(q^{\binom n2-n+1}(q-1)^{n-2}(q-1+M)\), where \(M\) is the product of one plus the base-\(p\) digits of \(n-2\). The nonmaximal-block locus is a reduced union of explicit coordinate hyperplanes and codimension-two planes, and its codimension changes precisely when \(n-1\) is a single nonzero base-\(p\) digit times a power of \(p\).
