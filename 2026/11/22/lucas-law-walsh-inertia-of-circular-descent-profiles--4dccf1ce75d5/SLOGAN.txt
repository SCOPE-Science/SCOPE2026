For unrestricted permutations read around a circle, the nonzero Walsh characters of the circular descent-position distribution are exactly vertex sets covered by matchings of \(C_n\); their signs alternate with half the character weight. A doubled perfect-matching boundary produces exact convolution rank \(L_n-\mathbf1_{2\mid n}\), and the entire positive, negative and zero inertia follows from the six-periodic signed matching polynomial.
