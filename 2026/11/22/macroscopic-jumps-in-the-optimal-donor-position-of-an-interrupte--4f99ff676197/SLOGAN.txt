A one-donor flower sequence with geometrically departing pollinators has a strictly convex adjacent-score sign factor, reducing the global placement search to an early block and the terminal flower. For every fixed pollen-retention rate and positive weighting of foreign-pollen receipt, some visitor-persistence probability makes an early donor position at most \(O(\log n)\) tie with the terminal position \(n\), an arbitrarily large positional jump; ten flowers already exhibit a rigorously checked tie between positions \(4\) and \(10\).
