For convolution on a quantum two-torus, three affinely independent common Fourier modes attain the sharp \(L^2\times L^2\to L^\infty\) Young bound exactly when their amplitudes are proportional and either the magnetic flux through their triangle is integral or at most two modes survive. At half-integral flux, the exact operator norm of every weighted three-mode packet has a sharp squared-amplitude triangle transition, with the equal-weight norm \(\sqrt6\) instead of \(3\).
