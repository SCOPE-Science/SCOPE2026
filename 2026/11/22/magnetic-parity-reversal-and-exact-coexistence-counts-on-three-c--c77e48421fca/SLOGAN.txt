On a three-colour antiferromagnetic Möbius ladder, changing the parity of the number of rungs changes the positive-field ground-state response from a single saturation crossing to three exact crossings at \(H/J=1,2,3\). Every finite-size plateau and coexistence degeneracy is explicit; at saturation the twisted-seam correction to the critical independent-set count is exactly \(-(-2)^n\).
