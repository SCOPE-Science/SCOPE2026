On the general \(Z_{15}\) K3 tail of a Kunev degeneration, the canonical gluing rational curve passes through every ADE singularity: two \(A_3\) and two \(A_1\) points. Its resolution meets one terminal curve in each ADE chain, with fractional corrections \(3/4,3/4,1/2,1/2\), realizing a smooth \((-2)\)-curve with weighted square \(1/2\).
