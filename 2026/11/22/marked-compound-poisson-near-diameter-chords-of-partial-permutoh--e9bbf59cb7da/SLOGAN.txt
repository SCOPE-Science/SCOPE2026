For two uniformly chosen vertices of a sparse partial permutohedron, the squared Euclidean diameter deficit has an exact zero-atom probability and a sharp birthday-scale transition. When the number of labels is of order the square root of the ambient dimension, the deficit converges to a compound-Poisson sum of independent products of two uniform marks, with atom \(e^{-\lambda}\) and nonzero jump density \(-\log t\).
