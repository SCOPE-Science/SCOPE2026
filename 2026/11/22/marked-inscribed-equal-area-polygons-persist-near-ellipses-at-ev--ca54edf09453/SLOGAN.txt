For every fixed odd \(n\ge5\), any sufficiently small \(C^2\) deformation of an ellipse contains a locally unique inscribed convex equal-area \(n\)-gon through **each** prescribed boundary point. An explicit step-two angular inverse gives the first variation and reveals an even-order obstruction to differentiable continuation, establishing a local case of a published marked-inscription question without asserting its global resolution.
