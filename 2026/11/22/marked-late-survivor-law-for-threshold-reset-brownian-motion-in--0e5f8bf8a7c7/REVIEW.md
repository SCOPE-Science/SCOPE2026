# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact multi-mark renewal identity follows by partitioning the path according to the number of already completed reset-edge excursions. The leading free-excursion tail is half on each future exit edge, whereas the total target-survival prefactor is computed independently from the geometric random-sum decomposition. For each fixed number of resets, the late-observation contribution is controlled by the polynomially decaying density of convolution powers; integration over a long already-completed excursion is asymptotically negligible. The fixed-count marked masses sum to one after normalization, excluding escape of conditional probability to arbitrarily many resets. Fresh post-threshold cycles independently provide the geometric future continuation. The analytic proof states all domains, including broad wedges with infinite expected excursion times; the 171 finite checker assertions do not substitute for asymptotic proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Biswas, Majumdar and Pal (arXiv:2504.13501v1, first public April 18, 2025) provide threshold-reset renewal rules, while Najeeb, Pal and Prasad (arXiv:2505.04208v1, first public May 7, 2025) provide the free absorbing-wedge kernel. A subsequent direct wedge threshold-reset paper (Najeeb, Pal and Prasad, arXiv:2611.26345v1, first public November 19, 2026, after the initiating literature window) explicitly establishes the free-exit current tails and the overall target-survival tail, as well as unconditional reset-count PGFs. Those facts are fully credited. Duvall's arXiv:2606.27488 derives survivor-conditioned geometric completed-return counts for an open intermittent map, and Vezzani, Barkai and Burioni's arXiv:1804.02932 treats the generic one-big-jump mechanism. Thus geometric survivor-count convergence *alone* is not independently novel. The present combined, precise, wedge-specific statement includes the fair retrospective *exit side* of the ongoing excursion, the tight unscaled last-reset epoch, and the distinct *eventual total* reset-count law. No inspected complete primary source or accessible closest published summary stated or implied all those marked quantities together. The result is susceptible to being derived as an unstated specialization of an existing generic marked renewal theorem; a further specialist literature comparison could change this qualified originality assessment. The Chupeau–Bénichou–Majumdar wedge paper's full publisher text was not accessible; its bibliographic abstract was reviewed.

## Value — PASS

This is a physically natural extension of reset-count statistics from whole-trajectory totals to observation-time conditional path structure. It distinguishes how many interventions happened, whether the currently active long excursion will eventually end in another intervention, and the distribution of interventions still to come. The half-fair rare-survivor exit mark persists for arbitrarily biased unconditional wedge splitting and the last reset remains on a bounded physical time scale despite a large search time. The exact multivariate law and its constructive decomposition are useful conditional benchmarks; no paper-sized universality claim is needed.

## Scope and residual limitations

The result is distributional and asymptotic for a fixed reset point and pure threshold resetting. The current excursion's future exit mark is retrospective. Finite-time error rates, moment convergence of the finite-time total count, spatially varying transport and independent Poisson resets are not claimed. General heavy-tail renewal theory remains a potential prior equivalent.
