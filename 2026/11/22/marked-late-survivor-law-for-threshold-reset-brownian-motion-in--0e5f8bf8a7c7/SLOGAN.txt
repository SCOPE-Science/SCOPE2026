For a threshold-reset Brownian search in a planar wedge, conditioning on unusually late target absorption leaves a geometric number of past resets and selects the currently long excursion's eventual exit edge with asymptotically equal probabilities, despite unequal unconditional exit probabilities. The last reset remains at a tight, unscaled epoch, while the eventual total number of resets converges to \(K+B(1+G)\), with independent geometric \(K,G\) and a fair Bernoulli \(B\).
