For linear preferential-attachment trees with reinforcement \(a_n\) of order the final size \(n\), first-time nonroot parent activations converge as a birth-age-marked point process to a homogeneous Poisson cloud on the unit square. An exact thinning coupling controls the **entire marked history** in total variation by \(2(n-2)/a_n^2\), not just its event count.
