When each arriving vertex independently chooses two distinct keys from a pool of \(m\), its first graph triangles appear on the \(m^{2/3}\) scale, and their birth times together with their normalized witnessing keys converge to a Poisson process of intensity \(4t^2\,dt\,du\). The entire triangle-birth count converges to a nonhomogeneous Poisson process, and at each bounded time its Poisson approximation has a uniform \(O(m^{-1/3})\) total-variation error.
