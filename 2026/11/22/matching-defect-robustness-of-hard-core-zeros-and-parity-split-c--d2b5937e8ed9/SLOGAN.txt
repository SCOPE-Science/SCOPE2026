Deleting any matching of cross-edges from the balanced complete bipartite hard-core gas preserves strict left-half-plane stability of its fugacity zeros, with exactly two negative real zeros at even shore size and three at odd shore size. In the full crown-graph defect, the zeros closest to the origin form a scaled vertical lattice, while the three negative-real scales separate as \(n^{-1}\), \(n^{-1/2}\), and a parity-selected branch approaching \(-2\).
