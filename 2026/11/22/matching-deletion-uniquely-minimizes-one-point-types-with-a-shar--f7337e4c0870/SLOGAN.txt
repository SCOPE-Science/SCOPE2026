Among height-two named posets made by deleting \(k\) cross-comparisons from a complete two-level order, the number of one-point types is uniquely minimized when the deleted comparisons form a matching. The exact second minimum is achieved precisely by replacing two disjoint deleted edges with a two-edge star, producing a sharp additional cost of \(2+3^{k-2}2^{a+b-2k}\).
