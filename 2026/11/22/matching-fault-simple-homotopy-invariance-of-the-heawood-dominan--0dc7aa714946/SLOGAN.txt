Deleting any matching of up to seven edges from the Heawood graph leaves its dominance complex simple-homotopy equivalent to \(S^6\): every single matching-edge deletion is an explicit elementary-collapse inclusion, so the unique integral sixth-homology class persists. The result covers all \(3{,}462\) matchings, including those not extendible to a perfect matching, through \(121\) exhaustive symmetry-representative collapse certificates.
