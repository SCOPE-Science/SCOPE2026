On every triangular graph \(T_n=L(K_n)\) with \(n\ge3\), every minimum standard perfect Roman function has its label-two vertices at the edges of one root-clique matching of size \(\lfloor(2n+3)/8\rfloor\); the labels zero and one are then forced. The exact optimum weight is \(\binom n2+r-2r(n-2r)\) and the number of optimum functions is \(n!/(2^r r!(n-2r)!)\), where \(r=\lfloor(2n+3)/8\rfloor\).
