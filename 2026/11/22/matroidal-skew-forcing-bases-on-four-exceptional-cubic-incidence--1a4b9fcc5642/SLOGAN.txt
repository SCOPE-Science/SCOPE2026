On the Heawood, Möbius–Kantor, Pappus, and Desargues graphs, minimum skew-zero-forcing sets form matroid-base pairs: their complete cardinality polynomials are explicit squares, with optimum counts 784, 2,304, 5,184, and 15,625. The Desargues half-configurations are exactly the 125 spanning trees of \(K_5\); every optimum is frozen under token sliding, although all optimum sets can be connected by geodesic token jumps.
