Every maximal closed substring of the symmetric five-run binary word \(0^a1^b0^c1^b0^a\) belongs to four explicit endpoint families, giving exactly \(a+b+4+\min(a-1,c-1)+2\mathbf1_{\{c>a\}}\) occurrences. The complete length/count series is rational, and the sharp maximum at every length \(n\ge6\) is \(\lfloor2n/3\rfloor+4\).
