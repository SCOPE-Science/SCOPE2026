# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The nonstandard joint step is a common phase translation by \(2^{-s}\) that fixes every ratio indexed by \(J_d\) while flipping the central bit. This gives independence from the whole generated finite sigma-algebra, not just pairwise correlations. The Fourier-series sawtooth kernel yields a closed rational covariance and the strict exclusion of all indices outside the independent family. The exact rational checker replays 10,000 binary joint cells and 6,232 complete-vector fibers. Finite checks are supplementary; the proof treats all even denominators.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
Wu's June 2026 paper treats a different but natural binomial GCD family indexed by lower entries; Guo and collaborators classify a further gcd family using digitwise congruences. Neither derives this full common-phase carry vector. Cui and Yang's later November 2026 paper establishes the single-ratio fair marginal and counts simultaneous conditions across prime-power phases, but does not supply the common-phase cross-ratio joint law. Ford and Konyagin address central binomial coefficients and distinct phases. A classical sawtooth \(\gcd\)-integral already implies the **pairwise kernel** and is not claimed as separately novel. The new assessed content is the complete eventwise independence of the maximal odd-reduced-denominator vector and its sharp obstruction. The closest published related results concern valuations over discrete blocks, rather than arbitrary-ratio Lebesgue phase vectors. Earlier implicit coverage in general Fourier or dynamical literature remains a priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The arbitrary-ratio Kummer framework naturally asks what happens when different binomial ratios are tested at the same phase. This provides a full structural characterization: an explicitly constructible and uniquely maximal jointly conditionally independent family, plus a strict positive-correlation certificate for all excluded ratios. Such joint independence cannot be concluded from fair individual marginals or zero pairwise correlations alone. It does not, by itself, establish an asymptotic count over integers.

## Scientific and verification limitations

Literature coverage and exact arithmetic checks are reproducible but not independent mathematical validation.
