In a free nilpotent semigroup where every word product exceeding length \(N\) becomes zero, all inclusion-maximal commutative subsemigroups consist of one primitive-root power chain together with a complete long-word tail, plus a single extra all-tail family precisely when \(N\) is odd. This gives an exact family census, complete size and centralizer spectra, and a parity transition from one maximum family (odd \(N\)) to \(a^{N/2}\) maxima (even \(N\)).
