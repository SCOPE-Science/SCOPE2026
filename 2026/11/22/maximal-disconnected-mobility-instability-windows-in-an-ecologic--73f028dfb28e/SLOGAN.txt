A fixed ecological cooperation network can lose, regain, and lose homogeneous linear stability repeatedly as only overall mobility increases: a weighted \((m+1)\)-habitat star has exactly \(m\) disconnected Turing-instability windows, and no two-species connected graph of that size can have more. The game parameters and strategy mobility ratio are constant, all corridor weights are positive and normalized, and the claim is linear—not a theorem on nonlinear pattern formation.
