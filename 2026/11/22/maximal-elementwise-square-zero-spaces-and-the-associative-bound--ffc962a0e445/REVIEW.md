# Scientific review: maximal elementwise square-zero spaces in the three-generator exterior algebra

## Correctness — PASS

The scalar-square obstruction removes the unit coordinate in odd characteristic. The remaining square equation is exactly the split quadratic form \(Q(a,b)=a\wedge b\) on \(V\oplus\bigwedge^2 V\); the top-degree line is radical. The proof classifies every maximal totally singular subspace by its tangent projection \(U\) and a form \(\beta\in\bigwedge^2U^*\otimes\bigwedge^3V\), so the four strata, their dimensions, and the finite-field counts have complete—not sample-based—arguments. Multiplication of two vectors of a totally singular space cancels the cubic term; it reduces to the wedge of their tangent parts, giving the sharp criterion \(\dim U\le2\) for associative closure. Inner automorphisms and linear changes give exactly four orbits. An explicit affine-projective identification establishes the entire intersection graph. A separate orthogonal-incidence count establishes containment degrees.

The standalone independent enumeration of all 33,880 three-subspaces of a split six-space over \(\mathbb F_3\), and the direct pairwise tests over \(\mathbb F_3\) and \(\mathbb F_5\), reproduced the predicted census. These finite checks are supplementary, not proofs for all fields.

## Originality — PASS, provisional

The classical generators of the Klein quadric and their projective incidence are acknowledged as known. Ghorpade–Patil–Pillai, arXiv:0710.5161, classify decomposable subspaces inside one homogeneous exterior power. Domokos–Zubor, arXiv:1403.2916, study pairwise-zero-product spaces and commutative subalgebras; their term "square zero subspace" expressly requires \(D^2=0\), unlike the present *elementwise* condition. Bavula, arXiv:math/0703352, gives the automorphism group used in the orbit proof. Bovdi–Leung, arXiv:1803.03457, continue commutative-subalgebra classification. The closest published computations of homomorphisms into and retractions of the three-generator exterior algebra give relations for generator images and retraction-image counts, but do not state this maximal-space incidence/multiplication classification. A published result for a free anticommutative antiassociative algebra has a different degree-one/degree-two multiplication sign and does not imply this associative exterior-algebra census.

The new content is the unified embedded-space, \(W^2\)-rank, associative-closure and algebra-automorphism-orbit classification. The classical orthogonal-geometry half is not claimed as novel. A general treatment of isotropic subspaces in quadratic algebras could subsume some assertions; historical-priority verification by an independent specialist remains open.

## Value — PASS

Elementwise square-zero is a natural but strictly weaker property than pairwise annihilation in noncommutative algebras. The classification determines precisely when the maximal linear objects actually form associative subalgebras, finds an intrinsic four-orbit boundary, and makes their entire intersection structure and element-incidence multiplicities explicit. The result treats a complete canonical algebraic family, not an arbitrary finite parameter slice. The exact multiplicative distinction is invisible from the pre-existing abstract Klein-quadric generator count alone.

## Limits

Only the three-generator exterior algebra in characteristic different from two is classified; the projective and numeric forms require finite odd fields. The statement is not an exhaustive list of maximal square-zero associative subalgebras under a different inclusion order. Absolute novelty and independent validation are not certified.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
