In the three-generator exterior algebra over any odd finite field, exactly \(2(q^3+q^2+q+1)\) four-dimensional linear spaces are maximal with every individual element square-zero, but only \(1+(q+1)(q^2+q+1)\) are closed under multiplication. Their full intersection graph is the point–hyperplane incidence graph of \(\mathrm{PG}(3,q)\), and four algebra-automorphism orbits distinguish the multiplicative boundary.
