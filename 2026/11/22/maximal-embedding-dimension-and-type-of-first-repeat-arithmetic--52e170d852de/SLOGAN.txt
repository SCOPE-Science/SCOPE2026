For every coprime arithmetic triple \((a,a+d,a+2d)\) with \(a\ge3\), the semigroup of integers admitting at least two distinct nonnegative representations has maximal embedding dimension \(2(a+d)\) and maximal pseudo-Frobenius type \(2(a+d)-1\). An exact two- or three-ideal decomposition explains this rigidity, including the exceptional smallest parameter.
