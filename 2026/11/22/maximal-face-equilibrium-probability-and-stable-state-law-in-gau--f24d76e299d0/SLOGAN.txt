In a Gaussian two-strategy evolutionary game with \(m\) interacting populations, having an equilibrium on every one of the \(3^m\) cube faces has an exact row-dominance criterion, and the number of attracting equilibria then follows a binomial power-of-two law. This maximally multistable phase has probability \(q_m^m\), decaying as \(\exp[-\tfrac12m^2\log m+\tfrac12(1+\log(2/\pi))m^2+O(m^{3/2})]\); for two populations its probability is exactly \(1/36\).
