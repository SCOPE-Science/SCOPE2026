For the exponent-\(p\) extraspecial group of order \(p^{2n+1}\) (odd \(p\)), the maximum proper image size \(p^{n+1}\) occurs among composition-nilpotent endomorphisms for the first time at index three, with an exact Lagrangian–square-zero-restriction sum counting every such map. This separates cube-zero maximal-image behavior from the previously studied square-zero Heisenberg endomorphism count.
