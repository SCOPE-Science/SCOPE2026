For the maximal intersecting families of pairs on \(n\ge4\) points, membership shattering yields exactly the two-skeleton of the complete-graph line-graph clique complex, with both VC and online dimensions equal to three. The pure shattering complex is a wedge of \(\binom{n-1}3+n\binom{n-2}3\) two-spheres, exhibiting a growing topological distinction from the full clique complex.
