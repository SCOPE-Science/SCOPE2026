The edge-membership supports shattered by inclusion-maximal matchings of the three-cube are exactly the matchings of at most three edges without three parallel edges, giving \(1+12z+42z^2+32z^3\) and both VC and Littlestone dimensions three. Their nonpure shattering complex has reduced integral homology \(\mathbb Z^6\) in degree one and \(\mathbb Z^7\) in degree two, with no torsion.
