The four-by-four strictly upper-triangular algebra has exactly \(2(q+1)\) inclusion-maximal linear spaces of square-zero matrices over \(\mathbb F_q\), including one exceptional four-space; all are associative subalgebras, and their two-dimensional-intersection graph is \(K_{q+1,q+1}\). Exactly \(q-1\) have nonzero multiplication, with a characteristic-two switch from noncommutative to commutative multiplication.
