# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact claim is supported by the backward Laplace boundary-value problem, an explicit modified-Bessel solution, the standard Bessel infinite product, rational moment coefficients, a Laplace-asymptotic/exponential-tilting argument and a strictly certified principal-eigenvalue comparison. The positive-profile robustness follows from action convergence and a compact Rayleigh-quotient argument. The accompanying checker reproduces symbolic constants and independent numerical special-function and shooting comparisons. A finite numerical replay is not substituted for these analytical steps.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk

Menon (2026; arXiv:2611.17470v1), inspected through its main text and appendix, gives the mean optimizer and first moment but not the two-tail first-passage comparison. Menon et al. (2023; arXiv:2311.00818) give bounded-interval FPT machinery, including Bessel formulas for specific positive profiles and an extension to power laws. Dos Santos et al. (2022; arXiv:2206.14229) give power-law first-passage formulas with an unbounded domain. None of these inspected materials states the present combination: exact law at the newly determined mean optimizer; sharp loss of the fastest arrivals; higher late-time decay; and stability under positive regularization. The older transform machinery could implicitly produce parts of the claim, so publication priority is not independently established. Comparisons with available published indexed findings about frozen-mixture diffusivity crossovers do not imply this spatially heterogeneous reflecting–absorbing result.

## Value — PASS

First-arrival quantiles and tail survival are natural risk-sensitive transport objectives distinct from mean optimization. Demonstrating their opposite response *for the exact unconstrained mean optimizer*, with closed constants and regularized physically positive profiles, prevents a mistaken stochastic-dominance inference from mean/variance improvements. No uniqueness of the crossing or experimentally robust quantile estimation is promised.
