For independent site percolation on grandparent-augmented Bienaymé trees, the infinite-root-cluster threshold is exactly \(q_m=[m+1-\sqrt{(m+1)^2-4}]/(2m)\) for every offspring law of mean \(m>1\), unlike the offspring-law-sensitive bond threshold. The subcritical root susceptibility is \(1/[1-mp-m^2p(1-p)]\), and with finite second factorial moment the supercritical survival probability grows linearly with an explicit amplitude depending on that moment.
