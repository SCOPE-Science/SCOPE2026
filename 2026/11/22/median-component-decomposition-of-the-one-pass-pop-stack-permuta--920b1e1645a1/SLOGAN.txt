The graph of permutations sortable by one pop-stack pass under feasible adjacent swaps is a disjoint union of Cartesian products of Fibonacci cubes, with immovable decreasing layers of length at least three determining the components. Every within-component shortest path is given by Hamming distance on the movable two-layer coordinates, and the component-count generating function is \(\frac{1-z}{(1-z)^2-z^3}\).
