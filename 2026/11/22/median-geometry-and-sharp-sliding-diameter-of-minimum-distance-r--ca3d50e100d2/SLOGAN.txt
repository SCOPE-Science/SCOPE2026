For every radius \(r\ge2\), the token-sliding graph of minimum distance-\(r\) dominating sets of a labelled path is a median graph with an explicit ideal-lattice representation. Its exact diameter and minimum isometric hypercube dimension are both \(kd-2\max\{d-r,0\}\), where \(k=\lceil n/(2r+1)\rceil\) and \(d=(2r+1)k-n\), and its vertex count is an explicit two-endpoint-capacity binomial formula.
