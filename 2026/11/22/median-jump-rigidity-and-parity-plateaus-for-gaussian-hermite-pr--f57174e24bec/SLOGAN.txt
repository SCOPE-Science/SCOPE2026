Every Gaussian-weighted Hermite orthogonal projector has its sharp \(BV\)-to-\(L^2\) error at the unique median jump, regardless of degree. The exact error constants occur in odd–even plateaus and obey \(C_n\sim2^{-1/4}\pi^{-3/4}n^{-1/4}\), even though individual Hermite coefficients can be maximized by off-center jumps.
