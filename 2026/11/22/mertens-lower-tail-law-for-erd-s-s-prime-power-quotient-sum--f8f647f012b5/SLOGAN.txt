For Erdős's prime-power quotient sum \(w(n)/n=\sum_{p^a\parallel n}p^{-a}\), the limiting natural density below a small threshold \(t\) is asymptotic to \(e^{-\gamma}/\log(1/t)\), with the sharp Mertens constant. Conditioned on such a small value, the logarithmic depth has a Pareto limit with survival \(1/\alpha\) for \(\alpha\ge1\).
