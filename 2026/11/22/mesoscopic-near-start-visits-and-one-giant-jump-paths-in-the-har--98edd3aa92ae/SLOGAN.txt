For the harmonic descent chain, visiting the mesoscopically near-start state \(n-h_n\) with \(\log h_n/\log n\to\theta\in(0,1)\) has probability asymptotic to \([h_n\log n(1-\theta)^2]^{-1}\). Conditional on this rare visit, the jump count is size-biased geometric, and its path has one uniformly positioned giant decrement while all remaining decrements have independent uniform logarithmic scales.
