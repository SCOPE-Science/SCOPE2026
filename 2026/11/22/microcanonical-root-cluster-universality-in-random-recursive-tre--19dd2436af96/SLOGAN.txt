Uniformly deleting exactly \(h_n\) edges from a random recursive tree produces the same non-Gaussian Mittag--Leffler root-cluster limit as independent percolation whenever \(p_n=1-h_n/(n-1)\to p\in(0,1)\), with the exact normalization \(n^{p_n}\). An explicit elementary-symmetric formula gives every finite-sample rising-factorial moment, and a tilted Bernoulli local limit proves equivalence even though the deletion count is fixed exactly.
