# Scientific review: minimal generators of identity-preserving transformation-monoid endomorphisms

## Correctness — PASS
The proof has four independently checkable components: specialization of the published complete endomorphism classification to maps fixing the identity; bijection between conjugacy classes and multisets of \((a,b)\) rooted-fiber types; explicit singular-composition rule, including the exceptional constant-identity left zero; and a matching lower and upper bound on the generator number. The crucial converse in the essentiality argument is that an involution commuting with a retraction must fix its roots and permute nonroots inside each retraction fiber. Thus a nontrivial involution exists exactly when a fiber contains at least two nonroots. Direct labeled enumeration, orbit counting, closure of constructed generators, and evaluation of actual map compositions corroborate the proof in the indicated small dimensions. These tests cannot prove the all-degree theorem without the preceding structural argument.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected records
Schein–Teclezghi classify and count all endomorphisms, rather than generating the identity-preserving submonoid. Gould–Grau–Johnson give the generating-rank reduction for the larger semigroup-endomorphism monoid; §8 specifically separates identity-preserving monoid endomorphisms as a further direction. The published full-monoid plane-partition result counts essential orbits in that larger object, not the distinct weighted rooted-fiber orbits or the present size-two essentiality criterion. The inspected published-finding corpus searches and other relevant literature searches revealed no formula for the new monoid's generator rank. Absence of search matches is not proof of uniqueness: unindexed or independently derived equivalent formulae remain possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Minimum generating rank is a natural invariant measuring algebraic complexity. The answer completely resolves the identity-preserving variant highlighted in the 2023 foundational paper for all degrees \(n\ge5\), replacing the full-monoid plane-partition statistic with the exact weighted product \(\prod_{j\ge1}(1-z^j)^{-\lceil j/2\rceil}\) and proving a sharp structural criterion for indispensable retraction generators. No stronger asymptotic or low-degree exceptional classification is asserted.
