The minimal ideals of the four-variable squarefree cubic-truncated algebra have exactly two automorphism orbits over every even finite field, but \((q^2+7q+46+2\gcd(3,q-1))/6\) orbits over \(\mathbb F_q\) when \(q\) is odd. The odd-characteristic classes are controlled by weighted support graphs and three opposite-edge products, while the even-characteristic classes are the two alternating-rank types.
