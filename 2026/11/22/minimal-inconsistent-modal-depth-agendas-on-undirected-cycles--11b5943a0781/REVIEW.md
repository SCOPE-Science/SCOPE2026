# Scientific review: minimal inconsistent modal-depth agendas on undirected cycles

## Correctness — PASS
For an irreflexive symmetric \(n\)-cycle, exact-length \(j\) walk endpoints form \(B_j=\{j-2t\bmod n:0\le t\le j\}\). A positive box requires the marked set to contain its support; a negative box requires a witness outside it. Inclusion-minimal inconsistent supports have exactly one negative and no more than one positive from each of the two parity-nested chains. Bipartite even cycles forbid three-formula cases. On odd \(n=2r+1\), the two positive supports cover the whole cycle exactly in the relevant threshold regime \(e+o\ge n-2\), and the negative support must be strictly beyond its same-parity positive support. These facts prove the complete parameterization and sum \(r(r-1)(2r+5)/6\), and establish the least triple depth \(r+1\) for \(r\ge2\). The independent algorithmic cross-check is the local Python file `verify.py`: `VERIFY_OK walk_orders=59 exhaustive_valuations_orders=13`; finite experiments are supplemental rather than an all-order proof.

The full statements/proofs of these three published-finding corpus findings were read. Targeted published-finding corpus searches for exact covers, minimal inconsistent agendas, iterated box depth and odd/even cycle spectra returned no dominating statement.
