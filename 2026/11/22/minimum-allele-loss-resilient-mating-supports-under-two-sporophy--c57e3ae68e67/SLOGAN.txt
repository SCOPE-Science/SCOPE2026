For \(k\) distinct S-alleles, one-allele-loss-resilient mating needs five heterozygote types at \(k=4\) with pollen dominance but is impossible under full codominance; at \(k=5\), the minima are four versus five, while both equal \(\lceil k/2\rceil\) for \(k\ge6\). The five-allele codominant baseline is the classical five-cycle matching condition, whereas the directed pollen-dominance model yields the sharper small-allele advantage.
