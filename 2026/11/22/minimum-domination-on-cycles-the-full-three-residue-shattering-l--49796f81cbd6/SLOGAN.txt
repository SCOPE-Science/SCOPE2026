For minimum-cardinality dominating sets of the cycle \(C_n\), the full vertex-membership shattering complex has degree one, two, or three depending on \(n\bmod3\), with an exact all-order polynomial and a residue-only test for every shattered pair or triple. A three-edge subdivision lift and a two-defect obstruction give an unrestricted proof; inclusion-minimal domination and minimum independent domination are not conflated.
