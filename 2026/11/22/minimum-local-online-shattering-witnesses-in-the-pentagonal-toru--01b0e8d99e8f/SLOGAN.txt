Exactly 2,050 eight-point induced subgraphs of the pentagonal torus \(C_5\square C_5\) realize a strict local adaptive/static shattering gap \(\operatorname{Ldim}=3>\operatorname{VCdim}=2\); no smaller vertex set can do so. Their 12 coordinate-symmetry orbits and complete distribution of valid decision-tree roots are determined by a reduced exhaustive incidence classification.
