On the graph formed by subdividing every edge of \(K_n\), all minimum power-dominating sensor placements have size \(n-2\) and their optimality-preserving adjacent-slide graph is exactly the one-edge subdivision of the Johnson graph \(J(n,2)\). It is connected with sharp radius \(\min\{n,5\}\) and diameter \(\min\{n,6\}\), for every \(n\ge3\).
