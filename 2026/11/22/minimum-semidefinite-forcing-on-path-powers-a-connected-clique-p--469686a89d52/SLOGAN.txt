For every \(k\ge2\) and \(n\ge k+2\), the minimum positive-semidefinite zero-forcing sets of the path power \(P_n^k\) are exactly its \(2k\) endpoint-anchored seeds plus \(n-k-1\) interior consecutive-block seeds. Their one-vertex-exchange graph is connected with diameter \(n-k\), in sharp contrast to the disconnected ordinary-minimum exchange graph for \(n\ge k+4\).
