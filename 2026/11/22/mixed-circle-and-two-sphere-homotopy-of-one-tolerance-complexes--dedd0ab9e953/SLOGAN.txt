The one-tolerance complex of any disjoint union of nonempty cliques is an integral wedge of \(\binom{r-1}{2}\) circles and \(\sum_{i<j}(a_i-1)(a_j-1)\) two-spheres, where \(r\) is the number of cliques and \(a_i\) their sizes. Its degree-two integral homology splits naturally into pairwise tensor products of block augmentation lattices.
