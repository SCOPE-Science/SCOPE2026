Ordered commuting idempotent pairs on an \(n\)-chain, with one factor a contraction and the other any monotone map, have generating function \(z(1-2z)^2/((1-z)^2(1-3z+z^2)^3)\). Their independent-uniform commutation probability tends to exactly \(1/5\), unlike the exponential rarity for two unrestricted monotone idempotents.
