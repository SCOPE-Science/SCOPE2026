For the ordinary four-cut complex of the ternary Hamming three-cube \(H(3,3)\), the combinatorial Alexander dual is integrally homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{1386}S^2\vee\bigvee^{109}S^3\). Consequently the original pure 22-dimensional cut complex has free reduced homology of ranks 109 in degree 21 and 1,386 in degree 22, and is nonshellable.
