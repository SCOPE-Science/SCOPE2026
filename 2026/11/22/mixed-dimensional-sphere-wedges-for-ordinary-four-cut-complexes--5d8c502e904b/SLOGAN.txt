For every \(n\ge4\), the Alexander dual of the ordinary four-cut complex of the three-row rook graph \(K_3\square K_n\) is a wedge of \(n(n-1)(n-2)\) two-spheres and \((n-1)(n-2)^2/2\) three-spheres. A uniform rectangular-core Morse matching explains both integral sphere families and the resulting nonshellability of the original cut complex.
