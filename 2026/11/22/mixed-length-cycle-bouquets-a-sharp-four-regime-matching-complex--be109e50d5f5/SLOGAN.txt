For a bouquet of arbitrarily sized cycles joined at one hub, the ordinary matching complex has exactly four integral homotopy regimes determined by the number of petal lengths divisible by three and congruent to two modulo three. In particular, two lengths divisible by three force contractibility, whereas exactly one produces two spheres, and the remaining cases give a single sphere or \(2\nu_2-1\) spheres in an explicitly determined dimension.
