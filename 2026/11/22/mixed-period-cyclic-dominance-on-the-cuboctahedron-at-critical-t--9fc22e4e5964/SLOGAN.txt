At the critical two-neighbor contact threshold on the cuboctahedral graph, three-color cyclic dominance has primitive clocks of lengths \(3,6,8,12\), while the refractory Greenberg–Hastings rule supports only length \(3\). An exact full-state census shows that cyclic dominance attracts \(789\) times as many uniformly random configurations to oscillation, and that its shorter nonfixed clocks are isolated while the eight- and twelve-cycles have nontrivial attraction basins.
