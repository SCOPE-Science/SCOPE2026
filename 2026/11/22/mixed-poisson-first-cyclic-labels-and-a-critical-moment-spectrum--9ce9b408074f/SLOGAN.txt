The first cyclic labels in a uniform random mapping converge on the \(\sqrt n\) scale to a Rayleigh-mixed Poisson process, yet their minimum has a critical variance \(n\log n+O(n)\). Every fixed higher integer moment has an explicit, rare-small-core-dominated leading term of order \(n^{s-1}\), exposing a sharp failure of moment convergence despite a well-defined weak limit.
