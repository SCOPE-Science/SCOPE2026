The packaged standalone program checks all 120/540/900/540 original cell counts, 351/3060/5940/3240 quotient simplex counts, all consecutive signed boundary compositions, and integer unit reductions. Armstrong's theorem and the explicit six half-turn-fixed cube centers give the fundamental group.

## Value

**PASS.** The natural first composite six-color rotation has nontrivial isotropy, unlike the previously analyzed prime rotations. Its exact integral homology contains order three in degree one and order two in degree two, while rational homology sees only the ten-dimensional top group. This sharp distinction between stabilizer-induced and residual torsion provides a motivated boundary case for cyclic-color topology, not an arbitrary graph sample or an exercise in nonequivariant sphere counts.
