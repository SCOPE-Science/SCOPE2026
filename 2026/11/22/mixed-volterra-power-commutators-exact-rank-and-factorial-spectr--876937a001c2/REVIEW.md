# Review of the mixed Volterra-power commutator theorem

## Correctness — PASS
The proof reconstructs the precise adjoint and factorial normalization on the unit interval. The all-orders rank-two kernel and telescoping identity prove an upper bound; the exact homogeneous coefficient anti-diagonal and independent polynomial moments prove equality for every positive integer pair. In the self-adjoint case the Hilbert Gram determinant yields the factorial product, while reflection produces exactly equal positive and negative inertia. The included exact-rational checker passed 205 checks covering all pairs of exponents from one to five. These finite computations supplement, but do not prove, the infinite family.

## Originality — PASS, with historical risk
Khadkhuu–Tsedenbayar (2022) studied the first two self-commutator norms; Ransford–Tsedenbayar (arXiv:2402.09287, full text read) studies the real and imaginary parts of powers. A previously published exact polynomial-pencil theorem establishes rank and determinant for the *sum* of forward and backward integer integration powers. Its proof overlaps the algebraic tools but not the statement or leading-coefficient minor for the *commutator* of two powers. The first two orders and rank-two building block are not claimed new. Older general truncated-convolution commutator theory remains a residual priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
An existing finite-order spectral question receives an exact classification at every integer order, including all unequal exponent pairs. In addition to the nontrivial rank boundary, the factorial product is a natural invariant of the entire nonzero self-commutator spectrum, not just a small numerical improvement.

## Limits
The statement does not cover fractional integration orders or provide elementary closed forms for each individual higher-order eigenvalue. Historical priority is not an independent verification.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
