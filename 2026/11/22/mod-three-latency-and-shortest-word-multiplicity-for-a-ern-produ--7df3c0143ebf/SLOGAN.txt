For a shared-input product of the \(n\)-state Černý automaton and its three-state counterpart, the exact reset threshold is \((n-1)^2\) when \(3\mid n\) and \((n-1)^2+1\) otherwise, for every \(n\ge3\). The shortest common reset words are unique in the divisible case and form explicitly classified modular split families with exact quadratic counts in the other two residue classes.
