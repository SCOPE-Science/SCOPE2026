The Shrikhande and four-by-four rook graphs share the strongly regular parameters \((16,6,2,2)\), but the mod-two geodesic general-position homology distinguishes them: their reduced third Betti numbers are 57 and 98, and only the rook complex has a one-dimensional fourth homology group. The complete claims are proved by exhaustive face enumeration and exact finite-field boundary ranks.
