For every square-zero almost-abelian Lie algebra with \(r\) length-two Jordan blocks and \(s\) central directions, the ordinary characteristic-two Betti polynomial is \(\frac{(1+q)^{s+1}}2((1+q)^{2r}+(1+q^2)^r)\). Its contrast with the classical rational formula determines, in every degree, the exact number of even-order cyclic summands in the integral Chevalley–Eilenberg cohomology, with the first nonzero torsion occurring at \(r=3\).
