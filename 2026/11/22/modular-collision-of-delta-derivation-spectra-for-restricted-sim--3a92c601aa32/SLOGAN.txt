For every restricted simple \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\)-module in odd characteristic, the full \(\delta\)-derivation dimension spectrum is determined by three uniform eigenvalue blocks and two exceptional highest weights. At the Steinberg boundary \(m=p-1\), the \(\delta=2\) space has dimension \(p+2\) because two generic branches collide without doubling geometric multiplicity; earlier characteristic-zero branches are explicitly credited.
