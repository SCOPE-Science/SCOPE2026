# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The necessary and sufficient image-subalgebra / kernel-module condition is derived directly from the operator identities. Over \(\mathbb F_q\), the primitive idempotents of a subalgebra of \(\mathbb F_q^n\) yield the exhaustive block normal form; the compatible complements yield exactly the normalized weight vectors. Stirling counting gives the labeled rank distribution, while additive Fourier inversion proves the characteristic-dependent multiset formula and its integrality. The resulting multiset construction gives the full conjugacy Euler product. A standalone checker directly exhausts small matrices and automorphism orbits, including two proper field extensions. The full theorem rests on the proof, not on those finite checks.

**Originality — PASS relative to inspected evidence.** Guo–Pei, Proposition 2.3, already establishes the universal image/kernel criterion and is credited. Das's 2023 paper gives averaging-operad and deformation context but does not compute finite-field split-coordinate orbit series. Bremner–Elgendy classifies identities rather than this solution set. Closely indexed matrix, quadratic local and Lie examples concern different algebras and do not state the target \(\mu_s(q)\) or Euler product. Searches specifically for finite direct-product averaging projections, block weights, and finite-field permutation conjugacy did not locate a directly dominating result. Some indexed full texts could not be inspected; absence in search results is not a uniqueness proof. The new assertion assessed here is the complete finite-field rank and conjugacy enumeration, especially the characteristic-divisible multiset correction.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Rank and intrinsic automorphism-conjugacy are natural exact invariants of the full idempotent averaging locus on a basic family of finite-dimensional algebras. The result resolves them simultaneously in every dimension and prime-power field, and the arithmetic correction detects modular failure of naive equal-sum multiset uniformity. It connects the structural decomposition criterion to a concrete, nontrivial additive-character and Euler-product enumeration, rather than merely rephrasing the criterion or computing a small table. The formulas also supply reproducible benchmark counts for operator classification in characteristic \(p\).

**Closest literature / remaining limits.** The universal splitting criterion is prior art and is not presented as a new theorem. Primary source arXiv:2303.17798v1 is over characteristic zero; arXiv:1401.7386 provides characteristic-independent general background. No result is claimed for arbitrary non-idempotent operators, nonsplit products, or scheme-theoretic multiplicities. Broader publication priority is provisional, especially with inaccessible indexed full texts.
