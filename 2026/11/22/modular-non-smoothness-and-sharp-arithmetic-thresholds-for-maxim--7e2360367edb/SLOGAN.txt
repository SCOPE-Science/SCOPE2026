For normalized maximal-index nilpotent evolution algebras, the full automorphism group scheme is \(\mathbb G_a\rtimes\mu_g\) rather than merely its known ordinary-point formula, and it is non-smooth exactly when the field characteristic divides the weight gcd \(g\). Every non-chain member is non-smooth in characteristic \(2\); in odd characteristic \(p\), the first possible dimension is sharply \(\operatorname{ord}_p(2)+3\).
