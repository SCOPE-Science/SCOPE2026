Every connected-spanning-edge-set Walsh multiplier of a complete bipartite graph with both shore sizes at least two is congruent to \((-1)^{m+n}\pmod4\), so XOR convolution loses no Fourier mode. Its least multiplier magnitude is \(1\) whenever one shore has at most three vertices, but the first thicker case \(K_{4,4}\) has sharp gap \(3\) and exact condition number \(36317/3\).
