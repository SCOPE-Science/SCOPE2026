A centroid-fixed Euclidean equilateral triangle in \([-1,1]^d\) has squared side at most \(2d\), with equality exactly in dimensions divisible by three; all equality configurations form one cube-symmetry orbit and have an explicit factorial count. In the other two residue classes there is a dimension-independent positive gap, while near-equality in divisible dimensions forces quantitative closeness to a balanced \((1,-1,0)\)-coordinate design.
