For every \(n\ge5\), the geometric quotient of the three-row, \(n\)-column chessboard complex by cyclic row rotation is the Moore space \(M(\mathbb Z/3,1)\) wedged with exactly \(n(n-2)(n-4)/3-1\) two-spheres. Explicit uniform reductions of all nonabelian two-cell attaching words establish the full homotopy type, not merely the fundamental group and homology.
