In an independent-patch eDNA survey with a fixed per-grab processing loss and positive per-assay false-alarm probability, the Youden discrimination score is strictly log-concave in the real-valued replicate count, and increasing available volume can **reduce** the uniquely optimal count: five equal grabs at budget \(8\), but four at budget \(10\), in an exactly certified rational example. For every fixed positive false-alarm probability the optimum eventually stabilizes at an explicit finite count, unlike the unbounded replication growth without false alarms.
