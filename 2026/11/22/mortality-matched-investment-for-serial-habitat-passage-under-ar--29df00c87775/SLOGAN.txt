In a two-connection habitat corridor with independent per-stage loss, any arrival-time reward that is bounded, nonnegative and nontrivial is maximized only by allocating strictly greater traversal rate to the higher-mortality stage. Under a hard deadline, the unique optimal split at sufficiently short horizons departs from parity by \(C^2(d_1-d_2)T^2/96+O(T^3)\), and every optimizer tends to an explicit unequal eventual-success split at long horizons.
