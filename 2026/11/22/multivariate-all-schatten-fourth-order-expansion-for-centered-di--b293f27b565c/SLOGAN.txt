The unique fixed additive clock \(n+A/2\) makes Dirichlet Bernstein–Durrmeyer spectral powers converge to their Jacobi diffusion semigroup with a sharp \(N^{-2}\) error in every Schatten class, including trace class, and a uniform \(O(N^{-4})\) corrected remainder at positive times. The leading error is an explicit positive fourth-order polynomial in the Jacobi generator, and no other fixed clock shift cancels the first-order term.
