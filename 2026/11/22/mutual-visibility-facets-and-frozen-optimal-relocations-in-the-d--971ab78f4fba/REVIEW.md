# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The incidence-model program constructs every geodesic, applies a necessary-and-sufficient pairwise emptiness test, and enumerates the complete hereditary set complex without omissions. A separate generalized-Petersen-model exhaustive checker uses blocked-vertex BFS rather than the same geodesic-mask algorithm. Both reproduce the face polynomial. The first program verifies inclusion-maximal sizes, a 240-map symmetry action, both optimal orbits, and the entire pairwise exchange relation; its edge-equivalence check identifies the two line graphs of the Petersen graph and proves slide isolation. The theorem is limited to the specified finite graph.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Mutual visibility models shortest-path communication between vertices that cannot use other selected vertices as internal relays. On a canonical highly symmetric cubic graph, the complete feasible complex is nonpure, and its optimums distinguish an entire 120-state symmetry orbit of immobile choices from 30 mobile independent choices. The precise two-component Petersen line-graph structure and absolute slide rigidity answer a motivated optimal-configuration reconfiguration question, providing natural structure beyond a standalone graph invariant or small computational increment.
