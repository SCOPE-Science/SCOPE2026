For every finite chain of size \(n\ge2\), the square-zero elements of its small Schröder semigroup have the exact Narayana domain-size distribution \(Q_{n,k}=\frac1n\binom nk\binom n{k+1}\), totaling the Catalan number \(C_n\). Among nilpotent elements, the square-zero fraction decreases exponentially with base \(4/(3+2\sqrt2)\).
