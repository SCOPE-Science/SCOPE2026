The natural-conjugacy classes of partial isometries of a star with \(m\ge2\) leaves admit a complete cycle/path classification, including all class sizes, with exactly \(2p_2(m)+2\) classes in total, where \(p_2\) is the two-colored partition function. At fixed positive rank \(r\), the class count stabilizes sharply at \(m=2r\), and its stable values are coefficients of \((1+z)\prod_{j\ge1}(1-z^j)^{-2}+2z^2\).
