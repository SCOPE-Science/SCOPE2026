# Supported mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem has a complete quantified scope (all finite nonempty loop-free simple digraphs; full graph-automorphism group and every subgroup). Its two mutually exclusive cases are determined by whether condensation edges occur. When such edges exist, the invariant face of all inter-component arcs gives an explicit equivariant contraction. In every strongly connected component of size at least two, the automorphism-natural sink map from nonempty DAG arc sets to the opposite proper Boolean lattice has stabilizer-equivariantly contractible lower fibers via all incoming arcs to the selected nonempty proper subset. Thévenaz–Webb's equivariant fiber criterion supplies the required group-level equivalence. The component join and orthogonal-sphere join carry component-permuting automorphisms correctly. Fixed-point dimensions, quotient models, and orientation determinants follow by exact linear algebra. Empty complexes and singleton SCCs are handled explicitly. The bundled finite verifier confirms 2,811 tested symmetry cases and 362,900 admissible fixed-orbit faces; such tests are corroboration, not the proof.

## Originality — PASS, subject to disclosed priority risks

The full thesis text (Hultman 2003, Paper 3, §3.3) and the 2004 journal article show the **same objects** and already prove exactly the nonequivariant sphere/contractible trichotomy. These are explicitly excluded from the claimed novelty. Their theorems and proofs do not assert a natural automorphism-equivariant representation-sphere model, complete geometric fixed-space dimensions for all subgroups, or the precise integral orientation character. Their ordinary homotopy-equivalence claims do not imply those equivariant statements. The general equivariant poset fiber lemma is previously known and credited to Thévenaz–Webb; its application through this explicit natural sink map is additional. Björner–Welker (1999) is a particularly relevant earlier complete-graph precursor whose full text was not obtained; its full contents could change the priority evaluation. Searches of related acyclic orientation complexes, graph symmetry complexes, and specialized research records found no directly covering equivalent or dominating result. Absence of a hit is not a proof of uniqueness.

## Value — PASS

This is a uniform structural upgrade for the entire class of finite directed graphs, not an isolated numerical computation. It identifies one canonical orthogonal representation controlling the *genuine* action on homotopy type; the dimensions of fixed spheres for arbitrary subgroups and the integral degree character are then computable from permutation-orbit data. These distinctions are invisible in the classical nonequivariant sphere dimension and are relevant in equivariant topological combinatorics. The result requires a stabilizer-equivariant fiber argument and includes component-permuting automorphisms, so it is not merely a renaming of the earlier sphere calculation.
