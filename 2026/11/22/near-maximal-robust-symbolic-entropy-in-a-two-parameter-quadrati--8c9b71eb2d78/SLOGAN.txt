For the quadratic map \(F_{a,b}(x,y)=(ax+3y/2,bx(1-x))\), every sufficiently small *nonzero positive* coupling admits a robust compact expanding subsystem whose entropy can be made arbitrarily close to \(\log2\) by choosing the parameter neighborhood appropriately. In particular, its sixth iterate has a sixteen-symbol full-shift repeller throughout a nonempty open parameter region near \((a,b)=(0,8/3)\).
