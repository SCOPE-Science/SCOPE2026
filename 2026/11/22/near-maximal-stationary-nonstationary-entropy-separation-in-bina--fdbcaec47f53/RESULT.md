# Near-maximal stationary–nonstationary entropy separation in binary Ruelle channels

## Finding
For every integer \(q\ge 2\) and \(0<p<1/2\), there is an explicit strictly positive locally constant normalized binary Jacobian \(J_{q,p}\) for which the one-step dual Ruelle channel \(\Phi_{q,p}=\mathcal L_{J_{q,p}}^*\) has the following exact **fair-input** output-entropy suprema:
\[
\sup_{\mu:\,\mu[0]=1/2}h_{ni}(\Phi_{q,p}\mu)=\log 2,
\qquad
\sup_{\substack{\sigma_*\mu=\mu\\\mu[0]=1/2}}h_{ni}(\Phi_{q,p}\mu)=H(p)+\frac{\log 2-H(p)}q,
\]
where \(H(p)=-p\log p-(1-p)\log(1-p)\). The second supremum has **exactly one** maximizing invariant input, the uniform probability on the \(2q\)-cycle generated by \((0^q1^q)^\infty\). Consequently the exact gap is
\[
\Delta_{q,p}=(\log2-H(p))\left(1-\frac1q\right).
\]
Taking \(p=e^{-(q+1)}\) gives stationary fair-input supremum \((\log2)/q+o(1/q)\), while unrestricted fair-input supremum stays \(\log2\). The universal sharp bound on the fair-input supremum gap over **all strictly positive continuous normalized binary Jacobians** is therefore \(\log2\), approached but never attained by an individual Jacobian.

## Assumptions and scope
Write \(\Omega=\{0,1\}^{\mathbb N}\) with left shift \(\sigma\), and use cylinder notation \([a_0\cdots a_k]\). Put \(W_q=[0\,1^q]\cup[1\,0^q]\), the two opposing run-transition markers of length \(q+1\). Define
\[
J_{q,p}(0x)=\begin{cases}1/2,&x\in W_q,\\p,&x\notin W_q,\end{cases}
\quad J_{q,p}(1x)=1-J_{q,p}(0x).
\]
These functions are defined on the two one-symbol preimages of each \(x\in\Omega\). They are strictly positive, locally constant, and normalized by \(J(0x)+J(1x)=1\). The channel \(\Phi_{q,p}\) prefixes one random symbol according to \(J_{q,p}\). Here \(h_{ni}(\rho)\) is the conditional Shannon entropy of the first coordinate of \(\rho\) given its infinite tail, in nats; this is the non-invariant entropy of Lopes and Oliveira. All suprema constrain the **input** to satisfy \(\mu[0]=1/2\). No invariance of the channel output is assumed or needed.

## Proof
**1. Exact entropy identity.** Let \(\rho=\mathcal L_J^*\mu\). Since \(\sigma_*\rho=\mu\), disintegration over the tail \(x\) gives \(\Pr_\rho(Y_0=0\mid\sigma Y=x)=J(0x)\), and similarly for \(1\). Therefore, directly from the definition of non-invariant entropy,
\[
h_{ni}(\mathcal L_J^*\mu)=\int H(J(0x))\,d\mu(x).
\]
For the constructed Jacobian this becomes
\[
h_{ni}(\Phi_{q,p}\mu)=H(p)+(\log2-H(p))\mu(W_q).
\tag{1}
\]
This channel entropy identity is a special case of the cited source's Theorem B; the novelty claim is not to that identity.

**2. Unrestricted fair inputs.** By \(H(u)\le\log2\), formula (1) is at most \(\log2\). The balanced nonstationary input
\[
\mu_{\mathrm{free}}=\tfrac12\delta_{0\,1^\infty}+\tfrac12\delta_{1\,0^\infty}
\]
has \(\mu_{\mathrm{free}}[0]=1/2\) and is supported on \(W_q\), so it attains \(\log2\). No uniqueness of this unrestricted optimizer is asserted.

**3. Stationary marker packing.** Two beginnings of the words \(0\,1^q\) and \(1\,0^q\) cannot lie fewer than \(q\) positions apart. Indeed, equal-type markers cannot start within the other's constant tail, while an opposite-type marker beginning \(1,\ldots,q-1\) sites later would immediately demand the opposite symbol in that tail. Thus every \(x\in\Omega\) satisfies the *pointwise* inequality
\[
\sum_{j=0}^{q-1}\mathbf1_{W_q}(\sigma^j x)\le1.
\tag{2}
\]
For every invariant input \(\mu\), integrate (2) to obtain \(q\mu(W_q)\le1\). Combining with (1) yields the advertised stationary upper bound, with no reliance on a finite periodic-orbit census.

**4. Attainment and uniqueness.** The periodic sequence \(z=(0^q1^q)^\infty\) has precisely two marker starts per \(2q\) positions, and its orbit measure \(\mu_*=(2q)^{-1}\sum_{r=0}^{2q-1}\delta_{\sigma^rz}\) is invariant and has one-coordinate marginal \(\mu_*[0]=1/2\). Hence \(\mu_*(W_q)=1/q\), proving attainment. Suppose an invariant \(\mu\) attains equality. Extend it to its stationary two-sided process. Every translate of (2) must then be an equality almost surely. Subtracting the equality for successive length-\(q\) windows shows that the bi-infinite indicator of marker starts is \(q\)-periodic and contains exactly one start per \(q\) consecutive sites. Consecutive markers \(q\) sites apart must have opposite types: the final letter of \(0\,1^q\) is \(1\), and the final letter of \(1\,0^q\) is \(0\). These conditions determine the entire two-sided symbolic sequence up to translation as the alternating runs \((0^q1^q)^\infty\). The only invariant probability on this finite orbit is uniform, proving uniqueness even without the fairness constraint.

**5. Sharp universal separation and nonattainment.** For every strictly positive continuous binary normalized Jacobian \(J\), compactness of \(\Omega\) gives some \(\delta_J>0\) with \(\delta_J\le J(0x)\le1-\delta_J\). Consequently the output entropy of *every* input is at least \(H(\delta_J)>0\) and at most \(\log2\), so the difference between fair unrestricted and fair invariant output-entropy suprema is strictly below \(\log2\) for each fixed \(J\). In our family choose \(p=e^{-(q+1)}\): then \(H(p)=O(qe^{-q})\) and the stationary maximum is \(\log2/q+o(1/q)\), proving that \(\log2\) is the sharp nonattained universal supremum.

## Verification
The proof's two critical nonstandard assertions are the all-sequence marker exclusion (2) and its equality classification, proved above by local overlap and the stationary two-sided extension. The accompanying standalone `checker.py` enumerates all cyclic binary words of periods \(1\) through \(14\), for each \(q\in\{2,3,4,5\}\), checking the sharp marker-density bound, the complete equality-orbit classification, fair marginals, and the exact entropy formulas at twelve real parameter values. It reports 131,064 tested cyclic words, 42 equality cases and 12 entropy comparisons. These finite checks do not prove statements about all invariant measures; those follow from (2) and the stationary extension argument. Reproduce with `python3 checker.py` using Python 3 standard library only.

## Relationship to prior work
Lopes and Oliveira, *Thermodynamic Formalism for Non-Invariant Probabilities: Entropy, Divergence, Channels* (arXiv:2611.13904v1, first public 2026-11-10), establish the general identity for output non-invariant entropy, and introduce the distinction between unrestricted and invariant input optimizations (Theorem B and Section 6). The present result takes those as premises. Their inspected results do **not** identify this fair-input binary channel family, its unique periodic stationary optimizer, or the asymptotically full-bit discrepancy. Wolf and Yang's classification of locally constant potentials, arXiv:1804.07822 (Introduction and Theorem A), provides general information about maximizing invariant measures but no calculation of this particular two-marker frequency or Ruelle-channel entropy gap. *A stationary occupancy inequality for variable-length non-overlapping codes* (public published-finding corpus record dated 2026-09-17) proves a broader packing principle only under a cross-bifix-free requirement; the two opposite markers here **do** overlap by one symbol, so that condition cannot be applied directly. Nonetheless local exclusion/finite graph max-cycle methods are standard, and the claim is the full exact fair-input separation, not originality of elementary event packing alone.

## Limitations
This is an exact output-entropy optimization theorem, not a statement about Shannon transmission capacity or mutual information. Strict positivity is essential for the strict nonattainment statement; a limiting zero Jacobian is not included. The example's input marginal constraint is one-symbol balance, not an arbitrary prescribed higher-order stationary marginal. The result does not assert analogous classification for all finite-memory Jacobians, nor any independent mathematical validation. Older literature may have an equivalent extremal example not found by the inspected searches; the relationship to classical finite-state ergodic optimization remains a residual priority risk.
