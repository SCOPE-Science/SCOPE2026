For every \(m\ge2\) and finite field, the \(m\)-th power map on strictly upper-triangular matrices of size \(m+2\) has exactly three nonempty fiber levels, with explicit formulas for all prescribed targets. Conditional on a nonzero power, the probability of a central output is \(\frac{(m-1)q+1}{mq+q^2+1}\), tending to one at fixed field size; the corresponding unitriangular power-word law holds when \(m\) is a power of the characteristic.
