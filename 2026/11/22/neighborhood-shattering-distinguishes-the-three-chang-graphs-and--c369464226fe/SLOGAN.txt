The three Chang graphs have different complete open- and closed-neighborhood shattering polynomials, even though all share the strongly regular parameters of the triangular graph \(T(8)\). Unlike the pure open-neighborhood shattering complex of \(T(8)\), each Chang complex has maximal shattered triples and quadruples, with exactly \(1328\), \(1624\), or \(1786\) maximal triples according to the switching type.
