For equal-length repunits in two integer bases separated by a positive shift, an unbounded common divisor is possible only for consecutive bases and lengths divisible by six; it then contains exactly one unavoidable factor \(b^2+b+1\). At the first two exceptional lengths, six and twelve, the normalized GCD is completely explicit: for length twelve it has sharp period \(455\), takes every divisor of \(455\), and has a fully determined eight-point frequency law.
