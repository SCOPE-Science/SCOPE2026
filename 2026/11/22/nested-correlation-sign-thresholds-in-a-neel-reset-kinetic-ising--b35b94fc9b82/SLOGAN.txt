Globally resetting a ferromagnetic zero-temperature Glauber chain to a randomly phased Néel pattern creates an exact hierarchy of spatial correlation-sign reversals: even separations stay positive, while every odd separation has a unique reset-rate threshold. On the infinite chain these thresholds obey \(r_n\sim4W(n)^2/n^2\), and the sign-change length is \(\ell_*(r)\sim\log(4/r)/\sqrt r\), reflecting competition between short-range ferromagnetic coarsening and persistent staggered order.
