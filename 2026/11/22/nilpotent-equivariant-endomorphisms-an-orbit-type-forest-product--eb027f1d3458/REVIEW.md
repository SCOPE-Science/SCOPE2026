# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The proof explicitly identifies equivariant orbit maps with stabilizer-fixed image points, proves that any directed cycle in an orbit graph is confined to a single orbit isomorphism type, and evaluates independent colored rooted-forest factors by a reduced Laplacian determinant. It separately proves the two-probe inversion, including the equal-cardinality nonisomorphic-orbit case. The checker independently enumerates full point maps for cyclic and nonabelian examples, reconstructs the \(S_3\) table of marks, and tests the equal-size incomparable case for \(C_2\times C_2\). Finite checks are corroboration only.

## Originality

PASS relative to the primary full text and indexed records inspected. The 2022 source treats endomorphism generators and orbit-type structure, the 2025 cardinality source treats all maps and units, and the 2025 Green-relations source treats Green classes; no inspected theorem gives nilpotent weighted-forest factors or reconstructs the table of marks from two-orbit nilpotent counts. The classical fixed-point table and matrix-tree lemma are acknowledged, as are earlier published results about regular equivariant endomorphism profiles. Residual risk: a broadly stated forest theorem or older wreath-product nilpotent enumeration may subsume parts of the new statement despite different terminology.

## Value

PASS. It determines a natural semigroup-theoretic invariant for every finite group action with a selected zero, and, unlike a one-off count, furnishes a compact factorization for arbitrarily many stabilizer types. The two-orbit inverse theorem recovers the full Burnside table of marks, an invariant that controls subgroup fixed-point data and permutation representations. The exact maximal nilpotency-height consequence further reads a weighted chain invariant from the same orbit-type structure. This is a motivated classification, not an arbitrary parameter slice.
