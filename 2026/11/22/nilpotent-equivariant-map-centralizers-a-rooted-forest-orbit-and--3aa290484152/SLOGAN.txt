Nilpotent pointed equivariant maps on \(b\) free orbits of a finite group of order \(q\) are classified under unit conjugacy by unlabeled rooted trees on \(b+1\) vertices. An explicit rooted-forest recursion counts the **entire commuting endomorphism monoid** for each class and gives the joint image-size, nilpotency-index, centralizer-size spectrum, which depends only on \(q\), even for nonabelian groups.
