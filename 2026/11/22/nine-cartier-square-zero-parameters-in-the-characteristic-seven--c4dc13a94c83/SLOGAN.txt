For the characteristic-seven cyclic quintic genus-four family \(y^5=x(x-1)(x-t)\), every smooth Jacobian has \(7\)-rank zero and \(a\)-number two, but Cartier-square vanishing occurs at exactly nine parameters. These form exactly two marked branch-configuration classes, characterized by the cross-ratio invariants \(j=4\) and \(j=6\).
