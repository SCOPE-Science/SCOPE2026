# Scientific review of the double Kummer pencil classification

## Correctness

PASS. The divisor is identified with the classical 24-curve product Kummer configuration; local resolution explains precisely two distinct transverse intersections on each exceptional component. The unambiguous integral Laplacian is constructed in the source verifier. The graph has 24 components and 32 nodes, so the connected curve has arithmetic genus nine. Its critical group is the reduced Laplacian cokernel. The perfect invariant graph monodromy pairing identifies the Frobenius-fixed group with the dual of the coinvariants, and the proof explicitly constructs the integral orbit matrices rather than assuming that fixed integral divisors commute with forming the quotient. Exact Bareiss determinants and independent modular two-adic Smith reductions on two distinct presentations prove the nine rows. An explicit integral solution of a Laplacian equation certifies the universal order-32 element and a four-generator relation presentation bounds the full fixed subgroup. The finite computations are exhaustive over the *three possible conjugacy classes on each 2-torsion set*; no extrapolation from tested field sizes is made.

## Originality

PASS, provisional historical priority. The full double Kummer pencil is known (Clingher–Doran–Malmendier 2015). Glass's 2015 study treats different dihedral graph actions and explicitly loses information at the prime two in its main theorem, whereas the proposed groups are entirely two-primary. D'Adderio–Le Borgne 2016 concerns configurations and rank on the *unsubdivided* complete bipartite graph. The classical Kummer node–trope configuration concerns a different 32-vertex, 96-edge graph, not this 24-vertex, 32-edge double pencil. Searches of published mathematical literature and indexed mathematical results did not identify the nine equivariant invariant-factor rows or the full \(S_3\times S_3\) fixed \(C_{32}\). Search failure is not proof of absolute priority; an unindexed calculation of this particular subdivision and action remains possible.

## Value

PASS. The double Kummer pencil is a canonical family of 24 geometric rational curves in the classical product Kummer surface construction. Its integral graph Jacobian and its finite-field fixed groups give arithmetic information about a natural genus-nine nodal boundary beyond mere component counts, cycle rank, and order of the graph critical group. The distinction between \(2^{21}\), \(2^{16}\), \(2^{13}\), \(2^{11}\), \(2^8\), and \(2^7\) and their different invariant factors is a complete arithmetic classification of the possible two-torsion splitting behaviors. The global fixed subgroup being exactly the same canonical \(C_{32}\) and not merely containing an order-32 class is a structural assertion likely useful for comparing degenerations and descent. This is a finite self-contained classification, not a claim of a new moduli theory or a Néron-model existence theorem.

## Scope and risks

Characteristic two and nonproduct Kummer configurations are excluded. The boundary is semistable nodal, but not itself a stable curve because the exceptional curves are two-valent. Potential Néron interpretations are strictly conditional on a separately supplied suitable smoothing. No independent mathematical audit has been carried out, and older unindexed explicit tables may remain undiscovered.
