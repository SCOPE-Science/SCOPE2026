The 24 rational curves of the double Kummer pencil form a genus-nine nodal boundary whose graph critical group has nine explicitly classified Frobenius-fixed structures, indexed by the two elliptic two-torsion splitting types. Even under the entire \(S_3\times S_3\) label group, exactly a distinguished \(C_{32}\) survives, generated by the difference of an identity-section curve and the identity exceptional curve.
