# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The quantifier in ordered separation is checked in both orders for each distinct nonzero pair. Square-free Boolean normal forms modulo linear terms have precisely twenty coefficients, ten quadratic and ten cubic, and exact degree three excludes precisely the 1024 masks with a zero cubic block. The exact monomial-polar XOR rule yields the correct rows; the 32-bit antichain predicate is equivalent to the quantified condition. The complete 1,047,552-word Gray-code enumeration and 690,432 valid count are reproduced by the supplied checker. Four adjacent swaps and one transvection generate the complete invertible linear group; Möbius inversion exactly computes their action even on induced quadratic coefficients. The union-find connected components therefore are the full orbits; the nine class sizes sum to 690,432 and divide the complete group order 9,999,360. Direct truth-table tests over all ordered pairs separately validate each representative. Finite exhaustive verification is appropriate here because the entire mathematical domain has been exhaustively covered, not sampled.

## Originality — PASS, with historical caveat
Pineda Montoya's arXiv:2611.02544v1 constructs and proves separating counterexample families, including a five-variable seed, but explicitly excludes an exhaustive classification and calls the quadratic-extension stabilizer action a separate problem. The foundational Morier-Genoud–Ovsienko article introduces the symmetric normal forms and the nondegeneracy condition but does not supply a five-variable nine-orbit census. Khoruzhii–Gelß–Pokutta arXiv:2606.28473v1 classifies homogeneous cubic-layer orbits, modulo quadratic and lower terms, and therefore cannot determine the present quadratic-sensitive antichain classes. Targeted published-mathematics searches using aliases (ordered separation; cubic Boolean generating function; GL(5,2); quadratic extensions) found no equivalent; accessible own claims concerning dimension-existence and a particular family witness census do not imply this complete orbit table. Unindexed literature remains a priority risk, and the negative searches alone do not prove uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This answers the concrete natural classification problem arising after published five-variable counterexamples to the symmetric-normal-form conjecture. The result is complete rather than a one-off finite example, counts all valid polynomials, gives inequivalent normal representatives and stabilizer orders, and separates the different quadratic refinements of the two relevant third-polar types. It supplies a reusable exact benchmark for twisted-algebra generating functions and their second-polar graphs.

## Remaining limitations
No classification is claimed in greater dimensions or under arbitrary affine input transformations or arbitrary ungraded algebra isomorphism. The all-domain finite proof relies on reproducible exhaustive arithmetic; there is no closed-form invariant separating the nine classes. The older published classification of alternating three-forms is not claimed as new.
