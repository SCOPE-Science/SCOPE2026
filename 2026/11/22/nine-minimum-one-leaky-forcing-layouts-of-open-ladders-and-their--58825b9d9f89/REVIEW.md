# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The proof first establishes a two-seed lower bound in each disjoint two-column terminal window using explicit leak-and-white-trap certificates. It then excludes diagonals and inner-rung pairs, leaving exactly nine four-vertex candidates. Two independently directed ordinary forcing processes provide two possible forcers for every initially white vertex; the published one-leaky characterization proves their robustness. The nine-set product classification proves the exchange and slide graph identities. Independent exhaustive code checks all one-leak choices and all candidate sets through sixteen rungs. The infinite claim uses the symbolic proof, not extrapolation.

## Originality — PASS (provisional)
The 2019 first leaky-forcing treatment gives grid bounds; the 2020 resilience study gives a general criterion and already proves the scalar minimum four for two-row ladders of length at least eight (Theorem 4.11). The newly assessed content is the exact nine-set all-length optimum census and two distinct optimum reconfiguration graphs. Relevant indexed records concern ordinary forcing of circular ladders, leaky forcing of clique prisms and ordinary forts of open ladders, none of which logically supplies the claim. Publication priority remains provisional because unindexed theses, differently described configurations and inaccessible work may exist.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A robust-control placement count that stays at nine for arbitrarily long open grids is a natural exact classification beyond the known scalar grid upper bound. The difference between global exchange connectivity and complete local immobility gives a concrete nontrivial boundary in optimum reconfiguration, rather than a routine numerical bound. The all-order structural proof is constructive and independently reproducible.

## Residual limitations
Only ordinary one-leak forcing on \(P_n\square K_2\), \(n\ge5\), is classified; no larger-width theorem, all-subset polynomial or independent review is claimed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
