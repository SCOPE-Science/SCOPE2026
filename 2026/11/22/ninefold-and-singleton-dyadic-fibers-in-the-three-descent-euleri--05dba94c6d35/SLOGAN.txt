For permutations with exactly three descents, every residue modulo every power of two occurs infinitely often, but beyond modulus eight each late period gives **nine occurrences** for residues \(0,10\pmod{16}\) and **one** for every other residue. The least eventual period is \(2^{k+1}\) modulo \(2^k\), explained by one even-index isometry and four odd-index charts onto just two dyadic residue balls.
