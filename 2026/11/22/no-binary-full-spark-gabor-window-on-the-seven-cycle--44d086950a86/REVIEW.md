# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The theorem is limited to full cyclic Gabor systems over \(\mathbb Z_7\) with a binary sign window, using the explicitly fixed time-frequency normalization. Global sign, cyclic translation and coordinate reversal preserve the full-spark property by invertible row operations and complete column reindexing. Nine disjoint symmetry classes cover all 64 windows normalized at their zeroth coordinate. On each class representative, an explicit seven-column determinant has all seven integer group-algebra coefficients equal to zero, established by complete enumeration of 5,040 permutation terms per determinant. The exact checker reproduced every class and coefficient identity. A separately implemented modular Gaussian-elimination checker reproduced singularity for all nine determinants at all six primitive seventh roots in a prime field; this is a cross-check rather than a proof of characteristic-zero vanishing.

## Originality

PASS under the inspected evidence, with residual historical priority risk. Lawrence–Pfander–Walnut (2005) and Malikiosis (2013) establish full spark for generic complex windows and construct nonbinary examples. Genericity says nothing about intersection with a finite sign alphabet. Malikiosis (2016) proves noncyclic-group obstructions and certain Clifford-eigenvector cyclic obstructions, not this assertion for every binary window on the cyclic seven-group. Nearby phase-code studies of full spark in five dimensions, Gabor self-ambiguity ranks, and partial sublattice orthogonality do not logically contain the seven-dimensional binary obstruction. Searches for aliases such as Haar property, linear general position, sign window, and finite Gabor minor were compared against both academic sources and related public mathematical records. Unindexed equivalent computations remain possible. A 2026 paper on explicit algebraically perturbed full-spark windows was available only at abstract level in this review; its complex algebraic construction is not a binary-window theorem, but the full text was not available for comparison.

## Value

PASS. Uniform recovery from arbitrary surviving seven coefficients among the 49 time-frequency measurements is a natural erasure-robust frame-design property. The theorem rules out the cheapest phase alphabet in a prime cyclic dimension where generic complex full-spark windows exist. It does so uniformly over all sign codes, not just through a bad example, and the complete nine-class exact witness certificate provides reusable failure configurations. It does not claim a three-phase optimum or characterize all failing configurations.

## Source comparison and risks

The closest primary scientific references are the Lawrence–Pfander–Walnut prime-dimensional genericity theorem, Malikiosis's all-dimensional construction, and Malikiosis's Clifford-eigenvector/noncyclic obstruction theorem. None of their inspected statements gives the nine binary classes or a universal sign restriction at order seven. A comparable unpublished or unindexed finite computation is the main originality risk; no priority beyond checked evidence is asserted.
