For every dimension \(n\ge3\), the multivariate variable-power FGM formula \(G(\mathbf u)=(\prod_i u_i)^{1+a\prod_i(1-u_i)}\) defines a copula **only at \(a=0\)**. Two complementary mixed-density obstructions rule out every nonzero parameter, resolving the exact parameter question posed for this ansatz in the source paper.
