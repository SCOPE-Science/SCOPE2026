The 42 flex tangents of the smooth Hurwitz quintic \(XY^4+YZ^4+X^4Z=0\) over \(\mathbb F_5\) form a simple line arrangement: exactly 861 ordinary double crossings and no triple concurrences. Frobenius acts on the crossings with cycle type \(1^3 3^4 6^3 12^{69}\), so every crossing splits over \(\mathbb F_{5^{12}}\) and this extension degree is sharp.
