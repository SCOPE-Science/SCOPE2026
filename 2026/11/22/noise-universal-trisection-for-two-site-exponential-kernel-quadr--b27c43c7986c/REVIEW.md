# Scientific review — noise-universal trisection for exponential-kernel quadrature

## Correctness — PASS
The precise domain is two arbitrary sites in the closed unit interval, including repetitions, for a centered stationary Gaussian process with covariance \(e^{-\lambda|u-v|}\), \(\lambda>0\), and two independent noises of equal positive variance \(\eta\). The posterior-variance formula follows from the positive-definite two-by-two Gaussian observation covariance. The proof's principal nonstandard step compares every translated pair with the unique reflection-centered pair at the same separation: its explicit hyperbolic inequality is strict for all displaced centers. The remaining scalar objective has a derivative whose sign is a strictly decreasing function, forcing exactly one interior global maximum. Substitution at \(\eta=1\) proves decay-independent trisection and exact weights and variance; the sign at the trisection point establishes the two surrounding noise regimes. The actual standalone verification program passed. Numerical tests supplement, but do not prove, the global quantifiers. No unproved Gaussian-process regularity assumption beyond existence of the continuous covariance and integrable Gaussian process is needed.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The 2018/2019 Karvonen–Kanagawa–Särkkä work supplies general kernel-quadrature formulas and weight-positivity principles, but neither a complete global optimal two-point positive-noise location law nor noise-independent trisection. Cai–Lam–Scarlett (2022) treats noisy Matérn quadrature in general, with sample-budget error rates and algorithms rather than this exact two-site global design. A published exponential-kernel result covers arbitrary node count at zero observation noise, which does **not** logically imply positive-noise optimization because the observation covariance becomes \(K+\eta I\). An early Mukherjee (2003) paper treats exact integral sampling for product-covariance processes, but its full text could not be retrieved; its precise coverage of measurement-error models could not be excluded from the abstract alone. The present claim is the complete positive-noise result plus an explicit, decay-invariant trisection at exactly one natural noise-to-signal ratio, rather than an assertion of novelty for Gaussian conditioning or the noiseless design.

## Value — PASS
With a fixed budget of exactly two noisy evaluations and an important rough Matérn kernel, the correct sites are not obvious from general convergence rates. The theorem rules out endpoint designs, repeats, and all asymmetric pairs; gives an implementable monotone scalar equation and posterior error; proves a strict decision boundary at \(\eta=1\), at which the sites become independent of the covariance range; and supplies equal optimal weights. This is a complete natural design classification, not an arbitrary numerical instance or a claim of improved runtime.
