For two equally noisy point observations of a stationary exponential-covariance Gaussian process, the globally optimal design for its integral is a unique symmetric, strictly separated pair. When the noise variance equals the process's pointwise variance, the nodes are exactly \(1/3\) and \(2/3\), independent of the covariance decay rate, and both optimal weights equal \((1-e^{-\lambda/3})/\lambda\).
