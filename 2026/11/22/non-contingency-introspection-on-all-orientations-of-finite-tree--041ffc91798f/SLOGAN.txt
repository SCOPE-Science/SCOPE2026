For ordinary non-contingency semantics on every finite oriented forest, \(\Delta p\to\Delta\Delta p\) is valid for all valuations exactly when vertices of outdegree at least two are pairwise nonadjacent. An exact four-series bivariate exponential generating function counts every valid orientation of every labelled tree by the number of such branching vertices, with first total counts \(1,2,12,128,1940,38292,937048\).
