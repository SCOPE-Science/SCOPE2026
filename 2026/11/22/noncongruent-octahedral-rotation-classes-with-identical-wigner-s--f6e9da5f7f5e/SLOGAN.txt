Two noncongruent six-rotation conjugacy classes of the cube have exactly identical pairwise rotation angles, so every central two-point kernel gives the same Gram matrix on both. Their exact-degree \(SO(3)\) Wigner sampling spectrum has three explicit branches in every positive degree, and equal-weight interpolation enjoys the sharp degree-uniform squared singular-value bounds \(2/5\) and \(8/5\), both attained only at degree two.
