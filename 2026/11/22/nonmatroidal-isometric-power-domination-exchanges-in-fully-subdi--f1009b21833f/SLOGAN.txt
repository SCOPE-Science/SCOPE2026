In the full one-edge subdivision of every \(K_n\), minimum power-dominating sets have two explicit forms, and their single-sensor exchange graph is an isometric subgraph of a Johnson graph despite violating matroid basis exchange for \(n\ge4\). Power domination itself is equivalent to a residual-forest condition, yielding the complete placement polynomial and \(n(n-1)^2/2\) minimum sets of size \(n-2\).
