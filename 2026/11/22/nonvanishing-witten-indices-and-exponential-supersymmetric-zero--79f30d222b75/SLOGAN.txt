The standard supersymmetric hard-core fermion Hamiltonian on every step-three generalized Petersen graph \(\mathrm{GP}(n,3)\), \(n\ge7\), has a nonzero Witten index and therefore at least one exactly zero-energy state. Its index has eventual alternating sign and grows in magnitude as \(\rho^n\), where \(\rho>1\) solves \(\rho^5-\rho-1=0\), giving exponentially many protected zero modes in the rigorous lower-bound sense.
