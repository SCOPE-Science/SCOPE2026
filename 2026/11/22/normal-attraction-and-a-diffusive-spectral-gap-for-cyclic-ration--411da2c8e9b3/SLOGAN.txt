The full positive period-two family of a cyclic rational-delay map is transversely attracting in every finite ring, despite having one neutral phase-amplitude direction. Its exactly factored Floquet spectrum shows a sharp \(k^{-2}\) collapse of the normal spectral gap as the ring size \(k\) increases, with coefficient \(2\pi^2d(1-3d)/(1-d)^3\).
