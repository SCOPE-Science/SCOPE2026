# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS

The normalized Green kernel is validated by the cyclic inverse recurrence. The Rademacher variance is derived from orthogonality of independent quadratic cross terms. For odd order, the strictly convex positive circular distance weights have an exact decomposition in positive window-energy coefficients. Each window inequality is proved for all binary signings, a near-alternating signing saturates all of them, and the positive two-window coefficient forces a unique monochromatic edge. The number of optimal unlabeled partitions and exact variance formula follow from cycle symmetry; the parity correction follows by absolutely convergent geometric series. The included exact-rational checker successfully evaluated 502,920 assertions in finite examples. Risks: arbitrary nonperiodic weights, other estimators, and approximate solves are outside the theorem.

## Originality — PASS on inspected material, with residual priority risk

The 2013 toroidal-lattice hierarchical-probing construction and the 2023 stochastic-probing analysis provide the distance-color motivation and generic variance identity; their inspected results do not classify the unrestricted odd-cycle two-class optimum. The full published even-cycle result proves a strictly disjoint graph-order statement using an alternating Fourier eigenvector unavailable on odd cycles. Direct mathematical comparisons were made with indexed results for hypercubes and complete bipartite resolvents, also different objects and distances. The plausible 1992 *Max-cut in circulant graphs* article could not be inspected in full despite open-access and authorized institutional-access attempts; the absence of this comparison is an explicit publication-priority risk rather than a claim of its noncoverage. Equivalence search cannot prove uniqueness of a result in the literature.

## Value — PASS

Odd cycle graphs have no proper two-coloring, so the optimal design is not supplied by the normal distance-one coloring heuristic or by the even-cycle spectral argument. A uniform analytic classification of every optimizer over all positive screening strengths replaces an exponential partition search. The exact minimum, the number of optimal partitions, and the strict order-one parity-frustration penalty provide natural numerical-analysis benchmarks for fixed-solve stochastic inverse-trace estimation. The fact is specific but naturally motivated, rather than an arbitrary sample of graph sizes.
