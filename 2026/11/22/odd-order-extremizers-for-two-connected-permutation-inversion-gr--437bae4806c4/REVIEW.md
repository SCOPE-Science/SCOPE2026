# Scientific review: Odd-order extremizers for two-connected permutation inversion graphs

**Correctness — PASS.** The published \(4\)-chordal edge bound fixes the odd minimum at least \(3m\). The constructive upper bound adds a degree-two vertex to the unique even extremal ladder. An anchored induced-ear equality analysis shows every optimal odd graph ends with a one-vertex step from an even extremizer or a forced two-vertex step from a smaller odd extremizer. The explicit commutation identities and complete insertion-cell table finish the converse and exact enumeration; this is an unrestricted proof. The standalone verifier independently enumerates the odd cases through nine vertices and tests the algebraic identities to length fifty. Dependence on published lemmas is explicit.

**Originality — PASS, with historical-priority risk.** The closest relevant source, Verkama's *Permutation patterns and higher connectivity in inversion graphs* (arXiv:2611.17237v1, November 12, 2026), proves the lower bound, the forced-ear lemmas and unique **even** extremizers, but does not state the all-odd insertion classification, count \(3m-2\), or \(321\)-stratified census. Its connectivity table through seven vertices does not give inversion-refined extremal counts. Searches for biconnected permutation graph minimum-edge enumerations, alternate inversion-graph formulations, and indexed related results yielded no stronger covering assertion. Tenner and Vatter's *Cyclomatic numbers and permutations* (arXiv:2607.06198) is a different graph-statistic direction; its full text could not be retrieved during this comparison. Unindexed priority and that access limitation remain genuine risks.

**Value — PASS.** The all-order count closes the parity gap left by the unique-even extremizer theorem, rather than adding a small isolated example. The explicit degree-two insertion characterization explains every minimizer, identifies four nontrivial insertion-cell coincidences, and separates those with exactly one decreasing triple from those avoiding that pattern altogether. These are natural extremal and permutation-pattern statistics motivated directly by higher connectivity.

**Limitations.** No independent audit or historical priority certification has been performed. The proof invokes the published general chordality and ear lemmas; finite executable tests are corroborative rather than a substitute for the induction.
