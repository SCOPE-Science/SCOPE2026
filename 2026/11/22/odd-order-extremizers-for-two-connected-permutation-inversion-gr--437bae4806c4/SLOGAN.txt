At odd order \(2m+1\), the minimum-inversion two-connected permutation graphs have exactly \(3m\) edges and precisely \(3m-2\) realizing permutations. Every minimizer for \(m\ge2\) comes from inserting one degree-two vertex into the unique even-order ladder extremizer; exactly \(m\) of the odd minimizers contain a single \(321\) pattern.
