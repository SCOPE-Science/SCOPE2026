At the critical rung conductance \(t/n^2\), a random ladder spanning tree has a finite rung population: twice the limiting count minus one is \(\operatorname{Poisson}(\sqrt{2t})\) conditioned to be odd. Conditional on that population, the boundary-inclusive rung gaps have \(\operatorname{Dirichlet}(1,2,\ldots,2,1)\) lengths, with independent uniformly marked rail cuts, and the rung locations form an explicit continuum determinantal process.
