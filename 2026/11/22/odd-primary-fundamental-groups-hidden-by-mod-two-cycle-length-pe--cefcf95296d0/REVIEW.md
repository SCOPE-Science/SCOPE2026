# Scientific review: odd-primary groups in cycle-length persistence

## Correctness — PASS

The claim is a complete group-valued filtration for integers \(q\ge3\), \(d\ge q+1\), not an extrapolation from a finite sample. The graph is the standard cubical-grid quotient by the explicit period lattice \(\Lambda=q^2\mathbb Z^d+q\mathbf1\mathbb Z\). Because nonzero periods have \(\ell_1\)-norm at least \(q^2\ge9\), all simple four-cycles are coordinate squares. Filling them identifies the fundamental group with the lattice. The two displacement inequalities show there is no new relation before \(q^2\), and no noncoordinate period below \(dq\). Embedded coordinate and diagonal cycles certify both changes, giving the quotient \(\Lambda/q^2\mathbb Z^d\cong\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z\) and then triviality. The free-group rank follows from the exact vertex/edge counts. The finite checker independently verifies these combinatorial facts in the first instance. No finite check is used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, subject to publication priority

Rajković (arXiv:2611.09967v1, 29 October 2026) introduces this cycle-length group filtration and computes the four-cycle stage for rectangular periodic grids. It explicitly warns that mod-two homology cannot detect odd torsion; the warning is not an example or an exact parameter-family calculation. The new claim constructs nonrectangular finite cubical quotients with a bona fide cyclic odd-primary stage and determines all cutoff groups and natural quotient maps. Indexed prior-result and topical literature searches for cycle-length fundamental-group torsion, Cayley graphs, and lattice quotients showed no matching or stronger covering theorem. Relevant indexed results on torsion in directed path homology and symmetry quotients concern different homology functors and do not imply the statement. The cited but presently unlocated companion manuscript is an unresolved priority risk, not assumed absent or declared noncovering.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The distinction between the full group and its mod-two abelianization is an explicit gap emphasized in the motivating paper. The construction realizes that distinction in an infinite family of natural finite vertex-transitive cubical quotient graphs, with a precise onset and disappearance of odd torsion. This supplies an exact benchmark for group-valued persistence and demonstrates why zero mod-two ranks cannot certify group triviality. The geometry of the period lattice explains both transitions; the result is not a single arbitrary finite parameter calculation.

## Boundaries

The group formula does not cover general Cayley graphs or graph filtrations with edge insertions. The independent confirmation is limited to a finite combinatorial instance, with all-parameter validity supplied by the printed proof. Publication-priority checking is necessarily limited to accessible indexed literature and the inspected primary full text. Independent audit has not been carried out.
