For every \(q\ge3\) and \(d\ge q+1\), a finite quotient-grid Cayley graph has a cycle-length fundamental-group filtration that passes from a free group through \(\mathbb Z^d\), then through \(\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z\), before becoming trivial. When \(q\) is odd, the cyclic stage is completely invisible to the mod-two persistence barcode; its exact onset and death occur at lengths \(q^2\) and \(dq\).
