Finite affine compositions on Hilbert Paley–Wiener spaces attain the sum of their individual norms, essential norms and every approximation number **for all real offsets** precisely when the coefficient phases satisfy explicit integer relations among logarithmic slopes. Rational independence makes this sharp additivity generic, whereas the resonant triple \(C_z+C_{2z}-C_{4z}\) has the strictly smaller exact norm \(3\sqrt5/4\).
