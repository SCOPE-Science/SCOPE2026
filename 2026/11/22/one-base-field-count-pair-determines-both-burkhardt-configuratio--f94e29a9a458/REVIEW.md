# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The claim quantifies over every twist of the split Burkhardt quartic in characteristic at least seven and over finite fields with a cube root of unity. Bruin–Filatov's twist theorem and the constant split automorphism group identify finite-field forms with all 20 conjugacy classes of the 25920-element symplectic group. Hoffman–Weintraub establish the equivariant models of the 40 planes and 45 nodes. The bundled exact checker independently enumerates all 25920 permutations from six transvections, verifies 360 incidences, partitions the group exhaustively into 20 conjugacy classes, and compares the entire CSV spectrum; exactly 15 distinct complete-spectrum pairs and exactly 15 distinct rational-count pairs match one-to-one. The extension-field zeta-function formula follows directly from finite Frobenius cycles. This is an exhaustive finite proof, not a finite sampling argument.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk
The 40-plane and 45-node geometric descriptions, the group action, and the theory of twists are not claimed as new. Hoffman–Weintraub treat the untwisted modular compactification and permutation representations; Bruin–Nasserden calculate the split threefold's zeta function; Bruin–Filatov classify twist obstructions and geometry; Cheltsov–Tschinkel–Zhang treat equivariant birational geometry. Searches in relevant arithmetic and published research records did not identify a source providing the *same 20-class joint configuration spectra* or the *one-count-pair recovery theorem*. Character tables could imply the count portion if evaluated; they do not, without the computed comparison, state the exact sufficiency and five permanent collisions. An unindexed older group-action calculation remains a real priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This is a natural inverse problem for two canonical geometric configurations on nontrivial quartic-threefold forms: it determines the entire arithmetic of 85 objects over every extension from just two rational point counts over the base field. The exact five-pair loss of Frobenius conjugacy information is an intrinsic mathematical boundary. No general theorem about the full threefold or its birational classification is asserted.

## Closest literature and limits
The most direct original sources are Hoffman–Weintraub's level-three modular variety manuscript, arXiv:1705.09006, arXiv:2207.04393, and arXiv:2308.15271. The claim concerns only the reduced finite étale configurations and fields where the indicated group action is constant; full variety zeta functions, small-characteristic cases and exact isomorphism class recovery are not claimed.
