On the ten-site Petersen quantum network, changing a single hopping strength from \(1\) to any positive nonunit value sharply reduces the minimum number of full-capture detectors from five to three. A fixed three-detector choice succeeds for every such perturbation but captures only \(7/10\) of a maximally mixed excitation at the symmetric point; its exact Krylov determinant is \(-2(t-1)^3\).
