When one river-drift design mixes larvae laterally faster but incurs more mortality per downstream distance, exactly one nursery distance switches which design has more surviving larvae on the nursery side of a downstream transect. The crossover has two sharp limiting scales: a \(\delta^{-2/3}\) law for small mortality-penalty difference and a \(\delta^{-1/2}\) law for large difference, with every comparison proved within an idealized Gaussian lateral-drift model.
