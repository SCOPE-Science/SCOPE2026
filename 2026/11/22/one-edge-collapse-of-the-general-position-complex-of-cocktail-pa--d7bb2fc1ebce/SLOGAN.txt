Deleting one ordinary edge from a cocktail-party graph on \(2n\) vertices, for every \(n\geq3\), changes its geodesic general-position complex from \(S^{n-1}\vee\bigvee^nS^1\) to \(\bigvee^{n-1}S^1\). Two explicit simplicial collapses and a contractible join core explain the loss of the arbitrarily high-dimensional sphere.
