On finite undirected loopless Kripke frames, validity of \(\Delta p\to\Delta\Delta p\) is equivalent to each connected component being complete multipartite. Every single-edge edit preserving validity is classified explicitly, and the exact labelled generating function counting safe edge deletions or insertions is \(\exp(x+\exp(e^x-1)-e^x)\bigl(\tfrac{x^2}{2}\exp(e^x-1)+x^2(e^x-1)\bigr)\).
