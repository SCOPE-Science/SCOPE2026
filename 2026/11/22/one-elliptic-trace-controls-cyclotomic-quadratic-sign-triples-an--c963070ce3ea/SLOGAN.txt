The eight quadratic-sign counts of \(x\), \(x+1\), and the cyclotomic value \(x^2+x+1\) over every finite field of odd characteristic at least five collapse to one elliptic Frobenius trace and two symmetry orbits. Every sign triple occurs exactly outside twelve small prime-power orders; the sharp universal cutoff is \(41\) for primes but \(53\) for prime powers, since the field of order \(49\) still misses \((+1,+1,-1)\).
