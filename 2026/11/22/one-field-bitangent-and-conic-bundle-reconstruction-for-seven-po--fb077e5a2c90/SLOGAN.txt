For every odd-characteristic degree-two del Pezzo surface obtained by blowing up a Frobenius-stable seven-point plane configuration in general position, the two ground-field counts of branch-quartic bitangents and geometric conic-bundle pencils determine the full seven-point Frobenius splitting partition. The 15 partitions give 15 distinct ordered pairs, recovering the surface zeta function and the zeta functions of both configuration schemes without any extension-field observations.
