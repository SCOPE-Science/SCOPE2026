On any smooth degree-one del Pezzo surface obtained from a Frobenius-stable general-position octad, four counts of Frobenius-stable exceptional-curve flags over the ground field recover all 22 possible octad splitting types. Counts of individual curves and all intersection-labeled pairs fail only for \((5,3)\) versus \((7,1)\); the counts of stable mutually-disjoint triples, \(4\) versus \(0\), resolve this sharp ambiguity.
