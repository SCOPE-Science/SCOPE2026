On every labelled wheel \(W_n=K_1\vee C_n\) with \(n\ge4\), an initial set withstands one disabled forcing vertex exactly when its hub-containing rim trace has an adjacent blue pair, or its hub-free rim trace has two selected cyclic triples. This yields a full finite-state forcing polynomial and an exact inclusion-minimal spectrum \(\{3,4\}\) until \(n=7\), then \(\{3,4,6\}\) thereafter, with \(n(n-7)/2\) minimal size-six configurations.
