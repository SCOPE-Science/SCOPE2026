Moving just one point of an odd Fourier quadrature grid toward its neighbor leaves every exact weight positive until exactly half a mesh step, after which precisely one weight is negative. Both the sharp sample-noise norm and full interpolation singular spectrum are explicit for every odd grid size; in the \(N^{-1}\) collision layer the noise norm approaches \(1+2/t\) while spectral condition number grows as \(2N/t\).
