# Scientific review

**Correctness.** The full weight-three reduced Świątkowski differential has 16,192 degree-three columns and rank 16,132 over \(\mathbb F_3\). Sixty explicitly reconstructed integral cycles have zero boundary and a coordinate minor of determinant \(-1\), forcing the exact integral top homology to be \(\mathbb Z^{60}\). Exhaustive geometric cycle enumeration yields 130 product tori (1,040 when each of the three factors is separately oriented). Every torus pairs to zero against the coordinate functional complementary to the 59 basis tori, so these 59 tori generate the full product subgroup over \(\mathbb Z\) and the quotient is \(\mathbb Z\). The verifier checks the witness directly.

**Relation to other results.** The closest indexed Nauru comparison concerns two points and second homology, not three points and third homology. The Pappus and Desargues calculations exhibit the same primitive quotient on different cubic graphs, and a four-cube calculation gives the same rational rank gap for a different graph without asserting saturation. These results put the Nauru calculation in context but do not determine its exact integral lattice. Unindexed equivalents remain possible.

**Value.** A one-dimensional *primitive* deficit among all geometric product tori is a precise obstruction to generation by disjoint simple-cycle products on a well-known cubic symmetric graph. It is not just a Betti number: it identifies the complete integral lattice and an explicit non-product top cycle, relevant to geometric generators for graph braid homology. No general theorem for other generalized Petersen graphs is claimed.
