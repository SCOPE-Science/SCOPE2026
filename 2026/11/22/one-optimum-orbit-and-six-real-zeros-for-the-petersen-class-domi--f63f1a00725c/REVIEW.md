# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The Petersen graph is represented as the two-subsets of a five-set, adjacent when disjoint. Its admissible open-neighborhood color classes have cardinality one, two (intersecting pairs of two-subsets), or three (full neighborhoods). The bijection between CD-color partitions and matchings in three residual triangular graphs is exact, with disjoint cases for zero, one, or two triple classes. Explicit vertex-deletion recurrences give the polynomial coefficients. One independent full partition enumeration agrees on every coefficient. The count and one-orbit classification of all 30 four-class optima are proved by the two disjoint center neighborhoods and the two perfect matchings of their residual four-cycle. Six exact sign changes on disjoint negative intervals certify real-rootedness.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk

The 2023 foundational full-text CD-coloring study, the earlier general CD-coloring algorithm, and the 2022 stricter domination-coloring study were compared by logical implication. The known scalar minimum and general polynomial-time/exponential-time algorithms do not imply explicit partition coefficients, optimum orbit multiplicities, or real-zero certificates. The 2022 result of five colors for Petersen concerns an additional condition absent here. Semantic searches in the available published research-record index returned other Petersen coloring polynomials, total-domatic partitions, and domination invariants, but not this precise class-domination partition enumerator. Unindexed papers or old computations could still contain it.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The main object is a canonical, highly symmetric graph for a naturally motivated coloring-plus-domination parameter. The result replaces the known scalar CD minimum by a complete rank enumerator, an exact geometric classification of every optimum, and an unexpectedly clean real-rootedness statement. The matchings-of-\(T_5\) decomposition makes the census interpretable and reproducible instead of being an arbitrary numeric table. No inference to larger graph classes is claimed.

## Literature and limitations

Relevant sources: Antony et al., arXiv:2307.12073; Krithika et al., author-posted *Parameterized and Exact Algorithms for Class Domination Coloring*; and Zhou et al., DOI 10.3390/math10060998 (distinguishing a stronger parameter). All conclusions are for \(P=\operatorname{KG}(5,2)\) and unlabeled color classes. The literature-priority check is provisional and expert review is still needed.
