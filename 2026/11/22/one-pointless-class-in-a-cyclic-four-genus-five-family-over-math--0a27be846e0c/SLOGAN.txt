Among all smooth genus-five curves \(y^2=ax^{12}+bx^4+c\) over \(\mathbb F_{17}\) with nonzero \(a,c\), exactly \(32\) of the \(4096\) coefficient-marked equations have no rational points. All \(32\) form one isomorphism class under diagonal changes of variables, represented by \(y^2=5x^{12}+3x^4+3\); each curve is a cyclic-four cover of an elliptic curve.
