Adding one self-feedback input to binary neighbor-sum dynamics on any conference strongly regular graph with \(v\equiv5\pmod8\) converts a noninvertible one-step transient system into a permutation of exact order six. Over every characteristic-two finite field of size \(Q\), its complete least-period configuration populations are \(Q\), \(Q^{v-2}-Q\), and \(Q^v-Q^{v-2}\) for periods one, three, and six respectively.
