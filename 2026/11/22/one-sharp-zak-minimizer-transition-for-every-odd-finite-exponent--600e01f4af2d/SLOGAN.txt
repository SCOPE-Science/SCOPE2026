For every odd cutoff length \(L\ge5\), the critical Gabor Riesz basis generated by a truncated one-sided exponential has exactly one sharp damping threshold: its weakest Zak frequency switches from a unique half-frequency to a conjugate pair. The full optimal lower frame bound is determined by one uniquely localized Chebyshev root; the pair emerges by an explicit square-root law, and the transition damping is asymptotic to \(2\log(2L)/L\).
