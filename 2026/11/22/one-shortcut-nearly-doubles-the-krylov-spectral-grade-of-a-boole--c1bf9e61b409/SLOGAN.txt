Adding just one positive-weight nonlocal edge to the \(d\)-cube nearly doubles the number of distinct eigenvalues—and the generic exact-arithmetic CG termination degree—from \(d+1\) to \(2d\). Exactly for even Hamming separations, one explicitly calculable positive edge weight produces a single spectral collision and reduces that count to \(2d-1\); for distance two the resonant weight is \((d-1)/(2-2^{2-d})\).
