For the countable one-column quandle \(\{\star\}\sqcup\mathbb Z\) with shift at \(\star\), every one-operation congruence is either equality, universal, a modular congruence, or an eventual-periodic tail congruence. The complete distributive lattice has an unusual finitary boundary: all nontrivial proper finitely generated one-operation congruences fail to be genuine quandle congruences, whereas all finite quotients remain quandles.
