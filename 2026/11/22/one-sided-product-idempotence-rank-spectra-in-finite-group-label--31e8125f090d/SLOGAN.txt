Ordered idempotent pairs in \(G\wr\mathcal T_n\) whose **one-sided product is idempotent** have an exact joint three-rank polynomial depending on the finite, possibly nonabelian, group \(G\) only through \(|G|\). For fixed \(n\), the corresponding probability for two independent uniform idempotents tends to \(1/n\) as \(|G|\to\infty\).
