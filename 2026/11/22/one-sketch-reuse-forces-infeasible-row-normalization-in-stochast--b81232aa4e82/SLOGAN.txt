Reusing one coordinate sketch in both factors of the stochastic Stiefel landing normal direction changes its target: every initially orthonormal \(n\times p\) frame with \(p\ge2\) almost surely converges to its row-normalized, **infeasible** version for each fixed effective step \(0<\eta\le1/(2n^2)\). The limiting squared constraint violation is at least \((p-1)^2/(2p)\), with equality for square identity frames; this is a modified shared-sketch scheme, **not** the independently sketched algorithm in the cited papers.
