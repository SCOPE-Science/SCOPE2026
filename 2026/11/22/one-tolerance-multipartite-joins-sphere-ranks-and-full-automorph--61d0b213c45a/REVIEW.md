# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact face predicate \(e(S)\le1\) gives a cover by joins \(X_i\) with every pairwise intersection equal to the original multipartite join \(K\). The inclusions \(K\to X_i\) are nullhomotopic, and the repeated homotopy pushout gives a wedge only in dimension \(r\), with the explicit integer multiplicity. For every part-permuting automorphism, an invariant face has at most one repeated vertex class, necessarily a fixed class; counting full vertex cycles yields the exact enumerator and signed reduced Lefschetz trace. The all-two-vertex lattice follows from the integral relative sequence. The packaged standalone script checked 2,448 signed full-group trace cases and all homology degrees over two prime fields for eight patterns. The proof, not the finite computation, establishes all sizes.

## Originality — PASS (with residual search risk)

The matroid-elongation identification and a qualitative sphere-wedge statement are already covered by standard matroid topology and Selvaraja's Section 8; neither is represented as an original general theorem. The newly evaluated statements are the closed multiplicity for all part sizes, the full wreath-product rational character allowing equal-size part exchanges, and the explicit integral twisted augmentation lattice in the all-two-vertex case. The 2026 primary source supplies Leray/collapsibility rather than these formulas; Moci–Pezzoli's general equivariant theorem does not state this particular evaluation. Targeted searches across indexed prior findings and published full texts found distinct objects (clique-free, path-free, graph-geodesic, higher independence) rather than the same one-tolerance partition-elongation statement. General equivariant theory might implicitly cover this as a derivable but nontrivial special case; unindexed prior computations remain a residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This complete closed character determines rational invariant dimensions for any subgroup of the full multipartite automorphism group by finite averaging, information not contained in the known Leray numbers or the homotopy multiplicity. It handles global exchange of equal-size color classes, not only within-class permutations, and exhibits an integral sign-twisted augmentation representation for the cross-polytope-boundary family. The object is a natural first elongation of a partition matroid, not an arbitrarily selected finite parameter slice. No claim of higher-tolerance or equivariant homotopy classification is made.
