The one-tolerance complex of the clique join of \(r\ge2\) discrete vertex classes is an integral wedge of \(r\)-spheres with explicit multiplicity, and its entire rational automorphism character has a closed formula using only vertex fixed points and two-cycles on the orbits of part permutations. When all classes have two vertices, the homology is explicitly the integral part-augmentation lattice twisted by the natural orientation character.
