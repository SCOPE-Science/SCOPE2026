# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The all-size proof reduces the graph adjacency operator to its four-dimensional image, whose matrix depends only on the parity of the two shores. Explicit rank filtrations and the nilpotent/invertible Jordan components establish the complete four-case normal form. The periodic populations follow from the invertible summands; the first-entry shells follow from the kernels of nilpotent powers; each orbit basin is counted by projection onto the invertible component. The stand-alone adjacency-mask checker independently tested 110 size pairs and exhaustively enumerated the functional graphs of 36 admissible cases with up to twelve vertices, plus six unedited cases. These finite checks are corroboration rather than the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

The full published *Sharp period-four census for complete multipartite parity automata* concerns unedited complete multipartite graphs and shows that their self-exclusive parity rule has no primitive four-cycles. Adding an internal edge in a shore with at least three vertices destroys the complete-multipartite property, so the new odd–odd four-cycle response is not a specialization of that result. The full published *A one-edge parity reversal in sequential XOR dynamics on triangular books* treats sequential self-inclusive site updates, not the synchronous self-exclusive adjacency update here, and hence does not imply the new Jordan form. Ciandrini et al. (arXiv:0809.1526v1) treat random-network XOR feedback; Kantic et al. (arXiv:2604.01548v1) provide general finite-linear-system methods rather than this graph-defect classification. Searches for equivalent formulations and one-edge bipartite modifications did not reveal a covering theorem; they do not establish universal priority. Earlier equivalent calculations could remain unindexed.

## Value — PASS

The claim gives a sharp phase diagram for how one added triangle-making edge changes the full temporal structure of synchronous XOR on every complete bipartite graph in an infinite two-parameter family. It changes a nilpotent rule into a system with nonzero recurrent configurations for three parity classes, and creates precisely three primitive four-cycles in the odd–odd class. The complete first-entry distributions and individual basin sizes are exact natural dynamical invariants, not arbitrary parameter samples or mere numerical coincidences.

## Limitations and validation

The result is restricted to the stated graph family and binary synchronous rule. It is not a claim about sequential updating, other graph edits or nonbinary fields. Exact proof and independently implemented small-state checks provide self-review, not a separate external audit or historical-priority guarantee.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
