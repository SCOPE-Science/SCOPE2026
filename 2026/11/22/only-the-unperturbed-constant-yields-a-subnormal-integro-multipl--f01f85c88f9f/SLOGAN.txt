For the tridiagonal analytic Hilbert spaces with orthonormal basis \((n+1+az)z^n\), multiplication by \(z\) is subnormal exactly when \(a=0\). An explicit radial Hausdorff measure has too little mass near the origin to support the required nonzero first angular moment for any \(a\ne0\), resolving the nonzero-constant question posed for integro-multiplication operators.
