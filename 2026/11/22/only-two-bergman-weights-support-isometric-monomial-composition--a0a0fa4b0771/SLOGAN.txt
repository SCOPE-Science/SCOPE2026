On the standard weighted Bergman spaces \(A_\alpha^2\), an isometry of the form \(f\mapsto w\,(f\circ z^N)\) with \(N\ge2\) and unrestricted analytic \(w\) exists exactly at \(\alpha=0\) and \(\alpha=1\). The former has a unique derivative-type multiplier up to phase, while the latter has precisely an \(N\)-torus of consecutive-coefficient multipliers.
