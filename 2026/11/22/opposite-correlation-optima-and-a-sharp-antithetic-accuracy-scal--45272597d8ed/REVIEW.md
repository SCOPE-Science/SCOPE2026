# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The stationary Gaussian variance is reconstructed via cross-covariance recurrences, and the Green–Kubo coefficient is obtained from an exact telescoping sum, independent oracle/thermal streams and absolutely summable AR(1) correlations. The derivative signs, endpoint limits and if-and-only-if small-step criterion follow by direct algebra; the exact-rational three-state Lyapunov checker passed 2,567 assertions. These finite checks are corroboration, not an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** The original and revised primary preprints state the SGLM update and independent-gradient results but do not give the colored-gradient phase diagram. The 2016 SGLD Gaussian analysis addresses a different Euler update and iid gradient sampling. Published related mathematical findings on constrained midpoint AR(1) perturbations, multiscale Langevin correlated probes and stochastic Runge–Kutta Gaussian sampling do not imply the formula for the specific SGLM step or the sharp joint efficiency/accuracy reversal. General linear systems covariance machinery can derive the moments, but does not by itself supply the SGLM-specific sharp phase classification. Unindexed exact prior coverage is possible; no independent priority certification was performed.

**Value — PASS.** This is a natural and actionable model problem: retaining the thermal-noise reuse, changing only temporal dependence of an equal-marginal stochastic-gradient oracle changes invariant-distribution fidelity and effective estimation variance in opposite directions at large stable timesteps. The sharp correlation sign threshold and exact \(O(h)\) distance-to-perfect-antithesis scale distinguish design regimes. A central limitation is that the colored-oracle model violates fresh-independent minibatch hypotheses; there is no claim that ordinary minibatches exhibit this reversal or that the result extends to nonlinear targets.
