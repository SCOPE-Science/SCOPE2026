For a Gaussian target sampled by stochastic-gradient Leimkuhler–Matthews with AR(1) gradient error, the unique sharp correlation threshold \(h\kappa=1\) reverses whether persistent gradient errors improve invariant-law accuracy, although persistence always worsens the long-run sample-mean variance. Antithetic correlation restores a second-order stationary covariance error precisely when \(1+\rho_h=O(h)\).
