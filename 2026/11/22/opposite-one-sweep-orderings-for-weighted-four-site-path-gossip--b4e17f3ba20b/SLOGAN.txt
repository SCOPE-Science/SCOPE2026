On a weighted four-agent path, using the two nonadjacent outer contacts together (with the central contact first or last) always minimizes average one-sweep squared disagreement, while sweeping monotonically from one endpoint to the other always minimizes worst-case squared disagreement. All six schedules have the same transfer eigenvalues, but their squared-singular characteristic polynomials differ by exactly \(4pq^2r(1-p)(1-r)\lambda(1-\lambda)\).
