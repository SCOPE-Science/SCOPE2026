Every positive-time heat kernel on the circle has a unique best one-kernel Poisson approximation in normalized \(L^2\), and the optimal Poisson radius is strictly below the radius obtained by matching the first Fourier moment. In the short-time limit, its logarithmic radius is \(c_*\sqrt t+O(t^{5/2})\), where \(c_*=1.0777422543\ldots\), and the unavoidable squared-error cost is \(0.06738055658\ldots/\sqrt t\), with explicit higher-order corrections.
