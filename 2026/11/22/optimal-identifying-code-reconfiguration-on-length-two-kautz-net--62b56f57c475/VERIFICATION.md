## Checks and limits
The graph definition, finite enumeration scripts and all-length proof are contained in this package. The functional generator checks every fixed-point-free map for \(3\le q\le6\); the independent bitmask subset enumerator checks all subsets of optimum size for \(q=3,4,5\). These computational checks are finite and supplement rather than replace the structural proof. No independent human review, formal proof certification, or claim concerning higher word lengths is asserted.
