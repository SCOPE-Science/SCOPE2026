For independent two-column sparse-sign sketches, full-rank probability is uniquely maximized at an interior nonzero-entry density \(p_m>2/3\), not by dense signs. The optimal density approaches \(2/3\) through a Lambert-\(W\) boundary layer, and its rank-failure probability is asymptotic to \(2\,3^{-m}\), exponentially smaller than the dense-sign probability \(2^{1-m}\).
