For estimating the Brownian quadratic path energy \(Q=\int_0^1B_t^2\,dt\), observing the first \(m\) Legendre white-noise coefficients is uniquely optimal among all \(m\)-coefficient subsets. The exact all-budget Bayes risk decays as \(1/(64m^3)\), whereas arbitrary optimal Gaussian linear observations improve its leading constant by the sharp factor \(3\pi^4/128\approx2.28303\).
