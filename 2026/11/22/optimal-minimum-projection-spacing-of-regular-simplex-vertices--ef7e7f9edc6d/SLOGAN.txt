For a uniformly viewed regular \(d\)-simplex, the largest possible minimum separation between its projected vertices is \(\sqrt{6/[d(d+1)(d+2)]}\), realized only by equally spaced projections. The probability of coming within \(\varepsilon\) of this optimum has an explicit \(\varepsilon^{d-1}\) law, and the weighted excess gaps conditioned on this event converge in total variation to a uniform Dirichlet vector; an exact finite-threshold integral is also available.
