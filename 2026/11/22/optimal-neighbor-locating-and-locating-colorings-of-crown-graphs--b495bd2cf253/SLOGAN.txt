On a crown graph \(K_{n,n}-M\), \(n\ge3\), locating and neighbor-locating proper colorings coincide, and the optimal number of colors is exactly \(n+1\). All optimum partitions have a two-block-plus-singletons normal form, yielding precisely \(n3^{n-1}-(n-2)2^n-2\) unnamed optimal color partitions.
