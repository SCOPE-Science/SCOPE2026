For a rate-\(\lambda\) Poisson counting path, the Bayes-optimal estimate of its area from any one deterministic bit of one count has an exact integer-threshold reduction. At high intensity, the uniquely optimal observation time tends to \(2/3\), the cutoff tracks the expected count, and the bit retains the sharp variance fraction \(16/(9\pi)\) (rather than vanishing as with a fixed presence bit).
