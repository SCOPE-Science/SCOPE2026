A planar Latin-hypercube design can be extended to any larger target size while keeping the largest possible set of original points in linear time, because its conflict graph is a union of two matchings. For a random \(n\)-point design enlarged to \(n+1\) strata, the optimal retained fraction converges to \(3/4\), while the probability of retaining every original point is exactly \((2^n n!/(n+1)^n)^2\).
