Every finite rooted Kripke pretree has a smallest regular type-\((k,m)\) bounded-morphic cover exactly when the target branching and cluster sizes fit \(k\) and \(m\); its sharp order is \(m(k|I|+1)\), and its unlabelled source shape is unique. A weighted leaf-surjection product counts every optimal map, refining the known qualitative regularization construction.
