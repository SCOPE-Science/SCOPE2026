# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In the stated ideal model, write J(t) for the weighted unsaturated tail. Boundary terms cancel in H', giving H'=gamma t^(gamma-1)J>0 and H''=gamma(gamma-1)t^(gamma-2)J-gamma f(t)/t<0 for 0<t<b, including across a by continuity of the zero-extended density. The numerator of the throughput derivative is (t+u)H'-H and decreases strictly from a positive divergent limit to -1 for u>0; throughput vanishes at zero and decreases for t>=b. Thus every optimum is interior and unique. Its inverse overhead U=H/H'-t has U'=-H H''/(H')^2>0 and ranges from zero to infinity. The first-order equation supplies H'/H=1/(t+U), and endpoint normalization H(b)=1 removes the multiplicative ambiguity. Substituting the resulting H' and H'' into gamma f=-tH''-(1-gamma)H' gives precisely the reported density formula. Below a the power law gives the exact linear segment; at t>a, the newly saturated positive mass raises U above that line, since H/H' is t/gamma plus that saturated mass divided by H'. The conditional tail equivalent gives H' asymptotic to gamma c(b-t)^(beta+1)/(b(beta+1)), yielding the stated inverse constant. This is ideal-data identification, not stability or recovery from finitely many noisy choices.
