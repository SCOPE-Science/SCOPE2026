# Reproducibility and verification

The proof proceeds from the harmonic interpolation power sums to the positive three-anchor Gram and its convex two-endpoint contributions. Its strictness uses the failure of a constant vector to be an eigenvector at unequal gaps; finite spectral tests do not substitute for this infinite statement. The reflection-symmetric matrices give the exact upper bound and asymptotic penalty.

Run `python verification/check.py` with Python 3 and NumPy. The standalone deterministic program performs 90 exact-rational gap-moment checks, all 12,341 ordered three-gap spectra for cycle lengths 3 through 43, and seven normalized Fourier-Parseval comparisons. The finite cutoff is explicitly bounded; the analytical balancing proof is the argument for every integer cycle length.

The source comparison covers the 2026 host harmonic-frame article and two full published closely related findings, with an additional spline-projection record inspected at abstract level. No independent audit, full bibliography exhaustion, weighted-cycle result, or extension to four or more anchors is asserted.
