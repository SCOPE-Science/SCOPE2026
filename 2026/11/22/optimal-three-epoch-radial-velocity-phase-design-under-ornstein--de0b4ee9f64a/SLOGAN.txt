For three unknown sinusoidal radial-velocity coefficients observed at exactly three epochs under stationary Ornstein–Uhlenbeck noise, the globally D-optimal observing gaps are equal, and each is strictly between \(0.2869573P\) and \(P/3\). Longer-lived correlation moves the unique optimum toward the shorter limit; the usual white-noise spacing loses about 12.7% of the optimal Fisher determinant in the persistent-noise limit.
