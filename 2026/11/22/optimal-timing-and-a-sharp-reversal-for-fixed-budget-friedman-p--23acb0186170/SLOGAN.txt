For a two-colour balanced Friedman–Pólya urn with \(a>b>0\), applying a fixed budget of Friedman steps first uniquely minimizes finite-horizon squared colour imbalance and the corresponding risk under an eventual pure-Pólya continuation; applying them last uniquely maximizes both. On the \(a<b\) side, the preferred adjacent order reverses according to the starting imbalance, so no universal timing rule persists.\n
