For the unit half-cycle boxcar on \(\mathbb Z_{2m}\), all cyclic translations with two freely weighted modulation channels have a unique equal-power minimax design. Its optimal gap is antipodal for odd \(m\), the two nearest odd gaps for even \(m\), and exactly antipodal for \(m=2\), with sharp parity-dependent squared-analysis condition numbers that grow like \(m^2\).
