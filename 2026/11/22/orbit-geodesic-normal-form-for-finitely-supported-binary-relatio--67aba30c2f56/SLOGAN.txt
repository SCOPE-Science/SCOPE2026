Every finite-support directed relation on an infinite set of equality atoms has an exact five-case shortest-path normal form: its named distance matrix is a min-plus rank-one correction of a finite digraph's distance matrix. The same formula classifies the strong-component behavior of all unnamed atoms and yields a sharp \(m+1\)-edge reachability cutoff for \(m\) named parameters; the cutoff alone is already implied by general orbit-iteration bounds.
