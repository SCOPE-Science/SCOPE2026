For pointed monoids with independently eventually periodic generators, every orbital pair is a subgroup fiber of a coordinate-subset cyclic group, giving an explicit stable \(K\)-theory wedge decomposition. When all periods equal a fixed prime \(p\), the full quotient multiplicity polynomial and \(K_0\), \(K_1\) groups are exact, while the free \(K_0\)-rank has distinct sharp even- and odd-rank Gaussian–theta asymptotics of order \(p^{r^2/4}\).
