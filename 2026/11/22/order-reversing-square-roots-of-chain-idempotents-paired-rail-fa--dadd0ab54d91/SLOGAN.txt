Every order-preserving idempotent of a finite chain admits order-reversing square roots, and their complete image-rank polynomial factors into explicit opposite-side kernels \(H_{L,R}(v)=\sum_j(j+1)\binom Lj\binom Rjv^j\). Despite different pointwise square-root fibers, the global joint ranks of an order-reversing map and its idempotent square have exactly the same distribution as the previously counted order-preserving quasi-idempotents.
