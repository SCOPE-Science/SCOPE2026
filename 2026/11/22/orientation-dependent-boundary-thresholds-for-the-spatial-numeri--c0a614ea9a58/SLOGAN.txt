On complex \(\ell_p\), a positive rank-one boundary potential raises the spatial numerical radius of the forward shift precisely above strength \((p-1)/p\), but raises that of the backward shift precisely above \(1/p\); both supercritical radii and every maximizing geometric vector have exact formulas. Although their boundary thresholds reverse at \(p=2\), both transitions have the same sharp quadratic onset coefficient \(p^2/(2(p-1))\).
