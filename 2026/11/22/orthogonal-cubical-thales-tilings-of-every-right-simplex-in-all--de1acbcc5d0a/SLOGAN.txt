Every right \(d\)-simplex, with **arbitrary** positive orthogonal leg lengths and **any** interior anchor, admits an exact face-to-face tiling by \(d+1\) orthogonally bipolar \(d\)-cuboids formed from its orthogonal face projections. Each cuboid has \(2^d\) vertices and is precisely the corresponding Thales power cell; this proves a full all-dimensional family of the proposed orthogonal-kernel tiling equivalence.
