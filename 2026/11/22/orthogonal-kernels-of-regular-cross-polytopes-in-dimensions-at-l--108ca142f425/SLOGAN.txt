For every regular cross-polytope in dimension \(d\ge4\), the points whose perpendicular feet land in the relative interiors of all faces form exactly \(\{x:\|x\|_1+(d-2)\|x\|_\infty<1\}\). Their volume fraction is \(\binom{2d-2}{d}^{-1}\), and the closed kernel has \(3^d-1\) vertices and \(d2^d\) facets.
