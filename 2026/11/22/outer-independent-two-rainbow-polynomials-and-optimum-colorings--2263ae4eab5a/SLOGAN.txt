For every \(n\ge5\), the complete outer-independent two-rainbow weight polynomial of \(\operatorname{KG}(n,2)\) is an explicit star–triangle inclusion–exclusion sum. Its minimum weight is \(\binom{n-1}{2}\), and all minimizers are exactly the full-star empty supports with two singleton color classes having no common endpoint; their exact count is \(n\bigl[2^{\binom{n-1}{2}}-2\bigl(1+\binom{n-1}{2}+(n-1)(2^{n-2}-n+1)\bigr)\bigr]\).
