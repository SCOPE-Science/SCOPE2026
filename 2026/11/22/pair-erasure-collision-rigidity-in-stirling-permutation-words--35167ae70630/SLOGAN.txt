In a Stirling-permutation word, erasing both occurrences of different labels gives the same standardized descendant exactly when the intervening labels form a nested unary chain or a monotone row of doubled leaf siblings. The entire pair-collision census is explicit, and the expected number of distinct one-label-erasure outputs is \(n-\frac23-\frac{n-1}{2n-1}\) for order \(n\ge2\).
