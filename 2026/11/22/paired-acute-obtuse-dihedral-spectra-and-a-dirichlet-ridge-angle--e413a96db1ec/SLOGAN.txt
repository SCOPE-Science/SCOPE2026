The canonical Euclidean Loday associahedron has a complete, explicitly paired acute–obtuse ridge-angle spectrum with no right angles: exactly \(\binom{n+1}{4}\) ridges are acute, and the acute proportion tends to one-half from below. Its uniformly sampled ridge angle converges to a nondegenerate half-acute, half-obtuse mixture of explicit functions of five Dirichlet spacings.
