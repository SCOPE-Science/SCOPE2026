In a nonlinear two-to-one binary cellular automaton, the two predecessors of a uniformly selected attainable cyclic output disagree at pairwise-independent sites, each with probability \(3/4\), even though three consecutive disagreements are dependent. Their Hamming distance has exact mean \(3n/4\) and variance \(3n/16\) for every \(n\ge7\), a complete eight-state generating function at all lengths, and Gaussian fluctuations as \(n\to\infty\).
