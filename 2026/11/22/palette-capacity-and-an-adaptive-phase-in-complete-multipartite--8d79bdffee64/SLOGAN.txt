For proper named-\(q\) colourings of every complete \(r\)-partite graph, vertex–colour equality queries have VC dimension exactly \(r-1\) at \(q=r\) and \(\sum_{i=1}^{s}a_i+r-s\) at \(q>r\), where the part sizes are decreasing and \(s=\min\{r,q-r\}\). On the octahedral graph \(K_{2,2,2}\), five colours give VC dimension \(5\) but Littlestone dimension \(6\), in contrast with the equality of both dimensions on complete bipartite graphs.
