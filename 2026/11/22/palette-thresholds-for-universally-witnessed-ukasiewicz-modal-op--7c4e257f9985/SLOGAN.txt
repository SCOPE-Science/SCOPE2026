For Łukasiewicz fuzzy modal frames, universal one-step witnessing with every finite atomic truth palette is equivalent to reverse well-ordering of the positive accessibility grades, while palettes accumulating at truth degree \(1\) force finite positive cuts for possibility and palettes accumulating at \(0\) force the same condition for necessity. These thresholds separate on one infinite star, and finite positive cuts also characterize full-valued witnessing, product continuity and a sharp finite-truncation error.
