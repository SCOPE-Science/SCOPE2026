For every finite abelian palette of order \(q\ge3\), the full Fourier spectrum of proper colorings on any unicyclic graph has no extra balanced zero unless the unique cycle length is \(1\pmod q\); in that case, precisely the cycle-character multiplicities \((m+1,m,\ldots,m)\) vanish. The resulting exact convolution rank depends only on the palette size, cycle length and total vertex count, not on the attached trees or palette group structure.
