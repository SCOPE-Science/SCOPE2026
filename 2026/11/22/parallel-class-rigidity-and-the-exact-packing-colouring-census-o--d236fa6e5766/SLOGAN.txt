For every finite affine plane of order \(q\ge3\), its point–line incidence graph has packing chromatic number \(q^2-q+3\), and exactly \(q(q+1)(q(q-1))!\) optimal named colourings. Every optimum colours all points with \(1\), uses two distinct full parallel classes of lines for colours \(2\) and \(3\), and assigns distinct higher colours to all other lines.
