For every odd-order affine-plane qubit graph state, omitting two point–line controlled-\(Z\) gates reduces the initially maximal point-versus-line entanglement by exactly one bit if and only if the gates belong to distinct parallel lines; one omission and all other two-gate patterns leave it unchanged. Among two uniformly selected gate omissions, the exact probability of this loss is \(q(q-1)/(q^2(q+1)-1)\) for plane order \(q\ge3\).
