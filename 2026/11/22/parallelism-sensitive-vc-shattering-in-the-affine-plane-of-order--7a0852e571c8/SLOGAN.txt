In \(\operatorname{AG}(2,4)\), nonparallel two-line unions shatter precisely the \(1680\) zero-sum-quadruple-free four-sets and the \(2400\) five-sets with a collinear triple but no zero-sum quadruple. They match parallel unions on four-sets, but exclude \(1440\) five-sets shattered by unrestricted unions; those exclusions are invisible to the number of collinear triples alone.
