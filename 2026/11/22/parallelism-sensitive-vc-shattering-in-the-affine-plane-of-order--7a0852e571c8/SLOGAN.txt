In the sixteen-point affine plane \(\operatorname{AG}(2,4)\), unions of two parallel lines and unions of two nonparallel lines shatter the same \(1680\) four-point supports, precisely those containing no zero-sum quadruple, but their VC dimensions are \(4\) and \(5\). Their exact shattering polynomials are \(1+16z+120z^2+560z^3+1680z^4\) and \(1+16z+120z^2+560z^3+1680z^4+2400z^5\); allowing both kinds of line pairs adds \(120\) shattered four-sets and \(1440\) shattered five-sets, exposing a sharp additive-parallelogram obstruction invisible to ordinary collinearity.
