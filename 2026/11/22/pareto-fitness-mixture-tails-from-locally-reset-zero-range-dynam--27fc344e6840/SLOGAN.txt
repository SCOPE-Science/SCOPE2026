For a locally reset mean-field zero-range population, uniformly sampling the fitness level produces an exact stationary Pareto occupation tail of exponent \(1/\nu\) throughout the finite-mean regime \(0<\nu<1\). A universal high-fitness scaling distribution has Laplace transform \(\exp[-e^{1/u}E_1(1/u)]\), giving the sharp tail coefficients \(3/C^2\) when \(\nu=1/2\) and \(19/C^3\) when \(\nu=1/3\).
