Uniform sampling of a fitness level in the critically tuned local-reset mean-field system gives an exact stationary occupation survival asymptotic with exponent \(1/\nu\) throughout \(0<\nu<1\). The amplitude depends on the full conditional high-fitness scaling law, and equals \(3/C^2\) at \(\nu=1/2\) and \(19/C^3\) at \(\nu=1/3\).
