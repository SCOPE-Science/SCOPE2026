# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The position is never reset. The Itô integral gives the exact within-cycle Gaussian variance \(h(a)\sim 2Da/\mu^2\); the stationary reset-epoch affine recursion has moments exceeding the proposed spatial tail index. The stationary renewal-age tail is the integrated waiting-time survival, and Gaussian regular-variation product asymptotics give the sharp coefficient. An explicit control of the past-position contribution establishes the same asymptotic for the full physical position, not only its latest excursion. The layer-cake identity proves moment convergence and divergence at the exact boundary. Independently, stationary force covariance gives the variance resolvent and strict convexity proves unique deterministic-clock minimization. The numerical checker compares three independent stationary-variance constructions and Gaussian-mixture tail limits but is not used as a universal proof.

## Originality — PASS (with residual priority risk)

The 2023 Guéneau–Majumdar–Schehr model and Kesten recursion are prior art. Its analyzed protocols are periodic and Poissonian; the assessed statement is a continuous-time **non-Poisson heavy-tail theorem** with exact coefficient, a sharp moment-loss boundary and an all-renewal-clock variance principle. Published nearby results on Poisson-reset Brownian forcing address fourth-order temporal memory, not arbitrary renewal clocks. Two other full-text comparisons concern alternating-sign renewal propulsion and Brownian exploration with harmonic first-hit return; neither has this same forcing-only reset, position-preserving process. Directly reset Brownian motion has analogous integrated-renewal ages, but it lacks the stationary filtered recursion term whose negligibility must be proved here. A broader unpublished or unindexed renewal-filter theorem could imply the result; novelty is not externally certified.

## Mathematical value — PASS

A positive harmonic trap normally regularizes fluctuations; this theorem identifies a precise physically motivated exception when only the force renews, with a complete exact stationary-tail coefficient and full hierarchy of moment failures. It also characterizes the optimal variance design over every positive finite-mean clock, in the same model, rather than calculating an arbitrary isolated parameter value. These results distinguish two experimentally different reset mechanisms and provide quantitative observables for non-Poisson forcing models.
