Renewal resetting of a Brownian driving force, while leaving a harmonically trapped particle's position continuous, produces stationary Pareto spatial tails with exact exponent \(2(\alpha-1)\), coefficient and moment cutoff when the reset clock has survival exponent \(\alpha>1\). The stationary positional second moment is finite exactly when the renewal interval has a finite second moment, and deterministic reset intervals uniquely minimize it at fixed mean.
