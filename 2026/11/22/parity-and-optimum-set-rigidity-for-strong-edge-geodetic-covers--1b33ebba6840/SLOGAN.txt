For every crown graph \(K_{n,n}-M_n\) with \(n\ge5\), the strong edge geodetic minimum is \(n\) for odd \(n\) and \(n+1\) for even \(n\), with exactly two and \(2n\) optimizers respectively. Every optimum is a full bipartition shore, augmented by one opposite-shore vertex precisely when the order parameter is even.
