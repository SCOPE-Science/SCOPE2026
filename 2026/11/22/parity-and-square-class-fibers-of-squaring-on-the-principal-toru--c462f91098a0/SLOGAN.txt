On the dense nonzero-degree-two torus of a cyclic Nakayama radical of Loewy length four, the square map has completely explicit prescribed-root fibers over every finite field. For odd-length cycles in odd characteristic exactly half the torus is attained with uniform fibers, whereas even cycles have a square-class obstruction and image density asymptotic to \(1/(2q)\) as the odd field order \(q\) grows.
