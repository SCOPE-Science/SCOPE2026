Among four distinct roots of unity, the smallest sum magnitude is exactly zero at even orders and exactly \(2(\cos(\pi/N)-\cos(2\pi/N))\) at every odd order \(N\ge5\), with all equality configurations classified. This gives the sharp \(3\pi^2/N^2\) primitive-Fourier cancellation floor for four-point binary masks, three times the leading constant when repeated roots are allowed.
