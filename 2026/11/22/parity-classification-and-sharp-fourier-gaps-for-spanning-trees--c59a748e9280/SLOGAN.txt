For spanning trees of every simple generalized theta graph, counting convolution is invertible exactly when precisely one arm has even length, or all arms have odd length and their number is odd. The entire signed Walsh spectrum is a symmetric polynomial in the arm-sign sums, and its smallest surviving singular value is sharply \(1\), \(2\), or \(2^{s-1}\) according to the parity pattern, where \(s\) is the number of even arms.
