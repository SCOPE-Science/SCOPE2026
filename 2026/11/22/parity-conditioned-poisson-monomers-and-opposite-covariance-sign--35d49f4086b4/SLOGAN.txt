At dimer activity \(n/c\), equilibrium monomer counts on the balanced complete tripartite graph \(K_{n,n,n}\) converge at explicit \(O_c(n^{-1})\) total-variation rate to three independent Poisson counts conditioned on the parity of \(n\). This conditioning reverses the limiting sign of every distinct-vertex monomer covariance: positive along even orders and negative along odd orders after the natural \(n^2\) scaling.
