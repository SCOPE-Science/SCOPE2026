# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. Pairwise even-derangement completion counts are determined by ordinary rook inclusion-exclusion and the signed determinant of the residual allowed matching board. Conjugacy and the prescribed-edge sum determine the remaining signed chain class. On the zero-row/column subspace the covariance is exactly \(\alpha I+\beta\operatorname{transpose}\), with the two dimensions obtained from complete-graph incidence ranks. Positivity, the singular four-label boundary, the exact phase changes, and the asymptotic frame constants follow by exact derangement inequalities. The standalone checker passes 7,799 exact rational assertions for sizes four through eight. Finite checking is corroboration, not the infinite proof.

## Originality

PASS within the cited comparison, with historical priority risk. Ku–Wong's full archival paper develops irreducible-character eigenvalues of a Cayley graph on \(S_n\), which differ from Walsh-coordinate sampling on a subset of a Boolean additive cube. Miska's full text Section 5 contains counts and arithmetic of even versus odd derangements, not the conditional two-sector covariance action. The closest indexed published result gives the **unconditioned** derangement incidence covariance and its exceptional equality at five labels; that measure cannot imply the conditional covariance without additional parity-sensitive signed counts. Exact comparison: unrestricted \(n=5\) levels both equal \(15/44\), while parity-conditioned levels are \(1/4\) and \(5/12\). No equivalent conditional Gram theorem appeared in the actual targeted searches. Unindexed equivalent work and material outside the accessible corpus remain possible.

## Value

PASS. Conditioning on permutation sign is natural for alternating-group permutation constraints, yet it changes the identifiable Fourier geometry: an exceptional three-dimensional rank at four labels, a complete centered sampling rank at every larger size, and a nonmonotone symmetric/skew variance ordering whose last transition is at nine labels. Exact nonzero Riesz bounds and asymptotically tight conditioning quantify recovery. This is not merely a new parameter substitution into the unrestricted result because its parity-signed pair counts and covariance kernel are different.

## Limitations

The theorem covers the constant and off-diagonal degree-one Walsh characters only, not higher harmonics. It makes no claims for odd-parity conditioning or arbitrarily weighted measures.
