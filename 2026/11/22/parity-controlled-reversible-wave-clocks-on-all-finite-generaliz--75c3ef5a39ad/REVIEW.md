# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The cubic incidence identity is derived from the unique projection axiom, including the incident and nonincident boundary cases. The parity analysis proves that the spatial adjacency has only zero- and one-primary components with bounded Jordan lengths and rigorously establishes the sharp cases. The reversible companion construction transfers each spatial block to explicit temporal Jordan or quadratic-primary blocks. The resulting fixed-state ranks and Möbius counts have been checked against independent complete matrix powers in six concrete symplectic, duad–syntheme and thin examples. These computations corroborate a proof valid for every existing finite generalized quadrangle, not merely a finite enumeration.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

Inspected sources: Steeb–Hardy (2007), the readable full scientific text of the second-order reversible construction; Arrighi–Martiel–Perdrix (2015), the full readable preprint's graph-reversibility setup and results; Godsil–Guo–Myklebust (2015), the complete five-page arXiv paper including its generalized-quadrangle definition and quantum-walk calculation. Targeted searches of earlier graph-wave results, projective-plane incidence waves, strongly regular graph waves, and published research summaries did not locate the order-eight/order-twenty-four generalized-quadrangle incidence classification or its rank-parametric primitive orbit census. Strongly regular graph theorems do not directly cover the bipartite Levi graph, while quantum transition matrices are different from the present two-row finite-field map. A previously unindexed identical calculation or a broad modular adjacency-module theorem may exist; historical priority is not certified.

## Value — PASS

The result classifies the entire natural family of finite generalized quadrangles (including nonisomorphic and unequal-order geometries), identifies a sharp geometric parity transition in the reversible temporal clock, and supplies a computable orbit census even when incidence ranks vary. It distinguishes a universal dynamical order from geometry-dependent multiplicities and provides independent concrete realizations of both regimes. This is a structural dynamical invariant, not a new name for a single graph computation or a routine single-parameter increment.

## Scientific limits

Ranks of specific nonclassical geometries remain input data for their exact orbit populations. The theorem is limited to characteristic two and to the defined reversible two-layer neighbor sum; the formulas make no claim about quantum-walk matrices or Greenberg–Hastings thresholds. Search coverage is nonexhaustive and independent external validation has not occurred.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
