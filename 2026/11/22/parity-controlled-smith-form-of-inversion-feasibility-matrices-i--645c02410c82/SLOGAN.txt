For the generic structure with two independent linear orders, the matrix recording whether two complete one-point types over \(n\) individually named parameters admit an inversion has exactly \(n^2+n+1\) unit Smith factors. Its remaining factors alternate sharply with the parity of \(n\): for odd \(n\), there are \(n-1\) factors of two and one zero; for even \(n\), one factor of two and \(n-1\) zeros.
