## correctness

PASS

The first-arrival construction is exact because blocked footprints never become vacant again. In the start conflict graph of degree at most 2(k-1), an ancestor path of length l has at least ceil((l+1)/2) distinct starts of one parity, so its strictly decreasing order has probability at most the inverse factorial of that count. Multiplying by the exponential path count still gives a tail tending to zero uniformly in epsilon. A deterministic finite neighborhood therefore suffices up to small uniform error, including in the limiting even-before-odd priority order. On that neighborhood the common exponential coupling yields saturated evens and odd clocks cut off at s at time s/epsilon for fixed s>0. Terminal states follow by the same argument separately with no cutoff. Even-start compression gives length-r homogeneous RSA; in the empty-interval equation for l>=r-1 the trial probabilities solve the stated hierarchy. Bounded uniqueness is justified by variation of constants: for the difference of two bounded solutions the integral kernel for the diagonal term l-r+1>=0 is at most one, and iterating the remaining bound gives a factorial denominator and forces zero difference on compact time intervals. At fast jamming E_{r-1}=Q_r is the intensity of maximal gaps per compressed site. Their physical lengths are 2g for even k and 2g+1 for odd k, where g<=r-1. Thus no even-k slot survives, and the odd-k slots are precisely the g=r-1 gaps, each filled by at most one odd-start rod. Gap positions need not be independent; conditional on the fast packing the disjoint slots have independent clocks. Dividing the physical slot intensity Q_r/2 by the density 1/2 of each site parity, then multiplying by the rod's r-1 even and r odd sites and 1-exp(-s), gives the two increments. Terminal filling gives the jump independently of the finite-s limit; H_{r-1}=log(r-1)+gamma+O(1/r) gives the stated odd asymptotic and k=5 gives 5 exp(-3)/2.

## originality

PASS

The supported distinct result is the weak-positive-rate limit of same-length rods with periodic start rates on the physical lattice, including infinite-volume convergence for both time regimes, the even/odd geometric distinction, and the parity-resolved slow profile. The ordinary coverage and largest-gap intensities are known. Moreover, the compressed terminal odd-stage count is exactly a specialization of Pinsky's two-stage model once the geometry of the reduction is understood; it is not credited as new in compressed variables. The earlier exact trimer crossover is a material special case, not an all-length result. The accessible evidence for de Oliveira–Tomé does not determine its footprint and does not settle priority.
