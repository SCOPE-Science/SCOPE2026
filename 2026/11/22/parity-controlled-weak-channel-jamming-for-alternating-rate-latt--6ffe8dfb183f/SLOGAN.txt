For irreversible length-\(k\) rods with alternating fast and vanishingly slow start-site rates, every even length \(k\ge4\) has a continuous zero-rate jamming limit, whereas every odd length \(k\ge5\) has the exact positive jump \(\frac{k}{2}\exp[-2H_{(k-1)/2}]\). The slow-time coverage of both sublattices is explicit, and the odd-length jump decays as \(2e^{-2\gamma}/k\).
