# Verification scope

The proof has five separately checkable steps: the summable backward-priority ancestor bound uniformly in the slow rate; graph compression of even rod starts to homogeneous shorter rods; the exact empty-interval equation and maximal-gap density; parity classification of remaining physical gaps and disjoint second-stage rods; and the harmonic asymptotic. These are provided in `RESULT.md` and independently reconstructed in `REVIEW.md`.

The included `verify.py` script requires only Python 3 and reports `PARITY_STAGGERED_ADSORPTION_CHECKS_PASSED 2138` when executed. Its deterministic seeded periodic simulations check eight rod lengths \(k=4,\ldots,11\) at three positive slow times and verify the combinatorial absence or disjointness of eligible odd rods for each generated fast-stage configuration. Its numerical integration also checks the established uniform two- and three-site rod coverage constants. The exact infinite-volume statements do not rely on sample means or numerical tolerances.

An independent scientific audit has not been performed. Historical priority is qualified by the inaccessible full text of a closely relevant 1994 inhomogeneous-adsorption study. No conclusion about all staggered models, convergence rates, or simultaneous large-rod and small-rate limits is certified.
