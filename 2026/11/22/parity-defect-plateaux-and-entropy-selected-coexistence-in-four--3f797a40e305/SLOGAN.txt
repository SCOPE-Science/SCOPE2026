The four-state Potts antiferromagnet on an \(n\)-gonal bipyramid has exact field thresholds \(H/J=2,n\) for even \(n\ge6\), but an additional parity-defect threshold at \(H/J=1\) for odd \(n\ge7\). At \(H=2J\) its labeled ground-state count is \(3(2^n+(-1)^n)+6L_n\), with residual entropy \(\log2\) per rim vertex and entropy-selected favored rim occupancy tending to \(2/3\).
