For a uniformly ignited chain of fuel patches whose edge-crossing probabilities decay exponentially with protection investment, all protection stays on the central cut of an even path until the sharp budget \(\log(m/(m-1))\); on an odd path, it splits between the two central cuts until the threshold \(2\log(m/(m-1))\). Each threshold is exact, and the optimizer is globally unique even at equality; the four-patch problem also has an explicit unique post-threshold continuation.
