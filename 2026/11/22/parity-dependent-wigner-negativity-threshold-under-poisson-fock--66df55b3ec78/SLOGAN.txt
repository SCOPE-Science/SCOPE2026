For a photon-losing oscillator periodically re-prepared in \(\lvert n\rangle\), matching the re-preparation rate to the one-photon loss rate uniquely maximizes stationary entropy and produces a uniform, Wigner-positive Fock mixture. Wigner negativity returns immediately above this rate for every odd \(n\), but only beyond a strictly larger threshold for every even \(n\); for two photons that threshold solves \(\alpha^3-\alpha-2=0\).
