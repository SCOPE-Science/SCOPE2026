Reversing words in a cyclic Sturmian integral lift produces a sharp Hamming-distance parity law: for odd alphabet size \(m\), every forward/reversed pair differs in asymptotic proportion \(1-1/m\), while for even \(m\) the complete limiting spectrum is \([1-2/m,1]\). The convergence error is \(O(\log n/n)\) for bounded-type irrational slopes.
