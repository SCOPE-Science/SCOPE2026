On the odd \(2m+1\)-element zigzag fence, parity-filtering lower ideals decreases VC dimension from \(m+1\) to \(m\), but preserves the boundary-sphere generator of top shattering homology. The odd class has exactly \(\binom3{m-1}\) more maximum shattered supports than the even class, a transient \(1,3,3,1\) defect that disappears at \(m\ge5\).
