In the true second polyanalytic Fock space, compressing the angular winding \(e^{im\arg z}\) annihilates exactly one complex-Hermite mode for every even positive \(m\), and none for odd \(m\). Its missing mode is \(p=m(m-2)/4\); every winding has Fredholm index \(-m\), but only even windings acquire one extra kernel-and-cokernel channel.
