For an isotropic quadratic with one perfectly shared uniform noise draw per sign-gradient step, all initially same-parity lattice coordinates synchronize exactly in finite time, while opposite-parity coordinates become permanently adjacent; the settling-time law is a sum of independent geometric variables. The exact multivariate stationary law shows that averaging arbitrarily many same-parity coordinates does not reduce the variance below \(B\delta/2\), in contrast to the \(B\delta/(2d)\) variance under independent coordinate noise.
