Under synchronous three-opinion plurality with incumbent tie retention, odd-rim wheel graphs have only the three unanimous equilibria, but even-rim wheels support exponentially many additional equilibria consisting of a dissenting hub and a balanced rim with no singleton opinion runs. The exact run-composition formula grows as \(3\varphi^n/\sqrt{2\pi((5+3\sqrt5)/40)n}\) along even rim lengths, while the binary-opinion model has only unanimity at every length.
