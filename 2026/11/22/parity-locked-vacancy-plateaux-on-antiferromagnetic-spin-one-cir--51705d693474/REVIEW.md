Strečka, Michaud and Mila study a quantum ladder with rung Heisenberg terms and diagonal couplings, rather than the present classical prism Hamiltonian. The 2016 sawtooth-chain result has a different interaction graph; only its indexed abstract was available for comparison. Indexed published results for binary Ising circular ladders and spin-one antiferromagnetic wheels are also adjacent, not dominant. Searches cannot prove worldwide novelty; inaccessible or unindexed equivalent calculations remain possible.

## Mathematical value: PASS

Odd-cycle antiferromagnetic frustration naturally competes with spin-zero vacancies. This all-circumference classification shows a nontrivial intermediate phase containing precisely one paired vacant rung, not isolated independent vacancies, with a width independent of the positive rung coupling. Exact coexistence counts \(4n\) and \(2n+1\), together with the singular \(R\downarrow0\) change in degeneracy, make the result a reproducible benchmark for finite-volume vacancy ordering and frustration relief. It goes beyond a numerical example or an unmotivated small graph count.
