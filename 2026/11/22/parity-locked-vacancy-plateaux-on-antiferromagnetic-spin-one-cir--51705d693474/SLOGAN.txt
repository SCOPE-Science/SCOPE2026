On the antiferromagnetic spin-one circular ladder, every positive rung coupling locks the two parity-frustrated rims: odd circumference creates an exact single-vacant-rung ground-state plateau of crystal-field width \((n-2)/(n-1)\), independent of rung strength, while even circumference has no intermediate vacancy plateau. Every ground-state energy, population, critical value and labeled degeneracy follows from a nonnegative frustrated-bond identity.
