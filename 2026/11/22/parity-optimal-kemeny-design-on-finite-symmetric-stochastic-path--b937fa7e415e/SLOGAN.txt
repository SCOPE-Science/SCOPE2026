Among all symmetric stochastic nearest-neighbor walks on an \(n\)-vertex path, the unique minimizer of Kemeny's mean stationary-target hitting time alternates two explicit transition probabilities: both equal \(1/2\) for odd \(n\), but unequal for even \(n\). An exact positive-multiplier and squared-deviation identity certifies the complete all-size classification and quantifies why the classical fastest-spectral-mixing path walk is Kemeny-suboptimal precisely at even orders.
