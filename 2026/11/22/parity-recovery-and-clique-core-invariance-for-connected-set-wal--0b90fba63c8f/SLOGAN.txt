For XOR convolution by nonempty connected induced vertex sets of the complete split graph \(K_s\vee\overline{K_m}\), Fourier recovery is possible exactly when the independent-side size \(m\) is odd; even sizes lose precisely the central Walsh layer, independently of clique size \(s\). At \(m=2r\), the globally sharp endpoint projection norm is \(1+\binom{2r}{r}(2^{-r}-2^{1-2r})\), which grows exponentially while the proportion of lost modes tends to zero.
