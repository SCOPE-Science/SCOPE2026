The binary-length equiangular honeycomb on a labelled two-row torus has an exact parity-dependent closure count: \(\Omega_{2,n}=2^{n+3}\) for odd \(n\) and \(\Omega_{2,n}=12\cdot2^n+2(6^{n/2}+2^{n/2})\) for even \(n\). Genuine opposite-edge shears occur exactly at even periods \(n\ge4\), and the two parity subsequences have different per-face entropy limits \(\frac12\log2\) and \(\frac14\log6\).
