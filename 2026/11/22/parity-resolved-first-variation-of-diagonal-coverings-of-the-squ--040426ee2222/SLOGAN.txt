For every integer \(n\ge4\), the exact first-order loss of the covering radius of \(n\) equally spaced diagonal sites on a flat square torus, as its product metric passes from \(\ell_1\) to \(\ell_p\), is a sharp binary-entropy expression with different even and odd formulas. The parity difference persists in the leading \(n^{-2}\) correction, and does not claim any unrestricted site-placement optimum.
