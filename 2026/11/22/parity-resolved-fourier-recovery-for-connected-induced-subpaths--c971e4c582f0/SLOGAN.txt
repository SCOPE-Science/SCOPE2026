For parity-restricted connected induced subpaths of an \(n\)-vertex path, the complete binary Fourier spectrum is controlled by two prefix-sign imbalances. Even-order subpath convolution is singular exactly when \(n+1\) is a sum of two squares, whereas odd-order subpath convolution is invertible exactly when \(n\equiv1\pmod4\); explicit binomial sums give both nullities.
