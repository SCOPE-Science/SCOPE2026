For Gaussian minimal-norm Fourier extension from the diagonal circle into a two-torus, the exact singular-value profile is a strictly phase-ordered theta collision ratio. Under strong spectral localization its squared condition number tends to one precisely for rational anisotropy with odd reduced denominator, but to two for even-denominator or irrational anisotropy; irrational parameters also produce a continuous spectral counting law with inverse-square-root edges.
