# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The claim is an exact if-and-only-if criterion over arbitrary finite simple graphs. For each nonempty edge-support relation, the all-even or all-odd vertex-degree parity alternatives are necessary and sufficient because the annihilator of the even-weight shore space is the span of the all-ones vertex vector. A nonempty Eulerian subgraph exists exactly when the support has a cycle; for a forest the all-odd degree demand is solvable exactly when every component has even size. These facts prove both directions. Dimension, labeled-forest counting, and the topological consequence follow from explicitly cited standard lemmas. Exhaustive independent trace and matrix-rank computations agree through six vertices, recurrence checks through ten vertices, and direct boundary calculations at four and six vertices.

## Originality — PASS within investigated sources
The even-cut-matroid representation is already present in the 2016 Guenin–Pivotto text and is explicitly credited as prior knowledge. The 2019 parity-factor paper establishes a general background for prescribed degree parity but does not formulate the binary edge-query traces. The 2020 Johnson/Hamming VC study uses different concept families. The closest retrieved prior result for fixed-cardinality shores does not directly determine the shattering polynomial for the union of all even-cardinality shores. No located source states the complete forest/odd-component trace classification together with the specialized all-order labeled-forest formula and integral homotopy multiplicities. Historical-priority uncertainty remains, especially for possible specializations of existing binary-matroid Tutte calculations.

## Value — PASS
The problem is a canonical parity-restricted version of cut learning, where the concepts form a binary code but parity can remove a full dimension. The theorem characterizes *every* shattered support, gives a sharp graph-wide rank transition, and extracts the whole face polynomial for complete graphs. Its explicit topological multiplicities (7, 511, 98,099, 36,215,281) are not just a dimension restatement: they quantify the independent-set complex of the dual even-cut matroid and offer a natural test family for parity-sensitive matroid enumeration.

## Closest literature and limitations
Guenin–Pivotto, *Stabilizer theorems for even cut matroids*, Section 2.1, establishes the graft/cut framework; Bujtás–Jendrol'–Tuza, arXiv:1902.08689, establishes parity-forest context; Adler–Benediktsson–Macpherson, arXiv:2007.16042, treats different VC set systems. The closest related published-finding corpus descriptions are the fixed-cardinality cut record and the two-vertex cut record. Their full result files could not be inspected here and are therefore not represented as conclusively non-covering. No exact arbitrary-graph face polynomial is asserted.
