For two landmarks in an antipodally folded binary cube \(F_n\), every separation has an exact closed joint-distance polynomial and a sharp minimum equivalence-class size. For odd \(n\ge5\) adjacent landmarks maximize that minimum at \(n-1\), whereas for even \(n\ge6\) diametral landmarks uniquely maximize it at \(n\); the folded four-cube is the explicit small exception.
