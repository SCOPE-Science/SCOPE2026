# Scientific review

## Correctness: PASS
The model uses an explicitly uniformly marked circle site, ensuring Dirichlet rather than anchored-origin-biased spacings. The geodesic Voronoi midpoint identity is valid for the circular ordering. In odd order the cyclic linear system is bijective with determinant \(2^{1-n}\) on the sum-one chart; positivity characterizes the support. In even order the alternating length constraint is necessary and the interval parameter gives every feasible inverse and its conditional uniform measure. The triangular Jacobian and exact integration along the fiber justify the normalized density. Reproducibility includes 4,812 exact-rational test assertions. Generality rests on the proof rather than finite tests.

## Originality: PASS, with historical qualification
The accessible full-text HTML of Járai-Szabó–Néda (arXiv:cond-mat/0406116), Section 2, and Devroye–Györfi–Lugosi–Walk (arXiv:1512.04267v1), Theorems 1–2 and the dimension-one discussion, was materially compared: they concern one-dimensional typical-cell marginals and asymptotic measures, not the finite full cyclic vector and inverse fibers. The closest identified public published-finding corpus item addresses only threshold-indicator pair dependence; its full article was not retrievable, a stated residual risk. Other literature may formulate the result in moving-average Dirichlet terminology, so priority is not independently certified.

## Value: PASS
The simultaneous measurement of all Voronoi cell lengths is a natural summary of a point pattern. The theorem completely characterizes which such measurements are realizable, whether they identify the underlying site configuration, and how uncertainty is distributed when identification fails. Exact parity-dependent rank loss and conditional uniform recovery are structurally informative, not a small numerical improvement or arbitrary sliced statistic. The full joint density permits exact probability calculations for multiple cells without assuming independence.

## Principal limitations
Uniform sampling, prescribed number of sites at least three, intrinsic geodesic cells, labeled cyclic order and orientation are essential. The complete priority search is limited by inaccessible full texts of some related publications and the published-finding corpus threshold-dependence article; conclusions are qualified accordingly.
