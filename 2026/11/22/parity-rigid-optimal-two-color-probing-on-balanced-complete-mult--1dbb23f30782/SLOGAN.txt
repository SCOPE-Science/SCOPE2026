For the shifted Laplacian resolvent on every equal-part complete multipartite graph, the exact minimum-variance two-color Rademacher probing design is independent of the positive screening parameter: even numbers of graph parts split evenly by whole parts, whereas odd numbers force exactly one original part to be divided as evenly as possible. The odd-part optimality gap over all whole-part-only designs is exactly \(a^2(m^2-\epsilon^2)>0\) in the notation of the accompanying proof.
