For \(K_{2,n}\), the acyclic sink polynomial has exactly two simple real zeros when \(n\) is even, only the zero at \(n=3\), and exactly three simple real zeros for every odd \(n\ge5\); the two nonzero odd-case roots straddle \(-5\). A closed binomial-polynomial formula and Descartes' rule prove the complete classification without limiting the graph order.
