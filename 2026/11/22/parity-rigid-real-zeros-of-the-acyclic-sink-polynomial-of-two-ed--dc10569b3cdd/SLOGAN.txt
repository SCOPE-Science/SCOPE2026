For the two-edge spider with \(m\) arms, the complete acyclic sink polynomial is \((1+3z)^m+(z-1)(1+z)^m\): it has exactly two simple real zeros for odd \(m\) and three for even \(m\). Its escaping negative root has magnitude asymptotic to \(3^m\), while the additional even-arm root converges to \(-1/2\); real-rootedness holds only for one or two arms.
