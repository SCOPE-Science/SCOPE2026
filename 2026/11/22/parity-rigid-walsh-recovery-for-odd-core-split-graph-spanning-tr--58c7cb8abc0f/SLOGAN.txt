For odd clique size \(m\ge3\), spanning-tree Walsh convolution on \(K_m\vee\overline{K_n}\) is invertible exactly for even \(n\); at odd \(n\), every multiplier is divisible by \(2^{m-1}\) and an explicit exponential family vanishes. For triangular cores the smallest nonzero multiplier is exactly \(1\) at even orders and \(4\) at odd orders, giving sharp recovery conditioning.
