# Scientific assessment

## Correctness

**PASS.** The object is defined for every labelled complete multipartite graph with at least two nonempty parts. A disjoint forbidden-family decomposition yields the signed Walsh transform exactly. Character orthogonality supplies all eigenvalues. Parity handles zero and nonzero cases including the zero frequency; explicit weight-feasible witnesses attain the sharp one/two singular gaps. Coefficient extraction partitions all frequencies uniquely by their zero shores. The asymptotic follows from a union-bound error and Stirling. Integer verification covers 269 small multipartite profiles by an independent construction and transform. It is corroboration rather than proof for infinite parameters.

## Originality

**PASS under bounded comparison.** The closest checked primary result is Tittmann–Averbouch–Makowsky (2008, arXiv:0812.4147), especially the connected-subset polynomial and its join formula. That work supplies unweighted connected-set counting, not the complete character-resolved convolution spectrum or parity-based invertibility and sharp gap classification. Liao–Hou (2013, arXiv:1311.6856) treats unweighted graph-polynomial identification, Elbassioni (2014, arXiv:1411.2262) treats enumeration, and Trevisan (2016) treats general Abelian Cayley spectra. The search of indexed mathematical records and accessible historical claims did not identify an equivalent complete result. The elementary nature of the derivation makes prior unindexed rediscovery a material risk.

## Value

**PASS.** Connectivity-constrained vertex-set measures occur naturally in residual node reliability and network observability. The theorem settles exact information preservation under their translated counting kernel for *all* complete multipartite part sizes, including a sharp all-or-nothing parity criterion, computable nullity, exact surviving stability constants and the quantitative large-size loss law. Those functional-analytic invariants are not furnished by the scalar connected-subset count alone. The complete parameter coverage and transition matter more than the elementary proof length.

## Boundaries

No assertion applies to arbitrary non-multipartite graphs. The claim is an exact complete classification of the stated family, not a general theorem about graph connectivity convolution. Searches cannot establish absolute novelty. The checker verifies finite examples only. Independent external validation is outstanding.
