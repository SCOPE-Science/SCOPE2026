On a cyclic capacity-two Burgers traffic ring of every even length \(n\ge4\), exactly \(2nM_{n-3}\) initial configurations at each near-critical occupancy \(n-1\) and \(n+1\) require the maximal \(n/2-1\) updates to become periodic. A parity-sampled Motzkin transfer and a unique exceptional-peak criterion prove this law, and the proportion of maximally delayed states conditioned on either mass tends to \(2/9\).
