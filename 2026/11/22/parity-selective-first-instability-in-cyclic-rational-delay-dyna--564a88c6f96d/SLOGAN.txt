In a cyclic rational-delay map, the synchronous equilibrium loses stability at exactly \(t=3/4\) for every even ring, but at a strictly larger oscillatory threshold for odd rings. The odd-ring excess is asymptotically \(3\pi^2/(50k^2)\), and the three-component system first becomes unstable at \(t=0.820357195635835\ldots\), well before its in-phase scalar mode destabilizes.
