An open, equal-coupling triangular Ising antiferromagnet has a longitudinal-field crossing at \(h=2J\) precisely when its triangle count is odd; the exact coexistence count is \((3m+5)/2\), whereas the even chain has no such crossing. At \(h=4J\), both parities have exactly \(F_{m+1}\) ground states, producing an isolated entropy density \(\frac12\log\varphi\).
