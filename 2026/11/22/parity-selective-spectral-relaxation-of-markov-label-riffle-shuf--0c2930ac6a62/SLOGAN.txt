For Markov-label inverse riffle shuffles, permutation parity relaxes one order faster in adhesion loss on even decks than on odd decks: the sign bias starts at \(1-nq/4\) for even \(n\), but at \(1-(n^2-1)q^2/16\) for odd \(n\). An exact four-state recurrence determines the sign character for all deck sizes and gives distinct parity-resolved limits when \(nq\) stays finite.
