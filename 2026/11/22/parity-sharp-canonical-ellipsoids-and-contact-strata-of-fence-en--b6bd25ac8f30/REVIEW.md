# Scientific review: fence enriched chain polytopes

## Correctness — PASS
The signed-chain facet theorem identifies all \(4(n-1)\) supporting facets and establishes an independently sign-invariant full-dimensional body. Uniqueness of both canonical ellipsoids and sign invariance force centered diagonal shape. John's log-determinant optimization has edge constraints \(b_i+b_{i+1}\le1\): pairing proves the even case and explicit strictly positive KKT edge multipliers prove the odd case for every \(n\ge3\). For Löwner, partitioning the independent-set constraints by the odd and even classes gives exact AM–GM product upper bounds; equality choices are feasible for all independent sets. The algebraic relation \(c_i=(n b_i)^{-1}\), exhaustive equality cases, and volume products prove homothety, all John contacts, the exact Löwner contact supports and counts, and the nonvanishing odd-order volume ratio. The extracted standalone checker independently confirms exact combinatorial/KKT claims in orders 3–16 and 18 numerical determinant programs in orders 3–11. Finite checks do not replace the symbolic all-order proof.

## Originality — PASS, with historical-priority caveats
The original enriched-chain polytope and its facet theory are credited to Ohsugi–Tsuchiya and the unconditional formulation to Kohl–Olsen–Sanyal. The closest published geometric result, *A sharp entropy bound for John ellipsoids of enriched chain polytopes* (published-finding corpus identifier 86ee65f9050d), supplies a feasible width-sensitive diagonal ellipsoid which **coincides with the candidate for an odd fence**. But that prior statement certifies optimality only for its layered families and for the rectangular height–width equality case, not for the odd nonlayered fence; the strictly positive explicit edge multipliers give the new optimality argument. It does not provide the fence Löwner ellipsoid, exact vertex-contact classification, or its nonvanishing \(e^{-1/2}\) relative-volume limit. The published *Height and width are the exact Euclidean radii of enriched chain polytopes* (db93f92ff100) gives the two ball radii, not ellipsoid shapes. Our even John ball case follows from the broader roundness criterion and is included only for classification, not claimed as new. Independent general graph-entropy or antiblocker duality may imply homothety, so that identity alone is not claimed as historically new. Semantic searches by object aliases, parameter parity and both ellipsoid names did not locate the claimed full nonlayered two-ellipsoid/contact theorem. The mathematical comparison is stronger than matching titles, but priority cannot be certified from search absence.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The nonlayered fence is the simplest natural increasing family of comparability posets that varies parity while retaining a completely explicit facet model. The odd-order ellipsoid not only achieves the previously known general John entropy lower bound outside the published layered optimality examples; its unique Löwner ellipsoid touches exactly two support classes, in contrast with the unbounded \(m+1\) support classes in even order. Its relative volume remains \(e^{-1/2}\) as \(n\to\infty\) despite vanishing semiaxis anisotropy, a genuine accumulation effect relevant to canonical geometric approximation. These claims are exact, extend over infinitely many dimensions and are not simply a normalization check or a restatement of a known table.
