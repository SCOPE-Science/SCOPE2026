For the enriched chain polytope of every alternating fence, the canonical John and Löwner ellipsoids are exactly homothetic by \(\sqrt n\); both are round in even dimensions and have two explicitly alternating semiaxes in odd dimensions. The odd-dimensional Löwner ellipsoid contacts exactly two signed-antichain support classes and its volume remains asymptotically \(e^{-1/2}\) times the minimum enclosing ball volume, although its eccentricity tends to one.
