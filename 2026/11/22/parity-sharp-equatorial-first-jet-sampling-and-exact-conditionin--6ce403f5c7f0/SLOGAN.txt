For pure degree-\(\ell\) spherical harmonics on \(S^2\), equatorial function values plus normalized meridional derivatives at \(M\) equispaced sites recover every mode exactly if and only if \(M\) is odd and at least \(\ell+1\), or \(M\) is even and greater than \(2\ell\). At any such grid the normalized Gram matrix is diagonal, its sharp squared condition number is \((\ell+1)/2\), and a complete rank/spectrum formula also describes every undersampled grid.
