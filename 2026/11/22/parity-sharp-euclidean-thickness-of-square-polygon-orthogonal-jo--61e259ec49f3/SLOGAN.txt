An orthogonal join of a unit square and a unit regular \(q\)-gon has minimum Euclidean width \(2\gamma_q/\sqrt{1+2\gamma_q^2}\), with \(\gamma_q=\cos(\pi/q)\) for even \(q\) but \(\cos(\pi/(2q))\) for odd \(q\). The number of unoriented thinnest directions doubles from \(2q\) to \(4q\) across parity; the unique volume-maximizing inscribed nine-vertex four-polytope consequently has width \(\sqrt{(20+2\sqrt5)/19}\) in exactly twenty normal lines.
