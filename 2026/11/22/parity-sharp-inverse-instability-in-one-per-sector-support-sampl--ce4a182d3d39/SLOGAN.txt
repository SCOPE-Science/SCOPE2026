For a regular planar normal fan, one support sample from each consecutive sector always determines the polygon when the facet count is odd, but even counts admit an explicit smooth hypersurface of indistinguishable designs. Under independent smooth random offsets, even-sided polygons are identifiable almost surely yet the unconstrained inverse has a sharp \(t^{-1}\) conditioning tail and infinite mean-squared noise error; one additional facet-normal sample removes this instability.
