For the lattice simplex formed by cyclic shifts of \((1,w,0,\ldots,0)\), the exact lattice width is \(w-1\) in even coordinate order and \(w\) in odd coordinate order, for every integer \(w\ge2\). Even order has just one unoriented minimum-width lattice direction, whereas odd order has exactly \(L_n-1\) such directions, bijective with the nonempty independent sets of the \(n\)-cycle.
