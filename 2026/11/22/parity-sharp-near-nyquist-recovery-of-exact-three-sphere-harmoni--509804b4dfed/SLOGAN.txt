For exact-degree \(\ell\) spherical harmonics on \(S^3\), one equispaced closed Hopf-torus trajectory is enough for exact recovery using the sharp minimum \((\ell+1)^2\) sites when \(\ell\) is even and just one additional site when \(\ell\) is odd. An explicit primitive winding realizes each bound at every nonnodal torus radius; the odd-degree sample vectors form exactly the zero-mean hyperplane.
