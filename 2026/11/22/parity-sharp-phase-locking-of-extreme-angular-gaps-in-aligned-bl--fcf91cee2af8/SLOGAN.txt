For a finite Blaschke product with all zeros on one ray and at least one nonzero zero, the boundary fibre with the largest possible minimum angular gap is uniquely the fibre over \(1\), while the fibre with the smallest possible maximum gap is uniquely the fibre over \((-1)^N\). Thus optimal angular packing and covering coincide exactly in even degree, with explicit sharp linear phase-instability coefficients and an exact interval-length formula in every degree.
