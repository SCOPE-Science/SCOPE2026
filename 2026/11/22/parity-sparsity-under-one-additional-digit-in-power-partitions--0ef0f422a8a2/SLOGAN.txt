Allowing one extra copy of each base power increases every restricted-partition count but paradoxically lowers the growth exponent of the indices where that count is odd, for every base \(b\ge3\). The exact parity-count generating function is \(\frac{1+z-4z^2}{(1-z)(1-(b-2)z-4z^2)}\), with sparse-support exponent \(\log_b\frac{b-2+\sqrt{(b-2)^2+16}}2\).
