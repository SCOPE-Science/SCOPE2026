Among centrally symmetric equilateral \(2m\)-gons of fixed perimeter, the regular polygon uniquely maximizes minimum Euclidean width. The maximum has a first-order rigidity split: for odd \(m\) only rotations are infinitesimally neutral, whereas for even \(m\) one alternating direction remains neutral and loses width by exactly \(2\ell\csc(\pi/m)\sin^2t\).
