For each even \(d\ge2\), the Boolean cube \(Q_d\) has exactly \(6\cdot2^{2^{d-1}}\) named optimal strong odd four-colorings that use two colors exclusively on each parity side. The choices form two independent affine spaces with self-dual binary translation code of dimension \(2^{d-2}\), giving \(2^{2^{d-1}-2}\) partitions up to renaming colors; nonsplit optima are not counted.
