For acyclic orientations of a wheel \(K_1\vee C_n\), every signed edge-Walsh coefficient has a two-by-two transfer-matrix formula. Even rim lengths have precisely the global-reversal Fourier zeros, convolution rank \(2^{2n-1}\), and sharp nonzero multiplier gap \(2\); every odd rim length has at least \(2^{n-1}\) additional, explicitly identified blind frequencies.
