Equality-query supports of classical parking preferences are shattered exactly when their queried rows are distinct, fewer than all rows are used, and the selected target values themselves form a partial parking function. The resulting pure but generally nonmatroidal complex has face polynomial \((1+z)(1+(n+1)z)^{n-1}-(n+1)^{n-1}z^n\), and its reduced Euler characteristic has magnitude equal to the number of classical parking functions.
