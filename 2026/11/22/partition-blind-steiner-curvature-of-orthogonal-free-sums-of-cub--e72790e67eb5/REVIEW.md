# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Polar duality gives all facet and ridge incidences exactly; independent shell integration computes volume; radii-simplex Jacobians compute each of the two ridge-area classes. The polyhedral exterior-angle formula then determines the second Steiner coefficient. Strict monotonicity in singleton count follows from an explicitly differentiated strictly increasing function. An independent direct coordinate convex-hull computation checks every claimed facet intersection and ridge angle for thirteen block partitions, with complete finite re-enumeration; it does not replace the all-dimensional proof.

**Originality — PASS, provisional historical priority.** Sanyal–Winter establishes Hanner combinatorics, not the Euclidean Steiner curvature partition-invariance here. The nearest accessible indexed claims concern Hanner isotropic constants, curvature of different layered poset polytopes, or ellipsoids of free sums. Each differs in object, invariant, or both; no inspected result implied the two-coefficient formula. An unindexed general mixed-volume identity for polars of products could still subsume this claim. Search absence by itself does not prove uniqueness.

**Value — PASS.** This is a natural all-dimensional Euclidean invariant and gives a sharp shape-partition extremal principle. The cancellation is nontrivial: e.g. the two ten-dimensional block partitions with 192 and 256 facets have identical normalized first and second Steiner coefficients. It measures the curvature of a canonical infinite family of Hanner polytopes and provides a boundary for what low-order Steiner data can detect.

**Limits.** The assertion is for ordinary orthogonal coordinate realizations, and does not address arbitrary affine images or all Hanner constructions. Publication priority remains provisional.
