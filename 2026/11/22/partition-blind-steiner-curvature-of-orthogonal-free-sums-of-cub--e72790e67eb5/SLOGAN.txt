For the orthogonal free sum of \(m\) coordinate cubes of total dimension \(d\), the Euclidean surface-to-volume ratio is exactly \(d\sqrt m\), and its normalized second Steiner coefficient depends only on \(d,m\) and the number of one-dimensional blocks. For fixed \(d>m\ge2\), the second ratio strictly decreases with singleton-block count: its minimum has one nonsingleton block, while its maximum has as few singleton blocks as the total dimension permits.
