For a positive-weight Gibbs probability map restricted to scores of diameter at most \(R>0\), its exact \(\ell^\infty\)-to-\(\ell^1\) Lipschitz coefficient is \(\beta\operatorname{sech}^2((\operatorname{artanh}\rho-\beta R/2)_+)\), where \(\rho\) is the closest achievable weighted subset-mass imbalance. Uniform weights yield a sharp even–odd saturation threshold, while deciding exact saturation for arbitrary integer weights is NP-hard.
