The two thirteen-point Steiner triple systems have the same minimum incidence-graph domination number \(9\), but their optimal-set counts are respectively \(1456\) and \(1511\). Their one-vertex-replacement graphs differ sharply: the cyclic system has \(157\) connected components (\(117\) isolated optima), while its Pasch-switched companion has \(106\) components (\(82\) isolated optima).
