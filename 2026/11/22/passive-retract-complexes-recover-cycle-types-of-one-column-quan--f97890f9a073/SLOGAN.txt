The passive retracts of a one-nontrivial-column quandle are exactly the nonempty partial transversals of the underlying permutation cycles. If the cycle lengths are \(d_1,\ldots,d_r\), their image-size polynomial is \(\prod_i(1+d_i z)-1\), while each size-\(s\) image supports \(s^{r-s+1}\) retractions; these data recover the complete cycle type.
