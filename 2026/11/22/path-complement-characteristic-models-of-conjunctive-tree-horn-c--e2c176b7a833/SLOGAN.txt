For a rooted tree in which each nonleaf vertex is implied by the simultaneous presence of all its children, the Horn characteristic models are exactly the complements of ancestor-to-leaf paths. Their full omission-size spectrum recovers the leaf-depth distribution, and their number lies sharply between \(n\) and \(\lfloor(n+1)^2/4\rfloor\), with paths and optimally sized brooms as extremizers.
