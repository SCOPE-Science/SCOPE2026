The entropy-maximizing symmetric nearest-neighbor random walk with uniform stationary distribution on a long finite path has an exact alternating Pell-sequence boundary profile, beginning with transition probabilities \(11/27,5/16,41/121\), rather than the bulk value \(1/3\). Its maximal entropy per step is \(\log3-C/n+o(1/n)\), with an explicit positive boundary constant \(C=0.873382433026828\ldots\).
