For the bifiltration of \(n\) labelled isolated vertices entering at the antidiagonal grades \((i,n+1-i)\), all monotone path restrictions have exactly \(\big((1+\sqrt2)^n+(1-\sqrt2)^n\big)/2\) simplicial equivalence classes but only \(F_{n+1}\) combinatorial degree-zero barcode types. Every barcode composition \(b\) into singleton and doubleton birth events has exactly \(2^{s(b)-\mathbf1_{\{b_1=1\}}}\) distinct simplicial classes above it, realized by explicit directed polygonal paths.
