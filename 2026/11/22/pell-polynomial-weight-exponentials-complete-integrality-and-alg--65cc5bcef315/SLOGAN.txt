For integer-polynomial weights on Pell numbers, the exponential \(\exp(\sum_{n\ge1}P(U_n)x^n/n)\) is integral exactly when \(P\) is even and \(P(0)\equiv P(1)\pmod2\). All even weights have an exact, explicitly determined power-of-two algebraic degree; in particular the least integral multiplier for each positive even monomial is \(2\), and its degree is \(2^{3k-1}\), resolving the three Pell-series conjectures recorded in OEIS A208034.
