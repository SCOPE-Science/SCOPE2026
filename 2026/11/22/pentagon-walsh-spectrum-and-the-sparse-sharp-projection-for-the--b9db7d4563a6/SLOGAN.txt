The twelve pentagons of the Petersen graph have signed Walsh eigenvalues only \(-12,-4,0,4,12\), with exactly half of the \(2^{15}\) frequencies lost. The sharp recovery-range projector has an explicit seventeen-point kernel supported at the empty edge set, ten hexagons and six two-pentagon factors, and its unrestricted \(\ell^1\)/\(\ell^\infty\) norm is exactly \(5/2\).
