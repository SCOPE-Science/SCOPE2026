# Scientific review: Perfect double Italian domination of triangular graphs: minimum-label rigidity and cycle enumeration

## Correctness — PASS

For \(n\ge5\), the identity \(s_f(uv)=d_f(u)+d_f(v)-f(uv)\) reduces the closed-neighborhood constraints to root weighted degrees. Two degree-at-most-one root vertices are impossible. The degree-sum argument excludes weight below \(n\) and, at equality, eliminates the only possible nonconstant degree sequences. Every root degree exactly two is sufficient; decomposition into cycles and doubled edges is bijective. The exponential formula and logarithmic derivative give the exact optimizer count and recurrence. Direct exhaustive enumeration through \(n=7\) and separate support construction through \(n=9\) reproduce the predicted counts. Finite checks are corroboration, not the all-orders proof.

The primary 2023 definition and examples in Hao–Jalilolghadr–Mojdeh were inspected, including the graph-class statements and general characterizations. The statement for \(L(K_n)\) and its optimum-count EGF were not identified. ## Mathematical value — PASS

The finding supplies a complete classification and factorial-scale enumeration of all optimal labelings on the canonical line-graph family \(L(K_n)\) for infinitely many orders. It reduces a local weighted-domination condition to the global structure of spanning degree-two multigraphs, distinguishes ordinary cycles from doubled matching edges, and gives refined multiplicities and precise asymptotics. The contribution is the mathematical reduction and optimizer rigidity, not a renamed cycle-counting identity.
