For an arclength-random perimeter-halving chord of a convex quadrilateral, the smaller-area-fraction distribution has an atom exactly when the quadrilateral is a trapezoid. A genuine trapezoid with base lengths \(a,b\), leg lengths \(c,d\), and perimeter \(P\) has exactly one such atom, with mass \(2\min(a,b)/P\) at \(1/2-|c-d|/(2(a+b))\); every parallelogram yields a point mass at \(1/2\).
