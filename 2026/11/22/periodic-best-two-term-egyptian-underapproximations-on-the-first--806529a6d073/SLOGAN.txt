For the least reduced-numerator family beyond the known greedy threshold, namely \(\theta=5/(5m+1)\), the globally best two-term strict Egyptian underapproximation is explicitly determined for every \(m\ge1\). Greedy fails exactly when \(m\equiv2,3\pmod9\) or \(m=6,16\), and only \(m=5\) yields a tie; the failure density is \(2/9\).
