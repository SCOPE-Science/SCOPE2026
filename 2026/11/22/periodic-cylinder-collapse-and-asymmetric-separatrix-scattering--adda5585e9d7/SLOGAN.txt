On the integrable Duffing boundary of a published exponential three-coordinate flow, changing the exponential weight \(\beta\) away from zero destroys **every** nontrivial periodic lift and leaves only equilibria and heteroclinic motions bounded for all time. Its two formerly homoclinic branches have exact, nonzero vertical shifts, one growing exponentially and the other shrinking algebraically as \(\beta\to+\infty\).
