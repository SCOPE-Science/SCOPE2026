In a hierarchical two-species patch model, every nonconstant periodic modulation of the superior competitor's effective colonisation rate strictly improves invasion by a globally dispersing inferior coloniser compared with constant colonisation at the same mean. The exact gain is \(\ell(\langle Y\rangle-\langle Y^{-1}\rangle^{-1})\), and a fully admissible seasonal habitat-autocorrelation family converts a marginal static exclusion boundary into globally attracting periodic coexistence with invasion gain \(3\eta^2/340+O(\eta^3)\).
