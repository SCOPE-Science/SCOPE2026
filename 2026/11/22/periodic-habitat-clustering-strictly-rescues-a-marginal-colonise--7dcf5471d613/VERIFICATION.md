The general theorem is proved by a reciprocal-coordinate reduction of the resident occupancy equation, two exact period-averaged logarithmic identities, strict arithmetic/harmonic-mean inequality, and a positive periodic solution of the inferior coloniser's reciprocal logistic equation. The physical state space is forward invariant.

The reproducible standalone `check.py` verifies the rational model parameters, the static invasion boundary, the quadratic perturbation coefficient, and two numerical periodic-orbit integrations and quadrature identities. Its numerical checks are supporting evidence and not a proof for all waveforms or amplitudes. Reproduce by running `python check.py` with Python 3, NumPy, SciPy and SymPy. The expected terminal line is `ALL_CHECKS_PASS`.

The theorem does not address finite-population demographic stochasticity, nonperiodic forcing, seasonal colonisation of both species or perturbations of the strict-competition hierarchy. The literature comparison is not a guarantee of historical priority. Independent validation has not been performed.
