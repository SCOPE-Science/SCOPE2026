For a finite linear order with \(q\) named colours, one-point-extension rank at least \(t\) is equivalent to containing a recursively nested pair of rank-\(t-1\) witnesses around a permutation of all colours. The smallest such orders have exactly \(q(2^t-1)\) points and form precisely \((q!)^{2^t-1}\) distinct named-colour isomorphism types.
