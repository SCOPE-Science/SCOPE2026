The motivating gap is that knowing a sphere type, an orientation character and all reflection-fixed spaces does not determine the homotopy type of a geometric orbit space. The new equivariant representation model fills this gap for all uniform-arm spiders and produces an exact infinite family of real-projective suspension quotients. A concrete mathematically meaningful boundary is the 17-vertex path: its covering independence complex is a torsion-free five-sphere, but the geometric reflection quotient has integral two-torsion in degree four. This is a homotopy-level distinction invisible to the previous rational and fixed-point classifications.
