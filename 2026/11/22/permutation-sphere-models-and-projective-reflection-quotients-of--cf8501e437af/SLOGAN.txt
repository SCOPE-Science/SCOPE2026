Uniform-arm spider independence complexes admit genuine arm-permutation-equivariant sphere models determined by arm length modulo three, yielding all-subgroup fixed-space dimensions and integral orientation characters. In the two-arm case, the reflection quotients of ordinary odd-path independence complexes are explicit joins of spheres with real projective spaces; already the quotient of \(\operatorname{Ind}(P_{17})\simeq S^5\) has nonzero \(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\) homology.
