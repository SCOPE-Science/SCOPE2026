For every positive-entropy primitive finite-state Markov shift, the shortest periodic orbit entering a uniformly chosen length-\(n\) cylinder has period \(n+O_{\mathrm{law}}(1)\), with an exponentially convergent closing-offset law and uniformly bounded variance. An exact Perron–Frobenius border formula quantifies that law, while a three-symbol directed example has a positive limiting proportion (at least \(1/12\)) of cylinders requiring period \(n+1\).
