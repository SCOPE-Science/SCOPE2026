# Scientific review: persistent Chern-class spectral deficits

## Correctness — PASS

The claim concerns all integers \(k\ge2\), \(r\ge k\), and \(m\ge1\), and all rank-\(r\) complex bundles on the standard sphere \(S^{2k}\). General spectral-component/idempotent equivalence is supplied by Bračič--Kandić (2026, Proposition 3.1). For section endomorphisms, finite families of pairwise orthogonal idempotents are precisely direct-sum bundle decompositions. Nonzero \(c_k(E)\) persists under the \(m\)-fold Whitney sum; since lower positive Chern classes vanish on an even sphere, at least one direct-summand rank must be \(k\) or larger. The unitary clutching map stabilizes already in rank \(k\), providing the rank-\(k\) summand and the sharp matching construction. Bott integrality also shows that \(c_k(E)=0\) forces triviality at rank at least \(k\). The rank-profile rigidity follows from exact rank arithmetic and vanishing of \(H^2\). These steps prove an all-rank statement, rather than extrapolating finite checks.

## Originality — PASS, with bibliographic risk

The 2026 motivating paper gives the general orthogonal-idempotent criterion, but its inspected introduction, Proposition 3.1, and finite-dimensional computations do not evaluate endomorphism section algebras over even spheres. Its companion addresses infinite spectral-component classes and also contains no matching sphere-bundle classification in its inspected material. Classical clutching and Bott integrality results supply essential ingredients and are credited. The section-algebra K-theory literature is related but does not, in the material accessible here, give the computed spectral-depth defect or forced Riesz rank profile. Odd-sphere torsion phenomena can have different, nonpersistent stabilization behavior and cannot imply this persistent all-even-sphere formula. Searches did not identify an equivalent precise classification, which is not itself proof of novelty. Older broad bundle-splitting theorems remain a residual risk; independent bibliographic review remains necessary.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The family isolates a natural, precise obstruction to globally separating spectral branches in finite-matrix-fiber algebras: the obstruction is \(k-1\), persists under *every* finite matrix amplification, and forces a unique Chern-bearing rank-\(k\) spectral block in all extremizers. It is a complete, parameter-uniform classification for even spheres and ranks in the stable range, relating a new spectral invariant to integral bundle topology without asserting any new Bott-periodicity theorem. It gives, for example, projectionless algebras with arbitrarily large finite fiber size and an explicit maximal spectral-component count after arbitrary amplification.

## Sources and limitations

The closest general theorem is Bračič--Kandić, arXiv:2611.12208v1, Proposition 3.1; the companion is arXiv:2611.14394v1. Background bundle classification is Hatcher's *Vector Bundles & K-Theory* and Cnossen's clutching-classification notes; related section-algebra K-theory is Dror Farjoun--Schochet, arXiv:1101.0444v3 (only abstract accessible for the latter). The theorem excludes rank below \(k\), odd spheres, and the finer topology of nonmaximal strata. No computational experiment is used as an infinite proof.
