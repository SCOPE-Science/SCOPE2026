In the Petersen graph, optimum three-vertex feedback deletion sets require two spare deletions for a connected addition/removal reconfiguration, and at that budget every pair has shortest distance exactly \(|S\triangle T|\). The full 400-state budget-five network has a sharp eccentricity trichotomy \(8,9,10\) according to deletion-set size, while its budget-four predecessor fragments into five 29-state components and fifteen isolated four-cycles.
