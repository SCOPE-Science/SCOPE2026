For every prime base \(p\ge3\) and residue alphabet size \(q\ge2\), all offsets of nonoverlapping windows in \(u_i=\nu_p(i+1)\bmod q\) have exactly \(P(n/p^{\nu_p(n)})\) distinct blocks, with an explicit factor-bijection and a full radix recurrence for \(P\). Window complexity collapses to \(q\) at prime-power lengths but grows at lengths coprime to the base.
