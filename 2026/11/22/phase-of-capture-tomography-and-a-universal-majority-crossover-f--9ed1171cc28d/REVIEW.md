# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The renewal argument treats each independent search/return cycle with disjoint direct-capture, return-capture, and home-regeneration outcomes. The killed Brownian resolvent supplies the exact phase odds. The positive-kernel integral gives global monotonicity and a unique majority transition. Entire-function uniqueness plus a compact Hausdorff-moment change of variables proves identification, including rate sets accumulating at zero. For regular one-dimensional return diffusions, a positive derivative of the splitting profile justifies recovering the drift by logarithmic differentiation. The finite-rate ambiguity is proved by a polynomial nullspace and a sufficiently small perturbation preserving strict inward drift. All quantifiers and nondegeneracy restrictions are stated. The included numerical checker passed 57 finite diagnostics, including a direct stochastic bridge simulation. Residual mathematical risk: misapplication outside the specified regenerative, rate-independent return setting; numerical checks are not substitutes for analytic proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk

Biswas, Kundu and Pal (arXiv:2307.16294) provide the general return-renewal framework and explicit forward splitting probabilities. Biswas et al. (arXiv:2406.08975) and Dubey and Pal (arXiv:2610.18814) investigate mean first passage times for linear and harmonic return potentials, respectively. The inspected main and relevant supplement calculations do not identify an unknown splitting function from terminal phase fraction as reset rate varies, prove the rate-universal unique majority threshold, or establish smooth inward-force finite-readout nonuniqueness. Two published inverse-resetting results (published-finding corpus finding IDs ending 2af4cf3521b4 and 4278f0a9748c) use instantaneous, randomly distributed reset sites and MFPT target scans, not a finite stochastic return phase or terminal phase labels. General renewal formulae do imply the basic forward probability integral after algebra; the new contribution is the inverse/identifiability and complete comparison classification, not the input resolvent. An unindexed equivalent inverse theorem remains a genuine priority risk.

## Value — PASS

The inverse statement answers a natural experimental inverse problem: whether recording only which motor phase produced target detection, at controlled interruption rates, can identify the dynamics of the inaccessible return leg. It says exactly what ideal data recover, yields a universal robust majority crossover, and proves why finite exact data fail to identify even smooth inward forces. The free-Brownian/neutral-return calibration provides an exact benchmark even when the mean completion time diverges (because of long negative-side unbiased home returns). Its scope does not include noise-stable estimation, variable diffusion recovery, or return duration inference.
