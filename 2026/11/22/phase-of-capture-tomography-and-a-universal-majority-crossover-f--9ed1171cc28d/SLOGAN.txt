In Brownian search interrupted by stochastic home returns, the final capture-phase fraction at varying Poisson rates uniquely identifies the return-phase target-versus-home splitting law—and the return drift when diffusivity is known. Every nondegenerate return law has one exact search-to-return capture-majority transition, yet finitely many rate readings cannot identify even a smooth strictly inward return force. The exact phase law can remain informative even when the mean total search time is infinite.
