For iid uniform points on a circle, occupied-bin counts from two shifted equal grids have an exact, nonnegative covariance that is smallest at a half-bin shift, and independent counts occur at that shift only for two bins (apart from deterministic sample sizes). Under proportional occupancy, the entire fixed finite collection of phase counts has a Gaussian limit with an explicit strictly positive Fourier spectrum, and phase averaging yields an exact truncated-spacing identity and reduced variance.
