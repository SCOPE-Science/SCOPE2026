For vertex-membership queries on the 37 maximal independent sets of the Heawood graph, a support is shattered precisely when it is an independent set of size at most three or a mixed point-line independent set of size four. The complete face polynomial is \(1+14z+70z^2+154z^3+77z^4\); its nonpure facets form three symmetry orbits of sizes 14, 21 and 56.
