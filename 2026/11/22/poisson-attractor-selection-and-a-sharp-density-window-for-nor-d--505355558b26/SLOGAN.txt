For synchronous NOR dynamics on the binary \(d\)-cube, the first-image defect count is asymptotically Poisson under initial activation density \(p=1/2+c/d\), and the universal two-cycle attracts a limiting fraction \(\exp(-e^{-2c})\) of all initial states. Typical alternative attractors are geometrically isolated sparse-code two-cycles, with the number of defects obeying the same Poisson law.
