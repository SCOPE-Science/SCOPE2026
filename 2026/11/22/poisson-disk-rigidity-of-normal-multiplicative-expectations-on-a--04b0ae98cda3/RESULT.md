# Poisson-disk rigidity of normal multiplicative expectations on an irrational torus

## Finding
For the lexicographic noncommutative Hardy algebra in the irrational-rotation factor introduced in [1], the full set of **normal** conditional expectations onto its scalar diagonal that are multiplicative on the Hardy algebra is precisely an open unit disk. Every such normal expectation is automatically faithful. In contrast to an existence construction on a radial interval, the classification is exhaustive, including all possible normal states and excluding the unit-circle boundary.

More explicitly, for each \(\zeta\in\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}\), set
\[
H_\zeta=(1-|\zeta|^2)(I-\overline\zeta V)^{-1}(I-\zeta V^*)^{-1},\quad
\varphi_\zeta(X)=\tau(H_\zeta X),\quad E_\zeta(X)=\varphi_\zeta(X)I.
\]
Then the map \(\zeta\mapsto E_\zeta\) is a bijection onto the stated family and \(E_\zeta(V)=\zeta I\). As an exact internal geometry of this family,
\[
D(\varphi_\zeta\Vert\varphi_\eta)
=\log\frac{|1-\overline\eta\zeta|^2}{(1-|\zeta|^2)(1-|\eta|^2)}
=\log\left(1+\frac{|\zeta-\eta|^2}{(1-|\zeta|^2)(1-|\eta|^2)}\right).
\]
This information-divergence formula is the familiar Cauchy/Poisson law [3] transplanted to the commuting densities inside the factor; the novelty sought here is the **exhaustive operator-algebraic classification**, not the classical divergence identity considered separately.

## Assumptions and scope
Fix irrational \(\theta\), put \(\lambda=e^{2\pi i\theta}\), and use the factor \(M=W^*(U,V)\) with unitaries \(UV=\lambda VU\) and canonical faithful normal trace
\(\tau(U^nV^m)=\mathbf1_{\{n=m=0\}}\) for all integers \(n,m\).
The index semigroup is
\[
P=\{(n,m)\in\mathbb Z^2:n>0\}\cup\{(0,m):m\ge0\},
\]
and \(A\subseteq M\) is the ultraweak closure of the complex span of \(U^nV^m\) indexed by \(P\). Thus \(A\cap A^*=\mathbb CI\), and \(A+A^*\) is ultraweakly dense in \(M\), as verified in [1]. A normal state is a positive ultraweakly continuous functional of norm one. A normal expectation onto \(\mathbb CI\) has the form \(X\mapsto\varphi(X)I\) for a normal state \(\varphi\). We require the restriction to \(A\) to be multiplicative, not that the state be tracial.

## Proof
**Step 1: the irrational commutation eliminates all transverse moments.** Let \(\varphi\) be any normal state whose restriction to \(A\) is multiplicative, and write \(\zeta=\varphi(V)\). For any \(n\ge1\) and \(m\in\mathbb Z\), both \(V\) and \(U^nV^{m-1}\) belong to \(A\), while
\[
V(U^nV^{m-1})=\lambda^{-n}U^nV^m,
\qquad (U^nV^{m-1})V=U^nV^m.
\]
Multiplicativity makes the state values of these products equal, hence
\((1-\lambda^{-n})\varphi(U^nV^m)=0\). Irrationality gives \(\lambda^n\ne1\), so \(\varphi(U^nV^m)=0\) for all such \(n,m\). Taking adjoints, complex conjugation and using the commutation relation gives the same conclusion for all \(n<0\) and all integer \(m\). For the remaining modes, multiplicativity and positivity give
\[
\varphi(V^m)=\begin{cases}\zeta^m,&m\ge0,\\\overline\zeta^{-m},&m<0.\end{cases}
\]
These are **all** Fourier moments of \(\varphi\) on the ultraweakly dense \(*\)-algebra generated by \(U,V\).

**Step 2: the parameter cannot lie on the boundary.** Since \(V\) is unitary, \(|\zeta|\le1\). The restriction of a normal state to \(W^*(V)\) has a nonnegative \(L^1(\mathbb T,m)\) density relative to normalized Haar measure, because \(\tau(V^m)=0\) for every nonzero integer \(m\). A probability density absolutely continuous with respect to Haar measure cannot have its unit-circle first moment of modulus one: otherwise its positive mass would be concentrated on a singleton \(\{V=\zeta\}\), which has Haar measure zero. Thus \(|\zeta|<1\), without assuming initial faithfulness.

**Step 3: exact existence, normality and exhaustiveness.** For every \(|\zeta|<1\), functional calculus yields
\[
H_\zeta=I+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}(\overline\zeta^{\,k}V^k+\zeta^kV^{*k}),
\qquad
\frac{1-|\zeta|}{1+|\zeta|}I\le H_\zeta\le
\frac{1+|\zeta|}{1-|\zeta|}I.
\]
Here the series converges in operator norm. Hence \(H_\zeta\) is positive, boundedly invertible, and \(\tau(H_\zeta)=1\); consequently \(\varphi_\zeta=\tau(H_\zeta\,\cdot\,)\) is a faithful normal state. Trace orthogonality gives
\[
\varphi_\zeta(U^nV^m)=0\quad(n\ne0),\qquad
\varphi_\zeta(V^m)=\zeta^m\quad(m\ge0),
\]
and the adjoint values for negative powers. If two generators in \(P\) are multiplied, their first coordinates add and stay positive unless both are zero; in the latter case the product is a nonnegative power of \(V\). Therefore \(\varphi_\zeta\) is multiplicative on the analytic polynomial span. Separate ultraweak continuity of multiplication and normality extend this to all \(A\). Conversely every \(\varphi\) from Step 1 has exactly the same monomial moments as \(\varphi_\zeta\); normality and ultraweak density imply equality on \(M\). The map \(\zeta\mapsto E_\zeta\) is injective because its value at \(V\) recovers \(\zeta\), establishing the classification.

**Step 4: relative entropy.** The densities \(H_\zeta,H_\eta\) lie in the same abelian algebra \(W^*(V)\), so their Umegaki relative entropy reduces to the circle integral
\[
\tau\big(H_\zeta(\log H_\zeta-\log H_\eta)\big).
\]
Writing \(v=e^{it}\), the density is \(h_\zeta(v)=(1-|\zeta|^2)/|1-\overline\zeta v|^2\). Its harmonic-measure property says that for any \(w\in\mathbb D\),
\[
\int_{\mathbb T}h_\zeta(v)\log|1-\overline w v|\,dm(v)
=\log|1-\overline w\zeta|.
\]
Substitution and cancellation produce the displayed symmetric logarithmic expression. The identity \(|1-\overline\eta\zeta|^2=(1-|\zeta|^2)(1-|\eta|^2)+|\zeta-\eta|^2\) proves its nonnegativity and exact equality condition.

## Verification
The monomial commutation argument proves exact Fourier-moment rigidity for all integer indices; norm-convergent geometric series prove the density formula and faithfulness; ultraweak density proves uniqueness. The information formula uses the Poisson integral of a harmonic logarithm, with no finite truncation assumption. The companion `CHECK.py` implements a reproducible Fourier/quadrature cross-check for selected interior parameters. Those tests do not replace the analytical proof.

## Relationship to prior work
[1] constructs the same irrational-rotation factor and lexicographic algebra and exhibits a continuum of faithful multiplicative expectations indexed by **real** \(r\in[0,1)\), establishing its Arveson-finiteness counterexample. The present claim identifies **every** normal multiplicative expectation, proves that nonfaithful normal ones cannot occur, and supplies the unique full complex-disk parameter. The phase-rotation extension of the existing real construction alone is routine and is not asserted to be the contribution. [2] gives broad existence theory for noncommutative representing measures and conditional expectations but does not identify the explicit complete family for the lexicographic irrational-rotation example. [3] already gives symmetric classical Cauchy divergence laws; their application to these commuting Poisson densities is attributed rather than asserted original.

## Limitations
No classification of singular, nonnormal states or nonnormal expectations is claimed. No assertion about expectations on other positive cones, rational rotation parameters, or higher-rank torus algebras is made. The precise historical uniqueness of this concrete normal-character classification remains subject to independent bibliographic review; broader abstract ordered-crossed-product theorems could imply it. The result is analytically proved and self-reviewed, not independently audited.
