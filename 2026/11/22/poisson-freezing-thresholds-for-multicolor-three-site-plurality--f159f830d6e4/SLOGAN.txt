Incumbent-tie plurality on a \(q\)-color ring erodes alternating two-color defects exactly and has no attractors beyond fixed points and global checkerboard two-cycles. For uniform random initial colors with \(q\sim c n^{1/(2t+1)}\), the probability of fixing within \(t\) updates tends to \(\exp(-c^{-(2t+1)})\), giving distinct critical palette scales at every fixed time.
