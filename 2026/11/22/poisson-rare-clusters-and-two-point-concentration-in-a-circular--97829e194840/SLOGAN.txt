In the undirected nearest-neighbor graph of uniform points on a circle, the number of components of size at least \(k\) has a Poisson limit whenever \(nk2^k/(k+2)!\) approaches a finite value, with an explicit dependency-neighborhood error bound. The largest component is asymptotically confined to two adjacent integers at scale \(\log n/\log\log n\); the previously known distribution of individual peak-to-peak gaps is credited rather than claimed as new.
