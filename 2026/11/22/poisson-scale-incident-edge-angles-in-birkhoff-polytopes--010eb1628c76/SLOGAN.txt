For the standard Birkhoff polytope, two independent uniformly selected edges incident to a common vertex meet at an angle converging to \(\pi/3\), with the sharp non-Gaussian fluctuation law \(n(\Theta_n-\pi/3)\Rightarrow(S+T-2M)/(2\sqrt3)\) for three independent unit-Poisson variables. The exact all-dimensional edge-angle formula and a conditional cycle-overlap argument explain the universal discrete correction.
