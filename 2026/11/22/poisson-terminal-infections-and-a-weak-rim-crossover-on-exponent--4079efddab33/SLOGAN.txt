In irreversible exponential SI spreading from the hub of a wheel, the number of final susceptible rim sites becomes Poisson, with an explicit Gumbel completion-time shift \(2\beta/\alpha\) and rate \(\alpha+2\beta\) for fixed rim-contact rate \(\beta>0\). Even a rim-contact rate of order \(1/\log n\) creates a nontrivial finite Gumbel shift relative to the star network, with a sharp crossover controlled by \(\beta_n\log n\): when this quantity diverges while \(\beta_n\to0\), the star-centered finishing time tends to minus infinity in probability.
