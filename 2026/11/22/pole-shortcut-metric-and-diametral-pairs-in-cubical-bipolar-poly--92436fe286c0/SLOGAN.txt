For the bipolar polytope associated with the face lattice of an \(n\)-cube, the complete shortest-path metric is the minimum of a coordinatewise path and a shortcut through the empty-face pole. Its \(3^n+1\)-vertex graph is self-centered (and irregular for \(n\ge2\)) with radius and diameter \(n+1\), and all diametral pairs and the entire distance distribution admit explicit finite-sum formulas.
