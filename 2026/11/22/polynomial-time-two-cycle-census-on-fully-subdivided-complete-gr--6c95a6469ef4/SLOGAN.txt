For the complete graph with every edge subdivided once, the exact number of primitive two-cycle orbits of parallel chip firing admits a four-index binomial formula, evaluable in \(O(n^4)\) arithmetic operations for every \(n\ge3\). A reciprocal-edge inclusion–exclusion sieve replaces an exponential cut enumeration, yielding initial counts \(100,12312,3759616,2764042240\).
