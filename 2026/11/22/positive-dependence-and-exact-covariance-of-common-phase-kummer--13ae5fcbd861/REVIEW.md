# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

For every integer \(d\ge3\), the centered carry is the signed combination of the three sawtooth functions of slopes \(j\), \(d-j\), and \(d\). Each Fourier mode has the same sign across all indices because the only potentially negative case occurs at frequencies divisible by \(d\). Parseval therefore gives nonnegative pair covariances. At frequency \(d\), every noncentral index has a strictly negative coefficient, yielding strict pairwise positivity. The standard gcd-square Bernoulli correlation yields the exact rational formula. Discontinuity sets have zero measure, and a finite exact checker agrees with direct integration on 7,139 pair instances; those tests are corroboration, not an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

The 2026 eligible papers by Wu (arXiv:2606.20940) and Guo et al. (arXiv:2606.22997) concern gcd properties of binomial coefficients, not this common-phase covariance matrix. The 2018 Lemke Oliver–Soundararajan sawtooth identity supplies an established formula used in the argument, not the new positivity statement. A later 2026 paper by Cui–Yang (arXiv:2611.25794) explicitly defines individual carry indicators and proves the half-measure property; its inspected statements and its analytic simultaneous prime-condition estimates do not imply the common-phase two-index sign theorem. The separately known central-index independence classification is credited and not reasserted as new. Searches did not identify full coverage, but search absence does not establish priority; undiscovered implicit treatments remain possible.

## Value — PASS

Simultaneous local carry constraints are natural when comparing divisibility of binomial coefficients at different lower-index ratios. The exact full pairwise covariance law, especially the strict positivity away from one exceptional central coordinate, replaces a potentially misleading independent-coordinate heuristic with a uniform structural dependence law. The gcd kernel is a closed arithmetic expression for every ratio pair rather than a special finite case. It is a natural complete pairwise classification even without a higher-order theorem or a new integer-density asymptotic.

## Boundaries and priority risk

This result is limited to one shared Haar-uniform real phase and pairwise moments; it does not imply positive association of the full vector or an arithmetic sampling theorem. The classical Bernoulli identity is acknowledged, and an unindexed predecessor expressing the same sign theorem is a residual risk. A genuinely independent expert audit has not been performed.
