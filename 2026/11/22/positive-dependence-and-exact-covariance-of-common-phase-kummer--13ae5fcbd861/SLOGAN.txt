For the common-phase Kummer carry indicators \(F_j(X)=\lfloor dX\rfloor-\lfloor jX\rfloor-\lfloor(d-j)X\rfloor\), every pair has an explicit rational covariance given by an eight-term gcd-square formula. All pairs are nonnegatively correlated and every pair avoiding the central index \(d/2\) is strictly positively correlated, uniformly for all integer \(d\ge3\).
