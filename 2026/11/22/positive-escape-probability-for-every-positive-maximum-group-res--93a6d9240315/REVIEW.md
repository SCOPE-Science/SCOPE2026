# Scientific assessment of the quantitative statement

**Correctness.** The free two-particle transform is a valid upper bound for the continuation term of the killed process. Its explicit gap from one throughout the interval of trial exponents supplies the quantitative estimate. Combining it with the lower bound from first-cycle capture proves the logarithmic rate. The finite checker is diagnostic only.

**Distinction from related results.** The related inward-drift threshold already covers positive escape at zero drift. Its existential exponential estimate does not identify the sharp distance exponent. The quantitative claim uses the explicit contraction across the full subcritical range and the one-particle lower bound. The source comparisons are limited to inspected statements and do not establish historical priority.

**Value and limits.** The estimate fixes the scale of rare capture at large initial distance, even though an unlimited number of cycles can contribute. The prefactor in the bound need not be sharp, and neither the exact hitting-time law nor results under confinement follow from it.
