# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The Laplace–Beltrami product formula and round-sphere Bochner identity give a quadratic polynomial of the Laplacian for the complete two-jet bilinear form. Direct algebra factors it with zeros precisely at harmonic degrees \(2\ell-2\) and \(2\ell\). The remaining even-degree multipliers are strictly positive. The product-span converse is proved using a nonvanishing shifted-Jacobi coefficient for the harmonic pure power \((x_1+\mathrm i x_2)^\ell\), followed by rotation irreducibility. Coefficient positivity follows from the displayed rational formulas; a quadratic cannot annihilate three distinct upper degrees. The standalone checker independently evaluates both the icosahedral quartic and the 24-cell derivative-only quartic examples. No extrapolation from finite checks is used.

The inspected 2025 spherical-design sequel and derivative-dependent Sobolev cubature study likewise have different conclusions. Focused published-finding corpus searches and own-ledger claim comparisons did not identify a covering statement. An unindexed classical differential cubature formula remains a genuine priority risk.

## Value — PASS

Positive cubature of quartic harmonics at the twelve icosahedral vertices is an exact example requiring only a spherical four-design instead of the eight-design needed for value-only multiplication, while preserving a Parseval identity and explicit positive derivative-channel weights. The general theorem reduces the required parity-design strength by four, proves this reduction maximal among isotropic two-jet channels, and gives a sharp dimension-degree curve at which pure derivative observations suffice; the 24-cell provides a concrete 25-dimensional zero-value-channel example. These are structurally motivated sampling and frame properties, not a single unmotivated numerical test.

## Limits

The theorem treats only exact-degree spherical harmonics, scalar isotropic zero/one/two covariant-jet channels, and positive probability node rules. It does not claim uniqueness among all imaginable derivative sensors or priority beyond the inspected literature.
