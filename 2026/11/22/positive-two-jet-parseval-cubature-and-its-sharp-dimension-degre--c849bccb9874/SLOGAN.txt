A unique value/gradient/covariant-Hessian combination turns every antipodally exact spherical \((2\ell-4)\)-design into an exact Parseval frame for pure degree-\(\ell\) harmonics. Its three energy coefficients are nonnegative exactly when \(d\le(\ell-2)^2\); on the equality curve, values are unnecessary, and at the icosahedron a positive two-jet frame recovers all quartic harmonics despite the failure of degree-eight cubature.
