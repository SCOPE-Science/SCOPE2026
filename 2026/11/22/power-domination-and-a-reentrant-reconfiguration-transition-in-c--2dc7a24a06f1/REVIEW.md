# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The direct forcing proof verifies both halves of the hub-or-every-petal characterization and the unhit-petal obstruction, using the stated petal-size lower bound. A disjoint set split proves the two polynomials. In the reconfiguration graph, the hub states form one connected component above budget one, hub-free size-\(k\) transversals are isolated at budget \(k\), and one added hub reconnects them at budget \(k+1\). The attached standalone checker enumerates every selected set for tested examples through 18 vertices, independently tests forcing, counts minimal sets, and constructs TAR components. Finite testing is corroboration, not the infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS, provisional priority.** Brimkov et al. (2018) supply the power-domination polynomial framework and a vertex-identification decomposition, while Anderson et al. (2022) treat Cartesian-product bounds. The complete primary HTML texts inspected do not contain this specific bouquet-prism classification. The closest available indexed full statements address crown-graph TAR nonmonotonicity or clique-by-path low-budget power domination, neither implying this exact component sequence. Prior art includes nonmonotone TAR transitions in other graph families. Accessible abstract-only general reconfiguration work and unindexed literature remain risks; this review does not certify literature priority.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** These two-connected graphs have complete power-dominating and minimal-set enumerators with arbitrary gaps in minimal cardinalities. The exact reentrant budget threshold and exponential number of isolated configurations at one budget give a substantive natural structural phenomenon, rather than a mere minimum-number recalculation.

**Limitations.** Petal cliques must have order at least two, there must be at least two petals, and ordinary power domination with single-token addition/deletion is assumed. The exact all-order proof is elementary and accompanied by finite verification; independent review has not occurred.
