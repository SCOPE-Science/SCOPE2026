In the ternary Hamming three-cube \(H(3,3)\), exactly \(1296\) triples achieve minimum power domination, partitioning into three symmetry types determined by pairwise distances. Unrestricted sensor replacements have diameter \(4\), while adjacency-restricted slides have diameter \(6\); the maximum extra cost for the same endpoints is sharply \(3\) slides, attained for exactly \(41148\) unordered pairs.
