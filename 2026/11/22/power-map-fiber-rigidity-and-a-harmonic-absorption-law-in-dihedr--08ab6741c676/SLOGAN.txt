In each finite dihedral \(0\)-Hecke monoid, every power map has only singleton fibers except at the absorbing longest element, whose exact root counts exhibit a mod-four oscillation. For a uniform nonidempotent, absorption time converges to the heavy-tailed law \(\Pr(T=k)=1/[k(k-1)]\), while its finite mean grows as \(\log m+O(1)\).
