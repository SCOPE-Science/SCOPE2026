For every real \(s>1\), the Möbius-weighted power-GCD quadratic form has a strictly positive explicit leading constant at scale \(N^{s-1}\), with a power-saving error in three parameter ranges. The normalized constant tends to \(1/(\zeta(2)(s-1))\) as \(s\to\infty\); the established \(s=2\) theorem is recovered exactly.
