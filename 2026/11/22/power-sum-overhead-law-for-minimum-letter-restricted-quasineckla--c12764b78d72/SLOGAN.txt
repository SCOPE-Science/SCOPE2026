For quasinecklaces over \(\{0,1,\ldots,s\}\) with a fixed count \(r\) of zeroes, an exact two-variable enumerator gives the sharp asymptotic candidate-to-necklace ratio \(\frac{r}{s^r}\sum_{t=1}^s t^{r-1}\) as the number of nonzero letters grows. At each fixed nonbinary alphabet size, this ratio increases like \(r/s\) when the prescribed zero count grows, despite the global unrestricted constant-overhead theorem.
