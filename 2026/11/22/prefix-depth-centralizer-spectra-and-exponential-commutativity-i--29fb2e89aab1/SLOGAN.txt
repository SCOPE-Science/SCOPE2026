For finite depth-bounded degree-zero inverse submonoids of the polycyclic monoid, the exact element-centralizer spectrum is controlled by the level and longest common prefix of the two words. Their commuting probability approaches one, with the sharp deficit \(\frac{2(r-1)(r+1)^3}{r(r^3-1)}r^{-N}+o(r^{-N})\) for an \(r\)-letter alphabet and depth \(N\).
