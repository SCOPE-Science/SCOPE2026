For the two-step Lie algebra of every complete bipartite graph \(K_{a,b}\), the fiber of a prescribed bracket is determined exactly by the target matrix rank: it has one of three explicit nonzero sizes for ranks zero, one and two, and is empty above rank two. Every derived element of matrix rank \(r\) needs exactly \(\lceil r/2\rceil\) brackets, so the commutator width is \(\lceil\min(a,b)/2\rceil\) over every field.
