Every prescribed nonzero commutator in a finite rectangular-band semigroup algebra has an exact fiber size determined solely by its coefficient-matrix rank and which of its row- and column-sum vectors vanish. The rank-two image has a sharp missing stratum: targets with exactly one of those two sums zero are never commutators, while admissible rank-two fiber sizes are governed by \(|\mathrm{GL}_2(q)|\) and \(|\mathrm{SL}_2(q)|\).
