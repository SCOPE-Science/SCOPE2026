# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The target idempotents are exactly partial identities. The proof classifies their square roots by disjoint moving domains and images in independent fixed-point gaps, yielding the exact central-trinomial product. The second claim is justified by the analytic renewal generating series for root-fiber logarithms under uniform target idempotents and a simple-pole quasi-powers argument. The differentiation of the renewal identity gives explicit positive linear variance. The independent exhaustive checker covers all targets on chains of sizes zero through nine, with coefficient and moment-recurrence checks. Enumerations supplement, but do not replace, the all-dimensional and asymptotic proofs.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The primary 2016 Alvarez square-root paper covers existence through seven points, not the complete prescribed-root fibers or the uniform-target logarithmic limit. The closely related published extensive-only Motzkin theorem has a different gap count and sampling law. Crucially, the exact **aggregate** bivariate numerical sequence is **known** as OEIS A111960 and its row sums and scalar asymptotic as A111961: those are not claimed new. The contribution is the **individual positional fibers and their log-fiber Gaussian law**, not the numeric table. Laradji–Umar (2015) is a material priority risk because its same-monoid full text was not accessible; this limits novelty assurance but the available abstract does not provide a decisive stronger coverage comparison.

## Value — PASS
Prescribed square-root multiplicities depend on idempotent position even at fixed rank (19 versus 9 among rank-one targets in degree five). The explicit Gaussian law quantifies typical target behavior for every size and distinguishes it from the larger exponential rate of the average fiber. This resolves a natural power-map fiber statistic, not an arbitrary numerical slice, and the new logarithmic target statistic is not merely the previously tabulated row-sum sequence.
