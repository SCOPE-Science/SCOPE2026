# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

If f²=e_S, strict increase excludes nontrivial 2-cycles, so f fixes S and no other point. A moving image lies outside the domain, and strict increase at points of S confines moving arrows to individual gaps. Within a gap, every pair of disjoint, equicardinal domain and image subsets specifies a unique increasing bijection. This gives the multinomial sum T_m and the product, including n=0, empty S, and zero gaps. The probability generating calculation uses 2^{-n} times [z^n] of B_t(z)/(1-z B_t(z)), where B_t(z)=sum T_m^t z^m and t is the log-fiber moment parameter. On a fixed circle 1/2<R<1 the series is analytic for small complex t, by T_m<=3^m. At t=0 the denominator has one simple zero inside R; uniform perturbation and residue subtraction produce the stated analytic exponential approximation. The first two derivatives of the pole equation are the stated constants since the mean geometric block length is 2; the second derivative is positive because log T_m is not proportional to m+1. Cauchy estimates give the O(1) cumulant errors and the Taylor expansion at t=O(n^{-1/2}) proves the CLT. At t=1 the unnormalized generating function has a simple pole at 1/(1+sqrt(5)), with coefficient 1/sqrt(5) in the normalized principal part, and the branch points lie farther away. Dividing coefficients by 2^n proves the arithmetic mean; its rate is strictly greater than the typical rate. An independent replay composed all 184756 maps at n=10 and checked all 1024 targets (58558 roots in total), also checking n=0. The constants computed by repeated trinomial convolution at cutoff 120 are 0.252931378211030114406007 and 0.304190135280173835045576, with analytic truncation/substitution error bounds below 2.53e-35 and 3.61e-33, respectively. These bounded checks corroborate the proof, not the convergence by themselves.

## originality

PASS

The supported distinct claim is the individual positional fiber and its uniform-target logarithmic law, with explicit constants and bounded cumulant errors. No novelty is credited to the central trinomial sequence, aggregate coefficient triangle, arithmetic-mean asymptotic, or general analytic/renewal CLT methods. The strongest exact-scope sources actually read either give existence in restricted dimensions, aggregate rather than individual data, or count a different class of maps under a different sampling law. The 2015 enumerative paper's unresolved full-text comparison is a real access risk, not proof of absence or a reason alone to fail.

## value

PASS

The exact gap rule distinguishes same-rank idempotents that rank counts cannot distinguish, and makes it possible to separate typical multiplicity from arithmetic average in a natural monoid. The explicit fluctuation scale and variance concern uniformly chosen targets rather than roots and are usable without enumerating partial maps. This is a substantive application to a prescribed-target statistic, not a claim of new renewal theory or a restatement of a numerical table.
