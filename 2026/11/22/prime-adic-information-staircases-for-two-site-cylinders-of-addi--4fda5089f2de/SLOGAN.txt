Two pinned coordinates of a surjective additive cellular automaton over \(\mathbb Z/p^r\mathbb Z\) leave exactly \(e(t)\log p\) nats of recoverable pair information at time \(t\), where \(e(t)\) descends in an explicit prime-power staircase. Every two-site cylinder becomes the full shift for the first time at \(t=p^{\nu_p(d)}+1\), independently of the alphabet exponent.
