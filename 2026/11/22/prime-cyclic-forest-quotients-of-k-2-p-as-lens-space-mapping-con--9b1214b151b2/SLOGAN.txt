For every odd prime \(p\), cyclically rotating the leaves of the graphic-matroid forest complex of \(K_{2,p}\) produces the mapping cone of the suspended degree-\(p\) lens-space covering. This simply connected quotient has integral \(\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z\) homology in every even dimension from \(2\) to \(p-1\), even though the covering complex is a wedge of spheres.
