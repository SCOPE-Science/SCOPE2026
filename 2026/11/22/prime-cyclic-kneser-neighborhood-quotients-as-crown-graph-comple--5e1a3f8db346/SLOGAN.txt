For every odd prime \(p\ge7\), the cyclic quotient of the Lovász neighborhood complex of \(\operatorname{KG}(p,2)\) has a concrete homotopy model as a weighted lens space minus a two-vertex, \(p-1\)-edge embedded voltage graph. Its fundamental group is \(C_p\), it carries order-\(p\) homology in every odd degree below \(p-4\), and its exact mod-\(p\) cup-length is \((p-3)/2\).
