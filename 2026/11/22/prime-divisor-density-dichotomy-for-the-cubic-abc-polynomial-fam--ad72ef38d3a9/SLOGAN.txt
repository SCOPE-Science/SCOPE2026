For every fixed integer \(u\), the proportion of primes dividing at least one value of \(X(X-1)(X-u)+1\) is exactly \(2/3\), except at \(u=-4\) and \(u=3\), where it is \(1/3\). These two exceptional cubics generate the same cyclic conductor-seven field; the full zero-, one-, and three-root prime-density spectra are determined, with identical good-prime-power lift counts.
