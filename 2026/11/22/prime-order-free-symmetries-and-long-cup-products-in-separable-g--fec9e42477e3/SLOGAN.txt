The cyclic relabeling action on the simplicial complex of non-two-connected labeled graphs is geometrically free exactly for prime numbers of labels. For every prime \(p\ge5\), the quotient has \(\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z\) homology in every positive odd degree below \(2p-5\), and its canonical degree-two integral cohomology class has a nonzero \((p-3)\)-fold product, giving unbounded cup-length and a corresponding category lower bound.
