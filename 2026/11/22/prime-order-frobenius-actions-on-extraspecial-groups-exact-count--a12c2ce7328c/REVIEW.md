The result is an evaluated all-rank census with a sharp exponent-type obstruction; earlier unindexed conjugacy calculations are a residual priority risk, not proof of novelty by failed search alone.

This is the full parameter-uniform spectrum and conjugacy-type census of natural odd-prime cyclic Frobenius complements on **all** extraspecial groups. It makes a sharp distinction between exponent \(p\) and exponent \(p^2\), gives exact finite-group enumeration without enumeration of \(\operatorname{Aut}(G)\), and identifies explicit symplectic and general linear stabilizers that control the finite counts. The uniform rank-one and ternary formulas provide immediate tests and the full formula supports further construction of Frobenius semidirect products.
