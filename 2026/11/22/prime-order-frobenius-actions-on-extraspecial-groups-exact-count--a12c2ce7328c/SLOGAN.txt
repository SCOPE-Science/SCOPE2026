For extraspecial \(p\)-groups, the exact count and conjugacy-class spectrum of fixed-point-free automorphisms of odd prime order \(\ell\) is a symplectic/linear-group composition sum whenever \(\ell\mid p-1\) and the exponent is \(p\), and is zero otherwise. Extraspecial groups of exponent \(p^2\) admit no fixed-point-free automorphism of any order.
