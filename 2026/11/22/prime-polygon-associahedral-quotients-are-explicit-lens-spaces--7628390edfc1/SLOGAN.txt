For every prime \(p\ge7\), the cyclic rotation quotient of the noncrossing-diagonal complex of a regular \(p\)-gon is homeomorphic to the explicit lens space \(L(p;2,3,\ldots,(p-1)/2)\), with \(p\)-torsion in every odd homology degree below the top. Among all polygons with at least four vertices, the rotation action on the geometric complex is free exactly at prime numbers of vertices.
