The source-to-claim chain is the regular-polygon secondary-polytope theorem, the three exact affine moment constraints, equivariant polar duality and radial projection, then discrete Fourier decomposition and standard linear lens-space topology.

The standalone `verify_associahedral_lens.py` script checks all triangulations for \(n=5,7,11\) (counts 5, 42, and 4862), their rotation stabilizers, and the exact Fourier weight/fixed-subspace conditions for prime and representative composite polygons. The recorded output in `finite_check_output.txt` is reproducible with `python3 verify_associahedral_lens.py` using only the standard library.

The finite cases are consistency tests, not an exhaustive verification of all polygons. The general claim relies on the cited dimension and face-lattice theorem for secondary polytopes. No exact smooth-manifold structure beyond the claimed homeomorphism to a standard lens space, and no classification of composite-order orbit-space homology, is asserted. No independent external audit has been performed.
