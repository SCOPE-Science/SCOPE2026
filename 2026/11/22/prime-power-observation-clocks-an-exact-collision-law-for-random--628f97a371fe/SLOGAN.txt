For the difference cellular automaton over \(\mathbb F_p\), a sparse observed cylinder fills the entire shift at Frobenius clock \(\ell p^a\) exactly when no residue class modulo \(p^a\) contains more than \(\ell\) observed coordinates. Uniformly sampling the observed sites yields an exact finite-size rank-deficiency distribution and sharp Poisson crossover at observation count of order \(p^{a\ell/(\ell+1)}\).
