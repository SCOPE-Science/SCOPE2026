Maximal runs of consecutive integers coprime to \(n\), centered at an irrational polynomial floor, have a universal positive density \(\delta_r\) for every fixed length \(r\). The density is asymptotic to \(e^{-\gamma}/(r^2\log r)\) when \(r+1\) is prime, but only \(e^{-\gamma}/(r^2(\log r)^3)\) when \(r+1\) is composite: an exact prime-resonance enhancement by two logarithms.
