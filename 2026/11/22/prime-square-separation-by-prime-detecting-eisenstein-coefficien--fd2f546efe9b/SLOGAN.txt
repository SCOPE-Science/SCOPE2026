For every pair of positive odd exponents \(r<s\), the Fourier coefficients \(A_{r,s}(n)=(1+n^s)\sigma_r(n)-(1+n^r)\sigma_s(n)\) distinguish primes (zero), prime squares (strictly between zero and \(n^{s+r/2}\)), and all remaining composites (strictly above that threshold). The exact pointwise composite lower envelope is attained precisely at prime squares, giving a quantitative refinement of the partition-theoretic prime detector.
