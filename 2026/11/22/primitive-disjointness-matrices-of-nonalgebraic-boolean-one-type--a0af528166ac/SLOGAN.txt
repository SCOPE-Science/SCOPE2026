In the countable atomless Boolean algebra, the existential disjointness matrix on complete nonalgebraic one-types over any finite named subalgebra with \(m\ge1\) atoms is unimodular of order \(3^m-2^m\), despite deleting all \(2^m\) algebraic types from a tensor-product matrix. Its real signature is always exactly one, and it has full rank over every field.
