Among indexed primitive binary necklaces of a fixed weight, every coordinate support below the uniform-layer VC threshold remains shattered, and the only missing top faces are periodic subsets. The resulting pure shattering complex is a wedge of \(A(n,k)-\binom{n-1}{r-1}\) spheres of dimension \(r-1\), where \(r=\min(k,n-k)\) and \(A(n,k)\) is the classical Möbius count of labeled primitive words.
