Deleting parallel line vertices one at a time from an affine-plane incidence graph produces a primitive integral radius-two Rips erosion: each deletion loses exactly \(q-1\) degree-two graph-cycle classes, while intermediate steps remove \((q-1)^q\) degree-\(q\) classes. Removing the final line changes the two pure shores simultaneously and creates \(2(q-1)^q\) classes in degree \(q-1\), with a precisely controlled rank-sixteen kernel when \(q=3\).
