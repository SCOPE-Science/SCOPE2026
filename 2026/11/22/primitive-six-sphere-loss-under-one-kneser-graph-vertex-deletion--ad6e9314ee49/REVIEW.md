# Scientific review: a punctured Kneser neighborhood complex

## Correctness
PASS. The graph and open-neighborhood definition fix the domain and distinguish deleting a graph vertex from deleting a vertex of the simplicial complex. The embedded Python-standard-library verifier exhausts both face posets, checks 492 elementary simplicial collapses, eliminates 41 and 31 nonabelian fundamental-group generators by valid Tietze moves, and cancels 99 integral unit boundary pivots in the relative pair. Its final checks give two simply connected two-complexes with Euler characteristics 20 and 14, and relative integral homology \(\mathbb Z^6\) in degree two alone. Hurewicz, Whitehead, and the long exact sequence then establish both homotopy types and the primitive injection. Scientific risk: the finite certificate is self-review, not externally audited.
