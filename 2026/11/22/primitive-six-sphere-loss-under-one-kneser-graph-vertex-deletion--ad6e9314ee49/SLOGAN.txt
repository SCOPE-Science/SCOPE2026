Deleting any single vertex from the fifteen-vertex Kneser graph \(\mathrm{KG}(6,2)\) changes its Lovász open-neighborhood complex from \(\bigvee^{19}S^2\) to \(\bigvee^{13}S^2\). The natural integral second-homology inclusion is primitive with free cokernel \(\mathbb Z^6\), giving an exact six-class robustness loss rather than just a comparison of Betti numbers.
