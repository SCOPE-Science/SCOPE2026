For a nonzero two-scale difference cellular automaton \((I-S^a)(I-S^b)\) on a finite cyclic ring of length coprime to the field characteristic, some completely aperiodic configuration becomes zero in one update exactly when the two stride periods jointly generate the ring length. The exact number of such configurations is a divisor-Möbius formula, and nontrivial one-step erasure is possible precisely at ring lengths with at least two distinct prime divisors.
