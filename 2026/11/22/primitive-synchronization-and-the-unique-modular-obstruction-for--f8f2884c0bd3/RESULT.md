# Primitive synchronization and the unique modular obstruction for nine-state circular defect-two automata

## Finding

Let \(Q=\mathbb Z/9\mathbb Z\), let \(a(i)=i+1\pmod 9\), and let \(b:Q\to Q\) be any transformation of rank exactly \(7\). The deterministic binary automaton generated by \(a\) and \(b\) is **not synchronizing if and only if**
\[
b(i)\equiv b(i+3)\equiv b(i+6)\pmod 3\qquad(i=0,1,2).
\]
Equivalently, nonsynchronization holds precisely when the transition monoid preserves the congruence of states modulo \(3\). Consequently, every *primitive* automaton in this family is synchronizing.

Among all \(83{,}825{,}280\) labeled rank-seven choices for \(b\), exactly \(36{,}936\) are nonsynchronizing and exactly \(83{,}788{,}344\) synchronize. The nonsynchronizing transformations split as \(1{,}944\) with a single three-state kernel fiber and \(34{,}992\) with two two-state fibers. Under simultaneous cyclic relabeling, all orbits have size \(9\): there are \(9{,}313{,}920\) classes in total, of which precisely \(4{,}104\) are nonsynchronizing.

## Assumptions and scope

A transformation's rank is its number of distinct images. A word *resets* an automaton if its action on the full state set is a singleton. A congruence is an equivalence relation preserved by each transition; *primitive* means there is no nontrivial congruence (equivalence is neither equality nor universal). States are labeled \(0,1,\ldots,8\), and the cycle letter \(a\) is fixed. There are no restrictions on the rank-seven map \(b\) beyond its rank. Counts refer to every labeled \(b\), not to randomly sampled or connected subclasses; cyclic equivalence means conjugation by powers of \(a\), not arbitrary state permutations or alphabet exchange. No assertion is made for any other state count or rank.

## Proof

**Step 1: possible congruences.** A congruence preserved by the full cycle \(a\) is necessarily a subgroup congruence on \(\mathbb Z/9\mathbb Z\). The only proper nontrivial possibility is equality of residues modulo \(3\). It is preserved by \(b\) exactly when the three displayed congruences hold. Because \(b\) has rank \(7\), its three input residue classes must have images in three *different* output residue classes: otherwise the full image occupies at most six states. Hence \(b\) induces a permutation of the three quotient states. The letter \(a\) also induces a permutation of them. Any word consequently permutes these three classes and cannot send all nine states to one. This proves that every listed congruence-preserving map is nonsynchronizing and imprimitive.

**Step 2: compressibility reduces to four distances.** A finite deterministic automaton is synchronizing precisely when each unordered pair of distinct states can be merged by some word. Indeed, merge any two states of a current image and repeat, lowering the image cardinality at each step. For a fixed cyclic distance \(d\in\{1,2,3,4\}\), let
\[
T_d=\left\{\operatorname{dist}_9(b(i),b(i+d)):i\in\mathbb Z/9\mathbb Z\right\},
\qquad \operatorname{dist}_9(x,y)=\min(|x-y|,9-|x-y|).
\]
Here the value \(0\) records a merged pair. Form a directed graph on distance vertices \(0,1,2,3,4\), with arcs \(d\to t\) for \(t\in T_d\); the vertex \(0\) is absorbing. Rotation by \(a\) moves any pair of distance \(d\) to any desired pair with that same unoriented distance. Thus the automaton synchronizes **if and only if every distance vertex \(1,2,3,4\) has a directed path to \(0\)**. A five-vertex backward-reachability search is exact, not a heuristic. The complete implementation is `distance_census.cpp`.

**Step 3: exhaustive converse with independent encodings.** Each rank-seven map has one of two kernel shapes: a single fiber of size three, or two fibers of size two. There are respectively \(\binom93=84\) and \(\binom92\binom72/2=378\) possible partitions into seven fibers. Every partition admits \(9!/2!=181{,}440\) distinct injections of seven image labels. Consequently the total is \((84+378)181{,}440=83{,}825{,}280\). The program `pair_census.cpp` loops independently over these disjoint partitions and injective assignments, and, unlike Step 2's quotient construction, builds the full \(36\)-pair automaton using the two letter transitions, closes backward all pairs reaching a collision and tests the modulo-three congruence separately. It yields **zero** primitive nonsynchronizing maps and finds exactly \(36{,}936\) nonsynchronizing maps. The independently organized `distance_census.cpp` enumerates all \(9^9\) maps in base nine, filters to rank seven, and tests the four distance vertices. The two exhaustive answers coincide, proving the missing implication for all rank-seven transformations, not only selected samples. The directly calculated shape-specific totals are \(1{,}944\) and \(34{,}992\) nonsynchronizing maps.

**Step 4: independent counting formula.** Fix a permutation of the three residue classes induced by \(b\). For a source class of three points, the number of functions onto a prescribed number \(j=1,2,3\) of points of its three-point target class is respectively \(f_1=3\), \(f_2=18\), and \(f_3=6\). Total rank \(7\) forces the three local ranks to have profile \((1,3,3)\) or \((2,2,3)\), in any order. Counting the three profile positions and the six quotient permutations gives
\[
6\bigl(3f_1f_3^2+3f_2^2f_3\bigr)
=6(324+5832)=36{,}936.
\]
The first summand gives \(1{,}944\), the second \(34{,}992\), independently verifying the kernel-shape split. Subtraction yields the synchronizing count.

**Step 5: cyclic orbit sizes.** If conjugating \(b\) by a nonidentity power of \(a\) fixed \(b\), its image would be invariant under that power of the nine-cycle. Nontrivial powers have orbits of length \(3\) or \(9\), so any invariant image has cardinality divisible by \(3\), contradicting rank \(7\). Hence all conjugacy orbits under the cyclic group have size nine. Divide the two labeled counts by nine to obtain the stated class counts.

## Verification

Compile `distance_census.cpp` and `pair_census.cpp` using a C++17 compiler with optimization, and execute both. The first enumerates rank-seven maps by their base-nine transition tables and outputs \(83{,}825{,}280\) total, \(36{,}936\) nonsynchronizing, and zero primitive nonsynchronizing. The second independently enumerates the two kernel types and constructs the full reverse pair graph, reproducing every number and their split. Run `subset_crosscheck.py` with Python 3: it independently compares full subset-automaton breadth-first search against the reduced distance criterion for every rank-defect-two transformation on \(4\), \(5\), and \(6\) states, and checks \(1{,}500\) synchronizing and \(1{,}500\) nonsynchronizing nine-state examples. The script separately checks the analytical formula. The exhaustive nine-state proof is provided by the complete enumeration programs, not by these sampled checks alone.

## Relationship to prior work

Volkov's 2023 paper on synchronization of primitive automata establishes general sufficient conditions, including a result for an almost injective letter with a single nontrivial kernel fiber; this covers the single-three-fiber subfamily but does not provide the nine-state double-two-fiber converse or the full count. Rystsov and Szykuła's 2023 study distinguishes primitivity from synchronization and supplies examples showing the implication fails for some automata. Their prime-cycle condition does not directly settle the first odd-composite cyclic order. The present result pins down all rank-seven letters at order nine, including the two-pair kernel shape, by a complete finite determination. It is distinct from published eight-state rank-seven reset-threshold enumerations and from formulas for one specially specified two-contraction letter, whose hypotheses or state counts do not yield the universal classification above.

## Limitations

This is an exact finite theorem at nine states, not a general conjecture or theorem for all circular automata. Exhaustive verification relies on the completeness of finite iteration and the stated independent implementations; it is not a formal proof-assistant certificate. Source searches do not establish absolute novelty: an unindexed equivalent statement or a more general result proving the same rank-seven nine-state criterion remains possible. The source publications concern broader and different automata theorems; they are not cited as independently certifying the new census.
