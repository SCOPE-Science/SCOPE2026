# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The result is an exact theorem for every finite commutative local ring of size \(T\), residue cardinality \(q\), and \(n\ge2\), restricted to *primitive* transvections. Expanding the braid equation gives \((1+t)(N-M)=0\), where \(t=\phi(v)\psi(u)\). Locality yields the exhaustive alternatives \(N=M\) or \(t=-1\). Both yield explicit conjugacy normal forms and concrete stabilizers. The direct-product complement in the second case is justified by invertibility of the two-by-two covector-pairing matrix and freeness of finitely generated projective modules over local rings. Reduction of general linear groups and orbit–stabilizer produce exactly the claimed counts and constant lift multiplicities. No finite test is used to infer infinite validity. Full matrix-composition enumeration matches the counts for ten checked finite-ring models, including three nonisomorphic rings with the same \((q,T)\) and a dimension-three dual-number test. The proof does not classify all braid representations.

## Originality — PASS (historical-priority risk remains)
Tuba–Wenzl (arXiv:math/9912013, initial version 1999-12-02), Proposition 2.5, gives the fundamental two-dimensional irreducible field representation. That known normal form is explicitly credited. Humphries, *Representations of Braid Groups via Determinantal Rings*, introduction and §2, uses transvections over commutative rings, their pairwise trace-coordinate structure, and braid actions; those methods and objects are also credited. Neither inspected source derives the exact two-orbit total, stabilizer sizes, and separate uniform lift degrees for arbitrary finite nonprincipal and mixed-characteristic local rings and arbitrary ambient dimension. Formanek's earlier complex-representation work was examined at abstract level only, and may encode related field cases. Searches of indexed mathematical records for the exact ring counts and equivalences found nearby braid-action and transvection results, but none covering these ring-theoretic enumerative statements. An undiscovered historical corollary remains possible; this review is not an independent priority certificate.

## Value — PASS
The braid equation on elementary unipotent generators is a natural representation-counting problem, and finite local rings are the natural arithmetic thickenings of residue-field representations. The result is a complete, nontrivial exact count for this structurally motivated family, with a uniform lifting theorem that distinguishes the diagonal and nontrivial representation strata by different powers of the residue kernel. It establishes independence of the internal multiplication structure of a finite local ring, including a nonprincipal/mixed-characteristic comparison. The value rests on the exact natural classification and arithmetic lifting profile, not on claiming the known two-by-two braid matrices as new or requiring an additional downstream theorem.
