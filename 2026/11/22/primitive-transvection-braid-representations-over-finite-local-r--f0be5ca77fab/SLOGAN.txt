Primitive-transvection solutions of the three-strand braid relation over every finite commutative local ring form exactly two simultaneous-conjugacy orbits. In dimension \(n\ge2\), if the ring has \(T\) elements and residue field of order \(q\), there are \(P_n(R)(1+T^{2n-3})\) ordered solutions, where \(P_n(R)=(T/q)^{2n-2}(q^n-1)(q^{n-1}-1)/(q-1)\); every field solution has an exact ring-independent lifting multiplicity on its orbit.
