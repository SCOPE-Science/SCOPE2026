# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The theorem has an exact domain of integer pairs \(m>n\ge2\). The support-separation argument uses singleton matrix units and the fact that each nonzero coefficient is an \(n\)-th root of unity. The equivalence-class criterion combines two marginal insertions with the published margin classification; the principal-generation proof explicitly transports a primitive phase from a single endpoint-supported rank-\(n\) projection to every smaller support. The maximal idempotent-separation argument uses conjugates of singleton idempotents. All binomial counts distinguish zero from nonzero supports. Exact code checked the claim and principal congruence closure in seven finite configurations. Computations corroborate rather than replace the universal proof.

## Originality

PASS, qualified. The motivating paper establishes the semigroup, marginal formula, and a strict inequality between marginal equivalence and its generated congruence in the nonnormal regime; it does not state the full congruence or rank-wise equivalence classes. An unrestricted partial-monomial result has a normal margin for odd exponents and is not equivalent to this restricted increasing example. General congruence theory describes idempotent-separating congruences without selecting them through this power-word marginal relation. The newly assessed claim is the explicit principal saturation and complete class spectrum for \(m>n\). It depends closely on the motivating paper, and this short derivation remains a priority risk.

## Mathematical value

PASS. The central question in the motivating work is whether a marginal relation itself is a congruence. Its finite counterexamples leave a natural next question: what identifications appear in the congruence generated by that failed relation? The theorem answers this throughout the whole nonnormal two-parameter family, gives one minimal generator, identifies the canonical quotient, and measures exactly how much the unclosed relation differs. These are natural invariants of a specifically published construction, not an isolated parameter lookup.

## Literature and limitations

Compared in substance against Duan--Zhang, arXiv:2611.05078v1, especially Theorems 2.4 and 3.1; Araújo--Araújo--Kinyon (2025), Problem 11; Brookes, arXiv:2002.00891v1, on general inverse-semigroup congruences; and a published full partial-monomial result with different support/phase freedoms. Historical priority remains provisional, and no extension to other rank cutoffs or coefficient distributions is claimed. Independent verification of the mathematics has not been performed.
