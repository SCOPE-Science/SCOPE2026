In the nonnormal finite inverse semigroups of Duan--Zhang, the congruence generated by power-word marginal equivalence erases every phase label and is generated by a single marginal pair, although the marginal equivalence itself leaves most top-rank phase labels distinct. Its exact class spectrum gives a sharp limiting fraction \(1/n\) of marginally equivalent pairs among congruent pairs when the ambient degree grows with fixed exponent \(n\).
