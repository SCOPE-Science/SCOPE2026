The polar of the equilateral-triangle similarity-containment polytope in a regular \(n\)-gon has only tetrahedral and triangular-prism facets, with exactly \(n\) prisms or \(2n\) according as \(3\nmid n\) or \(3\mid n\). Its tetrahedra supported by edges from exactly two component regular polygons number \(3n\lfloor(n-1)/3\rfloor\).
