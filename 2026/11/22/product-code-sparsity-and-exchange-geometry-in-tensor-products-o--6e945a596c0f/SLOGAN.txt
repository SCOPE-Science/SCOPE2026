Tensor products of complete bipartite graphs have \(B^{2^{r-1}}\) total perfect codes but only \(B=\prod_i a_i b_i\) factorwise codes; all codes form an explicit connected Hamming-type exchange graph, while distinct factorwise codes require at least \(2^{r-1}\) exchanges. The factorwise codes are nevertheless within \(2^r-2\) exchanges of every code, with the bound sharp for sufficiently large shores and for every two-factor instance.
