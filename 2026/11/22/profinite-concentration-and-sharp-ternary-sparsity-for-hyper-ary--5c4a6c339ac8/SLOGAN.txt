For every integer base \(b\ge3\), restricted hyper-ary partition counts are divisible by each fixed modulus at almost every index, with an explicit exponential block-density bound and profinite concentration at zero. The exceptions modulo three have an exact rational level-count generating function and sharp growth \(\Theta_b(X^{\log_b\lambda_b})\), where \(\lambda_b\) is the Perron root of a natural four-state digit matrix.
