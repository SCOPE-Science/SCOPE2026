The target is precisely the degree-three central piece of a free class-three nilpotent Lie algebra over each finite field. Reduction to generator coordinates is exact because all length-four brackets vanish; the number of discarded coordinates is \(2(D-d)\) in an ordered input pair. The critical rank lemma is proved in the free Lie algebra's tensor embedding, with an explicit unit-coefficient tensor term that survives even in characteristics two and three. The critical intersection lemma uses a single \(\mathrm{GL}_d\)-change of basis and disjoint multidegree supports, not an unverified generic-rank assumption. These give an injective flag parametrization and the full exact image and three fiber strata. `verify.py` independently exhausts eight small-field configurations, including a nonprime field; its output is supplied. Finite tests do not establish the general theorem by themselves.
