The two-Engel map on every free class-three nilpotent Lie algebra over \(\mathbb F_q\) has nonzero projective image exactly parametrized by point–line flags, with images for distinct first-argument lines disjoint except at zero. Every nonzero attained target has exactly \(q^{2(D-d)+1}(q-1)\) ordered preimages; the complete zero and nonimage fibers follow from the same decomposition.
