For a particle alternating between diffusion and ballistic relocation under full Poisson restarting, the extreme-distance outward-versus-inward velocity polarization \(\mathcal P(r)=v\lambda_-/(r+\beta)\) is identical whether each reset restores diffusion or a symmetrically chosen ballistic phase. For every positive choice of model rates it has exactly one finite maximizing reset frequency, characterized by an admissible cubic root.
