Removing the identity from any finite rank-\(r\) join-prime coordinate subgroup of the simplicial-complex Grothendieck space reveals a signed-permutation-rigid order complex of homotopy type \(S^{r-1}\), with top integral orientation character \(\det\). Antipodal quotients model \(\mathbb{RP}^{r-1}\), while the order complex of the entire infinite-rank puncture is contractible.
