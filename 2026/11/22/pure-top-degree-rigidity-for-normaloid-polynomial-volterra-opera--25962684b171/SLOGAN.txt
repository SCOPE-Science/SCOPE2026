For the super-Gaussian power-Fock Hilbert space with exponent \(\alpha>2\), every nonzero bounded polynomial Volterra operator is normaloid, hyponormal or subnormal precisely when \(\alpha=d\ge3\) is an integer and its symbol is a pure degree-\(d\) monomial plus a constant. Any lower-degree perturbation creates an explicit positive two-monomial norm gap of order \(n^{q/d-1}\) over the unchanged spectral radius.
