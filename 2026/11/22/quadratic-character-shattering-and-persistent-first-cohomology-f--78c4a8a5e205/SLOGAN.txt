For the point-image membership concepts of \(\operatorname{PSL}_2(q)\) on the projective line in every odd prime power \(q\ge5\), shattered triples are exactly the square-determinant half of all three-rook matchings, giving an explicit complete VC polynomial and dimension three. Their shattering complexes carry a nonzero determinant-character class in \(H^1(-;\mathbb F_2)\) for every such \(q\), with exact first Betti number one at \(q=5,7\).
