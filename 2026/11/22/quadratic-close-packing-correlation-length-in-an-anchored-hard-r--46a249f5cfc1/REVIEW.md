# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The domain and limit order are explicit. The exact exponential similarity maps the nonselfadjoint hard-rod transfer operator to a killed centered walk with jumps \(\varepsilon(E-1)\). Total nonnegativity guarantees real nonnegative nonzero eigenvalues. The explicit gamma return density, conditioned-bridge functional central limit theorem, domination by the free diagonal, and the Brownian Dirichlet heat trace jointly prove convergence of spectral counting-measure Laplace transforms. Positivity and uniqueness identify both eigenvalues (rather than merely the leading one). All coefficients and normalizations follow algebraically. The independent discretization checker passed; it is not used as proof. Risks: the conditional bridge limit is a substantive standard probabilistic input, and spectral order would fail without the total-nonnegativity argument.

## Originality — PASS, with residual uncertainty

The source manuscript [1] establishes the transfer kernel, its finite-regime characteristic polynomials, the contact-transition hierarchy and the one-time close-packing positional profile. Its explicit spectral asymptotic addresses the low-density onset, not the close-packing leading two-eigenvalue constants and quadratic correlation law proved here. Published related hard-body papers consider different continuum or orientational systems; results about killed random-walk bridges give reusable methodology, not the stated hard-rod constant. Searches for the hard-rod kernel, equivalent Dirichlet-transformed formulations, exact constants and broader spectral coverage found no direct implication, but unindexed equivalent results remain possible.

## Value — PASS

The opposite endpoint from the source's low-density correlation onset controls collective positional order as close packing is approached. The exact \(2/(3\pi^2)\) coefficient and order \((\delta/d)^2\), together with the free-energy spectral correction, quantify a natural, measurable length scale. The exponential-conjugation representation also clarifies the mechanism by which a nonselfadjoint equilibrium transfer kernel has Brownian Dirichlet spectral asymptotics. This is not merely a numerical root table or an arbitrary parameter slice.

## Scope and limitations

Only fixed well width, one rod per site, one-dimensional nearest-neighbor exclusion, and thermodynamic limit before close packing. No finite-size dynamical interpretation, no universality claim over other confinement potentials. Publication priority still needs external literature review.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
