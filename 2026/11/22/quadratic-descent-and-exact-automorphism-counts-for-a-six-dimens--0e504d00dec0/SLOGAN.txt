For the six-dimensional centrally essential algebra family with radical square a line, binary quadratic Arf type controls both descent and the exact automorphism-group order: the automorphism map to the binary orthogonal-similitude group has uniform fiber size \(q^7\). The two distinct binary algebras merge over \(\mathbb F_{2^m}\) exactly when \(m\) is even, while their split and anisotropic automorphism counts are \(2q^7(q-1)^2\) and \(2q^7(q^2-1)\).
