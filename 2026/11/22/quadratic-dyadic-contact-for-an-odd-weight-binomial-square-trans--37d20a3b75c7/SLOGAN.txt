For every odd weight \(t\equiv3\pmod4\), the binomial-square transform \(F_n(t)=\sum_{j=0}^n\binom nj^2(2t)^j\) has exactly quadratic 2-adic contact with one: \(\nu_2(F_n(t)-1)=2\nu_2(n)+2\) whenever \(8\mid n>0\). The depth-eight threshold is sharp, while the ordinary Delannoy weight \(t=1\) has a previously known cubic contact instead.
