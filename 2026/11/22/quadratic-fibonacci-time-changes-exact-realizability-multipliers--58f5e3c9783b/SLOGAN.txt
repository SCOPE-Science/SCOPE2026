For every \(a\ge1\) and \(b,c\ge0\), the Fibonacci quadratic sample \(F_{an^2+bn+c}\) is realizable with least multiplier \(1\) when \(b=0,5\mid a\), with least multiplier \(5\) when \(b=0,5\nmid a\), and with no finite multiplier when \(b>0\). The obstruction at inert primes factors exactly as \(-F_bL_{a+c}\).
