The source generating function is explicitly verified against Li–Tang and Barman–Singh–Singh. Factorization through \(P(q)=E(q^2)/E(q)^2\) is exact, the divisor-parity reduction and inverse expansion are coefficientwise valid modulo four, and Euler's pentagonal theorem converts the prefactor into squares \((6t-1)^2\). In the prime case, the \(3\)- and \(12\)-norm representations pair exactly, while the Gaussian representation has a unique odd coordinate. Inertness at primes congruent to eleven modulo twelve excludes every contributing form at odd local valuation. The independent product/representation checker verifies all indices through 2000, all primes therein, and the inert-index subfamily. The finite checks do not prove the infinite statement; the displayed derivation does.

This turns a modular generating-function problem into a pointwise number-theoretic classification: infinitely many primes have a specified nonzero residue, the remaining primes have an explicit Gaussian-coordinate criterion, and a multiplicatively described family of composite indices has guaranteed vanishing. The exact norm reductions reveal arithmetic structure not supplied by earlier generic lacunarity, while making no novelty claim about the previously established density-one divisibility.
