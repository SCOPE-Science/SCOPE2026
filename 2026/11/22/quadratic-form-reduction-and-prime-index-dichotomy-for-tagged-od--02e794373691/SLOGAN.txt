The tagged-part total \(\mathrm{PDO}_{\mathrm t}(n)\) for odd-part designated partitions has an exact modulo-four expression in three quadratic representation counts and a pentagonal-square term. At every prime \(p\ge5\) it is \(2\pmod4\) precisely when the unique positive odd coordinate in \(p=a^2+b^2\) is not divisible by three; an odd-valuation prime factor congruent to \(11\pmod{12}\) forces vanishing modulo four at composite indices as well.
