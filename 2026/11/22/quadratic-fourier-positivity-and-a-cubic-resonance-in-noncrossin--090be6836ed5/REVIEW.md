## Correctness — PASS

The successor-arc embedding is injective. Catalan contraction gives the exact single-arc counts, and a double adjacent-arc contraction supplies the only required joint count. The three explicit inequalities prove positive low-degree multipliers for every \(n\ge5\). At six labels the triple-star character cancels by exact inclusion-exclusion; the integer-only checker exhausts the Boolean arc cube by two combinatorially distinct generators, recomputes all signed sums independently of the Walsh transform and verifies the cubic-zero census, nullity, and fourfold divisibility. No numerical tolerance, unverified limit or finite-to-infinite inference is used.

## Originality — PASS, bounded priority comparison

The closest source is Reading's arXiv:1405.6904, which explicitly recognizes left noncrossing arc diagrams as Catalan noncrossing partitions; it supplies the object but not signed positional Fourier spectra. Chen et al. arXiv:math/0501230 and Armstrong et al. arXiv:1101.1277 address crossings/nestings and noncrossing-partition bijections, respectively. A relevant earlier same-block-pair Fourier study uses transitive-closure coordinates, rather than consecutive-block successor-arc coordinates, and its congruence information does not imply this rank or quadratic positivity. Targeted searches did not locate an equivalent classification; an unindexed signed-arc evaluation remains a priority risk.

## Value — PASS

The arc representation is the canonical positional representation of Catalan partitions, rather than an arbitrary coordinate selection. The theorem establishes an infinite zero-free quadratic Fourier band and an explicit sharp cubic failure, which is valuable for recovery of low-degree Boolean observables. The complete six-label nullity determines the precise finite loss of recoverable information; no claim of an all-size nullity formula is made.
