## Originality — PASS relative to inspected literature

Wang--Bai--Liu--Sheng (2017) establishes the general pre-Lie Nijenhuis framework and includes restricted two-dimensional Burgers examples. Gao--Kang--Lü--Yu (first public 2023) classifies two-dimensional complex pre-Lie and three-dimensional complex associative algebras. A 2026 journal revision retains those scopes. An earlier shifted-rank-at-most-one Nijenhuis result concerns the identical underlying bilinear product (in left Leibniz notation), giving the universal equations and covering the zero-deviation branch for irreducible quadratic actions. Its restriction excludes the invertible-deviation centralizer branch, which is essential to the full Nijenhuis census and geometric descent. Two other closest previous finite-field findings cover two-dimensional Burgers descent and two-dimensional orbit families, not the genuinely nonassociative three-dimensional unrestricted classification here. The present implication distinction is the exact all-prime-power classification for a two-dimensional *irreducible* action, four geometric components of dimensions \(4,4,3,3\), and alternating extension-field/zeta counts. Source coverage is credited; standard Nijenhuis torsion, centralizers, elementary point-counting, and Galois descent alone are not claimed as novel. Residual priority risks are unindexed work and possibly more general left-symmetric operator classifications.

These are not arbitrary table entries or a mere reparametrization of prior two-dimensional results.
