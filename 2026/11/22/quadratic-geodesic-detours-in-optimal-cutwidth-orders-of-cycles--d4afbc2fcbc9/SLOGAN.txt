Minimum-cutwidth vertex orders of a labelled cycle form a connected, explicitly counted adjacent-swap graph, but preserving optimum width incurs a sharp quadratic detour between suitably rotated clockwise orders. For a cycle of length \(n\ge3\), a clockwise cyclic order rotated by \(k\) positions has feasible swap distance \((n-1)\min\{k,n-k\}\), versus ordinary Kendall distance \(k(n-k)\); fixed-start orders nevertheless retain their exact unconstrained Kendall distances.
