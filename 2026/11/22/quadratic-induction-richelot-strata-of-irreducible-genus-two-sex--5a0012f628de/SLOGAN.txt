In a genus-two curve defined by a monic irreducible sextic, the unique rational Richelot kernel has a split elliptic-product target exactly for a split branch involution, but a quadratic Weil-restriction target for a nonsplit one. In the latter case every odd-degree extension has exactly \(q^{2r+1}+1\) points, and the first two prime fields have a complete, explicitly verified Weil-polynomial spectrum; the underlying count of invariant sextics is acknowledged as known.
