In the published sine–cosine memristive plane map, every target within the exact image strip has infinitely many one-step predecessors, and their number inside a radius-\(R\) window grows as \(2bR^2/\pi^2\) in the interior but as \(bR^2/\pi^2\) on either edge. Interior sheets are asymptotically regular, whereas every boundary predecessor is critical; the remainder in each case is \(O(R)\).
