For the printed two-dimensional logistic–sine cosine map, every interior target has \(256/(\pi b^2)+O(b^{-1})\) one-step ancestors as \(b\downarrow0\), uniformly in coupling and phase shifts. These ancestors approach the product law with marginal density \((\pi/4)|\cos(\pi x)|\) at \(O(b)\) Wasserstein-1 rate, while the singular-inverse fraction is only \(O(b)\).
