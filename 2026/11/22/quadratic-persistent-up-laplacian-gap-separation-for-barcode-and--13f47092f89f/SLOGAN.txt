Two contractible simplicial fillings have identical integral barcodes and exactly the same face counts at both filtration stages, but the smallest positive degree-one persistent up-Laplacian eigenvalues differ by a factor of order \(n^2\). A triangle book has a constant spectral gap, while deleting one facet of an \(n\)-gonal bipyramid gives a Dirichlet prism whose gap decays quadratically.
