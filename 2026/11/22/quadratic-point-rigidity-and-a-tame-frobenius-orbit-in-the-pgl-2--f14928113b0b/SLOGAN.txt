Every smooth non-Hermitian member over \(\mathbb F_q\) of the Borges--Korchmáros--Speziali \(\mathrm{PGL}(2,q)\)-invariant pencil has exactly \((q^3+q+2)/2\) points over \(\mathbb F_{q^2}\), independent of its parameter; the new quadratic points form one complete tame short orbit. The Hermitian member has \(q^3+1\) quadratic points, exhibiting a sharp arithmetic jump at the enhanced-symmetry parameter.
