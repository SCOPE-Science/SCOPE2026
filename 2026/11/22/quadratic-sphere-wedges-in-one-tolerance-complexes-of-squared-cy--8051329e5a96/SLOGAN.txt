For every \(n\ge9\), the one-tolerance complex of the **filled clique complex** of the squared cycle \(C_n^2\) is integrally homotopy equivalent to a wedge of exactly \((n^2-7n-2)/2\) two-spheres. An explicit three-stage elementary collapse works uniformly for all cycle lengths, converting this nonchordal family into a simply connected two-complex with exactly computable integral homology.
