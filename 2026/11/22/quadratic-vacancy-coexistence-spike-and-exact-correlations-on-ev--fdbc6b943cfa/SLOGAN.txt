For every even-dimensional folded hypercube \(FQ_n\) with \(n\ge4\), the zero-field antiferromagnetic spin-one model has exactly one vacancy threshold \(D=(n-1)J/2\), where \(2n(n+1)\) half-occupied ground states appear alongside \(2(n+1)\) fully occupied and one empty state. Outside this exact threshold there is no partially occupied ground-state phase, and the entire critical two-point correlation is an explicit Hamming-distance function.
