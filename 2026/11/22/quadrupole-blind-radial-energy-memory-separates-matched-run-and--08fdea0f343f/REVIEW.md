## Originality
**PASS, with specific literature-priority risk.** The 2023 run-and-tumble stationary recurrence by Frydel and the 2020 arbitrary-dimensional active Brownian moment hierarchy of Chaudhuri–Dhar already cover individual fourth moments and provide algebraic tools underlying this calculation. They are credited rather than presented as novel. The 2015 comparison by Solon–Cates–Tailleur establishes differing confinement profiles. Caraglio–Franosch (2022) supplies a full two-dimensional active Brownian propagator and ordinary positional autocorrelations, without the three-dimensional RTP comparison. The distinct claim here is the exact all-lag *normalized* squared-radius covariance collapse for angular generators with shared first harmonic, together with a strict matched-persistence RTP/ABP fluctuation-amplitude gap. The existing moment hierarchies might allow this statement as a derived consequence, but the inspected materials do not state the full model-independent temporal identity and cross-protocol variance comparison. Unindexed or implicit equivalents cannot be excluded and require independent priority checking.

## Value
**PASS.** Matching the ordinary orientation autocorrelation and stationary mean squared displacement ordinarily leaves the quadrupolar relaxation mechanism concealed. The theorem identifies a physically interpretable cancellation: the normalized energy memory sees dipolar relaxation alone, while the energy variance quantitatively distinguishes sudden reorientation from continuous angular diffusion. The ratio diverges for rare tumbling and converges to one for rapid turning, giving an all-rate benchmark for nonequilibrium confinement. It is a complete structural identity and not merely a routine numerical improvement. A single stationary fourth moment combined with the covariance curvature recovers the first two angular eigenvalues, subject to calibration.
