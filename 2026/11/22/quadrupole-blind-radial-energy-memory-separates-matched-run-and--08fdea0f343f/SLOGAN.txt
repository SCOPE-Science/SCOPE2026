In a three-dimensional harmonic trap, matched-persistence run-and-tumble and active Brownian swimmers have exactly the same normalized squared-radius autocorrelation at every lag, despite different stationary energy fluctuations. The run-and-tumble energy variance is strictly larger by \((\alpha+4)(3\alpha+2)/[3\alpha(\alpha+2)]\), because a hidden quadrupolar angular mode controls amplitude but not normalized temporal shape.
