# Scientific review: Quantitative tropical entry–exit for acyclic positive cascades and a Gaussian crossing law

## Correctness (C): PASS
The finite positive Duhamel expansion into directed paths is exact under the topological ordering. For each path, the exponent is uniformly Lipschitz on the compact time-simplex; an inward homothetic simplex of diameter at most \(O(\varepsilon)\) gives a lower integral of order \(\varepsilon^k e^{M/\varepsilon}\), with an upper bound of order \(\varepsilon^{-k}e^{M/\varepsilon}\). The finite log-sum-exp then proves the claimed graph-wide \(O(\varepsilon\log(1/\varepsilon))\) error for \(t\ge\theta>0\), without a spectral-gap assumption. The two-coordinate example is exactly solvable by an error function. Its saddle-point Gaussian prefactor yields the sharp one-half logarithmic coefficient and the exit-time correction. Positivity and derivative signs give unique upward return; the feed-time measure is a truncated standard normal, which proves total-variation convergence. The included standalone script checked 1,944 finite constant-rate cases and five explicit return values; the infinite assertions rely on proof, not experiments.

## Originality (O): PASS within inspected evidence
The October 2026 nilpotent entry–exit analysis by Borsotti proves logarithmic shifts for a common diagonal rate plus fixed nilpotent matrix, and for block-diagonal sums of these structures. Its Sections 2–4 have been inspected: those formulas do not provide arbitrary time-dependent, positive acyclic off-diagonal feeding, graph-wide uniform finite-parameter error, or a normalized interior feed-time law. The June 2023 open-access work of Kaklamanos et al. treats two-dimensional intersecting eigendirections and a one-way-coupled example, but gives leading exit-point relations, not this all-graph quantitative statement or its Gaussian correction. The multidimensional theory of Hsu and Ruan concerns relaxation cycles under structural hypotheses and does not imply this linear graph bound from its inspected abstract. Related singular perturbation and max-plus literature supplies general techniques, not a verified dominating theorem. Searches of equivalent phrases and stronger variants in a mathematical results database and available prior published claims found no equivalent statement. Residual priority risk: a general WKB path-integral theorem, not discovered here, might imply the positive-system part. The result is not presented as a discovery of Laplace asymptotics themselves.

## Mathematical value (V): PASS
This solves a natural finite-size quantitative question arising in multidimensional entry–exit dynamics: when many positive, directed, time-dependent channels compete, can the tropical action determine every concentration with a uniform error despite rate crossings and exponential entry imbalance? The path-simplex bound does so for arbitrary fixed acyclic topologies, gives a sharp order independent of accidental spectral degeneracies, and establishes a concrete non-Jordan mechanism with a computable exit-time constant and a nondegenerate feed-time occupation law. That combination provides useful precision for threshold locations; it does not claim nonlinear coupled networks, cycles, or feedback dynamics.
