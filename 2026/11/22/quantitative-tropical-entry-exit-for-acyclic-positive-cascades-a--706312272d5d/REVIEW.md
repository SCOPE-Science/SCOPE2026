# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Topological iteration of scalar variation of constants gives each directed path exactly once, with positive coefficient ε^(−number of fast edges) times a weighted integral over Δ_k(t); the zero-length path is exact. Rate boundedness makes each phase Lipschitz in the k times uniformly in t. For t≥θ and ε<θ, contraction of the simplex toward a maximizer by ε/t retains volume ε^k/k!, within ε in each coordinate of the maximizer, including at any boundary or multiple maximum. Together with the positive compact feed bounds, each path is sandwiched between c ε^k exp(M/ε) and C ε^(−k) exp(M/ε). A fixed finite positive path sum preserves the uniform O(ε log(1/ε)) estimate. For the example A_x=t²/2−3t, A_y=t²−4t, the feed phase is exactly 1/2−(s−1)²/2. The logarithmic downstream error at t*=2+√(7/2) is ε/2 log(2π/ε)+o(ε), proving class-wide sharpness. Also ε(x+y)'=(t−2)(x+2y); the total is below 1 at t=2 for small ε and strictly increases afterward, crossing once. Taking fixed times on either side of t* with A_x<0 localizes the crossing; the Gaussian tails and initial y contribution are exponentially small there. Setting y=1−x gives A_y(t_ε)+1/2=(ε/2)log(ε/(2π))+o(ε), hence a displacement of order ε log(1/ε) and the stated second-order formula with ℓ*=2√(7/2). The rescaled normalized feed is exactly a conditioned standard Gaussian and its total variation discrepancy is the omitted mass. This proves the limit, not just convergence of simulated moments.

## originality

PASS

The path expansion, log-sum-exp and a quantitative Lipschitz Laplace bound already supply the general estimate once assembled; neither those ingredients nor logarithmic shifts in general are credited as new. The inspected entry–exit papers do not give this stated DAG synthesis together with sharpness via an interior transfer rather than a Jordan block, a resolved return constant, and the normalized feed-time law. This is a best-of-knowledge comparison of that submitted combination, not a new general Laplace principle or priority certification.

## value

PASS

The uniform estimate supplies a usable error scale without smooth crossings, spectral ordering, or favorable initial exponent order. The sharpness witness is motivated by the question whether the logarithmic penalty is only a Jordan phenomenon: a single nondegenerate interior transfer times the departure, yields a half-log prefactor instead of an integer nilpotent depth, and gives a normalized law for when the feed accumulated. Connecting those facts to a specified return level makes the example an informative benchmark rather than a parameter-only exercise.
