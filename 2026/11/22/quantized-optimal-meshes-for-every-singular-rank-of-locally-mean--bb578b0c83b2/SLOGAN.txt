Local mean removal turns Volterra integration into independent Dirichlet singular ladders, and its \(k\)-th singular value is minimized at exactly the meshes whose cell lengths are positive integer multiples of \(1/(N+k-1)\). This produces genuinely nonuniform second-rank optimizers even though uniform meshes uniquely minimize every Schatten norm \(\mathcal S_p\) for \(p>1\).
