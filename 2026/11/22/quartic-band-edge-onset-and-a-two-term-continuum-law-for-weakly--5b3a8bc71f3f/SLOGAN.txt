For a unilateral shift whose first \(m\) links are enhanced by \(1+\alpha/m^2\), every exterior Jacobi eigenvalue converges to an exact half-line square-well branch with an explicit first lattice correction at order \(m^{-3}\). At the discrete-to-continuum binding thresholds \(\alpha=(2j-1)^2\pi^2/8\), the newly born eigenvalue instead sits just \(4\alpha^2m^{-4}\) above the essential spectral edge.
