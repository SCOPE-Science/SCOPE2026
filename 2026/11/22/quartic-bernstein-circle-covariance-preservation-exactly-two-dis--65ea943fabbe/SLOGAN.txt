Among strictly increasing five-node degree-four Bernstein schemes on the geodesic circle, every positive-definite kernel remains positive definite exactly for the two grids \((0,\pi/4,\pi/2,3\pi/4,\pi)\) and \((0,\pi/3,\pi/2,2\pi/3,\pi)\). The continuous node freedom at degree three collapses to exactly two discrete quartic choices.
