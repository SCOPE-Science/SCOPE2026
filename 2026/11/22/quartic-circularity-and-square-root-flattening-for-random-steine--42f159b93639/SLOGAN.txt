For three independent uniform circle points, the eccentricity \(e\) of their maximal-area Steiner inellipse has opposite endpoint laws \(\Pr(e<t)\sim3\sqrt3\,t^4/(16\pi)\) and \(\Pr(e>1-t)\sim4\sqrt6\,t^{1/2}/\pi\). Near-circular inellipses localize at equilateral triangles with a uniform elliptical rescaling; nearly flat inellipses localize at one colliding vertex pair with a uniform signed-gap law.
