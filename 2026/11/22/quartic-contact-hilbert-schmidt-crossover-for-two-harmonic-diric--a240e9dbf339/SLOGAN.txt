The Hilbert–Schmidt mass of a quadratic one-prime Hardy–Dirichlet composition operator diverges like \(\delta^{-1/2}\) on either side of a sharp quartic-contact transition and like \(\delta^{-3/4}\) at contact. A critical \(\sqrt\delta\)-scale coefficient detuning has an exact positive crossover function describing the splitting of the single quartic minimum into two quadratic minima.
