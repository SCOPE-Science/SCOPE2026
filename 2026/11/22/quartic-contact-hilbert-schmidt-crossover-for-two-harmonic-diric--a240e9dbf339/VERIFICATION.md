The stated Hilbert–Schmidt norm is obtained from an exact one-circle Parseval identity and the zeta Laurent expansion. The proof reduces the critical limit to a positive quartic rational integral with a dominating integrable tail.

Run `python checker.py` with Python 3 and SciPy installed. It checks selected finite-parameter integrals, the exact minimum, and asymptotic constants against independent numerical integration. These are finite corroborations, not proof of the asymptotic assertions or historical priority. The infinite-dimensional results follow from the analytic arguments in RESULT.md.
