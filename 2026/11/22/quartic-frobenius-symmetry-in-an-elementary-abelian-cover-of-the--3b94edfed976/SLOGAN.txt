For the normalization of \((x+x^{q^2})^{q+1}+(y+y^{q^2})^{q+1}+1=0\) over every odd \(\mathbb F_q\), all rational-point counts outside extension degrees divisible by four coincide with those of the Hermitian quotient. The complementary Jacobian Weil numerator is an integral polynomial in \(T^4\), with an explicit first coefficient, and the distinguished Weierstrass boundary has a complete degree-one/two/four Frobenius spectrum.
