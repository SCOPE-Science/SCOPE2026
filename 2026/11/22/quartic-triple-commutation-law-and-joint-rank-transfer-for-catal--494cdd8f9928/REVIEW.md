# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS
Every idempotent is a predecessor projection. A complete three-color membership scan applies the exact two-map gap criterion to each of the three pairs; its reachable thirteen states are explicitly enumerated. Quotienting these states by the six permutations of the three maps gives a four-state integer transition matrix, with an explicit polynomial certificate for the quartic denominator. Reset at a simultaneous fixed point proves the product-rank renewal formula; monomial weights then give every joint image rank. The included independent checker reproduces the exact state transitions, integer certificate, all total counts through chain length eight, all four-rank profiles through chain length six, and bivariate rank counts through eight. Infinite validity rests on the proved transfer and renewal bijections, not on finite enumeration.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
Theorem 3.6 and Corollary 3.9 of Andrenšek (arXiv:2604.15497v1) already characterize relevant two-idempotent compatibility, and general semilattice homomorphism criteria encode the possibility of mutually commuting triples. These premises are not claimed as discoveries. The inspected full paper does not evaluate the ordered triple counts or joint-rank spectrum. Earlier pair-count and prescribed-pair-product research records address edges, not the triangle count; the inspected binary-search-tree maximal-clique classification describes a stronger family structure from which triangle counts could in principle be derived but does not give the evaluated rank-refined quartic law. Konieczny–Korkmaz (arXiv:2611.21071v1) computes individual centralizers, not this triangle distribution. The closest checked records contain no theorem that states the exact rational formula or all-four-rank transfer. Residual risk: unpublished or unindexed enumerations, and indirect derivations from the known maximal-family structure.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Commuting-idempotent pairs give the commuting graph's edge statistics, but mutually commuting triples give its triangle incidence and product-rank spectrum; these are intrinsically different invariants and cannot be recovered from an unrefined edge count. The short quartic law and exact rank-resolved renewal describe every chain size in a natural classical monoid. The weighted transfer additionally resolves all four simultaneous ranks, supporting comparisons of commuting semilattices and higher clique questions. This is a substantive evaluated classification of a natural invariant rather than a routine single-parameter restatement.
