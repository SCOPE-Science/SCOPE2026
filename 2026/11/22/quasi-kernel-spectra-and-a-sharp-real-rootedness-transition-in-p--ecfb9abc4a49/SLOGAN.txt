For a consistently directed \(n\)-cycle with \(k\) outward pendant sinks at each vertex, every quasi-kernel is controlled by cyclic gaps of length two or three, yielding a closed cardinality polynomial. That polynomial is real-rooted for every \(n\ge3\) when \(k=1\), but for \(k\ge2\) it is real-rooted exactly for \(n\in\{3,4,5,7\}\).
