When a one-dimensional lattice searcher always resets to its *once-sampled* starting site, the population-average mean absorption time has at most one finite-rate stationary optimum, with a sharp alternative at the edge of exponential-moment integrability. If a fraction \(\varepsilon\) of searchers starts farther away, the most distant occupied site \(K\) forces the optimal reset rate to diverge like \(\varepsilon^{-1/K}\), regardless of closer rare sites.
