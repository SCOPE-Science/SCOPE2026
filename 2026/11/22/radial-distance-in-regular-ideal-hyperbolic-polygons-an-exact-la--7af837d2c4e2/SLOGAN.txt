A hyperbolic-area-uniform point in every regular ideal \(n\)-gon has an exact elementary radial-distance distribution, with a sharp \(3/2\)-power transition at the incircle and an exponential cusp-distance tail. After subtracting \(\log n\), its distance from the center converges to an explicit two-branch law, with exactly \(2/\pi\) limiting probability below the incircle cutoff and an upper tail asymptotic to \((2/\pi^2)e^{-s}\).
