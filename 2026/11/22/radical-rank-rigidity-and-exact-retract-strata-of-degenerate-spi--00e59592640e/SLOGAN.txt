For degenerate spin-factor Jordan algebras with a nondegenerate quotient of dimension at least two, every nonzero endomorphism fixes the unit and acts orthogonally on that quotient; its arbitrary freedom lies entirely in the radical. This yields an exact finite-field census of every endomorphism and every retraction, with one conjugacy orbit per nonzero retraction rank and precisely \(r+2\) geometric components when the radical has dimension \(r\).
