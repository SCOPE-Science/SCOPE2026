Uniformly retaining a fixed number of atoms from any full finite cyclic Gabor orbit gives an exact trace-square variance equal to a universal sampling factor times the squared deviation of the window's nontrivial ambiguity magnitudes from the SIC value. The variance is maximal exactly for stabilizer windows, vanishes exactly at Weyl--Heisenberg SICs when they exist, and for prime Alltop windows equals \(p^{-2}\) of the maximal variance.
