For random-priority monomer adsorption on the Heawood graph, the complete jammed-size law is \((\Pr(J=4),\Pr(J=5),\Pr(J=7))=(4/21,7/15,12/35)\): only \(12/35\) of runs reach a maximum independent set, although this Fano-plane graph is extremal for equilibrium independent-set counting among cubic graphs of girth at least five. Its full finite-time expected occupancy is an explicit fourteen-degree Bernstein polynomial with exact rational coefficients, giving a finite-girth kinetic density \(577/1470\) above the cubic-tree value \(3/8\).
