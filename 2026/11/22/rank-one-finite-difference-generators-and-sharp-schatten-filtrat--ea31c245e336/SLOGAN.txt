For every integer \(h\ge1\), the \((h+1)\)-st derivative-order finite difference of critical Gaussian-Fock integral operators is exactly a nonzero rank-one operator; these identities generate all compact shifts on a fixed matrix diagonal, with an optimal \(h+2\)-generator threshold. Lower finite differences have sharp singular-value asymptotics \(s_N\sim (h!/(h-r)!)N^{-r}\), giving every corresponding Schatten and weak-endpoint cutoff.
