The intrinsic volumes of one isotropic random zonotope do not fluctuate independently: in every fixed dimension \(d\ge3\), all \(d-1\) nontrivial volumes have asymptotically perfectly correlated, non-Gaussian second-order fluctuations driven by one explicit spherical-harmonic chi-square law. Exact finite-sample cross-covariances with \(V_2\) identify the common component, whose limiting distribution has a sharp finite upper endpoint and no finite lower endpoint.
