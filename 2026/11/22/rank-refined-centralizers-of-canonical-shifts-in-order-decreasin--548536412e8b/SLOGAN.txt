For every nonzero power \(\pi^k\) of the canonical nilpotent shift on a finite chain, the centralizers inside both order-decreasing partial-isometry monoids have explicit rank polynomials: increasing commutants are unions of residue chains, while reversing commutants are exactly the maps whose two products with \(\pi^k\) vanish. The generator \(\pi\) is self-centralizing, and the reversing contribution stabilizes once \(n\ge2k-1\).
