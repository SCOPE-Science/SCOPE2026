Each fiber input lies in its target. Maximal directional runs isolate the Boolean coverage conditions; endpoints must be selected, and adjacent omissions are forbidden. The binomial count is obtained by distributing zeros among the gaps between selected ones. Injecting pairs of allowable words by inserting one zero yields coefficientwise dominance and its strict top-degree criterion. Evaluating at one proves the sharp Fibonacci maximum. The program independently checks 3,066 finite target/step pairs and every allowed run partition through total size sixteen. The proof, rather than finite samples, handles arbitrary dimension and target cardinality.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Inverse multiplicities of a fixed atomic summand are a natural quantitative invariant in a noncancellative power monoid. A recent published lemma isolated only one separated class of outputs. This result classifies **every** nonempty output, distinguishes all fiber cardinalities by input rank, and proves a sharp universal coefficientwise extremum and equality characterization. This exact structure can be used to quantify failure of cancellation and to compare fibers without enumerating subsets. The claim is more than the already-known separated-block count, though its local Fibonacci ingredient is elementary.
