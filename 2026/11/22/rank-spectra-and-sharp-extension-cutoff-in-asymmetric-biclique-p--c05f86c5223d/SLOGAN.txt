For an asymmetric complete bipartite graph \(K_{a,b}\), \(a>b\ge1\), the rank polynomial of partial automorphisms that do **not** extend to a global automorphism factors exactly as \(C_{a,b}(z)(C_{a,b}(z)+2)\), where \(C_{a,b}(z)=\sum_{k=1}^{b}\binom ak\binom bk k!z^k\). Every rank from \(1\) through \(2b\) has nonextendible maps, and every larger-rank partial automorphism extends, with exactly \(\binom ab^{\!2}(b!)^2\) obstructions at the sharp boundary.
