For the one-point-extension rank relative to finite triangle-free graphs, rank at least three is equivalent to the existence of a nonedge pair that realizes all four possible neighbourhood signatures; this gives a six-vertex witness and a complete nine-shape forbidden induced-subgraph basis for lower rank. The sharp minimum has four edges, uniquely \(P_5\) with isolates, and exactly \(2292\) labelled six-vertex triangle-free graphs in nine isomorphism classes reach rank three.
