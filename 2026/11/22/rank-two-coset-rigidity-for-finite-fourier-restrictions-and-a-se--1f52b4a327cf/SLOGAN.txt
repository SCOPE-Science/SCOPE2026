In every finite abelian character table, Fourier-sampling rank at most two is governed exactly by whether the sampled rows or columns occupy at most two annihilator cosets. Consequently, for each prime \(p\ge5\), exactly \(90\binom p4\binom p2\) four-by-four minors at cyclic order \(3p\) have rank two, with no rank-one examples.
