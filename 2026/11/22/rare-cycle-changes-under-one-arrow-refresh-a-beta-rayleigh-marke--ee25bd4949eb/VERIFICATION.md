# Reproducibility

Execute `python3 verify.py` using Python 3 and the standard library; no downloads or third-party dependencies are needed. The included script enumerates every mapping with one outgoing arrow frozen, every old/new target, and both cycle-count signs for \(n=2,3,4,5\). It separately counts target depths in rooted Cayley trees generated by Prüfer codes for sizes two through six. Larger finite values illustrate convergence to the analytic beta mean and rare-event intensity.

The exact formulas and the infinite limit are proved in `RESULT.md`. The executable tests are finite consistency checks; they do not establish the limiting assertions. No independent external audit, formal proof certificate, or computer-assisted infinite verification is claimed.
