Among \(n\) uniform sites on an interval, the worst uncovered point lies at an endpoint with exact probability \(B(1/2,n)-1/n\sim\sqrt{\pi/n}\). Conditional on this rare boundary event, \(2n\) times the covering radius minus \(\log n\) converges to the non-Gumbel law with CDF \(\operatorname{erfc}(e^{-y/2})\), and either endpoint is equally likely to be the witness.
