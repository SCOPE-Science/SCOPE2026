For two area-uniform points of a planar Lamé region with fixed \(0<p<2\), the probability of almost attaining its Euclidean diameter follows the sharp power \(\varepsilon^{2+2/p}\) with an explicit gamma-function coefficient and marked Dirichlet endpoint law. In the near-circle regime \(p=2-\lambda\varepsilon\), the exponent instead becomes \(5/2\), with an explicit angular crossover whose support splits into four axial arcs at \(\lambda=2/\log2\).
