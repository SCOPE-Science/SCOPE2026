For binary block-affine scrambled integration of \(f(x)=x\), the error is an exact dyadic series with rare, independent activations of probability \(1/N\), giving standardized kurtosis \(3(N+2)/5\) and a universal exponential limit for its negative logarithmic magnitude. Fully nested uniform scrambling has the *same* exact variance \(1/(12N^3)\) but Gaussian, bounded-kurtosis normalized errors.
