Among uniform permutation-inversion trees of even order \(2m\), the probability of admitting a perfect matching is exactly \(2^{1-m}\); conditional on this rare event the leaf count is \(2+\operatorname{Bin}(m-2,1/2)\). The typical diameter fraction jumps from \(1/2\) to \(3/4\), with an exact formula for matching probability at each prescribed leaf count.
