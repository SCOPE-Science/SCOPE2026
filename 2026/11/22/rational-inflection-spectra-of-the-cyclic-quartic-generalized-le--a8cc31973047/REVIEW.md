# Mathematical review

**Correctness (PASS).** The exact Hessian yields four rational weight-two hyperflex supports and sixteen geometric ordinary flex supports. Its quartic factor has a regular Klein-four root action. A two-square resolvent criterion and a character-switching parameter involution prove both the absence of intermediate rational-flex counts and the marked-parameter frequency for every stated prime power. The displayed determinant, discriminants, resultants, involution identities, quadratic character sums and boundary exclusions provide explicit reconstruction. Independent finite-prime tests pass through \(211\); they are not used as an all-fields proof.

**Originality (PASS, bounded evidence).** Babu--Venkataraman identify the generic geometric Weierstrass partition of this exact curve family, so that partition is not novel. Goodson analyzes the same family over finite fields via hypergeometric curve point counts, not rational canonical inflection-support frequencies. The closest indexed same-family result concerns superspecial Hasse loci rather than the Hessian-flex configuration; its full public text was inaccessible, so only its indexed statement can be compared. Searches on rational flexes, Hessian-resolvent aliases, finite-field marked-parameter counts, and existing research-claim summaries did not produce the present formula. The principal residual priority risk is a historical unindexed equivalent character-sum calculation.

**Value (PASS).** For the natural canonical ramification divisor of a well-studied genus-three family, the calculation gives a sharp, nontrivial all-or-none rationality phenomenon and an exact frequency over every permitted field, including arbitrarily large extension degrees. This is more than the classical geometric flex count or an isolated finite table. The exceptional harmonic members, other twists, and curve-isomorphism classification are outside scope.
