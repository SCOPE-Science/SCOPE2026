For the smooth cyclic-quartic generalized Legendre pencil over every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\) with \(q\equiv3\pmod4\), generic marked fibres have exactly four or twelve rational flex supports, never eight. Exactly \((q-11)/8\) or \((q+1)/8\) marked parameters have twelve, according as \(q\equiv3\) or \(7\pmod8\).
