Two-colouring every permutation cycle gives a fixed-position-set XOR convolution that is singular only at \(n=3\), but is positive definite only at \(n=6\) and all \(n\ge8\). Its sharp eventual smallest Walsh eigenvalue is the explicit shifted-gamma moment \(M_n\), with exact Euclidean condition number \((n+1)!/M_n\) tending to \(e^4\); positivity unexpectedly fails at \(n=7\) between two positive dimensions.
