# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Unique path endpoints in an acyclic multiquiver force the nonstationary commutation obstruction: a nonzero concatenation cannot be matched by the reversed product. Every maximal commuting set is a full directed reachability cut with exactly the permitted stationary paths, and each proposed cut is closed and maximal. The endpoint count proves the centralizer formulas. The directed-line count follows from three-color words with distinguished first and last active symbols, and the maximum from ordered source-target pairs. A standalone independent multiplication/enumeration checker reproduced all maximal families and centralizers in 79 finite instances and the line counts through six vertices. Direct proof, not enumeration alone, establishes all dimensions.

**Originality — PASS subject to residual archival priority uncertainty.** The complete inspected §8.1 of Mazorchuk–Steinberg (arXiv:1107.1413) concerns effective representation dimension of path semigroups, not commutants. The inspected §2 of Ma–March (doi:10.1016/j.jalgebra.2009.02.028) characterizes triangular matrix-unit semigroups, not their maximal commuting families. Existing broader Brandt-semigroup rectangles share the endpoint mechanism; however the arbitrary-quiver cut admissibility and weighted path-multiplicity spectrum require a separate incidence restriction, and the directed-line count is not stated by these sources. Semantic searches for 'commuting paths', 'acyclic path semigroup centralizer', 'maximal commutative path semigroup' and the exact line sequence found no decisive covering result. Search nonappearance is not proof of novelty.

**Value — PASS.** A complete graph-dependent classification and exact distribution of a natural intrinsic finite-semigroup invariant is provided for all finite acyclic multiquivers. The difference between reachability-dependent family counts and path-multiplicity-dependent sizes, and the nontrivial ternary-word count in the directed line, identify structural information not captured simply by the total semigroup size. The same endpoint analysis determines every element centralizer.

**Residual limitations.** Cyclic quivers are excluded, unlabelled quotients are not counted, and historical equivalence to an unlocated monomial-semigroup theorem cannot be ruled out. Small finite computations do not certify the infinite claim.
