For a run-and-tumble particle searching for a partially absorbing center, weak reactivity makes the speed minimizing absolute search-time variance exceed the mean-optimal speed by \(3(4/3)^{2/3}a^{2/3}/40+O(a)\). Strong reactivity reverses the ordering, with mean-optimal speed exceeding variance-optimal speed by \(a\log(3/2)/(\log a)^2\) to leading order; variance-optimal speeds are proved unique in both limiting regimes.
