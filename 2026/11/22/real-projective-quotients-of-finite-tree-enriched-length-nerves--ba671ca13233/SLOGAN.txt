For a finite unweighted tree with a fixed-vertex-free involution, every integer-stage ordered length nerve has a free geometric involution quotient homotopy equivalent to \(\mathbb{RP}^{L}\vee\bigvee^{(n-2)/2}S^L\), where \(n\) is the number of tree vertices and \(L\ge1\). Its covering nerve is always a wedge of \(n-1\) copies of \(S^L\), with an explicit signed integral edge-permutation homology action; the quotient introduces projective-space two-torsion uniformly, even for branching trees.
