# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The filtered normalized chains have magnitude associated graded; Hepworth–Willerton Cor. 31 gives the natural integral basis of alternating edge words and no off-diagonal groups. The first length drop comes only from endpoints on these words. In each edge block the image is generated by the primitive vector with entries 1 and ±1, exactly the next kernel; at degree one the oriented incidence columns of the tree form a basis of the augmentation ideal. Higher length drops cannot connect positions on the diagonal. Thus only the top reduced homology survives, with literal cycles A+(-1)^(L+1)B, exchanged with that sign under edge reversal. For L=1 the nerve is a graph with two directed edges per tree edge. The written connectivity argument for L>2 omits newly entering 1- and 2-cells; independently, every 1-cell (x,y) of distance ≤L is reduced to the tree geodesic by the 2-cell (x,z,y) with z the next geodesic vertex. The backtracks at unit edges make opposite directions inverse. Hence all loops reduce to tree loops for every L≥2. Hurewicz and homology Whitehead then give the asserted cover wedge. A fixed-vertex-free involution swaps the endpoints of the middle edge on the path from any vertex to its image. Removing that edge gives two exchanged halves, so no other edge reverses. Ordered nondegenerate open simplices are disjoint and none is stabilized, hence the action is free. On the middle edge the stage k two-point nerve adds two exchanged k-cells; each boundary is ± the preceding top generator (for k=1 the endpoints are distinct). The degree-one attachments let an inductive equivariant sphere map extend over one hemisphere by homotoping its attaching map and over the second by translation. This gives an equivalence of the simply connected covers for k≥2 and therefore of their free quotients, so the edge quotient is RP^L (for L=1 it is a circle). As a direct check of the full splitting, the union of all stage-L two-point edge nerves is a tree of homotopy spheres attached at vertices; for L≥2 it is simply connected and its inclusion gives the explicit integral homology basis of X_L. The inclusion is equivariant and a homotopy equivalence on universal covers, giving a homotopy equivalence on free orbit quotients by Whitehead. In that union's quotient, the noncentral edge orbits are indexed by one tree half, attached successively at single vertices to the central-edge projective subspace. This yields the claimed projective wedge, with the graph argument applying at L=1. The integral homology follows, and constant noninteger stages plus disjoint surviving degrees give the indicated half-open bars. From /workspace, python3 reproductions/tree_orbits.py checked the literal top cycles, orientation transport and cover/orbit mod-2 and mod-3 Betti vectors for the edge and a symmetric double-star at L=1–4. The frozen script was inspected, not executed. The modular run does not prove the integral or homotopy claims, and the analytic proof makes no weighted tree or homotopy-orbit assertion.
