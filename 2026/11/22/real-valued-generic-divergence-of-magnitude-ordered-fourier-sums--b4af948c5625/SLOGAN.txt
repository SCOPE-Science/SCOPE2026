For every odd integer \(p\ge3\), the real-valued continuous functions on \((\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z)^{\mathbb N}\) whose magnitude-threshold Fourier sums are unbounded almost everywhere form a dense \(G_\delta\). Pairing each complex finite-model polynomial with its conjugate on one extra cyclic phase coordinate preserves every threshold level, while separated independent blocks amplify a positive-probability obstruction to generic divergence.
