The deformed \(K\)-exclusion ring has a hidden *dynamical* reciprocity: complementing occupations, reflecting the ring and replacing \(t\) by \(1/t\) conjugates its full jump process after the exact time change \(s\mapsto t^{2-2K}s\). This transfers every finite-time path statistic, current-count law and relaxation eigenvalue between complementary densities, despite the failure of either stationary symmetry to preserve the dynamics on its own.
