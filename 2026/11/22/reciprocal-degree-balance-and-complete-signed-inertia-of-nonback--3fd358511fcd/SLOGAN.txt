For every connected simple graph of minimum degree at least three, the nonbacktracking self-commutator has exactly \(n-1\) positive and negative eigenvalues each, except when the graph is bipartite with balanced reciprocal-degree sums, when it has exactly \(n-2\) of each sign. The exceptional cancellation is precisely \(\sum_{L}(d_v-2)^{-1}=\sum_{R}(d_v-2)^{-1}\), and the entire regular-graph signed spectrum reduces to principal angles determined by adjacency eigenvalues.
