In a one-critical bifiltration, strict monotone-path entrance orders are exactly the permutations whose every prefix is closed under coordinatewise-maximal rectangular completion. For \(n\) incomparable antichain grades, an explicit graded-persistence example has \(2^{n-1}\) distinct monotone-path combinatorial barcodes, while positive-slope affine lines have at most quartically many strict orders and already miss two of the 32 types at \(n=6\).
