In a one-critical bifiltration, strict monotone-path entrance orders are exactly the orders with rectangularly closed prefixes. An antidiagonal wedge-of-spheres family has exponentially many distinct graded combinatorial barcodes in its number of grades, but only quartically many possible strict affine-line orders at most. With arbitrary intercepts and full-line entrances, six grades give the first gap: 30 affine orders versus 32 path orders. Clipping to a fixed box can reduce the affine count further.
