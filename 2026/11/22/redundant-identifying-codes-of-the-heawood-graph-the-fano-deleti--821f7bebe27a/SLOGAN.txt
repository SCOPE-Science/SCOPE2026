On the Heawood graph, a closed-neighborhood identifying code survives arbitrary single-detector deletion exactly when its omitted vertices form one of three explicitly classified Fano-incidence patterns (or a smaller subpattern). There are exactly \(98z^{10}+224z^{11}+91z^{12}+14z^{13}+z^{14}\) such codes by size, with 98 minimum and inclusion-minimal ten-detector codes in two symmetry orbits of sizes 14 and 84.
