# Review of the rectangular-prism tour profile

## Correctness — PASS
Every tour-edge class and the parity-tetrahedron and antipodal-pair lower witnesses have been reconstructed. The exact inequality generators are valid on their specified regimes. The standalone standard-library checker verifies that 64 explicit tour witnesses cover all 32 symmetry orbits (247 subsets) and that each upper-bound difference is an exact nonnegative rational sum of valid inequalities. For other orbits the inequalities are strictly positive away from the excluded degenerate limits. The endpoint crossings are monotone and unique.

## Originality — PASS (provisional historical priority)
The 2026 open-problem collection concerns arbitrary point sets. The 2024 primary paper shows the parity-tetrahedron lower example and treats the cube-vertex restriction only in high dimensions, but not a non-isotropic aspect-ratio classification. An accessible earlier exact cube-vertex result already covers the unit-cube specialization; the new result is explicitly confined to the two-parameter competition and reentrant transition as \(t\) varies. Targeted mathematical-index, primary-literature and historical-claim searches found no stronger prism result. Unindexed sources could change priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The exact sharp optimization of a natural geometric extremal quantity throughout a continuous prism-deformation family has two nontrivial transitions and a reentrant optimizer type. It supplies complete equality geometries rather than a single sample computation and identifies how a previously known cube-corner extremizer fails under anisotropy.

## Scope and residual risk
No claims about arbitrary interior or boundary points, unequal horizontal sides, alternative exponents or independent certification. The finite checker is a proof of exhaustive corner upper bounds, not a computer proof of global mathematical novelty.
