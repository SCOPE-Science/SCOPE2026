In a cyclic three-species quadratic Lotka–Volterra update, all three axis-supported logistic two-cycles become hyperbolic sinks on a sharp competition region that splits into reentrant bands at the algebraic threshold \(R^3-3R-6=0\). The restored high-competition sinks are inaccessible to perpetually positive populations, while the first band contains three simultaneous sinks with open positive basins.
