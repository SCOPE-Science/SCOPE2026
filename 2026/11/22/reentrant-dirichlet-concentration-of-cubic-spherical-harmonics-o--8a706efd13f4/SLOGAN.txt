For pure cubic spherical harmonics in every dimension \(d\ge3\), the fraction of intrinsic tangential-gradient energy captured by two antipodal polar caps undergoes a sharp reentrant maximizing symmetry transition \(V_1\to V_0\to V_1\) at squared cutoffs \(1/(d+2)\) and \((2d+5)/(3(d+2))\). The complete four-branch spectrum has one additional lower-level crossing and a sharp thin-cap condition-number exponent of two; the result is distinct from scalar-value Slepian concentration.
