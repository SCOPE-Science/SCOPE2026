For spectral-power heat on the four-vertex path, the endpoint transfer is initially negative for \(1<\alpha\le2\) but, for **every** \(\alpha>2\), becomes initially positive, then negative on exactly one bounded time window, and finally positive forever. The window begins at asymptotic time \((\log2)/(2\cdot2^\alpha)\) and ends at asymptotic time \(\log(1+1/\sqrt2)/(2-\sqrt2)^\alpha\), giving a complete exponent-dependent sign classification and sharply separated scales.
