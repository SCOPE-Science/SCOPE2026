In an idealized seed-bank rescue model with two independent opportunities, optimal fire-return gaps exhibit an exact reentrant phase diagram: skipping one opportunity, using unequal positive gaps, equalizing them, then splitting them again as the horizon grows (when the maximal per-opportunity success exceeds \(3/4\)). A quadratic identity gives every global optimizer and the three sharp thresholds; the result is not a field-calibrated prescription.
