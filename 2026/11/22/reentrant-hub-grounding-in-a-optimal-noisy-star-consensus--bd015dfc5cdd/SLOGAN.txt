For total-budget A-optimal diagonal grounding on the unit star \(K_{1,m}\), every leaf receives positive gain, but the optimal hub gain is positive–zero–positive as the budget increases exactly when \(m\ge4\). The two sharp thresholds are the positive roots of \(B^3+4mB^2-m(m^3-m^2-5m)B+2m^3\), asymptotic to \(2/m\) and \(m^2\).
