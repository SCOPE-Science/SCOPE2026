On the two-hub bipartite graph \(K_{2,q}\), the unique budget-optimal grounding design for inverse-trace noisy consensus drops both hub actuators and then restores them as total budget increases, if and only if \(q\ge6\). The exact zero-hub interval is determined by \(q(q-1)(B/q)\ge(B/q+2)^3\), with endpoints asymptotic to \(8/q\) and \(q^2\).
