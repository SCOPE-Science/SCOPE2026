For two equal finite-gain consensus leaders on a uniform path, exact inverse-trace minimization reduces to a single integer gap; under gain \(\kappa=c/n\), the optimal normalized gap first grows, overshoots its infinite-gain limit \(3/5\), and then recedes. Its unique maximum is \(1-1/\sqrt7\) at \(c=(7+4\sqrt7)/3\), and the 21-vertex path already exhibits an exact finite reentrant pair of optimal placements.
