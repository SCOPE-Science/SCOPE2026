On a four-vertex weighted path, the unique single-pin site minimizing augmented-Laplacian condition number moves from vertex 2 at gain \(10\), to vertex 4 at gain \(50\), and back to vertex 2 at gain \(500\); vertex 2 also wins for all sufficiently large gains. Exact rational eigenvalue brackets and grounded-spectrum bounds prove the excursion, its high-gain return, and perturbation robustness.
