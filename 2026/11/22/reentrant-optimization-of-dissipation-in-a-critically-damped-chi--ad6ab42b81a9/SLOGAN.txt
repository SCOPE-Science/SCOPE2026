For a critically damped harmonic oscillator driven by a chiral active swimmer, the angular-diffusion rate maximizing translational heat dissipation exhibits two distinct transitions as squared chirality increases: a discontinuous jump to zero at the unique positive root \(t_*=0.190531907893624\ldots\) of an explicit sextic, followed by a continuous departure from zero at \(t=3+2\sqrt2\). The exact global phase diagram is independent of translational bath temperature and does not include the energetic cost of driving chirality.
