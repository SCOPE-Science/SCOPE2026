In a predator–prey model with saturating food intake, prey counterattack and saturated prey harvesting, predators can invade from rarity only for a sharply determined intermediate window of harvest, even though they cannot invade at either lower or higher harvest. The onset of invasion is forward or backward according to a unique explicit predator-self-limitation threshold, while the high-harvest loss of invasion always has a locally attracting coexistence branch on its viable side.
