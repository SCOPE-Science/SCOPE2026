Active second-order Richardson extrapolation of backward Euler has an exact **rate-imbalance threshold** for probability positivity on an irreducible two-state Markov chain: below minority equilibrium mass \(0.0348583466\ldots\), its one-step transition becomes negative on precisely one bounded timestep interval. Every even iterate remains stochastic, while odd iterates have a complete sharp threshold and square-root boundary law; away from the singular step, even iterates are also continuous-time Markov embeddable.
