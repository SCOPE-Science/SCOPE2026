A balanced quadratic Bargmann–Fock state \(1+a z-a z^2\) has a reentrant best-single-coherent-state geometry: one positive real site, then exactly two conjugate sites, then one negative real site. Both transitions have exact algebraic thresholds \(0.515138\ldots\) and \(3.626198\ldots\), and the conjugate splitting follows a sharp square-root law.
