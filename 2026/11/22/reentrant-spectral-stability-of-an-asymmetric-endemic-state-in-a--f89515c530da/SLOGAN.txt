In an infection-induced opinion-switching SIS model, the asymmetric endemic equilibrium has an exact quadratic test for a strictly stable spectrum when parameterized by its susceptible-opinion ratio. At ratio \(3/8\), two explicit algebraic thresholds separate stable, oscillatory-unstable, and stable intervals along the fixed-ratio family. The crossings do not decide nonlinear stability at the thresholds or establish periodic orbits.
