In an infection-induced opinion-switching SIS model, the asymmetric endemic equilibrium has an exact quadratic Routh–Hurwitz stability test when parameterized by its susceptible-opinion ratio. At ratio \(3/8\), increasing transmission and opinion-change rates together makes that equilibrium stable, then oscillatory-unstable, then stable again, with two explicit algebraic spectral-crossing thresholds; no nonlinear periodic-orbit claim is made.
