For a mean-square-stable multiplicative-noise SDE, two-step Euler–Maruyama covariance minimization has a sharp noise threshold at \(\sigma^2/\lambda=2-\sqrt2\): below it, mesh collapse, unequal steps, equal steps, and unequal steps again occur in sequence as the terminal horizon grows. The closed-form optimizer follows from a strictly convex quadratic in the product of the two step lengths; minimizing this numerical covariance is **not** the same as minimizing numerical solution error.
