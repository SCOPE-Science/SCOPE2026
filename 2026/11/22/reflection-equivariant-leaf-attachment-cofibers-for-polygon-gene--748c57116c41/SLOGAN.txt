Adding one leaf to an \(n\)-cycle gives a general-position homotopy cofiber with \(p\) circles and \((h-1)^2\) two-spheres, where \(h=\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor\) and \(p\) is one or two according to parity. Reflection acts on the integral relative two-homology by minus transposition, with eigensublattice index \(2^{\binom{h-1}{2}}\), while the genuine reflected cofiber is a wedge of \(p\) circles and \(\binom{h-1}{2}\) two-spheres.
