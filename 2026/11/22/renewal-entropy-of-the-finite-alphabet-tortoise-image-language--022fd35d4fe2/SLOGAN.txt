The distinct one-pass tortoise images over every finite alphabet form a regular language whose exponential growth constant is exactly the reciprocal of the unique positive solution of \(rA_{q-1}(r)=1\), giving a strict growth ladder. Binary outputs occupy asymptotically half of all possible words, while every alphabet of at least three letters has exponentially sparse images; the four-letter growth constant is \(3.5503790656826695\ldots\).
