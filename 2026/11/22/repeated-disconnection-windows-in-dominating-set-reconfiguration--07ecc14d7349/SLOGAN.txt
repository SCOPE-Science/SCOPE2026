For dominating-set token-addition/removal on the two-token star with \(n\) leaves, the complete component count is \(1+\sum_{r=4}^{n}\binom nr\mathbf1_{\{k=n-r+\binom r2\}}\) at every budget \(k\ge n+1\). For \(n\ge4\), connectivity appears at budget \(n+1\), fails again at exactly \(n-3\) separate budgets, and becomes permanent only at budget \(\binom n2+1\).
