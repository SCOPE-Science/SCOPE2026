At equal reset-position variance, the rare-reset entropy-production rate obeys \(\Sigma(r)\sim c\Gamma(p+1)(D/\sigma^2)^{p/2}r^{1-p/2}\) for stretched-exponential reset tails \(f(x)\asymp e^{-c|x|^p}\), giving vanishing, finite, and divergent regimes separated sharply at \(p=2\). For every standardized even-power reset law beyond the Gaussian, the exact dissipation is a strictly decreasing polynomial in \(1/r\), so rarer resets can dissipate **more**.
