For the gaps of every coprime two-generator numerical semigroup, increasing either generator by a modulus gives an exact residue-by-residue sawtooth increment, including when a generator is not invertible modulo that modulus. The gap-count discrepancy has the uniform sharp leading term \(((\gcd(a,m)-1)b+(\gcd(b,m)-1)a)/m\), with error bounded solely by the modulus.
