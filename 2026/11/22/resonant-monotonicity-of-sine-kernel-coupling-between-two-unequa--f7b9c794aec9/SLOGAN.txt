For two unequal separated intervals under a fixed Fourier band, the quadratic concentration-spectrum interaction is eventually decreasing **if and only if** at least one interval length is a positive integer multiple of \(\pi/W\); otherwise it has infinitely many alternating strict extrema. The exact inverse-square interference envelope is governed by \(\sin(Wa)\sin(Wb)/W^2\), so a resonance in either interval alone removes all leading oscillations.
