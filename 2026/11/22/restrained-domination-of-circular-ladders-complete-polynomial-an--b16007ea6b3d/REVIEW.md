## Mathematical value — PASS
The cubic prism family is a standard test graph for domination and reliable guard deployment. The exact polynomial measures every feasible set size, not just an easily inferred minimum; the residue-dependent minimum multiplicity and the exact \(2n\) ordinary-optimum loss identify a strict boundary between nominal and restrained protection. The exponential ratio establishes that this difference grows globally even when both optimization numbers agree. This supplies a meaningful distributional answer to an established graph parameter, rather than a substitution or a small isolated count.

## Limitations and closest literature
The proof is for labelled \(C_n\square K_2\) with \(n\ge4\), not other ladder families or multi-restriction variants. It uses an explicit sixteen-state symbolic determinant; correctness of that finite identity is fully reproducible, while novelty cannot be conclusively certified from indexed searches. The primary comparisons are Hemalatha et al. (2023, Theorem 5.2), Velmurugan–Kala (2023), Brešar–Henning (2024), and Allagan et al. (2026, Theorem 2). Independent review remains outstanding.
