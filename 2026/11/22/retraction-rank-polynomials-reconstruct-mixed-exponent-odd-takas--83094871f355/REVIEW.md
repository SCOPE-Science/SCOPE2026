# Review: Retraction-rank polynomials reconstruct mixed-exponent odd Takasaki quandles

**Correctness — PASS.** The affine rigidity step is proved from the quandle equation using invertibility of multiplication by \(2\); the idempotent criterion, ordered direct-sum decomposition bijection, automorphism group count, and orbit–stabilizer calculation are proved with all boundary cases. The independent enumeration program corroborates five small group types and directly checks all affine quandle maps in a group of order \(27\). It is not an infinite proof by enumeration.

**Originality — PASS relative to inspected literature.** Saki–Kiani classify the entire subquandle lattice and its reconstruction power, while the new inverse result uses the much coarser retraction-size polynomial; the exact coefficient \([T^p]R_A(T)\) is the distinguishing statement. The semisimple Alexander-quandle census excludes mixed-exponent additive groups, and the core-quandle affine-rigidity classification concerns automorphisms, not all retractions. Standard finite abelian automorphism-group and orbit formulas are antecedents, and possible unindexed or implicitly dominating results remain a priority risk.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The rank-polynomial determines the underlying group in a natural infinite mixed-exponent family using only its top exponent and rank-\(p\) coefficient. This gives a motivated recognition theorem plus a complete type-refined enumeration and an image-rank gap boundary, rather than a finite computation alone.

**Scope and risk.** Only odd prime-power abelian groups of exponent at most the square of the prime are asserted; no general characterization of all quandles or higher p-power exponents is claimed. External priority and independent mathematical validation are not certified.
