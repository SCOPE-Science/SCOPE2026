Every idempotent endomorphism of a free class-\(c\ge3\) nilpotent Lie algebra is conjugate to a generator projection, with an exact finite-field rank census expressed through Witt dimensions. For each fixed class and field size, the typical proportion of retained generators converges exponentially sharply to the unique maximizer of \(a+(1-2a)a^c\), yielding a class-dependent image-dimension law.
