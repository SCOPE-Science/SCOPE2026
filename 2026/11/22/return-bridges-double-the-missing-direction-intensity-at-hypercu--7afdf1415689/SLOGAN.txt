A uniform hypercube walk forced to return to its starting corner has more unflipped coordinate directions than an unconditioned walk. At \(L/d-\log d\to c\), their number converges to \(\operatorname{Poisson}(2e^{-c})\), exactly doubling the ordinary coupon collector's critical intensity; at fixed positive \(L/d\), the supporting-face codimension instead has an explicit positive-variance Gaussian fluctuation law.
