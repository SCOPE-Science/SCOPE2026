# Review of the two-memory return-gap finding

## Correctness — PASS

The exact scope is two positive relaxation rates \(0<a<d\), positive amplitudes \(A,D\), bounded centered fluctuations in strict convex order, and nonnegative elapsed times. Strict convexity plus the martingale-coupling form of Jensen proves the two positive excess Laplace transforms. Exponential factorization yields a strictly decreasing one-zero bracket and the stated boundary cases. A separate rational witness has exactly matched means and variances and exact opposite response signs. The reproducibility script checks its rational arithmetic and admissibility. The all-law theorem relies on the proof, not numerical enumeration.

## Originality — PASS on inspected evidence

The journal primary texts of Schoepf–Sanderson–Larcombe (2021 first public date), Brown et al. (2023), and Pfab et al. (2024) were inspected at the scientific-methods/results/model level. These address variability regimes, incomplete acclimatization and recovery, and mechanistic bioenergetics respectively. They do not imply the stated all-convex-order gap-distribution sign criterion or the same-mean-and-variance counterexample. Database searches against published records on two relaxation modes, environmental memory, convex order and equal-moment aliases returned no direct stronger coverage. The convex-order inequalities and exponential algebra themselves are classical; the narrow result claims no priority over them. Broader signed exponential response literature might subsume the algebra, and exhaustive global priority is not certified.

## Value — PASS

The motivated question is whether a common mean-and-variance summary of the recurrence of environmental stress events can determine the direction of a two-mode priming-versus-damage response. The constructive pair supplies a sharp negative answer in the simplest plausible opposing-memory surrogate, with exact margins rather than simulated noise. The one-switch frontier explains why the impact of recurrence variability can change as mean recovery time changes, without incorrectly treating variance as a full stochastic ordering. This is a limited theoretical benchmark: no empirical calibration, no claim of measured coral bleaching probability, and no recommended management action.
