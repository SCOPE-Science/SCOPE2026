# Scientific assessment of the equal-moment return-gap witness

The exact scope is a fixed two-exponential response and two positive gap distributions with equal first and second moments. Each random gap is compared with the same deterministic gap. The exact threshold interval follows from two affine numerators in the exponentiated mean. Both the signs and the strict support condition can be checked with rational arithmetic.

The contribution is the direction-of-effect counterexample, not a general theorem about the number of zeros of a sum of exponentials. The related fixed-mean Gamma transit comparison uses different variances; the related moment-matched arrival-fraction comparison concerns differences of two cumulative distribution functions, not changes relative to a deterministic response. Historical coverage is assessed to the best of available evidence; inaccessible literature is not ruled out. The witness is a benchmark for what mean–variance summaries can miss in the stylized model, not an empirical ecological claim.
