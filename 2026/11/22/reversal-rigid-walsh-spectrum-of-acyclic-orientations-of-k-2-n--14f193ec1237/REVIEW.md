# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The acyclic-orientation condition is exactly the absence of a pair of opposite mixed columns, because every directed cycle in \(K_{2,n}\) has length four. Inclusion–exclusion over two product alphabets gives the exact integer Walsh formula. The elementary prime-factor argument excludes all zeros beyond global reversal for every \(n\ge2\). The Fourier diagonalization, sharp gap, and range projection follow with no asymptotic or unproved step. The delivered exact checker independently reconstructs the transform for all frequencies through \(n=7\) and directly verifies graph acyclicity for \(n\le5\).

**Originality — PASS under bounded comparison.** Stanley [1] gives the chromatic evaluation, West [2] proves a dependent-edge realization theorem, Kamano–Ohno–Yamamoto [3] provide a **total number of ones** weight enumerator, and Cameron–Glass–Rekvényi–Schumacher [4] give counts and the lonesum bijection. None of the inspected full texts gives the character-by-character edge-weighted transform, the exact absence of extra zeros, or the sharp condition number on the recoverable range. Indexed related work on bipartite orientations addresses parity signatures, not this Fourier convolution. Remaining risk: an unindexed entrywise signed-weight lonesum enumerator or equivalent specialization may already imply the theorem.

**Value — PASS.** This is an all-width reconstruction theorem for a natural arrangement/lonesum combinatorial family. It distinguishes unavoidable information loss from global arrow-reversal symmetry from additional cancellation, proves the latter never occurs, and gives exact Fourier modes, rank, gap, stability and a norm-one range projection. The full multivariate harmonic refinement is materially stronger than evaluating one total-count or total-weight polynomial.

**Scope and limitations.** The result is confined to labelled \(K_{2,n}\) for \(n\ge2\). Fourier singular values refer to the Hilbert-space convolution operator; inverse estimates on other \(\ell^p\) spaces are not claimed. A comprehensive historical priority guarantee is not possible from the checked sources alone.
