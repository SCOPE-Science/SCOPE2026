For every \(n\ge2\), the Walsh transform of the acyclic-orientation indicator of \(K_{2,n}\) has zeros **only** at characters odd under reversal of every edge. Counting convolution recovers exactly the reversal-invariant functions, with sharp surviving singular-value range from \(2\) to \(2\cdot3^n-2^n\) and an explicit complete multiplier formula.
