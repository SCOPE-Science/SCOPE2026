The inspected complete open-access paper develops transition probabilities, marginal stationarity, moments, inference and forecasting, but does not state the exact three-band adjacent-minor sign law or its all-parameter tension with monotone dependence. Littlejohn's 1992 discrete-minification reversibility work, and the 1996 geometric-tailed reversibility paper, are important historical comparators. Their available abstracts concern other constructions and do not decisively cover the signed-minor theorem; full-text access was unavailable, so historical priority is provisional. Search nonmatches alone are not a novelty certificate.

**Value — PASS.** TP2 and likelihood-ratio monotonicity imply useful stronger conditional-order and inferential properties beyond ordinary stochastic monotonicity. This existing count-time-series model might naturally be treated as strongly positively dependent because it is stationary, reversible and positively quadrant dependent. The all-indices, all-parameters signed-minor classification identifies precisely why such a stronger inference is invalid, and the constructive common-shock representation explains which weaker ordering remains valid. The result is structural rather than a model-name substitution, a selected numerical minor or a restatement of the prior pgf.
