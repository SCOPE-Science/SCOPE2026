For every finite quiver, even with loops and directed cycles, the right-polynomial functions of its radical-square-zero algebra over \(\mathbb F_q\) are classified by independent vertex values, outgoing-vertex derivatives, and arrow offsets, giving exactly \(q^{q(v+r+m)}\) functions and \((q!)^v(q-1)^{qr}q^{qm}\) bijections. These functions are composition-closed precisely when the quiver has no arrows between distinct vertices.
