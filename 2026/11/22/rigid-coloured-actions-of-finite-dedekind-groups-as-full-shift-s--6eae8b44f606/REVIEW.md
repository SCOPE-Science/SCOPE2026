# Review: Rigid coloured actions of finite Dedekind groups

## Correctness — PASS

The proof establishes an equivariant colour-profile map into the shift, proves injectivity is sufficient for rigidity without a group restriction, and proves its necessity by constructing nontrivial colour-preserving equivariant automorphisms when a point stabilizer is strictly contained in its profile stabilizer or when two distinct points have the same profile. Normality of the point stabilizers, guaranteed in finite Dedekind groups, is explicitly used for the first construction. The normal-form bijection with invariant shift subsets gives the generating polynomial, unique sharp capacity and complement symmetry. Subgroup-lattice Möbius inversion counts orbits of each stabilizer type. The index-two coefficient and the non-Dedekind \(S_3\) counterexample follow directly. The extracted standalone checker reproduced direct shift orbits, a separately implemented quotient-pattern enumeration and exact subgroup inversion on finite examples including \(Q_8\), with additional exhaustive small labelled-action corroboration. All universal assertions rest on the written proof rather than the finite tests.

## Originality — PASS, provisional historical priority

Searches for finite coloured \(G\)-set rigidity, full-shift subobjects, subgroup Möbius counts, orbit automorphisms, and cyclic/elementary-abelian analogues were compared at the statement and implication level. Murphy-Hernandez (arXiv:2608.23866v1, first submitted 2026-08-24) already proves the cofree \(G\)-set adjunction, describes the full shift and its canonical map, and applies Burnside counting; these substantial ingredients are credited rather than claimed new. Its inspected propositions do not assert the full-equivalent-rigidity characterization or its rigid-subset generating polynomial. Other relevant published mathematical texts concern one coloured permutation's automorphism distribution, uncoloured commuting involutions and two-variable counting equivalence, coloured idempotent unary structures, and joint orbit statistics of elementary abelian actions. The cyclic primitive-necklace enumeration and ordinary Burnside/subgroup methods are antecedents. None of the inspected statements supplies the stated rigidity equivalence for every finite Dedekind group together with its complete polynomial and sharp capacity. This is not a proof of historical uniqueness. A prior general cofree-action or equivariant-colouring theorem might imply the result, and the motivating 2026 paper was checked in its publicly accessible definitions, automorphism-marking section and related-work discussion, but other detailed appendices were not assessed line-by-line.

## Value — PASS

The claim completely classifies a natural finite-model class whose automorphism group is trivial, gives a uniform structural size ceiling and an exact computable spectrum for all finite Dedekind groups, and detects the difference between cyclic and Klein four actions with identical cardinality. The classification is not restricted to a selected finite computation, and the counterexample states the exact scope of the normal-stabilizer hypothesis. Such rigidity spectra are relevant to unlabelled model counting and automorphism-sensitive enumeration; no unsupported downstream claim is made.
