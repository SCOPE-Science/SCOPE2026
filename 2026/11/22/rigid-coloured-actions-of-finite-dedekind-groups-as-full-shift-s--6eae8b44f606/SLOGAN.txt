Every rigid \(q\)-coloured finite action of a finite Dedekind group \(G\) is canonically a shift-invariant subset of \([q]^G\), giving the universal sharp size limit \(q^{|G|}\). Its complete isomorphism-class polynomial is the orbit-selection product \(\prod_{\mathcal O}(1+z^{|\mathcal O|})\); unlike the maximum size, the individual coefficients detect subgroup structure.
