Positive mixtures of primitive closed geodesics on the flat two-torus preserve every squared Fourier norm in a square band only if **each direction is independently exact**: different slopes cannot cancel aliasing. Each direction costs at least \(2\pi(2N+1)\) in total arclength, so the unique globally shortest design uses \(2N+1\) equally spaced coordinate loops; arbitrary positive reweighting and additional slopes cannot improve it.
