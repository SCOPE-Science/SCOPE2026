For generalized Petersen graphs \(P(n,k)\), the next four jumps \(k\in\{4,5,6,7\}\) have exactly \(1\), \(1+2\mathbf1_{4\mid n}\), \(1\), and \(1+2\mathbf1_{4\mid n}+5\mathbf1_{5\mid n}\) perfect matching cuts, respectively, for every \(n>2k\). A complete finite-state classification proves that the only nonspoke possibilities arise from period-four and period-five rim transitions, revealing a rigid–resonant alternation.
