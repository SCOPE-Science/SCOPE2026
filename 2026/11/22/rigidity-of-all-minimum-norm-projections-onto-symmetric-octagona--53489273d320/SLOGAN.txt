For the real signed-symmetric octagonal plane \(N_\beta(x,y)=\max\{|x|,|y|,\beta(|x|+|y|)\}\), every norm-minimizing retraction of its canonical embedding into \(\ell_\infty^4\) is the same explicit linear map, without imposing symmetry on competing projections. Eight simultaneous norm-saturation constraints have determinant \(64\beta^4\), forcing full rigidity for all \(1/2\le\beta\le1\); the supporting norm value \(\beta/(2\beta^2-2\beta+1)\) is covered by classical planar perimeter theory.
