Every shortest arclength-exact tensor-product \(t\)-design curve on the standard \(d\)-torus is a translated straight Korobov geodesic: its winding vector is exactly a signed coordinate permutation of \((1,t+1,\ldots,(t+1)^{d-1})\). This completely classifies all equality cases of the known sharp length bound and leaves exactly \(2^{d-1}d!\) unoriented minimizing directions.
