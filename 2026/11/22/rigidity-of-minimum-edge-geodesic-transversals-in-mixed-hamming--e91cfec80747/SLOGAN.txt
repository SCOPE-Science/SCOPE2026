In every Cartesian product \(K_{q_1}\square\cdots\square K_{q_d}\) with \(q_i\ge2\), the minimum edge sets meeting every maximal geodesic are exactly the complete coordinate edge classes of smallest alphabet size. Their size is \(\tfrac12(\prod_iq_i)(\min_iq_i-1)\), and the number of distinct optimum sets is the multiplicity of the smallest alphabet.
