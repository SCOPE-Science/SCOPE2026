**Correctness — PASS.** A distinct primitive-necklace argument gives the sharp upper ceiling of 55. The rational-prefix-density construction contains 55 mutually non-overlapping words, and the 55-group finite forcing certificate rules out every other maximum in the same orientation. A reversal argument covers the other orientation. Exhaustive verification regenerates all 715 fixed-weight words, 89 eligible oriented candidates, and all 55 forced representatives. The proof is finite, with no unsupported extrapolation to other lengths.

**Originality — PASS (qualified).** Prior indexed fixed-weight-four results explicitly end at length 12, where the primitive-necklace limit is not attained. Bilotta et al., Chee et al., and Blackburn provide relevant general coding methods and bounds without this exact fixed-weight length-thirteen rigidity statement in the sections inspected. The prefix-density construction is not independently novel, and the literature comparison cannot exclude unindexed equivalents.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** At the first unstudied length after a documented non-overlapping-code necklace-packing defect, the proof determines both the exact packing maximum and the entire family of optimizers, including a surprisingly branch-free structural forcing mechanism. It distinguishes attainment from rigidity; a single construction or an upper bound does not settle the latter.

**Limitations and risks.** This is one natural finite parameter, not an asymptotic or all-length characterization. Independent verification of mathematical priority is outstanding.
