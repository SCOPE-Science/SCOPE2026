For parent-linked duplication graphs, a closed three-moment recurrence rigorously establishes the previously anticipated quarter-threshold for edge-count variance: linear below \(p=1/4\), \(n\log n\) at the boundary, and order \(n^{4p}\) above it, uniformly for every fixed parent-link probability \(0<r\le1\). The result additionally gives exact asymptotic constants and a nondegenerate \(L^2\) martingale limit above the threshold; the threshold itself was predicted in earlier work for \(r=1\).
