At the exact border collision of a two-player piecewise-smooth competition map, every asymmetric orbit eventually enters an invariant nonlinear wedge and contracts by an exact asymptotic factor \(3/4\) per two steps, with a convergent parity-dependent phase. Symmetric orbits instead obey a closed scalar recurrence and decay as \(2^{-t}\); this proves a rate previously supported only by numerical and leading-order evidence.
