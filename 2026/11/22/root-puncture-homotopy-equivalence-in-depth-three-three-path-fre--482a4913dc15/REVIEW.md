The proof explicitly supplies free-face pairings to collapse both \(D\) and \(L\) onto \(U\), and uses the mapping-cone decomposition of \(K\). All quantified parameters satisfy the requirements for the chosen external leaves. The ranks and degrees follow from joins over the integers. The checker directly enumerates two finite instances and checks their elementary collapse pairs and independent boundary ranks; no finite extrapolation is used.

Search limits and possible unindexed equivalents are noted in the structured audit; this does not establish absolute priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A single root deletion produces arbitrarily high-dimensional and high-rank integral homology from a contractible starting complex. The explicit link-to-deletion equivalence gives a structural explanation of this sharp local topological instability, for an infinite family of naturally branched trees rather than a selected numerical instance.
