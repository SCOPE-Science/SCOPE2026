For a broad irregular family of rooted depth-three trees, the two-independence complex is contractible even though deleting its root exposes a wedge of \(\prod_{i,j}(q_{ij}-1)\) integral spheres of dimension \(2\sum_i s_i-1\). The root link embeds in the deletion as a homotopy equivalence, with both complexes collapsing onto the same join of star complexes.
