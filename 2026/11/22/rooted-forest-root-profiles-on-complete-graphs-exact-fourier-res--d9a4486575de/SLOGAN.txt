Root-set XOR convolution for rooted spanning forests of \(K_n\) loses Fourier information exactly at the positive root activities \(t=n\) and \(t=n-2j\) with \(1\le j\le\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor\), with an explicit nullity at every resonance. At unit activity, recovery is possible exactly in even orders and the smallest nonzero Walsh multiplier is \((n^2-1)^{m-1}\) for \(n=2m\), or \(2(n+1)^{m-1}(n-1)^m\) for \(n=2m+1\), yielding sharp nonzero-subspace conditioning.
