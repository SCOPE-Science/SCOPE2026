The singular maps fixing a designated point have an exact idempotent-generation criterion: every arrow into the fixed point is compulsory, and the remaining arrows must generate the ordinary singular transformation semigroup. For \(n\ge4\), the minimum generating sets are precisely rooted stars combined with strongly connected tournaments, so the idempotent rank is \(\binom n2\), with an exceptional value \(4\) when \(n=3\).
