For the two-sided binary word game that deletes prefixes ending in `ab` or suffixes beginning in `ba`, the losing positions are exactly \(b^*\cup a^+(b^{\ge2}a^+)^*\), with an explicit rational length enumerator. This smallest overlapping two-letter variant contrasts with the classical Dyck-language Timber game, and its largest Sprague–Grundy value at length \(n\) is exactly \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\).
