The Shrikhande and \(4\times4\) rook NOR networks have identical one-step input/output cardinality statistics and exactly \(654\) recurrent states each, yet their fixed-state counts are \(48\) and \(24\), and their full attraction-basin spectra differ. A concrete two-sided isomorphism of their bipartite doubles explains the one-step agreement while the exact temporal orbit census exposes the effect of identifying input and output vertices.
