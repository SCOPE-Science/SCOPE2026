A single yes/no event detector has a unique optimal query time for estimating the integrated count path of a Poisson process, and its optimal time shifts from \(2/3\) toward the first-arrival scale \(c_*/\lambda\) as the event rate grows. Conditioning on exactly \(m\) events gives a distinct finite-count law but the same sharp high-density fraction of explained variance, \(3c_*(2-c_*)/m\) (versus \(3c_*(2-c_*)/\lambda\) without conditioning), where \(c_*=1.59362426004004\ldots\).
