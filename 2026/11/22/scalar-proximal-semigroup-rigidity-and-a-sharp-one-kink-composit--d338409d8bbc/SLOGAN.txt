Every everywhere-finite convex penalty on the real line has exactly time-additive quadratic proximal maps if and only if it is affine or a possibly asymmetric distance-type penalty to a minimizer interval. For a nonminimizing two-slope kink, the exact worst inputwise composition discrepancy is \(\min\{s\min(|a|,|b|),\,t(b-a)\}\), while a kink whose slopes straddle zero has zero discrepancy.
