Every finite connected honeycomb subgraph, including bond deletions and holes, has radius-two graph-distance Rips type \(\bigvee^{b-h}S^1\vee\bigvee^h S^2\), where \(b\) is its graph cycle rank and \(h\) its number of complete hexagonal faces. Deleting one shared interior bond converts two local two-sphere classes into one new circle class.
