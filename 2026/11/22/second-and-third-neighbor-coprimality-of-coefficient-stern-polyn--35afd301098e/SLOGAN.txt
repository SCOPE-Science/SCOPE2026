For the monic polynomials \(P_n(X)=\sum_{j=1}^{n+1}s(j)X^{n+1-j}\) built from Stern's diatomic sequence, \(P_n\) has no common complex root with either \(P_{n+2}\) or \(P_{n+3}\), for every \(n\ge0\). A rational-root obstruction settles the second neighbor; a real-root/primitive-quadratic dichotomy settles the third.
