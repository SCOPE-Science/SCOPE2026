For every weighted sunlet \(C_n\circ K_1\), minimizing second-order noisy-consensus coherence under a fixed total edge-conductance budget has one globally optimal symmetric allocation, characterized by a unique positive cubic root. Its pendant-edge weight scales as \(n^{-2}\), strictly below the first-order-optimal weight for every \(n\ge3\), which instead scales as \(n^{-3/2}\); using the first-order design incurs asymptotic second-order regret \((\sqrt6/90)n^{11/2}\) in unnormalized squared-inverse Laplacian trace.
