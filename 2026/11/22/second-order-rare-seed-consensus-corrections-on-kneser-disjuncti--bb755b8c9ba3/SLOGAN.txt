For synchronous Boolean OR on \(KG(n,k)\) with fixed \(k\ge3\) and rare independent seeds of mean \(\lambda\), every nonempty configuration reaches consensus by its second update. The probability of one-update consensus has an explicit \(n^{-2}\)-accurate expansion whose new coefficient separates three-seed common-point collisions from two disjoint intersecting pairs; both motifs are needed beyond the already known minimum total-domination threshold.
