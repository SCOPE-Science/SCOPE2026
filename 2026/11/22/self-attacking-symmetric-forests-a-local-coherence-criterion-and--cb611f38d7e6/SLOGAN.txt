For symmetric attacks on any finite forest, allowing selected self-attacks preserves equality of preferred and stable semantics exactly when every self-attacking vertex touches an isolated non-self-attacking vertex. On a labelled path the number of such markings has generating function \(\frac{1+t^2-t^3+t^4}{1-t-t^2+t^4-t^5}\), and grows at base approximately \(1.54990607\).
