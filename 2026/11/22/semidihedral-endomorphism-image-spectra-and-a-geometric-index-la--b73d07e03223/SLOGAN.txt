Every endomorphism of the semidihedral group of order \(2^n\), for \(n\ge4\), has an explicitly enumerated image type: proper nonabelian images are dihedral, but every proper nontrivial retract has order two. There are exactly \(2^{n-1}+2\) retractions in four automorphism orbits, and the index of a random endomorphism converges to a geometric law on powers of two, with mass \(1/2\) at index one.
