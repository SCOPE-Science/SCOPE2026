The exponentiated discrete Lindley law of El-Morshedy, Eliwa and Nagy has a uniquely determined shape exponent \(0.779<b_*<0.78\) at base parameter \(q=21/25\), where exactly two nonadjacent counts, \(0\) and \(2\), are tied as global modes. The maximizing count switches directly from \(0\) to \(2\) across this exponent, with the infinite tail controlled analytically rather than by a finite plot.
