The 48,384 minimum maximal matchings of the point–line incidence graph of \(\operatorname{PG}(2,4)\) form 168 individually connected Tutte–Coxeter fibers, but no batch of six edge replacements can move between fibers. Seven replacements are sharply sufficient: every pair of hyperoval fibers sharing three points has exactly 216 cross-fiber exchanges, and the resulting connected optimal-matching graph has 1,669,248 edges with degree classes 67 and 71.
