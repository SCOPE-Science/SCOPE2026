In the three-axis squarefree complete intersection \(\mathbb F_q[x,y,z]/(x^2,y^2,z^2)\), every nilpotent regular-multiplication operator over an odd finite field has one of exactly seven explicitly counted Jordan types. A nonlinear term changes the Jordan partition of a one-supported linear form with conditional probability \(1-q^{-1}\), and the generic type jumps from \((3,3,2)\) in characteristic three to \((4,2,2)\) in all larger odd characteristics.
