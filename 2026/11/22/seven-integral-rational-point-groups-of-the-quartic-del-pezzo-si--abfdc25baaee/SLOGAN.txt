The connected Picard torus of the sixteen exceptional curves on a five-point quartic del Pezzo blowup has an explicitly determined finite abelian rational-point group for every one of the seven permutation Frobenius types. In the mixed types \((3,2)\) and \((4,1)\), odd-characteristic point groups expose a genuine integral two-primary gluing that rational Frobenius eigenvalues do not determine; the five-cycle case was already known.
