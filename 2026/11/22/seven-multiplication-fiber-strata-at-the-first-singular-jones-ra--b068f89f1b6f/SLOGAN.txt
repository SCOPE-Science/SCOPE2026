In the Jones monoid, multiplying two arbitrary corank-two diagrams has a fully explicit prescribed-target fiber law: rank-preserving targets each have \(3n-5\) factorizations, while rank-decreasing targets have fiber sizes in \(\{1,2,3,4,5,6,8\}\), with all seven values occurring for \(n\ge6\). A four-equality formula gives every individual fiber and a polynomial census proves that multiplicity \(7\) is impossible.
