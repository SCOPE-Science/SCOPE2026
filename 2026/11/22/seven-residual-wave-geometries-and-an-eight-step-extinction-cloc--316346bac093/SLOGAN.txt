On the twelve-vertex icosahedral graph, requiring two excited neighbors leaves exactly \(200\) three-state Greenberg–Hastings wave cycles in seven symmetry classes, with only \(1260\) of \(3^{12}\) initial configurations eventually oscillating. Every state is periodic by the sharp eighth update, even though raising the threshold from one to two reduces the primitive three-cycle count by a factor of \(242\) while increasing the sharp entry cutoff from six to eight.
