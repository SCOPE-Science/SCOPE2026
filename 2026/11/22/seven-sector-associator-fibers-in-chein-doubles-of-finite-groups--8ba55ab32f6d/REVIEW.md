# Scientific review: prescribed associator fibers in Chein doubles

## Correctness — PASS

The four Chein multiplication rules are explicitly fixed; the left-valued associator definition gives a unique element of the base group in every parity sector. The eight-sector table is derived using exact group products, with distinct quotient conventions for barred and unbarred intermediate products. For six nontrivial parity sectors, reversible changes of variables identify a free group parameter and an unrestricted ordered commutator pair; in the all-barred sector, conjugacy invariance of commutator fibers supplies the same count. Direct finite enumeration checks the exact table and all target fibers for cyclic groups of sizes 2 and 3, dihedral groups of orders 6 and 8, quaternion group of order 8, and symmetric/alternating groups through \(S_4\). No finite experiment is treated as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS with historical-priority risk

The 2015 Ahmadidelir presentation proves the scalar associativity-degree identity \((1+7k(G)/|G|)/8\), not the entire value distribution; this scalar result is expressly excluded from the novelty claim. The full 2007 Vojtěchovský Chein construction and the section-9 analysis of Drápal–Vojtěchovský give structural loop facts but not the all-target seven-sector fiber formula. The full accessible 2025 Levin arXiv text proves upper bounds for general Moufang loops, not these prescribed-value Chein fibers. The group-character formula is classical, not claimed novel. Published-result searches using Chein, associators, commutator word distributions, character sums, and exact fibers revealed no direct equivalent. A potentially older unindexed statement cannot be excluded merely by these searches.

## Value — PASS

The exact nonidentity fibers form a complete natural invariant for the associator word on Chein doubles; they determine its support and generated associator subloop, and distinguish distinct nonidentity target classes even when the overall probability of associating is fixed. The uniform seven-sector mechanism is structural and holds without restrictions on the finite base group. It strengthens a previously known scalar probability to a pointwise class-function identity and a character-theoretic description. For example in the Chein double of \(S_4\), a nontrivial double transposition and a three-cycle have respectively \(9408\) and \(6048\) representations, invisible to the identity-only count.
