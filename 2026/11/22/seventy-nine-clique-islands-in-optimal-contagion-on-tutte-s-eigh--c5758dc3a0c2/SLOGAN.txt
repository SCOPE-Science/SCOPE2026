The Tutte–Coxeter eight-cage has exactly \(12{,}780\) optimal eight-vertex two-neighbour contagion seeds, whose complements are induced trees of order \(22\). Their one-vertex-exchange graph is eight-regular but splits into seventy copies of \(K_9\) and one connected component on \(12{,}150\) vertices; the exact orbit and completion-time distributions are also determined.
