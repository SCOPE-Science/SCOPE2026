Every smooth convex body in real \(n\)-space has exactly the same sharp Euclidean-projection sensitivity \(\frac12(1+\|w\|_2/\min_iw_i)\) in both dual weighted coordinate norms, regardless of curvature or shape. Rounded boxes retain this factor while converging to boxes whose sensitivity is only one, so the sharp projection modulus is discontinuous under Hausdorff approximation.
