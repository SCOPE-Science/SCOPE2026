For every simple quadratic, parallel and cyclic Ehrlich–Aberth root finding share a generic cross-ratio basin separator but differ at finite-pole exceptional starts, while their transformed errors iterate by third and fourth powers respectively. Both methods admit regular nonroot cycles of every period at least two, with transverse multiplier \(3^m\) or \(4^m\), so none is attracting; their root errors have sharp double-logarithmic rates \(\log3\) and \(\log4\).
