Over tree-shaped named parameters in the generic three-distance metric structure, the scalar number of nonalgebraic one-point types first fails to distinguish two nonisomorphic trees at nine vertices; the distance-occupancy polynomial first collides at ten, and even the complete distance-graded type polynomial first collides at twelve. An exhaustive unlabelled-tree census proves that each first collision consists of exactly one pair, with exact witnesses and independently checked coefficients.
