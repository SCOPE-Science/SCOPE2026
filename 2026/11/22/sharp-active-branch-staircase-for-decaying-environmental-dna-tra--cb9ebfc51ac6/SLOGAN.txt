For identical parallel eDNA-transport reaches with equal inlet source flux and fixed combined water discharge, the flow allocation maximizing surviving downstream tracer always opens exactly \(k\) channels at equal flow, where \(k\) maximizes \(k e^{-ak}\). As the attenuation-to-flow ratio \(a\) rises, the optimal active count descends by one at each sharp threshold \(a=\log((k+1)/k)\), never skipping a count.
