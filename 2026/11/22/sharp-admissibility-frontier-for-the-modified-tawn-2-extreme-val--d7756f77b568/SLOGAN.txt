The modified-Tawn-2 extreme-value copula has a sharp positive-cross-term admissibility bound \(d\leq2(a-1)(bc)^{a/2}\) for \(a\geq2\), larger by a factor \(\sqrt{a-1}\) than the formerly published sufficient bound. For \(1\leq a<2\), every positive cross term destroys convexity; the critical quadratic case \(a=2\) collapses exactly to independence.
