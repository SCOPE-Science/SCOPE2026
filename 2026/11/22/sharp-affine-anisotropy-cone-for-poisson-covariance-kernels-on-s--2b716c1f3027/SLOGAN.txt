For the spherical Poisson covariance \(P_r(x,y)[a+\langle v,x+y\rangle]\), positive semidefiniteness on every finite set in every sphere dimension is equivalent to the sharp cone \(a\ge(1+r)\|v\|/\sqrt r\). Every nonzero admissible kernel remains strictly positive definite even on the cone boundary; an exact Poisson-bridge first-moment identity proves both the threshold and its dimension independence.
