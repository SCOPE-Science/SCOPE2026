In the symmetric matching-type four-population predator–prey model, all positive but type-asymmetric trajectories converge to coexistence with a sharp, universal type-imbalance envelope proportional to \(t^{-1/2}\), although exactly type-symmetric perturbations decay exponentially. For unit parameters, \(t[(n_0-n_1)^2+(h_0-h_1)^2]\to260/9\), a model-specific coefficient generated by the interaction of neutral oscillations with dissipative population totals.
