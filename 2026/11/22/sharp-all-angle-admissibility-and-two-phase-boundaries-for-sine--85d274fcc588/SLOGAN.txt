For the sine-FGM copula with exponent one, the full admissible dependence amplitude at every positive angle \(\beta\) is exactly \(1/[\beta Q(\beta)]\), where \(Q\) is an explicit one-variable maximum; its first tightening occurs at a sharp threshold near \(103.9213\). Quadrant dependence disappears earlier, exactly beyond \(16\pi\), while the maximal amplitude decays asymptotically as \(108/\beta^2\).
