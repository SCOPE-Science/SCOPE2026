On homogeneous strongly convex scalar blocks, unrelaxed proximal Jacobi ADMM is stable from every initialization exactly for \(s>(3m-6)/4\), and its unique all-block optimal spectral-rate parameter is \(s=(3m-10+\sqrt{9m^2+4m+4})/8\). This optimum beats the general-purpose sufficient choice \(s=m-1\), improving the leading large-block spectral gap by a factor \(4/3\).
