The exact worst-case \(L^2\) error of Legendre polynomial projection on real bounded-variation data is attained by one jump at the innermost zero of \(P_{n+1}\). Its sharp constant is \(|P_n(\xi_n)|/\sqrt2\), with a central extremizer at even degrees, two displaced extremizers at odd degrees, and asymptotic size \(1/\sqrt{\pi n}\).
