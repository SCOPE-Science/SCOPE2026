# Scientific review — Euclidean chord-perimeter endpoint law

## Correctness (PASS)
The claim fixes each integer ambient dimension \(d\ge3\), uses normalized sphere measure, and distinguishes Euclidean chords from intrinsic arcs. The equality case in \(\sum_{i<j}\|X_i-X_j\|^2=9-\|\sum_i X_i\|^2\) localizes all maximal configurations to equilateral triples. The exact joint coordinate density is positive at the unique aligned maximum; explicit differentiation yields the positive-definite \(d\)-dimensional Hessian, with determinant \((\sqrt3/4)^{d-2}\). Compact localization and dominated convergence give the exact ellipsoid-volume constant, and Scheffé's lemma establishes total-variation convergence for the whitened local shape. The normalized deficit is asserted only in distribution jointly with the shape, avoiding an unsupported joint total-variation claim. The packaged checker reproduces the symbolic Hessian and independently integrates finite-threshold event probabilities; neither finite test is substituted for the proof.

## Originality (PASS, provisional historical priority)
The nearest archival random-triangle study is Edelman--Strang (arXiv:1501.03053, public January 9, 2015), which works primarily with Gaussian planar triangles and triangle-shape geometry, rather than Euclidean perimeters of three independent uniform points on \(S^{d-1}\). Hille (1966) supplies deterministic regular-configuration extremality, not the probabilistic endpoint. Koroleva--Nikitin (2013) and earlier circle U-max results cover the \(d=2\) circumference case, deliberately excluded from the asserted theorem. Finch--Jones (2010) and an inspected published all-dimensional marked-limit treatment concern *geodesic arcs*, with maximum \(2\pi\), not chords with maximum \(3\sqrt3\). Akopyan--Edelsbrunner--Nikitenko establish a spherical triangle shape/circumradius factorization but do not evaluate the specific perimeter endpoint in the inspected discussion. A published four-vertex six-chord energy endpoint law is for a different regular tetrahedral optimizer, not a corollary covering the present three-vertex statement. These comparisons use the actual objects and logical implications, not title matching. The complete papers of Le (1989) and Miles (1971) remained inaccessible; abstract-only evidence cannot rule out implications of their general spherical shape-density theory, so historical priority requires outside verification.

## Mathematical value (PASS)
The exact fixed-sample, entire-dimension-family coefficient and the aligned uniform-ball shape law are a natural test of the geometry of nearly equilateral sphere-inscribed triangles. In particular, they reveal a dimension-dependent exponent \(d/2\) differing by one power from the geodesic-perimeter exponent \((d-2)/2\), despite identical vertex sampling, and calibrate against the known planar circle endpoint. The explicit formula, full conditional limit and strong sampling-model contrast are more substantive than a routine substitution into a precomputed table. They are useful as exact baselines for finite spherical random simplices and extreme chord energies.
