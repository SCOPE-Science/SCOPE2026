For three independent surface-uniform points on a unit sphere in every fixed ambient dimension \(d\ge3\), the probability that their Euclidean chord-triangle perimeter lies within \(\varepsilon\) of its maximum \(3\sqrt3\) is asymptotic to an explicit gamma-function constant times \(\varepsilon^{d/2}\). Conditioned on this event, the appropriately aligned and Hessian-rescaled triangle deformations become uniform in the Euclidean unit \(d\)-ball in total variation.
