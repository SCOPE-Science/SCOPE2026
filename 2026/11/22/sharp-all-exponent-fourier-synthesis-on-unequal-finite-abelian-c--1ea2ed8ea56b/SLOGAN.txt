For the union of the coordinate subgroups of any two finite abelian groups, possibly of unequal orders, the sharp Fourier \(\ell^\infty/\ell^r\) synthesis constant and every complex extremizer are explicit for every real \(1<r<\infty\). The extremal intersection coefficient has a uniquely determined sign-changing exponent precisely when the two group orders \(m,n\) satisfy \((m-2)(n-2)>4\).
