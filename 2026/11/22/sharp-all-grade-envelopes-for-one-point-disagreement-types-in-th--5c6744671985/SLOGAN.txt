For every disagreement grade \(0\le k\le n\), the number of singleton extension types over a named \(n\)-point permutation lies sharply between \(2\min\{k+1,n-k+1\}\) and \((k+1)(n-k+1)\). Explicit decreasing, increasing and folded permutations attain the appropriate bounds, giving a quadratic-versus-linear range at central grades although every base has exactly \((n+1)^2\) types in total.
