A two-coordinate absolute-feedback fold with incremental slope at least \(a\) contracts toward zero after every pair of updates at the sharp universal factor \(1-a^2\); the exact optimal finite-horizon envelope is \((1-a^2)^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}\). At the critical two-player maxima map, the fixed smooth feedback has the sharp factor \(3/4\), yielding a uniform global error bound that is stronger in quantifiers than previously known orbitwise asymptotics.
