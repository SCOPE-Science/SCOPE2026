Among all labelled optimum \(K_{s+1}\)-free graphs, either no two configurations can exchange fewer than \(q\) edges (unbalanced block sizes), or fewer than \(2(q-1)\) edges (equal block sizes)—yet permitting exactly that first possible batch size already connects every optimum. The theorem also determines the degree of this first connected exchange graph, including perfect-matching exceptions, and yields a sharp batch-relocation law for maximum mutual-visibility sets of \(L(K_n)\).
