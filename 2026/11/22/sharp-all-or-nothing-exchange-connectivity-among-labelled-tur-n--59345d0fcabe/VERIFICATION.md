# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Turán equality identifies the complement with a disjoint union of balanced cliques and yields the partition count by unlabelling equal-sized blocks. The one-sided replacement count is the number of old co-block pairs no longer co-block, i.e. half the pair-disagreement distance because all optima have equal size. Splitting a t-block destroys at least t−1 pairs, equality only for (t−1,1). In the equal-size case at least two split; in the unequal case q>=2 either a large one splits or at least two small ones split, since intact blocks cannot share a target. At equality two large (q−1)-chunks for equal sizes q>=3 must occupy different targets and exchange singletons; q=2 gives the two alternate pairings. With one split large block its q-chunk cannot join an intact block, so it occupies the one vacant target and the singleton appends to a small intact block. The extra q=2 unequal equality case splits two pairs into four vertices that must fill the two vacant targets. At q=1 the missing edges are a partial matching: after deleting one pair only its endpoints and the f unmatched vertices are available, excluding the original pair, giving 2f+binom(f,2) choices. Larger chunks identify their old blocks when needed, so these counts have no hidden overcount. For connectivity, labelled same-size patterns are generated by cross-block swaps. For unequal sizes a large-small swap uses two transfers; for swapping x in large A with y in large B via a small S use x:A→S, y:B→A, x:S→B; for two small parts S,T use a large L and moves w:L→S, x:S→T, y:T→S, w:S→L. Each move is large-to-small when made. One-step transfers switch which labels are large and can be used repeatedly, establishing connectivity. For disjoint edges of K_n in the line graph, all four common neighbors are selected iff the pair extends to a fully selected K4; incident selected edges are adjacent and cause no obstruction. This proves the visibility translation. These are endpoint-only atomic batches, not paths through feasible intermediate graphs.
