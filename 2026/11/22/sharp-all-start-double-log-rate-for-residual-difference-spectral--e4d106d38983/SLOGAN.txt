The residual-difference (short BB2) rule, started with one residual-Rayleigh step on a consistent two-level linear system, has no mixed-data geometric exception: every nonzero two-mode residual obeys the sharp double-log law \(\lim_{k\to\infty}k^{-1}\log\log(\|r_0\|_2/\|r_k\|_2)=\tfrac12\log2\). This concerns exact arithmetic and two squared-singular-value levels, not general RD convergence.
