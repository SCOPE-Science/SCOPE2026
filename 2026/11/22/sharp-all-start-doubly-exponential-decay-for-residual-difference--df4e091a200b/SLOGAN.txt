With one ordinary residual steepest-descent initialization, pure residual-difference (short-BB) iteration on every consistent two-spectral linear system converges doubly exponentially from every mixed residual, at sharp double-log exponent \(\log\sqrt2\). Its oscillation amplitude cannot collapse into the slow balanced orbit: it is uniformly at least \(2\log(a/b)\), with equality at one explicit initial spectral-energy ratio.
