For a two-state rate matrix, the Caputo propagator of order \(1<\alpha\le2\) remains an entrywise probability matrix at every time precisely when the rate ratio lies in \([c_\alpha,c_\alpha^{-1}]\), where \(c_\alpha=-\min_{s\ge0}E_\alpha(-s^\alpha)\). The sharp admissible interval expands without bound as \(\alpha\downarrow1\) and collapses to balanced rates as \(\alpha\uparrow2\), yet even an admissible propagator cannot arise from a continuous-time Markov chain with locally integrable finite jump rates.
