For exactly \(t\ge2\) deletions from a circular word, an alphabet of \(2t+1\) symbols is necessary and sufficient to realize all \(\binom nt\) different deletion masks as distinct necklaces at **every** length \(n\ge t+1\). Through length \(2t+1\), a full shadow forces all symbols to be different, but at length \(2t+2\) explicit marker-and-periodic words achieve the same perfect distinguishability despite repeated symbols.
