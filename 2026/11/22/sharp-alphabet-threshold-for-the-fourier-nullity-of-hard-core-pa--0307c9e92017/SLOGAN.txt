For words over any finite abelian alphabet with no adjacent nonzero letters, counting convolution is always invertible except at lengths \(n\equiv1\pmod3\). At length \(n=3k+1\), the sharp alphabet threshold \(q\ge k+2\) makes the Fourier kernel exactly the characters nontrivial at every third coordinate, of dimension \(q^{2k}(q-1)^{k+1}\); the threshold fails one alphabet size below.
