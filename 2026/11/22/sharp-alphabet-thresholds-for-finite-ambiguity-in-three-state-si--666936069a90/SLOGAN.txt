For trim, finitely ambiguous three-state chain NFAs with singleton components and adjacent-only forward bridges, the sharp maximum accepting-run multiplicity is \(1\) over one input symbol, \(3\) over two, and \(4\) over every alphabet of at least three symbols. A complete two-family, 48-matrix finite-semigroup certificate proves the all-word upper bound, and explicit short words attain every threshold.
