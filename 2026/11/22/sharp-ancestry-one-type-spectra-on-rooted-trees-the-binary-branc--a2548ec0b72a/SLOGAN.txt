With \(m\) named vertices and only strict ancestry and equality, a finite rooted tree has at most \(4m\) realized quantifier-free unary types, or \(2m+1\) on a path; the bounds are sharp, and the branching bound is attained by a binary tree on exactly \(4m\) vertices. Equality requires a full binary laminar hierarchy of descendant-mark supports and witnesses for every empty-support ancestral profile.
