For the canonical minimal-jump-rank affine Markov interval maps with \(n\) prescribed increasing/decreasing branches, the **unique** smallest-entropy orientation is strict alternation beginning with a decreasing branch, while exactly two constant orientations maximize entropy. The complete orientation-word entropy multiset has binomial multiplicities, and uniformly random orientations have entropy loss \((2n)^{-1}\) to leading order with a Gaussian \(n^{-3/2}\) fluctuation law.
