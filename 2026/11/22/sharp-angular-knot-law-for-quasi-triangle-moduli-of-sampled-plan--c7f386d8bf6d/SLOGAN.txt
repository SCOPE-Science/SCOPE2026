For sampled planar \(\ell_p\) quasi-norms with \(0<p<1\), every axis-aligned nonuniform conic fan has an **exact** sharp quasi-triangle constant determined only by its nearest knot to a coordinate diagonal. The original constant \(2^{1/p-1}\) survives exactly when a diagonal is sampled; otherwise the loss is quadratic in the missed angle with sharp coefficient \(2^{1/p-1}(1-p)/2\).
