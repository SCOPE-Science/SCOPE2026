On the four-point diamond order, isotonic least-squares regression has the exact two-input graph-variation amplification \(\bigl(1+\max\{r,r^{-1}\}\bigr)/2\) when opposite Hasse edges have weights \(r,1,1,r\). A concrete pair of inputs attains the sharp coefficient, quantifying the deterioration under directional anisotropy rather than merely detecting noncontractivity.
