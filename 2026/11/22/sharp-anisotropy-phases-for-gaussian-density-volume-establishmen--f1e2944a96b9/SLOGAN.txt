For Gaussian point dispersal with fixed mean squared displacement and a finite anisotropy bound, the minimum founder mass needed to exceed both a density cutoff and a target spatial volume has an exact three-regime anisotropy law: maximal anisotropy, determinant matching, then isotropy. In dimension \(d\), the intermediate regime has the sharp linear threshold \(N_{\min}=\alpha V(2\pi e/d)^{d/2}/v_d\); this is a static screening result, not proof of long-term establishment.
