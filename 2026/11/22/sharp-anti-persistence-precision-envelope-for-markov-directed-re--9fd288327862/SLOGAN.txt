For bounded renewal rewards whose signs form a stationary two-state Markov chain, the exact long-time current variance has a sharp minimum at maximal admissible anti-persistence, with a universal fixed-bias suppression factor \(|m|\) for the orientation component. At identical one-time sign frequencies, a Markov-directed walker can exceed the precision bound derived for independent directional choices; this is an explicit boundary of the independence assumption, not a violation within its published scope.
