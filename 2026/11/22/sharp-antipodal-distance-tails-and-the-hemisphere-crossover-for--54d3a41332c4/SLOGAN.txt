For independent surface-area-uniform points on a spherical lune of opening \(0<\alpha<\pi\), the near-antipodal distance probability is \((1-\alpha\cot\alpha)\varepsilon^4/(16\alpha^2)+O_\alpha(\varepsilon^6)\), with a strictly increasing coefficient that determines the opening. As the lune approaches a hemisphere on the scale \(\pi-\alpha=\lambda\varepsilon\), a sharp integral crossover changes the power from four to three, with the hemispherical constant \(1/(3\pi)\).
