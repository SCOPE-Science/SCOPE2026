For two unequal antipodal Poisson sources on a unit sphere in \(\mathbb R^d\), the weaker angular maximum survives exactly when the mass imbalance is less than \(\tanh((d+2)\operatorname{artanh}r)\). The complete extremum geometry and sharp critical law are explicit: the disappearing peak has quadratic height above its neighboring latitude minimum, which approaches at a square-root angular rate, with a quartically flat weak pole at equality.
