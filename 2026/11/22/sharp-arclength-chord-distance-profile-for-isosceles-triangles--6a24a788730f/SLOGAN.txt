For the isosceles triangle with unit equal sides and base ratio \(t\), the perimeter-normalized mean distance of two independent arclength-uniform boundary points rises strictly to its unique equilateral maximum \((2+\log3)/18\), then falls to the sharp nonattained lower value \(1/6\). The two distinct degenerate triangles approach that same lower value at different logarithmic orders, \(t^2\log(1/t)\) and \((2-t)\log(1/(2-t))\).
