At the critical unequal-weight nilpotent shift, the two previously known monomial spectral-set extremizers have different sharp local Hardy-space stability constants: \(1/\sqrt3\) around \(z\) and \(1\) around \(z^2\), measured against the square-root spectral deficit. A universal coefficient-mixing inequality \(3-\|f(A)\|^2\ge2|f'(0)|^2(1-|f'(0)|^2)\) quantitatively separates the two extremizer classes.
