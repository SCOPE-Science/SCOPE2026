For strictly convex parallel-opposite hexagons of prescribed Minkowski asymmetry \(1<s<2\), the normalized Wigner-caustic area fills exactly \(\bigl[3(s-1)^2/(4(4s-s^2-1)),\;(s-1)/(4(3-s))\bigr)\). The lower endpoint has a unique affine threefold-symmetric equality class, the upper endpoint is attained only in a degenerate limit, and an exact variance identity measures the gap.
