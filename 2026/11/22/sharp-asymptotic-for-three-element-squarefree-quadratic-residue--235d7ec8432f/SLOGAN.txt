Among unordered triples of distinct squarefree integers in \(\{2,\ldots,N\}\), those containing a square modulo every prime have count \((\kappa+o(1))N^{3/2}\), with \(\kappa=\tfrac23\prod_p(1-p^{-1})^3(1+3p^{-1})\). Unique pairwise-coprime factor coordinates turn the residue-covering question into a lattice-volume calculation and a squarefree sieve.
