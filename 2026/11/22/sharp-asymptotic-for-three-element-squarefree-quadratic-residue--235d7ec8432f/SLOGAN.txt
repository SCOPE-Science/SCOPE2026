Among three distinct positive squarefree integers at most \(N\), the triples that contain a quadratic residue modulo every prime have exact leading count \(\kappa N^{3/2}\), where \(\kappa=\frac23\prod_p(1-p^{-1})^3(1+3p^{-1})\). Each such triple has a unique pairwise-coprime factor form \((uv,uw,vw)\), converting the residue-covering problem to a sharp lattice-volume and squarefree-sieve law.
