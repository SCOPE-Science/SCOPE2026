Among complete-bipartite Laplacian-column simplices of increasing dimension, the largest volume-normalized Euclidean boundary area occurs asymptotically at a unique **unbalanced** shore ratio: the smaller shore has fraction \(0.128419268660\ldots\) of the vertices. The limiting area quotient divided by dimension is \(3.095514491980\ldots\), about \(13.88\%\) above the balanced partition's limit, and exact facet-area formulas prove the result uniformly down to star graphs.
