For \(q\) finite linear orders, \(m\) named parameters and \(s\) visible immediate-successor relations, exactly \(m+(m+1)^q+2sm\) atomic one-point types can be realized, and this is sharp for every prescribed parameter ordering. For a uniform random marked subset of one finite order, exact saturation has probability \(\binom{N-3m-1}{m}/\binom Nm\), with a sharp square-root threshold and limiting profile \(e^{-3c^2}\).
