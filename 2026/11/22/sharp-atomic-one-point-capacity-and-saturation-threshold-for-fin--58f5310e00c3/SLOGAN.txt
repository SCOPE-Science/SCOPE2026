For \(q\) finite linear orders, \(m\) named parameters and \(s\) named exact successor relations, the sharp maximum number of atomic one-point types realized in one structure is \(m+(m+1)^q+2sm\), for any prescribed ordering of the parameters. A uniformly marked single order attains its maximum with probability \(\binom{N-3m-1}{m}/\binom Nm\), giving the square-root-scale profile \(e^{-3c^2}\) when \(m\to\infty\) and \(m/\sqrt N\to c<\infty\).
