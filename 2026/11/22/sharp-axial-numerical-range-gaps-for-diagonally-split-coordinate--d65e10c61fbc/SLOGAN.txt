For the diagonally split coordinate flip on complex \(\ell_4^2\), the exact imaginary-axis numerical-range deficit is \(\frac{1}{2\sqrt2}(1-(1+2\delta^2)^{-1/2})\), with explicit extremizers and sharp quadratic onset. For every fixed \(1<p<\infty\), \(p\ne2\), the analogous axial gap has a strictly positive, explicitly determined quadratic coefficient, and its imaginary-axis height decays as \(|p-2|/(2p|\delta|)\) under large splitting.
