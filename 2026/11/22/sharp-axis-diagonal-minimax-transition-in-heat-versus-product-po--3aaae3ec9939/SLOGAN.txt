The optimal \(L^2\)-operator-norm approximation of \(d\)-torus heat smoothing by a tensor product of Poisson smoothers has a positive sharp small-time error \(E_d\), and every optimal Poisson time satisfies \(s=c_d\sqrt t+O_d(t^{3/2})\). The worst Fourier direction switches exactly once from coordinate axes to balanced diagonals, and the optimal error rises strictly to \(1\) as dimension grows.
