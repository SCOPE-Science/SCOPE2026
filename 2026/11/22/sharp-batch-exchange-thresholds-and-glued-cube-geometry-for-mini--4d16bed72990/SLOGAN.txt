For minimum triangle-edge transversals of any labelled complete tripartite graph \(K_{a,b,c}\) with \(2\le a\le b\le c\), every nontrivial optimum-preserving exchange requires batch size at least \(b\) when \(a<b=c\), or at least \(n\) when \(a=b=c=n\). At the threshold, the graph of optimum covers is respectively a Boolean cube \(Q_a\) or three copies of \(Q_n\) pairwise glued at antipodal corners, the latter having exactly \(3\cdot2^n-3\) states and diameter \(\lfloor3n/2\rfloor\); with a unique largest class the optimum is isolated.
