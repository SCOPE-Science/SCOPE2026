# Self-review: bilateral-front correlation under complete resetting

## Correctness — PASS
The exact exponential common-age Gaussian-scale representation gives an identity for the entire finite-\(N\) Pearson coefficient. Direct Gaussian order-statistic integrations yield the signed small-population cases \(N=2,3,4\) and show the first positive population is four. The two-sided Gaussian extreme-value theorem, with explicitly bounded fourth moments, proves the asymptotic covariance and the sharp \((\log N)^{-2}\) coefficient. All positive-rate and finite-\(N\) hypotheses are explicit. The numerical checker passed 28 independent deterministic comparisons; these do not establish an infinite-population theorem.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)
The main 2022 Brownian reset gas paper and the 2023 general conditionally independent order-statistic paper were inspected in accessible full text. The 2025 renewal resetting generalization was inspected in full PDF. The relevant published adjacent-gap correlation theorem and opposite-tail count covariance theorem were also examined directly. These sources provide known joint-law premises and different observables; none states the assessed paired-extrema sign onset and precise logarithm-squared correlation coefficient. Equivalent unindexed Gaussian order-statistics or copula results remain possible, and this is a priority risk, not a claim of universal uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Extreme spatial fronts, not arbitrary indices, are natural physical measurements for a many-particle reset gas. The four-particle crossover and exact slow synchronization rate quantify the effect of a common reset age on joint extremes, a feature not fixed by individual extreme marginals. No broader generality, new stationary law, or finite-\(N\) monotonicity is asserted.
