The negative-binomial–logarithmic conflation law has two separated count-mode components exactly when \(2<r<7\) and \(H(r)^{-1}<\theta<\min\{1,4/(r+1)\}\), with explicit integer-computable \(H(r)\). Its two-mode region opens quadratically at shape two and closes linearly at shape seven, and at \(r=6\), \(\theta=3/(2\sqrt7)\), counts one and three are exactly tied global modes.
