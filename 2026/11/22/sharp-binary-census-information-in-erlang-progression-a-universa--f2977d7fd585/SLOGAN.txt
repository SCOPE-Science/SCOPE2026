In a synchronous \(k\)-stage Erlang progression model, retaining just completed-versus-pending cohort counts has one globally D-optimal follow-up cutoff, even among all binary summaries of exact completion times. As \(k\to\infty\), the optimal normalized cutoff is \(k+2/[3(\pi-2)]+o(1)\), and the retained rate information tends to \(2/\pi\) with an explicit positive \(1/k\) correction.
