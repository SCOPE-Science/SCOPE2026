For signed permutations, the type-B Eulerian row at every \(n\ge2\) agrees with the Pascal row modulo exactly \(2^{1+\nu_2(n(n-1))}\), and the coefficient at one descent proves sharpness. At the power-of-two rows, this gives the entire two-adic valuation spectrum: \(\nu_2(B(2^r,k))=r-\nu_2(k)\) for all interior indices, with explicit multiplicities.
