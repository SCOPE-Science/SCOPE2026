For a fair binary step-reinforced walk with fixed copy probability \(0<\alpha<1\), every odd-time position is exactly uniform, while the even-time distance to uniform has the sharp asymptotic \(d_n\sim R_\alpha^{-n}/(1+\alpha)\), where \(R_\alpha\) is an explicit beta integral. The full finite-time parity discrepancy has an inverse-beta generating function, giving the exponential rate and constant, including the critical case \(R_{1/2}=\pi/2\).
