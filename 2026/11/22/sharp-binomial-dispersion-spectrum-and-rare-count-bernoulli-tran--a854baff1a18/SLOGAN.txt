For the 2024 bimodal beta-binomial family on \(\{0,\ldots,n\}\), every integer \(n\ge2\) has the full, sharp equimean-binomial dispersion interval \((0,n)\), including genuine underdispersion despite the overdispersed baseline. At each nonzero fixed tilt, a rare-count limit becomes a Bernoulli law with an explicit sharp \(\beta^{-1/2}\) total-variation convergence rate.
