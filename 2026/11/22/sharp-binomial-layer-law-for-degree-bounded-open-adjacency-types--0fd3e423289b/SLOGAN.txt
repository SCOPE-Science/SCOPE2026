For every positive maximum-degree bound \(d\), the exact number of open-adjacency types over \(m\) named vertices is given by a sharp sequence of binomial-cardinality layers with balanced partial layers, and every optimum has a host with precisely one vertex per type. In the stable range \(m\ge d\), the value is \(1+\lfloor m(d+1)/2\rfloor\), while full shattering occurs exactly when \(d\ge2^{m-1}\).
