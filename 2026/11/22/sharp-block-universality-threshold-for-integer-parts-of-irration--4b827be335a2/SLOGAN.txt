For a degree-\(d\) real polynomial with irrational leading coefficient, the word \(\lfloor P(n)\rfloor\bmod m\) contains exactly \(m^d\min(m,2^d)\) different consecutive blocks of length \(d+1\); every such block has positive frequency. All these blocks occur precisely when \(m\le2^d\), and an explicit weighted-uniform convolution gives every frequency independently of the lower coefficients.
