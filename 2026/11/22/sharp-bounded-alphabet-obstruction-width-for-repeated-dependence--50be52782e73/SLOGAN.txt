For \(r\) separate copies of \(\operatorname{dep}(x,y)\) and one reverse-dependence clause, finite-team falsity on at most \(q\) values of \(y\) has the sharp minimal-obstruction cutoff \((r+1)(q+1)\) whenever \(q\geq r+1\); below this palette threshold all teams satisfy the split. The largest minimal obstructions are exactly the \((r+1)\)-uniform row-neighborhood families on \(q+1\) rows satisfying a proper-subset Hall sparsity condition, and cyclic windows attain the cutoff for every parameter pair.
