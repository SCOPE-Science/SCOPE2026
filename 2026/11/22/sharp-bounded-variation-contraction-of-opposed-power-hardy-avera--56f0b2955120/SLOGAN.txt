Two oppositely oriented normalized power-Hardy averages, each individually of \(BV\) seminorm one, form a strict contraction in either order, with exact constants determined by unique incomplete-beta crossing equations and attained only by one-jump inputs. The two orders can be inequivalent: for power parameters \((\alpha,\beta)=(1,2)\), their sharp constants are \(1/2\) and \(0.512390465\ldots\), respectively.
