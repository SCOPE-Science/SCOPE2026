The sharp total-variation-to-weighted-Haar energy constant stays exactly \(1/2\) through smoothness \(s=0\), with a unique midpoint-jump extremizer, but increases immediately for positive smoothness and becomes unbounded at \(s=1/2\). Approaching that boundary, its squared blow-up has the exact coefficient \(1/9\) and the energy-maximizing jump's discounted dyadic orbit concentrates on the two-cycle \(\{1/3,2/3\}\).
