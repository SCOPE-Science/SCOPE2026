For discontinuous signals the exact variation amplification, rather than the Hilbert-space projector norm, measures edge overshoot. The result gives a frequency-uniform sharp penalty, shows exactly which jump phases realize it, and quantitatively distinguishes selective odd-harmonic projection from the full cubic spectral cutoff. It supplies a reproducible benchmark for sparse spectral truncation on BV inputs without claiming a general theorem for all frequency sets.
