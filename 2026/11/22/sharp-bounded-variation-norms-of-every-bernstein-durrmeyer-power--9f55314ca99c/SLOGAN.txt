Every positive power of the classical Bernstein–Durrmeyer operator has an exact sharp real-BV variation norm given by a finite alternating odd-Legendre eigenvalue sum, uniquely attained by a jump at the midpoint. Its sharp long-power decay is \(\frac32(n/(n+2))^r\), substantially faster than iterating the already-known one-step coefficient \(1-2^{-n}\).
