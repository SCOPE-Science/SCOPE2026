The interpolation rule and parameter are completely defined on bi-infinite real sequences of finite discrete variation. Constant preservation and the absolutely summable jump decomposition reduce its induced variation norm exactly to a single step. The step is supported on three active cubic cells. Keys (1981) states the interpolation-kernel formulas and approximative properties. Meijering and Unser (2003) derive osculatory equivalences and further kernel families, not the variation norm. Comparisons with mathematically related database records found a fixed-parameter variation constant and bounded-monotone range bounds, neither of which gives the whole real-parameter variation law by implication. This is a qualified best-knowledge assessment, not evidence of independent publication priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The cubic convolution family is a standard model in signal and image resampling. The new claim completely classifies its exact variation stability, with no finite-grid selection or numerical extrapolation. It identifies the sharp penalty of slope overshoot and the first central-cell monotonicity failure as a nonanalytic threshold; in the usual \(0\le\lambda\le1\) design interval it gives an exact linear stability tradeoff. The full parameter classification and its extremizer are mathematical objects useful for choosing interpolation tension under a variation budget, beyond restating one known standard parameter value.
