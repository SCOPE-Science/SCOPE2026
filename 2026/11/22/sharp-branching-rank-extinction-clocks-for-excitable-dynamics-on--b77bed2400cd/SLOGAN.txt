For threshold-\(\theta\) Greenberg–Hastings excitation on every nonempty finite forest with \(\theta\ge2\) and \(\kappa\ge3\) states, the sharp worst-case extinction time is exactly \(H_\theta(F)+\kappa-1\), where \(H_\theta(F)\) is the tallest complete \(\theta\)-ary subtree height. Among all \(n\)-vertex forests this becomes the attained logarithmic clock \(\kappa-2+\lfloor\log_\theta((\theta-1)n+1)\rfloor\), because every late excitation forces a disjoint branching causal witness.
