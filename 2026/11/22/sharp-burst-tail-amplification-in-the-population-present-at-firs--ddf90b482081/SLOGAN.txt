At an exceptionally early target binding in a burst-produced Brownian search population, the living particle count follows the size-biased production batch, and its conditional mean has a sharp transition at the burst second-moment threshold. For \(\Pr(J=j)\sim c j^{-1-\alpha}\), it grows exponentially in \(1/t\) when \(1<\alpha<2\), grows as \(1/t\) at \(\alpha=2\), and approaches a finite value when \(\alpha>2\), despite finite ordinary population means throughout.
