For every powered Fejér–Jackson kernel with integer exponent \(q\ge2\), the sharp real periodic-BV to unnormalized \(L^1\) approximation error is its exact first circular moment, attained only by two-level semicircle signals. The loss from displacing the extremal arc has order two for odd base degree but order \(2q+2\) for even base degree; for the classical Jackson square, the sharp constant satisfies \(nC_{n,2}\to6\log2/\pi\).
