For every alphabet size \(q\ge2\), the maximum size of a \(3\times3\) cross-bibifix-free matrix code is exactly \(q^6b(q-b)^2\), where \(b=\lfloor(q+1)/3\rfloor\). For \(q\ge3\) every optimum is a lifted two-block diagonal construction, but the binary case has exactly \(32\) optimal codebooks, including \(28\) that are not complete off-diagonal lifts.
