For every nonzero two-frequency complex exponential sum with distinct real frequencies, the sharp energy expansion from a centered interval of radius \(r\) to one of radius \(R>r\) is the unattained supremum \((R/r)^3\). Consequently the exact normalized two-sided exterior Fourier transfer constant is \(\Gamma_2(\delta)=2((1+\delta)^3-1)\), approached only through coalescing-frequency linear profiles.
