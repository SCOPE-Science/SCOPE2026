For the parabolic circle flows \(\dot\theta=(1-\cos\theta)^\gamma\), \(\gamma\ge1/2\), the time-average Koopman semigroup has an exact, dimension-free Lipschitz-to-uniform error attained when the initial orbit reaches the antipode halfway through the averaging interval. Its sharp decay changes from \(T^{-1}\) to \((\log T)/T\) at \(\gamma=1\), then to \(T^{-1/(2\gamma-1)}\), while its norm on all continuous observables remains exactly \(2\) at every finite time.
