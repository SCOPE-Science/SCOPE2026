A nonnegative two-harmonic circular smoothing kernel has a sharp, attained bounded-variation contraction coefficient whose worst-case input jumps switch abruptly from antipodal to shorter arcs when the second-to-first Fourier multiplier ratio crosses \(\sqrt{(11+5\sqrt5)/2}/4\). Under repeated smoothing, the ratio is raised to successive powers, so a single operator can change its worst-case arc geometry between its first and second application.
