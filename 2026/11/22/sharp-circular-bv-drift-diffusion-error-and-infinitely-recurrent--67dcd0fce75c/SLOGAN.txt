For circular heat flow with any constant nonzero drift, the exact real \(BV\)-to-\(L^1\) identity error is the mean geodesic displacement of a wrapped drifted Gaussian, and only two-level half-circle steps are extremizers. Unlike zero-drift diffusion, its sharp approximation error oscillates forever around \(\pi/2\), with an explicit exponentially accurate alternating sequence of peak and trough times.
