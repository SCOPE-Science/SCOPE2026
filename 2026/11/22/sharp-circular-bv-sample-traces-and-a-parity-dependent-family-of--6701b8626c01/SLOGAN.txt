For \(N\) circular point measurements, the sharp mean-zero periodic \(W^{1,1}\)-to-\(\ell^2\) sampling constant is minimized at squared value \((\lfloor N^2/4\rfloor+1)/(4N)\). Surprisingly, when \(N\equiv2\pmod4\), all alternating-gap designs with contrast at most \(\sqrt2-1\) are equally optimal; otherwise only equal spacing is optimal.
