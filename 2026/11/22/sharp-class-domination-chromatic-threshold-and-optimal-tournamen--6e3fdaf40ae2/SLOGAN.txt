For \(n\ge5\), the dominated (class-domination) chromatic number of \(\operatorname{KG}(n,2)\) equals \(n-1\) exactly at \(n=5,6,7\) and drops to the ordinary chromatic value \(n-2\) from \(n=8\) onward. Every optimum from order eight is a triangle plus a bounded-outdegree tournament on the remaining ground elements, giving exact optimal-colouring counts.
