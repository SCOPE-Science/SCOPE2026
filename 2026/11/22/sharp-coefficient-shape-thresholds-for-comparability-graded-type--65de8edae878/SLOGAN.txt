In the generic homogeneous poset, counting singleton types by the number of named comparable parameters gives a sharp coefficient-shape transition on disjoint unions of chains: unimodality first fails at eight parameters, uniquely for chain sizes \((3,5)\), while log-concavity first fails at six, uniquely for \((2,4)\) and \((3,3)\). For two chains of lengths \(a\le b\), the entire unimodality and log-concavity classification is explicit and remains valid at every size.
