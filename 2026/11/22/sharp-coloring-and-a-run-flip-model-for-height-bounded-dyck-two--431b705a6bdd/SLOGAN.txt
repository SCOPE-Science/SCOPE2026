For every height ceiling at least two, the two-read deletion-confusability graph of semilength-\(n\) Dyck words has exactly \(n\) as both its chromatic and clique numbers. At height two it becomes a constant-block-flip graph on \(n-1\) bits, with exact run-based degrees, \((n-2)2^{n-1}+1\) edges, and induced five-cycles from \(n=5\) onward.
