Finite oriented cyclic orders with \(q\) named colours have a complete gap-based one-point-extension rank formula; rank at least \(t\ge1\) first occurs at precisely \(q2^{t-1}\) points, with \(2^{t-1}\) points forced in each colour. At the first nontrivial binary threshold, exactly \(62\) labelled eight-point cyclic colour words attain rank three, forming \(9\) orientation-preserving rotation types.
