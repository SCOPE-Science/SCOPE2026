Two equal-width integer block-averaging grids, staggered by any nonzero offset, have an alternating-projection step that is exactly a discrete heat step; their commutator has a complete imaginary essential spectral interval and a sharp \(1/8\) diffusivity threshold for attaining the maximal norm \(1/2\). A single normalized block impulse decays at the exact rate \(N^{-1/4}\), with each vanishing coarse-grid moment accelerating decay by a further \(N^{-1/2}\), even though the iteration never converges in operator norm.
