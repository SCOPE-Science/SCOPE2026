# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The all-dimensional statement follows from an exact step decomposition modulo constants, an interval-of-activity representation for each crossing pair, and the bijection to the edges of the complete graph on \(m\) vertices. Each pair increment lies in \([-1,1]\) after dividing by the step size. Maximizing over signs gives at most twice the balanced maximum cut. The arithmetic-progression input and a one-unit step supply a *finite*, exact attaining pair at every \(n\ge2m\). This proof does not assume generic distinctness for the bound or approximate numerics. The accompanying script tested exhaustive abstract crossing signs through width six and rational witnesses and random pairs.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

The relevant 2014 full-text preprint establishes the Gini sample statistic and statistical efficiency but does not concern perturbations of **overlapping output windows**. The inspected 2016 full-text scale-change article uses the pairwise absolute difference kernel and an ordinary Lipschitz/variation condition in a dependent-data limit theorem; that condition does not entail the exact \(m\)-dependent adjacent-variation coefficient or finite attaining examples. The indexed comparisons on moving median, moving MAD, moving variance and output Gini metrics address different operators or seminorms. Specific unindexed equivalences cannot be excluded; this is a historical-priority limitation, not a claim of global exhaustive novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Gini mean difference is a natural scale statistic with well-studied robustness and efficiency tradeoffs. Its overlapping-window perturbation stability is not encoded by those statistical properties. The exact parity staircase \(2,4/3,4/3,6/5,6/5,\ldots\), near-nonexpansive large-window behavior, and finite worst-case witnesses supply an operational sharp stability budget. The cut-graph proof also applies to the entire class of even one-Lipschitz pairwise contrasts; that additional universal structure is mathematically more informative than a finite table calculation.
