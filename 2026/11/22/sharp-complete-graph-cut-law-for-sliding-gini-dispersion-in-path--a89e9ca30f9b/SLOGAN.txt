For the mean of all absolute pairwise differences in each consecutive window of width \(m\), the exact and attained two-input adjacent-variation gain is \(2\lfloor m^2/4\rfloor/\binom{m}{2}\) once the path has at least \(2m\) sites. A complete-graph maximum-cut identity proves the sharp bound and extends it as a universal envelope to all even one-Lipschitz pairwise-difference kernels.
