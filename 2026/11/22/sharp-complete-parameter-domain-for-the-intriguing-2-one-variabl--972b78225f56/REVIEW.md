# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The mixed density has a strictly positive factor multiplying an explicit scalar function. The proof minimizes this scalar function globally over every interior point and proves both necessity (negative local density outside the parameter region) and sufficiency (integrated nonnegative density and correct margins) with all endpoint cases included. Independent symbolic differentiation and numerical consistency checks are documented in the checker; finite tests are not the basis for infinite-domain conclusions.

**Originality — PASS (best-knowledge, qualified).** Proposition 7 of Chesneau (2023) concerns the identical formula but gives a sufficient range, while the new classification is necessary and sufficient on the whole real parameter plane and yields a genuinely larger region and a transcendental terminal boundary. Searches for the exact Int2 formula, power-copula aliases and prior exact parameter domains did not identify a stronger covering result. Related rational, polynomial and other power copulas use different joint distributions. The 2024 modified Ali–Mikhail–Haq paper was compared at abstract level only; historical priority cannot be certified exhaustively.

**Value — PASS.** Admissible parameters define which purported dependence models are actual copulas. The exact classification enables valid use of previously excluded negative shapes, positive shape values above one and exponents exceeding one. The unique analytic terminal boundary prevents invalid density-based statistical models, replacing conservative sufficient constraints with a complete test.
