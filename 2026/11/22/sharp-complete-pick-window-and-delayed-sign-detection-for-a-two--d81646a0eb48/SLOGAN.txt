For the Hardy coefficient norms \(1,a,ab,ab,\ldots\), complete Pick interpolation holds exactly when \(a\ge1\) and \(4a/(a+1)^2\le b\le a\), including not expansive, nonconcave shifts. Just below the sharp lower boundary, the first negative coefficient in \(1-1/K\) occurs at a precisely determined index diverging like \((4a/(a+1)^2-b)^{-1/2}\) with an explicit sharp constant.
