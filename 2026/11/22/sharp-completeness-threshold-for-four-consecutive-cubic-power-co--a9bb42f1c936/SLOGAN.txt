All 81 four-letter cubic-residue coset-color words occur among four consecutive nonzero residues for every prime \(p\equiv1\pmod3\) with \(p\ge571\), and 39 precisely identified smaller primes are the only exceptions. At \(p=547\), a mixed word is the sole obstruction, even though all-four-cubic words have been available since \(p>379\).
