For a natural two-term fractional-moment family of unilateral weighted shifts, the \(m\)-concavity boundary is an explicit sharp finite-difference quotient, followed by a strictly ordered, uniformly separated staircase in the rank of the positive \(m\)-th defect. Every defect has the same universal \(j^{-m}\) singular-value tail and belongs to \(\mathcal S_p\) exactly when \(p>1/m\), regardless of the perturbation parameter.
