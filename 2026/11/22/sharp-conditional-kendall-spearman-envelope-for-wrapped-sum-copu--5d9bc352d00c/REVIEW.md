# Review: sharp fixed-Spearman Kendall envelope

## Correctness: PASS
The result is precisely about bivariate unreflected wrapped-sum copulas generated by arbitrary Lebesgue densities. The published population-coefficient identities reduce the claim to minimizing the centered squared \(L^2\)-norm of the generator CDF at fixed centered second moment. For nonpositive Spearman rho, Cauchy–Schwarz gives the exact affine-CDF lower bound; CDF endpoint continuity excludes its attainment except for the uniform generator. A normalized, explicit endpoint-mixture density retains the prescribed second moment exactly and approaches the bound. For positive rho, the CDF of the centered uniform interval is a constrained minimizer by an exact obstacle-sign comparison and nonnegative centered squared difference; strict equality forces that CDF. All endpoint domains and strictness conditions are included. Exact-rational piecewise-density calculations support the critical identities; finite tests are not used to prove the continuum claim.

## Originality: PASS, with historical-priority limitations
Lalancette–Zimmerman (2022, arXiv:2204.04336) introduced the family. Their 2025 follow-up (arXiv:2509.26635), which was read in its relevant full-text sections and proof, **already** establishes the separate rank formulas, the unconditional \(1/6\) Kendall maximum and centered-uniform maximizer, and \(\xi=2\rho-3\tau\ge0\), hence \(\tau\le2\rho/3\). None of these statements is presented as new. At every nonzero rho the conditional sharp upper envelope proved here is **strictly below** that known general family bound, and supplies a previously unprovided exact fixed-rho extremizer structure including an unattained negative branch. The unrestricted exact rho–tau region of Schreyer–Paulin–Trutschnig applies to all copulas and does not establish this sharper subclass boundary. The fixed-concordance extreme-value-copula literature uses a different copula class. Closest accessible mathematical findings on asymmetric quadratic and two-mode Legendre copulas likewise have different defining densities and cannot imply this conditional formula without an additional, nontrivial reduction not found in their statements. Searches and source reading do not establish priority universally; equivalent unindexed literature remains a risk.

## Mathematical value: PASS
For a previously studied probability family with independently known rank coefficients, conditioning on a fixed Spearman value is a natural statistical calibration question. The result solves the resulting nontrivial constrained CDF variational problem exactly, not just globally over all rho, identifies all equality conditions, and distinguishes attained uniform-generator regimes from nonattained endpoint-limit regimes. It supplies a strict quantitative improvement over the known \(2\rho/3\) relation at every nonzero rho. This is a model-specific structural concordance boundary, not merely a numerical instance or routine substitution.
