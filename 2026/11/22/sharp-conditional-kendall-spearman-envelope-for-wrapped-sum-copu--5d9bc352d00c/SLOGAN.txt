For wrapped-sum copulas, Kendall's tau has an exact sharp upper envelope at each fixed Spearman rho: \((2\rho-\rho^2)/3\) for negative rho, and \(\frac23(\sqrt{1-2\rho}-(1-2\rho))\) for nonnegative rho. Positive-rho bounds are uniquely attained by centered-uniform generators; negative-rho bounds are strict but approachable at exactly fixed rho by explicit endpoint-mixture densities.
