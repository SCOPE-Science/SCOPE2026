# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The statement is restricted to the complete-refresh limit of ordinary BAOAB on the unit harmonic oscillator and two strictly stable alternating steps. The full-refresh BAOAB map is reduced exactly to a one-step position recurrence with one reused and one fresh Gaussian. The cross-covariance with the reused normal is carried through the two coupled stationary variance equations. Both phase expressions, their sum and difference, the stable denominator factorization, and the fourth cumulant follow by exact algebra. The strict upper bound \(2\) is proved over the complete open square \((0,2)^2\) by minimizing the admissible product of the step lengths at each fixed sum, not by a finite grid. The boundary supremum is unattained. The independent matrix-covariance implementation and symbolic checks in the bundled verifier are supplementary.

## Originality — PASS (publication-priority qualified)
Leimkuhler–Matthews (2013) establish exact harmonic configurational covariance for a **fixed** BAOAB step; their constant transition matrix does not furnish a two-phase covariance or the global unequal-step ceiling. Fass and collaborators (first public preprint 2018-02-16) analyze errors of established fixed-timestep schemes and the high-friction motivation, not the two-periodic stationary law. Kieninger–Keller (2022, openly readable preprint) prove an equivalence among GSD, BAOA and BAOAB at constant step length, not periodic unequal-step phase laws. The related alternating-Verlet theorem concerns an unthermostatted deterministic map, so its Floquet boundaries do not imply the thermally refreshed invariant variance. Exact formula, phase hot/cold boundary, sharp factor-two bound and mixture fourth cumulant were compared by statement rather than title. No searched accessible result covered all four, but a historically equivalent unindexed statement remains possible.

## Value — PASS
Fixed-step exact Gibbs configurational sampling is a major reason for using BAOAB on harmonic modes. Periodic variable steps are a natural minimal departure from that setting. This theorem characterizes the entire two-step stable parameter square, shows unavoidable phase-mean heating, classifies the surprising cooler short-step phase, proves the best possible global inflation bound, and identifies intrinsic non-Gaussianity of unlabelled phase observations even though both individual phase laws are Gaussian. The facts matter for interpreting equilibrium diagnostics when a sampler alternates timesteps; they are not claimed to improve trajectory accuracy or describe a general time-adaptive method.

## Closest literature and limitations
The fixed-step canonical covariance appears in B. Leimkuhler and C. Matthews, *J. Chem. Phys.* 138, 174102 (2013), DOI 10.1063/1.4802990. Configurational bias and high-friction context appear in J. Fass et al., *Entropy* 20, 318 (2018), DOI 10.3390/e20050318 (preprint DOI 10.1101/266619). BAOA/BAOAB fixed-step equivalence is treated by S. Kieninger and B. G. Keller, arXiv:2204.02105 (2022). The primary results inspected do not address this exact two-periodic full-refresh regime. Full-refresh, stable individual steps, and equal observation count are necessary scope restrictions. Independent historical and mathematical validation have not been conducted.
