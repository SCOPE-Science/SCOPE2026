For harmonic BAOAB with alternating stable steps and a complete momentum refresh, unequal step lengths destroy exact configurational sampling: the sharp equally phase-averaged position variance is \(1+4(s-t)^2/[32-(4-st)(s+t)^2]\), strictly between \(1\) and \(2\). The two phase variances admit exact hot/cold classifications, and a uniformly sampled phase has a strictly non-Gaussian position mixture whenever the steps differ.
