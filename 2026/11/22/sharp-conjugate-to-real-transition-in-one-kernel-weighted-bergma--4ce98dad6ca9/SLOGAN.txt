Best one-kernel approximation of \(1+a z-z^2\) in weighted disk Bergman spaces has two conjugate optimal nodes exactly for negative standard weights below the sharp curve \(a_c=2\sqrt{4-\lambda^2}/\lambda\), where \(\lambda=\alpha+2\in(1,2)\). Their common radius stays fixed as \(a\) varies, and they merge continuously into one positive-real optimum with a square-root angular law and a kink in the optimal radius.
