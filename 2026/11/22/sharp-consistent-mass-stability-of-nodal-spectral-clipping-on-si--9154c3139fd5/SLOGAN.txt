Nodal spectral clipping of piecewise-affine symmetric tensor fields has the sharp mesh-independent consistent-\(L^2\) Lipschitz factor \((d+3)/(2\sqrt{d+2})\), exactly attained by finite inputs on a single \(d\)-simplex. Scalar clipping is strictly less amplifying in even spatial dimensions, whereas the lumped-mass version remains nonexpansive.
