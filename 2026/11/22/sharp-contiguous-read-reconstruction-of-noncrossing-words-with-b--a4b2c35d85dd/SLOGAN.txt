For every read length \(k\ge2\), all noncrossing words with at most \(2k-2\) occurrences of each letter are globally reconstructible from complete contiguous factor counts through length \(k\), and the bound is sharp. Thus bounded-block noncrossing partitions admit an optimal code containing every partition, with the exact capacity encoded by \(F_k(z)=1+\sum_{j=1}^{2k-2}z^jF_k(z)^j\).
