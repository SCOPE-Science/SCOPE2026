For four-knot natural cubic interpolation with symmetric gap lengths \((1,r,1)\), all strictly increasing samples with positive secant-slope contrast at most \(\min\{r+3,3+4/r\}\) yield a nondecreasing spline, and every larger contrast admits a sharp counterexample. The unique symmetric knot geometry maximizing this unconditional monotonicity radius is \((1,2,1)\), with optimal contrast \(5\) rather than the equal-spacing value \(4\).
