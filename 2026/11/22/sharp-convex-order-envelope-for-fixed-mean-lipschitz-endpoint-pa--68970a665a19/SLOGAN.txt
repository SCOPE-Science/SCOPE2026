For fixed endpoints and mean, a real \(L\)-Lipschitz path has exactly the output laws \(L^{-1}\mathrm{Leb}+ (1-L^{-1})\nu\) with a prescribed residual mean—even when the path is not monotone. This forces simultaneous sharp Jensen-gap bounds for every convex loss, uniquely attained for strictly convex losses by a plateau-with-two-ramps path and a clipped single-ramp path.
