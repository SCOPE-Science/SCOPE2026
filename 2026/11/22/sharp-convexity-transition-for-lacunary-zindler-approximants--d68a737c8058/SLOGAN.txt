Each finite lacunary Zindler approximant bounds a convex body exactly when its paired-chord half-length is at least \(R_n=\sum_{k=1}^n(\beta^{3k}-\beta^k)/\alpha^k\). At equality, precisely \(\beta\) points have quadratic curvature zeros. The minimum convexity-preserving halving-chord length grows exponentially with truncation order, with successive-length ratio tending to \(\beta^3/\alpha\).
