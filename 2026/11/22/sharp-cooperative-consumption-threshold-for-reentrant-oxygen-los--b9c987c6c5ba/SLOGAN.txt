For a vertically mixed oxygen reservoir whose biological consumption increases according to a cooperative Hill law, deep oxygen can rise, fall, and rise again with mixing intensity precisely when \(p>1\) and \(r_1/r_0>4p/(p-1)^2\). The theorem gives both turning rates explicitly, classifies every oxygen-threshold crossing, and measures the square-root opening of the reentrant window; it is conditional on the specified phenomenological response law.
