In a planar inhibitory feedback flow, unequal decay rates impose a sharp universal half-turn barrier: repeat coincidences of a measured coordinate are separated by at least \(\pi/\sqrt{AB-(\gamma_1-\gamma_2)^2/4}\) when \(|\gamma_1-\gamma_2|<2\sqrt{AB}\). At or beyond the complementary damping threshold, any two distinct trajectories can share that coordinate at most once; both sides are sharp for bounded monotone \(C^1\) regulation.
