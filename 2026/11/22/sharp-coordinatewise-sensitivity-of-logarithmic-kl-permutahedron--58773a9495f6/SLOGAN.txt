The logarithm of KL projection onto any nondegenerate positive permutahedron has exact coordinatewise Lipschitz coefficient \(2(1-\min_i b_i/\sum_i b_i)\), with the worst amplification approached near vertices but never attained by distinct finite inputs. For entropy-regularized soft ranking this yields a sharp dimension- and smoothing-dependent stability law, strictly larger than the quadratic-regularized counterpart.
