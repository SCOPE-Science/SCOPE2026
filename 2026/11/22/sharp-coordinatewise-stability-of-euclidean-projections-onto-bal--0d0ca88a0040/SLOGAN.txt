Euclidean nearest-point projection onto a Euclidean ball intersected with a coordinate cube has an exact all-dimensional maximum-coordinate Lipschitz constant given by a finite active-face formula. At thirteen coordinates and ball radius \(\sqrt5\), a singly clipped face is strictly most sensitive, with coefficient \((3+\sqrt{33})/4\); for dimensions at least three, the optimal coefficient jumps downward when the ball first contains the whole cube.
