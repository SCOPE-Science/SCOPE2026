The Euclidean projector onto the monotone extended second-order cone has the exact coordinate-maximum Lipschitz factor \(\max\{(1+\sqrt q)/2,\ 1+pG_q/(p+1)\}\) for \(q\ge2\), where \(G_q=\max_{0\le a\le1}a(1-a+\sqrt{(q-1)(1-a^2)})\); at \(q=1\) the sharp factor is one. The competing sharp mechanisms are a two-site terminal PAVA pool and an all-site terminal pool, giving the exact example \(L_{2,6}=17/9\).
