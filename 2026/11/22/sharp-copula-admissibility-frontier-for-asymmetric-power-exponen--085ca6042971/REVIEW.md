# Scientific review: sharp asymmetric power-exponential copula frontier

## Correctness (C): PASS

The new claim is an iff statement over all finite \(a,b>0\) and \(\phi\ge0\). Uniform margins and all four boundary conditions follow from the candidate by continuity. The exact interior mixed density has sign determined by \(H=(x+at)(y+bt)-ab t\), where \(t=\phi x^a y^b\). When either exponent is below one, a boundary asymptotic makes \(H\) negative, regardless of how small positive \(\phi\) is. When both exponents are at least one, the explicit bijective \((x,y)\mapsto(r,s)\) substitution reduces global nonnegativity to the product of two one-variable infima, including the cases where an infimum is approached but not attained. Exact rational boundary and invalidity witnesses are reproduced by the embedded checker. The finite checker does not replace the continuous proof. There is no positivity assumption stronger than nonnegativity at the sharp endpoint.

## Originality (O): PASS, with historical-priority qualification

Wu, Dui and Hu (2024), equation (17) and Proposition 2, address the exact candidate but do not identify the complete validity region; the general positive assertion has subunit-exponent counterexamples. The classical \(a=b=1\) case is fully excluded from any novelty claim. Díaz and Cuadras (2022) treat a broader two-function Gumbel–Barnett construction; Chesneau (2023) presents a broad product-exponential template. These are potentially stronger frameworks, but the exact sharp monomial-exponent boundary and no-positive-coupling condition are not located in inspected material. Searches for the source DOI, the exact power-exponential form, aliases and the exact integer threshold show no known equivalent result; comparing formulas rather than titles distinguished an earlier asymmetric cross-power copula result with different functional dependence. The 2022 source's abstract and first-page preview were inspected; its unavailable full text leaves a material but not demonstrably decisive historical-priority risk. No assertion of uniqueness or exhaustive global priority is made.

## Value (V): PASS

The 2024 candidate was designed to model asymmetric bivariate failures, where applying a non-copula as a probability dependence model can make downstream reliability calculations invalid. This theorem supplies the complete sharp feasibility domain: a discontinuous obstruction at the exponent-one boundaries and a computable maximum dependence parameter elsewhere, with nontrivial asymmetric admissible examples. The result is a motivated parameter-classification problem, not an arbitrary parameter slice or numerical restatement of the density. Its value is restricted to validity and does not presume new inference guarantees.
