For the asymmetric power-exponential survival copula candidate, positive coupling is possible exactly when both logarithmic exponents are at least one, with sharp coupling ceiling \(\Phi(a,b)=(a+b-1)^{2(a+b-1)}/[a^{2a}b^{2b}(a-1)^{a-1}(b-1)^{b-1}]\). Every subunit exponent destroys copula validity at arbitrarily small positive coupling; the independent member remains valid for all positive exponents.
