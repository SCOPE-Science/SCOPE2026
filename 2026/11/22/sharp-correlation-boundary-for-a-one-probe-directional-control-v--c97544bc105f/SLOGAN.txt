A Gaussian directional reference estimated from one probe loses its control-variate protection when that probe is too correlated with the exploratory direction: on the isotropic quadratic, the sharp descent-to-mean-instability boundary is \(|\rho|=1/\sqrt{k+1}\), where \(k\) is the hint rank. Below that boundary the complete worst-case one-step mean-square factor and its optimal positive step are explicit; the result concerns this coupled-reference modification, not the published independent-reference method.
