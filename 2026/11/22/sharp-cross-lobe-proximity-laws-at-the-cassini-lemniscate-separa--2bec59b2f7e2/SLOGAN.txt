Uniform cross-lobe point pairs in the Bernoulli lemniscate have probability \(\tfrac12\varepsilon^4+O(\varepsilon^6)\) of lying within distance \(\varepsilon\). A near-separation Cassini deformation has an exact hyperbolic-cone crossover, vanishing at scaled gap \(2\) with a cubic onset and matching the quartic lemniscate law at large scales.
