The exact \(\ell_\infty\)-to-\(\ell_1\) Lipschitz constant of diagonally quadratic regularized simplex prediction is \(A-\delta^2/A\), where \(A\) is total curvature-reciprocal weight and \(\delta\) is its minimum two-way partition discrepancy; the bound is achieved by distinct full-support inputs. For binary-encoded positive integer weights, deciding whether this constant reaches \(A\) is NP-complete although pointwise prediction is efficiently computable.
