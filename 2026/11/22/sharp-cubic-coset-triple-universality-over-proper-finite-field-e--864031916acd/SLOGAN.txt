For every proper finite-field extension with a nontrivial cubic-residue quotient, all \(27\) consecutive three-term cubic-coset patterns occur exactly when the characteristic is odd and the field has at least \(121\) elements. The only odd-characteristic exceptions are \(\mathbb F_{25}\) and \(\mathbb F_{49}\), with respectively \(10\) and \(8\) missing patterns; characteristic two forces the first and third colors to coincide.
