Cubic angular modulation preserves the entire positive-definite Legendre cone supported in degrees \(n\ge2\), but fails at both excluded low degrees. Consequently, the geodesic wave \(e^{-a\theta}\cos(3\theta)\) is positive definite on \(\mathbb S^2\) exactly for \(a\ge2\sqrt2\), with strict pointwise definiteness but a missing constant mode at equality.
