For the diameter-one real-projective Veronese orbitope \(K\subset\mathbb R^5\), the partial reflection hull \(\operatorname{conv}(K\cup(-tK))\) is unchanged up to the sharp threshold \(t=1/2\), then its Euclidean mean width grows by exactly \(\sqrt3(2t-1)^3/[4(3+(2t-1)^2)]\). The onset is cubic, with a sharp coefficient \(2/\sqrt3\), so the width profile is \(C^2\) but not \(C^3\) at the transition.
