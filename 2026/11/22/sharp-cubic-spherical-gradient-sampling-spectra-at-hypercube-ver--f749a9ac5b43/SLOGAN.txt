Tangential gradients alone at every vertex of the signed cube recover all pure cubic spherical harmonics in every dimension \(d\ge4\), but miss precisely the \(x_1x_2x_3\) mode in dimension three. The complete three-sector Dirichlet-normalized spectrum has a sharp maximizing-mode crossover at \(d=9\), and the optimal squared condition number grows like \(d^2/2\).
