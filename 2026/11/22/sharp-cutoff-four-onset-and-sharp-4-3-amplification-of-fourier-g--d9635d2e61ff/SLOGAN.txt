For strictly positive two-harmonic circle diffusion at fixed Fourier-coefficient energy, the exact worst Fourier–Galerkin boundary leakage changes from a pure second harmonic to a unique mixed-harmonic amplitude allocation at cutoff \(K=4\), regardless of relative phase. The optimally mixed leakage exceeds every pure-harmonic profile's leakage by an asymptotically sharp factor of \(4/3\).
