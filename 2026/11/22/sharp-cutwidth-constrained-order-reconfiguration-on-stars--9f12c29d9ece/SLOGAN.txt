For labelled stars \(K_{1,r}\), feasible cutwidth-bounded adjacent-swap orderings split into \(\binom{r}{r/2}\) permutahedral product components exactly at the minimum width when \(r\) is even; otherwise every pair is connected by a feasible geodesic of exactly its unrestricted Kendall distance. In the connected regime every ordering has eccentricity \(\binom{r+1}{2}\), so one unit of extra cutwidth above the even-star optimum repairs both reachability and shortest-path optimality.
