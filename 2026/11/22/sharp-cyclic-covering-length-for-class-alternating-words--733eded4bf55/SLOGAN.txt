Every length-\(n\) word alternating between two disjoint alphabets fits in a single shortest circular alternating cover of length \(2(ab)^{n/2}\) for even \(n\), or \(2(ab)^{(n-1)/2}\max\{a,b\}\) for odd \(n\). When the alphabet sizes are unequal and \(n\) is odd, exactly \((ab)^{(n-1)/2}|a-b|\) repeated windows are unavoidable, and rotating-and-duplicating selected edge labels attains that bound.
