For any \(q\ge2\), a shortest circular word containing every ordinary four-letter palindrome \(abba\) has length \((5q^2+q)/2\) for odd \(q\) and \((5q^2+2q)/2\) for even \(q\). The parity jump follows from the missing Euler tour in the odd-degree complete graph; matching Euler-tour constructions attain both bounds.
