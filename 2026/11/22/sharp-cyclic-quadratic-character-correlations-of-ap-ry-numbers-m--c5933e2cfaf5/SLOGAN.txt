For classical Apéry numbers modulo seven, every cyclic quadratic-character pair count on a block of length \(7^r\) is determined by a two-branch digit-carry recurrence. The sharp largest nontrivial correlation is \(3\cdot7^{r-1}\) only at shifts \(\pm7^{r-1}\), while the sharp smallest is \(-(5\cdot7^{r-1}-2)/3\) only at shifts \(\pm5\), for every \(r\ge2\).
