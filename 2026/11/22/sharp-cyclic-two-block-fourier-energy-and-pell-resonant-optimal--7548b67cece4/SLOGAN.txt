Two equal cyclic interval blocks have the smallest possible fourth Fourier moment precisely when the group can fit three nonoverlapping convolution packets, a sharp threshold of \(n\ge6a-3\). In the entire first \(a\) steps below that threshold, the optimum is an explicit cubic minimization with exactly classified spacings, and its otherwise unique optimum bifurcates into two adjacent optima precisely at negative-Pell defect values \(t=2,26,166,\ldots\).
