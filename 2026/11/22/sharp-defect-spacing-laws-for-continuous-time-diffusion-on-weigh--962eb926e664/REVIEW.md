# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The weighted-cycle identity follows by writing two parallel arc resistances and summing edge-pair coefficients. Expansion at two resistance levels leaves a positive multiple of a discrete concave pair energy. Strict concavity establishes unique clustered minimizers; simultaneous integer Jensen smoothing and a mechanical circular construction establish all balanced maximizers, exact residues, and equality cases for every \(n\ge4\), \(2\le k\le n-2\), and \(1+\delta>0\) with nonzero contrast. A runnable standard-library checker verified 16,028 rational direct-resistance cases; independent grounded-Laplacian rational inverses verified 30 examples. A finite check is not substituted for the analytic proof.

## Originality — PASS on available evidence

Zhang and Yang (2006, DOI 10.1002/qua.21068) calculate unweighted circulant resistance indices, not heterogeneous edge-placement optima. Carmona, Encinas, and Mitjana (2014, DOI 10.1016/j.endm.2014.08.029) give general weighted-cycle/network formulae, and this stronger general evaluation coverage is explicitly credited. The checked cycle-price spectral-gap, exceptional-edge price-cutoff, weighted bouquet, and outage-design results optimize different variables or criteria. The present binary-rate cyclic rearrangement theorem, its all-count exact balanced equality classification, and gcd correction were not found to be stated or entailed directly by the inspected sources. Nonetheless a general weighted formula can be specialized, so a prior unindexed extremal rearrangement argument remains a material priority risk; no claim of independent historical certification is made.

## Value — PASS

For a physically natural periodic diffusive transport process with exactly \(k\) tunable bonds of one given conductance, knowing where to place a fixed inventory of slow/fast defects is a motivated design question. The result covers all period lengths and defect counts, gives sharp nonasymptotic mean-hitting-time bounds, completely classifies optimal patterns rather than a single test case, and exhibits the non-obvious invariance of the optima under reversing the sign of the defect contrast. The arithmetic gcd correction resolves ties when equal spacing is impossible.

## Sources and limitations

The resistance/spectral formulas predate this result. The foremost historical risk is an equivalent weighted-cycle rearrangement theorem derived elsewhere from the general 2014 formulas or circular energy inequalities. No independent audit has been performed, and the result does not apply to multilevel or directed bond disorder.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
