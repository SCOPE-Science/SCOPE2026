For complete bipartite graphs \(K_{a,b}\) with \(a\in\{3,4,5\}\) and every integer \(b\ge a\), the spanning-forest count first strictly exceeds the product of degrees at the sharp larger-shore sizes \(b=14,22,31\) for \(a=3,4,5\), respectively, and remains above forever. Consequently, the already-known \(K_{3,14}\) counterexample is the unique smallest counterexample within all complete bipartite graphs of minimum degree at least three.
