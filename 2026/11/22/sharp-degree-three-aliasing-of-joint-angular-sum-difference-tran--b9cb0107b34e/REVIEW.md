The two-to-one torus homomorphism has exactly the inverse sheets specified in the finding; the pushforward Jacobian is two. Consequently the pointwise sheet-average formula is exact for any integrable copula density. Fourier uniqueness proves the converse. Uniform marginal Fourier constraints plus the parity image lattice establish the minimal degree-three boundary, and the analytic sine-product witness is rigorously a copula. The exact integrals yield \(\rho_S=3\varepsilon/(2\pi^2)\), \(\tau_K=\varepsilon/\pi^2\), and total variation \(2\varepsilon/\pi^2\). Symbolic checks in `verify.py` passed. Scope is precisely the oracle transform, not rank plug-in tests.

## Originality — PASS (with historical-priority risk)
The full available 2023 preprint by Fernández-Durán and Gregorio-Domínguez was inspected, particularly the joining density in Section 2, the APIT independence procedure in Section 3, and the conclusions. The paper uses univariate circular uniformity under a model with a restricted joining density and asserts equivalence to independence in the general setting. It does not give the joint two-sheet characterization, a positively quadrant-dependent copula with both transforms jointly uniform, or the sharp first Fourier degree. Pewsey and Kato's earlier goodness-of-fit study concerns a different parametric joining-density setting. Related generic subindependence and copula Fourier results explain some ingredients, not the precise combined theorem. Searches of mathematical research records and the literature produced no directly covering published result. This absence is not definitive priority proof; earlier examples or algebraic transform kernels could exist outside inspected sources.

## Value — PASS
This is an exact obstruction to identifying bivariate independence from two commonly proposed circular summaries, even when they are retained jointly, and even under positive quadrant dependence. The result gives the complete absolutely-continuous null fiber, a sharp minimal trigonometric complexity threshold, concrete positive association coefficients, and an explicit detectable Fourier contrast when one original transform is retained. The obstruction is directly motivated by the general APIT equivalence statement, not an arbitrary numerical counterexample. Its implication concerns oracle experiments; the practical rank-based test is a separate scientific question.
