## Correctness — PASS

The exact normalized two-agent update yields a rational signed-imbalance recurrence. A strict sliding-window contraction proves global exponential equality for \(\beta<c\); a compact omega-limit equality-case argument proves global convergence at \(\beta=c\), where the linearized characteristic polynomial is Schur stable. For \(c<\beta<2c\), the analytic root continuation at large lag gives a multiplier outside the unit disk, while zero-delay attraction follows from the exact scalar derivative. Positivity and strict-interior hypotheses are explicitly checked. The supplementary program verifies finite exact-rational identities; the infinite statement is analytic.

This is a literature-bounded assessment, not a proof of worldwide uniqueness.

## Value — PASS

The delayed feedback extension is explicitly motivated by social fatigue and shifting influence. An exact all-history, all-delay stabilization ceiling that remains valid at its critical endpoint, together with a sharp opposite-side instability obstruction, is a meaningful structural boundary that connects the source's numerically exploratory delayed regime to analytical guarantees. It separates guaranteed influence equalization from delay-sensitive dominance dynamics without overstating oscillation or chaos. The result is about an interpretable specific model, not generic textbook delay recurrence relabeling.
