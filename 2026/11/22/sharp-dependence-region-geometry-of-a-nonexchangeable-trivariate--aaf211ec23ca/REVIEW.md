# Self-review

## Correctness: PASS

The new claim is a single complete theorem about the second 2024 polynomial trivariate copula: exact validity, exact simultaneous medial/Spearman region, excluded diameter, and sharp extrema. Copula margins follow from the boundary polynomial and the coefficient constraint. Mixed differentiation gives \(c=1+\lambda(1-2z)F(x,y)\), where \(F\) is bilinear; optimizing its absolute value over four vertices proves both directions of the validity test. Linear reparameterization gives a square with precisely one missing diameter; an explicit equal-parameter construction attains every other point. Integration and the midpoint formula give the two dependence coordinates, whose linear optimization proves all claimed sharp constants. The exact symbolic checker passes. There are no numerical-to-infinite extrapolations.

## Value: PASS

The originating construction was developed as a tractable nonexchangeable three-dimensional dependence model, and leaves open the globally admissible parameter range. The exact criterion enlarges its usable domain, e.g. from the published bound \(1/25\) to \(1/11\) in one tabulated example. The joint dependence geometry is stronger than separate formulas: it identifies every feasible pair, a nonclosed attainable set despite all four vertices being feasible, and precise maximizers of both scores. These facts matter when specifying feasible dependence targets and fitting parameter constraints and are not merely renamings or a small numerical improvement.
