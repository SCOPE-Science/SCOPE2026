Knowing both destination-valuation marginals does not identify congestion equilibrium: the exact possible first-destination share is \(\bigl(a/(1+2a),(1+a)/(1+2a)\bigr)\) even with strictly positive smooth valuation densities. The planner's attainable interval is \(\bigl(2a/(1+4a),(1+2a)/(1+4a)\bigr)\), and for each fixed dependence structure the planner's share is strictly closer to \(1/2\) unless the equilibrium is already balanced.
