For a qubit reset at Poisson rate \(r\) to an exponentially recency-weighted past state, clipped at the initial preparation, the exact coherence decay exponent never exceeds \(r\) and attains it precisely when the memory age rate equals \(r\) and the Bohr gap is at least \(2r\). The boundary gap produces a genuine second-order exceptional point, but the matched-rate evolution remains completely-positive-divisible and has no information backflow.
