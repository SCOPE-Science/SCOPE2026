A coherent pair of damped one-sided exponential frequency sidebands has an exact critical Gabor frame/nonframe phase diagram at every fractional detuning \(0<h<1\), with reciprocal amplitude thresholds. Below half a frequency unit, sufficient damping stabilizes equal amplitudes; above half a unit, equal amplitudes are unstable for every positive damping, and the Zak zero geometry can bifurcate from one interior zero to two.
