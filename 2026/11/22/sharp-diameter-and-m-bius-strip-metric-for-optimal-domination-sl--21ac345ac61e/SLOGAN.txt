For every cycle \(C_n\) with \(n\equiv1\pmod3\) and \(n\ge4\), the token-slide graph of minimum ordinary dominating sets is connected and self-centered, with every eccentricity exactly \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\). Its entire shortest-path metric is captured by moving two separated particles on a Möbius strip, and an explicit formula computes optimal relocation distances.
