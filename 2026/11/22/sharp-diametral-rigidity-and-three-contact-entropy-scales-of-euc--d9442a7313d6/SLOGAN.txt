For every proper Euclidean SIM-body, the exact diameter has only complementary balanced-support extremizers, while a one-variable finite formula classifies its unique enclosing ball and all contact ranks. Geometrically discounted parameters exhibit three sharply different contact-entropy scales: \((\log n)^2\), \(n^\beta(\log n)^2\), and \(n\log n\), despite retaining the same combinatorial type at each dimension.
