A fixed, naturally motivated cubic family has unchanged four-node/saddle topology but transitions between a genuine cusp, a corner, \(C^1\)-only regularity and \(C^2\) regularity. The exact global integral exposes why the singular coefficient cannot vanish and identifies a logarithmic resonance at the second wall.
