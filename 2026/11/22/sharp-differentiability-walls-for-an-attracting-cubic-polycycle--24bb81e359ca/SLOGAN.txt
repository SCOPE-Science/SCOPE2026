In a strictly contracting cubic star-node flow, the unique attracting invariant heteroclinic circle remains topologically unchanged as the angular coefficient varies, but is a regular \(C^1\) curve exactly when \(\omega<1-\varepsilon\), and a \(C^2\) curve exactly when \(\omega<(1-\varepsilon)/2\). An exact positive-integral formula gives the nonsmooth power-law cusp and a sharp \(u^2\log(1/|u|)\) singularity at the second threshold.
