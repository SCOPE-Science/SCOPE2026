# Scientific review

## Correctness (C): PASS

The formula and entire real domain are specified. The mixed derivative was reconstructed by differentiating the rational product, with exact symbolic checks in dimensions two through four; the factorization in the proof holds algebraically for all dimensions. For negative parameters, strict monotonicity of \(u(2+\alpha u)\) reduces all cube-volume inequalities to the all-one corner. A one-variable derivative proves uniqueness of the relevant subunit root and excludes the second formal endpoint at \(s=1\). The finite-dimensional cutoff, asymptotic expansion, positive binary mixture, and infinite-exchangeability obstruction follow by independently stated arguments. Fifty-one supporting checker assertions passed. These tests are not treated as proofs for unbounded \(n\).

Risk: the mathematical claims rely on exact continuous densities and on the ordinary rational formula; no singular boundary copula or all-purpose finite extension classification is claimed.

This source is a substantial starting point, not an unrelated comparison. The assessed claim is the new *necessary-and-sufficient* parameter domain, explicit maximal dimension for negative parameters, and the dichotomy between binary conditional-i.i.d. representation and impossibility of infinite exchangeability. Those statements are not found in its relevant full-text sections.

Dolati and Úbeda-Flores (2006) give general higher-dimensional FGM-type constructions and conditional positivity criteria; their explicit formulas differ from the rational GRP formula and do not provide its particular sharp \(n\)-dependent boundary or binary representation. Chesneau (2023, *Symmetry*) studies a different, two-dimensional original-ratio construction. Targeted exact-formula, parameter and latent-mixture searches in public mathematical records and literature produced no direct coverage. This is a best-knowledge assessment rather than an exhaustive certification of historical priority.

## Value (V): PASS

The original multidimensional model was published with a conservative feasible region. Its exact range is a natural, model-defining question: using a forbidden parameter destroys nonnegative joint cube probabilities. The sharp cutoff identifies the precise number of coordinates supportable by the same negative-dependence construction and shows its dependence strength must decrease with dimension. At positive parameters the exact binary latent representation gives a practical, all-dimensional simulation and interpretation, while negative pairwise covariance prohibits any infinite exchangeable extension, irrespective of the model formula. These results clarify the finite/infinite multivariate behavior of a published copula family rather than recalculating a grid value or arbitrary slice.

Risk: the original mixed-density formula is already known, and a reader can derive the sharp positivity constraint from it; the distinct contribution is the completed global phase diagram and exchangeability consequence, not a new density identity.
