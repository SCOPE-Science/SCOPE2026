The fixed-position set of a uniform permutation defines a Boolean-cube convolution that is singular only for \(n=2\) and becomes strictly positive definite at \(n=4\). Across every dimension \(n\ge4\) its normalized Walsh multipliers lie in \([1/15,1]\), with the optimal worst-case condition number \(15\) achieved uniquely at \(n=5\), and condition numbers tending to \(e^2\).
