Complete order-\(k\) harmonic jets on a regular circle grid recover every planar harmonic Fourier band of degree \(L\) with exactly the information-theoretically minimal \(\lceil(2L+1)/(2k+1)\rceil\) sites. A residue-wise one-sided Vandermonde formula determines the exact rank at every undersampling rate and quantifies the sensor advantage over tangential-derivative-only observations.
