For positive integrating-factor covariance quadratures, the first omitted scalar moment gives an exact dimension boundary: the covariance error is zero, then rank-one and one-sided, then necessarily indefinite. On a controllable nilpotent chain, IF-SSPRK2 crosses this boundary at dimensions \(2,3\), while IF-SSPRK3 crosses at dimensions \(3,4\), for every timestep; their numerical covariance ranks also have exact node-count thresholds.
