For a centered Gaussian disperser, \(m\) separated symmetric habitat distance bands permit at most \(m\) distinct local optima in dispersal standard deviation, and every bound is attainable. Two bands can even support two equally good **global** dispersal scales: at large separation \(R\), their tie occurs at outer-band width ratio \(q_R=2-2^{4/3}/R+O(R^{-2})\).
