The 2023 semi-Poisson count distribution is strictly underdispersed, and its Fano factor has one sharp global minimum, \(0.9099071739796849\ldots\), at \(\lambda=1.40741241822510068\ldots\), rather than the published overdispersion maximum. Its zero-mass ratio relative to a Poisson law with the same mean decreases strictly from \(1\) to \(1/2\), so the whole unmodified family is also zero-deflated.
