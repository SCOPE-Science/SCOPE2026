For two distinct odd exponents \(3\le d<e\), the complete ordered two-sum spectrum of their distinct power-residue values modulo every power of two has an explicit valuation-shell formula and an exact finite-depth mixed additive energy. Its largest fiber always has size equal to the smaller image set, attained on the nonzero valuation-\(d\) shell; when \(d\nmid e\), zero permanently loses its maximizing status at exactly depth \(e+1\).
