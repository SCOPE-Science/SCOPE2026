The real \(\ell_4^3\) nearest-neighbor skew path has an exact algebraic logarithmic norm \(\gamma=0.6679394527296159\ldots\), with every maximizing vector forced onto a single antisymmetric invariant line. This constant is the sharp damping threshold for contraction of its semigroup, even though its undamped generator is Euclidean skew-symmetric and has purely imaginary spectrum.
