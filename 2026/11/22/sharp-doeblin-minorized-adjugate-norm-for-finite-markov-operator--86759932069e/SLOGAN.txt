A nonuniform Doeblin lower bound has an exact sharp effect on determinant-weighted inverse instability: for \(n\)-state column-stochastic matrices with every column dominating \(\alpha\), the maximum adjugate \(\ell_1\)-operator norm is \(2(1-\min_i\alpha_i)(1-\sum_i\alpha_i)^{n-2}\). A deterministic channel with one omitted and one duplicated output is extremal, and invertible channels approach that value.
