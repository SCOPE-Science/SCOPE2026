For the box-window Gabor frame with \(2/3<\beta<3/4\), the sharp minimum \(L^1\)-norm of any dual window is \(4\beta-2\), and the sharp minimum essential support measure is \(4-2/\beta\). All complex-valued \(L^1\)-minimizers are classified by one measurable interval-valued function; precisely its binary-valued choices are simultaneously optimal for support.
