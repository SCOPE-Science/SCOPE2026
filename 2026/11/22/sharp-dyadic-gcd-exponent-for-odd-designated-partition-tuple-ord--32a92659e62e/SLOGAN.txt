For every odd tuple order \(k\ge1\), the highest power of two dividing every designated-partition tuple count \(A_k(4n+3)\) is exactly \(2^{\nu_2(k+1)-1}\), already sharp at \(n=0\). This proves the entire odd-order branch of a recent conjecture by combining a classical sum-of-two-squares eta-product identity with dyadic Frobenius lifting.
