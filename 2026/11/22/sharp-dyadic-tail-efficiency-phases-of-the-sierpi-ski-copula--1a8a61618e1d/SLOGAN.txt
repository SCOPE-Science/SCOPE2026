Among three pairwise-independent uniform variables, the canonical Sierpiński coupling is exactly optimal for simultaneous threshold exceedance below \(1/2\) and at dyadic upper-tail endpoints, but between those endpoints it loses up to a sharp \(25\%\) of the attainable probability. The complete dyadic gap formula also yields an explicit strictly increasing analytic score for which Sierpiński loses to another valid pairwise-independent coupling.
