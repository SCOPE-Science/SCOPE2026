For two unlabelled connected districts on a labelled cycle, each with at least \(s\) vertices, all single-vertex switches freeze at exact balance \(n=2s\); otherwise the configuration graph is connected. The precise eccentricity of a state with smaller district size \(d\) is \(\max\{\lfloor n/2\rfloor,n-s-d\}\), yielding sharp radius and diameter.
