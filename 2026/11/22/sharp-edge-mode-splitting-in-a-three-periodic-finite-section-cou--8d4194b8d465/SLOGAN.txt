The known invertible three-periodic Schrödinger counterexample has two finite-section edge eigenvalues \(\lambda_\pm=\pm\frac3{10}4^{-k}+O(k16^{-k})\) along symmetric cutoffs \(N=3k+1\), while its other eigenvalues stay uniformly away from zero. Its Dirichlet finite-section inverse therefore grows with the sharp law \(\|H_{3k+1}^{-1}\|\sim\frac{10}{3}4^k\), quantifying a qualitative finite-section instability.
