The uniform weight-\(w\) naturally oriented four-cycle defines a three-dimensional lattice simplex whose exact Ehrhart numerator is \(1+\binom{w}{3}t+(2w^3+w-6)t^2/3+\binom{w}{3}t^3\). Its coefficient sequence becomes unimodal exactly at \(w=3\), log-concave exactly at \(w=5\), and its roots become all simple, real and negative exactly at \(w=7\), even though every member with \(w\ge2\) fails the integer decomposition property.
