In the countable homogeneous two-graph, grading singleton types by the number of coherent triples with named parameters reconstructs every parameter two-graph through seven points but fails sharply at eight: 243 isomorphism classes give 219 polynomials in 198 singleton, 18 double, and three triple fibers. The multiset of coherent-triple incidence degrees separates all 21 ambiguous fibers at the first failure.
