For four-state epsilon-free binary NFAs with one fixed initial state, precisely eight transition edges are necessary and sufficient to realize the full \(16\)-state minimal DFA. Among the \(10{,}518{,}300\) labeled eight-edge transition relations, exactly \(5{,}808\) reach all subsets and \(56{,}544\) choices of transition relation and final set attain a minimal sixteen-state DFA.
