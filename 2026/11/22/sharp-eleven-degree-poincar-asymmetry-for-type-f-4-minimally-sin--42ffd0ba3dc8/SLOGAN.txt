Among the 1,152 full-flag Schubert varieties of type \(F_4\), precisely 217 have local Kazhdan–Lusztig polynomial \(P_{e,w}(1)=2\). Their exact sharp eleven-degree opposite-Betti asymmetry spectrum is \((1,4,9,15,18,15,16,14,8,5,2)\): the general coefficient bound is attained in degrees one through three and is strictly unattainable thereafter.
