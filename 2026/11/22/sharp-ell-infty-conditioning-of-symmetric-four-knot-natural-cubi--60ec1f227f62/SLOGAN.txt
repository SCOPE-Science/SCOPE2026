Among all reflection-symmetric four-knot natural cubic interpolants, the unique smallest worst-case \(\ell^\infty\)-to-\(L^\infty\) noise amplifier has middle-to-outer gap ratio \(r_*\) determined by \(9\sqrt3 r_*^3=8(r_*+1)\), rather than exact equal spacing. The proof gives the sharp Lebesgue constant for every ratio and balances distinct outer-cell and central-cell amplification mechanisms.
