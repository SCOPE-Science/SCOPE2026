In an SIS epidemic with finite-rationality logit distancing, an increasing payoff advantage of noncompliance creates exactly two endemic equilibria alongside a stable disease-free state if and only if \(d>1+e^c\) and \(\ell(c)<\gamma/\beta<W_0(e^{c+d-1})/d\). The lower endemic state is a saddle and the upper is locally attracting, so a locally subcritical invasion threshold can coexist with persistent endemic infection.
