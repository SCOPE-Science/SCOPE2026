On real functions of bounded Jordan variation with matching endpoint values, every power of the classical Bernstein operator has an exact, attained strict variation contraction coefficient: the survival probability after that many steps of the absorbing Wright–Fisher binomial chain started at \(1/2\). A unique interior-plateau nodal profile is extremal, the coefficients decay at the sharp rate \(((n-1)/n)^r\), and except at degree two they obey strict submultiplicativity.
