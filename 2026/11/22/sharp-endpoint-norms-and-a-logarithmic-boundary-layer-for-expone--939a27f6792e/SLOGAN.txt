The real \(L^2\) projector onto \(\operatorname{span}\{1,e^{-\lambda t}\}\) on \([0,1]\) has an exact \(L^\infty\) norm determined by just two endpoint kernel zeros. Its amplification rises from \(5/3\) at vanishing decay rate toward three, with a sharp logarithmic boundary-layer correction \((2\log(2\lambda)-2)/\lambda\) in the stiff limit.
