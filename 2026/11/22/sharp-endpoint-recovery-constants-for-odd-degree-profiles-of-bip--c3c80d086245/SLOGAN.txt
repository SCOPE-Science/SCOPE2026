For every complete bipartite graph \(K_{m,n}\) with \(m,n\ge2\), the sharp unrestricted endpoint norm for recovering the convolution range of spanning-tree odd-degree profiles is the exact product \(\Phi(m)\Phi(n)\), where \(\Phi(2r)=1+\binom{2r}r(2^{-r}-2^{1-2r})\) and \(\Phi(d)=1\) for odd \(d\). Along growing even shore sizes the surviving Fourier fraction approaches one, but the least possible \(\ell^1\) or \(\ell^\infty\) projection norm increases exponentially.
