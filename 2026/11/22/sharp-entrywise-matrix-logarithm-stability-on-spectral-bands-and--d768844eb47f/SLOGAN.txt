On real symmetric matrices with spectrum confined to \([a,b]\), the principal matrix logarithm has an exact maximum-entry Lipschitz coefficient \(c_n^2/a+(1-c_n)^2/b+2c_n(1-c_n)\log(b/a)/(b-a)\), where \(c_n=(1+\sqrt n)/2\). The worst-case amplification is linear in dimension for fixed nonzero relative spectral width, but at width \(b/a-1=x/\sqrt n\) it converges to the finite sharp crossover \(1+x/2+x^2/12\).
