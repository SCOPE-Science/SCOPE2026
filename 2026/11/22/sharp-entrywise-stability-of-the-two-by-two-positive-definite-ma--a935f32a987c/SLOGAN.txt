The sharp entrywise maximum-norm Lipschitz constant for the principal square root of real symmetric \(2\times2\) matrices with spectra in \([a,b]\) is \((3+2\sqrt2)/(8\sqrt a)-1/(2(\sqrt a+\sqrt b))+(3-2\sqrt2)/(8\sqrt b)\). The worst infinitesimal orientation is universally \(\pi/8\), and the high-contrast amplification exceeds the scalar bound by the factor \((3+2\sqrt2)/4\).
