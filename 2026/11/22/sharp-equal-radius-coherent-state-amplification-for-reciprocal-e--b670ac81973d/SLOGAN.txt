For reciprocal exponential Toeplitz products on power-weighted Fock spaces, the maximal equal-radius coherent-state matrix coefficient obeys the sharp law \(\log M_{m,g}(R)\sim[d^2|a_d|^2/(2m^2)]R^{2d-2m}\) for \(m>1/2\) and \(1\le d<2m\). Every maximizing angular gap has magnitude asymptotic to \([d|a_d|/m^2]R^{d-2m}\), giving a universal decay-to-one, finite critical, and stretched-exponential growth trichotomy.
