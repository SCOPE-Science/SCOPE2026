## Correctness — PASS

The argument starts from the forbidden degenerate cubes, obtains the sharp \(2/3\) bound by translating the complement in three ways, and propagates equality into the period identity \(\mathbf1_B(y+3x)=\mathbf1_B(y)\). The generator set \(S=A\cap2^{-1}A\) has at least one-third of the group and omits zero; in an odd-order group it cannot lie inside a proper subgroup, proving invariance by \(3G\). Full inverse images preserve cube-freeness in both directions. The rank-two obstruction uses an explicit repeated-generator cube, and subgroup-complement constructions are checked by a homomorphism to \(\mathbb F_3\). Exhaustive small-group checks reproduced the rank-one and rank-two classifications. Limit: rank at least three is not completely classified.
