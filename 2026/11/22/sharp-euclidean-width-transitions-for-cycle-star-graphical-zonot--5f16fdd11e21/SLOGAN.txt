For a cycle with all pendant leaves at one vertex, the canonical graphical zonotope's thinnest Euclidean directions are exactly single-leaf cuts or contiguous nonhub cycle arcs, with squared width \(N\min\{1/(N-1),4/[h(N-h)]\}\). Their competition has universal ties at total order \(15\) for rim lengths \(8\) through \(14\), together with two exceptional ties at \((m,t)=(6,15)\) and \((7,9)\).
