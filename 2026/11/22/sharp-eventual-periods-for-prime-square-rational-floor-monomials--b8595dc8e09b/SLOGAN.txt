For rational floor monomials with prime-square denominator, the exact least eventual coprimality period is \(p^{1+\mathbf1_{p\nmid k}}\operatorname{rad}(\operatorname{lcm}_r t_r)\); the prime-square factor drops to a single prime exactly when \(p\mid k\). The only failure of periodicity from the first index occurs at \(n=p\) for quadratic monomials, and every limiting density has an explicit totient formula.
