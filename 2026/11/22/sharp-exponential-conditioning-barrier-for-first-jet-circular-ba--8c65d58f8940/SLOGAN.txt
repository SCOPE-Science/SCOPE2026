A regular \(M\)-gon with value and normalized first-derivative data perfectly interpolates all Bargmann–Fock polynomials of degree at most \(2M-1\), but its best squared sampling condition number grows at the sharp exponential rate \(\exp((2/e+o(1))M)\). The asymptotically conditioning-optimal squared radius is \((2/e)M\), whereas the exactly determinant-optimal squared radius is \(M-1\).
