The generalized-Hartogs vertex polynomial \(Q_{N,\alpha}(z)=\sum_{j=1}^N j(N+\alpha-j)z^{j-1}\) has **every** zero strictly outside \(|z|=1\) for \(0<\alpha<1\), and all zeros on that circle precisely when \(\alpha=1\). Its complete endpoint radial-escape velocities are \((1-\cos\theta_j)/(N+2)\); consequently, fixed non-endpoint generalized-Hartogs Bergman kernels have a zero-free \(|s|\le t\) inner core on sufficiently small positive-\(t\) vertex sections.
