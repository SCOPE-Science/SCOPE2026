Among all finite simple line graphs, \(m\) named vertices realize at most \(1+\binom{3k}{2}\) distinct external open-neighbourhood traces for \(m=2k\) and \(2+\binom{3k+1}{2}\) for \(m=2k+1\ge3\), with the one-mark value \(2\). Exactly the marked-edge structures built from disjoint two-edge paths, with a single isolated edge or three-edge claw in the odd case, can attain the sharp bound (plus the exceptional two-mark matching).
