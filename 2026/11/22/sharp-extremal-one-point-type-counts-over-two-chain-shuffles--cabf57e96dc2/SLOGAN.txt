For a fixed pair of incomparable chains in the generic linearly ordered poset, the singleton-type count is minimized exactly when the chains are segregated and maximized exactly by the nearest-integer balanced shuffles. The exact maximum-minus-minimum gap is \(\frac{m}{6}(3d^2+6dm+4m^2-\varepsilon_d)\), where \(m\) is the shorter chain length, \(d\) the length difference, and \(\varepsilon_d\) equals four for even \(d\) and one for odd \(d\).
