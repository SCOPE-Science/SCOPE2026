For every positive-coefficient polynomial with a nonzero linear term and at least two monomials, the high powers of its Hardy-space Volterra operator collapse in relative operator norm to the explicit rank-one map \(f\mapsto f(0)g^k/k!\). Their exact leading factorial-decay constant is determined by the variance of the polynomial's degree distribution and the number of equal-modulus boundary saddles; for \(g(z)=z+az^m\), that constant is independent of \(m\ge2\).
