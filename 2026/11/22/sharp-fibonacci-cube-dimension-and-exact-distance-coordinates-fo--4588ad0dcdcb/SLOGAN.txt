For every even \(k\ge4\) and \(n\ge1\), the generalized Pell graph \(\Pi_{n,k}\) embeds isometrically into the Fibonacci cube \(\Gamma_{kn-1}\), and this Fibonacci-cube dimension is the smallest possible among isometric embeddings. Alternating complemented low thresholds with high thresholds gives the explicit optimal encoding, reducing the previously published coordinate bound by \((k-2)n\).
