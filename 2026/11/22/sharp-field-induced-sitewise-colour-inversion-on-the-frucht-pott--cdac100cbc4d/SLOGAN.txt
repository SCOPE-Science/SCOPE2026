The uniform three-state antiferromagnetic Potts model on the twelve-vertex Frucht graph has an exact first positive-field crossing at \(H/J=3/2\), where **every vertex reverses** whether it carries the favoured colour even though the total favoured population rises from five to seven. The two strictly stable ground-state classes have respectively 16 and 32 labelled configurations, with a second, saturation crossing at \(H/J=3\).
