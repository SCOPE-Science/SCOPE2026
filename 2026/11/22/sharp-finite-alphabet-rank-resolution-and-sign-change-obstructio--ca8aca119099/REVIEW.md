# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof identifies the randomized probability-integral transform, derives the binomial-mixture rank law exactly, proves strict positivity of all relevant moment-matrix minors through an integrated Vandermonde determinant, constructs minimal-sign-change null vectors via alternating cofactors, and computes the first nonzero uniform-alphabet rank response by a finite difference. All hypotheses, including independently regenerated reference samples and uniform tie labels, are explicit. The bundled exact-arithmetic checker passed 10,485 supplementary assertions. No finite computation substitutes for the general determinant and sign arguments.

**Originality — PASS with residual priority risk.** The primary 2019 stochastic-rank paper states exact null calibration, a general alternative rank formula, and eventual detection (Theorems 3.1, 3.4, 3.8); inspection of its main text and proof appendix did not identify the exact finite-alphabet universal cutoff, full fixed-budget blind-space dimension, sign-variation obstruction with cofactor sharpness, or the explicit first-visible rank spectrum. Searches for finite-support stochastic-rank identifiability, polynomial moments, and binomial-mixture aliases did not reveal a directly covering published statement. The argument uses classical Vandermonde and total-positivity ingredients. A broad binomial-mixture theorem, a subsequent detailed author thesis, or a previously unpublished result may subsume some or all of the claim; no search establishes uniqueness.

**Value — PASS.** Fixed resampling budgets are a natural practical parameter of this published goodness-of-fit test. A necessary-and-sufficient sharp budget cutoff and a complete classification of blind alternatives resolve when collecting *arbitrarily many observations* can be futile at a fixed simulator budget. The sign-variation theorem shows exactly which ordered alternatives can be missed and the alternating example supplies a reproducible worst-case. No general sample-complexity, power, or budget-efficiency guarantee is asserted.

**Closest literature and limitations.** Saad et al. (2019) gives a more general setup but weaker, existential-in-reference-count detection; neither its fixed-null validity nor its full alternative rank formula is new here. Classical total positivity gives structural background and limits the novelty claim to the sharp application and combined classification. Full support, finite ordered alphabets, and the original independent-reference tie convention are essential; prior broad mixture literature has not been exhaustively excluded.
