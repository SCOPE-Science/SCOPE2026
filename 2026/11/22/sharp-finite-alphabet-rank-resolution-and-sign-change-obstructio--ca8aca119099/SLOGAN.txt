For a fully supported \(K\)-letter null, the uniformly tie-broken stochastic rank test has invisible non-null alternatives at every fixed reference count \(m\le K-2\), but no invisible alternatives once \(m\ge K-1\). The exact blind-space dimension is \(K-m-1\), and invisibility requires at least \(m+1\) ordered sign changes, a sharp restriction for every positive null vector.
