A spherical heat covariance multiplied by \(1+\varepsilon(x_3+y_3)\) is indefinite for every nonzero tilt, yet its degree-\(\le L\) compression has a sharp positive-semidefiniteness threshold. That threshold is \(2e^{-tL}\rho(e^{-t})^{-1}(1-1/(8L^2)+O_t(L^{-3}))\), where \(\rho(q)\) is the leading eigenvalue of the compact geometric-weight Jacobi matrix, and every nonzero tilt produces infinitely many negative eigenvalues in the untruncated integral operator.
