For two independent equal-noise observations at reflection-symmetric sites of a Matérn-\(3/2\) process on \([0,1]\), posterior integral variance has a sharp replication transition at noise variance \(8\sqrt e-12\): the unique optimum uses separated sites below it and two midpoint observations at or above it. The optimal half-separation vanishes linearly, not with a square-root law, at the threshold; unrestricted asymmetric sites are not optimized.
