On a Brownian ring with random-position Poisson resetting, measuring both mean absorption time and final arrival direction at exactly \(K\) prescribed rates recovers every resetting law supported on at most \(K\) unknown sites, including which side each site occupies. A singular explicitly computable Hankel matrix certifies that sparsity even against arbitrary continuous alternatives, and fewer than \(K\) paired rate measurements cannot universally identify all \(K\)-site laws.
