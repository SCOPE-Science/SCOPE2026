The standard dyadic Walsh–Hadamard compression has an exact finite-size norm given by the smallest root of a simple tridiagonal determinant recurrence. Its deficit from \(1+1/\sqrt2\) is \(\sqrt2\pi^2(1+1/\sqrt2)^3/(N+5+3\sqrt2)^2+O(N^{-4})\), establishing the sharp quadratic convergence rate that prior work had suggested numerically.
