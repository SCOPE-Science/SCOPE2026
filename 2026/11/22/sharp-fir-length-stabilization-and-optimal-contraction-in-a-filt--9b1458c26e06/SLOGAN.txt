In the FIR-filtered Hénon map, the maximum gain that still permits a locally attracting positive equilibrium with \(n\) memory taps is exactly \(\sqrt{[4^n-(1-\beta)^2]/(4\alpha)}\), even if signed filters are allowed on the impossibility side. Above the low-feedback crossover, a unique strictly positive binomial-corrected filter minimizes the entire Jacobian spectral radius by placing all multipliers at the same negative real value; the negative equilibrium is always unstable.
