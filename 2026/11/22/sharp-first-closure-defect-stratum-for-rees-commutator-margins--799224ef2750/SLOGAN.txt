For commutator-marginal elements in a finite Rees matrix semigroup, nonclosure forces at least \(4|Z(G)|^2(r-1)(c-1)\) ordered bad products, and equality is rigid: after normalization, a single nonidentity quotient value fills one of four types of interior rectangle. The exact zero-defect and first-positive-defect matrix populations follow for every finite group and every \(r,c\ge2\).
