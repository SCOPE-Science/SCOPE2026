Every fair \((n-1)\)-wise independent Rademacher law has exactly the same first absolute moments as independence when \(n\) is odd; when \(n\) is even, its exact worst relative deviation is \(|\theta|/(n-1)\), where \(\theta\) is the full-product correlation. Flat sign coefficients attain both sharp bounds, giving an exact deterministic parity-half-cube norm discretization law.
