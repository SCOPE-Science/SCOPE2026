For the canonical three equally spaced diagonal sites on a unit square flat torus, the covering radius drops below \(1/2\) for every product exponent \(p>1\), with sharp first-order loss \(\bigl(\log12/4-5\log5/24\bigr)(p-1)\). This gives a quantified smooth-circle instance of the product covering-number failure away from the sum-metric endpoint, without claiming unrestricted site optimality.
