# Scientific review: sharp parity-detection threshold for permutation incidence

**Correctness — PASS.** The new first-leakage formula follows from the same inclusion-exclusion expansion at exactly \(n-1\) queried cells: all proper terms cancel, and the one full matching term contributes the unique completion’s signed parity. Each adaptive leaf contributes signed mass at most one; there are at most \(2^{n-1}\) leaves, so the optimal total-variation bound is attained by a nonadaptive near-perfect matching. A randomized tree is a mixture of deterministic trees and cannot improve this bound.  The central sign-cancellation identity is established by fixing all requested positive cells, applying inclusion-exclusion to negative cells, and pairing completions by swapping two unassigned images. Because there are at most \(n-2\) distinct queried cells, such a swap always exists. Nonvanishing of each compatible trace follows independently from a complete-bipartite one-factorization, whose \(m\) factors lose at most \(m-2\) to the forbidden cells. The size-\(n-1\) obstruction depends on the two possible residual completions having opposite signs. The online-dimension upper bound is an induction on the positive fixed-edge branch; that branch is the appropriate sign coset on one smaller board. The Python and C++17 checks test finite instances only and are not used in place of these arguments.

**Originality — PASS, with priority caveats.** Published permutation-pattern shattering concerns order types of selected symbols, not independent Boolean row-image tests (Johnson–Wickes, arXiv:2112.01946, main definitions and results). Perfect-matching/derangement enumeration (Bucić et al., arXiv:1906.05908) does not impose a permutation-sign coset or determine all signed incidence traces. Closest exact public records determine derangement trace universality, permutation-assignment online ranks after forbidden matching deletion, and parity-sensitive *comparison* queries; the latter has a different instance domain. A previous restricted six-symbol even-derangement census is not an all-order result on the full board. None of those statements, taken at their stated hypotheses, implies the present exact sign-balanced trace multiplicities plus the shattering-complex and adaptive-dimension conclusion. The fact that \(\mathfrak A_n\) is \((n-2)\)-transitive (hence all lower-order parity blindness), inclusion-exclusion for rook placements, and complete bipartite one-factorization are acknowledged as classical; they are not individually claimed as novel. The exact first parity-leakage coefficient and optimal bounded-query statistical test are the distinctive statements. Unindexed equivalences or stronger alternating-group statements remain possible and would affect priority.

**Value — PASS.** A natural information-loss question asks whether restricting the Birkhoff assignment codebook to one of its two parity cosets changes which arbitrary patterns of row-image tests are possible and their frequencies. The sharp information-theoretic answer quantifies the first detectable parity signal: every test up to \(n-2\) cells has zero signal, and the optimal possibly adaptive next-query experiment has exactly \(2^{n-1}/n!\) total variation and an explicit optimal statistic. This is not merely the textbook \((n-2)\)-transitivity observation; it determines a sharp Bayes risk and proves adaptivity buys no additional advantage at the first informative depth. It identifies the entire VC shattering complex and proves an exact online dimension, contrasting parity restriction with the previously studied forbidden-matching restriction. This is a complete structural classification of a canonical restricted permutation concept family rather than an isolated computation. The result does not resolve general graph matching or classification of the chessboard complex's integral homotopy type.
