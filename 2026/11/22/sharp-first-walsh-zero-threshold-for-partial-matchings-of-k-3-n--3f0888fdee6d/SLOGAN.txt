For counting convolution by all partial matchings of \(K_{3,n}\), every Walsh multiplier survives when \(n\ge3\) is even; for odd \(n\) the first vanishing multiplier occurs at frequency degree exactly \((n+3)/2\). Every lower-frequency multiplier is at least \(2n\), and at least \(6r\binom nr\) distinct minimum-degree zeros are exhibited, where \(r=(n+1)/2\).
