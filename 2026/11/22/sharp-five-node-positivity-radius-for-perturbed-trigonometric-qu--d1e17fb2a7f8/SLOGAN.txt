For five perturbed equispaced circle nodes, degree-two interpolatory trigonometric quadrature has nonnegative weights for every perturbation exactly through \(\alpha_* = \frac5{4\pi}\arccos(\cos(\pi/5)-1)-\frac12\approx0.20145942246\) of a grid spacing. Beyond this sharp radius, an alternating four-node perturbation forces a negative weight even while the perturbation remains below one quarter of a grid spacing.
