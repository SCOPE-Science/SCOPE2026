A single latitude ring with exactly \(2\ell+1\) equally spaced two-component measurements recovers every exact-degree-\(\ell\) poloidal–toroidal tangent field except at explicitly classified spin-one Jacobi nodal latitudes. For quadrupolar fields, five nodes are necessary and sufficient away from heights \(0,\pm1/2\), and the unique best absolute height is the root \(t_*\in(1/4,3/10)\) of \(10t_*^3-6t_*^2-3t_*+1=0\), with sharp squared condition number \((1+t_*)^2/(6t_*^2)\).
