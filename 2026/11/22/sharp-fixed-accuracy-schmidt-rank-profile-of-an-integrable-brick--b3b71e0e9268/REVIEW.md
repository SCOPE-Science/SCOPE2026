# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The exact block masses and flat singular spectra in the initiating integrable-circuit paper are treated as credited assumptions, and their gate-level finite-time content was independently checked at times 1–3 using exact integer folded-circuit calculations. The new proof does not assume simultaneous Schmidt diagonalization across overlapping count blocks. Orthogonal count projections yield the exact low-difference mass, rank subadditivity bounds its rank, and a blockwise triangle bound suppresses the high-difference operator norm. Eckart–Young, trace/operator duality, and a binomial difference central limit theorem then squeeze the complete optimal single-cut mass-rank curve to the half-Gaussian cumulative distribution, including the endpoint and the inverse fixed-error law. The test script passed 2736 exact checks. Finite experiments do not prove the infinite-time result.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Pozsgay's September 2026 source supplies the gate, folded construction, binomial weights, and individual count-block spectra. Supplement (S55) is linear in rank inside the minimum and already implies the sharp upper retained-weight profile when combined with the half-Gaussian limit. It is a necessary-rank estimate, not a matching construction. The retained low-difference approximation furnishes sufficient rank and the matching lower profile. The related record *The exact high-index Rényi coefficient in an integrable brickwork circuit* (12 October 2026) resolves fixed Rényi orders above one, whose moment law does not determine the fixed-accuracy mass quantile. The earliest preliminary PDF was not available for full comparison; an equivalent earlier treatment cannot be excluded.

## Value — PASS

The source's one-sided bound determines a necessary exponent but not whether it is attainable. The explicit sufficient rank at the matching exponent makes the exact resource-versus-error curve a single-cut benchmark for tensor-network truncation. The argument keeps coherence among count sectors and does not give a uniform MPO cost across all cuts.
