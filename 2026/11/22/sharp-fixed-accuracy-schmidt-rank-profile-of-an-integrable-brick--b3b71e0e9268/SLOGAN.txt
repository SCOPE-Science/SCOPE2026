For a four-state integrable Yang–Baxter brickwork circuit, the optimal fraction of squared operator-Schmidt weight retained by rank \(e^{a\sqrt t}\) converges exactly to \(\operatorname{erf}(a/\log2)\). Thus the sharp fixed-error single-cut compression cost is \(\log\chi_\varepsilon(t)\sim(\log2)\operatorname{erf}^{-1}(1-\varepsilon)\sqrt t\), strengthening the previously known qualitative stretched-exponential lower bound.
