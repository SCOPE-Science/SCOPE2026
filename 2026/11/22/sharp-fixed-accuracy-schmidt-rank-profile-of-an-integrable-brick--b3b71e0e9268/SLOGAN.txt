For a four-state integrable Yang–Baxter brickwork circuit, the optimal fraction of squared operator-Schmidt weight retained by rank \(e^{a\sqrt t}\) converges to \(\operatorname{erf}(a/\log2)\). A construction matches the prior necessary-rank bound on the \(\sqrt t\) scale, giving the sharp fixed-error single-cut cost \(\log\chi_\varepsilon(t)\sim(\log2)\operatorname{erf}^{-1}(1-\varepsilon)\sqrt t\).
