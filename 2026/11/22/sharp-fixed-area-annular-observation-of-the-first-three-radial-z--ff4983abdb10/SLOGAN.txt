Among all single concentric annular windows occupying a fixed fraction \(q\in(0,1)\) of the unit disk, observing the first three radial Zernike modes is uniquely best in worst-case energy when the annulus is centered in the **squared radial coordinate**. Its sharp least-energy fraction is \(\frac12(T-\sqrt{T^2-4q^6})\) for \(T=\frac q4(9-10q^2+9q^4)\), and every displacement suffers an explicit strict quadratic-or-higher observation loss.
