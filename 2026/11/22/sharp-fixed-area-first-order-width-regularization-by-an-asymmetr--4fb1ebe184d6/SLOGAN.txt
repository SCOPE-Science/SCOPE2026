For infinitesimal single-vertex cuts of an equilateral triangle at a fixed removed area, the best first-order improvement in random-direction width uniformity comes from two reciprocal asymmetric cut-arm ratios, approximately \(2.2806265621\) and \(0.4384759945\), not an equal-arm cut. The unique optimal unsigned cut angle in its fundamental sector is approximately \(0.4526761273\) radians, with a sharp decrease coefficient \(0.03321517066\) per \(\sqrt{st}\) for fixed arm ratio.
