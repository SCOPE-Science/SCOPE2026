The exact sharp Fourier ratio of mean-zero square-grid signals under a fixed relative discrete Dirichlet-energy budget is a unique reciprocal-affine Laplacian spectral profile. At every fixed energy \(E>4\pi^2\), its optimum equals \(\sqrt{(E-4\pi^2)\log N/(2\pi)}+2+O_E((\log N)^{-1/2})\); extremizing Fourier energy condenses onto four fundamental modes, and the vanishing tail has an explicit universal limiting distribution.
