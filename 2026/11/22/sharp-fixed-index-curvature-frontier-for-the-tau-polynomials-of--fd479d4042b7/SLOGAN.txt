For fixed degree \(d\ge3\), the coefficient-curvature ratio of high-girth regular-graph \(\tau\)-polynomials has a sharp exponential sign frontier: it tends exponentially below one at every fixed index \(2\le j\le d-2\), and above one at every fixed index \(j\ge d-1\). The identical frontier holds for augmented \(\tau\)-coefficients, with eventual positive curvature at every fixed index in cubic graphs, although no global unimodality claim is made.
