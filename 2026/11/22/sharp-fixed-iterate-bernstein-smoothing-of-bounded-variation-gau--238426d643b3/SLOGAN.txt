For every fixed iterate count \(r\) and finite \(L^p\) exponent, the sharp bounded-variation error of Bernstein–Kantorovich and Bernstein–Durrmeyer smoothing decays as \(n^{-1/(2p)}\), with exact Gaussian constants differing by the factor \(2^{1/(2p)}\). All asymptotically worst variation concentrates at the midpoint, whereas the sharp uniform-norm error remains exactly one at every finite degree.
