On finite transitive systems of two commuting permutations, proposition-free converse LoopPDL identifies exactly the stabilizer sublattice of \(\mathbb Z^2\); the number of logical types on at most \(N\) worlds is \(\sum_{d\le N}\sigma_1(d)\). Two distinct such types always admit an uncompressed flat-loop separator of length at most \(N-1\), and this linear bound is sharp, while removing loop collapses all types to one.
