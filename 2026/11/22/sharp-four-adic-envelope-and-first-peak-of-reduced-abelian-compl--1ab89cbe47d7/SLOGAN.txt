For the Thue–Morse word, the exact maximum run-reduced Abelian complexity among all lengths at most \(4^m\) is \(\lceil3(m+2)/2\rceil\) for every integer \(m\ge2\), with its first attaining length given explicitly by a parity-dependent four-adic formula. Despite these sharply growing peaks, the complexity is exactly \(4\) at every substitution length \(4^j\), so its normalized logarithmic lower and upper limits differ.
