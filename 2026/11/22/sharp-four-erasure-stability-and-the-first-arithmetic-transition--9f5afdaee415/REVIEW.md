# Mathematical assessment

## Correctness

**PASS.** The argument handles every integer dimension \(N\ge5\), every deletion cardinality one through four, every distribution of erasures between the two bases, and all eigenvalue multiplicities. The identity \(S_E=2I-U_EU_E^*\) is exact. Singular-value decomposition of the erased two-block Gram matrix reduces the bound to cross-basis Fourier submatrices. Their order-two spectrum depends on one product of nonzero modular differences; its prime/composite extrema and equality conditions are proved exactly. An ordered-pair double count supplies both mask-count formulas. Explicit rank failure at \(N=4\) checks the sharp dimensional boundary. The accompanying direct-matrix checker reproduces all four minima and the numbers of minimizing masks for every \(5\le N\le13\), including 18,336 complete two-plus-two spectral comparisons. The checker is supplementary, not an infinite proof.

Three close published exact-erasure findings were inspected in full: a two-character frame in dimension two, a cubic Alltop orbit with three erasures, and a weighted skew-conference frame with four erasures. The underlying singular-value reduction is classical; novelty is assessed for the complete theorem and its exact incidence, not for the elementary lemma alone. The Larson–Scholze (2015) work on bridging erasures in unions of two bases was identified from its abstract, but full text was unavailable; it remains a bibliographic risk alongside possible unindexed Fourier-minor formulations. No worldwide priority is asserted.

## Value

**PASS.** Two full mutually unbiased bases are a canonical minimal-redundancy measurement design. Four deletions are the first budget where worst-case conditioning depends on whether the dimension has zero divisors; for one, two and three deletions the values depend only on dimension. The all-modulus sharp loss, exact surviving spectrum, optimizer congruences, and closed counting formulas give a complete, usable deterministic stability threshold rather than a single small numerical example. This motivated arithmetic threshold is not a trivial restatement of a general erasure guarantee and identifies every most damaging deletion pattern. It does not optimize across different frames or classify larger erasure budgets.
