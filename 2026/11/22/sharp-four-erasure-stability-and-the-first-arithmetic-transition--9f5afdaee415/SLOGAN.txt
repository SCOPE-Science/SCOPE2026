For the twice-redundant frame formed by the coordinate and discrete Fourier bases, the sharp lower frame bound after up to four adversarial erasures is explicit for every dimension \(N\ge5\). Four deletions are the first to detect the prime/composite distinction: exactly two erasures per basis are worst, with difference products \(\pm1\) for prime \(N\) and zero for composite \(N\); every worst mask is counted exactly.
