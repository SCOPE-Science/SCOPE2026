Every finite field of odd characteristic at least five realizes all sixteen patterns of quadratic residues and nonresidues at four consecutive translates exactly when its order avoids \(\{5,7,11,13,17,19,23,25,29,31,41,49,53\}\). The sharp eventual cutoff is \(59\), with the extension fields of orders \(25\) and \(49\) providing genuine additional exceptions beyond prime fields.
