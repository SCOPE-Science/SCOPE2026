For a union of \(k\) distinct lines through the origin in \(\mathbb F_q^2\), with \(2\le k\le q\), the smallest possible largest nontrivial Fourier coefficient of a probability measure is exactly \(1/(2k-1)\) when \(q\le2k\) and \((q-k)/(k(q-1))\) otherwise. Every optimizer has the same rigid origin mass, which vanishes precisely when \(q\ge2k\); a canonical optimizer is Fourier-flat across all nonzero frequencies whenever \(q\le2k\).
