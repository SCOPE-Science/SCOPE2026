# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

For strictly positive four-heading circulant coins, the stationary tilted transfer matrix has a simple analytic Perron branch. Direct exact characteristic polynomials for axial and normalized diagonal displacement give a shared quadratic coefficient and a difference of quartic eigenvalue coefficients. Since the quadratic logarithm terms cancel in the fourth-cumulant difference, the rational contrast is exact with the stated normalization. Positivity gives the exact open range of the nematic eigenvalue, and the derivative of the contrast is strictly positive on it. The inverse and both sharp endpoint limits follow. The packaged checker verified the two matrix polynomial identities and inverse symbolically and cross-checked six independent finite Markov moment calculations; those finite tests do not replace the proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Sherf and collaborators (arXiv:2611.14446v1) establish the reduced active chiral coin, its polar/nematic modes and covariance blindness. Sadjadi and collaborators (Phys. Rev. E 91, 062715) already establish broad forward fourth-moment formulas and second-harmonic sensitivity. These are fully credited. The specific new theorem is the **global strictly monotone axial-minus-diagonal fourth-cumulant inverse plus the sharp feasible interval and unique realization of a strictly positive reduced discrete coin**. Closest checked published fourth-cumulant classifications for four-heading or arbitrary-turn walkers use a continuous-time Poisson-tumble update rather than the discrete-time coin. General forward moment formulas carry a material risk of implicit derivability and independent publication-priority assessment has not occurred.

## Value — PASS

The complete family of two-point transport measurements cannot identify the nematic mode, although that mode governs physically meaningful orientation memory. An explicit *single additional observable* reconstructs it and simultaneously tests whether a measured contrast is realizable by any strictly positive four-way coin. The proof gives a natural full classification rather than a sample or numerical table. No finite-sample sensitivity claim or recovery of overparameterized five-operation weights is made.
