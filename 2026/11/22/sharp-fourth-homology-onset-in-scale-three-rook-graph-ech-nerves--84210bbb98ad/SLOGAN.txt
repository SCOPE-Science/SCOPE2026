The intrinsic scale-three Čech nerve of the three-by-four rook graph is \(S^4\vee\bigvee^{16}S^5\), whereas the three-by-three nerve is \(\bigvee^4S^5\). Folding all later columns onto a fixed one retracts every larger three-row rook nerve onto the four-column case, so the first fourth-dimensional integral homology class survives as a split summand at every larger width.
