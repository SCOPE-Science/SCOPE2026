For a unit-side equilateral triangle placed by orientation-preserving similarities into an arbitrary rectangle, the four-dimensional volume of every admissible scale-rotation-translation parameter is exactly a linear aspect-ratio law plus \(\tfrac34(2/\sqrt3-q)_+^4\), after normalizing the shorter rectangle side to one. The fourth derivative, but no lower derivative, jumps at \(q=2/\sqrt3\), and at fixed target area the square uniquely maximizes this complete placement-space volume.
