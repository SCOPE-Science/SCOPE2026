For the split quadratic cone \(S_q=\{(x,y)\in\mathbb F_q^2:xy=0\}\), the sharp fourth-power Fourier synthesis constant for all complex weights is \([1+(q-1)^2/(1+2(q-1)^{1/3})^3]^{-1/4}\), with a unique normalized two-orbit extremizer up to modulation. Its central coefficient changes sign between field sizes five and seven.
