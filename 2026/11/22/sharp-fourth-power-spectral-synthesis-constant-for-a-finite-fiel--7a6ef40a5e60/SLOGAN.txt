For the union of two coordinate axes in \(\mathbb F_q^2\), the sharp Fourier \(\ell^\infty/\ell^4\) synthesis ratio is \(\left(1+(q-1)^2/(1+2(q-1)^{1/3})^3\right)^{-1/4}\) at every prime-power order. Every extremizer is a modulated two-level weight, whose shared-origin coefficient becomes strictly negative for all \(q\ge7\), unlike the positive-indicator extremizers for transitive supports.
