For the diagonal cyclic color action on \(\operatorname{Ind}(P_m\square K_q)\), the geometric quotient has fundamental group \(C_q\) exactly when \(m\ge3,q\ge4\) or \(m\ge4,q=3\); the sharp shorter-strip exceptions are a point or free-group circle bouquets. This yields arbitrary-order cyclic torsion in first integral homology of natural flag-complex quotients.
