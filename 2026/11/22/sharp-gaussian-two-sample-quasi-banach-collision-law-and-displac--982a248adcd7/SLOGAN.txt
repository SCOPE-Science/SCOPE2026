For two colliding Gaussian sampling sites, the optimal powered \(\ell^p\) quasi-Banach interpolation factor grows sharply as \(2^{1-p}(2e)^{-p/2}h^{-p}\), with an explicit relative \(h^2\) correction. The worst evaluation points remain near \(\pm1/\sqrt2\), and their first displacement depends explicitly on \(p\), unlike the bounded collision cost of the nonsmooth Laplacian kernel.
