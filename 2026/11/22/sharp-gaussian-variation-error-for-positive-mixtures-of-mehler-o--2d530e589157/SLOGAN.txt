Every nonidentity positive mixture of one-dimensional Gaussian Mehler operators has an exactly computable sharp ordinary-variation-to-Gaussian-\(L^2\) approximation error, and its only nonconstant extremizers are jumps at the Gaussian median. For the Ornstein–Uhlenbeck semigroup the sharp squared error is \(\frac14+\frac{\arcsin(e^{-2t})-2\arcsin(e^{-t})}{2\pi}\), with leading short-time order \(\sqrt t\).
