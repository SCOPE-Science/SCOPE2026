# Scientific review: sharp Gaussian–Weibull dichotomy under diffusivity-only resetting

## Correctness — PASS
The domain consists of a one-dimensional tracer with independent Brownian bath and tracer noises, and independently timed Poisson events that set **only** the bath amplitude to zero. The invariant bath law is exactly Laplace by its exponential backward-reset age. Conditional normality and the bath ergodic theorem prove the long-time Gaussian limit. Quadratic generator identities and their decaying stationary covariance prove the exact fourth-cumulant formula. A Brownian-path-supremum upper bound and a no-reset lower event yield matching logarithmic rare-tail rates, with variational constant attained at a strictly positive unique minimizer. Feynman–Kac Riccati analysis and a Poisson-excursion product bound establish both sides of the deterministic-start exponential-moment boundary, including equality. The checker evaluates 209 analytic and finite numerical assertions. All claims are proved analytically; numerical checks are not taken as infinite proofs.

## Originality — PASS, with stated priority risk
Santra, Basu and Sabhapandit (arXiv:2205.00549, sections 2, 4 and 7) supply the parent squared-Brownian diffusivity model but reset both tracer and bath; they expressly identify bath-only resets as an open variation. Bodrova, Chechkin and Sokolov (arXiv:1812.05664, introduction and model) reset the position but retain a *deterministic* time-varying diffusivity. The closest available published published-finding corpus entries concern position-only resetting of a stochastic bath, simultaneous bath/tracer resetting, or independent bath and position reset clocks, not bath-only refreshing with continuously moving tracer. Zhan and Wang (Phys. Rev. E 109, 044115, 2024) study a general ergodicity criterion and may subsume the generic bulk limit; the present priority claim rests instead on the exact fixed-time \(2/3\)-power rare-event rate, exact kurtosis profile and sharp transform domain. Only the abstract of this last plausible comparison was accessible, so an unrecognized stronger full-text implication remains possible. No originality is inferred merely from a failed keyword search.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The source model explicitly raises selective bath resetting as a meaningful physical question. The theorem distinguishes a nonstationary tracer with Gaussian long-time typical fluctuations from persistent non-Gaussian rare displacements whose exact logarithmic cost is determined. It also provides a measurable full-time kurtosis law and a sharp initialization-sensitive exponential-moment boundary. These observables can distinguish bath refresh protocols that are indistinguishable using the existence of a Gaussian bulk limit alone. The result is structural across all positive bath diffusivities and reset rates rather than a parameter-specific numerical slice. It does not establish precise tail prefactors, first-passage laws, or experimental accuracy.
