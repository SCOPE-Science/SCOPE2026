Resetting only a Brownian-squared diffusivity makes a continually moving tracer Gaussian on the long-time \(\sqrt t\) scale, yet at every fixed positive time its stationary-bath-initialized displacement has a sharp \(\exp[-C(t)x^{2/3}]\) logarithmic tail and no nontrivial exponential moments. Its excess kurtosis is exactly \(30(rt-1+e^{-rt})/(rt)^2\), and the deterministic-zero-bath exponential-moment horizon is \(\pi/(4\Lambda|k|)\), independently of reset rate.
