For analytic Gaussian Fock spaces of different weights, a disk cutoff turns an otherwise unbounded transfer into an operator in every Schatten class, with a sharp norm law \(\|D_R\|\sim e^{\beta R^2(u-1)}/[(u-1)\sqrt{2\pi u\beta R^2}]\) for \(u=\sqrt{\alpha/\beta}>1\). Its maximizing monomial follows a complete no-skipping staircase, whose first transition occurs at the exponentially small relative weight mismatch \(2\beta R^2e^{-\beta R^2}(1+o(1))\).
