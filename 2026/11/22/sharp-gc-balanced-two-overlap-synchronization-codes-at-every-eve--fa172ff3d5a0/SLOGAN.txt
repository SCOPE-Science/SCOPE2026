For four-letter DNA codebooks with exactly half GC content and no one- or two-symbol prefix–suffix overlaps, the maximum size at every even length \(n\ge4\) is \(2^{n-4}\bigl[20\binom{n-4}{(n-4)/2}+16\binom{n-4}{(n-6)/2}\bigr]\). Exactly twelve labeled codebooks attain the bound, and the optimum occupies asymptotically \(9/64\) of all balanced words.
