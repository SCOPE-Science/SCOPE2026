Deleting one intermediate vertex of Hamming weight \(k\) from the Boolean \(n\)-cube leaves all antipodal shortest-path rerouting states connected, but preserves every Kendall shortest rerouting distance if and only if \(k\in\{1,n-1\}\). At every interior layer, a concrete pair requires six legal one-vertex rerouting moves instead of four, although only the fraction \(1/\binom nk\) of underlying shortest cube paths is lost.
