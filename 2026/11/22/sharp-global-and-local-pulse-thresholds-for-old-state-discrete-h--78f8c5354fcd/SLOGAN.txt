For positive pure removal \(y^{i+1}=y^i/(1+\tau q u^i)\) with fixed total effort \(E\), old-state quadratic-penalty harvesting concentrates all effort in one step exactly when \(c=\lambda/(\eta y^0\tau q^2)\le(1+\tau q E)^{-1}\), for every finite horizon. Above this boundary but below \(c=1\), a last-step pulse remains a strict nonglobal local minimum, and optimal positive effort amounts always decrease in time.
