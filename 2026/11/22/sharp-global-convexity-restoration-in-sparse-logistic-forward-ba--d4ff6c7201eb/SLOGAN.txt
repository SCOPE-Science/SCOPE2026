For scalar sparse logistic regression with a soft target, the forward–backward envelope is globally convex exactly up to \(\gamma=3/[3/4+(|q-1/2|-\lambda)_+^2]\); increasing the \(\ell_1\) penalty can therefore restore convex merit-function geometry without changing the underlying smooth loss. The same shrunk displacement controls the unique fitted probability, and the sharp threshold extends coordinatewise to separable weighted logistic losses.
