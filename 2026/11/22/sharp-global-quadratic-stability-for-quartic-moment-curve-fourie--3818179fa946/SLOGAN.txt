For four independent draws from any \(q\)-symbol probability distribution, the loss in expected distinct rearrangements relative to uniformity has the sharp global quadratic coefficient \(23-49/q+33/q^2\), with equality only at the uniform and point-mass laws. Equivalently, this is the optimal amplitude-stability remainder for eighth-moment Fourier extension on the quartic moment curve over every finite field of characteristic greater than four.
