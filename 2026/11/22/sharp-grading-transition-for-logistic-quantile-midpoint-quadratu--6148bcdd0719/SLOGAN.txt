The ordinary logistic equal-quantile midpoint rule has sharp homogeneous Sobolev error \(E_n\sim\sqrt{2\log2/n}\), but power-logit grading with exponent \(p>2\) restores the optimal \(n^{-1}\) rate. Exactly at \(p=2\) a logarithmic penalty appears; all three regimes and their sharp constants follow from an exact weighted-CDF error identity.
