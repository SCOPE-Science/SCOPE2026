For every finite alphabet and every at-most-two-state DFA specifying legal suffix removals, the maximum length-\(n\) Sprague–Grundy value is exactly one of \(0\), \(n\bmod2\), \(1\), \(\lceil n/2\rceil\), or \(n\), decided solely by the four possible letter transition types. Boundedness is equivalent to the absence of an accepting reset and of a simultaneous rejecting-reset/swap pair; the latter mixed case has an explicit exact run-block formula.
