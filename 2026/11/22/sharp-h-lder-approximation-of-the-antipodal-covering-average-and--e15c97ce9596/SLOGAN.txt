For every strictly concave normalized circle-distance transform \(\phi\), the least Lipschitz-to-continuous error of an equal-weight \(m\)-map approximation to antipodal fiber averaging is exactly \(\phi(1/2)/m\) for odd \(m\) and \(1/m\) for even \(m\). For Hölder metrics \(\phi(s)=s^\alpha\), this gives the exact regularity threshold \(\alpha=\log_2((2k+2)/(2k+1))\) above which adding the \((2k+2)\)-nd map makes the optimal error *larger*.
