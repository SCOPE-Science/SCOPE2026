For every real \(\ell_1^d\) with \(d\ge3\), the sharp convex-hull distance of the Fermat–Torricelli median of \(\{0,x,y\}\), over unit Birkhoff–James-orthogonal pairs, is exactly \(1/2\). Equality pairs are precisely three disjoint half-unit coordinate blocks arranged as \(x=c+u\), \(y=c+v\); all are automatically orthogonal in both directions, and dimension three has one symmetry orbit.
