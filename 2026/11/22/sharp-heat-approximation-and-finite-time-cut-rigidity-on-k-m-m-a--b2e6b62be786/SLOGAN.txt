For unweighted graph heat on \(K_{m,m}\), the exact counting-\(\ell^1\) identity-defect norm relative to edge variation is \(2(1-e^{-mt})/m\); on \(K_{m,m+1}\), it has an explicit three-exponential expression. For every finite positive time, adding one vertex collapses the optimal cuts from every equal-shore-count cut to exactly one vertex from each shore and its complement.
