For single-column Nyström approximation of the combinatorial heat kernel on an unweighted \(m\)-leaf star, the trace-optimal landmark changes exactly once from any leaf to the hub, at time \(\tau_m\sim W(m^2)/m\), although every leaf always has the larger kernel diagonal. At any fixed positive time, the probability that rank-one leverage sampling selects the eventually unique optimal hub tends to zero, and both its expected excess trace error and that of diagonal-proportional sampling tend to \(1-e^{-t}\).
