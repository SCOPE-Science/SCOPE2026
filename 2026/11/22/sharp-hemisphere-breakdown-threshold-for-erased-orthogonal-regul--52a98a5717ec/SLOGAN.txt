For two orthogonal unit regular polygons, the spherical covering radius after **any** vertex erasures is determined solely by the two largest circular gaps, including configurations whose surviving convex hull no longer contains the origin. The first erasures making the radius exceed \(\pi/2\) require exactly \(\lfloor p/2\rfloor+\lfloor q/2\rfloor\) deletions and have exactly \(pq\) extremal patterns; for the volume-maximizing square–pentagon nine-site configuration this means twenty four-erasure failures, each with covering angle \(106.448803841\ldots^\circ\).
