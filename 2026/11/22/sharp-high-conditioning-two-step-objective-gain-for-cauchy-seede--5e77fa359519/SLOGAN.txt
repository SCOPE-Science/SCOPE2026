The first curvature-feedback step after a Cauchy seed can make a two-dimensional quadratic's objective gap rise above its initial value, even though the seed was an exact line search. The complete two-step gain is strictly below the condition number \(\kappa\), and its worst-case high-conditioning coefficient is sharply \(\lim_{\kappa\to\infty}\kappa^{-1}\sup F_2/F_0=1\).
