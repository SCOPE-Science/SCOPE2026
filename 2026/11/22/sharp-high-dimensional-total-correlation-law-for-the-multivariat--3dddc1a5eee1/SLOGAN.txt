For the homogeneous multivariate Raftery copula, the full \(d\)-variate relative entropy from independence reduces exactly to the entropy of the sample minimum. At any fixed interior dependence parameter \(\theta\), its total correlation equals \(d\log(1/(1-\theta))-\theta\log(d/(1-\theta))-H_b(\theta)+o(1)\), with an explicit nonasymptotic error bound.
