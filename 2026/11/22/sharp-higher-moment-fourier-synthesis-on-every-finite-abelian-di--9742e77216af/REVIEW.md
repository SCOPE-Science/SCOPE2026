# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. Character orthogonality and the exact uniform difference-fiber count produce the fourth-moment upper bound with an explicit sum of nonnegative defects. The quadratic Hermite interpolant at the two-point character profile has a signed third-order remainder for every real exponent greater than two. Combining the mean, the squared-moment bound and the pointwise Cauchy cap yields the claimed sharp value; strict contact at just two intensity levels forces an exact character restriction. The identity at the sixth moment is algebraically reconstructed from these two deficits. The standalone checker passed for cyclic and noncyclic examples; numerical examples are not substituted for proof.

## Originality
PASS at best-knowledge level. The closely related public fourth-moment difference-set result states the exact fourth-moment constant and deficit but explicitly does not treat higher moments. The public Fano Singer sharp sixth-moment result covers only a three-character, seven-point instance; the subgroup-hole sixth-moment theorem covers the complement-of-singleton difference-set subfamily; and the previously recorded order-three Singer computation covers a four-character, thirteen-point instance. Their values match the new general formula but do not imply its all-difference-set, continuous-real-exponent statement. The 2017/2019 harmonic-frame primary papers focus on construction and geometry rather than this sharp higher-moment synthesis law. The residual priority risk is an equivalent unindexed convex-order inequality or sharp higher-frame-potential theorem.

## Value
PASS. The theorem turns an infinite continuum of nontrivial sharp norm problems, across all finite abelian difference sets and arbitrary coefficient phases, into a parameter-only exact formula with a completely rigid equality locus for every real exponent greater than four. The sixth-moment deficit separates amplitude imbalance, difference-fiber phase incoherence, and spatial failure of the two-level intensity profile. Ordinary endpoint interpolation fails to give these sharp constants. It is a structural lifting theorem, not merely a new value for a chosen small group.

## Limits
No claim is made for nonuniform difference sets, for \(2<p<4\), or for uniqueness of fourth-moment extremizers. Historical originality remains best-knowledge rather than a guarantee of independence from all existing literature. No independent validation has been performed.
