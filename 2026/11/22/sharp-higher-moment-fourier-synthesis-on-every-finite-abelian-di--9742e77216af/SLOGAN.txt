Every finite abelian difference-set Fourier synthesis operator has an exact sharp \(\ell^2\)-to-\(L^p\) norm for every real \(p\ge4\), given by its two-level character-sum profile. For every \(p>4\), the only nonzero maximizers are the character restrictions, and at \(p=6\) an exact nonnegative three-term deficit quantifies failure of equality.
