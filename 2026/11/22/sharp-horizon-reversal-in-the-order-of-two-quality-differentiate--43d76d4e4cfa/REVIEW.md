# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The ODE impulse solution is explicitly reconstructed on each interval, and the integrated response is obtained exactly. The swapped-order contrast is affine in the single variable \(z=e^{-(a+b)(H-d)}\), with endpoint values \(E>0\) and \(-D\); this proves the sharp three-regime classification including all equality and boundary cases. The singular small-gap limit follows from first derivatives of the two exponentials. The standalone verifier passed 22813 supplementary assertions. It is not a substitute for the analytic proof.

## Originality

PASS on inspected evidence, not a claim of exhaustive historical novelty. The 2023 quantity-quality subsidy paper tracks transient nutritional quality but does not classify pulse ordering by horizon. The 2019 pulse-dynamics synthesis does not formulate this bilinear order comparison; a 2011/2012 experimental resource-pulse study varies type and frequency rather than interchanging two quality labels with equal masses. Searches of mathematical research records, generic bilinear representations, and primary literature found no explicit covering theorem. Generic quadratic-Volterra algebra could encompass the derived contrast, so an exact specialized antecedent remains a residual risk.

## Value

PASS. This is the minimal two-event experiment for distinguishing pulse quality ordering from pulse quantity: equal mass and a fixed gap isolate quality. The result demonstrates a sharp reversal of the accumulated ecological response despite unchanged pulse totals and proves the non-obvious irrelevance of quality contrast and pulse mass to the switch time. The positive limiting threshold as pulse separation vanishes is an instructive singular design boundary. This is a motivated, fully solved benchmark for time-window effects, not a calibrated ecological prediction.
