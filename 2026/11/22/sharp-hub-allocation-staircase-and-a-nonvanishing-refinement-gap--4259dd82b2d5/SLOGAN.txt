On every screened star, the globally minimum-variance \(k\)-color independent Rademacher trace-probing partition has balanced leaf-only classes and a hub class whose leaf occupancy follows an exact, sharp screening-dependent staircase. If an optimal \(k\)-class partition can be upgraded only by splitting one class, its best variance exceeds the unrestricted \(k+1\)-class optimum by an asymptotic factor \(1+(k-1)/(2k^2)\), including \(9/8\) when moving from two classes to three.
