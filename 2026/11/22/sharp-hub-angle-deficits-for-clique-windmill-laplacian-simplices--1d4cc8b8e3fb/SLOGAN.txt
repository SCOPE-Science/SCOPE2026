In the canonical Euclidean Laplacian simplex of a windmill made from \(m\) cliques of fixed size \(r\) around one hub, the hub's Gaussian normal-cone angle falls below \(1/2\) by exactly \(\sqrt{r^2+r-1}\,\sqrt{\log m}/(\sqrt\pi(rm+1))\) to leading order. At fixed graph order, adding one disjoint matching among the leaves of a star to make friendship-graph triangles amplifies this deficit by the sharp factor \(\sqrt5\).
