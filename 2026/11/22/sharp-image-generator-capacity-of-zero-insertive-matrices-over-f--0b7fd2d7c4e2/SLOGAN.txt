For every finite commutative chain ring of radical length \(k\), the largest minimal number of image generators among zero-insertive \(n\times n\) matrices is exactly \(n-\lceil n/(k+1)\rceil\). More sharply, fixing the first factor of the zero-product sandwich makes the capacity equal to matrix size minus its most frequent Smith exponent; this explains a sharp transition from the half-rank field boundary to nearly full image-generator rank.
