Geometric–negative-binomial(shape five) mixtures are compound Poisson exactly when \(((1-w)/w)(1-q)^4\ge4\), even though each component is infinitely divisible for every parameter. Specializing to Poisson–XRani counts gives a unique sharp infinite-divisibility cutoff at \(\lambda_*=0.537024530655189\ldots\); beyond it, infinitely many compound-Poisson logarithmic coefficients are negative despite persistent mixed-Poisson overdispersion.
