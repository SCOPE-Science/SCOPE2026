For unweighted cellwise affine \(L^2\) projection on any real interval mesh, the sharp integrated error on \(BV\) is exactly \(\kappa h\operatorname{Var}(f)\), where \(\kappa=0.2514976565\ldots\) is determined by a unique cubic root and \(h\) is the longest cell width. Worst-case unit jumps occur at the two off-centre relative positions \(0.2343280862\ldots\) and \(0.7656719138\ldots\) of any longest cell.
