Exact normalization: the local projection of a unit jump is the affine function determined by two moments. Rational piecewise integration yields equations (1) and (2) in `RESULT.md`, and elementary derivative analysis proves the global sharp constant. Standard-library checker `check_projection_error.py` recomputes a finite collection of moment, polynomial, symmetry, superposition and bounding identities; it is not a substitute for the analytic proof.
