The classical Bernstein–Kantorovich operator has an exact sharp integrated error on the unit ball of real \(BV\), attained only by one jump: for even degree the jump lies at \(1/2\), whereas for odd degree it splits into two strictly off-center, symmetric optimal locations. The sharp constants are explicit finite binomial expressions and satisfy \(\sqrt n\,C_n\to1/\sqrt{2\pi}\).
