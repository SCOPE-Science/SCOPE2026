For classical point-sampling Meyer–König–Zeller averaging, the sharp \(BV\)-to-integrated-\(L^1\) approximation error has an exact finite Beta-cell formula. Its worst error is an unattained left-boundary limit for degrees one through seven, while an interior jump strictly overtakes that benchmark from degree eight onward; asymptotically the sharp coefficient is \(2\sqrt2/(3\sqrt{3\pi n})\) and near-worst jump positions converge to \(1/3\).
