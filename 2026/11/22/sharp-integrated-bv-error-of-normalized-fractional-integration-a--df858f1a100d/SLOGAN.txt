The constant-preserving normalized Riemann–Liouville integral has an exact sharp \(BV\)-to-\(L^1\) identity-error coefficient, attained only by a single jump at a uniquely determined interior point. As the fractional order grows, that worst jump retreats to the origin on the sharp Lambert scale \(s_\alpha\sim W(\alpha)/\alpha\), and the integrated error deficit obeys \(\alpha(1-C_\alpha)=W(\alpha)+1+o(1)\).
