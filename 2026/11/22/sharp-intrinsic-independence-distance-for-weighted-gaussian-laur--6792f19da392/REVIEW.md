# Mathematical review of the intrinsic annular independence gap

## Correctness

**PASS.** The setting fixes curvature \(-1\), the complete annulus and a proper complex Gaussian field. Katori--Shirai's theta-quotient identity (2020, Proposition 2.2 and Lemma 2.3) gives every covariance zero, and proper Gaussianity turns it into the complete independence set; no assumption that zero correlation merely implies approximate independence is made. The strip uniformization gives the annulus metric with exact normalization. The upper-half-plane distance formula, minimum over all covering lifts, fixed product constraint, and strict monotonicity in the squared radial imbalance prove both the exact distance and uniqueness up to common rotation. The sharp core-length bound and the near-exception singularity are direct consequences of the exact formula. The standalone 180-case arbitrary-precision checker verifies representative algebra and spectral-covariance cancellations but is not used as an infinite proof. Exact exceptional weights and open boundaries are treated separately.

## Originality

**PASS under checked-literature, best-knowledge priority; residual risk remains.** Katori--Shirai (arXiv:2008.04177), inspected in its 2020 full preprint including Proposition 2.2, Lemma 2.3 and Proposition 2.4, already established the annular GAF, theta-quotient and covariance zeros, as well as the independence partner for the special invariant weight. Gafai--Murid--Wahid (arXiv:2202.09114), inspected at the weighted product identity, independently presents kernel-zero factors. Neither inspected text states a sharp *intrinsic hyperbolic separation minimization*, the log-periodic all-weight distance, its unique optimizing orbit or the logarithmic blow-up. The 2023 Gafai--Murid--Naqos--Wahid article's accessible article text and abstract concern computation of zeros on doubly connected domains; its full equation-level exposition was not accessible in the consulted view, creating a real but nondecisive priority risk. Searches of a published research index found annular Szegő powers and critical points, nonradial weighted boundary zeros, and ordinary Bergman annulus properties; none of the recoverable statements implies this distance theorem. An accessible earlier annular Szegő sampling result concerns maximal determinants on a fixed circle and does not evaluate shortest hyperbolic distances of orthogonal evaluations. This is a new computation of a natural invariant, not a claim to rediscover the zeros.

## Value

**PASS.** Exact independence of Gaussian evaluations is the natural zero-correlation endpoint of a sampling problem; on a multiply connected domain its meaningful separation must use the intrinsic complete hyperbolic metric. The theorem answers the sharp optimization over all pairs and all positive weights, shows which positive weights prohibit independent samples altogether, identifies the unique minimizing orbit of pairs, relates the sharp minimal gap to the systolic core length, and determines the singular weight asymptotics. These yield a complete weight phase diagram of a mathematically natural invariant and are not implied quantitatively by a bare zero-location formula. The result does not claim mixing estimates for nonzero correlations or independent zero processes; those are separate problems.
