In a volume-preserving cubic Hopf–Langford flow, uniform physical-volume sampling of a finite invariant region gives a sharp winding-ratio tail \(\Pr(\mathcal R>R)\sim (12\pi/\beta)R e^{-\pi R/\beta}\). An exact elliptic period formula and a Euclidean volume–action identity produce the coefficient; invariant tori and first integrals themselves are previously known.
