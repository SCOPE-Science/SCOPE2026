For every stable nonsymmetric inverse Ornstein–Uhlenbeck semigroup with positive diffusion, the exact \(L^p\)-to-\(L^\infty\) norm has a Gaussian spatial profile, sharp extremizers, and long-time decay \(e^{t\operatorname{tr}B}\) for each finite \(p\). Its normalized evolution approaches rank one, with a sharp first-order error proportional to \(\|Q_\infty^{-1/2}e^{tB}Q_\infty^{1/2}\|_2\) and an explicit universal Gaussian-moment coefficient.
