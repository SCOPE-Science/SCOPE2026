For the Zhu–Deng–Zhang–Zhu reciprocal sine-square map on \((0,1]\times[0,1]\), every strictly interior target has \(2aK_b(v)/\varepsilon+O(1)\) regular one-step preimages with first coordinate at least \(\varepsilon\), where \(K_b(v)\) is an explicit finite phase-count staircase. Every interior target has infinitely many preimages precisely when \(b\ge1/2\); their second coordinates converge in empirical distribution to an explicit equally weighted set of atoms.
