For the three-step nilpotent with unit adjacent couplings and complex shortcut \(c\), the least norm of an involution conjugating it to its negative is \(p+\sqrt{p^2-1}\), where \(p=1+|c|^2/4\) up to \(|c|=2\) and \(p=|c|\) thereafter. The unique balanced optimum splits into exactly two asymmetric optima beyond \(|c|=2\), with all minimizing involutions explicit.
