For every fixed degree \(d\ge3\), sufficiently small maximal Blaschke-zero radius \(r\) has a unique least-variance boundary-covering configuration up to rotation: one zero remains at radius \(r\), while every other zero moves strictly inward from the regular polygon. Its sharp boundary-speed variance is \(2d^2r^{2d}-2d^4r^{2d+2}/(d-1)^2+O_d(r^{2d+4})\), with an explicit first-order deformation of every zero.
