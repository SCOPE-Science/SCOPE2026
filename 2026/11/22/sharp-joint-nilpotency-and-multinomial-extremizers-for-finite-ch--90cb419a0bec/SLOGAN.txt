For nilpotent order-preserving endomorphisms of a finite zero-based chain, the pointwise maximum and every mixed composition word vanish by length \(1+\sum_i(\nu(f_i)-1)\), even when the generators do not commute. The bound is sharp, and on its smallest supporting chain the exact number of extremal ordered generator tuples with prescribed individual indices is \((N-1)!/\prod_i(\nu(f_i)-1)!\).
