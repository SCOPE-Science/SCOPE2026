For every connected vertex- and edge-transitive core, attaching one private leaf to each vertex produces a symmetric stochastic Kemeny-design problem with a unique exact square-root allocation, and a sharp global regret certificate valid even for asymmetric edge weights. On cyclic sunlets, optimal pendant transition probabilities decay as \(\sqrt{3/m}\), and equal-edge weighting incurs an asymptotically sharp \(3/2\) Kemeny penalty.
