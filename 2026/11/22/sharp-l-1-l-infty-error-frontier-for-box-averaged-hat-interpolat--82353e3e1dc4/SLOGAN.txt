For centered box-averaged linear hat reconstruction on a uniform real-line grid, the exact BV-to-\(L^1\) and BV-to-\(L^\infty\) approximation errors undergo different bandwidth transitions at \(3/2\) and \(2/3\), respectively. Increasing the averaging width to \(3h/2\) uniquely minimizes the sharp uniform jump error while retaining the smallest sharp integrated jump error; the two constants there are \(49/96\) and \(h/2\).
