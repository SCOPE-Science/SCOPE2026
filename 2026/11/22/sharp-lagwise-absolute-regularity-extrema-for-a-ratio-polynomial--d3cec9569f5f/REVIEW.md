## Correctness
PASS. The full copula domain follows from a continuous mixed-density positivity test. Exact rank-one kernel composition gives the full lag family, and the normalization integral is evaluated analytically. The total-variation coefficient reduces to a one-variable explicit function; a signed derivative proves the positive-branch optimizer. Strict rational inequalities compare the negative and positive branches at every lag. Exact asymptotic factors yield the final rate. The accompanying checker passed on actual supplied source code. Remaining scientific risk: the Markov interpretation assumes the stationary uniform-marginal chain and the stated total-variation convention, both explicitly fixed.

Longla (2024) gives general rank-one fold products, and Longla (2022, 2024) gives broader mixing classifications; these cover the composition technique, which is not claimed as novel, but do not state the specific phase transition, optimal shape \(\alpha=m+2\sqrt m\), or sharp lag asymptotic. Literature and mathematical research comparisons did not identify an equivalent or stronger exact result. The full admissibility boundary was already known to the accessible prior-claims comparison and is treated only as a prerequisite.

## Value
PASS. A natural operational measure of distributional dependence for a Markov model is optimized exactly over the entire parameter class. The sharp optimizer switches sign between the first and second lag, and the worst lag-\(m\) parameter moves to infinity at rate \(m\). The exact polynomial factor on top of geometric decay and the distinction between total variation and maximal correlation expose model-selection behavior invisible from standard rank correlation or qualitative mixing statements.
