For the stationary chain driven by the 2023 ratio-polynomial copula, the sharp absolute-regularity coefficient across every admissible parameter has its unique worst case at a negative boundary parameter at lag one, but at the positive value \(\alpha=m+2\sqrt m\) at every lag \(m\ge2\). The exact global envelope decays as \(2\cdot3^{-(m-1)}/(em)\), and the high-parameter model approaches independence in total variation even while its maximal correlation stays bounded away from zero.
