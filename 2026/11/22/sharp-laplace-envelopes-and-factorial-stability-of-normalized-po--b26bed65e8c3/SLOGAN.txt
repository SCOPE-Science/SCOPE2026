For every normalized positive infinitely divisible chain of depth \(N\), two explicit iterates of \(f\mapsto\exp(-\int_0^{\,\cdot}f)\) give the exact attainable pointwise Laplace-transform envelope, with one boundary attained and the other sharply approachable. Its distance from the mean-one exponential transform is at most \(s^N/N!\) for every \(s\ge0\), with locally optimal coefficient one.
