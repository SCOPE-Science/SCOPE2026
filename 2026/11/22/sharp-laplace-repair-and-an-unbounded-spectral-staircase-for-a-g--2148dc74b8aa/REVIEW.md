# Review: Sharp Laplace repair and an unbounded spectral staircase for a geodesic Matérn mixture on the circle

## Correctness — PASS
For every \(a>0\) and \(b\ge0\), the normalized Fourier coefficient is \((J_n+b I_n)/(1+b)\), where \(I_n>0\) is the explicit coefficient of the geodesic exponential. Differentiating \(I_n\) twice gives the exact ratio for the Matérn-\(5/2\) factor. Odd frequencies and frequency zero have strictly positive coefficients; on nonzero even frequencies, the positivity condition reduces *exactly* to \(b\ge L+cx_m-hx_m^2\). Completing the square proves a finite maximizer and at most two adjacent active indices. The derivative inequalities for the consecutive comparison ratios prove one crossing per pair and the no-skips staircase. Fourier positivity, consecutive-frequency Vandermonde inversion and the RKHS Fourier criterion prove the stated semidefiniteness, strict finite-site positivity and universality conclusions. The proof includes both asymptotic regimes. The included independent numerical quadratures and symbolic differentiation check 500 finite assertions. Numerical samples alone would not establish the universal statements; the analytic argument does.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge, subject to historical priority)
Huang–Li, arXiv:2201.12856, develops the SPDE-based *circular* Matérn model because directly substituting geodesic distance into ordinary Matérn profiles need not preserve positive definiteness. That construction is not the particular two-component geodesic Matérn-\(5/2\)/Laplace convex mixture and does not entail its least allowable ratio. Alegría et al., arXiv:2101.05394, introduce spherical hypergeometric covariance functions; their result does not provide this sharp two-component mixing law. Here the distance-polynomial mixture has differentiated coefficients and an exponentially growing critical frequency. ## Value — PASS
The usual geodesic Matérn-\(5/2\) replacement cannot serve as a stationary circle covariance without modification; the Laplace profile is a canonical admissible alternative. The exact least mixing proportion is a natural, application-relevant admissibility frontier for the simple two-scale-matched-component repair. The surprising interior spectral notch means the high-frequency endpoint positivity test is insufficient at every damping strength. The one-by-one critical even-mode staircase and exponential frequency law identify a qualitative instability not supplied by the closest harmonic-modulation analogues. This is a self-contained sharp natural classification, not an arbitrary point-value calculation. Its limitation is important: repair introduces a cusp and sacrifices smoothness.
