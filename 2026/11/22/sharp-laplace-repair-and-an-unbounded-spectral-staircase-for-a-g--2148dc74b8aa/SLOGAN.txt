Mixing a geodesic Matérn-\(5/2\) profile with a rough Laplace covariance repairs positive definiteness on the circle at an exact sharp mixing threshold. The critical Fourier obstruction moves through every successive even mode, with its index growing as \(\sqrt{a/\pi}e^{\pi a/2}\); at the threshold the covariance remains strictly positive definite on finite distinct sites but loses universality.
