# Verification of the mathematical claim

The proof supplies a sphere-extremum radial eigenvector, exact homogeneous radial solution, nonzero Lie-derivative values on that ray, top-degree induction, and lift obstruction. These steps cover every dimension and arbitrary real lower-order polynomial terms.

To reproduce the finite symbolic checks, install SymPy and run `python3 gradient_degree_checker.py`. In an exact rational polynomial ring the checker passed 27 cubic, 24 quartic, 21 quintic and 8 affine-boundary checks (80 total). This finite test suite is supplementary, not a proof of all iteration orders.

A polynomial finite lift is ruled out only when the full state is included as linearly observable; nonpolynomial lifts and approximate finite dictionaries are outside the claim. Independent audit has not been performed.
