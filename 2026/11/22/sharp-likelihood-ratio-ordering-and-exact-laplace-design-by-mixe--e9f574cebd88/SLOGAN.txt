A particle that switches between diffusion and ballistic relocation has a complete, sharply reversing likelihood-ratio ordering of stationary absolute displacement as the ballistic-start probability at resetting varies. For every nonbalanced set of positive rates, exactly one interior resetting mixture generates a pure Laplace spatial law, while every distinct pair of mixtures crosses at the same one spatial radius; the boundary also reverses the stationary density's logarithmic curvature.
