# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The regenerative occupation formula follows from finite expected cycle duration, the return path's exact occupancy at each radius, and the Brownian heat-kernel/Tanaka identity. The Gamma endpoint is a difference of independent Gamma variables by its characteristic function. For shape above one, log-concavity of Gamma densities and closure under convolution imply log-concave absolute-endpoint survival. Below one, an explicit positive exponential-mixture integral implies strict log-convexity of the survival. The mean-residual-life identity transfers the survival curvature to a strict phase-density likelihood-ratio order and hence to the entire speed-indexed family. The positivity and normalization checks guarantee strict tail order at every radius. The checker passed 114 deterministic assertions including two independent integral representations, exact anchor cases and the inversion identity. This finite corroboration is not used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS (with publication-priority risk)
Radice's 2022 full-text model contains exact stationary densities, established speed independence for exponential alarms, comparisons at the origin and far tail, and explicit integer-Gamma-shape cases. Its location-specific and asymptotic comparisons do not establish a strict likelihood-ratio order at every intermediate radius. Bodrova and Sokolov (2019) formulate general two-phase occupation ratios without the Gamma-shape monotonicity theorem. The published published-finding corpus extreme-Gamma-return finding treats a conditional large-excursion limit rather than the present finite-radius order. No equivalent all-shape likelihood-ratio theorem appeared in the recorded targeted published-finding corpus and literature searches. This is a best-knowledge originality judgment, not a guarantee of global publication priority; known log-concavity theorems are deliberately treated as prior mathematical tools.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The global order resolves the qualitative distributional effect of finite return speed, beyond previously reported center/tail behavior, with a sharp shape-one boundary. It implies every positive-threshold reliability comparison and monotone radial loss comparison, and it supplies a natural inverse experiment: one stationary exceedance fraction determines the otherwise hidden return speed (except at the physically distinguished Poisson reset clock). This is a structural result for a motivated transport parameter, not a routine isolated numerical invariant.
