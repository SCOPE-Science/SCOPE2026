For Brownian motion with Gamma-timed resets followed by finite-speed ballistic returns, raising the return speed strictly decreases every stationary absolute-displacement tail when the Gamma shape \(k>1\), and strictly increases every such tail when \(0<k<1\); the stronger likelihood-ratio ordering holds for all radii. At \(k=1\) the stationary law is speed-independent, while for every other shape a single positive-threshold exceedance probability uniquely identifies the return speed.
