# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof establishes local smoothness, computes a nonzero exact axial derivative, and promotes that derivative to an isometric-scale linearization in all directions by tetrahedral representation theory. The resulting norm bound implies local nonreturn for all noncentral points. Exact rational checks reproduce the algebra and facet feet; they supplement rather than replace the universal proof.

**Originality — PASS, provisional.** Zwierzyński establishes a central positive return and an explicit spatial nonreturn, but does not calculate this full three-dimensional differential. The closest accessible axial invariant-oval theorem does not control transverse displacements. The old universal return claim of Kostanyan has been contradicted by an explicit example; Abu-Saris and Hajja use a different orthic-simplex operation. Priority searches are bounded.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The exact coefficient resolves a natural stability question at the sole fully symmetric point and gives a geometric local rigidity theorem for arbitrary perturbation directions, not a one-dimensional shape formula alone. It identifies a quantitative boundary to planar pedal-shape return behavior.

**Limitations.** The result is local, not a classification of all interior points; independent scientific and priority review remains outstanding.
