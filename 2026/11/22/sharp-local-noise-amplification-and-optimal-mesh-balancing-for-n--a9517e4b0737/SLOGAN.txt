Nonuniform PCHIP preserves every monotone data vector's range, but its sharp interior-cell two-input uniform-norm noise amplification is \(1+2t(1-t)[q(a,b)(1-t)+q(c,b)t]\), with \(q(a,b)=3b(a+b)/(a(a+2b))\). For a fixed middle cell and fixed total neighboring length, equal neighboring lengths uniquely minimize this amplification; collapsing either neighboring cell makes the optimal cellwise sensitivity diverge.
