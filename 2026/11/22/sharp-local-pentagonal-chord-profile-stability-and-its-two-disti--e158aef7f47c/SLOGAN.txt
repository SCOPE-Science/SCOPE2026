For fivefold arclength chords of a strictly convex curve close to the circle, the exact sharp linearized inverse stability constants are \(\frac{3\sqrt5-5}{7}\sin(\pi/5)\) at fraction \(1/5\) and \(\frac56\sin(\pi/5)\) at fraction \(2/5\). Their unique least-stable perturbations occupy Fourier modes seven and six respectively, and the sharp bounds persist with a controlled \(O(\|f\|_{C^2})\) nonlinear loss.
