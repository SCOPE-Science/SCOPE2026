For three equally spaced Gaussian RBF centres with a narrower pair of outer kernels than the middle kernel, invertibility for every positive middle width holds sharply once \(\alpha h\ge\sqrt{-\log\rho}\), where \(\rho^3+\rho^2+\rho-1=0\). Below this threshold exactly one middle width causes singularity, so a binary finite-width localization mask has a complete analytic safety boundary even though the corresponding ideal delta-column matrix is nonsingular.
