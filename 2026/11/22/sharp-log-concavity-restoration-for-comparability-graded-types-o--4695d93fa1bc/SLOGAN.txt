For one-point types over \(k\) equal-length, mutually incomparable chains of length \(a\) in the generic poset, the comparability-grade sequence is log-concave exactly when \(a\le2\), or \(k=1\), or \(k\ge5\), or \((a,k)=(3,4)\). Five copies restore log-concavity uniformly in the chain length, although the natural two-sided extension correction breaks it for smaller numbers of copies.
