# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS
The normalizations of the Gaussian Fock measure and homogeneous-degree multiplicity are explicit. The strict derivative condition ensures exact monotonic ordering, and gamma second-order localization has an \(O(k^{-\beta-1})\) signed-moment error. Solving the scalar monotonic threshold, summing multiplicities, and accounting for jumps produces both the stated asymptotic and its sharp error exponent. The critical trace follows from exact radial eigenvalues, periodic harmonic averaging, and a logarithmic mean along complete blocks and arbitrary intermediate ranks. The finite checker is supplementary, not the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with bibliographic limitations
Xu–Dong's 2026 constant-coefficient Weyl law assumes an asymptotically homogeneous principal part, not the present logarithmically oscillating leading coefficient; their counterexample varies the weight. Bdarneh provides a general spectral representation, not the count or its optimal remainder. The older homogeneous-principal-part Fock Dixmier theorem likewise does not directly imply the periodic spectral mean. A published log-log oscillation example yields a different, nonmeasurable critical trace; the Bergman commutator periodic-count example acts on a different space and operator. Source texts and published-result searches did not reveal identical coverage. A general periodic pseudodifferential eigenvalue theorem not located in these comparisons remains a real residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The claim quantifies exactly how a natural smooth nonhomogeneous symbol defeats classical Weyl asymptotics, improves the generic leading-term estimate to the multiplicity-optimal remainder across dimensions, and proves that the critical singular trace can nevertheless be fully stable. Both the oscillation envelope and the period-averaged critical trace are invariant data of this full parameter family, not a one-off numerical calculation.

The coefficient restriction enforces monotonicity; no classification is claimed outside it or for general Fock weights. Independent publication-priority verification and independent mathematical audit are outstanding.
