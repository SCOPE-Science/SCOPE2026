For the base-\(3/2\) binary digit-parity word of Dekking, the numbers of \(01\) and \(10\) transitions in its first \(M\) positions each equal \(2M/5+O(\log(M+2))\). This logarithmic error is of optimal order: its maximum absolute value over prefixes up to \(X\) is \(\Theta(\log X)\), proved by an exact signed-pair recurrence and a Chinese-remainder construction.
