For every four positive run lengths, the longest deletion-minimal common supersequences of \(0^a1^b\) and \(1^c0^d\) have length \(a+b+c+d-1\). Their number is exactly \(H(a,b,c,d)+H(c,d,a,b)\), with the explicit binomial formula for \(H\) proved in the accompanying result; the two terms distinguish a single shared zero from a single shared one.
