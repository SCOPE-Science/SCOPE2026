For zero-temperature Glauber dynamics on the ground states of the antiferromagnetic tripartite graph \(K_{n,n,n}\), the five slow relaxation eigenvalues, scaled by \(2^n/n\), converge to \((1/4,1/4,3/4,3/4,1)\). The exact switching times between its six polarized junction states have the universal distance ratios \(5:8:9\), revealing the kinetic scale behind the exponentially slow equilibration.
