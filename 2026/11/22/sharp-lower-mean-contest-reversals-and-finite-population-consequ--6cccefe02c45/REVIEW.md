# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The opponent's exact cubic distribution function gives the bias as a linear expression in the focal third moment. Two Cauchy–Schwarz equality cases rigorously identify the unique atomic extremes for every feasible first/second moment class; a nonnegative polynomial establishes the sharp lower-mean \(4/9\) boundary and an exact discriminant establishes the full variance window. Uniform control of logistic comparison error by the bounded opponent density proves the explicit \(\lambda>75\) sign robustness. Absorbing birth–death harmonic equations and detailed balance, independently replayed with exact fractions, prove the all-community fixation and stationary likelihood-ratio order. Experiments do not substitute for the proofs.

**Originality — PASS, bounded to the specified contest classification.** The full 2025 initiating source compares correlated phenotypic traits under logistic fight probabilities, not the bounded beta-opponent sharp two-moment win/loss frontier, the lower-mean sign-reversal window or its finite-noise stability. Classical Hausdorff moment bounds and two-type birth–death comparison supply general ingredients, and the 2026 stationary Moran source derives related beta-binomial-like forms for different fitness-selection rules. None of the inspected source claims states or implies the entire specific joint classification without introducing the beta-opponent hard-comparison model and applying the resulting cubic contest functional. We do not claim the general techniques or generic stationary form as novel. Some older full text was inaccessible and unindexed priority remains possible.

**Value — PASS.** In phenotype-driven animal contests, mean fighting ability and its variance are natural field summaries. The theorem gives a sharp, interpretable range in which a lower-mean population can be more likely to win, and a complete region where identical first two moments can support opposite contest and replacement outcomes. The symmetric beta opponent is the simplest unimodal nonuniform integer-shape symmetric beta benchmark: a uniform opponent makes win probability equal to mean and forbids the reversal. The finite-noise guarantee links the exact boundary to the source's logistic contest mechanism; finite-size consequences hold for any community size, not a single decorative census. The exact \(\lambda>75\) bound is sufficient rather than necessary.

**Closest work and limits.** Morales, Palaoro and Peixoto (2025), DOI 10.1093/beheco/araf119, provide the animal-fighting-capacity and logistic mechanism; Rychtář and Taylor (2020/2021), DOI 10.3934/dcdsb.2020242, analyze random fitness in different Moran/Wright–Fisher update rules; Braha and de Aguiar (2026), arXiv:2607.06732, obtain exact stationary laws for prescribed fitness update schemes; McCandlish, Epstein and Plotkin (2013), arXiv:1312.1673, analyze general fixation identities. This contribution changes the trait distribution and extends the fight model to a new, separately defined Markov replacement mechanism; it does not purport to reproduce those source dynamics. Independent source priority remains uncertain beyond the inspected record. Atomic extremizers are not attained under an additional density-only restriction; the finite-noise exact optimum and spatial/empirical implications are unproved.
