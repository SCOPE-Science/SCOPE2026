Against a \(\operatorname{Beta}(2,2)\) competitor with mean \(1/2\), a focal animal population with lower mean ability can still win most independent pairwise contests exactly when its mean exceeds the sharp cutoff \(4/9\); an exact variance window allows opposite winners from identical first and second moments. The sign reversal persists for all logistic contest discrimination \(\lambda>75\) in a rational example, and the same hard-contest ability extremes simultaneously bound fixation and stationary abundance in every finite winner-replacement community.
