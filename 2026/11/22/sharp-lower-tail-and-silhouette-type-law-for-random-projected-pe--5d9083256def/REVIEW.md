# Scientific review

## Correctness (C): PASS
The complete proof is analytic, not simulation-based: the projected-edge perimeter formula splits into triangle and quadrilateral branches; two separate sharp global inequalities isolate twelve minima; a uniform homogeneous tangent gauge gives an exact area integral and a conditional planar limit by dominated convergence. The attached checker independently constructs planar convex hulls and performs 6,199 tests; it is corroboration, not an infinite-proof certificate. The normal, edge scale, branch boundaries, and conditional quantifiers are fixed explicitly.

## Originality (O): PASS with residual priority risk
Vickers–Brown (2001) supplies tetrahedral projected-area density and joint perimeter–area examples for other solids. Finch (2012) supplies tetrahedral moments. Publicly reported complete tetrahedral area/silhouette distributions, the third-order area-tail type bias, and the elliptic mixed area–perimeter moment do not imply a **perimeter-conditioned** lower-tail law or its marked angular distribution. Full primary/public result texts were compared, including their limitations, not titles alone. This positive differentiation does not exclude every inaccessible classical formulation.

## Value (V): PASS
The exact small-tail exponent and coefficient quantify the scarcity of near-minimal perimeter views of a fundamental convex polytope. Its roughly 21.18-percent triangular share differs sharply from the 50-percent leading share under area conditioning, giving a geometrically motivated distinction that is not a routine restatement of earlier formulas. The uniform tangent-body law provides more than a single numerical constant.

## Limits
The geometry is a fixed regular tetrahedron and Haar-orthographic views only. Leading asymptotics and weak limits are proved; full perimeter density, higher-order terms, other shapes, independent review and universal historical-priority certification are not claimed. Quadrature decimals are diagnostics and not rational interval certificates.
