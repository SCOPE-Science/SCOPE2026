For the regular tetrahedron of edge length \(2\sqrt2\), almost-minimum-perimeter random orthogonal shadows have sharp quadratic probability \(1.1953469179961405\ldots\,\varepsilon^2\). Only \(0.2118330336632897\ldots\) of those rare near-minimal shadows are triangular, and their rescaled viewing directions converge to an explicit uniform planar star-body law.
