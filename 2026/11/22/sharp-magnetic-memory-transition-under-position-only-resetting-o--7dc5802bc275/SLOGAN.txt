For an inertial thermal particle whose planar position is Poisson-reset but whose magnetically rotating velocity is not, the stationary position autocovariance changes sign infinitely often precisely when \(r>\gamma\), or when \(r=\gamma\) and \(0<\omega<\gamma/\sqrt2\). At the sharp resonant endpoint \(r=\gamma\), \(\omega=\gamma/\sqrt2\), infinitely many tangential zeroes occur without negative covariance.
