Among all minimal-cardinality disk-optimal prediction measures with \(n+1\) boundary atoms and exterior point \(1/a\), the unique phase minimizing the largest atom is \(-1\), while the unique phase maximizing the smallest atom is \((-1)^n\). These two load-balancing goals are compatible exactly for even atom counts, with sharp extreme-mass limits governed by the maximum and minimum values of the Poisson density.
