**Correctness — PASS.** Periodic closing of a word gives the sharp length \(n+m-1\); a shortest-border argument characterizes the extremal words; renewal automaton decomposition gives an exact coefficient recurrence; dominance of the unique simple entropy pole yields a strict positive limiting proportion, and a controlled small-\(\delta_m\) expansion gives the Lambert-W law. Independently exhaustive cyclic-completion checks covered 10,260 admissible words for several moduli, and exact coefficient identities were checked to length 300. Their finite nature is not used as the infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS within inspected evidence.** Pannipitiya (2026) motivates the local penetration invariant but accessible material does not give the specific modular-gap sharp-delay and density law. Holub–Shallit (2016) give an unrestricted-alphabet border recurrence, and Climenhaga (2014) provides an entropy root, neither of which directly implies the parameter-uniform modular periodic-completion law. A previous golden-mean computation shares the modulus-two recurrence, so the novelty claim explicitly excludes that special case. Comparison with the closest known finite-type closure theorem and other gap-shift counting work identifies no statement covering the precise all-\(m\ge3\) law or its Lambert-W asymptotic. Priority remains provisional because unindexed literature could contain a related calculation.

**Value — PASS.** The problem measures an explicit periodic shadowing/closing statistic for a natural family of strictly sofic renewal shifts. The exact extremal bound, all-length count, and sharp squared-logarithmic rarity law show a non-obvious tradeoff when permitted zero-gap congruence becomes sparse. The structural modulus-wide asymptotic goes beyond a finite exercise, a restatement of entropy, or a mere small numerical improvement.

The theorem is about uniformly selected cylinder words and does not determine the full distribution or results for arbitrary gap sets. The arithmetic proof remains subject to independent audit.
