For the binary shift whose zero gaps are divisible by an arbitrary integer \(m\ge3\), the largest least periodic-cylinder penetration time at word length \(n\ge2\) is exactly \(n+m-1\), with a complete Mahler recurrence for how many words attain it. Their positive limiting fraction behaves as \(W(m)^2/m^2\) when the modulus grows, despite the worst additive delay growing linearly in \(m\).
