# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof uses the sharp positive-contraction row-sum lemma, termwise Fréchet differentiation with the correct radius hypothesis, and simultaneous saturation of all power terms by a rank-one boundary projection and an all-ones perturbation. Strict interior sequences convert the boundary directional derivative into a valid global two-input Lipschitz supremum. The included numerical tests supplement this analytic proof.

**Originality — PASS, with priority qualifications.** The full open-access article of Kandolf–Koskela–Relton–Schweitzer (arXiv:2008.12926) gives Krylov derivative approximations and norm-dependent condition estimates, not the exact maximum-entry bounded-PSD formula. Crouzeix–Kressner (arXiv:2007.09784) concerns spectral-norm bounds. The closest known positive-exponential mixture result has an unbounded negative spectral half-line and cannot imply the strict finite-interval sharp constant. A previously published equivalent sharp entrywise statement remains possible; the second source's complete body was not inspected.

**Value — PASS.** Matrix polynomial filters and positive resolvents are natural nonlinear transforms of positive-semidefinite matrices. The result determines their exact worst-case coordinatewise amplification and explains a dimension-dependent gap from operator-norm conditioning. It is an exact structural formula for all nonnegative-coefficient analytic filters, not a mere numerical example.

**Limitations.** Real symmetric PSD matrices and nonnegative Taylor coefficients only. Sharpness is a supremum and does not assert distinct-pair attainment. Independent validation has not occurred.
