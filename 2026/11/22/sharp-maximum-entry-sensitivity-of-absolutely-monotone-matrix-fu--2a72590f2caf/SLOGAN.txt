For a real positive-semidefinite matrix with spectrum bounded by \(b\), every analytic function with nonnegative Taylor coefficients has an exact maximum-entry perturbation coefficient determined by \(c_n=(1+\sqrt n)/2\) and an explicit weighted sum of its coefficients. In particular, matrix powers have sharp coefficient \(b^{k-1}(2c_n+(k-2)c_n^2)\), while the positive resolvent on \(0\preceq A\preceq bI\), \(b<1\), has sharp coefficient \((1+c_n b/(1-b))^2\).
