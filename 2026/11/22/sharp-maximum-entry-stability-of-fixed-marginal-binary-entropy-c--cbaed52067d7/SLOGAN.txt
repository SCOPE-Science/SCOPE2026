For every pair of positive binary marginals, the exact global maximum-entry stability of the entropy-regularized coupling operator is \(4/\min H_{r,c}\), where \(H_{r,c}\) is a four-term reciprocal curvature on the feasible coupling interval. The sharp sensitivity equals the smaller marginal variance precisely when one marginal is balanced; otherwise the maximally sensitive coupling exhibits strict association and the coefficient is strictly smaller.
