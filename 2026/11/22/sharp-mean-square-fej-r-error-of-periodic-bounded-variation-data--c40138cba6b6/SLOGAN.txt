The sharp mean-square error of ordinary Fejér smoothing on real periodic bounded-variation data is asymptotic to \(0.231755332682\ldots\,N^{-1/2}\), and every extremizer is a two-jump arc. The worst arc shrinks to length \(0.532264718605\ldots/N\), rather than occupying half the circle, with a unique universal sine-integral scaling profile.
