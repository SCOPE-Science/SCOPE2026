For two-line proximal splitting, independently switching reciprocal scalar metrics has the exact mean-square factor \(c^2[c^2+(1-c^2)\mathbb E(m^2)]\), so binary reciprocal switching is stable for every nonzero line angle precisely when \(r^2\le2+\sqrt3\). There is a strict contrast window where the same two fixed metrics and their deterministic alternation converge, yet their independent random alternation grows exponentially in expected squared primal--dual error.
