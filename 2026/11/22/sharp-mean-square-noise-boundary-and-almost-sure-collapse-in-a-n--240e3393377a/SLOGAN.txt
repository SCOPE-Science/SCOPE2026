In the published two-coordinate nonexpansive reflection example, the expected squared residual has a sharp critical noise threshold \(\sigma B=1\) for \(\beta_k=B(k+K)^{-1/2}\), improving the known sufficient divergence criterion. Every sample path nevertheless converges to the origin, typically at the exact logarithmic decay rate \(-\tfrac12\sum_{j<k}(\sigma^2\beta_j^2/2)\), even when the mean-square residual diverges.
