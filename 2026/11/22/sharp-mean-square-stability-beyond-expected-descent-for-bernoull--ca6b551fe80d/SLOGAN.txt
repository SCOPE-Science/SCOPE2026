On every positive equicorrelation quadratic in dimension at least two, independent Bernoulli parallel-coordinate descent has an exact all-starting-state mean-square stability ceiling \(\eta_*=4/(S+\sqrt{S^2-4pK})\). This ceiling is strictly larger than the sharp one-step expected-descent bound, so some steps initially raise the expected objective even though the entire stochastic iteration converges geometrically.
