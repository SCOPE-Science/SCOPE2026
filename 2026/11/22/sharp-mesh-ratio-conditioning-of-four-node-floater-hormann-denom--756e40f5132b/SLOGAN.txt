For four arbitrary nodes, the degree-one Floater–Hormann denominator summation factor has a sharp mesh-ratio envelope \((\sqrt{2\mu+4}-1)^2/(2\sqrt{2\mu+4}-3)\), attained only by gaps proportional to \((\mu,1,1)\) or their reversal. Its unavoidable \(\sqrt{\mu/2}\) growth starts at the minimal four-node configuration, while two- and three-node configurations have factor one.
