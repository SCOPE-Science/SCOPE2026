The nonuniform Brodlie weighted-harmonic cubic interpolant preserves every real signal's total variation but has a sharp two-input variation Lipschitz factor \(1+\frac8{27}\sup_j[\Phi(h_{j-1}/h_j)+\Phi(h_{j+1}/h_j)]\), where \(\Phi(r)=3r(1+r)/(1+2r)\). Its perturbation stability is finite precisely on grids with uniformly bounded adjacent-length ratios, and the sharp factor is approached by nearly flat steps rather than attained by finite distinct data.
