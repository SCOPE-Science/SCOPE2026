Immediately below the failure of vertex-generated coverage, every nonsimplicial hypersimplex develops a computable family of regular-simplex voids, with exact volume loss proportional to the \((n-1)\)st power of the parameter excess but a linear sharp Euclidean gap. Uniformly sampled uncovered points have an exactly universal \(\operatorname{Beta}(1,n-1)\) normalized distance-to-cover law, and the deepest points stay fixed at maximal-simplex-face barycenters.
