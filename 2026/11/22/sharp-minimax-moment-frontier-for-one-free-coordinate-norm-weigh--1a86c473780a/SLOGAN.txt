In one-free-coordinate Kaczmarz with independent projected norm-power sampling, the sharp worst-case \(q\)-th noise moment is uniformly bounded exactly when the sampling exponent \(\beta\ge q\); above this threshold the unique worst projected-row geometry has one unit row and equally weak remaining rows. At the conventional \(\beta=2\), a single bounded corrupted measurement can produce unbounded uniform moments of every order \(q>2\) even when the full linear system has condition number at most \(\sqrt3\).
