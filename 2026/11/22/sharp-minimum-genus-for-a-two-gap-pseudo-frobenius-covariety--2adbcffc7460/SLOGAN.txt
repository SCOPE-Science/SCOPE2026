For every odd Frobenius number \(F=2k+1\ge5\), the numerical semigroups required to have \(F-2\) as a pseudo-Frobenius number attain minimum genus \(k+2\) in exactly \(\lfloor 2k/3\rfloor\) ways. All minimizers are explicitly classified by a single interval-switch parameter in an alternating forbidden-sum path.
