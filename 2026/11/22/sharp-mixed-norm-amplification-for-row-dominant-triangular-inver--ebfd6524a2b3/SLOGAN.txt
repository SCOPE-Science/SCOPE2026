Unit lower-triangular matrices with absolute off-diagonal row sums at most \(\tau<1\) have an exact inverse-defect Frobenius bound attained by a chain, while their largest Euclidean inverse norm is asymptotic to \(\tau\sqrt{n/(1-\tau^2)}\). A long chain feeding many leaves realizes the sharp leading spectral amplification, so strict row diagonal dominance does not prevent dimension-dependent nonnormal gain.
