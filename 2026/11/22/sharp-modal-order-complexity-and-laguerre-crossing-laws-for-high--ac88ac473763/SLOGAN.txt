For every pure Gaussian Landau level of order \(q\ge3\), adjacent centered-disk concentration eigenvalues cross at exactly \(2\min(k,q)+\mathbf1_{k<q}\) distinct positive radii, and the full infinite Hermite-indexed spectrum has at most \(q\) upward steps. The bound is attained at all sufficiently large nonresonant disk areas; its turning indices approach the \(q\) Hermite roots on the \(\sqrt{x}\)-scale.
