The fixed-origin wrapped Poisson–Lindley law has a complete, uniquely thresholded modal staircase: sectors \(1,2,\ldots\) become possible only when the circle has at least six discrete sectors, and the largest attainable mode is \(\max\{0,\lfloor(m-4)/2\rfloor\}\). The first transition grows to \(\sqrt2-1\) as the resolution increases, with an exact exponential asymptotic and an algebraically certified six-sector cutoff.
