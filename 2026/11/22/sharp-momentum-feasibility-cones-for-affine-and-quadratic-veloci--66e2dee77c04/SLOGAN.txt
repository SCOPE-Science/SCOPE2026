For equally spaced velocity nodes, fixed affine and quadratic velocity factors permit nonnegative mass-and-momentum-preserving core corrections exactly when the mean velocity lies inside explicit sharp finite-grid intervals, tending respectively to \([-1/3,1/3]\) and \([-1/\sqrt3,1/\sqrt3]\). Even arbitrarily small full-grid changes can move target moments outside these intervals, making an otherwise well-defined low-rank positivity correction infeasible.
