For an induced, pointwise named edge-coloured path in the homogeneous rainbow-triangle-free partial three-colour graph, the number of nonalgebraic one-point types is maximized exactly by monochromatic paths and minimized exactly by paths repeating all three edge colours in cyclic order. The respective extrema have different exponential growth bases, \((3+\sqrt{17})/2\) and approximately \(3.49086362\), and every equality case is classified at every path length.
