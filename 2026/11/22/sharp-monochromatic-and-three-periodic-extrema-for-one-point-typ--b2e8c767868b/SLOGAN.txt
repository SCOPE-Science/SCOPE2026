For an induced path on at least three pointwise named vertices in the homogeneous rainbow-triangle-free partial three-colour graph, the number of nonalgebraic one-point types is maximized exactly by monochromatic edge words and minimized exactly by prefixes of the six periodic repeats of three distinct colours. In particular, the two edges of a three-vertex minimizer differ. The maximum and minimum have different exponential growth bases, \((3+\sqrt{17})/2\) and approximately \(3.49086362\), respectively.
