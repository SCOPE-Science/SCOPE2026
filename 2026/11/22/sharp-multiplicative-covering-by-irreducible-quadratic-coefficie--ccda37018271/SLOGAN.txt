For every nonsquare constant term \(c\) over an odd finite field, products of two nonzero linear coefficients of irreducible quadratics \(X^2+bX+c\) fill all nonzero field elements when \(q\equiv3\pmod4\), and miss exactly \(\pm4c\) when \(q\equiv1\pmod4\). In the latter case three factors fill the group for every \(q>5\), while the field of order five is the unique exception.
