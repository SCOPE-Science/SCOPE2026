For the three-central binomial coefficients, the indices at which both \(c(n,3)\) and \(c(mn,3)\) are prime to five form a three-digit base-25 Cantor set for each \(m=2,3\), with exactly \(3^r\) members below \(25^r\). Yet the simultaneous conditions at \(n,mn,m^2n\) force \(n=0\), and this three-step collapse is sharp because \(2,8,32\) survive at ratio four.
