The local inversion of an ellipsoid's intrinsic volumes has sharp Hölder exponent \(1/m\), where \(m\) is the largest multiplicity of a semiaxis: distinct axes are Lipschitz-stable, but the Euclidean ball has exactly exponent \(1/n\). The conclusion follows from the established intrinsic-volume Jacobian nonsingularity together with a new multiplicity-sensitive polynomial-root matching and exact sharpness construction; it does not assert independent recovery of orientation or center.
