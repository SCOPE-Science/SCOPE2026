Every closed subset of an interval is the Milnor global attractor of a \(C^1\) flow whose stable multipliers at isolated attractor points can be prescribed arbitrarily in \((0,1]\), while all accumulation points necessarily have multiplier \(1\). The location and repelling multiplier of one separator in every complementary gap are independently prescribable, subject only to multipliers approaching \(1\) along shrinking gaps.
