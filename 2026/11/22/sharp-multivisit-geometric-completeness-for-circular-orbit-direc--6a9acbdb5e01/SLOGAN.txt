For every number \(m\ge3\) of freely timed observations of an isotropically oriented circular exoplanet, equally spaced phases modulo half an orbit minimize the probability of missing it behind the inner working angle, uniquely above the exact radius \(\cos(\pi/(2m))\). At that sharp guarantee boundary the optimal miss probability turns on like \(K_m(t-\cos(\pi/(2m)))^{3/2}\), with a closed positive coefficient and all zero-miss schedules exactly classified.
