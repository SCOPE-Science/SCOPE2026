In a two-Gaussian evolving-infectivity SIR equation, diffusion above the sharp threshold \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{crit}}=ks_0\Delta^3/(6\sqrt3)\) forces trait variance to increase for every initial mode mixture. Below that threshold, selective replacement of the two modes can cause variance to fall, and a rigorously specified example has an increase–decrease–increase trajectory despite positive diffusion.
