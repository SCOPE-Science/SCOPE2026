Two independent surface-area-uniform points on a ring-torus surface have a near-diameter Euclidean chord tail with sharp exponent \(3/2\) and coefficient \(2(R+r)^2/(3\pi^2 rR^{5/2})\). At fixed diameter, the dimensionless coefficient is minimized uniquely at \(R/r=5/2\), and its values just above the minimum correspond to exactly two noncongruent torus shapes.
