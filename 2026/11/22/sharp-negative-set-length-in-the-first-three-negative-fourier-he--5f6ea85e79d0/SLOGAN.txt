In the real Fourier \(-1\) Gaussian–Hermite span of orders \(2,6,10\), under \(f(0)=0\), the exact smallest nonpositive-set length is \(\sqrt{2/\pi}(\sqrt{(15+\sqrt{105})/4}-\sqrt{(15-\sqrt{105})/4})\). Adding the order-ten mode cannot improve the unique extremizing positive ray \(e^{-\pi x^2}z(2z^2-15z+15)\), where \(z=2\pi x^2\).
