Among all real self-Fourier Gaussian polynomials in the first nontrivial \(+1\) Hermite band that vanish at zero and have a strictly positive quartic leading form, the radial mode uniquely minimizes the volume of the negative set in every dimension \(d\ge2\). The sharp minimum is \(\omega_d((d+2)/(2\pi))^{d/2}\), and departure from radial symmetry incurs the explicit angular Fisher-information penalty \((1+\mathcal I/(4(d+2)))^{d/2}\).
