Among all real even Fourier-self-dual Gaussian quartics vanishing at the origin, the least possible volume of \(\{f\le0\}\) in every dimension \(d\ge2\) is exactly \(\frac{\pi^{d/2}}{\Gamma(1+d/2)}\bigl(\frac{d+2}{2\pi}\bigr)^{d/2}\), attained only by a positive radial multiple of \(|x|^2(|x|^2-(d+2)/(2\pi))e^{-\pi|x|^2}\). A sphere-gradient inequality quantifies the strict volume excess caused by every nonconstant positive leading quartic.
