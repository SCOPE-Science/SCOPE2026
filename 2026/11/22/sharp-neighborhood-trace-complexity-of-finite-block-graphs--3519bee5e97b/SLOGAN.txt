For the open-neighborhood set system of all finite block graphs, the exact maximum number of traces on \(m\) marked vertices is \(2^m\) when \(0\le m\le3\), and \(3m-2\) for every \(m\ge4\). A sharp path-of-triangles construction and a block-incidence forest bound give VC dimension \(3\) but the stronger exact linear shatter function with leading coefficient \(3\).
