For one optimally timed noisy observation estimating the integral of a zero-start once-integrated Wiener path, observing the path is better at noise variance \(\eta<0.126085210302255\ldots\), while observing its derivative is better above that sharp threshold. At equality the two unique optimal sites are \(1\) for the path and \(0.521234065743882\ldots\) for its derivative; the result assumes equal calibrated noise variance for the two instruments.
