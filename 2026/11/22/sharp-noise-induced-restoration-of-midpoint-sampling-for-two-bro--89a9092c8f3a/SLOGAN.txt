For one noisy evaluation of a standard Brownian bridge used to estimate its first two shifted-Legendre integrals with variance-normalized quadratic loss, the optimal site changes sharply at noise variance \(\eta=1/2\): two reflected off-center sites below, and the unique midpoint above. The complete global threshold for general relative weight \(w>0\) is \(w_c(\eta)=(9+72\eta)/(1+4\eta)\), with a square-root merging of the two optimal sites.
