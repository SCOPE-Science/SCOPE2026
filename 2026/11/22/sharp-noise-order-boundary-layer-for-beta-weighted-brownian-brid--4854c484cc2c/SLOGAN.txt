The unique optimal one-point observation time for each Beta-weighted Brownian-bridge polynomial integral is the interior root of an explicit polynomial, for every nonnegative sensor-noise variance. At high polynomial order, noiseless information retains about \(81.45\%\) of the prior variance with an \(O(1/n)\) endpoint layer, but any fixed positive noise produces an \(O((\log n)/n)\) layer and information decay \(2/(\eta n)\), joined by a sharp \(n\eta\) crossover.
