One high- and one low-fidelity Brownian-bridge observation should never be colocated when predicting the high-fidelity integral, regardless of the positive discrepancy variance. For large discrepancy variance the unique reflection pair of optimal designs puts the high-fidelity site near the midpoint and the low-fidelity site near \((3-\sqrt5)/4\) or its reflection, with a sharp extra explained-variance limit \((5\sqrt5-11)/32\).
