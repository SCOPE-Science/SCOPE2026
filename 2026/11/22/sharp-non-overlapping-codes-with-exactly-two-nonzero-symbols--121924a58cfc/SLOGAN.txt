For every \(q\ge2\) and \(n\ge3\), the largest non-overlapping length-\(n\) code with exactly two nonzero positions has size \((q-1)^2(n-1)/2\) at odd length and \((q-1)^2(n/2-1)+\lfloor(q-1)^2/4\rfloor\) at even length. An antipodal two-marker obstruction turns the even-length extremum into a sharp balanced-bipartition problem, while odd lengths attain the entire primitive-necklace bound.
