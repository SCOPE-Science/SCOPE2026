Over every alphabet of size \(q\ge3\), deleting one symbol yields at most \(q(q-1)^2+q+2\) distinct ordered dinucleotide histograms, and the bound is first attained at length \(q(q-1)^2+2q+2\). A directed Euler-trail construction makes both limits sharp; for random long words, the probability of saturation tends to \(q(q-1)(q(q-1)-1)/q^4\).
