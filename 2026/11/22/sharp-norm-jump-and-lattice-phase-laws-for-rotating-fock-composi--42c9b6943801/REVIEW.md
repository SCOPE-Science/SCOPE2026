A maximum-ratio principle reduces any finite diagonal family to the worst angular decay, whereas an exponentially flat small-angular transition quantifies subtle failure of norm continuity. The quadratic phase correction identifies the exact rate at which individual monomial spectra sample the continuous norm envelope and proves the sharp interval of possible normalized defects.
