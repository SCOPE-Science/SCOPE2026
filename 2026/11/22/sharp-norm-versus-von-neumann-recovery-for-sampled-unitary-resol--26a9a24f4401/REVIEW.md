The exact theorem follows by the closed-subgroup trichotomy for additive subgroups of the real line, strong continuity of the multiplication unitary group, its spectral multiplication calculus, a sharp scalar circle identity, and recurrence for uniform Bohr-almost-periodic functions. For the nontrivial period case, the norm lower bound uses periodicity almost everywhere and translates to infinity, not point evaluations of an equivalence class. The union of factorial clocks has no common nonzero period although each finite stage does. The finite exact checker passes 9,400 rational/integer assertions; these do not substitute for any infinite-dimensional step.

Query comparisons against relevant published mathematical records concern unitary spectral circles, perturbation-algebra distances and sampling orbit frames, not this same statement or a dominating general family. The W* recovery part is readily implied by classical group/spectral facts and is not separately asserted to be an original underlying theorem. The combined sharp minimax/operator-algebra classification was not found under the checked formulations. Residual risk: an older sampling/almost-periodic C*-algebra treatment may already package this exact dichotomy.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The motivating resolvent-sampling problem naturally asks whether changing the nonlinear postprocessing algebra can resolve aliasing. The result answers both measurable identifiability and uniform operator-norm stability, with a sharp error floor independent of the number and arithmetic of clocks, an exact alias-period criterion and a factorial example where every finite measurement family fails but the countable union succeeds in strong topology. Those are structural quantitative boundaries rather than an arbitrary numerical instance.

## Limitations

Only the Lebesgue-spectrum multiplication model is classified, and this is not a norm-stable recovery algorithm. Bohr recurrence, Stone spectral calculus and Kaplansky density are older facts. Literature searches do not establish uniqueness, and an older equivalent full theorem remains a possibility. Independent mathematical review has not been conducted.
