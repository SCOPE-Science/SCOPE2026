For the Lebesgue-spectrum unitary group, every sampled unital \(C^*\)-algebra stays at exact resolvent distance \((2\operatorname{Re}\lambda)^{-1}\), whereas the sampled von Neumann algebra contains the resolvent precisely when the clocks have no nonzero common alias period. Factorial clocks exhibit a sharp infinitary transition: every finite collection retains the full distance, but their union recovers the resolvent measurably.
