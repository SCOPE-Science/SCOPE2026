# Scientific assessment

The proof directly derives the upper Cassini boundary, its unique positive maximum, the concave outer arc, and the horizontal hull contacts. A rationalized identity yields a positive one-variable kernel whose derivative sign establishes global strict monotonicity. The two endpoint constants are evaluated from a uniform limit and from the Bernoulli lemniscate's exact sector area. Uniform small-parameter expansions independently give the \(5/2\) and \(7/2\) exponents. The accompanying checker confirms selected identities, quadratures, endpoints and expansions; it is not a substitute for the analytic argument.

## Value — PASS
The area of the convex hull minus the underlying region measures how much geometry is lost on replacing a nonconvex detection/level-set region by a convex approximation. The theorem does not merely restate the known transition: it gives a globally sharp dimensionless stability law and shows that area and perimeter fail at different powers near the critical parameter. These quantities are natural, not arbitrary slices of the family.
