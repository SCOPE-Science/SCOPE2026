For connected nonconvex Cassini ovals, the missing convex-hull area divided by \(a^2[4-(b/a)^4]^{5/2}\) rises strictly between two sharp exact constants. As convexity returns, missing area is \(a^2\delta^{5/2}/120+O(a^2\delta^{7/2})\) while excess boundary length is only \(a\delta^{7/2}/3360+O(a\delta^{9/2})\), separating two geometric onset scales.
