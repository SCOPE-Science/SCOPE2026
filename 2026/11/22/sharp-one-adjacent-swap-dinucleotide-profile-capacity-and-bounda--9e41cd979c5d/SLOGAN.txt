For every alphabet of \(q\ge2\) symbols, the largest number of ordered-bigram histograms obtainable by at most one adjacent symbol swap is exactly \(3+2\binom q2+14\binom q3+24\binom q4\), with de Bruijn witnesses and a sharp binary first-attainment length of eight. For uniform random long words, the number of outcomes converges to \(I_q\) plus an independent binomial \(\operatorname{Bin}(2,(q-1)/q)\) boundary contribution, where \(I_q=1+2\binom q2+14\binom q3+24\binom q4\).
