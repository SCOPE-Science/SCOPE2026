For each \(0<\beta<1\), a discrete Weibull count law has an explicit unique scale boundary \(q_*(\beta)\), above which its would-be one-count innovation is negative for every sufficiently high Bernoulli retention, despite ordinary additive infinite divisibility. The obstruction has sharp logarithmic small-shape and linear near-geometric endpoint asymptotics; no sufficiency assertion is made below the boundary.
