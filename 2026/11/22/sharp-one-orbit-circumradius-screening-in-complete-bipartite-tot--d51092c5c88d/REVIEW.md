## Mathematical value — PASS
The total-matching polytope is a natural, nontrivial graph optimization polytope with both vertex and edge variables. This exact result identifies when all but one natural, factorial-sized vertex orbit become irrelevant to its smallest enclosing ball. The sharp strict-window and three exceptional integral ties are not a generic property of zero-one polytopes, and the proof's orbit-mean certificate supplies a reusable structural tool. The answer is a complete classification of this naturally defined contact-only phase, without claiming the remaining radius phases.
