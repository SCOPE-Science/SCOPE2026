For complete-bipartite total-matching polytopes, an entire range of unbalanced dimensions has a uniquely determined minimum enclosing sphere supported **only** by saturated-edge matchings together with all unmatched large-side vertices. The precise screening window is \((q-p)(2p-q)>2p\), its squared radius is \(p(2q-p-1)/q\), and equality gives only three integer boundary cases.
