A three-site weighted circular Poisson estimator with a heavier central atom has either one or three strict modes, separated by the exact sharp threshold \(w-1=9r^2(1+r^2+r^4)/(1-r^2)^3\). The two new modes emerge at a simultaneous symmetric fold whose cosine location is \(-r/2\) and whose angular separation obeys a square-root law.
