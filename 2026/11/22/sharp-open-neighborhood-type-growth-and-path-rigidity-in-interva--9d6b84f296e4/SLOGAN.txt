On finite interval graphs, the maximum number of distinct **open-neighborhood** adjacency types over \(m\ge3\) parameters is exactly \(1+\binom{m+1}{2}+m-2\); equality forces the marked intervals to form an induced path in endpoint order. The smallest extremal graph has as many vertices as types except at \(m=3\), where nine vertices are necessary for eight types, and the resulting open-neighborhood VC dimension is exactly three.
