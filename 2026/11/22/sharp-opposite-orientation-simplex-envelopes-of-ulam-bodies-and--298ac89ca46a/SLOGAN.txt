Every Euclidean simplex has two uniquely sharp centroid-centered homothetic envelopes, one in each orientation, around its Ulam floating body; their exact scale gap reverses sign only at half-cap volume and stays nonzero in the high-dimensional limit. The envelope formula also distinguishes a triangle from its centroid reflection at every non-half density, giving singleton fixed-density buoyancy fibers for triangles and exact two-member fibers at half density when combined with known planar difference-body recovery.
