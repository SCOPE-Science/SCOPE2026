The printed normal calibration for the cumulative-residual Tsallis uniformity statistic overestimates its asymptotic null variance by the sharp factor \(\rho(\theta)=8(\theta+1)^2/(2\theta^2+\theta+2)\), ranging strictly between \(4\) and \(32/5\) for admissible fixed orders. At \(\theta=2\), its nominal \(5\%\) normal test consequently has limiting null rejection probability only \(1.57944924\times10^{-6}\); exact finite-sample variance and a corrected Gaussian limit are proved.
