For the two-parameter Poisson–Mirra count model, an explicit finite threshold decides exactly when zero loses global modal status, and the first transition can tie distant counts rather than adjacent ones: at \(\theta=1/5\), \(\alpha=279279/2442775\), only counts zero and six are global modes. As \(\theta\downarrow0\), the first competing count grows like \(1.59362426/\theta\), while the threshold shape is asymptotic to \(3.08827730\theta^2\).
