The canonical \(2\)-graph polytope of every \(K_{2,q}\), \(q\ge2\), has an explicit unique Euclidean circumsphere touching exactly \(q!+2\) of its \(q!\left(2\sum_{s=0}^q(q-s+1)/s!-1\right)\) vertices. Its contacts split into a factorial leaf-order orbit and precisely two hub-order vertices, with a positive-coefficient polynomial identity certifying that no other vertex touches.
