In random sequential covering by \(k\)-mers, the number of retained intervals at two sites has a sharp dependence cutoff at distance \(3k-3\); the last nonzero covariance is positive for every deposition time. At complete coverage its exact value is \((5k+2)/[k^2(k+1)^2(2k-1)(2k+1)]\), decaying as \(5/(4k^5)\) even while the dependence range increases linearly with \(k\).
