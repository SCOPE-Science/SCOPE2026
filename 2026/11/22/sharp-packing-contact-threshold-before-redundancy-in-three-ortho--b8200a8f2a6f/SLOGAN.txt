The unique largest axis-parallel box in three mutually perpendicular, unequal-radius cylinders undergoes an exact contact transition at \(\gamma_*=(\alpha+\beta+2\sqrt{\alpha^2-\alpha\beta+\beta^2})/3\), where squared radii satisfy \(0<\alpha\le\beta\le\gamma\). Past this threshold the box and its volume become completely independent of the largest cylinder radius even though that cylinder continues to cut away part of the body until \(\gamma=\alpha+\beta\); the optimal log-volume is \(C^1\) but not \(C^2\) at the transition.
