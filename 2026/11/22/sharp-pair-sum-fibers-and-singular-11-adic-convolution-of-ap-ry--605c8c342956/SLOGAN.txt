The indices of Apéry numbers not divisible by eleven form a one-digit-deleted \(11\)-adic Cantor set whose twofold sum reaches every residue, with exact sharp fibers \(9^r\le R_r(t)\le10^r\), a unique maximizer, and energy recurrence \(E_{r+2}=923E_{r+1}-12150E_r\). Its natural \(11\)-adic self-convolution has full support but is non-atomic and singular, with correlation dimension \(\log_{11}(10000/\lambda_+)<1\).
