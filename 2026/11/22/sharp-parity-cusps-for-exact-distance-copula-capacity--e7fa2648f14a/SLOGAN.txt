For two arbitrary dependent uniform \([0,1]\) variables, the sharp probability of exact separation \(d\in(0,1)\) equals \(md\) for even \(m=\lfloor1/d\rfloor\) and \(2-(m+1)d\) for odd \(m\). A measure-preserving adjacent-pair involution attains the bound, giving perfect fixed-distance couplings exactly at even reciprocals and strict cusped minima at odd reciprocals.
