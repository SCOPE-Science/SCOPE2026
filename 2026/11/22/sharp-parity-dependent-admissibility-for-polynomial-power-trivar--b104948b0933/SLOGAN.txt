The nonexchangeable trivariate polynomial-power copula of Chesneau (2024) has a sharp admissibility region that is symmetric and independent of the shape exponent for odd powers, but asymmetric with a unique shape-parameter transition for every even power. At \(m=2\) and \(\theta=1\), the full range \(-1/2\leq\lambda\leq1/3\) is strictly larger than the previously established sufficient range \(|\lambda|\leq1/6\).
