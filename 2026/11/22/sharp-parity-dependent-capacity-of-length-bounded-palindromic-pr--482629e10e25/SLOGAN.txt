For \(q\)-ary reversal-palindromic instantaneous codes with a maximum word length \(N\), a canonical largest dictionary consists exactly of the terminal nodes of the palindrome-prefix forest, and its size admits an exact period-avoidance inclusion–exclusion formula. Its asymptotic capacity is \(\frac{2q}{q-1}q^{N/2}\) for even \(N\) and \(\frac{q+1}{q-1}q^{(N+1)/2}\) for odd \(N\), up to exponentially smaller error.
