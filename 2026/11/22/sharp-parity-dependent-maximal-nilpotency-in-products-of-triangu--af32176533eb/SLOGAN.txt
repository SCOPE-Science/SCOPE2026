Two independent random strictly upper triangular matrices have a product with the largest possible nilpotency index \(\lceil n/2\rceil\) according to an exact finite-field law. The sharp probability is \(s^N\) in dimension \(2N+1\), but has a factor linear in \(N\) compared with that odd-dimensional probability in dimension \(2N+2\), where \(s=(1-q^{-1})^2\).
