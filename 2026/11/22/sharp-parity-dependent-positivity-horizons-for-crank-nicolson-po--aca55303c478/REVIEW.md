# Scope of the positivity classification

The sharp horizon for odd powers is the first loss of entrywise nonnegativity. At that boundary only the hub diagonal is zero. Above it the hub diagonal is negative; the leaf diagonals also turn negative after their own common, strictly larger zero. Strict positivity of off-diagonals at all finite positive steps follows from the scalar divided-difference inequality, including its signed odd-power case. The even-power conclusion and the one-step horizon are covered by existing spectral-filter and weighted-star results, respectively; the repeated odd-power horizon is the distinguishing calculation here.

The counterexample on a four-vertex path prevents extending unconditional even-power positivity to arbitrary trees. The thresholds are for the completed fixed-step map, not for internal positivity, accuracy, or stiff decay.
