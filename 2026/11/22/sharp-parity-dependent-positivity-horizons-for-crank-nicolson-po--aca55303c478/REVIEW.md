# Review

**Correctness — PASS.** Mutually orthogonal star Laplacian projectors give four complete entry formulas, and the key monotonicity lemma for the rational scalar secant slope is proved in the accompanying result for both parities. The hub diagonal is the only possible nonpositive entry; solving its unique odd-power root proves the exact horizon. An independent standard-library rational-matrix implementation verifies 33,958 assertions and the explicit nonstar counterexample. Finite tests are corroboration, not a proof of the all-size statement.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** Existing exact one-step Markov and weighted-star Crank–Nicolson thresholds are used and credited. The existing universal spectral-filter theorem for complete multipartite graphs already covers the even-power positivity component; this result does not claim that component as standalone new work. Its new content is the full parity-resolved all-odd-power threshold, strict entry-type classification and sharp exceptional location. The directed-cycle power classification is not implication-equivalent. An unknown equivalent under matrix-function terminology remains possible.

**Value — PASS.** A fixed-step numerical diffusion calculation can have a nonpositive intermediate step and a positive two-step completion. The theorem characterizes this phenomenon exactly for every star size and each power and proves when a completed odd number of steps first fails, yielding a usable natural exact output-positivity test. The four-vertex-path counterexample bounds the structural significance. It does not imply stagewise positivity, accuracy or stiff damping.

**Limitations.** The theorem assumes unweighted undirected stars and exact arithmetic. Part of the even-power behavior is covered by a broader existing theorem; the specific odd-power sharp horizon is the substantive proposed addition. Publication-priority risk remains.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
