For unweighted star diffusion, every completed even number of equal Crank–Nicolson steps is strictly positive for any positive timestep, while an odd number of steps is nonnegative exactly through \(H_{m,k}=2(1+m^{-1/k})/((m+1)(1-m^{-1/k}))\). The sharp odd-step boundary is controlled solely by the hub self-transition; even-power positivity follows in part from known complete-multipartite spectral-filter principles, whereas the exact all-odd-power classification is the new assertion.
