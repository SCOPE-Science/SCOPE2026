For the complex-geometric cyclic Gabor seed \(\gamma_N^N=2i\), the smallest normalized ambiguity coefficient has two different sharp dimension-scaled parity lower envelopes: \(\liminf_{N\to\infty,\,N\mathrm{\ odd}}N^2\alpha_N=\frac{10}{9}\sqrt{5/2}(\log2)^2\) and \(\liminf_{N\to\infty,\,N\mathrm{\ even}}N^2\alpha_N=\frac{20}{9}(\log2)^2\). An exact phase-resonance classification and irrational rotations prove both constants and show why the scaled margin does not converge.
