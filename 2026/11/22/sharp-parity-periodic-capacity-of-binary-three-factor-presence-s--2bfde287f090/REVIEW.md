# Scientific assessment: parity-periodic three-factor presence spectra

## Correctness — PASS
The claim concerns exactly linear binary words, length at least three, and unordered sets (not multisets) of contiguous length-three factors. The proof maps every word bijectively to one transition history in a \(4\times256\) finite suffix/presence state system. The exact certificate verifies equality of the **complete** reachable state sets \(E_{11}=E_{17}\), not merely their sizes; deterministic iteration therefore proves periodicity for all subsequent lengths. It checks the six representative support cardinalities and exceptional masks. An independent literal enumeration of every binary word at lengths three through fifteen agrees. The forced-entry/exit two-cycle argument proves the parity obstruction for the two singled-out profiles. A finite computation is essential to the small-universe classification; direct word enumeration alone would not establish the infinite result.

## Originality — PASS, with priority risk
Bernardini et al. (2022) establish the exact set-valued factor observation model and shortest-walk algorithms, but do not state the all-length \(101/103\) profile count. A highly relevant published 2026 result, *The sharp full-support threshold for binary three-factor spectra*, gives the same suffix/mask recurrence and tables of **preimage populations through length fifty**; these already imply finite values of the current profile count and are explicitly credited. Its main theorem instead compares **fiber populations** and gives an infinite-length full-support modal threshold, not the eventual profile-support cardinalities, parity exceptions or equality-of-reachable-state-set certificate. Multiplicity-retaining and cyclic three-gram capacity formulas concern different observations. The exact same finite-state method makes unnoticed equivalent corollaries a material residual risk, but no inspected theorem states or immediately entails the complete all-length parity classification without the additional finite closure computation and induction.

## Value — PASS
Fixed-length three-factor membership tests form a canonical, heavily coarsened sequencing/hybridization channel. Its optimal zero-error codebook size is the number of possible profiles, not the size of the largest inverse fiber. The sharp all-length classification and the pair of forced even-length profiles expose a persistent structural exception rather than a single finite sample statistic. The result supplies a complete exact capacity invariant for every source length and identifies precisely where the output alphabet changes with parity. It is narrow but motivated by the same observation operator in reconstruction literature.

## Limits and closest comparisons
The finite-state certificate, including the list of all attainable masks, is reproducible but not independently validated. The 2026 modal-support paper's finite table substantially overlaps the first fifty numerical cases. The earlier all-length modal-fiber theorem does not by itself establish the different all-length codebook count. A differently phrased or unindexed exact formula remains possible, so priority is provisional. This theorem does not count individual source words within a profile, assert anything for other factor lengths, or establish error correction.
