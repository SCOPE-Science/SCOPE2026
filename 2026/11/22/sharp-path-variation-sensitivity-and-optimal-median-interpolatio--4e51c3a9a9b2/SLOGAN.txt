Median-centred clipping never raises the path variation of a single signal, yet its sharp two-input variation gain is \(n-1\) for odd samples and \(\max\{n-2,1+(n-2)\max(\theta,1-\theta)\}\) for even samples, where \(\theta\) interpolates the central order statistics. Every bound is attained, and a whole explicit interval of even-sample median conventions is minimax once \(n\ge6\).
