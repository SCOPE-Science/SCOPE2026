For each finite real \(1<p<\infty\), sliding triple pairwise \(\ell_p\) dispersion has the sharp two-input path-variation gain \(2^{1/p}\) times \(0,3/2,5/2,3\) at lengths \(3,4,5,\ge6\). At both endpoints \(p=1\) and \(p=\infty\), the exact gain is strictly below the corresponding one-sided limit: a nonuniform stability transition invisible from pointwise convergence of the filters.
