For every integer \(n\ge4\), the geometric signed-product constant of the real cube of dimension \(2^{n-1}-1\) has the exact sharp value \(m^q(m+z)^{2-q}/(m+z^2)\), where \(m=n-1\), \(q=2^{3-n}\), and \(z=(2-q)m/(m+\sqrt{m^2+q(2-q)m})\). This is the unique last dimension below saturation at \(n\); the exact missing-coordinate deficit decays as \(2^{3-n}\), and explicit vectors attain every formula.
