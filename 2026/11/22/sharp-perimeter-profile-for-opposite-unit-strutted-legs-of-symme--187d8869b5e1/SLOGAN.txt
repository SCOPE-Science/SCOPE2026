For two opposite unit-strutted legs of an isosceles trapezoid, total leg length at least \(1\) forces the **sharp** perimeter lower bound \(11/4\), attained uniquely at the symmetric trapezoid with bases \(1,3/4\) and legs \(1/2\). The exact fixed-leg profile \(2+2a-a^2\) for \(0<a<1\) shows why the adjacent-strut perimeter-three theorem cannot be extended to opposite legs without additional assumptions.
