For \(F_b(x,y)=(x+3y/2,bx(1-x))\) on the real plane, primitive three-cycles appear at the exact threshold \(b=4\sqrt6/3\): none below, one neutral at equality, and two above. Exactly one is attracting on an explicit, exceptionally narrow algebraic parameter interval, with both three-step Floquet invariants in closed form.
