# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The signed derivative-measure coupling reduces the full real periodic bounded-variation class to indicators of circular arcs. With at least six cells, complementary errors do not overlap after periodization. The same-cell Gram integral yields the unique phase pair, while three exact positive Bernstein-coefficient constructions certify strict exclusion of every separated-cell regime. The attached standard-library checker reconstructs all rational positivity certificates, the endpoint identity, and finite pulse comparisons. The analytic proof—not its finite tests—establishes the global result.

## Originality
**PASS, with stated historical uncertainty.** The 2012 freely accessible Nochetto–Salgado manuscript develops the averaged-nodal interpolant and gives nonspecific approximation constants, not this exact sharp periodic squared residual and two-jump rigidity. The 2014 Nagler–Cerejeiras–Forster manuscript uses a different point-sampling Schoenberg operator. Previously recorded exact real-line \(L^1\) and \(L^\infty\) bounds for box-averaged hats do not algebraically imply the present circular \(L^2\) extremal distance: the needed cross term between two jumps is absent. Closely related orthogonal-spline projectors also have different defining operators and norms. No claim of exhaustive historical priority is made; the final 2015 journal text was inaccessible in full.

## Value
**PASS.** The exact sharp error answers a natural design question for a variation-diminishing positive finite-element quasi-interpolant. It identifies a subcell two-jump resonance that cannot be found by optimizing isolated jumps, a rigid maximizing geometry for all sufficiently large periodic grids, and a concrete quantitative benchmark beyond big-O and generic geometric constants.
