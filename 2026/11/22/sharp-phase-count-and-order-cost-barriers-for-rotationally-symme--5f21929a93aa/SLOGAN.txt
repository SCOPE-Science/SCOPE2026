For rotationally symmetric Gaussian Stein differentiation, the familiar sufficient phase count \(n\ge p\) is also necessary for variance stability uniformly over bounded, locally analytic inputs: every \(n<p\) admits an explicit exact-rate divergence witness. Even at adequate phase count, a normalized derivative witness has sharp limiting probe variance \(\sim\frac{1+\sqrt2}{\pi2^{5/4}}(6+4\sqrt2)^p/p\), exposing an exponential derivative-order sample requirement for this estimator.
