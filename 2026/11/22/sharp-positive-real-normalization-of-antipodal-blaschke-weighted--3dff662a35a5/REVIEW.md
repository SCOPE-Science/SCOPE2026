In this subspace the kernel is the ordinary one minus the projection of its evaluations onto the two removed evaluation vectors. Those vectors are independent and the 2-by-2 Gram subtraction gives exactly normalized formula (1) after dividing by B_a(z)B_a(η); analyticity removes the apparent zeros in this division. Clearing (1+t²)D, D=(1−tz²)(1−tη²), gives exactly (2). On the torus let p=zη=e^(iθ), u=cos θ and c=cos(arg z−arg η). Divide both the numerator and denominator of T_t=(S_t−1)(1+t²)/(2(1−t²)) by p. Their real parts are (1−t²)(u−1) and (1+t²)u−2tc, and their imaginary parts −(1+t²)sin θ and −(1−t²)sin θ. The real part after multiplying by the conjugate factors as (1−t²)(1−u)(1+t²+2tc), divided by |D|².
