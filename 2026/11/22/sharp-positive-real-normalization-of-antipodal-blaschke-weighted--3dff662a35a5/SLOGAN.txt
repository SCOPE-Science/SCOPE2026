For two antipodal simple Blaschke zeros, the weighted disk Bergman kernel obeys the sharp simultaneous half-plane inequality \(\operatorname{Re}[(1-z\overline w)^2K_a(z,w)]>1\). Its natural bivariate numerator has exact closed-bidisk minimum modulus \((1-a^2)^2(1+a^4)\), attained only at two antipodal boundary pairs.
