# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The projector identity \(E_a=(A_G-sI-(k-s)J/v)/(r-s)\) yields exact edge and nonedge heat entries. The complement adjacency spectral bound proves nonnegative edge coefficient \(U\). A single-exponential derivative-ratio crossing proves the unique recovery and norm maximum; an implicit-function expansion and rescaled root trapping prove both asymptotic regimes. Five independently constructed strongly regular graphs passed 200 heat-kernel/norm comparisons. Numeric samples are not proof of the universal statement.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)
The 2019 Benzi–Bertaccini–Durastante–Simunec full-text study treats fractional diffusion in the Markov range \(0<\alpha\le1\), not the above-order recovery of all strongly regular graphs. A published complete-multipartite fractional-heat theorem covers a proper subfamily and its special cases are not claimed here. Published full-text strongly regular Gauss–Legendre Padé results use a different scalar filter and have different positivity regimes; no implication of the present exact semigroup classification follows. Searches for aliases, exact graph-parameter formulas, three-level semigroups and positivity horizons did not locate a stronger all-strongly-regular fractional-heat theorem. Unindexed or older association-scheme results may contain overlapping identities.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The theorem provides an exact complete sign classification, sharp induced \(\ell^1\) and \(\ell^\infty\) norms, a unique restoration time and quantitative critical exponents on an entire natural spectral-combinatorial family. It includes primitive graphs beyond previously characterized complete multipartite examples. The linear versus quadratic onset and large-order limiting overshoot quantify a genuine positivity/stability transition rather than reparameterizing a single finite example.

## Qualifications
Only simple connected noncomplete strongly regular graphs, their unweighted combinatorial Laplacians, and exact spectral powers are addressed. Finite numerical checks do not establish publication priority, and related specialized graph-heat literature can remain unindexed.
