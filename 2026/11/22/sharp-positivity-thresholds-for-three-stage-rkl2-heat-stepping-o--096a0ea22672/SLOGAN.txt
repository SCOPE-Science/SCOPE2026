For three-stage RKL2 graph-heat stepping on the complete bipartite graph \(K_{p,q}\), the exact entrywise-positivity cutoff is \(15/[2(p+2q)]\) when \(2\le p\le q\), but an explicitly characterized hub-diagonal root governs stars. In every case scalar energy stability persists to \(\alpha/(p+q)\), where \(1-\alpha+\alpha^2/2-\alpha^3/15=-1\); except for the single-edge graph, stable steps can therefore produce negative heat states.
