For a canonical two-channel Haar projection, the commuting Toeplitz factors \(P+SQ\) and \(Q+SP\) multiply to an isometric shift although each has sharp \(\ell^p\) power norm \(2^{|1-2/p|}\). All norming sequences for \(1<p<\infty\) are classified by disjoint lag-separated supports, with a precise change from single-coordinate inputs below \(p=2\) to complementary two-channel inputs above \(p=2\).
