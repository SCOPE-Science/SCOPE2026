In the spatially uniform refuge-redistribution predator–prey model, an explicit sharp wedge \(1<\rho<(1+\sqrt2)/2\), \(0<\eta<1-2\sqrt{\rho(\rho-1)}\) contains a stable predator-free state, a positive saddle and a stable predator–prey equilibrium. Thus predators may fail to invade from rarity while a sufficiently established predator population can persist, even though no cooperative-hunting term is present.
