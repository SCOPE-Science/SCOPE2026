For independent rare outbreaks on three upstream buildings, adding more local wastewater sensors can increase the value of a pooled-junction sensor's information: expected Bayes localization accuracy plus any nonnegative outbreak-coverage weight is non-submodular exactly below prevalence \(p=1/5\), regardless of how highly coverage is valued. Above that sharp boundary an explicit four-phase weight threshold is necessary and sufficient for submodularity; two buildings never show the unrepairable failure.
