For Farhi's quotient of the product of \(k+1\) consecutive integers by their least common multiple, the proportion of starting points attaining the maximal value \(k!\) decays as \(\exp[-(\mathcal C+o(1))k/\log k]\), with the explicit sharp constant \(\mathcal C=\sum_{j\ge1}\log(1+1/j)/j\). This is a growing-window limit beyond the known exact fixed-window frequency distribution.
