# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The lower bound concerns cardinality p^n, including mixed additive exponents and characteristic p^a, not just F_p-algebras. Central essentiality forces every idempotent e to be central: a nonzero element of eR(1-e) or (1-e)Re stays in that off-diagonal corner on multiplication by the center and cannot have a nonzero central multiple. In a finite ring, the nilpotent radical permits lifting orthogonal idempotents; thus the semisimple quotient has no nontrivial matrix factors and the ring splits into local factors inheriting the property. For a local factor with residue field F_(p^f), all nonzero radical layers have sizes powered by p^f; hence f divides n. For n<=5 with f>1 and J nonzero the only possibility is n=4,f=2: J is square zero and one-dimensional over the residue field on the left. A nonzero central z in J exists by essentiality, and z,tz for a residue generator lift t span J additively and commute with t and J, forcing commutativity. For f=1, a cyclic additive R/C would imply commutativity, so |C intersect J|<=p^2, with a nonzero such intersection when J is nonzero. If its order is p, a noncentral j in J needs nonzero multiplication on its generator to give a central value, which would iterate nontrivially against nilpotence of j. At order p^2 one must have n=5 and J/(C intersect J) elementary of order p^2, since a cyclic quotient again forces commutativity. If jz for central z in J were noncentral, then j and jz would give independent classes: a nonzero dependence scalar would make j times the central unit z-lambda central and hence j central. These commuting elements would span the quotient, impossible. Therefore J acts by commuting nilpotent additive operators on the central radical. In the cyclic group of order p^2 their number is at most p; in rank two over F_p the centralizer of a nonzero nilpotent N consists of aI+bN and nilpotence forces a=0. The kernel has order at least p^3, contradicting essentiality because the kernel must be central. In the construction associativity follows from J^3=0 and the unit rules; ab-ba=s_1, c commutes with everything, and j times c detects each noncentral pair of a,b coefficients. Each unit has nonzero central product with s_1, and central elements can use multiplier 1. Thus the example realizes p^6 in each prime, with the p=2 case properly credited.

## originality

PASS

Pass for the sharp minimum in each odd-prime cardinality class, in particular the uniform exclusion of p^4 and p^5 without assuming characteristic p. The binary minimum and the construction's binary structure constants are prior and not new. The strongest inspected prior theorem is an absolute minimum with an order-64 classification, not the claimed prime-wise bound.

## value

PASS

A sharp cutoff in every odd-prime family answers a natural size question left open by the absolute minimum, which is attained only at the binary prime. Excluding small mixed-characteristic rings is essential to the scope; the small central radical and commuting nilpotent action give a concrete structural obstruction. The attainment construction alone is elementary and closely follows the binary example, but the matching prime-wise lower bound is a substantive addition.
