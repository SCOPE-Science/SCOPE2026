## Verification scope
The proof is symbolic for all primes, with no computation claimed as an infinite certificate. The potentially delicate boundaries have been checked separately: residue degree two forces a square-zero one-dimensional radical over the degree-two residue field; an assumed nonzero central radical element generates the radical and makes the ring commutative. A central radical of order \(p^2\) is either cyclic or elementary abelian, and the nilpotent commuting endomorphisms have at most \(p\) possibilities in either case. In the construction, \(J^3=0\) makes the six-dimensional multiplication associative; \([a,b]=s_1\) makes it noncommutative; multiplication by \(c\) detects every noncentral radical direction.

The previous characteristic-two minimum and the classification of order-64 rings are cited antecedents, not independently audited here. Historical priority for the odd-prime bound is subject to unindexed results.
