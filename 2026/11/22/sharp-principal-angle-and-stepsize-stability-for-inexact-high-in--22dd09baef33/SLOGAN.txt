For inexact preconditioned high-index saddle reflections on any nonsingular indefinite quadratic, arbitrary changes in the approximated unstable eigenspace still admit a universal strict absolute-Hessian-energy contraction exactly when its largest principal angle is below \(45^\circ\) and the step satisfies \(0<\eta<2\cos(2\vartheta)/L\). The entire sharp boundary follows from an orthogonal two-reflection identity and is attained by a two-dimensional index-one saddle.
