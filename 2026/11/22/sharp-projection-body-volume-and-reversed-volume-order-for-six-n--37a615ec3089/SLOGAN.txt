Among all six-node equally weighted spherical \(2\)-design Minkowski polytopes of total surface area \(4\pi\), the projection-body volume ranges sharply from \(64\pi^3/27\) at the cube to \(32\pi^3/9\) at the triangular bipyramid, decreasing strictly across the complete twist family. The affine-invariant quotient \(\operatorname{Vol}(\Pi P)/\operatorname{Vol}(P)^2\) decreases sharply from \(27/2\) to \(8\), reversing the ordinary polytope-volume order.
