For every alphabet of \(q\ge3\) letters, exactly \(M_q=q^5-2q^4+2q^3-3q^2+3q+4\) distinct oriented trigram-count profiles can result from single-symbol deletions of one sufficiently long word, and no word of any length produces more. Explicit de Bruijn-based witnesses attain the bound for all lengths at least \(q^5+12\), proving that the shortest saturating length is asymptotic to \(q^5\).
