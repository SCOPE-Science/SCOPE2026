Geometric compounding of a negatively dependent FGM copula creates an exact four-region phase diagram: CDF-total-positivity, PQD without CDF-total-positivity, mixed-sign quadrant dependence, and NQD, with sharp algebraic boundaries. The CDF-TP\(2\) threshold is \((1-p)(1+\theta)^3\ge-\theta\) for negative \(\theta\), strictly stronger than the PQD threshold \(\theta\ge-(1-p)\).
