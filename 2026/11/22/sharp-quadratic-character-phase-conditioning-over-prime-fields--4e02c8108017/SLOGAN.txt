For every odd prime \(p\ge5\), the unimodular quadratic-character phase matrix \(\bigl(e^{2\pi i\eta(st+c)/p}\bigr)_{s,t\in\mathbb F_p}\), with \(c\ne0\), has exactly one weakest right singular direction, the quadratic character, and its sharp least singular value is \(2\sin(\pi/p)\). The greatest singular value is explicit through a two-dimensional constant block, forcing the exact reconstruction condition number to grow as \(p^2/(2\pi)+O(1)\).
