The adjacency shatter function of finite circle graphs has the sharp quadratic leading constant \(2\): its maximum number of open-neighborhood traces on \(m\) marked chords lies between \(2m^2-4m+1\) and \(2m^2-m+1\) for every \(m\ge5\). An explicit all-size chord word realizes the lower expression exactly, with a complete classification of its crossing-trace run types.
