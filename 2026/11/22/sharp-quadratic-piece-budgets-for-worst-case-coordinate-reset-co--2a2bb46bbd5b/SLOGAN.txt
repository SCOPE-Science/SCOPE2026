For arbitrary switching among \(n\) coordinate-reset modes, the exact worst-case value has a sharp \(m\)-quadratic upper-envelope penalty \(\Gamma_{n,m}(\rho)=\lceil n/m\rceil/[\lceil n/m\rceil-(\lceil n/m\rceil-1)\rho^2]\). Balanced coordinate blocks attain it through an explicit co-complete graph, and no choice of off-diagonal quadratic forms can do better.
