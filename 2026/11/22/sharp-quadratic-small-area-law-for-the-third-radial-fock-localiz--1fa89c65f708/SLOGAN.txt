The optimal third Fock localization eigenvalue among small-area radial masks has an exact negative quadratic correction to the previously known linear law: \(M_3(s)=cs-cp^2s^2/2+o(s^2)\), with explicit \(c=0.2446018\ldots\) and \(p=0.0519745\ldots\). Near-extremizers at this precision must fill an origin-centered disk containing asymptotically the fraction \(p\) of the observation area, while their remaining area concentrates around an excited-state annulus.
