For the three-site nilpotent Jordan shift under an arbitrary real diagonal perturbation, the angular width variation and best-disk Hausdorff defect have exact sharp quadratic laws with two distinct Frobenius eigendirections. The least-detectable nontrivial perturbation has the profile \(\operatorname{diag}(1,-2,1)\), where both defects admit closed formulas for every coupling strength.
