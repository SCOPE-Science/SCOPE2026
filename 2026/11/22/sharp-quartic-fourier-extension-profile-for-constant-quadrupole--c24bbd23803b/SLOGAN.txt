The ordinary fourth-power spherical Fourier-extension norm has an exact sharp profile at every fixed energy fraction \(\beta\) in the constant-plus-degree-two harmonic band of \(\mathbb S^2\): \(\pi^2[(1-\beta)^2+\frac65\beta(1-\beta)+\frac{8\sqrt5}{49}\sqrt{1-\beta}\beta^{3/2}+\frac{33}{49}\beta^2]\) for normalized inputs. All mixed-energy complex extremizers are real-phase uniaxial quadrupoles plus a positive constant, while at pure degree two every real quadrupole up to complex scalar is extremizing.
