A quartic-norm exponential Fourier spectrum on the two-torus becomes subconvolutive exactly when its polynomial correction exceeds \(7/4\), rather than the Euclidean-radial exponent \(3/2\). At the sharp boundary, its axis convolution quotient diverges like \(\sqrt2\,\Gamma(1/4)a^{-1/4}\log N\), giving a precise signature of quartically flat spectral geometry.
