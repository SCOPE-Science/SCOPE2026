The fourth-power uniform Steinhaus modulus of every real Hilbert space of dimension at least two has the exact all-radius expression \(U_H^{(4)}(d;a)=4ad\sqrt{1-c^2}(1+a^2+2a\sqrt{1-d^2/4}\,c)\), where \(c=4a\sqrt{1-d^2/4}/(1+a^2+\sqrt{(1+a^2)^2+32a^2(1-d^2/4)})\). Both minimizing unit-vector separations and the two unique maximizing perturbations are classified; their optimal angular tilt peaks precisely at radius \(a=1\), and the modulus obeys exact reciprocal-radius quartic symmetry.
