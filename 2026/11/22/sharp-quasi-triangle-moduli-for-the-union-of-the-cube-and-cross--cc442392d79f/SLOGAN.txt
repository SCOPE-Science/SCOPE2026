For the quasi-norm \(q_{d,t}(x)=\min\{\|x\|_1,t\|x\|_\infty\}\), the sharp two-vector quasi-triangle constant is \(t(d-1)/(d-1+(t-1)^2)\) when \(1<t<d\), and one otherwise; its unique worst parameter is \(t=\sqrt d\), with value \((\sqrt d+1)/2\). The constant equals the exact convexification distortion of the union of the cube and cross-polytope balls, and on \(\ell_1\) the corresponding sharp constant is \(\max\{1,t\}\), unattained for \(t>1\).
