For the real planar gauge given by the minimum of two Euclidean ellipse norms with interchanged semiaxes \(a\ge b>0\), the exact quasi-triangle and convexification constant is \((a^2+b^2)/(2ab)\). When the ellipses differ, every sharp pair consists of equally scaled, coordinate-reflected common-tangent contact points, with a strict quadratic onset of nonconvexity as \(a/b\downarrow1\).
