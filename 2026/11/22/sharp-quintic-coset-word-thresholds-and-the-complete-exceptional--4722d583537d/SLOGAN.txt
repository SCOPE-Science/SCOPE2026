Across prime fields, the 125 possible ordered three-term fifth-power-coset patterns are simultaneously realized outside exactly 48 exceptional primes, with the sharp all-word threshold \(p=3361\). Excluding the previously studied all-residue word \((0,0,0)\), all other 124 patterns become universal already at the sharp threshold \(p=2341\), after 43 mixed-pattern exceptional primes.
