For every weighted polyanalytic Bergman disk space, the smallest possible mean squared radius is the first zero of a shifted Jacobi polynomial, attained only by one radial polynomial. The exact sharp stability gap is the difference between the first degree-\(q\) Jacobi zeros with endpoint exponents \(1\) and \(0\), and after multiplication by \(q^2\) it converges to a universal difference of squared Bessel zeros.
