For all \(q\ge3\) and \(n\ge5\), the smallest radius-two Hamming ball inside the space of adjacent-unequal \(q\)-ary words is attained only by period-three centers, and the largest only by period-two centers, with explicit quadratic formulas and exact equality-case counts. The ternary alphabet is exceptional: the smallest ball grows linearly while the largest grows quadratically; a local exact formula gives the ball size of every center.
