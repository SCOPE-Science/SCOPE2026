For Frobenius projection onto real positive-semidefinite matrices, the exact maximum-entry sensitivity of a diagonal output near a rank-one positive/flat-negative spectral boundary is \(\mathcal B_n=\max_{0\le p\le1}[np(1-p)+p+2\sqrt{n-1}\sqrt p(1-p)^{3/2}]\). This natural boundary class has \(\mathcal B_n=n/4+\sqrt n/2+3/4+O(n^{-1/2})\), proving that the unrestricted entrywise Lipschitz modulus grows linearly with dimension, without asserting its exact coefficient.
