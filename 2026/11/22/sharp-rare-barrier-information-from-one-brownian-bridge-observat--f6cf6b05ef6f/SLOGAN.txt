For a high upper barrier of a standard Brownian bridge, the mean-square information in one fixed interior observation has a sharp three-regime asymptotic law, with a transition at the quarter-time and the unique best exponential rate at the midpoint. At the midpoint the fraction of barrier-event variance explained is asymptotic to \(5/(3h\sqrt{2\pi})\), while every other fixed observation time explains an exponentially smaller fraction.
