In high-dimensional random cube orders, the chance that a point dominates at least \(r\ge2\) others has an explicit sharp Gamma-function equivalent whenever \(d_n/\log n\to c\in(e,r+1)\). In the nonempty interval \(e<c<(r+1)/\log(r+1)\), the expected number of such directed stars diverges even as the probability that any exists goes to zero.
