For a reversible hub–leaf Markov network with \(m\ge2\) identical outward rates \(a\), varying the common inward return rate gives a unique maximal nonnegative Crank–Nicolson timestep at \(b/a=m^2/(2m-1)\). The limiting diagonal switches from the hub to the leaves at this exact balance, and the optimal step is \(2-1/m\) times the symmetric-rate step.
