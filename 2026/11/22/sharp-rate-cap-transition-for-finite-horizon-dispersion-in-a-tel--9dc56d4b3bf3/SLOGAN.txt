For a finite-horizon free run-and-tumble particle with a fixed expected number of flips and a cap on the flip rate, the **unique variance-minimizing protocol** is one centered constant-rate pulse with zero flipping outside it. At the sharp cap \(R_c=(1+B)/T\), the optimal pulse stops saturating the cap, and below this threshold the excess terminal variance has an exact positive quadratic dependence on the inverse-cap deficit.
