# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** For every rational nonnegative barycentric system, facet non-incidence forces numerator vanishing and hence denominator vanishing on each residual cylinder. The polygon residual ideals have proved sharp minimal degrees for all stated factor classes, using distinct residual intersections on side lines. A disjoint-variable top-degree-monomial argument proves additivity of these compulsory vanishing degrees; product Wachspress coordinates attain the bound. The proof includes the quadrilateral with exactly one parallel opposite pair, as well as the centrally symmetric and no-parallel cases. Reproducible exact-arithmetic checks pass on five representative polygon shapes and the tensor product of two non-parallelogram quadrilaterals.

## Originality
**PASS, provisional historical priority.** The two closest preprints are Baumbach–Winter (arXiv:2611.02110v1), which introduces minimum rational GBC denominator degree and constructs tensor products, and Baumbach–Freyer–Weigert–Winter (arXiv:2508.04275v1), whose product law applies to the Wachspress *adjoint* degree. A separately published polygon result proves minimality in dimension two, not for the product. None of the materially inspected statements implies a minimum-denominator product law without a new lower-bound argument. Kohn–Ranestad and Brüser–Weigert impose projective adjoint conditions absent from arbitrary rational generalized barycentric systems. Searches did not locate an equivalent theorem; alternative historical terminology remains possible.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** This gives a sharp high-dimensional structural family for an active minimum-denominator conjecture. The natural products of \(k\) arbitrary non-parallelogram quadrilaterals have exact denominator complexity \(k\), while the conjectured universal expression \(m-2d\) equals zero; hence the result detects complexity that neither facet count nor the tensor-product upper bound detects. It also distinguishes factor geometry in a broad class of products.

## Scope and remaining risk
Not a result for arbitrary products of polygons; exotic multiple-parallel-pair noncentral factors are excluded except in the quadrilateral case. The algebraic proof, not finite symbolic tests, supplies infinite-family correctness. There is no independent audit and historical precedence cannot be guaranteed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
