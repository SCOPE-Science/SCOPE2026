For any Cartesian product of convex quadrilaterals and polygons that are centrally symmetric or have no parallel side lines, the least possible polynomial denominator degree of nonnegative rational generalized barycentric coordinates is exactly the sum of the factors' planar Wachspress adjoint degrees. In particular, every product of \(k\) non-parallelogram quadrilaterals has minimum denominator degree \(k\), even though its dimension and facet count make the general \(m-2d\) bound zero.
