For matched-length rank-one operator decompositions in dimension \(d\), the exact smallest constant comparing either unrescaled Bessel bound with the worst signed or phased multiplier norm is \(\sqrt\pi\Gamma((d+1)/2)/\Gamma(d/2)\) over the reals and \(\Gamma(d+1/2)/(\Gamma(3/2)\Gamma(d))\) over the complexes. Neither sharp constant is attained by a nonzero finite system when \(d\ge2\), but both are asymptotically attained by genuine spanning frame pairs.
