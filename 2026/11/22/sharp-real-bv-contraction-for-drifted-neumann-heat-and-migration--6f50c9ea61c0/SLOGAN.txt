For drifted reflecting heat flow on an interval, the exact real-BV contraction norm is the largest two-sided killed Brownian survival probability, attained precisely by a single jump at a unique, time-dependent location. The optimal jump starts at \(1/2+bt+O_b(t^2)\) while the leading exponentially small contraction deficit is independent of drift, then approaches a shifted principal-eigenfunction peak with an explicit first spectral correction.
