The sigmoid two-cell spiking map is globally reversible at every step \(0<T<1\), has an exact bounded image at \(T=1\), and for \(T>1\) is a full-plane diffeomorphism exactly when \(\alpha\le10(T-1)/(17T)\). On the other side of this sharp boundary, an open set of outputs has at least three regular preimages; for gains below \(10/17\), sufficiently large steps surprisingly restore unique backward reconstruction.
