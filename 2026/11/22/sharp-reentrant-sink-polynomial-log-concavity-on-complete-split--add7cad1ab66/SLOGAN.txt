For complete split graphs \(K_k\vee\overline K_r\) with \(k\ge2\), the full acyclic and unrestricted sink-count polynomials have elementary two-term formulas and exact log-concavity conditions. At \(k=6\), acyclic sink-count log-concavity holds for \(r\le3\), fails for \(4\le r\le7\), and returns for \(r\ge8\), even though real-rootedness fails permanently once \(r\ge3\).
