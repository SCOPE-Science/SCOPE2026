For a four-row reflective Kaczmarz system with the positive four-cycle Gram geometry, exactly the eight cyclic update orders maximize the reflection-product eigengap; all sixteen other orders tie at a strictly inferior value. Near singularity, cyclic ordering improves the inverse-eigengap averaging constant by a sharp factor approaching \(2/\sqrt3\), without promising better error at every finite averaging horizon. Products of at most three such reflections have order-independent spectra, making four the minimal size for an ordering effect.
