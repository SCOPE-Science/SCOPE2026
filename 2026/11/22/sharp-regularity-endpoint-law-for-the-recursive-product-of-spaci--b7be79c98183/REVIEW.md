# Scientific review — regularity endpoint of the recursive product of spacings

## Correctness (C): PASS
The precise domain is every fixed integer \(m\ge2\), with independent sampling under a fully specified continuous null and the original recursively normalized RPS statistic. The original recursion is exactly the normalized binomial-window map because intermediate normalizations cancel. The arithmetic–geometric mean inequality supplies the unique interior minimizer, and the first Moran term excludes other minimizing boundary sequences. Differentiation of every normalized level gives the displayed rational Hessian. Its first-level summand is positive definite, ensuring an isolated quadratic minimum. The constant Dirichlet density and ellipsoid-volume change of variables establish the exact leading coefficient and exponent. The bounded-score relation and comparison to Moran follow algebraically. The standalone checker passed 453 supporting finite assertions, including exact determinants and independent recursive evaluations; no finite computation is used as the proof of the arbitrary-size limit.

## Originality (O): PASS, qualified
The originating article by Eller and Shtembari (arXiv:2111.02252, 3 November 2021), materially read through its definition and null-calibration sections, supplies the statistic and its minimum but only gives the analytic CDF for the one-observation case; larger-sample null calibration is numerical. Later work on multivariate tests by Shtembari and Caldwell treats a different application, according to accessible abstract and references, but its full body was not established from the accessed page. Relevant research-record searches for the exact regularity endpoint, local Hessian, and Dirichlet ellipsoid coefficient found no directly covering result. Prior work on the opposite large-score/clustering tail was compared by implication: it concerns singular boundary faces of the simplex, whereas this result is controlled by the unique interior regular configuration. Those tail statements do not imply the interior Hessian or the coefficient here. The related 2023 doctoral thesis was located but its PDF was inaccessible during this comparison; exact historical priority therefore has residual uncertainty. A generic quadratic small-ball principle is known and not claimed as new.

## Value (V): PASS
The published test relies on calibrated finite-sample null distributions and gives a closed form only for one observation. This result provides an explicit, computable, sharp leading calibration at the opposite (near-equispaced) endpoint for **every** fixed sample size, and shows quantitatively that the complete recursive chain changes the leading regularity probability compared with Moran's statistic. Its \(m=3\) constant is exact and nontrivial. This is a naturally motivated structural invariant of the published statistic, not an arbitrary selection of parameters or a finite table. The limit does not quantify moderate thresholds or the clustering tail and is not a claim of a new testing procedure.

## Limitations and risk
The endpoint is \(T_m^*\uparrow1\) for fixed \(m\), not the opposite tail. No simultaneous large-sample or finite-threshold error theorem is supplied. The full doctoral thesis remains an uninspected plausible historical source, and independent historical-priority verification is advisable. The computations independently corroborate finite formulas but do not constitute independent proof certification.
