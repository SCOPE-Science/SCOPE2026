For the recursive product-of-spacings uniformity statistic with \(m-1\) observations, the probability of lying within \(\varepsilon\) of perfect regularity is asymptotic to an explicit positive determinant constant times \(\varepsilon^{(m-1)/2}\). The constant comes from an exact rational Hessian of the entire recursive smoothing chain and is strictly smaller than Moran's corresponding regularity-endpoint constant for \(m\ge3\).
