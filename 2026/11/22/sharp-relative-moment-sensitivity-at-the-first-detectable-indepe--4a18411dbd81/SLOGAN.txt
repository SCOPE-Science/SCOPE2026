For fair \((n-1)\)-wise independent signs at even order \(n\), the sharp worst relative change in an \(n\)-th absolute Rademacher moment is exactly \(|\theta|n!/M_n\), attained only by equal-magnitude coefficients when \(\theta\ne0\). Its exact first-detectable dependence sensitivity decays at the sharp exponential rate \(\frac{r\sqrt{2\pi n}}{2}(r/\cosh r)^n\), where \(r\tanh r=1\).
