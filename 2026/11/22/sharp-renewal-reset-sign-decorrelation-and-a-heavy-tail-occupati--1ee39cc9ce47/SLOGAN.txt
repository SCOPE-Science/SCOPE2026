For a symmetrically diffusing sluggish walker with independent, stationary renewal resets of a fixed mean interval, deterministic periodic resets minimize binary-sign autocorrelation simultaneously at every lag, strictly for nondegenerate clocks up to the mean interval. An exact incomplete-beta kernel also shows that heavy-tailed reset intervals produce a sharp occupation-fraction variance transition from \(T^{1-\beta}\) to \((\log T)/T\) and then \(T^{-1}\), with explicit coefficients.
