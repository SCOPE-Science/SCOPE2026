For symmetric one-dimensional weights \((|x|+\epsilon)^\alpha\), the first weighted Poincaré spectral gap has a sharp mass-times-resistance normalization, and the crossover at \(\alpha=1+t/\log(1/\epsilon)\) is \(2t/(e^t-1)\). On the logarithmic bottleneck coordinate, both the leading odd eigenfunction and its normalized derivative-energy law have explicit nontrivial limiting profiles.
