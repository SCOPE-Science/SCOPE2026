For a positive gain–loss time step, the very same diagonal decay ODE can have any effective decay rate between zero and its true rate when the nonnegative gain–loss representation is allowed to vary with timestep. The complete limiting family is \(a_i e^{-\kappa_i\theta_i t}\) for \(\theta_i\in[0,1]\), and convergence to the physical flow holds exactly when \(h\alpha_{i,h}\to0\) in every active coordinate.
