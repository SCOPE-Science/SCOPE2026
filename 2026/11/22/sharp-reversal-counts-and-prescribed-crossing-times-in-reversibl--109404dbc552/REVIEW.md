# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The full proof is an exact spectral exponential-sum zero bound followed by an explicit normalized Krawtchouk inverse-design construction. Degenerate eigenspaces are grouped before counting; the identically zero difference and \(n=2\) boundary are treated. The constructing amplitudes are differences of squares with arbitrarily small but strictly positive density perturbations. The four-state witness is exactly positive and its two roots are simple. Exact finite-size algebra and independent semigroup checks passed; finite tests are not used to establish the infinite family.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

The harmonic weighted-Hilbert theory of Pardhi–Abbas–Das, the general reciprocal mode expansion of Ben-Abdallah, the finite-level dynamics of Avitan–Factor–Gelbwaser-Klimovsky, Taye's finite-time entropy envelopes, and the classical Ehrenfest/Krawtchouk spectral theory were compared by mathematical implication. Mode decomposition and exponential zero-count theory are established antecedents. The reviewed claim instead supplies the joint sharp all-state \(\chi^2\) crossing count and construction of arbitrary exact maximal crossing times using fully positive laws on one canonical fixed chain, including a minimal two-spin witness. None of the inspected source sections asserts that precise combination. The main residual risk is an unindexed prior presentation of the same elementary-but-constructive consequence, or a broader theorem implying it.

## Value — PASS

The state-count ceiling, minimal dimension for two Pearson-distance reversals, and programmable exact reversal calendars answer natural sharpness and attainability questions for reversible relaxation diagnostics. The model remains detailed-balanced and each preparation individually contracts, so repeated comparative reversals do not require oscillatory physical trajectories. This provides a useful counterpoint to multiple relative-entropy or quantum crossings and an exact benchmark for preparations of independent spins or urn populations. It is not advertised as a thermal-Gibbs preparation effect or as a new exponential Chebyshev theorem.
