Geometric scale channels formed from the Laplacian of the Euclidean heat kernel have exactly solvable sharp Riesz bounds in every dimension: the strongest Gram frequency is always \(0\), the weakest always \(\pi\), and larger dimensions strictly improve both bounds at any fixed scale spacing. Their precise dense-scale condition number is \(\Gamma(\nu/2)^2 h^{\nu-1}e^{\pi^2/h}/(4\pi^\nu)\), with \(\nu=(d+4)/2\), exposing an exponentially severe coefficient-recovery penalty for oversampled Mexican-hat scales.
