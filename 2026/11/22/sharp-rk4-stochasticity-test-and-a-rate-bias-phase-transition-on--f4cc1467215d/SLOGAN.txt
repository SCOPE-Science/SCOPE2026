For a three-state directed Markov cycle with arbitrary positive rates, the classical RK4 endpoint is stochastic exactly when two scalar tests pass: the direct entry between the two fastest states and the diagonal entry at a fastest state. The test coefficients depend on all three rates. When two rates are equal and largest, a unique third-rate ratio \(r_*=0.542437577761975\ldots\) switches the first failure from a direct transition to a diagonal entry.
