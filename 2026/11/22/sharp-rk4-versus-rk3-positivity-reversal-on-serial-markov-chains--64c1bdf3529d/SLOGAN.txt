On all heterogeneous serial Markov chains with at least five states, the sharp positivity-step ratio of classical RK4 to RK3 lies between \(4/(3+\rho)\) and \(\delta/\rho\), where \(\rho\) and \(\delta\) are explicit one-variable cubic roots; the lower limit is attained, the upper is a sharp supremum. A five-state chain is the smallest possible one where the higher-order method has a strictly smaller admissible positivity step.
