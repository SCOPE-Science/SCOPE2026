For binary words with two marked ones, the full maximal-run exponent sum has an exact gap formula and a sharp length-\(n\) maximum of \(n+1-1/\lfloor(n+1)/3\rfloor\) when \(n\ge8\). After subtracting length for a uniform such word, the exponent sum has a nondegenerate limit with atoms of masses \(1/4\) and \(1/3\), and density \(6/(2+y)^3\) on \(0<y<1\).
