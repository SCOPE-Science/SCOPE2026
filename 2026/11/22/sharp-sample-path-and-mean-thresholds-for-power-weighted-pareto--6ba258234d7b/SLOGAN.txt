For the fully specified Pareto min-characteristic discrepancy with power weight \(t^{-1-\beta}\), an observation can yield a finite statistic almost surely even while its null expectation is infinite. The exact mean is \(2\alpha/[(\alpha-\beta)(2\alpha-\beta)(2-\beta)(\alpha+1-\beta)]\) whenever \(\beta<\min\{\alpha,2\}\), and the almost-sure existence threshold is instead \(\beta<2\min\{\alpha,1\}\).
