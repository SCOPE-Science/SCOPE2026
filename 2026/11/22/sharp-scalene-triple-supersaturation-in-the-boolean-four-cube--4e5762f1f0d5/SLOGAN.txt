For every \(k\)-point subset of the sixteen-vertex Boolean four-cube, the sharp minimum number of scalene triangles is zero through \(k=8\) and then \(16r+4\binom r2+4\max\{0,r-4\}\), where \(r=k-8\). Every minimizing subset above eight vertices contains an entire parity half-cube, and its remaining vertices minimize antipodal pair collisions; the complete equality classification is certified by exact finite enumeration.
