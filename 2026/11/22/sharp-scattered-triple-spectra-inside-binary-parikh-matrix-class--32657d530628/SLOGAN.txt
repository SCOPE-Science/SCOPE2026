Inside every binary Parikh-matrix class with \(a\) zeros, \(b\) ones and \(t\) scattered `01` pairs, the number of scattered `010` triples has explicit sharp lower and upper bounds, each attained by exactly one word. A single quadratic-weight product enumerates its entire conditional distribution; the same two-binomial data can conceal an arbitrarily large gap in three-subsequence counts.
