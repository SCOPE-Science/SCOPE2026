On the beta-weighted Bergman harmonic-Toeplitz map, the diagonal Schatten \(p\)-class has boundary eigenvalues exactly on the arc \(p(\beta+1)(1-\operatorname{Re}\mu)>1\), with \(\mu=(\lambda-c)/a\). The arc appears precisely when \(2p(\beta+1)>1\); at its excluded endpoints, formal eigenvectors have sharp weak-\(\ell^p\) decay.
