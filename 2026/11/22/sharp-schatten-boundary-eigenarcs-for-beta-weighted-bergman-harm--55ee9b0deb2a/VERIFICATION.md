# Verification

Analytic verification uses exact beta moments and a gamma-product recurrence, with explicit treatment of nonterminating and terminating eigenvectors and the sharp weak-ell-p endpoint. The optional standard-library exact-rational checker verifies finite algebraic instances, which do not establish the infinite-dimensional gamma asymptotic or Fredholm classification. The public source comparison distinguishes the previously known bounded-diagonal disk and ordinary Hilbert–Schmidt arc from the current two-parameter boundary phase diagram.
