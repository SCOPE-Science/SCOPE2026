# Scientific scope of the revised finding

This revision retains only the two-term exact-power laws. The original broader version also included first-order regular variation and persistence in a critical weak ideal. Those overlap the published results identified in the revised `RESULT.md` and are not part of the revised contribution.

The proof controls the logarithmic expansion uniformly in singular rank. Summing its relative error gives the stated Schatten remainder; the coefficient separates a trapezoidal endpoint correction from a binomial variance correction. The largest singular value is computed separately, and its second term corresponds to a different shift of the iteration scale. The finite diagnostics corroborate these coefficients, not the asymptotic orders or priority. The priority comparison is limited to the available statements of the closest prior results.
