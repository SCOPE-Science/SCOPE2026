## Mathematical value — PASS
Polynomial decay is the natural compact-shift family lying on the Schatten ideal membership threshold. Its positive iterates have infinitely many singular values in a scaling window comparable to iteration count, in contrast with the sharp finite or sublinear boundary layers of stretched-exponential models. The theorem quantifies convergence with a universal strong-ideal constant, a sharp nonzero second-order correction for the canonical exact-power family, and an exact weak-ideal obstruction. These facts explain why improved individual singular-value convergence does not imply convergence in a borderline operator ideal and provide concrete asymptotics for perturbation and ideal estimates; no larger classification is asserted.
