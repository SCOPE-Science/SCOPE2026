# Review of the collision-conditioning theorem

## Correctness — PASS

For every fixed \(N\ge2\) the monomial basis makes the sum of Fock dilations diagonal and converts the squared Hilbert–Schmidt ratio into the smallest eigenvalue of a normalized positive-definite Szegő matrix. The rational Hardy cardinal functions are explicitly interpolating and span the same kernel subspace. Their rescaled limit is a single Blaschke-weighted kernel of known Hardy norm, so inverse Gram matrices have the asserted positive rank-one limit. Taking reciprocal eigenvalues proves the exact exponent and constant, and the simple eigenvalue proves the minimizer direction. Fourier diagonalization proves the all-radius roots-of-unity identity. A separate high-precision program passed 88 numerical assertions; finite tests are not used as infinite proofs.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Lin, Lu, Yang and Zu (2026), Theorem 1.3 and Section 5, prove qualitative and two-sided comparisons for fixed distinct Fock symbols, with comparison constants depending on the symbols. They do not state the sharp coalescence-order constant. The classical two-summand article by Choe, Izuchi and Koo (2010) addresses Schatten non-cancellation; only its abstract was accessible for direct comparison, so independent priority remains uncertain. Neighboring results about Gaussian Fock evaluation-jet clusters, Szegő determinant designs, and near-boundary Fock dilation cancellations do not cover the present interior collision of the optimal *coefficient-independent* Schatten-two constant. Barycentric interpolation, Cauchy Gram identities, and finite Blaschke factors are acknowledged as standard ingredients, not original methods.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The parent theorem guarantees positive constants only when dilation symbols are fixed. Here the sharp constant is shown to vanish at the precise, dimension-dependent order \(2N-2\) as distinct symbols coalesce. The formula gives the entire geometry-dependent leading coefficient for arbitrary distinct complex slopes and the asymptotically worst scalar direction, with an exactly solvable symmetric benchmark. This directly quantifies ill-conditioning of the no-cancellation estimate for a natural family of compact composition operators, beyond a restatement of membership or a finite numerical observation.

## Remaining scientific limitations

The theorem is restricted to unweighted dilations, fixed interior collision center, and \(\mathcal S_2\); it gives no claim about general weights, other Schatten exponents, or simultaneous boundary scaling. The 2010 full journal text and some neighboring unpublished work were not comprehensively accessible, so possible undiscovered prior equivalent expressions remain a bibliographic risk. A separate mathematical audit is not included.
