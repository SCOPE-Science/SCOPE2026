On every odd screened cycle with at least five vertices, the optimal two-class independent Rademacher trace-probing partition has exactly one unavoidable monochromatic edge. The **smallest possible variance penalty among all other partitions** is exactly \(8(G_1^2-G_2^2)\) on a five-cycle and \(8(G_1^2-2G_2^2+G_3^2)\) on every larger odd cycle, and both bounds are attained.
