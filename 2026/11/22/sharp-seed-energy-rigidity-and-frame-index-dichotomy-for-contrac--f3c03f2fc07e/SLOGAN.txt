Every orbit frame of a positive contractive unilateral weighted shift with lower frame bound \(A\) needs initial-vector energy at least \(A(1+\sum_n(1-w_n^2))\), with equality forcing tightness; when the deficit sum is finite but infinitely many weights are defective, finite generators approach the sharp bound without attaining it. The frame index is exactly \(1\) for positive total weight product and infinite for zero product, even when the shift norm equals one.
