For the damped two-mode Jordan semigroup, the best continuous-frame condition number is exactly \(\max\{(c/2a)^2,(2a/c)^2\}\), but the sharp unit-sensor lower-frame ceiling \(1/(4a)\) becomes attainable already when \(c\ge2a\). The two optimal sensor designs coincide only at the tight-frame coupling \(c=2a\).
