In the countable homogeneous triangle-free graph, the counts of injective one- and two-point types over a named finite base distinguish every nonisomorphic triangle-free base through six vertices, but fail for exactly one pair at seven vertices: a six-cycle plus an isolate versus the three-arm tree of lengths \(1,2,3\). They have \(36\) unary and \(1893\) binary types, while their tertiary counts differ by \(78\); adjoining \(k\) isolates preserves the first two counts and magnifies the tertiary difference to \(78\cdot5^k\).
