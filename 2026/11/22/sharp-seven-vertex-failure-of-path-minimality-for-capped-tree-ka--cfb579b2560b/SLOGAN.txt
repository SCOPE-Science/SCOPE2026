For one-point types over capped tree metrics in the universal three-distance space, an exact neighbor-degree formula shows that the path uniquely minimizes through six vertices but first loses at the seven-vertex three-spider. This failure persists for every \(n\ge8\): the tree with arms \((2,2,n-5)\) has exactly \(2^{n-5}\) fewer nonalgebraic singleton types than the path on the same number of vertices.
