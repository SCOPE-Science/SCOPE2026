For a stable planar Jordan shear, two real implicit-midpoint steps with fixed total duration \(H>4/a\) minimize worst-direction terminal amplification by exactly canceling the shear if and only if \(s\geq2a\). The sharp optimal step product is \(h_1h_2=4/a^2\) and the exact norm is \((aH-4)/(aH+4)\); below that shear boundary the cancellation schedule is strictly improvable.
