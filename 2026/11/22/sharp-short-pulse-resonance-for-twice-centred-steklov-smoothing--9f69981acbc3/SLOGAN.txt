Twice-centred Steklov smoothing on the unit circle has an exactly sharp real \(BV\)-to-\(L^2\) error constant \(C(a)^2=0.0615835934466545\ldots\,a\) for every \(0<a\le1/3\). Its only nonconstant worst cases are short indicator pulses of length \(0.611114325406299\ldots\,a\), rather than well-separated jumps, revealing a precise two-edge interaction.
