Conditional on the number of instantaneous Poisson turns, Dirichlet-distributed flight segments yield a finite trigonometric expansion whose frequencies never exceed half the radial-moment order. The Gaussian binomial contraction identity proves that independent isotropic translational noise cancels *exactly* from the matched-law moment difference. This establishes all quantifiers, arbitrary atomic/singular laws, all integer \(n\ge1\), the global nonasymptotic error, and the root-law sharpness.
