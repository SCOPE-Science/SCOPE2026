For planar run-and-tumble particles, the first unmatched cosine turning harmonic of order \(n\) becomes visible in the \(2n\)-th radial displacement moment at the exact order \(t^{2n+1}\), with coefficient \(2\alpha v^{2n}(n!)^2/(2n+1)!\).
