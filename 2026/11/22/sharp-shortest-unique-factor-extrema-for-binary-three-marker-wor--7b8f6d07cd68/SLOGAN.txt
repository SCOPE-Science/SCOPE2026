Among all length-\(n\) binary words with exactly three ones, for \(n\ge5\), the largest possible global shortest unique substring length is exactly \(n-2\lfloor n/4\rfloor\). The unique extremal structure has equal interior zero gaps of length \(\lfloor n/4\rfloor\), and the two exterior gaps are bounded by that length; from length twelve onward the number of extremizers is \(4-(n\bmod4)\).
