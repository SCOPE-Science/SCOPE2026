Every ideal hyperbolic quadrilateral has exact side slimness \(\operatorname{arsinh}\sqrt{\kappa}\) for \(2\le\kappa\le3\) and \(\operatorname{arsinh}\sqrt{\kappa/(\kappa-2)}\) above that threshold, where \(\kappa\) is its unmarked cross-ratio parameter. The sharp bound \(\operatorname{arsinh}\sqrt3\) occurs in a unique nonregular shape, where each maximizing side changes from one farthest point to two.
