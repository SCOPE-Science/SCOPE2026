For every positive integer \(k\), a cosine-modulated circle kernel with Fourier weights \(e^{-t|n|^\alpha}\) is positive definite exactly for \(b\ge -2e^{-tk^\alpha}/(1+e^{-t(2k)^\alpha})\) when \(0<\alpha\le1\), and only for \(b\ge0\) when \(\alpha>1\). At the negative endpoint, fractional damping deletes exactly the two frequencies \(\pm k\) yet leaves all distinct-site Gram matrices positive definite, whereas the Poisson exponent \(\alpha=1\) collapses the spectrum to exactly \(2k-1\) consecutive frequencies.
