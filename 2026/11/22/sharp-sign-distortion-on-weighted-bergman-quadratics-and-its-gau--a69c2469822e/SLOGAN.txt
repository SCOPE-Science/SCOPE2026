Every weighted Bergman space has a unique sharp sign-distortion profile on \(\operatorname{span}\{1,z,z^2\}\): its fourth-power unconditional constant is \((1+\delta_\beta)/(1-\delta_\beta)\), where \(\delta_\beta\) is the reciprocal minimum of an explicitly strictly convex one-variable function. The distortion decreases strictly with disk-weight concentration but converges to a nontrivial Gaussian limit, and every equality case has an exact three-coefficient magnitude and phase classification.
