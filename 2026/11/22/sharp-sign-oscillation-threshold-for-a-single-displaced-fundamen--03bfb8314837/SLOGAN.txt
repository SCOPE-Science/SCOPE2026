When just the fundamental cosine frequency is shifted from \(1\) to \(0<\alpha<1\), while all harmonics \(n\ge2\) remain, every nonzero real uniformly convergent zero-mean series changes sign in each interval of length \(2\pi\) exactly for \(\alpha\ge1/\sqrt2\). Below that threshold an explicit absolutely convergent series equals a strictly positive Poisson kernel throughout one full \(2\pi\)-interval.
