On \(K_2\vee\overline{K_n}\), the sharp signed radial Schur norm is \(1+2\max\{0,t^2-(1+t)/(2n)\}/((1+t)/2+1/n+2|t|)\), with exact asymmetric contractivity thresholds. The infinite-leaf limit has norm \(1+4t^2/(1+t+4|t|)\), revealing a strict difference between positive and negative unit edge weights.
