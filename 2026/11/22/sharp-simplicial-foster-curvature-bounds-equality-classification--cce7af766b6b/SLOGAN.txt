For a simplicial vertex \(v\) of degree \(d\), the local Foster resistance curvature obeys the sharp inequality \(p(v)\ge1/(d+1)\), with equality exactly when each component outside \(N[v]\) attaches to at most one of its neighbours. Consequently no noncomplete connected chordal graph is resistance-regular, and every noncomplete simplicial vertex forces the explicit gap \(p(v)-1/n\ge(n-d-1)/(n(d+1))\).\n
