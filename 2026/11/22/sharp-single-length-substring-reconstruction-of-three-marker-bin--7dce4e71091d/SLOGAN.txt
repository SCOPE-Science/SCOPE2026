For length-\(n\) binary words with exactly three ones, the last ambiguous complete contiguous-window deck occurs at \(k=n-\lceil(n+2)/4\rceil+1\) for every \(n\ge8\). Every larger read length reconstructs the source even from its unordered reads’ positionwise one-counts, while distinct phases of a periodic three-marker construction collide exactly at the boundary.
