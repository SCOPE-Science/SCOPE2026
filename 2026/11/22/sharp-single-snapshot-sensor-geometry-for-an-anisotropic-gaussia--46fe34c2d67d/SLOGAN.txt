For an ideal anisotropic Gaussian contaminant puff observed at one known age, recovering unknown source location, mass and the full diffusion tensor is possible from exact sensor values if and only if the sensor locations determine every quadratic polynomial. The sharp requirement is \((d+1)(d+2)/2\) suitably placed sensors; a second snapshot additionally determines an unknown release time, whereas a single snapshot cannot disentangle age from diffusivity even with dense spatial measurements.
