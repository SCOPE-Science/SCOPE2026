For nonalgebraic one-point types in the homogeneous three-distance metric space, increasing one parameter distance from \(1\) to \(3\) always increases the count through five points, but first decreases it at six points, in exactly three marked-isometry classes. A two-shore construction has the exact all-order defect \(2-(2^p-2)(2^q-2)\), giving an exponentially growing loss for suitable parameters.
