The 120 proper two-colourings of the sixteen-point affine plane split into five canonical 24-state classes, one for each balanced parallel direction. All are frozen under fewer than six point flips, but six-point flips connect them in a 48-regular graph of diameter three with color complement the unique antipode; exactly eight-point flips instead produce five disjoint copies of a complete 24-vertex graph minus a perfect matching.
