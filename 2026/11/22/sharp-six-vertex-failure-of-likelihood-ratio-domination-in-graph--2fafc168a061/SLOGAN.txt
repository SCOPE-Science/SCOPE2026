Uniform sequential graph parking likelihood-ratio dominates uniformly chosen maximal-independent-set size on **every** graph of at most five vertices; six vertices is the sharp first failure. The infinite connected family \(K_1\vee(K_2\sqcup K_{1,m})\), \(m\ge2\), preserves ordinary first-order domination while reversing the first two likelihood ratios, showing why these stochastic orders must be distinguished.
