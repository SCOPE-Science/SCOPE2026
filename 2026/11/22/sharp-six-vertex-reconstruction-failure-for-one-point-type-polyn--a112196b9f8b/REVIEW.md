# Review of the six-vertex 2-tree reconstruction boundary

## Correctness — PASS

The one-point extension property of the universal \(K_4\)-free graph identifies adjacency sets with triangle-free subsets. Each 2-tree is chordal, making this exactly the induced-forest polynomial. The six-vertex finite census is exhaustive by edge attachment and isomorphism-free canonicalization; independent direct induced-forest tests reproduce its coefficients. The unique six-vertex collision and the rooted-edge identity are checked on explicit triangle lists. The all-order propagation and persistent nonisomorphism follow by exact subset partition and degree multisets, independently of finite experiments.

## Originality — PASS, subject to historical-priority risk

The mathematical claim is not that the acyclic polynomial is new, nor that arbitrary graphs have collisions. Barton–Brown–Pike explicitly develop this polynomial and exhibit non-reconstruction for arbitrary graphs. Their relevant first sections were inspected, including the examples and formulas, but the restricted-class sharp threshold and infinite edge-rooted construction are not supplied there. A 2019 paper on 2-tree triangle hypergraphs concerns improper sum-list coloring. A targeted comparison with a published induced-forest result on crown graphs concerns a bipartite class rather than 2-trees and does not supply this reconstruction threshold. Search and accessible material cannot exclude unindexed or older equivalent enumerations, so historical priority remains a risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Maximal treewidth-two (2-tree) parameter graphs are a natural highly structured test class for reconstruction from finite-parameter logical type spectra. The result proves exactly how soon the entire degree-refined type count ceases to identify a connected base, classifies the first ambiguity, and gives a structural mechanism propagating it at every larger size. This is stronger than displaying one arbitrary colliding graph pair, and it connects a logical type invariant with the established induced-forest polynomial.

## Scope and remaining limits

Exhaustiveness ends at order six. The displayed family proves existence at all higher orders without enumerating their remaining collision classes. Unlabelled polynomial equality does not imply pointwise type equality. The accessible comparison does not constitute a historical-priority guarantee; no independent audit has been performed.
