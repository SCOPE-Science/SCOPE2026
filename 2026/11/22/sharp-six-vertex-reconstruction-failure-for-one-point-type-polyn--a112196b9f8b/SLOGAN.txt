In the homogeneous \(K_4\)-free graph, the number of one-point types by adjacency degree over an individually named 2-tree first fails to reconstruct the base at six vertices: exactly one nonisomorphic pair shares \(1+6z+15z^2+16z^3+6z^4\). Attaching any number \(t\) of new simplicial vertices to the same distinguished edge yields connected nonisomorphic 2-trees with identical polynomials and exactly \(4+40\cdot2^t\) types.
