Over any pointwise-named two-part base of the homogeneous semigeneric digraph, the out-neighbor-graded singleton-type polynomial is \((1+z)^{a+b}+z^r+z^{a-r}+z^s+z^{b-s}\). Its inverse fibers are completely reconstructed by three perfect matchings of four exponents; at most six nonisomorphic bases share the same polynomial, and the bound first occurs with six named vertices.
