For a thermally blurred four-direction swimmer in a harmonic trap, fourth directional cumulants reveal the hidden propulsion axes, while a sharp sixth-to-fourth cumulant ratio uniquely recovers the turning rate and completes the parameter inversion. The resulting exact three-cumulant feasible region determines the stationary entropy-production rate from position-only snapshots, with sixth order provably necessary when both propulsion speed and thermal noise are unknown.
