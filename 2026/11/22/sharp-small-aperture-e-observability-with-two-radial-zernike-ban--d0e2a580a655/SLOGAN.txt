For continuous recovery of the first three radial Zernike modes from at most two concentric annuli of total area fraction \(q\), the sharp best least-captured energy is \(\frac{5+3\sqrt5}{2}q^3+o(q^3)\). Every asymptotically optimal two-annulus design has one vanishingly thin satellite ring and squared-radius centers tending to \((1\pm5^{-1/4})/2\), whereas any single annulus has only order-\(q^5\) worst-mode energy.
