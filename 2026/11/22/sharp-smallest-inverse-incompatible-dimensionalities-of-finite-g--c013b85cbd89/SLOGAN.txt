The inverse-compatibility hypothesis for dimensionality quotients is sharp: the first finite-group counterexample occurs on \(S_3\) (order \(6\)), and the first abelian counterexample on \(C_{12}\) (order \(12\)). Exact exhaustive boundary censuses give respectively \(12\) of \(22\) and \(4\) of \(44\) inverse-incompatible dimensionalities, and explicit quotient products show failure of the hypergroup inverse axiom.
