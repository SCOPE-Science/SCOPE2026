For Euclidean isotonic regression on an \(n\)-point path, the exact gradient-\(\ell_1\) two-input sensitivity is \(1\) while gradient-\(\ell_\infty\) sensitivity is \(n/2\). For every \(1\le p\le\infty\), the sharp gradient-\(\ell_p\) coefficient is \((n/2)^{1-1/p}\) up to relative error at most \(1/n\), uniformly in the exponent: dimension-free stability ends exactly at \(p=1\).
