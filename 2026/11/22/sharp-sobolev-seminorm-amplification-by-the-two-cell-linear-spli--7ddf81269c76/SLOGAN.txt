For the ordinary \(L^2\) projector onto continuous linear splines on just two intervals, the exact \(W^{1,\infty}\) and \(H^1\) derivative-seminorm amplification factors are explicit, attained functions of the knot position. As the shorter interval collapses, they diverge at the different sharp rates \(4/(9\mu)\) and \(\sqrt{3/(10\mu)}\), while balancing the intervals uniquely minimizes both.
