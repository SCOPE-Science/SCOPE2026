For every bounded-type irrational circle rotation, mean-zero real \(H^s\) cocycles have continuous real transfers whenever \(s>1\), while \(H^1\) contains explicit measurable-but-not-continuous coboundaries. Their modulus can be exactly \(h\log\log(1/h)\), or smaller than \(hL(1/h)\) for *any* prescribed divergent nondecreasing gauge \(L\), revealing a sharp Sobolev threshold and arbitrarily near-Lipschitz endpoint behavior.
