For the two recently proposed softmax-based local steps, gradient-aligned single-example rates obey \(\eta_{\mathrm{so}}/\eta_{\mathrm{cs}}=1/\cos^2\phi\). The sharp three-class disagreement grows like \(1/(12p)\) as the correct-class probability \(p\) vanishes, while the proposed angular rejection threshold \(\cos\phi\ge1/4\) exactly caps this local discrepancy at \(16\).
