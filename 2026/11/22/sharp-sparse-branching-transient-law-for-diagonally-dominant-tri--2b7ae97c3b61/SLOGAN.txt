For every strictly row-diagonally dominant unit triangular system with at most \(d\) nonzeros in each column, the exact worst \(k\)-sweep Euclidean Jacobi residual amplification is \(\tau^k\sqrt{M}\), where \(M\) is the largest leaf population allowed by the finite \(d\)-ary depth budget. The sharp all-dimension safety threshold is \(\tau\sqrt d\le1\), and the extremizers are explicit sparse parent trees despite every iteration matrix being nilpotent.
