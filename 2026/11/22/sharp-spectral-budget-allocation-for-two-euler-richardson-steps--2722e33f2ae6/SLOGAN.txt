For two Euler–Richardson steps on a positive spectral interval, fixing their total duration creates a sharp up-to-five-regime minimax allocation law, with a condition-number bifurcation at \(\kappa=1+\sqrt2\). The largest total duration retaining robust final-time nonexpansion is exactly \(2\kappa/[\mu(\kappa-1)]\) for \(1<\kappa\le2\), and \(8/(\mu\kappa)\) for \(\kappa\ge2\), while equal steps allow only \(4/(\mu\kappa)\).
