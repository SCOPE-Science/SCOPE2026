## originality

PASS

The supported distinct contribution is the full boundary family n=r−1, including its exact value by a tight triangle packing, hence the sharp thresholds. No separate credit is assigned to the value above the boundary or strict failure below it: both follow from the general token-graph bounds of Alejandro-Soto et al. Even uniqueness for n≥r can be deduced by examining equality in their projection-sum proof. Boundary multiplicity via parity lift is an immediate consequence once the boundary upper bound is supplied. Two small boundary cases are already covered by the Johnson and single-edge-deletion results; the general boundary is not covered by those slices.
