For the ordinary two-token graph of the complete split graph \(K_r\vee\overline K_n\), the exact maximum cut is \(rn(n+r-2)\) precisely from the sharp threshold \(n\ge r-1\) onward, and the optimum is uniquely the mixed-versus-pure cut when \(n\ge r\). A weighted triangle-packing certificate proves the value, while parity-lifted base cuts establish nonuniqueness at the boundary and strict failure below it.
