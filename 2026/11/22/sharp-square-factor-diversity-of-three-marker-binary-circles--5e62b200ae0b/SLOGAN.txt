Among binary circles with exactly three ones, the greatest number of distinct literal circular squares is exactly \(n-4+\lfloor m/2\rfloor\) at \(n=3m\ge25\), and \(n-6+\lfloor(m+1)/2\rfloor\) at \(n=3m+1,3m+2\ge25\), attained only by two reversal-related nearly balanced gap necklaces. The asymptotic maximum density is \(7/6\), and the first above-circumference example has length \(30\) and gaps \((8,9,10)\), with \(31\) squares.
