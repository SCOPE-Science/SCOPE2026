The shortest element of the type-A \(0\)-Hecke monoid whose Demazure square is the longest element has Coxeter length exactly \(\lceil n(n-1)/4\rceil\), for every \(n\ge2\). In degrees \(n\equiv0,1\pmod4\), the minimizers are precisely ordinary group square roots of reversal, and number \((2k)!/k!\), with \(k=\lfloor n/4\rfloor\).
