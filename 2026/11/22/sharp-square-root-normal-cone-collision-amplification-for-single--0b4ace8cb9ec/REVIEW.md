## Mathematical value — PASS

A hypersimplex has equal Gaussian normal-cone angles for all vertices, so the effect of imposing one natural split inequality on the angular sampling law is an intrinsically motivated geometric gap. The result quantifies a discontinuity between two natural vertex-sampling modes: the boundary orbit has asymptotically vanishing uniform-vertex proportion yet accounts for asymptotically every repeated Gaussian optimum, with a sharp growing collision enhancement. This is a structural rare-event phenomenon at the critical hypergeometric threshold, not an arbitrary finite tabulation or a mere graph-parameter substitution.
