For three positive prescribed cylinder-radius ratios tangent to a unit ball, the sharp common scale is the classical Descartes expression, and every maximal packing has parallel axes. More significantly, every near-maximal packing is within \(O(\sqrt\varepsilon)\) of that geometry in both contact points and axis directions, and explicit pairwise skew packings show the exponent \(1/2\) is optimal for every positive weight triple.
