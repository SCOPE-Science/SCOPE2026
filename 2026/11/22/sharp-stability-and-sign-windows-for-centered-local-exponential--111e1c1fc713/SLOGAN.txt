For centered five- and seven-node local polynomial-exponential heat updates, positivity fails at arbitrarily small positive steps, recovers on an explicit bounded step interval, and fails again for large steps, despite mass conservation. Their sharp periodic spectral-stability ceilings are \(2/3\) and \(0.8413602280\ldots\), respectively, and every centered stencil of five or more nodes has both a small-step and a large-step positivity obstruction.
