For the complete \(r\)-uniform hypergraph on \(n\) labelled vertices, every proper named-colour reconfiguration graph with at least \(n+1\) colours has sharp diameter \(n+\lfloor n/(2(r-1))\rfloor\). Saturated colour-class Euler tours give the upper bound, while disjoint swaps of full classes force the matching lower bound, determining the stable diameter without claiming the exact stability threshold.
