For every modulus \(m\ge2\) and one-run cap \(r\ge2\), accepting exactly the binary words with no contiguous \(1^r\) and with scattered `001` count divisible by \(m\) requires exactly \(rm^2+1\) complete deterministic states for odd \(m\), or \(2rm^2+1\) for even \(m\). Every state in the natural combined run-length and modular-counter automaton is reachable and distinguishable.
