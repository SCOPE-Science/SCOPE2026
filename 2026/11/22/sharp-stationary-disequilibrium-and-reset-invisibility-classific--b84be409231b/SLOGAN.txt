For every finite irreducible random walk with arbitrary independent finite-mean renewal resets to one vertex, the equilibrium occupation law differs from its no-reset stationary law by at least \(\Pr(X_L\ne v)/(2\mathbb E L)\) in total variation, with a sharp universal constant. The reset is stationary-invisible exactly when every pre-reset endpoint is already the reset vertex; on connected loopless undirected graphs this happens only for a hub-reset star with even-valued reset intervals.
