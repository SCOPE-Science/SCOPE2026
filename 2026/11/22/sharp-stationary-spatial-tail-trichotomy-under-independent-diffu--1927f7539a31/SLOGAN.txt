Independently resampling a bounded Brownian diffusivity while resetting only the particle position produces a sharp stationary spatial-tail trichotomy at \(r_c=\lambda(\int W(d)/(1-d)\,\mathrm{d}d-1)\): pure exponential, critical \(|x|^{\nu-1}\) times exponential, and supercritical \(|x|^{-\nu-1}\) times exponential. All leading amplitudes and the exact boundary exponential-moment cutoff follow from a positive pole-plus-continuum spectral representation.
