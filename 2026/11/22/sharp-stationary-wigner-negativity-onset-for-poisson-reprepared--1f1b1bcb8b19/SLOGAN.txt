An odd optical cat under zero-temperature photon loss and full Poisson re-preparation has a Wigner-positive stationary state exactly up to the sharp reset/loss ratio \(r/\gamma=1\): below it the Wigner density is everywhere strictly positive, at it only the phase-space origin is zero, and above it the origin is negative. The integrated Wigner-negative mass emerges quadratically above threshold with an explicit positive coefficient depending on the cat amplitude.
