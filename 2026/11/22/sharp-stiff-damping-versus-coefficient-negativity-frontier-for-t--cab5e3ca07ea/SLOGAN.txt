For two-stage algebraically stable Runge–Kutta methods of order at least three, requiring stiff-limit amplification at most \(\rho<1\) forces a sharp minimum negative Butcher entry: \(1/[12(1+\rho)]\) up to \(\rho=1/2\), followed by a unique interior-root frontier. Every budget has a unique minimizing tableau up to relabeling; its zero-budget endpoint is two-stage Radau IIA.
