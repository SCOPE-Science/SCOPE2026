# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The density is 1+delta alpha² g(u)g(v) with g antisymmetric on reflection. The absolute maximum of g is 1 for alpha<=2 and sqrt(2/alpha) exp(alpha/4-1/2) otherwise, so nonnegativity gives exactly the claimed admissibility interval. The u-derivative of the conditional CDF for nonzero alpha and positive delta has sign alpha(1-2u)²-2. For alpha>2 it is strictly positive on an interior interval near an endpoint; for alpha<=2 it is nonpositive everywhere. Negative coupling fails at u=1/2, and the independent cases are SI. Rank correlation is 12 delta h(alpha)²; on the positive upper boundary for nonzero alpha<=2 its envelope strictly increases because h(alpha)/alpha is the integral of x times the average of exp(t alpha x) over 0<=t<=1. The actual alpha=0 member has zero correlation, even though the rescaled boundary limit is 1/3. At alpha>2 the positive envelope has derivative sign opposite Q(s), s=sqrt(alpha)/2. Direct differentiation yields Q'(s)=s²(2s²-1)exp(-s²), and Q(0)=0 with positive limit at infinity, giving one root beyond 1/sqrt(2) and strict unimodality there. Its tail tends to zero. This yields both sharp maxima, the negative mirror bound, and exactly two upper-boundary shapes at intermediate high correlations. Proportionality of the rank-one factors for different nonzero shapes is impossible by the linear and quadratic Taylor coefficients in u(1-u), so the two copulas are distinct. An independent integrated-power-series computation in alpha reproduced both maxima and the maximizing shape.
