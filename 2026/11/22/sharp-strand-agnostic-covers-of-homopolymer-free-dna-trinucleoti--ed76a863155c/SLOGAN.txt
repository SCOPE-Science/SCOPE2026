All 18 reverse-complement classes of DNA three-mers without repeated adjacent bases first fit in a 21-base linear word or a 20-base circle, and both minima incur forced repetitions of alternating complementary three-mers. Exactly 57,984 labeled shortest linear words and 14,496 shortest circles up to rotation exist; the four-odd-vertex obstruction and independent exhaustive enumerations prove both sharp claims.
