For periodic scalar advection with the entropy-conservative power-mean flux, every positive trajectory stays strictly positive for all time **if and only if** the power-entropy parameter satisfies \(p\leq0\); for every \(p>0\), three cells suffice for finite-time contact with vacuum. The sharp first-touchdown law changes from \(\varepsilon^p\) to \(\varepsilon\log(1/\varepsilon)\) to \(\varepsilon\) as \(p\) passes through one, distinguishing nonnegative boundary invariance from strict positivity.
