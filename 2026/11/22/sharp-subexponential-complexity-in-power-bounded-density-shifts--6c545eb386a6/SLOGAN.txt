In the natural binary shift where every length-\(k\) window contains at most \(\lceil k^\alpha\rceil\) ones, the exact leading growth law is \(\log p(n)\sim(1-\alpha)n^\alpha\log n\) for every \(0<\alpha<1\). These shifts are topologically mixing yet uniquely ergodic at the zero fixed point, and their nested invariant subsystems realize the full interval of diagonal entropy dimensions.
