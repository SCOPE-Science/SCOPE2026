The standard skew \((0,n)\)-Hochschild polytope has an exact, unique Euclidean minimum enclosing sphere obtained by a water-filling projection. Although it is a combinatorial cube with \(2^{n-1}\) vertices, its sphere touches exactly \(2^{b-1}\), where \(b\sim(3/2)^{1/3}n^{2/3}\); every contact belongs to a single exposed cubical face.
