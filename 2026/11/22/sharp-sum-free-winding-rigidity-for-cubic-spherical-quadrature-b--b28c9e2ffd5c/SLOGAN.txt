Primitive one-parameter diagonal phase curves on \(S^{2d-1}\), for \(d\ge3\), integrate every real cubic exactly if and only if all amplitudes are equal and their distinct absolute integer windings form a sum-free set. Their sharp shortest length is \(2\pi\sqrt{(4d^2-1)/3}\), achieved only by the odd-frequency symmetric moment spectrum, within this restricted torus-orbit class; global shortest-curve optimality is not asserted.
