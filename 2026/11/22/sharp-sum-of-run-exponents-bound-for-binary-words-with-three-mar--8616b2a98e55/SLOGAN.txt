For every binary word with exactly three \(1\)s, the largest possible sum of exponents of its maximal periodic runs is exactly \(n+3-1/m-2/(m+1)\) at all lengths \(n\ge13\), where \(m=\lfloor(n-1)/4\rfloor\). An explicit three-marker family attains this sharp value, giving the exact sparse-composition extremum for the run-exponent statistic.
