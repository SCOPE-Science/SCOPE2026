For every \(2<p<4\) and every regular circle grid with \(m\ge2\) equally weighted nodes, the exact largest relative \(L^p\)-moment error on the complex span of \(1\) and \(z\) is a positive convergent Gamma series, uniquely attained (up to scaling and phase) by balanced binomials vanishing at a node. Unlike the subquadratic regime, every extremizer is an **overestimate**; at \(p=3\) the sharp constant has an explicit cotangent formula, and at \(p\uparrow4\) a two-node resonance survives while all grids with at least three nodes become exact.
