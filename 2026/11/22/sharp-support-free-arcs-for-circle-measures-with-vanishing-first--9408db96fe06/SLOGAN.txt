A positive circle measure whose complex Fourier coefficients vanish at frequencies \(1\) and \(4\) cannot avoid any open arc longer than \(2\pi-2\arccos(\sqrt{1-2y}-\sqrt y)\), where \(y=(2+\sqrt{10})/12\). The sharp bound is attained by a unique four-atom positive rule up to rotation and scaling, and a factorized degree-four Chebyshev certificate proves both optimality and rigidity.
