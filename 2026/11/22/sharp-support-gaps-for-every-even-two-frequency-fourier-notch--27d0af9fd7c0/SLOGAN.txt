For positive circle measures with Fourier coefficients vanishing at \(1\) and any even \(k\ge4\), a unique two-contact Chebyshev witness gives the exact largest empty arc and a unique four-atom extremizer up to rotation and scaling. The newly determined all-even family has sharp gap \(G_k=\pi-2a_*/k+O(k^{-3})\), where \(a_*\) is fixed by two explicit scalar root equations; the known \(k=4\) case is recovered rather than reclaimed.
