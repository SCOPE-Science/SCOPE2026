For \(q\)-ary words with no run of three equal symbols, the complete all-length spectrum of *distinct one-deletion outputs that also obey the run limit* has an exact four-state rational generating function. The minimum survivor degree is sharply \(\lceil(n+2)/4\rceil\), and at lengths \(n=4h-2\) exactly \(q(q-1)^{h-1}\) words attain it.
