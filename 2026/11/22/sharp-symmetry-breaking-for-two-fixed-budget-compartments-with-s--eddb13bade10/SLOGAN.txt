Under the classical substrate-inhibited Haldane–Andrews rate, distributing a fixed concentration budget equally between two identical biomass compartments is optimal only while the dimensionless mean is at most the unique root of \(m^3-3m-\alpha=0\). Above it, exactly two unequal allocations optimize total instantaneous response; the imbalance emerges with square-root scaling, and at high loads one compartment approaches the individual-optimal concentration \(\sqrt{K_sK_i}\).
