For \(DGP(n,1)\), three vertices resolve every edge only when \(n\ge10\) and \(n\equiv2\pmod4\); the missing finite boundary has exactly \(160\) three-vertex generators at \(n=10\), in six natural symmetry orbits, and none at the smaller congruent value \(n=6\). Two exhaustive implementations determine every pair, triple and four-set count for \(3\le n\le12\), completing the previously published law restricted to \(n\ge13\).
