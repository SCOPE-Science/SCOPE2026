For \(\binom{4n}{n}\pmod3\), the nonzero residues have a strictly positive but exponentially smaller signed imbalance on every ternary-power interval: its generating function is \((1+3z+z^2)/(1-3z^2-z^3)\). The signed discrepancy over arbitrary prefixes has the **sharp** power-law exponent \(\log_3(2\cos(\pi/9))\), while the nondivisibility support was already known.
