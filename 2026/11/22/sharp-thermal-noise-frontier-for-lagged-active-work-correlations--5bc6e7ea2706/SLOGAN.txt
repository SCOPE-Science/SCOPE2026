A stationary harmonically trapped active Ornstein–Uhlenbeck particle has exactly one positive-to-negative crossing of its separated active-work covariance when \(D/A<(\kappa+2)/[\kappa(\kappa+1)^2]\), and strictly negative covariance at every positive lag above that sharp thermal threshold. The result includes the resonant trap \(\kappa=1\), where the crossing time is exactly \(3/4-D/A\); a nonnegative white-noise contact term remains, strictly positive for \(D>0\).
