# Scientific scope and verification

The all-retention result is proved by expanding the exact probability generating function quotient near retention one and by constructing a compound-Poisson quotient when every canonical difference is nonnegative. The parity reduction decides every positive index, including ties at the boundary. `verify.py` provides finite exact checks of the formulas, not an all-index certificate.

The general canonical-sequence criterion is due to Steutel and van Harn. Letac, Malouche and Maurer study real powers of the same convolution; the ordinary additive infinite-divisibility boundary is prior work. The mass-curvature result in Resultary `eb5c7d74b649` covers the log-concavity cutoff for this same model, so this version makes no mass-shape or hazard claim. The published abstract of Ben Salah's real-power paper does not settle whether an equivalent thinning boundary appears in the full text.

The exact criterion determines when this published count model can be the stationary marginal at every Bernoulli thinning rate with an independent innovation. The overdispersed example distinguishes this property from ordinary infinite divisibility.
