Every Poisson–xgamma count law is compound Poisson, but its binomial-thinning self-decomposability holds exactly when \(\theta\ge0.425326408449\ldots\), the unique positive root of \(\theta^4+8\theta^3+13\theta^2-3=0\). Below this boundary, even a law with strictly decreasing probabilities can fail thinning self-decomposability.
