The two-parameter Poisson generalized Lindley count law is invariant under every Bernoulli-thinning autoregression with independent innovations exactly when \(\alpha\ge(\theta+1)^3/[\theta(\theta+2)^2]\); the allowed innovation laws have explicit positive compound-Poisson jump rates. This sharp frontier also supplies a rational counterexample to a previously claimed unconditional thinning self-decomposability of an overlapping weighted negative-binomial Lindley family.
