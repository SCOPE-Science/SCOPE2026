# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The sole final claim is the six-length integral homotopy classification plus the specified primitive top cycles. The simplices are generated *from the two-tolerance definition* for every subset, with all degree-four and degree-five chains represented by finite signed matrices. The conceptual exterior-chain proof establishes two-connectedness independently. Exact integer Smith forms of \(\partial_4\) show all invariant factors are units, eliminating torsion and permitting the three/four-cell cancellation proof. The canonical top-cycle matrices at lengths eleven and twelve are primitive of exactly the full top-homology ranks. The extracted standalone checker was replayed against the packaged content. Risks: correctness depends on implementation of standard integer Smith reduction, though both boundary composition and independently computed mod-two ranks are cross-checked. Finite computations apply to exactly six lengths and are exhaustive within that domain.

## Originality — PASS, provisional priority
The full open text of Selvaraja's arXiv:2611.14384v1 (10 November 2026), especially the stated main bounds, its flag-complex theorem and the top-dimensional-homology section, supplies general bounds and conditional nonvanishing but no exact squared-cycle two-tolerance homotopy types. The input \(\operatorname{Cl}(C_n^2)\) has vanishing top homology, so the cited conditional theorem is not a stronger classification here. The Kim–Lew original exposition likewise concerns tolerance definitions and Leray bounds, not these integral sphere ranks. Indexed results for squared-cycle ordinary cut complexes, two-independence, and one-tolerance were compared **by face conditions and implied homology**, not merely titles. One indexed ordinary cut-complex result has a different homotopy type even at the matching underlying graph size. Search and comparison are not a novelty certificate; inaccessible indexed full text and unindexed equivalent work remain priority risks.

## Value — PASS
The natural first six lengths run from the beginning of the stable triangular-clique regime through the first disappearance of tetrahedral faces at length twelve. Within this canonically delimited range the theorem finds a sharp transition from solely four-sphere wedges to mixed three/four-sphere wedges at length ten, hence a natural nonshellability obstruction for those pure four-complexes. It also gives a specific, integral, geometrically defined basis of all top cycles at lengths eleven and twelve, and an exact one-rank deficiency at length ten. These facts are not automatic from Leray bounds, Euler characteristics, or the lower-tolerance classifications. It does not pretend to solve an unrestricted all-length conjecture.

## Comparison and limitations
The closest available sources are the full primary tolerance-complex papers and indexed ordinary squared-cycle cut-complex computations. The first post supplies bounds; the second uses different facets and does not imply the six exact homotopy types. A recoverable earlier one-tolerance classification on the same graph is distinct, as is a general strictly one-dimensional graph-tolerance classification: the present filled triangles create additional four-cells and change homology. Full comparison with some indexed manuscript bodies was unavailable, and independent verification remains outstanding.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
