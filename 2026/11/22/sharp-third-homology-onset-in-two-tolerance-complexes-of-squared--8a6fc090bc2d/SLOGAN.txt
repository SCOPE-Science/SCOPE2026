The two-tolerance clique complexes of squared cycles first acquire three-dimensional integral homology at length ten: \(\mathcal T_2(\operatorname{Cl}(C_{10}^2))\simeq S^3\vee S^3\vee\bigvee^{26}S^4\), while lengths seven through nine are wedges of four-spheres. Exact integral homotopy types are determined through the first missing-tetrahedron case at length twelve, and disjoint-triangle cycles give primitive top-homology bases at lengths eleven and twelve.
