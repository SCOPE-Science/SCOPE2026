For an unbiased scalar multiplicative-gradient oracle on a strongly convex quadratic, fixed gradient clipping can create two additional attracting nonoptimal equilibria on opposite sides of the true minimizer even when the clipped drift points inward near zero and at infinity; three distinct curvature atoms are the sharp minimum. An explicit three-atom instance makes diminishing-step clipped SGD converge to the wrong point with probability at least \(1-\pi^2/24\), whereas unclipped SGD with the same oracle and steps converges to the true minimizer almost surely.
