For every alternating weighted four-cycle with positive edge lengths \(a,b\), three balls cover its sixteen-point \(\ell^p\)-product at the sharp radius \((a^p+b^p)^{1/p}\). For finite \(p>1\), the optimizer set jumps from 32 triples for unequal edges to 96 in the square limit, while the factor covering numbers multiply to four but the product needs only three.
