For a regular-simplex system with \(m\ge4\) equations, the uniquely optimal three-block randomized Kaczmarz design for worst-case one-step expected squared-error reduction uses one block of \(m-2\) rows and two singleton blocks, sampled with probabilities \(m/(3m-2)\) and \((m-1)/(3m-2)\) each, achieving exactly \(m/(3m-2)\). All other block-size profiles are strictly inferior, including every balanced paving when that differs from this design.
