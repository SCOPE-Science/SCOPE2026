At the sharp three-chain threshold of a high-dimensional random order, the count has a Poisson limit but its second factorial cumulant decays unusually slowly: \(\mathbb E[(W)_2]-(\mathbb EW)^2\sim 2n^5 20^{-d}\). An exact finite-dimensional overlap formula shows that triples sharing one extreme vertex cause the correction, with exponent \(3\log20/\log6-5\approx0.01585\).
