The elementary orientation count for shared vertices is explicitly reconstructed in the proof. A standalone exact-rational checker compares its expected value and second factorial moment with complete permutations of coordinate rankings for seven finite parameter pairs. The asymptotic comparison relies on the exact powers \(20^{-d}\), \(12^{-d}\), and \(6^{-2d}\), with \(d=3\log n/\log6+O(1)\). Finite checks do not certify the infinite theorem.
