In a nonsingular real three-row Kaczmarz system, optimizing the sharp one-step mean-square contraction and optimizing the exact annealed asymptotic mean-square rate select different unique row-sampling laws. The one-step rule loses a sharp multiplicative factor of \(6-2\sqrt6\) in asymptotic contraction at the worst correlation, while every real planar frame was previously known to exhibit coincidence.
