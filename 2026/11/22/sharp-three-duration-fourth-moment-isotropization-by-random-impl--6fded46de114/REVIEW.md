That result therefore does not imply the simultaneous-harmonic criterion, sharp three-node law, mean \(8/(3\sqrt3\,\omega)\), or cap \(2\sqrt3/\omega\). Searches also examined common aliases through angular quadrature and truncated trigonometric moments. No equivalent priority result was identified in inspected material; unindexed classical circular-quadrature theory remains a genuine residual risk.
