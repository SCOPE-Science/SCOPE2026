A random implicit-midpoint step on a harmonic oscillator can erase both quadratic and quartic angular anisotropy in one update only if its mean duration is at least \(8/(3\sqrt3\,\omega)\). The unique sharp law chooses \(0\), \(2/(\sqrt3\,\omega)\), and \(2\sqrt3/\omega\) equally; it also attains the sharp necessary maximum-step budget \(2\sqrt3/\omega\).
