The Petersen graph needs at least three deleted edges before its radius-two, recomputed-graph-metric Vietoris–Rips complex can acquire nontrivial topology. Among all connected three-edge faults, exactly 60 produce \(S^2\), five produce \(\bigvee^4S^2\), and 380 remain contractible; exhaustive symmetry-orbit and acyclic-matching certificates establish these integral homotopy types.
