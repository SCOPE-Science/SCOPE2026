For three critically sampled Fourier nodes, positive reweighting reaches the sharp operator-norm defect floor exactly for motion ratios \(u\in[(9-\sqrt{21})/18,(9+\sqrt{21})/18]\). At symmetric motion, operator norm and spectral half-spread have different sharp improving weights pointing in opposite directions, while the uniform rule remains optimal in Frobenius norm; no two-node Fourier grid can exhibit such a spectral gain.
