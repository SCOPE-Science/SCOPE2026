On every labelled wheel with at least nine rim vertices, every minimum singleton two-rainbow dominating function colors the hub, and the exact number of optimal named-color assignments is \(6\), \(n(n+11)/3\), or \(2n\) as the rim length \(n\) is congruent to \(0\), \(1\), or \(2\) modulo three. The quadratic branch comes from a unique possible same-color exception on the rim, absent in the other two residue classes.
