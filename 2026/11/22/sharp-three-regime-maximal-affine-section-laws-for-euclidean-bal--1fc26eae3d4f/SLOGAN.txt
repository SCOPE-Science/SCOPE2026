For motion-invariant affine hyperplanes through a solid Euclidean ball cap in any dimension \(d\ge3\), near-maximal section volumes exhibit sharp probabilities of orders \(\varepsilon^{1/2}\), \(\varepsilon^{d/2}\), and \(\varepsilon^d\) on the three sides of the hemispherical cutoff. An exact piecewise-algebraic critical crossover at cutoff height \(h=\lambda\sqrt\varepsilon\) also gives the conditional distribution of the nearly maximizing plane's scaled inclination and offset.
