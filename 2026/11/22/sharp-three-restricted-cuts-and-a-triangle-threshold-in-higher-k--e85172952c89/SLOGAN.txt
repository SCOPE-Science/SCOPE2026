For all \(k\ge3\), the three-restricted edge connectivity of \(\mathrm{KG}(n,k)\) is exactly \(3\binom{n-k}{k}-4\) at \(n=3k-1\), but \(3\binom{n-k}{k}-6\) thereafter. Every optimal failure isolates exactly a three-vertex path at the threshold or a triangle beyond it, and all optimal edge sets are counted explicitly.
