For free \(q\)-state pair structures, the first host of one-point-extension rank at least three has exactly \(q^2+q+1\) vertices, and the sharp minimum number of nonzero unordered pairs is \(q^3-\binom q2-1\). At that simultaneous minimum the designated root-and-partner extremizers have exactly \((q-1)^{\binom q2}(q!/2)^{q(q-1)}\) isomorphism types; in particular oriented graphs first reach rank three on 13 vertices with 23 arcs and 5832 certified extremal types.
