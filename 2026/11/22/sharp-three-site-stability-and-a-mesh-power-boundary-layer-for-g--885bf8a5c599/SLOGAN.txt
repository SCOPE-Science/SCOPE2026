For the three-site degree-one generalized Floater–Hormann interpolant, equal gaps uniquely minimize the exact Lebesgue amplification, with \(\gamma(\Lambda_{\min}-1)\to W(e^{-1})\). A sharp mesh–power boundary layer \(W(e^{4|\beta|-1})\) shows why a middle-node displacement of order \(1/\gamma\) changes the leading stability constant, while any fixed nonzero displacement prevents the amplification from approaching one.
