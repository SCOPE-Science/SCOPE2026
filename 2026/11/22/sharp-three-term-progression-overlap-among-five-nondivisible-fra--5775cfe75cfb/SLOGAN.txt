Among indices where the Franel number is not divisible by five, the largest normalized frequency of three-term arithmetic progressions \((n,n+h,n+2h)\) with fixed positive step is exactly \(1/3\). Equality occurs precisely when the step has one nonzero base-five digit, \(h=a5^j\) for \(a\in\{1,2,3,4\}\).
