# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The joint density is obtained with the affine Haar Jacobian and a direct Gamma integral, and its normalization is checked analytically with two beta integrals. The independent beta-prime and Student-type representation yields all nonnegative fractional moments and the sharp divergence at the critical exponent. A dominated-convergence rescaling yields the exact tail constant and total-variation scale law. The actual standalone checker passed 39 finite quadrature and asymptotic diagnostics. The analytic proof, not those tests, supports the continuum and infinite limits.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge)

The analytic Cauchy wavelet and exact kernel in Abreu–Mouayn–Voigtlaender (arXiv:2201.11705, Section 2.1) are known and are not claimed new. The closest publicly indexed Cauchy-wavelet records concern localization band spectra, not those moment laws. ## Mathematical value — PASS

The normalized self-ambiguity governs blur in analytic wavelet time-scale analysis. The theorem exactly determines which moment-based time localization penalties make sense for every real Cauchy-wavelet order, quantifies rare large displacements and explains their scale dependence through a nondegenerate limit distribution. These precise endpoints and constants give a reproducible structural localization invariant beyond the previously recorded pointwise kernel.
