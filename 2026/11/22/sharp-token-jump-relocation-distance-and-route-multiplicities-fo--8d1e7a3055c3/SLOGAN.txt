For minimum terminal separators in a three-row rectangular grid, moving all three cut vertices from the leftmost to rightmost column requires exactly \(\lceil3L/2\rceil+1\) token jumps when the horizontal displacement is \(L\ge1\). The number of shortest routes has a sharp parity law: \(2^{L/2}\) for positive even \(L\), but \(2^{(L+3)/2}\) for odd \(L\ge3\), with six routes at \(L=1\).
