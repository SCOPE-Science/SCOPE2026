The full text was checked for the exact total-defect extremum and involutive equality classification; neither is reported. Burrull–Li–Zeng–Zhang study an asymmetry polynomial and bounds restricted to type-\(A\)/\(B\) minimal-KL strata, not this all-element type-\(F_4\) optimization. The accessible mathematical-record searches found no covering claim, but absence of results does not prove publication novelty. Potentially unindexed precomputed tables remain a residual risk.
