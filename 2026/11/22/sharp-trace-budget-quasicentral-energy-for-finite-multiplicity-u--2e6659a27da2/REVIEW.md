# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
Gauge and channel-symmetry averaging reduce the unrestricted operator minimization to a trace-constrained scalar Dirichlet problem. The explicit profile satisfies all inequalities; an exact energy-defect decomposition proves the lower bound for every summable competitor, not just truncated samples. Uniqueness follows because compact operators commuting with the pure shift vanish. An exact-rational checker passed 263,215 supplementary assertions but is not treated as infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)
Liu and Zhao (arXiv:2611.15604v1) prove qualitative Hilbert--Schmidt quasicentral vanishing. Voiculescu (arXiv:2109.07633v7) treats condenser problems with two fixed boundary projections and general Euler equations. The inspected primary results do not state the finite trace-budget optimum, quadratic cutoff and transitions; public indexed half-line Dirichlet point-evaluation results use a different constraint. An older general mass-constrained capacity formula could subsume the theorem; that risk is not dismissed by negative searches.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Trace mass is a natural quantitative cost of finite-rank quasicentral approximation. All-budget exact energy, unique unrestricted optimizer, cutoff-size phase changes and the sharp constant \(4/9\) are structural quantitative data, including a strict \(8/9\) improvement against a linear taper.

## Limitations
Only positive finite-rank contractions fixing the wandering subspace of finite-multiplicity unweighted unilateral shifts, with a Hilbert--Schmidt commutator, are covered. Other ideals and general almost normal operators are not claimed. Independent validation has not occurred.
