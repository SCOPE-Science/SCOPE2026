For real-diagonalizable deterministic closed-loop dynamics, the number of independently initialized trajectories needed to identify an unknown symmetric quadratic value matrix from noiseless measurements is exactly the largest multiplicity among the pairwise eigenvalue products, despite the rank-one restriction on every measurement. Explicit modal initializations attain the bound; for stable geometric spectra a single trajectory identifies only \(2n-1\) of the \(n(n+1)/2\) coefficients and \(\lceil n/2\rceil\) trajectories are necessary and sufficient when each has at least \(2n-1\) time samples.
