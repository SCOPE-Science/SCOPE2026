For \(r\le d\), the separate GCDs of \(n\) with the \(r\) shifted integer parts \(\lfloor\alpha(n+h_j)^d\rfloor\) have a complete universal joint Euler-product law, despite their shared-number dependence. The cutoff is sharp: at \(d+1\) consecutive translates, a bounded finite-difference obstruction destroys the universal law for some irrational \(\alpha\).
