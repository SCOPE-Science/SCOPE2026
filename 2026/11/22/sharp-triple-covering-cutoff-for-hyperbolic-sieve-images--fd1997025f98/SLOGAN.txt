Three distinct hyperbolic sieve images \(\{ax+N/x:x\ne0\}\) cover an odd prime field exactly at 16 specified primes, with the last possibility at \(p=73\). At that prime, every covering triple of normalized coefficients is \(\{s,8s,64s\}\) for a nonzero square \(s\), giving exactly 12 triples; a genus-two Weil bound and exhaustive small-prime verification prove sharpness.
