For permutation complexes on four letters, **twelve** full permutation facets are necessary and sufficient for integral two-primary torsion, and exactly **52** labeled sets attain the threshold in **five** relabeling/reversal orbits. Every minimizer has integral homotopy type \(\Sigma\mathbb{RP}^2\vee\bigvee^3 S^2\), refining the known twelve-facet alternating-group example with a sharp lower bound and a complete minimum-set classification.
