# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For any fixed nonempty binary block set P of positive block length b, the sequential adjacent-swap balls of radius two intersect for distinct codewords exactly at graph distance at most four, by reversal and a midpoint of a shortest path. Unequal weights never collide. For equal weight, each swap changes only one prefix imbalance at its cut by one. The ordered ones can be moved to their ordered destinations with no unnecessary crossings, attaining the lower bound ∑|Δ_j|. Equivalently their ordered position differences sum to this cut cost: at any cut all mismatched ordered ones cross in the same direction; left-moving ones can be placed in order and right-moving ones in reverse order using only zero crossings. In a minimum failing power, assuming P² is safe, m≥3 by padding. A zero interior block-boundary imbalance splits off a smaller failing equal-weight pair; equal nonzero boundary levels let the intervening equal-weight blocks be removed, preserving every retained cut imbalance and a differing prefix. Hence all interior levels are distinct and nonzero. If b≥2 and some boundary level has magnitude at least two, its two adjacent cuts are not block boundaries and contribute at least one each; another internal boundary contributes at least one, costing at least five. The only remaining minimum collision has three blocks and opposite signs h1,h2=±1. The two words x¹z³ and z¹x³ in P² differ and have weight difference h1+h2=0. Prefix imbalances are the original first-block imbalances and the negatives of the original third-block interior imbalances, retaining the first splice cut only once. Thus the splice cannot increase the distance and gives the required contradiction. At b=1 only the singleton codes and {0,1} occur; the latter is safe at power one but 01 and 10 collide at power two. Independent finite checks used literal received-word balls generated by zero, one and two swaps rather than the prefix metric: safe set counts at b=1,2,3,4 were 2,4,17,91, with all 342 later-power cases (m=3–5) safe. The recorded 66,196 ordered equal-weight pairs through length nine also agree with the sum of the central binomial coefficients. These finite checks are supplements, not the all-length justification.
