For binary sources forbidding `11`, the complete two-adjacent-deletion diversity spectrum is a three-state rational series. Its maximum ball size is \(1+\lfloor2(n-1)/3\rfloor\), with exactly \(2\), \(1\), or \(\lfloor n/3\rfloor+3\) maximizers according as \(n\equiv0,1,2\pmod3\); the typical diversity grows at rate \(3/\sqrt5-1\).
