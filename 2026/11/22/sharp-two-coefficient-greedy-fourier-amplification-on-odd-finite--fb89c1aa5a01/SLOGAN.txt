On any finite abelian group of odd order, retaining the two largest Fourier coefficients amplifies the supremum norm by at most \(2/[p\sin(\pi/(2p))]\), where \(p\) is the smallest prime divisor of the group order, and the bound is attained. Every extremizer is a translated, modulated scalar multiple of the phase of \(1+\psi\) for a character \(\psi\) of order \(p\).
