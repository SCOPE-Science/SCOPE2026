At two-contact excitation, every state of the Grötzsch and Chvátal graphs dies out within five and six updates, respectively, and both cutoffs are sharp, with exact initial-state extinction histograms. Both graphs have persistent three-cycles at one-contact activation, while the triangle-free four-regular comparison graph \(K_{4,4}\) still oscillates at two contacts, showing that the collapse is not simply a consequence of regularity or triangle-freeness.
