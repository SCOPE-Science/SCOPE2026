Two interleaved geometric families of normalized Laplace exponentials form a Riesz sequence with exact sharp two-by-two Toeplitz-symbol bounds. Their optimal lower bound collapses quadratically as the logarithmic offset vanishes, with a collision coefficient that decays like \(16\pi^3h^{-3}e^{-2\pi^2/h}\) at dense logarithmic sampling and approaches \(1/8\) at sparse sampling.
