# Scientific assessment of sharp binary two-deletion dinucleotide-profile capacity

## Correctness — PASS
The all-length bound is reduced to a finite exhaustive *local-context* classification, not a census of bounded source lengths. An internal deletion changes its four-entry histogram by one of four vectors; an adjacent pair of deletions contributes one of six interior types, with two explicitly listed boundary corrections. Their boundary-conditioned envelopes have maxima at most seventeen, as certified by the displayed complete sixteen-entry table and independently reconstructed in `verify_local.py`. Seventeen explicit local-change witnesses survive insertion of arbitrarily many zeroes in a stated word family, so the bound is attained at every source length at least thirteen. The smaller lengths are completely enumerated by `direct_histogram.cpp`, including every binary source word and every two-deletion pair. The limiting law is a finite sixty-four-case boundary calculation coupled with an exponentially high-probability interior saturation argument. Both verification programs were run from their packaged paths and matched their recorded output. This is self-review; no external audit or formal proof certification was performed.

## Originality — PASS against inspected evidence
The most relevant fully inspected published sources concern **one-deletion dinucleotide-profile** distributions, **one-insertion dinucleotide-profile** distributions, or the cardinality of entire **word** descendant sets after two deletions. None states or implies the worst-case size or limiting law for the distinct dinucleotide **profiles** of a fixed word under exactly **two deletions**. The older DNA profile coding paper establishes the measurement model and coding goals, not the asserted local two-deletion classification in its accessible description. The recent September 2026 substring-count hashing preprint presents asymptotic code construction results in the accessible abstract and motivates study of local difference vectors, but its inaccessible full text leaves a genuine priority risk; this is not represented as a verified full-text noncoverage claim. published-finding corpus title/summary similarity searches and word-level implication comparisons found close but different objects. An unindexed or differently termed exact histogram result cannot be excluded.

## Value — PASS
This is a channel-wide, not a parameter-arbitrary, exact result: two source-symbol deletions collapse potentially quadratically many descendants into a universally bounded set of adjacent-pair observations. The proof isolates the structural reason for the bound—the four interior change types, six adjacent change types and incompatible boundary signatures—and determines the **first** length at which the sharp capacity is feasible. The full asymptotic six-atom law quantifies how often the limit is observed under a natural independent source, rather than relying on sparse experimental cases. The numerical result is relevant to designing and assessing substring-count-based low-resolution reconstruction and deletion-detection schemes; it does **not** establish a stronger code rate or deletion-correction capability.
