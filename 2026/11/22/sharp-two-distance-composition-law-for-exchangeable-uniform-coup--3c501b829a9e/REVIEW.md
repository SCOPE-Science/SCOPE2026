# Review of sharp two-distance composition law

## Correctness — PASS

The result uses a precise disintegration of Lebesgue-uniform marginals over residues modulo the permitted jump length. With exact two-distance support, the resulting conditional matrices are symmetric, have unit row sums, and are supported on edges of length one or two in a finite path square. The lower finite optimization is established by ordinary even/odd-index degree imbalance; the upper by index-class imbalance modulo four. Explicit two-, three-, four- and five-vertex gadgets, followed by four-block pairings, attain all finite bounds. Integrating the two possible fiber lengths yields the full interval; convexity supplies intermediate values. The graph-supported deterministic subclass is treated separately via symmetry-implied involutivity and odd-fiber parity. The standard-library checker passed 2516 exact rational tests of witnesses, sharp objectives, and integrated formulas. The proof, not the finite checks, covers all real jump lengths in the claimed domain.

## Originality — PASS against inspected evidence

The closest contemporary full text is Trutschnig and Úbeda-Flores, arXiv:2610.33646v1 (2026-10-24), especially Example 3.1 and Section 4, which studies the support of copulas on off-diagonal chains and restrictions on associative and Schur-concave subclasses. It does not give a sharp compositional interval for two fixed jump lengths. Côté and Wang, arXiv:2611.25237v1 (2026-11-18; later supporting source), Sections 3–4 and 6.1, provide general upper-capacity and fractional-matching connections but no corresponding specific two-distance exchangeable probability interval. The classical fractional matching theory of Scheinerman and Ullman explains half-integral odd-cycle witnesses; the new result is the integrated, exactly attained parity/mod-four dependence for uniform exchangeable couplings. A related earlier exact-one-distance formula already covers the deterministic success envelope, which is not claimed as new. Searches for aliases, exact parameter expressions, stronger implications and exact databases did not find an equivalent published specialized theorem. An unlocated weighted matching or singular-copula specialization remains a residual risk; failed searching is not proof of historical novelty.

## Value — PASS

The object is a natural dependence-constrained probability model: two uniform observables exchangeable under reversal, with their separation restricted to two prescribed scales. The sharp interval determines how much probability can be allocated to the longer scale, rather than merely whether a two-distance coupling exists. Its lower endpoint is controlled by odd-fiber constraints, the upper by a separate four-residue invariant; their different phase laws distinguish constraints that cannot be extracted from the one-distance capacity alone. It gives explicit measurable realizations and a sharp boundary between deterministic and genuinely randomized reversible dependence. This is a complete classification for a natural family, not an arbitrary finite slice or an unsupported downstream claim.

## Scope and residual risks

Equal uniform marginals; exactly the separations \(d\) and \(2d\); exchangeability; \(0<d\le1/2\). No uniqueness, multi-scale extension, or absolutely continuous optimizer is asserted. Broad mathematical frameworks cover the finite relaxation but not the inspected special arithmetic formulas. Independent external mathematical validation remains outstanding.
