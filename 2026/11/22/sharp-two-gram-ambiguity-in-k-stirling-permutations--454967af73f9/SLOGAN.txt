For \(k\)-Stirling permutations observed through all oriented adjacent-pair multiplicities, the entire inverse-fiber size is recovered by repeatedly removing the largest homogeneous block; the sharp worst-case ambiguity is \((k-1)^{n-1}\), with a unique maximizing profile when \(k\ge3\) and \(n\ge2\). For \(k\ge3\), uniquely reconstructible sources are exactly the \(n!\) permutations of homogeneous blocks, so their uniform-source probability is \(n!/\prod_{j=1}^{n-1}(kj+1)\).
