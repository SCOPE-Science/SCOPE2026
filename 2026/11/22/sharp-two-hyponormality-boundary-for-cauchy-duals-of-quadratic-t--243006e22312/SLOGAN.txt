For the Cauchy dual of a unilateral shift whose moment polynomial is \(((n+a)^2+b^2)/(a^2+b^2)\), two-hyponormality is equivalent to one exact cubic inequality \(F((a+2)^2,b^2)\ge0\), with a unique sharp positive boundary and a distinct, larger hyponormality boundary. Every positive \(b\) in this sharp two-hyponormal region nevertheless gives a non-subnormal Cauchy dual of a strict three-isometry, and the critical ratio \(b^2/a^2\) tends to an explicit cubic root.
