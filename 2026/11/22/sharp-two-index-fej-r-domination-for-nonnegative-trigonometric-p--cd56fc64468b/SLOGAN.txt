For any independent Fejér order \(m\ge1\) and nonnegative trigonometric polynomial degree \(n=qm+r\), the exact sharp pointwise smoothing loss is \(C_{m,n}=(q+1)(q+2)m/((q+2)m-r)\). A unique explicit quotient–remainder Fejér–Riesz factor attains equality at a prescribed point, and all its zeros lie on the unit circle.
