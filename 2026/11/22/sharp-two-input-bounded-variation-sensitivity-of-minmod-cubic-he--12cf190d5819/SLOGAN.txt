Minmod-limited cubic Hermite interpolation preserves the variation of every input for all slope scales \(0\le\lambda\le3\), yet the exact attained two-input variation amplification is \(1+\frac{8\lambda}{27}\min\{2,n-2\}\). The sharp unit-scale factor is \(43/27\) from four knots onward; outside that slope-scale interval even one-input variation preservation fails.
