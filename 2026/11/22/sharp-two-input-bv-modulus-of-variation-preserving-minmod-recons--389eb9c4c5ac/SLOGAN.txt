Cellwise-affine minmod reconstruction preserves each real input's total variation exactly, yet its sharp two-input variation amplification is \(1\) for two cells, \(1+\lambda\) for three, and \(1+2\lambda\) for four or more, for every \(0\le\lambda\le1\). The sharp full-slope factor is three, attained by a single input jump that alters two neighboring limited slopes.
