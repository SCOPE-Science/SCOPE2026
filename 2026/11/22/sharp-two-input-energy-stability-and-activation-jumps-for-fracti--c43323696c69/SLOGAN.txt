The centered fractional maximal operator on every complete graph has an exact sharp two-input modulus from vertex \(\ell^2\) perturbations to quadratic edge variation, determined by feasible activation counts and two explicit spectral modes. As the fractional mass coefficient crosses \(c=1/2\), its squared modulus drops discontinuously in every dimension \(n\ge3\) except \(n=5\), despite continuity of each maximal output.
