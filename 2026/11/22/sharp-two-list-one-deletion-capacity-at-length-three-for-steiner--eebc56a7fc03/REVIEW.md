# Scientific review

**Correctness (C): PASS.** The all-alphabet incidence bound \(3|C|\le2q^2+3q\) is proved by separating one-, two- and three-output deletion shadows. A two-reversal-pair construction on Steiner triples saturates off-diagonal pair capacity, and the replacement \(abc\mapsto\{aab,bbc,acc\}\) uses spare diagonal capacity only on disjoint triples. Published Steiner existence results supply the infinite families, and a separate 39-codeword certificate settles the exceptional order seven. Executable checks cover explicit orders three, seven, nine and thirteen, including all outputs and code distinctness. They do not purport to verify the infinite design-existence theorem.

**Originality (O): PASS, with priority limits.** Chee–Ge–Ling (2010) classifies perfect uniquely deletion-correcting codes rather than the two-list packing optimum; the main 2026 list-decoding preprint investigates principally binary asymptotic length and is not a direct exact alphabet-size result. The factor-critical Steiner theorem provides a construction premise, not the deletion-capacity statement. The searched coding and design records did not identify an equivalent or dominating claim. The Lin full text was inaccessible here, and an unindexed equivalent finite-length corollary remains possible.

**Value (V): PASS.** The result pins down an exact all-alphabet upper bound and proves its attainment on two infinite natural residue classes, using a sharp structural connection with Steiner matchings. This is substantially beyond a one-parameter finite census or a routine decoding check: it determines extremal cardinalities at arbitrarily large alphabet sizes.

**Limitations.** Sharpness for other alphabet residues and lengths is not asserted. No independent expert or machine-certified audit was performed.

**Closest literature.** Lin, arXiv:2609.26650 (2026); Bryant–Davies–Neubecker, DOI 10.1002/jcd.70038 (2026); Chee–Ge–Ling, arXiv:1008.1343 (2010); classical Kirkman existence.
