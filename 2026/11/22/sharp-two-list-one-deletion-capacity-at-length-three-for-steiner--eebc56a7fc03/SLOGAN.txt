For every alphabet size \(q\ge3\) with \(q\equiv1\) or \(3\pmod6\), the maximum size of a length-three code decoding one deletion to at most two candidates is exactly \(q+\lfloor2q^2/3\rfloor\). The sharp bound follows from a diagonal-shadow incidence inequality and is attained by a Steiner-triple-system construction, with an explicit seven-symbol exception.
