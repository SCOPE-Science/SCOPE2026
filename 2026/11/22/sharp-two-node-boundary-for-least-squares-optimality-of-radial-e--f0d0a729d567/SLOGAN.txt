For every positive-weight nodal quadrature with at least three points, an everywhere-strictly-convex scalar entropy admits globally least-squares-optimal mean-preserving radial limiting for all means and entropy budgets if and only if the entropy is quadratic; with two points, radial limiting is always least-squares-optimal. A three-point Simpson-rule quartic-entropy example gives an exact, strictly closer conservative and entropy-feasible positive correction.
