For the Tsallis \(\alpha\)-entmax map, \(1<\alpha\le2\), the sharp global score-\(\ell_\infty\)-to-probability-\(\ell_1\) sensitivity is \(4(m\ell)^{\alpha-1}/(m^\gamma+\ell^\gamma)^{3-\alpha}\), with \(m=\lfloor n/2\rfloor\), \(\ell=\lceil n/2\rceil\), and \(\gamma=(\alpha-1)/(3-\alpha)\). Exact parity rigidity makes the coefficient \(n^{\alpha-1}\) in even dimensions and strictly smaller in odd dimensions; a two-block interior probability and balanced sign perturbation witness sharpness.
