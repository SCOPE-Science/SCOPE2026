For alternating rank-one modified-gradient steps in two dimensions, the exact two-phase Schur region is \(a+b-ab>0\) and \(4-2(a+b)+ab(2-s)>0\), where \(s\) is the squared sine of the angle between the update directions. At \(b=1\) and \(a=1/s\), the quadratic reaches the fixed point in four updates and every matching \(C^3\) nonlinear model has a locally quadratically convergent four-update map, even with \(a>2\).
