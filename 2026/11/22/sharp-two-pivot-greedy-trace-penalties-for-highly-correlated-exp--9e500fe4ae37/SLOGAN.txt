For highly correlated exponential Toeplitz kernels, even the best first-pivot tie break in two-step diagonal-greedy Cholesky loses a sharp factor \(5/4\) in residual trace against globally chosen two-column Nyström landmarks; the worst tie break loses \(5/3\). The determinant-maximizing endpoint pair itself realizes the latter sharp trace penalty, while the trace-optimal locations approach one-fifth and four-fifths of the sampling interval.
