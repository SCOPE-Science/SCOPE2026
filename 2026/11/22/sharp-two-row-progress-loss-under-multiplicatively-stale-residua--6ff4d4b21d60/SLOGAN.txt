For two-row Kaczmarz with stale residual-power *selection* but a fresh exact correction, the worst possible fraction of expected squared-error progress has a unique finite-exponent minimizer and approaches the sharp limit \(e^{-4\varepsilon}\) when both residual magnitudes are known within multiplicative factors \(e^{\pm\varepsilon}\). The bound is independent of row angle and relaxation and does not assert asynchronous trajectory stability.
