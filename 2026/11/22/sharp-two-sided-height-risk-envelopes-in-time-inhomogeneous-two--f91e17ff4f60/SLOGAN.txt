For two-predecessor growing trees with arbitrary independent time-varying attachment biases, the complete set of attainable height laws is exactly a fixed shift of all sums of \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\) independent Bernoulli variables. At every prescribed mean height, an adjacent-two-point law and a shifted binomial law are the simultaneously attainable lower and upper convex-order envelopes, giving sharp finite-size bounds for every convex height risk.
