The explicit \(\ell_2\)-negative-tail extension of the unilateral \(\ell_p\) shift has exact one-sided power amplification \((n+1)^{|1/p-1/2|}\), while its opposite powers have norm one. Despite having spectrum exactly \(\mathbb T\), this particular invertible extension cannot be made isometric by any equivalent norm unless \(p=2\); a different isometric bilateral extension still exists.
