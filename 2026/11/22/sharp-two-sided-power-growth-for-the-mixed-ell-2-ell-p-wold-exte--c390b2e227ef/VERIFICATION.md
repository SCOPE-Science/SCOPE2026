The formulas were checked by a direct two-tail decomposition for every integer power \(n\ge1\). For the increasing direction, the upper bound is the sharp identity-embedding norm between scalar \(\ell_p^{n+1}\) and \(\ell_2^{n+1}\), with an explicit \(n+1\)-coordinate equality vector. The opposite direction is contractive. Spectral and renorming conclusions follow from the power bounds and rotation covariance. Finite-dimensional numerical optimization was also compared to the exact formula for \(p\in\{1.25,1.5,2,3,4,7\}\) and \(n\in\{1,2,3\}\), but it is supplemental, not proof. Independent audit has not been performed.
