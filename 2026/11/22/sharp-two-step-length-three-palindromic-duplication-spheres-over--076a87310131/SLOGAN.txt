Two sequential length-three palindromic duplications have exact maximum sphere size \((n-2)(n+1)-\binom{\max\{n-4,0\}}2\) over **every fixed alphabet of at least three symbols**, at all source lengths \(n\ge3\). The periodic ternary word \(012012\cdots\) attains it, and its only history collisions are the commuting orders of disjoint original blocks.
