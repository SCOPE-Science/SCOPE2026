For unrelaxed residual-weighted Kaczmarz on identical scalar measurements with a minority of equal positive outliers, the unique worst-start two-step risk-minimizing downweight ratio is a root of an explicit cubic; downweighting strictly helps exactly when the outlier fraction is below one half. In the rare-outlier regime, its optimal weight is asymptotic to \((2p)^{1/3}\) and its squared-error risk to \(3\cdot2^{-2/3}p^{4/3}\), improving the uniform-sampling order-\(p\) risk.
