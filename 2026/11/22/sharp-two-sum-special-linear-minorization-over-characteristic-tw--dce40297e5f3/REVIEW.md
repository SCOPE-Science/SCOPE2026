# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The determinant polarization reduces every ordered two-sum fiber to a determinant-one trace section. Residue rank one has \(q(q-1)\) nonsingular-section solutions and \(q^2\) smooth lifts per level. Primitive invertible targets reduce to the separable quadratic characteristic-polynomial count, again with \(q^2\) lifts per level. For divisible targets, the rank-one valuation law and the \(q^2-1\) smooth rank-two residue solutions give strict lower bounds; zero has the full group cardinality. The precise target counts and mixing constant follow by finite-field enumeration and normalization. Four complete small-ring enumerations validate these deductions, but do not replace the proof for all lengths.

## Originality
PASS, with historical-priority limitations. The recent motivating article concerns sum-of-units and normalized-unit existence, with the matrix discussion over fields. The 2017 finite-field existence theorem and 2025 field representation-count study are expressly prior art. The closest publicly indexed odd-prime-power two-sum theorem has the same numerical mixing constant but assumes odd residue characteristic, so neither its field restriction nor its odd-characteristic lifting proves the characteristic-two nonreduced-ring theorem or its equality locus. Searches for characteristic-two chain-ring special-linear convolution and stronger local-ring results found no matching covering statement. The full text of the odd-prime-power record and some older finite-field formula details could not be inspected; this remains a residual overlap risk, not evidence of uniqueness.

## Mathematical value
PASS. An all-length optimal uniform minorization is a natural precise mixing invariant for random sums of special-linear matrices. The characteristic-two case is structurally different from odd characteristic because scalar trace-zero residues occur and the separability condition is governed by absolute trace; the proof identifies which residues attain the sharp floor and calculates their exact proportion, uniform in the nilpotent depth. The problem is motivated by the 2026 unit-sum and finite-ring Cayley-graph literature and sharpens its existence viewpoint, without claiming a new field-level two-sum result.

## Limits
The nonprimitive, full-rank-leading fibers are bounded sharply enough to exclude minima but not fully enumerated. Novelty is based on available accessible literature and indexed records. Independent validation is not claimed.
