For \(2\)-by-\(2\) determinant-one matrices over every finite characteristic-two commutative chain ring with residue field \(\mathbb F_q\) and length \(k\), the minimum number of ordered two-sum representations is exactly \(q^{2k-1}(q-1)\), with all equality targets characterized by residue rank and an absolute-trace condition. The sharp additive mixing floor is \(q^3/((q-1)(q+1)^2)\), independent of ring length, and the number of minimizers is explicit.
