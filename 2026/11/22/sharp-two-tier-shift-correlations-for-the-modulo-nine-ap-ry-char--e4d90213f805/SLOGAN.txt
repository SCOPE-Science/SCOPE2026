For every generalized Apéry family with parameters \(r\equiv2\pmod3\) and \(s\equiv2\pmod6\), the modulo-nine sixth-root character has positive rational correlations at every positive shift. The sharp maximum \(1/2\) occurs exactly at one-nonzero-digit ternary shifts, and the sharp next level \(1/3\) exactly at two adjacent nonzero digits; every correlation has an explicit two-state carry product.
