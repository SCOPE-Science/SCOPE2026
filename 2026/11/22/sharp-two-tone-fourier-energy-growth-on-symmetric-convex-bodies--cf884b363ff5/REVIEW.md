# Review of sharp two-tone Fourier energy growth

## Correctness — PASS
The proof treats every origin-symmetric convex body with interior in dimensions at least two, all positive concentric dilation factors and all complex two-tone coefficients. Explicit Gram diagonalization and the positive scalar monotonicity derivative are exact, while Brunn–Minkowski supplies the even unimodal projection needed for the mixture argument. Sharpness follows from a frequency collision, and the directional defect follows from fourth-order characteristic-function expansion. The standalone checker passed 18,308 supplementary cases. Finite sampling is not a substitute for this analytic proof.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected evidence
Ruizhe Zhang (arXiv:2607.10501, 2026-07-11) proves general sparse-Fourier exterior bounds but not this all-convex-body sharp constant and shape-moment gap law. The published *Tight exterior-energy resonances and parity switching for two-frequency Fourier sums* already determines one-dimensional symmetric exterior transfer spectra; **that one-dimensional fixed-gap formula is acknowledged and is not treated as new.** The published *Sharp two-frequency exterior Fourier leverage and the quadratic collision defect* treats point evaluation, not integrated multi-dimensional convex-window energies. The available result catalog and the accessible local claims were checked for direct or dominating results; no matching higher-dimensional shape-independent theorem was identified. An older implicit convex-geometric corollary remains possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A universal exact sharp energy-doubling exponent for all symmetric convex windows is a natural Fourier extrapolation invariant in dimensions at least two. The directional fourth-to-second moment coefficient additionally gives quantitative information on the loss of optimal growth away from frequency collision. Unlike an arbitrary special-case numerical constant, these statements quantify all shapes, all dilations and all two-frequency directions without separation assumptions.

## Limitations and closest literature
Only two real-frequency plane-wave atoms and centered symmetric convex observation bodies are covered. The upper constant is a supremum not attained by any nonzero two-frequency signal with fixed gap. No three-tone result is claimed; no noncentered nonsymmetric-window result is claimed. The classical one-dimensional interval fixed-gap spectrum is covered by the cited published exterior-energy work, and the new contribution is specifically multidimensional geometric universality and its exact kurtosis-controlled gap defect.
