For every positive even number \(k\) of designated-partition components, the greatest common power of two dividing all \(A_k(4n+3)\) is determined by the first two terms \(A_k(3)\) and \(A_k(7)\). Its exact exponent is \(\nu_2(k)\) when \(4\mid k\), otherwise \(2\) for \(k\equiv6\pmod8\) or \(3\) for \(k\equiv2\pmod8\), thereby settling the even-parameter half of the published sharpness conjecture.
