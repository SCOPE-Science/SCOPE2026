For the ordinary least-squares projector onto two distinct real decays \(e^{-at}\) and \(e^{-bt}\) on the half-line, the exact \(L^\infty\) gain is \(4(R+1)R^{-1-1/(R-1)}\), where \(R=b/a>1\), and the unique worst output time is zero. The gain increases strictly from the coalescing-rate limit \(8/e\) toward the universal sharp envelope \(4\), even though the coefficient Gram matrix becomes ill-conditioned near both extremes.
