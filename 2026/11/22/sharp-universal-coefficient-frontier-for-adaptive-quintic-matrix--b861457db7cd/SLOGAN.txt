The adaptive quintic matrix-sign and polar step has an exact sharp coefficient cap: every coefficient sequence in \([0,U]\) converges from normalized full-rank input when \(U<a_*\approx1.47222926097203\), where \(a_*\) is the large positive root of \(128a^3+496a^2-1026a+27=0\). At the boundary a scalar input lands at a nonunit fixed point, and above it a constant-coefficient scalar orbit can diverge; the result strengthens the original empirical \(29/20\) cap into a sharp uniform safety theorem.
