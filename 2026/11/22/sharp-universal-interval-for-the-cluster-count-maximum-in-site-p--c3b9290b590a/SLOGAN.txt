For independent site percolation on every connected cactus other than a single edge or triangle, the expected number of surviving components has exactly one interior maximum, occurring at the zero of an explicit cycle-length polynomial. All its maximizers lie in the sharp universal interval \((0.409279025863\ldots,3/4]\): the upper endpoint is attained by the three-vertex path, while bouquets of four-cycles asymptotically approach the lower endpoint.
