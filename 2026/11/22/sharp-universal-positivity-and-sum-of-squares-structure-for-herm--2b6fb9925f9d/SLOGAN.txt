The Hermite partial sums conjectured positive in scaled Gauss–Hermite Gaussian-kernel quadrature admit an explicit nonnegative sum-of-squares representation and are uniformly strictly positive for every degree and \(0\le\gamma\le1\). This parameter interval is sharp for positivity across all degrees, and it certifies positive Mercer-truncated approximate Gaussian-kernel quadrature weights without asserting positivity of the different exact weights.
