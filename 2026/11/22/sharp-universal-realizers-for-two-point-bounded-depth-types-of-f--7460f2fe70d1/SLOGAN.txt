Every two-point term-equality pattern of depth \(d\) occurring in a finite permutation appears simultaneously in one permutation of the sharp minimum size \(\frac{(d+1)(5d+2)}2\); the unique optimal cycle multiset has two cycles of each length through \(d\) and one of every length from \(d+1\) to \(2d+1\). The number of distinct patterns is exactly \(2d^2+5d+2\).
