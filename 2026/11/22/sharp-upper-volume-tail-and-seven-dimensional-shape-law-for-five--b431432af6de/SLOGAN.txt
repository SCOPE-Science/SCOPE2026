The convex hull of five independent uniform points on the unit sphere lies within relative volume deficit \(\varepsilon\) of the sharp triangular-bipyramid maximum with probability asymptotic to \((24/7)\varepsilon^{7/2}\). Conditioned on this rare event, the twenty labelled maximizing configurations have equal weights and the seven rotation-reduced, Hessian-normalized shape coordinates become uniform in a unit ball in total variation.
