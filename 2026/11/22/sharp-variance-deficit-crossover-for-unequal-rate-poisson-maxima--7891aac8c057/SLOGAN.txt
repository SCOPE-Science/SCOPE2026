For independent Poisson counts of rates \(at\) and \(bt\), the variance deficit of their maximum is strictly positive and, when \(a>b>0\), is asymptotic to \(a^{3/4}b^{1/4}\sqrt t\,e^{-t(\sqrt a-\sqrt b)^2}/[\sqrt\pi(\sqrt a-\sqrt b)]\). When the rates coincide, the same deficit is instead asymptotic to \(at/\pi\), a sharp singular change in how underdispersion persists at high intensity.
