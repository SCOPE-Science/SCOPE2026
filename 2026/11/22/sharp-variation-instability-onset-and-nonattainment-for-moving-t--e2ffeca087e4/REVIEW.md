# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Expand each length-three population standard deviation into \(\sqrt2/3\) times the quadratic norm \(F(a,b)=\sqrt{a^2+ab+b^2}\) of its adjacent first differences. The local two-column derivative has a sharp common-coordinate restriction: for a positive shared difference both partial derivatives belong to \((-1/2,1]\). The induced path-variation column formulas have strict finite upper bounds and the announced limiting sharp witnesses. Line-segment absolute continuity handles nondifferentiability; finite random tests are corroboration only.

**Originality — PASS (best-knowledge, not priority certification).** The full inspected Lee 1980 paper computes and uses local mean/variance for adaptive noise processing. Herbert et al. 2016 develop time-series windowed standard-deviation hardware and updates. Neither gives the sharp two-input \(BV\) coefficients, the five-sample onset, the six-sample saturation, or nonattainment. an indexed-research corpus comparisons to three-point moving medians, finite window extrema, and variation-ball projection involve distinct nonlinear operators and do not imply the claimed values. This is a bounded literature review, not a proof of universal absence.

**Value — PASS.** The statistic is an elementary, widely used local noise/contrast measurement, so the exact perturbation budget and the first finite-length amplification threshold are intrinsic properties rather than arbitrary slices. The sharp constants distinguish single-signal statistics from sensitivity to perturbations, and the nonattainment identifies the high-contrast limit responsible for worst cases. This provides a benchmark for any downstream method that consumes windowed standard-deviation profiles.

**Residual risks.** Wider-window variants, cyclic or padded signals, and external historical treatments remain unresolved. The finding does not establish improved denoising performance.
