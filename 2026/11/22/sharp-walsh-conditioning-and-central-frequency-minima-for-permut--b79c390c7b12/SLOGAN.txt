For uniformly random permutations, XOR convolution by the incidence profile of all vertices in two-cycles is positive definite at every order except two; at every order at least six its smallest Walsh eigenvalue occurs precisely at central character weight. Its Euclidean condition numbers have exact finite sums, are uniformly at most three (sharply at order three), and converge to \(e^{1/2}\), while the empirical normalized spectrum concentrates at \(e^{-1/2}\).
