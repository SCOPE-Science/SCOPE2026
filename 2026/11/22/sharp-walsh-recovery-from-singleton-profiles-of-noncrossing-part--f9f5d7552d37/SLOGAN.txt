For singleton-block incidence profiles of all noncrossing partitions of \([n]\), unnormalized Boolean convolution is invertible exactly at odd \(n\); at even \(n\) precisely the \(n\) Walsh characters of weight \(n-1\) vanish, with an exact sharp Catalan-valued nonzero spectral gap. Although the relative lost Fourier dimension is only \(n/2^n\), every \(\ell^1\) or \(\ell^\infty\) projection onto the recoverable range has norm at least \(1+\frac{n}{2^n}(\binom n{n/2}-2)\), attained by the Fourier projector and asymptotic to \(\sqrt{2n/\pi}\) along even \(n\).
