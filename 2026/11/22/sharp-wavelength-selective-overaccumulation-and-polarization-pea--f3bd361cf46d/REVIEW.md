# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

For each fixed positive flip rate, base speed, memory time and circle size, the speed is uniformly trapped in the admissible activity interval. Stationary measures exist by compactness. At zero landscape amplitude the invariant law of the scalar speed collapses to the base speed and the orientation-position telegraph process has the unique uniform stationary law. The exact Fourier generator identities therefore have a unique limit, yielding two nonsingular complex linear systems and the claimed rational response. Strict mode-by-mode contrast inequalities follow from a factorization, and the two optimizers follow from derivatives with exactly one positive zero each. The independent matrix resolvent, telegraph characteristic-function integral and symbolic identities agree across 72 parameter combinations. A finite simulation offers only corroboration, not proof.

Risk: the result is a limit as landscape amplitude tends to zero. No finite-amplitude extremality, all-order spectral ordering, ergodic convergence rate or strict uniqueness of finite-amplitude invariant laws is asserted.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

The motivating exponential-memory model and periodic landscapes are previously known. Rein–Kroy–Holubec mainly analyze current rectification and diffusivity. Holubec–Fischer–Volpe–Cichos discuss short and long delays in a different angular Brownian, principally fixed-delay setting. Töpfer–Bailey–Schreiber–Paratore–Isa already observe nonmonotonic over-localization; Garcés and collaborators study density scaling and diffusivity. Therefore qualitative finite-delay-enhanced localization is *not* a new claim. The proposed distinct statement is the exact susceptibility at every spatial frequency for exponentially adapting run-and-tumble speed, the sharp enhancement cutoff and the separate polarization-optimal clock. Published comparisons and semantic record searches did not identify a theorem implying all these mode-specific formulas.

Risk: supplemental material associated with the motivating paper and some older delayed-adaptation theory could not be exhaustively inspected. Unindexed equivalents and priority uncertainty remain possible, so the conclusion is limited to inspected evidence.

## Value — PASS

Mode-specific contrast and polarization identify two experimentally distinct measures of spatial organization. The exact inequality separates wavelengths strengthened by memory from wavelengths suppressed by memory, and the polarization optimum's independence of the orientation-flip rate provides a second calibration scale. This resolves a motivated quantitative gap between known qualitative over-localization and a complete perturbative response of the same physically used exponential-memory mechanism. The claim does not require a general theory of finite-amplitude transport to be meaningful.
