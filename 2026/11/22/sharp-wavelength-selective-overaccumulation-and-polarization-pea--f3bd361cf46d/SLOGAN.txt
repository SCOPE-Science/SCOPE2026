For an unbiased run-and-tumble swimmer with exponentially adapting speed, weak periodic motility landscapes have an exact Fourier density-response multiplier \((1+4\alpha\tau)/(1+2\alpha\tau+(kv\tau)^2)\). Each wavelength has a sharp delay interval of enhanced accumulation, whereas its induced polarization peaks at \(\tau=1/(|k|v)\), independently of the tumbling rate.
