In a deterministic trait-diffusion ecology model, the largest global competition coefficient compatible with a positive localized stationary population is asymptotic to \(\frac{3\sqrt{15}}{125\varepsilon\sqrt D}\) as the establishment cutoff \(\varepsilon\to0\). At this capacity boundary the effective mortality tends to \(3/5\), and the normalized pulse has an explicit cosine core joined to exponential tails.
