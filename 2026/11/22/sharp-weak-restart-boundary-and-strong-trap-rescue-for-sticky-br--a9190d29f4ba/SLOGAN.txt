For a sticky reflecting Brownian interval with an absorbing opposite endpoint, the sign of the first response to Poisson restarting is governed by exactly one critical launch position, which moves from \(L/\sqrt5\) toward the sticky wall with sharp asymptotic \(x_*/L\sim1/(6\eta)\). Every strictly interior reset site rescues the escape mean from arbitrarily strong trapping at fixed positive reset rate, but resetting exactly at the sticky wall always increases mean escape time.
