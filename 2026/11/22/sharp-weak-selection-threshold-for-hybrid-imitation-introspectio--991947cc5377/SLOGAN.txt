In a finite public-goods population mixing Fermi imitators and counterfactual introspectors, the stationary cooperation majority under weak selection has an exact threshold determined only by the two learning groups' aggregate contributions and neutral pair correlations. With no mutation, equal contributions and just one introspector among \(N\) individuals, the return threshold is \(N^3/(2N-1)\), which grows quadratically rather than linearly with population size.
