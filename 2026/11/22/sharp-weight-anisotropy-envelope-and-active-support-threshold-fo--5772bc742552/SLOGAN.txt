For proximal maps of the squared weighted \(\ell_1\) norm, an exact sorted-prefix law determines the global coordinatewise Lipschitz coefficient without enumerating supports. Among normalized positive penalty weights, the sharp worst-case amplification is \(1+\frac{\alpha}{1+\alpha}\frac{\sqrt n-1}{2}\), attained exactly by a one-exceptional-weight profile; uniform weights are worst-case optimal only in dimension four.
