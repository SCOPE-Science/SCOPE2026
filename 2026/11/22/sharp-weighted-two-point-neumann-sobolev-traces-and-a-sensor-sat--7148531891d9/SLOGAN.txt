For two positive point-evaluation weights on the mean-zero interval Sobolev space \(H^1(0,1)\), the design minimizing the joint trace norm is unique up to reflection and has a scalar evaluation Gram matrix below the weight ratio \(5-2\sqrt3\). At and above that threshold the heavier sensor sits at the midpoint and the lighter one at either of two fixed zero-correlation points, with squared sharp constant \(\alpha/12\).
