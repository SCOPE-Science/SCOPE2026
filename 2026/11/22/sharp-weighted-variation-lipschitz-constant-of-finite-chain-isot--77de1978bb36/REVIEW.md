# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The global coefficient follows from all active contiguous-block averaging derivatives, an exact step-function basis for the weighted variation quotient, and locally realizable partitions that attain every relevant chord. Continuity and segment integration globalize the finite derivative bound. The least-concave-majorant identity and endpoint conventions are proved, and the standalone exact-rational checker passes 8,680 supplementary comparisons; it is not treated as an all-size proof.

**Originality — PASS, with residual historical risk.** Yang–Barber (arXiv:1706.01852, Theorem 1) already characterizes nonexpansivity for equal observation masses via NUNA; the equal-mass *contraction decision* alone is not novel. The present claim is the sharp coefficient across all positive observation masses and edge seminorm weights, including cases with amplification above one and a complete chord/concavification formula. Chen–Li–Su (arXiv:2304.00244) treats generalized active-set algorithms, while the published cycle-cover rigidity result concerns a distinct order-cover geometry and does not imply the chain modulus. Exact semantic searches found no stronger statement. A previously unstated derivation from NUNA or older convex hull/isotonic sensitivity literature remains possible.

**Value — PASS.** This quantitatively resolves how edge-variation error can be amplified by a natural nonlinear estimator, not merely whether the estimator is contractive. The sharp factor identifies a computable optimal concavification of admissible edge weights and explicitly shows four-site amplification by a factor of four, with unbounded possible factors as an interior weight tends to zero. This supplies a meaningful robustness and weight-design criterion for heterogeneous observations.

The result does not extend to nonlinear losses or arbitrary posets, and no independent mathematical validation has occurred.
