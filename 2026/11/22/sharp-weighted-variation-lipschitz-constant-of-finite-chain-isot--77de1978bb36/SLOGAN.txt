For weighted least-squares isotonic regression on a finite chain, the exact two-input weighted-total-variation amplification is \(\max_{1\le i<n}\widehat w(Q_i)/w_i\), with \(\widehat w\) the least concave majorant of variation weights at cumulative observation masses. Contraction holds exactly for concave weight profiles, while nonconcavity can yield arbitrarily large, sharply attained amplification.
