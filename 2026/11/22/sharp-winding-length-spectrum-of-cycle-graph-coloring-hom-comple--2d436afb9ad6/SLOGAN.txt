For every nonisolated winding sector of \(\operatorname{Hom}(C_m,C_n)\) with \(n\ge5\), the shortest noncontractible loop of single-vertex recolorings has precisely \(mn/\gcd(n,2)\) edges, independently of the sector. More generally, the shortest loop representing the \(q\)-th primitive fundamental-group power has \(|q|mn/\gcd(n,2)\) edges; an exclusion-particle displacement invariant proves optimality and gives an explicit realizing loop.
