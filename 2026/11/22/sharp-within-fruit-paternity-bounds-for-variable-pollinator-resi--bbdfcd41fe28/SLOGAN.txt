When pollinators visit a random number of flowers per donor plant, an averaged geometric pollen sire profile understates same-father probability within individual single-probe fruits by an exact positive variance term. For fixed pollen carryover and mean sire profile, consecutive residence lengths uniquely minimize that probability and an explicit unattained upper bound is sharp; under geometric residence, the mean nearest-donor share and sibling-match probability uniquely recover both carryover and departure rates.
