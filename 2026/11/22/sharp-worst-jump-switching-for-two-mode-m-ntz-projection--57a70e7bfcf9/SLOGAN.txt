For sharp real \(BV\)-to-\(L^2\) approximation by \(\operatorname{span}\{1,x^q\}\), exactly one step location is worst when \(q\ne1\): it lies to the right of the midpoint for \(q<1\) and to the left for \(q>1\). At the linear exponent the two classical symmetric knots tie, producing a discontinuous optimizer switch and an exact cusp in the squared residual norm.
