The exact indices attaining the published bound \(
u_3(T(12\cdot3^m n))\ge m+2\) are independent of the level \(m\) and obey an explicit prime-exponent test. Their count up to \(X\) is asymptotic to \(C X/\sqrt{\log X}\), with the new explicit positive Euler-product constant \(C\approx0.31006165\).
