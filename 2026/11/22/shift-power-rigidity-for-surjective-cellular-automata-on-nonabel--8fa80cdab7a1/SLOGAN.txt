Every surjective one-dimensional group cellular automaton over any nonabelian group of order \(p^3\) has a unique automorphism-bearing input site and only central perturbations elsewhere. If that site has offset \(k\) and the automorphism has order \(t\), then the entire infinite-system map satisfies \(F^{pt}=\sigma^{kpt}\), so its entropy is exactly \(3|k|\log p\); the same structure gives an exact count of all reversible local rules.
