For the commuting graph of all square-zero transformations of the Catalan monoid on \(n\ge3\) points, the shifted adjacency matrix has rank exactly \(2n-5\), despite having \(2^{n-2}\) rows. Its positive, negative, and zero inertia is exactly \((n-2,n-3,2^{n-2}-2n+5)\), giving an exponentially large \(-1\)-eigenspace for the unshifted adjacency matrix.
