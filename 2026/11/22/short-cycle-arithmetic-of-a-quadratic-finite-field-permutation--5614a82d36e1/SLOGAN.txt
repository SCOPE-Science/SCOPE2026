The quadratic permutation \(T_\delta(x,y,z)=(x+zy,y+\delta zx,z)\) has no nontrivial cycle of length at most \(15\) except a sharply classified trio: length \(8\) occurs only over odd extensions of characteristic \(3\), length \(12\) only over odd extensions of characteristic \(5\), and length \(15\) only over odd extensions of characteristics \(11\) or \(19\), each with an exact cycle count. It has \(q^2+q-1\) fixed points, is always even, and its entire cycle spectrum admits an explicit polynomial-gcd formula.
