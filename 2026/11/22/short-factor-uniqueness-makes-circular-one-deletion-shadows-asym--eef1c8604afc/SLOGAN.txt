If every cyclic length-\(\ell\) factor of a word is unique for \(2\ell\le n\), its one-deletion output necklaces are in exact bijection with its constant-symbol runs. For a uniformly random \(q\)-ary source, the complete shadow-size law is within \(O(n^{-3})\) in total variation of an explicit cyclic-transition polynomial, giving a Gaussian limit and sharp leading mean and variance.
