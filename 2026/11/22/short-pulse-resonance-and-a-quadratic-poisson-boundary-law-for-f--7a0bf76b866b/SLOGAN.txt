For every stable fractional circle heat order \(1<\alpha<2\), the short-time sharp periodic \(BV\)-to-\(L^2\) smoothing error is governed by an interacting two-jump pulse rather than widely separated jumps. As \(\alpha\downarrow1\), the excess sharp squared-error coefficient is \((\pi/8)(\alpha-1)^2+o((\alpha-1)^2)\), and the optimal pulse width divided by \(t^{1/\alpha}\) grows like \(2/[\pi(\alpha-1)]\) when the small-time limit is taken first.
