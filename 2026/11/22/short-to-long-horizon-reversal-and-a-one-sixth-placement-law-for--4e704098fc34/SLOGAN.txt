Closing an unweighted path into a cycle is the unique best one-edge repair for sufficiently short-time heat-trace diffusion, but for every \(n\ge9\) a distinct interior shortcut wins at all sufficiently long times. Among eventual optimal shortcuts, the two free tails asymptotically occupy one-sixth of the path length each; this is a second-eigenmode refinement, not a re-claim of the previously known spectral-gap optimum.
