In the four exceptional cubic symmetric girth-six graphs—Heawood, Möbius–Kantor, Pappus and Desargues—the minimum lengths of closed dominating patrols are \(10,8,12,10\), with exactly \(546,6,225,12\) cyclic-reversal classes of optimal walks. Those walks form exactly ten graph-symmetry orbits, revealing a tree-only Heawood optimum, pendant excursions in Heawood and Pappus, and exclusively simple-cycle optima in Möbius–Kantor and Desargues.
