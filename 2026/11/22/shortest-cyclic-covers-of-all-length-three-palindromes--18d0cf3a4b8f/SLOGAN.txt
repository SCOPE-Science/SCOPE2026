For every alphabet of size \(q\ge2\), a shortest cyclic word containing all \(q^2\) ordinary length-three palindromes has length \(3\binom q2+2q\) for odd \(q\) and \(3\binom q2+5q/2\) for even \(q\). A parity obstruction at every vertex and an expanded Euler tour of a looped complete graph prove sharpness, with the even case requiring a perfect matching.
