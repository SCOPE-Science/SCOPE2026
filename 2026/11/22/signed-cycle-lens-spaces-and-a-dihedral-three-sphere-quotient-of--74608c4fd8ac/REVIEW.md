# Review of signed-cycle balanced Bier orbit spaces

## Correctness — PASS
The deleted-join/polytope facial equivalence is proved for every odd label count. Its equivariant radial homeomorphism and Fourier decomposition make the prime-order weighted lens model exact, and fixed-vector analysis gives the converse for odd composites. For five labels, the dihedral generators are checked as orientation-preserving orthogonal transformations with circle fixed sets. Armstrong's theorem gives trivial orbit fundamental group, every local quotient is three-dimensional Euclidean, and the Poincaré theorem yields a homeomorphic three-sphere. The separate finite cellular verifier passes and confirms the exact tenfold quotient Smith form and the three-tetrahedron orbit decomposition.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected sources
The 2003 Bier-sphere and 2021 Jevtić–Živaljević polytope results are explicitly prior: in particular the latter already provides the key convex realization. The new asserted conclusions concern free signed action exactly for prime odd label counts, the order-\(2p\) weighted lens quotients, and the lens-to-sphere transition under the five-label dihedral extension. The closest published analogues involve associahedral, cyclohedral, and nonmatching complexes and do not cover these objects or actions. Their exact published indexed statements were compared with the present hypotheses. Residual risks include an unindexed equivalent treatment and limited access to a relevant published GitHub body.

## Value — PASS
The claim is one coherent classification of a canonical symmetry of self-dual maximal-volume Bier spheres rather than a parameter-free renaming or a bare homology enumeration. It supplies the precise arithmetic freeness threshold and full geometric quotient topology, including a concrete one-vertex, three-tetrahedron lens presentation and its collapse to a three-sphere after adding a geometric reflection. This sharp contrast cannot be read from the nonequivariant sphere or facet counts alone.
