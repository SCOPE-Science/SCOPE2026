Balanced Bier spheres have a natural signed cyclic action that is free exactly at prime label counts, and every prime quotient is the explicit weighted lens space \(L(2p;p+2,p+4,\ldots,2p-1)\). In the first three-dimensional case the tenfold lens space \(L(10;7,9)\) has a three-tetrahedron quotient presentation, while adjoining one label reflection makes the ordinary quotient homeomorphic to \(S^3\).
