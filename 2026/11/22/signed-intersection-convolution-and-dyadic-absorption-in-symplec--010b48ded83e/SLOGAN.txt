Rank-uniform products in the symplectic rook monoid are exactly uniform signed permutations restricted to intersections of independent admissible subsets, giving explicit prescribed fibers for every product length. Products of maximal singular-rank factors have exactly \(\operatorname{Binomial}(n,2^{-(k-1)})\) rank and reach zero in a dyadically centered logarithmic window with a lattice-phase extreme-value law.
