# Scientific review of the signed-pairing first-homology theorem

**Correctness — PASS.** The previously established strong-two-monophonicity of Johnson graphs implies that the monophonic-position complex \(\mathcal P_n\) has exactly the clique-complex faces in dimensions at least two, with all pairs of vertices allowed. The known line-graph clique-complex homotopy equivalence ensures that its clique subcomplex \(C_n\) is connected with \(H_1(C_n;\mathbb Z)=0\). The relative simplicial chain complex of \((\mathcal P_n,C_n)\) is concentrated in dimension one, with one oriented basis edge per unordered pair of disjoint root edges. The natural long exact sequence identifies absolute \(H_1\) with this signed relative lattice, integrally and equivariantly. Its stabilizer is \((\mathfrak S_2\wr\mathfrak S_2)\times\mathfrak S_{n-4}\), with block exchange acting by \(-1\). The three-dimensional block module has character \(\#\operatorname{Fix}-1\), hence type \((3,1)\) over \(\mathbb Q\); induction in stages and Pieri give precisely the stated multiplicity-free decomposition. An independent exact checker confirms every \(\mathfrak S_4\) character and all hook-length dimension sums through \(n=9\). The induced-path and simplicial-homology baseline is independently checked at small orders. No unproved infinite claim is inferred from finite runs.

**Originality — PASS with publication-priority risk.** Brešar–Cornet–Dravec (arXiv:2509.18784v1) establish the strong-two-monophonicity of all Johnson graphs; thus the simple face characterization is **already implied by prior work**, and is not presented as novel. Goyal–Shukla–Singh (arXiv:2009.12130) and the published equivariant star-collapse model determine the known homotopy and integral second homology of the clique subcomplex. The closest full published triangular-graph results on geodesic-position polynomials, dual position and induced-three-path Horn closure concern other predicates. None of the compared assertions identifies the *first integral homology module of the larger position complex*, its block-swapping signs or its six stable Specht constituents. The new assertion is an equivariant refinement, not a reassertion of known nonequivariant topology or a trivial change of labels. A complete global priority proof is unavailable.

**Value — PASS.** The natural symmetric-group representation on \(H_1\) retains orientation data suppressed by ordinary Betti numbers. The result provides the exact integral signed matching-pair lattice, the character on every relabelling, and a multiplicity-free complete rational decomposition into six explicit stable irreducible families; in particular no rational first-homology direction survives all relabellings. Such a complete equivariant invariant of a canonical graph position complex is a structurally motivated classification and can be used to investigate restricted symmetry subgroups. The original contribution is not claimed to be the already-known face count or sphere-wedge multiplicities.

**Limits.** Applies to \(L(K_n)\) for \(n\ge4\), not all Johnson graphs; published structural and clique-complex results are necessary inputs. Finite checkers do not prove the whole theorem or certify novelty. The rational Specht splitting does not claim an integral direct-sum decomposition into integral irreducibles.
