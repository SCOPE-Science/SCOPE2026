For every \(n\ge4\), the natural integral first-homology representation of the monophonic-position complex of \(J(n,2)\) is the signed permutation lattice on the \(3\binom n4\) unordered pairs of disjoint root edges: swapping the two root edges reverses each generator. Over the rationals it decomposes multiplicity-free into the Specht modules indexed by horizontal-strip extensions of \((3,1)\), with six distinct constituents for \(n\ge7\).
