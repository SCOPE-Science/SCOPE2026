For the Newton-degenerate real cusp product \((x^p-y^q)^2-y^{2r}\) with even \(p\), odd coprime \(q>p\), and \(r>q\), the signed real log canonical thresholds are exactly \((p+q)/(2pq)\) and \(1/2-[q(p-1)-p]/(2pr)\). The second threshold recovers the contact order of the two complex cusp branches, despite their identical individual Puiseux characteristic.
