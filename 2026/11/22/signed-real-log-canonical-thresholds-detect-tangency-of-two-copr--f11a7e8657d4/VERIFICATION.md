# Verification and scope

The proof controls the local positive and negative integrals by explicit coordinate substitutions and two-sided comparisons. The negative-side integral reduces to a beta integral, yielding the exact convergence condition; the positive side is settled by a divergent wedge and an exhaustive domain split. The formal local intersection algebra has colength \(pr\), and primitive Puiseux parameters certify individual branch irreducibility.
