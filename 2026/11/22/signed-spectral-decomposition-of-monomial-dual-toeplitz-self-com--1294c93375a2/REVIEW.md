# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The exact ambient-normal-multiplier identity reduces the self-commutator to a difference of finite-rank angular forms. The harmonic projection is rank one in each angular mode, and explicit beta moments determine its two Gram vectors. In central modes their linear independence forces strictly opposite eigenvalue signs; conjugation pairs the signs. In tail modes a generator vanishes, leaving one scalar eigenvalue of each sign. The gamma-ratio expansion and telescoping establish the infinite-dimensional spectral and nuclear claims, not merely numerical evidence. The standalone standard-library checker completed 177256 finite assertions. The proof and checker are self-reviewed, not independently certified.

## Originality — PASS, with priority qualification
Wang–Zhao (2021) establish qualitative hyponormal-normal equivalence but no complete signed spectral sequence; Yang–Lu (2015) and Liu (2024) focus on commuting/zero-product conditions. Closest spectral literature on the harmonic Bergman *subspace* or the analytic Bergman *complement* does not imply this harmonic-complement theorem: the subspace and complement even give different degree-one unweighted operator norms, respectively \(1/6\) and \(1/12\). The exact two-vector central formulas and all-weight finite nuclear norm were not identified in searched records. Peng–Zhao (2021), a plausible paper on the same complement, was not accessible in full; historical equivalence remains possible and no exhaustive priority certification is claimed.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A complete spectral classification for a natural family of nonnormal dual Toeplitz operators fills the quantitative gap between qualitative nonnormality and operator-ideal descriptions. Its central rank-two blocks can determine the global norm, and its infinite signed tails give sharp weak-Schatten behavior. These conclusions are structural and parameter-complete, not an isolated small-n computation.

## Limitations
Only a single analytic monomial and the beta-weighted disk family are treated. The checker is finite and does not prove the infinite-dimensional theorem or historical novelty. No outside referee or independent audit has inspected the argument.
