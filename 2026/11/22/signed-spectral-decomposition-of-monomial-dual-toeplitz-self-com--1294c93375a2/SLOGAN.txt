The self-commutator of dual Toeplitz multiplication by \(z^m\) on the orthogonal complement of a beta-weighted harmonic Bergman space has a complete, exactly sign-paired spectrum, with finite rank-two central angular blocks and \(k^{-2}\) tails. This yields an explicit finite nuclear-norm formula, a sharp \(\mathcal S_p\) threshold \(p>1/2\), and a central-block norm overshoot already for \(m=2\) on the unweighted disk.
