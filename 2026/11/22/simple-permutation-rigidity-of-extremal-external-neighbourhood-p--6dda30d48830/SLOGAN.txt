For \(m\ge4\), a marked two-line permutation diagram realizes the largest possible number \(m^2-1\) of external open-neighbourhood traces exactly when its marked permutation is simple; every non-simple diagram has at most \(m^2-2\), sharply. The overall numerical maximum and simple-permutation constructions are established prior work; the new assertion is the complete equality characterization and sharp non-simple boundary.
