Two naturally paired cubic specializations \(X^3+c(X^2+X+1)\) and \(X^3+c(X^2-X+1)\) are simultaneously irreducible over \(\mathbb F_p\) for some parameter \(c\) if and only if \(p\notin\{2,3,5,13\}\). Their parameter count obeys the explicit genus-thirteen bound \(\lvert9N_p-(p+1)\rvert\le26\sqrt p+93\) for every prime \(p\ge11\), and a complete exact finite sieve handles the smaller primes.
