# Scientific review: unequal reversal-rate allocation in two-particle ring encounters

## Correctness — PASS

The exact claim is about **first** encounter on a finite ring, with independent uniform initial positions and unbiased headings, common speed and a fixed positive sum of the two individual Poisson reversal rates. The four internal states and their velocities are explicitly defined. The mean generator equations impose just one incoming boundary condition per moving direction, not four incorrect simultaneous absorbing conditions. Eliminating two zero-velocity phases yields quadratic conditional mean polynomials. The two second-moment boundary equations reduce to a triangular polynomial integration problem, with both boundary conditions fixing the integration constant. The fully integrated raw second moment, variance, and squared coefficient of variation are algebraic identities, checked by the accompanying executable. The derivatives are positive for every allowed rate split, including both endpoints. A uniform finite-time collision probability proves the moments exist even with one permanently nonflipping particle. Exact symbolic generator residuals and 240,000 seeded event-driven paths pass. Simulation is corroboration rather than an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual publication-priority uncertainty

The earliest source establishes the ring two-particle active model and analyzes invariant jamming distributions, not fixed-total *individual* reversal-rate optimization of first-encounter moments. The 2018 circle-network paper contains a conditional first return mean for **equal** rates and is acknowledged; the equal-rate specialization is not claimed as new. The closest already-published ring first-contact transform uses **equal** particle rates and a particular initially closing state. The closest speed-ratio result changes **speeds** at a common flip rate, and the closest heterogeneous reversal-rate result concerns a **stationary separation exponent** under attraction on the infinite line. None of these inspected results states a simultaneous mean, absolute-variance, and relative-risk optimum for independently allocated individual reversal rates at fixed sum. This distinction is by domains, quantifiers, parameter maps and implications, not title matching. A broader unpublished or unindexed matrix-fluid first-passage identity might cover some formulas; the literature examination does not certify global priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS

A natural resource-design question is how to distribute a fixed total reorientation budget between two active searchers before their first encounter. The complete all-positive-budget classification is structurally motivated, quantitatively exact, and not restricted to a numerical example or arbitrary parameter slice. The noteworthy effect is that concentrating all reversals in one particle (leaving the other persistently ballistic) simultaneously improves the expected meeting time, its **absolute** variance, and its dimensionless relative fluctuations; a balanced split is uniquely worst on all three. Finite-speed stationary or equal-rate formulas alone do not establish this three-way dominance. The result offers an explicit benchmark for paired active search in cyclic geometry without requiring a larger downstream theorem.

## Closest literature and limits

Relevant prior materials: Hahn, Guillin and Michel, arXiv:2306.00831 (first public 2023-06-01); Cheliotis et al., arXiv:1808.03830, Section 4.2; Le Doussal et al., arXiv:1902.06176; and the published research results *First-collision transform and rare-reversal moment singularity for a run-and-tumble pair on a ring*, *A sharp speed-ratio threshold for beneficial tumbling in two-particle ring encounters*, and *Heterogeneous tumbling sharpens the exponential binding length of a jammed two-swimmer pair*. The result assumes independent instantaneous flips, independent uniform initial data, a circle, and equal propulsion speeds. It proves only the three stated statistics, not stochastic ordering, correlated or non-Poisson turning, or post-contact dynamics. Broader generic Markov-modulated-fluid literature remains a publication-priority risk.
