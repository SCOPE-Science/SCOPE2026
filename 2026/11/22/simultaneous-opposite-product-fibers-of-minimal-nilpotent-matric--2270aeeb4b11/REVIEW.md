# Scientific review of simultaneous opposite-product fibers

## Correctness — PASS
The parameterization \(X=u\varphi\), \(Y=s\psi\) uses exact rank-one and square-zero hypotheses. The two independent cross-evaluations determine \(XY\) and \(YX\). The nonzero/nonzero case is completely reconstructed from mutually annihilating rank-one outputs with a common nonzero trace, with one unique factor pair for each nonzero field parameter. In the one-sided and zero cases, counting normalized projective image lines and covectors gives the stated multiplicities with valid \(n=2\) boundaries. Counts of target populations provide a second complete mass check. Exhaustive independent direct-multiplication computations across seven configurations passed. No finite test is used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The closest published results determine the individual multiplication fibers and the separate Lie-bracket fibers for precisely the same minimal nilpotent orbit. Their proof texts were read and their stated conclusions compared with the present simultaneous prescribed-output claim. They explain important overlapping marginal counts and are explicitly credited. Neither published statement gives an exact target-by-target joint distribution or the unique multiplicative-parameter reconstruction per specified pair of opposite products; individual marginals cannot reconstruct joint correlations. The two archival matrix-operator sources inspected concern a more general nilpotent commutator variety and product-nilpotency preservers, respectively. Searches for twisted commutators, Jordan anticommutators, orthogonal idempotent product pairs, and two-sided multiplication found no decisive stronger published coverage. Residual risk: an unlocated broader orbital-convolution formula may imply the result, and the joint theorem is close enough to the existing marginal proofs that priority remains provisional rather than established uniquely.

## Value — PASS
Joint opposite-product fibers answer a natural two-sided factorization question in matrix algebra, not an arbitrary slice or a changed sign of one word map. They determine precisely which *pairs* of targets can occur, construct every compatible factorization, show a uniform multiplicative torsor over each two-sided nonzero target pair, and expose the correlation between symmetric Jordan and antisymmetric Lie outputs. This yields strictly finer information than either known marginal enumeration, with exact image sizes and a rank-two reconstruction phenomenon uniformly in dimension and field order. The result is deliberately limited to rank-one square-zero factors.
