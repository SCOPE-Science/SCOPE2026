# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Independence of the natural squares forces every isomorphism between perfect algebras to be monomial. Over F3, an automorphism U with permutation part P obeys UA=AP since all nonzero scalars square to one. The image H in S_n acts on each coordinate orbit; on a transitive orbit a fixed vector is determined by one coordinate, and if nonzero then all its coordinates are nonzero. The intertwiner A makes the total fixed-space dimension equal to the number of orbits for the permutation representation, even if 3 divides |H|. Thus every orbit contributes one fixed line, and joining nonzero representatives gives one full-support fixed vector and one diagonal normalization for the entire group. For Burnside, a signed cycle contributes a fixed dimension exactly when its sign product is positive, and all positive products suffice for diagonal similarity. This gives 2^(n−ℓ) signs per cycle type, and the fixed-matrix count is the corresponding centralizer order. The primary-module computation uses the full elementary divisors, including repeated factors from cycles divisible by 3. Independent exact arithmetic reproduced the seven n=5 contributions 123845436,252720,1458,8424,48,27,16 and their sum 124108129; the earlier stated counts follow by the same partition sum. The F4 diagonal counterexample also checks. The uploaded verifier was inspected, not executed.

## originality

PASS

Credit is for the single diagonal normalization of the entire rational F3 automorphism group, for arbitrary orbit structure. The general monomial orbit method and the finite-field Burnside enumeration already exist; the centralizer specialization and I5 are checked consequences, not independent originality claims. Existing monomiality, graph-kernel results and permutation realizability do not alone imply a common diagonal conjugator. The comparison is best-of-knowledge and does not certify first publication.

## value

PASS

One natural-basis rescaling removes the diagonal twists of every symmetry at once, so the whole automorphism group can be treated as permutations commuting with a common structure matrix even without transitivity or a support assumption. This is a reusable structural constraint; the fifth-dimensional numerical example is not the reason for passing value.
