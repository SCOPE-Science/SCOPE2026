Every perfect evolution algebra over \(\mathbb F_3\) admits one natural-basis sign rescaling that simultaneously turns its **entire** rational automorphism group into permutation matrices, even when that group has multiple coordinate orbits. The resulting centralizer-count formula gives exactly \(124{,}108{,}129\) five-dimensional isomorphism classes, and a two-dimensional example over \(\mathbb F_4\) proves the normalization is field-specific.
