For every finite Desarguesian projective plane, the point–line incidence graph modulo **any** subgroup of its Singer–Frobenius normalizer is a completely classified wedge of circles. Its order-three fixed graph undergoes a sharp arithmetic transition: an interval in characteristic three, two isolated points for \(q\equiv2\pmod3\), and either a genuine hexagon or the empty set for \(q\equiv1\pmod3\).
