For every primitive irreducible binary cellular automaton, the complete three-pinned-cylinder image-index filtration is controlled by Singer-line incidences and dyadic site separations. The sharp worst first full-reachability time for a triple of span \(L\) is \(1\) below the minimal divisible-trinomial span \(m(P)\), and thereafter \(1+2^{\lfloor\log_2(L/m(P))\rfloor}\).
