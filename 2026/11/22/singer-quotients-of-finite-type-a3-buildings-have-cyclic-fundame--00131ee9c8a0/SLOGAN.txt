For every prime power \(q\ge2\), the ordinary quotient of the type-A3 finite building by its full projective Singer cycle has \(\pi_1\cong\mathbb Z/(q^2+1)\), \(H_2\cong\mathbb Z^{q^2(q-1)}\), and no other reduced integral homology. The fundamental-group torsion grows without bound even though the original building is simply connected.
