For every edge \(e\) of the dodecahedral graph \(D\), the perfect-matching-extendibility complex after deletion is \(\mathcal P(D-e)\simeq M(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z,2)\vee\bigvee^4 S^3\). Its order-two second-homology class maps isomorphically to that of the intact complex: a single edge fault lowers the free third-Betti number from twenty to four without destroying the integral torsion.
