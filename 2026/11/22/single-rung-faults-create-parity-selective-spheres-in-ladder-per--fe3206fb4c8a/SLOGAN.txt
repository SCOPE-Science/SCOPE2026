Deleting one vertical rung from a two-row ladder produces a perfect-matching complex homotopy equivalent to \(S^{(n-3)/2}\) precisely when the column count \(n\) is odd and the missing rung is at an even-numbered interior column; every other single-rung fault yields a contractible complex. The proof identifies an exact two-cone intersection as a join of the perfect-matching complexes of the two separated subladders.
