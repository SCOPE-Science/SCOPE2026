The closest prime-power theorem is Seck–Nitaj (2024, ePrint 2024/385, Lemma 3), and it **already covers** the split-prime formula for \(p\equiv1\pmod3\). The current theorem claims no priority for that formula. Nitaj–Seck (2024) restricts its encoding constructions to primes congruent to one or two modulo three; Shevelev (2015) studies restricted positive-integer representations instead of modular root fibers. Murru–Saettone (2018) assumes a noncube parameter and does not apply here. Exact general norm-one group calculations for non-étale cubic orders might implicitly yield the result; this residual priority risk is acknowledged. An unsuccessful text search is not proof of global novelty.
