On the singular cubic Pell norm-one surface \(x^3+y^3+z^3-3xyz\equiv1\pmod{3^k}\), there are exactly \(3^{2k}\) ordered triples at every depth. Only three of the nine solutions modulo three survive to higher levels, and at every stage exactly one third of the current solutions lift, each with all \(27\) possible next-digit refinements.
