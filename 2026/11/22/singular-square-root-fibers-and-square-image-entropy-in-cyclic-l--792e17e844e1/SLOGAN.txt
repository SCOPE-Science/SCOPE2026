In the radical of a cyclic Loewy-three Nakayama algebra, the number of square roots of a prescribed degree-two element with mixed zero and nonzero coordinates depends only on its cyclic zero-gap lengths, with isolated zero coefficients forming an exact obstruction. A four-state transfer matrix gives the full radical square-image size in every finite-field order, whose attainable-target fraction decays as \((\rho_q/q)^n\) for a uniquely determined \(q-1<\rho_q<q\).
