# Review of the singular-value rigidity finding

**Correctness — PASS.** For every regular binary sandwich matrix, multiply two Rees elements explicitly to obtain the mutual-annihilation entry formula. The matrix lift is \(X\mapsto(ZXZ)^{\mathsf T}\); the rectangular SVD gives diagonal modes \(s_a^2\), symmetric/antisymmetric modes \(\pm s_as_b\), and the full nullspace. The odd-moment generating function reconstructs the positive singular values, proving reverse spectral rigidity rather than only a forward formula. The exact count of mutually annihilating ordered pairs is \(z^2\), where \(z\) is the number of zeros in the sandwich matrix. The accompanying checker passes 327 small exhaustive cases and 400 seeded larger cases; its experiments do not replace the general proof.

**Originality — PASS, with risk.** Jackson–Volkov (2009) establish the Rees adjacency construction; Paulista (2025), Lemma 3.2, explicitly gives the zero-pattern commuting criterion in the same family. Neither fact is claimed new. The inspected mathematical claims do not include the complete real spectrum, inertia, energy, trace moments or reverse cospectrality classification. Related published mutual-inverse and rook-monoid spectra use similar linear algebra for different semigroups; their full texts were not read. Existing linear-algebra results could imply this as a corollary without using the present terminology. See AUDIT.json for exact comparisons and source-access limitations.

**Value — PASS.** A high-dimensional semigroup relation collapses to a much smaller bipartite zero-pattern singular spectrum. The theorem gives all closed-walk numbers and a stable inertia law for every sandwich pattern, while proving that the resulting spectrum can recover the underlying singular spectrum. It therefore supports systematic comparison of finite completely \(0\)-simple semigroup annihilation networks instead of just individual finite enumerations. This is not a classification of the ordinary loopless commuting graph or of nontrivial-group Rees semigroups.

**Principal references and limits.** Jackson–Volkov, arXiv:0907.2634v1 (2009); Giudici–Kuzma, arXiv:1505.05397 (2015); Paulista, arXiv:2510.23871v1 (2025); Nagy, DOI 10.1007/s00233-025-10597-0. Independent verification and historical-priority certification have not occurred.
