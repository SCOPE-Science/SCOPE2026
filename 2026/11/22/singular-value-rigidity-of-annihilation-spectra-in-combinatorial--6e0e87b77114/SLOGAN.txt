For every combinatorial completely \(0\)-simple Rees semigroup, the full real spectrum of its nonzero-element mutual-annihilation relation is determined by the singular values of the sandwich matrix's zero pattern: the inertia is \((r(r+1)/2,r(r-1)/2,mn-r^2)\), and the energy is the square of its nuclear norm. Conversely, the annihilation spectrum reconstructs those nonzero singular values exactly, so cospectrality reduces to bipartite zero-pattern cospectrality for fixed index-set sizes.
