For every graph \(H\) of minimum degree at least two and girth at least five, the complete ordinary zero-forcing polynomial of its complement has four terms, and the number of minimum forcing sets is \((n-3)\sum_v\binom{d(v)}2-\sum_v\binom{D(v)}2+2c_6(H)\). Thus optimal forcing configurations of a dense complement recover the exact six-cycle count of the sparse underlying graph from local degree-two-neighborhood data.
