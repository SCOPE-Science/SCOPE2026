# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. For the four-regular cube, threshold-three infection equals synchronous deletion of vertices with residual degree at most one. This proves the forest criterion and the maximality equivalence. Exhaustive integer-set, bitmask, and forest-component implementations independently reconstruct exactly \(936\) minimal sets and confirm the six disjoint automorphism orbits and the timing classification. The three standalone source files contain executable assertions rather than unverified output logs.

## Originality

PASS for the joint symmetry/diameter/timing classification, not for the known six-seed optimum or for the raw feedback-set relation. Morrison–Noel proves the optimum and provides one attaining configuration. Existing research on four-cube feedback sets already constrains the same configurations, including a reported \(936\)-component small-buffer graph; that prior work may imply parts of the size counts through the forest duality. Comparisons with the published record therefore separate those covered quantities from the new leaf-pruning time stratification and five non-minimum orbit shapes. Full appendices of the closest published feedback-set and induced-forest studies remained inaccessible; priority of a previously unreported table cannot be certified.

## Value

PASS. Irredundant initial states are natural robustness-critical placements, and the six complete orbit types describe them without arbitrary slicing. The reduction to residual-tree diameters explains a nontrivial timing split (\(160\) versus \(768\) among the \(928\) seven-seed configurations), refining the established minimum-size answer with exact dissemination speed and structural geometry. The result is finite and does not assert a general-dimensional theorem.
