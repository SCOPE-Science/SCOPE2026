For the common-determinant dependence split \(\operatorname{dep}(x,y)\lor\operatorname{dep}(x,z)\), every inclusion-minimal false finite team is exactly one of six part-preserving bipartite patterns, with either three or four assignments in a single \(x\)-fiber. Their complete labelled census for all finite alphabet sizes is an explicit cubic/quartic generating polynomial, refining the known four-assignment coherence cutoff into a canonical forbidden-pattern classification.
