On the fifteen-vertex Petersen line graph, the complete \(k\)-metric dimension sequence for \(1\leq k\leq10\) is \((4,5,7,8,9,10,12,13,14,15)\), with exact minimum-set counts \((960,408,3480,1815,375,6,455,105,15,1)\). Precisely six minimum \(6\)-metric bases survive, the complements of the six perfect matchings of the original Petersen graph; from \(k=7\) onward every subset of the sharp requisite size is optimal.
