For finite \(T_0\) Alexandrov spaces, the inseparability and closure-meeting graphs together determine weak homotopy type through five points, and six is the first failure. The ordinal sums \(A_2\oplus A_2\oplus A_2\) and \(A_2\oplus[2]\oplus A_2\) have the same four separation graphs on six labels but order complexes \(S^2\) and a point.
