For a juvenile–adult population with fixed survivals, permuting the same seasonal fertility values cannot change its relative growth advantage as adult survival varies until the calendar has at least six seasons. Six seasons suffice: the calendars \((1,2,3,4,5,6)\) and \((1,2,4,3,5,6)\) exchange growth rankings exactly at adult retention \(t=\sqrt{7s/3}\), whenever that threshold lies in the admissible interval.
