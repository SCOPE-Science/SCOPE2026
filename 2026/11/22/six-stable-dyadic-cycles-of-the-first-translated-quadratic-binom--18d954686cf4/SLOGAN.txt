For the shifted Pascal-column map \(F(x)=\binom{2x+4}{2}\), the permutation modulo every \(2^k\) has exactly six cycles once \(k\ge4\): two even cycles of length \(2^{k-2}\) and four odd cycles of length \(2^{k-3}\). An exact two-step displacement identity proves both parity clocks at every precision.
