For the eleven smooth generalized Legendre plane quartics \(Y^4=XZ(X-Z)(X-\lambda Z)\) over \(\mathbb F_{13}\), exactly six Jacobian Weil polynomials occur. The three anharmonic cross-ratio parameter orbits each split into exactly two Weil types; the equianharmonic pair \(\lambda=4,10\) has \(28\) versus \(4\) rational points.
