For exact point-lens microlensing with unknown blended and source flux in every passband, the peak derivatives through order five leave a continuous ambiguity even with arbitrarily many bands. A single sixth derivative breaks it: the flux-independent ratio \(F''F^{(6)}/(F^{(4)})^2=(20/9)(1+(u_0^2+2)^{-2})\) uniquely recovers the impact parameter, after which all time-scale and flux parameters follow algebraically.
