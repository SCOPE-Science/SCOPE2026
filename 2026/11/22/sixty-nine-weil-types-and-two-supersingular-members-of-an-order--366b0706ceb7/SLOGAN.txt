For the \(101\) smooth marked curves \(y^2=x^{10}+x^7+a x^4+b x\) over \(\mathbb F_{11}\), there are exactly \(69\) Jacobian Weil types and the \(11\)-rank distribution is \((2,5,15,79)\) for ranks \((0,1,2,3)\). Exactly two marked members, at \((a,b)=(3,7),(8,3)\), are supersingular but not superspecial, and have identical zeta numerators.
