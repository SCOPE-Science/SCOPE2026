In the one-spin-facilitated Fredrickson–Andersen process on every \(K_{m,n}\) with \(2\le m\le n\), an exactly specified relaxation eigenvalue depends on the smaller shore only and supports at least \(n-1\) independent contrasts on the larger shore. Its deficit from unit rate behaves as \(\frac{m}{m-1}(1-q)^m\) near full vacancy, sharply different from the square-root star law; the result establishes a spectral mode and gap upper bound, not an exact full spectral gap.
