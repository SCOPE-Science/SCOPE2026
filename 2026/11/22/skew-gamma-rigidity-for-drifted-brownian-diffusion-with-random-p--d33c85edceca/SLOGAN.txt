For Brownian diffusion with nonzero drift and independent random proportional Poisson resets, a target difference of independent gamma variables at the excursion's two natural exponential scales is stationary only for complete resetting or when its two shape excesses are proportional to the corresponding scales. Every nontrivial admissible skew-gamma target determines a **unique** beta contraction law, and no unbalanced pair of gamma shapes is possible.
