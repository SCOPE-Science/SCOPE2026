For all orders \(n\), every compatible set of at most \(n-1\) Latin-square entry queries is shattered, and the cutoff is sharp. At order three, the exceptional three-query supports are exactly 216 Latin triangles: the nonpure shattered-support complex has face polynomial \(1+27z+270z^2+216z^3\) and reduced mod-two Betti ranks 111 and 83 in degrees one and two.
