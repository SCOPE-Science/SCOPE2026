A one-parameter family of strictly positive polynomial nonuniform densities has exactly the null mean and asymptotic variance of the Kattumannil–Sreedevi scalar uniformity U-statistic, making its Gaussian-calibrated two-sided test have limiting power equal to its nominal size against every member. Yet each alternative has Kolmogorov distance at least \(|c|/4\) from uniformity, and the family is obtained by two exact polynomial moment cancellations rather than an approximation.
