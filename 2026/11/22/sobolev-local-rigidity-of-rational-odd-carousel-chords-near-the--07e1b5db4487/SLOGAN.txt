For every coprime perimetral fraction \(p/q\) with odd \(q\ge7\), a sufficiently smooth strictly convex curve near the circle is a circle whenever all chords joining points separated by that fraction of its perimeter have equal length. More sharply, the tangent-angle deformation is bounded by the squared-chord-length oscillation in a Sobolev norm exactly one derivative stronger, and that derivative loss cannot be reduced.
