# Review of the spanning-tree asymptotic

## Correctness — PASS

The proof reduces full \(2\)-Abelian reversal symmetry to zero cotree currents of closed walks, obtains an exact Fourier integral, evaluates a simple isolated spectral maximum and computes its Hessian determinant exactly. Multivariate Gaussian integration gives the stated constant. Boundary \(q=2\) is exact; \(q=3\) is checked against an independent central-trinomial count. The two accompanying programs were executed successfully, and symbolic and numerical Hessian checks independently tested the nonstandard matrix identity. Enumerations support but do not prove the asymptotic.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

The closest primary literature is Karhumäki–Puzynina's 2014 study of rich and poor \(k\)-Abelian palindromic factors, the later Cassaigne–Karhumäki–Puzynina presentation, and Saarela's equivalence-class count in Section 6 of his factor-count equivalence paper. The present theorem counts *all indexed fixed-length words*, not equivalence classes or distinct factors of one host. No checked primary statement or queried mathematical record implies the complete-alphabet decay exponent together with the explicit spanning-tree constant. The ternary exact count might follow from the published ternary reductions and is not independently offered as novel.

The complete DLT 2014 conference text and the later 2018 full paper could not be inspected. Their abstracts concern factor richness/poverty. Priority cannot be independently established, and an unindexed equivalent formulation remains possible.

## Value — PASS

The joint equality of opposite directed bigram counts is a natural local-symmetry statistic for word reconstruction and combinatorics. The complete dimension-dependent decay law distinguishes binary, ternary and larger alphabets and makes the spanning-tree invariant mathematically explicit, instead of reporting isolated finite computations.

## Scientific limits

Fixed uniform alphabet \(q\), fixed \(k=2\), linear indexed words only; no claim about general \(k\), changing alphabets, or independent validation.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
