For every fixed alphabet size \(q\ge2\), the probability that a random length-\(n\) word is a \(2\)-Abelian palindrome decays as an explicit constant times \((n-1)^{-(q-1)(q-2)/4}\). The constant is governed by the square root of the complete graph's spanning-tree count, revealing a sharp change between the binary, ternary and larger-alphabet cases.
