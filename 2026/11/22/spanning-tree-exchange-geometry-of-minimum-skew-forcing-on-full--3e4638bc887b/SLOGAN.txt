The minimum skew-forcing configurations of a fully edge-subdivided connected graph are exactly rooted spanning trees, and their optimal token-exchange graph is the Cartesian product of a clique with the spanning-tree basis-exchange graph; every adjacency-restricted slide is frozen. For subdivided complete graphs, the exact exchange degree is the Wiener index of the associated spanning tree, and the sharp diameter is \(n\) for all \(n\ge4\).
