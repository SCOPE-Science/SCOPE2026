In sparse Poisson samples, all fixed exponential-weight Stein–Chen goodness-of-fit discrepancies collapse onto a single rare-doubleton coordinate: Gaussian calibration holds exactly when \(n\lambda_n^2\to\infty\), while the critical boundary has a centered Poisson limit. At \(n\lambda_n^2\to0.3\), nominal 5% normal calibration instead rejects about 13.93% of true Poisson nulls.
