In a shrinking-mean true Poisson null with \(n\lambda_n^r\to c>0\), the fixed-Laplace-weight empirical goodness-of-fit distances have explicit nonzero discrete limits driven by a single rare-cell Poisson count, whereas the companion empirical- and fitted-Poisson-weight distances vanish. Between critical levels, deterministic unseen-cell tails form a sharp count-indexed staircase.
