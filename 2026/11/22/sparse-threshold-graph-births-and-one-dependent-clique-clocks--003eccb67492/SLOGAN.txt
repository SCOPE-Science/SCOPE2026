At the sharp edge-onset scale \(2-t/n\), a uniform-weight random threshold graph converges on bounded windows to a Poisson-marked graph process, with a total-variation error at most \(b^2/n\). Successive clique-birth gaps are Gamma-distributed but one-dependent with adjacent correlation \(1/2\); the limiting edge count is non-Poisson and the unique nontrivial component has an explicit size law.
