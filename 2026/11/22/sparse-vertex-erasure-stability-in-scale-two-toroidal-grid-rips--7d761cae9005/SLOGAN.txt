For every \(m,n\ge7\), deleting any set of mutually distance-at-least-three grid vertices from the radius-two toroidal Vietoris–Rips complex preserves simple homotopy type by an explicit **36-pair collapse per vertex**. A periodic choice removes one fifth of the grid points and three fifths of its simplices while retaining the homotopy type \(S^1\times S^1\); deleting two adjacent points need not preserve it.
