For the Walsh transform of unoriented Hamiltonian tours of \(K_n\), every edge frequency supported on at most four edges is nonzero for \(n\ge6\), except exactly the triangle/star-plus-isolated-edge frequencies at \(n=9\) and the triangle/star frequencies at \(n=10\). Thus every degree-four edge-Walsh signal is recovered by Hamilton-tour convolution at every \(n\ge11\), despite sharp, explicitly counted lower-degree cancellations at those two exceptional orders.
