# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The finite-memory lift is explicitly defined. A positive eigenvector at the unique product-root gives both an eigenvalue and a matching weighted sup-norm bound, with no irreducibility assumption; a small perturbation of admissible belief histories attains the bound. Strict Jensen inequality proves the exact aggregate-delay bound and its equality case. Discrete convex chords prove sharp individual-mean optimizers. Numerical tests re-evaluate the lifted eigenvalues but do not replace these proofs.

**Originality — PASS, literature-bounded.** Singh–Moharir–Sahasrabudhe introduce the memory-limited delayed belief model and general asymptotic behavior, but the accessible original abstract does not state the exact cyclic spectral product or the spatial-versus-temporal redistribution dichotomy. Yao–Li study delays and convergence rates, but their accessible original abstract only establishes qualitative rate effects, not the cyclic aggregate-delay conservation identity. The full published result on randomized Gauss–Seidel already proves convex-order slowdown and endpoint/adjacent extremizers for a different scalar mean-square recurrence. Those similarities are credited rather than counted as independent novelty. Its full RESULT was read and does not cover coupled cyclic opinion-belief dynamics. The published infinite-fading-memory Laplacian gain result was likewise read and applies to another dynamical model. No exact prior implication of the spatial-delay identity was located. Residual risk: the inaccessible main paper or contemporaneous analysis could contain equivalent cyclic statements; the search cannot prove uniqueness.

**Value — PASS.** Finite memory and communication delays are intrinsic model choices. The theorem distinguishes costly *temporal* mixing from spectrally cost-free *spatial* heterogeneity at identical total delay, gives a sharp rate and attainable aggregate-memory lower bound for every cycle size and delay budget, and identifies best and worst memory laws under separate mean constraints. The result is structural rather than a numerical gain, and provides a concrete design distinction that a fixed-point convergence theorem does not supply. The network class is deliberately narrow.

## Scope and residual risks

The underlying network is exactly one directed cycle with identical baseline and peer sensitivity. General directed graphs need not exhibit conservation of the aggregate delay. The comparison with two recent full original manuscripts was limited to abstracts and associated lawful public summaries; relevant hidden theorems or appendices could affect historical priority. The mathematical result is self-contained and does not depend on those uninspected sources for its premises.
