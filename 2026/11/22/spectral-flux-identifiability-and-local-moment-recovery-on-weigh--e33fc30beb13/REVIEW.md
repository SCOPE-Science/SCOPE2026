# Review

## Correctness — PASS

For every \(m\ge2\), the exact Schur complement of the known Hermitian hopping matrix gives the displayed characteristic polynomial. Evaluating its quotient at distinct positive \(\tau_r\) proves nondegenerate recovery of all equal-strength cosine sums, even at exceptional flux values. Each rooted cubic moment is a sum over exactly two directed triangles. The explicit equal-coupling isospectral pair is symbolically checked. Six exact cases and 240 independent seeded numerical configurations corroborate, but are not relied upon in place of, the all-size algebraic proof.

## Originality — PASS on examined evidence

The 2011 gain-graph foundations [1] and the 2013 unweighted friendship-graph classification [2] do not provide multivariate inverse magnetic spectroscopy. The strongest relevant general theorem [3] characterizes gain-graph cospectrality through closed-walk characters, but does not establish the specialized exact equivalence classes, finite recovery formula or local-moment restoration. Earlier common-flux triangular-bouquet scattering is one-parameter with an external wire, not an independently variable phase inverse theorem. An unindexed equivalent source or a not-yet-inspected specialized result could defeat historical priority; independent priority validation remains outstanding.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The question is a natural inverse-transport problem: can the energy levels of a flux-threaded tight-binding network reveal the magnetic field in each separate loop? The answer distinguishes complete individual identifiability at distinct known bond strengths from sharply characterized hiding under equal weights, then gives a minimal third-order site-resolved observable that resolves the ambiguity. A physically important qualitative threshold is established for every network size and every positive coupling choice, not a merely computed small instance.
