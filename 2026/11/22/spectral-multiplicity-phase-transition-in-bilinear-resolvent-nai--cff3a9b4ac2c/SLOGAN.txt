For every bilinear-resolvent joint Hausdorff measure in Tian's simple-bottom-eigenvalue cyclicity class, the minimal Naimark dilation has constant full spectral multiplicity \(\dim\mathcal H\) in the strictly interior completion regime. At the critical rank drop it splits into two planar wedges of multiplicity \(\dim\mathcal H-1\) and a singular exponential curve of multiplicity one, including infinite-dimensional examples.
