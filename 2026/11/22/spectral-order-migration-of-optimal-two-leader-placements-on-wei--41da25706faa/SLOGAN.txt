On every weighted double star with at least two leaves per hub, the globally optimal pair of perfectly pinned leaders for every inverse-Laplacian spectral moment of real order \(r\ge1\) switches exactly once from both hubs to a hub and an opposite-side leaf as bridge conductance increases. The critical conductance strictly increases with spectral order, so first- and second-order criteria provably select different leader types on a nonempty interval, and the large-order threshold grows with slope \((m-1)/\log2\) for \(m\) leaves on the smaller side.
