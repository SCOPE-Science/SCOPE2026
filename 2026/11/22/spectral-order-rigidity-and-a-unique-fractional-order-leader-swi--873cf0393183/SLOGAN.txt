On every unweighted complete split graph, the globally optimal two-leader placement for every real inverse-Laplacian spectral order \(r\ge1\) follows a rigid clique-versus-independent rule, except for one five-vertex graph. On \(K_2\vee\overline{K_3}\), an exact, unique fractional-order threshold \(r=1.1293316509672375\ldots\) switches the optimum from two independent vertices to both clique vertices, and all boundary ties are classified.
