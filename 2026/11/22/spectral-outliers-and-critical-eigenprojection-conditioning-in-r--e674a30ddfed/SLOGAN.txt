The diagonal restriction of the radial Bergman Toeplitz completely positive channel has a universal essential spectral circle, but its ordinary spectrum can have finitely many isolated real points detected exactly by moment-ratio inequalities \(\delta_j>2\). For beta weights, the number of islands is \(\max(0,\lceil\alpha\rceil)\), and the norms of their Riesz projections diverge at successive merger thresholds with the distinct sharp powers \(3,5,7,\ldots\).
