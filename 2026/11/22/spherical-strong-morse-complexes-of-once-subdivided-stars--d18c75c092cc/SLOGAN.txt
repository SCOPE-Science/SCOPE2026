For every once-subdivided star with at least three arms, the full strong discrete-Morse matching complex strongly collapses onto an embedded cross-polytope boundary \(S^{n-1}\), and arm permutations act by the integral sign character. The original nonpure complex has dimension \(2n-1\) and exactly \(2^n+n\) maximal facets, contrasting with the known higher-dimensional wedge of spheres for its ordinary Morse complex.
