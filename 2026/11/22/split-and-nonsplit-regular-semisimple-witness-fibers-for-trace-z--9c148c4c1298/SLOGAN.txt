For trace-zero commutators over every odd-residue finite chain ring, prescribing the split or nonsplit regular-semisimple type of the first factor gives a complete, explicit three-discriminant-stratum fiber formula. Split factors represent every target, but nonsplit factors miss exactly \((q^2-1)(q^{3k}-1)/(q^3-1)\) nonzero targets with isotropic leading residue.
