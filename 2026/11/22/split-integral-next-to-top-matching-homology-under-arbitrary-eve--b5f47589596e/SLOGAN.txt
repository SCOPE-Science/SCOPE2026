For any nested graphs on a fixed even number \(2m\) of vertices with \(m\ge3\), their ordinary matching complexes have split-injective reduced integral homology in degree \(m-2\): graph-edge insertions can only create, never destroy, primitive classes in that degree. In the eight-vertex perfect-matching deletion example, the exact homotopy cofiber is a wedge of 53 two-spheres; the guarantee fails one degree lower.
