For dissociation complexes of \(K_{a,b}\) under successive matching-edge deletions, every integral first-homology inclusion is a split surjection and every second-homology inclusion a primitive split injection. Their entire integral barcode is explicit: degree-one classes all start at stage zero and die with multiplicities \(a+b-2\) at stage one and \(a+b-2s+1\) at stage \(s\ge2\), while \(s-2\) degree-two classes are born permanently at each stage \(s\ge3\).
