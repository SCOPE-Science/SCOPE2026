Deleting any two-run pattern \(0^a1^b\) of length \(k\ge3\) from the alternating word \((01)^n\) produces exactly \(\binom nk+\binom{n-1}{k-2}\) distinct residuals when \(n\ge k-1\). More strongly, the entire histogram of how many embeddings produce each residual depends only on \(n\) and \(k=a+b\), not on the split between the two runs.
