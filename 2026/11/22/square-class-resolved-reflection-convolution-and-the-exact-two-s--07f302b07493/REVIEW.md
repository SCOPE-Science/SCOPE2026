# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The stated formula is an evaluated enumeration, not merely an existence assertion. The proof correctly matches the factor order through the moved space of the inverse, uses the multiplicative Wall spinor sign, and treats identity, non-rank-two, binary rank-two, binary rank-one, and totally singular moved spaces separately. The first nonstandard dependency—the direct factorization / Wall parametrization—is a cited published theorem, not presented as new. Elementary finite-field norm counts, the two-by-two Wall-form determinant, and the quadratic-character identity explicitly derive the numeric factors and the full three-dimensional target census. The exact total-variation expression follows by summing the positive deviations. A standalone finite-prime-field matrix enumeration passes ten configurations and checks all three-dimensional target multiplicities and total-variation values. Extension fields and arbitrary dimensions rely on the algebraic proof, not finite experiments.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** The most directly relevant source, McCammond–Paolini, arXiv:2103.02507, already establishes the line-to-factorization geometry and the Wall spinor norm. Those are **not** submitted as original. The specific contribution is their finite-field numerical refinement simultaneously for both norm-square classes at every prescribed target, including the rank-one moved-plane class exclusion, and the complete ternary target-count and sharp two-step total-variation law. Hollmann–Xiang, arXiv:math/0503573, evaluates intersection parameters in a *line* association scheme for an action of PGL2; the inspected odd-characteristic sections do not give the *group* conjugacy class multiplication constants asserted here. Guralnick–Zieve, arXiv:0707.1835, counts PGL2 involutions and their two classes but not products. An accessible historical result on determinant-minus-one involutions in GL2 gives an all-time convolution on SL2; its group, projective quotient, and sampled factor set differ from the two-class orthogonal reflection law. The direct Wall correspondence means this is a non-independent arithmetic evaluation; older character-table calculations and line-scheme reductions may already imply the same counts. No exhaustive uniqueness proof is claimed.

**Value — PASS.** The object is a canonical generating set of a finite classical group, and its two spinor-distinguished conjugacy classes. Exact prescribed-target structure constants are natural class-algebra invariants. The cubic group case gives an entire positive-fiber spectrum, an exclusion phenomenon at degenerate moved planes that a total count conceals, and a sharp two-step mixing distance of order reciprocal field size. This goes beyond reflection-length existence and involution class sizes while maintaining a specific natural target/invariant and fully quantified bounds.

**Sources and residual risks.** The relevant primary treatments are McCammond–Paolini (2021; material read: Wall forms, Lemma 2.6, Theorems 2.2, 2.7, 2.10 and 3.5), Guralnick–Zieve (2007; Appendix Lemma A.3), and Hollmann–Xiang (2005; conic-line orbit description, Sections 4–7 and intersection tables). The biggest priority risk is an equivalent two-involution class-algebra calculation from PGL2 character tables or a corollary of those intersection tables not explicitly expressed in the inspected material. No independent mathematical audit was undertaken; finite checks are reproducibility evidence, not proof in untested characteristic or dimension.
