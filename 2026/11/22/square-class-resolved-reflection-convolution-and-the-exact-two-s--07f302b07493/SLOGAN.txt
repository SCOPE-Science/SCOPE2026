For every odd finite field, the complete square-class-resolved count of ordered products of two orthogonal reflections is determined by the quadratic rank of the moved plane and the Wall spinor sign. In ternary orthogonal groups this gives every target-fiber size, a sharp exclusion of one reflection type on degenerate moved planes, and exact two-step total-variation distance \((q^2-q-1)/(2q^2(q-1))\) from uniform on \(SO_3(q)\).
