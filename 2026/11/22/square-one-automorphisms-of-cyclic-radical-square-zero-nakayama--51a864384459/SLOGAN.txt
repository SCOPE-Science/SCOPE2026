For odd finite fields, square-one automorphisms of a radical-square-zero directed-cycle Nakayama algebra have binary-necklace conjugacy classes plus one half-turn class exactly when the cycle length is even. In the large-field limit, odd cycles almost surely have semisimple fixed algebra, whereas even cycles split equally between semisimple fixed algebra \(\mathbb F_q^n\) and the smaller cyclic Nakayama algebra \(A_{n/2}\).
