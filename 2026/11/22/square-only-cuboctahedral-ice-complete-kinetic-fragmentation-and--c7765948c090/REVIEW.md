# Scientific review: square-only cuboctahedral ice

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem is precisely limited to Eulerian orientations of the twelve-vertex cuboctahedral graph, square-face reversals only, and strictly positive square-specific hopping amplitudes for the spectral gap. Each edge belongs to one of the six squares, each vertex belongs to two, and the coefficient extraction in RESULT.md imposes exactly two outgoing arrows at every vertex. Two independent exhaustive programs (`verify_coordinates.py` and `verify_line_graph.py`) construct the graph differently, enumerate all 600 directed ice states, and obtain the same full labelled face-activity polynomial. The second program also computes actual square-flip graph components by BFS, finding 263 components of the asserted hypercube orders and 576 resonance edges. A separate algebraic argument proves that square reversals commute and preserve all other squares' flippability, hence that a component with active set \(I\) is exactly a full \(Q_{|I|}\). The quantum spectral decomposition follows by simultaneous diagonalization of independent bit flips. Strict positivity establishes the unique ground state and the sharp positive gap. The positive RK Laplacian has exactly one kernel vector per component.

Potential risks addressed: a finite sample is not treated as an infinite proof; the computations are exhaustive for this fixed finite graph. The six-square activity coefficients were cross-checked independently and not inferred from the previously known single-square marginal. All spectral statements specify which face moves are allowed, their weights and degeneracy conventions.

Lieb's foundational planar square-ice solution addresses a different geometry and thermodynamic enumeration. Independent searches for equivalent ring-exchange, cuboctahedral ice, hypercube sectors and square-only fragmentation in the examined mathematical research index did not surface these exact results; the closest square-ice torus and antiprism sector papers use different graphs and move sets.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The archived 2013 square-plaquette quantum-ice Hamiltonian supplies the physically motivated kinetic observable, and the 2024 curved-ice model explicitly motivates comparing local dynamics of defect triangles and ordinary squares. Suppressing triangle flips is a physically meaningful kinetic restriction and changes the problem from a connected tunnelling model to a highly fragmented one. The exact 263-sector result, the unusual lack of four- and five-face active components, and the 144 frozen configurations reveal structural restrictions not visible in the 600-state total or 192-per-square marginal. The complete weighted hopping spectrum and sharp gap are directly motivated finite-network quantum observables, while the 263-dimensional RK zero space relates the combinatorial fragmentation to degeneracy. This is a complete natural finite classification; no arbitrary slice or speculative thermodynamic extension is necessary for the stated result's mathematical value.

## Limitations and next validation

This result is for one finite graph, one exact face-reversal rule and positive face weights. It neither describes larger ices nor robustness under triangle resonances, defects or noise. Independent scholarly validation and definitive historical-priority confirmation have not been performed.
