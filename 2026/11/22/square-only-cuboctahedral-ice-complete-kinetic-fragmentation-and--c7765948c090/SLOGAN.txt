Restricting cuboctahedral quantum ice to its six square-ring flips splits the 600 labelled configurations into exactly 263 hypercube sectors, including 144 frozen states and one six-dimensional cube. Their complete joint face-activity polynomial determines the spectrum for arbitrary positive square-dependent hopping, a unique ground state with gap \(2\min_i t_i\), and exactly 263 Rokhsar--Kivelson zero modes.
