For binary periodic words \((1^h0^{q-h})^\omega\), Abelian maximal pattern complexity has a sharp square-root growth law \(P_{q,h}(k)=\sqrt{2hk}+O(h+1)\) whenever \(k\ge h\) and \(q\ge1+\lfloor\sqrt{2hk}\rfloor\). The proof identifies sampled factors with cyclic sums of nonnegative integer vectors and builds a staircase realizing consecutive counts.
