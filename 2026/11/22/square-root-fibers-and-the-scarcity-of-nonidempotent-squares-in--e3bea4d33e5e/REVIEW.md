# Scientific review: exact squaring-map fibers in cyclic inverse monoids

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem defines all input and output maps as partial translations of a finite cyclic set. The crucial identity \(f_{k,A}^2=f_{2k,A\cap(A-k)}\) follows from direct partial-function composition. Passing to the disjoint \(+k\)-orbits yields an exact product of cyclic Boolean AND-precursor problems. A cyclic output has a precursor exactly when it avoids an isolated-zero pattern \(101\). Positive-edge components force their endpoint vertices, while each intervening zero run gives \(F_{r-1}\) independent choices; all-zero targets produce a Lucas independent-set count and all-one targets one precursor. The empty map is counted once after eliminating \(n\) duplicate empty-domain representations. For odd \(n\), the bijection \(k\mapsto2k\) makes the totient-weighted image count exact, and a uniform forbidden-triple bound proves the asserted asymptotic without appealing to experiments. Boundary cases \(n=1\), orbit sizes one and two, zero output, and full output have been verified. The standalone program exhaustively composes actual partial maps through degree ten and all local Boolean inputs through cycle length sixteen, with no observed discrepancies. Finite checks do not establish infinite cases; the proof above does.

## Originality — PASS, qualified by historical-priority risk

The closest directly relevant full-text source is Fernandes (arXiv:2211.02155, 2022); its precise results concern sizes, ranks and presentations of the same cyclic inverse monoid. The original size formula is credited and not presented as new. Elliott–Levine–Mitchell (arXiv:2303.12387, 2023) concern the **number up to isomorphism of monogenic submonoids**, not square roots of specified transformations or the image of squaring; their classification of generated submonoids does not supply these elementwise image/fiber counts. Relevant indexed findings concerning Motzkin square-root fibers of an injective partial Catalan monoid and nilpotency/rank spectra of cycle partial-isometry monoids involve different operations or objects; the available record summaries do not imply the new theorem. The binary forbidden-word recurrence is standard finite automata, and its isolated combinatorial sequence is not claimed as original. Targeted indexed and literature searches found no direct covering result, but indexing is not exhaustive, and full texts of several published indexed findings were inaccessible; an alternate description of the same square map remains the principal risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The image of a natural power map is a structural invariant distinct from size, rank, presentations and the abstract classification of monogenic subobjects. The theorem provides **all individual square-root multiplicities in every degree** rather than merely counts or examples; in odd degrees it gives an explicit divisor-sum census and establishes a uniform asymptotic law. The asymptotic is nontrivial because all \(2^n\) idempotent partial identities are squares yet the other square outputs occupy exponentially small relative density, so the total square proportion behaves like \(1/n\). A direct parity obstruction explains why the clean image formula is restricted to odd degrees. The gap between even-degree individual fibers and their total union is an explicit further mathematical question.
