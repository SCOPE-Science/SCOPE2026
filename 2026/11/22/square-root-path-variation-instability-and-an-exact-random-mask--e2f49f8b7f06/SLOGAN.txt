Walsh–Hadamard soft thresholding of dyadic real sequences has worst-case two-input open-path variation gain of order \(\sqrt n\), despite being Euclidean-nonexpansive; the gain is attained and independent of the positive threshold. Independent fair selection of its nonconstant spectral modes has an exactly computable impulse-variation mean \(\frac12+\frac{n-2}{2}\binom{n/2}{n/4}/2^{n/2}\sim\sqrt{n/\pi}\) for \(n\ge4\).
