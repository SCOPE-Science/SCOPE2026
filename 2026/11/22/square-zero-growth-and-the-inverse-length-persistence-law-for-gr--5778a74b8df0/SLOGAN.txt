For every finite directed multigraph, the nonzero normal forms in its graph inverse semigroup have rational total and nonzero-square length enumerators computable directly from the adjacency resolvent. If the adjacency matrix is primitive with Perron root exceeding one, the proportion of length-\(n\) forms with nonzero square is asymptotic to an explicit positive constant divided by \(n\); moreover, no nonzero element has nilpotency index greater than two.
