For generalized dihedral group algebras \(\mathbb F_{2^f}[H\rtimes C_2]\) with \(|H|\) odd, the square-zero locus has exactly \(q^{|H|}\) points yet is geometrically irreducible and is covered by a fully classified arithmetic family of maximal linear spaces. Their common dimension is \((|H|+1)/2\), and Frobenius/inversion orbits determine every pairwise intersection dimension and the complete left-multiplication-rank spectrum.
