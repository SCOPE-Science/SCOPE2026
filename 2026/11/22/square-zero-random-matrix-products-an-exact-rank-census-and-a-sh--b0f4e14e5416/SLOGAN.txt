For products of two independent uniform \(n\times n\) matrices over a fixed finite field, square-zero products have exact rank-refined counts and probability \(\Theta_q(q^{-3n^2/7})\). Their conditioned factor and induced ranks concentrate around \(5n/7\) and \(2n/7\), and square-zero products are exponentially more frequent than uniformly sampled square-zero matrices.
