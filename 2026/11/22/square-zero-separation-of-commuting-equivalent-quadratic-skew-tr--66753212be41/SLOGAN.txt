In every odd characteristic, split and nonsplit quadratic skew truncations can have identical commutation graphs yet different exact square-zero loci: the split algebra has \(q^{n-1}(1+2\lfloor(n+1)/4\rfloor(q-1))\) square-zero elements and the nonsplit one has \(q^{n-1}\) for every odd \(n\ge3\). A complete valuation-layer count explains the discrepancy and specializes to modular dihedral group algebras.
