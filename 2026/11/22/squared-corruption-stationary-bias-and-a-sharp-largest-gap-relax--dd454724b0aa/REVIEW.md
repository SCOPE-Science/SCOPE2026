## Value — PASS
Residual rejection is intended to resist gross data corruption. This theorem exposes a concrete, unavoidable finite-weight stationary error in a natural inconsistent one-dimensional model and distinguishes **three different quantities**: how many corrupt rows cluster at a common value (stationary bias), how close the nearest corrupt level is (first error time), and how wide the largest consecutive gap is (global relaxation time). The sharp largest-gap coefficient and exact outlier-partition bounds yield testable calibration and reliability limitations that cannot be inferred from the source's one-step inequalities. The result is deliberately not presented as an improvement in performance or as a higher-dimensional theorem.
