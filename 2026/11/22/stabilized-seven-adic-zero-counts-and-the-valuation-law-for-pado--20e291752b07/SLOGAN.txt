For the standard Padovan recurrence, the number of zero terms modulo \(7^k\) in one least period is exactly \(12\), \(30\), or \(51\), according as \(k=1\), \(k=2\), or \(k\ge3\). Nine simple and three split seven-adic zero branches yield the complete valuation distribution, with \(\Pr(\nu_7(P_n)\ge k)=17/(16\cdot7^{k-1})\) for every \(k\ge3\).
