For the genus-five normalization of \(XYZ^3+X^5+Y^5+tX^3Y^2=0\) in characteristic eleven, the Jacobian \(11\)-rank is exactly \(0\), \(2\), or \(4\), according as \(t=0\), \(t^5=2\), or \(t\) is any other smooth nonzero parameter. The five rank-two parameters form one degree-five Frobenius orbit, while every smooth nonzero fiber has Cartier \(a\)-number one, so the entire family is nowhere ordinary.
