The ordinary dihedral-subgroup quotients of stable two-subset Kneser independence complexes have explicitly classified fundamental groups and integral homology for every cyclic length at least eight: cyclic-subgroup fundamental groups have order \(d/\gcd(d,6)\), while reflection-containing quotients are simply connected. At the first two simply connected covering parameters, the full rotation quotients are exactly \(M(\mathbb Z/4,1)\) and \(M(\mathbb Z/3,1)\vee S^2\), respectively.
