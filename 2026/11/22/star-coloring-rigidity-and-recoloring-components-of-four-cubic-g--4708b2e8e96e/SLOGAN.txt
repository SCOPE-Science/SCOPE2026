The minimum star vertex colorings of four cubic girth-six symmetric graphs all use four colors, but their single-vertex recoloring graphs exhibit a sharp contrast: the Heawood graph gives \(336Q_3\), while the Möbius–Kantor, Pappus, and Desargues graphs have exactly \(2280\), \(3072\), and \(7200\) frozen optimum colorings, with all mobile components explicitly classified. A complete finite enumeration and two standalone implementations establish every component count.
