A closed-neighborhood common-neighbor certificate shows that, when \((n-3)p^3-3\log n\to+\infty\), with high probability every vertex of \(G(n,p)\) anchors an entire nested chain of isometric induced subgraphs, with an explicit finite failure bound. Consequently almost all labelled and unlabelled connected graphs are sequentially distance preserving, proving the classical almost-all distance-preservation conjecture in a stronger form.
