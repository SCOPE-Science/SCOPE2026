For every finite projective plane of order \(q\ge2\), the named optimal packing-colouring recolouring graph of its point–line incidence graph is exactly \(2(q^2+q+1)(q^2+q)!\) disjoint \(q^2\)-leaf stars. An optimal colouring can move one vertex only by trading a nonincident point–line pair into the second colour; no singleton higher colour ever moves.
