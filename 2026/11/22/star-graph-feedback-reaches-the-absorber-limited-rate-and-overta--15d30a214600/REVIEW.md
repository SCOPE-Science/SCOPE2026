# Scientific review

**Correctness.** At unit bond clocks the exact SWAP-measurement adjoint identity gives the star incidence block, whose singular-value decomposition proves its entire pair-sector spectrum. The uniform-vector Rayleigh quotient gives \(\lambda|\mathcal F|/\binom n2\), which equals \(2\lambda/n\) on trees with feedback on every edge. Rationalization proves asymptotic saturation on the star. The sign of a quadratic at the exact ring rate gives the sharp crossover. The equality with the full Liouvillian gap in the connected SWAP feedback model is supplied by arXiv:2611.13094v2, Eq. (78), not by the finite tests or by pair closure alone.

**Originality.** The general pair generator, the absorber-fraction bound, the exact ring rate and the cited full-gap identity are established. The explicit star blocks, saturation of the tree bound and exact ordering relative to the ring are distinct from the known benchmarks. The closest indexed star–cycle size crossover concerns epidemic thresholds from adjacency spectra, not killed two-particle dynamics. An earlier independent star calculation outside the inspected literature cannot be excluded.

**Value.** This compares two natural sparse monitoring geometries in the same protocol. For equal edge clocks it determines when the star improves the rate and why the size scaling changes. The equal-per-site-clock comparison does not have the same ordering asymptotically. No hardware feasibility or preparation-time conclusion follows merely from these decay rates.
