For singlet-measurement feedback with independent unit-rate clocks on the edges of a star, the exact pair-relaxation rate approaches the universal absorber-fraction limit \(2\lambda/n\) without extra scrambling. At maximal feedback, the star overtakes the unit-edge-rate ring at precisely seven qubits, with equality at six; this is a pair-sector result, not an exact full-Liouvillian-gap or hardware-depth claim.
