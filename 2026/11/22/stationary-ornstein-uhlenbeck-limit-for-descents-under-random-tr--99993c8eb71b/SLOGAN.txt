Stationary cyclic and ordinary descent counts under uniform random transpositions, centered and divided by \(\sqrt n\), converge together on the \(n\)-swap time scale to the same Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process with drift \(-4Z\) and diffusion coefficient \(\sqrt{2/3}\). The cyclic count has an exact one-mode finite-time covariance, while its conditional swap variance concentrates even though the finite-state descent count is not itself a sufficient Markov state.
