In a first-passage-reset Brownian gas, the probability that a fixed fraction of particles is missing from a shrinking central interval below its known limiting hard edge decays at the sharp fraction-dependent stretched-exponential scale \(N^{1-1/u_\kappa}/\sqrt{\log N}\), with coefficient \(\sqrt{u_\kappa/\pi}\). Such depleted snapshots select a unique atypically long collective reset age \(L^2u_\kappa/(4D\log N)\) to leading order.
