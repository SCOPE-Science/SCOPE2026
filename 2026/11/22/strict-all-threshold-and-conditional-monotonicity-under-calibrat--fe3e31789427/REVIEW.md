Gaussian radial mixing transfers its positive cross-threshold comparison to squared positions. The conditional reset-age survival is piecewise explicit, increasing in the observed age; the gamma scale-family posterior is strictly monotone-likelihood-ratio ordered by the observed energy. This proves the conditional statement for all strictly positive parameters and horizons, including synchronized and independent boundaries. **Value — PASS.** Fixing the individual hazard is a physically meaningful equal-budget comparison: all single-particle observables are held invariant while simultaneous-excursion and conditional-risk dependence changes. The result classifies every positive radial threshold and every finite observation horizon instead of just one covariance or an extreme-tail exponent. It provides a reproducible benchmark for synchrony detection in tagged Brownian measurements and explains the important signed-versus-radial boundary. These statements need neither a downstream general theorem nor a change to the original model.

The new claim requires complete origin resets, independent Gaussian driving, fixed-size uniform batches, calibrated Poisson rate, and origin initial conditions or stationarity. Prior fixed-global-rate nonmonotonicity is not contradicted. Unindexed equivalent analysis remains a publication risk.
