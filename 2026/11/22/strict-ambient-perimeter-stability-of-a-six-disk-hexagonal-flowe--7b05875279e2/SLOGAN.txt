Six equal unit disks placed at the center and five consecutive vertices of a regular hexagon form a strictly isolated local minimizer of convex-hull perimeter against *all* feasible perturbations. The exact first-order contact-gap law has sharp stability coefficient \(1-1/\sqrt3\); among all configurations in which one disk touches the other five, the unique minimum disk-hull perimeter is \(8+2\sqrt3+2\pi\).
