In a harmonically trapped planar swimmer making random quarter-turns, the stationary position-angle fingerprint \(\mathbb E[\cos(4\theta)]\) decreases strictly from \(1\) to \(0\) as the turn-to-trap rate \(\gamma/\mu\) increases, and equals \(\pi-3\) at rate ratio one. Consequently one stationary angular harmonic uniquely calibrates the turn-to-trap ratio without knowing propulsion speed.
