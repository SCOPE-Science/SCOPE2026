Two opposing first-passage clocks measured along the same Poisson-reset Brownian path have an exactly **negative covariance** for every pair of target distances: \(\operatorname{Cov}(T_-,T_+)=-(A+B)\sinh A\sinh B/[r^2(\sinh A+\sinh B)]\), with \(A=a\sqrt{r/D}\) and \(B=b\sqrt{r/D}\). Their mixed second moment diverges only as \(r^{-1/2}\) under weak resetting, even though the product of their means diverges as \(r^{-1}\).
