For a single-source branching walk on Cartesian products of a symmetric finite-range lattice walk, the positive growth eigenvalue is strictly convex as a function of dimension wherever it exists: every additional coordinate causes a smaller loss in growth rate. At high source intensity \(\beta\), the second dimensional difference is asymptotic to \(2(\sum_{z\ne0}b_z^2)^2/\beta^3\), independent of all higher details of the jump kernel.
