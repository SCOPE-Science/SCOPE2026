For every \(n\ge6\), the homogeneous random-cluster model on \(K_{2,n}\) makes the connection events of two disjoint pairs of leaves strictly positively correlated at every activity and every cluster weight \(q>0\), including \(0<q<1\) where standard positive association fails. The exact covariance is explicit and decays at a sharp exponential rate as the number of leaves increases with the model parameters fixed.
