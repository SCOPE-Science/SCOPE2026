For ordinary Motzkin excursions, the signed Walsh sum over sharp \(UD\)-peak starting positions is minimized when **every** position is marked; it is strictly positive in all lengths \(n\geq4\). Consequently sharp-peak-profile convolution is invertible in every such length, with exact Hilbert condition number \(M_n/p_n\sim(3\sqrt3/2)(3/2)^n\).
