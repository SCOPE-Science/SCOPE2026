# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Reintegrated the two covariance products on the intervals cut by the sensors. In the Gaussian information trace, the coefficient of cosh(2α(c−1/2)) is strictly negative at every fixed gap, so a global optimum must be centered. With T=e^(−α), b=1+η and gap coordinate t=2αd, the derivative numerator at t=0 is (1−T)(b−1)^2; at t=α it is b[−(1−T²)(b²+T²)+4αT²b]/2. The latter is negative because b+T²/b≥1+T² and 1−T⁴>4αT². Compactness and the strict boundary signs give 0<d<1/2. For ε=1/b the information divided by ε has limit 2[1−T cosh(2αd)]/α with strictly negative second derivative uniformly on the compact separation interval. Smoothness therefore gives strict concavity for small ε. Its slope at d=0 is 4(1−T)ε+O(ε²) and limiting curvature there is −8αT. The resulting root and optimized quadratic gain give both displayed constants and orders, with fixed α. This proves uniqueness only in the high-noise regime.
