For two equally noisy observations of a stationary exponential-covariance Gaussian path, every integrated-posterior-variance optimum is an interior, midpoint-symmetric pair with distinct times, at every finite positive noise level. At high noise the optimal half-separation is \((e^\alpha-1)/(2\alpha\eta)+O_\alpha(\eta^{-2})\), and forcing the best collocated design costs \((1-e^{-\alpha})^2/(\alpha e^{-\alpha}\eta^3)+O_\alpha(\eta^{-4})\) in integrated posterior variance.
