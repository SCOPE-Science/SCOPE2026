For every integer \(n\ge3\), the affine-invariant isotropic constant of the intersection of unit disks centered at the vertices of a concentric regular \(n\)-gon grows strictly as the center radius moves from zero toward one. It rises from the disk value \(1/(16\pi^2)\) toward a sharp unattained regular-polygon value \((1+\tan^2(\pi/n)/3)^2/(16n^2\tan^2(\pi/n))\), with exact area and polar-moment formulas along the entire deformation.
