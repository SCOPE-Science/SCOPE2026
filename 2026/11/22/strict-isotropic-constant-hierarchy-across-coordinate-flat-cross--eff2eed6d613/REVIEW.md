# Mathematical review

**Correctness (PASS).** Orthant coarea and uniform Dirichlet moments establish the base root-section covariance. Beta mixing of an \(\ell_1\) join gives the whole covariance spectrum and Euclidean volume. The adjacent isotropic quotient reduces to an integer-binomial estimate and a strictly positive exact rational factor. Boundary cases \(m=2\), \(m=n\), and \(n=3\) are covered; the independent hull computation verifies low-dimensional instances, without being treated as an infinite proof.

**Originality (PASS, priority risk retained).** The primary literature discusses logarithmic concavity of section volume, algorithms for moment integrals, and isotropic constants of *regular* polytopes. A published exact root-polytope inertia computation already gives one endpoint and the endpoint comparison, which is explicitly credited. Neither that comparison nor the inspected papers contains the strict intermediate-rank isotropic chain or the all-integer positive adjacent ratio certificate. Searches by aliases and by \(\ell_1\)-join reductions did not identify equivalent coverage; unpublished/unindexed literature remains a risk.

**Value (PASS).** At fixed ambient dimension, support rank is a canonical discrete geometric invariant of the hyperplane normal. The theorem resolves the natural entire-rank comparison, not an arbitrarily chosen slice: although volume and the two endpoint constants can be separately derived, affine isotropic constants need not inherit rank monotonicity from either volume or endpoint comparison. An explicit strict margin and exact neighboring-rank ratio quantify every step, giving a reusable model for asymptotic convex-geometric section invariants.

The claim is restricted to equal-coordinate normals. Sources inspected: Nayar–Tkocz, Brandenburg–De Loera–Meroni, Kipp, and the published complete-graph root-polytope finding. Broader bibliographic coverage and independent mathematical auditing remain outstanding.
