For two congruent overlapping planar disks, separating their centers strictly increases the affine-invariant isotropic constant of their intersection from the disk value \(L^4=1/(16\pi^2)\) toward the sharp unattained parabolic-lens limit \(L^4=9/1400\). An exact rational Taylor certificate proves the monotonicity for every separation, beyond previously known covariance-anisotropy and polar-volume-product laws.
