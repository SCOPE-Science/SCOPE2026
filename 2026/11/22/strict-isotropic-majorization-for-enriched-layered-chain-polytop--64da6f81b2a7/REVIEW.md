PASS. The exact free-sum representation follows from the signed-antichain vertex definition. Uniform-volume integration produces a Dirichlet vector of block sup-norm radii. Conditional cube-shell moments give every covariance eigenvalue and the exact volume. The potential \(f(k)=k\log[(k+1)(k+2)]-2\log(k!)\) has strictly positive second differences for all integer \(k\ge1\): a direct rational check handles the first case, and sharp elementary logarithm inequalities handle the full remaining range. Strict transfers establish all extrema and equality conditions. Exact rational hull computations independently match small cases.
