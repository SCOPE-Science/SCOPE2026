For two independent equal-noise measurements of Brownian motion, every integrated-path-variance-optimal design uses distinct interior times, no matter how large the finite noise variance. At high noise \(\eta\), the unique optimum has times \(1-1/(2\eta)+O(\eta^{-2})\) and \(1-1/(6\eta)+O(\eta^{-2})\), improving even the *best* repeated-site design by \(1/(18\eta^3)+O(\eta^{-4})\) in integrated variance.
