In the symmetry-preserving radial hull of a regular icosahedron and its outward face normals, the exact Santaló volume product rises strictly from the icosahedron to the sharp thirty-two-vertex inscribed volume maximizer. Its explicit polar-volume law changes at the regular rhombic triacontahedron, \(t=2a/(1+\sqrt5/3)\), yet the product remains strictly increasing.
