In a transient leaky-immunity SIRI epidemic, vaccinees experience strictly fewer repeat infections per primary infection than unvaccinated hosts, even though both groups have the same susceptibility after their first infection; all-or-none vaccination instead gives equal repeat-to-primary ratios. A new strict three-level inequality converts the source model's known aggregate final size into computable upper and lower bounds on each subgroup's otherwise undetermined cumulative reinfections.
