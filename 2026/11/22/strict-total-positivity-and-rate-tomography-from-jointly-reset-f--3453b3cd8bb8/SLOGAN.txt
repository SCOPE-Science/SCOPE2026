For a globally Poisson-reset Edwards–Wilkinson interface, squared cosine/sine Fourier energies have an exact strictly totally positive Cauchy covariance matrix at every finite cutoff, despite zero cross-covariance of unsquared modes. A normalized off-diagonal energy covariance, calibrated using same-wavenumber sine–cosine covariances, determines the reset rate exactly when diffusivity is known, independently of thermal noise strength.
