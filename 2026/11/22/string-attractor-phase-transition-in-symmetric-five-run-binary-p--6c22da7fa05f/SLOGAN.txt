For every positive triple \(a,b,c\), the palindromic binary word \(0^a1^b0^c1^b0^a\) needs three string-attractor positions exactly when \(a,b\ge2\) and \(c<a\); otherwise two suffice. Among symmetric five-run binary words of length \(n\), the three-position class has generating function \(2z^9/((1-z^2)^2(1-z^3))\) and limiting density \(1/3\).
