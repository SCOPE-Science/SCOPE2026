For every \(n\ge5\), every strong odd coloring of \(\mathrm{KG}(n,2)\) is a partition of the edges of \(K_n\) into singleton edges, edge-disjoint triangles and at most one full star (the last permitted only for even \(n\)). For odd \(n\), optimal colorings are exactly maximum triangle packings with singleton leaves; for even \(n\ge8\), they are exactly full-star colorings of a vertex together with a maximum triangle packing of the remaining complete graph, while \(n=6\) is a two-type tie. These descriptions give a four-case exact formula for \(\chi_{\mathrm{so}}(\mathrm{KG}(n,2))\) modulo six.
