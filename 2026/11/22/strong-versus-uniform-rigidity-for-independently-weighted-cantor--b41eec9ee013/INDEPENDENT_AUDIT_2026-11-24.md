Strictly positive probabilities on all finite digit cylinders give full support on the compact K_S. The multiplication operator has spectrum equal to the essential range, here the entire image of K_S, and its norm return is the supremum of the phase error there. Strong return is equivalent to L² return of 1 by bounded multiplication and density of bounded functions. Along cuts with g_j=s_{j+1}−s_j→∞, n=b^{s_j} gives integer head and uniformly vanishing tail. With eventually bounded gaps L, for every large m the first s_j with b^{s_j}≥2m satisfies 1/(2b^L)<m/b^{s_j}≤1/2, so a single-digit point gives a positive norm obstruction for any bias. The conditional variance in that digit is 4p_j(1−p_j)sin²(πm/b^{s_j}), giving a positive L² obstruction for an interior limiting bias. For p_j→0 the expected nonnegative tail along b^{s_j} vanishes. For p_j→1, split off the deterministic all-one tail a_j, and approximate q_j a_j by an integer with 1≤q_j≤Q_j and error ≤1/Q_j, where Q_j→∞ and Q_j sup_{i>j}(1−p_i)→0. Dominated convergence for the linear term and 2−2Re of the product give exactly 4 times the sum of sin²(π b^(−h)), positive and finite.
