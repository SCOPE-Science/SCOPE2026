For a Sturmian opportunity clock with gap lengths \(a<b\), projected reset-to-threshold firing has an exact jump in rate at travel time \(c=a\). Above that threshold and through \(c=b\), the spike-gap word remains Sturmian but its slope renormalizes from \(\alpha\) to \(\alpha/(1-\alpha)\), with an all-horizon physical timing error strictly below \(a\).
