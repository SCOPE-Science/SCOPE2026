For reflected Brownian surface reaction with a positive local-time threshold, Poisson position resets that retain boundary-contact progress have a unique mean-time-optimal rate at which the completion-time coefficient of variation is strictly below one: \(\operatorname{Var}(T_h)=(\mathbb ET_h)^2-[h e^{z_*}/\sqrt{r_*D}]^2\). The optimal rate decreases strictly as the required accumulated contact increases, and is strictly higher than the optimum when each reset erases that progress.
