For two independent uniformly chosen stable positions of an \(n\)-tier Pyranim game, the probability of game isomorphism has \(n\)-th root tending to \(1\). A factorial-sized class of positions, assembled from many distinct connected unit interval components, proves the quantitative lower bound \(\Pr(X_n\cong Y_n)\ge\exp(-O(n\log\log n/\log n))\) and resolves the exponential-scale question posed in arXiv:2611.16153.
