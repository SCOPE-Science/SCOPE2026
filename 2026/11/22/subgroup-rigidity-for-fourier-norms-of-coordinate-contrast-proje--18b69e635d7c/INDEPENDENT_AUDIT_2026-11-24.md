# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The zero-sum vectors on the n-shore form the m-eigenspace for 1≤m<n: the other shore contrasts have eigenvalue n and the two block-constant eigenvalues are 0 and N. The projector has rank n−1 and Frobenius norm √(n−1). In unitary Fourier convention UPU^T differs from M=UPU* by inversion of the column character, so neither entrywise absolute sum nor Frobenius norm changes. Translate B=A∖C to contain zero; this preserves the Fourier magnitudes and gives M1=0. In each row the off-diagonal entries sum to minus the nonnegative diagonal. Summing the triangle inequalities gives 2 tr M, with equality precisely when the off-diagonal entries are real nonpositive (a zero diagonal then requires the off-diagonal entries to vanish). In that case the quadratic form is a nonnegative weighted graph Laplacian. For every b∈B, Ue_b lies in the kernel. Each positive-weight edge must therefore join characters agreeing on B and thus on H=⟨B⟩. As an independent necessity check, for any h∈H the vector Ue_h is constant on all such edges, and hence is in the kernel of M. It follows that P e_h=0. For h∈C its squared norm would be 1−1/n>0; thus H⊂B and B=H. This also bypasses the frozen Parseval contradiction. For sufficiency, translate a subgroup coset to H. For nonidentity χ its sum over C is −m if χ is trivial on H and zero otherwise. The identity character row is zero. For distinct nonidentity χ,ψ in different restriction classes the diagonal-term Fourier coefficient at χψ⁻¹ vanishes and so does the product term. In the same class those entries have the form −(m+|S_C(χ)|²/n)/N, hence attain the rowwise bound. Lagrange and existence of every divisor subgroup order in a finite abelian group give attainment exactly for m|N, equivalent to m|n. The finite number of subsets then gives strict inequality otherwise, without claiming the nondivisible minimizers or value.
