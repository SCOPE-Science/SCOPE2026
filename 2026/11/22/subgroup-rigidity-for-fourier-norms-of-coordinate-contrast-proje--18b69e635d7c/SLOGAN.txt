For every finite abelian group of order \(m+n\) with \(1\le m<n\), the two-dimensional Fourier \(\ell^1\)-norm of the orthogonal projection onto zero-sum vectors supported on any \(n\)-point subset is at least \(2(n-1)\). Equality holds exactly when the missing \(m\)-point set is a subgroup coset; equivalently, the eigenvalue-\(m\) projector of \(K_{m,n}\) has sharp Fourier ratio \(2\sqrt{n-1}\) exactly when \(m\mid n\).
