For the rank-one unipotent Alexander quandle \(V^2\) over every finite \(\mathbb F_p\)-vector space, the complete size distribution of nonempty embedded subquandles modulo **all** ambient automorphisms is an explicit two-stage affine Burnside sum. Over \(\mathbb F_p\) its polynomial is \(\mathscr B_p(z)+\mathscr B_p(z^p)-2-z^p\), and over \(\mathbb F_2^2\) and \(\mathbb F_3^2\) the exact orbit totals are \(9\) and \(39\).
