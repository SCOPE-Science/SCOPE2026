For the 2023 discontinuous density-dispersal logistic map at \(\mu=2\), an attracting period-two orbit disappears at \(\varepsilon_*=(\sqrt5-1)/2\), leaving a non-invariant ghost at the boundary. Above this threshold, the first descent below the switching density from \(x_0=3/5\) takes \(2\log_2\log(1/(\varepsilon-\varepsilon_*))+O(1)\) iterates, a superattracting double-logarithmic law rather than the standard logarithmic transient.
