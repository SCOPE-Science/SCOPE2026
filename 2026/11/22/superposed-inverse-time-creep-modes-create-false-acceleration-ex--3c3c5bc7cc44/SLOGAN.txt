Adding several exact inverse-time creep velocities makes their reciprocal velocity strictly concave and forces every two-point straight-line extrapolation to predict a time *after* the earliest component singularity, even though each individual component predicts its own singularity exactly. For two distinct singular times, the measured acceleration exponent has exactly one false peak above \(2\), reaches \(9/4\) at equal amplitudes, and can grow without bound as the later-mode amplitude increases.
