For every admissible cyclic-by-cyclic prime-power group, the full fiber distribution of each product of \(t\) independent commutators is exactly a sum-of-determinants law over \(\mathbb Z/p^k\mathbb Z\), independent of the action depth. Consequently, for odd primes there are equal-order groups of classes \(2\) and \(k+1\) with identical exact fibers for *every* such surface word.
