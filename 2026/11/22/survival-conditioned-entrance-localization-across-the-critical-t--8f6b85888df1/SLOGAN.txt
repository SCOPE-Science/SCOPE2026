For a birth–death walk absorbed at zero, conditioned long-time survival selects a hidden entrance-location phase transition under critically geometric initial tails: diffusive gamma-shaped entrances for \(0<\beta<2\), a uniform logarithmic entrance scale at \(\beta=2\), and a tight power-law entrance law for \(\beta>2\). In every regime the entrance limit is independent of the same minimal quasi-stationary terminal state.
