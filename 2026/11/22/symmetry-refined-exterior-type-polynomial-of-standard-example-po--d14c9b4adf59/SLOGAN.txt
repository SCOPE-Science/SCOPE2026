For the \(2n\)-vertex standard-example poset, exterior one-point types in the generic poset have an explicit two-variable profile, while the full automorphism group reduces their \(3^n+2^{n+1}+2n-2\) named types to exactly \((n+1)(n+6)/2\) symmetry classes. The proof classifies every class by the old points below and above the new point.
