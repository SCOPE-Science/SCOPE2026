# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The arbitrary-field classification is derived directly from the two original averaging equations and composition idempotence, not from a black-box criterion. The central-image elimination uses the nondegenerate symplectic form; the two cases \(c=0,1\) are exhaustive over a field. The \(c=1\) condition is equivalent to the image/kernel being orthogonal complementary nondegenerate symplectic spaces. Symplectic transitivity, the central-shear action and stabilizer orders prove the orbit and cardinality formulas. The reduced component statement follows from rank-locally-constant idempotents and smooth Grassmannian bundles. The supplied executable checks both original equations in explicit small examples. Risk: scheme nonreducedness is neither investigated nor claimed; arithmetic checks for \(q=9\) are integer-substitution tests, not exhaustive computations over that field.

## Originality — PASS against the inspected evidence, not independent priority certification
The previously published full Heisenberg retraction census already proves the entire \(c=0\) branch, which is credited without a new-priority assertion. The full-text comparisons of Laraiedh–Silvestrov (arXiv:2304.00662), Das–Sen (arXiv:2308.16668), and Das (arXiv:2403.06250) locate the general averaging identity and theoretical framework but not the all-dimensional \(c=1\) classification. Published Heisenberg splitting Rota–Baxter projections require a subalgebra kernel, while the new nonidentity \(c=1\) operators have nonabelian kernels that fail this condition. Existing three-dimensional embedding tensor work and the rank-one center projection are treated as prior boundary cases. The higher-dimensional symplectic nonendomorphism branch, exact counts, automorphism transitivity and forbidden rank spectrum are not implied by the inspected records. Residual risk: an alternate general embedding-tensor classification may dominate this result; literature coverage does not establish uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Idempotent averaging is a motivated operator identity related to embedding tensors, but the distinction between subalgebra projections and Lie retracts changes sharply on Heisenberg algebras. The classification is complete for every dimension and characteristic, determines the genuinely non-homomorphic family with a single conjugacy orbit per nontrivial rank, and derives an exact all-field rank gap and finite-field census. The already known retraction count is used for contrast, not to inflate the contribution. The generality and structural orbit theorem distinguish this from a routine small-matrix computation.

The proof does not classify non-idempotent averaging operators or prove reducedness of the original operator scheme. Relevant closest sources are the previously published Heisenberg retraction and splitting Rota–Baxter classifications, the Hom-Heisenberg embedding-tensor census and arXiv:2304.00662, 2308.16668 and 2403.06250.
