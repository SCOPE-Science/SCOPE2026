For every finite-field Heisenberg Lie algebra of dimension at least five, the idempotent averaging operators consist of the already known isotropic-image Lie retractions and a complementary family indexed by nondegenerate symplectic subspaces. Each non-retraction rank \(2r+1\) forms one automorphism orbit with an exact symplectic-group count, and every even rank above the isotropic dimension bound is forbidden.
