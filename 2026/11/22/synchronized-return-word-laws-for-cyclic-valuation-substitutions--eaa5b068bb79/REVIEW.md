# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The factorization \(\{t:u_t=r\}=b^r\{k:\nu_b(k)\equiv0\pmod q\}\) handles all composite as well as prime bases. Omissions in the base shell are isolated, so the gap dichotomy is exhaustive. The equality of all return-type sequences follows by scaling the ordered occurrence positions; a blockwise coding gives the exact morphic word. Divisibility-class densities give the geometric type law and show each type has positive probability. Two independently formulated programs passed more than nine million individual string/type comparisons or code-symbol comparisons. Finite samples are corroboration, not an infinite certificate.

## Originality
PASS under the inspected literature and searched records, subject to priority risk. Durand establishes a general finiteness/characterization theorem rather than the explicit formulas. Huang–Wen already cover return sequences of the classical binary period-doubling word, which is credited; their binary theorem does not imply the simultaneous all-base and all-modulus letter synchronization with exact frequencies. Espinoza–Popoli–Stipulanti (2026) study the use of return-word decomposition to understand substitutional complexity, but do not give this special arithmetic return law. Semantic searches of related public findings located generalized period-doubling factor-complexity, unbordered return-gap, and palindrome results, none of which states or implies the present assertion. Unindexed equivalent work remains possible.

## Value
PASS. Return words are canonical induced dynamical encodings, and their distinct types/frequencies are nontrivial computable invariants. The result gives a complete alphabet-uniform classification for a natural family of primitive cyclic substitutions, not just an isolated small parameter: it identifies the exact return-language, exact transduction of the original fixed point, a simultaneous synchronization across all letters, and exact geometric frequencies with a mean-return identity. This is useful as a reproducible model for return-word reconstruction and induced symbolic dynamics; it does not claim a general theorem on return words of arbitrary factors.
