Among the \(66\) smooth bidegree-\((4,3)\) curves filling \(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1\) over \(\mathbb F_2\), exactly \(12\) have tame cyclic trigonal projections and \(54\) have wildly ramified \(S_3\) projections. Their eight branch values form either a single Frobenius orbit of degree eight or two orbits of degrees two and six, with every local different exponent equal to two.
