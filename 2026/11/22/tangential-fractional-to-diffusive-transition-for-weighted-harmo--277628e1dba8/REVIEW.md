# Review of tangential harmonic Berezin dispersion

## Correctness — PASS

The horizontal Fourier mode norm density for the harmonic half-plane Bergman space gives the kernel \(C_\beta\operatorname{Re}(y+v-i(x-u))^{-\beta}\) for every \(\beta>1\). The reproducing identity gives a normalized positive density. Its tail follows by one scaling substitution and a single integrable domination; the explicit constant is evaluated by real and complex beta integrals. The characteristic-function asymptotics use a directly justified cosine-integral scaling (fractional), logarithmic split (borderline), and Fourier-Plancherel second-moment calculation (diffusive). The exact critical multiplier is independently derived by Fourier transforming the squared rational kernel. The included checker passes 58 finite diagnostics, with no finite calculation used to certify infinitely many parameter values.

## Originality — PASS, provisional historical priority

The nearest primary text is Jaroslav Bradík, *Harmonic Berezin transform on half-space with vertical weights*, arXiv:2310.17562, first submitted 26 October 2023. The inspected HTML text covers its full introduction, Lemma 2, Theorems 1 and 2, proof of Theorem 2, and verification section. Its Fourier representation concerns the base harmonic kernel, and its Berezin asymptotic theorem explicitly restricts symbols to the **vertical** coordinate. No tangential horizontal-frequency tail, exponent-zero phase transition, or exact \(2/\alpha\) variance law is stated or obtained by an implication of that theorem. Engliš, arXiv:1601.03716, gives high-power asymptotics in a different limiting variable; only its abstract could be inspected. Choe–Koo–Lee (2007) concerns Toeplitz/Schatten characterization, not a sharp horizontal multiplier, but its full text was not examined. Broader priority remains a risk, and no claim of exhaustive discovery is made.

Related mathematical records on weighted harmonic Bergman ball compressions and disk kernel zero thresholds concern distinct spaces or different invariants; general Fock Gaussian-Weyl Berezin results concern a different kernel and limit. Actual source-alias and implication queries were made against the mathematical findings database. None of the nearest returned descriptions implies the present horizontal Fourier law; database absence alone is not evidence of uniqueness.

## Value — PASS

This determines the exact leading regularity of a natural smoothing transform in the tangential direction for the entire admissible power-weight family. The change between a fractional order, a sharp logarithmic borderline, and finite-variance diffusion happens exactly at the unweighted case and has explicit positive constants. The critical multiplier and the exact second moment furnish quantitative benchmarks for horizontal smoothing and PDE-multiplier comparisons. This is a motivated structural threshold rather than an arbitrary finite parameter substitution.

## Limitations

Fixed exponent only; no uniform double scaling at zero weight, arbitrary-domain theorem, higher-dimensional extension or independent external validation. Some older primary material was available only as an abstract; historical priority is therefore provisional.
