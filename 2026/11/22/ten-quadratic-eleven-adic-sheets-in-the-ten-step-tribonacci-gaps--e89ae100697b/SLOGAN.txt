The ten-step Tribonacci differences \(T_{n+10}-T_n\) have ten disjoint, exactly quadratic value sheets over \(\mathbb Z_{11}\): each is a translated copy of \(11(3\cdot7^r)\mathbb Z_{11}^{\,2}\). Their complete image has Haar measure \(5/12\), with explicit all-depth residue counts, square-root fiber multiplicities, and least period \(10\cdot11^{k-1}\) modulo \(11^k\).
