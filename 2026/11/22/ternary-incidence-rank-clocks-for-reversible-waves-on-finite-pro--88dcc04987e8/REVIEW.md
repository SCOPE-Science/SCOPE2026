# Scientific assessment: ternary projective-plane incidence wave

## Correctness — PASS
The statement fixes the plane axioms, finite state-field characteristic, both time levels, incidence rank, time valuation and primitive-orbit normalization. The proof derives the universal incidence identity and splits all possible plane orders modulo three. For orders congruent to two it proves a constant order-four sector plus an order-eight zero-sum sector. For orders congruent to one it obtains one size-four temporal Jordan block and the stated number of size-two blocks at each eigenvalue. For orders divisible by three it proves the nilpotent sector's Jordan multiplicities from the incidence rank and the sharp twelve-step order; this also derives the fourth-iterate code-rank formula. Fixed-space exponents follow from the exact module decomposition, and Möbius inversion counts primitive periods without overcounting. No sampled computation is elevated to the all-plane proof. Two independently written exact checkers, with different projective-plane coordinate constructions and matrix algorithms, reproduce the finite exemplars in the Verification document. All formula boundaries and field-extension quantifiers have been checked. The correctness claim is limited to the mathematical specification in RESULT.md; an external independent audit has not occurred.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The full text of the closest published characteristic-two incidence-wave classification was inspected and compared by implication: its hypotheses require characteristic two, its temporal orders are six/twelve, and its use of binary incidence rank cannot give the stated characteristic-three trichotomy or the fourth-iterate ternary rank identity. The full text of the published ternary Hamming-graph wave paper was also inspected; it concerns Cartesian products of complete graphs, not arbitrary projective-plane incidence graphs, and does not imply their modular incidence decomposition. Vlasov's foundational reversible second-order construction is treated as prior work and contains no projective-plane temporal census. The Lavrauw–Storme–Van de Voorde projective incidence-code paper concerns codeword and dual-code structure, not this temporal system. Targeted semantic record searches and primary-literature searches for equivalent formulas, aliases, and greater generality did not identify a decisive prior theorem. The absence of a search hit is not taken as proof of priority: a general finite-field graph-wave module classification or unindexed preprint could cover this result. This remains the main originality risk.

## Value — PASS
Projective-plane incidence graphs are a canonical all-order family, including non-Desarguesian planes, with exact design identities but potentially variable modular code ranks. The all-plane characteristic-three period trichotomy distinguishes three arithmetic regimes with genuinely different temporal periods eight, twelve, eighteen. Beyond simple order determination, the exact periodic-point formula links the observable dynamics to the ternary incidence-code rank precisely when the plane order is divisible by three; it is therefore a structural relationship between finite geometry and reversible dynamics, not an arbitrary small-graph census. The result gives the exact primitive-cycle populations with a single rank input and identifies the precise boundary of this dependence. It does not claim new incidence ranks, a general theorem for other field characteristics, or a practical plane-isomorphism test.
