Conditioning an iid-endpoint random interval graph to be triangle-free turns its entire interval matching into an iid three-letter renewal word: connected components are caterpillar trees, their count is exactly \(1+\operatorname{Bin}(n-1,1/3)\), and the limiting degree probabilities are \(1/9,16/27,16/3^{d+2}\) for degrees \(0,1,d\ge2\), respectively. The largest component and maximum degree grow on distinct scales, \(\log n/\log(3/2)\) and \(\log n/\log3\).
