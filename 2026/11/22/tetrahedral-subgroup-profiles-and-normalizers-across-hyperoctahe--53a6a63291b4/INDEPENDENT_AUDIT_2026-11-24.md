# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

A transitive block of sign pairs is specified by the stabilizer K of one pair and its sign homomorphism chi. Induction gives the converse; changing the anchor conjugates (K,chi). An equivariant signed bijection has precisely 2[N_A4(K,chi):K] choices for the signed image of the anchor. The kernel is the core of ker chi; only the trivial and unsigned three-pair blocks have nontrivial kernel. All seven types are invariant under Aut(A4): the nonzero V4 characters are already conjugate, and the other characters are determined by stabilizer type and triviality. Conjugation by S4 realizes 24 distinct automorphisms and no more are possible because an automorphism is determined by the images of a generating 3-cycle and involution. Consequently the full 24 multiplies the centralizer, with factorials from identical blocks. The two generating functions follow by product enumeration and orbit–stabilizer, subtracting exactly the profiles supported on types 0,1, including rank zero. Partial fractions of the proper class series give quasipolynomials for all n, not merely eventually; a primitive twelfth-root pole survives subtraction. The order-seven pole at 1 has coefficient 1/15552 in powers of (1-z), producing the stated main term. An independent finite reconstruction enumerated the subgroups and every binary character inside the permutation model of A4, checked conjugacy under A4 and S4, kernels and centralizer factors, and confirmed the displayed class and subgroup sequences. This corroborates the table; the argument, not the finite ranks, establishes all n.

PASS

The supported distinct claim is the explicit faithful image-conjugacy and normalizer classification together with the resulting class quasipolynomial. The total embedded-subgroup generating function by itself is a recoverable specialization of prior counting methods, not an independent novelty. The strongest accessible comparisons do not cover the complete claim.

## value

PASS

This is a complete all-rank classification of embeddings of a natural small nonabelian group in the standard signed permutation family. The two degree-three actions have different kernels and centralizers: conflating them changes even the first faithful rank. The profile determines the conjugacy multiplicity of each actual subgroup and the class series has a sharp least period, quantities not supplied by a single total homomorphism count. The work is an explicit structural specialization rather than a new theory of wreath products.
