Although the Petersen graph has the known equal optimum \(\rho_{\mathrm{opt}}(P)=\pi_{\mathrm{opt}}(P)=4\), it has exactly \(175\) solvable four-pebble distributions under rubbling and only \(10\) under ordinary pebbling. The extra optimum placements have explicit geometric criteria: triple-edge configurations for the double-plus-single case and induced forest types for four singleton pebbles.
