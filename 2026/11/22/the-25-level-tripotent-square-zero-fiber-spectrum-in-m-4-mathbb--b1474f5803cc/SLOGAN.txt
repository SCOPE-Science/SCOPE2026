For every binary \(4\times4\) matrix, the number of ordered decompositions into a tripotent and a square-zero matrix has one of exactly 25 positive values; an exact census identifies all 34 similarity-class fibers. The zero matrix alone has one such decomposition, while the sharp minimum for nonzero matrices is three and occurs precisely on the similarity class of \(0_2\oplus C(x^2+x+1)\).
