The Fermat quartic's 44 distinct geometric bitangent-contact points form an explicit disjoint union of degree-12 hyperflex and degree-32 complete-intersection schemes. Their exact Frobenius cycle types depend only on \(q\bmod24\): for \(q\equiv17\pmod{24}\), all 28 bitangent lines are rational but only 12 contact points are rational; for \(q\equiv5\pmod{24}\), 16 bitangent lines are rational while none of their contact points is.
