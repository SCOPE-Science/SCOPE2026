For every odd prime and every residue-field extension, the natural affine permutation group of a length-two commutative chain ring has an explicit complete cycle index: its fixed-point distribution depends only on the residue-field order, but its \(p^2\)-cycle spectrum detects whether the ring has characteristic \(p\) or \(p^2\). This extends the classical prime-cyclic case to all residue-field extensions and gives an exact characteristic/reconstruction dichotomy.
