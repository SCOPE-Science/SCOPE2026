# The anti-cycle apex triangle-free complex: two sphere dimensions and dihedral signs

## Finding
For each integer \(n\geq3\), let \(C_n\) denote the graph cycle on vertex set \(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z\), let \(G_n=K_1\vee\overline{C_n}\) be the graph obtained by adjoining one universal vertex \(h\) to the complement of the cycle, and let \(X_n=\operatorname{CF}_3(G_n)\) be the simplicial complex of vertex sets inducing no triangle. Then, **as ordinary integral homotopy types**,
\[
X_n\simeq
\begin{cases}
*,&n=3,\\
S^2,&n=4,5,\\
S^2\vee S^3,&n\ge6.
\end{cases}
\]
For every \(n\ge6\), the dihedral group \(D_n\) acts on \(X_n\) through its natural permutation action on the cycle vertices, fixing \(h\). Its reduced integral homology is free of rank one in each of degrees two and three and vanishes otherwise. Every rotation acts as \(+1\) in both degrees; every reflection acts as \(-1\) on \(\widetilde H_2(X_n;\mathbb Z)\) and \(+1\) on \(\widetilde H_3(X_n;\mathbb Z)\).

The auxiliary deletion \(B_n=\operatorname{CF}_3(\overline{C_n})\) is, for every \(n\ge6\), simplicially isomorphic to the boundary of the cyclic four-polytope on \(n\) ordered vertices. This geometric identification explains the third-dimensional sphere and its orientation-preserving dihedral action. The second-dimensional sphere enters through the cone over the cycle link of the new apex.

## Assumptions and scope
The graph join \(K_1\vee H\) joins the new vertex to every vertex of \(H\). No edges of \(H\) are otherwise changed. The complement \(\overline{C_n}\) is the simple graph on the same \(n\) vertices with exactly the non-cycle edges; in particular it must not be confused with a power of the cycle except for accidental small coincidences. The complex \(\operatorname{CF}_3(G)\) consists of all vertex subsets containing no graph-theoretic three-clique, with the empty simplex included. The symbols \(S^d\) and \(\vee\) refer to ordinary topological spheres and their wedge. No equivariant homotopy equivalence is asserted; the symmetry conclusion concerns the *actual integral homology representations*.

## Proof
**1. Deletion and link.** Deleting the apex \(h\) gives \(B_n=\operatorname{CF}_3(\overline{C_n})\). Its simplicial link is \(L_n=\operatorname{Ind}(\overline{C_n})\), because a face containing the universal vertex must contain no edge in the remaining selected vertices. For \(n\ge4\), independent sets in the complement are cliques of \(C_n\), so \(L_n\) is exactly the unfilled cycle \(C_n\), homeomorphic to \(S^1\). Moreover \(X_n=B_n\cup(h*L_n)\), glued along the actual inclusion \(L_n\hookrightarrow B_n\). Consequently \(X_n\) is the homotopy cofiber of that inclusion. At \(n=3\), the graph \(G_3\) is a star, so \(X_3\) is a simplex. For \(n=4,5\), every subset of the cycle avoids an independent triple of \(C_n\), hence \(B_n\) is the full simplex on \(n\) vertices. Its link is a circle, so \(X_n\simeq\Sigma S^1=S^2\).

**2. Four-faces and the cyclic polytope.** Suppose \(n\ge6\). A subset of the cycle lies in \(B_n\) precisely when its induced subgraph in \(C_n\) has independence number at most two. Five selected vertices necessarily contain an independent triple: if the five vertices are a proper subset of the cycle, their induced graph is a disjoint union of paths and admits a matching of size at most two, so its independence number is at least three; if all cycle vertices are selected, the cycle itself has an independent triple since \(n\ge6\). No face therefore contains five vertices.

For four distinct cycle vertices \(F\), the induced graph \(C_n[F]\) is a disjoint union of paths. By the elementary matching--vertex-cover equality for a bipartite graph, its independence number is \(4-\nu(C_n[F])\), where \(\nu\) is the maximum matching size. Thus \(F\in B_n\) exactly when \(F\) is the union of the endpoints of **two disjoint cycle edges**. Every smaller admissible face extends to such a four-face: an admissible triple has an adjacent pair; the remaining vertex has a cycle neighbor outside that pair (because \(n\ge6\)), providing a second disjoint edge. Any pair extends to an admissible triple by adjoining a cycle neighbor of one vertex; singletons extend to pairs. It follows that the maximal faces of \(B_n\) are exactly \(\{i,i+1,j,j+1\}\) with the two cycle edges disjoint (all indices modulo \(n\)).

The Gale evenness description of the facets of the cyclic four-polytope \(C(n,4)\) identifies precisely those four-subsets: two disjoint edges of the cyclically ordered \(n\)-gon, including the wraparound edge. Therefore \(B_n\cong\partial C(n,4)\), as labelled abstract simplicial complexes, and \(|B_n|\cong S^3\). In particular, \(B_n\) is simply connected.

**3. Homotopy cofiber.** For \(n\ge6\), the inclusion \(|L_n|\cong S^1\to|B_n|\cong S^3\) is nullhomotopic because \(\pi_1(S^3)=0\). Its mapping cone is homotopy equivalent to \(S^3\vee\Sigma S^1=S^3\vee S^2\). This is the asserted *integral* homotopy type, not an inference solely from ranks over a field.

**4. Dihedral actions.** Naturality of the Mayer--Vietoris sequence for \(B_n\cup(h*L_n)\), using \(\widetilde H_2(B_n)=\widetilde H_1(B_n)=0\), yields an isomorphism of integral \(D_n\)-modules \(\widetilde H_2(X_n;\mathbb Z)\cong\widetilde H_1(L_n;\mathbb Z)\). A cyclic rotation preserves the fundamental orientation of \(L_n=C_n\), while a reflection reverses it. This proves the claimed degree-two signs.

For degree three, the inclusion \(B_n\hookrightarrow X_n\) induces the natural \(D_n\)-equivariant isomorphism on \(H_3\). Orient each cycle edge in the forward direction, writing \([i,i+1]\). Form the integral simplicial three-chain \(\Omega_n\) by adding \([i,i+1,j,j+1]\) once for each unordered pair of vertex-disjoint cycle edges. Interchanging the two two-vertex blocks is an even permutation and hence does not change its sign. Along every triangular ridge of \(\partial C(n,4)\), the two incident four-facets contribute opposite boundary coefficients; equivalently, expand the alternating boundaries of the two-edge terms to see each signed triple cancel. Thus \(\partial\Omega_n=0\). Because every top simplex appears with coefficient \(+1\) or \(-1\), \(\Omega_n\) is a primitive nonzero generator of the rank-one top integral homology of the connected triangulated three-sphere. A rotation carries the forward edge pairs to forward edge pairs, with no sign change. A reflection reverses the direction of **both** edges of every pair, making a product of two negative signs, hence also preserves \(\Omega_n\). This proves the degree-three trivial character.

Run `python verify.py` using Python 3 and only its standard library. The recorded output is `verification_output.txt`. For each \(3\le n\le12\), the program independently forms all forbidden three-cliques of \(G_n\), enumerates the full face set, and compares the deletion's facets with all pairs of disjoint cycle edges. It checks the entire simplicial four-dimensional facet profile, signed ridge cancellation for \(\Omega_n\), and rotation and reflection action signs for \(6\le n\le12\). It separately computes all reduced mod-two boundary ranks for \(3\le n\le10\), giving the claimed Betti profiles. Finite computations are evidence only for the tested inputs; the preceding four steps establish the statements for all \(n\).

## Relationship to prior work
Moradi, *\(t\)-clique ideal and \(t\)-independence ideal of a graph* (first public version 2 December 2017), Lemma 2.10 and Theorem 2.11, **already prove** purity and shellability of \(\operatorname{CF}_t(\overline{C_n})\). The deletion \(B_n\) and its shellability therefore are not claimed as new. The cyclic-polytope identification sharpens that precursor for \(t=3\). Ghosh and Selvaraja, *Shellability in Clique-Free Complexes of Graphs* (first public version 10 February 2026), study shellability and clique attachments. The claim here instead concerns the apex-joined graph \(G_n\), an exact all-length mixed-dimensional homotopy transition, and two distinct natural integral dihedral homology characters. These do not follow from Moradi's stated shellability conclusion alone without the additional apex cofiber and orientation arguments. Previously published triangle-free-join splittings assume the non-apex factor is triangle-free; \(\overline{C_n}\) contains triangles when \(n\ge6\), so that hypothesis does not apply. Published ordinary three-cut calculations for complements of cycles concern disconnected induced triples, not the independent cycle triples defining this clique-avoidance complex.

## Limitations
No general higher-clique-threshold or non-cycle-base statement is made. The homology representation is integral, but no equivariant *homotopy* splitting or fixed-point space classification is asserted. The finite checker does not constitute a proof of the infinite theorem or of an integral attaching map. A complete publication-priority guarantee is unavailable; independent mathematical validation has not been performed.
