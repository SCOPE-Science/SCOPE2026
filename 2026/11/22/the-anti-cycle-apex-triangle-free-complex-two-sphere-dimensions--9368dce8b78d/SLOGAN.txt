For the anti-cycle apex graph \(K_1\vee\overline{C_n}\), the triangle-free vertex-subset complex is contractible at \(n=3\), a two-sphere for \(n=4,5\), and \(S^2\vee S^3\) for every \(n\ge6\). On the two integral homology generators, all cycle rotations act trivially, while reflections act with signs \(-1\) in degree two and \(+1\) in degree three; the cycle-complement deletion is a cyclic four-polytope boundary.
