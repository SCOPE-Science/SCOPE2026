# Scientific review: the associative Veronese stratum

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof derives the cubic-product vanishing in dimension three by an explicit seven-element independence argument; it then forces a one-dimensional annihilator and a rank-one alternating tensor. The converse, reduced prime ideal, Hilbert function, singularity and finite-field count follow explicitly. All 512 and 262,144 multiplication tables in characteristics two and four, respectively, were independently tested by the included program; no mismatches occurred. The finite tests are not invoked as a proof over infinite fields.

**Originality — PASS with residual historical-priority risk.** Hernández–Martin–Rodrigues–Gomes Silva prove that the square-zero component is affine nine-space and classify relevant isomorphism classes. Cedilnik–Jerman's earlier classification is also credited. Both full primary texts were inspected, including the former's statements on the square-zero component, associative cases, and degenerations. The present claim is not that there is a single nonzero associative class, but that its **embedded reduced associative locus** is a symmetric rank-one determinantal cone with degree four, a specified Hilbert series, a single singular vertex and a uniform finite-field point count. No direct covering statement was found in the compared full texts or targeted searches. Residual risk: some general preexisting geometry theorem or unindexed appendix could imply the same determinantal statement; the directly relevant 2019/2020 characteristic-two classification by Shirayanagi and collaborators could not be inspected beyond its abstract and remains a priority risk.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** A 9-dimensional geometrically irreducible Jordan component was newly isolated in recent classification work. The codimension-six associative sublocus is naturally defined by a structural identity, not by arbitrary numerical cutoffs. The result describes its precise equations, intrinsic projective geometry, singularity and finite-field density, which are unavailable from isomorphism classes alone and useful in studying degeneration boundaries and families of algebra laws.

**Scope of confidence.** The identified ideal is the *reduced* locus. A nonreduced associativity scheme is not claimed to be resolved, and all-field statements are proved analytically rather than inferred from finite enumeration. Priority remains provisional pending independent literature review.
