For every Fibonacci-power exponent divisible by four, the prime-five valuation of the gcd of all length-\(k\) consecutive power sums is exactly \(\max(0,\nu_5(k)-1)\). More strongly, every block of \(5^r\) terms is congruent to \(-5^{r-1}\) modulo \(5^r\), exposing a one-level loss of divisibility at the ramified prime.
