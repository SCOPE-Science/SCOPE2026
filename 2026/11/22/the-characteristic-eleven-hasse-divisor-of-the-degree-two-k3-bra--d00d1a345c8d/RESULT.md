# Character-resolved Hasse–Witt maps and their rank schemes for a plane-sextic pencil

## Finding

Let \(k=\overline{\mathbb F}_{11}\), \(R=k[t,(t^3-6)^{-1}]\), and let \(\pi:C\to\operatorname{Spec}R\) be the pencil of smooth plane sextics
\[
 X^6+Y^6+Z^6+tX^2Y^2Z^2=0.
\]
Put \(E=R^1\pi_*\mathcal O_C\). The group \(G=\{(\lambda,\mu)\in\mu_6^2:(\lambda\mu)^2=1\}\) acts by scaling \(X,Y\), leaving \(Z\) fixed. Use the adjoint-dual basis \(e_i\) given by inverse monomials \(X^{-u-1}Y^{-v-1}Z^{-w-1}\) in \(H^2(\mathbb P^2_R,\mathcal O(-6))\), with \(u+v+w=3\), ordered first by increasing \(u\), then by increasing \(v\) and indexed by \(0,\dots,9\). Each basis vector spans a distinct character line, of character \(\chi_i(\lambda,\mu)=\lambda^{-u-1}\mu^{-v-1}\).

Let \(\mathcal F_n:(F_R^n)^*E\to E\) be the linearization of the \(n\)-fold absolute Frobenius action on cohomology. Define
\[
 A=t^2(1-t^3),\qquad B=2t(2t^3+1),\qquad C_0=t(t^3-1)(t^3+2)(t^3+4),\quad s_n=1+11+\cdots+11^{n-1}.
\]
For every \(n\ge1\), the map sends \(1\otimes e_i\) to \(h_i^{s_n}e_{\sigma^n(i)}\), where \(\sigma=(1\,8)(2\,7)(4\,6)\), and \(h_i=A\) for \(i=0,3,9\), \(h_i=C_0\) for \(i=5\), and \(h_i=B\) otherwise. In particular, the cokernel on target character \(\chi_i\) is \(R/(h_i^{s_n})\); this is a statement of the character and annihilator of each summand, not just the total Hasse-divisor multiplicity.

Consequently all determinantal rank schemes of \(\mathcal F_n\) are specified by this table of elementary orders divided by \(s_n\), arranged in increasing order. An exponent on a number indicates its repetition.

| Geometric parameter | Ordered elementary orders divided by \(s_n\) |
|---|---|
| \(t=0\) | \(1^7,2^3\) |
| \(t^3=1\) | \(0^6,1^4\) |
| \(t^3=5\) | \(0^4,1^6\) |
| \(t^3=7,9\) | \(0^9,1\) |
| Other smooth points | \(0^{10}\) |

Precisely, the ideal of the locus of rank at most \(r\) at a geometric parameter has local order \(s_n\) times the sum of the first \(r+1\) entries, for \(0\le r<10\); order zero means the ideal is the unit ideal there. For instance, the local rank-at-most-seven scheme at the Fermat curve has order \(9s_n\), not \(8s_n\). The geometric support of every rank condition is independent of the iterate, but its scheme structure need not be.

## Proof

The partial derivatives are cyclic permutations of \(2X(3X^4+tY^2Z^2)\). No singular point has a zero coordinate, and multiplying the nonzero-coordinate equations gives \(t^3=-27=6\). Conversely for such \(t\), taking \(X=Y=1\) and \(Z^2=-3/t\) gives a singular point since \(Z^6=1\). The smooth degree-six fibers have genus ten. The exact sequence with ideal generated by the displayed sextic identifies \(E\) with \(H^2(\mathbb P^2_R,\mathcal O(-6))\), with the indicated basis. Distinctness of the characters follows by reducing \((u+1,v+1)\) modulo the subgroup generated by \((2,2)\) in \((\mathbb Z/6)^2\). For example, for \(u+1=1,2,3,4\) the allowed second entries are \(1,\ldots,5-(u+1)\); none of the ten pairs differ by \((2,2)\) or \((4,4)\) modulo six. Frobenius is semilinear and sends each character to its eleventh power, giving the permutation \(\sigma\) by comparing these ten pairs.

In Čech cohomology Frobenius raises the inverse monomial to the eleventh power and multiplies by the tenth power of the sextic. Thus the coefficient from \((u,v,w)\) to \((u',v',w')\) is the coefficient at exponent vector \(11(u+1,v+1,w+1)-(u'+1,v'+1,w'+1)\). For an exponent vector \((m_1,m_2,m_3)\), the contribution of \(t^j\) is \(10!/(i_1!i_2!i_3!j!)\) modulo eleven if \(i_l=(m_l-2j)/6\) are nonnegative integers and \(i_1+i_2+i_3+j=10\), and zero otherwise. By coordinate symmetry, the \(A\) entries have exponent vector a permutation of \((40,10,10)\); the \(B\) entries a permutation of \((32,20,8)\); and the \(C_0\) entry has exponent vector \((20,20,20)\). The degrees and coefficient lists (in increasing degree) are respectively \((2,5):(1,-1)\), \((1,4):(2,4)\), and \((1,4,7,10):(3,2,5,1)\) modulo eleven. These give \(A,B,C_0\). Both ends of each two-cycle have the same polynomial, so composition with the successive Frobenius twists yields the exponent \(s_n\) and permutation \(\sigma^n\).

For cokernels, reorder the source free generators so that the matrix is diagonal with each diagonal element assigned to its target character. In a discrete valuation ring, each nonzero minor is a signed product of the corresponding row factors; the ideal is generated by a minor of minimum order. The nonzero roots of the displayed factors are simple at the level of \(t^3-c\). This proves the table and rank-scheme formula. Fiberwise the zero entries break no nonzero two-cycle: each orbit of the permutation is identically zero or stays invertible under all semilinear iterates. Thus the one-step rank agrees with the stable rank, and the Jacobian \(a\)-number is ten less the one-step rank.

## Relation to earlier work

The sextic family is the branch family of the characteristic-zero K3 pencil studied by Bouyer, Costa, Festi, Nicholls and West, arXiv:1703.02127. In the exact same plane-sextic family over finite fields, Bartoli, Giulietti, Kawakita and Montanucci, *New examples of maximal curves with low genus*, arXiv:1910.11098, §5, Definition 5.1 and Proposition 5.2, exhibit the isogeny to \(E_1^3\times E_2\times E_3^3\times E_4^3\). At characteristic eleven the elliptic Hasse coefficients in their Weierstrass models are respectively \(-B,C_0,A,B\), by taking the coefficient of \(x^{10}\) in the fifth power of each cubic. Their product with multiplicities is exactly \(-A^3 B^6 C_0\), also the determinant of the one-step matrix here. The ranks in the scope of their isogeny follow by adding ordinary elliptic dimensions. The scalar determinant and numerical rank lists are therefore not asserted as a distinct contribution. The result here identifies the marked character lines and their Frobenius maps, and gives the induced infinitesimal rank schemes for the iterated, relatively linearized maps. No invariance of these scheme structures across arbitrary isogenies is asserted.

The result is over the displayed smooth coefficient-marked parameter line; it is not a Newton polygon or K3 cohomology computation, and does not classify unmarked isomorphism classes. Priority of the character and rank-scheme refinement is provisional.

## Reproducibility

`check_character_ranks.py` verifies the character permutations, exact sparse polynomial maps and their twists through the third iterate, the elliptic coefficient comparison, and the determinantal order table. The all-iterate assertion uses the polynomial composition and local minor argument above, not finite experimentation.
