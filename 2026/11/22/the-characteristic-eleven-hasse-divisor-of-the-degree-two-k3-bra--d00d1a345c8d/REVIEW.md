# Scientific scope of the character-resolved computation

The previously studied sextics admit an elliptic isogeny decomposition. In characteristic eleven the standard elliptic coefficient computation already gives the scalar Hasse factor product and the associated ranks in the domain of that decomposition. The claim here is the assignment to the ten character lines of the marked plane model and the local determinantal ideals of every relatively linearized Frobenius iterate. These contain information not specified by a fiberwise unmarked list of ordinary or supersingular elliptic factors. The coordinate calculation and the discrete-valuation-ring minor formula verify the scheme statement, rather than inferring it from ranks of sampled fibers. Historical priority of this narrower refinement is not certified.
