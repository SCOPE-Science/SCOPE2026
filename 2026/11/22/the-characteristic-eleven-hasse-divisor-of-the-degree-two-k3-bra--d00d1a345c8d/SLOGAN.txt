For the genus-ten branch sextics \(X^6+Y^6+Z^6+tX^2Y^2Z^2=0\) of a degree-two K3 pencil in characteristic \(11\), the complete geometric Jacobian \(11\)-rank stratification has ranks \(0,4,6,9,10\), controlled by an explicit factored Hasse determinant. The nonordinary locus has exactly \(13\) geometric parameters, with one superspecial Fermat fiber and four distinct types of Hasse multiplicity.
