# Scientific scope of the marked clearance result

The equal-cap area formula is prior; equation (9) and Proposition 2 of García-Portugués, Navarro-Esteban and Cuesta-Albertos yield the unmarked survival area directly after conditioning on the original lune. The contribution claimed here is the conditional angular/radial law at fixed and critical opening, with explicit normalization and compact-uniform critical limits. The change of support, and the change from a first-order to a second-order radial deficit, distinguish this from an unmarked cap-area calculation. The proof is analytic and uses convergence in integrated absolute density difference, including in dimension three. Priority of the conditional results is subject to the limitations of the literature comparison.
