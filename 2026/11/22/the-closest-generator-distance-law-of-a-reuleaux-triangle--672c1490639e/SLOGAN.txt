For an area-uniform point in a width-one Reuleaux triangle, the Euclidean distance to its nearest generating corner has an elementary three-branch law, with transitions at \(1/2\) and \(\sqrt{2-\sqrt3}\) before its maximum \(1/\sqrt3\). The first transition has a sharp \(3/2\)-power CDF correction; the second has a second-derivative jump \(24/A\), where \(A=(\pi-\sqrt3)/2\).
