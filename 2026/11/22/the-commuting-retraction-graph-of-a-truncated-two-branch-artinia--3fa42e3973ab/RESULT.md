# The commuting-retraction graph of a truncated two-branch Artinian node

## Finding
Let \(k\) be any field, \(N\geq3\), and
\[
A_N=k[x,y]/(x^2,xy,y^N).
\]
Consider only **unital, coefficient-field-fixing** algebra endomorphisms \(P:A_N\to A_N\) with \(P^2=P\), where the square denotes composition. Write \(s=y^{N-1}\) and \(m=\lfloor N/2\rfloor\).

All such retractions are the augmentation \(Z\), the identity \(I\), and the following two disjoint families:

* **Long-branch retractions** \(H_{c,t}\), with \(c,t\in k\), given by
  \[
  H_{c,t}(x)=ts,\qquad H_{c,t}(y)=y+cx-cts.
  \]
  Their image has \(k\)-linear dimension \(N\).
* **Short-branch retractions** \(L_{a,h}\), with \(a\in k\) and \(h\in y^{\lceil N/2\rceil}k[y]/(y^N)\), given by
  \[
  L_{a,h}(x)=x+h(y),\qquad L_{a,h}(y)=a(x+h(y)).
  \]
  Their image has \(k\)-linear dimension \(2\).

These parameters are unique. For two **distinct** nontrivial retractions \(P,Q\in\{H_{c,t},L_{a,h}\}\), composition commutes exactly when
\[
 \{P,Q\}=\{H_{c,t},L_{-c,-ts}\}
 \quad\text{for some }c,t\in k.
\]
The two commuting retractions have composite \(Z\) in both orders. In particular, no two distinct retractions of the same nontrivial rank commute.

Over \(k=\mathbb F_q\), the graph on nontrivial retractions, with adjacency given by commuting composition, is **exactly**
\[
\boxed{q^2K_2\ \sqcup\ \bigl(q^{m+1}-q^2\bigr)K_1.}
\]
Here \(K_j\) means a complete graph on \(j\) vertices and \(\sqcup\) means disjoint union. Thus there are no isolated vertices when \(N=3\), but isolated vertices appear at every \(N\geq4\). Its clique polynomial is
\[
1+\bigl(q^2+q^{m+1}\bigr)z+q^2z^2.
\]
The full rank polynomial of all unital retractions and the ordered commuting-pair count, **including** \(Z,I\), are respectively
\[
\boxed{z+q^{m+1}z^2+q^2z^N+z^{N+1}},\qquad
\boxed{5q^{m+1}+7q^2+4}.
\]

## Assumptions and scope
The algebra \(A_N\) is a commutative local Artinian algebra of dimension \(N+1\), with maximal ideal \(\mathfrak m=(x,y)\) and basis \(1,x,y,\ldots,y^{N-1}\). It is the fiber product \(k[x]/(x^2)\times_k k[y]/(y^N)\). The field is arbitrary for the classification and commutation criterion, including characteristics two and three. The graph and counts over \(\mathbb F_q\) use all labeled retractions, not automorphism conjugacy classes. The augmentation means \(Z(1)=1\) and \(Z(\mathfrak m)=0\); it is not the zero linear map. The identity has rank \(N+1\).

## Proof
**1. The tangent projection and its four cases.** Every unital endomorphism preserves \(\mathfrak m\); an idempotent endomorphism induces an idempotent linear map on \(\mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2\), with basis the residue classes of \(x,y\). Since \(P(x)^2=0\) and \(N\geq3\), the coefficient of \(y\) in the linear part of \(P(x)\) must vanish. Thus the tangent matrix has shape
\[
\begin{pmatrix}a&c\\0&b\end{pmatrix},\quad
 a^2=a,\ b^2=b,\ c(a+b-1)=0.
\]
Over a field, the four possible types are zero, identity, a rank-one projection whose image line is \(kx\), and a rank-one projection with nonzero \(y\)-coefficient. The zero tangent type forces \(P(\mathfrak m)=0\): as \(P\) fixes its own image and maps \(\mathfrak m\) into \(\mathfrak m^2\), iterating gives \(P(\mathfrak m)\subseteq\mathfrak m^{2^j}\) for all \(j\). The identity tangent type makes \(P\) an automorphism by the nilpotent local Nakayama criterion, hence \(P=I\).

**2. Short branch.** In the first rank-one case the tangent matrix is \(\begin{psmallmatrix}1&a\\0&0\end{psmallmatrix}\). The subalgebra \(B=P(A_N)\) is local, and \(\mathfrak m_B/\mathfrak m_B^2\) is precisely the image of the tangent projection: if \(b\in B\cap\mathfrak m^2\), then \(b=P(b)\in P(\mathfrak m^2)=P(\mathfrak m)^2\). Consequently \(B\) is generated by \(P(x)\) as a unital algebra. Because \(P(x)^2=0\) and its coefficient of \(x\) is one, \(B=k1\oplus kP(x)\), and \(P(y)=aP(x)\). Write \(P(x)=x+h(y)\); its prescribed tangent term and its square-zero condition are equivalent to \(h(y)\in y^{\lceil N/2\rceil}k[y]/(y^N)\). Conversely, all these choices satisfy the defining relations of \(A_N\) and fix their own images; hence they give exactly the \(L_{a,h}\) maps.

**3. Long branch.** In the second rank-one case the tangent matrix is \(\begin{psmallmatrix}0&c\\0&1\end{psmallmatrix}\). The image is generated by \(u=P(y)\), whose \(y\)-coefficient is one, so \(u^j=y^j\) for every \(2\leq j<N\). Hence \(B=k[u]\) has dimension \(N\). It is the graph of the linear functional on \(k[y]/(y^N)\) taking a polynomial to \(c\) times its coefficient of \(y\): equivalently,
\[
B=\{f(y)+c[y]f\,x:f\in k[y]/(y^N)\},
\]
where \([y]f\) denotes the coefficient of \(y\). The one-dimensional kernel is an ideal contained in \(\mathfrak m\), with tangent direction \(x\). Because this ideal is annihilated by \(\mathfrak m\), it has the unique form \(k(x-ts)\). Projecting the ambient generators onto the direct complement \(B\) along this ideal gives the displayed \(H_{c,t}\). Conversely, those formulas are multiplicative and idempotent.

**4. Composition and exact commuting criterion.** Put \(H=H_{c,t}\), \(H'=H_{d,u}\), \(L=L_{a,h}\), \(L'=L_{b,g}\). Since \(H(y)^j=y^j\) for \(j\geq2\), and \(L(x)^2=L(y)^2=0\), direct substitution gives
\[
(H\circ L)(x)=ts+h(y),\quad (L\circ H)(x)=0,
\]
\[
(H\circ L)(y)=a(ts+h(y)),\quad
(L\circ H)(y)=(a+c)(x+h(y)).
\]
Therefore \(H\circ L=L\circ H\) exactly when \(h=-ts\) and \(a=-c\); then both composites equal \(Z\). For \(H,H'\), equality of the two compositions on \(x\) first forces \(t=u\), and equality on the \(x\)-coefficient of the image of \(y\) then forces \(c=d\). For \(L,L'\), the images of \(x\) under the two compositions are \(x+h(y)\) and \(x+g(y)\), forcing \(h=g\), and the images of \(y\) then force \(a=b\). Distinct same-family retractions never commute.

**5. Finite-field formulas.** The short-branch parameter \(h\) has \(m=\lfloor N/2\rfloor\) independent coefficients, so its family has \(q^{m+1}\) members. The long branch has \(q^2\). Every long-branch retraction has exactly one short-branch partner \(L_{-c,-ts}\), and all partners are distinct. Hence the graph is a matching of \(q^2\) edges with the stated number of isolates. The rank and clique polynomials follow immediately. Finally, \(Z,I\) commute with all \(T=2+q^{m+1}+q^2\) retractions. The number of ordered commuting pairs is \(T+2(2(T-2)+1+q^2)=5q^{m+1}+7q^2+4\).

## Verification
Run `python verify.py` with ordinary Python 3. The checker enumerates generator images for every unital homomorphism, tests \(x^2=xy=y^N=0\) using the original quotient multiplication, and tests \(P(P(x))=P(x)\), \(P(P(y))=P(y)\). It then computes the actual image rank and all pairwise commuting relations. It checks the predicted distribution over \(\mathbb F_2,\mathbb F_3,\mathbb F_4,\mathbb F_5\) at \(N=3\) and several larger truncations, using an independent four-element-field multiplication routine. The tests are finite corroboration, not a proof of the theorem for all fields or \(N\).

## Relationship to prior work
Díaz, Liendo, Manzano-Flores and Regeta (first public 2024-08-05) analyze automorphism groups of zero-dimensional monomial algebras, motivating study of the full noninvertible retraction structure. Endo, Goto and Isobe (2019 preprint; 2020 online publication) supply structural context for local fiber products. A 2026 study of the entire endomorphism variety of \(k[x,y]/(xy,x^m,y^n)\) already gives the general generator-image constraints, an exact total endomorphism count, and geometric irreducible components. That prior work is credited: the claim here is specifically the fixed-point-under-composition classification and the compatibility graph, whose edge conditions and truncation transition are not asserted by the prior total-count/component theorem. Related work on commuting retractions of a four-dimensional quantum complete intersection concerns a different multiplication and a different complete-bipartite component graph.

## Limitations
The result is for unital \(k\)-algebra retractions and does not classify nonunital idempotents, all endomorphism conjugacy classes, or infinitesimal scheme structures. The companion full-endomorphism parametrization substantially overlaps the preliminary algebraic setup, while this calculation adds composition-idempotence and pairwise commutation. No claim of absolute priority follows from an index search. The 2024 automorphism paper was verified by its arXiv record/abstract rather than a complete inspection of the current revision; an inaccessible or unindexed broader retraction result could affect priority. The computational examples do not substitute for the algebraic proof.
