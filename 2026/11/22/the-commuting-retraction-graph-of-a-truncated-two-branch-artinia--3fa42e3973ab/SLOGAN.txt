The nontrivial unital algebra retractions of the two-branch Artinian node \(k[x,y]/(x^2,xy,y^N)\), for \(N\ge3\), have commuting graph exactly \(q^2K_2\sqcup(q^{\lfloor N/2\rfloor+1}-q^2)K_1\) over every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\). A complete normal-form proof gives all retractions, their ranks, and the sharp onset of isolated vertices at \(N=4\).
