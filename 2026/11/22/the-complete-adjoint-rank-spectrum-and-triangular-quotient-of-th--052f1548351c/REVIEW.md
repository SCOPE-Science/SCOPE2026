# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** The Hopf generators and antipode determine four explicit action matrices, from which the rank formula is derived by a surjective three-parameter change of variables with \(q\) preimages per parameter triple. The case count sums to \(q^4\). The kernel and triangular-image identification follow from the same matrices and the standard algebra-representation property of Hopf actions. An independent basis-level checker enumerated all elements for field orders \(3,5,7,11\). Mathematical risk: characteristic two is excluded and finite enumeration alone would not establish the theorem.

**Originality — PASS (qualified).** The full motivating paper establishes Hopf polynomial identities and ordinary codimension results rather than adjoint-action rank fibers. Nguyen's full paper uses the left adjoint module for cohomology and computes the Sweedler algebra's cohomology, but no all-element rank census was located in the inspected relevant sections. The general adjoint-representation literature concerns properties of adjoint modules rather than the displayed finite-field rank polynomial. Related published research about adjoint ranks in Lie and truncated associative algebras has a different action and different objects. Residual risk: an existing treatment of the Sweedler adjoint module may make the triangular quotient or full polynomial an immediate implicit corollary; historical priority is not certified.

**Value — PASS (bounded).** This is an exact, natural numerical invariant of the canonical smallest noncommutative noncocommutative Hopf algebra, motivated by the role of its adjoint module in cohomology and by recent quantitative Hopf identities. The complete nonzero rank spectrum, forbidden rank three, one-dimensional annihilator, and indecomposable summand give a reproducible benchmark for rank-stratified operator questions. It does not purport to prove a general theory of all finite Hopf algebras or a large-dimensional asymptotic.
