# Reproduction and limits
Run `python3 checker.py` with Python 3.9+. Its independent finite generator-language construction checks the proposed complete forbidden family for each ratio from one through four and all lengths up to twelve. The general theorem is proved symbolically by a run-extension argument in RESULT.md; exhaustive finite verification alone would not certify all lengths. No external independent audit has been performed.
