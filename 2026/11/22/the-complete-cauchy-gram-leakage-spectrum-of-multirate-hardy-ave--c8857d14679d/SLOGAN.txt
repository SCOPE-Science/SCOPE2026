For a fixed finite mixture of distinct weighted Hardy averages, the commutator with degree-\(n\) orthogonal-polynomial projection has exactly \(m\) nonzero limiting singular values, determined by the Cauchy–Gram matrix \(C_{ij}=1/(c_i+c_j)\). These commutators tend strongly to zero but not in norm; a natural two-rate cancellation has exact limiting singular values \(1/5\) and \(1/30\).
