For the structure graph \((a,b)\mapsto(a+b,ab)\) over \(\mathbb F_{2^m}\), the only fields with no directed cycle longer than a fixed point are \(\mathbb F_2\) and \(\mathbb F_4\). An additive trace argument produces cycles in every extension degree with an odd factor, while one explicit eight-cycle over \(\mathbb F_{16}\) covers all degrees divisible by four.
