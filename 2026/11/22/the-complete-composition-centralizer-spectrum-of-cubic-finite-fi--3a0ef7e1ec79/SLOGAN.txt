The full composition-centralizer distribution of \(\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb F_q\text{-alg}}(\mathbb F_q[t]/(t^4))\) has six explicit strata in odd characteristic but only three in characteristic two. Consequently its ordered commuting-pair count is \(3q^4+9q^3-11q^2\) for odd \(q\) and \(5q^4-4q^2\) for even \(q\), with exceptional center sizes at \(q=2,3\).
