# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The finite domain \(Q_4\) has \(16\) distinguished vertices and \(32\) distinguished edges. Exhaustive geodesic enumeration generates \(576\) forbidden triples. The two search programs are independent on (i) path enumeration (coordinate-permutation vs breadth-first distance layer) and (ii) legal-family enumeration (recursive forbidden-bitmask states vs iterative explicit family). Both independently verify every coefficient and the exact six coordinate-class maximum configurations. The accompanying exact recurrence invariant rules out omissions and erroneous maximality; no experimental extrapolation is used.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The exact face and facet polynomials of a canonical hypercube identify a strong failure of purity: seven inclusion-maximal sizes range from \(6\) to \(16\) and contain a long missing-size segment. The minimum-maximal versus maximum gap, plus the count of all extremizers, makes this a natural saturating-set benchmark not recoverable from the known maximum alone. The result intentionally makes no infinite-family claim.
