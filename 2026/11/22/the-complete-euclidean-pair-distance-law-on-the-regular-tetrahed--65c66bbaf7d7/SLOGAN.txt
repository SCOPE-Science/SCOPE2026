Two arclength-uniform points on the six-edge wireframe of a unit regular tetrahedron have a complete elementary Euclidean separation law, with a density jump at \(1/\sqrt2\) and a \(-56/27\) three-halves-power singularity at \(\sqrt3/2\). The probability of separation greater than \(1-\varepsilon\) is asymptotic to \(25\varepsilon^2/6\), while the mean squared separation is exactly \(5/12\).
