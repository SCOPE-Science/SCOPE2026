For the numerical semigroup generated by all \(F_a+F_j\) with \(a\ge5\), every maximal block of missing integers has length \(F_a-1\) or \(F_j-1\) with \(3\le j\le a-2\). The first occurs exactly once, and the frequency of each \(F_j-1\) is exactly \(F_{a-j-1}\); in particular the total number of blocks is \(F_{a-2}\).
