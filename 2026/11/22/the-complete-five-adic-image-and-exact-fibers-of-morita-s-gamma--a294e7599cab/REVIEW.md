# Review: complete five-adic image and exact fibers of Morita's gamma function

## Correctness — PASS

The proof expands the five exact gamma-step polynomials, derives the two possible branch valuation slopes from the 5-adic logarithm and a complete-block sum lemma, and applies compactness to convert normalized isometries into surjections. This identifies the image, each fiber, and the least input period. The separate checker passed 8,190 exact assertions through modulus \(5^5\); this finite test is not an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

The closest recovered early full text (Barsky, November 1977) proves analytic interpolation and a radius estimate rather than the exact range. The 2022 Li–Reiter–Tang–Wang–Yi Proposition 2.2 is a general one-Lipschitz bound, which does not fix the exact branchwise valuation exponents or cover the codomain balls. Finat studies a related log-gamma distribution identity, and Adamus proves differential transcendence. The original Morita (1975) article and the broad Robert textbook were not fully accessible, so an earlier implicit equivalent remains possible; lack of a database hit is not a proof of novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS

An exact prime-power value-set classification for Morita's classical gamma function is a natural representability problem. The nontrivial three-ball range has Haar measure \(11/25\), and all fibers have exactly prescribed multiplicities. Distinct local contraction orders are necessary to explain the exceptional fivefold contributions.

## Boundaries

The theorem is five-adic only, assumes the standard Morita normalization and unit-valued extension, and has not undergone independent mathematical audit. No assertion is made about its historical priority beyond the inspected sources.
