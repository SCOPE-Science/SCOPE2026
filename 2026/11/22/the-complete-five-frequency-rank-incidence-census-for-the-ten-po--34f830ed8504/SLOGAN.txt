Every five-by-five restriction of the ten-point Fourier matrix has rank three, four, or five, in exactly \(240\), \(6{,}640\), or \(56{,}624\) ordered row-column cases respectively. The deepest rank loss occurs precisely when one index set is an even- or odd-residue coset and the other has modulo-five occupancy profile \((2,2,1,0,0)\); nine affine row-set types give the full recovery census.
