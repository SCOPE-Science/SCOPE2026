For every prime power \(q\geq5\), the nine-term anticanonical torus code of the split degree-eight del Pezzo surface \(\mathbb F_1\) has common-zero maxima \((3t,2t+1,2t,t+2,t+1,t,2,1,0)\), where \(t=q-1\). Its eight higher generalized Hamming weights follow by subtraction from \(t^2\); the ordinary minimum distance was already known.
