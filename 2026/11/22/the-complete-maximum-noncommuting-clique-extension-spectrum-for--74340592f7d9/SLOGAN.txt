For finite groups with derived group \(C_2\) and central quotient \(\mathbb F_2^{2r}\), the exact number of maximum pairwise noncommuting supersets of every prescribed noncommuting set depends only on its size, the center order, and—at odd smaller sizes—whether the quotient-vector sum vanishes. Every projected \((2r-2)\)-clique extends uniquely, and the total number of maximum group subsets equals \(|Z(G)|^{2r+1}|\operatorname{Sp}_{2r}(2)|/(2r+1)!\).
