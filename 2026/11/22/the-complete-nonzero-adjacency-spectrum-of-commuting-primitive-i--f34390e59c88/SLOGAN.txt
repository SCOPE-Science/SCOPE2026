For every finite field and matrix size \(n\ge4\), the commuting graph of rank-one idempotents has an exact six-level adjacency spectrum, dropping to five levels at field size two; every eigenvalue and multiplicity is explicit and none is zero. Its two large opposite bands are \(\pm q^{3(n-2)/2}\), giving nonintegral commuting graphs in odd dimensions when \(q\) is nonsquare.
