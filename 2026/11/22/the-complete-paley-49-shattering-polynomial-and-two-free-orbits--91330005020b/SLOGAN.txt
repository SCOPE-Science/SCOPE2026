The open neighborhoods of the 49-vertex Paley graph have complete shattered-support polynomial \(1+49z+1176z^2+18424z^3+199920z^4+4704z^5\), with VC dimension five. The 4,704 extremal supports form exactly two free affine semilinear orbits, while 188,160 shattered four-supports are maximal, so the shattering complex is nonpure.
