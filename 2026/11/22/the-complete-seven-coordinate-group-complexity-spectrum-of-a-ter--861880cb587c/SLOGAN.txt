For the ternary Fibonacci image \(\psi(0)=0\), \(\psi(1)=12\), the complete spectrum of coordinate-permutation group complexities at factor length seven is exactly \(\{3,5,6,7,8,9\}\): the intermediate value \(4\) is impossible. This gives a fully enumerated first nontrivial odd Fibonacci-standard instance of the published ternary group-complexity intermediate-value question.
