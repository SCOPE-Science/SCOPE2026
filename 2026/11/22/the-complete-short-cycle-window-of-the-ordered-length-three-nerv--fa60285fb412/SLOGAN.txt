At inclusive length three, the ordered enriched nerves of the four short cycles have complete integral homotopy types \(\bigvee^{16}S^3\), \(\bigvee^9S^3\), \(\bigvee^9S^2\vee\bigvee^5S^3\), and \(\bigvee^5S^2\vee\bigvee^6S^3\). For each cycle, the inclusion from length two to length three kills every positive-degree integral homology class, so the new two-dimensional classes of the pentagon and hexagon are genuinely born at the cubic threshold.
