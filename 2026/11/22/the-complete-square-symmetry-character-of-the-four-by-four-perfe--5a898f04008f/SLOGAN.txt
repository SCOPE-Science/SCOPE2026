The perfect-matching extendibility complex of the nonperiodic \(4\times4\) grid has rational square-symmetry homology \(\widetilde H_2\cong E\) and \(\widetilde H_3\cong\eta\), where the quarter-turn acts as \(-1\) on \(\eta\) and axial reflections act trivially. Consequently its full dihedral quotient is rationally acyclic, even though the underlying complex has the previously known homotopy type \(S^2\vee S^2\vee S^3\).
