A sixteen-state exact polynomial counts, for every even length, how many GC-balanced homopolymer-free DNA words have each aligned Hamming distance from their own Watson–Crick reverse complement. Every half-distance from \(m\bmod2\) to \(m\) occurs at length \(2m\); the distance is asymptotically Gaussian, and at lengths divisible by four the self-complementary fraction is asymptotic to \(\sqrt2\,3^{-m}\).
