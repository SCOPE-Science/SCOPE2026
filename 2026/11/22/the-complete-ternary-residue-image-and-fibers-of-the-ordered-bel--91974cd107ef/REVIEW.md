# Scientific review: complete ternary residue image and fibers of the ordered Bell numbers

## Correctness — PASS
The proof constructs a compatible bounded ternary-adic measure with prescribed integer moments, then restricts its moments to the units. The Mahler expansion coefficients have explicit valuations, certified by finite weighted sums modulo 81. The binomial-difference bound proves exact valuations for differences of the two interpolating maps, rather than merely congruences on finitely many indices. Surjectivity onto the two residue balls follows from these distance identities and compactness. The finite-stage checker verifies 5,374 exact assertions, but the infinite statement relies on the displayed proof. The exceptional index zero and the distinction between an eventual index period and an initial segment are handled explicitly.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk
The 2013 Mező paper treats periodicity and Fubini background. Caicedo–Moll–Ramírez–Villamizar (2019) determine the modulo-nine residue pattern and parity valuations. Schreyer's periodicity results establish least eventual index periods but not exact residue images and multiplicities. A published study of Fubini prime-power shift defects proves the least ternary preperiod and states explicitly that it does not determine residue-class distributions. Its full result and proof were compared: an exact shift-defect formula is not a surjectivity or exact-fiber theorem, and the published claim does not contain the two branch isometries. Research-record semantic searches did not identify the present statement. Nonetheless, an unlocated implicit equivalent in earlier Fubini or ternary-adic interpolation literature is possible; this is not an independent historical-priority certificate.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Prime-power periodicity and the known modulo-nine parity obstruction do not give a converse existence criterion. This result supplies a complete ternary-adic value closure, exact per-residue multiplicities at all depths, and explicit geometric index-to-value isometries. Such a classification is a natural and motivated refinement of established Fubini congruence theory. The Haar measure 4/9 quantifies the precise local obstruction.

## Limitations and sources
The result applies to the classical ordered Bell numbers and the prime three. It does not classify other primes, other Fubini families, joint residue patterns or optimal first occurrences.
