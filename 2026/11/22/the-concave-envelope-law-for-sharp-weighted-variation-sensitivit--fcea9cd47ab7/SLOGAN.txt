For weighted least-squares isotonic regression on any finite real path, the exact two-input edge-variation amplification is \(\max_j\widehat w(T_j)/w_j\), where \(\widehat w\) is the least concave majorant of the variation weights plotted against cumulative observation masses. The bound is always attained; concavity is precisely the contractivity condition, while the envelope quantifies the sharp loss when it fails.
