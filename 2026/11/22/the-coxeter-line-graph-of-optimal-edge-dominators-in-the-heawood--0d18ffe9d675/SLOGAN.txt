The Heawood graph has exactly \(42\) minimum edge-dominating sets, all five-edge matchings, and its inclusion-minimal edge-domination polynomial is \(42z^5+1246z^6+24z^7\). Their sharp two-edge-replacement graph is the line graph of the \(28\)-vertex Coxeter graph, giving a connected four-regular exchange geometry where every one-edge move is forbidden.
