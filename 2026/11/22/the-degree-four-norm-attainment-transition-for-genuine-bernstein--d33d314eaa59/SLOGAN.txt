For genuine Bernstein–Durrmeyer approximation, the sharp real \(BV\)-to-integrated-\(L^1\) error norm is unattained exactly at degrees \(1,2,3\), but is attained by an interior jump at every degree \(n\ge4\). An exact two-binomial absolute-deviation formula gives the coefficient, whose sharp asymptotic is \(C_n\sim1/\sqrt{\pi n}\) with every worst-jump location tending to \(1/2\).
