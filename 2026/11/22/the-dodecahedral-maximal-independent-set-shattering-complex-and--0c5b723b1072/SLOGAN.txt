The maximal-independent-set concepts of the dodecahedral graph have a nonpure shattered-support complex with facet cardinalities four, five and six, and exact polynomial \(1+20z+160z^2+660z^3+1470z^4+1572z^5+310z^6\). Its ten four-point facets are the previously known pairwise-distance-three tetrahedra, while its reduced mod-two Betti ranks in dimensions three, four and five are \(10,326,5\).
