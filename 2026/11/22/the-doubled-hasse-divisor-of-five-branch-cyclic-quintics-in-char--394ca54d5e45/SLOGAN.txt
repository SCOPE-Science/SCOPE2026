For the smooth five-simple-branch cyclic quintics in characteristic \(11\), the nonordinary Hasse divisor is twice an explicit reduced quartic in symmetric branch parameters. Every split five-point configuration over \(\mathbb F_{11}\) is ordinary, while exactly \(210\) of the \(14,042\) ordered split configurations over \(\mathbb F_{121}\) are nonordinary, forming three rational projective branch-configuration orbits of sizes \(30,60,120\).
