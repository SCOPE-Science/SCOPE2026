In a uniform \(r\)-Stirling permutation, the earliest label whose \(r\) copies remain consecutive lies on the scale \(n^{(r-1)/(2r-1)}\), and the entire collection of early intact labels converges to a Poisson point process with intensity \(x^{(r-1)/r}\,dx\). Thus the minimum has a sharp Weibull limit with survival function \(\exp[-r t^{(2r-1)/r}/(2r-1)]\), even though distinct finite intactness indicators have nonpositive pairwise correlations.
