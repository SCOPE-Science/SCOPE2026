# Review of the eight-level annihilation spectrum

## Correctness — PASS

The proved claim is the ordered zero-product pair count distribution for all 88 ring isomorphism classes of order 64, conditional on the published complete classification. The key derivation splits products with a unit from products in the radical; distinguishes the perfect central-action cases by \(\dim W_0\); counts central lifts using ranks one and two; and evaluates either three diagonal-square indicators or binary-cubic multiplication ranks. The symbolic reductions give the eight values, and direct finite multiplication over all 88 canonical representatives reproduces them. The checker confirms the table and arithmetic rather than the independent completeness of the source classification.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk

The primary full classification [1] determines isomorphism classes, characteristic, radical structure and square-zero element counts, not the invariant \(N(R)=|\{(x,y):xy=0\}|\) nor its eight-level frequency distribution. Its tables logically permit computation but do not contain the characteristic-uniform lift-rank reduction or the cross-family collision of annihilation probabilities. The earlier structural source [2] only partially classifies these rings. Published records on zero-product counts in triangular or niltriangular rings, commutative square-zero **ideal** products, and nilpotence of products do not imply the present finite noncommutative ring-class census. Specialist unindexed priority remains possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Ordered zero-product probability is a canonical multiplication statistic connected to annihilators and zero-divisor relations. The minimum-order noncommutative centrally essential rings form a complete natural test class newly classified in [1]. The eight-level histogram, sharp extreme probabilities and unique failure of distinguishing radical regimes give a concrete structural comparison beyond reproducing individual ring tables. A rank-based formula substantially compresses 88 separate multiplication enumerations.

## Closest literature and limitations

The source classification [1] is essential and fully credited; the previous paper [2] establishes only structural foundations and a partial list. The finding has no extension above minimum order, no claim of complete multiplication-fiber classification and no independently audited proof.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
