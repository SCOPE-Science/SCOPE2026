For the 90 octagons of Tutte's eight-cage, Boolean edge-incidence convolution has an exact eighteen-level signed Walsh spectrum with no zeros: every multiplier is congruent to \(2\) modulo \(4\). Its sharp \(\ell^2\) inverse norm is \(1/2\) and its condition number is \(45\), proved by an incidence-parity argument and an independently cross-checked 65,536-character integer enumeration.
