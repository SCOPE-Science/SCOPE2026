For every two-point directed quasi-metric with positive forward and reverse distances, the total-length enriched-nerve filtration has an explicit infinite sequence of integral sphere stages, with contractible intervals precisely when the two distances are unequal; there is exactly one reduced barcode interval in every degree, all of length \(\min(a,b)\). The full barcode determines both directed distances up to interchanging labels, although its supremal degreewise bottleneck distance from the point barcode depends only on the smaller distance.
