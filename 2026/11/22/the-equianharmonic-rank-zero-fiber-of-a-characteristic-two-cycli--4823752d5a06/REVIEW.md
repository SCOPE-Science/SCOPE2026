# Scientific review: equianharmonic Cartier-rank jump in a cyclic-septic genus-six family

## Correctness — PASS

The order-seven Kummer curve is smooth for every \(t\notin\{0,1\}\) and has four fully ramified points, giving genus six by Riemann--Hurwitz. Valuations at the finite branch points and infinity yield an explicit basis of six regular differentials. Exact characteristic-two Cartier extraction gives the displayed semilinear six-by-six matrix. Its rank is four, and its square has rank three on the entire smooth parameter line. The threefold return on the one-dimensional denominator-three character equals multiplication by \(t^{1/8}+(1+t)^{1/4}\), vanishing exactly at \(t^2+t+1=0\). The remaining cyclic blocks prove the full stable-rank sequence: generic stable rank three, exceptional ranks \((4,3,2,1,0)\). The finite-field verifier independently constructs the matrix by coefficient extraction and checks all smooth parameters in \(\mathbf F_4\) and \(\mathbf F_8\). Those tests are not used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, provisional

The natural cyclic-cover family and its local arithmetic setting are classical: Moonen (2010) treats cyclic covers, Bouw–Wewers (2012) examines superelliptic reduction, and Li–Mantovan–Pries–Tang (2018) studies Newton polygons of special cyclic-cover families. The relevant publications inspected did not state the exact characteristic-two ramification-type \((1,1,1,4)\) Cartier-power jump at \(t^2+t+1=0\). published-finding corpus searches for order-seven genus-six characteristic-two covers returned nearby characteristic-five septic and characteristic-two trigonal results, not this theorem. Other literature could present the same family under a changed exponent vector or hypergeometric description; this remains a priority risk. No claim of independent novelty certification is made.

## Value — PASS

The theorem solves a complete natural one-parameter moduli problem: the stable Cartier rank jumps from three to zero at one geometric equianharmonic class while the initial Cartier rank, hence Jacobian \(a\)-number, remains constant. The full nilpotent rank sequence identifies a finer arithmetic boundary, not a small finite example or a standard consequence of knowing only the branch points. The relationship of wild Frobenius action to the finite order-seven character cycle is structurally informative.
