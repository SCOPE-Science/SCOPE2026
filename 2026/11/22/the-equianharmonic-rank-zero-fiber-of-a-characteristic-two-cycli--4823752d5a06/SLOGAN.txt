For the smooth characteristic-two genus-six cyclic-septic family \(y^7=x(x-1)(x-t)\), the Cartier-power ranks are generically \((6,4,3,3,\ldots)\), but become \((6,4,3,2,1,0,\ldots)\) precisely at the two equianharmonic parameters satisfying \(t^2+t+1=0\). The Jacobian \(2\)-rank jumps from three to zero while its \(a\)-number stays two; the exceptional parameters form a single geometric cyclic-cover class.
