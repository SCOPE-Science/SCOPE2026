# Review

## Correctness — PASS
The 2-adic Mahler interpolation is uniformly convergent. Exact rational coefficient computations and explicit Gauss valuation tail inequalities establish the two disk isometries and the cubic factor. The essential quadratic cancellation is proved using the dilogarithm involution at \(2\), and reflection covers the final index class. The resulting image set and Haar sum are exact; modular computations through \(2^{11}\) independently confirm finite consequences.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)
Guo–Zeng (2010) study congruences of sums rather than scalar images. Lengyel (2021), especially the main statements on pages 4–6, studies 2-adic valuations, including conjectural valuation behavior near \(\pm1\). A subsequent published result establishes a valuation formula but does not supply onto statements for every odd-unit shell. A complete valuation formula does not imply unit surjectivity; the additional isometry establishes the missing implication. Targeted searches of related published findings and the literature did not reveal an equivalent complete 2-adic image theorem. The full text of the closest later published valuation result was not available, leaving historical priority qualified.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The central Delannoy sequence has a natural, previously studied 2-adic arithmetic. A complete local representability theorem with an exact image measure answers a substantially stronger question than valuation restrictions or minimal periods. Its two unrestricted balls coexist with thin cubic-shell towers; this structure gives an explicit decision procedure and image counts at all precisions.
