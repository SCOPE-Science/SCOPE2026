# Mathematical review

**Correctness — PASS.** The totient expansion into nested congruence matrices is exact. Normalized congruence matrices are commuting orthogonal projections; their eigenvalues are positive and determine the full rational inverse. Every inverse coefficient is recovered as a difference of actual matrix entries, so the least common denominator of those coefficients equals the largest Smith invariant factor. The modular rank proof uses an invertible \(p\)-by-\(p\) block-sum operator. Standalone exact arithmetic independently confirms small full Smith forms and higher-depth inverse identities.

**Originality — PASS, with residual historical risk.** The relevant classical GCD-matrix literature uses \(\gcd(i,j)\), not the cyclic difference kernel; the inspected polynomial-circulant and companion-ring papers give broader tools, but no displayed special formula for the largest integral invariant factor of this specific prime-power kernel. Indexed results on Ramanujan-convolution circulants, divisor-indexed Gram matrices and squarefree cyclic GCD kernels concern different matrices or domains. Historical coverage by an unlocated general Smith-form result is possible; the novelty claim is restricted to the concrete evaluated exponent and characteristic-prime corank.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The largest invariant factor is the exact minimal denominator clearing the inverse and the exponent of a natural finite arithmetic lattice quotient. This invariant is not determined from rational eigenvalues at the characteristic prime, as the explicit depth-dependent examples demonstrate. The result complements a sharp modular generator-count obstruction and gives an effective exact integer formula at every prime-power depth without computing exponentially large Smith matrices.

The proof does not determine all intermediate Smith factors or arbitrary composite moduli. Finite computation is supportive rather than a substitute for the universal algebraic argument. Priority remains qualified by limited historical coverage.
