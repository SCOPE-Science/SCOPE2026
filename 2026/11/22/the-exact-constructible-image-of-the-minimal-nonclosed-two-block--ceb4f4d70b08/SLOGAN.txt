For the minimal nonclosed \((2,3,2)\) two-block rank-one pattern map, the **only** nonattainable matrix pairs over any field are \((X,\lambda X)\) with \(X\) invertible and \(\lambda\notin\{0,1\}\). Their geometric missing locus has codimension three, and over \(\mathbb F_q\) exactly \((q-2)(q^2-1)(q^2-q)\) pairs are omitted.
