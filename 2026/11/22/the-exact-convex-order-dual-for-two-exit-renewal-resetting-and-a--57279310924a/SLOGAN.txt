For any two-outcome renewal process with integrable integer reset clocks, the universal direction of splitting-probability change under convex-order spreading is characterized exactly by two discrete-curvature inequalities at the extremal deterministic-deadline probabilities. For ordinary biased gambler's ruin on an even interval, both opposite responses are achievable by one-step mean-preserving spreads at every non-midpoint start; the midpoint alone has a clock-independent splitting probability.
