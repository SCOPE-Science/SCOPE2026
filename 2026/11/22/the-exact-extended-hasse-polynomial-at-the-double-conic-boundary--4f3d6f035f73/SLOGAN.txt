For symmetric Ciani quartics in every prime characteristic \(p\ge5\), the natural extended Hasse coefficient is exactly \((a-2)^{(p-1)/2}H_{(p-1)/2}(-a-1)\), where \(H_m\) is the Legendre–Deuring polynomial. This determines the scheme-theoretic collision multiplicity of the smooth superspecial parameters with the double-conic boundary, including its exceptional one-step jump.
