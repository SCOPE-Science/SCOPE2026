For uniform integer \(2\times2\) matrices in a box of height \(H\), the first possible band of bounded-commutant counts has the exact limit law \(\Pr(C_H(A)\le zH)=(z-2)^2/(16\zeta(3))+O(H^{-1})\) for every \(2\le z\le4\). An exact finite Möbius formula classifies this entire first band, whose limiting mass is \(1/(4\zeta(3))\).
