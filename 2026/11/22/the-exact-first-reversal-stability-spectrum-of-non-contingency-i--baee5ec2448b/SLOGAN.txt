Among the \(\binom n2\) single-arc reversals of an \(n\)-vertex transitive tournament, precisely \(n\) preserve the non-contingency introspection axiom \(\Delta p\to\Delta\Delta p\): the adjacent reversals and one terminal skip-one reversal. Every other reversal has a singleton-valued counterexample, with an exact position-dependent count of failing valuation–world pairs.
