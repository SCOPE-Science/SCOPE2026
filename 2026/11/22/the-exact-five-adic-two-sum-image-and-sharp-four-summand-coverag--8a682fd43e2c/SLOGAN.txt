For the indices where the Motzkin number is divisible by five, the exact twofold residue support modulo \(5^k\) has \(8\cdot5^{k-2}+1\) classes for every \(k\ge2\), with five-adic Haar measure \(8/25\). Four such indices represent every residue modulo every power of five, whereas three never suffice, and explicit self-similar five-adic formulas describe the complete two-sum image.
