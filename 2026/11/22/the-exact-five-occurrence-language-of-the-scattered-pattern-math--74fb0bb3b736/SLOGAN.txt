The binary words having exactly five scattered copies of \(\mathtt{001100}\) form an unambiguous two-core regular language with cores `00101100` and `00110100`, each permitting four arbitrary inert one-runs. Consequently, at length \(n\ge8\) there are exactly \(2\binom{n-5}{3}\) such words, exhibiting a strict degree-three drop below the general degree-four bound.
