Every binary word containing exactly four ones has \(0\), \(2\), \(3\), or \(4\) unbordered cyclic rotations; a complete four-gap criterion gives the all-length, four-class rational enumerator with common denominator \(\prod_{h=1}^4(1-z^h)\). Almost every such word has four unbordered rotations, and the exceptional terms are governed by opposite-gap equalities absent in the corresponding three-one law.
