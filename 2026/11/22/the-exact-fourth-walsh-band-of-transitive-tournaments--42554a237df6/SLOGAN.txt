For the transitive-tournament inversion indicators on \(\mathbb F_2^{\binom n2}\), every Walsh multiplier of degree at most four is classified by seven graph-support shapes (including the empty support); all other shapes vanish. The exact recoverable rank in this fourth Fourier band is \(1+3\binom n3+3\binom n4+125\binom n5+90\binom n6\), and a triangle with one pendant edge gives a connected four-edge Fourier zero.
