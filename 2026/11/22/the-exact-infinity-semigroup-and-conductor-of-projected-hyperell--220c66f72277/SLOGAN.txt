The infinity branch of every admissible genus-\(g\ge4\) Hilario–Salomão–Vidal Martins hyperelliptic space-curve projection has exactly the three-generator value semigroup \(\langle2g-5,2g-3,2g+1\rangle\), not merely a semigroup containing these values. Its conductor and delta invariant admit explicit all-genus formulas, making the known maximality deficit exact.
