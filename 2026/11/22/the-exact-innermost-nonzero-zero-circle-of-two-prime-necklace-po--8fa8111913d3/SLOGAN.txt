For every necklace polynomial at a length \(n=p^a q^b\) with two distinct prime divisors, its nonzero root of smallest modulus sits on **exactly \(p^{a-1}q^{b-1}\gcd(p-1,q-1)\) equally spaced simple roots** and nowhere else on that circle. At fixed \(p\), the circle's squarefree-kernel radius approaches one with a precise Lambert-\(W\) boundary layer as \(q\to\infty\).
