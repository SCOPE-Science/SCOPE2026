In the type-I generalized discrete half-logistic model, every nonnegative integer becomes the unique modal bin on exactly one interval of the shape parameter; the only ties are between adjacent bins at strictly increasing, unique thresholds. At large bin index, the thresholds obey \(\alpha_k e^{-k\theta}=C_{e^{-\theta}}+O_\theta(e^{-2k\theta})\), with an explicit one-root characterization of the sharp constant.
