For two distinct positive scales of a finite-multiplicity unilateral shift, the exact operator-norm and essential-norm distance to all bounded \(k\)-th powers is \(|a-b|/2\) when a scale block has multiplicity not divisible by \(k\), provided total multiplicity is divisible; otherwise it is zero. The sharp positive gap follows from a Fredholm-index divisibility obstruction and is attained by merging the scales at their midpoint.
