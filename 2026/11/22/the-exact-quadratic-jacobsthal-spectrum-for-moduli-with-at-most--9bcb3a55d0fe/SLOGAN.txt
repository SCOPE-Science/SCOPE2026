For \(f(T)=T^2\), the polynomial Jacobsthal function is completely classified for every modulus with at most two distinct prime factors: the longest initial non-coprime run has length \(1,2,3\), or \(5\), with an exact prime-support criterion. Explicit formulas determine the number of residue classes supporting every run length, including the unique extremal class modulo each qualifying squarefree radical.
