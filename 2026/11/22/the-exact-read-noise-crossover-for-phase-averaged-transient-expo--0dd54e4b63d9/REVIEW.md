## Originality — PASS, subject to residual risks

Macquart (2014) optimizes transient detection *rates across survey fields*; Law et al. (2015) optimize a spectroscopic observing strategy under sky and read noise. These do not imply the present phase-averaged fixed-pulse squared-SNR optimizer. Shamir and Nemiroff (2009) handle finite optical frames and coaddition, and Bélanger (2013) addresses likelihood-based transients. Published databases and relevant searches returned no exact equivalent; generic matched-filter theory is acknowledged as a premise. The 2009 and 2013 sources could not be inspected in full, and the priority assessment is necessarily provisional.
