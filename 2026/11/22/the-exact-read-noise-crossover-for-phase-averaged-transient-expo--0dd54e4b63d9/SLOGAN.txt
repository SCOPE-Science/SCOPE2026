For a rectangular astronomical transient with unknown uniform exposure-grid phase, independent background-plus-read noise, and known-template matched-filter scoring, the uniquely best exposure is shorter than the event if \(R^2<\nu T\), longer if \(R^2>\nu T\), and exactly event-long at equality. Explicit piecewise formulas give both its width and the sharp phase-averaged squared-SNR efficiency, which is exactly one-third of the ideal sky-only continuous-time value at the crossover.
