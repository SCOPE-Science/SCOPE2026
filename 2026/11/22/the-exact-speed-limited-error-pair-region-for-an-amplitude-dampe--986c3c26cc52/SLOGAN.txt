For a qubit inversion with bounded angular drive speed and fixed duration, the **entire pair** of first-order amplitude-damping errors for the two computational-basis inputs fills an exact parabolic region; every point is achieved by a fastest meridian flip with at most two dwells. The globally sharp directional error ratio is \(1+8(\Omega T-\pi)/(3\pi)\), attained by dwelling at the damping-dark pole before the flip.
