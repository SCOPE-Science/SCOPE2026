Every fiber of the cubic Dickson polynomial \(X^3-3X\) modulo \(3^k\) has an explicit square-root-count formula, including the singular target classes missing from earlier nonexceptional Galois-ring preimage theorems. For every \(k\ge3\) exactly two residues maximize the fiber, and their locations switch with the parity of \(k\) once \(k\ge6\).
