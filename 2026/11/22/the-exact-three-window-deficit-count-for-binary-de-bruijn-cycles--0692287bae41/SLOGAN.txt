For every binary window order \(n\ge3\), the rotation-class count of circles containing exactly \(2^n-3\) different length-\(n\) windows has an explicit all-order closed form, split into two constant-plus-alternating forbidden triples and two primitive-period-three forbidden triples. The count is asymptotic to \(20/21\) times the number of full binary de Bruijn cycles, and the two forbidden-triple mechanisms contribute asymptotic shares \(7/10\) and \(3/10\).
