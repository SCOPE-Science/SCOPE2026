On the square rook graph \(K_n\square K_n\) for every \(n\ge3\), the optimal power-dominating placements form a connected adjacent-slide graph with exactly \(n(n-1)(n^n-n!)\) edges and diameter \(2n-2\). Exactly \(n^2\) configuration pairs attain that diameter: they exchange a one-column placement missing row \(r\) with a one-row placement missing column \(c\), with \(r,c\) arbitrary.
