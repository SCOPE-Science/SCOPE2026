# Assessment of the exposure-depth count-order theorem

## Correctness

PASS. The claim is for \(\lambda>0\), \(0\le\eta<1\), and \(0<p_L<p_H<1\), and an arbitrary nondegenerate prior. Conditioning on the opportunity flag and thinning a Poisson predecessor count gives exact zero-inflated Poisson masses. For positive count indices the likelihood ratio is a geometric progression. The only possible violation is the zero-to-one step; cross multiplication yields an exact critical structural-zero probability. A separate exponential comparison has strictly positive derivative for every positive exposure, giving a unique depth transition and the asymptotic. Coupling proves strict first-order stochastic dominance without implying likelihood-ratio order. Exact rational arithmetic confirms the witness. `checker.py` passed 120,000 supplementary positive-count formula comparisons; finite checks are not proofs.

## Originality

PASS on inspected evidence, with priority risk. The accepted manuscripts of Cavatorta, Guarino and Huck and of Guarino, Harmgart and Huck treat endogenous, finite strategic action histories, cascades, and equilibrium posteriors, not the independent, sometimes-absent Poisson opportunity pool here. The standard zero-inflated Poisson literature directly implies the mass function but the inspected materials did not state the unique count-order reversal threshold and exposure-depth phase diagram for two fixed state intensities, or the interpretation of a recorded opportunity bit. Searches of relevant indexed mathematical records and targeted public literature located related zero-inflated count, likelihood-ratio, and social-information work, without decisive dominating coverage. The phase boundary is an elementary consequence of established ZIP algebra; unpublished or uninspected stochastic-order treatments may cover it. This review does not certify worldwide priority.

## Value

PASS, narrowly. Unknown opportunity to act is directly motivated by aggregate social learning in which affirmative choices are visible while negative choices and predecessor counts are not. The exact threshold identifies when comparing zero and one visible actions reverses the usual evidence ordering, even as all positive-count tail probabilities move upward in the better state. A single exposure-eligibility bit removes this reversal within observed eligible cases. The distinction is decision-relevant for the interpretation and design of coarsened social-action observations, rather than simply a recoding of a count probability. No behavioral or field-calibration effect is inferred.

## Limits

Independent Bernoulli actions, state-independent zero exposure, Poisson predecessor opportunities, and known model parameters are essential assumptions. No endogenous equilibria, causal interventions, finite-sample guarantees, or unbounded claims beyond this model. Literature coverage and mathematical priority remain incomplete.
