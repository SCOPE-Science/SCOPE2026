The 28 bases of the Fano matroid, identified with six-bit points inside the odd-parity seven-cube, form a \(64,28,12\) Hadamard difference set and a degree-three bent Boolean support. Their complete signed Walsh spectrum is \(28\) once and \(\pm4\) at the 63 nontrivial frequencies, giving invertible convolution with condition number \(7\).
