Every finite-dimensional symmetric \(m\)-ary algebra over an infinite field embeds as an ideal of an \(m\)-ary evolution algebra of dimension at most \(\binom{n+m-1}{m}\), in every characteristic. Over \(\mathbb F_q\), universal embedding holds exactly when \(m\le q\); for every \(m>q\), a two-dimensional algebra violates a Frobenius identity satisfied by all \(m\)-ary evolution algebras.
