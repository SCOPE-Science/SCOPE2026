For the unweighted Bergman kernel of \(\Omega_m=\{(z,w_1,\ldots,w_m):|z|^4+|w_j|^2<1\}\), central-axis zeros are absent for every integer \(1\le m\le8\) but appear for the first time at \(m=9\) as exactly two simple nonreal conjugate scalar-product zeros. A beta-integral kernel formula and an exact rational Routh–Hurwitz certificate establish this sharp nine-fibre threshold without asserting full-domain zero-freeness below it.
