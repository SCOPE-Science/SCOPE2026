At the first permitted part appearing fewer than \(s\) times in a uniformly random \(\ell\)-regular partition, the actual multiplicity is asymptotically uniform on \(\{0,\ldots,s-1\}\), but has an exact linear first finite-size bias of order \(n^{-1/4}\) favoring smaller multiplicities. Consequently the chance that two distinct least-multiplicity gaps coincide exceeds its limiting value \(r/s\) by an explicit positive multiple of \(n^{-1/4}\), for every fixed \(\ell\ge2\) and \(1\le r<s\).
