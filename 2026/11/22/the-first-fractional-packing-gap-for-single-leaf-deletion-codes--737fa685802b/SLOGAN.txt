Unrooted, labeled binary phylogenetic tree codes that correct deletion of any one known-by-its-absence taxon attain the natural fractional packing bound with respectively 1, 3 and 15 codewords at 4, 5 and 6 leaves. At seven leaves the fractional optimum is 105, but an exhaustive exact-cover obstruction proves that every integer code has at most 104 members: the first strict packing gap.
