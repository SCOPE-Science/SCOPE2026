For exponential-weight Laguerre orthogonal projection, the unique worst bounded-variation step jump moves toward the hard edge through degrees \(0\)–\(4\), then abruptly relocates to an interior lobe at degree \(5\). An exhaustive exact-rational certificate locates every stationary jump through degree five and proves the sharp transition, with the two competing squared errors separated by more than \(0.0000521944\).
