For quadratic strict 3-isometric weighted shifts, the first negative Hausdorff difference of the Cauchy-dual orbit moments occurs at an exact arctangent phase crossing, globally minimal over all starting indices. Near the real-root discriminant it appears only at exponentially high order \(K\sim e^{\psi(u)+\pi/v}\), quantifying a sharp delay invisible in the known qualitative subnormality criterion.
