For a tensor trigonometric operator-norm integration ball, the spectral cubature lower bound is attained by zero, one, or two physical-cube evaluations but is strictly unattainable with every count \(3\le n\le d+1\), even for adaptive nonlinear algorithms. The associated leading-shell matrix has a positive physical-cube atomic decomposition exactly when it retains at most three single-coordinate excitations, requiring precisely twice as many nodes as excitations.
