The ternary cyclic morphism \(0\mapsto001\), \(1\mapsto112\), \(2\mapsto220\) has lattice-saturated Parikh spectra at substitution-power lengths \(1,3,9\), but its length-\(27\) spectrum has exactly 21 holes forming seven three-element cyclic orbits. This is the first integral-convexity failure along those four natural power scales, and its complete hole language is explicitly classified.
