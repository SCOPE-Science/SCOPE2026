The first hyperbolic toral Koopman operators with equal dynamical characteristic polynomials and equal matrix singular values but different isotropic heat-commutator energies occur at squared Frobenius cost \(18\). Their discrepancy is invisible to every algebraic power of heat time yet has the explicit nonzero leading scale \(-2\pi(\sqrt5\,t)^{-1}e^{-\pi^2/(10t)}\).
