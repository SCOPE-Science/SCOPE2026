The first prime \(p\equiv1\pmod4\) where dihedral genus-two curves can have isogenous Jacobians but different rational two-torsion is \(p=13\): two explicit curves share \((1+2T+13T^2)^2\), yet their rational Jacobian two-torsion orders are \(4\) and \(16\). The full marked characteristic-thirteen family has exactly two mixed Weil classes, and a general branch-splitting argument gives the two-torsion strata over all odd fields of size congruent to one modulo four.
