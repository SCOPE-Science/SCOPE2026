# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS
The finite proof is complete because the independent-dominating equivalence characterizes *all* 42 concepts and every one of 65,536 support masks is checked. An independent maximal-clique implementation produces the same 42 hypotheses and reconstructs precisely the downward closure of four explicitly listed cubical facet orbits, agreeing support by support. All 384 automorphisms are generated and checked. No higher-dimensional generalization or inferred asymptotic claim is made.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence
The Hamming neighborhood VC theorem, the asymptotic MIS enumeration and size polynomials, and the general MIS incidence VC upper bound are credited. The nonpure eight-antipodal-pair versus 104-four-support facet structure is neither a cardinality count of maximal independent sets nor a consequence of these broad bounds. Actual published-finding corpus searches found shattering results on forests, cycles, whiskered graphs, but no unwhiskered four-cube face-orbit census. Ignatov's 2023 chapter shares the objects and may include undocumented tables: its full text was not accessible, so historical priority is a genuine residual risk rather than a proven negative.

## Value — PASS
A natural, highly symmetric graph exhibits a shattering complex with two distinct facet sizes for the first time along the four smallest Boolean cubes. Four simple orbit representatives characterize every shattered support, and the eight antipodal facets provide a sharp geometric obstruction with a direct nonmatroidal consequence. This is a motivated complete finite invariant classification, not a renamed generic theorem or a restatement of known total MIS counts.
