For vertex-membership queries against all maximal independent sets of the sixteen-vertex Boolean cube, the shattered-support complex has polynomial \(1+16z+88z^2+160z^3+104z^4\) and is nonpure: eight antipodal pairs and 104 quartets are its facets. Four cubical-symmetry orbit representatives give a complete support characterization, and exhaustive verification shows this is the first nonpure case through cube dimension four.
