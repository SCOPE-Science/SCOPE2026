In the generic structure of three independent linear orders, incomparability-feasibility matrices on one-point types over at most three named parameters have Smith forms \(I_3\oplus0_5\), \(I_{15}\oplus(2)\oplus0_{11}\), and \(I_{44}\oplus(3)\oplus0_{19}\). Thus the first odd-primary torsion appears already at three parameters, as one order-three cokernel factor; exact unimodular operation certificates prove all three finite cases.
