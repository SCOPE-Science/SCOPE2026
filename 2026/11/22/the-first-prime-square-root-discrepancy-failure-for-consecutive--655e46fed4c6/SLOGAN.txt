The least prime at which dyadic counts of consecutive Stern residues exceed square-root root growth is \(11\): every prime below it has discrepancy root growth at most \(\sqrt2\), whereas the pair \((1,2)\pmod{11}\) has root growth at least \(\rho=1.5213797068\ldots\). The obstruction is witnessed by an exact irreducible cubic mode of a finite integer transfer matrix.
