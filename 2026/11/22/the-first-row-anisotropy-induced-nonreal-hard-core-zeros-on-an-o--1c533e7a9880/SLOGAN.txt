On an open hard-core two-row ladder with constant but unequal row fugacities \(z\) and \(tz\), the first nonreal complex-fugacity partition zeros appear at exactly five rungs. Their onset is sharp: five-rung zeros are all real for \(T^{-1}\le t\le T\), with double roots at equality, and include one conjugate pair outside, where \(T=4.0117873025\ldots\) is the unique specified algebraic threshold; every shorter ladder retains exclusively real zeros for all \(t>0\).
