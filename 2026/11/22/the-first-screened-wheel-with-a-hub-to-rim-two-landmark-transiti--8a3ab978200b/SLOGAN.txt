For two noiseless covariance landmarks on uniformly screened wheel graphs, the first hub-to-rim optimal-design reversal occurs on the wheel with seven rim vertices, at the unique positive root \(\alpha=0.175354708826\ldots\) of an explicit degree-ten polynomial. Smaller noncomplete wheels never favor the hub, while every larger wheel favors it under sufficiently weak screening and avoids it under sufficiently strong screening.
