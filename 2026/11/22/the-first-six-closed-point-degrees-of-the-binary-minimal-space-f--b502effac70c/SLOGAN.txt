For the unique projective class of smooth minimal binary space-filling genus-\(19\) complete intersections, the first six extension counts are \((15,15,15,19,50,15)\), giving exactly one closed point of degree four and seven of degree five and no closed points of degree two, three or six. The natural cyclic order-five symmetry permutes the seven degree-five places as \((1,1,5)\), so its genus-three quotient has three split and two inert rational fibers.
