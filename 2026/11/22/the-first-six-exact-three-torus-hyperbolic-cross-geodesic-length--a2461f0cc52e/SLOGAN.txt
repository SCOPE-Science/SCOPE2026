For exact hyperbolic-cross quadrature along one primitive closed geodesic on the flat three-torus, the first six sharp squared winding lengths are \(21,65,101,133,133,314\), with exactly one optimal absolute-coordinate triple at each strength. The unexpected equality of the strength-four and strength-five optima is followed by a sharp length jump at strength six; every bound and equality case has a complete finite integer certificate.
