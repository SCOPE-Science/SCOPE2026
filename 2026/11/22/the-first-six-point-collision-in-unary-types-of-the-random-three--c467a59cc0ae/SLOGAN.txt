For the unordered random three-polyhedron, the number of nonalgebraic one-types over a named base is \(\sum_{S\subseteq V}2^{e(G[S])}\), determined solely by its edge skeleton. This scalar first fails to distinguish nonisomorphic canonical triangle-filled bases on six vertices, in exactly three two-element graph fibres, with a unique pair of connected graphs at value \(832\).
