For the binary two-plane Grassmannian queried by individual Plücker minors, the complete shattering polynomials in ambient dimensions four, five and six have respective VC dimensions four, six and eight, yet all three shattered-support complexes are nonpure. Their maximal queried-edge configurations range from disjoint triangles to theta graphs, and their reduced mod-two homology is nonzero in two adjacent degrees for the last two dimensions.
