The derangement-generated six-letter permutation complex has reduced integral homology \(\widetilde H_3\cong\mathbb Z\), \(\widetilde H_4\cong\mathbb Z^{74}\), and \(\widetilde H_5\cong\mathbb Z^{20}\), and no other reduced homology or torsion. It is the first such derangement-generated complex, among alphabet sizes at least two, with nonzero homology in three degrees.
