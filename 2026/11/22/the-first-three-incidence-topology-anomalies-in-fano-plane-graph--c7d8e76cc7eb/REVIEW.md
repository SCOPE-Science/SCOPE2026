# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The theorem is limited to the completely enumerated 1562 edge sets of sizes zero through three, with 14 disconnected cases excluded. The standalone exact binary Gaussian elimination independently constructs every simplex and every boundary for each connected graph, verifies all positive-degree dimensions, and tests the incidence criterion without extrapolating to uncomputed cases. One boundary-rank identity and direct enumeration establish the entire stated finite result. A separate exploratory implementation reproduced the triple-deletion rank distribution. No integral or infinite-family assertion is made.

## Originality — PASS against inspected evidence
Adams–Coskunuzer and Feng–Nukala address general graph-power persistence and different scale-two finite metrics. The closest published Fano incidence result computes only the intact graph's homotopy type and uses distance-two adjacency that breaks under deleted edges. The closest Fano induced-matching result knows the 84 matching types and their exchange graph but not the Vietoris–Rips homological response under deletion of their flags. Actual source and archive searches for incidence-deletion Rips ranks did not uncover an equivalent or stronger failure classification. Unindexed similar calculations cannot be excluded.

## Value — PASS
This is a motivated sharp edge-failure threshold in a canonical highly symmetric incidence geometry, not an arbitrary selection of configurations. One and two failures obey predictable rank loss; precisely at three failures, the homological rank rebounds on 84 configurations and distinguishes the geometrically meaningful 28/28/28 induced-matching types, isolating 28 configurations with two extra classes. This furnishes a complete finite mod-two sensitivity classification and exposes an interaction of graph-metric perturbation with finite geometry. Its value does not require an unproved general theorem.

## Closest literature and limitations
The intact finite-projective-plane classification proves eight two-spheres at scale two, which is a special undamaged case and does not imply the failure distribution. The known Fano matching-exchange paper supplies the 84 triple types and their counts but does not compute homology of damaged incidence graph squares. Mod-two homology is not integral homotopy; no priority claim excludes unindexed results. The certificate addresses all 1548 connected cases, not higher-order planes.
