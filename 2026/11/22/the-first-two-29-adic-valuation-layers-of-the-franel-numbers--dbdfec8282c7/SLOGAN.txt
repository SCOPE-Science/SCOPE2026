For the first odd type-III Franel prime \(29\), the valuations \(\nu_{29}(F_n)=0\) and \(1\) have an exact base-\(29\) digit characterization; the first shell excludes precisely two adjacent-digit pairs, \((12,17)\) and \((16,11)\). Below \(29^L\), the exact count of valuation-one indices is \(3\cdot26^{L-1}+76(L-1)26^{L-2}\) for every \(L\ge2\).
