On the unit square torus with intrinsic taxicab metric, the exact covering-number plateaux are five balls for \(1/3\le r<3/8\), four for \(3/8\le r<1/2\), and two for \(1/2\le r<1\). Together with a sharp fiber obstruction below \(r=1/3\), explicit rational diamond covers show that neither three nor six can ever be the minimum number of covering balls.
