For the regularized graph Green kernel \(K_G=(I+L_G)^{-1}\), diagonal-greedy selection of two Nyström columns is trace-optimal on every connected graph of at most four vertices, but first fails on exactly one five-vertex graph topology: the diamond with a pendant leaf. Its unique optimal pair has residual trace \(37/39\), whereas both greedy tie choices give \(22/23\); an exact exhaustive enumeration covers all \(728\) connected labelled five-vertex graphs.
