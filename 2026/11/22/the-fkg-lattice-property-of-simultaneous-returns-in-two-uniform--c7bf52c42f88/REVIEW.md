# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The exact block factorization is a bijection at every fixed simultaneous return set. Squared Catalan numbers are moments of a product of independent positive Marchenko–Pastur variables. A Schur-complement resolvent produces a Stieltjes moment representation of the no-common-return block coefficients from index two, while the index-one boundary inequality is checked algebraically. Strict Catalan log-convexity yields positive pairwise contact covariances. Four-cell inequalities imply finite-lattice log-supermodularity, and FKG gives positive association, including arbitrary positive contact fugacities. The independent exact checker passes 1,340 assertions through size eight.

**Originality — PASS with risk.** Targeted searches in published-finding corpus and source comparisons found no statement of full FKG lattice positivity for the exact simultaneous-return set of two uniform Dyck paths. The closely related literature develops individual Dyck contacts, pair encodings, and meandric component enumeration; general FKG and generic negative-binomial sequence limits are acknowledged, not claimed new. An equivalent renewal or Boolean-cumulant formulation in work not inspected in full is the main risk; independent historical scrutiny is needed.

**Value — PASS.** The theorem gives a strong positive-dependence property for an entire natural random contact configuration, uniform across all orders and every finite path length, rather than a single covariance or numerical statistic. It persists with heterogeneous pinning weights, making it directly applicable to contact-conditioned Dyck/meandric models.
