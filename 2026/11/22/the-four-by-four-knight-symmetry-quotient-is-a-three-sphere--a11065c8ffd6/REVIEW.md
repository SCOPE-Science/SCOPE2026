# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In the barycentric subdivision, ranks along a flag are distinct and preserved by D4. If points of the same closed flag simplex lie in one orbit, their support masks must match rank by rank, as must their coefficients. This gives injectivity on each closed simplex, not just pointwise stabilizers, and makes the flag-orbit cells regular. Orient by increasing rank; deleting different positions cannot identify faces because their rank sets differ. An independent enumeration from actual board masks found 24 attack edges, 1,364 nonempty independent faces, and flag-orbit counts (217,4801,35910,126666,239340,249840,136080,30240). A reverse-ordered free-face traversal checked unique live codimension-one cofaces, including maximality of the coface, and removed 411,365 pairs. The resulting core has (22,102,160,80) cells and no higher cells. Its integral boundaries compose to zero, with Smith ranks 21, 81 and 79 and all nonzero Smith factors one. The torsion of H_(d−1) equals that of coker ∂d, since the latter maps onto the free group im ∂_(d−1) with kernel H_(d−1). Thus the integral homology has one copy of Z each in degrees 0 and 3 and nothing else. A spanning tree and oriented triangle relators give a presentation in which all 81 generators are eliminated by Tietze moves. A separate reconstruction also matched the frozen certificate flag by flag and boundary by boundary. With π1 trivial, Hurewicz first gives π2=0 and then represents an H3 generator by a map S³ to the quotient. This map is an integral homology isomorphism between simply connected CW spaces and is a homotopy equivalence by homological Whitehead.

The distinct contribution supported here is the integral sphere homotopy type of the ordinary knight orbit space.
