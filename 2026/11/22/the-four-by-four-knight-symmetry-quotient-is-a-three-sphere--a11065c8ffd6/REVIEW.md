## Correctness

**PASS.** The claim concerns the full ordinary geometric quotient, modeled by flag-orbits of barycentric simplices with pointwise chain stabilizers. The independent-set enumeration has 1,364 nonempty faces, and the quotient has 823,094 orbit-flag cells across dimensions zero through seven. Exactly 411,365 verified elementary collapses leave 22, 102, 160 and 80 cells in dimensions zero through three. Integral boundary compositions vanish, and 21, 81 and 79 unimodular cancellations leave one zero-cell and one three-cell at chain level. Independently, a spanning-tree presentation with 81 generators is completely eliminated using 160 triangle relations; this proves simple connectivity, which integral homology alone would not prove. Hurewicz and the simply connected homological Whitehead theorem establish the claimed sphere homotopy type. The supplied source files and certificate were executed directly for this classification.
