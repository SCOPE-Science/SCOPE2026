# Scientific assessment: four-level ordered-witness spectrum

## Correctness — PASS
The domain is precisely a cubic seed in three Boolean variables plus any quadratic refinement of a nondegenerate binary symplectic form. The seed supports give symmetric-difference and directed-difference multiplicities, while nondegeneracy of the bilinear form yields exactly one-quarter or one-half of the available symplectic vectors in each relevant case. The five disjoint cases establish the infinite-family theorem; a separate exhaustive checker recomputes the defining polar and all ordered-pair witnesses at symplectic dimensions 2, 4, and 6. The checker is a finite confirmation, not the general proof.

## Originality — PASS (historical priority qualified)
The November 2026 motivating paper establishes existential ordered separation for this family and provides ingredients used here, but not a quantitative all-pairs witness histogram or an optimal uniform density. The 2011 origin of the condition addresses existence in other normal forms; the 2015 classification concerns real graded-algebra isomorphisms. Search comparisons with relevant indexed research findings found no equivalent or stronger statement for the same cubic–symplectic family and ordered count. The closed formulas are derived rather than extracted from a published table; unindexed historical studies or consequences hidden in inaccessible literature remain possible. This is a refinement, not an independent reproof of the earlier existence theorem.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The original condition is existential. The exact minimum gives a robust fraction \(1/8\) of all ambient vectors for every pair, the exceptional maximum is attained for exactly six ordered pairs, and the entire multiplicity law explains why almost all pairs have witness density \(1/4\). These are natural quantitative properties of the published counterexample family in every admissible symplectic dimension, not arbitrary small-case parameters.

## Scope and limitations
The result is specific to this seed and a nondegenerate binary symplectic summand; it does not claim generic or universal properties of all cubic forms. Its novelty is an informed primary-source comparison rather than an independent historical certification. The independent audit has not been performed.
