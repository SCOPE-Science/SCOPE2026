For the cubic–symplectic Boolean forms on \(\mathbb F_2^{3+2m}\), every ordered distinct nonzero pair has at least \(2^{2m}\) separating witnesses, a sharp one-eighth fraction of the ambient space. All witness counts are exactly \(Q,2Q,3Q,5Q\), with multiplicities \(98Q-62,64(Q-1)(Q-2),70(Q-1),6\) respectively, where \(Q=2^{2m}\).
