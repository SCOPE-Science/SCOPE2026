The generalized Petersen graphs \(P(n,3)\) have edge metric dimension three exactly at orders \(n=9,10\), with dimension four for every other admissible \(n\ge7\). The four excluded small orders \(n=7,8,9,10\) have respectively \(469,368,18,160\) minimum resolving landmark sets, establishing a sharp two-order exception before the published stable range.
