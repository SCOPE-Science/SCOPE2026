The sevenfold source-rotation quotient of \(\operatorname{Hom}(C_7,K_4)\) has fundamental group exactly \(\mathbb Z/14\mathbb Z\), not just first homology of that order. Its only nonzero positive-degree integral homology is \(H_1\cong\mathbb Z/14\mathbb Z\) and \(H_3\cong\mathbb Z\); the covering graph-colouring complex has fundamental group \(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\).
