For every finite field, the nine-dimensional quantum complete intersection \(\mathbb F_q\langle x,y\rangle/(x^3,y^3,yx-\lambda xy)\) has a complete, four-phase element-centralizer rank distribution. A primitive fourth root \(\lambda\) causes the otherwise attainable commutator rank three to disappear while ranks one, two, and four survive; the primitive-third-root generic specialization was previously known.
