# Scientific review: the full center of the type-A 0-Hecke monoid

## Correctness — PASS

The one-step multiplication rules yield an exact double-descent/fixed-pair criterion for commuting with each simple generator. A complete boundary-peeling induction forces the nested outer-pair reversal normal forms and excludes every other permutation. A reduced expression for the endpoint transposition gives the exact power law and terminal absorption. The independently implemented finite checker enumerates all permutations through degree eight and verifies every proposed central product. Finite experiments supplement, rather than replace, the proof.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected primary sources

König (arXiv:2112.01768) studies the *linear center of the monoid algebra*, not the monoid's individual central permutations. Berenstein, Greenstein and Li (arXiv:2605.09761), especially Proposition 4.18 and Corollary 4.20, give parabolic product laws that already explain the product portion of the stated theorem once the elements are known; their main classification of commuting idempotents does not determine all central nonidempotent elements. The published Catalan maximal-clique result classifies only sets of commuting idempotents and explicitly excludes nonidempotent commuting submonoids. No inspected primary source directly gave the exhaustive full-center criterion and classification. Historical priority remains provisional, especially for implicit folklore consequences of parabolic-product theory.

## Value — PASS

Determining the entire center of a standard family of finite Coxeter monoids is a natural structural question. The center is exactly monogenic and its nilpotent-to-absorbing power chain has length growing with degree, in contrast with its two idempotents. The result identifies an infinite-family invariant and separates the monoid center from the much larger algebra center, rather than reporting an arbitrary finite count.
