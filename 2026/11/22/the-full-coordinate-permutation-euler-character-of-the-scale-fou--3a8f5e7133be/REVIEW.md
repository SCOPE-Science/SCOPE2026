# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The claim has a fixed finite scope: the inclusive Hamming six-cube at scale four, the coordinate permutation group, and rational alternating homology. Barycentric-orbit coordinates establish a PL model of every geometric fixed locus as the independence complex of an explicit incompatibility graph. Reduced Lefschetz implies that the signed character is exactly the independence polynomial at \(-1\). Complete orbit enumeration for all eleven conjugacy types and the exhaustive deletion/closed-neighborhood recurrence yield the displayed integers. Two pivot orders agree for every case; the dimension-two and dimension-three analogues are checked by an independent orbit generator against direct independent-set enumeration. Exact Murnaghan–Nakayama projections recover the eleven Specht coefficients, and all class-weighted character orthogonality identities hold. Invariant rational homology and trivial-module averaging give quotient reduced Euler characteristic \(-7\). Every numerical part is a finite computation on at most 64 vertices, not an assertion inferred from partial enumeration.

## Originality — PASS with priority uncertainty
The most relevant cohomological-topology paper [Bendersky–Elia–Grbić, arXiv:2605.00705] treats connectivity and classes rather than coordinate-permutation characters; the relevant paper on hyperoctahedral representations [Galetto–Montaño–Wellner, arXiv:2606.20784] classifies scales at most three or the distinct scale \(n-1\). The closest publicly indexed six-cube scale-four character covers translations \((C_2)^6\) only, while the closest coordinate-group fixed-space theorem determines simultaneous fixed points of the entire group, not Lefschetz traces for each coordinate-conjugacy type. The eleven values and quotient Euler invariant were not found as statements or implications in the inspected material. Unknown equivalent calculations remain a priority risk; a search miss alone is not a proof of novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The scale-four six-cube is a natural first intermediate case left outside previously completed equivariant ranges, not a contrived arbitrary subcomplex. The full \(S_6\)-class function refines the known nonequivariant rank and translation-subgroup character; its trivial multiplicity determines a concrete rational invariant of the genuine geometric symmetry quotient. The complete, reproducible finite result gives an equivariant boundary datum relevant to studying larger hypercube Vietoris–Rips complexes and symmetry-based reductions. A paper-sized extension or a new all-dimensional theorem is not claimed.

## Limits
This character is virtual alternating homology, and it does not specify the two individual homology degrees, all signed-coordinate symmetries, or integral quotient torsion. The exact checker can require substantial memory. Independent mathematical validation is outstanding.
