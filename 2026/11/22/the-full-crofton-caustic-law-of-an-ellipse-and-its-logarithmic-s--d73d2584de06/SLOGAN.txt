For Crofton-uniform lines meeting a noncircular ellipse, the confocal caustic parameter has a complete two-branch elliptic-integral distribution with a sharp logarithmic singularity at the focal-segment separatrix. Its full law reconstructs both ellipse axes, and the exact probability of a hyperbolic caustic is the focal-segment length divided by the ellipse's mean line-width normalization, namely \(4\sqrt{a^2-b^2}/P\).
