# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. Each perfect matching is an exact one- or two-column tiling, and a support of physical edge queries is shattered precisely when the chosen horizontal positions are disjoint and all residual queried rung columns have distinct unqueried adjacent partners. The proof resolves this condition by alternating runs and enumerates it using a formal transfer matrix, then explicitly counts the top/bottom physical edge alternatives. Exact graph-based finite tests agree in 246,930 support instances and verify all stated coefficients through one hundred columns. These checks supplement, rather than replace, the universal proof.
