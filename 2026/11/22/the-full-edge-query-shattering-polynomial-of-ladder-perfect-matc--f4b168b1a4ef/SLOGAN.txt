The complete physical-edge shattering polynomial for perfect matchings of every two-row, \(n\)-column ladder has generating function \(1/(1-t-4zt^2+zt^3)\), with explicit coefficients and a local alternating-run test for every support. Its shattered-support complex is already nonpure at six columns, even though the previously known VC dimension is \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\).
