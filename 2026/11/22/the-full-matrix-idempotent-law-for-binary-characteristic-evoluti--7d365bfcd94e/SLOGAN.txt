For uniformly random, not necessarily perfect evolution algebras over every binary finite field, the complete finite-dimensional idempotent-count law has an explicit Gaussian-binomial formula whose frame moments are \(\prod_{i=0}^{k-1}(1-q^{i-n})\). Although this distribution depends on field size, it converges uniformly in that size to the known binary general-linear fixed-space law, and the same limiting law persists even when matrices are conditioned to be singular at any fixed field size.
