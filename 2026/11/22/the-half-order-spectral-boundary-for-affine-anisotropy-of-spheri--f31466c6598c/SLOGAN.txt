For fractional spherical heat covariances with spectrum \(e^{-t[\ell(\ell+d-2)]^\alpha}\), a nonzero affine anisotropy factor \(a+\langle v,x+y\rangle\) can preserve positive definiteness exactly when \(0<\alpha\le1/2\); for every \(\alpha>1/2\), any nonzero \(v\) fails regardless of \(a\). More generally, a strictly positive isotropic spherical spectrum allows such a perturbation precisely when adjacent harmonic coefficient ratios are uniformly bounded, with the exact admissibility threshold given by a degree-neighbor operator norm.
