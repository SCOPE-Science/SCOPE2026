For Bergman Toeplitz operators with symbol \(z+C|z|^s\), the previously implicit Jacobi norm criterion simplifies to the sharp bound \(|C|\leq1/s\) exactly for \(s\geq1/2\). Below that exponent the normalizing Jacobi matrix acquires infinitely many symmetric exterior eigenvalue pairs, with positive-edge accumulation \(\lambda_j-s\sim s(1/2-s)^2/(2j^2)\), forcing a strictly smaller hyponormality window.
