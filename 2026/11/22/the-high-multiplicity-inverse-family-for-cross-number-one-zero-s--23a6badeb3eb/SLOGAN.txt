In the twice-prime cyclic group \(C_{2p}\), the sharp-length cross-number-one-avoiding sequences with a term repeated at least \(2p-1\) times form an explicitly classified family of exactly \((p-1)^2(p-2)\) multisets. Their residual terms come from one or two precise residue classes, yielding a complete two-/three-support census for every odd prime \(p\).
