For every rank-two sandwich variant of the full transformation semigroup on \(n\) points, its idempotent commuting graph is the edge subdivision of \(2^{n-2}K_{u,v}\), with a complete explicit adjacency spectrum and full automorphism group for \(n\ge4\). The spectrum recovers the sandwich's two kernel-fiber sizes and hence its variant-isomorphism type, although the underlying kernel-partition classification itself was previously known.
