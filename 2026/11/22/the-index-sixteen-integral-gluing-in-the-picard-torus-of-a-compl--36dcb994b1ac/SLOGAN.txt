For the six lines joining four geometric points of a projective frame, the six-dimensional Picard-torus character lattice contains its three natural primitive rank-two Klein-four eigensublattices with quotient exactly \((\mathbb Z/2)^4\). All five finite-field Frobenius types admit explicit **integral** Weil-restriction and norm-one decompositions, including a four-cycle torus with rational-point group \(C_{q^4-1}\times C_{q^2+1}\).
