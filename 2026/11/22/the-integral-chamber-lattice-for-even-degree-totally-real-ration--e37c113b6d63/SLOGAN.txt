For totally real arrangements of rational plane curves of even degrees with degree gcd \(d\), the orientable real chambers span an integral homology lattice of exact index \((d/2)2^\epsilon\), where \(\epsilon\) records a nontrivial cycle in the real projective plane. Each normalization circle contributes the unique order-two torsion class, so the complete integral relations are governed by the parity of the normalized component degrees.
