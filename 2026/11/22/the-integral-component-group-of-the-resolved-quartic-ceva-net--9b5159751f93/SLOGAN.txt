Resolving the nineteen multiple points of the quartic Ceva twelve-line net yields a stable genus-thirty boundary with critical group \((\mathbb Z/11)^3\oplus(\mathbb Z/33)^4\oplus(\mathbb Z/528)^2\). Its unexpected nine-dimensional \(11\)-primary torsion is an integral component-group invariant that the classical multinet incidence data alone do not specify.
