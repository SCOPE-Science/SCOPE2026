The canonical 28-vertex Reye incidence graph has exact critical group \(\mathbb Z/2\oplus(\mathbb Z/8)^2\oplus(\mathbb Z/24)^4\oplus(\mathbb Z/96)^2\), spanning-tree count \(2^{29}3^6\), and exponent \(96\). This group is also the geometric Néron component group for any regular unit-thickness smoothing of the associated abstract genus-\(21\) stable rational-components curve.
