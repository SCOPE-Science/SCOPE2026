For the 126 conic pencils and 56 exceptional curves on a smooth degree-two del Pezzo surface, the unsigned 12-component support map has Smith factors \(1^{28},2^6,6\), with 21 zero factors. Its cokernel is \(\mathbb Z^{21}\oplus(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^6\oplus\mathbb Z/6\mathbb Z\); the three-primary obstruction is intrinsic to integral support reconstruction.
