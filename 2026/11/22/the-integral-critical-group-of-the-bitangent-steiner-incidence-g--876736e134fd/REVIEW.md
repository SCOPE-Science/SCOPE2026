# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The classical theta criterion identifies the bitangent-to-Steiner incidence with a fixed binary quadratic-refinement matrix. Direct enumeration proves the \(2\)-design Gram identity; the block Laplacian spectrum and Kirchhoff formula give the exact torsion-prime set and total order. The included standalone verifier executes the entire finite Smith reduction at those primes and confirms it for both sorts of deleted vertices. Every local pivot is certified below the modulus precision, and independent modular ranks and the determinant provide consistency checks. The group formula is only for the geometric integral graph critical group.

## Originality

PASS, subject to historical-priority limits. Dolgachev and Plaumann–Sturmfels–Vinzant establish the geometry; the 2011 Crnković–Mikulić Crnković article constructs the symplectic design; Bergvall analyzes classical quartic structures. The closest accessible integral calculation, *Integral Steiner reconstruction lattice of plane-quartic bitangents*, proves Smith factors of the rectangular \(28\times63\) incidence map, not the reduced \(90\times90\) graph Laplacian. These are not the same group. General quasi-symmetric-design Smith literature has not been exhaustively ruled out. No inspected full statement contains or logically states the present invariant-factor classification.

## Mathematical value

PASS. This computes a complete canonical integral invariant of the entire intrinsic theta/Steiner incidence configuration, not an arbitrary labelled slice. Its nontrivial \(2\)-primary depth and simultaneous \(3\)-, \(7\)-, and \(11\)-primary torsion are invisible from graph cycle rank and rational spectrum alone. It also determines the geometric Jacobian component group of a natural class of stable genus-\(666\) dual-graph degenerations under explicit regular unit-thickness hypotheses.
