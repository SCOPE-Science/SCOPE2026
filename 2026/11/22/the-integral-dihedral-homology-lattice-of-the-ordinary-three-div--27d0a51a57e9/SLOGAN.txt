For every \(k\ge1\), the two-dimensional integral homology of \(\operatorname{Ind}(C_{3k})\) has explicit unimodular dihedral rotation and reflection matrices arising from three concrete signed cross-polytope cycles. Its full-dihedral coinvariants are \(\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z\) exactly when \(k\equiv3\pmod4\), and vanish otherwise; this distinguishes integral symmetry information invisible in the rational character.
