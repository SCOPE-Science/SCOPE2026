The \(36\) Schläfli double-sixes and \(45\) tritangents on any smooth complex cubic surface have an integral two-line-overlap incidence map with cokernel \(\mathbb Z^{15}\oplus(\mathbb Z/3)^5\oplus\mathbb Z/18\). Its complementary non-overlap map has cokernel \(\mathbb Z^{15}\oplus(\mathbb Z/3)^7\), revealing a three-primary relation between two classical \(E_6\) configurations.
