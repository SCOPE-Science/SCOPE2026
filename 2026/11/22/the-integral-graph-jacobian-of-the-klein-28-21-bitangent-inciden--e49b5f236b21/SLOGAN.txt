The canonical 49-vertex Levi graph joining the 28 bitangents of Klein's quartic to their 21 four-line meeting points has critical group \((\mathbb Z/2)^4\oplus\mathbb Z/18\oplus\mathbb Z/54\oplus\mathbb Z/108\oplus(\mathbb Z/756)^5\). An explicit labelled duality with the 21 Klein lines and their 28 triple points gives an independent exact verification of this integral invariant.
