The normal-crossings boundary of the classical Desargues ten-line arrangement has genus \(26\) and integral graph critical group \((\mathbb Z/2)^3\oplus\mathbb Z/14\oplus\mathbb Z/28\oplus(\mathbb Z/140)^2\oplus\mathbb Z/420\). For a regular semistable unit-thickness smoothing this is the complete geometric Néron component group, distinguished from the critical group of the ordinary Desargues Levi graph.
