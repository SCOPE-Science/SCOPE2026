The eighty Rosenhain tetrads of a genus-two Jacobian Kummer quartic generate an index-\(128\) lattice in the formal integer node group with quotient \(\mathbb Z/8\mathbb Z\oplus(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^4\). Every individual node has exact order eight in this quotient, unlike the order-four obstruction for the complementary Göpel-tetrad lattice.
