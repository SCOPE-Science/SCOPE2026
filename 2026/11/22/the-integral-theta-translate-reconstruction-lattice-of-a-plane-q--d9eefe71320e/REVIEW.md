# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

For every smooth nonhyperelliptic genus-three curve in characteristic different from two, choosing an even theta characteristic turns effectivity of the shifted theta characteristics into the standard nondegenerate binary quadratic condition on six coordinates. The parity-support calculation proves the exact symmetric design identity \(M^2=16I+12J\), so invertibility, determinant, and the inverse reconstruction bound do not depend on computation. The standalone finite modular Smith algorithm implements explicit invertible operations over \(\mathbb Z/2^r\), returns exactly 8, 14, 20, 14, 8 invariant factors of successive powers \(1,2,4,8,16\), and the known determinant fixes the remaining unique \(7\)-factor. Its results agree with a separate computer-algebra Smith computation. Limits: no claim about Cartier, cohomology, or intrinsic divisor class relations is made.

## Originality — PASS (with residual risk)

Bergvall's original arXiv paper (2021, section 2.2 and theta-torsor discussion) identifies the geometric action, but contains no integral quotient statement. Polujan–Pott (2020, section III and Table A.1) define exactly the associated quadratic-bent support translation design, but the Smith form in Table A.1 is for its different 128-point graph-development incidence; the table cannot by mere identification supply the 7-primary factor of the present 64-point lattice. Crnković–Švob (2021, section 3) and Parker–Spence–Tonchev (1994, available abstract) establish the same Menon parameters and classical symmetries. The latter's full text was not accessible, so prior equivalence in older design-theoretic tables is the main residual priority risk. Searches included theta-divisor, symplectic symmetric design, quadratic bent translation, exact invariant factors, and existing integral bitangent incidence findings.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The 64 translated theta supports are canonically motivated by genus-three two-torsion and the 28 bitangents; their integral span, not an arbitrary selected submatrix, is the complete natural invariant. Knowing the parameters or rational rank does not establish the \(2\)-primary torsion profile or the sharp order \(112\) of a formal torsion-point class. The result specifies exactly the integral information lost in recovering pointwise weights from all even-theta translations. It is a complete, reproducible lattice classification for a natural geometric configuration, not a routine corollary of the published graph-design Smith table.

## Scope and residual questions

The result describes a free formal incidence group, not a Chow group or the Néron–Severi lattice. Recognition of the equivalent symplectic \(2\)-design in older coding literature raises priority risk beyond the inspected articles; independent mathematical audit is outstanding.
