Every Euclidean triangle has the same unrounded affine isotropic constant, but the first loss under outer disk rounding is exactly its Euclidean isoperimetric deficit times \(t^2/(108A^2)\); this identity remains valid for all scalene triangles. The signed inner/outer profile is \(C^1\) but not \(C^2\), and equilateral triangles uniquely minimize the scale-free right curvature.
