For uniform points inserted sequentially on a circle, the next strict decrease in the longest gap has an exponential waiting-time limit, but the original rank of the replacement longest gap has a heavy tail \(\Pr(K>k)=1/k\). The appropriately scaled size of that first drop has the explicit distribution \(\Pr(Y\le y)=1-y/(e^y-1)\) and mean \(\pi^2/6\), with a joint construction linking the waiting time, replacement rank, and drop.
