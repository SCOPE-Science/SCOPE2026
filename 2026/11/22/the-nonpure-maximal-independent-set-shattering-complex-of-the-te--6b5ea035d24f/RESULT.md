# The nonpure maximal-independent-set shattering complex of the ternary Hamming three-cube

## Finding
Let \(H(3,3)=K_3\square K_3\square K_3\) be the graph on the \(27\) words in \(\{0,1,2\}^3\), with adjacency at Hamming distance one. For every inclusion-maximal independent vertex set \(C\), use the Boolean concept \(x\mapsto \mathbf{1}_{x\in C}\). There are exactly \(390\) concepts, of sizes \(5,7,9\) in respective multiplicities \(54,324,12\).

The complete shattering polynomial is
\[
\Phi(z)=1+27z+270z^2+1278z^3+2862z^4+2322z^5.
\]
Thus \(\operatorname{VCdim}=5\). The associated simplicial complex of shattered supports is **nonpure**: its inclusion-maximal faces are precisely \(36\) three-vertex supports and \(2322\) five-vertex supports, with none of size four. The \(36\) three-facets are exactly the coordinate-transversal triples: in each of the three coordinates their entries are \(0,1,2\) in some order. Under the full \(S_3\wr S_3\) automorphism group, these triples form one orbit of size \(36\), and the five-facets form five orbits of sizes \(54,324,648,648,648\).

## Assumptions and scope
Only the specified finite \(27\)-vertex Hamming graph is considered. The term *maximal independent* means inclusion-maximal rather than maximum-cardinality. A support \(S\subseteq V\) is shattered if for each \(T\subseteq S\) some maximal independent set \(C\) satisfies \(C\cap S=T\). The empty support is included. Vertices are numbered from \(0\) through \(26\) by their base-three digits; changing the digit order does not change any enumerated quantity.

## Proof
**Step 1 (complete list of possible supports and concepts).** Every independent subset is generated by the recurrence: starting with \((I,A)=(\varnothing,V)\), choose one available vertex \(v\), replace \((I,A)\) by \((I\cup\{v\}, A\cap\{w:w>v\}\setminus N(v))\), and recurse. Every independent subset has a unique increasing vertex sequence, so this procedure is exhaustive and duplicate-free. It gives the independent-set counts by size
\[
(1,27,270,1278,3078,3834,2412,756,108,12)
\]
for sizes \(0\) through \(9\). A generated independent set \(I\) is maximal exactly when \(I\cup N(I)=V\). This test yields \(390\) concepts with the asserted size distribution. This criterion is an equivalence, not a heuristic: an undominated vertex can be added to \(I\), and a dominated vertex cannot.

**Step 2 (complete trace test).** A shattered support must itself be independent, since its all-positive labeling must be realized by an independent concept. Thus Step 1 enumerates every possible shattered support. For an independent support \(S=\{s_0,\ldots,s_{k-1}\}\), enumerate each of the \(390\) concepts and form the integer trace \(\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}2^j\mathbf{1}_{s_j\in C}\). Mark each distinct trace and accept \(S\) exactly if all \(2^k\) integers occur. Since \(390<2^9\), no support of size at least \(9\) can be shattered, and the supplied checker enumerates every independent support of size at most \(8\). This exact procedure produces the shattered counts
\[
(1,27,270,1278,2862,2322,0,0,0,0)
\]
for sizes \(0\) through \(9\). In particular, the \(2322\) shattered quintuples and absence of shattered sextuples give the exact dimension.

**Step 3 (all maximal faces).** The family of shattered sets is downward closed, so \(S\) is a facet exactly when it is shattered and none of its \(27-|S|\) one-vertex extensions is shattered. Exhaustively testing these extensions produces \(36\) facets of size three, no facets of sizes zero, one, two or four, and \(2322\) facets of size five. Checking the three coordinates of each of the \(36\) triple facets identifies all of them as the coordinate-transversal triples. Independently, there are \(27\cdot8/6=36\) such triples: choose a first word in \(27\) ways, a second word differing in all three coordinates in \(8\) ways, and the third uniquely, then divide by the \(3!\) orderings.

**Step 4 (symmetry orbits).** The group generated by swaps of adjacent coordinates and by swapping either adjacent pair of symbols in the first coordinate is \(S_3\wr S_3\), acting on the vertices as coordinate and alphabet permutations. Applying these four generators to the facet sets and taking orbit closures yields the asserted one triple orbit and five quintuple orbits; the quintuple orbit cardinalities sum to \(2322\). No unexamined higher-dimensional faces remain, by Step 2.

## Verification
The accompanying files `verify.cpp` and `verify.py` construct the graph independently using base-three integer digits and Cartesian word tuples. The C++17 executable enumerates all \(11776\) independent sets, all \(390\) concepts, every possible independent support, the trace polynomial and the facet-size counts. The standard-library Python program independently reconstructs the concepts, uses a downward-closure-pruned all-traces test, checks all facet triples against the coordinate-transversal characterization, and computes all symmetry orbits. The programs both pass their explicit assertions and print `TERNARY_HAMMING_MIS_CPP_PASS` and `TERNARY_HAMMING_MIS_SHATTER_PASS` respectively. No random sampling is used.

To reproduce from this directory, compile with `g++ -O3 -std=c++17 verify.cpp -o verify_cpp` and run `./verify_cpp`, then run `python3 verify.py`. The Python checker requires only the standard library.

## Relationship to prior work
Kahn and Park (2018, 2019) establish general maximal-independent-set stability and binary-Hamming-cube asymptotic counts, which motivate investigating the exact local trace structure of maximal codes; their inspected theorems neither describe ternary three-coordinate concepts nor classify shattered supports. Dong (2020) studies minimum maximum degree of large induced subgraphs of Hamming graphs, a different invariant. Related published finite-Hamming graph analyses concern graph neighborhoods or the homotopy of the ordinary independence complex; the latter is constructed from *all* independent sets rather than from supports shattered by *maximal* independent sets. Those results do not, merely from their stated invariants, imply the Boolean trace polynomial or facet orbit counts here.

## Limitations
The classification is an exhaustive finite result, not a formula for arbitrary Hamming dimensions or alphabet sizes. The computations independently verify all claimed finite cases but are not independent mathematical peer review. A close full text on independence-complex topology was not accessible during the literature comparison, and unindexed finite-graph tables may contain related computations. No priority claim is based merely on a failed search.
