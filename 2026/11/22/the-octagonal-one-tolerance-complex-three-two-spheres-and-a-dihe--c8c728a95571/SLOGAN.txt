The one-tolerance complex of the filled square of the eight-cycle has integral homotopy type \(S^2\vee S^2\vee S^2\), while its second rational homology splits under the natural dihedral symmetry as \(\varepsilon_{-,-}\oplus\rho_2\). The two inequivalent reflection-axis quotients retain respectively one and two independent rational two-dimensional classes, even though the full dihedral quotient is rationally acyclic.
