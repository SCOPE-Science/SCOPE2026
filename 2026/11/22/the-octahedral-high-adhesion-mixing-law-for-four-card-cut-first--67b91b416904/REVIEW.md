The sharp statement concerns the card-weighted cut-first rule for four labelled cards. The one-break calculation distinguishes card counts from block counts after an entire head block is released. The full law converges after rescaling to a lifted law on cyclic orders; the six-state generator is octahedral, and the spectral calculation gives the limiting total-variation distance and the mixing constants for thresholds strictly between zero and \(5/6\).
