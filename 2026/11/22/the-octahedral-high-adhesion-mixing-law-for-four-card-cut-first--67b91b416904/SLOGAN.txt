When four-card adhesive cut-first riffles approach perfect sticking, their full-deck mixing is governed by a six-state octahedral chain of cyclic orders. Card-weighted, block-weighted, and midpoint releases share the exact limiting total-variation shape \(e^{-4at}/2+e^{-6at}/3\) but have distinct rates \(a=9/64,7/48,7/16\), giving precise and different high-adhesion mixing-time constants.
