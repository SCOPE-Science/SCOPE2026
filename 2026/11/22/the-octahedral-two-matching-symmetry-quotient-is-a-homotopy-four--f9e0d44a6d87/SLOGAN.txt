The two-matching complex of the octahedral graph is a mixed-dimensional sphere wedge, but its ordinary quotient by all 48 graph symmetries has integral homotopy type \(|M_2(K_{2,2,2})|/\operatorname{Aut}(K_{2,2,2})\simeq S^4\). Exact signed quotient chains and fixed-point generators establish both its integral homology and simple connectivity.
