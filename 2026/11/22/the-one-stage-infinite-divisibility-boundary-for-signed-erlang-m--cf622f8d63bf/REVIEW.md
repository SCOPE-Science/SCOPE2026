## Mathematical value — PASS
The sign-selected waiting-time model is an existing natural two-sided generalized-gamma family. Infinite divisibility determines whether it can be embedded in a Lévy process as an increment law; this is not a routine property of mixtures even when both ingredients are themselves infinitely divisible. The result classifies the *whole common integer-stage family* at once and locates its sharp one-stage boundary, uniformly in the sign probability and both rates, with a constructive Lévy measure at the positive boundary and an exact analytic obstruction beyond. It distinguishes Bernoulli mixture from independent gamma difference, resolving a structural gap meaningful to distributional modeling and convolution representations. No broad extrapolation to noninteger shapes or applications is asserted.
