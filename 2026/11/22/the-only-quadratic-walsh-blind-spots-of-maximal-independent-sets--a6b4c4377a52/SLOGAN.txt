For the indicator of maximal independent sets on a labelled cycle, the Walsh transform has no zero frequency of degree at most two except four singleton modes at length \(4\) and eighteen pair modes at length \(9\) (cyclic distances \(3\) or \(4\)). Thus every linear and quadratic Fourier mode is recoverable by convolution at all cycle lengths \(n\ge10\), even when higher-order modes may be invisible.
