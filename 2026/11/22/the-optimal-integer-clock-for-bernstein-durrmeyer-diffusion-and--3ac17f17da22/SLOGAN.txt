For the classical Bernstein–Durrmeyer Markov operator, precisely \(n+1\) steps are the first integer iterate lying below time-one Legendre diffusion in selfadjoint order. This unique first-order-free clock has a sharp, nonzero trace-class error limit: \((n+1)^2(e^{-L}-M_n^{n+1})\to L^2e^{-L}/6\), with an explicit global order bound.
