The ordinary four-cut complex of the five-regular Clebsch graph has torsion-free reduced integral homology \(\mathbb Z\) in degree nine and \(\mathbb Z^{34}\) in degree ten, and no other reduced homology. Its combinatorial Alexander dual is exactly \(S^4\vee\bigvee^{34}S^3\), with a finite integral unit-pivot and oriented-incidence certificate.
