For every odd prime power \(n=p^a\), the multiplicity-weighted cyclic-vertex profiles of all self-maps of \([n]\) give an invertible Boolean convolution whose **entire local \(p\)-primary Smith spectrum** is explicit: the Walsh multiplier at weight \(k\) has valuation \(a n\) at \(k=0\), \(\nu_p((n-1)!)+\nu_p(k)\) at interior weights, and \(\nu_p((n-1)!)+a\) at \(k=n\). This also determines exactly which Fourier modes survive every \(p\)-adic precision level.
