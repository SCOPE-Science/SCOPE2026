For a stationary \(d\)-bit chain with independent per-coordinate flip probability \(a/d\), first passage to a fixed corner, divided by \(2^d\), tends to an exponential law of rate \(1-e^{-a}\), rather than rate one. Its exact spectral mean has a signed \(1/d\) correction whose coefficient changes sign at \(a=\log3\), separating two finite-dimensional approaches to the persistent-corner limit.
