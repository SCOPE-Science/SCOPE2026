For a phase-randomized planet observed through two equal short windows, the exact best one-orbit revisit gap changes discontinuously with the prior: a decreasing phase-weighted period density favors the shortest-period edge, an increasing density favors the longest-period edge, and a flat density produces a whole plateau. For power-law period priors the sharp transition is at exponent \(\beta=1\), with an explicit optimal lag \(\bigl(a+\sqrt{a^2+4aw}\bigr)/2\) for a uniform period prior.
