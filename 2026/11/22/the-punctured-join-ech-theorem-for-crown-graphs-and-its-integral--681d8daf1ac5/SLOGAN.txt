For every crown graph \(Q_n=K_{n,n}\setminus M_n\) with \(n\ge3\), the intrinsic scale-three Čech nerve is \(\bigvee^{n-1}S^{2n-4}\), with top integral homology the quotient permutation lattice \(\mathbb Z^n/\mathbb Z(1,\ldots,1)\) twisted by shore exchange. At scales four and five the nerve is respectively the boundary of a \((2n-1)\)-simplex and a full simplex, so deleting a matching from a complete bipartite graph creates an exact, symmetry-controlled high-dimensional intermediate stage.
