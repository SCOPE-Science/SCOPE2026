The 21-vertex incidence graph of the nine flexes and twelve collinearity lines of a smooth complex plane cubic has critical group \(\mathbb Z/3\oplus(\mathbb Z/9)^4\oplus(\mathbb Z/27)^3\), with exactly \(3^{18}\) spanning trees. A split unit-thickness stable genus-16 curve with this dual graph has the same geometric Jacobian Néron component group.
