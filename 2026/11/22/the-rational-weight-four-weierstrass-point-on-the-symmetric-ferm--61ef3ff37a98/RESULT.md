# The rational weight-four Weierstrass point on the symmetric Fermat quotient of degree eleven

## Finding

Let \(F_{11}:X^{11}+Y^{11}+Z^{11}=0\) be the smooth Fermat curve over \(\mathbb Q\), let \(D_{11}=F_{11}/S_3\) be the smooth projective quotient by coordinate permutations, and let \(Q\) be the image of \(P=(1:-1:0)\). The canonical vanishing sequence of the genus-five curve \(D_{11}\) at \(Q\) is
\[
(0,1,2,5,6).
\]
Consequently, its gap sequence, Weierstrass weight and Weierstrass pole semigroup are
\[
G(Q)=(1,2,3,6,7),\qquad w(Q)=4,\qquad H(Q)=\langle4,5,11\rangle.
\]
The point \(Q\) is \(\mathbb Q\)-rational and the non-gap \(4\) gives a degree-four morphism \(D_{11}\to\mathbb P^1\) over \(\mathbb Q\) with totally ramified fiber at \(Q\). The claim is about this distinguished point, not a census of all Weierstrass points.

## Assumptions and scope

Work in characteristic zero. The quotient denotes the unique smooth projective curve with function field \(\mathbb Q(F_{11})^{S_3}\). A Weierstrass gap at a point is a positive integer \(m\) not realizable as the pole order of a rational function with no other poles; the canonical vanishing orders of a genus-\(g\) curve are \(m_i-1\) for the \(g\) gaps \(m_i\). A pole semigroup includes \(0\).

The three points \((1:-1:0)\) and its coordinate permutations form one \(S_3\)-orbit and are defined over \(\mathbb Q\); hence \(Q\) is a rational point. The stabilizer of \(P\) in \(S_3\) has order \(2\), generated by swapping \(X\) and \(Y\), so the quotient map \(\pi:F_{11}\to D_{11}\) has tame local ramification index \(2\) at \(P\).

The genus is \(5\): the Fermat curve has genus \(45\), while the degree-six quotient has ramification degree \(3\cdot12+2\cdot2=40\), from three transpositions with twelve fixed points each and a pair of order-three fixed points. Riemann–Hurwitz gives \(88=6(2g(D_{11})-2)+40\).

## Proof

Write \(e_1=X+Y+Z\), \(e_2=XY+YZ+ZX\), \(e_3=XYZ\), and \(\Delta=(X-Y)(X-Z)(Y-Z)\). By the invariant adjunction description for the symmetric Fermat quotient [1, §§2.1–2.2], its five holomorphic canonical differentials pull back to the Fermat curve as \(\operatorname{Res}(\Delta B\,\Omega/F)\), where \(B\) runs through the space of homogeneous symmetric degree-five forms. The following monomials give a basis of that space:
\[
 e_1^5,\quad e_1^3e_2,\quad e_1e_2^2,\quad e_1^2e_3,\quad e_2e_3.
\]
This agrees with the five distinct-triple Schur differentials in [1, Lemmas 2.1–2.2 and §4]. In the affine chart \(X=1\) at \(P\), take \(t=Z\) as uniformizer and put \(y=Y\). The Fermat equation yields the unique formal branch
\[
 y(t)=-(1+t^{11})^{1/11}=-1-\frac{t^{11}}{11}+O(t^{22}).
\]
The residue of \(\Delta B\,\Omega/F\) is, up to sign, \(\Delta(1,y,t)B(1,y,t)\,dt/(11y^{10})\). The factor multiplying \(B\,dt\) is a unit, since \(\Delta(P)=-2\) and \(y(P)=-1\).

For the restricted elementary forms one obtains
\[
 e_1=t-\frac{t^{11}}{11}+O(t^{22}),\quad
 e_2=-1-\frac{t^{11}+t^{12}}{11}+O(t^{22}),\quad
 e_3=-t-\frac{t^{12}}{11}+O(t^{23}).
\]
In particular the cancellation
\[
 K:=e_1e_2-e_3=\frac{t^{11}}{11}-\frac{t^{13}}{11}+O(t^{14})
\]
has exact order \(11\), not merely a lower bound. Five symmetric degree-five sections can now be chosen as
\[
 B_0=e_2e_3,\quad B_1=e_1^3e_2,\quad B_2=e_1^5,\quad
 B_3=e_2K,\quad B_4=e_1^2K.
\]
They have distinct exact \(t\)-orders \(1,3,5,11,13\), respectively, with leading coefficients \(1,-1,1,-1/11,1/11\). Each lies in the aforementioned five-dimensional invariant canonical space. Having distinct orders, they are linearly independent and hence form a basis. Therefore these are *all* canonical differential vanishing orders upstairs at \(P\).

Because \(\pi\) has local ramification index \(2\), every quotient canonical differential of order \(a\) pulls back to order \(2a+1\). The five downstairs orders are thus \(0,1,2,5,6\). The gap theorem gives \(G(Q)=(1,2,3,6,7)\). Its weight is \((6-4)+(7-5)=4\), and the complementary semigroup is \(\{0,4,5,8,9,10,11,12,\ldots\}=\langle4,5,11\rangle\).

Since \(4\) is the smallest non-gap, \(\dim H^0(D_{11},\mathcal O(4Q))=2\), while \(\dim H^0(D_{11},\mathcal O(3Q))=1\). These dimensions hold over \(\mathbb Q\) by flat base change, since \(Q\) is rational. A nonconstant \(\mathbb Q\)-rational function in the former space has pole divisor exactly \(4Q\), providing the claimed morphism of degree four and total ramification at \(Q\).

## Verification

An attached standard-library-only Python program, `d11_weierstrass_check.py`, carries out rational truncated-polynomial arithmetic to order \(13\), verifies the Fermat branch equation, checks the five exact leading orders, and independently computes the jet rank jumps of the symmetric monomial basis at exponents \(1,3,5,11,13\). The selected jet matrix has determinant \(1/121\). The exact checker proves a finite Taylor certificate; the preceding invariant-differential and ramification argument supplies the mathematical implication for the whole curve, rather than interpreting finite sampling as a proof.

## Relationship to prior work

Tafazolian [1] proves the quotient's genus, absolute simplicity and CM structure, trivial automorphism group, and exhibits the invariant Schur differential basis. It does not state the Weierstrass semigroup at this rational branch point. The gap set calculated here numerically matches the CM-type exponent set displayed in [1, Proposition 4.1], but no general identity between these notions is claimed. Tzermias [2, §2] tabulates the Weierstrass gaps at a Fermat-coordinate point **upstairs on \(F_{11}\)** and [2, §4] constructs a quotient model; these are different from the gaps **downstairs at \(Q\)**. The new content is the exact cancellation order in the invariant canonical basis and the descended vanishing sequence.

## Limitations

The result is confined to \(D_{11}\) over characteristic zero and its one distinguished rational point. It does not classify the other ramification points, all Weierstrass points, or the curve's global gonality. Prior-literature searches and full-text comparisons reduce, but cannot exclude, unpublished or unindexed equivalent calculations. Independent validation has not been performed.
