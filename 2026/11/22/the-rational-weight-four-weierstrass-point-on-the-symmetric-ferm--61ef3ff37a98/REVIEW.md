# Review of the rational Weierstrass point on the symmetric degree-eleven quotient

## Correctness — PASS

The five-dimensional invariant canonical space is identified using the explicit basis in Tafazolian (2026, Lemmas 2.1–2.2). At the order-two ramification point \((1:-1:0)\), the exact branch \(y^{11}=-(1+t^{11})\) yields the cancellation \(e_1e_2-e_3=t^{11}/11-t^{13}/11+O(t^{14})\). Five independent degree-five symmetric sections have exact upstairs vanishing orders \(1,3,5,11,13\). Their quotient orders are \(0,1,2,5,6\), and the five gaps, weight and semigroup follow. Genus five is independently checked by Riemann–Hurwitz. The attached checker verifies exact formal jets and a full-rank basis without floating-point arithmetic. Risk: the argument is characteristic zero and relies on the explicitly stated invariant-adjunction description, not on a numerical sample.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

Direct statement-and-implication comparisons with Tafazolian, arXiv:2611.08176v1 (full text, §§1–4 and 7), and Tzermias (Acta Arith. 108, 2003, especially §§2 and 4, full 11-page article) distinguish the downstairs canonical-gap claim from the source's Schur basis and the earlier upstairs Fermat-point gap calculation. The original basis does enable this computation, but it does not assert or force a particular vanishing sequence without the nontrivial order-eleven cancellation calculation. Targeted semantic searches for alternate naming, rational branch points, semigroup generators, and equivalent canonical jets found no covering result. Risk: a computation in an unindexed article or unpublished notes might be equivalent, and search absence does not prove global novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The distinguished \(\mathbb Q\)-rational point is geometrically canonical as the image of the coordinate-zero transposition-fixed orbit on an automorphism-free, absolutely simple CM genus-five quotient. The weight-four jump and exact semigroup are natural local invariants, revealing the first totally ramified rational degree-four linear system \(|4Q|\). The precise gap sequence is not a textbook deduction from genus or CM alone, and it provides a concrete input for the study of rational divisors and maps on this curve.

## Coverage and limits

The original work proves the invariant basis and CM type. The earlier 2003 article computes gaps on \(F_{11}\), not on the quotient. The result does not claim an all-point classification, global gonality, or independence from all literature.
