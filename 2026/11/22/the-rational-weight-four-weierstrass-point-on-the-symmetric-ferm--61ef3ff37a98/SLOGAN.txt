For the genus-five symmetric Fermat quotient \(D_{11}=F_{11}/S_3\), the rational point below \((1:-1:0)\) has Weierstrass gaps \((1,2,3,6,7)\), weight \(4\), and semigroup \(\langle4,5,11\rangle\). In particular its first nonconstant single-pole linear system gives a rational degree-four map totally ramified at that point.
