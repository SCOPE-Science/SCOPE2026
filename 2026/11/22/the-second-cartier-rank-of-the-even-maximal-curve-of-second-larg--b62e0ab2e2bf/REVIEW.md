# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The smooth function field, the unique point at infinity, and the canonical differential divisor are established using the additive Galois cover and an explicit uniformizer. The proposed monomial basis contains exactly the genus number of linearly independent holomorphic differentials. The Cartier operator's four parity cases follow directly from the separating variable and the additive-polynomial identity. An injectivity lemma for polynomials of degree below the additive polynomial's degree prevents cancellation in the second iterate, and a complete parity count evaluates the rank in all permitted powers of two. The exact binary checker reproduces both the known first ranks and the new second ranks for \(q=8,16,32,64\). The formula for arbitrary \(q\) rests on the proof, not on these four computations.

## Originality — PASS with publication-priority qualifications
Higashine (2026) explicitly studies this curve and its Galois lines, not the Cartier filtration. Nourozi–Tafazolian–Rahmati (2021) already compute the \(a\)-number \(q^2/16\); the corresponding first Cartier rank is excluded from the originality claim. Almeida Filho–Tafazolian–Vieira (2026) determine higher Cartier ranks for odd-characteristic Artin–Schreier curves, not this characteristic-two Kummer-over-additive cover. Pries–Weir (2015) study Hermitian curves of different genus. A database and own-prior-claim search found no identical second-iterate formula or stronger theorem implying it. The 2021 Nourozi dissertation is a specifically identified but not materially inspected priority risk; older unindexed equivalences remain possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The second Cartier rank refines the known \(a\)-number by specifying a further step of the nilpotent Cartier filtration on a distinguished maximal-curve family. It is a closed formula in unbounded genus for a natural moduli-theoretic invariant, rather than a check of an arbitrary isolated parameter. The exact rank of the second iterate is not determined by the genus, \(a\)-number, and supersingularity alone.

## Limits
The theorem does not supply all higher ranks or the full Ekedahl–Oort type, and assumes \(q\ge8\). Priority remains conditional on accessible source coverage. The stated theorem has not undergone independent review.
