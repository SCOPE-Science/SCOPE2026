For the one-pass ternary tortoise map, the exact all-length squared-preimage-fiber enumerator is \(\frac{1-8z+32z^2-68z^3+83z^4-60z^5+24z^6}{(1-z)^2(1-2z)^2(1-5z+7z^2-5z^3)}\). Consequently, for a uniform ternary input the image has order-two Rényi entropy rate \(\log_2(9/\lambda)=1.421762606445352\ldots\) bits per symbol, where \(\lambda^3-5\lambda^2+7\lambda-5=0\) and \(\lambda>3\).
