The first nonunit integer Smith factor for pairwise-adjacency feasibility between one-point types of the countable homogeneous \(K_5\)-free graph occurs at seven named parameters, uniquely for the wheel \(K_1\vee C_6\). Its \(128\)-type matrix has \(\operatorname{SNF}_{\mathbb Z}=\operatorname{diag}(I_{22},2,0_{105})\); every base with at most six named vertices, and every other seven-vertex base, has torsion-free integer cokernel.
