An individual one-point type forbids a K4 in its old neighborhood. To realize two adjacent new vertices neither type alone creates a K5; a K5 using both exists exactly when their common neighborhood contains a triangle. Homogeneity and universality give the converse, including two distinct realizations of the same type. The type family is hereditary, so the subset zeta matrix is unitriangular and integer Möbius inversion diagonalizes the meet matrix by unimodular congruence. Each diagonal coefficient depends only on the induced K4-free graph on the corresponding type's neighborhood. I independently exhausted all labelled K4-free graphs up to order seven by adding vertices and allowing only triangle-free neighborhoods, with an incremental bitset update for the triangle-free subsets. Every graph is generated once through its unique labelled predecessor. The 420 nonunit cases all have coefficient 2, a universal vertex and six vertices of degree three; a K4-free such graph cannot have a rim of two triangles, so it is exactly the wheel.
