# Scientific review: the resolved Hesse boundary component group

## Correctness — PASS

The twelve source line equations are independently checked over the Eisenstein integers to have nine ordinary fourfold points and twelve nodes. Blowing up the fourfold points gives a 21-component, 48-node, genus-28 stable curve. The exact proof reconstructs the full integral Laplacian from the affine-plane incidence matrix, derives eigenvalues \(0,3^{11},7^9\), and proves mod-3 and mod-7 Laplacian ranks \(10\) and \(12\). Combining these with the matrix–tree formula yields \((\mathbb Z/3)^2\oplus(\mathbb Z/21)^8\), not just the order. The attached checker independently confirms the full integer Smith normal form. The Néron interpretation is conditional on proper regular split semistability and unit node thickness.

## Originality — PASS (provisional)

Artebani–Dolgachev (arXiv:math/0611590, 2006), sections on the Hesse configuration and four triangles, and Innamorati (DOI 10.3390/axioms13050321, 2024), §3 on the twelve ordinary nodes, supply geometry but not the graph-critical-group calculation. The closest earlier Hesse Levi-graph group pertains to only 36 edges and is pure 3-primary; a Hesse-divisor arithmetic result concerns Picard torus and zeta invariants, not this resolved graph pairing. General graph/Néron literature supplies a bridge between the invariants but no observed exact decomposition for this 48-edge graph. Search incompleteness and unindexed priority remain real risks.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The complete stable boundary, unlike the incidence-only graph, retains twelve natural triangle intersections. The full group exposes an 8-dimensional elementary 7-primary part and exponent 21; neither incidence counts, arithmetic genus, nor the previously known Levi-group calculation determines it. The proof's low-complexity spectrum and prime-local rank explanation make the answer structural and potentially useful in Jacobian degeneration. The contribution is an exact motivated invariant, not a general theorem about all line arrangements.
