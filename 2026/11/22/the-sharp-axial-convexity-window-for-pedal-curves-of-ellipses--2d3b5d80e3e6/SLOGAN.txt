The pedal oval of an ellipse, viewed from any interior point of its major axis, is convex exactly on an explicit closed interval on either side of the center. For an ellipse longer than \(\sqrt2\) times its minor semiaxis, the center pedal is nonconvex but convexity reappears around the foci and disappears again toward the vertices; the thin-ellipse window has sharp width \(5b^4/(8a^3)+O(b^6/a^5)\).
