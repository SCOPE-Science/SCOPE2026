For estimating the integrated squared energy of a pinned Brownian bridge from exact point observations, the sharp large-budget sampling law is \(\operatorname{Beta}(4/3,4/3)\), with optimal risk \(\operatorname{B}(4/3,4/3)^3/[3(n+1)^2]\) to leading order. Its quantile grids reduce asymptotic mean-square error by approximately \(10.678\%\) relative to equally spaced observations.
