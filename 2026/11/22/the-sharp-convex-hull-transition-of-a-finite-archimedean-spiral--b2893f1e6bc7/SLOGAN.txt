The convex hull of the finite Archimedean spiral \(z(t)=te^{it}\), \(0\le t\le T\), first loses its initial arc at the sharp threshold \(T=\pi\); afterward exactly one terminal spiral arc and one tangent chord form its entire boundary. The missing exposure starts with quadratic area gain and cubic perimeter saving, and the long-arc hull has a sharp square-root angular boundary layer with explicit three-term area and perimeter expansions.
