For the Riemann–Liouville commutator with a singular power multiplier on \(L^2(0,1)\), the line \(\alpha+\beta=0\) separates Hilbert–Schmidt compactness from unboundedness through a bounded but noncompact operator with an exact gamma-function norm. The same positive coefficient controls both the compact-side Hilbert–Schmidt pole and the critical spatial-cutoff logarithm.
