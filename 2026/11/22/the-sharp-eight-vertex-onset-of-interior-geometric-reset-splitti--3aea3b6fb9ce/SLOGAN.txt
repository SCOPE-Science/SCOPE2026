An inhomogeneous birth–death walk needs at least eight vertices before geometric resetting can have a genuine finite-intensity optimum for its probability of exiting at a chosen boundary. At the first possible starting site, the optimum exists exactly when \(p_1q_2>(1+p_4q_5+p_5q_6)/(3-p_4q_5-p_5q_6)\), and the optimal reset intensity is given by an explicit radical.
