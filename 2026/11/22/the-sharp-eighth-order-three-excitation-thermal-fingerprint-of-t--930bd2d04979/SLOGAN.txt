For the cospectral Shrikhande and \(4\times4\) rook networks, three hard-core XY excitations have identical normalized spectral moments through order seven, but the eighth traces differ by exactly \(2,193,408\). Their normalized partition-function and coherent trace-amplitude difference therefore first appears as \((17/175)z^8+O(z^9)\), quantifying the earliest trace-level distinction beyond the already-known three-excitation spectral separation.
