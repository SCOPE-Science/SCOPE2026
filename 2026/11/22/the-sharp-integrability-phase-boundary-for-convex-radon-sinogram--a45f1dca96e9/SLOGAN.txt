For a real integrable density supported in the \(d\)-dimensional unit ball, convexity of almost every hyperplane Radon section is possible nontrivially in all \(L^p\) spaces for \(d=2,3\), only for \(1\le p<2\) when \(d=4\), and in no \(L^p\) space when \(d\ge5\). A sharp boundary-layer obstruction proves the nonexistence half for arbitrary nonradial data, while explicit radial weights attain every admissible regime.
