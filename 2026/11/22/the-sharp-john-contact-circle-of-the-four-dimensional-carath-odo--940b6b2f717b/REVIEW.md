The universal degree-two Carathéodory orbitope is the first nontrivial four-dimensional Fourier moment body, a standard spectrahedral convex body, and also a spin-one rotation-state model. Its unrestricted maximal-volume inscribed ellipsoid and full boundary-contact orbit give a natural sharp affine-invariant volume efficiency, allowing concrete comparisons between spectrahedral and ellipsoidal normalization. The result does not assert a theorem for other degrees.
