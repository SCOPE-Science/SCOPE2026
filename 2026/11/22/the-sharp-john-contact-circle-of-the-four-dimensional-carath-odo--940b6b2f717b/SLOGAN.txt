The four-dimensional Carathéodory orbitope \(C=\operatorname{conv}\{(e^{i\theta},e^{2i\theta}):0\le\theta<2\pi\}\) has the unique maximal inscribed ellipsoid \(3|z|^2+9|w|^2/8\le1\), a complete opposite-phase contact circle containing a regular-four-simplex John certificate, and the sharp occupied-volume fraction \(4/9\). Its outer Löwner ball is already covered by an earlier general theorem for orbitopes; the new geometric classification is the inner contact geometry and fraction.
