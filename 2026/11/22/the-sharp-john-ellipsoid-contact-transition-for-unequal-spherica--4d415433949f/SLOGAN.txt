The unique maximum-volume ellipsoid in a three-dimensional unequal-ball lens has an exact two-regime formula, with a sharp transition from axial-pole contact to two latitude-circle contacts at separation \(s_c=3(R^2-r^2)/(2R)\). The second regime exists if and only if the larger ball's radius is less than three times the smaller radius, and a positive isotropic contact certificate proves unrestricted global optimality.
