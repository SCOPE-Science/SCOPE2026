For the Pythagorean parametrization \((t^2-1,2t,t^2+1)\) modulo an odd prime, all six mixed quadratic-residue/nonresidue sign words occur exactly when the prime is outside \(\{3,5,7,13,17,29\}\). The sharp universal mixed-pattern threshold is \(31\), and the last failure at \(29\) omits exactly two mixed words.
