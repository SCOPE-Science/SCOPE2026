Symmetric Fourier truncation destroys the guaranteed angular decay of the circle heat kernel at very short times, but recovers it uniformly at the sharp scale \(t\sim\pi/(2N)\). This \(N^{-1}\) scale, rather than the cell-diffusion scale \(N^{-2}\), is forced by antipodal Gaussian decay competing with the omitted spectral tail, for even and odd cutoffs alike.
