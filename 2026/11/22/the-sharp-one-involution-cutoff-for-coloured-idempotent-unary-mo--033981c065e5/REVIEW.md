# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

A coloured idempotent map decomposes uniquely into coloured rooted stars. Counting colour-preserving automorphisms gives an exact product of factorials and component-multiplicity factorials. A product has order two precisely when there is one internal doubled leaf-colour or one doubled internally rigid component type, and all other types are selected at most once. These disjoint cases yield the displayed generating polynomial. Consecutive subset-sum support of rigid star weights proves every degree through the sharp cutoff, and comparison of top degrees yields exactly \(q^2\) extremizers. The standalone exhaustive checker tested all 11,951 labelled models in the specified small ranges and formal polynomial cutoffs for five values of \(q\). Finite checks alone are not used to prove the theorem.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence

Kuželka [1] gives general automorphism-marked model counting, not the exactly-two-automorphism enumerator. The closest published idempotent-map result [2] proves the rigid and unrestricted series, including the canonical star decomposition, but not the order-two polynomial or its sharp support. The nested-equivalence result [3] counts order-two automorphism classes for unrestricted coloured hierarchical partitions; it does not enforce one distinguished fixed root per idempotent fibre, so its univariate polynomials do not imply the present restricted numerical formula. General rooted-tree group decompositions [4] provide a standard technique. Searches for the exact group-order-two/idempotent/coloured formula did not identify a previously published instance; that absence is not proof of uniqueness. A possible earlier multivariate rooted-star enumeration is a residual risk.

## Value — PASS

Automorphism factors create the concrete difference between labelled and unlabelled model counts. The exact first nontrivial group-order stratum measures where the rigid-only approximation first fails, gives all finite cardinality counts, and establishes a sharp upper capacity and extremal multiplicity for a natural logic-definable class. It is not asserted as a general new group-action principle. The sharp cutoff and gap-free support give information that rigid and unrestricted totals cannot individually reconstruct.

## Limitations and sources

The single-operation idempotence and fixed named colour partition are essential. The theorem does not extend automatically to arbitrary endofunctions. Relevant full results [2] and [3] and the automorphism-marking portion of [1] were inspected; [4] was accessible only as a targeted excerpt. Historical priority and independent validation remain open.
