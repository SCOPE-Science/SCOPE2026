## Mathematical value — PASS
A disconnected family of geodesic sensor tracks is a motivated acquisition architecture when relocation is allowed but total sensor travel is charged. The sharp alias threshold answers how many tracks are needed for a winding budget; the integer Pareto cost and unique optimizer quantify the tradeoff. It reveals that splitting the sampling trajectory can have strictly shorter total arclength than the optimal single connected design curve. The result is a restricted exact optimization, not a general mobile-sampling lower bound.
