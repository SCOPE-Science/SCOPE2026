For a square Fourier band on the flat two-torus, the exact Parseval sampling cost of any prescribed number \(L\ge2\) of parallel closed geodesics is a sharp arithmetic staircase governed by \(1+\lfloor2N/L\rfloor\). The optimal disconnected design uses exactly \(2N+1\) parallel coordinate circles, whose total length is strictly shorter than the known shortest connected design curve.
