For a positive operator on a unital AM-space, the optimal absolute Cesàro constant \(K\) bounds **every** power by \(K^2\), and this quadratic bound is best possible at every prescribed \(K>1\). Finite nilpotent shifts with two widely separated spikes asymptotically attain \(K^2\), quantifying an endpoint rigidity absent from general finite-exponent \(L^p\) theory.
