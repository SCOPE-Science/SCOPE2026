In an idealized Weibull-wildfire forest with age-value \(t^p\) for \(p>1\), every salvage fraction below one has a unique mean-yield-optimal finite harvesting age, which strictly increases with salvage. At complete salvage the optimal planned rotation disappears, and the near-complete-salvage age diverges with a sharp two-term power law; this is a mathematical benchmark rather than an empirical forest-management recommendation.
