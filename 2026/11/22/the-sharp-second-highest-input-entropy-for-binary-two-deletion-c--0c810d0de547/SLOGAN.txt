In the binary two-deletion channel, the exact second-highest posterior input entropy for every received length \(n\ge2\) occurs precisely at words with one centrally placed doubled bit and otherwise alternating runs. The sharp entropy gap below the alternating maximum is \(\Delta_n/[4\binom{n+2}{2}]\), with \(\Delta_n=(6\log_2 3-8)n+6-6\log_2 3+f(\lfloor(n+4)/2\rfloor)+f(\lceil(n+4)/2\rceil)-f(n+1)\) and \(f(t)=t\log_2 t\).
