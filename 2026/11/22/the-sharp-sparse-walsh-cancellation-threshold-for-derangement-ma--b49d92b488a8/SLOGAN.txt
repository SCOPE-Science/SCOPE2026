For the signed hollow-permanent Walsh transform of every \(n\)-letter derangement codebook, a \(k\)-edge frequency has eigenvalue at least \(d_n(1-2k/(n-1))\), with equality precisely on row/column stars for \(n\ge4\) and \(0\le k\le n-1\). Derangement convolution is invertible exactly for even \(n\); for odd \(n\), its first Fourier zeros occur at weight \((n-1)/2\), and every first-weight zero is a star.
