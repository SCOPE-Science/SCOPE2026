In the fixed-point-free cosine–quadratic-memristor map, an attracting primitive two-cycle is possible for some positive forcing exactly when \(1<\beta<3/2\), throughout the natural range \(0<\beta\le2\). For every \(\beta\ge3/2\) in that range, **every** primitive two-cycle is unstable regardless of forcing, whereas each \(1<\beta<3/2\) has attracting cycles for all sufficiently small positive forcing.
