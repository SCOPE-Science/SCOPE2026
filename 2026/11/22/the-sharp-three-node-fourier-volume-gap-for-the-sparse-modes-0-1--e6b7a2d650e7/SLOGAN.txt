Three unrestricted unit-circle samples of the sparse Fourier modes \(\{0,1,4\}\) have a unique optimal isosceles geometry (up to rotation and permutation), with sharp squared determinant \(\rho^2(1-\rho)(1+\rho)^2(3+\rho)^3\), where \(4\rho^3+7\rho^2-4\rho-3=0\) and \(\rho\in(3/4,4/5)\). More generally, three-sample tightness for \(\{0,1,m\}\) is possible exactly when \(m\equiv2\pmod3\).
