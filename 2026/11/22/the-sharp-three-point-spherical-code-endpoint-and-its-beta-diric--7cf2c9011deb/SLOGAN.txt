For three independent uniform points on any fixed sphere \(S^{d-1}\) with \(d\ge3\), the probability that every pair is separated by more than \(2\pi/3-h\) is asymptotic to \(C_d h^{(d+2)/2}\), with an explicit gamma-function constant. Conditioned on this near-optimal spherical code, the three rescaled pairwise angular slacks converge in total variation to an independent Beta–Dirichlet radial-and-shape law.
