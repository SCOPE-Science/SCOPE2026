For \(1\)-\(11\) graph representations by concatenated permutations, exactly three blocks suffice precisely when the complement is a comparability graph of poset dimension at most three. Deleting a matching of size \(s\) from \(K_{a,b}\) needs exactly three permutations for \(s\le2\) and exactly four for \(s\ge3\), apart from the one-permutation complete graph \(K_2\).
