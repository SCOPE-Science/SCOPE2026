Among real two-neighbor filters of integer Gaussian translates, the unique optimal coefficient up to reciprocal scaling is a ratio of square roots of Jacobi theta constants. It preserves the Gaussian shift-invariant space while improving the sharp squared Riesz-condition-number excess from order \(e^{-\pi s/2}\) to order \(e^{-\pi s}\) in the weak-overlap limit \(s\to\infty\).
