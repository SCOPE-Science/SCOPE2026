For every unitary modulation in dimension \(N\ge4\), the combined energy of its last two aperiodic autocorrelation lags is at most \(5/4\), independently of \(N\), with exactly classified four-column Walsh-type extremizers. The full Fourier basis attains only \(5/N\), so it is strictly suboptimal for this non-prefix pair when \(N>4\).
