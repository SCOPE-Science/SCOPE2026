For every fixed \(0<t<1\), the compact generalized Cesàro operator has a trace-class self-commutator with infinitely many eigenvalues of both signs; the precise fraction of negative eigenvalues in its first-\(N\)-coordinate compressions tends to \(\arccos(t)/\pi\). The signed density is obtained from an explicit remote Toeplitz symbol and jumps from zero at the exactly diagonal parameter \(t=0\), even though the self-commutator tends to zero in trace norm.
