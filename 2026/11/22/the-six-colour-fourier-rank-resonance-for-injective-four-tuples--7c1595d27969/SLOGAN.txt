For injective four-tuples over any finite abelian group of size \(q\ge4\), counting convolution recovers every diagonal-translation-invariant mode except at \(q=6\), when exactly 48 additional balanced modes vanish and the rank falls to 168. The complete signed Fourier spectrum is explicitly determined by \(q\) and the size of its two-torsion subgroup, distinguishing palettes invisible to the ordinary colouring count.
