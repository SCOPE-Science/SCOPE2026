For independent isotropic segment directions in three-dimensional random zonotopes, the nonnegative deficit between squared volume and one-eighth of projection-body volume is an exact six-generator interaction, with mean \(\frac{5\pi^2(32-3\pi^2)}{512}\sum_{|S|=6}\prod_{i\in S}\mathbb E R_i\). The deficit retains a finite expectation under arbitrary independent radial lengths having finite means, even when the two individual volume expectations are infinite.
