# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The finite cellular model includes all disjoint pairs of nonempty simplices of dimensions at most two on seven labelled vertices. The embedded standalone checker generates all 1,302 cells, verifies all cellular boundary compositions, constructs the full forty-dimensional mod-two cohomology quotient, and checks the actual Alexander–Whitney cup pairing on every top-dimensional cell. Its diagonal vanishes on a basis and its rank is forty. Separately the signed top chain has zero integral boundary and coordinate swap changes its sign, giving zero signature. The published 2025 connectivity and integral-homology theorem and the 2026 closed-PL-four-manifold theorem provide the premises for Wu duality and Freedman's classification. A finite experiment alone would not prove an all-size statement; the final claim concerns this one finite complex and follows from exhaustive exact operations plus established theorems.

## Originality
PASS, subject to residual unindexed-priority risk. González–González (2025) identify homology, connectivity and minimal CW structures but leave the middle cup-form unresolved at this parameter. González–González–Pérez-Morales (2026), arXiv:2611.12847v1, explicitly isolate the four-dimensional form and formulate the connected-sum conjecture. A calculation of Betti numbers alone would be subsumed; the spin/intersection-lattice determination and topological homeomorphism are logically stronger. published-finding corpus comparisons with existing two-point graph-torus and other simplex-skeleton findings found different objects/invariants and no identified covering result. Neither the absence of a keyword hit nor the assigned topic boundary is taken as proof of novelty.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The precise intersection form is highlighted as a mathematically consequential gap in the primary 2026 source. Determining its parity and signature identifies a naturally occurring simply connected PL four-manifold up to TOP homeomorphism and settles a specific case of its connected-sum homotopy conjecture. This is not a routine Betti computation; middle-dimensional cup products distinguish manifolds with identical Betti ranks.
