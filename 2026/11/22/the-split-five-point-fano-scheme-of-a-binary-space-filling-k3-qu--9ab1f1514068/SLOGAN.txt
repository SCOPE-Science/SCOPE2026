The smooth space-filling K3 quartic of Bourgeois-Conti–Moon–Perez–Warren over \(\mathbb F_2\) has exactly five geometric lines, all already rational over \(\mathbb F_2\). Its complete line Fano scheme is the split finite étale scheme \(\coprod_{j=1}^{5}\operatorname{Spec}(\mathbb F_2)\), not merely a set of five rational points.
