For every odd prime \(p\), positive multiple \(c\) of \(p\), and scale \(s\ge1\), the three quadratic valuation sequences at shifts \(0,p^s,2p^s\) have a complete exact trivariate prime-power distribution: below their shared root depth all valuations agree, but above it at most one can grow. The connected three-point cumulant is negative only for \((p,s)=(3,1)\), despite strictly positive pairwise covariances in every case.
