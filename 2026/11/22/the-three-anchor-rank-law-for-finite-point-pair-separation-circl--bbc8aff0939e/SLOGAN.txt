For finite unoriented circles with four-place point-pair separation, the one-point-extension rank is controlled exactly by the smallest unmarked gap: a marked set has rank \(\min_i\lfloor\log_2(g_i+1)\rfloor\), and a circle of size \(n\ge3\) has rank \(3+\lfloor\log_2\lfloor n/3\rfloor\rfloor\). An exact binomial census counts every marked subset at each rank and proves the sharp three-anchor threshold.
