Every smooth Hesse cyclic cubic surface \(X^3+Y^3+Z^3+W^3+tXYZ=0\) over an odd finite field of characteristic other than three has exactly one of three Frobenius spectra on its \(27\) lines: all fixed, nine three-cycles, or three fixed plus twelve transpositions. A single cubic residue test on \(-(1+t^3/27)\) determines the complete zeta function and every extension-field point count.
