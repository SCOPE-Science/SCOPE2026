The three nonisomorphic combinatorial \((9_3)\) point–line configurations have distinct complete synchronous NOR attraction-basin spectra: their fixed-point counts are 110, 112, and 113, yet their respective fixed-attraction populations decrease from 914 to 912 to 911. Every one of the \(2^{18}\) initial states per configuration enters a fixed point or primitive two-cycle after at most one update, and exact individual basin histograms are given for all three types.
