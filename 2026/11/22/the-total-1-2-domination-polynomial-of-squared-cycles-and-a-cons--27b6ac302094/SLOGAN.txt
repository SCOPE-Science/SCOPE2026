The full total \([1,2]\)-domination polynomial of every squared cycle is the trace of an explicit sixteen-state weighted transfer matrix. Among the \((2q+1)(7q+1)\) ordinary minimum total-dominating configurations of \(C_{7q+1}^2\), exactly \(2(7q+1)\) violate the two-neighbor load cap; the surviving \((2q-1)(7q+1)\) configurations still achieve the ordinary optimum size.
