The reduced \(\operatorname{tr}C=0\) boundary in the three-crosscap \(\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb C)\)-character variety is exactly \(\mathbb G_m\times\mathbb A^1\), with a complete seven-trace defining ideal. Its four-trace projection has one-dimensional fibers over a smooth conic, resolving the boundary classification left open in arXiv:2611.08513.
