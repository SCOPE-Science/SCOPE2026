# The truncated-tetrahedral four-cut complex: 117 seven-spheres and a full tetrahedral homology character

## Finding

Let \(T\) be the twelve-vertex truncated-tetrahedral graph. Its ordinary four-cut complex \(K=\Delta_4(T)\), with facets the complements of disconnected induced four-vertex sets, and its combinatorial Alexander dual \(L=K^\vee\) satisfy integral homotopy equivalences

\[
K\simeq\bigvee^{117}S^7,\qquad L\simeq\bigvee^{117}S^2.
\]

The full graph-automorphism group is the tetrahedral group \(S_4\). On \(\widetilde H_7(K;\mathbb Q)\), its character at cycle types \((1^4),(2,1^2),(2^2),(3,1),(4)\), respectively, is \((117,7,1,3,1)\), and the irreducible multiplicities are (here \(S^\lambda\) denotes the Specht module indexed by the partition \(\lambda\))

\[
8S^{(4)}\oplus4S^{(1^4)}\oplus16S^{(3,1)}\oplus13S^{(2,1,1)}\oplus9S^{(2,2)}.
\]

In particular, the full tetrahedral symmetry fixes an eight-dimensional rational subspace of the seventh reduced homology.

## Assumptions and scope

All complexes are finite abstract simplicial complexes over the twelve graph vertices. Define the ordered graph vertices to be the directed edges \((a,b)\), where \(a,b\in\{0,1,2,3\}\) and \(a\ne b\), in lexicographic order. Two distinct such vertices \((a,b)\) and \((c,d)\) are adjacent precisely when either \(a=c\), or \((a,b)=(d,c)\). This gives four vertex triangles and six reversed-edge connections, the standard cubic truncated-tetrahedral graph. The action of \(S_4\) simultaneously permutes both entries of each ordered pair.

For \(X\subseteq V(T)\) of cardinality four, call \(X\) connected if its induced graph is connected. The ordinary four-cut complex \(K\) is generated by \(V(T)\setminus X\) over the disconnected four-sets \(X\). Its Alexander dual is \(L=\{A\subseteq V(T):V(T)\setminus A\notin K\}\), using the empty face convention. Statements concern integral nonequivariant homotopy and the stated rational equivariant homology; they do not assert an equivariant wedge equivalence or shellability.

## Proof

**1. Finite combinatorics.** Enumerate the \(\binom{12}{4}=495\) four-sets using the explicit graph definition. Exactly 48 induce connected graphs and 447 are disconnected. The resulting cut complex has 447 seven-dimensional facets and contains every simplex of dimension at most six. Its face vector by dimensions zero through seven is

\[
(12,66,220,495,792,924,792,447).
\]

Computing the Alexander dual *from the entire cut-complex face set* gives the complete two-skeleton of the eleven-simplex together with the 48 tetrahedra arising from connected induced four-sets. Thus the dual face vector, in dimensions zero through three, is \((12,66,220,48)\). It is important to include all 220 triangular faces, not just triangles lying in those tetrahedra. Every listed enumeration is checked independently by `verify.py`.

**2. Explicit collapses and simple connectivity.** The file `CERTIFICATE.json` lists 48 pairs \((\tau,\sigma)\), each with \(\tau\) a triangle and \(\sigma\) a tetrahedron. In the order given, the verifier checks that \(\sigma\) is the unique proper coface of \(\tau\) and is maximal at that stage; deleting each pair is an elementary simplicial collapse. The residual two-dimensional complex \(L'\) has \((12,66,172)\) vertices, edges, and triangles.

The same certificate gives an eleven-edge spanning tree of the one-skeleton of \(L'\), followed by 55 pairs \((e,t)\) with \(e\) an edge and \(t\) a residual triangle. At every step \(e\) is the unique edge of the boundary of \(t\) not previously known to represent a nullhomotopic edge path after contracting the spanning tree. The triangular relation therefore makes \(e\) nullhomotopic. Because all 66 edges become trivial, van Kampen's presentation proves \(\pi_1(L')=0\). The argument checks each triangle and each edge; it is not an inference from integral first homology.

The reduced Euler characteristic of \(L'\) is \(-1+12-66+172=117\). Its second integral homology is free, since it is a subgroup of its free two-chain group, and the vanishing of its first homology makes the rank 117. Any simply connected two-dimensional CW complex with free second homology of rank 117 is homotopy equivalent to a wedge of 117 two-spheres: represent a basis of \(H_2\) by maps from two-spheres (Hurewicz) and use the homology version of Whitehead's theorem. Hence \(L\simeq L'\simeq\bigvee^{117}S^2\).

**3. Original complex.** Combinatorial Alexander duality on twelve vertices gives \(\widetilde H_i(K;\mathbb Z)\cong\widetilde H^{9-i}(L;\mathbb Z)\); thus it is \(\mathbb Z^{117}\) in degree seven and zero otherwise. Since \(K\) contains the complete six-skeleton of an eleven-simplex, it is at least five-connected. The Hurewicz theorem and vanishing of \(H_6(K)\) imply it is six-connected. A wedge of 117 seven-spheres maps into \(K\) to give an isomorphism on seventh homology; Whitehead's theorem then makes this map a homotopy equivalence.

**4. Group character.** Every permutation of \(\{0,1,2,3\}\) acts on the twelve ordered pairs and preserves the graph. The graph has exactly 24 automorphisms (its four disjoint 3-cycles of triangle edges force a permutation of their four triangles), so this is the full group. The verifier evaluates the reduced simplicial Lefschetz trace

\[
\widetilde\Lambda(g;K)=\sum_{\sigma\in K\setminus\{\varnothing\}}(-1)^{\dim\sigma}\operatorname{sgn}(g|_\sigma)-1,
\]

where only simplices fixed setwise contribute. Since only seventh reduced homology is nonzero, \(\operatorname{tr}(g|\widetilde H_7(K;\mathbb Q))=-\widetilde\Lambda(g;K)\). On the five conjugacy types this yields \((117,7,1,3,1)\). Taking inner products with the five usual irreducible \(S_4\) characters produces multiplicities \((8,4,16,13,9)\) in the order stated. All 24 group elements are checked, not just representatives.

## Verification

Run `python3 verify.py` beside `CERTIFICATE.json` using Python 3.8 or later (no nonstandard packages required). The code independently rebuilds both complexes, verifies all 48 free-face removals, the spanning tree and all 55 fundamental-group eliminations, checks the full six-skeleton, evaluates every signed simplex trace, and recomputes the irreducible multiplicities. `verification_output.txt` records the successful replay. The certificate uses bit masks on the lexicographically ordered twelve vertices. Finite checking establishes the enumerated premises; the infinite-dimensional-homotopy implications used here follow from the explicitly stated standard theorems, not from sampled computations.

## Relationship to prior work

Bayer, Denker, Jelić Milutinović, Rowlands, Sundaram and Xue introduced ordinary cut complexes and studied various graph families, including trees, cycles and clique prisms (arXiv:2304.13675, first posted April 26, 2023). Their sequel (arXiv:2407.08158, first posted July 11, 2024) determines the three- and four-cut complexes of rectangular grids and develops homology representations; its Theorem 2.16 supplies a general full-skeleton Euler-characteristic method, but not the present graph's homotopy equivalence or its character.

Published work classifies the ordinary three-cut complex of triangle-containing graphs without four-cycles, including this truncated-tetrahedral graph, but does not determine its four-cut complex. Another known four-cut sphere-wedge classification assumes girth at least five; \(T\) contains four triangles and is excluded. The result here is therefore a four-cut and full-symmetry classification at a natural, low-girth, vertex-transitive boundary case rather than an application of either stronger-looking statement.

## Limitations

This is a single canonical finite graph, not a universal theorem for all cubic graphs. The integral homotopy equivalence is nonequivariant; the representation decomposition is over \(\mathbb Q\), not a classification of the integral \(S_4\)-module. The complete simplicial enumeration and certificates can be independently replayed, but are not a computer-formalized proof in an external theorem prover. Published and indexed searches reduce, but cannot eliminate, risk from unindexed equivalent calculations. No independent external audit has been performed.
