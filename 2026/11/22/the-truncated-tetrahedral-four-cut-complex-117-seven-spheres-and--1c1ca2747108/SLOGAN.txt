The ordinary four-cut complex of the truncated-tetrahedral graph is homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{117}S^7\), with Alexander dual \(\bigvee^{117}S^2\). Its full tetrahedral \(S_4\)-action on rational seventh homology has character \((117,7,1,3,1)\) on the five conjugacy types and fixes exactly eight independent homology classes.
