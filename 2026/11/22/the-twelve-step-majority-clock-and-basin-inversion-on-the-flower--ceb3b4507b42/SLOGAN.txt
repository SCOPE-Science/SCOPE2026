On the twenty-vertex flower snark \(J_5\), synchronous binary majority has exactly \(154\) equilibria, \(7{,}397\) two-cycle orbits, and a sharp twelve-update entrance clock. Compared with the equally sized cubic dodecahedron, it has fewer oscillatory orbits yet a larger oscillatory basin: \(874{,}268\) versus \(860{,}448\) starting states.
