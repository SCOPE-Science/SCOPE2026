# Scientific assessment

## Correctness

PASS. The coefficient identities and irreducible boundary cubic are reconstructed explicitly; the coefficient of the hyperplane pulls back with multiplicities one and two, and both components have generically one-to-one images. The projective bundle is smooth, the map is proper and quasi-finite by polynomial degree and uniqueness, and it is birational by the source theorem, so it is the normalization. The two lines have two preimages generically, proving singularity; on their complement the morphism has one preimage and a nonzero rank-three Jacobian in explicit local charts. The separate all-parameter Stirling recursion follows from pushforwards and Segre classes. The included checker corroborates critical identities but does not substitute for the proof.

## Originality

PASS, qualified. Piene--Shapiro establish the smooth affine composition locus, birational projective scroll and degree seven; their Section 10 leaves the singularities at infinity open. This result explicitly determines the finite normalization, two-line singular support and nonreduced boundary cycle. A separately documented degree-nine cubic-after-cubic composition threefold has a single-line singularity; it concerns a different bundle and does not cover the two-line degree-eight case or its doubled cubic boundary. Related constructions concerning other threefolds or cubics also do not imply these assertions. Targeted research-record queries found no equivalent statement. Publication priority remains subject to incomplete indexing of older work and inaccessible records.

## Value

PASS. The first nontrivial projective compactification of a polynomial composition locus exhibits both a doubled boundary component and two precise nonnormality lines, turning a degree formula into a concrete geometric degeneration. The geometric Stirling recurrence explains the boundary multiplicities of a natural enumerative family. The result supplies usable compactification data for interpolation, intersection and degenerations, without claiming an unproved local Jacobian scheme.
