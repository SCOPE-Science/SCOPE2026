The two seven-vertex subdivisions of \(K_{2,3}\) have identical degree sequences but their fully bidirected multipath complexes have different integral homotopy types: \(\bigvee^5 S^3\vee\bigvee^4 S^4\) for arms \((2,2,4)\) and \(\bigvee^5 S^3\vee\bigvee^6 S^4\) for arms \((2,3,3)\). Exact finite acyclic matchings and integral boundary certificates establish both sphere wedges.
