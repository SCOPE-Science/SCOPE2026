The nine quadruple points and twelve nodes of the complex Hesse twelve-line arrangement have exact two-orbit weighted Seshadri constant \(1/\max\{3\alpha+2\beta,4\beta\}\). At \(\beta=3\alpha/2\), its minimizing lines switch from the twelve original lines to nine external four-node lines, and the corresponding blown-up nef ray is uniquely isotropic.
