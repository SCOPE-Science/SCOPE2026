For independent uniform random intervals, the expected total number of pairwise intersections conditional on the number covering a fixed point is affine in that count **only at the midpoint**. There the coverage count is an exact optimal predictor of the global edge count, explaining \(5(n-1)/[4(2n+1)]\) of its variance, approaching \(5/8\), with complete finite-sample formulas for every other fixed point.
