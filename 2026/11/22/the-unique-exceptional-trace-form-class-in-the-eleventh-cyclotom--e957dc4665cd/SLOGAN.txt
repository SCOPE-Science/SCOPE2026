In the eleventh cyclotomic field, a positive unary trace form has a shortest nonunit if and only if its weight is a positive rational multiple of \((\pi\overline\pi\varepsilon\overline\varepsilon)^{-1}\), where \(\pi=1-\zeta_{11}\) and \(\varepsilon\) is a unit. Hence the exceptional weights form exactly one class up to scaling and unit norms; an exact five-by-five Gram determinant proves the rigidity.
