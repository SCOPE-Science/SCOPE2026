For the 25-point discrete Fourier transform, the 500 affine images of \(\{0,1,4\}\) are exactly the three-sample designs that maximize the worst squared singular value over three-frequency supports. This bounds noise amplification in amplitude recovery on a supplied support; it does not identify the support. Each of the three universal affine types has exactly 25 worst supports, consecutive for the stated representatives, and exact rational certificates separate their bounds.
