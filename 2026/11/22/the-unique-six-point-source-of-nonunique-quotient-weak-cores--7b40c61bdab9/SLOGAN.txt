Among finite \(T_0\) spaces with at most six points, the sole source of two non-homeomorphic quotient weak cores is \(K_{3,3}\) with two independent edges removed, viewed as a height-two poset. Its two weak-core quotients, \(P_{2,3}\) and \(P_{3,2}\), are obtained by exactly one pair identification each; the six-point example itself was previously known, while the sharp cutoff and unique-source classification are the refinements.
