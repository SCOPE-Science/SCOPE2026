For every fixed number \(r\) of new labelled vertices, the induced-extension count over an empty \(n\)-point partial Steiner triple system is asymptotic to \(I_n^r e^{-\binom r2/2}2^{\binom r2}P_r(1/8)\), where \(P_r\) is the labelled linear-triple-system packing polynomial. At three new vertices, the eight old-witnessed pair-presence patterns and the single internal-triple pattern each have limiting probability \(1/9\).
