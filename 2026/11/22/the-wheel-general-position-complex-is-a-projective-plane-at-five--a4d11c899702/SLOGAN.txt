The simplicial complex of ordinary geodesic general-position sets on the wheel \(K_1\vee C_n\) is the real projective plane at \(n=5\) rim vertices, carrying genuine integral two-torsion, but for every \(n\ge6\) it splits as \(S^2\vee\operatorname{Ind}_2(C_n)\). Together with the known four-periodic cycle homotopy theorem, this gives a complete integral homotopy classification for all wheels with \(n\ge3\) rim vertices.
