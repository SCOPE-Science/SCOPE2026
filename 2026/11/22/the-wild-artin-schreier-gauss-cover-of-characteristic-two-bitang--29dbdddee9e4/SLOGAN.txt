The Gauss map of every integral Hilario–Stöhr bitangent quartic in characteristic two is a degree-four map with a connected wild Artin–Schreier separable factor and an inseparable quadratic factor. Its normalized formula \(t=\lambda/(s^4+s^2+D)\) implies that exactly half of all rational smooth tangent lines over every \(\mathbb F_{2^m}\) have two rational contacts; the other half have quadratic-conjugate contacts.
