PASS. Under independent continuous margins, the paired rank map is a uniform permutation. Exact without-replacement first/fourth moments yield the common mean and variance. The sixth moment is evaluated through a transparent 203-partition equality-pattern identity and complete coarsening Möbius sum; the included independent integer-polynomial checker verifies every claimed rational coefficient identity, not merely several numerical instances. The third-centered formulas and strict inequalities follow by algebraic factoring. Exhaustive enumeration at sizes 7 and 9 separately agrees. The proof applies to every odd sample size at least 7 and every claimed frequency, without extrapolation.

## Originality
PASS, qualified. The closest rank-testing primary literature develops distribution-free and asymptotic procedures, not these three exact skewness classes. A published order-three resonance for fixed-weight cyclic binary autocorrelations has different probabilities and objects and does not determine the rank-permutation Fourier moments. Previously established sharp fourth moments of real permutation rank-one coefficients do not imply the complex Fourier sixth moments: fourth moments determine at most the first two moments of the squared Fourier energy. Actual searches of mathematical research records and the named primary articles found no equivalent sixth-moment classification; an unindexed general permutation moment formula could still imply it and is an explicit priority risk. Numerical coincidences, related fourth-order optimization, and common use of Möbius inversion were not treated as coverage.

This is a structural all-frequency phenomenon, not an arbitrary slice or a tiny numerical tweak. It does not claim improved power or an exact combined-test distribution.
