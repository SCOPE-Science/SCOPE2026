For the two-parameter noisy-majority cellular automaton, a fair independent input on every ring of size at least four (or on the whole line) produces **three-wise independent fair output** whenever \(r=1/2\), yet its consecutive four-spin parity moment is exactly \((2p-1)^4/16\) on rings of size at least five and on the line. Consequently the fair product law is stationary at every ring size at least four only at \(p=r=1/2\), whereas the three-site ring has the exceptional stationary line \(p+r=1\).
