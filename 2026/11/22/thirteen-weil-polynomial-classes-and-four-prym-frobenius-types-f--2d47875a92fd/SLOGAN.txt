The \(42\) smooth marked genus-three quartics \(y^4=x^3+ax+b\) over \(\mathbb F_7\) realize exactly \(13\) rational Jacobian Weil polynomials, while their geometrically superspecial Prym surfaces have precisely four distinct \(\mathbb F_7\)-isogeny types. For every odd extension degree, the quartic and its elliptic quotient have equal point counts, forcing each Prym numerator to be even; the complete thirteen-type spectrum is established by an exact exhaustive field calculation.
