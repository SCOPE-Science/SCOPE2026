For every smooth degree-two del Pezzo blowup of a Frobenius-stable general-position septuple over an odd finite field, and for its Geiser quadratic twist, the complete permutation of all \(33\,645\) geometric Zariski chambers is classified by thirty explicit cycle spectra. For one seven-cycle of base points the two forms have, respectively, three and one ground-field-rational chambers, and all extension-field counts follow from the cycle spectra.
