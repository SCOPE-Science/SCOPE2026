The genus-three Picard family \(y^3=x^4+ax^2+bx+1\) in characteristic thirteen has precisely \(36\) reduced marked parameters with Jacobian \(13\)-rank zero, even though all share Cartier \(a\)-number two with a rank-one locus. The exceptional parameters form three degree-four and two degree-twelve closed points over \(\mathbb F_{13}\).
