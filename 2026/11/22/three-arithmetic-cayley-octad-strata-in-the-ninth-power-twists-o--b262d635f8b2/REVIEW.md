# Assessment of the arithmetic Picard-quartic twist classification

## Correctness — PASS

The stated family is smooth in every allowed characteristic. The order-nine automorphism acts irreducibly on the six-dimensional binary Jacobian torsion representation, whose symplectic centralizer is precisely cyclic of order nine; this forces arithmetic Frobenius to belong to that cyclic group. The explicit ninth-power twist transports Frobenius through all nine powers equally often. The unique fixed theta characteristic of the order-three subgroup is odd because an even theta has stabilizer \(S_8\), which contains no order-nine elements. All asserted cycle partitions and minimum determinant extension degrees follow. The supplied exact integer program exhausts the symplectic group and verifies the centralizer and theta cycles; a second program checks all rational lines for all coefficient-marked curves over \(\mathbb F_{19}\). Finite checks are supplementary, not the proof over arbitrary fields.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Liang's geometric order-nine bitangent formulas and earlier abstract \(36\)-octad cycle data are prior work and are not rebranded as new. The earlier abstract full symplectic theta-spectrum classification likewise covers the listed permutations but not their occurrence in a specified ninth-power twist family over every appropriate finite field. Bergvall's moduli statistics, Elsenhans–Jahnel's invariant-octad framework, and the examined determinantal literature do not supply the coefficient-fiber counts and uniform sharp geometric realization asserted here. A short Kummer descent argument is required to pass from known geometric actions to the nine finite-field coefficient strata. There remains a risk of an older unindexed calculation of the same arithmetic twist family.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The classification is complete for a canonical geometrically constant family with an exceptional order-nine automorphism. It settles a concrete arithmetic realization question (not just a possible group cycle type) for every odd \(q\equiv1\pmod9\), including exact frequencies and a sharp ninth-extension obstruction to symmetric representations despite a rational bitangent. The full extension-field profiles are natural finite-level descent invariants; no unrelated parameters or arbitrary cutoffs are introduced.

## Closest literature and limitations

The closest geometric paper is Liang's full treatment of the order-nine Picard quartic (New York J. Math. 26, 2020), including its explicit bitangents. Bergvall (arXiv:2106.13072) gives the marking geometry and stabilizer. Elsenhans–Jahnel (arXiv:1710.07279) address other Galois realization cases. Existing abstract theta-cycle tables are stronger in their group-theoretic universe but do not specify arithmetic realizability for the present family. The theorem does not classify all quartic twists in all characteristics or produce literal determinant matrices; priority risk remains in older explicit finite-field literature.
