For every odd prime power \(q\equiv1\pmod9\), the ninth-power twists \(Y^3Z=X^4-cXZ^3\) form exactly three coefficient-count strata with least symmetric-determinant extension degrees \(1,3,9\), occurring \((q-1)/9\), \(2(q-1)/9\), and \(6(q-1)/9\) times. Every degree-nine case has a rational hyperflex bitangent but no rational Cayley octad even after extensions of degrees less than nine.
