The sixteen marked quadratic descents of the genus-five Humbert cover ramified at the five affine points of \(\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_5)\) form exactly three abstract \(\mathbb F_5\)-isomorphism classes, with respective multiplicities \(1,10,5\) and point counts \(16,8,0\). Their complete Weil numerators are \((1+2T+5T^2)^{5-m}(1-2T+5T^2)^m\) for \(m=0,2,4\), and every member has \(56\) points over \(\mathbb F_{25}\).
