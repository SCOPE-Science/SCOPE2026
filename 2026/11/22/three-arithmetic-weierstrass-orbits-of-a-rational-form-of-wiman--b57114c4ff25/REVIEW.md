Their sixteen-sheeted Kummer quotient, five eight-point branch fibers, smoothness, and canonical structure are established in the proof. Local Vandermonde jets give every branch point canonical orders \((0,1,2,4,6)\); forty weights of three exhaust the genus-five Wronskian degree. The relative radical identities are checked exactly. His complete text was inspected and does not state the \(\mathbb Q\)-Galois orbits or minimal splitting field of the displayed rational Fourier-descended form. The general automorphism results of Hidalgo–Kontogeorgis–Leyton-Álvarez–Paramantzoglou (2014) do not determine arithmetic points or splitting fields. A published 2023 account on type-five Humbert–Edge curves concerns genus seventeen, not this genus-five object. Historical sources may contain overlooked special-curve arithmetic; Edge's 1951 full paper was unavailable, so originality is not certified priority.
