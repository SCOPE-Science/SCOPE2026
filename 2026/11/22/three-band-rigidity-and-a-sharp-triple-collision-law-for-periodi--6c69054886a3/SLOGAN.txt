Symmetrically jittering every triple of nodes in a critically sampled Fourier grid produces exactly three singular-value bands, independent of the grid size, with a unique upper-band crossing at a quarter-grid displacement. When each triple collapses, the smallest singular value vanishes quadratically and the exact condition number diverges as \(\frac{81}{4\pi^2}(1-\varepsilon)^{-2}\).
