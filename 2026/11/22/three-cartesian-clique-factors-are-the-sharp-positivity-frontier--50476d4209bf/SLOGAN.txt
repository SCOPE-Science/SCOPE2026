For the second-order Padé \([0/2]\) update of graph heat flow, **every positive timestep is entrywise positive on an arbitrarily weighted Cartesian product of complete graphs exactly when there are at most three factors**, regardless of factor orders. Four factors already force a negative small-step transition, with leading coefficient \(-6\) times the product of the four coordinate conductances; the positive side follows from strict alternating scalar derivatives and tensor finite differences.
