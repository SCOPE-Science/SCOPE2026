# Reproducibility and boundaries

Run `python3 artifacts/verify.py` with the Python 3 standard library. The script returns `VERIFY_OK: 10681 exact rational assertions; all true`. It checks the scalar derivative numerators through order four, the fourth-order Maclaurin coefficient, all entries of nine small mixed-alphabet clique products by both spectral formula and independent matrix inversion at three rational timesteps, row-stochasticity, and two negative entries at distance four on the four-cube. All comparisons use rational arithmetic. The finite tests do not establish the quantified theorem; its proof uses the finite-difference integral and the exact four-coordinate shortest-walk coefficient. The experiments take unit edge rates; the proof covers arbitrary positive factor conductances.

Independent audit was not performed. The accessible 2012 positive-systems manuscript and published same-map graph statements were compared in mathematical content; the historical 2004 positivity-preserving Padé paper was available only at abstract level, and an authorized institutional full-text attempt was unavailable. This is an originality access limitation, not a counterexample or proof failure.
