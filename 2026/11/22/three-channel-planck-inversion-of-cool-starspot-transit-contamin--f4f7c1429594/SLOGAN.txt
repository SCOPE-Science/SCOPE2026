In an idealized unocculted cool-starspot transit model, three monochromatic depths at \(\lambda,2\lambda,4\lambda\) uniquely recover spot temperature, spot coverage and achromatic planet depth by an explicit quadratic inverse and a sharp data-feasibility inequality. Any two wavelengths leave a continuous physical ambiguity, while the three-channel limit becomes ill-conditioned near a spot-free or equal-temperature photosphere.
