In one-sided tie-retaining plurality, every cyclic word over \(q\ge3\) colors becomes either a fixed proper coloring or a rigidly translating configuration with no singleton runs, in at most the sharp \(n-2\) updates. Exactly at \(q=3\), the frozen and translating families both grow like \(2^n\); for every \(q\ge4\), frozen configurations dominate exponentially.
