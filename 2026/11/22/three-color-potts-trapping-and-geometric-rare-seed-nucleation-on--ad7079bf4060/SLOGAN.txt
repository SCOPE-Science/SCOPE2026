On the Petersen graph, three-color ferromagnetic strict energy-descent dynamics has exactly 579 absorbing states and a nonconsensus trapping probability of \(1130139522395301525645463/3686384171559531241800000\) from uniformly random initial colors. Among \(7{:}2{:}1\) initial color populations, trapping occurs with probability \(11/270\) precisely when the two sites of the double-minority color are nonadjacent and the singleton minority site touches exactly one of them; otherwise its probability is zero.
