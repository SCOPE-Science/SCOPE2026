# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof fixes the right-module convention and proves a three-line implication using two complementary axes and an invertible graph parameter. For repeated-diagonal tuples, a direct centralizer equation gives the exact fixed-line family. The semialgebraic dimension follows from a conjugation parametrization, the seven-dimensional normalizer of the diagonal quaternion subalgebra, and an open noncommuting pair generating that subalgebra. No finite sampling is used to infer an infinite theorem. Exact auxiliary quaternion-linear checks passed.

**Originality — PASS (historical priority provisional).** The November 14, 2026 source derives an at-least-two-subspaces dimension and explicitly distinguishes that from the infinitely-many-subspaces question. Its three-line threshold and normalizer fiber computation are absent from the material inspected. A claim-level search including synonyms for three common right eigenlines, finite-versus-continuous invariant lines, quaternion matrix tuples, and normalizer orbits found no dominating published result in the examined sources. The complex comparison and general quaternion linear algebra do not themselves imply the quaternionic semialgebraic dimension. Specialist literature not indexed in accessible sources could contain an equivalent elementary case.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The finding supplies the first higher-multiplicity stratum after a recently determined two-line locus. It resolves a natural rigidity threshold, proves an exact codimension valid for every tuple length, and distinguishes three topologically different projective fixed-line families. The sharp difference between exactly two and a continuum has meaning independent of computation.
