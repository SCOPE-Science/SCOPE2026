For tuples of \(2\times2\) quaternion matrices, three distinct common invariant right lines force an entire projective family \(\mathbb RP^1\), \(\mathbb CP^1\), or \(\mathbb HP^1\): exactly three or any finite number above two cannot occur. For every \(r\ge2\), the three-line locus has exact real semialgebraic dimension \(4r+9\), of codimension \(4r-1\) inside the known two-line locus.
