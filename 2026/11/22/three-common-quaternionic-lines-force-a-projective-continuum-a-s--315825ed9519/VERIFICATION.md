# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Two distinct invariant right lines give a quaternionic basis. A third is generated by (1,t) for t nonzero, so b_i t=t a_i for each diagonal pair, and changing the second basis vector by right multiplication by t produces the repeated diagonal. This does not require any A_i to be invertible. In these coordinates the full common-line set is the common centralizer of the a_i in the affine chart, plus the point at infinity. The imaginary-part cross-product identity gives precisely the centralizers R, a copy of C, and H. The seven-dimensional normalizer is checked by its entrywise real centralizer GL_2(R) of dimension four and the three-dimensional inner automorphism group of H; this is the exact fiber on the nonempty open set where the first two parameters generate H. Independently, the incidence of ordered distinct triples and tuples has dimension 12+4r: the base has dimension 12 and each triple imposes the repeated diagonal form with 4r free parameters. Its fibers over tuples have dimension 3, 6, or 12 according to the projective alternative, with the minimum 3 attained on a nonempty open set via noncommuting a_1,a_2. This gives the same image dimension 4r+9. The ordered-pair incidence has dimension 8+8r and generically finite fibers: distinct real parts of a_1,b_1 forbid quaternionic conjugacy and hence any third line, so the subtraction really gives codimension 4r-1. The finite CHECK.py diagnostics are consistent with, but not the basis of, these all-rank conclusions.
