For every standard weighted Bergman disk and monomial degree at least three, projection onto a same-point value/first/second-derivative jet has exactly three strict radial peaks, computable at the roots of one shifted Jacobi cubic. In the sharp high-degree Gamma limit, the preferred peak shifts from the largest Laguerre root at weight \(\beta=2\) to the middle root for sufficiently large \(\beta\), forcing an intermediate tie in optimal sampling radii.
