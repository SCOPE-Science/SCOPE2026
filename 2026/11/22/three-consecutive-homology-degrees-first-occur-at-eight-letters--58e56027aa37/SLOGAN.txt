For the ordered-word complex generated by all fixed-point-free involutions, eight letters is the first even alphabet size with three distinct nonzero reduced \(\mathbb F_2\)-homology degrees: the ranks are \(8,294,9\) in degrees \(3,4,5\). Exhaustive exact chain reductions determine every mod-two Betti number for two, four, six and eight letters; no infinite-family claim is made.
