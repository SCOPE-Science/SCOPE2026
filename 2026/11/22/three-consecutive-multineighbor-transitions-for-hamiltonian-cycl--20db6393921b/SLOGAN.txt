Deleting a Hamiltonian cycle from \(K_{n,n}\) creates a sharp three-stage multineighbor transition: two circles, then two wedges of \(\binom n2-2n+1\) circles, then two wedges of \(n(n-5)/2-1\) two-spheres for every \(n\ge7\). The first inclusion preserves the original circle primitively, while the next inclusion kills every first-homology class.
