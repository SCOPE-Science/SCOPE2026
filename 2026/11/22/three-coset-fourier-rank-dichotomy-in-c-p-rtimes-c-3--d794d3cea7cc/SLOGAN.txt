On every prime-order Frobenius group \(C_p\rtimes C_3\), coefficient-one three-tap convolution has exactly the ranks \(p\), \(3p-2\), and \(3p\). Each three-coset transversal loses just two Fourier dimensions unless it is an order-three subgroup coset, in which case its rank collapses to \(p\); exact support counts hold for every prime \(p\equiv1\pmod3\).
