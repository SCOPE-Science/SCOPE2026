## Correctness — PASS

The mathematical domain is the stationary one-dimensional harmonic telegraph model with independently Gaussian Brownian forcing, known positive relaxation, and positive finite propulsion, tumbling, and thermal scales. The stationary moment generator gives independent exact cumulants of orders two, four, and six. Their temperature-free scale ratio is strictly decreasing on the full open rate domain, with proven endpoints, and the radical root uniquely reconstructs the rate. The fourth and second cumulants successively fix speed and thermal diffusivity, giving exact necessity and sufficiency; continuity gives a genuine two-cumulant parameter ambiguity. `check_exact.py` passed the full symbolic recurrence and rational witness. Boundary zero thermal diffusivity is stated separately.

The derived cumulants are not novel by themselves. No examined full text states this exact map. Other unindexed inverse characterizations remain a concrete publication-priority risk, especially because the forward beta/Gaussian law is classical.

## Value — PASS

This supplies a physically motivated minimum-data identifiability criterion and falsifiable inequality for a standard active-matter model when position alone can be observed and thermal noise masks active variance. Distinguishing a run-and-tumble mechanism from pure thermal diffusion using its higher-order spatial statistics is an intrinsic physical inverse problem. The sharp feasible set tells experimental analysts when a population cumulant triple is inconsistent with the hypothesized mechanics. The result is structural, finite-dimensional, and deliberately does not assert sample-efficiency or parameter-estimation accuracy.
