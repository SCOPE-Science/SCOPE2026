A trapped two-state run-and-tumble particle with unknown speed, tumbling rate, and thermal diffusivity is uniquely identified by its second, fourth, and sixth stationary position cumulants when the trap rate is known. The temperature-independent ratio \(K_6^2/(-K_4)^3\) must lie strictly between zero and \(32\), yielding an exact inverse and a sharp model-feasibility test; the first two cumulants alone admit infinitely many physical parameter triples.
