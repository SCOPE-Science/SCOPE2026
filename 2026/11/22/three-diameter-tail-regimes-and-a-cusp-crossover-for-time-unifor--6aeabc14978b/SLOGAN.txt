Two points sampled independently at uniformly random rolling phases along one trochoid arch have sharply different near-diameter probability exponents in the curtate, ordinary-cycloid and prolate regimes: \(2\), \(2/3\) and \(1\). At tracer-radius ratio \(\beta=1+\lambda\varepsilon^{2/3}\), a precise cubic planar sublevel set gives the crossover coefficient and the conditional endpoint geometry.
