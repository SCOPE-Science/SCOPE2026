The fourth-order exponential-completion AVF step of arXiv:2609.24692 has **exactly three dissipative real endpoints** on the scalar convex quartic \(\mathcal H(x)=x^4/4\) whenever \(hx^2\ge20\), although the physical flow has a unique solution. As \(hx^2\to\infty\), two branches nearly reverse the state and the third nearly freezes it, showing that energy dissipation alone does not determine a coarse-step map.
