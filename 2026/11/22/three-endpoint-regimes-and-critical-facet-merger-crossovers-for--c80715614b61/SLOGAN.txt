For a uniformly sampled point in the orthogonal join of two spherical sectors on \(S^3\), the exact nearest-boundary distance law has three distinct upper-tail exponents \(3\), \(5/2\), and \(2\), depending on whether zero, one, or both apertures are hemispheric. Explicit square-root aperture windows interpolate between these phases, while nondegenerate almost-incentral points have a four-slack \(\operatorname{Dirichlet}(1,1,1,1)\) shape law.
