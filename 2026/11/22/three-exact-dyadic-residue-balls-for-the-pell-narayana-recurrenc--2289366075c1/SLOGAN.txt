The Pell–Narayana recurrence \(P_{n+3}=2P_{n+2}+P_n\) attains exactly the two-adic residues in three disjoint balls, \((1+2\mathbb Z_2)\cup8\mathbb Z_2\cup(2+256\mathbb Z_2)\). Consequently its value set occupies exactly \(161/256\) of all sufficiently high dyadic residue classes, and every allowed residue occurs infinitely often.
