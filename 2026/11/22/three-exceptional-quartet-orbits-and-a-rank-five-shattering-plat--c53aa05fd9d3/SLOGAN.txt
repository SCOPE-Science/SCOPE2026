The open-neighborhood shattering complex of the Paley graph on \(\mathbb F_{81}\) has VC dimension \(5\), exact face polynomial \(1+81z+3240z^2+85320z^3+1658880z^4+9515880z^5\), and precisely three affine-semilinear orbits of nonshattered four-supports. Its inclusion-maximal supports occur in both sizes four and five; the \(4860\) maximal four-supports form exactly two further orbits, giving a nonpure finite shattering complex.
