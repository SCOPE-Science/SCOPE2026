For every \(n\ge2\), the Cayley cubic map \(f_n=y-T_n(x)\) yields a flat stable genus-\(n\) hyperelliptic family with exactly three singular finite fibers at \(\lambda=0,\pm2\), all nodal and with explicit component and normalization-genus counts. Its total space retains exactly the four Cayley nodes, and the branch-polynomial discriminant has a closed parity-dependent factorization.
