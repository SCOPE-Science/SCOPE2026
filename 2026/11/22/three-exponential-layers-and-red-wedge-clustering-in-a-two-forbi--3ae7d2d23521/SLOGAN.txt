Over an old red matching in a homogeneous three-coloured graph forbidding red and green monochromatic triangles, the first three fixed-arity tuple-type exponential bases are \(8^r\), \((7/8)8^r\), and \((25/32)8^r\). Among types having at least two nonneutral edges on the new variables, almost every type has exactly two incident red edges as the old matching grows.
