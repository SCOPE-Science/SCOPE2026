On every unweighted \(K_{2,q}\) with \(q\ge9\), minimizing the two-leader grounded heat trace at observation times \(1/5\), \(67/100\), and \(1\) uniquely selects, in turn, two leaders in the large part, one in each part, and both vertices in the small part. At fixed positive time and large \(q\), the three optimizer regimes have exact boundaries \(\log((1+\sqrt5)/2)\) and \(\log2\).
