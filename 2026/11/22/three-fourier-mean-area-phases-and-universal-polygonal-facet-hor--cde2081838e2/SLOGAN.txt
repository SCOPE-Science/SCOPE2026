Every planar convex polygon has three sharp Fourier-mean phases: compact for \(0<p<2\), unbounded with finite area for \(2\le p<4\), and the entire plane for \(p\ge4\). In the new intermediate regime each facet normal has a universal power horn, with its exact coefficient determined solely by the lengths of parallel edges, and every other ray admits an exact projected-vertex fractional-distance formula.
