# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The symbolic lift is an irrational circle rotation coded into \(m\) unit cells. Its length-\(n\) cylinder partition consists of \(mn\) different intervals. Kesten's interval criterion reduces bounded-remainder status to an endpoint-rotation-orbit equality. The proof explicitly identifies the gap displacement as a signed return index and derives the three possible successor displacements and their exact multiplicities. Neither the all-length formula nor the Fibonacci phase law relies on extrapolating finite checks. The finite independent cut-edge enumeration verified 3,840 formulas, all 640 corresponding successor maps, and thirteen Fibonacci identities.

## Originality — PASS within the examined literature
Kang–Jäger (2026, arXiv:2609.01140v2, Sections 1 and 4.3–5) proves strict ergodicity/minimality results for permutative lifts, but not a census of which *individual cylinders* have bounded frequency discrepancy. Kelly–Sadun (2014, arXiv:1404.0455, Theorems 1–2 and Sections 3–4) provides the general Kesten/Oren interval criterion, which is explicitly treated here as a known premise. The classical three-gap theorem is also known; the index-return lemma is re-proved here to expose the exact modular weights. The new claim is the all-length three-term count and the golden-lift Fibonacci phase law, not the underlying general results. Available research summaries on Sturmian cohomological transfers, Fibonacci cover statistics and finite lifts concern different invariants and do not state the same census. Historical priority has not been independently certified.

## Mathematical value — PASS
An invariant measure's strict ergodicity alone supplies no uniform bounded error for a particular cylinder. The census exactly partitions all \(mn\) admissible length-\(n\) cylinder observables into bounded and unbounded discrepancy classes using only two return indices, for every irrational slope and modulus. The golden Fibonacci specialization shows that the bounded class can vanish completely at arbitrarily long lengths and occupy positive fractions at other arbitrarily long lengths, with three explicit limiting proportions. It is a motivated full classification, not an arbitrary finite parameter slice.
