The number of bounded-remainder length-\(n\) cylinders in an \(m\)-fold cyclic Sturmian lift has an exact three-gap formula from just two extremal rotation-return indices. At the golden slope with \(m=2\), its proportions along Fibonacci lengths alternate between three asymptotic phases \(0\), \(\varphi^{-2}\), and \(\varphi^{-1}\).
