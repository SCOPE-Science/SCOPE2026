# Three-independence topology and integral symmetries of quadrilateral books

## Finding
Let \(m\ge3\), and let \(B_m=K_2\square K_{1,m}\), the graph of \(m\) quadrilaterals sharing their common edge. Its ordinary third higher-independence complex (the simplicial complex of vertex sets whose induced connected components all have at most three vertices) satisfies
\[
\operatorname{Ind}_3(B_m)\simeq\bigvee^{2m-1}S^2.
\]
The graph automorphism group is \(\Gamma_m\cong S_m\times C_2\). Let \(U=\{u,v\}\) be the common-edge endpoints and \(W=\{a_i,b_i:1\le i\le m\}\) the remaining vertices. For a finite \(\Gamma_m\)-set \(T\), write \(I_T=\ker(\mathbb Z[T]\xrightarrow{\sum}\mathbb Z)\). The complete **integral** top-homology action is canonically
\[
\widetilde H_2(\operatorname{Ind}_3(B_m);\mathbb Z)\cong I_U\otimes_{\mathbb Z}I_W,
\]
where page permutations simultaneously permute the \(a_i,b_i\), and the involution interchanges \(u,v\) and all \(a_i,b_i\). In particular, if \(\sigma\in S_m\) fixes \(f(\sigma)\) pages and \(\rho\) is the layer-exchange involution, the rational character is
\[
\chi(\sigma)=2f(\sigma)-1,\qquad \chi(\sigma\rho)=1.
\]
The full-group invariant integral homology is the primitive infinite cyclic lattice generated by the tensor of \(u-v\) with \(\sum_i(a_i-b_i)\). The full finite-automorphism quotient has exactly one nonzero reduced rational Betti number, equal to one in degree two.

## Assumptions and scope
The graph has vertices \(u,v,a_1,b_1,\ldots,a_m,b_m\) and only the edges \(uv\), \(ua_i\), \(a_ib_i\), and \(b_iv\), for \(1\le i\le m\). The graph is simple and undirected. Thus each page is an induced four-cycle \(u,a_i,b_i,v,u\). The cutoff equals exactly three, and the result is asserted for **all integers \(m\ge3\)**. No statement about higher cutoffs, arbitrary graph books or an equivariant wedge decomposition is claimed. Homology is reduced integral homology unless specified otherwise. Since the two common-edge vertices have degree \(m+1\) while all others have degree two, every automorphism permutes the pages, with an optional simultaneous layer swap: \(\Gamma_m=S_m\times C_2\).

## Proof
Put \(A=\{a_1,\ldots,a_m\}\), \(B=\{b_1,\ldots,b_m\}\), \(W=A\sqcup B\), and \(X=\operatorname{Ind}_3(B_m)\). The graph induced on \(W\) is a disjoint union of \(m\) edges, hence **every** subset of \(W\) is a face: \(\Delta(W)\subseteq X\).

Define \(L_u=\{F\subseteq W: F\cup\{u\}\in X\}\) and similarly \(L_v\). A face \(F\subseteq W\), with selected \(a\)-vertices indexed by \(T\subseteq[m]\) and selected \(b\)-vertices indexed by \(S\subseteq[m]\), belongs to \(L_u\) precisely when \(|T|+|T\cap S|\le2\). Indeed the connected component containing \(u\) then has \(1+|T|+|T\cap S|\) vertices; all other components are isolated vertices or edges. Consequently \(L_u\) is the union of \((\operatorname{skel}_0\Delta(A))*\Delta(B)\) and, for each unordered pair \(i<j\), the simplex \(\Delta(\{a_i,a_j\}\cup(B\setminus\{b_i,b_j\}))\). Its intersection with the initial join is exactly the portion not containing both \(a_i,a_j\).

For \(m\ge3\), fix \(k\notin\{i,j\}\). The simplex contributed by pair \(i,j\) collapses relative to the initial join by pairing every face containing \(\{a_i,a_j\}\) but not \(b_k\) with the face obtained by adjoining \(b_k\), in decreasing order of cardinality. All pairs are free, because distinct contributions cannot have a common face containing two specified \(a\)-vertices. After completing every pair, the survivor is \((\operatorname{skel}_0\Delta(A))*\Delta(B)\), a cone since \(B\ne\emptyset\). Therefore \(L_u\) is collapsible. Symmetrically, \(L_v\) is collapsible.

Let \(Y=\Delta(W)\cup (u*L_u)\cup(v*L_v)\). Coning off a contractible subcomplex does not change homotopy type; attaching these two cones successively to the full simplex \(\Delta(W)\) shows \(Y\) is contractible. The faces containing **both** \(u\) and \(v\) are exactly \(\{u,v\}\) and \(\{u,v,w\}\) for \(w\in W\), because their induced connected component has cardinality \(2+|F|\). Hence
\[
X=Y\cup J,\quad J=\Delta(\{u,v\})*W_0,\quad
Y\cap J=D=\{u,v\}_0*W_0,
\]
where \(W_0\) and \(\{u,v\}_0\) denote discrete zero-dimensional complexes. The join \(J\) is contractible (its first factor is an edge), and \(D\) is the complete bipartite one-dimensional complex \(K_{2,2m}\). Since a union of two contractible CW subcomplexes is homotopy equivalent to the suspension of their intersection,
\[
X\simeq\Sigma|D|\simeq\bigvee^{2m-1}S^2,
\]
because \(|D|\) is a connected graph with \(4m-(2m+2)+1=2m-1\) independent circles.

For the **canonical integral action**, the natural Mayer–Vietoris connecting isomorphism \(\widetilde H_2(X;\mathbb Z)\xrightarrow{\simeq}\widetilde H_1(D;\mathbb Z)\) is \(\Gamma_m\)-equivariant, as \(Y\), \(J\), and their intersection are invariant. The integral cycle lattice of \(K_{2,2m}\) is canonically \(I_U\otimes I_W\): the elementary tensor \((u-v)\otimes(w-w')\) maps to the oriented four-cycle with \(w,w'\) on the second shore. A spanning tree of \(D\) gives \(2m-1\) such fundamental cycles, establishing a free integral isomorphism rather than only a rational character identity. A page permutation fixes the \(u-v\) factor and has \(2f(\sigma)-1\) as the trace on \(I_W\); the involution \(\rho\) negates \(u-v\) and permutes \(W\) without fixed vertices, giving \(\chi(\sigma\rho)=(-1)(-1)=1\).

An \(S_m\)-fixed vector in \(I_W\) is an integer multiple of \(\sum_i(a_i-b_i)\). The layer swap negates this vector and also negates \(u-v\), so their tensor is invariant and primitive. Finally, rational simplicial homology of a quotient by a finite simplicial action equals the invariant rational homology (after barycentric subdivision), yielding exactly one rational invariant in degree two.

## Verification
The supplied standard-library program `verify.py` independently constructs all faces by induced-component enumeration. For \(m=3,4,5\), it checks the stated \(X=Y\cup J\) and \(Y\cap J=D\) identities, verifies explicit relative elementary-collapse pairs in \(L_u\), computes the entire reduced mod-two Betti table from boundary matrices, and calculates the signed Lefschetz trace of every automorphism in \(S_m\times C_2\). It returns the expected ranks \(5,7,9\) in degree two and the expected character on all \(12,48,240\) group elements, respectively. These finite computations corroborate, but **do not replace**, the preceding uniform integral proof.

## Relationship to prior work
Deshpande–Singh (arXiv:2001.05448, first publicly posted January 15, 2020) define higher independence and classify complete multipartite, fully whiskered, path, cycle and perfect-tree graph families; the quadrilateral book for \(m\ge3\) is none of these. Deshpande–Shukla–Singh (arXiv:2001.06775) prove connectivity and chordal-graph sphere-wedge results. The quadrilateral book has induced four-cycles, so the chordal hypothesis fails; the cited connectivity bounds do not provide the number of spheres or integral module. Abdelmalek et al. (arXiv:2106.10863) address chordal graphs, trees and caterpillars, again not the book graph. Camarena–Carnero Bravo (arXiv:2307.12401) study **ordinary** independence of categorical graph products, whereas the current theorem concerns **third** higher independence of a Cartesian product. A known classification of ordinary matching complexes of quadrilateral books concerns edge-set simplices, not the present induced-component vertex-set complex, and does not imply the present result.

## Limitations
The source comparison is a bounded literature search, not a proof of global novelty. Unindexed books, independently derived equivalent statements or consequences of broader unpublished homotopy decompositions remain residual risks. The homotopy equivalence to a sphere wedge is not asserted equivariant; only the integral homology representation is determined equivariantly. The excluded two-page case has additional one-link homology and is not covered by this proof. Nothing is asserted for other component cutoffs. Independent review has not been performed.
