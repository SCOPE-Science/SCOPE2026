On the four-dimensional perm superalgebra with \(e_1f_1=f_2\) and \(f_1f_1=e_2\), all even Nijenhuis operators form exactly three irreducible four-dimensional components meeting in one affine three-space. Over \(\mathbb F_Q\) there are exactly \(3Q^4-2Q^3\) such operators, of which \(Q^2(Q-1)(3Q-4)\) are invertible.
