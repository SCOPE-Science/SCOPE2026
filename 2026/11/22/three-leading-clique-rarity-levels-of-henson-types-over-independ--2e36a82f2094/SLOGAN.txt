For a large named independent set in the homogeneous \(K_{q+1}\)-free graph (\(q\ge3\)), uniformly sampled fixed-arity types become uniform on \(K_q\)-free internal graphs. Rare internal \(q\)-cliques concentrate first on a unique clique and then on exactly two cliques sharing \(q-1\) vertices, with three sharp exponential bases and an exact three-term type-count formula at arity \(q+1\).
