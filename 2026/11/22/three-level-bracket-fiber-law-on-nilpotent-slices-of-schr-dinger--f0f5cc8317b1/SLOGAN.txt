The complete ordered-commutator fiber distribution on every nonzero-nilpotent \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\)-slice of a finite Schrödinger Lie algebra has exactly three levels, governed by a single sum-of-squares character. In dimension \(n=1\), exactly \(q(q-1)/2\) targets in each such slice are not commutators; at all \(n\ge2\), every target is represented, with explicit multiplicities over every odd finite field.
