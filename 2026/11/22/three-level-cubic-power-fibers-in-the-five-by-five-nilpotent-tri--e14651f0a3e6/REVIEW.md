# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof is a full coordinate evaluation of \(X^3\) and an exhaustive partition of \((a,b,c,d)\) by output and functional degeneracy. It establishes the domain \(N_5(\mathbb F_q)\), all three fiber formulas, nonzero supports, normalization to \(q^{10}\), and exactly the characteristic-three group consequence. The checker independently computes each cube by matrix multiplication over five fields, including the extension field \(\mathbb F_4\); its tests reproduce all targets. Finite checks do not substitute for the symbolic proof. Residual correctness risk is ordinary hand-proof oversight, not an unproved required step.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge assessment)
Sosnovsky (arXiv:1201.6513v1, 2012) was inspected in full experimental HTML, especially the introduction, width results, and proof. Bier (DOI 10.1142/S0218196712500191, 2012) was accessible only as abstract/metadata, and is therefore retained as a priority risk. Dolfi–Singh–Yadav (arXiv:1905.04244v1, 2019) was inspected for its power-image results: identifying \(U(n,q)^p\) at \(n=p+2\) does not count roots of each prescribed image element. Menshov–Roman'kov (arXiv:1506.02806v1, 2015) studies root adjunction in larger groups, not this fiber census. Indexed records for five-by-five squaring in characteristic two and the general first nonsurjective \(p\)-power threshold concern different inputs. Actual targeted database searches returned no dominating exact three-valued cubic-root fiber theorem. The inability to fully read Bier and the possibility of an implicit power-word fiber table remain material historical-priority risks; this assessment is not a uniqueness proof.

## Value — PASS
The natural first nontrivial characteristic-three cube word in the five-by-five unitriangular group has a full three-dimensional target subgroup. The exact prescribed-root distribution refines previously known image membership and reveals a distinct concentration on the group center and at the identity: the nonidentity central fiber is asymptotically \(2q\) times the generic noncentral fiber, while the identity fiber is asymptotically \(5q\) times the generic fiber. This supplies a complete, nonuniform word-map invariant on a natural infinite family rather than an arbitrary finite slice.

No claim is made about arbitrary unitriangular sizes, about other prime-characteristic group cube maps, or about independent review. Earlier implicit calculations remain possible.
