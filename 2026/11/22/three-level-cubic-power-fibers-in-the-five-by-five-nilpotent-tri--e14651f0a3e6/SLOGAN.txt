The cubic-power map on strictly upper-triangular \(5\times5\) matrices over every finite field is onto its three-dimensional cube ideal, with exactly three prescribed-root fiber sizes distinguished by zero, nonzero central, and noncentral targets. In characteristic three the same law counts cube roots in \(UT_5(\mathbb F_q)\), giving \(\Pr(g^3=I)=5q^{-2}-5q^{-3}+q^{-5}\).
