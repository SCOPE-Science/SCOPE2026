For every odd finite field, the ordered noncommuting invertible solutions of \(X^2+Y^2=cI_2\) have exactly three fiber sizes, depending only on whether \(c\) is zero, a nonzero square, or a nonsquare. Noncommutativity forces both matrices to be traceless, yielding the exact counts \(q^2(q-1)(q^2-1)\) at zero and \(q(q^2-1)(q(q-2)-2\chi(c))\) at every nonzero scalar target.
