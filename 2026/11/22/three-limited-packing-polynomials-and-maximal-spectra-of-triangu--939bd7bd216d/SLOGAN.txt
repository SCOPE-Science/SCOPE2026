For every \(n\ge3\), all three-limited packings of the triangular graph \(L(K_n)\) are matchings with at most one small path or triangle, except for isolated three-stars; this gives explicit full and inclusion-maximal cardinality polynomials. Their largest size is \(\lceil n/2\rceil+1\), but maximal packings of size three persist at every order, making the packing complex nonpure for all \(n\ge5\).
