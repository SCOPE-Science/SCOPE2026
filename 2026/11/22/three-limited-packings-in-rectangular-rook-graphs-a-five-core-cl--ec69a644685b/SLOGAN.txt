Every closed-neighborhood three-limited packing of a rectangular rook graph is a one-shore two-star forest, an isolated three-star, or a matching augmented by one three-edge path, four-edge path, or four-cycle. This gives its full labelled counting polynomial and the exact maximum \(L_3(K_m\square K_n)=\max\{s+2,\min(2s,\ell)\}\), where \(s=\min(m,n)\) and \(\ell=\max(m,n)\).
