# Scientific review: three-scale chord spectrum of star signed-chain polytopes

**Correctness — PASS.** Every vertex is classified by a constructive slice decomposition and active-normal rank. The ordered-pair distance polynomial is an exact product of one-leaf enumerators; subtracting the diagonal and dividing by two gives the unordered spectrum. Conditional law-of-large-numbers and central-limit arguments establish all three atom weights and normal variances. Exact independently expanded finite coefficients match direct chord enumeration through eight leaves, including 786,420 ordered pairs; coefficient and extreme-shell checks extend through 100 leaves. The infinite-family conclusion is proved symbolically, not inferred from experiments.

**Originality — PASS, provisional historical priority.** Beck–Hlavacek give the signed-chain construction, and a book-graph norming-face record gives precisely the three-layer combinatorial vertex structure. Earlier exact signed-chain volume, book-graph continuous interior covariance, and general difference-body diameter results were compared by implication: they do not determine coefficientwise discrete pair distances or the three-scale Gaussian mixture. published-finding corpus meaning searches included chord-distance, book-graph and signed-chain aliases, and no exact covering polynomial was found. This absence does not establish worldwide priority; an unindexed two-point distance enumerator remains a risk.

**Value — PASS.** A complete Euclidean chord spectrum is a natural global metric invariant, substantially richer than a diameter or a vertex count. The three disjoint limiting scales show that uniform vertices are metrically nonconcentrating at order \(m\), while three conditional Gaussian laws describe second-order fluctuations. The sharp second shell is exact finite information. The object, coordinate normalization and novelty boundary are independently motivated by published signed-chain/book-graph polytope geometry.

**Scope and residual risks.** Results are for the anchored coordinate Euclidean metric, not the zero-sum graph metric; no generalization to other signed posets is claimed. Previously published diameter, vertex count and volume are credited rather than restated as novel. A differently indexed general chord-enumerator could be stronger. Full primary definitions and highly relevant recorded face and covariance statements were read; not every historical publication is accessible.
