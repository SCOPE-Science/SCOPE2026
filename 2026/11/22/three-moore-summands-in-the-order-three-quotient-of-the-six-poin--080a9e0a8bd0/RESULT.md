# Three Moore summands in the order-three quotient of the six-point partition complex

## Finding
Let \(\overline{\Pi}_6\) be the poset of set partitions of \(\{0,1,2,3,4,5\}\), excluding discrete and indiscrete partitions, ordered by refinement. Let \(X=|\Delta(\overline{\Pi}_6)|\), and let \(G=\langle r^2\rangle\cong C_3\), where \(r(i)=i+1\pmod 6\). For the ordinary geometric quotient, the complete integral homotopy type is
\[
 X/G\simeq\bigvee^{3}M(\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z,2)\vee\bigvee^{38}S^3.
\]
Here \(M(\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z,2)=S^2\cup_3 e^3\) is the degree-three Moore space. In particular, \(\pi_1(X/G)=1\), \(H_2(X/G;\mathbb Z)\cong(\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z)^3\), and \(H_3(X/G;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^{38}\); all remaining positive-degree integral homology vanishes.

## Assumptions and scope
The action consists of simultaneous permutations by \(r^2=(0\ 2\ 4)(1\ 3\ 5)\). It is an order-three subgroup of the regular six-cycle, not the full order-six action, not the full dihedral group, and not a free action. The chain complex is the strict-chain order complex; every chain is oriented by its strictly increasing partition ranks. The quotient is the ordinary geometric orbit space, not a homotopy-orbit construction.

## Proof
**Simple connectivity.** The proper-part partition complex on six labels is homotopy equivalent to a wedge of \(5!=120\) three-spheres by the classical geometric-lattice shellability theorem, hence is simply connected. The partition \(\{\{0,2,4\},\{1,3,5\}\}\) is fixed by the generator \(r^2\) (in fact, each block is preserved). Armstrong's orbit-space fundamental-group theorem then gives \(\pi_1(X/G)=G/N=1\), since the normal subgroup generated by fixed-point elements is all of \(G\).

**Integral orbit cells.** A strict partition chain cannot contain two vertices of the same rank. An automorphism preserving the chain setwise must therefore fix each vertex, so the action has no simplicial inversions. Its orbit space is thus a finite three-dimensional CW complex with cells represented by orbits of strict chains, and cellular boundary
\[
 \partial[p_0<\cdots<p_d]_G=\sum_{j=0}^d(-1)^j[p_0<\cdots<\widehat p_j<\cdots<p_d]_G.
\]
No division by stabilizer orders is made. Exhaustive restricted-growth enumeration produces 201 proper partitions and 9,011 strict chains, with original degree-wise simplex counts \((201,1865,4245,2700)\). Under this order-three action there are exactly \((71,623,1415,900)\) orbit cells in degrees zero through three, hence Euler characteristic \(-37\). The included standard-library verifier reconstructs all cells and all signed incidences and verifies \(\partial^2=0\).

**Exact integer homology.** The verifier performs 1,482 admissible unit-pivot chain cancellations. These are integral chain-homotopy equivalences; they do not invert three or other nonunits. The remaining free chain groups have ranks \((1,0,3,41)\); the sole nonzero differential is a \(3\times41\) integer matrix \(A\). The greatest common divisors of all minors of size one, two, and three are, respectively, \(3\), \(9\), and \(27\). Consequently its Smith invariant factors are exactly \((3,3,3)\), and it has rank three. Thus \(H_2\cong(\mathbb Z/3)^3\), \(H_3\cong\mathbb Z^{41-3}=\mathbb Z^{38}\), with the other homology groups as stated. The Euler characteristic independently agrees: \(1-38=-37\).

**Homotopy splitting.** Since the quotient is simply connected and at most three-dimensional, its two-skeleton is a simply connected two-complex, homotopy equivalent to a wedge of two-spheres. Attaching maps for the three-cells are therefore determined by their integer degree matrix on \(\pi_2\). Integral row and column basis changes realize its Smith normal form; nonzero degree-three attachments give three degree-two Moore summands, and the kernel contributes thirty-eight free three-sphere summands. Whitehead's theorem gives the displayed homotopy equivalence. This splitting is not asserted to be canonical or equivariant.

Run `python3 verify.py` in the same directory. It uses only the Python standard library, enumerates the 201 partitions, all 9,011 strict chains, all three group elements, every orbit boundary and its square, then performs a deterministic complete sequence of 1,482 integral unit cancellations. It checks the 3-by-41 residual differential by exact gcd-of-minors formulas and prints `PASS_PARTITION_LATTICE_SIX_ORDER_THREE_QUOTIENT_H2_Z3_CUBED_H3_Z38` if all tests pass. Its finite proof checks the integer homology; the simply connectedness and three-dimensional homotopy classification follow from the separate mathematical arguments above.

## Relationship to prior work
Donau (2012), Proposition 4.2, treats transitive, **free** prime-cycle actions on partition complexes with a prime number of labels; the present order-three action has two orbits on six labels and fixed vertices, so those hypotheses do not apply. Donau's 2010 fixed-label subgroup theorem likewise does not apply because this subgroup has no globally fixed label. A published calculation for the **full** six-cycle establishes two order-three Moore summands and twenty three-spheres for \(X/C_6\), not the intermediate quotient \(X/\langle r^2\rangle\). These exact source/parameter differences do not alone prove novelty, but the three-torsion multiplicity here is not implied by either preceding classification. The cyclic subgroup passage is nontrivial: the order-three quotient has **three** independent degree-two torsion classes while the full order-six quotient has only two.

## Limitations
The conclusion concerns only this specific order-three subgroup on the ordinary six-label partition complex. It does not claim an all-parameter subgroup formula, an equivariant integral splitting, or a canonical basis for the torsion. The mathematical audit presented here is same-model only, without independent external validation. A further unpublished or unindexed subgroup computation could establish earlier priority.
