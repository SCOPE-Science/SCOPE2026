The order-three subgroup \(\langle(0\ 2\ 4)(1\ 3\ 5)\rangle\) acting on the proper six-point partition complex has a simply connected quotient homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^3 M(\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z,2)\vee\bigvee^{38}S^3\). Its three independent integral three-torsion classes in degree two differ from the two surviving under the full six-cycle quotient.
