Exactly two Nyström landmarks on the screened Laplacian inverse of \(K_{2,n}\) switch between same-small-shore and same-large-shore selection; starting at \(n=6\), a genuine interval of cross-shore optimal pairs appears between them. Every transition, tie and optimizer multiplicity has a closed algebraic description, and the cross-shore interval approaches \((0,\sqrt2)\) as \(n\) grows.
