The perfect-matching extendibility complex of the nonperiodic \(4\times6\) square grid is 2-connected and has reduced integral homology \(\mathbb Z\), \(\mathbb Z^7\), and \(\mathbb Z\) in degrees \(3,4,5\), respectively, with no other reduced homology. A complete acyclic discrete-Morse matching and exact unit integral boundary prove this three-degree spectrum without asserting a homotopy wedge decomposition.
