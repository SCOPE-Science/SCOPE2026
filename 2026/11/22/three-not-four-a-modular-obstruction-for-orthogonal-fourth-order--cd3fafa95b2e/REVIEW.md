## Correctness — PASS

The exact full-space kernel is \(e^{\alpha z\bar w}L_3^{(1)}(\alpha|z-w|^2)\), whose three distinct positive zeros produce three pairwise-orthogonal points on an equilateral triangle. All six squared interpoint distances in a hypothetical four-point design must be roots of the irreducible cubic \(P(x)=x^3-12x^2+36x-24\). Its roots lie in one cyclic cubic field and reduce to \(29,40,49\) modulo \(53\). The determinant of three anchored planar displacement vectors must vanish, whereas an exhaustive complete enumeration of all \(729\) possible six-tuples gives a nonzero Cayley–Menger determinant. The packaged checker verifies the cyclic identities, the complete finite-field reduction, and equality of independently formed determinants. The analytic assumptions, the exact root set, the finite cardinality bound, and the distinction between full and true spaces are explicit. No finite numerical approximation substitutes for an infinite theorem; the finite residue space is proved exhaustive.
