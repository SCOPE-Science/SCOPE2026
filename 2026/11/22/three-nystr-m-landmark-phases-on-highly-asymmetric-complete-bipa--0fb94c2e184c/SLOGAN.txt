For two noiseless Nyström landmarks on a screened unweighted complete bipartite network with fixed smaller part size \(p\ge3\) and sufficiently large larger part, the optimal pair switches exactly twice: same smaller part, one in each part, then same larger part. The sharp screening transitions converge to \(\sqrt{p-1}-1\) and \(\sqrt p\), and explicit quartic tests certify the three-phase classification for individual finite networks.
