Three-color tie-retaining plurality on every friendship graph freezes within three synchronous updates, with a sharp third-update threshold at five triangles. The exact equilibrium count is asymptotic to \(3\cdot5^m\sqrt{5/(4\pi m)}\), while for uniform random initial states the first-fixation time converges to one and two updates with probabilities \(1/3\) and \(2/3\).
