For two equal-noise observations of a stable planar Jordan shear, the unique D-, A-, and E-optimal times all begin at zero but end at three ordered clocks: \(1/a\), \(c/a\), and \(z_{s/a}/a\), clipped by the horizon, where \(c=1.10885755\ldots\) and \(z_{s/a}>c\) increases strictly with shear. The E-optimal clock has a unique exact scalar equation and grows as \(\log(s/a)+\frac12\log\log(s/a)+o(1)\) in damping-normalized time at high shear.
