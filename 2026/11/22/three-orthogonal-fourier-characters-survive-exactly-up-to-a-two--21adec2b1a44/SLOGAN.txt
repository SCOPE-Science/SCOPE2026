A four-atom tetrahedral measure with masses \((a,a,b,b)\) supports three mutually orthogonal Fourier characters exactly when the two masses differ by at most a factor of two, and such a triple can always be taken at frequencies \(0,\xi,2\xi\). The maximum is four only for equal masses, and otherwise drops to two beyond the sharp \(2{:}1\) imbalance threshold.
