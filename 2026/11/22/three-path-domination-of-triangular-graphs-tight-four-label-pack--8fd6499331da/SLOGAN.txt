On the triangular graph \(J(n,2)\), the minimum number of dominating three-vertex paths is \(\lceil(n-1)/4\rceil\). At every order \(n=4k+1\), all optimum path collections are exactly disjoint four-label star-or-path blocks with one unused ground label; there are \((4k+1)!/k!\) of them, and their whole-path exchange graph is connected and \(119k\)-regular.
