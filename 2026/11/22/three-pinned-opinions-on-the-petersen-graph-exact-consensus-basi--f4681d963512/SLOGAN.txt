On the Petersen graph, exactly twenty triples of permanently one-valued vertices force unanimous consensus under synchronous strict-majority updating: the three pinned vertices must form a three-edge star when Petersen vertices are regarded as edges of \(K_5\). The other three pinned shapes have exact consensus counts \(98\), \(124\), and \(94\) out of \(128\), while star pins force all \(128\) within a sharp three updates.
