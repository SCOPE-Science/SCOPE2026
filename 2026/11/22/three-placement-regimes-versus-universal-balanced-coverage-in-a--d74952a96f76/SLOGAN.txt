Under independent Poisson effective-visit counts decaying exponentially with distance, the location maximizing the **expected number** of two crop units served passes sharply from midpoint to two interior sites to the crop endpoints as the source intensity declines. The location maximizing the **probability that both** are served is always uniquely the midpoint; the complete three-phase thresholds are \(Ae^{-kd}=1\) and \(Ae^{-kd}=kd/\sinh(kd)\).
