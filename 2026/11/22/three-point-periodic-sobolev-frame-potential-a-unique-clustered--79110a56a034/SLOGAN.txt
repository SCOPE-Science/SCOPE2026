For three equal-weight point-evaluation vectors in the mean-zero periodic \(H^1\) space, the frame potential is minimized uniquely by a *clustered* triple with cyclic gaps \((1/6,1/6,2/3)\), not by equally spaced sites. The sharp unnormalized value is \(5/216\), and the optimized placement halves the total squared off-diagonal correlations relative to uniform spacing.
