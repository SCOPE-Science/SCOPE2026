# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

A nonempty face contains at most one color per layer, so no such face can be invariant and no geometric point is fixed. The ordinary quotient is a finite CW complex and agrees up to homotopy with the Borel construction. Thapper Proposition 3.2 gives the three stated covering homotopy types, with d≥2 for m≥4. For a single odd sphere the top rank-one module is trivial, and the two rows are copies of Z[u]/(3u). If the transgression of the top generator were zero, u-linearity would leave unbounded bottom-row cohomology. It is therefore a unit mod 3, killing the positive-column top row and the high bottom row, with kernel 3Z on the top generator. For the pair of even spheres the Lefschetz trace of a generator on the top is −1, forcing characteristic polynomial T²+T+1, zero norm, no invariants and determinant of g−1 equal to 3. The integral periodic resolution gives Z/3 exactly in odd positive columns of the top row, where multiplication by u gives the period isomorphism. The same finite-dimensionality argument forces the differential out of column 1 to be an isomorphism there and in all odd columns. Thus in the even case only H^0 and the bottom even classes through degree d survive; in the odd case the bottom even classes through degree d−1 and top Z survive. There is at most one surviving filtration piece per total degree. The integral UCT recovers the specified groups and no others, including the asserted torsion orders. Independent Smith-form computations for m=4,5,6, using layer subsets with the first selected color normalized to zero, gave respectively only H1=Z/3; H1=Z/3 and H3=Z; and H1=Z/3 and H3=Z in positive degrees, as predicted.

## originality

PASS

The supported distinct contribution is the complete higher integral homology, notably the top/high-degree behavior in the two-sphere residue compared with the two single-sphere residues. The covering homotopy types, fundamental group, H1 and the standard low-degree group-homology mechanism are not separate originality claims. Considered analogues give related ladders or methods on other spaces, not the full stated classification.

## value

PASS

The simultaneous color quotient of this standard infinite family is natural, and the entire quotient integral homology is useful beyond the previously identified π1 or cover Betti numbers. It identifies the distinction between lens-space homology and rational acyclicity with arbitrarily long integral torsion ladders, including the terminal and high-degree information not supplied by low-degree group homology alone. The use of standard tools does not reduce the complete even-residue computation to a change of parameters in an inspected result.

The dated certificate retains the supplied scientific assessment, sources and limitations.
