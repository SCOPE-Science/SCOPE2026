For every triangular cylinder \(P_m\square C_3\) with \(m\ge4\), quotienting its independence complex by simultaneous cyclic relabeling of the three colors produces exactly one \(\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z\) homology class in each odd degree below a linearly growing cutoff. The quotient has a free top homology class precisely when \(m\not\equiv1\pmod3\), although every unquotiented complex has torsion-free homology.
