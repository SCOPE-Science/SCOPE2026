For Choudhry's Brahmagupta elliptic curve \(Y^2=X^3+4t^4X+4t^2\), all 54 geometric minimum-height sections admit explicit formulas in three packets of eight eighth-root choices, three exceptional polynomial sections, and their negatives. Their entire coefficient splitting field has a \(2\)-group Galois group, a special arithmetic collapse from the generic \(W(E_6)\) behavior.
