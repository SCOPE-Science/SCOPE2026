In the class-two radical brace \(tM_m(\mathbb F_q)[t]/(t^3)\), the exact left and two-sided principal-unit orbit spectra are \(N_{m,r}(q)q^{m(m-r)}\) and \(N_{m,r}(q)q^{(m-r)^2}\). At fixed field order, a uniform element has asymptotically full leading rank, a uniform left orbit has a half-rank discrete-Gaussian law, but a uniform two-sided orbit has an asymptotically uniform rank on the entire interval.
