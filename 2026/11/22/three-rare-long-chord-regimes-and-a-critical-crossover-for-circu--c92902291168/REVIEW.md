# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The unoriented line coordinates give invariant measure dα dt. Its mass on cap-hitting lines is the integral of projected width, namely the perimeter 2 arccos h+2√(1−h²), with no extra factor for orientation. Reflection permits integration on α≥0 with a factor of two. The intersection of the disk interval [−u,u], u=√(1−t²), with the cap half-line gives the frozen exact length formula. In particular for s>0 the exact event at 0<α<π/2 is u≥s/2 and t cos α+(u−s) sin α≥h: the first inequality leaves room for a segment of length s and the second tests its lower endpoint when placed against the upper disk endpoint. For h≤0 the horizontal diameter survives, with all surviving disk diameters at |sin α|≤−h. For h>0, writing each allowed height as cos θ with θ≤arccos h bounds the separation by 2 sin((θ1+θ2)/2)≤2√(1−h²), with equality only at the two opposite base endpoints. For fixed negative h set t=b√ε in the exact event: almost everywhere it tends to |b|≤1 and sin α<−h in the positive-angle half, giving unnormalized 4 arcsin(−h)√ε. For fixed positive h let r=√(1−h²) and s=2r−ε. Compactness of chord endpoints and unique diametral pair force t→h and α→0 uniformly among qualifying lines. Hence s−u≥r/2 eventually. The exact endpoint test gives t≥h and (r/2)sin α≤t−h, while the first inequality yields t≤√(1−s²/4)=h+O(ε). Thus t=h+εb and α=εa are uniformly bounded in scaled coordinates. The two limiting inequalities are b≤r/(2h) and b≥ra for a≥0, with convergence off their zero-area boundaries. The doubled area is r/(4h²). For h=λ√ε the first inequality instead bounds |t| by √ε(B+o(1)), B=√(1+(max{λ,0})²), and s−u≥1/2 makes α=O(√ε) by the exact endpoint test. The scaled tests tend almost everywhere to |b|≤B and 0≤a≤b−λ. The doubled area is 2∫_{−B}^B(b−λ)_+ db, giving all three stated pieces and agreement at −1 and 0; λ=0 gives the fixed semicircle constant. Dominated convergence applies on bounded scaled regions and P_h→π+2 in this last case. These exact tests include truncated chords, so no assumption excluding them is needed to close the limit argument.
