For three independent, imperfect early-warning receivers with equal short footprints on a uniformly exposed circular fault, the globally best geometry switches from separated to a maximally double-covered arrangement to coincident as the required fraction of two-confirmation actions increases. The exact transition fractions are \(1/2\) and \((2-p)/(4-3p)\); in the middle regime, three spatially distinct staggered receivers can attain the optimum.
