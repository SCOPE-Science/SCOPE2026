For optimal polynomial inversion at a repeated boundary zero \((1-z)^m\), \(m\ge2\), the residual converges uniformly off that zero on the closed disk throughout \(\alpha<1\), with sharp, explicitly different boundary profiles at \(\alpha<0\), \(\alpha=0\), and \(0<\alpha<1\). Negative weights produce a terminal oscillatory \(n^{-1}\) leak, positive weights produce a polylogarithmic \(n^{-(1-\alpha)}\) profile, and the Hardy threshold contains both endpoints.
