For two equal acoustic recording windows required to each capture a call from one stationary Erlang-renewal caller, one phase makes the intervening gap irrelevant, two phases favor unbounded separation, but three phases admit a unique finite optimal gap. That gap has an explicit arctangent formula, shrinks strictly with recording-window length, and stays between \(2\pi/(3\sqrt3\lambda)\) and \(2\pi/(\sqrt3\lambda)\).
