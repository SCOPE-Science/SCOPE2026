For perfect-square gap shifts under a strong penalty \(L\) on markers, the globally optimal marker-frequency free-energy stability coefficient has the sharp expansion \(C_L^2=1/(2L)+e^{-3L/2}/(2L^2)+o(e^{-3L/2}/L^2)\), whereas the exact infinitesimal coefficient decays as \(\sqrt{2\pi}e^{-L}\). The global extremizers have marker density \(1/2-(3/4)e^{-3L/2}(1+o(1))\), contrasting with the equilibrium density \((\pi/2)e^{-2L}(1+o(1))\).
