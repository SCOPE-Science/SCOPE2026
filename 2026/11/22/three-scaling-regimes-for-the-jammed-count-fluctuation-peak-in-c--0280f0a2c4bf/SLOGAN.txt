In charge-selective random sequential adsorption, the gap length of maximal terminal-count variance approaches the two-rod packing threshold on three different scales as interaction strength tends to its singular endpoint: exponential, \(\sqrt{t\log(1/t)/12}\) at critical composition, or a positive constant, where \(t=1-\lambda\). The sharp composition boundary is determined by whether the probability of at least one attractive contact exceeds, equals, or falls below \(1/2\).
