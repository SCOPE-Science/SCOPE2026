# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The claim has a fixed eight-vertex domain, a spectral definition valid for every positive exponent, and explicit four Walsh-distance entries. The proof establishes strict positivity for the subordinated range, uses a stated generalized-polynomial sign-variation root bound to exhaustively count distance-two and distance-three zeros, checks the double root at exponent two, and proves a chain of strict inequalities between the three positive characteristic times. The Lambert-scale intermediate root follows from the exact root equation and a matching two-sided asymptotic bound. The standalone checker independently computes the matrix exponential through a spectral decomposition, with 8,640 entry comparisons and 11 root-family checks passed. Finite computations are corroborative; the proof is analytic. Residual risks include the correctness of interpreting positive multiplicities in the generalized Descartes bound and numerical cancellation at extremely small times; the former is a standard mathematical theorem and the latter does not enter the proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The primary 2014 fractional-network paper by Riascos and Mateos studies the subunit Markovian range and does not state superunit-exponent signs on the three-cube; the relevant author-supplied full text was inspected. Benzi and Simunec's accessible full article explicitly restricts its fractional graph-heat applications to the subunit range. A published complete-multipartite exact fractional-heat result does not cover the four-eigenvalue, diameter-three cube. Published Radau hypercube sign results concern a different rational evolution map. A published sharp spectral-window result optimizes across generators, and does not imply a fixed-generator exact sign classification. The accessible own historical claims include a four-path sign reentry, but its asymptotic scales lack the cube's Lambert-enhanced intermediate phase and have different eigenvalues. Actual research-record searches for hypercube fractional heat, sign reentry, Krawtchouk entry roots, and spectral-window barriers did not return a dominating result. This is a best-knowledge literature comparison, not a proof of priority; unindexed equivalences and inaccessible full text remain risks.

## Value — PASS
The three-dimensional Boolean cube is a natural smallest nontrivial Cartesian power of a two-point graph with Hamming distance three and four spectral levels. For the exact semigroup, not a numerical time step, the theorem identifies the change from one negative-to-positive opposite-corner crossing to a positive-negative-positive reentry at exponent two. It also proves a *three-scale* separation and a nonobvious Lambert correction to the distance-two recovery, directly quantifying competing propagation channels. These features are not a restatement of qualitative eventual positivity, a sampling table or merely a renamed four-vertex-path calculation. The statement is intentionally restricted to the cube and does not claim a dimension-uniform extension.
