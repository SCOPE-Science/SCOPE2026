On the eight-vertex Boolean cube, exact fractional heat \(e^{-tL^\alpha}\) undergoes an opposite-corner sign-reentry transition precisely at exponent \(\alpha=2\), even though the whole matrix recovers positivity at one sharp later time. For large exponents, the initial sign loss, distance-two recovery and final global recovery occur at three separated scales, \(\log(3/2)/4^\alpha\), \(W(2^\alpha)/4^\alpha\), and \((\log3)/2^\alpha\).
