For every \(n\ge4\), the complete perfect double Italian domination polynomial of the matched two-clique prism \(K_n\square K_2\) has an eight-term closed form and exactly \(\binom n3^2+n^3\) minimum-weight labelings, all of weight \(6\). For \(n\ge5\), its attainable weights fall into precisely three separated integer bands, with two gaps whose lengths grow linearly with \(n\).
