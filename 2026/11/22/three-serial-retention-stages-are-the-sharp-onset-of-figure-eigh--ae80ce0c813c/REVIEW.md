# Scientific evaluation: sharp three-stage figure-eight retention window

## Correctness — PASS

The proposed claim is a classification of a uniquely defined periodic orbit of stable constant-coefficient scalar ODE stages. Squaring the positive sinusoidal input gives exactly zero-, first-, and second-harmonic forcing. The linear transfer functions therefore give all output phases without approximations. The equal-discharge requirement reduces every possible interior self-intersection to one paired rising/falling phase, and the sign of their difference is a linear function of discharge, proving the one-crossing maximum. For one and two stages, the exact second-to-first sine-response ratio is strictly smaller than two, while positive forcing amplitude is strictly below the mean; no crossing follows at any frequency and any positive stage rates. For three equal stages the sine coefficients are explicit, an exact signed rational derivative proves a single bounded frequency interval, and orthogonality plus exact quadrature proves cancellation of full signed area with nonzero individual lobes. The attached standalone checker verifies the polynomial and rational identities with exact arithmetic and gives supplementary numerical tests. The universal statement rests on the proof, not on finite sampling.

Boundary and scope checks: positive forcing avoids quadratic-source nonmonotonicity; one and two stages allow unequal rates; the exact three-stage window requires equal rates; at either threshold no *interior transverse* crossing is present. The zero-total-area example remains a transverse figure-eight at the internal frequency, with each lobe positive. Stable periodic response, not a finite transient, is what is classified.

## Originality — PASS on inspected literature, with residual priority risk

General sediment-discharge figure-eight loops are **already known** and are not claimed as new: Zhang et al. (2021) reproduce such patterns with a sediment-availability–transport model, and the review DOI 10.1029/2024WR037216 expressly catalogs them. Hassan et al. (2023) examine sediment-feed timing and bedload hysteresis under a single experimental hydrograph; Waite et al. (2023) study event-wise concentration-discharge hysteresis in a natural river. Their physical claims do not give this exact positive-quadratic serial-filter minimality, unique finite frequency window, or cancellation of opposite nonzero lobes. Zeman et al. (2025) use distributed time scales and higher-harmonic effects for elastic hysteresis, a mathematically neighboring yet different material model; no matching stage-count law appeared in the sections inspected. Published mathematical records about Nash-cascade runoff peak splitting concern temporal peak counts under pulse forcing, not a self-crossing against the sinusoidal input. Known Fourier and Lissajous identities can recover intermediate steps, so no claim is made for those methods. Exact historical priority of the specialized joint classification remains provisional: broad harmonic-response theory or an uninspected paper might imply it.

A separate scientific-research index was searched by explicit claims, synonyms, frequency windows, aliases and parent problems. Closest results discussed stormwater wash-off ordering, three-stage hydrograph double peaks, and four-stage pulse peak resolution. These do not imply the present statement; they study time-domain crest counting or scheduling rather than the paired-branch phase difference. ## Value — PASS

Figure-eight loops are a recognized environmental-response phenomenon but can indicate several distinct mechanisms, making a sharp *minimal* mechanism useful. Here the instantaneous source is single and strictly increasing, so the two-lobed loop is generated entirely by retentive phase filtering rather than a changing source sign or separate source pulses. The frequency interval and lower-stage impossibility supply a falsifiable structural benchmark for reduced models. The explicit equal-and-opposite lobe formula is a concrete warning that a global signed hysteresis index can vanish despite an observable figure-eight. No field inference, empirical validation, or automatic identification of environmental mechanisms is claimed.

## Limitations and verification

The system is a deliberately reduced periodic ODE model. The source follows an imposed quadratic law, all three rates are equal for the positive window, and the input is sinusoidal rather than a real isolated hydrograph.
