With normalized weighted Bergman evaluations on one circle and a budget of \(N+1\) positive-weight samples, the unique minimum-average-variance design is a regular polygon at a uniquely determined radius. That radius obeys three sharp regimes as the disk weight changes: constant-over-degree inward displacement for negative weight exponent, double-logarithmic displacement at exponent zero, and logarithmic displacement for positive exponent; the optimal mean-square recovery risk has three corresponding growth laws.
