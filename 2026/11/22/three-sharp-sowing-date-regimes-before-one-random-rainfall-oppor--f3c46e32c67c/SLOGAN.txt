For three seed cohorts exposed to a shared exponentially timed first rain and independent exponentially fading viability, the uniquely optimal sowing calendar has one, then two, then three distinct dates as establishment probability increases past the exact thresholds \(p_1=(8-\sqrt{10})/9\) and \(p_2=[3+\sqrt{9+48p_1(1-p_1)^2}]/8\). This is an idealized three-regime restoration benchmark, not a field-calibrated planting recommendation.
