Equality of both directional tail Kendall coefficients forces exchange symmetry for one- and two-shock positive max-linear laws, but a three-shock model has \(\tau_{X\to Y}=\tau_{Y\to X}=16/25\) despite a provably asymmetric extreme-value exponent. The blindness persists for some nonexchangeable max-stable laws with strictly positive real-analytic spectral densities.
