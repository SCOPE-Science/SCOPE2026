For every \(n\ge5\), the multipath complex of the cyclically oriented complete tripartite digraph with shore sizes \((1,2,n)\) is exactly a wedge of \(n^3-6n^2+8n-1\) three-spheres. The proof uses integral four-cycle generation in a crown graph to show that two families of cone attachments kill all second homology, rather than extrapolating from finite calculations.
