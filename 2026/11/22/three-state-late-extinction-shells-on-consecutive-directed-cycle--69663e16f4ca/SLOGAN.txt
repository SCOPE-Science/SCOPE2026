On two consecutive-length directed cycles sharing one hub, synchronous Boolean AND has an exact late-extinction law: in the last \(a\) updates before the sharp bound \(E=a(a+1)\), precisely three labelled initial configurations die at each step except the final step, at which exactly one dies. At time \(E-k\), the complete surviving initial-zero polynomial is \((2k-1)z+(k-1)z^2\), for every \(1\le k\le a\).
