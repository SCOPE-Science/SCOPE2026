For four equal-magnitude Fourier coefficients supported at an affine-independent four-point simplex over \(\mathbb F_p^d\), with odd prime \(p\ge5\), the Fourier zero count is exactly one of \(0\), \(p^{d-2}\), or \((2p-1)p^{d-3}\). Its zero set is respectively empty, one codimension-two affine flat, or two such flats meeting in a codimension-three flat; the sharp case occurs precisely for two feasible antipodal coefficient pairings.
