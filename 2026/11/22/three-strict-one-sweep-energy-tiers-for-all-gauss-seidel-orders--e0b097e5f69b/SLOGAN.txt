For the screened four-cycle \(A=I-t\operatorname{Adj}(C_4)\), all 24 Gauss–Seidel permutations fall into three strictly ordered worst-case one-sweep energy tiers: interlaced best, cyclic intermediate, red–black worst, uniformly for \(0<t<1/2\). The interlaced and red–black orders nevertheless tie at the optimal asymptotic spectral radius \(4t^2\), while red–black can suffer almost twice the optimal one-sweep squared-energy factor.
