When two environmental-DNA sampling methods have taxon-specific positive Poisson capture rates, one or two present taxa cannot make their expected-richness ranking reverse twice as effort increases, but three can. For rates \((2,3,11)\) versus \((1,6,8)\), the preferred method changes exactly at \(\tfrac12\log\rho\) and \(\log\rho\), where \(\rho^3=\rho+1\); the total rates are intentionally unequal.
