For common-phase binomial carry indicators, the expected number of denominators up to \(N\) with every carry zero has an exact finite Möbius--harmonic formula and a three-term asymptotic beginning \((3/\pi^2)(\log N)^2\). The underlying Farey-extremum geometry is classical; the additional result quantifies the all-index mean through its explicit constant term with a \(O((\log N)^2/N)\) remainder.
