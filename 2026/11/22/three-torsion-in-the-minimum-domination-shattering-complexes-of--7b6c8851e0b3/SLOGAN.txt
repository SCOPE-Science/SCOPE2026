The minimum ordinary dominating sets of a square rook graph shatter exactly those cell supports that have distinct rows or distinct columns, giving an explicit complete face polynomial and a union of two partition-matroid complexes. The \(5\times5\) shattering complex has \(\widetilde H_3\cong\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z\) and \(\widetilde H_4\cong\mathbb Z^{2104}\), and every square order at least eight has nonzero \(3\)-torsion one degree below the top; these torsion facts are transferred from established chessboard-complex theorems.
