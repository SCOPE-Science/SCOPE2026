## originality

PASS

The distinct supported contribution is the uniform first-three tritangent-count discrimination on this S8 subfamily and its sharp prefix length. The geometric labeling, the possibility of obtaining profiles from existing exceptional-curve tables, and the existence of geometric witnesses are not separate originality claims. In particular Karras's prior work gives stronger realization coverage than q>1456, and its rows already supply the lower-bound collision after accounting for the Bertini pairs. Neither that table's classification of the Weyl types nor the different-observable inverse results inspected give the sufficiency of this particular short tritangent prefix: the comparison of those three projected counts across all 22 types is the key remaining result.
