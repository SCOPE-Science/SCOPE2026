# Scientific review

## Correctness (PASS)

The construction of the branch sextic and the Bertini-pair description of its \(120\) tritangent planes are grounded in the cited primary geometric sources. All four families of index sets are equivariant under relabeling of the eight blown-up points. The fixed-subset formula follows directly by splitting invariant one-, two-, and three-subsets into full Frobenius cycles. The saved standalone checker creates all \(22\) permutations and \(120\) indices, computes their exact orbits, independently cross-checks the closed formula through the full permutation period, and proves injectivity by exhaustive finite enumeration. The geometric sharpness claim is supported independently by the invariant general-position polynomial and finite-field nonvanishing argument; no sample enumeration is used to prove it. Residual limitation: the field-size threshold is sufficient rather than sharp, and the general-position hypothesis is essential.

## Originality (PASS; qualified)

The source geometric \(8+28+56+28\) decomposition, Bertini correspondence, tritangent count and real involution restrictions are known. The distinct claim evaluated here is that the first *three finite-extension tritangent counts* recover every one of the \(22\) Frobenius splitting classes, and that two cannot do so for actual examples over a common sufficiently large field. The examined full geometric construction, two primary articles, and available published research search records did not present this uniform arithmetic inversion. An implicit or unindexed symmetric-group permutation-character calculation remains possible; this is an acknowledged originality risk rather than evidence of uniqueness.

## Value (PASS)

The tritangent configuration is a canonical finite étale arithmetic invariant of a smooth genus-four branch sextic. The theorem states exactly which information from a marked eight-point blow-up can be extracted from short extension-field counts, while also proving a sharp universal cutoff and realizability of the obstruction. It supplies a complete 22-class reconstruction dictionary and derived tritangent scheme zeta functions, rather than a routine single-instance point count. It is restricted to a natural geometrically marked subfamily and does not imply recovery of the full curve zeta function.

## Closest literature and limits

Kulkarni–Ren–Sayyary Namin–Sturmfels (2017), Celik–Kulkarni–Ren–Sayyary Namin (2018), and the MathRepo construction establish the geometric tritangent indexing; Agostini et al. develop a related geometric reconstruction method. Existing finite-field studies of genus-four tritangents on distinct models and isolated degree-one del Pezzo Frobenius examples are not a general inversion over all \(22\) partitions. The residual risk is a less-visible precomputed cycle-index table or an equivalent corollary in the geometric literature. Finite verification does not constitute an independent audit of the general-position descent argument.
