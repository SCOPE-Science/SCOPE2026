The fifteen-vertex line graph of the Petersen graph has exactly \(110\) minimum locating-total dominating codes, and every one selects three disjoint two-edge paths in the original graph while leaving one vertex isolated. Unlike its weaker ordinary locating-dominating codes, these six-edge optima form a connected single-edge-replacement graph with exactly \(210\) edges, radius \(6\), and diameter \(7\).
