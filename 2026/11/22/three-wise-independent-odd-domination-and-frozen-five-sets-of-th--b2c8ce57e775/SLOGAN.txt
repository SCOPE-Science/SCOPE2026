The eighteen-vertex Pappus graph has exactly \(128\) odd-dominating sets, whose weights have enumerator \(18z^5+92z^9+18z^{13}\) and whose membership bits are uniformly three-wise independent. Its eighteen five-vertex optima are rigid under one- or two-vertex swaps but become connected under three-vertex replacements, forming the graph of distance-three pairs in the Pappus graph.
