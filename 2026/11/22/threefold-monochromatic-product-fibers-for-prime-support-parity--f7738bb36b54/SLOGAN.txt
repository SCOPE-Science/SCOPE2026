For the canonical two-coloring \(\chi(n)=(-1)^{\omega(n)}\), every positive integer except \(1\), primes, prime squares, squarefree semiprimes, and squarefree four-prime products is the product of three nonunits of the same color. The exact number of ordered representations is an explicit product-form expression in the prime exponents, and the exceptional count is asymptotic to \(X(\log\log X)^3/(6\log X)\).
