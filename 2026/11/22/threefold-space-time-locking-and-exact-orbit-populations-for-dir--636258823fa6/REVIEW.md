# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The factorization \(M^3-I=(S^s-I)C\), with the invertible commuting matrix \(C\), yields exactly the spatial fixed subspaces at all times \(3\cdot2^j\). The local-ring reduction excludes other nonzero periods. Finite-field coordinate-cycle counting and differences of nested fixed populations establish the entire claimed distribution. The independent local-update checker exhausts 525,348 binary states across 15 size/stride cases; the proof, not the finite tests, covers all dyadic lengths and fields \(\mathbb F_{2^f}\).

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence
The closest sources are Castronuovo et al. (arXiv:2611.16746v1), the symmetric-neighbor wave census, the threefold sunlet wave, and the dyadic binary cat-map temporal census. The first proves entropy but no finite-ring temporal classification. The symmetric wave has global even-ring period \(n\), the sunlet theorem acts on a different four-component graph wave and gives different populations, and the cat map has a half-spatial-period rather than a threefold period. None of their inspected statements implies the arbitrary-stride directed Fibonacci rule. Earlier generic matrix methods and historical equivalence remain real priority risks; Macfarlane (2004) full text was not directly inspected.

It provides a natural finite-ring counterpart to recent entropy results and reveals that distinct infinite-line entropies can coexist with exactly identical finite-ring orbit spectra.
