On every power-of-two cyclic ring over a characteristic-two field, the directed Fibonacci cellular automaton \(T_s(u,v)=(S^su+v,u)\) has least temporal period exactly three times the joint spatial period under \(S^s\), except for the zero state. The entire period spectrum and orbit census depend only on \(\gcd(n,s)\); if the stride is nontrivial, exactly \(1-q^{-n}\) of all configurations attain the maximum temporal period.
