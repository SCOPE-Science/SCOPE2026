A Brownian walker that is intermittently reflected and contracted, \(x\mapsto-qx\), has an exact mean and an exact probability of crossing its target during a reset; the latter is exponentially small as \(q\downarrow0\). At the pure-reflection endpoint \(q=1\), arbitrarily fast reset events instead leave a finite mean capture time \(L^2/(2D)\), whereas every strict contraction has an exponentially divergent strong-reset mean.
