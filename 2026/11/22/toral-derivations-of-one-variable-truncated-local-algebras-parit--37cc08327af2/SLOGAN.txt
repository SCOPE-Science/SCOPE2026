Every composition-idempotent ordinary derivation of \(k[t]/(t^N)\), for \(N\ge3\), vanishes outside characteristic two; in characteristic two the nonzero solutions form an affine \(\lfloor N/2\rfloor\)-parameter family, all of rank \(\lfloor N/2\rfloor\). Over \(\mathbb F_q\) the total is exactly \(1+q^{\lfloor N/2\rfloor}\), while even truncation length introduces a separate orbit of derivations that do not preserve the maximal ideal.
