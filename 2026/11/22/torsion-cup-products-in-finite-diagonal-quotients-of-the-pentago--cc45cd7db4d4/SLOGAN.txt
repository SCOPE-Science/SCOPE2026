For every integer \(m\ge2\), the finite diagonal scalar quotient of the complex pentagonal moment-angle manifold has \(\pi_1\cong\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z\), with integral cohomology torsion \(\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z\) exactly in degrees two and six. Its degree-six torsion generator is a nonzero product of two primitive free integral degree-three classes.
