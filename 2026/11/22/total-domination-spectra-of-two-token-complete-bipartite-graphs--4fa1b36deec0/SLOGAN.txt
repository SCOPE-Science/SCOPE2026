The total-domination and inclusion-minimal total-domination polynomials of every two-token complete bipartite graph \(F_2(K_{r,t})\), \(2\le r\le t\), factor into an independent constrained-incidence polynomial and an edge-cover polynomial. The minimal sets admit a spanning-star-forest classification, giving \(\gamma_t=t-1+\lceil r/2\rceil\) and a sharp upper total-domination number \(\Gamma_t=2t-2\) for \(r\le3\), or \(\Gamma_t=r+2t-5\) for \(r\ge4\).
