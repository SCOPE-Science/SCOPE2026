The contiguous \(010\)-frequency in Brik’s binary word is transcendental: it equals \(2-2\sum_{i\ge1}a_i2^{-i}\), where \(a_i=1\) precisely when either \(b_i=1\) or \(b_ib_{i+1}b_{i+2}=010\). The proof combines an exact trigram-count recurrence with a sliding-block stammering argument and nonperiodicity of the coded digits.
