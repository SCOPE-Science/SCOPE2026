In the edge-extremal five-cycle blowups \(C_5(1,1,1,a,b)\), all minimum paired-dominating four-sets are exactly the bases of an explicit rank-four transversal matroid. They number \(a^2b+ab^2+a^2+ab+b^2\), and their single-vertex exchange graph is isometric in the Johnson graph, with diameter \(\min\{4,a+b-1\}\).
