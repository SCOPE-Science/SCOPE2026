For a quadratic hidden-attractor flow, the unbounded axial trajectory has a sharp transverse stability threshold at \(k=a/c\): generic linearized perturbations grow exponentially below it, decay exponentially above it, and decay by the exact power \((bt-z_0)^{-c^2/b}\) at equality. The critical threshold is also where the two finite equilibria disappear to infinity, while a one-dimensional transverse mode contracts at Gaussian-in-time speed for every \(k>0\).
