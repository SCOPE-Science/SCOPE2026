# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof is universal and integral: the three strip sizes each strongly collapse under an explicit leaf-induction, the complete cyclic three-window cover has contractible nonempty intersections, and its nerve collapses to a circle. The inclusion map is established using a primitive cellular square-boundary subgroup and a simplicial projection-section giving the surviving cyclic generator. The one-vertex tree at \(n=6\) yields the octahedral two-sphere and proves the sharp uniform boundary. Twenty exact small-instance face and binary chain checks independently corroborate, but do not substitute for, the unbounded argument. The argument explicitly assumes graph-vertex shortest-path distance and inclusive scale two.

## Originality — PASS against inspected evidence
Relevant primary mathematical comparisons are by implication, not title. The metric-gluing arXiv:1712.06224 discussion concerns metric wedge/path gluings and a different product metric; its results do not imply the tree-cylinder simplicial-window construction. Published records `4061be6b33be` and `ccd7a360a668` cover two-layer prisms, a specialization \(T=K_2\); `c8f696aa7300` covers clique fibers, not branching trees; `e28e5da49d34` covers clique fibers over cycles; the toroidal case `2502.07134` has cycles in both factors; `20dd298096a9` is the Čech rather than Rips complex. Full results of the three closest published records and primary literature sections were inspected. Published-result and literature queries found no theorem subsuming arbitrary tree fibers together with the precise integral square-persistence kernel. This remains a bounded novelty assessment, not evidence that no unindexed equivalent exists.

## Value — PASS
Arbitrary finite branched tree fibers form a natural infinite-dimensional parameter family, with unbounded vertex counts and nonisomorphic branching patterns. The finding gives a complete sharp graph-metric radius-two homotopy classification plus a geometric, integral, primitive description of all transient first-homology generators; neither follows merely from the known two-layer prism nor complete-clique-fiber results. It identifies a threshold that can be tested and used to understand persistence of structured graph products.

## Limits and residual risks
Only inclusive radius two, finite tree factors, and long cycles are established; other graph products and higher radius are left open. An equivalent result under a different nerve/decomposition formulation could have escaped indexed search. The result has not received independent mathematical verification.
