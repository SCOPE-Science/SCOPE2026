For every finite tree \(T\) and \(n\ge7\), the inclusive radius-two graph-metric Vietoris–Rips complex of \(C_n\square T\) is integrally homotopy equivalent to \(S^1\). Exactly \(n(|V(T)|-1)\) primitive integral circle classes die from radius one to two, with a basis given by product-square boundaries, while the cycle-direction class survives.
