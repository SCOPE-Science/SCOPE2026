Strong products of finite paths have an exact two-regime triameter and a complete closed-form count of all maximizing vertex triples, determined by the largest two factor orders and their multiplicities. In uniform \(d\)-dimensional king grids with side order \(q\), the proportion of maximizing triples undergoes a sharp crossover at \(d\) of order \(q^2\), with limit \((1-e^{-2\lambda})^3\) when \(d/q^2\to\lambda\).
