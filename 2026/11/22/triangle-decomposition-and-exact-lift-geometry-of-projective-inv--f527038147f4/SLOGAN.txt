For odd prime-power \(q\), the involution commuting graph of \(\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbb F_q[\varepsilon]/\varepsilon^2)\) has precisely two degrees \(q(q-1)\) and \(q(q+1)\), and every edge lies in a unique triangle. Reduction fibers are independent \(q^2\)-sets linked by \(q\) disjoint copies of \(K_{q,q}\), giving exactly \(q^4(q^2-1)/6\) Klein four subgroups.
