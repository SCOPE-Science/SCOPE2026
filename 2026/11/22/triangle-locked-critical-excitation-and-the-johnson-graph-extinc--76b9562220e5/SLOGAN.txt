At rounded-half-degree contact threshold, triangles lock every persistent three-state excitation into a balanced rotating three-coloring: no longer-periodic waves survive on connected graphs where every vertex lies in a triangle. Consequently every Johnson graph \(J(n,k)\), \(n\ge2k\ge4\), extinguishes from every initial state except the octahedral graph \(J(4,2)\), which has exactly two primitive three-cycles.
