For the numerical semigroup \(\langle n,n+1\rangle\), every strict decrease in the ordered-decomposition count occurs exactly at \(nt+r\) with \(1\le r\le n-2\) and \(r+1\le t\le2r\), and has size \(2r+1-t\). The complete descent histogram is triangular: there are \(n-1-d\) drops of each size \(d\), for exactly \(\binom{n-1}{2}\) drops in total.
