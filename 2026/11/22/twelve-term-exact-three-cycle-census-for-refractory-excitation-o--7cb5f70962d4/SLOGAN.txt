On every three-row rook graph \(K_3\square K_n\), threshold-one three-state Greenberg–Hastings excitation has an exact twelve-exponential formula for the number of configurations already in primitive three-cycles. Their fraction is \(1-9(2/3)^n+O((14/27)^n)\), and dividing the exact count by three gives the number of distinct temporal orbits.
