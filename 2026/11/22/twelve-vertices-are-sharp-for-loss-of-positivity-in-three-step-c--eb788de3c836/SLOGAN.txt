For the exact-spectrum degree-three Chebyshev approximate inverse of \(\mu I+L_T\), every unweighted tree on at most eleven vertices is entrywise positivity-safe for all \(\mu>0\), and twelve vertices are sharp: a five-leaf-per-hub double star has a negative bridge entry when \(0<\mu<6-3\sqrt{14}/2\). The complete sign mechanism is a local edge-degree test, while the sharp smallest-tree bound follows from double-star spectral comparison rather than an exhaustive graph census.
