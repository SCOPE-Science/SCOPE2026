For the smooth reciprocal-octic genus-three Howe family over \(\mathbb F_{11}\), the 91 coefficient-marked curves realize exactly 28 Jacobian Weil polynomials. Of these, 20 occur only when the two reciprocal quartic factors are defined over \(\mathbb F_{121}\) as a Galois-conjugate pair, rather than both over \(\mathbb F_{11}\); the sharp marked-family rational-point maximum is 24.
