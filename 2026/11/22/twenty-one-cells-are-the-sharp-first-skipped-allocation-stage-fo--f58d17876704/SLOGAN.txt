For independent ignitions on a path protected by two perfect firebreaks, \(21\) cells are the sharp first size where a canonical three-compartment layout is skipped entirely: \((6,6,9)\) is never optimal, while the globally optimal layout jumps directly from \((5,5,11)\) to \((7,7,7)\) at an exact algebraic ignition threshold. Every canonical layout for \(3\le n\le20\) is nevertheless uniquely optimal at some ignition risk.
