# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The gradient has no projective zero, so the 27-line theorem applies. I enumerated all 357 reduced-row-echelon two-dimensional F4 subspaces independently, retained exactly 27 lines on the cubic, and checked the 15 fixed lines and six skew Frobenius pairs. In particular I found a six-line skew configuration in which Frobenius fixes the first four and swaps the last two. Blowing these down geometrically gives a plane class H and exceptional basis E1,…,E6; Frobenius fixes H since it fixes −K=3H−∑Ei and the sum of Ei in a torsion-free lattice. The fixed integral lattice is spanned by H,E1,…,E4,E5+E6, with Gram diagonal (1,−1,−1,−1,−1,−2). It descends integrally since the Brauer obstruction over a finite field is zero. The effective cone is generated by the orbit sums by averaging the geometric line cone, and these classes are represented by ground-field effective divisors. Their negative squares and nonnegative pairwise intersections force all 21 to be extremal. In independent blowdown coordinates the generators span dimension six and are positive against the ample anticanonical divisor. Thus the dual extremal rays are exactly normals to supporting facets, each containing five linearly independent generators. Exhausting all 20,349 five-subsets by exact signed minors, in integral divisor coordinates, verifies 51 primitive nef rays and the reported 15/30/6 intersection profiles. Direct primitivity in the fixed basis is essential; normalization of a dual functional alone would not give the Cartier generator.

## originality

PASS

The line-orbit geometry, fixed rank/discriminant and integral Picard basis of this model are covered by prior results. In particular the 21 effective rays follow directly from the known skew-pair orbit structure and the standard line cone by averaging and negative intersections; I do not credit that datum as an independent novelty. The supported distinct contribution is the precise arithmetic nef census, including the count of primitive (2,4) rays, which requires identifying the actual facets and lattice normals. No inspected statement or table does that for the Frobenius type in question. The decision is best-of-knowledge, not a priority guarantee.

## value

PASS

A full exact arithmetic nef cone on the natural maximally symmetric, space-filling binary cubic describes the possible boundary nef directions over its ground field. The split geometry or fixed line count alone is not that invariant. The six primitive square-two, anticanonical-degree-four rays are particularly useful to distinguish the arithmetic boundary from counts based only on rational lines or on a different binary twist. The independently useful result is the complete finite nef census and its primitive profiles, rather than recomputation of the Picard basis.
