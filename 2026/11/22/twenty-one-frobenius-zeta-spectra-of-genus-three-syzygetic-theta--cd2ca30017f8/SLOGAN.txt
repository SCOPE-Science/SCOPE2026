The \(315\) syzygetic theta tetrads of a hyperelliptic genus-three curve yield precisely \(21\) Frobenius zeta spectra across the \(22\) eight-branch splitting patterns, with exactly one permanent ambiguity between \((1,1,1,1,4)\) and \((2,2,4)\). Counts of rational tetrads over the first five extensions recover the entire tetrad zeta function, and five is optimal.
