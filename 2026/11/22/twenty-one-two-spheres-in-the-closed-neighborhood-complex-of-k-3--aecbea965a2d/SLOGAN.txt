The closed-neighborhood complex of the twelve-vertex graph \(K_3\square C_4\) is integrally homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{21}S^2\), with no fundamental-group or integral torsion obstruction. The four-cycle is the first sharp boundary outside a published girth-at-least-five clique-fiber Čech classification, where the longer-cycle complexes retain a nontrivial circle.
