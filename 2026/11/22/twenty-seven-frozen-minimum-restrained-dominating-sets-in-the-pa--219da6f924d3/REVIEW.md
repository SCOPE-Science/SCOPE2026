# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The claim's graph, selection predicate, and move definitions are explicit. A two-shore geometric counting argument excludes all five-vertex restrained sets and explains the ordinary optimum exactly. Independent Python bitmask and C++17 recursive-subset programs were compiled/executed and agree on all 262,144 subsets, all 207 minima and full cardinality distribution. The Python program additionally constructs the group and verifies five disjoint orbits covering the entire optimum list. No extrapolation from a partial scan is used.

Related indexed Pappus semitotal and locating-total results have different predicates, despite sharing the ambient graph and some structural motifs. ## Value — PASS
The Pappus graph is the unique cubic symmetric graph on eighteen vertices and the classical Levi graph of a nine-point/line configuration. Restrained domination requires outside redundancy absent in ordinary domination. The computation resolves a genuine invariant gap (five versus six), completely classifies the optimum placements by geometric symmetry, and identifies a sharp and nontrivial frozen/mobile reconfiguration dichotomy with distinct jump/slide diameters. The 27 frozen configurations are characterized by a structural induced-matching property rather than an arbitrary index filter. These facts are not a renaming of the general cubic bound.

The orbit and mobility census is a finite verified result, not a theorem about all regular cubic graphs. Historic priority is not guaranteed, and the scientific review does not constitute independent validation.
