# Scientific scope and evidence

The enumeration covers every labelled subset and each pair of minimum restrained dominating sets. It distinguishes induced shapes by the degrees in the induced subgraph, not only by the number of induced edges. The two three-edge orbits are of different types: nine induced perfect matchings and eighteen induced claws with two additional isolated vertices. Both orbits are frozen under both move rules. The 180 remaining minima form a single component under each rule; their different jump and slide diameters are exact.

The result is a full restrained optimizer and reconfiguration classification for one specified graph. General restrained-domination bounds do not give it. The nine matching states coincide with known efficient open dominating configurations whose immobility was already known under the weaker ordinary predicate. The eighteen claw sets can be recovered from the known ordinary five-set classification. Neither comparison classifies the remaining restrained optima, their mobility, or the full restrained polynomial. The ordinary domination number and group order are not priority claims.
