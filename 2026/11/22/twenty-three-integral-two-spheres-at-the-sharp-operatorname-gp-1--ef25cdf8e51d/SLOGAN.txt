At the sharp radius-two boundary of the generalized Petersen family, \(\operatorname{VR}(\operatorname{GP}(18,3);2)\) is integrally a wedge of exactly 23 two-spheres. Three additional inner hexagons kill the circle that survives for every \(n\ge19\), and a paired-hexagon disk argument gives the complete homotopy equivalence, strengthening the previously known mod-two Betti numbers.
