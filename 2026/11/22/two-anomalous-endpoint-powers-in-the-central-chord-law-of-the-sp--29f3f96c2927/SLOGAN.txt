For a Haar-uniform center-passing line through the standard sphericon, chord lengths range from \(\sqrt2\) to \(2\) times the generating radius. The probabilities of lying within normalized distance \(\varepsilon\) of the shortest and longest chords have sharp, distinct powers \(\varepsilon^{3/4}\) and \(\varepsilon^2\), with explicit gamma-function and rational-\(\pi\) coefficients and conditional uniform tangent-geometry laws.
