## correctness

PASS

The two half-cones are interchanged by swapping x,y and reversing z, and both are invariant under independent horizontal sign changes; the centroid is therefore the origin. Homogeneity gives one reciprocal support-denominator per ray; if U3<0 the order of the two terms is exchanged, leaving the asserted sum unchanged. The two hemispheres and four sign choices give the projected density 2/(pi sqrt(1−x²−y²)). Extremizers of each reciprocal term yield exactly four diagonal equatorial minima and six coordinate-axis maxima. In an azimuth/height chart about a minimum the first-order displacements of the two absolute coordinates from 1/sqrt(2) are (±phi−z²/2)/sqrt(2); since f'=0 and f''=sqrt(2), the cross term cancels and the normalized chord excess is (phi²+z⁴/4)/sqrt(2)+o(phi²+z⁴). Area is uniform in azimuth and height, and the rescaled set has area 2^(7/8) B(1/4,3/2). Around a vertical maximum the length deficit is |x|+|y| to first order; around a horizontal maximum it is the transverse radius plus the absolute transverse horizontal coordinate. The associated unit sublevel areas are 2 and 4/3, respectively (the latter from the half-angle substitution in the polar area integral). Summing over poles yields 7/(3 pi) and class weights 3/7 and 4/7. For both endpoints compactness first confines the event to the extremal charts; coercivity of the leading forms bounds the scaled supports. Their level-one boundaries have planar measure zero, so the rescaled densities converge in L1 by dominated convergence, including after normalization. This establishes the conditional total-variation assertions and the powers 3/4 and 2 with the exact constants.
