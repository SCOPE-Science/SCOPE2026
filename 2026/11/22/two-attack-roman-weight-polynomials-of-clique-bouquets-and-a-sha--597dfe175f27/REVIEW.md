# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The domain is the universal-vertex join of at least two complete petals of order at least two, with numerical vertex labels \(0,1,2\) and one- or two-zero-vertex attack sets. The proof partitions all functions by the center label; each petal's allowed states are derived directly from the two-defender union-of-neighborhoods requirement. The only globally coupled cases are a single weak zero with the center labeled \(2\), and zero or one defender with the center labeled \(0\). Their exact exclusions produce the explicit polynomial. The minimum formulas follow from initial degrees and coefficients of the petal polynomials. In particular the exceptional triangular two-petal coefficient \(63=27+36\) is separately derived. The embedded `verify_attack.py` enumerates 215,298 ternary assignments across ten small heterogeneous and homogeneous graphs, checking every coefficient; an independent C++ base-three direct enumerator confirms minimum counts through a thirteen-vertex example. There are no unproved infinite-computation inferences: the finite tests corroborate the all-orders combinatorial proof.

## Originality — PASS (provisional literature priority)
The relevant initiating preprint (Koch–Shank, arXiv:2305.16256, first public 25 May 2023) has been inspected in accessible full text, including definitions, general structural results, optimization machinery, graph-family results and references. It does not contain a clique-bouquet coefficient formula or optimizer count. Full accessible proofs were also checked for ordinary, weak, and total Roman domination weight polynomials of generalized/heterogeneous windmills (public published-finding corpus records `60695442a75b`, `e0942c187585`, `5959e7c34d61`). They use different feasibility predicates. For example, center label \(2\) in ordinary Roman domination allows two different peripheral zeros with no other defenders, whereas this violates two-attack feasibility. Thus even same-graph, same-label-alphabet formulas do not imply this polynomial. Direct searches for two-attack Roman / `2aRD` under the aliases friendship graph, generalized windmill, clique bouquet and join of complete graphs found no covering formula. These searches are evidence of no identified overlap, not proof of universal priority. Unindexed equivalent literature remains possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The all-weight formula covers every number of petals and every nontrivial heterogeneous petal profile, a natural block-graph family and a natural simultaneous-defense invariant. The two-attack restriction creates genuine cross-petal coupling not present in ordinary Roman domination. In the homogeneous subfamily the minimum and complete optimizer geometry change qualitatively between petals of orders two, three and at least four, with an exceptional 36-function family only when two triangular petals are present. This structural classification and sharp phase change supply information beyond a renamed known scalar domination number, and the exact coefficient formula can be used to analyze weighted defense configurations and sampling without exponential graph enumeration.
