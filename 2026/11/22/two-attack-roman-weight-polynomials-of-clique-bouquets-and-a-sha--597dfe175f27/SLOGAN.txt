For every bouquet of at least two nontrivial cliques meeting at a universal center, all two-attack Roman defense labelings have an explicit product-and-correction weight polynomial. In the homogeneous family \(K_1\vee(qK_s)\), both the optimum weight and the number of optima jump sharply with petal order, with \(63\) minimum functions at the exceptional triangular two-petal bouquet.
