An open two-parameter family of geometrically spaced signed three-Cauchy windows has an exact critical-density Gabor frame diagram consisting of two explicit forbidden residue bands, with three stable phases when the bands separate. The first failure boundary is an interior double Zak zero that splits into two zeros at a sharp square-root rate; in one exact instance the bands are \([8/\sqrt{21},62/21]\) and \([92/19,308/19]\).
