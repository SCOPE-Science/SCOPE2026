## Value — PASS

The theorem isolates a meaningful structural issue: strictly convex depth-cost optimization does not ensure a single-peaked or monotone response of the selected refuge depth to changes in water transparency. It provides a rigorous two-band counterexample and an all-parameter one-band obstruction, so a single-spectral-channel simplification can exclude qualitatively possible sensitivity curves. This is a mathematically motivated boundary on interpretation of light-versus-depth survey models rather than a claim of observed multi-peak behavior. The large optical-rate ratio \(20\) is deliberately illustrative and needs no empirical authority to support the mathematical statement.
