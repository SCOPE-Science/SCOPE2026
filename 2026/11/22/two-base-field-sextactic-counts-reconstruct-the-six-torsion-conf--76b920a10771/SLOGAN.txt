For a smooth plane cubic over a finite field of characteristic at least five with a rational flex, the numbers of rational sextactic points and rational conics through six sextactic points, together with the known field residue modulo three, determine the complete Frobenius zeta functions of its sextactic points, trisecants, and six-secant conics. An exhaustive 24-class torsion calculation yields exactly 19 joint spectra, identified by those two base-field counts.
