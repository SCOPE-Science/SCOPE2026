# Scientific review: two-bond noncancellation on square toroidal cluster states

## Correctness — PASS
The sole accepted theorem is the exact \(S_m(F)=2m^2-2m+\nu(F)\) checkerboard entropy formula for every integer \(m\ge2\) and every edge set \(|F|\le2\) in the ideal qubit graph state on \(C_{2m}\square C_{2m}\). The graph-state stabilizer formula identifies entropy with binary cut rank and gives the entire flat Schmidt spectrum. The original cut matrix factors into two diagonal binary differences on the even sublattice. Their orbit intersections have cardinality two, establishing kernel dimension \(2m\). The key new lemma separates *every pair* of coordinate evaluations on both left and right kernels, including the exceptional diagonal displacement \((m,m)\). An elementary rank-two update lemma then gives the exact two-bit gain for disjoint edges; the rank-one update lemma gives one bit for incident edges. An independent, direct biadjacency construction and bit-elimination checker passed 36,480 two-edge instances (all pairs through \(m=5\), additional samples through \(m=8\)); it also verified the separate nonsquare cancellation example. Finite checking is not used as an all-size proof.

## Originality — PASS on examined evidence, provisional
Hein, Eisert and Briegel (2003) and the comprehensive Hein et al. review (2006) establish the general entanglement-as-cut-rank identity and bounds under edge edits; those are explicitly credited, not claimed as new. The closely relevant publicly accessible *Commensurability deficit and single-bond entanglement gain in periodic square cluster states* was read in full, including its proofs and limitations: it proves the base rank and **one** omitted bond, not the pair classification. The accessible *Paired omitted-bond cancellation in three-dimensional toroidal cluster states* was also read in full: it shows a two-defect cancellation in a different three-dimensional family, not the present square-torus noncancellation. The new assertion needs the additional kernel-functional separation lemma. Targeted semantic database queries for square-torus two-gate omissions, rank-two updates, graph-state entropy and cancellation returned these closest results but no equivalent or dominating theorem. Remaining risk: an unindexed equivalent in older binary square-grid and modular linear cellular-automaton literature; this has not been independently ruled out.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A precise two-gate interference classification for an infinite canonical lattice family is natural for studying ideal cluster-state preparation changes. It is not implied by the sign or size of individual one-gate responses: in a non-square two-dimensional periodic grid, two vertex-disjoint omissions can cancel their separate gains, and a published three-dimensional example has the same qualitative cancellation. The complete square-torus result distinguishes the two fundamentally different gate-pair geometries without imposing spacing or directional restrictions, and the proof identifies the structural invariant responsible for that rigidity. The claim concerns exact ideal-state entanglement, not device fault tolerance, noise, or algorithmic advantage.

## Limitations and references
The theorem is restricted to two or fewer missing edges on even square simple toroidal grids, a fixed checkerboard partition, and pure qubit graph states. The nonsquare example is an independently checked finite boundary case, not a classification of rectangles. Historical priority remains provisional.
