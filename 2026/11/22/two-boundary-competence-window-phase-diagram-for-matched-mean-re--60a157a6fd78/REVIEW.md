# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The result holds for every real Gamma shape greater than one, every nonnegative competence onset, and every positive window width. Two density crossings follow from the unimodal Gamma-to-exponential density ratio. Their signed cumulative difference has exactly one interior zero. Two monotone equal-height companion maps yield the lower and upper interval-probability crossing curves, including all boundary equalities. The high-shape expansion follows from Gamma concentration and the central limit theorem. The checker independently passed 59,007 finite assertions; general validity is analytical, not based on enumeration.

## Originality — PASS, provisionally

The inspected full texts of Siegel et al. (2003), Cetina-Heredia and Connolly (2011), and Figueiredo et al. (2013) motivate larval competence and residence but do not give the matched-mean exponential-versus-Gamma, all-window two-boundary classification or its large-stage critical-width limit. Publicly available mathematical findings addressing Gaussian-versus-exponential centered mass, reflected Gamma-density asymmetry, and Erlang-residence spectra concern different comparisons and do not imply the stated result without additional analysis. A 2010 article by Connolly and Baird was available by abstract only, and older stochastic-order literature may contain equivalent machinery; priority is provisional.

## Value — PASS

The result settles a natural complete boundary problem for a competence-timed *export* indicator when two otherwise mean-matched residence assumptions are compared. It exhibits a genuine negative–positive–negative ordering for short competence periods, a sharp disappearance of one boundary at a uniquely defined window, monotone threshold movement, and a large-stage quantitative limit. The exact signs cannot be inferred from the common mean residence time. The result is an idealized modeling benchmark, not a calibrated prediction of actual settlement or recruitment.

## Limitations

Competence onset and duration are deterministic, residence distributions are specified rather than estimated, and export while competent does not imply viable settlement. Historical priority and applicability to site-specific marine ecology require further independent review.
