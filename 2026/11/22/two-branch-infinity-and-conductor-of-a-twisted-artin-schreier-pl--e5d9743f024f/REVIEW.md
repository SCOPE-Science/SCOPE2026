# Mathematical review

**Correctness — PASS (same-model).** The argument constructs the two normalization places in separate local charts, including the Newton ramification at \(u=\infty\) and the formal implicit-function branch at \(u=0\). In the plane coordinates \(a=u/v\), \(b=1/v\), it obtains orders \((A,B)\) at the cusp and \((p,1)\) at the smooth branch, with \(A=d-p-1\), \(B=d-1\), \(\gcd(A,B)=1\), and \(p\nmid A\). These yield the semigroup, contact, delta and conductor. The plane-genus calculation agrees independently with the previously proved normalization genus; the standalone checker passed ten parameter pairs and exhaustive modest-degree gap tests. The tests are finite checks and do not replace the proof.

**Originality — PASS, with qualification.** The initiating paper proves genus, maximality and ramification of the degree-\(p\) map, not the embedded singularity of the ordinary plane closure. Related published calculations already give two omitted normalization points, and a distinct **one-point pole** semigroup, but neither claim implies the displayed **plane-branch** semigroup and conductor without further local analysis. Searches for this equation, its aliases, branch-pair parameters, and exact contact/conductor formulas found no equivalent result. Older general theorems on plane branches supply methods, and unindexed local computations remain a priority risk.

**Value — PASS.** The named plane model is the explicit one used for new high-genus maximal curves with trivial automorphism group. Exact singular-branch values, contact multiplicity and conductor orders specify how the two normalization points are glued, accounting for an enormous arithmetic-genus discrepancy; this is natural embedded geometry needed for adjunction and plane-model computations, not a restatement of the known smooth-model genus or a recomputed point table. The coefficient-independent theorem also identifies precisely which data depend only on the degree.

**Closest literature and limitations.** Bartoli et al., arXiv:2610.35788v1, Theorem 1.3(ii) and Proposition 2.1; *The Weierstrass semigroup of a non-Galois twisted Artin--Schreier cover* (published mathematical record); *First two point counts of prime-field maximal curves with trivial automorphism group* (published mathematical record). The theorem does not address smaller degrees, degree congruent to one modulo characteristic, other plane embeddings, full resolution graphs, or algebraic priority beyond the inspected sources.
