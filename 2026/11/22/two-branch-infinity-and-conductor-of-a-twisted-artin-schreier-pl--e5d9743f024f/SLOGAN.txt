The ordinary degree-\(d\) plane closure of \(uv^p+v=f(u)\), for \(d>p+2\) and \(p\nmid(d-1)\), has one boundary singularity where a smooth branch meets a cusp of semigroup \(\langle d-p-1,d-1\rangle\) with contact \(d-p-1\). This gives explicit branchwise normalization conductors and the complete genus defect for the new prime-field maximal-curve models.
