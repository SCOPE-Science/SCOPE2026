Over two disjoint individually named chains in the generic partial order, the number \(I\) of ordered incomparable-pair types depends only on the unary-type count \(N\): \(I=N(2N+1)/3\). The complete pair spectrum is a bivariate quartic polynomial, and the fraction of comparable pair types is always greater than \(1/2\) but tends to \(1/2\) as the total number of parameters grows.
