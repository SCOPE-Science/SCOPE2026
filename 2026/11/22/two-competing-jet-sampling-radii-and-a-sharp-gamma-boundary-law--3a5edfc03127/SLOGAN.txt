For every standard weighted Bergman space and each normalized monomial of degree at least two, the sharp capture by one value-and-derivative jet is the larger of **two explicit radial peaks**, with no other possible optimizers. At high degree the inner peak is uniquely optimal, and the captured fraction decays as \(2c_\beta^{\beta+1}e^{-c_\beta}/[n\Gamma(\beta+1)]\), where \(c_\beta=\beta+1+\sqrt{\beta+1}\).
