For the Narayana-cows recurrence, the indices \(7t+2\) and \(7t+3\) form exact complementary residue transversals modulo every power of two: each odd and each even residue appears once, respectively, in a least-period block. Both transversals obey the sharp isometry \(\nu_2(a_{7t+j}-a_{7s+j})=1+\nu_2(t-s)\), and no other index class modulo seven does.
