Finite tests do not replace the all-field proof.

## Value: PASS

Geometric component classification is a standard substantive structural target for varieties of algebras. Here the two-product identities enforce a nontrivial decomposition into components of different dimensions; the proof also isolates their precise boundary orbit and gives a closed finite-field census. The findings are a natural complete answer for the three-dimensional nilpotent LSDA locus, not a parameter renaming or isolated small-field computation. They do not resolve the entire degeneration poset or higher-dimensional geometry.
