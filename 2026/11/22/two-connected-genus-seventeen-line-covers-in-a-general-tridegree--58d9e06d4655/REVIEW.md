# Review of the line-cover theorem

## Correctness (PASS)
The two ambient rulings exhaust the sixteen anticanonical lines, each resulting from a tangent ruling fiber of the genus-one branch curve. Their quadratic discriminants define smooth genus-nine curves. The splitting covers are the two-torsion torsors \(\eta=\mathcal O_B(1,1)\otimes r^*\mathcal O(-1)\); the key nontriviality step uses the eight-point transverse complete intersection \(Z=V(a,b)\), restriction and Koszul vanishing, and excludes a section of \(\mathcal O_B(1,1)\) vanishing at all eight points. Riemann–Hurwitz gives genus seventeen. An exact characteristic-101 witness certifies nonempty generic conditions, including the matching squarefree degree-24 branch polynomials. The local \(A_1\) classification and two-transposition monodromy follow geometrically. The finite-field computation is not claimed to prove the universal result in isolation.

## Originality (PASS, qualified)
The 2026 Dvorsky–Kaliman–Zaidenberg paper establishes the three degree-four del Pezzo fibrations and the 2026 Chachay paper addresses lines on a single del Pezzo surface; neither gives the two genus-seventeen relative covers. Lehmann–Tanimoto supply an analogous étale cover construction for a different degree-four del Pezzo fibration, with twelve critical values rather than twenty-four. Their general techniques do not imply the concrete genus-nine/genus-seventeen calculation for this family. Targeted mathematical-record and primary-literature comparisons found no equivalently strong prior statement. Historical priority is not formally certified.

## Value (PASS)
The theorem identifies the connected geometry, genera, exact degeneration count, and local monodromy for the moving set of all sixteen lines in a newly studied natural Fano family. Proving connectedness of each eight-sheeted family is not a formal count of sixteen lines and supplies useful concrete data for the relative Hilbert geometry of the fibration.

## Scope and risks
The branch divisor is general over \(\mathbf C\); exceptional degenerations and full global monodromy are not determined. Similar methods in older del Pezzo-fibration literature require caution about unindexed specializations. The claim has not received independent audit.
