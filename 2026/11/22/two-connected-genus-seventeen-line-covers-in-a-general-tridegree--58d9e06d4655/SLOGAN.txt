For a general tridegree-\((2,2,2)\) branched double cover of \((\mathbf P^1)^3\), the sixteen lines on a smooth degree-four del Pezzo fiber form exactly two connected eight-sheeted families, each a genus-seventeen curve étale over a genus-nine discriminant curve. Both line families have local monodromy of two disjoint transpositions over the same twenty-four nodal fibers.
