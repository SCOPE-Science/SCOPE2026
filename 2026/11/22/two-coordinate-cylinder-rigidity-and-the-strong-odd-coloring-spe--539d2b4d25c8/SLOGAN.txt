In the Clebsch graph, every strong odd color class is either a singleton or a four-vertex two-coordinate cylinder. Its entire unlabelled coloring polynomial is \(70z^4+360z^7+300z^{10}+40z^{13}+z^{16}\); the \(70\) optimal four-color partitions split into \(10\) uniform-coordinate and \(60\) mixed-coordinate configurations.
