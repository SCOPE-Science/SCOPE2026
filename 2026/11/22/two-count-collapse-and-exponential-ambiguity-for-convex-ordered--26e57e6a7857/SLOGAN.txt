For the homogeneous lexicographic order with convex equivalence classes, the entire injective tuple-type spectrum over a named finite base is \((1-z)^{1-n}/(1-2z)^{k+1}\): two numerical counts recover its size and number of represented classes, but exponentially many nonisomorphic bases share every count. A natural same-class channel recovers the unordered represented-class sizes from finitely many refined counts.
