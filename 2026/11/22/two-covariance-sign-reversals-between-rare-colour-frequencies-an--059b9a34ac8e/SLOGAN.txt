In a symmetric Pólya urn with any positive reinforcement concentration, the covariance of the number of colour changes with the number of colours seen exactly \(r\ge2\) times switches universally from negative to positive and back to negative as sample length grows. Its positive interval contains the exact length \(n=qr\), whereas singletons undergo only one sharp sign switch at \(n=2q+2/a\); all boundaries follow from an explicit quadratic identity.
