The graph of six-symbol palindrome codewords confusable after two arbitrary deletions has exactly \(q^2(q-1)(9q-8)/2\) edges over a \(q\)-symbol alphabet, with an explicit five-type degree spectrum. Its largest two-deletion-correcting code has quadratic order \(\Theta(q^2)\) as alphabet size grows, with explicit algebraic lower and elementary upper bounds; the exact capacity is not claimed.
