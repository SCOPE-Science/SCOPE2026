Reversing two vertex-disjoint arcs in a transitive tournament produces exactly four possible directed-flag homotopy types: a point, \(S^1\), \(S^3\), or \(S^1\vee S^2\), with explicit necessary-and-sufficient conditions determined by the order of the four endpoints and their gaps. This complete integral classification holds at every vertex count and makes the topology of sparse orientation faults transparent.
