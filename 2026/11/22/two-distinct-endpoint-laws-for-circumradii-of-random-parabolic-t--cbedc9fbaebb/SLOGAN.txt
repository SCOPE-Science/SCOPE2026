For triangles formed by three independent parameter-uniform points on a bounded parabola, the circumradius has exact endpoints and two sharply different rare-event laws: an interior-ellipsoid lower tail with two computable terms of orders \(\varepsilon^{3/2}\) and \(\varepsilon^{5/2}\), and a two-corner upper tail of order \(\varepsilon^3\). The exceptional vertex configurations converge conditionally to explicit uniform ellipsoid and simplex laws.
