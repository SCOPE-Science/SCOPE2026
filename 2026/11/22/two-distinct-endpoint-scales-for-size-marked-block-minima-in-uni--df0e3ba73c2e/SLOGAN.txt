For uniform random set partitions, the first size-\(r\) block minimum for every fixed \(r\ge2\) appears on scale \(n/W(n)^r\), but the last appears within distance \(n/W(n)\) of the right endpoint. The complete two-sided, fixed-size hierarchy converges to independent Poisson point processes, giving exponential left-gap and size-\(r\) Weibull right-gap limits.
