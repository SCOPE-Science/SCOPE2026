In the matching complex of \(C_3\square C_4\), deleting a triangle-direction edge loses eight primitive three-sphere classes, while deleting a square-direction edge leaves both Betti ranks at forty-four but makes the natural third-homology map have a primitive rank-two kernel and rank-two cokernel. Exact discrete-Morse reductions of both deletion links show that the latter edge fault has a mixed \((\bigvee^2S^3)\vee(\bigvee^2S^4)\) homotopy cofiber.
