Deleting any two distinct edges from the dodecahedral graph makes its perfect-matching complex torsion-free: adjacent edge faults yield a contractible complex, while disjoint faults yield \(S^2\), \(S^2\vee S^3\), or \(S^2\vee S^3\vee S^3\). An exhaustive eight-orbit integral Morse classification identifies exactly 60, 120, 240, and 15 edge pairs of these four types.
