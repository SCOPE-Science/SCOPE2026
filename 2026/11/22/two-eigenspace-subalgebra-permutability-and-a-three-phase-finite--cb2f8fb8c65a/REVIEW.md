# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The classification reduces arbitrary subalgebras to subspaces inside the abelian ideal or one-coset extensions by an invariant subspace. The mixed-pair condition is exactly invariance of the sum with the inside subspace. For two exterior subalgebras, the quotient-difference criterion counts the union of two eigenspaces with the zero vector subtracted once. Gaussian intersection numbers and constant-size coset fibers give the exact all-parameter formulas. The asymptotic proof compares the interior/exterior polynomial degrees, explicitly enumerates five dominant types at \((2,2)\), and four at \((2,3)\), and handles the symmetric case by the known scalar-shift identity. Direct independent RREF enumeration agrees with the formulas in five cases, including characteristic two and the genuinely new dimension-five case. No conclusion about an infinite family rests on those finite tests alone.
