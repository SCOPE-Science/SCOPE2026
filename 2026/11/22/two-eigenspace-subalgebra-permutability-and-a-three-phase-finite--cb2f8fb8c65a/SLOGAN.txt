For central stabilizations of scalar-action affine Lie algebras with noncentral and central dimensions both at least two, an exact two-parameter Gaussian census determines all ordered permuting subalgebra pairs over every finite field. The large-field permutability probability is precisely \(2/5\) in dimensions \((2,2)\), \(7/16\) in dimensions \((2,3)\) or \((3,2)\), and \(1\) for every other pair of dimensions in this range.
