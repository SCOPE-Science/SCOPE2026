For uniformly oriented calipers of the classical oloid, the probability of a width within \(\varepsilon\) of its minimum \(\sqrt2\) is \(2\sqrt2\,\varepsilon^2/\pi+O(\varepsilon^3)\), while within \(\varepsilon\) of its maximum \(3\) it is \(\varepsilon/2+\varepsilon^2/64+O(\varepsilon^3)\). The marked near-minimal directions converge to a uniform square with a fifty-fifty switching of generating-circle support, whereas near-maximal directions converge to a uniform disk.
