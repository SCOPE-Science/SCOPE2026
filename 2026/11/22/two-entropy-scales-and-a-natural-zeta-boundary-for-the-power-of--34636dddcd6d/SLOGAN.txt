The power-of-two marker interspersion of the even shift has an exact Artin--Mazur zeta function \(\zeta(z)=(1+z)/[(1-z-z^2)(1-W(z))]\), with \(W(z)=\sum_{j\ge0}(F_{2^j+3}-1)z^{2^j+1}\). Its entropy pole is at \(r\approx0.442108\), yet its meromorphic continuation has a second, impenetrable natural boundary at \(|z|=\varphi^{-1}\), the entropy scale of its even-shift background.
