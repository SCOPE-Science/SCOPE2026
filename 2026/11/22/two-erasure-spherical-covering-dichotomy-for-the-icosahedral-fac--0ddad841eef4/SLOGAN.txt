The union of a unit icosahedron's vertices and its twenty unit face normals has an exact two-erasure spherical covering dichotomy: among all 496 erased pairs, precisely the 60 vertex–incident-face pairs have a worse covering radius, \(\arccos(0.768858636428994\ldots)\), while every other pair has radius \(\arccos\sqrt{(5+2\sqrt5)/15}\). Each exceptional erasure has one unique deepest uncovered direction, certified by a new supporting quadrilateral.
