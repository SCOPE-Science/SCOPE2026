# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS
The exact inverse seed map and scalar lag-two recurrence are checked directly. Explicit invariant logarithmic bounds control the perturbation relative to the dominant square uniformly over the initial chamber. The convergent odd/even series yield additive \(o(1)\) errors in logarithms, hence give exact negative-axis and negative-bisector projective limits. Continuous seed dependence and the intermediate-value theorem produce both sharp transition ratios. Finite exact-arithmetic checks support the algebra; the infinite proof is analytic.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence
The earliest public source is Muni, arXiv:2406.08317v1 (2024-06-12), whose full accessible text states the same map and analyzes periodic points and bounded hyperchaotic attractors. The closest indexed same-map published finding, *Exact saddle-index transition of the unique two-cycle in a three-dimensional Hénon map*, addresses a bounded periodic orbit rather than two escape clocks. Another dimension-general generalized Hénon result concerns bounded orbits at a fixed-point fold. Broader Böttcher-coordinate work for polynomial skew products is not directly a theorem for this invertible cyclic 3D map. Relevant Hampton–Meiss articles were available at abstract level only; unindexed equivalent theory remains a publication-priority risk. This review does not claim exhaustive world-wide novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A known chaotic map is shown to possess a distinct, structurally organized infinity dynamics: two continuous parity escape heights with a tunable ratio, two sharp projective transition values, and exact bisectors at criticality. The critical geometry is motivated by the two-step square recurrence rather than an arbitrary imposed parameter slice. The result distinguishes bounded hyperchaos from real escape channels and can inform future compactifications.

## Limits
