On the Perrin indices \(7t+1\), every even residue modulo \(2^k\) occurs exactly once for \(0\le t<2^{k-1}\); on indices \(7t+3\), every odd residue does. Both sections satisfy the exact collision law \(\nu_2(P_{7u+j}-P_{7v+j})=1+\nu_2(u-v)\), and the full \(2\)-adic value closure is \(\mathbb Z_2\).
