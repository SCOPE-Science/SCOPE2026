For every positive mixed second/fourth-order diffusion strength on a four-site path, distance-two and endpoint-to-endpoint heat transitions each have one exact recovery time after an initial negative interval; their maximum is the complete entrywise-positivity threshold. The two crossings scale as \(2\eta\) and \(6\eta\) for weak mixing, while the positivity threshold decays as \(1.552676056635\ldots/\eta\) for strong mixing.
