In the strong-Allee predator–prey tipping model, an explicit relative-entropy barrier certifies convergence to coexistence while a second Lambert-function comparison certifies permanent extinction for sufficiently high initial predator pressure, for every positive slow–fast parameter. With identical initial prey at \(4/5\), predator values \(1/10\) and \(1/4\) have provably opposite fates even though the prey starts below the fast-nullcline fold.
