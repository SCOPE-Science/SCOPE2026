For every degree, a twice-accelerated Bernstein operator sends each unit jump to a polynomial with at most two extrema, permitting an exact finite algebraic total-variation norm computed from step inputs. Even the first nodal jump retains an asymptotic variation amplification of \(1+2e^{-1}[(1-e^{-1})/2]^{e-1}\), so acceleration does not eliminate microscopic boundary overshoot.
