On every strictly unequal complete tripartite graph \(K_{a,b,c}\), the three-color Potts antiferromagnet has exactly two positive preferred-color field crossings, at \(H/J=a\) and \(H/J=a+b\). Their complete labeled ground-state counts are \(3(2^b+2^c)-6\) and \(3^c\), with explicit population polynomials and residual entropy densities controlled by the two smaller shores.
