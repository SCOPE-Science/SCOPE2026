A one-hub, many-leaf positive affinity matrix has an exact three-phase Sinkhorn local-rate diagram with a unique best hub affinity \(t=(m+2)/\sqrt{2m}\), while row-first exact finite termination occurs only at two different explicit affinities. Remarkably, the affinity achieving exact two-normalization completion also has a worst-direction local factor within \((1+1/(m+1)^2)^2\) of the optimum, even though the two parameters become widely separated as the leaf count grows.
