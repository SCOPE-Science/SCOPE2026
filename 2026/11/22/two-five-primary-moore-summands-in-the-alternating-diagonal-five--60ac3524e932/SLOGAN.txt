The ordinary geometric quotient of the five-by-five nonattacking-rook complex by simultaneous even relabeling of rows and columns is a wedge of **two** degree-two order-five Moore spaces, \(M(\mathbb Z/5\mathbb Z,2)\vee M(\mathbb Z/5\mathbb Z,2)\). The full diagonal symmetric-group quotient has only one such summand, so passing to the natural index-two alternating subgroup reveals a second primitive five-torsion direction.
