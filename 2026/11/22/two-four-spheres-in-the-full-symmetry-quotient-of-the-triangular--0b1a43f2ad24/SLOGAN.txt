The full geometric automorphism quotient of the triangular-prism graphic-matroid forest complex is homotopy equivalent to \(S^4\vee S^4\), while its labeled complex is \(\bigvee^{18}S^4\). Together with the established \(K_{3,3}\) result, this completes the full-symmetry quotient homotopy classification for the two connected simple cubic graphs on six vertices.
