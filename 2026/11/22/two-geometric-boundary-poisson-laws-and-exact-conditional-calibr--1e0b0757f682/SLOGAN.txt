For the sample-mean-fitted geometric zero-frequency test, asymptotic normality holds exactly when both \(np_n\to\infty\) and \(n(1-p_n)^2\to\infty\); either endpoint instead produces the same centered Poisson limit at its own sharp critical scale. These twin rare-event regimes can raise nominal five-percent normal-calibrated null rejection toward 16.18%, while conditioning on the sample sum gives an exact parameter-free finite-sample calibration.
