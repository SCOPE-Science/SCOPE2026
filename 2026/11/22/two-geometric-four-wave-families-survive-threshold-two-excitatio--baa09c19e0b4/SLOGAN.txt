On the five-regular Clebsch graph, three-state two-contact excitation admits exactly 90 primitive four-cycles—30 parallel-affine and 60 twisted—and only the all-rest fixed point. Every one of \(3^{16}\) labelled configurations becomes periodic within seven updates, but only 58,920 eventually oscillate, with orbit basin sizes precisely 676 or 644 according to wave geometry.
