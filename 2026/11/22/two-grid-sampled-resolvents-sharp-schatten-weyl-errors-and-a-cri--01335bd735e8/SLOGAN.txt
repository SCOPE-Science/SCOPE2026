Dyadic cancellation converts two noncompact sampled resolvents into a positive trace-class approximation, but the approximation error belongs to \(\mathcal S_p\) exactly when \(p\) exceeds the spectral Weyl dimension. Above that threshold its error has a sharp universal spectral-integral coefficient; at the weak endpoint the norm tends to the Weyl constant, and in trace class the coefficient reduces to a Riemann zeta value.
