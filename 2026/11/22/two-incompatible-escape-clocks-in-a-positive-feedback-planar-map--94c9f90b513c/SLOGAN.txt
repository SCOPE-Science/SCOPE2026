In the positive-feedback planar map of Zhou et al., \(a=1\) is the sharp threshold beyond which every nonzero real orbit escapes, and a pointwise-sharp quadratic two-step inequality supplies a universal \(\sqrt2\) logarithmic growth base off the invariant vertical axis. At the natural resonance \(a=5/4\), one forward-invariant cone has \(\log x_n\sim L\phi^n\) in its interior but \(\log x_{2m+s}\sim\Lambda2^m\) on its two boundary rays, giving incompatible double-exponential clocks for the same parameters.
