A linear cusp-area calculation yields quadratic minimum tails; disjoint spherical caps give exact linear maximum tails. The derivative gaps prove the two transition profiles. The standalone diagnostic checker tests the projection identity against a separate convex-hull area calculation and transition identities, without claiming an infinite certificate.

Published right-pyramid silhouette-vertex and section-polygon laws concern different observables. Originality priority is provisional because a wider historical search can reveal earlier work.

**Value — PASS.** Two non-coincident contact-switch thresholds, exact probabilistic endpoint exponents and constants, and a critical mixture of four and eight least-bright directions are an integrated classification of a natural geometric probability problem, beyond restating Cauchy's projection formula.

**Scientific limitations.** A fixed centered square-base right pyramid, uniformly random orthographic viewing direction, and fixed crossover parameters. The numerical checker verifies finite tests only; it is not proof of the all-directions claims.
