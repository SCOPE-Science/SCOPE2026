The symmetric circular bicone has two distinct sharp projected-area orientation switches, at half-height-to-radius ratios \(1\) and \(\pi/2\). At the first switch, the chance of a random shadow falling within normalized area \(\varepsilon\) of the minimum is asymptotic to \(3^{1/4}\varepsilon^{1/4}\), rather than the generic square-root law; the full area distribution follows from two explicit monotone inverse branches.
