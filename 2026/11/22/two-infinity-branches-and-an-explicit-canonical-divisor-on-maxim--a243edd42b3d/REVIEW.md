# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The theorem is about the **ordinary degree-\(d\) plane closure**, not the smooth model. The proof verifies irreducibility by a one-slope irreducible Newton polygon over \(k((1/u))\), smoothness of the affine curve by \(\partial_v F=1\), uniqueness of \([0:1:0]\), and the exact two lower Newton edges at that point. The cusp edge \(b^\alpha-ca^\beta\) is tame because \(\gcd(\alpha,\beta)=1\) and both exponents are prime to the characteristic; the other edge gives a smooth branch \(a=-b^p+\cdots\). Their transverse tangents yield \(I=\alpha\). The delta formula, componentwise plane conductor formula, adjunction and finite-field rational-point correction then give the asserted canonical and zeta identities. Exact Newton-support arithmetic was checked in the accompanying standalone program for 35 prime/parameter pairs. The program is a consistency test, not a proof of infinitely many cases. The proof uses standard tame branch and plane-conductor theorems; their hypotheses are explicitly checked.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
Bartoli--Borges--Timpanella--Zini (arXiv:2610.35788v1) give this *smooth abstract curve*, its affine equation and maximality. Their ramification calculation is for the \(u\)-map, not the embedded projective singularity. A closely related published computation, *The Weierstrass semigroup of a non-Galois twisted Artin--Schreier cover*, concerns the **one-point global pole semigroup** of the normalization. It states a genus formula, which is consistent with but does not supply the local two-branch conductor, embedded cusp value semigroup, canonical adjunction data or singular-model zeta correction. Another published linearized-quotient result has a different \(u^2v^p\) equation. Targeted searches for embedded infinity singularity, plane branch conductor, dual formulations, and zeta correction did not locate equivalent coverage. This is a best-knowledge comparison, not absolute priority certification. Some pieces follow from standard formulas after the new local computation, and that mathematical dependence is declared.

## Value — PASS
The original maximal-curve family is new, explicit and unbounded in genus. Its ordinary plane model is vastly singular at a single point. Identifying **both** branches and their conductor orders gives the exact adjunction defect, an effective two-point canonical class, and the full correction from smooth to singular rational-point counts in every extension. These are natural embedded-curve invariants useful for adjoint linear systems, compactification geometry and singular-curve arithmetic; the result is not merely another numerical genus calculation.
