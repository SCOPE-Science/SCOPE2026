The ordinary plane model of Bartoli--Borges--Timpanella--Zini's odd-characteristic maximal curves with trivial geometric automorphism group has just one singular point, with one smooth branch and one coprime-pair cusp \((d-p-1,d-1)\); both branches and their complete conductor orders are explicit. The normalization's canonical class is \(K\sim((p-1)d-p)P+(p-2)Q\), and identifying the two rational branches removes exactly one point over every finite extension, giving the singular plane curve's full zeta function.
