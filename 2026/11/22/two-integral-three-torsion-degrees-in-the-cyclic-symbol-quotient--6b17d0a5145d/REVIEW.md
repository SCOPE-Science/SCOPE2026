# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The face generator gives the complete 11,776-face original complex and 3,925 nonempty orbit cells, and independently a board-cell generator reproduces the latter. The symbol action is free, simplex stabilizers are trivial and the oriented quotient cellular boundary is valid. A full integer boundary-composition check and 1,954 coefficient-unit Schur-complement cancellations leave two multiplication-by-three differentials and twelve free dimension-four cycles. Independent modular ranks (primes two, three, five) corroborate the complete homology. A spanning tree and complete 1,278-triangle elimination establish simply connectedness of the covering complex, so covering theory gives the fundamental group.

**Originality — PASS relative to inspected evidence.** The first relevant paper on trade complexes concerns a distinct fixed-square construction (Hämäläinen, arXiv:0907.1481v1); Falcón (arXiv:1107.3248v1) treats symmetry and fixed-point counts. The two closest full-text public same-object results (`63721f81dfe5`, `77e44648bda3`) prove the unquotiented complex is a wedge of 38 four-spheres and explicitly leave equivariant classification outside their statements. That nonequivariant result does not imply the full integral homology of this threefold quotient. Equivalence searches used the Latin-square, Hamming-graph, rook-complex and orbit-space formulations. Unindexed equivalent work remains possible.

**Value — PASS.** The symbol cycle is a canonical Latin-square isotopism, not an arbitrary subfamily of faces. Its free geometric quotient has a complete, reproducible integral homology classification exhibiting two positive-degree order-three classes although the covering complex has only free degree-four homology. The exact integral torsion and lack of higher torsion are mathematically relevant refinements beyond rational or nonequivariant invariants.

**Limits.** This is a fixed-order classification, not a theorem for all partial Latin squares, not a full orbit-space homotopy equivalence, and not an external certification.
