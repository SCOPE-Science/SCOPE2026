For the order-three partial Latin-square complex, simultaneous cyclic relabeling of symbols acts freely, and the geometric quotient has \(H_1\cong H_3\cong\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z\) and \(H_4\cong\mathbb Z^{12}\), with no other positive-degree integral homology. Its fundamental group is \(\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z\), despite the original complex being a simply connected wedge of 38 four-spheres.
