# Two-line unions in the doily: exact static and online shattering, facets, and homology

## Finding

Let \(\mathcal Q=GQ(2,2)\) denote the unique generalized quadrangle of order \((2,2)\), with 15 points and 15 lines, and let \(\mathcal C\) consist of the unions of **two distinct lines**. Query a point by Boolean membership in an unknown member of \(\mathcal C\). There are exactly 105 distinct concepts. The complete shattered-support polynomial is
\[
\Phi_{\mathcal C}(z)=1+15z+105z^2+375z^3+495z^4.
\]
In particular, the VC dimension equals \(4\), whereas the Littlestone dimension equals \(5\).

The shattering complex is nonpure. It has exactly 510 inclusion-maximal faces: 15 three-point facets, which are precisely the lines of \(\mathcal Q\), and 495 four-point facets. Under the natural automorphism group induced by \(S_6\), those four-point facets form precisely three orbits, of sizes 90, 360, and 45. Over \(\mathbb F_2\), the only nonzero reduced homology groups are
\[
\widetilde H_2(\Delta;\mathbb F_2)\cong\mathbb F_2^{15},\qquad
\widetilde H_3(\Delta;\mathbb F_2)\cong\mathbb F_2^{226}.
\]
No claim is made about homotopy type or integral homology.

## Assumptions and scope

Use the standard 15-point realization of \(\mathcal Q\): its points are the 15 perfect matchings (one-factors) of \(K_6\), its lines are indexed by the 15 edges \(e\) of \(K_6\), and the line \(L_e\) contains exactly those three one-factors using \(e\). For distinct edges \(e,f\), the concept is \(L_e\cup L_f\). The ground set contains the 15 one-factors, not the edges of \(K_6\). A support \(S\) is shattered precisely if all \(2^{|S|}\) traces \(S\cap C\) occur among concepts \(C\in\mathcal C\). The online dimension is the maximum depth of a complete rooted binary query tree in which every answer path is realized by at least one concept.

All statements are for this finite incidence structure and for two **distinct** lines, with no extra singleton-line concepts. Point labels and concept labels are fixed by the lexicographically sorted one-factor and edge orders in the accompanying verification code. The same incidence structure is independently represented by the 15 nonzero vectors of a four-dimensional symplectic vector space over \(\mathbb F_2\).

## Proof

**1. Exact model and concepts.** The recursive one-factor construction takes the smallest remaining symbol and pairs it with each of the five, then three, then one available symbols. Its number of outputs is \(5\cdot3\cdot1=15\). Each of the 15 edges belongs to exactly three perfect matchings. An adjacent edge pair belongs to no common matching, so the corresponding line union has six points; a disjoint edge pair belongs to one common matching, so it has five. There are 60 adjacent edge pairs and 45 disjoint ones. The finite incidence test given below also establishes that all 105 unions are different.

**2. Complete shattering classification via finite symmetry.** Encode each of the 15 points by one bit, so the traces on a support mask \(s\) are exactly the integers \(s\mathbin{\&}c\) for the 105 concept masks \(c\). The test
\[
\big|\{s\mathbin{\&}c:c\in\mathcal C\}\big|=2^{|s|}
\]
is equivalent to shattering, with no approximation or sampling. It is enough to classify supports of sizes at most five: if none of size five is shattered, downward heredity excludes every larger support.

Here is a shorter orbit certificate for the complete search. Enumerate \(S_6\) acting on the 15 perfect matchings, and take the least bit mask in every orbit. For three-point supports the five orbit sizes and numbers of traces are respectively \((15,8),(180,8),(180,8),(60,7),(20,7)\). Therefore exactly \(15+180+180=375\) three-point supports are shattered. For four-point supports the nine pairs are
\[
(180,15),(90,16),(360,16),(45,16),(360,14),
(60,13),(180,13),(60,13),(30,11).
\]
Only the three orbits of sizes \(90,360,45\) have all 16 traces, totaling \(495\). For five-point supports there are 15 orbits; their sizes are
\[
360,45,360,360,90,360,72,180,360,360,60,180,120,90,6,
\]
and their respective trace counts are
\[
28,23,26,28,25,26,31,21,24,24,25,21,20,23,15.
\]
The orbit sizes total \(\binom{15}{5}=3003\), and the maximum trace count is only 31, less than the 32 required. Singletons and pairs are all shattered (15 and 105), and the empty support is shattered. These exhaust all cardinalities and prove the displayed polynomial. The complete orbit representatives, counts, permutation-generated orbits, and direct trace checks are regenerated by `verify_orbits.py`; the two other programs independently enumerate **all** masks rather than relying on the orbit reduction.

**3. Facets.** Every shattered four-set is maximal by the five-set obstruction. A shattered triple is contained in no shattered four-set exactly when its three matchings all use a common edge of \(K_6\), equivalently when it is a line of the doily. The orbit table confirms this assertion: the orbit of line triples has size 15, while every triple in the two other shattered orbits extends to a shattered four-set. Thus the facet count is \(15+495=510\). The three four-facet orbits are not inferred merely from total cardinality; they are distinct full \(S_6\)-orbits of sizes 90, 360, and 45 as directly verified.

**4. Exact binary homology.** Form boundary maps over \(\mathbb F_2\) by sending each face of cardinality \(k\) to the sum of its \(k\) immediate subfaces. The face counts in cardinalities one through four are \(15,105,375,495\), and independent binary Gaussian elimination gives boundary ranks \(14,91,269\), respectively. The calculations work on explicitly generated 0/1 incidence matrices, not on floating-point ranks. They yield
\[
\widetilde\beta_0=15-1-14=0,\quad
\widetilde\beta_1=105-14-91=0,\quad
\widetilde\beta_2=375-91-269=15,\quad
\widetilde\beta_3=495-269=226.
\]
There are no faces in higher dimension. These are exact homology ranks **over the stated field only**.

**5. Adaptive dimension.** Number the 105 concepts, and let \(F\) be any subset of them. Write \(F_{x,1}\) and \(F_{x,0}\) for the hypotheses that label point \(x\) by one or zero. The integer recursion
\[
D(F)=\max_{x:\,F_{x,0},F_{x,1}\ne\varnothing}\left(1+\min\{D(F_{x,0}),D(F_{x,1})\}\right),
\]
with \(D(F)=0\) when no query splits \(F\), is exact by induction on \(|F|\): each internal node must choose a point and both children must support the remaining depth. Memoized exhaustive evaluation on the 105-bit hypothesis set visits exactly 58,598 distinct states and gives \(D(\mathcal C)=5\). The file `depth5_tree.json` contains an explicit 31-internal-node, 32-leaf witness tree; its leaf paths are separately checked against the concept incidence masks. The same exhaustive recursion excludes depth six, proving the exact Littlestone dimension.

## Verification

All source files are self-contained and require no external datasets. Run, from the extracted artifact directory:

```sh
python3 verify_symplectic.py
python3 verify_orbits.py
c++ -O2 -std=c++17 verify_matchings.cpp -o verify_matchings
./verify_matchings
```

`verify_symplectic.py` constructs the incidence system from orthogonal pairs of distinct nonzero vectors of \(\mathbb F_2^4\); `verify_matchings.cpp` reconstructs it from all one-factors of \(K_6\). They independently enumerate face masks, compute exact binary boundary ranks and solve the full adaptive recursion. `verify_orbits.py` independently constructs all 720 permutations of six symbols and partitions the finite supports into their full orbits. The two structural realizations give the same polynomial, ranks and adaptive dimension. All bounded loops and recurrence states are explicit; there is no appeal to an incomplete numerical experiment.

## Relationship to prior work

Bishnoi's *Finite Geometry and Ramsey Theory* minicourse, publicly taught in January 2021, describes the 15-point doily as perfect matchings of \(K_6\) and its lines as edges (Example 3.3). It establishes the ambient incidence model, not this double-line Boolean shattering classification. Iosevich, Pham, Senger, and Tait (2023) use VC dimension in finite-field point-line incidence bounds but do not treat unions of two lines of the order-two generalized quadrangle.

Related published research on unions of two projective-plane lines proves a different complete shattering polynomial for a geometry in which every pair of distinct lines meets. The doily has 60 pairs of disjoint lines, so no parameter substitution or incidence duality turns that projective-plane result into the present theorem. Published work on exact two-coordinate OR hypotheses supplies the general Boolean-column criterion for an unrestricted query domain. Here the query domain is constrained to the 15 one-factor incidence vectors; the exact finite polynomial, exceptional facets, binary homology, and adaptive dimension are not obtained from the unrestricted dimension table.

## Limitations

The assertion is a complete **finite** classification for the doily only; it does not prove an analogous statement for arbitrary generalized quadrangles. The topological ranks are computed over \(\mathbb F_2\); torsion, integral homology, and homotopy equivalences have not been determined. Although the bounded exhaustive proofs are reproduced in two independently constructed incidence models, no independent external mathematical audit has been performed. An equivalent result under group-testing, finite polar-geometry, or hypergraph-trace terminology could remain unindexed. The unrestricted exact-two-OR reference was inspected; no claim is made that the generic OR-trace identity itself is new.
