For unions of two distinct lines in the fifteen-point generalized quadrangle, the complete shattering polynomial is \(1+15z+105z^2+375z^3+495z^4\), with VC dimension \(4\) but Littlestone dimension \(5\). The nonpure shattering complex has 15 line-triangle facets, 495 tetrahedral facets, and mod-two reduced Betti ranks \(\widetilde\beta_2=15\) and \(\widetilde\beta_3=226\).
