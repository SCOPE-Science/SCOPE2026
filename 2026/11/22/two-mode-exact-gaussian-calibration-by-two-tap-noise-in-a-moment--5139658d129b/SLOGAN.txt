A two-tap Gaussian noise correction can calibrate a stable gradient-extrapolation momentum sampler to **any two** distinct Gaussian curvatures, but no fixed common two-tap choice can match three. Once two curvatures are calibrated, the stationary variance is strictly too small between them and too large outside them, with the exact error \(\eta(1+2\eta)(t-t_-)(t-t_+)/[(1-\eta t)(1+(1+2\eta)t)]\).
