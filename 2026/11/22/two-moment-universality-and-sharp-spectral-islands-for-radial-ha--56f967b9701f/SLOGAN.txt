The entire spectrum of every quadratic harmonic Toeplitz compression for an arbitrary rotationally invariant disk measure reaching the boundary depends on that measure only through the ratio \(\rho=m_2/m_1^2\). Its unique possible isolated eigenvalue appears precisely for \(1+1/\rho<|b/a|<1+\rho\), yielding both unexpected isospectral singular/continuous measures and a weight-driven spectral phase transition.
