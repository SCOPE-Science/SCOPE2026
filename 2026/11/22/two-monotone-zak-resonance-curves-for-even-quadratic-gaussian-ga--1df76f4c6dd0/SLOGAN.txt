At rectangular Gabor density \(1/2\), even quadratic-Gaussian windows possess two explicit, disjoint, strictly monotone theta-quotient Zak-resonance curves, exchanged by reciprocal lattice dilation. In particular, **every** strictly positive window \((t^2+s)e^{-\pi t^2}\), \(s>0\), fails the Gabor frame property at a unique lattice aspect along the second prescribed resonance curve; this does not classify all other possible non-frame aspects.
