For a persistent particle escaping an absorbing interval, the conditional number of direction reversals has a unique **variance-ordering** crossover at every positive reversal rate, while its **mean-ordering** crossover appears only above the sharp ballistic-length threshold \(\lambda L/v=\sqrt3\). When both exist, the mean crossing lies strictly before the variance crossing, and their separation shrinks as \(12(v/(\lambda L))^3\) at rapid reversals.
