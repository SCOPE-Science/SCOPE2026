# Reproducible verification

The exact claim is the two-sided, endpoint-conditioned reversal-count law for a constant-speed telegraph particle with independent Poisson velocity flips, and the strict ordering of its two spatial crossover thresholds. The complete analytic proof is in RESULT.md. Run `python check.py` with SymPy, NumPy and SciPy installed to check independent matrix-transfer evaluation, conditional moment differentiation, positive-polynomial monotonicity certificates, the negative variance gap at the mean crossing and the large-rate asymptotics. The successful same-model local execution reported 250 transfer and numerical-root comparisons, alongside exact symbolic identities.

The numerical finite checks do not prove universal signs, uniqueness, or asymptotics. Those claims depend on the algebraic identities, positive derivatives, endpoint signs, and implicit-expansion argument written in RESULT.md. No independent validation has been carried out; no historical results, external proof assistants or experiment logs are treated as certification.
