For reflected Brownian motion with Poisson two-stage Erlang-capacity resetting \(X\mapsto(B-X)^+\), the stationary spatial tail hazard is bathtub-shaped until a sharp dimensionless threshold \(t_h=1.171586\ldots\), then becomes strictly increasing. Its squared coefficient of variation crosses one much earlier, at \(t_v=0.524162\ldots\), proving a nonempty region of underdispersed stationary positions with a bathtub-shaped spatial hazard.
