For \(K_m\)-free homogeneous graphs with \(m\ge5\), the first two numerical types over a complete \((m-2)\)-partite named base determine every shore size, even though unary types only detect the total number of named vertices. The correction \(\prod_i(4^{a_i}-3^{a_i})\) is multiplicatively rigid by Bang--Zsigmondy, while bases with fewer parts are completely invisible to two-type counts beyond their cardinality.
