# Scientific review: two observation-horizon laws for star-network leaders

## Correctness — PASS
The scope and quantifiers cover all integer star sizes \(m\ge2\), all exact budgets \(1\le k\le m\), and every real finite horizon \(T>0\). All feasible leader sets fall into one of two automorphism classes. The follower spectra follow from a two-dimensional arrowhead block, with the \(k=m\) case separately treated. A strictly convex two-exponential difference has exactly one positive crossing when \(k<m\). Integrating that difference yields a strictly decreasing-then-increasing function whose limit is the exact budget-dependent sign \((m+1-2k)/(2k)\), proving both the switch and no-switch regimes including the equality boundary. The large-star limit follows by uniform convergence near unique positive roots. ## Value — PASS
Two standard, physically distinct performance measures on the same ideal leader-controlled diffusion show sharp but different horizon behavior. The all-budget classification identifies a persistent regime where accumulated stochastic error favors leaf leaders at every finite horizon even though instantaneous error eventually favors a hub leader. The exact budget boundary and the two unequal universal fixed-budget large-star limits are substantive structural information for the question of whether horizon-based leader choices can be transferred between objectives. This is a natural complete classification, not a numerical example or arbitrary selected parameter slice.
