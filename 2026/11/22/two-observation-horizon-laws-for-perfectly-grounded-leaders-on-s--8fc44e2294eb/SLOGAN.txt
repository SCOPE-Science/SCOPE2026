On an unweighted star, for every leader budget \(1\le k<m\), instantaneous squared-propagator error switches exactly once from pinning leaves to pinning the hub, while integrated white-noise variance switches only below the sharp budget boundary \(2k<m+1\). For \((m+1)/2\le k<m\) the instantaneous and integrated criteria eventually have opposite preferred leader types, whereas pinning all leaves is always optimal when \(k=m\); the two crossing horizons have distinct universal fixed-budget large-star limits.
