On the four-site path, endpoint transfer under \(e^{-tL^\alpha}\) is positive at every time for \(0<\alpha\le1\), has exactly one negative-to-positive crossing for \(1<\alpha\le2\), and has precisely two crossings (positive–negative–positive) for every \(\alpha>2\). The recovery time emerges as \(\sqrt{3\log2/2}\sqrt{\alpha-1}\) at the first threshold, while the short positive-time window above order two opens linearly in \(\alpha-2\).
