For every square rook graph \(K_n\square K_n\) with \(n\ge3\), its minimum ordinary zero-forcing placements split into exactly two connected token-slide components, distinguished by a missing row versus a missing column in the white-cell spanning-tree support. Each component has \(n(n-1)^{n-1}n^{n-2}\) placements, but allowing unrestricted single-token jumps connects the two; the minimum size and placement count are previously known.
