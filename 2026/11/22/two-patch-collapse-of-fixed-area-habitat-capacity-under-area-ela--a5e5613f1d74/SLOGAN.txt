For a fixed habitat-area budget, every optimal design of a symmetric zero-diagonal metapopulation-capacity proxy with source–destination area elasticity \(\gamma\geq1\) concentrates exactly half the habitat on each endpoint of a strongest dispersal link. This two-site collapse can fail when area elasticity is weaker: at \(\gamma=1/2\), three equally connected habitat sites with equal area outperform every two-site allocation by a factor of \(4/3\).
