In four-neighbour bootstrap percolation on the triangular graph \(L(K_n)\), \(n\geq5\), the only minimum four-seed configurations are root-edge copies of \(P_5\) and \(P_3\sqcup P_3\), giving exactly \(60\binom n5+90\binom n6\) optimum sets. Their single-seed exchange graph is connected but becomes nonregular at \(n=6\), with two explicit degree classes and a closed edge count.
