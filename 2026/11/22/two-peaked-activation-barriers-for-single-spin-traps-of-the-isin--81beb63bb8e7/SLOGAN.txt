In the twelve-spin antiferromagnetic Ising cuboctahedron, 36 low-field and eight intermediate-field excited single-spin traps coexist with the same nine equilibrium ground states. Every trap reaches a ground state optimally in three flips, with exact activation barriers \(2J\min\{h/J,2-h/J\}\) and \(2J\min\{h/J-2,4-h/J\}\) in the respective regimes, producing two distinct kinetic barrier peaks without a change of equilibrium ground state.
