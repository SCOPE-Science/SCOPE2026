Over a balanced complete tripartite base with one singleton shore, almost every tetrahedron-free hypergraph one-point type chooses hub-links on only one of the two large shores; the probability of using both is asymptotic to \(n(3/4)^n\). Conditioned on this rare event, exactly one hub-link occurs on one shore with high probability, while the opposite shore has asymptotic selected density \(1/3\).
