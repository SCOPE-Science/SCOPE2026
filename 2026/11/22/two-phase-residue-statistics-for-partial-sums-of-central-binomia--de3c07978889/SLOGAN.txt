The residues modulo five of the running central-binomial sums \(S(n)=\sum_{k<n}\binom{2k}{k}\) have no natural frequency for any nonzero residue. On cutoffs \(5^{2j}\) and \(5^{2j+1}\), their exact limiting frequency vectors are respectively \((1,8,2,1,2)/14\) and \((1,2,1,2,8)/14\), with a uniform absolute count error at most \(3^r\) at depth \(r\).
