For every \(n\ge3\), deleting a scattered `010` from \((01)^n\) produces exactly \(n-1\) exceptional residuals, each obtained by \(n-1\) different deletion triples; every other residual is generated uniquely. The full spectrum consists of \(\binom{n-1}{3}\) singleton fibers and \(n-1\) fibers of multiplicity \(n-1\), unchanged when the deleted pattern is `101`.
