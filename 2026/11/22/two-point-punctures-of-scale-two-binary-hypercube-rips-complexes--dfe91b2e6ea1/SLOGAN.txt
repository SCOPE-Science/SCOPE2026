Removing two data points from the scale-two binary-cube Rips complex has a distance-sensitive integral homotopy type. Across dimensions \(3\le n\le6\), all 18 Hamming-distance pair orbits are classified by complete discrete-Morse certificates: dimensions four through six give wedges of three-spheres whose ranks jump between distances at most two and distances at least three, while the three-cube has an exceptional two-sphere.
