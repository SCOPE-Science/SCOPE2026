The two recently constructed pointless K3 surfaces over \(\mathbb F_7\) have different complete genus-one fiber spectra: \(8I_2+8I_1\) versus \(12I_2\), with explicitly determined Frobenius orbits. Their point counts over \(\mathbb F_{49}\) are \(3040\) and \(3320\), proving they are not isomorphic over \(\mathbb F_7\).
