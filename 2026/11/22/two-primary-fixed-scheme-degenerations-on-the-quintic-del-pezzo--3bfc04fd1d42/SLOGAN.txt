The two nontrivial two-primary permutation types on the split quintic del Pezzo surface have sharply different wild fixed-scheme collisions: an order-four fixed set of three points collapses to one length-three curvilinear point in characteristic two, while a double-transposition fixed section merges with a rational fixed curve and leaves a square-zero embedded function. Explicit integral equations determine their fixed-point counts over every finite field.
