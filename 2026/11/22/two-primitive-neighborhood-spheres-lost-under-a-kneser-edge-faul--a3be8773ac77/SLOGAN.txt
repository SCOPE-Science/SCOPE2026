Deleting any single graph edge from the fifteen-vertex Kneser graph \(\operatorname{KG}(6,2)\) changes its open-neighborhood complex from a wedge of 19 two-spheres to a wedge of 17 two-spheres. The induced map on integral second homology is a primitive injection with free cokernel \(\mathbb Z^2\), certified by an exhaustive acyclic matching and signed relative-chain reductions.
