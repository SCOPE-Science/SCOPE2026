With an increasingly coarse logarithmic binning of Pareto data, the maximum-likelihood-fitted Kolmogorov–Smirnov null statistic passes through a Gaussian-to-centered-Poisson transition when \(nq_n^2\) is of order one, and a second Poisson-squared boundary when \(nq_n\) is of order one, where \(q_n=b_n^{-\alpha}\). The second boundary also gives an explicit discrete limit for the ideal refitting-bootstrap tail probability and a nonvanishing probability that the finite-exponent MLE does not exist.
