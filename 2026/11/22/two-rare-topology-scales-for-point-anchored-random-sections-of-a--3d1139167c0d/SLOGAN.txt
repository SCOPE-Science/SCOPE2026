If a random plane through a uniform interior point of a thin circular solid torus has independent uniform orientation, the probability of a disk section is asymptotic to \(\frac{64\Gamma(3/4)}{15\pi^{3/2}\Gamma(5/4)}R^{-1/2}\), while an annular section has probability asymptotic to \(\frac{16}{9\pi^2}R^{-2}\). These powers differ from those under motion-invariant plane sampling conditioned on hitting the torus.
