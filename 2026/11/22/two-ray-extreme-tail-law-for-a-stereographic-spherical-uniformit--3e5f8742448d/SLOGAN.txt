For the untruncated two-sphere stereographic uniformity test, fixed-sample extreme events come from either a single nearly coincident pair or a single nearly antipodal pair, yielding an exact two-ray tail law for all parameter choices. The upper and lower null tails have sharp order \(x^{-2}\) with coefficients \(\tfrac{2(n-1)}n(1+a_+^2)\) and \(\tfrac{2(n-1)}na_-^2\), and the truncated variance grows with coefficient \(\tfrac{4(n-1)}n(1+a^2)\log x\).
