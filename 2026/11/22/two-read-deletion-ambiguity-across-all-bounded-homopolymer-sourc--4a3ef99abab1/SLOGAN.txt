For every alphabet size \(q\ge2\) and every maximum constant-run length \(r\ge2\), the entire length-and-disagreement-span spectrum of two-read single-deletion confusable pairs has the rational generating function \(\binom q2\frac{u^2z^2}{1-uz}\left(\frac{1+S_r(z)-2z^r}{1-(q-1)S_r(z)}\right)^2\). A uniformly selected confusable pair has an asymptotically shifted-geometric disagreement span, and the graph's average degree has an explicit linear-growth constant.
