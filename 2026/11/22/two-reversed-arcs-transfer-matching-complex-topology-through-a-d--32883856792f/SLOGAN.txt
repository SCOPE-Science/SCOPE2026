Reversing two disjoint arcs in a source-to-sink orientation of any bipartite graph produces a contractible multipath complex unless both cross edges complete a directed four-cycle; in that case the complex is the threefold simplicial suspension of the matching complex left after removing the four endpoints. For \(K_{7,7}\) the suspension transports the known integral \(\mathbb Z/3\)-torsion of the five-by-five chessboard complex into degree five.
