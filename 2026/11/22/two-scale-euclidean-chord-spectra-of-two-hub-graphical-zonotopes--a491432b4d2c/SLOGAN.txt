The complete Euclidean vertex-pair distance polynomial of the two-hub graphical zonotope \(K_{2,n}\) has a polynomial-time nine-kernel convolution formula. Half of its random squared chords fluctuate on the \(n\) scale with a shifted weighted chi-square limit, while the other half lie on the \(n^2\) scale with Gaussian \(n^{3/2}\)-fluctuations.
