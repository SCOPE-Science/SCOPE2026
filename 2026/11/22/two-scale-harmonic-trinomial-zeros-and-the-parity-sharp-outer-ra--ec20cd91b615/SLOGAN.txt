For large positive coefficients, all zeros of \(z^n+c\overline z^{\,k}-1\) split into two explicitly resolved regular-polygon clusters, with the first displacement of each zero given exactly. The farthest-zero radius has a sharp parity law: even analytic degrees attain the elementary triangle bound at one negative real zero, whereas odd degrees exhibit a strictly positive, quantified bound defect attained by exactly one conjugate pair.
