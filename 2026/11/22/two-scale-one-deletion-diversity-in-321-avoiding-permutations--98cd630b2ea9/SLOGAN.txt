In \(321\)-avoiding permutations, the joint ascending/descending consecutive-bond counts have the exact generating function \(T(z,u)/(1-(v-1)z^2T(z,u))\), where \(T(z,u)=C(z/[1-(u-1)z])\) and \(C\) is the Catalan series. Under uniform sampling, ascending-bond counts fluctuate on a Gaussian square-root scale while descending-bond counts approach an asymptotically independent negative-binomial law, yielding a two-scale description of distinct one-deletion patterns.
