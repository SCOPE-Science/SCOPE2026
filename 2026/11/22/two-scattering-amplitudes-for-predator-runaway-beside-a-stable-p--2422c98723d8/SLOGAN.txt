A discrete prey-refuge predator–prey map can have a stable prey-only state and, simultaneously, an open set of high-predator initial conditions that escape with predator growth \(y_n\sim Le^{sn}\) and superexponential prey extinction \(x_n\sim K\exp(n-C(e^{sn}-1))\). Both scattering amplitudes \(L,K\) are finite and strictly positive, and the coefficient \(C=aL/[H(e^s-1)]\) depends on the initial state.
