For two fractional-telegrapher searches with different temporal memory kernels, optimizing the Poisson-reset rate produces two **separated** target-distance reversals: the faster optimized search changes model before the preferred reset rate changes order. Exact analytic brackets hold for every anomalous exponent \(0<\mu<1\), with sharp near- and far-target laws; uniqueness of a crossover *within* either bracket is not asserted.
