For analytic three-character circle polynomials, the sharp Poisson \(L^2\)-to-\(L^4\) norm has two exact, separated mode-activation thresholds when the frequencies are not in arithmetic progression. At the resonant progression \(\{0,m,2m\}\), both nonconstant modes activate at the first threshold, with a sharp \(3/2\) enhancement of the leading norm excess.
