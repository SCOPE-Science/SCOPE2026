For three independent horizontal-parameter-uniform points on a compact parabola arc, the probability of a strictly acute triangle has a complete elementary law with sharp onset at \(a=1\), a second nonanalytic correction at \(a=\sqrt2\), and explicit \(5/2\)-power coefficients. Its long-arc decay is \((3\log a-3/2)/a^2+O(a^{-4})\), and the onset-conditioned geometry converges to a uniform parabolic simplex.
