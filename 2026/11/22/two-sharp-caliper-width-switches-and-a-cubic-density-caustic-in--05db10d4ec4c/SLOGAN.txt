Parabolic segments exhibit a sharp birth of two nonaxial width-critical directions at rise-to-half-chord ratio \(\sqrt2\), followed by different global maximum- and minimum-width switches. At the birth, uniformly random caliper widths have an interior \(|v-v_*|^{-2/3}\) density singularity, with an exact coefficient and a \(3/2\)-power separation law for the new critical values.
