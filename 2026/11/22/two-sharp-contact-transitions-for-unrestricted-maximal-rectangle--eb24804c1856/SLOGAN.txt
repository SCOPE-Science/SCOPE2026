Every circular segment has an exactly classifiable largest inscribed rectangle even when arbitrary rotation and translation are allowed: a free-square phase ends at cut height \(-1/\sqrt2\), and a second corner-contact transition occurs at \(-1/\sqrt3\). The optimum-area law is \(C^1\) but not \(C^2\) at both thresholds, and its occupied fraction tends to \(1/\sqrt3\) for a vanishing cap.
