# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

Color symmetry makes each draw sign mean zero. Conditioning the population imbalance gives an exact finite-time second-moment recursion; a separate conditioning calculation gives the complete pairwise sampled-color covariance with a strictly positive lag factor. The scalar recurrence solves exactly as a positive gamma-ratio product in the negative-drift regime. Monotonicity then gives all sign classes, and a locally uniform gamma-ratio asymptotic gives the critical crossing exponent and coefficient. The independent exact-rational checker reproduced finite distributions and multiple two-time covariances. Risks are limited to the stated model restrictions and independent proof validation; no finite experiment is used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS against inspected evidence

The two closest full-text primary sources, Alves–Rosales (arXiv:2502.16768) and Shi–Yin–Miao (arXiv:2605.26669), establish color-proportion convergence and central limit behavior, respectively. Neither inspected article states a two-threshold sign phase for correlations of sampled colors, the all-lag invariance of its sign, or the critical sign-reversal age. Related stochastic-urn findings on critical imbalance Brownian behavior and logarithmic-time Ornstein–Uhlenbeck fluctuations concern different observables and parameter regimes. Searches considered aliases such as draw autocovariance, colored urn response, reinforcement sign reversals, and generalized urn second moments. Explicit priority risks remain: earlier treatments of generalized urn sample-color correlations might imply this statement without matching terminology, and an uninspected historical formula could dominate the present special case. Full-text inspection of every historical source has not been possible; no claim of complete database coverage is made.

## Value — PASS

The sampled-color process is a direct observable of the urn and can look either persistent or anti-persistent. The exact classification separates immediate anti-persistence from eventual anti-persistence, and the fractional-power divergence of the negative-to-positive switch quantifies a metastable finite-sample deception. The distinction of time-indexed correlations from the standard color-proportion limit is mathematically motivated and survives beyond a routine single-moment identity. The theorem does not claim a general extension to all urn models.
