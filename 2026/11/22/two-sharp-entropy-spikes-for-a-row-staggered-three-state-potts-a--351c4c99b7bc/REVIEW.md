# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
A nine-column-state representation accounts for each rung, both horizontal bonds, one oriented diagonal, and both row-specific color preferences exactly once. Three explicit rational dual potentials certify all closed walks at the supporting fields \(h=0,1,2\); interpolation extends the bound throughout the specified nonnegative-field domain. Three witnessed periodic words attain all branches whenever the circumference is divisible by three. A zero-slack transfer matrix then counts exactly the full set of minimizing closed walks, not just representative patterns. Its characteristic polynomials give the stated Lucas and cubic-root multiplicities and critical entropies. The independent exhaustive six-column test and the exact arithmetic checker passed. No statement about all finite lengths' degeneracies, finite-temperature phase transitions, or independent verification is used.

## Originality — PASS, subject to historical priority
The closest full-text comparisons were Shrock–Tsai's zero-field cyclic strip work (1999), Chang–Shrock's zero-field width-two triangular strip (2000), Chang–Shrock's one-common-color field transfer formalism (2009), and Shrock–Xu's generalized uniform color-set field (2010). In particular, Chang–Shrock (2009) includes exact width-two triangular-strip matrices, a serious close comparison, but its Hamiltonian favors the same color at every vertex. The present row-dependent preference for different colors cannot be converted to that Hamiltonian by a global color permutation, and a row-only permutation twists equality interactions across rows. No inspected theorem states or covers the specified staggered-field sharp thresholds and exact two coexistence multiplicities. Residual risks include an earlier unindexed site-dependent-field computation or a more general local-color transfer construction yielding these exact formulas.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Contrasting a uniform color field with two incompatible row-specific preferred colors is a natural modification of an antiferromagnetic ladder in a staggered environment. The result gives complete all-field finite-size energies on an infinite, commensurate family, exact ground-state counts, and two algebraically distinct coexistence entropy spikes separating three zero-entropy phases. The Lucas-number first threshold and cubic-algebraic second threshold express distinct defect freedoms rather than simple isolated numerical examples. The zero-temperature boundary of the response is explicit and may be used to test inhomogeneous transfer-matrix predictions.

## Limitations and sources
All statements are for a three-state Potts model with homogeneous edge penalties and row-specific, equal-strength favored colors; multiplicities are proved at circumferences divisible by three. These finite-size results do not assert positive-temperature nonanalyticities.
