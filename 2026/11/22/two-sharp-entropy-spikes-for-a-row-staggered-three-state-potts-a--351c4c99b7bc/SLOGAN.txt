A row-staggered two-color magnetic field on a cyclic triangular Potts ladder has exactly two sharp zero-temperature thresholds, at \(H/J=1\) and \(H/J=2\), and the ground energy is explicitly known for every length divisible by three. Both thresholds have exponential ground-state degeneracy, with exact Lucas-number growth at the first, although every neighboring open-field phase has zero residual entropy.
