For the alternating type-C Gale-interval feasible polytope on \(2r\) coordinates, the two longest squared Euclidean chord shells have exactly \(2^{r-1}\) and \(2(3^r-2^r)\) unordered vertex pairs, respectively, for every \(r\ge2\). Its entire diameter graph is a matching with \(2^{r-1}\) edges, while the next shell grows exponentially faster.
