For the published spherical Poisson-kernel uniformity statistic, exponentially many uniformly random points exhibit two different sharp thresholds: the off-diagonal kernel average converges to zero below \(\alpha_{\mathrm P}=-\tfrac14\log(1-\rho^2)\), to one above it, yet its variance diverges until the larger threshold \(\alpha_{\mathrm V}=2\alpha_{\mathrm P}\). The exact finite-dimensional variance is previously known; the new result is the probability-versus-mean-square transition and its sharp logarithmic rate.
