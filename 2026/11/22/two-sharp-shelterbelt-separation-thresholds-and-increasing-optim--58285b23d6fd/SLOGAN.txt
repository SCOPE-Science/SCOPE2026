In a three-identical-windbreak model with exponential downstream wind-speed recovery, the unique placement maximizing whole-field integrated shelter switches sharply from all rows together, to two rows together, to three separated rows as field length grows. Whenever rows separate, the optimal consecutive leeward gaps increase strictly; at 50% attenuation the two dimensionless length thresholds are \(\log(4/3)\) and \(\log 3\).
