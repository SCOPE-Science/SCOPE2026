For five fixed equally spaced interpolation nodes, a positive exponential integration weight makes Boole's quadrature lose positivity at exactly two sharp tilts: the midpoint weight changes sign at \(\alpha\approx3.111842\), followed by the opposite endpoint weight at \(\beta\approx3.225120\). All weights are nonnegative exactly when \(|\lambda|\le\alpha\), and exact signed Taylor moments prove that no further sign reversals occur.
