The real least-squares projector onto a damped conjugate mode pair has two sharp uniform-norm limits: \(8/e\) as the frequency coalesces and \(8/\pi\) as it separates to high frequency, while the unrescaled coefficient Gram condition number diverges as \(4/r^2\). The coalescent limit is discontinuous from the rank-one zero-frequency projector norm \(2\), and an exact endpoint-amplification formula holds at every positive frequency.
