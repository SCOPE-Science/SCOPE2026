For a biased random comb with unbiased reflecting teeth and tooth-local Poisson returns, power-law tooth-length tails produce a five-regime sharp weak-return drift law, with a zero-velocity threshold at \(\alpha=1\) and a finite-mobility-susceptibility threshold at \(\alpha=3\). The two marginal exponents have exact inverse-logarithmic and \(r\log(1/r)\) laws, respectively.
