In two-layer stationary hierarchical Ornstein–Uhlenbeck transport, negative velocity autocorrelation begins at \(D_1/D_2>1-(\gamma_2/\gamma_1)^2\), yet an instantaneous subdiffusive MSD slope \(tM'(t)/M(t)<1\) occurs only beyond the strictly higher threshold \(D_1/D_2>1+\gamma_2/\gamma_1\). The intervening phase has genuine negative velocity memory and a unique running-diffusivity overshoot without any local subdiffusion, while the second critical boundary has an exact exponentially fast slope correction.
