On a budgeted two-clique network joined by a perfect matching, every nonunit matching-edge price forces an exactly computable positive interval of observation times during which the unique heat-trace-optimal design deliberately disconnects the network: cheap matching edges suppress all clique edges, while expensive matching edges themselves disappear. The two reactivation thresholds grow respectively as \(m\) and \(m\log m\) for fixed prices on opposite sides of one, although every edge becomes active eventually.
