For quadratic energy \(Q=\int_0^1B(t)^2\,dt\) of a standard Brownian bridge, one pointwise sign conveys no mean-square information, while two distinct signs have an exact positive energy-sign covariance proportional to the square root of the product of the three sampling gaps. Every globally optimal two-sign placement is reflection-symmetric and strictly closer to the midpoint than the trisection sites; its risk is characterized by an explicit one-variable maximum, without a uniqueness claim for that scalar maximizer.
