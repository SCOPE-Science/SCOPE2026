Deleting one triangular or one reversal edge from the truncated-tetrahedral graph gives, after recomputing distances, radius-two Vietoris–Rips complexes homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^3S^2\) and \(\bigvee^2S^2\), respectively; both become contractible at radius three. Their inclusions into the intact radius-two complex induce primitive integral injections with cokernels \(\mathbb Z^2\) and \(\mathbb Z^3\).
