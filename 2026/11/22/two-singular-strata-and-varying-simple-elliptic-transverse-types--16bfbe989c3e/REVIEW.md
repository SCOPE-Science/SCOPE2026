# Scientific review of the two-stratum Cox-space theorem

## Correctness — PASS
The claim uses the stated reduced Weierstrass discriminant and Picard-number-two hypotheses. The pole-order basis computes the complete multigraded coordinate ring, rather than only a subring. Differentiating its one hypersurface equation and using simple discriminant zeros gives precisely the plane and line as the **reduced** singular locus. The weighted blowup of the degree-six elliptic cone has one smooth genus-one exceptional curve of normal degree minus one; the weight-product formula gives transverse Milnor number ten. A separate weighted-hypersurface calculation gives \(2(6\chi-1)^2\) for the isolated threefold slice along the line. The exact symbolic checker tests discriminant squarefreeness, two distinct moduli and the weight formulas. It is supplementary to the proof.

## Originality — PASS, with priority limitations
The August 2026 paper of Laface--Li--Liu treats existence and Mori-dream geometry for these elliptic surfaces, not the asserted Cox-spectrum stratification. The November 18, 2026 paper of Laface--Li already supplies the precise five-generator Cox-ring presentation: **that presentation is not claimed as a discovery**. Its relevant full-text sections give no global plane-plus-line singular locus, two transverse Milnor numbers or recovery of a variable \(j\)-modulus. Classical elliptic singularity theory describes isolated weighted cones in general, not this Cox-space family-wide computation. Targeted searches and the recovered comparison-claim lists found no statement covering the combined result. Priority can still be affected by unindexed literature or implicit deductions from earlier Cox-ring formulae.

## Value — PASS
The two singular components have different dimensions and reflect distinct geometric degenerations: the plane remembers the continuously varying smooth elliptic fibers, while the line is the homogeneous-base origin with an isolated threefold transverse singularity and an explicit large Milnor number. The nonconstant analytic type at fixed Milnor number is a natural structural invariant of the total coordinate space, rather than an arbitrary example or a repackaged finite count. The result is a meaningful refinement of the Mori-dream construction and the known ring presentation; it does not purport to solve all singularities at the origin or Jacobian-scheme multiplicities.
