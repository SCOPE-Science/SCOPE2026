For Picard-number-two semistable Jacobian elliptic surfaces, the affine Cox spectrum has a reduced singular locus consisting of a plane and a line. Smooth-fiber transverse plane slices are degree-one simple elliptic singularities with Milnor number \(10\) and varying elliptic \(j\)-modulus, while the line has an isolated threefold transverse singularity with Milnor number \(2(6\chi-1)^2\).
