On every labelled binary Rule 22 ring, primitive temporal six-cycles occur precisely when the circumference is divisible by eight or ten: the eight-site resonance contributes four orbits, while the ten-site resonance contributes \(\frac53(4^{n/10}+2)\) orbits when \(10\mid n\). An exact space-time transfer determinant proves this all-length count and distinguishes bounded from exponentially proliferating oscillations.
