The explicit non-isotropic kernel \(K(x,y)=(1+x_3y_3)/(1-(x_1+i x_2)(y_1-i y_2)/2)\) is strictly positive definite on the two-sphere even though it occupies exactly two of the \(2\ell+1\) spherical harmonic modes at each positive degree \(\ell\). Its real part also remains strictly positive definite with vanishing harmonic occupancy density, while neither kernel is universal for continuous-function approximation.
