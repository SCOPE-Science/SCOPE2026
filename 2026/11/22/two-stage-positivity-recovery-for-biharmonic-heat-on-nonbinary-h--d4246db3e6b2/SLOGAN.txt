Exact biharmonic heat on every nonbinary Hamming three-cube has two successive positivity recoveries: distance-two entries recover first, and distance-three entries recover last at the universal time \(1.055640655052\ldots/q^2\). The complete induced \(\ell^\infty\) norm is determined by two explicit negative-part polynomials, and the earlier recovery has a distinct, strictly alphabet-dependent limit.
