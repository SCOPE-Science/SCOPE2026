For unmodified two-stage RKL1 graph diffusion, every branching graph with maximum degree \(\Delta\ge3\) loses positivity at the sharp universal step \(h_-(\Delta)\), while remaining spectrally stable immediately beyond it. On a star \(K_{1,m}\), positivity returns for all \(h\in[h_+(m),6/(m+1)]\), and the complete graph-local sign test is explicit.
