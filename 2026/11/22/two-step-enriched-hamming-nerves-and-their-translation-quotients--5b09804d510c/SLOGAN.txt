For every Hamming graph \(K_q^{\square d}\), the inclusive ordered length-two enriched nerve is a wedge of \(q^d[1-d(q-1)+\binom{d+1}{2}(q-1)^2]-1\) two-spheres. Its free coordinate-translation quotient has fundamental group \((\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z)^d\), exact integral \(H_2\) rank \(\binom{d+1}{2}(q-1)^2-d(q-1)\), and a completely determined rational translation character upstairs.
