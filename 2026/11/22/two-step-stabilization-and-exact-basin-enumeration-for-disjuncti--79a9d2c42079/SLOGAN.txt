On every finite two-agent \(\mathsf{S5}\) rectangular grid, repeatedly asking whether either agent knows whether an event stabilizes after at most two applications, with exactly \(1+(2^m-2)(2^n-2)\) terminal events. Every terminal-event fiber has a closed inclusion–exclusion count, and a uniformly random event on an \(N\times N\) grid has a non-universal terminal event with asymptotic probability \(N^2 2^{2-2N}\).
