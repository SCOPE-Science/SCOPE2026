## Value — PASS

The existence of several interior reset-rate optima is an explicit open structural issue in the motivating primary source. This example establishes that both a local minimum and maximum are possible using ordinary geometric renewal resets on a finite inhomogeneous nearest-neighbor chain, without changing initial site, reset destination, or absorption mechanism. The proof yields a reusable exact-derivative/Descartes certificate for similar finite models, and supplies a qualitative counterexample to single-extremum intuition. The numerical difference in probability is small but the mathematical statement is about the existence and count of turning points, not an optimization gain. No minimal-length or general classification is claimed.
