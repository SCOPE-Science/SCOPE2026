# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The theorem specifies all positive rate/shape parameters and a precise, normalized incidence. Direct gamma-tail integration yields exact formulas for both reported counts and their derivatives. A self-contained integral representation of the reciprocal gamma hazard establishes its strict shape-dependent monotonicity; the relative-slope ratio therefore has exactly one turning point and a controlled asymptotic direction. The exact derivative cancellation for the auxiliary ratio function yields a single positive turning point of the reported-count ratio. Its endpoint and strict initial shape inequality produce exactly one count crossing, earlier than the unique positive logarithmic-growth crossing, in both shape regimes. Shape one and time-zero equality are explicitly handled. The standalone rational/numeric verifier passes 2,398 assertions on the integer-stage subfamily; the real-shape proof is analytic and does not rely on the finite test set.

## Originality

**PASS on inspected evidence, with historical-priority risk.** Eales and Riley (2023; arXiv:2307.03415) develop the gamma-like reporting convolution and discuss distorted apparent reproduction numbers, while Yu (2016; arXiv:1607.02689) proves much more general gamma-distribution CDF crossing results. Generic gamma single-crossing and gamma-hazard monotonicity are acknowledged prior facts, not contributions. The claim instead concerns the exact joint classification of count ratios and *instantaneous logarithmic-growth* ratios of two matched-mean reporting convolutions, including a strictly later second reversal and all positive real gamma shapes. No inspected primary full text supplied this complete assertion or dominated it. Bizzotto et al. (arXiv:2309.14801) was only available at abstract level; further stochastic-order and phase-type-filtering comparisons remain possible.

## Value

**PASS.** A matching *mean* reporting delay does not guarantee agreement of observed count rankings or of local growth-rate rankings. The complete exact sign and crossover ordering applies to every gamma shape, showing that these rankings can change at different horizons and that their directions are decided sharply by whether delay shape lies above or below one. This is a motivated theoretical surveillance diagnostic, particularly relevant to interpreting reporting-kernel variability after an abrupt change, rather than an isolated numerical calculation. It neither predicts observed outbreaks nor validates an intervention.
