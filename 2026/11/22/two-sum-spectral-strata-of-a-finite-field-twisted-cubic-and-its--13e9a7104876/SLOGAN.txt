The affine twisted cubic together with its tangent line has a complete four-level two-sum convolution: \(1,2,4,q\), with the four-representation level appearing exactly when \(\operatorname{char}\mathbb F_q\ne3\) and \(-2\) is square. Its exact fourth Fourier moment is \(q^3+6q^2-9q+3+8(q-1)\varepsilon_q\), and its twofold sumset has size \((3q^2-q)/2-(q-1)\varepsilon_q\).
