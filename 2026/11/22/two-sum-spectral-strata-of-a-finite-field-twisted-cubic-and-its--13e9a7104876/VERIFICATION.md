The collision equation factors as \((x-t)^2(x+2t)\), and the two roots merge exactly in characteristic three. The count of discriminant-square and discriminant-zero fibers then yields a complete histogram, sumset and additive energy. These deductions prove the all-odd-prime-power assertion.
