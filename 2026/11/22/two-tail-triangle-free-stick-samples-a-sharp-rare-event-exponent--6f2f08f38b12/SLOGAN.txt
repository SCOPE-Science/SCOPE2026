For independent stick lengths whose log-density decays exponentially on both sides, forbidding every triangle costs \(\exp\{-\frac{\alpha\beta}{2(\alpha+\beta)}(\log\varphi)n^2+o(n^2)\}\), rather than the one-sided Pareto rate. Conditioned on this rare event, the fraction of sticks below any fixed positive cutoff obeys an explicit quadratic large-deviation law centered at \(\alpha/(\alpha+\beta)\).
