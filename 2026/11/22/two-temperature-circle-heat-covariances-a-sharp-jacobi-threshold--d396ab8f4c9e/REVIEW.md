# Review of the two-temperature circle heat covariance theorem

## Correctness — PASS
The coefficient matrix has diagonal \(e^{-tn^2}\) and only distance-two offdiagonal terms \(\varepsilon e^{-s(n+1)^2}/2\). Diagonal normalization gives exactly \(I+(\varepsilon e^t/2)J_{s-t}\), so PSD is equivalent to the stated three cases: a diverging two-by-two normalized minor for \(s<t\), the free-adjacency spectrum when \(s=t\), and the symmetric extreme eigenvalues of a compact Jacobi operator for \(s>t\). Uniformly bounded-below cases give integrally strict positivity. At the compact spectral boundary, any null eigenvector has super-Gaussian decay by iterating its recurrence; division by the diagonal heat weights yields a nonzero smooth annihilating measure, while finite atomic Fourier coefficients cannot tend to zero. At equal time the free Jacobi adjacency has no eigenvectors at its spectrum edges. Perron–Frobenius/Jacobi positivity establishes spectral-branch monotonicity. Three distinct rational power ratios have reproducible exact Collatz–Wielandt inequalities plus rigorous infinite-tail bounds; their odd-even-odd ordering proves at least two switches. The executable standard-library checker was replayed successfully. The infinite theorem does not rely on sampling eigenvalues at finitely many parameters.

## Originality — PASS (best knowledge)
The primary preprint of Buhmann–Jäger (2021, arXiv:2105.02586) introduces spherical harmonic coefficient-block conditions for general nonradial positive definite kernels and studies different special block structures. The published single-temperature sum-modulated heat-kernel classification (*Exact Fejér–Riesz positivity and universal criticality for sum-modulated circle heat kernels*, 2026) reduces to a nonnegative trigonometric weight and asserts universality on all admissible boundary points. It does not address two unequal heat temperatures, Gaussian decaying offdiagonal Jacobi couplings, boundary non-universality or branch switches. The stationary quadratic-multiplier heat result (*A countably faceted positivity frontier for quadratically modulated circle heat kernels*, 2026) has diagonal rather than sum-coordinate Fourier couplings. The general nonstationary Fourier-feature setting of Jawaid–Karatas–Seewig (2026) is not a sharp parameter classification. Source full texts and published-finding corpus claim searches were compared by implication, not only by title. ## Value — PASS
The precise question is when two heat scales may be combined into a nonstationary *positive semidefinite covariance kernel* on the circle. Positivity determines whether the kernel is a valid covariance and universality determines whether its feature space is dense for approximation. The theorem provides a complete parameter classification and an unusual critical distinction between finite-data interpolation and universal approximation. The parity changes prove that the sharp threshold is not controlled by any single fixed Fourier parity and give rigorous, independently checkable arithmetic spectral certificates. This is a natural two-time extension motivated by nonstationary covariance design, not a minor normalization or fixed-parameter calculation.
