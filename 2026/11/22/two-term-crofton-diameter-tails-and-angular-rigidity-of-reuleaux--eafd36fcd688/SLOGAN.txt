For every fixed Reuleaux polygon of unit width with \(m\) positive arc angles \(\ell_j\), the invariant-line near-diameter chord probability has the sharp two-term expansion \(\frac{\varepsilon}{\pi}[m\log(1/\varepsilon)+m+2\sum_j\log(\sin\ell_j)]+o(\varepsilon)\). Its angular correction uniquely favors the regular Reuleaux \(m\)-gon among all shapes with the same arc count, giving a strict asymptotic geometric rigidity law beyond the previously known Reuleaux-triangle leading term.
