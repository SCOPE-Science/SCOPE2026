Two symmetric lattice searchers sharing a heavy-tailed time-varying jump-rate environment have an exact *unequal-observation-time* late-survival enhancement, governed by one positive-stable subordinator integral. At rate index \(\alpha=1/2\), observing the second survivor at twice the first observation time increases joint survival by the sharp factor \(4/3\) over independent rate histories, although this enhancement vanishes when the observation-time ratio tends to infinity.
