Auxiliary exact integer computation (finite cross-check, not an all-size proof)

For the complete signed simplicial boundary matrices of the two-tolerance complex of the disjoint union of three filled simplices, a Smith normal form was computed over ZZ, without reducing coefficients modulo a prime, using SymPy's smith_normal_form.

Block sizes (2,3,3): face vector in dimensions 0..4 = (8,28,56,70,20). Nonzero Smith ranks for d_1,d_2,d_3,d_4 = (7,21,35,19). Every nonzero Smith diagonal entry equals 1. Thus H_3=Z^16 and H_4=Z and all other reduced integral homology vanishes.

Block sizes (3,3,3): face vector in dimensions 0..4 = (9,36,84,126,45). Nonzero Smith ranks for d_1,d_2,d_3,d_4 = (8,28,56,42). Every nonzero Smith diagonal entry equals 1. Thus H_3=Z^28 and H_4=Z^3 and all other reduced integral homology vanishes.

These exact arithmetic checks corroborate the all-size proof but do not replace it or provide an independent audit.
