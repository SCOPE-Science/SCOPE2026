# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The cover pieces Uᵢ are joins with a full simplex, hence contractible. Counting vertices outside each block gives the pair intersection (Sᵢ*Sⱼ)∪B∪Sₖ and triple intersection B∪S₁∪S₂∪S₃, with B=D₁*D₂*D₃. B is simply connected with H₂ free of rank xyz. Adding a same-block edge creates one H₁ generator without changing H₂; to select it choose a return path through a vertex of the other block. In a pair intersection the i,j paths may be chosen inside Dᵢ*Dⱼ, so the corresponding cycles map to zero inside Sᵢ*Sⱼ, while every k-edge cycle survives. Thus the H₁ differential is a signed integral isomorphism. The overlap of Sᵢ*Sⱼ and B is the connected graph Dᵢ*Dⱼ, whose free H₁ is the quotient of H₂ of their union by H₂(B). This supplies a retraction to H₂(B) for each pair. In particular the signed triple-to-pair H₂ differential is split injective by projecting to just one pair and using its retraction. Its cokernel has rank Σ_{i<j}(nᵢ−1)(nⱼ−1)nₖ−xyz=B₃. H₃ of a pair is the H₃ of the join Sᵢ*Sⱼ, with rank the product of its graph cycle ranks, so their direct sum is B₄. The cover spectral sequence has only these two positive-degree entries after the first differential and no higher differentials or extension problems. Van Kampen applied first to two cover pieces and then the third makes X simply connected, and H₂=0 gives π₂=0. For an independent check of the homotopy upgrade, choose A=∨^{B₃}S³→X inducing an isomorphism on π₃ and H₃. Its mapping-cylinder pair with A is 3-connected. Relative Hurewicz in degree four and the homotopy and homology sequences show π₄(X)→H₄(X) is onto: π₄(X) surjects onto π₄ of the pair and H₄(X) maps isomorphically onto H₄ of the pair. Representatives of a free H₄ basis therefore extend A to the asserted homology-equivalent sphere wedge, and the simply connected Whitehead theorem finishes. The argument includes size-two blocks; B₄ is positive exactly when at least two blocks reach size three. For (2,3,m) the ranks are 7m−5 and binom(m−1,2).

## value

PASS

A natural fixed-tolerance three-facet family has an exact integral homotopy classification, including a sharp transition to two simultaneous sphere dimensions. This explains how lower homology arises from triple gluing while the top rank is a sum of pair contributions. It substantially extends the single nine-vertex profile and answers more than either a Leray bound or modular Betti computations can.
