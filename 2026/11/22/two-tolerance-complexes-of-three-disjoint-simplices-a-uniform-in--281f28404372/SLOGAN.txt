The two-tolerance complex of three disjoint filled simplices, of arbitrary sizes at least two, has a complete integral homotopy type: a wedge of spheres only in dimensions three and four, with explicit polynomial/binomial multiplicities. The fourth-dimensional family appears exactly when at least two blocks have at least three vertices; for block sizes \((2,3,m)\), the answer is \(\bigvee^{7m-5}S^3\vee\bigvee^{\binom{m-1}{2}}S^4\).
