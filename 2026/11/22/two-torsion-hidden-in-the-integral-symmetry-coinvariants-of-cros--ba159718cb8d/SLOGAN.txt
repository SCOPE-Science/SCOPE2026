For the \(r\)-tupling of a \(2r\)-dimensional cross-polytope boundary, integral first homology is free, but its coordinate-flip coinvariants are \((\mathbb Z/2)^{r^2\binom{2r}{r}/2}\), and one order-two class survives every signed-coordinate symmetry for all \(r\ge2\). The full geometric quotient is nevertheless contractible: these are torsion classes of the homology coinvariant module, not of quotient-space homology.
