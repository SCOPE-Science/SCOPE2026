# Scientific assessment of the rotational homotopy classification

The proof computes the geometric orbit cell structure after barycentric subdivision. Valid elementary collapses eliminate all three-cells. After a spanning-tree contraction, homotopies of attaching maps and removals of generator–disk pairs give the exact two-complex with one loop, one degree-two disk and one constantly attached disk. Each step after the tree contraction is a move on the homotopy type of the two-complex, not just on a group presentation.

The earlier *Two-torsion and a nonzero cup square in the octagonal noncrossing matching quotient* already treats the identical object and actions. It computes the rotational homology, fundamental group and cup square and shows the dihedral quotient contractible. Those established invariants and the dihedral classification are not new claims here. The earlier rotational calculation does not remove the three-cells by homotopy equivalences or otherwise identify the full rotational homotopy type.

The wedge model determines actual attaching maps and exhibits a spherical representative splitting off the known free second-homology class, a fact not furnished by chain ranks or a fundamental-group presentation. It gives a concrete homotopy model of a natural quotient without purporting to classify a family of polygon sizes. Unlocated equivalent coverage remains possible.
