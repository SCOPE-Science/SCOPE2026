The ordinary geometric quotient of the Möbius–Kantor graph's independence complex by all 96 graph automorphisms has homotopy type \(M(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z,3)\), a Moore space with one order-two third-homology class. It is rationally acyclic but integrally noncontractible; the result is certified by exact quotient-cell collapses, integral Smith forms, and a complete fundamental-group reduction.
