A gambler's-ruin walk with just two complementary sitewise transition probabilities already supports two strict turning points of its lower-boundary absorption probability as a geometric reset rate varies, uniformly for every bias \(0<\varepsilon\le3/50\). This is a sharp transition-alphabet boundary because the spatially homogeneous one-probability case is monotone; at \(\varepsilon=1/20\), an exact quartic Sturm certificate gives precisely two turning points.
