# Scientific review: two-transvection fibers in finite symplectic groups

## Correctness — PASS
The universal count is supported by a direct Wall-line bijection with a unique first transvection for each nonisotropic rank-two image line. The rank-one and rank-at-least-three exclusions follow from rank-one operator arithmetic. Separate \(\operatorname{SL}_2(q)\) and symmetric-binary-form enumerations give the exact multiplicity tables, including the alternating binary exception in characteristic two. The stated ambient plane counts and support formula agree with all checked finite-field cases. The packaged checker exhaustively enumerates all ordered transvection pairs in ten small fields/dimensions, including extension fields. Finite tests are validation checks, not universal proofs.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)
The closest classical source is Knüppel, *Factorization into Symmetries and Transvections of Given Conjugacy Classes* (1992), especially the familiar Wall-form shortening argument. The current formulas do not claim originality for that argument. Ellers–Malzan (1991) treat minimal lengths with fixed conjugacy classes and remain a risk because their full text was unavailable. Özden (arXiv:1812.04720, targeted Introduction and Section 4.5) proves stability of structure constants without stating this evaluated multiplicity census; the two bodies of statements are not interchangeable. Arias-de-Reyna–Dieulefait–Wiese (arXiv:1405.1258) classify subgroups with transvections and do not compute two-factor fibers. Existing published exact two-factor results for the full general linear group do not imply symplectic-restricted counts. The available comparisons support provisional originality of the complete explicit all-characteristic symplectic evaluation, not independence or exhaustive historical priority.

## Value — PASS
The square of the full transvection class sum is a natural first convolution invariant. The result gives both each prescribed target's structure coefficient and the complete rank-two target multiplicity spectrum, not merely the support or a minimal-length decision. The characteristic-two alternating-symmetric obstruction and the \(q=2\) rank-one gap are genuine boundary behavior, while the formulas hold for an unbounded family of symplectic ranks and field sizes. This exact census is a motivated algebraic structural invariant.

## Sources and residual risks
The Wall shortening method and standard symplectic rational conjugacy classes are classical. An all-characteristic evaluated result could exist in older association-scheme, finite classical-group or character-theoretic literature not indexed by the consulted searches. The fixed-class factorization article of Ellers–Malzan was not available in full. Independent mathematical and historical audit is necessary before public priority claims.
