The matched-null three-bin discrete roughness variance is not monotone along the reflection-symmetric probability profiles \((a,1-2a,a)\): its Gaussian-scale coefficient has exactly one strict global peak at \(a=0.2400332425\ldots\) and one later strict local valley at \(a=0.4749144718\ldots\). A strictly convex derivative numerator proves that there are exactly two turning points, giving a sharp variance-maximizing null shape for this minimal two-edge histogram.
