In an idealized two-visit ecological occupancy survey with unknown false-alarm, true-detection, and occupancy parameters, **unequal** visit lengths are necessary and sufficient for global identification from two binary visit histories. For short fixed total observation budgets, the two D-optimal duration fractions approach \((1-1/\sqrt3)/2\) and \((1+1/\sqrt3)/2\), while equal durations lose a full information dimension.
