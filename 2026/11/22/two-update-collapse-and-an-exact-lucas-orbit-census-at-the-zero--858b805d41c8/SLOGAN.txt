At the zero-field selection edge of the normalized separable-field automaton, every finite periodic grid reaches either extinction or a row/column-stripe two-cycle within two updates. For all side lengths at least four, the complete primitive two-cycle count is \((C_H+C_W)/2\), where \(C_n=L_n+2\cos(n\pi/3)-2\) and \(L_n\) is the Lucas sequence.
