For the backward-Euler biharmonic resolvent \((I+hL^2)^{-1}\) on any positively weighted star, the **two least-conductive leaves** are always the last pair to become nonnegative; the full matrix is entrywise nonnegative precisely at \(h=0\) and for \(h\ge\tau\), where a strictly decreasing one-variable equation determines \(\tau\). A sharp dimension-dependent interval for \(\tau\) has a factor of \(m-1\) between its lower and supremal upper endpoint for \(m\) leaves.
