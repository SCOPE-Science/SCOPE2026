For two hard-core bosons initially occupying opposite shores of \(K_{r,r}\), the probability of finding both on one shore has exact supremum \((2r-1)/r^2\), but this supremum is never attained at any finite time for any integer \(r\ge3\). At time \(\pi/(2r)\), the coherent probability vanishes as \(2/r\), while the corresponding classical exclusion probability tends to \((1-e^{-2\pi})/2\).
