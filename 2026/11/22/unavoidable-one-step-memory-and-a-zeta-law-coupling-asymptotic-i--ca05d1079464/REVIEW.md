# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The map is defined on the half-open cube with fixed translation and integer diagonal expansions. Haar invariance is proved by three successive integer-multiplication integrations. A direct finite-branch covariance identity reduces the target to one periodic Bernoulli integral. Its positivity for every real quadratic coupling is proved using the exact first-period contribution \(1/15\), convexity on each subsequent complete period, and a strict bound on any incomplete period. The asymptotic coefficient follows from the absolutely convergent Fourier series of the second Bernoulli polynomial and the Fresnel integral, with an explicit order of the tail. The included standalone numerical and exact-rational checks corroborate the algebra but are not substitutes for these infinite proofs.

## Originality — PASS within searched evidence

The complete lawful full text of Zhang–Dong–Zhang–Ding (2023) was compared on the actual recurrence, stationary versus encrypted-image correlations, and statistical sections. Published results on this same map about integer-fiber periodic lifting and periodic-grid equidistribution were also read in full. Neither makes the all-quadratic-coupling positivity assertion or computes the zeta-law correlation constant. Related general skew-product and quadratic-mod-one literature has distinct objects or conclusions. A classical equivalent identity for the exact Bernoulli integral may exist outside the inspected corpus, so publication priority is not independently certified.

## Value — PASS

The originating map was proposed partly for statistical behavior of chaotic coordinates, yet uniform one-time output and positive Lyapunov exponents do not settle temporal decorrelation. The theorem establishes a previously unquantified obstruction to eliminating one-step real-coordinate memory by merely increasing quadratic coupling, and its sharp algebraic decay constant quantifies the remaining signal. This is a natural invariant of the actual published map, not an arbitrary test statistic or a tiny numerical improvement. The theorem does not make a cryptographic-security conclusion.

## Closest literature and limits

Zhang, Dong, Zhang and Ding (Entropy 2023, DOI 10.3390/e25071005) introduce the map, including a time-varying disturbance in experiments. The two cited public mathematical results concern periodic-point arithmetic and periodic-point equidistribution, not two-time coordinate covariance. The present claim assumes a fixed disturbance and an explicitly specified stationary measure. General higher-lag laws and noninteger diagonal gains remain outside its scope.
