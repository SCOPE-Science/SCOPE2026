In the quadratic three-dimensional modular polynomial map, a uniform stationary distribution never eliminates the one-step covariance of either real fiber coordinate, regardless of how strong its finite quadratic coupling becomes. That covariance has an exact Bernoulli-polynomial integral and decays with the sharp asymptotic constant \(\zeta(5/2)/(8\pi^2 a)\) for the first coordinate at coupling magnitude \(B\), times \(B^{-1/2}\).
