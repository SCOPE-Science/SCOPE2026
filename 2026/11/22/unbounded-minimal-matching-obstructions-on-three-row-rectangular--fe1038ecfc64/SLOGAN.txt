Every three-row grid \(P_3\square P_{2n}\) with \(n\ge2\) has eight explicit inclusion-minimal partial domino tilings of size \(n+1\) that cannot be completed to a perfect tiling, although removing any one domino makes completion possible. Thus the perfect-matching complex of a fixed-width three-row strip has squarefree minimal nonfaces of unbounded cardinality, in sharp contrast to the bounded two-edge obstructions of two-row strips.
