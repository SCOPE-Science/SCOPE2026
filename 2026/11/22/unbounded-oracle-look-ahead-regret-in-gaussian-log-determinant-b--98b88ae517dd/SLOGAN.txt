For any fixed rational look-ahead fraction \(0<\beta<1\) and probe cost \(m\ge2\), two-level positive-definite spectra arbitrarily close to zero make even an **exact logarithmic** short-rank look-ahead select one Gaussian log-determinant probe, while a same-budget multiprobe alternative has an arbitrarily smaller mean-square error. With \(\beta=3/4\) and \(m=2\), the exact one-to-many risk ratio is \((4j+1)(1-1/(128j))\).
