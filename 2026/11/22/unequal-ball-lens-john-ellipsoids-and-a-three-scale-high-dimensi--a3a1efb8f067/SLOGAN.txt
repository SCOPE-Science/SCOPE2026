For the intersection of two unequal Euclidean balls in every dimension at least four, the unique maximal-volume inscribed ellipsoid changes from axial-tip contact to two latitude contacts at an exact radius-dependent threshold. At fixed unequal radii, its positive contact latitude exhibits a sharp high-dimensional transition at center separation \(\sqrt{R^2-r^2}\): a critical \(m^{-1/4}\) power separates order-one and \(m^{-1/2}\) behavior.
