For every prime at which the Bell numbers have full least period \(N_p=(p^p-1)/(p-1)\), each nonzero index spacing yields exactly \((p^{p-\ell}-1)/(p-1)\) length-\(\ell\) progressions of Bell zeros modulo \(p\), for \(1\le\ell\le p\). Thus every spacing, including nonunit spacings, admits exactly one \((p-1)\)-term zero progression and no \(p\)-term one; the consecutive-spacing instance was previously known.
