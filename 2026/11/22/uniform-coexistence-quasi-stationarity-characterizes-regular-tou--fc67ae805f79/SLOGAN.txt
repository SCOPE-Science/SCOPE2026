For a multi-allele tournament Wright–Fisher diffusion with positive selection, the uniform simplex law is quasi-stationary **exactly when the tournament is regular**. In that case the first species-loss time is exponential with rate \(\gamma\binom d2\), independent of selection intensity and tournament orientation, and the product-coordinate Doob transform has invariant \(\operatorname{Dirichlet}(2,\dots,2)\) law.
