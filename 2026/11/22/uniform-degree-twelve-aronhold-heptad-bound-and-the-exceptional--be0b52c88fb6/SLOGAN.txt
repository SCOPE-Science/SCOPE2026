Every smooth plane quartic over an odd finite field acquires a Galois-invariant Aronhold heptad over an extension of degree at most 12. The exact least-degree spectrum of the abstract symplectic Frobenius action is \(\{1,2,3,4,6,8,9,12\}\), with degree 12 forced by one class acting in 24 twelve-cycles; realization of that class by a curve is not asserted.
