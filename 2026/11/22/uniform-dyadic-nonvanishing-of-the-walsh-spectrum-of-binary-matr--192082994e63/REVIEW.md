# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof fixes the binary field, off-diagonal frequencies, unnormalized transforms, and all dimensions \(n\ge2\). Formula (1) is derived by subspace-lattice Möbius inversion and the Gaussian binomial identity; (3) is exact diagonal orthogonality; (5) uses the top multilinear determinant coefficient; (6) proves the exact valuation, including the separate size-two endpoint. Two independent programs passed against all characters in sizes two through four, and a complete direct transform passed in size five. Exhaustive small instances do not substitute for the all-size proof.

## Originality — PASS under bounded comparison
Lewis et al. establish the unsigned q-derangement count but not a universal signed-character theorem. Delsarte's full-matrix rank-transform character table is a premise rather than an implication of the restricted transform, since summing over diagonal completions requires (5). Salmond et al. analyze unsigned probabilities for fixed-zero patterns. Indexed related statements on trace-parity slices of invertible binary matrices and rank-two matrix balls involve different subsets and do not imply the asserted all-frequency congruence. The bounded comparisons did not identify stronger covering results; priority risk remains for unindexed equivalent derivations in rank-metric harmonic analysis.

## Value — PASS
The zero-diagonal invertible matrices are a canonical finite-field analogue of permutation derangements. Exact uniform two-adic nonvanishing answers a natural Fourier information-recovery question at every size, rather than a single enumerative instance. It determines the full convolution rank and exact determinant valuation, and supplies a uniform quantitative inverse bound. This information is not determined by the known unsigned count alone.

## Limits and publication status
The sharp global minimum multiplier for all \(n\) is not proved; the field and zero pattern are fixed. The checks are a self-review, not independent certification.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
