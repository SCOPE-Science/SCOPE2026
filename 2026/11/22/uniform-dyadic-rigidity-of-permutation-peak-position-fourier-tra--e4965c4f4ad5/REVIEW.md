# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof is uniform over every permutation order and every Fourier character. The two central lemmas are rederived: an alternating-block maximum-position recurrence shows each normalized odd Euler zigzag number has an odd denominator, and the i.i.d.-rank model factors joint peak events across disjoint alternating blocks. The exact Walsh expansion is a dyadic unit. A singleton frequency proves the optimal modulus, and character diagonalization proves the determinant valuation. All claims use unnormalized counting conventions. Three genuinely different exact methods corroborate the formulas through the explicitly bounded finite test ranges; no finite data is used as an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS under bounded comparison.** The 2012 peak-set polynomial paper enumerates profile fibers but does not state a uniform Fourier congruence or multiplier nonvanishing. The 2003 peak-algebra paper studies group-algebra structure, not this additive Boolean pushforward transform. The 2015 Coxeter-type paper generalizes peak enumeration rather than giving all-character arithmetic invertibility. Related peak/valley covariance literature concerns second-order sampling properties, not all Walsh characters. These related results do not by their stated formulas establish the exact valuation: this requires the alternating-block probability integrality argument. Priority risk remains for unindexed equivalents, and some related literature could be reviewed only through abstracts.

**Value — PASS.** The finding gives a natural all-order invertibility theorem and an optimal, frequency-independent congruence for a widely studied peak-position statistic. The exact dyadic determinant valuation translates the arithmetic rigidity into a global invariant for every convolution dimension, and the proof explicitly explains why peaks avoid Fourier cancellation despite nontrivial dependencies at separation two. This is a structural statement rather than a cutoff, an enumeration of an arbitrary example or a simple restatement of the known peak-polynomial formulas.

**Limitations.** The odd spectrum and the optimal Hilbert inverse norm are undetermined in general; variants with cyclic or value-defined peaks are excluded. Residual historical-priority uncertainty is not independent validation.
