# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The exact law follows from the graphical voter/coalescing-random-walk duality and a rate count: with \(k\) occupied peripheral ancestries, each of \(k\) random walkers has exactly \(k\) killing destinations among its \(r\) possible targets. The resulting death rate \(k^2/r\) is independent of the occupied subset, giving independent exponential holding times. Partial fraction residues are exact factorial ratios. The uniform tail proof has an absolute coefficient bound and a uniform spectral gap \(4-1=3\) in normalized time, and a uniform-integrability argument establishes the mean. A separate forward-generator exact-rational checker passed 155 checks. Risk: the limit concerns increasing petal count and not an arbitrary initial voter distribution.

## Originality — PASS on examined evidence, provisional
The founding voter duality and complete-graph fixed-zealot mean calculations are established. The nearest star-hub theorem is exactly the \(r=1\) boundary, not the \(r\ge2\) laws or their uniformity. Published clique-bouquet first-passage results use a distinct pure-birth infection process, with two slow poles and a double-logarithmic shift, and do not imply the single-slow-pole voter law. A general finite-dimensional phase-type or coalescent theorem could provide an alternative implicit route, but the examined primary papers and indexed results did not explicitly cover the uniform all-size full-distribution statement. Historical priority requires independent corroboration.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This is an exactly solved first-consensus-time distribution and a genuinely two-parameter universality statement in a natural family of social/contact networks. It demonstrates a physical kinetic distinction: identical topology yields distinct extreme-value centerings under voter imitation and bond first-passage infection. The uniform-in-petal-size aspect gives a limit not obtained merely by applying a fixed-size extreme-value theorem separately to each graph. The sharply motivated observable is whole-network absorption time, not a contrived graph invariant.

## Limitations
One fixed hub and one-color initial condition; independent rate-one node updates, symmetric within-petal connections, and an unrestricted petal size but a growing number of petals. Independent audit is not yet performed and novelty is not a proof of historical priority.
