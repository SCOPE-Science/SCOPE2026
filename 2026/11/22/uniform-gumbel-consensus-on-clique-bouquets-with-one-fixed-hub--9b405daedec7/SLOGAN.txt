In a clique bouquet with one permanently fixed positive hub, the exact consensus time for initially negative voters is a maximum of independent sums of exponentials with rates \(j^2/r\). After dividing time by petal size \(r\), centering by \(\log(2mr/(r+1))\) yields a standard Gumbel limit **uniformly over every petal-size sequence**; unlike bond first-passage flooding on the same network, there is no \(\log\log m\) correction.
