Products of independent finite-field matrices with uniformly random one-dimensional kernels lose rank by a universal pure-death law, whether the factors are idempotent projections, regular nilpotents, or another corank-one ensemble. Their exact zero-absorption time is a sum of independent geometric variables, with an explicit finite-length survival spectrum and a sharp \(\exp(-(q-1)t)\) limiting tail after scaling by \(q^n-1\).
