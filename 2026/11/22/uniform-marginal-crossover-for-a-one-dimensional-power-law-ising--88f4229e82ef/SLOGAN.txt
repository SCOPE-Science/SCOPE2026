For an infinite power-law Ising chain, tuning the decay exponent within \(O(1/\log(1/h))\) of the marginal value produces a universal crossover of the optimized contiguous-droplet barrier. Its leading profile is \(\Phi(\lambda)=(1-e^{-\lambda})/\lambda\), and the complete bounded correction is \(\gamma-\lambda e^{-\lambda}\), uniformly across both sides of the transition.
