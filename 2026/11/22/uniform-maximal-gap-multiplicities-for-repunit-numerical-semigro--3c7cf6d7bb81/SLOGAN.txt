For every base \(b\ge2\) and repunit-semigroup length \(n\ge2\), the maximal missing-integer blocks have exactly \(b^{n-1}\) distinct lengths, **each occurring exactly \(b\) times**. Their complete length generating polynomial factors as \(b z^{n-1}\sum_{v=0}^{b-1}z^v\prod_{j=2}^{n-1}\sum_{v=0}^{b-1}z^{vR_j}\), where \(R_j=(b^j-1)/(b-1)\).
