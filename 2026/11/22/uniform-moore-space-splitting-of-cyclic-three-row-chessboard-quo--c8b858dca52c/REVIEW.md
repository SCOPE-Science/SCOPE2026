# Scientific review of the uniform cyclic three-row quotient splitting

## Correctness — PASS
The claim is about the geometric quotient of the ordinary chessboard complex, not a vertex-orbit simplicial quotient. A face contains at most one rook in each row, so nonidentity cyclic column shifts preserve no nonempty face. The quotient has exactly three vertices, \(3(n-1)\) edges and \((n-1)(n-2)\) triangles. The three families of edge generators and every triangle attaching word are explicitly derived. After collapsing a spanning tree, the proof removes exactly \(\(3n-6\)\) one/two-cell pairs, using separate relators containing each eliminated generator exactly once. The remaining one-loop presentation has \((n-2)(n-4)\) two-cells, with all degrees in \(\{0,-n\}\) and at least one equal to \(-n\); its homotopy cofiber is the claimed Moore space wedge \(\(n^2-6n+7\)\) two-spheres. The arithmetic and relator reindexing hold for *every* \(n\ge5\). Exhaustive finite face-orbit, boundary-composition and modular-rank checks at widths five to ten, plus symbolic tests of the degree formula through width one hundred, corroborate but do not replace the uniform proof.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected evidence
The classical 1997 Friedman–Hanlon work and the 2004 Shareshian–Wachs first public preprint determine nonequivariant homology and connectivity, not the homotopy type of a geometric column-rotation quotient. The closest inspected quotient results have a different number of rows (four-by-five, five-by-five), different matching capacity (two rather than one), or the exceptional three-by-four torus. The existing quotient statements and generic covering calculations can determine the fundamental group and the free homology rank, **but those implications do not establish the full Moore-space wedge homotopy splitting**. The new exact two-cell homotopy transformations provide that missing implication. An unindexed equivalent splitting or general topological presentation theorem remains a residual novelty risk; no absolute priority assertion is made.

## Value — PASS
The natural three-row family enters its simply connected covering range at width five, immediately after the established three-by-four surface exception. The theorem determines every full cyclic-column quotient's homotopy type, including all composite widths, exhibits an unbounded family of fundamental-group torsion orders, and shows that all the remaining quotient two-dimensional topology separates as a free sphere wedge. This is substantially stronger than a Betti-number table or an arbitrary isolated computation, and the explicit uniform presentation reduction can be reused in adjacent symmetry-quotient questions.

## Scope and limitations
The conclusion does not address arbitrary column permutations, other row counts or higher-capacity matching complexes. No independent audit is available; potential equivalent literature outside indexed sources remains the chief scientific uncertainty. The included checker is not an infinite-range proof.
