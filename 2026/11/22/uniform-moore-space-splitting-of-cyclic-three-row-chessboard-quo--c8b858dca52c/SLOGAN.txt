For every \(n\ge5\), the geometric quotient of the ordinary three-by-\(n\) nonattacking-rook complex by cyclically shifting columns has homotopy type \(M(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z,1)\vee\bigvee^{n^2-6n+7}S^2\). A uniform elimination of its two-cell attaching words yields this exact Moore-space splitting, not merely its integral homology.
