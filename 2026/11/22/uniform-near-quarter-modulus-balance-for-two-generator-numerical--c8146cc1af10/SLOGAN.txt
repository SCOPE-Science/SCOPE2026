For every coprime pair of generators that are units modulo \(m\), the counts of missing integers in different residue classes differ by at most \(\lceil m/4\rceil\), no matter how large the generators are. A single cyclic recurrence reconstructs every count, and infinite families achieve this bound whenever \(m\equiv0,3\pmod4\); for all other moduli the bound is within one of an explicit witness.
