For odd \(q\), every permutation binomial \(X^r(X^{d(q-1)}+a)\) over \(\mathbb F_{q^2}\) with \(\gcd(d,q+1)=2\) has the same signature \((-1)^{(r-1)/2}\), independent of its admissible coefficient. Every nonzero admissible coefficient is necessarily a square, so this entire two-to-one index family has a rigid parity spectrum.
