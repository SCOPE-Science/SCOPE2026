For the cubic global-center flow \(\dot x=y\), \(\dot y=-x-\alpha x^3-\beta xy^2\) with \(\beta>0\), its physical period decreases strictly and obeys \(T(A)\sim2\pi/[A\sqrt\beta\sqrt{1+\alpha A^2}]\) uniformly over every \(\alpha\ge0\). The original-time turning-point deficit \(e^{-\beta(A^2-x^2)}\) converges in total variation to the universal arcsine law, including the singular crossover \(\alpha=O(A^{-2})\).
