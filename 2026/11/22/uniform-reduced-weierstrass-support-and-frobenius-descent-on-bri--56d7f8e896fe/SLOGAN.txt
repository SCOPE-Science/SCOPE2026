For every finite field of characteristic at least seven, Bring's genus-four curve has exactly 60 simple Weierstrass points, all with gap sequence \((1,2,3,5)\). Their Frobenius cycles are uniformly \(1^{60}\), \(2^{30}\), or \(3^{20}\), according to the splitting of \(U^3+2U^2+3U+4\); no intermediate number of rational Weierstrass points occurs.
