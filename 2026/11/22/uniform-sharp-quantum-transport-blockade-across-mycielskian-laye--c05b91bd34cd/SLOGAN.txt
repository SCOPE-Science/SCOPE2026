On the Mycielskian of *any* fixed-degree regular graph, a coherent adjacency quantum walker launched from the hub reaches the entire original graph with probability at most \((4r^2+o(1))/n\) uniformly over all times, and this bound is asymptotically sharp. The classical edge-rate-one walk reaches the original layer with limiting probability \((1-e^{-2rt})/2\) at every fixed positive time.
