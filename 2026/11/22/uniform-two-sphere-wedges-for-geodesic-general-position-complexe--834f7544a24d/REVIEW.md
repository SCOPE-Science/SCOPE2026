# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In the stated categorical product every distinct nonadjacent pair has a common neighbor, so exactly the induced P₃ triples are forbidden. A P₃-free induced graph is a union of cliques. Independent sets lie in a row or column. A transversal three-clique cannot have a cell nonadjacent to all of it, and for a two-clique the only possible cells outside its component are the two opposite rectangle corners. This recovers the four face families. A row subset of at least three cells missing the chosen vertex has no coface outside its row; removing in descending cardinality leaves just its coface obtained by adjoining the chosen vertex. A rectangle triangle lies in exactly one higher-dimensional simplex, its rectangle. These are elementary collapses, leaving a finite two-complex with the complete one-skeleton. In the original complex, any missing triangle consists of two cells sharing a row or column and a third cell adjacent to both. In the row case insert X in the triangle and then Y in BCX to obtain precisely the five valid faces in the proof; transpose for the column case. Thus every triangular loop in the original complex contracts, and these generate edge loops. The row, column, matching and rectangle contributions to χ are 3, −2n, −6 binom(n,2)+n(n−1)(n−2), and 9 binom(n,2). The collapsed complex is simply connected with free integral H₂ of rank χ−1 and no other positive homology. Spherical representatives of an integral basis give a homology equivalence from the wedge, hence a homotopy equivalence by the simply connected Whitehead theorem. The Chinese remainder isomorphism for q prime and q≠3 identifies unit differences with nonzero differences in both factors, proving the Cayley specialization.

The face classification, face-count polynomial and extremal size are prior. A previously indexed rook-graph result also covers the n=3 wedge through an actual graph isomorphism. The supported distinct contribution is the integral homotopy type for the categorical family beyond that overlap, with the two-dimensional collapse and uniform loop contractions. ## value

PASS

The result determines a natural simplicial complex up to integral homotopy for an infinite graph family, not merely an extremal size, count or mod-two invariant. The collapse removes facets of unbounded dimension, and the explicit loop fillings explain why the remaining rank is all in two-spheres. This sharpens the known face enumerator in a way that counts alone cannot supply.
