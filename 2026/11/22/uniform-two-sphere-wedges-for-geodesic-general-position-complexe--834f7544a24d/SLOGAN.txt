For every \(n\ge3\), the geodesic general-position complex of the categorical product \(K_3\times K_n\) is homotopy equivalent to a wedge of exactly \((2n^3-3n^2-3n+4)/2\) two-spheres. A uniform simplicial collapse and explicit fillings of every missing triangle give a two-dimensional simply connected spine, including the unitary Cayley graphs on \(\mathbb Z/(3q)\mathbb Z\) for primes \(q\ge5\).
