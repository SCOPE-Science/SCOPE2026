For exponentially decaying \(\ell^q\)-radial Fourier weights on \(\mathbb Z^d\), every finite \(q\ge2\) has the exact subconvolution threshold \(p>1+(d-1)(1-1/q)\). Below this threshold, axis convolution ratios grow by a precise power; at it they grow logarithmically, while above it the axial ratio has an explicit convergent limit.
