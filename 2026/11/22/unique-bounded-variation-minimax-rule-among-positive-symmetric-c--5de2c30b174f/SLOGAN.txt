Among all positive symmetric three-node quadrature rules exact on cubics, a unique rule minimizes worst-case error for real Jordan-bounded-variation integrands: its outer nodes are \(1/2\pm d_*\), where \(12d_*^2(1-d_*)=1\), and its sharp error is \(d_*/2\). The optimum balances two different extremal jump locations and reduces the sharp variation-one error by about 18.7% relative to three-point Gauss–Legendre quadrature.
