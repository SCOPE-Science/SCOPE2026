# Exact verification and limits

`verify.py` runs under Python 3 with SymPy installed. From a directory containing the extracted files, execute `python verify.py`. The code compares exact symbolic algebra in the quadratic field generated by the square root of 337; it checks the third critical image and its factorization, the admissible radical control and three distinct critical orbit points, all five Markov endpoint images, the transition matrix characteristic polynomial, and primitivity of the two-state core.

Analytical proof obligations beyond a checker are the parameter-wide branch-order inequalities, strict expansion, the standard entropy formula for finite expanding Markov interval maps, and the finite-entry argument. Their derivations appear in RESULT.md. Numerical simulations, sample orbits and finite graph tests would not establish these global statements. No independent mathematical audit or hardware reproduction was performed.
