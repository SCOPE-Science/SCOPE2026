For a damped three-vertex diffusion star with one controlled hub and a fixed total edge budget, the unique unordered conductance pair maximizing the infinite-horizon Gramian determinant is never balanced: its product is the unique positive root of an explicit quadratic. As leakage tends to zero, the weaker optimal edge scales as \(2(1+\sqrt5)\mu/3\), and the maximal determinant grows like \((5\sqrt5-11)/(1152\mu^2S)\).
