Two regular \((N+1)\)-gons give a minimal-node interpolation design for bianalytic disk polynomials; their exact Gram determinant is maximized uniquely by a half-step angular stagger, an outer boundary ring, and one explicitly characterized interior squared radius \(t_N\). The optimal radius satisfies \(t_N=1-4/N+O(N^{-2})\), and degree one has a special aligned-grid singularity absent at every higher degree.
