On every wheel, unrestricted minimization of the combinatorial-Laplacian eigenratio \(\lambda_{m+1}/\lambda_2\) has a unique conductance design: every rim edge is \(m/[4\sin^2(\pi\lfloor m/2\rfloor/m)]\) times each spoke. As the rim grows, this design sends almost the entire budget to the rim and sacrifices a factor asymptotic to \(m/4\) in absolute algebraic connectivity compared with the separately known fastest-diffusion design.
