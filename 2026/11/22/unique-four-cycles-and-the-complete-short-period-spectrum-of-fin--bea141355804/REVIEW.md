# Self-review

**Correctness: PASS.** Integral Sylvester-resultant identities force every four-periodic field point into the explicitly checked four-point orbit. The same elimination excludes three-cycles and elementary equations exclude two-cycles. Coordinatewise finite-product decomposition then yields an exact count; the packaged checker reproduces the algebraic identities and tests many finite fields.

**Originality: PASS (provisional historical assessment).** Chernev–Chernev proves existence of a four-cycle in a specified prime-field congruence class, whereas the claim characterizes the complete four-periodic set for all fields and counts all four-cycles of every finite reduced commutative ring. Lipkovski's earlier results cover aspects of periods two and three, which are not claimed as novel. Semantic database searches and accessible prior findings showed no statement implying uniqueness of four-cycles. A historical overlap could remain in earlier unindexed short-cycle calculations.

**Value: PASS.** This is a complete sharp short-cycle invariant on a natural, all-size class of finite rings. It replaces mere existence of the first nontrivial cycle by exact multiplicity, reveals that field factors have at most one such orbit, and gives an explicit multiplicative census for any finite product of fields. This is a useful baseline for the source's open longer-cycle and component-classification problems.

**Closest literature and limitations.** Chernev–Chernev, arXiv:2611.07521v1, §5; Lipkovski, *Structure Graphs of Rings* (2017), especially early cycle sections and Proposition 7; Lipkovski (2012), referenced for short-cycle criteria. The result does not classify periods above four or nonreduced rings. The 2012 original full text was not accessible at the checked endpoint and retains priority risk.
