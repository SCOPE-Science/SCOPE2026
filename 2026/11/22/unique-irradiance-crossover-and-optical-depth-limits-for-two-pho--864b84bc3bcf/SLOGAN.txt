In an idealized two-stratum Beer–Lambert canopy with Steele responses \(t e^{-at}\) and \(t e^{-bt}\), \(a>b>0\), the globally best vertical ordering reverses **exactly once** as incident light increases: the pointwise less productive type belongs above only beyond the unique crossover. The crossover has distinct thin-canopy and thick-canopy scaling laws, and a concrete example gains about 83.8% in modeled productivity from correct high-light ordering.
