# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The renewal-derived full-rank stationary matrix, equatorial qubit SLD metric, Jacobian and determinant are explicit. Descartes' sign criterion plus sharp sign evaluations at the bracket endpoints gives one and only one global maximizer; an implicit derivative establishes strict normalized monotonicity. Independent two-by-two SLD computations and 72 numerical parameter cases corroborate the proof, without substituting sampling for all-parameter arguments.

**Originality — PASS (qualified).** The 2014 joint phase/diffusion theory contains the two-parameter qubit information geometry, the 2022 qubit-reset paper contains the renewal construction, and the 2023 variational-dephasing study develops a different multiparameter protocol. Their full text/accessible relevant sections were compared for a statement implying the exact global D-optimal rate across unknown frequency and damping. The comparison did not find that rate theorem. A published discrete-time single-parameter resetting optimum, a separate single-frequency continuous-time stationary calculation, and the 2026 scalar reset-QFI response identity do not directly establish the two-parameter determinant maximizer. The later v2 conditional-reset no-go concerns benchmarking against optimized transient sensing, not this stationary-local objective. Risks: the result is accessible by combining prior general formulas, and uncatalogued design results could already state the optimized cubic; independent priority evaluation is needed.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Joint frequency-and-decoherence inference is a motivated task because nuisance damping makes scalar frequency-only optimization incomplete. The result gives a rigorous one-rate design rule, a sharp parameter-uniform interval, strict detuning monotonicity and a universal high-frequency optimum. It separates an asymptotically quantum-compatible information volume from the incompatible individual single-copy SLDs. The value is in the all-parameter optimization and constraints, not merely the familiar algebraic QFIM.

**Closest literature and limits.** Vidrighin et al. (2014) provide the generic equatorial-qubit QFIM; Sevilla and Valdés-Hernández (2022) provide quantum reset renewal; Le et al. (2023) provide the dephasing multiparameter motivation. Measurements with finite preparation time, fixed POVMs, unknown reset rate, other channels and time-normalized sensing are not analyzed. Mathematical correctness has not been independently audited.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
