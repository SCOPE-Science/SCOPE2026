Exactly one preselected Gaussian-noisy reading of Brownian motion is needed to estimate its integrated quartic energy, and its uniquely optimal time solves an explicit algebraic equation at every finite noise variance. That time is always strictly later than the optimal reading for integrated quadratic energy; without noise it is \(0.8217603636785\ldots\), and at high noise it approaches the endpoint as \(1-1/(8\eta)+O(\eta^{-2})\).
