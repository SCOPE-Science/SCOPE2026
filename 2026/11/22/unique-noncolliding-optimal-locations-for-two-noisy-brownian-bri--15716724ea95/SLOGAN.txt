Two independent noisy measurements of a Brownian bridge's integral are optimally placed at the unique symmetric pair \((1\pm d_\eta)/2\), with \(d_\eta=(2+4\eta-\sqrt{16\eta^2+16\eta+1})/3\). At every finite positive noise variance the optimal points are distinct; their separation decays as \(1/(8\eta)\) for large noise, and the minimum posterior integral variance is \(1/12-d_\eta(1-d_\eta^2)/4\).
