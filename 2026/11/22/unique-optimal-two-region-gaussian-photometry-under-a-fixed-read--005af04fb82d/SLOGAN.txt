When only two photon totals can be retained from a fixed circular Gaussian-source field, joint source-flux and sky-background information is uniquely maximized by one central disk and its surrounding annulus, even among arbitrary measurable partitions. The optimal disk occupies strictly less than half the field; its area fraction approaches \(1/2\) for a narrow field, while its physical radius approaches \(1.585201\ldots\) Gaussian widths for a wide field.
