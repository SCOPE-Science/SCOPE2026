Among positive centrally symmetric quadrature measures on \([0,1]\) with at most four nodes and exactness through cubic degree, the unique rule minimizing worst-case error on the Jordan-variation unit ball has node offsets \(d_*\) and \(3d_*\), where \(9d_*^2-32d_*^3=1/12\). Its sharp, attained error is \(d_*=0.13216552074896658\ldots\), about 22.25% below the four-node Gauss rule's BV error, with less polynomial exactness.
