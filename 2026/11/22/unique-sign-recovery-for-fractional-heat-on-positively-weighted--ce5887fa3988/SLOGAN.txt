On every finite star with arbitrary positive spoke conductances, fractional heat \(e^{-tL^\alpha}\) is strictly positive for \(0<\alpha\le1\), while for every \(\alpha>1\) each leaf-to-leaf entry starts negative and changes sign at exactly one positive time; all hub-to-leaf entries remain positive. The final leaf-pair recovery time is exactly the return-to-contractivity horizon, and the single-crossing result holds more generally for strictly increasing, strictly convex spectral functions vanishing at zero.
