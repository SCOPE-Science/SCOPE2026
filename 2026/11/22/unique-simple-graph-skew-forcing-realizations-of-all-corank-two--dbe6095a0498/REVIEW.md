# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** A rank-two matroid's hyperplanes are its loop set plus one nonloop parallel class. Their complements are exactly the circuits of the dual and exactly the nonempty neighborhoods of the multipartite realizing graph, so circuit completion and skew forcing have identical closure on every subset. Uniqueness follows rigorously from additivity of skew forcing across components and DeAlba's published connected-graph characterization. The enumerator follows by dual bases, and the Tutte identity is checked by the rank-two subset partition. The standalone checker independently exhausts small instances but is not relied on as an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS (priority provisional).** DeAlba proved the scalar forcing-number characterization, and Cooper–Fickes proved the bipartite matroidal case; neither of these statements classifies all corank-two matroids by their unique labelled simple-graph realization. Hall–Pfeil–Taylor proves uniform realizability, and its circuit-neighborhood sufficient criterion covers a key mechanism but does not assert the all-parallel-class result or uniqueness. These stronger implications were compared, not merely paper titles. Accessible mathematical-index searches found no explicit covering statement. Unindexed work remains a residual historical-priority risk.

**Value — PASS.** The exact image and inverse of the skew-forcing realization map are determined on the entire corank-two stratum, including arbitrary loop and parallel-class patterns. Uniqueness of the realizing simple graph is stronger than existence for uniform matroids and yields complete minimum-set and Tutte data. This resolves a natural restricted version of the general realizability question studied in the motivating paper; it is not simply a scalar forcing-number recomputation.

Independent external mathematical validation has not been performed; historical priority remains provisional. No assertion is made for multigraphs or other coranks.
