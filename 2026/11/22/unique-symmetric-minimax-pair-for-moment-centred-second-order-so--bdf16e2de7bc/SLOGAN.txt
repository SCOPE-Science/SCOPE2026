For a second-order Sobolev function with zero mean and zero first moment, the unique two-site pair minimizing the worst combined squared evaluation norm is \(\{0.1991954850\ldots,0.8008045150\ldots\}\). Its two evaluation kernels are exactly orthogonal and equally long; the sharp squared norm is \(0.00040982270636359\ldots\), established by a global algebraic exclusion argument rather than a grid search.
