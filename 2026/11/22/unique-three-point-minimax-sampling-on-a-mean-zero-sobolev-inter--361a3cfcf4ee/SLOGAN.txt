For the real mean-zero derivative-energy Sobolev space on an interval, the unique configuration minimizing the worst combined energy of exactly three unit-weight point evaluations is \(\{1/2-a_*,1/2,1/2+a_*\}\), where \(3a_*^3+6a_*^2-9a_*+1=0\) and \(a_*\approx0.121561325176484\). The optimal squared trace norm is exactly \(a_*\), and its top Gram eigenvalue is double.
