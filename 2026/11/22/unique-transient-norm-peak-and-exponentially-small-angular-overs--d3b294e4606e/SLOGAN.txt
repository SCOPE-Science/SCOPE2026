For affine Bargmann–Fock composition semigroups contracting around a nonzero fixed point, any nonzero angular drift creates one unique global norm peak and infinitely many alternating local extrema, whereas purely radial contraction approaches its limit monotonically. The first overshoot is beyond all algebraic orders as the angular-to-radial speed ratio vanishes, with exact leading coefficient \(\frac{|c|^2}{e}\kappa e^{-\pi/(2\kappa)}\).
