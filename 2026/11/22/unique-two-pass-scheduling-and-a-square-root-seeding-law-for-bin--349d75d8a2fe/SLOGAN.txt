For two-pass parallel decoding of a positively correlated binary Markov source, the path-error density has a unique strictly convex optimum, always revealing less than half the sites in the first pass. As the domain-wall probability \(f\) vanishes, the optimal initial fraction obeys \(p_*\sim\sqrt{f/\log2}\) and the minimum entropy cost per site obeys \(b_*\sim2\sqrt{f\log2}\).
