For standard Brownian motion, the integrated quadratic path energy \(\int_0^1B(t)^2\,dt\) has a unique globally optimal pair of noiseless observation times, \((0.5184446065\ldots,0.8523344849\ldots)\), exactly characterized by a sextic polynomial and rational reconstruction. These sites reduce squared Bayes risk by about \(15.59\%\) relative to the two sites that are optimal for the linear Brownian area.
