For two noiseless Brownian-bridge quadrature sites, each individually uniform on \([0,1]\), the **unique optimal dependence** is a half-period cyclic shift, with exact expected posterior integral variance \(1/64\) over every Borel coupling. It improves on independent uniform sites by \(37.5\%\), while the usual central-reflection antithetic pair is even worse than independence for this criterion.
