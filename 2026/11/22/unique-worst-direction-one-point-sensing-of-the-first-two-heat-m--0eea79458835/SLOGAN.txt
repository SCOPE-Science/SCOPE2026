The worst-direction optimal point sensor for the first two Dirichlet heat modes has different short- and long-observation limits. For its left-half site, the limiting squared cosine is (√5−1)/4 as the horizon vanishes and the unique root in (0,1) of 50u³+19u²+4u−9 at infinite horizon, corresponding to sites near 0.312376 and 0.273761.
