For any positive-definite temporal information matrix, one continuous spatial sensor observing the first two sine modes has exactly two reflected E-optimal locations, and a logit transformation makes the inverse worst-direction information strictly log-convex. For the first two Dirichlet heat modes, the optimal squared cosine has the limiting value \((\sqrt5-1)/4\) as the horizon vanishes, and the limiting value at infinite horizon is the unique root in \((0,1)\) of \(50u^3+19u^2+4u-9=0\).
