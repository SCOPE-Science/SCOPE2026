On every noncomplete unweighted complete multipartite graph, mixed heat \(e^{-t(L+\eta L^2)}\) has a sharp positivity-recovery time controlled solely by the largest vertex part. The delay has a unique explicitly determined global maximum over \(\eta>0\), and the ratio of worst-star to best-near-clique optimized delays tends to \(1/(2\log2-1)\).
