For every nontrivial finite group, idempotent equivariant maps on a union of fixed points and free orbits have unit-conjugacy classes classified by two multisets of rooted-star sizes; explicit formulas give each class's cardinality and its image rank. The **number of classes** depends only on the counts of fixed and free orbits, not on the group, whereas individual class sizes retain a power of \(|G|\).
