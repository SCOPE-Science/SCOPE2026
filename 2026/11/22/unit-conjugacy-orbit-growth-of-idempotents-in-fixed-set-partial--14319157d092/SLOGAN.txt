Idempotents in a finite partial-transformation monoid with individually distinguished fixed points have a complete unit-conjugacy classification by decorated rooted stars, with exact stabilizer sizes and a rank-refined partition-product generating function. For a fixed number \(a\) of distinguished points and \(b\) free points, the number of classes is asymptotic to \(p(b)(\sqrt{6b}/\pi)^{a+1}\), and the fraction omitting any distinguished point is asymptotic to \(a\pi/\sqrt{6b}\) when \(a\ge1\).
