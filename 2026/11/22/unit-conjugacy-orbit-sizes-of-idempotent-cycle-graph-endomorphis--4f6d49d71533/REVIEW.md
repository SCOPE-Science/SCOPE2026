# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
A proper idempotent image is a consecutive path and fixes that path pointwise. The complementary arc supplies a single endpoint-to-endpoint path walk, and its image-stabilizing reflection reverses and complements the walk. This gives explicit fixed-point counts from the middle vertex or middle edge; the subgroup-stabilizer argument proves orbit sizes \(n\) and \(2n\). A four-vertex path recurrence gives a Fibonacci lower bound, while reflection symmetry forces half a walk, establishing exponential scarcity. The direct composition-and-conjugation enumerator exhausted every dimension \(4\le n\le16\). The proof, not this finite computation, establishes the general formulas.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence
The 2023 cycle-monoid study by Dimitrova, Fernandes, Koppitz and Quinteiro treats the underlying strict and weak graph monoids, total counts, regularity, generating sets and Green relations, but does not state the dihedral unit-conjugacy spectrum for retractions. The previously published labeled cycle-retraction rank formula is explicitly credited. A published reflection-orbit classification for path retractions has a different decomposition: two independent tails versus a single complementary cycle arc, and does not determine these cyclic midpoint symmetry counts. Michels–Knauer's 2009 cycle-congruence work is a historical priority risk because only its abstract was inspected. Searches and source comparisons do not certify absolute uniqueness.

## Value — PASS
Unit-conjugacy is an intrinsic invariant of the cycle endomorphism monoid: it distinguishes retractions under the monoid's actual units, not arbitrary graph labelings or Green equivalence. The result gives every image-rank and orbit-size stratum in an infinite family and proves an asymptotic symmetry-breaking law. This is more informative than the known labeled rank census and resolves a natural complete classification question.
