For the affine monomial operators \(f(x)\mapsto x^bf(x^a)\) on \(L^2(0,1)\), every positive-damping generalized Aluthge iterate is unitarily equivalent to the original and has the same closed spectral disk, yet the iterates converge strongly-star to a scaled unitary when \(a>1\) and to zero when \(0<a<1\). Neither convergence occurs in norm: every successive norm gap is the identical explicit positive constant \(a^{-1/2}|1-q|q^{-q/(q-1)}\), where \(q=1-\eta+\eta/a\).
