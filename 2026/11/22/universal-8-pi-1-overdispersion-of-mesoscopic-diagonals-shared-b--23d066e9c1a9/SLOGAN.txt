Two independent uniform triangulations share only a bounded expected number of diagonals whose smaller side has size of order \(\sqrt n\), but these coincidences arrive with universal **overdispersion**: in every fixed nonempty length band, \(\operatorname{Var}(X_n)/\mathbb E X_n\to8/\pi-1\). The same factor multiplies the limiting covariance density of all compactly supported tests of their rescaled common-diagonal lengths.
