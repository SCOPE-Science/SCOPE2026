The canonical Euclidean edge polytope of any complete multipartite graph with at least three classes, each containing at least three vertices, has precisely \(\binom n2+n\) acute ridges and \(n(k-1)\) obtuse ridges, independently of class sizes. Its entire dihedral-angle multiset is explicit and reveals each distinct class size, even though the facet-adjacency graph always has the same complete-split form.
