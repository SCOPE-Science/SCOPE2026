Every nonidentity positive mixture of circular Poisson means has a sharp real periodic \(BV\)-to-\(L^2\) approximation-error norm attained **only** by jumps at antipodal points, with a closed odd-moment series for the constant. Fractional Poisson smoothing has the same rigid extremizers at every fixed order and a sharp small-time transition at order \(\alpha=1/2\): quadratic, quadratic-logarithmic and fractional-power regimes with explicit coefficients.
