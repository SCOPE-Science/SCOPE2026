Every even cyclic Gabor system with all translations and two modulation channels has a uniquely optimal antipodal, equal-power design whenever its window has strictly positive Fourier energy decreasing with cyclic frequency distance. For a cyclic graph-heat window, both sharp frame bounds and the optimal squared condition number are explicit hyperbolic-cosine expressions, with a parity-dependent gap between cycle orders divisible by four and those congruent to two.
