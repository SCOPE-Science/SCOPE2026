On every double cone over a connected vertex- and edge-transitive regular graph with \(n\ge3\) base vertices, the unique symmetric stochastic Kemeny optimum assigns probability \(1/n\) to each apex edge and \((n-2)/(nd)\) to each degree-\(d\) base edge, with no holding anywhere. For cyclic bases its Kemeny sum grows like \(n^{3/2}/2\), whereas the standard maximum-degree symmetric walk grows like \(n^2/(2\sqrt3)\), a multiplicative gap asymptotic to \(\sqrt{n/3}\).
