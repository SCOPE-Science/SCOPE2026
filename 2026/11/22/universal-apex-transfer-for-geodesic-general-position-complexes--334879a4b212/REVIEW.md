## Correctness — PASS
The proof establishes an *exact* simplicial decomposition into an apex-free join of dissociation complexes and an apex cone over the original disconnected rim graph. Connectedness of the two or more join factors makes the target simply connected; every graph-map attaching component is nullhomotopic, so the mapping cone splits without any field restriction. The unreduced suspension contributes one fundamental circle per extra component and a two-sphere per independent rim cycle. Four-cycle factors are one-dimensional complete graphs, producing an exponential high-dimensional sphere wedge. Such computations are checks, not substitutes for the infinite proof. General factors are not asserted to be torsion-free.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The statement is a general homotopy-transfer theorem for a natural graph operation. It determines free fundamental groups and cycle-rank two-sphere summands despite potentially complicated higher homotopy in the join factor. The all-square family displays simultaneous homology in three widely separated degrees, with exponential growth in top rank; counting polynomials alone do not determine attaching maps or the free fundamental group. This is a motivated structural classification, not merely an enumerative or finite computation.
