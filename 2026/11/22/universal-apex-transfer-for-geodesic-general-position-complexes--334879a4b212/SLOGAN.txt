For a universal-apex graph whose rim is a disjoint union of at least two connected triangle-free graphs, its geodesic general-position complex splits up to integral homotopy into the join of their two-independence complexes, one circle per extra component, and one two-sphere per independent rim cycle. For \(m\) disjoint square rims this gives exactly \(m-1\) circles, \(m\) two-spheres, and \(3^m\) spheres in dimension \(2m-1\).
