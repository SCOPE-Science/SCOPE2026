Pressure fluctuations across spatially separated walls cannot communicate before finite-speed travel, and their earliest observable joint signal combines propagation and surface desorption. The exact first-derivative coefficient depends only on residence means, while the next fractional power identifies the smaller short-residence exponent: a physically interpretable separation of bulk persistence from non-Markov boundary kinetics.
