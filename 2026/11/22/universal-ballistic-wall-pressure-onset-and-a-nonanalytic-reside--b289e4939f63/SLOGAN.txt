For a stationary run-and-tumble particle between two sticky walls with arbitrary positive finite-mean renewal residence times, opposite-wall pressure correlation has a sharp first possible arrival at the ballistic lag \(L/v\) and a universal linear onset. If the wall-release probabilities behave like fractional powers near zero, the next cross-wall term has exponent \(1+\min\{\beta_0,\beta_L\}\), revealing the faster-release boundary law from a two-time observable.
