In ballistic threshold resetting, every bounded position observable has an exactly zero centered reward over each complete trip, so all its long-time integrated fluctuations reduce to two renewal-endpoint terms rather than accumulating diffusively. Under finite third duration moment and a continuous waiting law, the time-average fluctuations have a generally non-Gaussian \(1/t\) endpoint-difference law, and every nonconstant Lipschitz spatial observable must exhibit negative temporal covariance at some lag.
