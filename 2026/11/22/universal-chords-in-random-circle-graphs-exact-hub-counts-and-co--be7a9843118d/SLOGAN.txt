In a uniform random chord diagram, the entire count of chords crossing every other chord has an exact composition-factorial moment law, and the existence of such a chord has probability asymptotic to \(\sqrt{\pi n}/2^n\). Conditioning on this rare event converts the remaining matching asymptotically to a uniform permutation diagram, shifting crossing density from \(1/6\) to \(1/4\) and yielding Gaussian fluctuations with variance scale \(n^3/36\).
