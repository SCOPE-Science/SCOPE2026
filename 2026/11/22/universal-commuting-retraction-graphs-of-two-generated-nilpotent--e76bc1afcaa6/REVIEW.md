# Scientific review: commuting retractions of two-generated nilpotent Lie algebras

## Correctness: PASS

The proof derives the all-class rank restriction from the nilpotent generation lemma, establishes that proper nonzero retractions have rank one, gives exact cardinalities of their quotient-projection fibers, and reduces commutation to equal or complementary rank-one projections of the two-dimensional abelianization. The graph theorem and each enumerative consequence follow algebraically. An independent direct generator-image enumerator checks full endomorphism and idempotence equations for eight cases across two nonisomorphic five-dimensional nilpotent Lie algebras. The tests corroborate but do not replace the general proof; all quantifiers and characteristic-two cases are covered by alternating Lie-bracket arguments.

## Originality: PASS, relative to inspected statements

The nearest full text is *Clique polynomials and maximal retraction families in free class-two Lie algebras*. It includes the free class-two two-generator specialization (Heisenberg) and therefore that specialization is NOT claimed to be new. It does not cover arbitrary two-generated nilpotent quotients of class three or higher or demonstrate the new universal isomorphism/blindness principle. The companion free-class-two retraction-spectrum paper has the same restriction. The exterior-algebra commutation graph is analogous, not a Lie-algebra implication. An analogous rank-one commuting-retraction formula was previously obtained for finite groups of prime exponent; its quotient-lifting mechanism and graph shape are acknowledged as known. The ordinary Lie classification for arbitrary nilpotency class is not a direct consequence, and includes positive-characteristic classes beyond the usual group–Lie transfer range. Ebrahim–Facchini's 2023 work supplies a pre-endomorphism splitting framework, but no quotient-independent labeled Lie-retraction graph. Residual risk: hidden equivalent results in semidirect decomposition or older free-Lie retract literature, particularly Mikhalev–Yu (1997), whose full text was not inspected. Negative searches are not treated as proofs of priority.

## Value: PASS

The sharp class-independent graph classification unifies all two-generator nilpotent Lie algebras, not a special dimensional slice: it proves identical full commuting-family combinatorics for arbitrary nilpotency classes, identifies a four-element Boolean maximal-family law, gives an exact positive limiting commutation probability, and recovers the field order and ambient dimension while proving failure to recover nilpotency class. The comparison of the two five-dimensional nonisomorphic examples exhibits this nontrivial information-loss boundary. This is a natural structural/invariant classification, with a precise and reproducible formula rather than a routine instance table.

## Scope and uncertainty

Only ordinary Lie endomorphisms of finite-dimensional two-generated nilpotent Lie algebras are covered. Publication priority is qualified as above; the small-field verifier is not an infinite proof. No independent external mathematical validation was obtained.
