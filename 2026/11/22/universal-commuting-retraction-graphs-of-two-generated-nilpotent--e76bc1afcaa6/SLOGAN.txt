Every finite-dimensional two-generated nilpotent Lie algebra of dimension \(n\ge3\) over \(\mathbb F_q\) has the same commuting graph on its nontrivial retractions: \(q(q+1)/2\) copies of \(K_{q^{n-2},q^{n-2}}\), independent of the bracket and nilpotency class. The graph recovers \(q\) and \(n\) but not the Lie-isomorphism type, and every maximal commuting family is a four-element Boolean monoid.
