Every translational tile in every sixteen-element abelian group has one Fourier-character spectrum shared by all of its tiling complements. The exact counts of tiles for which no subgroup-coset spectrum can be shared are \(764,1056,1168,896,0\) across \(\mathbb Z_{16}\), \(\mathbb Z_8\times\mathbb Z_2\), \(\mathbb Z_4^2\), \(\mathbb Z_4\times\mathbb Z_2^2\), and \(\mathbb Z_2^4\), respectively.
