All \(2{,}155\) translational tiles in \((\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z)^2\) admit a single Fourier-character basis simultaneously on every one of their tiling complements, but exactly \(1{,}168\) tiles cannot choose that basis as a coset of a character subgroup. An exact \(19\)-orbit certificate distinguishes universal spectra from the stronger, often impossible, subgroup-spectra requirement.
