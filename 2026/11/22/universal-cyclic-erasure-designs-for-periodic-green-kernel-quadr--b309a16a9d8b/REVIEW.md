## Correctness — PASS

The covariance's Fourier coefficients are \(2s\kappa/(\kappa^2+(2\pi m)^2)\), so it is the inverse of \(( -\partial_t^2+\kappa^2)/(2\kappa s)\) on the circle. Conditioning on retained values imposes Dirichlet constraints and decomposes residual integral variance into independent arc contributions. Solving the scalar Dirichlet equation and integrating gives the exact function \(f(\ell)\), including the singleton case. Each retained arc is counted exactly once by the events defining \(q_k\). Strict convexity, Jensen equality and cyclic subgroup arithmetic prove both the global optimum and the full periodic-gap minimizer face; there is no inference from a finite parameter grid. For independent erasures, explicit Bernoulli probabilities and the cubic Taylor law, with geometrically dominated summands, give the exact finite risk and asymptotic ratio. The local verifier directly compares the kernel-matrix Schur complement, Green-gap sums, conditional-mean weights and expectation over every dropout mask in 687 parameterized cases. ## Originality — PASS (provisional historical priority)

Pronzato and Zhigljavsky (2018, arXiv:1808.10722v1) treat general kernel-energy Bayesian quadrature and interval exponential kernels, without the exact circular correlated-erasure risk/optimizer face. Sikolya and Baran (2018, arXiv:1808.03444v1) optimize complex OU pointwise integrated prediction error and entropy, not the posterior variance of a single circular integral. Huang and Li (2022, arXiv:2201.12856v1) establish circular Matérn covariance and exact order-one Markov structure; those premises are acknowledged, not claimed as new. The public mathematical note *Exact optimal nodes and stratification penalty for Ornstein–Uhlenbeck Bayesian quadrature* establishes a different endpoint-bearing, no-erasure interval problem, not this cyclic erasure invariant. Searches of related published mathematical records, primary texts and equivalent aliases did not locate an implication covering the gcd-optimality classification together with the exact independent-dropout variance factor. No claim of comprehensive uniqueness is made; other older circular process design results and a partially accessible dissertation remain precedence risks. A search miss alone is not treated as proof of originality.

## Value — PASS

When observation requests may fail, the best places to request them need not optimize the no-failure risk. This theorem proves a robust universal design under every cyclic-invariant correlated erasure law; it also describes precisely when dependence destroys uniqueness without destroying uniform optimality. The explicit factor \(1+6(1-p)/p^2\) measures the leading numerical-integration price of independent losses and sharply distinguishes noise-free sampling with missing values from mere reduced sample size. The four-site alternating-failure example identifies a natural nonuniform optimizer family, not an arbitrary low-dimensional slice. This is a mathematical statement about posterior integral variance, not a generic improvement claim for all spatial prediction losses.

## Limits and closest sources

The sampling sites are fixed in advance, distinct and circular; the kernel is exactly the positive order-one periodic Green covariance. The process parameters and mean are known. Observation noise, adaptive replacement, failures depending on values, moving-domain boundaries, other kernel smoothness and reliability that varies with sample size are excluded.
