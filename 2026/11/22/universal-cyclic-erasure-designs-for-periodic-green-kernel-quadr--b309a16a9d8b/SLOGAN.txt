For periodic Ornstein–Uhlenbeck Green-kernel quadrature with any cyclically invariant correlated observation-erasure law, equally spaced nodes always minimize expected posterior integral variance, and a gcd of the supported surviving-arc lengths classifies every nonuniform co-optimum. With independent per-node success probability \(p>0\), the uniform design is uniquely optimal up to rotation and suffers the sharp asymptotic erasure penalty \(1+6(1-p)/p^2\) relative to no erasures.
