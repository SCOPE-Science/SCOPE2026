Strong second-difference smoothing on the infinite integer lattice has a nonvanishing sharp coordinatewise noise gain: its \(\ell^\infty\) operator norm tends to \(1+\sqrt2 e^{-3\pi/4}/(1-e^{-\pi})\approx1.140093467\). On finite cycles the same gain tends to one when smoothing strength grows first, so the large-grid and large-smoothing limits do not commute.
