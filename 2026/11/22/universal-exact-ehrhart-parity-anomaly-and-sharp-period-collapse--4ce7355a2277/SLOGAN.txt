The minimal Ehrhart quasiperiod of an odd-cycle adjacent-sum polytope with positive integer capacities is two precisely when its unique all-tight corner is strictly positive and half-integral; then the even-minus-odd constituent polynomials differ by exactly \(2^{-d}\). For alternating capacities \(s+1,s,s+1,s,\ldots,s+1\), this happens exactly for \(s\ge(d-1)/2\) with \(s\equiv(d-1)/2\pmod2\).
