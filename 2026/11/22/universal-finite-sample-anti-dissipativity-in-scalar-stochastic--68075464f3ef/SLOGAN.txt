Every fixed finite sample of a one-observable stochastic Galerkin generator carries a positive chance of spurious growth when the bounded observable is strictly positive, nonconstant, and the population is dissipative. On circle Brownian motion with \(\psi(x)=2+\cos x\), the population Richardson step is strictly contractive for \(0<h<18\), yet the expected squared long-horizon forecasting error grows at least as \(3^{-m}(1+h/3+h^2/18)^{2k}\) up to its explicit lower-order correction.
