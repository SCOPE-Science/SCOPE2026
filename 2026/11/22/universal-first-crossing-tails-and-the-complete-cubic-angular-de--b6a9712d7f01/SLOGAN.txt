For a radially contracting homogeneous planar flow, the order \(k\) of an upstream angular equilibrium controls the first downstream-ray crossing of uniformly annular starting data: simple zeros yield an exponential logarithmic rate, whereas orders \(k\ge2\) yield sharp power-law survival tails of index \(1/(k-1)\), with exact constants and moment cutoffs. All three heavy-tail indices \(1\), \(1/2\), and \(1/3\) allowed in the cubic case occur in explicit strictly contracting polynomial examples.
