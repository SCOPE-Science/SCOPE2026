# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The construction uses disjoint length-three directed paths glued at articulation vertices, retains every consecutive cover edge, adds the direct length-three shortcut at each birth, and adds a length-two shortcut at each finite death. A single block has integral cycle rank zero, one, or two; before death the allowed two-path boundaries have unique forbidden faces and cannot form \(\Omega_2\). After death the two explicit invariant two-path boundaries form a primitive integer basis of the cycle lattice. Crossing allowed paths of degree at least two have a unique forbidden articulation-deletion face and thus cannot occur in invariant chain combinations. Higher path chains vanish, and the generators give an actual integral direct-sum persistence decomposition. Exact-rational finite tests check 39 independent stage configurations and five example times. The proof, not those finite tests, establishes the arbitrary finite family theorem.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk

The closest conceptual source is Muranov–Muranova, arXiv:2611.15308v1, which defines the induced topology of concatenation path complexes but does not give the barcode realization. Hepworth–Roff, arXiv:2312.01378, already exhibit static disparities between reachability and path homology, so static non-determination is not claimed new. Chaplin–Harrington–Tillmann, DOI:10.1007/s10208-024-09679-2, treat a distinct grounded persistence invariant. Another recorded result establishes a single finite path-homology interval for cyclic powers; its cyclic filtration does not yield arbitrary prescribed intervals inside acyclic graphs with fixed total reachability. No inspected source gives a theorem covering the entire all-barcode construction or its integral inclusion maps. Search non-detection is not proof of global novelty; inaccessible or unindexed equivalent results remain possible.

## Value — PASS

Persistent path homology is intended to detect directional information not captured by reachability. Instead of an isolated example of this discrepancy, the theorem gives a full constructive realization of every finite first-degree barcode with all reachability-based topological information held fixed. The primitive integral splitting makes this a structural statement about what a fixed reachability topology cannot constrain, and includes arbitrary finite death times. This addresses an identifiable limitation in comparing path-based and reachability-based invariants, rather than a numerical or normalization exercise.
