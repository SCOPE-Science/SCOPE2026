For every fixed number \(m\ge2\) of parallel unmasking rounds in a strongly ordered one-dimensional Ising chain, the exact minimum path-entropy error scales as \(m(\log2)^{1/m}f^{(m-1)/m}\) when the spin-switch probability \(f\) vanishes. Every globally optimal schedule forms the same geometric reveal hierarchy \(p_j\sim(f/\log2)^{(m-j)/m}\); this finite-round law is not the smooth many-round geodesic approximation.
