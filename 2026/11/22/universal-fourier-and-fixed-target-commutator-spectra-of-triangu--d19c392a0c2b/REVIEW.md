# Review of the universal triangular commutator Fourier spectrum

**Correctness — PASS.** Direct multiplication produces the six-by-six alternating pairing (equation (3) of RESULT.md). Its entries and all nonzero four-by-four Pfaffians identify the two nontrivial alternating Smith exponents; the remaining two coordinates are null. Nondegenerate additive-character orthogonality converts the resulting kernel size to the entire Fourier transform, and exact valuation-shell orthogonality inverts the transform for *every target*. The proof makes no assumption about residue characteristic or choice of chain-ring presentation. The standalone script tests all frequencies over two nonisomorphic length-two rings as well as smaller and sampled larger parameters, and separately checks direct matrix-pair commutators over the smallest field. These experiments corroborate rather than substitute for the infinite-family argument.

**Originality — PASS with residual priority uncertainty.** Earlier work on Lie products of triangular matrix algebras gives the general setting, and determinant fibers over finite principal ideal rings solve a related two-by-two boundary. Published three-by-three *field* commutator fibers provide the \(\ell=1\) specialization of this formula, and published two-by-two triangular-chain-ring commuting enumerators concern a different algebra and the zero target. The full statements of those two especially close published mathematical results were read and compared, rather than inferred from titles. Neither implies the complete nonzero-target and Fourier spectra for three-by-three upper triangular matrices over **all non-field finite chain rings** by a parameter substitution. A general non-field alternating bilinear-word-map distribution may still contain an implicit equivalent; lack of identified overlap is not a guarantee of priority. The determinant paper was compared through its available abstract and bibliographic description; its complete primary text was not accessible in the available HTML representations.

**Value — PASS.** Full additive-commutator fiber counts are a natural finite-ring invariant. Formula (1) determines every prescribed output, not only the commuting probability or total number of commutators. The universal positive Fourier spectrum yields a sharp random-walk spectral gap, distinguishes \(2\ell+1\) eigenvalue levels, and proves equality of the entire distributions for nonisomorphic finite chain rings with the same \((q,\ell)\). The family is unbounded in both residue-field cardinality and chain length; this is not a minor low-rank parameter increment. Limits: no higher matrix sizes or nonchain coefficient rings; the field case is existing work; mathematical priority is not externally certified.
