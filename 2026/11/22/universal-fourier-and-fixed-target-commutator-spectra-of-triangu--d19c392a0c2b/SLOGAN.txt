All fixed-target additive commutator counts for upper triangular \(3\times3\) matrices over every finite commutative chain ring admit a universal three-valuation finite sum, independent of the ring's characteristic or presentation. Their Fourier transforms have exactly \(2\ell+1\) positive levels, and the normalized commutator walk has sharp nontrivial spectral radius \(q^{-2}\); the non-field lengths \(\ell\ge2\) extend the previously counted finite-field case.
