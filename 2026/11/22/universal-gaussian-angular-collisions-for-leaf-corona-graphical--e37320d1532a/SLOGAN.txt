For the graphical zonotope obtained by adding \(n\) pendant leaves to every vertex of any fixed core graph \(H\) on \(k\ge3\) vertices, the probability that two Gaussian directions expose the same vertex is asymptotic to \(\kappa(H)n^{-2k}\), with an exact induced-subgraph orientation partition function for \(\kappa(H)\). Adding any core edge strictly lowers this coefficient, whose sharp extremes are \(\binom{2k}{k}/k!\) for a clique and \(2^k\) for an edgeless core.
