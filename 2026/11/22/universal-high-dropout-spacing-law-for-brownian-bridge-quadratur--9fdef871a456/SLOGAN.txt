For \(n\) independent, failure-prone exact observations of a Brownian bridge, the unique Bayes-optimal quadrature design concentrates near the midpoint as failure becomes nearly certain—but its adjacent sensors stay separated by \((1-q)/4+o(1-q)\), with universal equally spaced rescaled positions. The two-sensor design admits an exact formula for every failure probability, unlike the exact co-location regime known for worst-case distance coverage.
