For every fixed budget of at least three independent noisy parallel threshold queries of a uniform location, the unrestricted mean-square-optimal thresholds form a universal midpoint staircase with spacing asymptotic to \((1-2p)^2/2\) as the bit-flip probability \(p\) tends to \(1/2\). This separation lowers Bayes risk relative to repeating the midpoint by the sharp amount \(k(k^2-1)(1-2p)^6/96+o((1-2p)^6)\).
