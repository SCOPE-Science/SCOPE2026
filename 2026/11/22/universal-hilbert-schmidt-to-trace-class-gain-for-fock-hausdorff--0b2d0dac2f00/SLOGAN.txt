Every positive Fock Hausdorff dilation mixture has a Hilbert–Schmidt first commutator with differentiation and trace-class commutators from the second order onward, with sharp bounds \(\|B_1\|_2\le M\) and \(\|B_k\|_1\le\sqrt{k!}M\) for \(k\ge2\). A precise boundary-mass criterion and beta-family singular-value laws prove that neither universal Schatten endpoint can be improved.
