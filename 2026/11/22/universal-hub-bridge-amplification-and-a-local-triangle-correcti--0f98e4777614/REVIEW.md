# Scientific review of the fan-versus-matching threshold law

## Correctness — PASS
The matching root is exact by regularity. The fan eigenvector equations give a scalar resolvent identity with a unique positive root above the internal degree; its converse produces a strictly positive eigenvector. The star-plus-bounded-perturbation estimate makes \(\lambda\asymp\sqrt n\) uniformly. The first closed-walk moments are \(1,0,d,t_v\), giving the displayed coefficient through order \(n^{-1}\). The remainder is uniform because every rooted walk count is bounded by a power of the fixed degree; the derivative of the original root equation is at least one. The standalone independent numerical paths check the formula on nine test graphs. The infinite statement rests on these analytic arguments, not on those tests.

## Originality — PASS, subject to literature-priority limits
Dung–Phuc (2026) establish only global-versus-local threshold inequalities and discuss network examples, not a fixed-edge-budget contrast diverging with community size. Sahneh–Scoglio–Chowdhury (2012) establish a broad coupling resolvent method, correctly credited. Wang et al. (2013) already give the exact matching-network spectral formula and study general coupling, so that component alone has no originality claim. Its general large-coupling perturbation also anticipates the leading fan growth and constant degree correction. The narrowly claimed new part is the **uniform fixed-degree expansion through the vertex-triangle term** coupled to the all-size, same-community, same-budget construction; no equivalent statement was identified in the read primary texts or the indexed comparisons. Broader classical graph-spectral asymptotic literature could contain an equivalent result and remains a residual priority risk.

## Value — PASS
Community-local spectral thresholds and an aggregate bridge budget are natural coarse observables in metapopulation SIS screening. Their complete failure to control the scale of a global linearized threshold, even with identical full internal contact graphs, motivates tracking the concentration of intercommunity links. The universal law is not just a restatement of an existing Perron bound: it predicts a precise growth scale, three universal coefficients, the first local motif-sensitive correction, and a concrete same-order triangle/no-triangle distinction. It does not claim novelty for the standard spectral model or eigenvalue technique.

## Limits and closer sources
The threshold is a deterministic or independent-neighbor spectral approximation, not an exact finite stochastic epidemic survival threshold. The dependence on a single unbounded-degree intercommunity hub is intentional. Primary research consulted: Dung and Phuc, https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14048 ; Sahneh, Scoglio and Chowdhury, https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.4194 ; Wang et al., https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.0781 . The 2021 walk-moment graph spectral bounds (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2021.04.023) were seen through a publicly accessible excerpt rather than a full article. Priority in the unrestricted literature has not received independent assessment.
