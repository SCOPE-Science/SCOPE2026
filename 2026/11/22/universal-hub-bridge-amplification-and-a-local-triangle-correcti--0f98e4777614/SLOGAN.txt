Two epidemic contact networks can have exactly the same regular communities and the same number of intercommunity edges but radically different spectral SIS thresholds: matching gives \(1/(d+1)\), while concentrating the links at one hub gives a threshold asymptotic to \(n^{-1/2}\). The fan's Perron root has a universal degree-controlled expansion whose first within-community structural correction is the number of triangles touching that hub.
