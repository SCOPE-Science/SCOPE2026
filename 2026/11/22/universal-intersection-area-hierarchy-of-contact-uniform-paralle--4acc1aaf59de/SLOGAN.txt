Independently selecting \(m\) four-side-tangent ellipses of any parallelogram by uniform contact on one fixed side produces the universal mean-intersection-area fraction \(\tfrac14B(\tfrac12,1+\tfrac m2)^2\), equal to \(4/9\) for two and asymptotic to \(\pi/(2m)\). Every two members also have a closed trigonometric intersection-area kernel and, when distinct, exactly four transverse boundary crossings.
