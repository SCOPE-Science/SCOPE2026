For a one-way chain of unsuitable habitats, every habitat-blind germination strategy loses a universal asymptotic factor proportional to \(1/n\) against perfect recognition of the suitable target, even when seed survival and movement vary between patches. The uniquely optimal uniform germination probability is asymptotic to the reciprocal of an explicit sum of patch residence factors, and its accumulated premature-germination penalty approaches \(e^{-1}\).
