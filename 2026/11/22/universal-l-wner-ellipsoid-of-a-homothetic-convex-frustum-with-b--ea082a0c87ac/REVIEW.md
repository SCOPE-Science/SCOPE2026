# Mathematical review

**Correctness: PASS.** After affine normalization, the two endpoint disk contact identities are exact consequences of a single quadratic equation. Every base Löwner contact measure is lifted, with strictly positive layer weights, to a mean-zero isotropic contact measure on the enclosing unit sphere in one higher dimension. Together with endpoint convex-hull containment this gives a complete global optimality and uniqueness certificate for any full-dimensional convex base. The rational verifier confirms the algebra on 969 interior parameter configurations and the special endpoints. It does not certify the infinite theorem without the proof.

**Originality: PASS, provisional historical priority.** Güler–Gürtuna's 2007 §8 theorem treats ellipsoidal-base truncated second-order cones, not arbitrary convex bases. The 2022 Ivanov–Naszódi paper gives general Löwner conditions but no matching universal frustum formula. Previously published simplex-frustum covering results are included as special cases; previously published universal *inscribed* John-frustum results have different optimization direction and contact structure. The arbitrary-base replacement principle and two-rim contact design were not found in these inspected statements or semantic database searches. An unindexed general lifting theorem could affect priority.

**Mathematical value: PASS.** The result reduces a high-dimensional covering-ellipsoid problem for arbitrary asymmetric and nonsmooth frustum bases to the lower-dimensional base's outer ellipsoid, with explicit closed formulas, a complete global contact certificate, and a clear contrast with the known inscribed-ellipsoid phase transition. This is a structural reduction valid for a broad natural family, beyond a calculation for a circular base.

**Closest antecedent and limitation.** O. Güler and F. Gürtuna, arXiv:0709.0707, Theorem 8.1, covers ellipsoidal truncated cones. The full claim requires homothetic parallel sections and has not undergone independent historical or mathematical audit.
