For all collapsing Lipschitz convex lenses, the boundary-mean-distance-to-perimeter ratio exceeds \(1/6\) by a universal leading term \(\varepsilon^2\log(1/\varepsilon)\int h^2/32\), depending only on the vertical thickness profile. At fixed unscaled lens area, its logarithmic coefficient lies sharply between the unattained lower infimum \(A^2/64\) and the tent-profile maximum \(A^2/48\); the concave-profile extremal inequality itself is classical.
