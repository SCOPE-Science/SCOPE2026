At the neutral Neimark--Sacker boundary of a cubic multiplier-accelerator map, every sufficiently small nonzero orbit decays like \(n^{-1/2}\) but acquires the exact logarithmic angular drift \(\tfrac12\sqrt{(1-\alpha)/(1+\alpha)}\log n\). After multiplication by \(\sqrt n\), its subsequential limit set is a completely specified ellipse even when the linear rotation has rational angle.
