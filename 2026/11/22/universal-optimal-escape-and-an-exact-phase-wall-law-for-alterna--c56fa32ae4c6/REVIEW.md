# Assessment of the alternating binary Markov-hole theorem

**Correctness: PASS.** The survival expressions follow from the explicitly computed defective two-step matrix and its exact power. The determinant of each killed matrix is the negative of the same positive quantity, giving a universal schedule-independent lower survival bound. Strict positivity of the cyclic transfer matrix for every nonalternating periodic schedule provides a strict Perron spectral gap, proving uniqueness among periodic schedules. The conditional crossing distribution is obtained from disjoint paths, not finite simulation. `CHECK.py` confirms 846 exact-rational finite instances as a supplementary check.

**Originality: PASS within inspected evidence.** Agarwal–Cheriyath–Tikekar's arXiv:2401.05118 treats fixed Markov cylinder holes and supplies the spectral-radius framework. The two closest publicly indexed results already cover uniformly weighted binary/doubling alternating-hole escape, including a stronger finite-time comparison in that restricted case. Those facts are expressly excluded from the novel claim. The nonuniform stationary Markov family, determinant optimality across all deterministic schedules, exact Jordan coefficients, and conditional one-way phase-wall law are not implied by the inspected prior statements without additional analysis. Full text was not accessible for the two closest indexed findings; their available summaries support the fair-case overlap but cannot rule out all possible broader statements. An unindexed or otherwise inaccessible equivalent result remains a material publication-priority risk.

**Mathematical value: PASS.** Uniform Bernoulli optimality does not plainly extend to memory-bearing sources: the optimal *finite-time* statement is false already at an explicit interior positive Markov matrix. Nonetheless a sharp *asymptotic* universal rate and unique periodic optimizer persist for all such sources. The exact linear survival prefactor and a uniform conditional transition-time law expose a nontrivial Jordan resonance in an open dynamical system; they quantify metastable path geometry beyond the ordinary escape exponent.

**Limitations.** The equality characterization covers periodic schedules, not arbitrary nonperiodic ones. The initial distribution must be stationary. All probabilities are interior; degenerate boundary matrices are excluded. No independent validation is asserted.
