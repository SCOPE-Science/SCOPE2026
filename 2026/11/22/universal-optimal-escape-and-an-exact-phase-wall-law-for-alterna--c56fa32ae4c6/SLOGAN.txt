For every strictly positive two-state Markov chain, alternating the forbidden adjacent words \(00\) and \(11\) maximizes the asymptotic escape exponent over all predetermined schedules and uniquely among periodic schedules up to phase, with exact survival \(S_{2n}=[b(1-a)]^n(1+n\gamma)\) where \(\gamma=(a+1-b)/(b+1-a)\). Conditional on long survival, the permitted even-time path switches channels exactly once with probability tending to one, at an asymptotically uniform fraction of the observation horizon.
