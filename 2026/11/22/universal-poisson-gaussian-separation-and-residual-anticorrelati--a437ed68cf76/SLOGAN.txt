At activity \(c/n\), the hard-core gas on the one-edge subdivision of **any** dense graph sequence has almost independent occupied original sites, with a graph-uniform \(O(n^{-1})\) total-variation bound, degree-marked Poisson statistics, and independent Gaussian fluctuations of the occupied subdivision sites. The unscaled population covariance nevertheless tends to a finite negative degree-profile integral; on asymptotically regular hosts the rare original population rises and falls according to \(ce^{-\rho c}\).
