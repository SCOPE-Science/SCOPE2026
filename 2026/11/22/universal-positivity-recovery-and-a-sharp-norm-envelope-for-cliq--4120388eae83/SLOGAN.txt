On every unweighted cone over at least two cliques, any strictly increasing spectral generator \(\psi(L)\) has a positive semigroup at all positive times exactly when \(\psi(N)\le N\psi(1)\); otherwise a unique topology-independent recovery time exists. Among all clique decompositions on \(N\) vertices, the sharp transient \(\ell^1\) and \(\ell^\infty\) norm envelope is achieved precisely when a singleton base clique is present.
