For the attracting invariant graph of a cat-driven Möbius circle map, the fiber Lyapunov exponent has the universal forcing correction \(\pi^2\mu^2/2\) and the exact positive quartic correction \(\pi^4(\varepsilon^2-\varepsilon+1)\mu^4/(16\varepsilon)\). Both corrections are independent of the forcing phase, owing to an exact absence of Fourier resonances through order four.
