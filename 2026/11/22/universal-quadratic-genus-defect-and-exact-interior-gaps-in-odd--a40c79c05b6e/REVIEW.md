# Review

## Correctness

PASS. A total-coin-count decomposition gives an exact finite sum for every triple representation. For the entire second-phase range, only three deficit terms can survive, and their exact simplification is a universal nonnegative tent function \(E_s(r)\). The integer intervals on which \(E_s(r)\ge t-s\) are completely solved for every residual band, with no truncation because \(t\le m+2\). Counting their disjoint lengths gives the quadratic defect. A separate DP and direct enumerator passed 160,598 band tests, 22,154,736 full-gap predicates and 1,351 small direct coefficients. The proof, not the tests, establishes all infinite quantifiers.

## Originality

PASS on the inspected literature with a substantial implicit-corollary risk. Komatsu–Ying (arXiv:2206.13052) studies the earlier \(p\le m\) range. The publicly available exact three-phase Frobenius theorem (identifier 6dc430dbb315) provides \(g_p\) for these parameters and an exact generic denumerant identity but does not give the full interior-nongap structure or the universal quadratic formula for \(p\)-genus. Previously reported results address the first odd threshold or the even parity branch. The new result covers every threshold of the odd second phase and a quadratic rather than a single-level defect. Because the general counting identity can be specialized, a known unstated corollary remains the central priority risk.

## Mathematical value

PASS. The complete generalized gap set is a natural strictly finer invariant than the generalized Frobenius endpoint. The theorem resolves its entire second-phase topology as explicit disjoint interior intervals and proves an unexpectedly \(a\)-independent quadratic genus–Frobenius deficit, with a sharp first break at the fourth threshold. This gives a full natural parameter phase, not an arbitrary isolated slice or a routine single-value recomputation.
