For every odd \(a\ge5\), the generalized \(p\)-gap set of the three consecutive coin denominations \(a,a+1,a+2\) is an initial interval with explicitly located interior subintervals deleted, throughout the entire second Frobenius phase \(p=(a-1)/2+t\), \(1\le t\le(a+3)/2\). The genus is exactly \(a(a+t)-\lfloor(t-2)^2/3\rfloor\); its quadratic defect is independent of \(a\) and first becomes positive at \(t=4\).
