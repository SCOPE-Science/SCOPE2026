For an invariantly random plane cutting a very thin convex-meridian toroid into an annulus, the scaled normal tilt and offset converge to a universal triangle law with density \(6x\), whose tilt marginal is \(\operatorname{Beta}(2,2)\). The exact first-order total-variation defect is \(2q/R\), where \(q\) is the exposed top-face half-width; a circular meridian instead has an explicit, strictly positive second-order coefficient.
