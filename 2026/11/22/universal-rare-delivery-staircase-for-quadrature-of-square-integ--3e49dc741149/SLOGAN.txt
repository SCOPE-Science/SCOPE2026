For any square-integrable Lévy process, optimizing the posterior mean-square error of its time integral under independently erased exact samples gives the same observation times, regardless of drift or jump law. With fixed budget \(n\), rare successful delivery makes the unique optimal sites form an arithmetic staircase around \(2/3\), separated by \(2p/9+o_n(p)\), with an exact risk advantage \(n(n^2-1)p^3/486+o_n(p^3)\) after variance normalization.
