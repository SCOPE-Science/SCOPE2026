For every prime \(p\ge5\) and every three-site aperture, the nonzero singular-value condition numbers of two distinct quadratic-chirp rows have explicit support-independent sharp minima and maxima, each attained by exactly six choices of a second outcome per first outcome. Balanced root-of-unity triples minimize the row correlation, whereas two coincident phasors and one nearest neighbor maximize it; the result concerns the two-dimensional observable subspace, not recovery of all three coefficients.
