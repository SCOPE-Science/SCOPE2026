The shifted two-adic valuations of every fixed-index quartic-integral coefficient sequence have correlation at least \(-1/2\), sharply and uniformly over all positive displacements. Equality occurs exactly at the two residue families \(h\equiv\pm2\ell\pmod{2^{1+\lceil\log_2(2\ell)\rceil}}\); every other displacement has strictly greater correlation.
