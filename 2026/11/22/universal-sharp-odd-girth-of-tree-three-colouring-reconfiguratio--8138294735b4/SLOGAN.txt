For every \(n\)-vertex tree, the proper three-colouring reconfiguration graph has a sharp shape-independent odd girth of \(3n\) when \(n\) is odd and is bipartite when \(n\) is even. For \(n\ge3\) it also contains squares, so arbitrarily short even cycles coexist with an arbitrarily long minimum odd cycle; the same theorem describes maximum-independent-set token slides on \(T\square K_3\).
