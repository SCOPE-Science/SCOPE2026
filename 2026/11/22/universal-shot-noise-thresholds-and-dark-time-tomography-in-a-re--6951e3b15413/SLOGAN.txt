In a reset fluorescent emitter with arbitrarily distributed dark sojourns, the long-time photon Fano factor crosses one exactly when \(p\sum_i s_i\int_0^\infty t e^{-rt}\Pr(U_i>t)\,dt=1\), and can cross at most once as resetting accelerates. Sweeping the pump-rate crossing over all reset rates uniquely recovers the aggregate hidden dark-time survival function, while the third count cumulant remains non-Poissonian even at the Fano crossing.
