For the self-commutator of \(f\mapsto f'(e^{-h}z^m)\) on every coefficient-weighted Hardy–Dirichlet Hilbert scale, the near-boundary spectrum splits into positive and sign-changing arithmetic branches. The negative branch has the universal cutoff \(m\log m/(m-1)\), independent of the Hilbert weight, and yields sharp trace-, Hilbert–Schmidt- and operator-norm amplification constants.
