# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The claim is the augmented-ancestor (formal-parent) susceptibility, with all undirected parent and grandparent edges independently retained with common probability. Starting from Ménard–Sénizergues's proven block decomposition, the first-block expectation is independently calculated through parity-refined pgfs and the boundary expectation through the two-variable derivative system. A monotonicity calculation verifies a strictly positive derivative at the critical point; no second moment is needed because all pgf derivatives are evaluated strictly inside the unit disc. All stated normalization, endpoint and quantifier restrictions are explicit. The standalone finite checker passed 247 checks; infinite conclusions are proved analytically.

## Originality — PASS against inspected sources

The motivating source determines the threshold and the independent-block construction, but the inspected relevant sections contain no explicit augmented-ancestor susceptibility identity, no pole amplitude, and no finite-mean universal pole theorem. Špakulová supplies a finite-type tree-of-pieces method and a regular example; Hutchcroft's nonunimodular quasi-transitive results can dominate the *exponent-only deterministic case*, but not the full arbitrary-offspring formula/residue. A related earlier result on *site* percolation is not a bond-percolation implication. Published finding searches using the exact observable, alternative terminology, and critical exponent found no dominating claim. The full texts of the two older comparison sources were not inspected, so stronger historical coverage remains a risk; this is not independent priority certification.

## Value — PASS

Susceptibility is the canonical expected-cluster response and its amplitude refines a threshold statement. The result gives a quantitative universal critical boundary for random grandparent graphs with arbitrary finite-mean offspring, including infinite-variance distributions not covered by standard finite-moment arguments. The proof exposes why the singularity remains a simple pole despite shortcuts and correlations. Exact amplitudes allow law-by-law comparison, so this is more than computing another threshold or a low-order expansion.

## Limits and nearest literature

The statement is annealed and augmented-ancestor, not a theorem about the exact original-root susceptibility or supercritical cluster-size tails.
