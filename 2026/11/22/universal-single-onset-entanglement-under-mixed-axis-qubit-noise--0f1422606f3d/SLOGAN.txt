A coupled two-qubit system with bit flips on one qubit, phase flips on the other, and simultaneous Poisson resetting to a separable state has exactly one reset-rate entanglement onset for every strictly positive coupling and pair of noise rates. The critical rate is the unique positive root of an explicit eighth-degree polynomial, and the stationary concurrence stays positive at every larger finite rate with \(C(r)\sim 2J/r\), even when local noise rates are arbitrarily large but fixed.
