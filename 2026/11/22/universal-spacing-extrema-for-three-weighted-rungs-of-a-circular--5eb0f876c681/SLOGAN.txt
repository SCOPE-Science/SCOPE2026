On every circular two-rail ladder with at least five rungs, marking exactly three rungs with any common conductance \(t>0\), \(t\ne1\), maximizes the weighted spanning-tree sum precisely when the marked rungs are as evenly spaced as possible, and minimizes it precisely when they are consecutive. The strict spacing order survives unchanged when the conductance contrast reverses, by an exact positive three-defect determinant factorization.
