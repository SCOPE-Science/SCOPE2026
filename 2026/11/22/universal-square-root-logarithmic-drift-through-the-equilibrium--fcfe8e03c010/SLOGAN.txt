At the equilibrium-line degeneration of a quadratic homoclinic-flip flow, an explicit open set of global trajectories has \(z(t)\sim-\sqrt{8\log t}\) while \(x(t)\sim1/[t\sqrt{8\log t}]\) and \(y(t)\sim1/[8t^2\log t]\). The constants follow from an invariant ratio–product region and exact differential identities.
