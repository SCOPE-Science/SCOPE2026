For arbitrary one-dimensional sampling sites, any positive target measure and any nonnegative heteroscedastic diagonal nugget, exponential-kernel Bayesian quadrature has nonnegative weights whose sum never exceeds the target mass. Within the power-exponential family \(1\le\alpha\le2\), this universal stability property holds exactly at \(\alpha=1\): every smoother exponent admits a two-node negative-weight counterexample.
