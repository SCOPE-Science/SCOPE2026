For every nonprincipal finite local ring with square-zero maximal ideal, the Heisenberg prescribed-commutator map has exactly three positive fiber sizes, indexed by zero, nonzero radical elements, and units, with explicit formulas in the residue-field size, embedding dimension, and symplectic rank. For fixed residue size and symplectic rank, its commuting probability tends to \(q^{-4h}\) as embedding dimension increases, with commuting pairs asymptotically concentrated over horizontal coordinates in the radical.
