In a ferromagnetic random-cluster model on any three-path theta network, the third connected occupation correlation of one edge on each path is strictly negative for \(1<q\le2\), but for every \(q>2\) it reverses exactly once from positive to negative as all bond activities scale upward. An explicit all-weight cubic sign criterion and a rational exact-enumeration verifier establish the universal boundary without implying a thermodynamic phase transition.
