For any strictly decreasing, symmetric positive-definite circular kernel satisfying a natural sign condition, the unique three-site design minimizing the largest sampling Gram eigenvalue has gaps \(d,d,1-2d\), where \(K(d)+K(2d)=0\). Every periodic mean-zero fractional Sobolev space \(H^s\), \(s>1/2\), belongs to this class, with a sharp smoothness-dependent node law and an explicit \(4^{-s}\) advantage over equispacing.
