# Scientific review of the two-peak Poisson-kernel compression theorem

## Correctness — PASS
The statement fixes the probability-measure normalization and the amplitude of each kernel. The Poisson convolution identity yields the exact objective (1) and a coercive limit as the trial radius approaches one. For small source radius, every global maximizer is confined to an order-radius disk on which the Hessian is negative definite; reflection and the gradient equation fix its location. For the boundary limit, a positive order-inverse-depth witness forces every global maximizer into the compactly scaled neighborhood of exactly one source. The limiting two-variable rational objective has one strict global maximizer. A nondegenerate implicit-function argument gives one local/global optimizer per source, and differentiation of the exact angular equation yields the nonzero fourth-order inward drift. The norm identity completes the squared-error coefficient. The accompanying finite tests independently check normalization and several asymptotic coefficients but are not substitutes for these steps.

## Originality — PASS on inspected evidence, historical priority not certified
Bailey's publicly deposited thesis (8 January 2020), chapter 6 and appendix C2, studies L² comparison of wrapped-normal and wrapped-Cauchy models, including concentric two-wrapped-Cauchy mixtures with a common direction and varying concentration. Chaubey's paper gives the wrapped-Cauchy/Poisson estimation framework and Fourier representation. Neither inspected source supplies an angularly separated two-source best-one-kernel analysis. The closest recoverable prior results concern the excluded coincident or antipodal separations, a different optimization over boundary observation arcs, or Bergman reproducing-kernel fitting. The present theorem's open interval of source separations, two asymptotic optimizer branches, and nonzero fourth-order angular shift do not follow from those cases. The Bailey–Codling accepted-version PDF could not be materially read; its available abstract describes wrapped-normal input mixtures rather than the theorem here. Priority remains best-knowledge and would benefit from independent checking.

## Value — PASS
Equal-strength separated Poisson sources are a canonical harmonic measure and wrapped-Cauchy mixture. The best single normalized source is a natural nonlinear compression invariant. The proved change from a unique bisector fit to exactly two peak-associated fits and the universal boundary-depth/error coefficients describe an actual resolution limit, while the fourth-power angular shift quantifies how the otherwise independent peaks interact. These exact facts are not a renaming of the Fourier convolution identity or a small unmotivated constant refinement.

## Boundaries and residual risks
The theorem is asymptotic at low and high concentration, not a classification of the intermediate interval. It requires a fixed nonzero, nonantipodal angular separation and unchanged kernel amplitudes. The case of separation scaling with boundary depth is not settled. The accepted Bailey–Codling article remains only partially accessible; historical priority has not been independently audited.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
