The best single circular Poisson kernel for two fixed, nonantipodal equal-strength sources is uniquely centered at low concentration but splits into exactly two optimal fits near the boundary. Each boundary fit has universal depth \((1+\sqrt2)(1-r)\), moves inward by an explicit positive multiple of \((1-r)^4\), and incurs the sharp squared-error blow-up \((2\sqrt2-5/2)/(1-r)\).
