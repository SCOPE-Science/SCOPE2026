Even exact single-site predictions cannot prevent ideal independent parallel unmasking from contracting every signed two-point correlation of a zero-field Ising chain. With independently scheduled site reveals over \(K\) rounds, the extra density of unfavorable bonds is universally at least \(\rho(1-\rho^2)^2/[2K(1+\rho^3)^2]\), for either interaction sign and every finite chain length; the conclusion does not extend to coordinated, collision-avoiding schedules.
