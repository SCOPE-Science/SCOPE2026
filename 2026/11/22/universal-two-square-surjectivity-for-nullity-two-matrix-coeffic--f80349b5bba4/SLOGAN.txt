For every algebraically closed field \(F\) of characteristic zero and every \(n\ge7\), each matrix is a sum \(X^2+BY^2\) for **every** coefficient \(B\in M_n(F)\) of nullity two. A rank-preserving square-completion argument, including an explicit nilpotent three-chain root, resolves the exponent-two part of an open matrix-power-surjectivity question.
