# Mathematical review

**Correctness: PASS.** Every cyclic retraction factors through the elementary abelian quotient and is uniquely represented by a rank-one projection and one of \(|G'|\) lifts. Direct composition makes the graph an independent-fiber blow-up, and the clique and diameter formulas then follow from independent lines and dual functionals. The included checker verifies finite cases, while the general argument proves all group and parameter ranges.

**Originality: PASS relative to inspected sources.** Anil Kumar–Pradhan (2019) gives general extraspecial endomorphism formulas, and the published extraspecial retract census counts idempotents by image rank. Neither asserts universality across arbitrary finite exponent-\(p\) groups or the complete cyclic-retraction clique and diameter statements. The previously classified elementary generalized dihedral retraction graph and generic matrix commuting graphs cover different objects; structural overlap is acknowledged. Residual risk: equivalent graph formulations using linear projection terminology or general transformation semigroup theory may exist.

**Value: PASS.** The complete graph isomorphism discards all nonabelian multiplication data, even higher nilpotency class; the explicit clique polynomial and sharp dimension-two/three/four distance transition give recoverable natural combinatorial invariants for a broad class of finite groups. It is not merely a restatement of an individual extraspecial group count.

**Scope and limitations.** Only cyclic-image retractions of groups of prime exponent are included. The general abstract graph isomorphism does not distinguish groups sharing the two quotient orders. Independent publication priority and external review are not established.
