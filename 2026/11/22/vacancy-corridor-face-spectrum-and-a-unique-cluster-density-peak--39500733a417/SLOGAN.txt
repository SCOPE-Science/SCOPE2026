In three-row square-lattice site percolation, every nonunit enclosed face is a vacancy corridor, yielding an exact all-length cluster-count formula and a rational limiting density. The density has a unique maximum at \(p=0.328618598\ldots\), with an \(11(1-p)^3\) high-occupancy tail and a matching asymptotic occupancy–fragmentation covariance sign reversal.
