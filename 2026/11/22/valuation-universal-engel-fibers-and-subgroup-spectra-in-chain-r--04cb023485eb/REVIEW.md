# Scientific assessment: valuation-universal right-Engel fibers

## Correctness — PASS

The assertion specifies every finite commutative chain ring, residue size, nilpotency length, right-Engel depth and prescribed target. Direct group multiplication gives the exact word identity; the two translation variables map onto an explicitly identified principal ideal. The kernel-image formula gives the correct fiber factor \(q^{m+\rho}\), and unit valuation shells account for every multiplier with no double counting. The fixed-argument subgroup is a crossed-homomorphism kernel, and its order follows from a precise solvability congruence. The limits \(q=2\), \(q>2\), \(m=1\), and the final two depths are proved separately. Every prescribed-target fiber and every fixed-argument subgroup order was checked independently by complete multiplication enumeration in sixteen specified finite rings; the executable and captured results are supplied. Finite checks support, rather than replace, the universal proof.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

The 2005 affine-group archival antecedent classifies abelian regular subgroups and radical rings, not these Engel-word fibers. The 2013 full-text 2-Engel work gives probability bounds and known subgroup closure, not all-depth target multiplicities or valuation-resolved subgroup orders. Existing all-depth Engel formulas for abelian-by-involution groups and coprime abelian-by-abelian groups cover special cases, but not the full noncoprime chain-ring affine family. An exact conjugacy/character table of the same affine family covers first commuting statistics but does not determine the all-depth valuation law without additional analysis. A closely related **Lie-algebra** Engel-fiber result over chain rings has been compared; it concerns additive brackets and has no group-valued Engelizers or unit-multiplier restriction. These prior results constrain the priority claim and are expressly credited, not repackaged as new. Broad Engel-word, metabelian group and unpublished word-map results may contain equivalent formulas, which cannot be completely excluded by a bounded bibliography.

## Value — PASS

An Engel word is a canonical group-word invariant, with direct connections to nilpotence, finite-group word distributions and fixed-element Engelizers. Complete target fibers for all depths are stronger than a commuting probability; the exact right-argument subgroup-size distribution is stronger than the known statement that an Engelizer is a subgroup. The sharp contrast between a nilpotent uniform-depth law in residue size two and a nonvanishing, stationary mixture in larger residue fields is a structural boundary, while identical distributions for nonisomorphic affine groups show precisely what the invariants fail to detect. The theorem applies to an unbounded natural family, including different characteristics, and is not an arbitrary small census.

## Scientific limitations and closest coverage

The theorem is restricted to one-dimensional affine groups over finite commutative chain rings; no claim is made for general semidirect products or higher-dimensional affine groups. It does not classify all Engelizer subgroup embeddings or ring isomorphism types. The closest primary sources are Caranti–Dalla Volta–Sala (2005), Erfanian–Farrokhi (2013), and the three accessible works on coprime abelian-by-abelian Engel words, abelian-by-involution Engel words, and chain-ring affine character tables. Direct finite testing covers sixteen cases but no parameter-infinite enumeration. Originality is a self-review, not an independent priority certification.
