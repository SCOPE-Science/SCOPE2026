The hub-opposite facet of a wheel Laplacian simplex carries only \(1.930486975\ldots/m^2\) of its Euclidean boundary area, even though the same hub vertex carries asymptotically half of the unique minimum-enclosing-sphere support measure. Exact intrinsic facet areas, volume, in- and enclosing-spheres establish the sharp divergence \(R_m/r_m\sim 0.259002006\ldots\,m^2\).
