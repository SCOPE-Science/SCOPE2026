For the inverse-Gaussian CDF–Stein goodness-of-fit statistic, the moment estimator leaves a nonzero large-threshold residual while maximum likelihood cancels it exactly. At every deterministic vanishing weight \(a_n\to0\), the exponentially weighted moment statistic scaled by \(a_n\) converges to \(\frac{3}{2\phi}\chi_1^2\), while its likelihood counterpart collapses to zero; the Gaussian weight has the analogous \(\sqrt{a_n}\) law.
