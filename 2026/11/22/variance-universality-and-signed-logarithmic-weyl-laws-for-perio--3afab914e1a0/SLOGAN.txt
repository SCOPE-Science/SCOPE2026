For a real periodic diagonal multiplier of the Hilbert matrix, the selfadjoint commutator has essential spectrum \([-\pi\sigma,\pi\sigma]\), where \(\sigma\) is the periodic standard deviation. For \(\sigma>0\) there is one absolutely continuous channel of each sign, and at each fixed \(0<t<\pi\sigma\) the positive and negative finite-section counts both grow as \(\pi^{-2}\operatorname{arcosh}(\pi\sigma/t)\log N\). These laws survive a weighted summable deviation from periodicity; at \(\sigma=0\) there is no absolutely continuous part.
