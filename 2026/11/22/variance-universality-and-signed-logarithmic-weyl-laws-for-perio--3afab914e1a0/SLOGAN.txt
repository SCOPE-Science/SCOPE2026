For a real periodic diagonal multiplier of the Hilbert matrix, the selfadjoint commutator has an essential spectral band of width \(2\pi\sigma\) and one absolutely continuous channel of each sign, where \(\sigma\) is exactly the periodic standard deviation. The positive and negative finite-section counts both grow as \(\pi^{-2}\operatorname{arcosh}(\pi\sigma/t)\log N\) below the band edge; these laws survive a weighted summable deviation from periodicity.
