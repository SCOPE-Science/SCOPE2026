For cubic interpolation at the symmetric nodes \((-1,-a,a,1)\), the exact amplification from nodal total variation to continuous total variation is the maximum of two explicit step-response functions, uniquely minimized at \(a=0.48013692628\ldots\) with sharp gain \(1.42359392505\ldots\). This optimal grid differs from those optimizing pointwise range escape, because total variation counts all oscillation lobes rather than only the largest excursion.
