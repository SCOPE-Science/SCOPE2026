Every primitive temporal three-cycle of deterministic Toom majority on an indexed three-row torus is a vertical rotating wave, and on every circumference \(n\ge3\) its exact number of temporal orbits is \(\bigl(2^n+2\cos(n\pi/3)+2\,3^{n/2}\cos(5n\pi/6)\bigr)/3\). These cycles occur at every circumference and their count grows as \(2^n/3\).
