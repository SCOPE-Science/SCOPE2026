The Shrikhande and four-by-four rook graphs have identical strongly regular parameters, yet their ordinary mutual-visibility polynomials first differ at degree four. The Shrikhande graph has exactly \(32\) maximum mutually visible sets of size \(10\), whereas the rook graph has exactly \(96\) of size \(9\); in each graph these maxima form a single, exchange-isolated symmetry orbit.
