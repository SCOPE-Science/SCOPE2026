For reversible dimer adsorption on an odd ring with one weak closing bond of rate \(\varepsilon\), exactly \((N-1)/2\) relaxation modes become slow, and the sharp gap is \(4\varepsilon/N+O_N(\varepsilon^2)\). The full first-order slow spectrum is a Krawtchouk spectrum, while initially empty coverage approaches \(1/2-e^{-4s/N}/(2N^2)\) on the time scale \(t=s/\varepsilon\).
