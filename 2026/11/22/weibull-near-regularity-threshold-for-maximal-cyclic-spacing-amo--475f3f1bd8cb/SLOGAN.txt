For each fixed \(m\ge2\), the loss from perfectly even cyclic spacing of the best \(m\)-subset of \(n\) sampled circle points has a sharp Weibull law on scale \(n^{-m/(m-1)}\), with a density-sensitive Poisson approximation rate. Under uniform sampling, a specified subset is within \(\varepsilon\) of regularity with exact probability \((m\varepsilon)^{m-1}\).
