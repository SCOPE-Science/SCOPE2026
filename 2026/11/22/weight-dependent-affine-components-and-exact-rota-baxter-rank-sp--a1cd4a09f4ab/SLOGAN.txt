On the nonnilpotent left Leibniz algebra \(L_n=ku\oplus kv\oplus W\) with \([v,u]=u\), weight-zero Rota–Baxter maps form three affine-linear components, while every nonzero weight yields six components in two disconnected triples. Over \(\mathbb F_q\), every nonzero weight has exactly twice as many operators as weight zero, with a complete Gaussian-binomial rank census and an explicit positive count of invertible operators that cannot occur at weight zero.
