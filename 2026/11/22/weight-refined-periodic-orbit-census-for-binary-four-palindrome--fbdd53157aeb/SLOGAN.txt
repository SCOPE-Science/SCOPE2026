Binary circular words avoiding all four ordinary length-four palindromes have the exact weight-refined zeta function \(1/(1-uz^2-(u+u^3)z^4-u^3z^6)\). Exactly even circumferences are possible; at each even circumference \(n\), every one-count from \(\lceil n/4\rceil\) through \(\lfloor3n/4\rfloor\) occurs, and every even length occurs primitively.
