Every real periodic discrete first-order Sobolev norm \(\sum_i|f_i|+\gamma\sum_i|f_{i+1}-f_i|\) on \(n\ge3\) vertices has only global-sign and dihedral linear symmetries, except at \((n,\gamma)=(3,1)\), where a hidden tetrahedral reanchoring symmetry enlarges the isometry group from order \(12\) to order \(48\). This is the unique symmetry-enhancement point among all uniform positive gradient weights and cycle lengths.
