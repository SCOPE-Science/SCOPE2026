# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** For every solution \(g^2=f\), restriction to the fixed-point set of \(f\) is an involution, and commutation with \(f\) forces each kernel fiber to follow its fixed point or two-cycle. The direct-to-root subsets and their remaining assignments give exact bijections with the two finite binomial sums; conversely they always reconstruct roots. Functional digraphs with \(g^4=g^2\) consist exactly of cycles of lengths one and two with attached trees of height at most two. This yields the exponential series. Explicit enumeration of all \(n^n\) maps up to \(n=7\) confirms every possible idempotent target; the exact rank-weighted series is also checked. The argument covers empty first-level sets with the \(0^0=1\) convention.

**Originality — PASS relative to reviewed evidence.** Kozyra (2018), Propositions 14 and 17, already counts square roots of the identity and the constant map; these claims are *not* attributed as new. The result unifies every intermediate idempotent kernel type, introduces a quantitative exchanged-fiber interaction and provides the global rank-profile enumeration. The full relevant material read in Kozyra (2018) and Bhat–Gopalakrishna (2022) did not provide an equal or stronger enumerator. The accessible Kozyra (2021) abstract addresses existence, not an enumerative specialization. This does not prove worldwide historical uniqueness.

**Value — PASS.** This is a natural complete answer to counting the fiber of the squaring map over every idempotent of \(\mathcal T_n\). It gives explicit nonfactorizing interaction weights for pairs of kernel classes rather than a case-by-case algorithm, and yields an exact rank distribution for the algebraic identity \(g^4=g^2\) across all finite degrees. Those data are useful for transformation-semigroup equation solving and finite functional digraph enumeration. It is not merely a small numerical slice.

**Closest literature and risks.** The 2018 finite-root paper covers rank-one and full-rank targets, the 2021 graph paper supplies general existence criteria, and the 2022 root paper supplies broader obstructions. An equivalent count hidden in another published paper remains possible; full text of the 2021 paper was inaccessible, although its official abstract was reviewed. No result about maximizers over all transformations is established.
