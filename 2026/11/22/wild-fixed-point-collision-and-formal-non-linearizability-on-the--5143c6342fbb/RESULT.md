# Wild fixed-point collision and formal non-linearizability on the quintic del Pezzo surface

## Finding

Let \(S=\overline{M}_{0,5}\) be the smooth split degree-five del Pezzo surface over \(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z\), and let \(\sigma\) be the automorphism induced by cyclically permuting its five marked points. The **entire scheme-theoretic fixed locus**, including all characteristics and the boundary of the compactification, has an integral presentation
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Fix}_S(\sigma)\simeq\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z[u]/(u^2-u-1).}
\]
In particular, this is a finite flat degree-two scheme whose discriminant is \(5\). Over fields of characteristic different from \(5\) it consists of two distinct geometric points. In characteristic \(5\) it consists of **one geometric point of scheme length two**, with completed local algebra \(k[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)\).

The tangent representation at that unique characteristic-five point, in natural cross-ratio coordinates, is
\[
D\sigma=\begin{pmatrix}2&4\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
(D\sigma-I)\ne0,\qquad (D\sigma-I)^2=0.
\]
**Nevertheless the fixed scheme is isolated.** Consequently, the wild order-five action is not formally conjugate to its tangent linear representation at this point. The obstruction holds over every algebraically closed field of characteristic \(5\), and is independent of any description of the singularity of the wild quotient.

## Assumptions and scope

The compactification \(\overline M_{0,5}\) is the moduli space of stable five-pointed genus-zero curves with **ordered** markings, defined over \(\mathbb Z\). It is smooth and proper of relative dimension two and is the split degree-five del Pezzo surface. The action is the standard relabelling action of the constant five-cycle, not an arithmetic Frobenius twist. Fixed loci mean **equalizers as schemes**, not sets of geometric points. The exceptional characteristic is exactly \(5\); no claim about the minimal resolution or singularity classification of the wild quotient is made.

## Proof

**1. The stable interior and an integral action formula.** Every geometrically smooth marked rational curve is uniquely normalized to the ordered tuple \((\infty,0,1,u,v)\), where \(u,v\) are distinct from \(0,1\) and from each other. This identifies the interior with
\[
M_{0,5}=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z[u,v,\{uv(u-1)(v-1)(u-v)\}^{-1}].
\]
Relabel cyclically to \((0,1,u,v,\infty)\), and renormalize its first three markings to \((\infty,0,1)\). The resulting automorphism is
\[
\sigma(u,v)=\left(\frac{u(v-1)}{v(u-1)},\frac{u}{u-1}\right).
\]
All displayed denominators are units on the interior, and the formula has order five because it realizes a permutation of the five markings (the order-five identity may also be checked directly by rational-function composition).

**2. Fixed equalizer as a ring.** The second fixed equation gives \(v=u/(u-1)\). Substituting this into the first yields the equation
\[
0=\frac{u(v-1)}{v(u-1)}-u=-\frac{u^2-u-1}{u-1}.
\]
Thus the full fixed ideal on the interior is generated by \(v-u/(u-1)\) and \(u^2-u-1\). Modulo these equations, \(u(u-1)=1\), so \(v=u^2=u+1\) and \(u-v=-1\). Furthermore \(u-1\), \(u\), \(v=u+1\), and \(v-1=u\) are all units: in particular \((u+1)(u-2)=u^2-u-2=-1\) modulo \(u^2-u-1\). Therefore none of the interior denominators needs further localization. The fixed scheme on the interior is precisely \(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z[u]/(u^2-u-1)\), with no hidden components or embedded points.

**3. No fixed points in the compactification boundary.** The boundary of \(\overline M_{0,5}\) is a relative simple-normal-crossings union of ten irreducible divisors, indexed by unordered two-element subsets of the five labels. The five-cycle has two free orbits of length five on this set. Any geometric boundary point belongs to at most two distinct boundary divisors. A fixed boundary point would be contained in the entire five-element orbit of each divisor passing through it, which is impossible. Therefore the boundary contains no geometric fixed point over any algebraically closed field. A finite-type scheme with no geometric points is empty; hence the scheme-theoretic fixed locus on the whole compactification is the interior fixed scheme computed above.

**4. Ramified integral degeneration.** The monic equation \(u^2-u-1\) gives a free rank-two module over \(\mathbb Z\), with basis \(1,u\) and discriminant \(1+4=5\). Its discriminant is invertible away from characteristic five. In characteristic five,
\[
u^2-u-1=(u-3)^2.
\]
Putting \(\varepsilon=u-3\) identifies the geometric fixed local algebra with \(k[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)\); the fifth marking has coordinate \(v=u+1=4+\varepsilon\). Thus the two ordinary fixed points collide into a single curvilinear nonreduced point, without any boundary specialization.

**5. Wild tangent action and obstruction to formal linearization.** Differentiating the explicit action, and using \(v=u^2\) together with \(u^2-u-1=0\), gives at every geometric fixed point
\[
D\sigma=\begin{pmatrix}-u&u^{-2}\\-u^2&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad \det D\sigma=1,
\qquad \operatorname{tr}D\sigma=-u.
\]
At \(u=3\) in characteristic five this reduces to the displayed matrix \(J\). The nonzero nilpotent matrix \(J-I\) has square zero, so its linear fixed subspace is one-dimensional. If the action on the completed two-dimensional regular local ring were formally conjugate to the linear action \(J\), the formal fixed subscheme would be a one-dimensional formal line. But the actual completed fixed local algebra has length two and dimension zero. Such a conjugacy cannot exist.

For comparison, in characteristic different from five the characteristic polynomials \(t^2+ut+1\) at the two fixed points combine to \(\Phi_5(t)=t^4+t^3+t^2+t+1\). Their eigenvalues are primitive fifth roots in inverse pairs, in accordance with the familiar tame cyclic-quotient calculation. This comparison is not a claim of a new tame quotient classification.

## Verification

The accompanying \(\texttt{verify.py}\), run with Python 3 and SymPy, checks the rational-action identity after five iterations; the exact elimination identities for the fixed ideal; the cyclotomic resultant; the tangent matrix reduced at characteristic five, its nontrivial Jordan block and fifth power; the factorization of the quadratic modulo five; and the two free five-element orbits of boundary components. The boundary's simple-normal-crossings property and the formal-linearization obstruction are proved above and do not rest on finite sampling. The checker additionally tests rational root patterns over representative prime fields, strictly as a supplementary consistency check.

## Relationship to prior work

Wolter (2018, §4) identifies the **two geometric fixed points in characteristic zero** of a cyclic order-five subgroup of the automorphism group of the quintic del Pezzo surface. Boitrel (2023, Lemma 3.27 and Proposition 3.28) describes order-five automorphisms of degree-five del Pezzo surfaces and their two five-element orbits on exceptional curves. Dolgachev and Martin (2024, §3.1.5) classify the degree-five del Pezzo automorphism group over odd characteristic, including characteristic five. Those results establish the ambient action, not the global integral fixed equalizer, its nonreduced characteristic-five fiber, or failure of formal linearization.

A separate tame finite-field quotient analysis treats the two \(A_4\) singularities in characteristics other than five and gives the same fixed-point cross-ratio equations on that restricted base. The present assertion is the **wild characteristic-five extension and scheme-theoretic collision**; it does not present the previously known tame calculations as new. Related cyclotomic fixed-point collisions on Klein cubic varieties involve different ambient varieties and different fixed schemes, and do not imply the result for \(\overline M_{0,5}\).

## Limitations

This result does not determine the local analytic type, dual graph, rationality properties, or minimal resolution of the **wild quotient** \(S/\langle\sigma\rangle\) in characteristic five, and formal non-linearizability does not itself classify those structures. It concerns the canonical split model and the standard five-cycle; arithmetic twists have not been classified. The explicit quadratic equation in prior tame calculations makes the integral-specialization step short, so priority relative to other unpublished modular fixed-scheme observations is a meaningful residual risk. Reproducibility checks support exact algebraic steps but are not independent proof certification.
