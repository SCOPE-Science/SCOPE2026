The proof defines the scheme-theoretic fixed equalizer on the integral stable five-pointed interior, eliminates one cross-ratio and obtains a monic quadratic. It checks all removed denominators are units in the fixed ring, and excludes the boundary by the two free five-cycles on the ten SNC components. ## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk

Boitrel, *Del Pezzo surfaces of degree 5 over perfect fields* (2023, Lemma 3.27 and Proposition 3.28) and Dolgachev–Martin, *Automorphisms of del Pezzo surfaces in odd characteristic* (2024, §3.1.5) describe the automorphisms, not this integral fixed equalizer or its wild structure. A closely related tame quotient calculation already uses the polynomial \(u^2-u-1\), but **explicitly excludes characteristic five** and concerns tame \(A_4\) chains and arithmetic Frobenius. Its statements do not imply a classification of the characteristic-five fixed scheme or the nonlinearizability obstruction without checking specialization on the whole compactification and the scheme-level tangent argument. Related cyclotomic fixed-scheme collisions on Klein cubic varieties involve different ambient spaces. Priority is not certified: the short quadratic specialisation might have appeared in unindexed sources.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The standard order-five subgroup of the split quintic del Pezzo's full symmetric automorphism group is a distinguished geometric action. Determining its entire integral fixed scheme identifies the *only* bad characteristic, proves a flat collision rather than disappearance of fixed points, and gives a concrete **isolated wild fixed point with a nontrivial unipotent tangent Jordan block**. This shows that local tame linearization fails in a specific natural rational surface action and establishes the geometry that any study of its wild quotient must accommodate. The result is intentionally narrower than a resolution of the wild quotient singularity, and its value is the structural obstruction rather than the mere polynomial discriminant.
