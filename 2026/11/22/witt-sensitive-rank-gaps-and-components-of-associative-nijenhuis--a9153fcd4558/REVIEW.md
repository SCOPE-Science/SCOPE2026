# Scientific review: isotropic-image Nijenhuis classification

## Correctness — PASS
The original associative Nijenhuis identity is reduced for arbitrary operator blocks; the mixed annihilator equation forces preservation of the one-dimensional annihilator, while the generator equation becomes precisely a Gram-zero condition. No characteristic-specific division except the assumed exclusion of characteristic two enters the proof. The finite-field rank formula follows by counting surjections onto isotropic subspaces and Möbius inversion on their subspaces. The exact Witt-index recursion, the component argument, and sharp rank attainability are spelled out in RESULT.md. The standalone Python checker exhaustively verifies the original identity for the three-dimensional split and nonsplit cases over \(\mathbb F_3\) and the split case over \(\mathbb F_5\), plus the higher-dimensional rank and isotropic-space tests. The infinite claims rest on the proof, not on experiments.

## Originality — PASS, subject to priority risk
The 2021 paper of Abdujabborov–Karimjanov–Kodirova (DOI:10.2478/cm-2021-0020), full PDF Theorem 5 and accompanying proof, treats three-dimensional complex nilpotent associative algebras. The 2023 original arXiv preprint 2308.12121 and its later updated PDF focus on two-dimensional pre-Lie and three-dimensional complex associative algebras. These cover or touch the two-dimensional bilinear-space base case, which is explicitly excluded from any novelty claim. The present theorem concerns the full unbounded family \(n\ge3\), a specific rank polynomial for all odd finite fields, a nonsplit-even middle rank gap beginning at \(n=4\), and geometric components over algebraic closures. Indexed related results for unital square-zero algebras and the three-dimensional commutative nilpotent algebra address different underlying objects or only the base case. The searched literature did not establish an implication covering the new theorem. A broader unlocated theorem about orthogonal matrix nullcones or Nijenhuis maps could still subsume some conclusions; publication priority is therefore provisional.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This is an exact classification for a canonical arbitrarily large family of two-step commutative associative algebras, not an arbitrary parameter slice. Its multiplication pairing is intrinsic (the algebra's square and annihilator are both one-dimensional), so Witt type is a natural invariant. The rank gap is mathematically sharp and distinguishes the two even-dimensional finite-field Witt forms; the component count exposes the associated orthogonal-geometry structure. The complete rank polynomial makes the phenomenon quantitatively usable and reproducible. The three-dimensional known special case is acknowledged; higher-dimensional geometry and arithmetic are the contribution.

## Scope and risks
The hypothesis that the pairing is nondegenerate is essential for the block reduction. Characteristic two, degenerate pairings, and operator-orbit classification are not asserted. The polynomial equations defining the operator space may carry nonreduced scheme structure, which has not been analyzed. External publication priority is not certified. Finite checks are not a substitute for the general arguments.
