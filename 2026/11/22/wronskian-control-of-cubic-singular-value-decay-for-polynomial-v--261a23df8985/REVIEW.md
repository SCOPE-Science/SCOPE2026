# Mathematical review

**Correctness — PASS.** The exact subdiagonal coefficient identity is reconstructed in the proof and its order-three leading coefficient equals the polynomial Wronskian. The finite-band weighted Toeplitz counting lemma is derived by fixed-band norm truncation, asymptotic finite Toeplitz moments and block Riemann sums. The higher-order remainder has faster approximation-number decay. Monomial exact singular values and 4,815 rational assertions independently check the algebra. Numerical experiments are supplementary and not infinite-dimensional proofs.

**Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk.** Chalmoukis–Stylogiannis (2021, original 2020 preprint) studies the same class of generalized Volterra integrals, but its checked full text proves properties of individual operators, rather than the singular spectrum of a noncommuting pair. Böttcher–Grudsky supplies classical Toeplitz singular-distribution machinery, not the explicit Wronskian coefficient. Accessible abstracts of Volterra-related Dirichlet-series and Fock commutators involve different domains and estimates. A general symbolic calculus could imply this case, and inaccessible specialist full texts leave historical uncertainty; no exhaustive uniqueness is claimed.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The theorem is a natural all-polynomial structural classification of commutators with an exact principal-symbol formula, strict Schatten cutoff and singular-trace coefficient. This is more than a monomial or numeric parameter exercise and illustrates cancellation of leading Volterra orders.

**Scope and limitations.** Complex polynomials on the disk Hardy Hilbert space only. All singular-value laws are asymptotic rather than exact per-index formulas, except the monomial check. Independent mathematical audit remains to be performed.
