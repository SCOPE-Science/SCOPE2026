For polynomial generalized Volterra operators on \(H^2(\mathbb D)\), the commutator vanishes precisely for proportional derivatives; every other pair has sharp cubic singular-value decay \(s_N\sim J^3N^{-3}\), where \(J\) is the angular \(L^{1/3}\)-mean of \(z^3(g''h'-g'h'')\). Consequently the exact Schatten threshold is \(p>1/3\).
