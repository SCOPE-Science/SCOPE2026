For every full Young subgroup of the symmetric group, the actual geometric fixed-point space of the complete-graph forest complex is PL-homeomorphic to a smaller forest complex joined with explicit discrete sets. This gives a complete integral homotopy classification: for \(m\ge3\) fixed graph labels and \(b\) permuted blocks, the answer is a wedge of \(B_m m^a(m-1)^{b-a}\) spheres of dimension \(m+b-2\), where \(a\) counts the size-two blocks; all smaller-\(m\) exceptions are determined as well.
