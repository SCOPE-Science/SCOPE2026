For synchronous elementary Rule 110 on every labelled binary ring, primitive temporal eight-cycles occur in exactly one orbit when the circumference is divisible by eight, primitive nine-cycles occur in exactly \(2\mathbf1_{6\mid n}+4\mathbf1_{12\mid n}+2\mathbf1_{18\mid n}\) orbits, and primitive ten-cycles never occur. These consecutive short-return classes exhibit bounded arithmetic wave populations even though shorter five- and seven-period classes have exponentially growing populations.
