The reduced union of all \(q^3+1\) Hermitian-curve tangents over \(\mathbb F_{q^2}\) has exactly \(q^2(q^2-q+1)\) ordinary \((q+1)\)-fold crossings and a uniform two-parity zeta function over \(\mathbb F_q\). Its rational crossing branches have opposite configurations in odd and even characteristic, although the tangent union covers every point of both \(\mathbb P^2(\mathbb F_q)\) and \(\mathbb P^2(\mathbb F_{q^2})\).
