# Scientific review

**Correctness: PASS.** The explicit quartic is the smooth characteristic-two companion-matrix construction proved in the cited initiating paper. Complete projective-chart sums were evaluated over each of the first thirteen extensions. A separate direct homogeneous evaluation agrees for the first five; exact Newton identities and Poincare duality uniquely determine the degree-22 Frobenius polynomial from the first eleven; the last two extension counts agree independently. A finite-field gcd certificate proves the residual degree-twelve factor irreducible, and its Newton polygon yields height two. Picard ranks follow from the established characteristic-two K3 Tate conjecture. The supplied C++ and Python source files executed successfully in the reviewed state.

**Originality: PASS, with priority caveat.** Bourgeois-Conti--Moon--Perez--Warren, arXiv:2611.15768v1, Section 9, prove construction and smoothness, not the explicit zeta function. Targeted searches for the defining polynomial, companion-matrix quartic, Picard rank and formal Brauer height did not locate another determination. Published computations for different characteristic-two K3 quartics (notably the seven-\(A_3\) Klein model and other singular quartic resolutions) do not imply this polynomial's factorization. A disguised isomorphic model and unindexed calculations remain possible.

**Value: PASS.** A complete Hasse--Weil zeta function, exact geometric and arithmetic Picard numbers, and finite formal Brauer height form a natural arithmetic-geometric characterization of a newly constructed smallest-degree smooth space-filling surface. These data distinguish a finite-height example from the supersingular K3 surfaces frequently appearing in small-characteristic quartic constructions. This is not merely a restatement of the construction and does not require a general family-level theorem.

**Limits.** The method is an exhaustive finite-field computation coupled with cohomological reconstruction and proved structural theorems; it is not a formal proof of the software and it does not determine an integral Picard lattice. Publication priority outside the inspected full source and accessible research records is not certified.
