# Mathematical assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The two independent triangular face equations give a universal three-term recurrence with period three, and both cap sums are checked. The \(3\nmid n\) case is killed by the gcd argument even over groups with three-torsion; the \(3\mid n\) case has exactly two freely chosen group elements. Strong labels reduce to distinct nonzero doubles in \(2\Gamma[4]\). Full graph symmetries act as the symmetric group on the three labels, with the distinct fixed-point counts required for Burnside's lemma. All quantifiers, exact vertex degrees, group domains, and the \(n=3\) boundary are explicit. The standalone checker passes 132 seed/group cases and ten independent full-vertex enumerations; exact integer Smith forms and automorphism orders corroborate the structure at small sizes. Computation is not presented as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, historical priority provisional

Bowling (2025, DOI:10.61091/um124-05) gives the group-label definitions, the octahedron example and a **necessary** \((\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z)^2\) subgroup theorem for 3-connected graphs of the relevant degree pattern. The necessary condition is prior work, not a new claim. The closed antiprism-family \(n\bmod3\) classification, universal torsion-free Smith factors, exact counts and symmetry orbit formula are not in its inspected full text. Bowling–Low (2026, arXiv:2611.23164), posted after the 2025 motivating source, develops general Smith-form methods without this particular family’s explicit answers. A 2022 paper on directed-antiprism group-distance-magic labels uses different constraints. Searches of public mathematical records, descriptions, aliases and nearby graph-labeling invariants found no covering result. A historical-priority risk remains for unindexed alternate terminology; no independent novelty validation is asserted.

## Value — PASS

The theorem gives a complete all-parameter answer on a natural infinite 3-connected polyhedral graph family, separating arbitrary zonal, strong and generative labelings, and distinguishes ordinary labels from symmetry classes. The period-three disappearance outside one residue class, absence of Smith torsion, and sharp two-independent-order-four requirement are motivated by face geometry and are not numerical substitutions into a published table. The strong-and-generative existence and count answer a concrete instance of a question asked in the primary 2025 paper.

## Limits

The exceptional octahedron is not part of the automorphism theorem. Counting formulas assume finite palette groups, while existence criteria are for arbitrary abelian groups. Historical priority is provisional. No independent mathematical audit has been performed.
