For every antiprism \(A_n\) with \(n\ge4\), facial zero-sum abelian-group labels exist exactly when \(3\mid n\) and the group has at least three elements; the universal solution group is then \(\mathbb Z^2\), otherwise zero. For finite groups, the complete ordinary and strong labeling counts and full-automorphism orbit counts have explicit torsion-dependent formulas; strong generative labels exist precisely over \((\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z)^2\), where there are \(96\) labels in \(16\) graph-symmetry orbits.
