## 2. Method (auditable, stdlib only)

1. **Permutation model.** Permutations are image-tuples. Groups are built
   by BFS closure from the stated generators; the run asserts
   |Gi| = 24, 28, 30, 33, 35, 36 and aborts otherwise.
2. **Subgroup enumeration (complete).** Collect all cyclic subgroups,
   then iterate binary joins `⟨A ∪ B⟩` to a fixpoint. *Completeness
   proof:* every subgroup H is the join of the cyclic subgroups of its
   elements, so the least join-closed family containing all cyclics is
   the full subgroup lattice. Each candidate is asserted closed and of
   order dividing |G|; additionally the closure of every 1- and
   2-element subset is asserted present (audit step 2 of the plan).
3. **Normality / classes.** `H` normal iff `gHg⁻¹ = H` for all `g`
   (full conjugation, no sampling). Element classes are conjugation
   orbits; the run asserts the class sizes sum to |G|. Subgroup classes
   are conjugation orbits on the lattice.
4. **Derived series / centre / nilpotency.** Derived subgroup by
   commutator closure; series iterated to 1 (asserted; hence solvable).
   Centre by direct commutation test. Nilpotency by Sylow-count
   uniqueness (all Sylow counts 1 ⟺ nilpotent for finite groups).
5. **G24 extras.** Hasse covers = minimal strict inclusions, each with a
   verified single-element extension witness; transitive-reduction
   completeness is asserted (inclusion reachability = cover
   reachability). Coset action uses left cosets `xH` with
   `g·(xH) = (gx)H`; homomorphism, kernel, and core
   (`h ∈ H` with all conjugates in `H`) are computed independently and
   asserted equal.
