## Final claim

The nine listed embedding-dimension-3 numerical semigroups have exactly the displayed global Delta sets and catenary degrees, yielding five equality and four strict cases for max Delta plus 2 versus catenary degree, with the stated extremals inside the nine-row list.

## Correctness

**FAIL** — The finite certificates and fresh brute-force scan reproduce the displayed Betti factorizations, Delta values, and catenary values through 400. However, the RESULT globalizes the full Delta set by saying the global Delta set is the union of Betti-element Delta sets. The standard Betti theorem guarantees attainment of the maximum Delta at a Betti element, not equality of the entire Delta set with that union in general. A finite scan to 400 is not an all-elements proof. The specialized embedding-dimension-3 algorithms in the literature could in principle certify each row, but the package does not execute or reconstruct those all-elements arguments row by row. Therefore the stated global exact table is not fully established by the supplied proof.

## Originality

**FAIL** — Prior work from 2015 and 2017 gives algorithms to compute the entire Delta set for every embedding-dimension-3 numerical semigroup, and García-Sánchez–Martín-Cruz 2020 characterizes equality in max Delta plus 2 versus catenary degree for embedding dimension three using known Betti data. The paper also explicitly includes the 5,6,9 example with Delta {1} and catenary degree 3. Thus the nine-row table is a routine specialization of established general machinery rather than an original mathematical result.
