Striped maximal outerplanar graphs (dual-path triangulations, two ears) have maximum domination number exactly floor((n+2)/4) for 10<=n<=30, certified by complete dihedral-quotiented peel-word census to n=20 and explicit 3+1-periodic comb extremals to n=30.
The ceiling is the n2=2 case of Campos-Wakabayashi/Tokunaga (n+n2)/4; sharpness, exact distributions, and extremal counts are new, and the alternating zigzag does not attain the bound.
