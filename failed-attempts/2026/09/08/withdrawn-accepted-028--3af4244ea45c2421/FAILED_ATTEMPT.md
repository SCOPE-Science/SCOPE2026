FAILED ATTEMPT — NOT A VALIDATED FINDING

The record's order-6 transversal and packing table is reproducible and was independently rechecked, but the central scientific novelty claim is already covered by earlier exhaustive work.

Ian Wanless, *A Generalisation of Transversals for Latin Squares* (EJC 9 (2002), R12), published the complete order-6 **main-class** distribution of the maximum number of parallel transversals: six classes at 0, two at 2, and four at 4. Thus the spectrum {0,2,4} and exact maximum 4 were already known. McKay, McLeod and Wanless (DCC 40 (2006), 269-284) subsequently reported exhaustive transversal counts for a representative of every isotopy class through order 9, directly covering exhaustive order-6 transversal enumeration.

The present 22-row McKay-file-order mapping and explicit witnesses are useful reproducibility/catalogue annotations, but after the prior packing classification and all-isotopy transversal enumeration are accounted for they do not provide a sufficiently new theorem, structural classification, order/regime, or algorithmic advance.

A legitimate retry should begin from these earlier exhaustive results and establish a genuinely new packing or structural consequence beyond reindexing/witnessing the classical order-6 catalogue.
