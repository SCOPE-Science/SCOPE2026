FAILED ATTEMPT — NOT A VALIDATED FINDING

The finite computation is correct, but the headline Mahler spectrum is not an original result. Grassi, Gugiatti, Lutz and Petracci (2023), DOI 10.1007/s12215-023-00922-3, §3.5, publish the 16 reflexive polygons ordered by normalized volume and all polar pairings. Because normalized area is twice Euclidean area, their data immediately gives the products 27/4, 8, 35/4 and 9 with multiplicities 2, 6, 4 and 4. The residual observation that the three centrally symmetric classes consist of two product-8 parallelograms and one product-9 hexagon is a tiny finite-case check, not a substantive new stability theorem.

A legitimate retry would require a genuinely new general theorem, new regime, or reusable technique beyond repackaging the known 16-class volume/duality data.
