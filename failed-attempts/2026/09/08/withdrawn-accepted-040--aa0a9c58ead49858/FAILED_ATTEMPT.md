FAILED ATTEMPT — NOT A VALIDATED FINDING

The numerical census in this record is independently reproducible: there are 297200 labelled 6×6 binary matrices with all row and column sums three, their permanents have distribution 17:21600, 18:216000, 20:59400, 36:200, and the minimum-17 matrices form one row/column-permutation orbit. The finding is nevertheless withdrawn on scientific-value grounds. At this order the underlying unlabelled cubic-bipartite universe has only five connected 12-vertex types (plus the single disconnected K3,3∪K3,3 type), so the result is a small finite invariant table rather than a substantive theorem, general pattern, or improvement of the known permanent bounds.

A legitimate retry would need a genuinely broader structural result—for example a nontrivial sequence of exact minima, a characterization of minimizers across orders, or another general theorem—not merely another presentation of the n=6 exhaustive table.
