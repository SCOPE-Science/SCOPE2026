FAILED ATTEMPT — NOT A VALIDATED FINDING

The record's scientific framing is contradicted by Michaël Rao, *On some generalizations of abelian power avoidability*, Theoretical Computer Science 601 (2015), DOI 10.1016/j.tcs.2015.07.026, which proves that 2-abelian cubes are avoidable over a binary alphabet by an infinite construction. Consequently a 200000-bit existence witness does not double a live scientific frontier or bear on an unresolved binary threshold. The filed words may still be valid computational fixtures, but the claimed novelty and scientific significance are not validated.
