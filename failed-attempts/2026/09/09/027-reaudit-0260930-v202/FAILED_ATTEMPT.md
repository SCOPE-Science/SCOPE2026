# FAILED ATTEMPT — NOT A VALIDATED FINDING

The fresh independent audit on 2026-09-30 rejected this package as a validated finding.

The finite graph/defect census is internally consistent, but its intrinsic equilateral angle-defect data are the direct transform \(d_v = 6-\deg(v)\) of a previously classified finite family of triangulated 2-spheres. That makes the intrinsic headline covered by prior classification data rather than an original mathematical result.

The remaining Willmore extremal is explicitly relative to one chosen spring-embedding protocol. The audit found no invariant or independently motivated mathematical reason that makes the maximum under that protocol a worthwhile exact invariant. The protocol-generation provenance of the stored coordinates was also not independently regenerated.

The original package and its computational evidence are retained for diagnosis and possible future reformulation; this file does not erase or rewrite that evidence.
