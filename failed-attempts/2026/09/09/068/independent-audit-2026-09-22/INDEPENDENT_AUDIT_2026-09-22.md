# Independent audit — 2026/09/09/068

## Correctness — PASS for the quotient calculation

Let K=ker ε. Since b has infinite order, any lift g has infinite order and L(<g>) is diffuse abelian. For group words s,t, the Fourier conditional expectation E_{L(K)}(u_s* u_{g^n} u_t) is nonzero exactly if b^n=ε(s)ε(t)^{-1}, at most one n per pair. Finite sums therefore have zero expectation for all sufficiently large |n|, and L2 approximation plus uniform boundedness extends this to all x,y∈M. Popa's criterion gives L(<g>) not intertwining into L(K). This argument works in any group extension with an infinite-order quotient element, independently of property (T), wreath structure, or amenability of K. CIOS I supplies the wreath-like context and ICC hypotheses; its explicit property-(T) constructions use other fibers, and a finite-fiber instance can be formed by quotienting a property-(T) Z-fiber example by twice its normal fiber, subject to retaining the stated extension/action conditions.

## Originality — FAIL

The “obstruction” is the immediate Fourier-support separation of an infinite cyclic subgroup's quotient powers from the kernel of a group extension, exactly the elementary instance of Popa's standard intertwining criterion. It has no feature peculiar to the difficult property-(T) wreath-like factor or to tensor decompositions.

## Scientific value — FAIL as a research result

It correctly rejects a universal statement that is already too broad for any such extension. A diffuse tensor leg in a hypothetical splitting has much stronger structure than an arbitrary amenable cyclic subalgebra; this example gives no information about primeness or a feasible fiber-location theorem with suitable hypotheses.
