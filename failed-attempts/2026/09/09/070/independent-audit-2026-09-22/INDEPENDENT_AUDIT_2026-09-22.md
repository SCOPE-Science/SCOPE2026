# Independent audit — 2026/09/09/070

Date: 2026-09-26. Disposition: archive failed attempt.

## Correctness — FAIL

The record's explicit apolar calculations are substantially plausible: the nine degree-two derivative images listed in RESULT.md are linearly independent over characteristic zero, giving Hilbert function (1,4,9,4,1). At (1,1,1,1), the classical Hessian is the 4×4 matrix with first row (0,1,1,1), remaining diagonal 12 and other entries 1; its determinant is −3·11² = −363. Thus this form has **nonvanishing** Hessian and can have the claimed strong Lefschetz behavior.

The central classification is false. Definition 2.7 of Miró-Roig and Pérez Díez (arXiv:2402.09188, pp. 5–6) requires n,m≥2 and an algebraically dependent, linearly independent collection p₀,…,pₙ; the definition implies identically vanishing classical Hessian. Here there is only one leg p₀=UVW, which is algebraically independent as a singleton, and the Hessian is nonzero at (1,1,1,1). Calling this a Perazzo quartic in the flagged ternary-base regime, and importing that paper's non-generalization to position this example as an open-case certificate, is a material false premise. The paper's theorem cannot be applied to this example. This verdict addresses the headline research claim, without denying the valid isolated linear-algebra calculations.

## Originality — FAIL

The claimed novelty is an instance in an unsettled Perazzo/Jordan family. It is outside that family's defining conditions. A computed Jordan partition for this explicit non-Perazzo polynomial is not evidence of a new Perazzo result. The general mixed-Hessian/Jordan and apolar Lefschetz machinery is already described by Costa and Gondim (arXiv:1811.02072); this record supplies a particular calculation, not a new method or theorem for the cited open setting. No exhaustive priority claim about all instances of the polynomial is made.

## Scientific value — FAIL

The exact polynomial can serve as a small calculation example, but its advertised significance as a minimal ternary-base Perazzo test collapses under the definition and Hessian check. The unclassified Jordan drop locus and finite characteristic scans do not establish another consequential result. The supported residual value does not justify retaining this accepted research record under its core claim.
