## Correctness — PASS

The cited Adian finite-subgroup theorem at odd n≥665 and centerlessness are confirmed in Adian–Atabekyan, arXiv:1811.07167 (Corollary 1's proof and Lemma 2.1). A finitely generated solvable torsion group is finite: its finitely generated torsion abelianization is finite, its derived subgroup therefore has finite index and is finitely generated, and induction on derived length applies. Any two elements of a solvable H generate such a finite subgroup, hence a cyclic subgroup by Adian, making H abelian. An abelian exponent-665 subgroup whose finite subgroups are cyclic has at most one F_p dimension for p=5,7,19, so is itself finite cyclic. For finite normal N, conjugation B→Aut(N) has image of exponent dividing 665 and order dividing φ(|N|), coprime to 665; hence N is central and trivial. I independently checked φ(d) for all divisors d of 665 and their coprimality. The full amenable radical remains open by these inputs.
