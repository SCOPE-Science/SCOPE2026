Thus [v]≠0 and x[v]=0; the E7 hypersurface is a domain by the odd x-adic valuation of x(x²+z³). This establishes nonzero torsion and non-MCM behavior.

The stronger headline claim that **ann([v])=(x)** is false. E7 is an isolated surface singularity: for f=−y²+x³+xz³, the partials (3x²+z³,−2y,3xz²) vanish together on f=0 only at the origin in characteristic zero. M6 and M7 are MCM, hence locally free at every non-origin regular local point (Auslander–Buchsbaum). Their tensor is locally free there, so its torsion submodule, including R[v], is supported only at the closed singular point. If ann([v]) were (x), R[v]≅R/(x) would have support the entire curve V(x), including smooth points such as the prime (x,y) with z invertible. This contradiction shows its annihilator has maximal-ideal radical and strictly contains (x). The record only proves inclusion (x)⊆ann([v]), not equality. The named exact annihilator is a material false statement.
