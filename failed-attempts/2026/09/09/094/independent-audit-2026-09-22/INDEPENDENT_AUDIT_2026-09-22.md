## Correctness — PASS

The claimed counterexample is valid. Partition each bipartition class into sizes 60+3 and 3+60 and use disjoint K₆₀,₃ and K₃,₆₀. Each has 180 edges and any cycle in either component uses at most three vertices on its smaller side, so no C₈ can occur. The union has 360 edges on 63+63 vertices, immediately disproving the proposed 189-edge C₈-only bound. This does not determine z(63,63;C₈). The premise conflated forbidding C₈ alone with the much stronger girth constraints of the generalized-hexagon incidence example.

## Originality — FAIL

The construction is an elementary disjoint union of complete bipartite graphs with a three-vertex side, with a one-line cycle bound. It supplies no new extremal theorem, sharp value, or nontrivial method; the numerical refutation follows directly from definitions. The cited Bukh–Jiang result is an asymptotic upper bound, not a source for a 189-edge sharp claim.
