# Review status

Fresh independent audit: **failed on originality**. The seven-color construction is mathematically sound, but Gao-Jackson-Krohne-Seward already proves the general 2 chi minus 1 theorem for graphs with weakly orthogonal decompositions and constructs that machinery for the free Z^n Bernoulli action. The king/grid factor-two metric transfer is an elementary specialization of those hypotheses.
