# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

On D+ over K=Q(sqrt(-2)), substituting x0=alpha x1 gives the smooth plane quartic 6x1^4-5x2^4-10x3^4=0, hence a geometrically integral prime divisor. At a point with x1=1, x3=0 and 5x2^4=6, the divisor meets X* transversely, so v_D(x0-alpha x1)=1 while x0+alpha x1 and x2 are units; therefore v_D(f)=1. The quaternion residue is [-10]=[5] because -2 is a square in K. Since 5 is not a square in K and a constant that becomes a square in the function field of a smooth projective geometrically integral curve would already be a square in its constant field, the residue is nonzero. Unramifiedness would survive base change, so ramification over K rules out membership in Br(X*) over Q.

## originality

PASS

Searches found general calculations of Brauer groups of diagonal quartic surfaces and a later SCOPE record using a similar ramification test on a different quartic, but no prior source with this exact surface and quaternion symbol.

## value

FAIL

The mathematics is correct, but the result is essentially a negative type-check on a nominated Brauer symbol: a single codimension-one residue already shows it is not Azumaya. The exact surface/symbol was not shown to be a previously motivated candidate in the primary literature, and the record does not replace it with a new obstruction or determine the arithmetic of X*. Under the explicit value rule, a cheap negative membership check needs stronger substantive motivation than is supplied here.
