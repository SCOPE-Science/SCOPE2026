# FAILED ATTEMPT — NOT A VALIDATED FINDING

This package is withdrawn from accepted findings after independent audit.

The mathematical lower bound in `RESULT.md` is internally supportable, but the claimed research novelty is not. Johnson and Schechtman, *Diamond graphs and super-reflexivity* (Journal of Topology and Analysis 1 (2009), DOI 10.1142/S1793525309000114), Section 6 explicitly give the Laakso-graph analogue of their quantitative Proposition 2 via the same kind of one-cell uniform-convexity contraction followed by induction. With the exact Hilbert modulus, their recurrence yields the stronger squared-distortion growth `M_n^2 >= n+1` under the standard normalization.

Accordingly, this record is retained only as provenance for a correct rederivation. It must not be cited as a new SCOPE research finding or as establishing priority for the Laakso distortion-growth result.
