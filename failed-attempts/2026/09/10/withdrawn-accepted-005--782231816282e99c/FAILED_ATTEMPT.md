# FAILED ATTEMPT — NOT A VALIDATED FINDING

This package is withdrawn from accepted findings after independent audit.

The local calculations on the critical manifold are reproducible: the gradient, Levi matrix, tangential kernel, winding rates, and the displayed coupling coefficients are consistent with direct differentiation. The package does **not** prove its stronger conclusion that the relevant boundary problem is an irreducible coupled `2x2` system or that scalar/slice reduction is impossible. Nonzero coefficients in one chosen frame are not, without an invariant argument, such a proof.

Because the record also disclaims any Diederich–Fornaess index bound and global pseudoconvexity/smooth-boundedness certification, the remaining pointwise coefficient table is not sufficient as a standalone validated research finding. It is retained only as exploratory/provenance material.
