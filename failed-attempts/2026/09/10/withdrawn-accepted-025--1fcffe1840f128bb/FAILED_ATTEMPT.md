# FAILED ATTEMPT — NOT A VALIDATED FINDING

This package is retained as evidence of an attempted finite-residue obstruction, but its main conclusion is not validated.

## Decisive defects

1. The cited Cartwright–Jensen–Payne Proposition 3.2 gives inclusions into theta translates and does not justify treating the selected theta equations as a complete defining system for the Brill–Noether locus or its initial degeneration. The claimed product ideal for `W0` is therefore not established as the actual geometric initial degeneration.

2. Zero smooth `F2`-points is only the failure of a sufficient Hensel-lifting criterion. It does not imply that there are no `Q2`-points, because a generic-fiber point can reduce to a singular special-fiber point. No additional argument in this package proves that any lift would have to reduce smoothly.

## What may remain useful

The explicit finite-field calculation for the displayed torus scheme can be retained as a heuristic or test example. It must not be presented as a proof that no class on the circle `T` lifts.

## Repair requirements

A future validated result would need (a) a rigorous computation of the actual initial degeneration or another necessary reduction condition for the relevant Brill–Noether locus, and (b) a non-lifting argument that excludes lifts through singular residue points, not merely the absence of a smooth Hensel starting point.
