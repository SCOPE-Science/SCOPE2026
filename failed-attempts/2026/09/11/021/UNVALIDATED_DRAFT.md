# The claimed mod-2 Sinha `d^4` in total degree 4/5 at filtration −4 does not exist

## Claim (disproof of the admitted target)

In the mod-2 homology Sinha (Goodwillie–Weiss Bousfield–Kan) spectral
sequence for oriented long knots modulo immersions `Embbar_3` in `R^3`,
with bidegrees and `d^r` indexing **exactly as in Marino–Salvatore
(2025)** [arXiv:2504.16785], there is **no** nonzero differential

```
d^4(a) = b
```

with source in total degree 4 (resp. 5) exiting filtration `−4`. In fact:

1. **(Unconditional window vanishing.)** Under the paper's conventions
   (`E^1_{p,q} ⊆ H_q(Conf_{−p}(R^3))`, `p = −n` minus the number of
   points, `q = d` internal degree, total degree `p+q`), the total-degree
   4 source at filtration `−4` is `(p,q) = (−4,8)` and the total-degree 5
   source is `(−4,9)`. Both `E^1` groups are **zero at chain level**,
   hence both `E^4` groups are zero. No nonzero `d^4` can exit them.
2. **(General even-differential vanishing.)** Every even differential,
   in particular `d^4`, is identically zero everywhere in this spectral
   sequence, by the same parity reason the paper notes `d^2 = 0`.

Consequently the target existence claim — an explicitly logged `E^4`
source cycle `a` in that window with nonzero `d^4(a) = b` — is
**mathematically impossible as stated**. No claim is made here about
whether stage-4 evaluation `ev_4` is universal over `F2` in general;
vanishing of this `d^4` does not prove universality (failure could in
principle come from elsewhere). Only the claimed `d^4` witness is refuted.

## Conventions pinned (Marino–Salvatore)

- **Multicomplex/spectral-sequence indexing** (§2.4, Defs 2.5–2.6,
  formula (∗)): `D_i` and `δ^r` have bidegree `(−r, r−1)`, i.e.

  ```
  D_r, δ^r : (p,q) ↦ (p−r, q+r−1),
  ```

  total degree `p+q` drops by 1.
- **`E^1` identification** (§2.3): `E^1_{p,q} ⊆ H_q(Conf_{−p}(R^m))`;
  **Sinha `p` is minus the number of points** (explicit statement in
  §2.3 bigrading-shift discussion). So filtration `−4` means `n = 4`
  points, and total degree `t = p+q = −n+d` gives `d = 8` (for `t = 4`)
  resp. `d = 9` (for `t = 5`).
- **Vanishing lemma** (§2.3, item (1)): `E^1_{p,q}(m) = 0` unless
  `(m−1) | q`, because `H_*(Conf_n(R^m))` is generated by planetary
  systems with `(m−1)`-sphere parameters. Consequence stated there:
  `E^r ≅ E^{r+1}` unless `r ≡ 1 (mod m−1)` (or `r = 0`).
  For `m = 3`: nonzero groups have `q` even, and only **odd** `r` can
  carry a nonzero differential. The Introduction (§1) states explicitly
  "`d^2` is zero by dimensional reasons".
- **Even concentration** (§5.2, footnote 24, ref [27]):
  `H^*(Conf_k(R^3))` (hence `H_*`) is concentrated in even dimensions.
- **Truncated model** (Thm 4.24): first three pages ≅ 3-truncated
  `FN~_3` spectral sequence; chain group `FN~_3(n)_d` is the `F2`-span
  of Fox–Neuwirth trees on `n` points of dimension `d = Σ depths`.
- **Known nonzero differential** (Thm 5.1): `d^3 : E^3_{6,8} → E^3_{9,10}`
  in the paper's `(n,d)`-style subscript (i.e. Sinha `(p,q) = (−6,8) →
  (−9,10)`), consistent with `r = 3` odd shifting `d` by `+2` (even).

## Proof

### Lemma 1 (Fox–Neuwirth dimension bound; pure combinatorics)

A tree in `FN^{≤}_3(4)` carries `n−1 = 3` depth indices each in
`{0,1,2}` (Def 2.1, `m = 3`), so its cell dimension `d = Σ a_i ≤ 6`.
Hence

```
FN~_3(4)_8 = 0  and  FN~_3(4)_9 = 0,
```

so `E^1_{−4,8} = 0` and `E^1_{−4,9} = 0`, and therefore
`E^4_{−4,8} = E^4_{−4,9} = 0`.

*Replay:* `python3 output/artifacts/verify.py` enumerates all
`4! · 3^3 = 648` trees, confirms max dimension 6 with histogram
`{0:24, 1:72, 2:144, 3:168, 4:144, 5:72, 6:24}`, and count 0 at
dimensions 8 and 9.

### Lemma 2 (parity; cited concentration)

`E^1_{p,q} = 0` for `q` odd (§2.3(1) + §5.2). Since every page is a
subquotient, `E^r_{p,q} = 0` for `q` odd at all `r`. The differential
`δ^r : (p,q) → (p−r, q+r−1)` shifts `q` by `r−1`. For `r` even, `r−1`
is odd, so `δ^r` always connects an even-`q` group to an odd-`q`
(zero) group or vice versa. Hence **every even differential vanishes
identically**; in particular `d^4 ≡ 0` and `d^2 ≡ 0`.

### Theorem (target impossible)

By Lemma 1, the two named source groups are already zero at `E^1`,
so no `E^4` source cycle `a` in total degree 4 or 5 at filtration `−4`
exists, and no nonzero value `d^4(a) = b` is possible. By Lemma 2,
`d^4` is zero everywhere regardless of window. The target claim is
false as stated under its own pinned conventions.

### Robustness to "total degree" readings

The disproof does not depend on which standard reading of "total
degree" is intended:

| reading | source `(n,d)` | outcome |
|---|---|---|
| `t = p+q` (paper's totalization) | `(4,8)` / `(4,9)` | both `E^1 = 0` by Lemma 1 (unconditional) |
| `t = q` (internal degree) | `(4,4)` / `(4,5)` | `(4,4) → (8,7)`: target `q` odd = 0; `(4,5)`: source `q` odd = 0 (Lemma 2) |
| `t = d+n` or `d−n` variants | small `d` | every live even-`q` source hits an odd-`q` target under `+3` shift (Lemma 2) |

In all cases a nonzero `d^4` is excluded.
