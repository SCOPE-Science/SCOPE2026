# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

With the period-one Fourier basis, the eigenvalue of −Δ is 4π²N for integer N=k₁²+k₂². Parseval gives η=r_h²/(4π²h²)² as the sum of shell masses times squared distance to R²=1/(4π²h²). Choose a nearest integer N*, including the possibility of a tie. All other integer shells are at distance at least 1/2, so the complementary squared norm is at most 4η=o(1); for small h the selected shell must be populated, since otherwise this bound would contradict normalization. For v on that shell, Parseval in x₁ at each x₂ leaves one sum for each k₁, with at most two terms (k₂ solving k₂²=N*−k₁²). Cauchy–Schwarz bounds the x₁-integrated density by 2||v||² everywhere, and the x₂ measure of the tube is 2√h. Thus M(v)≤4√h||v||² and M(u_h)≤8√h||v||²+2||u_h−v||²→0. The bounds do not presume any Diophantine support or any particular rate within o(h²), and imply the 7/8 limsup corollary.

## originality

FAIL

The qualitative no-concentration result for the exact residual regime is an immediate corollary of the classical uniform square-torus eigenfunction L⁴ bound and projection across the constant integer-shell spacing, and holds in any measurable sets of shrinking area. The additional O(√h) estimate on the projected eigenfunction is simply the elementary multiplicity-two coordinate restriction estimate integrated over the width. The exact named match is the same record, not independent prior evidence.

## value

FAIL

The residual is smaller than one spectral-shell gap, so the motivating scarring threshold is already excluded by elementary projection and classical uniform integrability. For the exact horizontal tube the added shell bound is a direct coordinate orthogonality exercise, and the tail term gives no independent rate of decay for arbitrary o(h²) residuals. The record does not resolve a wider spectral window or geometrically nontrivial concentration mechanism. The stated case is a useful illustration, not new substantive evidence about scarring.
