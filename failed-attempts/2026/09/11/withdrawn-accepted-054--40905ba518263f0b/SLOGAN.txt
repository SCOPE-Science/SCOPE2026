For the normalized symmetric Pareto alpha=3 Wigner ensemble (X=sqrt2 Y, p(y)=(3/4)(1+|y|)^-4, E[X^2]=1) the truncated fourth cumulant at M_N=N^{1/6} grows like N^{1/6}, so the fourth-cumulant loop monomial L_N=kappa_N m_sc^4 at edge scale z_N=2+wN^{-2/3} satisfies |L_N|>=(1/8) ln N for N>=max(10^12,(25|w|)^{3/2}), while the GOE analogue is identically 0.
