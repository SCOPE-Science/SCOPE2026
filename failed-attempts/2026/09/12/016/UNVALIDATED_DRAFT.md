Indeed ||v||^2 = sum e_i^2 + (sum e_i a_i)^2, sum e_i^2 <= 5B^2, and
|sum e_i a_i| <= C|Lambda| + mu sum|e_i| <= C|Lambda| + 5B/2.

vector has length l (any certified lower bound l <= lambda_1 may be used),

sufficient impossibility test uses ||b_1|| >= lambda_1: firing needs
