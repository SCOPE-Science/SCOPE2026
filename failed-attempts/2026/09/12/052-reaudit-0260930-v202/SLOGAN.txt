In the specified inhibitory LIF/Fokker–Planck model, gamma-distributed delay dispersion moves the ~65 Hz asynchronous-state Hopf crossing from unstable to stable near sigma_D≈1.1 ms (N=600: J_c≈-602 mV at 1.10 ms and -605 mV at 1.125 ms for J_eff=-600 mV); matched spiking simulations show the gamma peak collapsing with dispersion. This is a linear-stability crossing plus finite-network quench, not a proof of a supercritical nonlinear FP branch.
