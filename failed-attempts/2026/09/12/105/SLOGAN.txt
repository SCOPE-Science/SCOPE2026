SCOPE attempted Let R range over all commutative self-dual rank-5 fusion rings over Z with trivial universal grading, multiplicity at least 2 in some triple product, and at least one non-integral Frobenius-Perron dimension. For each such R, decide: does there exist a complex fusion category C with Grothendieck ring K(C) isomorphic to R? A complete answer enumerates every fusion-rule table satisfying these hypotheses up to basis permutation and, for each table, either exhibits explicit F-symbols satisfying all pentagon equations realizing it, or proves impossibility by an explicit obstruction…
