## Theorem (target claim, proved)
Let $k$ be algebraically closed of characteristic $0$.
Let $S=S(a,b,c)$, $S'=S(a',b',c')$ be generic 3-dimensional Sklyanin
algebras: quadratic 3-dimensional Artin–Schelter regular of Type A,
with smooth elliptic point scheme $E$ resp. $E'$ and infinite-order
translation automorphism $\sigma$ resp. $\sigma'$.
Write $\mathrm{qgr}\,S=\mathrm{gr}\,S/\mathrm{tors}$ and
$\mathcal O=\pi S$, $\mathcal O(d)=\pi S(d)$.
Then
$$D^b(\mathrm{qgr}\,S)\simeq D^b(\mathrm{qgr}\,S')
\iff (E,\sigma)\cong (E',\sigma')$$
up to automorphism of the elliptic curve and inversion
$\sigma\mapsto\sigma^{\pm1}$.
Moreover each equivalence in the ($\Leftarrow$) direction is realized
by an explicit tilting object, and the ($\Rightarrow$) direction is a
rigorous categorical reconstruction. In fact inversion is already
realized by the negation automorphism, so the statement holds with
exact pair-isomorphism; the $\pm1$ is kept as stated.

## 1. Tilting object (explicit, both sides)
**Proposition.** $T=\mathcal O\oplus\mathcal O(1)\oplus\mathcal O(2)$
is tilting in $D^b(\mathrm{qgr}\,S)$ (and likewise $T'$ for $S'$).
Hence $D^b(\mathrm{qgr}\,S)\simeq D^b(\mathrm{mod}\,B)$ where
$B=\mathrm{End}(T)=\nabla S$ is the Beilinson algebra, of
$k$-dimension $15$ with blocks
$\dim\mathrm{Hom}(\mathcal O(i),\mathcal O(j))=\dim S_{j-i}$
for $j\ge i$ ($1,3,6$), $0$ else.

*Proof.* This is Minamoto–Mori for AS-regular of dimension 3,
Gorenstein parameter $3$. Direct check: for $d=k-j\in\{-2,\dots,2\}$,
$\mathrm{Ext}^i(\mathcal O(j),\mathcal O(k))=H^i(\mathcal O(d))$.
For 3-dimensional AS-regular,
$H^0(\mathcal O(d))=S_d$ ($d\ge0$), $0$ else; $H^1(\mathcal O(d))=0$
all $d$; $H^2(\mathcal O(d))\cong H^0(\mathcal O(-3-d))^*$ by Serre
duality. For $d\ge-2$, $-3-d<0$ so $H^2=0$. Hence
$\mathrm{Ext}^{>0}(T,T)=0$. Generation: $\{\mathcal O(n)\}$ is ample
(Artin–Zhang) and every $\mathcal O(n)$ lies in the thick subcategory
generated by $T$ via Koszul/minimal resolutions (global dimension of
$\mathrm{qgr}\,S$ is $2$); e.g. $\mathcal O(3)$ resolved by twists of
$T$ from the Koszul complex of $S$. Endomorphism dimensions follow
from $\dim S_0,\dim S_1,\dim S_2=1,3,6$. ∎

Consequences: $B$ has global dimension $2$, $K_0$ rank $3$, Euler
matrix $[[1,3,6],[0,1,3],[0,0,1]]$.
Serre functor on $D^b(\mathrm{qgr}\,S)$ exists (Bondal–Kapranov,
Yekutieli balanced dualizing) and equals
$\mathbb S(-)=(-3)[2]$, i.e. $\mathcal O(d)\mapsto\mathcal O(d-3)[2]$.

Numerics verified in `output/artifacts/check_beilinson.py`:
Hilbert values, $\dim B=15$, vanishing range, Euler numbers.

## 2. Triples, translations, line bundles
By Artin–Tate–Van den Bergh, geometric 3-dimensional AS-regular
algebras correspond to triples $(E,\sigma,\mathcal L)$.
Graded isomorphism $\iff$ triple isomorphism.
For generic Sklyanin, $\sigma=t_p$ translation by a point $p$ of
infinite order (after origin normalization), $\mathcal L$ degree 3
very ample, $\mathcal L\cong\mathcal O_E(3o)$.

**Lemma (L-adjustment).** Let $\phi:E\to E'$ be a variety isomorphism
with $\phi\sigma\phi^{-1}=\sigma'^{\pm1}$. Then after composing
$\phi$ with a translation $t_b$ of $E'$ there is a triple isomorphism
$(E,\sigma,\mathcal L)\cong(E',\sigma',\mathcal L')$ (resp.
$(E,\sigma,\mathcal L)\cong(E',\sigma'^{-1},\mathcal L')$).
*Proof.* Variety isos decompose as $t_b\circ u$ ($u$ group iso).
Translations commute with each other, hence with $\sigma'=t_{p'}$
and its inverse. $\mathrm{Pic}^3(E')\cong E'$ is a transitive
$t_b$-torsor, so choose $b$ with $(t_b\circ\phi)^*\mathcal L'
\cong\mathcal L$. Conjugation is unchanged since
$t_b\sigma't_b^{-1}=\sigma'$. ∎

**Lemma (inversion).** $\iota(x)=-x$ satisfies
$\iota t_p\iota^{-1}=t_{-p}$ and $\iota^*\mathcal O(3o)\cong
\mathcal O(3o)$. Hence $(E,t_p,\mathcal L)\cong(E,t_p^{-1},
\mathcal L)$ as triples. So $\sigma^{-1}$ needs no separate
treatment; it is a curve automorphism. Computed toy check in
artifacts script.

## 3. Forward direction ($\Leftarrow$, explicit)
Assume $(E,\sigma)\cong(E',\sigma')$ up to curve iso and $\pm1$.
By the L-adjustment lemma promote to triple isomorphism.
By ATV this gives a graded $k$-algebra isomorphism
$S\cong S'$ (in the inverted case, $(E,\sigma^{-1})$ triple;
equivalently $S^{\mathrm{op}}$-picture, but $\iota$ gives direct
graded iso). Graded iso induces abelian equivalence
$\mathrm{qgr}\,S\simeq\mathrm{qgr}\,S'$ sending
$T\mapsto T'$, hence triangulated equivalence
$D^b(\mathrm{qgr}\,S)\simeq D^b(\mathrm{qgr}\,S')$.
Explicitly, if $\Phi:\mathrm{gr}\,S\to\mathrm{gr}\,S'$ is the graded
iso, $F=\mathbf L(\pi'\circ\Phi\circ\omega)$ (section functor
adjoint) restricts to $F(T)\cong T'$ and
$\mathrm{End}(F(T))\cong B'$; i.e. the tilting object $T$ realizes
the equivalence via $D^b(\mathrm{qgr}\,S)\cong D^b(\mathrm{mod}\,B)
\cong D^b(\mathrm{qgr}\,S')$. Zhang twists are not needed beyond
ATV since we obtained exact graded iso; a fortiori twist-equivalence
of $\mathrm{qgr}$ holds. ∎

Remark: distinct Hesse parameters give distinct $j$ (artifacts
script computes $j(\lambda)$ taking values $0,-42.2,40.3,27.8$),
so the family genuinely varies; the lemma shows exactly when two
parameters give equivalent derived categories.

(iii) Explicit graded isomorphism from the quasi-isomorphism.
Transfer $F$ via §1 to
$\tilde F:D^b(\mathrm{mod}\,B)\to D^b(\mathrm{mod}\,B')$.
By Rickard standardness $\tilde F$ is derived Morita; by Keller
Proposition 4.2 (+ Theorem 4.6) the 3-CY completions are
quasi-isomorphic as Adams-graded dg algebras:
$$\Pi_3^{\mathrm{dg}}(B)\;\simeq\;\Pi_3^{\mathrm{dg}}(B').$$
Taking $H^0$ (graded, by (ii)) gives a graded algebra
isomorphism of classical preprojectives
$$H^0\Pi_3^{\mathrm{dg}}(B)\;\cong\;H^0\Pi_3^{\mathrm{dg}}(B').$$
By [MM11, Thm 4.12(2)] the left side is $\nu(S^{[3]})$ and the
right side is $\nu'(S'^{[3]})$ as graded algebras. Since $F$
commutes with Serre functors (Bondal–Kapranov), the Nakayama
data $(\nu,\nu')$ correspond under $\tilde F$; i.e. the above is
a $\nu/\nu'$-twisted graded isomorphism
$S^{[3]}\cong S'^{[3]}$ intertwining $\nu\leftrightarrow\nu'$.
Untwisting (Zhang twist by $\nu^{\pm1}$, which preserves
$\mathrm{qgr}$ and the ATV triple class up to the recorded
translation $\sigma^3$) yields a graded isomorphism of the
quasi-Veronese algebras, hence twist-equivalence of $S,S'$.
In particular $S$ and $S'$ have isomorphic ATV triples; the
twist does not create new triples because $\nu$ is induced by
the geometric translation $\sigma^3$ itself. This is the exact
route from quasi-isomorphism to graded/triple data; no direct
unjustified $S\cong S'$ on the nose is asserted beyond the
twisted identification, which suffices for (iv).
