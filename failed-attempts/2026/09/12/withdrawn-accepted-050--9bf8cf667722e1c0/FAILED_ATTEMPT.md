## Why this attempt fails

The argument identifies the constant fppf group sheaf `Z/3` with `mu_3` over `Spec O_K` merely because `zeta_3` is a global unit. That identification is false at the prime above 3. On a characteristic-3 fiber, `mu_3` is nonreduced (`T^3-1=(T-1)^3`) and is not étale, while the constant group scheme `Z/3` is finite étale. Therefore the three Kummer classes counted by `H^1_fppf(X,mu_3)` are torsors for a different group scheme from the constant finite gauge group `G=Z/3` used in the cited arithmetic Dijkgraaf–Witten construction.

The claimed extra two full-ring gauge fields, and hence the value `Z_full=1`, do not establish a comparison with the formal HKM reinsertion value for the same constant gauge group. The advertised `1` versus `1/3` discrepancy therefore does not validate a wild-gluing counterexample.

## Salvageable material

The class-number, unit, Kummer, and ramification calculations may remain useful for a future theory formulated explicitly with the finite flat group scheme `mu_3`. Such a theory would need its own definition of the action class, trace/Chern–Simons functional, normalization, and a theorem relating it to the constant-group HKM framework before any numerical comparison could be made.
