Two-input S² bilinear Schrödinger system with couplings z=cos θ and x=sin θ cos φ is not locally exactly controllable around the constant ground state in any time: the reflection y→−y commutes with the Laplacian and both potentials so all trajectories stay reflection-even, while odd targets (φ₀+εη)/√(1+ε²) with η∝y lie arbitrarily H³-close and at L²-distance |ε|/√(1+ε²)>0 from every reachable state.
