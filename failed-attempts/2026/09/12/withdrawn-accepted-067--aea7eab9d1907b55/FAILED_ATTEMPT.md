# FAILED ATTEMPT — NOT A VALIDATED FINDING

The package correctly computes the cubic seed and three rational pullbacks, but it does not prove the advertised obstruction for the full cubic-del-Pezzo Vianna mutation class. The arbitrary-depth step is explicitly left as an expectation, and W3 is not itself certified as a torus potential.

A future valid record would need either (i) a complete induction on legitimate Landau–Ginzburg seed mutations proving that every Laurent mutant has no additional admissible transverse Morse critical points after all cancellations, with seed-direction bookkeeping included, or (ii) a narrower claim restricted to the finitely many certified torus potentials actually checked, followed by a fresh originality/value assessment. The current finite symbolic calculation should be preserved as evidence, not presented as the class-wide theorem.
