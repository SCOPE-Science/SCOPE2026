# FAILED ATTEMPT — NOT A VALIDATED FINDING

The computation is correct, but the central research claim is not original. For an odd prime p, conjugacy of Sylow p-subloops in Chein loops is already stated in Gagola (2009). In M(S3,2), all coset elements are involutions, so every order-3 subloop is contained in S3, where the unique subgroup of order 3 is A3. Hence n3=1 and the one-orbit conclusion are immediate specializations.

The multiplication-table and |Inn|=216 computations may be retained as educational or verification data, but a future research record would need a genuinely new invariant, classification statement, or theorem not implied by the general Chein-loop Sylow theory and the elementary subgroup structure of S3.
