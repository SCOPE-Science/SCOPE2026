# FAILED ATTEMPT — NOT A VALIDATED FINDING

The sign-integral calculation is mathematically sound, but the advertised no-collision theorem is not original. Published mKdV Whitham theory already identifies the characteristic speeds with KdV speeds evaluated at the squared positive invariants and gives strict hyperbolicity for beta3>beta2>beta1>0. The family beta=(d,1,3), 0<d<1, is a direct specialization.

The submitted derivation can be preserved as a pedagogical proof or consistency check. A future research record would need to address a regime not covered by the established positive-invariant theorem, such as a genuinely different symmetry class, sign-changing invariants, a nonstandard flow where the reduction fails, or a new quantitative estimate beyond distinctness.
