# FAILED ATTEMPT — NOT A VALIDATED FINDING

## Record

`2026/09/12/093` — diagonal Zhang-twist witness for multiparameter quantum polynomial planes.

## Why this is not accepted as a validated research finding

The explicit calculation itself is mostly correct: a diagonal Zhang twist with scale ratios induced by (a,b,c)=(2,1,1) sends q=(2,3,5) to q'=(4,6,5), preserves J=q12*q23/q13=10/3, and produces an equivalence of graded module categories; q' is visibly outside the finite permutation/inversion orbit of q.

The research claim nevertheless fails the originality and scientific-value axes. The equivalence mechanism is exactly the established Zhang-twist construction. Once that theorem is invoked, choosing a numerical triple and scale factors and checking that one new coordinate lies outside the finite permutation/inversion alphabet is a direct specialization, not a new classification theorem or a nontrivial new invariant.

There is also a mathematical error in the explanatory text: the generic diagonal-twist orbit in the three-parameter q-space is two-dimensional, since the three scales act only through two independent ratios. The quotient is one-dimensional and can be parametrized by J. The record incorrectly calls the orbit itself one-dimensional.

## Preserved value

The package remains a clear pedagogical counterexample to an overly restrictive permutation/inversion biconditional. It is retained intact in the designated failed-attempt location as evidence of the explored route, but it should not be imported as a validated standalone research finding.
