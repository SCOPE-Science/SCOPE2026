Disposition: **FAILED**

## Correctness — PASS

On a finite torus, the semigroup generated by the positive coordinate steps reaches every vertex, so a nonempty set closed under every forward step must be the whole torus. For a proper product cube, any outside vertex misses at least one coordinate interval; if it misses two or more, no forward neighbor can enter the cube, and if it misses exactly one, at most the forward step in that single coordinate can enter. Thus every outside vertex has at most one forward neighbor in the cube, so the forward-2 rule adds no vertex and the cube is sterile. This proves the droplet conjunct impossible for all eventually proper \(\lfloor F\log n\rfloor\) cubes.
