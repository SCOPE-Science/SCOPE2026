## Correctness

On a finite torus, the semigroup generated by the positive coordinate steps reaches every vertex, so a nonempty set closed under every forward step must be the whole torus. For a proper product cube, any outside vertex misses at least one coordinate interval; if it misses two or more, no forward neighbor can enter the cube, and if it misses exactly one, at most the forward step in that single coordinate can enter. Thus every outside vertex has at most one forward neighbor in the cube, so the forward-2 rule adds no vertex and the cube is sterile. This proves the droplet conjunct impossible for all eventually proper \(\lfloor F\log n\rfloor\) cubes.

## Originality

Standard \(r\)-neighbor and modified bootstrap models use different neighborhoods/activation conditions, so their threshold theorems do not imply the stated directed sterility lemma. Best-of-knowledge originality therefore survives, subject to the residual risk of unindexed oriented-bootstrap literature.

The scientific content is a cheap incompatibility check against the target's own droplet specification: on a finite torus 'closed under every +e_i' already forces the whole torus, and the cube sterility count is a one-step boundary observation. Although correct and apparently not separately published, it does not determine the actual critical window, identify a nontrivial natural boundary, or supply a mechanism likely to be reused beyond rejecting this malformed droplet ansatz.
