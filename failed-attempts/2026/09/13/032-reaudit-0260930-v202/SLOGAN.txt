For every projective K3 surface and n>=1, the nested flag Z_n=Hilb^{n,n+1} embeds by (xi subset eta) |-> (xi,x,eta) as a smooth compact Lagrangian of dimension 2n+2 in Hilb^n x S x Hilb^{n+1} with form +sigma_n+sigma-sigma_{n+1}. Isotropy follows from exact cancellation on the dense disjoint-point locus plus etale descent and continuity.
