## Correctness — PASS

For fixed real deformation parameter the Rossi structure and the standard contact form are homogeneous. The published structure equations give constant Webster curvature, so a constant unit-volume conformal factor is an exact stationary solution of the normalized CR Yamabe flow; uniqueness then makes the reference flow stationary.

## Originality — FAIL

Cheng-Malchiodi-Yang explicitly compute the Rossi Webster curvature as R_(s)=2(1+2s^2) for the standard contact form. After the elementary parameter conversion to the record's t, the constant-curvature premise is already published; stationarity of constant-curvature initial data under normalized CR Yamabe flow is standard.

## Value — FAIL

Once the already-published constant Webster curvature is inserted into the normalized flow equation, the reference trajectory being stationary is a routine deduction. It does not isolate a new mathematical gap beyond that direct substitution.

## Residual risks
