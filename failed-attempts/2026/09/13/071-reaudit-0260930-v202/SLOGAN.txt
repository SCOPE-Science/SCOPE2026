For the De Laet t=1 Heisenberg quotient Q of S_[0:0:1], the reduced V_3(Q) in (P^2)^3 is six smooth rational curves with K_{3,3}-minus-matching incidence (a connected 6-cycle). The point ([0:1:0],[1:0:0],[0:1:0]) lies on exactly E_0 and D_1 with affine Jacobian rank 4, hence a singular intersection.
