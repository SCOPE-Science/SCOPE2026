## 1. Scope and data

Let $k=\bar k$, $\mathrm{char}\,k=3$. Let $G$ be finite abelian with
$3\nmid |G|$ (hence $kG$ is semisimple commutative). Let $V\in{}^{kG}_{kG}\mathcal{YD}$,
$\dim V=2$, principally realized: there are $g_i\in G$, $\chi_i\in\hat G$ and
$0\ne x_i\in V_{g_i}^{\chi_i}$ ($i=1,2$) with
$g\cdot x_i=\chi_i(g)x_i$. Put $p_{ij}=\chi_j(g_i)$ (so the braiding is
$c(x_i\otimes x_j)=p_{ij}\,x_j\otimes x_i$). **Cartan type $B_2$** means for a
primitive $N$th root $q\in k^\times$, $N>2$, $3\nmid N$:

$$p_{11}=q,\qquad p_{22}=q^2,\qquad p_{12}p_{21}=q^{-2}.$$

Then $a_{12}=-2$, $a_{21}=-1$ (i.e. Cartan matrix $\begin{pmatrix}2&-2\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}$:
$1$ short, $2$ long). Write $N_1=N$, $N_2=M:=\mathrm{ord}(q^2)$
($M=N$ if $N$ odd, $M=N/2$ if $N$ even). Positive roots, root vectors,
group-likes and characters:

| root $\alpha$ | vector $y_\alpha$ | $g_\alpha$ | $\chi_\alpha$ | self-braiding | order |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $\alpha_1$ | $x_1$ | $g_1$ | $\chi_1$ | $q$ | $N$ |
| $\alpha_2$ | $x_2$ | $g_2$ | $\chi_2$ | $q^2$ | $M$ |
| $\alpha_{12}=\alpha_1+\alpha_2$ | $y_{12}=x_1x_2-p_{12}x_2x_1$ | $g_1g_2$ | $\chi_1\chi_2$ | $q$ | $N$ |
| $\alpha_{112}=2\alpha_1+\alpha_2$ | $y_{112}=x_1y_{12}-qp_{12}y_{12}x_1$ | $g_1^2g_2$ | $\chi_1^2\chi_2$ | $q^2$ | $M$ |

Self-braidings: $\chi_{12}(g_{12})=p_{11}p_{12}p_{21}p_{22}=q$;
$\chi_{112}(g_{112})=q^2$ (standard $B_2$ root computation).
Hence $\dim\mathcal B(V)=N^2M^2$ with PBW basis
$x_1^{a}x_2^{b}y_{12}^{c}y_{112}^{d}$, $0\le a,c<N$, $0\le b,d<M$.

Quantum Serre elements (iterated braided adjoints, with $w=x_2x_1-p_{21}x_1x_2$):

$$S_1 = x_1y_{112}-q^2p_{12}\,y_{112}x_1
   =\sum_{k=0}^3(-1)^k\binom{3}{k}_{\!q}q^{k(k-1)/2}p_{12}^k\,
     x_1^{3-k}x_2x_1^k,$$
$$S_2 = x_2w-q^2p_{21}\,wx_2
   =x_2^2x_1-p_{21}(1+q^2)\,x_2x_1x_2+q^2p_{21}^2\,x_1x_2^2.$$

## 2. Mod-3 quantum-binomial ledger

Since $3\nmid N$ and $\mathrm{ord}(q)=N$, every $q$-number $(i)_q$,
$1\le i<N$, and every $q$-factorial $(i)^!_q$ is a unit in $k$; denominators
never vanish. Consequences (checked in `output/artifacts/verify_b2_ledger.py`):

- (i) Power $q$-binomials: $\binom{N}{k}_q=0$, $\binom{M}{k}_{q^2}=0$ for
  $0<k<\text{order}$, exactly as in characteristic $0$. No new vanishing mod 3.
- (ii) Serre $3$-term: $\binom{3}{k}_q\ne0$ since $(3)_q=1+q+q^2=0\iff N=3$,
  excluded. So $S_1$ always has 4 nonzero terms.
- (iii) Serre $2$-term: $1+q^2=0\iff q^2=-1\iff M=2\iff N=4$.
  For $N=4$, $S_2=x_2^2x_1+q^2p_{21}^2x_1x_2^2$ (middle term drops);
  otherwise $S_2$ has 3 nonzero terms. This vanishing is characteristic-free.
- (iv) Ordinary integers entering coproduct/exponential denominators are
  $1,2$ and the $q$-factorials above — all prime to $3$. Division by $3$
  never occurs. The only characteristic-$3$ effect is $3=0$ in $k$, which
  meets no denominator.

In particular no restricted ($x_i^3$) or extra truncated relations appear:
$q_{ii}\ne1$ and $3\nmid N$ keep the characteristic-$0$ root system.

## 3. Theorem (explicit finite family list)

$g x_i g^{-1}=\chi_i(g)x_i$, $\Delta(g)=g\otimes g$,

$$\begin{aligned}
S_1 &= \lambda_1(1-g_1^3g_2),\\
S_2 &= \lambda_2(1-g_2^2g_1),\\

x_2^M &= \mu_2(1-g_2^M),\\

y_{112}^M &= \mu_{112}(1-(g_1^2g_2)^M)+J_{112}(\lambda,\mu_1,\mu_2),
