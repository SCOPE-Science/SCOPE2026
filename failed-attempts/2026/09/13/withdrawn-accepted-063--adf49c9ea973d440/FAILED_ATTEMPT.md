# FAILED ATTEMPT — NOT A VALIDATED FINDING

Independent audit date: 2026-09-28 (UTC)

The standard holomorphic tangent extension is convincingly shown to be nonsplit and therefore not polystable, but the record overstates what follows. A same-slope line subbundle does not establish strict semistability without a separate semistability proof, and failure of an HYM Chern connection for the standard holomorphic tangent bundle does not rule out every HYM tangent connection on the underlying smooth tangent bundle. Existing Hull-Strominger torus-bundle constructions use precisely such alternative holomorphic bundle structures. Consequently the claimed two-horn divergence theorem is not established; the surviving standard-tangent obstruction and vanishing checks are narrower.
