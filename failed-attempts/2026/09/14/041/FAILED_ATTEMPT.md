## Problem

Prove or disprove the following: there exist absolute constants c>0 and C>=1 such that for every prime p, every nonempty A subset of F_p with |A| < p^{1/2}, and every M>=1 with |A*A| <= M*|A|, one has |A+A| >= c * M^{-C} * |A|^{81/50}. Here A*A={ab: a,b in A} and A+A={a+b: a,b in A}. A complete answer is either a rigorous proof of this inequality for all such p,A,M with fixed explicit c,C, or a rigorous disproof via an infinite family of primes p and sets A,M violating the inequality for every fixed c,C.
