RELATIVE QI RIGIDITY THEOREM (target, reading (a)+necessity):
Class C: W_Gamma with Gamma finite/connected/triangle-free/planar, no separating
vertex/edge, no induced 4-cycle, not a cycle, with a cut pair
=> W one-ended (Davis Thm 8.7.2), hyperbolic (Cor 12.6.3, square-free), not cocompact
Fuchsian (Dani-Thomas App A / Cor A.3: triangle-free => Fuchsian iff cycle >=5),
splits over 2-ended (Mihalik-Tschantz: cut pair <=> split; unique cut pair per
2-ended splitting up to conjugacy/finite index), Bowditch JSJ nontrivial.

Visual JSJ (Dani-Thomas Thm 3.37): V1 orbits = approx-pairs (cut pairs with >=3
comps, Cor 3.5) + sim-pairs (A-sets with <A> 2-ended, Prop 3.10) + subdiv midpoints;
V2 orbits = A-sets with <A> infinite non-2-ended (hanging Fuchsian, Prop 3.24);
V3 orbits = B-sets satisfying (B1)-(B3) (stars, Prop 3.25/3.35); edges iff
stabilizers intersect; all stabilizers are visual special subgroups; edge groups
2-ended (conjugates of cut-pair dihedrals <a,b> or <a,b,c>).

THEOREM. Let W_Gamma, W_Lambda in C with type-preserving isomorphic Bowditch JSJ
trees T_Gamma ~= T_Lambda. Then:
 (NEC) If Phi: W_Gamma -> W_Lambda is a QI, then after the induced type-preserving
 tree iso, each matched rigid pair (R, P) -> (R', P') is relatively QI (Phi coarsely
 respects rigid stabilizers and their incident peripheral families).
 (SUF) Conversely, if matched rigid pairs are relatively QI (and matched hanging
 pairs are relatively QI -- flexible case), then W_Gamma, W_Lambda are QI.

Proof ingredients (attributed, standard):
 (NEC): Bowditch QI-invariance of JSJ (Remark 2.8: QI => homeomorphic boundaries =>
 same colored tree); Papasoglu [33]: QI takes rigid (non-hanging, non-2-ended)
 vertex stabilizers to within bounded Hausdorff distance of rigid stabilizers and
 gives bijection of incident 2-ended peripheral cosets (universally elliptic edge
 groups). Visual identification: peripherals = W-conjugates of cut-pair dihedral
 special subgroups (Thm 3.37 + Lemma 3.7/Prop 3.24/3.35), so restriction of Phi
 (adjusted by group elements) is a relative QI of rigid pairs.
 (SUF): Tree-of-spaces assembly over the common JSJ tree (Behrstock-Neumann [9]
 fattened-tree / Malone [30] / Cashen-Martin [14] machine): build equivariant QI
 from piece-wise relative QIs: cylinder (2-ended: all QI via dihedral models,
 Papasoglu uniqueness of cut pair per splitting), hanging MHF (flexible:
 Behrstock-Neumann/Malone: same-type hanging pieces admit peripheral-respecting
 QIs once tree matched; their peripheral patterns are discs with punctures
 classified by tree valence data), rigid (by hypothesis). Glue along edge spaces
 (2-ended, uniformly quasi-isometrically embedded) using tree-of-QI / QI-tree
 axioms (Cashen-Martin [14] Thm 3.9 framework extended with rigid inputs; edge
 patterns matched by the type-preserving tree iso). Result: global QI.
 Wheel-pair illustration: both wheels in C (verified computationally); each has
 one V3 orbit (B = all essentials, (B1) verified, maximal, |B|>=4), no approx
 pairs (max 2 comps), essential sim-pairs in 2 W-orbits (spoke/rim), conjecturally
 no V2; trees type-preserving isomorphic (same orbit/valence pattern). Their
 rigid pairs (R6,10 periph) vs (R7,12 periph) are the test case: the theorem says
 global QI of wheel groups is EQUIVALENT (modulo flexible hangings) to relative
 QI of these two rigid pairs. Whitehead computation shows crude min-cut does not
 separate them (both 3), so the theorem is stated as equivalence, not as a
 decision procedure -- honest limitation.
