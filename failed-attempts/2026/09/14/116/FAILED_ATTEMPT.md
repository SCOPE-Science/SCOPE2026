## Problem

Let k be algebraically closed of characteristic 0, R=k[x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4], and for d>=6 let CI(d,d,d,d) be the space of ideals generated by a regular sequence of four degree-d forms. For I in CI(d,d,d,d) put A=R/I (socle degree 4d-4). For fixed I let m(I)=min_L h_{A/(L)}(2d-1) over linear forms L and let U_I be the open set where the minimum is attained; call L general for I if L in U_I. Say A fails WLP by one if m(I)=1 and by more than one if m(I)>=2, i.e. xL:[A]_{2d-2}->[A]_{2d-1} has 1-dimensional, resp. >=2-dimensional, cokernel for L general for I. Let Y(d) be the locus failing by exactly one and X(d) the locus failing by more than one. Prove or disprove: X(d) is contained in the closure of Y(d); equivalently, if some J in CI(d,d,d,d) fails WLP by more than one then some (not necessarily the same) I in CI(d,d,d,d) fails WLP by exactly one.
