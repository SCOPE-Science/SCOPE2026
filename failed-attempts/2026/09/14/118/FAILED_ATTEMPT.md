## Problem

Let Gamma satisfy the Standing Assumptions of Nguyen–Tran: Gamma is connected, triangle-free, planar, has at least 5 vertices, and has no separating vertex or edge. Assume Gamma is non-CFS. Then G_Gamma is hyperbolic relative to CFS right-angled Coxeter subgroups and its (global) divergence satisfies Div(G_Gamma) asymptotic exp. Let Spec(G_Gamma) denote the divergence spectrum in the sense of Tran: the set of asymptotic-classes of lower divergences ldiv_alpha as alpha ranges over Morse bi-infinite geodesics in the Davis complex / Cayley graph. Is Spec(G_Gamma) = {[r^2], [exp]}, with [r^2] realized by Morse geodesics in CFS peripheral subgroups and [exp] realized by a non-peripheral (e.g. periodic) Morse geodesic? In particular, if Gamma,Gamma' are two such non-CFS planar graphs whose collections of maximal CFS peripheral subgroups have non-bisimilar visual decomposition trees, do G_Gamma and G_Gamma' have the same spectrum, so that the divergence spectrum is blind inside the planar exponential regime, or does it refine the peripheral tree structure and hence distinguish quasi-isometry classes with the same global exponential divergence?
