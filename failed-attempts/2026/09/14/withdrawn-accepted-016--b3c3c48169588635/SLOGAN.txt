phi2 in Out(F4), a->b, b->ca, c->d, d->a, is reducible via the swapped rank-2 factors <a,c> and <b,d> and toroidal via the period-4 commutator orbit [a,c]=acAC->bdBD->caCA->dbDB->acAC. Hence it is not fully irreducible, not atoroidal, and not an ageometric iwip, so the Mosher-Pfaff lone-axis/IW cut-vertex dichotomy is vacuous.
