# FAILED ATTEMPT — NOT A VALIDATED FINDING

The advertised nonexistence theorem is not validated.

The package correctly computes a useful weight/Euler table for the Higgs
deformation complex, but its decisive Maurer-Cartan obstruction confuses the
Serre trace pairing with the deformation dg-Lie bracket. The bracket on
endomorphism-valued Higgs deformations is induced by commutators, and the
trace of every commutator is zero. At a stable simple Higgs point, Serre
duality makes H^2 one-dimensional and the trace map to H^1(K) nonzero, hence
an isomorphism. Therefore the induced obstruction bracket
H^1 x H^1 -> H^2 has zero trace and is zero. It cannot be the claimed
perfect pairing H^1_0 x H^1_1 -> H^2_1.

Thus the asserted nonzero quadratic term [Q_2,pi_2] is not established.
The weight calculation alone does not rule out a weight-1 1-shifted Poisson
bivector, and the record supplies no all-orders higher L-infinity
obstruction. A future attempt would need an explicit minimal formal model
and a correct analysis of higher brackets. The original package is
preserved as evidence of the failed route.
