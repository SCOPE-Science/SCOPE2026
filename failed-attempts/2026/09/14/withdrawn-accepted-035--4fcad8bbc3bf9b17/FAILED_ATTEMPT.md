# FAILED ATTEMPT — NOT A VALIDATED FINDING

The central shifted-coisotropic theorem is not validly formulated in the
Melani-Safronov convention used by the cited literature.

An n-shifted coisotropic morphism lies over an n-shifted Poisson target and
induces an (n-1)-shifted Poisson structure on the source. In particular,
the map to a trivial (n+1)-shifted point recovers n-shifted Poisson
structures on the source. The ordinary 0-shifted Poisson/symplectic
structure on the Higgs moduli therefore is not encoded by the record's
asserted 0-shifted coisotropic structure over a zero 0-Poisson base in the
way claimed.

The record also calls a custom Hamiltonian-anchor determinant
"nondegenerate coisotropic" although the target Poisson structure is
deliberately degenerate, and it says the curve case has Ext^2=0 even though
the unrigidified stable Higgs deformation complex has the scalar H^2 dual
to scalar H^0.

The classical material in the package—Hitchin Hamiltonians, dimensions,
smooth spectral fibers, and nodal critical behavior—may be useful in a
separate reformulation. It does not validate the claimed Melani-Safronov
0-shifted theorem. A new attempt must first fix the shifted degree and the
precise derived source/target/rigidification, then re-prove the appropriate
coisotropic and nondegeneracy conditions from the correct definitions.
The original package is preserved as evidence of the failed route.
