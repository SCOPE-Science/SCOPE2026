## Problem

Let N be prime, B/Q the definite quaternion algebra of discriminant N, O ⊂ B a maximal order, Cl(O) its finite set of right ideal classes, M(O)={φ:Cl(O)→C} with Eisenstein subspace C·1 and cuspidal subspace S its orthogonal complement under the normalized Eichler pairing, equipped with Hecke operators T_ℓ (ℓ∤N) via Brandt matrices. Let E_{2,N} denote the weight-2 Eisenstein series of level N with eigenvalues ℓ+1 (ℓ∤N). Let p be an odd rational prime, p a prime of the compositum of the rationality fields of eigenforms in M(O) above p, and r≥2. Suppose f∈S_2(N) is a newform corresponding via Jacquet–Langlands to an integral eigenform φ∈S, normalized so the gcd of its values is minimal, with Hecke eigenvalue congruence a_ℓ(f)=λ_ℓ(φ)≡ℓ+1=λ_ℓ(1) (mod p^r) for all ℓ∤N. Does there then exist c in the ring of integers of Q(φ), c not ≡0 mod p, such that as functions on Cl(O), φ(I)≡c (mod p^r) for all I∈Cl(O)?
