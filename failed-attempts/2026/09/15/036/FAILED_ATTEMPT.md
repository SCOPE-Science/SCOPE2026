## Problem

Let A,B be unital separable simple nuclear Z-stable C*-algebras. Suppose there exist an invertible element kappa in KK(A,B) with kappa_0([1_A])=[1_B], and an affine homeomorphism gamma: T(B)->T(A), compatible on the trace-K_0 pairing: <gamma(tau),x> = <tau,kappa_0(x)> for all tau in T(B), x in K_0(A). Does it follow that A is isomorphic to B? In particular, is this true without assuming (kappa,gamma) is induced by a unital *-homomorphism A->B and without assuming the UCT for A,B? If the stated pairing compatibility is insufficient, what is the minimal corrected KK-trace compatibility that implies A is isomorphic to B?
