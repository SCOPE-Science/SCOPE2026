## Problem

Let G=Sp(4,R), Gamma<G an irreducible lattice, P<G minimal parabolic, M a closed connected 4-manifold, omega a smooth volume form or nowhere-vanishing density, r>r_min(G), and alpha:Gamma->Diff^r(M;omega) volume-preserving. Let M^alpha=(GxM)/Gamma be the suspension with induced G-action. Suppose M^alpha carries an ergodic P-invariant Borel probability measure mu projecting to Haar on G/Gamma that is not G-invariant. Does there then exist a compact embedded (possibly disconnected) C^r 3-dimensional submanifold N in M, a parabolic Q<G of codimension v(G)=3, and a C^r covering h:N->G/Q, Gamma-equivariant up to the standard projective Gamma-action, such that a finite-index Gamma'<Gamma preserves each component N_i, acts on it C^r-conjugate to the standard projective action, and mu is supported on (GxN)/Gamma -- in particular when G/Q is the 3-dimensional Lagrangian Grassmannian of planes in R^4 (with pi_1=Z)? Concretely: can the projective Gamma-action on Lagrangian planes be so embedded as an invariant locus supporting such a mu, e.g. by a volume-preserving blow-up of a fixed point for the standard Sp(4,Z)-action on T^4 or by modifying the standard Gamma-action on Gr(2,4) to preserve the Lagrangian locus while preserving volume?
