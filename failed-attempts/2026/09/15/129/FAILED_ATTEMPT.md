## Problem

Let Omega be a bounded Lipschitz pseudoconvex domain in C^n (n>=2) admitting a Lipschitz plurisubharmonic defining function in the sense of Michel-Shaw. For fixed 1<=q<=n let N_q be the L^2 d-bar-Neumann operator on (0,q)-forms. Is N_q compact iff the boundary contains no complex analytic variety of dimension >= q? In particular, does a 1-dimensional analytic disc in the boundary necessarily force noncompactness of N_1 in this rough class, or can N_1 remain compact?
