Suppose that for each rigid v: (i) (G_v,P_v) is relatively hyperbolic and admits a weakly relatively geometric action on a CAT(0) cube complex in the sense of Groves–Manning Definition 1.9, with cyclic peripherals satisfying separability Assumptions 1.1–1.2 on hyperplane–peripheral intersections and double cosets; and (ii) after finite-index passage the family P_v can be made malnormal in G_v, equivalently there are finite-index dot-P normal in P for each P in P_v such that every peripherally finite Dehn filling with kernels N normal in dot-P is hyperbolic and virtually compact special in the sense of Einstein Theorem 2. Then G is virtually compact special. Decide this conditional dichotomy: either G is virtually compact special, or some rigid pair (G_v,P_v) fails (i) or (ii) and is the sole obstruction.
