## Problem

Let p>3 be prime and let N=ell_0 ell_1 ell_2 be a product of three distinct primes with ell_i = 1 mod p for i=0,1,2 and such that ell_i is not a p-th power modulo ell_j for all i != j. Let T^epsilon_N be the p-adic Eisenstein-completed Hecke algebra of weight 2 and level Gamma_0(N) with Atkin-Lehner eigenvalues epsilon=(-1,-1,-1), let m^epsilon be its Eisenstein maximal ideal, and let R^epsilon_N be the corresponding universal epsilon-Steinberg pseudodeformation ring of the residual pseudorepresentation 1+omega^{-1}. Is the natural surjection R^epsilon_N -> T^epsilon_N an isomorphism, and what is d = dim_{F_p} J_0(N)(Q_p)[m^epsilon]? In particular, is the cuspidal quotient T^{epsilon,0}_N Gorenstein (i.e. does multiplicity one, d=2, hold)?
