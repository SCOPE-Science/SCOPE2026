# FAILED ATTEMPT — NOT A VALIDATED FINDING

## Reason for relocation

The package is mathematically correct as an explicit example, but it does not meet the originality and scientific-value threshold for a validated SCOPE finding.

The scalar reduced free product of finite-dimensional abelian algebras already supplies simple unique-trace examples outside Avitzour's trace-zero-unitary hypothesis.  The present construction sets
\[
A_i=M_2\otimes B_i,\qquad E_i=\operatorname{id}_{M_2}\otimes	au_i,
\]
so the reduced amalgamated product is canonically
\[
(A_1,E_1)*_{M_2}(A_2,E_2)
\cong
M_2\otimesig((B_1,	au_1)*(B_2,	au_2)ig).
\]
Thus the claimed ``amalgamated'' counterexample is a formal tensor amplification of the known scalar non-necessity phenomenon, not a new amalgamated construction.

The numerical witness itself checks: for the weights `(0.6,0.2,0.2)`, the largest atom exceeds the sum of the others, so no unitary in `C^3` can have zero trace; the uniform `C^5` factor has the obvious fifth-root trace-zero unitary; and the scalar free product lies in the simple unique-trace regime of Dykema's classification.

## Audit disposition

Relocate the complete package to the designated failed-attempt path. Preserve the original proof and verifier as a correct worked example, but do not present it as a validated new research finding.
