Affirmative answer to ABM Problem 2(iii): on the dual X^{n*}_{0,1} of the reflexive Argyros-Beanland-Motakis space, every product of n+1 strictly singular operators is compact. Proof via a duality lemma (T* strictly singular implies T strictly singular on reflexive unconditional-basis spaces), the primal Theorem 0.1(ii), and Schauder compactness duality.
