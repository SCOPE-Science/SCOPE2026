For a finite direct sum of infinite type-I factors, the exact symmetric-ideal norm of a self-adjoint generalized derivation is obtained by forming the Huang--Pliev--Sukochev--Xu positive/negative profile separately inside each central factor and only then globally rearranging the profiles. Cross-center sign matching can strictly overestimate the norm: on two summands a rank-one Schatten-q example has exact norm 2^(1/q) while the naive global factor profile gives 2.
