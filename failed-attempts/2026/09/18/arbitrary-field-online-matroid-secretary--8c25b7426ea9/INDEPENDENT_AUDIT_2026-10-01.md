## Originality — FAIL

The claim is already mechanically implied by the motivating paper's own finitary-modular-extension proof. That paper states the online representation theorem only for finite fields, but in its later modular-extension argument it constructs the same online acceptance process in a fixed finitary modular extension, handles infinitely many flats by exactly the finite intersection-rank signature used here, uncrosses to chains, and completes the same feasibility proof. A fixed finite-dimensional vector space over any field is precisely such a finite-rank modular ambient geometry. Specializing that already-published construction to the vector-space lattice gives the audited arbitrary-field online theorem without a new mathematical ingredient.
