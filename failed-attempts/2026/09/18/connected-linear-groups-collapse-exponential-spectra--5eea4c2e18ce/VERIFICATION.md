# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The collapse equivalence is exact and valid, with norm topology on the invertible group. If every invertible belongs to its identity component, resolvent membership and identity-component membership coincide. Conversely, every invertible u can be tested as 1−a for a=1−u, so equality of the spectra at that one point forces u into the identity component. Away from λ=0, invertibility of λI−ST implies invertibility of λI−TS and vice versa, by the usual inverse identity (λI−ST)^−1=λ^−1[I+S(λI−TS)^−1T], applied in either direction. De Rancourt explicitly lists connectedness for the complex canonical spaces c_0, all ℓ_p, C[0,1], all L_p[0,1], and Theorem 1.3 gives it for every complex HI space. The author seminar abstract confirms the stated Bochner/sequence/Besov families in the ranges 1<p,q<∞, reflexive symmetric E, and s>0 in the Besov case; this is confirmation of the abstract's statement, not an independent verification of its proof. The Hilbert-space stable-rank observation follows from the two known facts and does not use a converse from commutativity to connectedness. For the ancillary disconnected comparison, de Rancourt's Douady summary explicitly covers distinct summands among c_0 and finite-p ℓ_p spaces; the Daniel–Ghosh published abstract states commutativity in the named direct-sum algebra. The precise Daniel–Ghosh exponent range was not recovered, so do not extend that comparison outside the range treated in their paper or extrapolate the cited Douady passage to ℓ_∞ direct sums. The named classical-space result does not settle Horváth–Kania's broader, undelimited question.

## originality

FAIL

The main exclusion is established prior coverage, not merely a failure to locate priority. The public MathOverflow question already states both collapse when invertibles are connected and the consequent Jacobson-pair invariance, away from zero, in any unital Banach algebra. Substitution of the published connectedness theorems into that result gives the classical-space, HI, and newer listed applications without a new mathematical premise. The converse is the definitional translation u=1−a. The paper-specific enumeration and framing may be useful exposition, but they do not give a distinct claim outside that prior implication. Failure does not depend on the unavailable Daniel–Ghosh or Pliev–Sukochev–Tomskova journal full texts.

## value

FAIL

The compilation has expository value for readers of Question 9.2, and its collective examples may be worth mentioning in a survey. As an independent mathematical finding, however, the exclusion of each listed family consists of reading a known connectedness result and applying a known sufficient condition. The one-line converse is definitional and does not give a new test for connectedness; the stable-rank contrast is immediate from two known Hilbert-space facts. No new phenomenon, boundary example, or class outside those known premises is proved. No narrower original, scientifically valuable result survives as a repair.
