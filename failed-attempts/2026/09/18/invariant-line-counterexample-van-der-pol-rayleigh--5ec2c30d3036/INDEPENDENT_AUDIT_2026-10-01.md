# Independent audit — Invariant-line obstruction to a Van der Pol–Rayleigh limit-cycle conjecture

**Disposition: FAILED.**

## Correctness

**PASS** — On \(y=mx\), direct differentiation gives \(\dot H=x(-1-am-m^2)-m(\alpha-m+\beta m^2)x^3\); the stated quadratic and \(a=-(m+m^{-1})\) make the line invariant. The origin is the unique finite equilibrium. A periodic Jordan orbit would have index one and enclose the origin, hence intersect the complete invariant line; uniqueness forbids such a crossing. Independent symbolic elimination reproduced \(\alpha\beta a^2+(\alpha+\beta)a+(\alpha-\beta)^2+1\), and the example roots \(5\pm2\sqrt6\).

## Originality

**FAIL** — Chen--Fang--Zhang (Advances in Mathematics 483, 2025) predates the audited record and its public primary bibliographic abstract states that it completes the classification of invariant algebraic curves for exactly the ambient Rayleigh--Liénard family when the mixed coefficient is nonzero. The audited invariant straight line is therefore within that complete prior classification. Once that line is known, exclusion of a periodic orbit around the unique equilibrium is a standard planar consequence. Under the required implication-based bar, the source-specific 2026 conjecture counterexample is a mechanically implied specialization of prior structure, even though the later conjecture itself could not have been mentioned in 2025.

## Scientific value

**PASS** — Recording that a newly stated universal limit-cycle conjecture conflicts with a pre-existing invariant-line classification is mathematically useful: it gives an exact continuum of counterexamples and a simple rational-coefficient witness. The value survives even though the underlying obstruction is not original.
