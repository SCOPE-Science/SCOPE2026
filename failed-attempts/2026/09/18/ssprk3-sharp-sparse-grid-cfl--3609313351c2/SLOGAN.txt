For Huang's piecewise-constant total-level sparse-grid DG transport operator, SSPRK(3,3) is L2-stable exactly up to Δt = 1.256372663309164… h/max|c_l|, not merely the SSP-coefficient bound h/max|c_l|. The proof combines the source paper's extremal contraction with von Neumann's polynomial inequality and a sharp stability-disk calculation.
