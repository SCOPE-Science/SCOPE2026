# Independent scientific audit

## Final claim

For bijective approximate Fischer--Muszely maps on JB positive cones, finite inverse defect is equivalent to an order-isomorphic asymptotic core, the two cores are mutual inverses, and the weighted \(c_0\) family has inverse defect \(\varepsilon/\inf_n w_n\).

## Correctness — PASS

Apply the one-sided Hatori--Oi stability theorem to the map and its inverse. The compositions of the two linear cores differ from the identity by a uniformly bounded quantity on \(na\); linearity and division by \(n\) force both compositions to be the identity. The coordinate example follows from its two affine inverse branches.

## Originality — FAIL

The structural criterion is a direct corollary of the published one-sided stability theorem applied in both directions. The weighted \(c_0\) formula is an elementary coordinate specialization of the source's dense-nonsurjective mechanism.

### Equivalent formulations

### Broader coverage
