The square-power witness inside a Fibonacci-totient residue class has an exact dichotomy: density 1 on the multiples of z(q) for a Wall--Sun--Sun prime, but only density 1/q for an ordinary prime. Hence every Wall--Sun--Sun prime would automatically make S(q) nonempty, while outside that persistent branch infinitely many terms must use an external prime p congruent to 1 modulo q.
