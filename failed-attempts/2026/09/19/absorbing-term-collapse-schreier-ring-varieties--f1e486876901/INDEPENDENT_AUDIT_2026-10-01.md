## Final claim

The nontrivial locally finite Schreier varieties of rings are exactly zero-multiplication exponent-p varieties, with the stated fixed-field and unital specializations.

## Correctness — PASS

The classification follows correctly from the prior recovery/permutational characterization. Ring multiplication has 0 absorbing in two coordinates, so the recovery principle forces xy=0. The one-generated free ring then has cyclic additive group of finite order n; if n is composite, multiplication by a proper prime divisor of n is a nonconstant nonpermutation unary polynomial, contradicting the finite permutational condition, hence n=p is prime. The p-element zero-product ring generates exactly the zero-product exponent-p variety, whose free algebras are F_p-vector spaces and whose subrings are subspaces. The unital case collapses to 1=0. In the full K-linear language the same argument leaves only zero-product K-vector spaces, locally finite exactly for finite K.
