## Correctness

PASS. Given the pointwise Fermi-coordinate lower and upper time-separation estimates quoted in the package, the identity transverse coupling yields the lower bound and integrating the uniform upper bound against any coupling yields the matching upper bound because the curvature term depends only on the first marginal. ## Originality

FAIL. Braun–Li’s prior work already provides the unaveraged Fermi quadratic curvature term and a time-separation upper estimate uniform over every endpoint pair in an arbitrary causal coupling; its isotropic Ricci formula is obtained only after averaging that quadratic form over a ball. Replacing the ball average by an arbitrary matched probability law therefore immediately gives the second-moment contraction claimed here. Rank-one sectional recovery and finite tensor reconstruction are elementary specialization and polarization. Under the audit rule that prior implications and corollaries count as coverage, this is broader prior coverage even though the exact anisotropic wording is absent.

## Value

It does not isolate a new nonstandard lemma, difficult boundary, or independently motivated invariant beyond that routine consequence.
