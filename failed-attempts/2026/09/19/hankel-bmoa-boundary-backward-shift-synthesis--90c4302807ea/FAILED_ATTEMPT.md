## Record

Hankel--BMOA boundary for backward-shift orbit synthesis

## Reason for scientific rejection

The explicit Hankel calculation and counterexample are mathematically correct, but the
central result is already covered by the published 18 September 2026 result
*The sharp BMOA boundary for a backward-shift Hankel operator*. That earlier result
identifies the same Hankel matrix, gives the same BMOA boundary, supplies an explicit
\(H^2\) counterexample, and traces the same source-specific failure through the
Lemma 2.1 / Proposition 2.2 proof route.

The additional reuse of the same non-Bessel orbit obstruction for the 2024 precursor
does not supply a distinct, sufficiently motivated mathematical result after that
coverage is removed.

the calculation and its history. Rejection is for prior coverage and lack of a
surviving independently valuable claim, not for a false counterexample.
