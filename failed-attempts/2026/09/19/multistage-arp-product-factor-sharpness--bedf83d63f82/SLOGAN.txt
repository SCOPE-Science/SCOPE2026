The MSARP factor \(\prod_r(1+k_r)\) is asymptotically sharp even when the staged basis is the exact leading right-singular subspace and the final approximation uses the optimal orthogonal projector onto the selected columns. In particular, singleton stages can realize the advertised \(2^d\) expected-error factor, while one-shot ARP on the same final subspace remains at \(d+1\).
