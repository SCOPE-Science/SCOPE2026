For the unit-weight three-Stuart--Landau mixed phase model of arXiv:2609.20632v1, the double transverse synchrony exponent is exactly -3 eps cos(rho) - 9 eps^2 sin^2(rho)/(2a) - 3 eta cos(xi). In the paper's engineered PN model, eta=3 eps^2/(4a) cancels the entire second-order transverse correction and restores the first-order synchrony edge rho=pi/2, whereas the paper's harmonic-matching eta=eps^2/(4a) leaves two thirds of the second-order edge shift.
