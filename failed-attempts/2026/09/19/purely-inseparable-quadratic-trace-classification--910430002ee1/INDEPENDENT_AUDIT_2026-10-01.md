## Correctness — PASS

PASS. The classification argument is correct. A nonzero trace operator on a quadratic field has image dimension one or two. Surjective image forces multiplication by \(t(1)\). One-dimensional image is forced into the base field, after which every nonzero \(F\)-linear functional \(E	o F\) satisfies the trace axiom. In the separable case the nondegenerate field-trace pairing parametrizes these functionals; in a purely inseparable quadratic extension of characteristic two the field trace is zero, so that parametrization misses every nonzero scalar-valued functional. The explicit \(F_2(s)\subset F_2(s,u)\), \(u^2=s\), example is valid. The primary Centrone--Barbosa--Yasumura paper was read in full at Lemma 7 and Proposition 11, and its proof indeed assumes a one-dimensional kernel for the field trace, which fails in the purely inseparable case.
