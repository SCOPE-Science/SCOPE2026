For the logarithmic small-Fock weight h(r)=0 on [0,1] and h(r)=1/2(log r)^{3/2} for r>1, bounded radial Toeplitz operators are norm-dense in the entire U(n)-commutant: their eigenvalue sequences have uniform closure ell-infinity. Arbitrary bounded sequences are interpolated modulo c0 by disjoint saddle shells, while localized Jacobi probes recover c0 in uniform norm.
