For every d>=1 and every unequal pair 0<gamma,eta<=1, the off-critical trace-defect power R^{d-min(gamma,eta)} for discrete Fourier concentration is attained along an explicit sequence of scales. A balanced q-adic alternating-gap construction realizes every fractional roughness exponent, settling both off-critical sharpness questions in Remark 9.4 of arXiv:2609.12226v1.
