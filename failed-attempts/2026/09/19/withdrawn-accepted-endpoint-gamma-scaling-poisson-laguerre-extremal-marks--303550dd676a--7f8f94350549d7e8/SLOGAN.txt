For bounded Poisson–Laguerre marks with endpoint tail P(A-M<=y)~C y^kappa, large-inradius extremal marks concentrate at A on the scale d(v_d gamma)^(2/d)(log rho^d)^(1-2/d), and the rescaled deficit converges to Gamma(kappa, rate 2A), independently of the Gumbel height. In d=3 this yields a universal -(kappa/3) log log correction and exposes a missing +log gamma in the two displayed v1 example centerings (3.23) and (3.24).
