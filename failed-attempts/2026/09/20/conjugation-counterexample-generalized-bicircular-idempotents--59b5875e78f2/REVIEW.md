# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The semilinear algebra is correct. For a conjugation J and distinct unit phases, conjugate-linearity gives JP_i=lambda_i P_i; together with P_1+P_2=I this yields complementary nonzero real-linear idempotents and J=lambda_1P_1+lambda_2P_2. Every conjugation has a nonzero fixed vector u, and on C u the recombination alpha P_1+beta P_2 has scalar form a conjugate(z)+b z. Constancy of its modulus for all phases forces a conjugate(b)=0, hence alpha=beta or alpha/beta=lambda_1/lambda_2; both cases are indeed isometries. The C^1[0,1] example with pointwise conjugation and phases 1,i gives the displayed sqrt(5) failure for P_1-P_2 and therefore contradicts the target preprint's stated dichotomy.
