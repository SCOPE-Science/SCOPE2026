If a knot attains the universal lower bound \(\operatorname{stick}(K)=2b(K)+2\), then connected summing any number of trefoils raises its stick number by exactly two per trefoil.
Applied to the nineteen newly known \(10\)-stick, \(4\)-bridge prime knots, this gives \(\operatorname{stick}(K\#T_1\#\cdots\#T_m)=10+2m\) for arbitrary trefoil chiralities.
