The new skip-step chi permutations are not single nonlinear circles: after reindexing they are exactly gcd(n,v) parallel copies of the ordinary odd-dimensional chi on n/gcd(n,v) bits, and the second family is affine-conjugate to the same product. This gives their complete composite-chi differential/linear profiles, inverse degree (n/gcd(n,v)+1)/2, identical order to the small chi block, and permanent absence of cross-block diffusion under iteration.
