Kanter's gamma lower bound for e^{-2r}(I_0(2r)+I_1(2r)) extends to every real order nu >= -1/2: Phi_nu(2r) >= 2^{-2r-nu} Gamma(2r+1)/(Gamma(r+1)Gamma(r+nu+1)). A one-sign-change reduction and a positive digamma-exponential-integral boundary functional yield the full order range, with equality at nu=-1/2 and asymptotically sharp constants.
