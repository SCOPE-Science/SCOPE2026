For exact conjugate gradients on a finite-dimensional SPD matrix, every Euclidean residual step is nonincreasing for every right-hand side and initial guess if and only if kappa_2(A) <= 3 + 2 sqrt(2); the sharp per-step amplification factor is (kappa-1)/(2 sqrt(kappa)). A tracked direction-history factor sharpens the bound, and the same threshold transfers to fixed-preconditioner CG in the M^{-1} residual norm.
