Combining those lemmas yields the staircase bound mechanically. The strict/inclusive rounding and two-level witnesses are routine equality refinements of the same implication.

Because originality fails and no separately motivated valuable claim survives after that coverage is removed, the package is retained as a failed attempt rather than repaired by disclaimer.
