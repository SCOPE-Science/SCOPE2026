For a minimal τ-tilting-infinite algebra with radical square zero, unfaithful bricks are completely controlled by the underlying sink-source affine Dynkin graph. The algebra is nondistributive exactly in the Kronecker case, exactly when it has infinitely many unfaithful bricks; an alternating affine n-cycle has exactly n^2 unfaithful bricks, while affine D/E tree types have only finitely many and every sincere brick is faithful.
