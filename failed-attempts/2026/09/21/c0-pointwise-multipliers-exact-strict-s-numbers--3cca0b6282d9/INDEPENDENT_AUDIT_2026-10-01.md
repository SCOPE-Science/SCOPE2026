## Final claim assessed

Exact strict s-numbers of pointwise multipliers on C0-spaces

## Correctness — PASS

PASS. Finite superlevel sets are finite open subsets of a Hausdorff space and hence clopen, so truncation gives the finite-rank upper bound. Below the \(n\)-th threshold, disjoint compactly supported bumps and point evaluations factor the identity on \(\ell_\infty^n\) through the multiplier, which gives the lower bound for every strict s-number from the strict normalization and ideal axioms. Infinite superlevel sets yield disjoint bumps spanning an isometric copy of \(c_0\), proving the strict-singularity obstruction and the common ideal-distance lower bound. The derived-set formula on compact spaces follows from the accumulation-point characterization of infinite superlevel sets.

## Originality — FAIL

FAIL. A published 20 September 2026 result on weighted composition operators between scalar \(C(K)\)-spaces already proves the exact approximation, Bernstein, Gelfand and Kolmogorov profile from threshold-image counting, the same limiting distance to compact, weakly compact, finitely strictly singular and strictly singular operators, and the same \(c_0\) witness mechanism. Pointwise multiplication on compact \(C(K)\) is an immediate special case. The passage in the assigned package from compact \(C(K)\) to locally compact \(C_0\), and from the four classical s-numbers to arbitrary strict s-numbers, repeats the same finite-superlevel truncation and finite \(\ell_\infty^n\) factorization with standard \(C_0\) bump functions. Under the required implication standard, this is a routine extension of already-published structure rather than an original scientific theorem.
