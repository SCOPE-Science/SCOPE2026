# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The equality in conditional variance gives exactly that the score is constant almost everywhere on every shifted quantizer cell. For a positive full-support density this makes the score finite-step with minimal knots K and equality iff K+θ⊆T. Intersecting over a finite Θ gives T⊇K+Θ; the real-line sumset inequality gives |K+Θ|≥|K|+|Θ|−1. Integrable full-support one-knot scores are asymmetric Laplace, so the sharp m−1 equality case is correct.

## originality

FAIL

Hobza–Molina–Vajda's complete 2005 primary paper, Theorem 3.1, already gives for every finite interval quantization the exact iff equality condition that the quantile-coordinate score derivative is constant in each cell. Under the positive full-support location model this is exactly the package's single-location knot criterion. The advertised multi-location law is its direct conjunction over θ∈Θ; the minimum cell count is simply the number of distinct forced thresholds plus one, and the cardinality bound is the elementary one-dimensional finite-set sumset inequality. Thus the claimed new result is mechanically implied by a stronger theorem, even though the same multi-location notation is absent.

## value

FAIL

Once the published iff equality criterion is fixed, applying it at finitely many θ and counting the union of forced thresholds is an elementary design restatement. The source offers no independently useful unknown exact invariant or structural step beyond that direct implication; under the SCOPE bar, correct/reproducible corollaries of stronger prior work do not acquire value from renamed multi-location notation alone.
