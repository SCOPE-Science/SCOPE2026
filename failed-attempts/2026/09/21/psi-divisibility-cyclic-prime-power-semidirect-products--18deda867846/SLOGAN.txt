For G=C_{p^alpha} semidirect C_{q^beta} with nontrivial action image of order q^gamma, the gcd of psi(C_{p^alpha}) and psi(G) is exactly gcd(psi(C_{p^alpha}), (q^(2gamma)-1)/(q+1)), so the normal cyclic p-subgroup itself witnesses failure of psi-divisibility. This removes the exponent-size restriction from the prime-power-complement slice of the known ZM-group obstruction and extends to cyclic complements whose action image is a prime power or whose image and kernel orders are coprime.
