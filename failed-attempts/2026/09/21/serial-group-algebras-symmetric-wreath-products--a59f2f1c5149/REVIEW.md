# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The classification reconstructs. If the characteristic divides |A|, the base A^n contains a product of at least two nontrivial p-subgroups, so a Sylow p-subgroup of the wreath product is noncyclic; a finite-dimensional serial algebra is representation-finite, so Higman excludes seriality. Seriality also descends to quotients, forcing F[S_n] serial. If p does not divide |A|, Maschke makes F[A] semisimple; after a splitting extension, the tensor-simple-type orbits under S_n give full corners Morita equivalent to group algebras of Young stabilizers. The inner permutation action on each matrix corner untwists to a matrix algebra tensored with the stabilizer group algebra. Combining this with the cyclic-defect/Brauer-tree classification for symmetric groups gives exactly the listed n<p, (p,n)=(2,2),(2,3), and p=3,n=3,4,5 cases.

FAIL

The final classification is mechanically implied by broader prior Morita-equivalence machinery for wreath products together with the pre-existing seriality classification of symmetric-group algebras and the elementary orbit/Young-stabilizer decomposition. Under the required implication standard, an unstated corollary of those stronger inputs is covered.
