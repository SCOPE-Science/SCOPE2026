Over a field of characteristic p>0 and for n>=2, the group algebra of A wr S_n is serial exactly when p does not divide |A| and the group algebra of S_n is serial. Equivalently, aside from semisimple n<p cases, the only modular possibilities are p=2 with n=2,3 and p=3 with n=3,4,5; in particular every 3'-base A gives a non-3-solvable serial group algebra F[A wr S_5] in characteristic 3.
