Cintioli's local Join Property forces much more than existence of jump inverses: for every \(\mathbf a\geq\mathbf0'\), the reals in the extension with jump degree \(\mathbf a\) form a dense copy of \(\mathbb Q\). The extension is therefore partitioned into continuum many pairwise disjoint dense rational fibres, one for each jump degree above \(\mathbf0'\).
