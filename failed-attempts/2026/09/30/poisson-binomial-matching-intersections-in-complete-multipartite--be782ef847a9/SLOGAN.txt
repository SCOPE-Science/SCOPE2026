For a fixed matching \(M\) between two parts of a complete multipartite graph, the number of matching edges captured by a uniform spanning tree is exactly Poisson-binomial: \(m-1\) Bernoulli variables share one parameter and a final Bernoulli variable carries the finite-size correction. Equivalently, every \(q\)-edge submatching has joint inclusion probability \(\left(1-q/N\right)\left((N-n_i)^{-1}+(N-n_j)^{-1}\right)^q\).
