# Independent audit — 2026-10-01

## Final claim

For every bounded-to-one Borel action of a countable commutative monoid, the induced orbit equivalence relation is generated by one Borel automorphism and is either smooth or Borel bireducible with \(E_0\).

## Correctness — PASS

The orbit relation is Borel as a countable union of Borel equalizer sets, and every class is countable because it is a countable union of finite fibers. Shinko--Weilacher--Yu give hyperfiniteness under exactly the bounded-to-one countable commutative-monoid hypotheses. The classical hyperfinite equivalence-relation theorem supplies a Borel \(\mathbb Z\)-action with the same relation, whose generator is one Borel automorphism, and supplies the reduction to \(E_0\). For a nonsmooth relation, Glimm--Effros supplies the reverse reduction \(E_0\le_B E\), giving bireducibility.
