For Kato's new construction realizing a closed braid as the indefinite fold locus, the exact type-\(\mathrm{II}^2\) cost is \(4r+o\), where \(r\) counts syllables away from \(\sigma_1\) and \(o\) counts odd exponents. Consequently the construction has no outputs with exactly \(2\) or \(3\) type-\(\mathrm{II}^2\) fibers, and its cost parity is \(n-c(\widehat b)\).
