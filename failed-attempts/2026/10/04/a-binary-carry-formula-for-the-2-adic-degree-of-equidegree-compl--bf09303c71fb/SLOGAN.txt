For the discriminant of \(c\) equal-degree equations in \(\mathbb P^N\), the full 2-adic valuation of its total degree is an explicit sum of endpoint valuations and the binary carry count in \((c-1)+(N-c+1)=N\). The degree is odd only for even-degree hypersurfaces in even-dimensional projective space, while every nonempty equidegree complete intersection of codimension at least two has discriminant degree divisible by \(4\), sharply.
