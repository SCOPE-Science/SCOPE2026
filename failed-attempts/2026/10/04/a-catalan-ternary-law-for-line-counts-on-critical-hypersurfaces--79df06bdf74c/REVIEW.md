# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The coefficient formula reduces exactly as claimed modulo three. For ambient dimension divisible by three one product factor vanishes modulo three; in the other two residue classes the factor product reduces to a monomial times \((x+y)^{2m}\), and the target coefficient difference is \(\binom{2m}{m}-\binom{2m}{m+1}=C_m\). The ternary digit rule and density count are valid consequences of the Catalan residue theorem.

## originality

FAIL

The principal claimed bridge is already stated explicitly in Gruenberg–Moree–Zagier, Lemma 14: their line-count sequence satisfies \(v_{1+3k}\equiv v_{2+3k}\equiv \frac1{k+1}\binom{2k}{k}\pmod 3\) and \(v_{3k}\equiv0\pmod3\). This is exactly the audited Catalan congruence, not merely row equality. The ternary digit description and density-one corollary then follow routinely from the known Deutsch–Sagan classification of Catalan residues.
