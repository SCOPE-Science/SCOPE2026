For the critical hypersurface line count \(L_n\), the entire modulus-three table is governed by Catalan numbers: \(L_{3m}=0\), while \(L_{3m+1}=L_{3m+2}=C_m\) modulo three. Consequently the nonzero residues are read directly from the ternary digits of \(m+1\), and three divides \(L_n\) for a density-one set of ambient dimensions.
