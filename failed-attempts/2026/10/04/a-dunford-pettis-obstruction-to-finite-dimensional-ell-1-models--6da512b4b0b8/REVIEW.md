## correctness

PASS

A bi-Lipschitz bijection linearizes to an isomorphism of Lipschitz-free spaces, and every finite-dimensional normed space is linearly isomorphic to Euclidean space. Mason's 2026 theorem, visible already in the primary arXiv abstract, gives the Dunford--Pettis property for \(\mathcal F(M)\) whenever \(M\subset\mathbb R^d\). The Dunford--Pettis property is preserved by countable \(\ell_1\)-sums and complemented subspaces. Godefroy--Kalton's lifting theorem embeds every separable \(X\) as a complemented copy in \(\mathcal F(X)\), so failure of the property for \(X\) forces failure for \(\mathcal F(X)\). The contradiction for a complemented embedding into the sum is therefore valid.
