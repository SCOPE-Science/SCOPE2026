For the García--Maestre--Pinasco--Zalduendo diagonal witness \(D_c\), every point of the closed ball \(\sqrt{1-c^2}\,B_{X_c}\) is the weak limit of an explicit maximizing sequence. For the normalized one-spike family \(u_n(x)=(x+e_n)/\|x+e_n\|\), this radius is exact: the sequence is maximizing exactly when \(\|x\|\leq\sqrt{1-c^2}\).
