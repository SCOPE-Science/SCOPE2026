In their natural minimal homogeneous embeddings, the Lagrangian Grassmannian \(\operatorname{LG}(n,2n)\) and the equal-dimensional spinor variety \(\operatorname{OG}(n+1,2n+2)\) have projective degrees differing by exactly \(2^{\binom n2}\). Consequently their odd parts are identical, while \(\nu_2(\deg\operatorname{LG}(n,2n))=\frac{n(n+1)}2-s_2(\frac{n(n+1)}2)\).
