Finite models that satisfy the Farey approximation axiom \(\psi_n\) cannot stay topologically simple: \(\varphi_0+\psi_n\) already forces minimum degree at least \(n\), hence orientable genus at least \(1+\left\lceil (n-6)(n+1)/12\right\rceil\). Thus every finite pseudofinite approximation sequence has genus growing at least quadratically with its axiom depth.
