For the Gaussian-mixture sequential \(t\)-martingale family, the exact post-hoc envelope over all fixed prior precisions has optimizer \(c_*^2=n/(T_{n-1}^2-1)\) when \(|T_{n-1}|>1\). This yields a sharp tuning-independent fixed-time evidence barrier, while explicitly not treating the data-adaptive maximum as an e-value.
