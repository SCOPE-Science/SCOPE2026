# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Gerretsen's upper inequality \(s^2\le4R^2+4Rr+3r^2\) and Euler's \(R\ge2r\) give \(p-4R\le r\Phi(r/R)\), where \(\Phi\) is increasing on \((0,1/2]\) and \(\Phi(1/2)=6\sqrt3-8\). For a lattice-free triangle the incenter lies within \(\sqrt2/2\) of a lattice point, so an open incircle with larger radius would contain an interior lattice point. Hence \(r\le\sqrt2/2\) and the stated constant follows. A fresh grid check verified positivity of the derivative numerator and the exact numerical constant; the proof is analytic.

## originality

FAIL

The triangle bound is a direct synthesis of two classical inputs: Gerretsen's textbook inequality for every Euclidean triangle and the elementary covering-radius bound \(r\le\sqrt2/2\) for every lattice-free convex body. After substituting \(t=r/R\), a one-variable monotonicity check produces the displayed coefficient. The 2013/2015 paper supplies the motivating lattice-free perimeter-circumradius conjecture, but the assigned subclass theorem is mechanically implied by these older general inequalities rather than requiring a new geometric mechanism.

## value

FAIL

The triangle subclass is mathematically natural, but the claimed strengthening is obtained by a short textbook inequality chain and a one-variable derivative check, and the constant is not asserted sharp. It therefore does not clear the value bar against routine deductions from standard geometric inequalities.
