For the standard mutation orbit in \(K_0(\mathbf P^{p-1})\), the normalized skew Euler matrix \(p^{-1}(B-B^{\mathsf T})\bmod p\) is fixed up to signed permutation. Its triangle-square fingerprint is therefore invariant; for \(p=5\) it is \(\{1^{\times5},4^{\times5}\}\), and for \(p=7\) it is \(\{1^{\times14},2^{\times21}\}\).
