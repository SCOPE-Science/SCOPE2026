# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The numerical window follows correctly from the cited ingredients. Schröder's Exercise 4-34 explicitly restates that every finite ordered set with the fixed point property and at most nine elements is connectedly collapsible. Baclawski's Corollary 6.9 gives connectedly collapsible \(\Rightarrow\) link collapsible \(\Rightarrow\) pseudo cone for finite posets, and the paper states that a pseudo cone has an acyclic complete matching whose realization is contractible. Thus an FPP finite \(T_0\)-space of size at most nine has contractible order complex and cannot have weak circle type by McCord. Barmak's Lemma 10 supplies an explicit 14-point finite space with FPP and weak homotopy type \(S^1\). Hence \(10\le m_{\mathrm{FPP}}(S^1)\le14\).

The claimed interval is already forced by two published endpoint results: the at-most-nine FPP collapse theorem gives the lower bound 10, while Barmak's explicit 14-point weak-circle FPP model gives the upper bound 14. The package combines these known implications but adds no new mathematical step needed to obtain the interval. Under implication-based comparison, the absence of the exact string '10 to 14' in one source does not make the combined bound original.
