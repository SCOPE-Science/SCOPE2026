For a planar monodromy with a Calabi-Yau positive factorization and integral trivialization \(u\), every competing positive factor has \(u\)-value only \(0\) or \(1\). The length excess \(m\'-m\), and for allowable competitors the excess in both \(b_2\) and \(\chi\), is exactly the number of vanishing cycles on which \(u\) vanishes.
