For every Banach space \(Y\), every \(1<p<\infty\), and every finite \(r\ge p^*=p/(p-1)\), WMP for \((\ell_p,Y)\) is equivalent to \(\mathrm{SMP}_r\). If WMP holds and \(Y\) contains an isometric \(\ell_p\), the same \(p^*\) is the exact threshold for both \(\mathrm{SMP}_r\) and the \(r\)-convergent perturbation property.\n
