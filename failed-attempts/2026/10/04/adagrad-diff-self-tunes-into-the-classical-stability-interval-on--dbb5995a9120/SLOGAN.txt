On every active coordinate of a positive-definite diagonal quadratic, AdaGrad-Diff's cumulative gradient-difference metric converges to a value \(w_i^\infty>\eta\lambda_i/2\), so its limiting effective step \(\eta/w_i^\infty\) automatically falls inside \((0,2/\lambda_i)\) for any base \(\eta>0\). This forces eventual Q-linear last-iterate convergence on the whole finite-dimensional separable quadratic.
