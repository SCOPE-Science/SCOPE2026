On one fixed very general nine-point blow-up \(X_9\), a single common very general point supports infinitely many primitive ample classes of square \(20\) with \(\varepsilon=2\sqrt5\). An explicit affine-\(E_8^{(1)}\) translation sends the Laface--Ugaglia class to classes of plane degree \(9+12m^2\), so the phenomenon is not a scaling artifact.
