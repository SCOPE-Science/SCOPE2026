# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Direct substitution of \(P(e_1)=ae_1+ce_2\), \(P(e_2)=be_1+de_2\) into the averaging identity gives \(b=0\) and \(a(a-d)=0\). Thus the operators are exactly \(P_{a,c}\) with \(d=a\) or \(Q_{c,d}\) with \(a=0\). The algebra automorphisms are \(\phi_{\xi,\nu}(e_1)=\xi e_1+\nu e_2\), \(\phi(e_2)=\xi^2e_2\). Direct matrix conjugation gives \(P_{a,c}\mapsto P_{a,\xi c}\) and \(Q_{c,d}\mapsto Q_{\xi c-d\nu/\xi,d}\), yielding exactly the three displayed orbit families and stated stabilizers. The finite-grid verifier agrees but is not used for the infinite proof.

The conjugation reduction to scalar/non-scalar Jordan and diagonal representatives is a routine two-by-two calculation. Adding the omitted zero/boundary parameter values corrects the table's presentation but does not make the resulting orbit classification implication-independent.
