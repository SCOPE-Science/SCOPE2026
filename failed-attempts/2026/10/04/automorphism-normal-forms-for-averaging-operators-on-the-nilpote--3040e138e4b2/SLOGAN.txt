For the complex pre-Lie algebra with \(e_1\cdot e_1=e_2\), every averaging operator is isomorphic to exactly one of three explicit normal-form families: \(aI\), \(aI+E_{21}\), or \(\operatorname{diag}(0,d)\) with \(d\ne0\). Their stabilizers are, respectively, the full automorphism group, a one-dimensional additive subgroup, and a one-dimensional multiplicative subgroup.
