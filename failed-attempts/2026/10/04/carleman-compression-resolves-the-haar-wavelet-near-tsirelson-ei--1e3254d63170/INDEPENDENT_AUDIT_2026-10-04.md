# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The matrix entries are the Haar-basis matrix coefficients of the positive Carleman operator after reflection. Hence every finite matrix is a compression and has norm at most \(\pi\). The union of the finite Haar spaces is dense in the mean-zero subspace of \(L^2(0,1)\). A compactly supported Carleman near-extremizer can be dilated into \((0,1/2)\), corrected by a term of norm \(O(a^{1/2})\) supported in \([1/2,1]\) to enforce zero mean, normalized, and then approximated by a finite Haar sum. Its Rayleigh quotient stays arbitrarily close to \(\pi\), proving the directed lower limit and therefore the stated limit. No finite numerical extrapolation is needed.

The audited final claim is therefore directly covered, not merely implied by an adjacent theorem.
