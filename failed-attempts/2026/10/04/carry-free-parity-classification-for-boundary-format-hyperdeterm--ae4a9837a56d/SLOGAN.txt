A boundary-format hyperdeterminant has prime-adic degree controlled exactly by base-prime carries: \(p\nmid D\) precisely when \(p\nmid N+1\) and \(k_1+\cdots+k_r=N\) is carry-free in base \(p\). For even \(N\), the odd-degree formats with \(r\) smaller factors are counted up to permutation by \(S(s_2(N),r)\); for odd \(N\) there are none.
