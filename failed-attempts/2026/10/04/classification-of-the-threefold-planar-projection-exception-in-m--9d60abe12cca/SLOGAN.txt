A convex body in \(\mathbb R^3\) has every planar projection threefold-rotationally symmetric after translation exactly when, after one global translation, its support function is \(c+H\) with \(c>0\) and \(H\in\mathcal H_3(S^2)\) satisfying the support-function curvature inequality. At fixed width, the entire exceptional family is a seven-dimensional compact convex body of cubic spherical harmonics.
