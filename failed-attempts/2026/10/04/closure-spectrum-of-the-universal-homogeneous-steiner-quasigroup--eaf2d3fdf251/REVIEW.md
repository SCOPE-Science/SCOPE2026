## originality

FAIL

The exact closure-spectrum wording was not located, but the statement is implication-covered by standard facts. Horsley--Webb explicitly view Steiner triple systems as Steiner quasigroups and state that any homogeneous Steiner triple system is 2-point transitive. Hence the unary and binary closures are the full symmetric group. For the upper threshold, the multiplication graph/block relation is a \(G\)-invariant ternary relation. By the definition of \(3\)-closure as the largest permutation group preserving the \(G\)-orbits on ordered triples, every element of \(G^{(3)}\) preserves every union of those orbits, in particular the ternary graph of multiplication; therefore it is already an automorphism. No special new Steiner lemma is needed beyond the known 2-transitivity and the generic closure definition.
