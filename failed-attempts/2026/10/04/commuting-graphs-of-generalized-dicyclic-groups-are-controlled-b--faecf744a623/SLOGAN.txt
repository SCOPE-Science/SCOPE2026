For every nonabelian generalized dicyclic group \(\operatorname{Dic}(A,y)\), the full commuting graph is \(K_{|A[2]|}\vee\left(K_{|A|-|A[2]|}\mathbin{\dot\cup}(|A|/|A[2]|)K_{|A[2]|}\right)\). Thus it is always a cograph and chordal, and within this family its isomorphism type remembers exactly \(|A|\) and \(|A[2]|\).
