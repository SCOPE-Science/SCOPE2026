For Enomoto's two-dimensional family, the pairwise Ext groups occur in adjacent degree pairs: \(\operatorname{Ext}^{2r}\) and \(\operatorname{Ext}^{2r+1}\) are one-dimensional exactly when \(\mu=q^r\lambda\), and otherwise vanish. In particular, infinite-order \(q\) gives a nonprojective nonperiodic module with self-Ext algebra \(k[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)\), while finite order \(m\) gives exact period \(2m\).
