# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In the stated normalized, nonmagnetic equilateral square graph with U=e^(i mu) R, 0<mu<pi/2, positive k and Bloch Q=cos(theta1)+cos(theta2), Exner--Tater Eq(20)-(21) gives F(k,Q)=F(k,0)+2k(k^2-1)sin(k ell) Q, with overall nonzero prefactor for k>0. Positive point spectrum requires the fiber determinant vanish on positive measure in theta; a nonconstant affine expression in Q has zero set of measure zero. Thus k=1 or sin(k ell)=0. For sin(k ell)=0, F=4k^2 sin(2mu)>0, impossible. At k=1, F=4sin(2(mu+ell)), independent of Q, vanishing iff mu+ell is a multiple of pi/2. Every fiber then has E=1, giving infinite-multiplicity point spectrum by Floquet direct-integral theory. Finite code checks coefficients but is not proof of the all-k statement; endpoints, negatives and magnetic cases excluded.

FAIL

The original Exner--Tater paper already supplies the exact Floquet dispersion equation (20)-(21) for ALL positive k and Bloch parameters and explicitly derives the k=1 flat-band relation (23), equivalent to sin(2(mu+ell))=0. The proposed uniqueness assertion is the immediate missing one-line coefficient comparison: Q-coefficient 2k(k^2-1)sin(k ell), and the only other branch sin(k ell)=0 is visibly nonzero in the SAME published coefficients. Together with standard direct-integral point-spectrum equivalence, the complete claimed theorem is a routine corollary of a published exact dispersion characterization, not new independent spectral information. Source says merely no infinite series, but prior mathematical implication still covers the simple exclusion.
