For every complete multipartite graph, connected dom-\(k\)-forcing reduces to a two-cap optimization around one part: apart from the universal-vertex exception, \(F_{cdk}(G)=N-\max_i[\min(k,n_i-1)+\min(k,N-n_i-1)]\). The formula simultaneously recovers the known complete-graph, connected-dom-forcing biclique, and dom-2-forcing biclique cases.
