For the Pareto type-I mean-threshold problem, the unique optimizer for every \(\kappa>1\) is exactly \(\theta_\kappa=W_{-1}(-1/(e\kappa))/(W_{-1}(-1/(e\kappa))+1)\). As \(\kappa\downarrow1\), the optimizer diverges like \((2\log\kappa)^{-1/2}\) and the minimum rises from \(1-e^{-1}\) with an explicit square-root law.
