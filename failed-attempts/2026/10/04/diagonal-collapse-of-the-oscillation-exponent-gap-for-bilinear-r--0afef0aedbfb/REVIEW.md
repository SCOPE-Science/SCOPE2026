# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The algebra is correct. Direct substitution gives \(S_{r,r}(b,b)=\|b\|_{\mathrm{BMO}_r}^2\) and \(T_u(b,b)=\|b\|_{\mathrm{BMO}_{2u}}^2\). The cited source lower estimates therefore dominate a finite BMO moment square, and choosing one fixed positive bump in the upper estimates gives only higher finite BMO moments. John--Nirenberg converts both sides to \(\|b\|_{\mathrm{BMO}}^2\). The stated local \(L^4\) hypothesis is sufficient for the repeated-position product integrability used by the package.

## originality

FAIL

The final theorem is a direct same-paper specialization. Wu's fresh result supplies the two-symbol lower bounds and bumped upper bounds at the natural exponent triples. On the diagonal, the definitions immediately become \(S_{r,r}(b,b)=\|b\|_{\mathrm{BMO}_r}^2\) and \(T_u(b,b)=\|b\|_{\mathrm{BMO}_{2u}}^2\); the classical John--Nirenberg theorem then identifies every finite moment. Thus the claimed removal of the bump for \(b_1=b_2\) is implication-covered by the published general inequalities plus a textbook theorem, regardless of whether Wu explicitly prints this corollary.
