# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The statement is mathematically correct. At fixed \(\mu_0\), changing \(\gamma\) from 6 to \(6\pm\eta\) shifts the Fredholm determinant by \(\pm\eta\). For the lower branch, \(\partial_z\Delta=-1-\mu_0^2\int(w_1-z)^{-2}<0\), so a negative eigenvalue exists exactly on the sublevel \(\Delta_6(k;0)<\eta\). The source's unique threshold minimum and expansion \(\Delta_6(k;z)=C\sqrt{(6/5)|k|^2-2z}+O(|k|^2)+O(|z|)\) yield the radius \(R=(C\sqrt{6/5})^{-1}\), Hausdorff ball limit, cubic volume law, and, after \(k=\eta q,z=-\eta^2E\), the displayed parabolic energy profile. The identities \(\varepsilon(k+\pi)=6-\varepsilon(k)\) and \(I(k+\pi;18-z)=-I(k;z)\) give the exact upper/lower reflection. The source PDF has a prose monotonicity typo near Theorem 3.8, but its inequalities and the direct derivative have the sign used by the audited proof.
