For every real two-dimensional Lorentz space \(d^{(2)}(w,2)\), the square of its Euclidean Banach--Mazur distance, its Jordan--von Neumann constant, and its Ptolemy constant are all exactly \(2w_1/(w_1+w_2)\). The identity follows from a sharp Euclidean norm comparison plus a two-ellipse lower-bound argument.
