On two symmetric scalar quadratics, constant-step Euclidean BSPPA is exactly a contractive random affine recursion, so its stationary law is a scaled Bernoulli convolution with an explicit nonzero objective-error floor. The stationary support changes sharply from the full interval \([-c,c]\) to a Cantor set when \(\alpha a\) crosses \(1\), with the critical law uniform on \([-c,c]\).
