# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Multiplying by e^{2lambda tau} factors the characteristic equation through y=lambda e^{lambda tau}, where y solves y^2+Py+Q=0. For real negative roots, the first imaginary crossing is generated by the root of largest magnitude and occurs at pi/(2|y|), giving the first branch. For conjugate roots, both have modulus sqrt(Q) and the phase condition gives the second branch. Differentiating lambda e^{lambda tau}=y gives d lambda/d tau=-lambda^2/(1+lambda tau), whose real part at a simple imaginary root is positive. Fresh independent numerical checks on both discriminant regimes and the repeated boundary reproduced zero characteristic residuals and positive crossing direction.

The quadratic factorization itself is immediate algebra.
