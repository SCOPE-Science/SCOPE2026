For an inscribed triangle \(XYZ\), the excess of \(XY^2+YZ^2+ZX^2\) above the Lemoine-pedal minimum \(12\Delta^2/(a^2+b^2+c^2)\) splits exactly into a centroid-displacement square and a nonpedality square. The identity gives a sharp stability certificate and corrects a factor-three constant in an inspected 2007 statement of the classical minimum.
