For the orthogonal-simplex family used to show near-sharpness of the Minkowski-asymmetry Banach--Mazur bound, the factor \(\sqrt{2(\sqrt2-1)}\) is the exact unattained infimum. An exact sum-of-squares defect formula shows that near-extremizers are precisely the one-full-block regime with residual ratio tending to \(\sqrt2-1\).
