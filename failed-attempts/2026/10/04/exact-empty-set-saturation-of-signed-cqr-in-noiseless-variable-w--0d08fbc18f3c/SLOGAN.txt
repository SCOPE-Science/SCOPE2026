In a noiseless variable-width model with exact CQR centers, a negative global conformal correction makes the prediction set empty on exactly \(\lfloor\alpha(m+1)\rfloor/(m+1)\) of test points in expectation. Conditional on width quantile \(u\), coverage is exactly \(\Pr\{\operatorname{Bin}(m,u)\ge\lfloor\alpha(m+1)\rfloor\}\), which asymptotically becomes a hard cutoff at \(u=\alpha\).
