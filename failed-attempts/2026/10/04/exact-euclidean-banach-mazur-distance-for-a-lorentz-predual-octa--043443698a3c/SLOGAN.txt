For the real plane \(X_w=d_*(1,w)^2\), \(0<w<1\), with \(\|(x,y)\|_w=\max\{|x|,|y|,(|x|+|y|)/(1+w)\}\), the Euclidean Banach--Mazur distance is \(d_{\mathrm{BM}}(X_w,\ell_2^2)=\sqrt{2(1+w^2)}/(1+w)\) for \(0<w\le \sqrt2-1\), and \(d_{\mathrm{BM}}(X_w,\ell_2^2)=\sqrt{1+w^2}\) for \(\sqrt2-1\le w<1\). It is uniquely minimized at \(w=\sqrt2-1\), where it equals \(\sqrt{4-2\sqrt2}\). Thus the previously recurring threshold \(w=\sqrt2-1\) is the unique Hilbert-nearest member of this octagonal Lorentz-predual family.
