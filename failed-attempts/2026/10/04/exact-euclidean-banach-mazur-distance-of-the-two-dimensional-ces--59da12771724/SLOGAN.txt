For the real two-dimensional Cesàro space \(\mathrm{ces}_2^{(2)}\), with \(\|(x,y)\|=\bigl(|x|^2+((|x|+|y|)/2)^2\bigr)^{1/2}\), the Banach--Mazur distance to the Euclidean plane is exactly \(d_{\mathrm{BM}}(\mathrm{ces}_2^{(2)},\ell_2^2)=\sqrt{1+1/\sqrt5}\). After the standard linear normalization, the unit ball is the intersection of two quadratic ellipses, and their two positive-semidefinite containment conditions force the sharp factor \(\sqrt{1+1/\sqrt5}\).
