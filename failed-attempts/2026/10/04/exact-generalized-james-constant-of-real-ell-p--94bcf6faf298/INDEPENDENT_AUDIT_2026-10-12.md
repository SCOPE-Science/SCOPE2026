# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Checked the actual defining supremum of min(||a x+b y||p,||a x-b y||p), a,b>0, a+b=1, over real unit vectors and at least two coordinates. Hanner Theorem 1 for 1<p<2 applied to z=a x+b y and w=a x-b y gives (U+V)^p+|U-V|^p<=2^p(a^p+b^p), so min(U,V)<=(a^p+b^p)^(1/p); e_i,e_j attain equality. For p>2 the same primary theorem directly gives U^p+V^p<=(a+b)^p+|a-b|^p; the two-coordinate pair 2^(-1/p)(e_i+e_j),2^(-1/p)(e_i-e_j) makes U=V and attains the bound. The primary paper explicitly supplies sharpness and these equality geometries, not merely an unsharp estimate. At p=2 the parallelogram identity gives both matching branches; p=1 follows directly from the triangle inequality and disjoint supports; p=infinity follows from the triangle inequality and the (1,1),(1,-1) witness. Finite-p vectors have countable support even for an arbitrary index set, and finite truncation/continuity extends the norm inequalities. No finite sample or uploaded checker is treated as a proof. The scalar field and lambda range are preserved.
