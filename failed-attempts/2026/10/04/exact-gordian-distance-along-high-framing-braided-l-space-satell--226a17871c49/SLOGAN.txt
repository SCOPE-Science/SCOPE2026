For every braided L-space satellite operator on \(p\ge2\) strands and every companion with \(\epsilon=1\), the high-framing satellites form an exact Gordian geodesic: \(d_G(P(K,m),P(K,n))=\binom{p}{2}|m-n|\) whenever \(m,n\ge2\tau(K)\). The affine \(\tau\)-formula supplies the lower bound and the full-twist braid factorization realizes it.
