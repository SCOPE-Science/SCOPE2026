# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The induction from the strict-successor formula is valid, the complement of an upset is a downset, and a maximal point of any nonempty finite complement shows the top is the unique fixed upset. Iterating the sequence inequalities proves the forward forcing direction; the source-style valuation by iterates of the empty upset proves the converse. The package's exhaustive small-poset checker is corroborative only.

## originality

FAIL

Kocsis already gives the general strict-successor coderivative formula on any poset and, in Lemma 5.13, explicitly iterates it for the empty upset to turn nonmembership in an N-th iterate into a long-chain witness while using the same canonical finite-prefix valuation. On a finite complement, taking the maximum chain length makes the assigned cutoff immediate. This is covered by theorem-level implication even though Kocsis does not package the finite height as a named theorem.

## value

FAIL

Finite height is a natural summary, but the result is a routine finite corollary of Kocsis's exact coderivative formula and his own finite-prefix countermodel mechanism. It does not add a structural lemma, new boundary phenomenon or independently difficult invariant.
