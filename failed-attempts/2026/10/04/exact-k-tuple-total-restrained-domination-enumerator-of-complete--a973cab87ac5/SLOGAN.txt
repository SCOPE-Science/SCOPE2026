For \(G=K_{n_1,\ldots,n_r}\), a set \(S\) is \(k\)-tuple total restrained dominating exactly when each part sees at least \(k\) selected vertices outside itself and every non-full part also sees at least \(k\) omitted vertices outside itself. These two profile inequalities yield an exact generating function counting all such sets by cardinality for arbitrary part sizes and every admissible \(k\).
