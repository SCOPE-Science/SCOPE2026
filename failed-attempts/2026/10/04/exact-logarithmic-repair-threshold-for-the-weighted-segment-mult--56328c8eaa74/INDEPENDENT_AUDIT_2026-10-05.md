# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The recent source's primary abstract states the exact characterization of segment-multiplier boundedness by the large-scale Muckenhoupt condition obtained by restricting the usual \(A_p\) test to intervals of length at least one. For the submitted weight, the reciprocal is \(|x|^{-1}(\log(e/|x|))^{-\gamma/(p-1)}\); the substitution \(u=\log(e/x)\) makes its compact-defect mass \(\int_1^\infty u^{-q}du\), finite exactly for \(q>1\), hence \(\gamma>p-1\). Because both factors equal one outside the defect, that integrability is also sufficient for all large intervals. The classical small-interval product and the blow-up of the truncated characteristic follow from the same exact reciprocal integral and standard endpoint integration.

## originality

FAIL

The final claim is a direct elementary specialization of the newly published characterization for every weight. Once boundedness is known to be equivalent to the large-scale \(A_p\) condition, inserting this one-parameter weight reduces the threshold to the textbook integral \(\int_1^\infty u^{-q}du\). The classical \(A_p\) failure and the threshold blow-up are likewise immediate one-dimensional endpoint-integral consequences. Under the audit rule that special cases and corollaries are covered even without matching wording or numbers, the general characterization already dominates the submitted result.

## value

FAIL

The power-log family is natural, but the claimed threshold is obtained by substituting it into an already-published if-and-only-if criterion and evaluating a standard \(u^{-q}\) integral. The additional divergence and characteristic-rate formulas are routine endpoint asymptotics. This is useful exposition of the new criterion, not a motivated new structural lemma or nontrivial exact classification under the common value bar.
