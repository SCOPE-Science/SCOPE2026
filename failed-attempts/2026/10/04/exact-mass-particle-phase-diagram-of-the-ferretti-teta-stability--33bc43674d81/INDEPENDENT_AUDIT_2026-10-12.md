# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Writing q=N−1 and r=(varsigma+1)^{−1} gives γ_c=(2/π)[arcsin(r)/r−sqrt(1−r²)/q]; derivative of first term is positive (numerator derivative r²/(1−r²)^{3/2}) and derivative of second positive in r. Thus γ_c strictly falls with varsigma and rises with N; mass endpoint L_N=(2/π)(N−2)/(N−1), strict mass attainability γ>L_N; integer ceiling carefully respects strict inequality. At fixed finite mass lim_N γ_c=G and each finite γ_c<G, so uniform criterion is γ≥G. Taylor arcsin(r)/r=1+r²/6+O(r⁴) yields q r²→6 at γ=2/π; uniform G−2/π=r²/(3π)+O(r⁴) yields second scaling. The theorem is scoped explicitly to an existing sufficient certificate, not necessity of actual physical stability.

FAIL

The claimed phase region is a reparameterization and elementary monotonicity/Taylor analysis of an exact, already published two-parameter sufficiency function; its most conspicuous particle-number threshold comes immediately from the paper’s explicitly printed mass-infimum formula. This is strong prior implication, not an inaccessible-literature default rejection.
