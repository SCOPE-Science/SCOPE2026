For the preferred-orientation unit cube, every non-Dirichlet spectral wave number \(k>1\) obeys exactly \(\cos k=\pm(k^2-1)/(k^2+3)\), and each corresponding eigenvalue has multiplicity three. The high-energy offsets are \(\pm2\sqrt{2}/(n\pi)+O(n^{-3})\), sharpening the published localization bound.
