For Grokfast-EMA in front of gradient descent, the exact SPD-quadratic learning-rate ceiling is set by the filter's Nyquist gain: \(\eta L<2/[1+\lambda(1-\alpha)/(1+\alpha)]\). At the paper defaults \(\alpha=0.98\), \(\lambda=2\), the filter triples DC gain while shrinking the ordinary gradient-descent stability margin by only about \(1.98\%\).\n
