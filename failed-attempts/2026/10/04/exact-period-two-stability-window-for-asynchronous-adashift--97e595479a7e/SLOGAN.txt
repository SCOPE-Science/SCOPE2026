One-step asynchronous AdaShift has an exact nonzero sign-flip cycle on a scalar quadratic, and its stability is controlled solely by the normalized ratio \(\alpha\lambda/\varepsilon\): the cycle is stable exactly for \(2\varepsilon<\alpha\lambda<4\varepsilon\). With \(\varepsilon=0\), the cycle is always unstable and its two-step Floquet multipliers have exact modulus \(2-\beta_2\).
