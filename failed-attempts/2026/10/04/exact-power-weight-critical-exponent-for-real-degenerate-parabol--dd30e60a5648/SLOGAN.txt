For real degenerate parabolic operators with power weight \(|x|^\beta\), the lower resolvent exponent is exactly \(p_-=2n/(2n+\beta)\) for negative \(\beta\) and \(p_-=1\) for nonnegative \(\beta\). The parabolic Sobolev threshold \(2_*\) occurs at the exact critical power \(\beta=-n^2/(n+2)\), even though that boundary weight fails the source's endpoint reverse-Hölder hypothesis.
