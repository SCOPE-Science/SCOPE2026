# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The direct order-map classification is correct. Maps with the two minima equal, with a maximal image at a minimum-domain point, or with a constant maximal-level restriction are comparable to constants and hence lie in one null component. If the minima are permuted and the maximal-level map is nonconstant, any pointwise comparable self-map must agree coordinatewise, so the map is isolated. Such a map sends a four-edge K_{2,q} cycle with two distinct maximal images to a nonzero four-cycle; because the order complex is one-dimensional there are no 2-boundaries, so H1 action is nonzero. Counting the four order-map forms gives the stated total, isolated and null-component sizes. The frozen q=2,3,4 enumeration independently checks components and H1 action.
