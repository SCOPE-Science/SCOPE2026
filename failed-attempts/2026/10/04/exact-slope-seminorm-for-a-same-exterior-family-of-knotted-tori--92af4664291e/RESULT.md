# Exact slope seminorm for a same-exterior family of knotted tori
## Finding
Let \(N\ge2\) and let \(\widehat T_{N,r}\subset S^4\) be a core torus obtained from Nifa's filling construction, with \(r\ge0\) and
\[
\gcd(N,6r-1)=1.
\]
Write \((\lambda_r,s_r)\) for the natural tangent-coordinate basis on the core torus. Then the induced slope seminorm of Liu--Ni--Sun--Wang is
\[
\left\|a[\lambda_r]+b[s_r]\right\|_{\widehat T_{N,r}}
=
\frac{N-1}{2N}|a-Nb|
\qquad ((a,b)\in\mathbb R^2).
\]
Consequently,
\[
\ker\|\cdot\|_{\widehat T_{N,r}}
=
\mathbb R\bigl(N[\lambda_r]+[s_r]\bigr),
\]
and the unit ball is the rational strip
\[
|a-Nb|\le\frac{2N}{N-1}.
\]

There is also an exact unstable statement for primitive integral slopes on the index-\(N\) sublattice. If \((a,b)\in\mathbb Z^2\) is primitive and
\[
a-Nb=Nq\ne0,
\]
then the singular slope genus is
\[
g_{\mathrm{sing}}\bigl(a[\lambda_r]+b[s_r]\bigr)
=
1+\left\lceil\frac{|q|(N-1)}2\right\rceil.
\]

For \(N=2k\), Nifa's Theorem 1.1 supplies \(N\) pairwise ambient-homeomorphism-inequivalent tori with one smooth exterior. Under their natural tangent-coordinate identifications, every member has the same induced seminorm above. Thus the complete stable slope seminorm does not distinguish the members of these same-exterior families, even though their normal meridian classes do.

## Assumptions and scope
The result uses the induced seminorm defined by Liu--Ni--Sun--Wang from stable normalized singular slope complexity. Their Remark 4.5 identifies this seminorm on an integral slope with stable commutator length of the corresponding element of the exterior group.

Nifa's exterior is denoted \(E_N\), with group \(\Gamma_N=\pi_1(E_N)\). The relevant filling is standard \(S^4\) when \(\gcd(N,6r-1)=1\). The tangent coordinate circles satisfy, in \(\Gamma_N\),
\[
\lambda_r\longmapsto\lambda,
\qquad
s_r\longmapsto\lambda^{-N}.
\]
Nifa also proves that \(\lambda\) is a commutator in \(\Gamma_N\), so every tangent slope lies in the commutator subgroup and stable commutator length is finite.

The final assertion about \(N\) inequivalent members uses the even case \(N=2k\) and \(0\le r<N\) from Nifa's Theorem 1.1.

## Proof
For an integral tangent class
\[
\alpha=a[\lambda_r]+b[s_r],
\]
the peripheral homomorphism gives
\[
[\alpha]\longmapsto\lambda^a(\lambda^{-N})^b
=
\lambda^{a-Nb}
\quad\text{in }\Gamma_N.
\]
Liu--Ni--Sun--Wang identify the induced seminorm with stable commutator length, hence
\[
\|\alpha\|_{\widehat T_{N,r}}
=
\operatorname{scl}_{\Gamma_N}(\lambda^{a-Nb}).
\]

Nifa's Theorem 6.5 sets \(z=\lambda^N\) and proves
\[
\operatorname{scl}_{\Gamma_N}(z)=\frac{N-1}{2}.
\]
Stable commutator length is homogeneous, so
\[
N\operatorname{scl}_{\Gamma_N}(\lambda)
=
\operatorname{scl}_{\Gamma_N}(\lambda^N)
=
\frac{N-1}{2}.
\]
Therefore
\[
\operatorname{scl}_{\Gamma_N}(\lambda)
=
\frac{N-1}{2N},
\]
and another use of homogeneity gives
\[
\operatorname{scl}_{\Gamma_N}(\lambda^{a-Nb})
=
\frac{N-1}{2N}|a-Nb|.
\]
This proves the formula on the integral lattice. Liu--Ni--Sun--Wang prove that the stable function extends uniquely to a continuous seminorm on \(H_1(T^2;\mathbb R)\), so the same linear-absolute-value formula holds for every real pair \((a,b)\).

The kernel and unit ball follow immediately. The kernel condition is \(a-Nb=0\), generated by the primitive integral class \(N[\lambda_r]+[s_r]\).

For the unstable sublattice statement, suppose \((a,b)\in\mathbb Z^2\) is primitive and \(a-Nb=Nq\ne0\). Then the boundary slope represents
\[
\lambda^{Nq}=z^q.
\]
Nifa's Lemma 6.6 identifies singular slope genus with commutator length, and Theorem 6.5 gives
\[
\operatorname{cl}_{\Gamma_N}(z^q)
=
1+\left\lceil\frac{|q|(N-1)}2\right\rceil.
\]
This is the claimed exact genus.

Finally, the tangent curves \(\lambda_r,s_r\) have the same images \(\lambda,\lambda^{-N}\) for every filling index \(r\). Hence the displayed marked seminorm is independent of \(r\). When \(N=2k\), Nifa's Theorem 1.1 proves that the \(N\) tori with \(0\le r<N\) are pairwise inequivalent under ambient homeomorphisms, completing the final assertion.

## Verification
The critical source statements were checked directly in the full texts.

Nifa's Proposition 2.3 gives the peripheral map
\[
\mu\mapsto\mu,\qquad \lambda\mapsto\lambda,\qquad s\mapsto\lambda^{-N},
\]
and the filling section states that the tangent curves \(\lambda_r,s_r\) map to \(\lambda,\lambda^{-N}\). The same paper proves \(H_1(E_N)\cong\mathbb Z\langle\mu\rangle\), so tangent slopes are null-homologous. Theorem 6.5 proves the exact commutator length of every nonzero power of \(z=\lambda^N\) and the stable value \((N-1)/2\); its proof also explicitly realizes \(\lambda\) as one commutator. Lemma 6.6 identifies singular genus with commutator length for a null-homologous boundary circle.

Liu--Ni--Sun--Wang's Definition 4.4 and Remark 4.5 identify their induced slope seminorm with stable commutator length and extend it to \(H_1(T^2;\mathbb R)\).

## Relationship to prior work
Liu--Ni--Sun--Wang introduced the slope seminorm and observed that it equals stable commutator length in the exterior group. They study several constructions and ask structural questions about the unit disk, but their paper predates this exterior family.

Nifa constructs the same-exterior tori, records the full peripheral homomorphism, and computes exact commutator length for powers of the central element \(z=\lambda^N\). The paper states the exact singular genus only for the specified surgery direction \(s_r\). It does not state the induced seminorm on all tangent classes, does not contain the term “seminorm”, and does not identify its kernel or unit ball.

The new step is to combine the full peripheral map with homogeneity of stable commutator length. This collapses every tangent class to one exponent \(a-Nb\), converting the one central stable value into a complete two-dimensional slope-seminorm formula. The resulting formula also shows that this entire stable invariant is identical across Nifa's pairwise inequivalent members.

## Limitations
The exact unstable singular-genus formula is proved here only for primitive integral slopes satisfying \(a-Nb\in N\mathbb Z\), because Nifa's exact commutator-length theorem concerns powers of \(z=\lambda^N\). The stable seminorm formula, by contrast, is valid on all of \(H_1(T^2;\mathbb R)\).

The seminorm is degenerate: it has a one-dimensional kernel. No claim is made about embedded slope genus, only the induced stable seminorm and the stated singular-genus sublattice.

Equality of the marked seminorms does not imply equivalence of the tori; Nifa's meridian obstruction proves the opposite for the even-\(N\) family.
