In the ferromagnetic XYZ dimer, the finite-temperature zero-concurrence region around the DM quantum critical point has exact analytic edges \(D_-(T)\) and \(D_+(T)\). As \(T\downarrow0\), its width is \((2\nu_c/D_c)T e^{-2A/T}(1+o(1))\), so thermal broadening is exponentially narrow and asymptotically symmetric to leading order.
