In \(2\times2\) matrices, Adafactor's factored second-moment error is exactly \(\mathcal P(V)-V=(\det V/\sum V)\begin{bmatrix}-1&1\\1&-1\end{bmatrix}\). Row and column marginals alone admit no finite coordinatewise multiplicative approximation to the full second moment, and identical compressed histories can hide arbitrarily different adaptive denominators.
