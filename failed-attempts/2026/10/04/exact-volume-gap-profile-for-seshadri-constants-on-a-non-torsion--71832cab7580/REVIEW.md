## correctness

PASS

Fuentes García's Theorem 9 gives exactly \(\varepsilon(A,x)=(2n(n+1)b+a)/(2n+1)\) with \(n=\lfloor\sqrt{a/(2b)}\rfloor\), and the standard volume bound is \(\varepsilon(A,x)\le\sqrt{A^2}\) with \(A^2=2ab\). Expanding and refactoring gives the claimed product \((a-2bn^2)(2b(n+1)^2-a)/(2n+1)^2\). On a fixed chamber \(n^2\le t=a/(2b)<(n+1)^2\), dividing by \(A^2\) reduces the sharp relative-gap bound to the elementary product inequality maximized at \(t=n(n+1)\). Equality with the volume bound occurs exactly at square slopes. A fresh exact rational replay across 3,481 integer pairs independently reproduced the factorization and equality conditions; the infinite conclusion rests on the algebra, not the finite replay.

Under an implication-based originality standard, the volume-gap profile is mechanically contained in the source even though the source does not print the factored expression.
