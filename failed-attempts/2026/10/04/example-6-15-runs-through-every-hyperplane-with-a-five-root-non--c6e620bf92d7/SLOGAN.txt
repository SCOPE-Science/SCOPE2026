In Bartoli--Grimaldi--Stănică Example 6.15, the \(21\) singular image codes are exactly the \(21\) hyperplanes of \(\operatorname{PG}(2,4)\), via \([y]\mapsto \rho=y^{15}\). The five non-LCD cases are precisely the trace-zero normals, equivalently the roots of \(\rho^5+\rho^4+1=(\rho^2+\rho+1)(\rho^3+\rho+1)\).
