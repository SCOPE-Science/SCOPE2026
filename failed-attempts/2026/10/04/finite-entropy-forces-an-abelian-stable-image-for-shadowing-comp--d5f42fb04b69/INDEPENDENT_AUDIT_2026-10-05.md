# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Peng's stable-image reduction gives a surjective shadowing restriction beta on K. If K is nonabelian, its connected semisimple derived part is nontrivial; Peng's theorem and Remark 5.10 produce a one-sided or two-sided full-shift factor on a nontrivial simple connected compact Lie group H. Choosing m distinct points of H gives m^n n-step separated words, so the shift entropy is at least log m for every m and therefore infinite. Entropy monotonicity under restriction and factors then gives h_top(alpha)=infinity. The argument works through metrizable Lie-group factors even if the ambient compact group is nonmetrizable.

## originality

FAIL

Peng's full primary preprint already proves the stable-image reduction and, in Remark 5.10, states that the semisimple quotient under shadowing is topologically conjugate to a product of one-sided and two-sided full shifts on simple connected compact Lie groups. If the stable image is nonabelian, one such nontrivial shift component exists. A full shift on an infinite compact Lie alphabet has infinite topological entropy by the standard separated-word argument, and entropy is monotone under factors/restrictions. Thus the submitted finite-entropy obstruction is a direct standard corollary of the published structural theorem even though Peng does not spell out the word 'entropy'.

## value

FAIL

The entropy conclusion is useful as an interpretation of Peng's theorem, but after the source explicitly identifies every nonabelian shadowing component with a full shift, infinite entropy is a textbook one-paragraph consequence. Under the required bar excluding corollaries and routine deductions, it is not an independently valuable scientific result.
