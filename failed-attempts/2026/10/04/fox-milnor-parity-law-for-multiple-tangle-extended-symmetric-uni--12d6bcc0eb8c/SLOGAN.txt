For a Kitano--Nakae multiple-tangle extended symmetric union, the classical Fox--Milnor slice obstruction depends only on \(\prod_i\Delta_{N(T_i)}\): the squared partial-knot factor cannot change it. Repeating any non-norm numerator tangle gives an exact odd/even law, and the explicit \(5_2\)-numerator tangle obstructs sliceness for every odd number of repeated regions.
