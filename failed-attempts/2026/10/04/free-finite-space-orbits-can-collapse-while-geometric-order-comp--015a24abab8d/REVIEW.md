# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The source poset has one orbit at each level \(-1,0,\ldots,r\), and its vertical relations make the finite orbit exactly the total chain, hence contractible after realization. Barmak--Minian explicitly give the equivariant deformation retract to the two-level subposet \(Y\); its order complex is a graph with two vertex orbits and \(r+1\) edge orbits under left translation, so the geometric quotient is two vertices joined by \(r+1\) parallel edges and has first Betti number \(r\). Point freeness is immediate from left translation. The local neighborhood of a level-one point contains two distinct bottom points in the same orbit, proving the orbit map is not locally injective and hence not a finite-poset covering.

## originality

FAIL

The 2009 primary paper already defines exactly this poset and action, gives the same two-level equivariant deformation retract, and computes the source graph from its vertices and generator edges. Taking orbit sets of the displayed levels and edges immediately produces respectively the chain and the two-vertex \((r+1)\)-edge multigraph. Thus the quotient mismatch is a short calculation inside an already fully described equivariant model; the later covering paper supplies the standard covering framework. Under implication-level originality, lack of the comparison sentence in the source is not enough.

## value

PASS

The example cleanly marks a genuine conceptual boundary: freeness on points in finite Alexandrov spaces does not imply a covering action and can make the finite quotient disagree drastically with the quotient of the geometric realization. That counterexample is mathematically motivated even though, in this specific published construction, its derivation is too direct to pass originality.

The dated certificate retains the supplied scientific assessment, sources and limitations.
