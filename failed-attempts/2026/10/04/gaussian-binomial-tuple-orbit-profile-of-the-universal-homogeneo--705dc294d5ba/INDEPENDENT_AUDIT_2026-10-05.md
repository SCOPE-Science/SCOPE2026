# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For an ordered tuple \(v\), its linear-relation kernel \(K_v\subseteq\mathbb F_q^n\) and pulled-back symmetric trilinear form on \(\mathbb F_q^n/K_v\) determine the induced finite cubic space on the tuple span. Ultrahomogeneity extends an isomorphism of these spans to a global automorphism, and universality realizes every such finite quotient form. Counting codimension-\(r\) kernels by \({n\brack r}_q\) and symmetric trilinear forms by \(q^{\binom{r+2}{3}}\) yields the formula. A fresh independent computation reproduced the submitted \(q=5\) values \(1,6,656,9785156,95368955582656,2910383120155532786132656\).

## originality

FAIL

Harman--Snowden Proposition 5.10 is decisive prior coverage for the substantive orbit classification: for linearly independent \(n\)-tuples, their classifying map is exactly the pulled-back tensor structure, is surjective by universality, and its fibers are exactly automorphism orbits by homogeneity. Their positive-characteristic remark states that the same theorem holds when the characteristic exceeds the tensor degree, which covers the submitted \(p>3\) regime. Neretin independently states the finite-field universal cubic space and transitivity on embeddings of each finite cubic space. Passing from an arbitrary tuple to its quotient by the kernel of coordinate relations and then summing the number of kernels is a routine linear-algebra extension of the published classifying-map theorem. Under the audit's implication rule, the final orbit-profile formula is therefore covered.

Exact orbit profiles of oligomorphic groups are intrinsically natural, but here the classification and the count are a routine specialization of a published complete classifying map: quotient by the linear-relation kernel, count subspaces, and count tensor coefficients. Under the common value bar this is not a remaining structural gap or a nontrivial new classification.
