For the double star \(D_{a,b}\) with \(a,b\ge2\), the general position sets split exactly by which centers are selected, giving \(\psi(D_{a,b};x)=(1+x)^{a+b}+x(1+x)^a+x(1+x)^b+(a+b+1)x^2\). The all-leaf set is the unique maximum general position set, and the graph has exactly \(a+b+4\) inclusion-maximal general position sets.
