For the Boolean lattice \(B_d\), the generic \(B_d\)-ultrametric is \(\mathbb N^d\) with Hamming-support distance and automorphism group \(S_\infty^d\). Its all-tuple orbit profile is \(B_n^d\), while its injective profile is the classical Pittel–Canfield count of \(d\)-tuples of partitions with discrete meet.
