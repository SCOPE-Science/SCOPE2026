On a connected complete multipartite graph, every independent double Roman function is supported on one entire part, uses only labels \(2\) and \(3\) there, and uses label \(0\) everywhere else. Hence \(\mathcal I_G(z)=\sum_i z^{2n_i}(1+z)^{n_i}-s z^2\), where \(s\) is the number of singleton parts.
