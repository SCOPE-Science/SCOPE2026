For \(c\)-crossing 2-bridge knots, the average first-Betti cost of requiring a spanning surface to be orientable is \(c/6+1/18+o(1)\). More strongly, for every \(0<\delta<1/6\), a lower asymptotic proportion at least \((1/6-\delta)/(1-\delta)\) has orientability penalty at least \(\delta c\); at \(\delta=1/12\) this gives \(1/11\).
