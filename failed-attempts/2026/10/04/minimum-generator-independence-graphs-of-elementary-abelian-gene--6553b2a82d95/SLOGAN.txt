For \(G=\mathbf F_p^t\rtimes C_2\) with inversion, \(p\) odd and \(t\ge2\), the minimum-generator independence graph is exactly a reflection clique joined to the complete multipartite graph on nonzero translations. Its degrees are \(2p^t-p\) on nonzero translations and \(2p^t-2\) on reflections, so Lucchini's order-divides-degree question has an affirmative answer for this nonnilpotent family of unbounded generator rank.
