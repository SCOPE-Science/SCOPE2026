# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Ideals correspond bijectively to nonempty proper supports and adjacency is nonempty support intersection. A two-vertex total dominating set must be adjacent and must cover every coordinate, because an uncovered singleton-support ideal would meet neither selected ideal. Conversely an intersecting pair whose union is all coordinates dominates every nonempty support and its two selected vertices dominate each other. Such a two-set is automatically paired. Counting ordered pairs amounts to surjections of the r coordinates onto the three nonempty classes A\B, B\A and A intersect B, giving 3^r-3*2^r+3, then dividing by the fixed-point-free swap. The r=2 graph is two isolated vertices. The frozen verifier exhausts r<=7 and agrees with this all-r proof.

FAIL

A published SCOPE theorem on finite commutative principal ideal rings strictly contains this result. Setting every local nilpotency length ell_i=1 in that theorem makes the ring a product of fields, gives the identical existence boundary, pair criterion, gamma_t=gamma_pr=2 for r>=3, and specializes its count to (3^r-3*2^r+3)/2. Thus the entire final claim is a direct parameter specialization of prior published coverage.
