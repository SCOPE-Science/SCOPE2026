For \(R\cong\prod_{i=1}^{r}F_i\), the ideal-intersection graph has no total or paired dominating set when \(r=2\), but for every \(r\ge3\) both minimum sizes are \(2\). The minimum sets are exactly the pairs of proper supports that intersect and cover all \(r\) coordinates, so there are \(\bigl(3^r-3\cdot2^r+3\bigr)/2\) of them.
