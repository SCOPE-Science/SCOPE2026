For smooth bidegree-\((a,b)\) complete-intersection surfaces in \(\mathbb P^4\) with fixed \(a+b=S\ge6\), the largest absolute signature is not attained at exact balance: even \(S\) has a unique gap-two maximizer, while odd \(S\) has an exact gap-one/gap-three tie. Equivalently, the sharp signature extremizers at fixed canonical-polarization exponent are completely classified.
