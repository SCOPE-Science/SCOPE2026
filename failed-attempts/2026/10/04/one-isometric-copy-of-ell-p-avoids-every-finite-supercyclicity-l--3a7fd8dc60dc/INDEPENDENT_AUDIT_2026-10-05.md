# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The coordinate-replication formula gives the exact identity \(\|T_\lambda x\|_p^p=\|\lambda\|_p^p\|x\|_p^p\), so each nonzero operator is injective and bounded below, hence has closed range. On every output block \(M_i\) the range is the one-dimensional pattern \(x_i\lambda\); choosing a coordinate vector outside \(\operatorname{span}\{\lambda\}\) gives infinitely many linearly independent quotient classes, so the algebraic codimension is infinite. If a finite-dimensional subspace had dense orbit, all positive iterates would vanish in the quotient and the quotient image of the orbit would be one finite-dimensional closed subspace, impossible in the infinite-dimensional Hausdorff quotient. This works for the quasi-Banach cases because only Hausdorff topological-vector-space facts are used.
