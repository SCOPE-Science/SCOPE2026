For a finite reduced commutative nonfield \(R\cong\prod_i F_i\), paired domination of the total graph is the least even integer at least \(q=\min_i|F_i|\). Thus \(\gamma_{\mathrm{pr}}=\gamma_t=q\) for even \(q\), while odd \(q\) forces the sharp one-vertex matching surcharge \(\gamma_{\mathrm{pr}}=q+1\).
