# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The package's support argument is correct: singleton-support vertices form a dominating clique of size r, the r disjoint witness-unions give the lower bound for r at least three, and the two-factor case is immediate. Fresh enumeration on small field products agrees exactly.

## originality

FAIL

For products of at least three fields, Kiani–Maimani–Nikandish's Theorem 2.4 already gives gamma_t equal to the number r of maximal associated primes and constructs pairwise-zero annihilator witnesses, hence a minimum total-dominating clique. For the two-factor exceptional regime, their preceding remark gives gamma_t=2 when gamma=1, and the zero-divisor graph visibly contains a dominating edge. Therefore the claimed value 2 ceil(r/2) is a direct parity corollary of the existing total-domination result on precisely this object.
