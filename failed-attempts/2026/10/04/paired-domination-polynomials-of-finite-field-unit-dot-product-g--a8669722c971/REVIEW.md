# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Writing a unit vector uniquely as u(1,a), adjacency between slope classes is exactly b=-a^{-1}. The fixed slopes are the solutions of a^2=-1, giving one in characteristic two, two when q is odd and congruent to one modulo four, and none when q is congruent to three modulo four. Hence the graph is precisely the published clique/biclique disjoint union. On K_m, paired-dominating sets are exactly positive even subsets; on K_{m,m}, they are exactly equal positive selections from the two sides. Multiplication over components gives the formula. Fresh independent coefficient calculations reproduced the minimum degrees and leading-degree reconstruction across q=3,4,5,7,8,9,11. The analytic component argument proves all prime powers.

## originality

FAIL

The complete graph decomposition needed for the theorem is already Theorems 2.1--2.2 of Abdulla--Badawi (2020): in characteristic two the unit dot-product graph is one K_m plus the stated K_{m,m} components, and in odd characteristic it is exactly the stated all-biclique or two-clique-plus-biclique decomposition. Once this published decomposition is known, the claimed paired-domination polynomial is the componentwise product of the elementary paired-domination polynomials of K_m and K_{m,m}; the minimum coefficient and polynomial reconstruction are immediate coefficient/degree read-offs. The exact wording is new, but the final claim is mechanically covered by stronger prior graph structure plus routine paired-domination facts.

## value

FAIL

The polynomial is a clean derived invariant, but after the exact component decomposition is already published, its derivation is a routine multiplication of elementary clique/biclique paired-domination enumerators. The minimum value, minimum coefficient, and recovery of q are simple read-offs. Under the required bar excluding textbook deductions and known-structure recomputation, this does not carry independent mathematical value.
