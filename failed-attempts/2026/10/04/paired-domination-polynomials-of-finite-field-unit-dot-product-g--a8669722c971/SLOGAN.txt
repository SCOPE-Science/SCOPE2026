For the unit dot-product graph of \(\mathbb F_q\times\mathbb F_q\), the paired-domination polynomial factors into one term for each self-orthogonal slope and one term for each paired slope orbit. This gives \(\gamma_{\mathrm{pr}}=q\) for even \(q>2\), \(q+1\) for \(q\equiv1\pmod4\), and \(q-1\) for \(q\equiv3\pmod4\), and the polynomial itself recovers \(q\).
