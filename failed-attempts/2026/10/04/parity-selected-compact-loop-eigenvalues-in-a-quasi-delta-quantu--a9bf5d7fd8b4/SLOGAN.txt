In a \(\mathbb{Z}\)-invariant quasi-\(\delta\) chain with one loop at every vertex, the loop relative phase is an exact parity switch for compact one-loop eigenstates: \(e^{i\alpha}=1\) keeps the even Dirichlet modes and \(e^{i\alpha}=-1\) keeps the odd ones. Each selected level occurs with countably infinite multiplicity and is independent of the quasi-\(\delta\) strength.
