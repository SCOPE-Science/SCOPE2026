For abelian group laws on \(p^a\) labeled points, polymorphism-homogeneity has the exact density \(\rho_p(a)=\sum_{m\mid a}p^{-(m-1)a}\prod_{j=m+1}^a(1-p^{-j})\). As \(a\to\infty\), this converges to the positive constant \(\prod_{j=2}^\infty(1-p^{-j})\), so the homocyclic-Sylow condition remains visible at positive labeled density.\n
