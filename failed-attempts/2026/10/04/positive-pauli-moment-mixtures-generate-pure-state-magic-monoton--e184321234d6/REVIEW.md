# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Leone–Bittel's prior theorem states that stabilizer entropies are monotone for pure-state magic resource theory at every order at least two. With the submitted normalization, the negative Rényi prefactor converts that theorem exactly into coordinatewise inequalities \(P_m(\psi)\le P_m(\phi)\) for a deterministic pure-state conversion. Nonnegative summation preserves these inequalities and the decreasing dependence of \(\log(C/G)\) on \(G\) gives the claimed monotonicity direction. Faithfulness follows because equality at any positively weighted order above one forces every Pauli expectation magnitude to be zero or one; Parseval then gives a maximal stabilizer group. Expanding \(\cosh(\beta x)-1\) termwise yields the stated stabilizer-work coefficients and normalization.

## originality

FAIL

The arbitrary-mixture statement is a routine closure consequence of Leone–Bittel's already established coordinate monotones: if every \(P_m\) has the required order relation, any nonnegative summable linear combination has the same relation, and composing with the stated logarithm is elementary. The hyperbolic-cosine member is even already asserted in Salazar et al. as stabilizer work. Therefore the final claim is implication-covered despite the absence of an earlier theorem phrased as an 'arbitrary positive-mixture cone'.

Under the required value standard, closure of a known coordinatewise family under nonnegative summation and a monotone scalar transform is a routine resource-theory exercise rather than a motivated new mathematical gap. The equality-case faithfulness argument is useful bookkeeping but does not elevate the routine closure into an independently valuable theorem.
