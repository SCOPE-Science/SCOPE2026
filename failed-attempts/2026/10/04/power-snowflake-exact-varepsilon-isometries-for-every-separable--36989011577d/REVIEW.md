# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The free-space Dirac distance is exactly max(r,a^(1-alpha) r^alpha). Its positive additive defect has a unique critical point and maximum K_alpha a, giving the stated normalization and equality radius. The gauge is nontrivial, so Kalton's theorem gives a Schur target; separability is inherited. Any isometric embedding of the separable source would linearize, contradicting that a subspace of a Schur space is Schur. All boundaries in alpha are stated correctly.

## originality

FAIL

Sun–Zhang's complete proof already isolates all ingredients in general form: Kalton's Schur theorem is stated for every metric space and every nontrivial gauge, and Godefroy–Kalton linearization is stated for every separable Banach source. Their only source-specific choices are X=ell_2 and the square-root gauge. Replacing the source by an arbitrary separable non-Schur space and replacing the square root by the power gauge max(t,t^alpha) requires only the same proof plus a one-variable maximization. This is a direct parametric abstraction of the published construction rather than an independent mechanism.

## value

FAIL

The sharp scalar constant is useful bookkeeping, but the theorem is obtained by substituting a power gauge into the already published construction and replacing ell_2 by the exact abstract property the published contradiction uses. Under the common bar this is a routine generalization rather than a new structural result.

The dated certificate retains the supplied scientific assessment, sources and limitations.
