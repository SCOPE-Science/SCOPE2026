For every fixed \(d\) and integer \(r\ge2\), the \(r\)-th BHEP spectral power sum satisfies \(\sum_j\lambda_j(\beta,d)^r\sim r^{-d}\beta^{-d(r-1)}\) as \(\beta\to\infty\). Consequently the standardized excess kurtosis is \(12\beta^{-d}(1+o(1))\), and the limiting null law admits a Berry--Esseen bound of order \(O_d(\beta^{-d/2})\).
