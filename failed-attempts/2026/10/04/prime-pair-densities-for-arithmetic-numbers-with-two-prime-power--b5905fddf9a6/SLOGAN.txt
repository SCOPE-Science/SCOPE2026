For fixed primes \(\ell<m\), arithmetic numbers of the form \(P^{\ell-1}Q^{m-1}\) have an exact prime-pair density, and when \(\ell\mid m-1\) a positive-density subfamily is arithmetic even though \(Q^{m-1}\) is not. The extra mass is exactly \((m-2)/(m-1)^2\), coming from primes \(P\) whose residue has order \(\ell\) modulo \(m\).
