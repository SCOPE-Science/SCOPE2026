For every prime \(p\) and \(d\ge3\), the join graph of \(C_p^d\) has no accidental symmetries: \(\operatorname{Aut}(\Delta(C_p^d))\cong\operatorname{PGL}(d,p)\), and the graph determines \(p\) and \(d\) within the elementary-abelian family. In rank three its characteristic polynomial is \(x(x^2-(p^2+p)x-p^4)(x^2+x-p)^{p^2+p}\).
