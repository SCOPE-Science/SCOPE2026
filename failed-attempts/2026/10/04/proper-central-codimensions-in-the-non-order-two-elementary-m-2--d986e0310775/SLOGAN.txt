For the non-order-two elementary \(G\)-gradings of \(M_2(F)\) with either admissible graded involution, the proper central codimension is exactly \(\binom{2n}{n}-2^{n-1}\). Equivalently, it is the preceding total \((G,*)\)-codimension, so proper central classes asymptotically occupy one quarter of the multilinear quotient.
