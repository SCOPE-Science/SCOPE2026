For two natural max-norm deformations of the Euclidean plane, the Ptolemy constant is exactly the square of the Euclidean Banach--Mazur distance. Concretely, \(d_{\mathrm{BM}}(A_\lambda,\ell_2^2)^2=2\lambda^2\) and \(d_{\mathrm{BM}}(B_\lambda,\ell_2^2)^2=\lambda^2\), so arbitrary linear reparametrization cannot beat the obvious coordinate comparison.
