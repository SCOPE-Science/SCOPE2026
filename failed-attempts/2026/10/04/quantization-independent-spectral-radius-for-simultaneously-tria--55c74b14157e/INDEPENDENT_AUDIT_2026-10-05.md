The similarity characterization converts the radius into the infimum of matrix norm over scalar similarities. In a common upper-triangular basis, diagonal scaling suppresses all strict upper entries and yields the upper bound by Ruan's direct-sum axiom. A norming scalar functional on the Banach space is completely bounded with the same norm; after amplification, the resulting scalar upper-triangular matrix retains the selected diagonal value as an eigenvalue under every similarity, proving the reverse bound. Lie's theorem gives the solvable-Lie corollary.

## originality

FAIL

The statement is a direct corollary of already-published general machinery plus standard triangular-matrix facts. The 2025 similarity characterization applies to arbitrary matrix tuples, not merely commuting ones. Once a tuple is simultaneously triangularized, the standard diagonal contraction and a norming functional give the exact diagonal-character formula immediately; Lie's theorem then turns simultaneous triangularizability into the solvable-Lie formulation. The 2026 paper's headline theorem stops at commuting tuples, but absence of the word 'triangularizable' does not make this routine implication original.
