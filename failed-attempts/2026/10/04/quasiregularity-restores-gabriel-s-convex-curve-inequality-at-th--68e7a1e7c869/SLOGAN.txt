Harmonic \(K\)-quasiregularity restores the full convex-curve Gabriel inequality at the excluded endpoint \(p=1\): every \(\mathbf h^1\) map in this class has an \(L^1\) boundary trace and obeys an explicit \(K\)-dependent curve bound. This gives a concrete positive answer to the \(p=1\) part of the endpoint question posed for quasiregular Gabriel estimates.
