Off-center circular numerical ranges for finite-dimensional partial isometries have an exact rank threshold: they are impossible in ranks at most \(3\), but occur at every rank \(r\ge4\). A concrete construction realizes rank \(r\) in every dimension \(n\ge6+\lceil3(r-4)/2\rceil\) by adjoining small centered Jordan-block discs to the rank-four counterexample.
