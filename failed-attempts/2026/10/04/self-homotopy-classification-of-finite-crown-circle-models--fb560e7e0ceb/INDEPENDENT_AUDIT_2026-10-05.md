Lifting a crown self-map to the infinite fence gives increments in \(\{-1,0,1\}\), so the degree lies in \(\{-1,0,1\}\). Thus the image omits a point and factors through a finite fence, which is contractible by beat-point deletion. Minimality of the crown makes distinct automorphisms nonhomotopic.
