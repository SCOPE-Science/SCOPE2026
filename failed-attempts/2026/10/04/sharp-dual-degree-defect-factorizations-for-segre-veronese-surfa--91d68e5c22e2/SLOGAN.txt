For the Segre--Veronese surface \(X_{a,b}\subset\mathbb P^{(a+1)(b+1)-1}\), the dual degree is \(6ab-4a-4b+4\), and its deviations from the lower and upper fixed-degree envelopes factor exactly as \(4(a-1)(b-1)\) and \(4(\sqrt b-\sqrt a)^2\). Thus fixed embedded degree favors the divisor pair closest to a square.
