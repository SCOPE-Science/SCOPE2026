# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The class-size proof is correct. Embeddings inject equivalence classes into distinct target classes of at least the same size. In the bounded regime with finitely many infinite classes, mutual embeddings determine the infinite-class count, the largest finite size occurring infinitely often, and every finite tail multiplicity above it; lower classes can be absorbed by infinitely many copies of that maximal size. The unbounded and infinitely-many-infinite-class constructions give continuum many pairwise nonisomorphic siblings. The three regimes are exhaustive.

## originality

FAIL

The final trichotomy is already covered as a direct specialization/synthesis of published sibling theory. Braunfeld--Laskowski prove directly that a cellular non-finitely-partitioned structure has aleph0 siblings and a finitely partitioned one has one sibling; their paper explicitly uses equivalence relations with infinitely many infinite classes as continuum-sibling examples and explicitly notes that arbitrarily large finite classes also give continuum siblings. For a pure equivalence relation, bounded finite class sizes with finitely many infinite classes are cellular by grouping the finitely many repeated class sizes into cellular blocks, and finite partitionability is exactly the case of only finitely many nonsingleton classes. Thus the submitted three class-size cases are a routine translation of the published dividing-line results. Bazhenov et al. independently supply the underlying unbounded-character bi-embeddability criterion.
