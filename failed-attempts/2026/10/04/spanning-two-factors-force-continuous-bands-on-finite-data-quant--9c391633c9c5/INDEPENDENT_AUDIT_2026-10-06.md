# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The extension is mathematically correct. The inverse image of a spanning two-factor has degree two at every lifted vertex; because the universal cover is a tree, each component is a bi-infinite line. The lifted subgraph is invariant under deck transformations and contains every vertex. In the primary quantum-tree proof, the finite Hamiltonian cycle is used only to create precisely such a full invariant line ensemble; the subsequent labeling and von Neumann dimension argument depend on that ensemble rather than on connectedness of the base two-factor. The source's independent spectral-bottom argument gives a point below the least Dirichlet value, so the eigenvalue obstruction forces a nonisolated spectral point and hence a nonempty absolutely continuous band.

## originality

FAIL

The primary source already defines the load-bearing hypothesis at the universal-cover level as an invariant line ensemble containing the root almost surely, and its Proposition 4.2 proof explicitly uses a Hamiltonian cycle only to manufacture that ensemble before invoking the line-ensemble labeling. A spanning two-factor lifts, by a routine covering-space observation, to exactly the same kind of full invariant line ensemble. Thus the audited statement is a direct sufficient-condition extraction from the published proof mechanism rather than a new quantum-spectral theorem.

## value

FAIL

The Petersen example is useful exposition, but the only new step is the elementary fact that a spanning two-factor lifts to a deck-invariant union of bi-infinite lines. Once the source has isolated the line-ensemble mechanism, this is a routine sufficient-condition observation rather than an independently substantial mathematical gap.
