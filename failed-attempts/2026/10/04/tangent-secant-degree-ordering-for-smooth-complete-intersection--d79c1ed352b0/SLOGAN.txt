For every smooth complete-intersection curve in \(\mathbb P^N_{\mathbb C}\) with \(N\ge4\) and all defining degrees at least two, the secant degree exceeds the tangent degree except for the three types \((2,2,2)\), \((2,2,3)\), and \((2,2,4)\) in \(\mathbb P^4\). The unique equality case is \((2,2,4)\), where both degrees are \(80\).
