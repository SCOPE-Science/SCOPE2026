For a bounded multiplication \(m\), the norm gap \(\Delta_A(m)=\lVert m^{\square}-m^{\diamond}\rVert\) changes by at most twice the multiplication perturbation norm, so every Arens-irregular algebra has an explicit regularity exclusion radius \(\Delta_A(m)/2\). The factor \(2\) is exact, already for a cube-zero Banach algebra on \(\ell_1\oplus_1\ell_1\oplus_1\mathbb C\).
