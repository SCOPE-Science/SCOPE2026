The degree-six axis-signature surface \(\mathcal A_{(1,2,1),3}\) is the anticanonical model of the blow-up of \(\mathbb P^2\) at three successive infinitely-near points. Its singular locus consists exactly of one \(A_1\) point and one \(A_2\) point, corresponding to an isolated \((-2)\)-curve and a two-curve \(A_2\) chain in the resolution.
