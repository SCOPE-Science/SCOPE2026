At each vertex the normalized defect is E=x^2+y^2+z^2-sqrt(2)y(x+z), and exact expansion gives 2E=(x-z)^2+(x+z-sqrt(2)y)^2. Nonnegative vertex defects imply the global inequality. Interior equality forces every vertex defect to vanish and cyclically forces all half-arc gaps to pi/4; the square converse is immediate.
