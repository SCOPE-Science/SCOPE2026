# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The containment argument is correct. Membership of an extension class in the span of an effective divisor D of degree g minus 1 means the pullback along L(-D) to L splits. Hence L(-D) lifts into the extension bundle. Since deg L(-D)=g, Riemann--Roch gives a nonzero section, and its lift is independent of the canonical section from O_X because their images in L differ. Thus every point of the span has at least two sections. The cohomology dimensions h1(L inverse)=3g-2 and h1(L inverse(D))=2g-1 give projective span dimension g-2.

## originality

FAIL

The same primary paper already identifies the degree-(g-1) secant locus with the unstable-extension locus and defines the hypersurface by the condition h0(E) at least two. For a rank-two bundle of degree 2g-1, instability supplies a line subbundle of degree at least g; Riemann--Roch then gives a nonzero section of that line subbundle, which is automatically independent of the canonical O_X section in the extension. Therefore the submitted containment is a direct textbook corollary of the published same-paper lemma and definitions, even though Remark 3.8 says the authors did not know whether a P3 lies in the genus-five hypersurface.

## value

FAIL

Pointing out the overlooked consequence is editorially useful, but mathematically the result is a short textbook Riemann--Roch deduction from a lemma in the same paper. Under the common value bar, this does not survive as an independent research finding despite resolving the paper's stated uncertainty.
