The data-sharing stock in the published stochastic game is a square-root diffusion with exact noncentral-chi-square transitions and a Gamma stationary law, not a Normal state variable. In the paper's Nash benchmark the printed stationary 95% band is \([-13.632,61.632]\) because it uses variance rather than standard deviation; the exact Gamma equal-tail band is approximately \([16.19269922,33.31906995]\).
