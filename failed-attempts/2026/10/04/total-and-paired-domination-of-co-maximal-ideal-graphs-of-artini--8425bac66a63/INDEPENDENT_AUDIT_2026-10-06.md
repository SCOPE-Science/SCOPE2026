# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Locality reduces adjacency to union of whole-coordinate supports. The r maximal ideals have co-singleton supports, form a clique and totally dominate. The published ordinary domination value gives the sharp lower bound for r at least three, with r=2 direct. Even-cardinality and explicit matching give the paired formula.

## originality

FAIL

Kavitha and Kala already prove for this exact Artinian co-maximal ideal graph that ordinary domination equals the number of Artinian factors and explicitly identify the maximal ideals as a minimum dominating set. Distinct maximal ideals are pairwise co-maximal, so this published minimum set is a clique. Hence total domination has the same value, while paired domination is the least even size at least it, with the odd case supplied by one adjacent support vertex. Under the required implication standard, the final formulas are covered as routine corollaries even though the older paper does not use the strengthened invariant names.
