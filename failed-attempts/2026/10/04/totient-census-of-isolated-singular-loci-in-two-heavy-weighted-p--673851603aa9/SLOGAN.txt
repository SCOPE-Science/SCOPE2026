In the two-heavy family \(\mathbb P(1^r,a,b)\), zero-dimensional singular locus is exactly the coprimality condition \(\gcd(a,b)=1\). Inside the canonical and terminal triangles this yields exact counts \(3\Phi(r)\) and \(3\Phi(r-1)\), with exactly \(3\varphi(r)\) isolated-singularity members on the canonical non-terminal boundary.
