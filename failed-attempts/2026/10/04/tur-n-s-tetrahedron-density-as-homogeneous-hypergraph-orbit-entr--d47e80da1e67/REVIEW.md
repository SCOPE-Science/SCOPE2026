# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For the countable universal homogeneous tetrahedron-free 3-uniform hypergraph, an injective ordered \(n\)-tuple labels its induced structure by tuple position. Ultrahomogeneity makes two tuples automorphic exactly when these labeled induced hypergraphs agree, and universality realizes every finite labeled tetrahedron-free 3-graph. Hence the orbit count is exactly the labeled forbidden-hypergraph count. The Nagle--Rödl theorem \(\log_2\operatorname{forb}(n,F)=\operatorname{ex}(n,F)+o(n^3)\) then yields the normalized Turán-density limit. An independent exhaustive replay through six vertices reproduced counts \(1,1,1,2,15,768,477965\) and extremal edge counts \(0,0,0,1,3,7,14\).

## originality

FAIL

The final asymptotic identity is a direct synthesis of two published facts with no surviving nonstandard lemma. Siniora--Solecki explicitly identify the universal homogeneous tetrahedron-free 3-hypergraph as the Fraïssé limit of the finite tetrahedron-free age; the standard ultrahomogeneous orbit-age correspondence immediately identifies injective tuple orbits with labeled age structures. Nagle--Rödl explicitly prove the labeled forbidden-3-graph counting exponent in terms of the Turán extremal number. Combining those statements and dividing by \(\binom n3\) mechanically gives the claimed entropy limit. The absence of the exact model-theoretic wording in Resultary does not overcome this decisive implication coverage.
