# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The source norm formula reduces exactly to minimizing \(a+b\) over affine majorants of the continuous nonnegative profile \(m_f\). Since \(a+b=2L(1/2)\), the infimum is twice the least concave majorant at one-half. The concavification formula at a single barycenter is a linear program over probability measures with two moment constraints, whose extreme measures have support at at most two points, yielding the displayed two-slice maximum. Compactness gives attainment and the diagonal case is the continuous limit.

## originality

FAIL

The source's Theorem 6.2 already gives the exact free-product norm as the infimum of endpoint sums over all affine majorants of the slice profile. Passing from that affine-majorant infimum to twice the least concave majorant at one-half, and then to a two-point barycentric formula in one dimension, is standard convex/concave-envelope duality. The package is a clean reformulation and attainment certificate, but under the implication-level originality rule it is a routine equivalent form of the published norm theorem.

## value

FAIL

The two-slice certificate is convenient, but its proof is a standard concave-envelope repackaging of an exact published norm formula on exactly the same class. Under the common value bar this is a routine reformulation rather than a substantive new mathematical gap.
