For a connected chain graph whose canonical open-neighborhood twin classes all have size at least two, zero forcing is exactly the condition of leaving at most one white vertex per twin class. Thus \(\mathcal Z(G;x)=x^{N-2p}\prod_i(x+\alpha_i)(x+\beta_i)\), simultaneously giving the entire zero-forcing-set distribution, the minimum size, and the minimum-set count.
