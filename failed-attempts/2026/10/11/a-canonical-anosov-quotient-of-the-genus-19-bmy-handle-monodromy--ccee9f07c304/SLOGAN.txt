The genus-\(19\) BMY handle monodromy has a canonical rank-\(34\) symplectic homology quotient on which the action is hyperbolic, yielding an Anosov \(34\)-torus automorphism with \(17\)-dimensional stable and unstable spaces. Its entire unit-circle sector is a single nonsplit \((t^2+1)^2\) primary block, so \(i\) and \(-i\) each occur in one size-two Jordan block.
