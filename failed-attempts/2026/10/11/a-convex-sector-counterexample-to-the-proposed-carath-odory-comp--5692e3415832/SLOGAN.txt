The proposed universal positivity of Grigoriciuc's \(\alpha\)-Carathéodory component for convex univalent functions fails at \(\alpha=1/2\). A translated convex-sector map \(f(z)=\sqrt{(1+z)/(1-z)}-1\) has an explicit interior point with \(-1/2<\operatorname{Re}h_{1/2}[f](z)<-1/4\), certified by exact rational arithmetic.
