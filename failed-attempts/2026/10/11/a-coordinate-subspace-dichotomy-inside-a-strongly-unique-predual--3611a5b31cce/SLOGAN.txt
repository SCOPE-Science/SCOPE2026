In the explicit strongly unique-predual renorming of \(\ell_1\) from Aliaga--Cúth--Vico, every canonical coordinate projection has norm \(1\). Among infinite coordinate subspaces, containing coordinate \(1\) gives an isometric copy of the original strongly unique-predual space, while omitting it gives \(\ell_1\) and hence nonunique preduals; both behaviors occur continuum many times and at every positive finite codimension.
