Cube extension cannot reduce weak covering efficiency: for every convex body \(K\), adjoining an \(r\)-dimensional parallelotope multiplies \(\operatorname{coin}_{\mathrm w}\) by at least \(2^r\), and it multiplies it exactly by \(2^r\) whenever the base optimum is attained at ratio at least \(1/2\). Consequently the triangular- and affine-regular-hexagonal-prism estimates left at \(\le18\) in the introducing paper are both exactly \(18\), and their \(r\)-cube extensions have value \(9\cdot2^r\).
