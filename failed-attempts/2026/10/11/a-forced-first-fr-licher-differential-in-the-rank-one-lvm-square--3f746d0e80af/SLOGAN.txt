In the rank-one square of the new Dolbeault--Hochster theory, the first Frölicher differential \(d_1:H_{\bar\partial}^{0,1}\to H_{\bar\partial}^{1,1}\) is forced to have rank exactly \(1\). Along the source's proper holomorphic square family this rank jumps from \(0\) on every punctured fibre to \(1\) at the deficient-curvature central fibre.
