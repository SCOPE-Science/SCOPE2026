The group-level \(\operatorname{FC}(G)\setminus Z(G)\) obstruction in Remark 5.3 of arXiv:2609.26479v1 fails for nonfaithful actions: \(G=\mathbb Z\times S_3\) inherits a strong doubly minimal action from \(\mathbb Z\) by the paper's own pullback proposition. The kernel collapses the offending finite-conjugacy element to the identity, so its invariant graph is merely the diagonal and does not violate the stated self-joining condition.
