For fixed block size \(k\ge3\), common vertex-pair overlaps between independent uniform \(k\)-block partitions converge to a Poisson law, giving an explicit sharp \(\ell^1\)/\(\ell^\infty\) Fourier-projector norm growth constant for the \((kn-2,2)\) harmonic block. As the block size subsequently grows, the growth constant approaches \(1/\sqrt\pi\).
