Under uniform nilpotence of noninvertible Calkin elements, the compact-approximation coefficient forces a quantitative short-time norm jump for every \(C_0\)-semigroup with unbounded generator: \(\beta_{\mathcal K}(X)\le(\limsup_{t\downarrow0}\|T(t)\|)^m\). The qualitative Lotz criterion is exactly the infinite-coefficient endpoint of this inequality.
