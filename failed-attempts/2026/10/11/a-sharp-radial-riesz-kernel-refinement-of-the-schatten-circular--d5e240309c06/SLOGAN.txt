The sharp complex-Hessian estimate for Schatten powers admits a stronger radius-resolved form: the circular-mean defect dominates a logarithmic Riesz-kernel integral with the local envelope \(\|A\|_{\mathrm{op}}+s\|B\|_{\mathrm{op}}\). For \(0<p<2\) this strictly improves the published frozen-radius bound, and rank-one rays from the origin attain the new inequality exactly.
