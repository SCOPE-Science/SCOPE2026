# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

FAIL

For nonzero B the main formula is mathematically correct. Xu–Zhang–Liu Prop.2.2 yields ∂∂bar Fε(A+wB)≥(p²/4)(((a+b|w|)²+ε)^(p/2−1))||B||₂². In the scalar disk Green formula, (2/π) times the polar 2π times p²/4 gives p². ε↓0 is monotone for p≤2, and finite-dimensional Fε converges uniformly on the compact circle. For arbitrary H restrict A,B to the common separable reducing support generated by their compact ranges and adjoint ranges, then finite-rank compressions converge in S_p and S₂, and use ||A_N||op≤a, ||B_N||op≤b. For 0<p<2,B≠0, s<t implies (a+bs)^(p−2)>(a+bt)^(p−2), while ∫_0^t s log(t/s)ds=t²/4. At p=2 equality follows by Fourier orthogonality. For A=0 and rank-one B, p²∫_0^t s(bs)^(p−2)log(t/s)ds=t^p b^(p−2), so equality holds; independent numerical quadrature checked p=0.5,1,1.5,2 for rank-one b=2,t=1.3. HOWEVER the printed universal quantifier includes A=B=0. For each 0<p<2 the integrand is s(0)^(p−2)log(t/s), not locally finite on (0,t], while ||B||₂²=0; the product 0·∞ is undefined and the stated “if a=0 integrable” convention is not applicable when b=0. The submitted RHS therefore has no value in an expressly included case. A separate B=0 trivial case or exclusion is required. This is a boundary-definition defect rather than a counterexample with a reversed finite inequality; source unchanged because independent O and V also fail.

## originality

Then (2/π)∫_|w|<t log(t/|w|)Kε(w)dA = the exact radial bound; polar integration gives p²||B||₂²∫_0^t s(((a+bs)²+ε)^(p/2−1))log(t/s)ds. Monotone ε↓0 yields the stated RHS on B≠0 without any new structural argument. The frozen-radius Theorem 3.1 is not by itself equivalent, but the stronger pointwise published premise combined with a published identity decisively dominates the claim.
