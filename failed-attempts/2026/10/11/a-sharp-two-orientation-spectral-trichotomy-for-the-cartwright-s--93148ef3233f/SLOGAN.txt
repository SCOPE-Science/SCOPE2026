For the two cyclic orders of the three Cartwright–Steger nodal twists, the unresolved intersection parameter \(m\) has a sharp spectral trichotomy: both homological quadratic factors are unit-circle for \(|m|\le1\), exactly one is unit-circle for \(|m|\in\{2,3\}\), and for \(|m|\ge4\) both spectral radii jump to at least \(9+4\sqrt5\). The gap is sharp at \(m=\pm4\).
