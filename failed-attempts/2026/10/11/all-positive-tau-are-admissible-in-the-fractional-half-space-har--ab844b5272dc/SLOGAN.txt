For the Kijaczko--Szczukiewicz half-space remainder, the weight is \(x_d^{-\beta}(x_d/\sqrt{x_{d-1}^2+x_d^2})^\tau\), so it decreases pointwise as \(\tau\) increases. Their positive seed ranges therefore propagate to every \(\tau>0\) in both the fractional Gagliardo and Sobolev--Bregman inequalities, and the corresponding best remainder constants are nondecreasing in \(\tau\).
