On \([0,1]\), the doubling, lower-regular logarithmic measure \(d\mu(t)=\log(e/t)\,dt\) admits every subcritical Campanato-to-Morrey embedding \(L_C^{p,\lambda}\hookrightarrow L_M^{p,\lambda}\) for \(1\le p<\infty\), \(0\le\lambda<1\), but it is not upper \(1\)-regular. At \(\lambda=1\), the constant function already destroys the embedding, giving a sharp negative answer to the all-subcritical regularity question.
