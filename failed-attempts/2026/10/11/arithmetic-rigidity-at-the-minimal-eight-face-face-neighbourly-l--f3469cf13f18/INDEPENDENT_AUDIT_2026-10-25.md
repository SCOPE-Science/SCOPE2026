# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The exact stated theorem is correct under its explicit manifold, edge-incidence, face-simplicity, trivalence, face-neighbourliness and e_ij≤2 hypotheses. Eight k-gons yield 8k=2E, and trivalence gives 3V=2E, so k is a multiple of three. All 28 distinct face pairs share at least one edge, hence E≥28, so k≥7 and therefore k≥9. Euler then gives genus 2k/3−3; D=E−28=4k−28. At k=9, the face-vector is (8,36,24,3) and local surplus per face is k−7=2. When all e_ij≤2, this local surplus equals degree in the simple doubled-pair graph, hence it is 2-regular. Any simple 2-regular eight-vertex graph has cycle partition 8, 5+3 or 4+4; no other partition of 8 has parts ≥3. Existing 2026 examples certify sharpness k=9 and exhibit types 8 and 4+4. None of these constraints proves a geometric realization of 5+3 or any classification without the multiplicity bound. Independent generator enumerated 3507 labeled simple 2-regular graphs, all with those cycle partitions; that finite check is corroborative, not the universal proof.

## originality

FAIL

Implication-based comparison defeats substantive originality. The complete proposed result is a direct special-case assembly of published general polygon-side lower bounds, elementary equivelar incidence/Euler equations, and the textbook classification of finite simple 2-regular graphs. Arseneva et al. explicitly give the lower bound of d sides for adjacency degree d; for complete eight-face adjacency this gives k≥7. Röst–Vígh explicitly use 3V=2E and face-adjacency counts. Combining with 8k=2E makes k divisible by 3, forcing k≥9; subtracting one compulsory edge for each pair gives D=4k−28 and local surplus k−7 by definition. At k=9 and e_ij≤2, the residual simple graph has degree two; cycle partitions 8, 5+3 and 4+4 follow without any new embedding, obstruction, enumeration or proof idea. Röst–Vígh have already defined and used that graph and displayed two example types. No exact table of all three was located, but absence of the precise printed list is not new mathematical content when it is mechanically implied by these stronger standard facts. The 2020 inaccessible full text is a remaining priority risk but is not used as a reason to fail.

## value

FAIL

The object is natural and the two known {9,3} constructions motivate comparing their doubled-pair graphs. Nevertheless, the submitted final claim adds only immediate handshake/Euler arithmetic and a textbook classification of 2-regular graphs on eight vertices. The sharp k=9 threshold and common face-vector are obtained without any new construction or nontrivial obstruction, while the 5+3 pattern is merely not excluded by this coarse local count. No realization, nonrealizability, finer feasibility restriction, natural full classification of surfaces, or other mathematically unresolved structural fact is established. The statement repackages mandatory elementary necessary conditions; its useful role is an explanatory paragraph in the comparison literature, not a standalone valuable research finding. The specific 5+3 target is motivated but no evidence shows it is even feasible as an abstract or embedded surface, so the enumeration does not solve a substantive missing invariant.
