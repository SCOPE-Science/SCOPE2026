For every compact stationary Tinkerbell state, the barycenter fixes the entire traceless covariance matrix: \(\operatorname{Var}(x)-\operatorname{Var}(y)=A(u,v)\) and \(2\operatorname{Cov}(x,y)=B(u,v)\). In particular, every centered invariant state is exactly second-order isotropic, with equal coordinate variances and zero cross-covariance.
