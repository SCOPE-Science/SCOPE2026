# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For each 1<q<infinity, 0<omega<=1 and 0<t<1, x=(1,t) is in a differentiable ordered sector of N^q, with gradient q(1,omega*t^(q-1)). Thus the displayed y=(a,-1), a=omega*t^(q-1), has zero pairing and x is Birkhoff-orthogonal to y. Since 0<a<1, y lies in the other smooth ordered sector where the derivative of N^q is q(omega*a^(q-1),-1); reverse orthogonality holds iff omega*a^(q-1)=t, equivalently omega^q*t^((q-1)^2-1)=omega^q*t^(q(q-2))=1. For q>2 the left side is <1. For q=2 it is omega^2, equal to 1 exactly when omega=1. For 1<q<2, omega<1 gives unique t=omega^(1/(2-q)), and omega=1 gives no interior t. In each non-Euclidean parameter case there exists an explicit smooth nonreciprocal pair, proving non-Radon; (omega,q)=(1,2) is Euclidean and Radon. This reconstructs the unlimited-parameter proof, not finite sampling. Exact-rational examples and D4 invariance were independently checked in independent_reciprocity_check.py. Correctness does not imply originality/value.

## originality

FAIL

The central claimed global classification “this Lorentz norm is Radon iff it is the Euclidean member” was already a direct consequence of Sahoo (2022), Corollary 4.8, which applies to every quarter-turn symmetric Radon normed plane. The exact N_omega,q family has quarter-turn symmetry by definition. A scalar Euclidean equality argument (compare N(1,t)^q=1+omega*t^q with the Euclidean (1+t^2)^(q/2) for all t) forces q=2,omega=1. Therefore stronger primary published work substantively covers the record’s advertised global rigidity even though it does not spell out these parameters. The smooth local criterion omega^q*t^{q(q-2)}=1 may be an unprinted elementary derivative identity, but is an immediate standard supporting-functional substitution and does not make the original combined headline original. No negative judgment rests on inaccessible literature or failed searches.
