## correctness

At the actual pinned RESULT.md theorem and Theorem 4.6 of Lambropoulou–Lorenzi, each original braid letter contributes its usual Artin exponent ±1 and each bond contributes A_ij^p or a pure conjugate, whose braid exponent is 2p. Thus for any bonded braid word w with d_B bond letters, e(Φ_p(w))=e(Φ_0(w))+2p d_B(w); source defining relations are bond-homogeneous, so d_B is invariant. Since all bond images are pure, π(Φ_pβ)=π(Φ_0β); if the latter is identity, each closure has n oriented components and T=e/2 by Stoimenow §2.6 eq. (2.19). For vector λ on P_n, each bond contributes one positive generator class transported by a strand permutation through braid blocks, so slope b∈N^{n choose2}, Σb=d_B, and b=λ(Φ_1β)−λ(Φ_0β) is intrinsic. For d_B>0, distinct p give distinct total linking, hence distinct oriented closures. Negative p, threaded bonds, pure underlying words, d_B=0 excluded, and n=2 example β=b12 giving lk=p are consistent. This is an analytic all-word argument, no computational experiment or unjustified infinite extrapolation.
