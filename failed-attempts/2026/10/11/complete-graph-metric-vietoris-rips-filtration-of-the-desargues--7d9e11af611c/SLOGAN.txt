The twenty-vertex Desargues incidence graph has an exact graph-metric Vietoris–Rips filtration with successive homotopy types \(\bigvee^{11}S^1\), \(\bigvee^{19}S^2\), \(S^3\), and \(S^9\) before becoming contractible. The previously known scale-two term is included for completeness; the scale-three term identifies the independence complex of the Cartesian Petersen prism with \(S^3\), supported by a fully reproducible integral collapse calculation.
