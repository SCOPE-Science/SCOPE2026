# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

FAIL

The three boxed eigenvalue formulas are analytically consistent for n≥4 and all κ: for diagonal curvature weights w_ij, Γ_11(Rκ)S has coefficients κ∑_{k≠i,j}(w_ik+w_jk). Taking w_ij=1, w_ij=s_i+s_j (∑s_i=0) and a nonzero zero-row-sum Weyl weight yields respectively 2(n−2)κ, (n−4)κ, −2κ; O(n)-equivariance extends these to the whole modules, and dQ=-2Γ follows from the quadratic map. Independent integer derivation matrices corroborated these three representative identities for n=4,5,6,7,9. HOWEVER the unqualified statement that n=4 is the UNIQUE dimension n≥4 with traceless-Ricci neutrality is FALSE for κ=0. Indeed R_0=0 and quadraticity gives dQ_0=0 on every curvature perturbation for every n≥4, e.g. n=5. The correct uniqueness statement requires κ≠0. The original final claim explicitly permits κ=0; the full claim is therefore not correct as written.

## originality

FAIL

Not original as a mathematical implication. Böhm–Wilking (2008), Lemma 2.1, establishes S+I#S=(n−1)S_sc+((n−2)/2)S_Ric0 for arbitrary algebraic curvature S; equivalently I#S has eigenvalues n−2,(n−4)/2,−1 on the three Ricci pieces. Belkhirat (2026) identifies the later central Clifford action and sharp quadratic derivative, with a normalization that makes Γ_11(Rκ)=2κ(I#_{BW}·) and dQ=-2Γ. The claimed full spectrum and n=4 coefficient cancellation follow immediately. The finding uses different names and factors, but supplies no independent new spectral mathematical fact beyond the union of those prior primary results.

The space-form curvature-module decomposition is a natural object, but the assigned spectral answer and n=4 zero mode are direct normalization/substitution consequences of the explicit 2008 all-curvature sharp-product identity and the 2026 operator dictionary.
