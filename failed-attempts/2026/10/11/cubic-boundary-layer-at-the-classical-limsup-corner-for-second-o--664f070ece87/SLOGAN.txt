For the sharp second-order delay boundary \(b=\beta(a)\), the classical corner has cubic contact:
\[
\beta(a)=\sqrt2-\frac13a^3-\frac{\sqrt2}{24}a^4+O(a^5).
\]
Consequently \(1/3\) is the exact cubic-scale coefficient separating the universally oscillatory side from sharp constructible nonoscillation as \(a\downarrow0\).
