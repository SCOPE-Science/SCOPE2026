For the symmetric edge polytope of \(C_n\), the Euclidean inradius is \(\sqrt{48/(n(n^2+8))}\) for even \(n\) and \(\sqrt{48n/((n^2-1)(n^2+3))}\) for odd \(n\). Only linearly many facets touch the maximal insphere: \(n\) in the even case and \(2n\) in the odd case; odd cycles have a unique incenter, while even cycles have an incenter family of dimension \(n/2-1\).
