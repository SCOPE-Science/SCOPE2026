# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For every nonempty face with at least two vertices, C4-freeness forces a unique common-neighbor center. For each center v and chosen r_v, descending-cardinality pairings (sigma,sigma union {r_v}) for sigma subset N(v)\{r_v}, |sigma|>=2 have unique remaining coface: every other larger coface was paired earlier and none belongs to a second center. The residue is exactly the union of rooted stars, with 2e-n distinct edges. Graph paths in the neighborhood one-skeleton are exactly even-length G-walks up to repetitions, so connected nonbipartite G gives a connected spine and rank 2e-2n+1. Independent code rebuilt all 3610 qualifying labeled n<=6 graphs, rechecked 7916 roots and 19974 elementary collapses; further exact face-replay tests checked large degree friendship graphs with and without a leaf. The finite tests corroborate, but do not prove, the universal statement; the proof is analytic. The arbitrary-root quantifier, triangles, pendant vertices and minimal n=3 were all considered.

## originality

FAIL

Decisive exact prior coverage: the published bipartite root-spine theorem applied to the connected C4-free canonical double cover G x K_2 yields the exact nonbipartite N(G) collapse on the original n labeled vertices with every prescribed root choice and the identical 2e-2n+1 circle count. Both full published bipartite RESULT.md proofs were inspected. The prior claim was incorrectly dismissed because the base graphs are in disjoint bipartite and nonbipartite classes; the canonical cover removes that apparent distinction.

## value

FAIL

The direct root-selected graph spine is a natural and useful structural object, but every mathematical assertion and its monotone collapse certificate is already delivered by the published square-free bipartite spine theorem after the routine canonical bipartite-double-cover substitution. The proposed nonbipartite presentation adds no independent structural lemma, new exact invariant, counterexample, boundary classification or non-implied classification. Its graph counts and wedge rank are immediate substitutions |V(H)|=2n, |E(H)|=2e. Thus correctness and the general utility of the inherited construction do not establish a distinct valuable research contribution.
