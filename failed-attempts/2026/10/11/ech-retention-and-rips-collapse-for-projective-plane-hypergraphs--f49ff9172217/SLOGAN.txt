For every finite projective plane \(\Pi\) of order \(q\ge2\), the natural singleton-plus-line hypergraph has order and Čech complexes homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{q^3}S^1\). Its pairwise-intersection Vietoris–Rips complex is nevertheless contractible, giving an all-orders family where pairwise Rips data loses every nontrivial loop.
