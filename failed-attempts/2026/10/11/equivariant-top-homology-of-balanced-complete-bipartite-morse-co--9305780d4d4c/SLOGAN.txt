For every \(m\ge2\), exchanging the paired halves of \(K_{m,m}\) acts on the top homology of its ordinary Morse-matching complex with trace \((-1)^m(m-1)^{m-1}\). At \(m=4\), the complete \(20\)-class character of \((S_4\times S_4)\rtimes C_2\) has a six-dimensional invariant subspace, refining the previously known nonequivariant \(5103\)-sphere homotopy type.
