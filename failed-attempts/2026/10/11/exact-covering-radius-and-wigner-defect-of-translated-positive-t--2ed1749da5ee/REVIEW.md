## originality

FAIL

The advertised pointwise identity is the familiar positive-approximant projection theorem, not an independent new quantum-cone result: Bouldin (1987), Theorem 3, gives (B−P)_+ as a trace-norm nearest positive operator to B−P (normal, indeed self-adjoint). Thus the exact minimum equals Tr(B−P)_−, including noncommuting positive B and P. Dax (2014), Theorem 14, independently states the same positive-part minimizer for every unitarily invariant matrix norm and calls it not new. Once that identity is substituted, the remaining two global equalities are elementary evaluations at the origin plus the choice W=U. The nonseparable-H case reduces to separable trace-class spectral support, and the entire construction specializes already to H=C as x↦x+p. Resultary returns only the submitted item itself as a close match, but absence of an identically titled database entry does not overcome exact prior mathematical implication. Failure is originality, not alleged copying or author misconduct.
