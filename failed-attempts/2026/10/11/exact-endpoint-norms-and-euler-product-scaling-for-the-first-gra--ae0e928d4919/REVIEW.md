## originality

FAIL

The general orthogonal Grassmann projector kernel is already given in Marco–Parcet 2005: Theorem 3.6 gives q-Hahn polynomial spherical kernels in every harmonic degree, and Remark 5.5/eq. (23) identifies these kernels, with dimensions, as actual orthogonal projections. Specializing s=1 supplies the same first-degree linear-in-intersection kernel (up to the standard normalization). Its absolute row sum is then resolved by the elementary inequality [k]_q^2/[n]_q<1, the standard disjoint-subspace Gaussian-binomial count, and the standard matrix infinity-norm identity. The complementary norm changes only the diagonal; the fixed-excess Euler product is the Gaussian-binomial product limit. Thus all numerical conclusions are direct elementary corollaries of a substantially broader published same-object kernel theorem and routine counts, not an independently original mathematical result. Search alone is not the rejection basis.
