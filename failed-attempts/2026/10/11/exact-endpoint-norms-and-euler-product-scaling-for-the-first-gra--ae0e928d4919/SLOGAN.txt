The canonical first nonconstant Fourier projector on a finite \(q\)-Grassmannian has an exact sharp endpoint norm: it is twice a simple dimension factor times the probability that two uniformly sampled \(k\)-subspaces are disjoint. When the ambient dimension is \(2k+s\), its normalized amplification converges to the explicit Euler product \(\frac{2}{q-1}\prod_{r=s+1}^{\infty}(1-q^{-r})\).
