# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For the canonical reduced centrally symmetric generators R, Winter Eq. (2.1) gives Z=sum_{alpha in R+}[-alpha,alpha] and h_Z(u)=sum_{alpha>0}|<u,alpha>|. For a centrally symmetric convex body the maximal inscribed-ball radius is min_{||u||=1}h_Z(u), since opposite support inequalities eliminate any advantage of translation and the centered ball achieves the minimum. W-invariance reduces to a closed Weyl chamber C. There, root signs are nonnegative, so h_Z(u)=<s,u>. A simplicial chamber is cone(lambda_i), and m=min_i <s,lambda_i>/||lambda_i||>0 gives <s,u>>=m sum c_i||lambda_i||>=m||u||; equality on precisely the minimizing extreme rays by strict triangle inequality off a single ray. The Coxeter fan identifies exactly the facet normals and contact orbits. Winter semistar-to-vertex and W-transitivity give the orbit vertices Ws, all norm ||s||; opposite vertices s,-s force the circumradius. This proves all dimensions and reducible/unequal-root-length cases, not only sampled cases.

## originality

FAIL

The entire theorem is already implied by stronger 2019/2020 primary literature, not merely absent under different keywords. Winter identifies every candidate as a root-system orbit zonotope and fixes its generator normalization. Ardila–Castillo–Eur–Postnikov explicitly present the same Coxeter permutahedron as conv(W rho) and by all W-conjugate fundamental-weight facet inequalities. Taking standard Euclidean distances to those published facets and the common norm of orbit vertices directly gives the candidate radii and contact orbits. The candidate is therefore a mechanically obtained corollary of established stronger descriptions; matching numbers or explicit use of the word inradius is unnecessary.

## value

FAIL

Vertex-transitive zonotopes and their Euclidean radii are natural; the displayed quantities could be useful as reference formulas. However, the proposed mathematical result is only the standard radius-of-a-centered-polytope calculation on an already published complete facet presentation plus the immediate common-vertex-norm circumradius. Its facet-contact characterization is tautologically the set of shortest already identified facet distances. No uncomputed type-dependent value, nontrivial inequality, new boundary classification, or independent structural lemma survives the earlier general theory. This is a routine geometric specialization rather than a substantive new exact invariant or motivated gap.
