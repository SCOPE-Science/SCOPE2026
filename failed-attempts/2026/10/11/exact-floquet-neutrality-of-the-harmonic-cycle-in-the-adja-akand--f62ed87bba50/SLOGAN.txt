For every integer \(n\ge0\), the exact unit-circle harmonic orbit in the Adjaï–Akande–Yehossou–Monsia family is Floquet-neutral: both planar multipliers are exactly \(1\). The exact defect law \(\dot R=-2xy(y+S_n(x))R\) shows that the orbit is nonhyperbolic and cannot be exponentially attracting or repelling, although higher-order nonlinear stability is not determined.
