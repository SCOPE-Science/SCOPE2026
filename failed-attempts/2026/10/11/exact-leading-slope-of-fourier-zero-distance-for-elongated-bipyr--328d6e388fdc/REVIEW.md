## correctness

For d>=3 and alpha>0, the original paper's exact Fourier rescaling gives kappa(Omega_alpha,d)=inf_{F_{d-1}(u,s)=0,s>=0} (u^2+alpha^2*s^2)^(1/2). For every alpha this is >=alpha*rho_d; choosing for each epsilon a zero with s<rho_d+epsilon yields the opposite limsup after alpha tends to infinity. This requires neither infimum attainment nor a finite enumeration. The original paper proves nonempty zero set and a uniform positive zero-free strip (Prop. 1.1 and Lemma 2.3, pp.2,8-9); its equal-volume ball formula c_d alpha^((d-1)/d) supplies the normalization. Pointwise infimum gives both monotonicities. Boundaries d=3, alpha>0 and unattained rho are covered.
