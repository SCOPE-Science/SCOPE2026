The metric projection onto the constant-vector line in real \(\ell_p^n\) has sharp Lipschitz constant \(n^{1/p}\) for \(1<p<2\), exactly \(1\) at \(p=2\), and \((n/2)^{1/p}\) for \(p>2\), whenever \(n\ge3\). Thus a finite-dimensional nonlinear best-approximation map exhibits a genuine two-sided jump at the Hilbert exponent, with explicit sharp configurations; in dimension two the constant is always \(1\).
