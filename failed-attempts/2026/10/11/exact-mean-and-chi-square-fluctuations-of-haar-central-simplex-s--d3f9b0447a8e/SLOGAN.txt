A Haar-random central section of the regular \((m-1)\)-simplex has exactly \(\binom m2\arccos(-1/(m-1))/\pi\) expected vertices. Its deficit from \(m^2/4\), divided by \(m\), tends to \((1/4-1/(2\pi))\chi_1^2\): random section complexity is almost maximal but fluctuates by a non-Gaussian, order-\(m\) amount.
