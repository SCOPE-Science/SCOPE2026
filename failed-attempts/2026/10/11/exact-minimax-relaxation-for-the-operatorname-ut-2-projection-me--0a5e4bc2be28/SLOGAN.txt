For Huang's \(\operatorname{UT}_2\) projection-mean iteration, the exact worst-case asymptotic factor is \(\max\{|1-1/c|,|1-c_0/c|\}\), not merely an upper bound. Balancing the diagonal and off-diagonal modes gives the unique rate-optimal choice \(c=(1+c_0)/2\) with sharp factor \((c_0-1)/(c_0+1)\).
