Every three-site nilpotent weighted shift on complex \(\ell_p^3\), \(1<p<\infty\), has an exact closed-disk spatial numerical range: its radius is \(\frac{(p-1)^{(p-1)/p}}pR\), where \(R>0\) solves \((|a|/R)^p+(|b|/R)^{p/(p-1)}=1\). This mixed-exponent law also classifies all maximizing vectors and exhibits a strict reversal-of-weights asymmetry at fixed non-Hilbert exponent, despite exact conjugate-exponent duality.
