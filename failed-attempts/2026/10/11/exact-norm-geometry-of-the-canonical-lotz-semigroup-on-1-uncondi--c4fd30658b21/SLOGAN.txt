For the canonical \(n^{-t}\) Lotz semigroup on any 1-unconditional Schauder decomposition, every pairwise operator-norm distance is exactly the supremum of the scalar eigenvalue differences, so \(\|T(t)-I\|=1\) for every \(t>0\). The same path is norm-holomorphic for every positive time, and on a finite-dimensional decomposition its canonical truncation error is exactly \((N+1)^{-t}\).
