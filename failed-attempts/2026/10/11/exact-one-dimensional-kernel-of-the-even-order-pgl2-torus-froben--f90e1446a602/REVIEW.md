# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Let n=ord(q^2) be even, q+q^{-1} != 0, and t=q^(n^2), exactly as in the cited source. Proposition 5.2 gives a complex vector-space basis consisting of the unit/empty diagram, w and unoriented loop classes e_[u], u in Z^2\{0} modulo ±. Theorem 5.6, using Lemma 5.5, gives Fr(empty)=empty, Fr(w)=0 and Fr(e_[u])=e_[nu]. Since multiplication by nonzero n induces an injection on the ±-quotient of the lattice, the non-w images are a linearly independent subset of the target basis. Every skein has a FINITE linear expansion; hence its image vanishes precisely if its unit and every loop coefficient vanish, with w arbitrary. This proves ker Fr=Cw. The package proof implicitly needs canonical representatives for ± labels; making that indexing convention explicit resolves the harmless ambiguity without modifying the theorem.

## originality

FAIL

Theorem 5.6 of Higgins–Silva–Somers–Stephens–Sundar–Wokhlu (arXiv:2610.01881v1, October 1, 2026) already gives the complete image of EVERY torus basis element. With Proposition 5.2 and the even-order zero alpha_n, the claimed one-dimensional kernel follows as an immediate formal corollary by injectivity of lattice scaling. Thus the assigned assertion is mathematically covered by strictly stronger earlier published information regardless of the paper not spelling out the exact kernel. Searches and full primary examination confirmed this precise implication; this is not an insufficient-search rejection.

## value

FAIL

The dimension of the kernel of a naturally defined Frobenius map is a meaningful structural quantity, and identifying a minimal loss of injectivity could be useful in a new setting. Here, however, the supplied contribution is only the immediate linear-algebra extraction from an already complete published basis-action table: all the challenging skein construction, basis independence and parity computation occur in the source. No new structural lemma, quotient presentation, previously uncomputable invariant, or motivated nontrivial boundary is established. The exact fact is mechanically implied by stronger known data, so its isolated restatement does not meet the stipulated independent mathematical-value bar.
