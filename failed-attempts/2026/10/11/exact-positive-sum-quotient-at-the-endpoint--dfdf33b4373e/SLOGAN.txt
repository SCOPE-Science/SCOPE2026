At the \(p=1\) endpoint, every positive-sum norm \(\|f+g\|_{(1)}\) depends on \(f\) only through the normalized derivative deficit \(\delta_f=f'-\|f'\|_\infty\). The invisible fibers are exactly constant shifts of the derivative, and arbitrary surjections inside each fiber produce surjective norm-additive maps; the known affine counterexample is the fiber \(\delta=0\).
