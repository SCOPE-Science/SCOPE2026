For the regular octagonal Banach plane, Yang--Li's quadratic isosceles-orthogonality constant has an exact three-branch profile with breakpoints \(3-2\sqrt2\) and \((2\sqrt2-1)/7\). The first branch is the unexpected plateau \(4-2\sqrt2\), and the proof comes from an exact three-branch formula for \(\gamma_{X_8}(t)\).
