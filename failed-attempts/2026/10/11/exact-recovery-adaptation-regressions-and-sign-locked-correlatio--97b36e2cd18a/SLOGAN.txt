Every compact stationary Hindmarsh–Rose state obeys three exact coordinate regressions, including \(\mathbb E[x^2\mid y]=(c-y)/d\) and \(\mathbb E[x\mid z]=x_R+z/s\). They force exact sign-locked correlations—negative between \(x^2\) and fast recovery, positive between \(x\) and slow adaptation—with the correlation deficits equal to normalized mean-square coordinate speeds.
