For the compact generalized Cesàro operator \((C_t x)_n=(n+1)^{-1}\sum_{j\le n}t^{n-j}x_j\) on \(\ell_2\), the sharp singular-value limit \(\lim_{n\to\infty}n s_n(C_t)\) is the circular mean of \(|1-t e^{i\theta}|^{-1}\), expressible as a complete elliptic integral. It gives the exact logarithmic growth of singular-value sums and a sharp weak-trace-class boundary for every fixed \(0\le t<1\).
