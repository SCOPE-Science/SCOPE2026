On the regular hexagonal real Banach plane \(N(u,v)=\max\{|u|,|v|,|u-v|\}\), the skew Schäffer endpoint constant has the exact all-parameter profile \(S_{X,\infty}(\tau)=\tau+(1+\tau+\tau^2)^{-1}\) for every \(\tau\ge0\). Its value at \(\tau=1\) is \(4/3\), and its first nonconstant variation at zero is cubic.
