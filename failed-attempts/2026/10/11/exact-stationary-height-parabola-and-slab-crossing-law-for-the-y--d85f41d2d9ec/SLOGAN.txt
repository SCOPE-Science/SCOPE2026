Every compact stationary state of the Yang–Chen flow lies on the exact height-moment parabola \(\operatorname{Var}(z)=\bar z(c-\bar z)\) and obeys \(\int x^2\,d\mu=b\bar z\). Every non-equilibrium ergodic recurrent state therefore has \(0<\bar z<c\) and must visit \(0<z<c\) together with at least one of \(z<0\) or \(z>c\).
