For generalized Cesàro matrices, the optimal weighted self-commutator coefficient reaches the universal ceiling \(1\) exactly for \(\alpha\in\{0,1,\ldots,m-2\}\cup[m-1,\infty)\), with the finite set empty when \(m=1\). Thus the noninteger parameter bands newly brought into the hyponormal range are precisely the bands where the optimal coefficient is strictly below \(1\).
