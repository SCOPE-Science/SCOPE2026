For bounded-type half-interval discrepancy, a nonzero exponential local tilt \(t\) forces the even-continued-fraction sign drift to have the fixed sign of \(t\) and linear size. Consequently the Beck center is \(\operatorname{sgn}(t)\,\Theta(\log n)\), so exponential local bias reaches the maximal centering order permitted by the general bounded-type central limit theorem.
