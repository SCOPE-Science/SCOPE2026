Every compact stationary Shimizu–Morioka state is heightwise calibrated by \(\mathbb E[x^2\mid z]=\alpha z\), so squared amplitude and height have the exact covariance \(\operatorname{Cov}(x^2,z)=\alpha\operatorname{Var}(z)\). A second balance forces every non-equilibrium stationary regime to place positive mass above the nonzero-equilibrium height \(z=1\).
