Every fully active compact stationary Guckenheimer–Holmes state calibrates each cyclic competitor load exactly on the squared-amplitude fiber it drives: \(\mathbb E[Q_i\mid X_i]=\mu-aX_i\). When \(a,b,c\) are not all equal, this forces all three coordinate mean squares to the same parameter-selected value \(\mu/(a+b+c)\), with the loss of perfect load anticorrelation measured exactly by mean-square per-capita speed.
