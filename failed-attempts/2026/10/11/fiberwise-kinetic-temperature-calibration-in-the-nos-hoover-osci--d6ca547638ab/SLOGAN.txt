For every compact stationary Nosé–Hoover state, squared momentum is calibrated to the target temperature on every thermostat-variable fiber: \(\mathbb E[x^2\mid z]=1\). The exact defect \(\operatorname{Var}(x^2)=\alpha^{-2}\mathbb E[\dot z^2]\) is always positive, so every compact recurrent state crosses between \(|x|<1\) and \(|x|>1\).
