Every compact stationary Burke–Shaw state satisfies three exact fiberwise regressions, \(\mathbb E[y\mid x]=-x\), \(\mathbb E[xz\mid y]=-y/k\), and \(\mathbb E[xy\mid z]=-d/g\). Their residuals are exactly the coordinate speeds, forcing precise variance defects and, away from equilibrium-supported measures, the strict anticorrelation law \(-1<\operatorname{Corr}(x,y)<0\).
