Every compact recurrent FitzHugh–Nagumo state satisfies the exact global Hopf-slope calibration \(\mathbb E[(1-\varepsilon b-v^2)\dot v^2]=0\). For every non-equilibrium stationary state, the \(\dot v^2\)-weighted mean square voltage is therefore exactly \(1-\varepsilon b\), forcing the sharp necessary recurrence condition \(\varepsilon b<1\).
