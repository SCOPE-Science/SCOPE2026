For the harmonic-mean member of the Amini–Harandi–Rahimi family, the regular octagonal plane has the exact full exponent profile \(C_{h,p}(X_8)=\|(1,\sqrt2-1)\|_q\), where \(q\) is Hölder conjugate to \(p\). A single fixed pair of octagon vertices is sharp for every exponent, with only the optimizing scale changing.
