The regular dodecagonal Banach plane is non-Hilbert but has the three Hilbert values for the Chen–Yang–Liu–Li equilateral-midline constants: \(M_1=\sqrt3\), \(M_2=3\), and \(M_3=\sqrt[4]{3}\). An exact \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt3)\) facet decomposition proves the first two values, and concavity of the square root gives the third.
