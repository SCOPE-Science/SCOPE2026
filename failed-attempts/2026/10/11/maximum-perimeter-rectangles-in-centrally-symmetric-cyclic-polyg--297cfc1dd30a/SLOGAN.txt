For every centrally symmetric cyclic polygon, the largest inscribed rectangle's perimeter is determined exactly by the pair of vertex diameters nearest to perpendicularity, and every optimizer has four original vertices as corners. The rule gives a linear-time selection method and the sharp regular-even-polygon formula \(4\sqrt2 R\) or \(4\sqrt2 R\cos(\pi/(2n))\), according to the number of sides modulo four.
