The graph-metric Vietoris–Rips filtration of the Möbius–Kantor graph passes through \(\bigvee^9S^1\), then a mixed-dimensional \((\bigvee^9S^2)\vee(\bigvee^2S^3)\), then \(S^7\), before becoming contractible. A structural homotopy pushout proves the classification and gives the entire persistence barcode over every field.
