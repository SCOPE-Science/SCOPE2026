A nontrivial periodic cycle for a uniformly quasiregular self-map of \(\mathbb B^n\) forces the isometric uniformly quasiconformal homeomorphism branch of the new Denjoy--Wolff theorem. Outside that branch -- in particular for every non-injective self-map -- the periodic and recurrent sets are either one attracting fixed point or empty, according as the dynamics converges inside the ball or to its boundary.
