The explicit harmonic \(K\)-quasiregular Bloch Lipschitz coefficient from the recent sharp pseudo-hyperbolic theorem has a hidden quadratic stability law: its excess above the analytic endpoint is \(\alpha(\log K)^2+O((\log K)^4)\), not a first-order loss. The same exact reparametrization also shows that this coefficient is strictly increasing for \(K\ge1\).
