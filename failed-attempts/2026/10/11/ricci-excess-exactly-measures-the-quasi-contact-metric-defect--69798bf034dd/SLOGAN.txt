For a quasi-contact metric manifold with Killing characteristic field, the characteristic Ricci excess is exactly half the squared contact-metric defect: \(\|d\eta-2\Phi\|^2=2(\operatorname{Ric}(\xi,\xi)-2n)\), while \(\|d\eta\|^2=2\operatorname{Ric}(\xi,\xi)\). On the new five-dimensional Sasaki--Einstein deformation family, the incompatibility angle is exactly \(\arctan\mu\).
