For the genuinely higher-order \(P_m\) Heisenberg inequality, equality is rigid in the natural square domain: the \(P_{m-2}\)-profile must be radial and, up to scale and dilation, is exactly the hypergeometric profile constructed by Hu--Liu--Zou. The focal paper proves existence of such profiles; the equality conditions show they exhaust regular square-domain profiles for every \(m\ge3\).
