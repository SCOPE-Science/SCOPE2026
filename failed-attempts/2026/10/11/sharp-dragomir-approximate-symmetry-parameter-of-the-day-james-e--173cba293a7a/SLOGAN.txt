For the classical real Day--James plane \(\ell_2-\ell_1\), the sharp global modified-Dragomir Birkhoff-symmetry parameter is \(\varepsilon_D=1/\sqrt2\). Equivalently, the smallest reverse line distance over unit Birkhoff--James pairs is \(1/\sqrt2\), attained at a coordinate-axis point against the diagonal Euclidean unit vector.
