The metric projection onto the probability simplex is the same soft-threshold map for every \(1<p<\infty\), but its sharp global \(\ell_4\)-Lipschitz constant depends on the active-coordinate count and admits an exact finite maximization formula. The optimal uniform-in-dimension constant is \((1+2/\sqrt3)^{1/4}\); it is approached but never reached in any fixed finite dimension.
