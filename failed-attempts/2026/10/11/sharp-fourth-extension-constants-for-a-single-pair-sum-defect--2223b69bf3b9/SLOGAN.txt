A finite frequency set with exactly one repeated unordered pair sum has one of two universal sharp fourth-extension constants: a proper parallelogram gives \(\|E_S\|^4=9N/(4m^2)\), while a midpoint relation gives \(\|E_S\|^4=15N/(7m^2)\). In both cases every extremizer localizes completely on the unique additive defect, with an explicit modulus ratio and phase-alignment condition.
