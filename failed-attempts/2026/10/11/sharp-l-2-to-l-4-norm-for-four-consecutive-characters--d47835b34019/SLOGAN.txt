For the four consecutive characters \(\{0,1,2,3\}\), the sharp \(L^2\)-to-\(L^4\) norm is algebraic and is attained only by affine-linear phases with coefficient moduli proportional to \((1,\rho,\rho,1)\), where \(\rho^3+3\rho^2-3\rho-3=0\). The unrestricted optimum is strictly larger than the equal-modulus arithmetic-progression value.
