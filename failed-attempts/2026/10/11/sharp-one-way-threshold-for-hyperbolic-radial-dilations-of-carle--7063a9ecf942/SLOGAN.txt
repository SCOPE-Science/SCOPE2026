Hyperbolic radial dilation has an exact interpolation-preservation phase transition: \(D_a(R,\theta)=(aR,\theta)\) preserves every Carleson interpolating sequence forward exactly for \(a\ge1\), and bidirectionally only for \(a=1\). The smooth \(a=2\) example from the recent characterization is therefore one point in a complete multiplicative-semigroup classification.
