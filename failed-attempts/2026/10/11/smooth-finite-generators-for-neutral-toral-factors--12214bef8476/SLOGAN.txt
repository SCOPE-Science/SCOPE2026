The neutral family \(T_q(x_1,\ldots,x_q,y)=(x_1,\ldots,x_q,2y\bmod1)\) is nonexpanding but has an explicit smooth finite one-sided generator: binary digits recover \(y\), while comparisons with its dense doubling orbit recover every invariant coordinate. Thus a strict contracting direction, not mere nonexpansion, is essential to the smooth-generator obstruction proved in arXiv:2610.01642v1.
