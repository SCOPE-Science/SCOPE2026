For every finite connected triangle-free graph with no \(K_{2,3}\) subgraph, its scale-two intrinsic graph Čech nerve is homotopy equivalent to the square-completion of the graph wedged with one two-sphere for every four-cycle. In particular, a sufficiently large toroidal grid yields a torus wedged with \(mn\) two-spheres, retaining a nonzero degree-one cup product.
