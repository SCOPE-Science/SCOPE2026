Thus for unit polygonal x,y, ||x+t y||_X²+||t x-y||_X²<=sec²(theta)*2(1+t²) by parallelogram and |x|_2,|y|_2<=1. Independently checked n=1,2,3,7,20 numerically and symbolic direction geometry. Correct for all n analytically.
