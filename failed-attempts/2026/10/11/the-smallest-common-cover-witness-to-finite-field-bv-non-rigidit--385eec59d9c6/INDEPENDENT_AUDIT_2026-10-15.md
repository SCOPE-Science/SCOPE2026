# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For every odd prime p the central order-4 quotients of K=SU(4)xSU(2) are exterior pairs: the order of each kernel is a unit in F_p and SU factors have torsion-free integral homology (Mukilraj Theorem 3.2, Proposition 3.5, Example 9.7). Both inherit exponents {1,1,2,3}. For p ≡1 mod4, F_p[C4] = F_p^4 = F_p[C2×C2]; for p ≡3 mod4 the first is F_p×F_p×F_(p²), whereas the second is F_p^4. Corollary 9.3 then gives iff equivalence for graded and BV loop-homology algebras. The rank lower bound follows from simple-cover centre classification: a C4 element first appears in SU4 (rank 3), its cyclic centre has no V4 subgroup, so an extra even-centre factor of rank at least one is necessary, attained by SU2. This checks exactly the common-cover restriction; no cross-cover minimum is inferred.

## originality

FAIL

The full prior classification in Mukilraj, Corollary 9.3 and Remark 9.6, already proves the entire finite-field C4/V4 BV collision for equal exponents. Example 9.7 states all the permissible simple-centre types and arbitrary subgroups of their products. Choosing the smallest C4-centred factor SU4 (rank 3), seeing its cyclic centre has no V4, and adjoining SU2 (rank 1) is a direct elementary minimization from that exhaustive classification; the constructed quotients and rank-4 boundary do not furnish a logically independent new theorem beyond this already published structural coverage. No separate new obstruction, nonstandard lemma or unused parameter remains.

## value

FAIL

The finite-field collision itself and the exhaustive centre catalogue are already published. The proposed rank-four witness is formed by taking the first C4-centred simple factor and adding the cheapest C2-centred factor; this short textbook-level application of the same published table does not reveal a non-obvious mathematical gap, independent structural bound, or unexplained cutoff beyond existing coverage. The naturalness of smallest rank is acknowledged, but it does not by itself create substantive incremental value.
