## correctness

PASS

Theorem A of Kazaniecki–Lechner supplies almost projectional factorization S≈ADB with ||A||||B||→1. On the nonempty infinite-dimensional canonical range E_t, the coefficient λ is constant on the block, and λ^(-1)Q_t S|E_t=Id. Neumann inversion of λ^(-1)Q_t ADB|E_t yields an exact Id_E=A′DB′ with limsup||A′||||B′||≤||Q_t||/|λ|. For any strictly singular R, A′RB′ is strictly singular and cannot lie inside the unit norm ball centered at Id_E; taking infima yields dist(D,SS)≥|λ|/||Q_t||. Identity coefficients give 1−λ for Id−D. Compact⊂SS yields the stated essential-norm estimates. Critically all estimates are valid but are direct standard consequences of the cited factorization.

## originality

FAIL

The supposedly novel blockwise estimate is already an immediate specialization of Kazaniecki–Lechner, arXiv:2610.01628v1, Corollary 11.6 proof, equations (11.68)–(11.73). That primary proof defines T=sum λ_t^(-1)Q_t, proves TS|E=Id, and explicitly bounds exact factor norms by ||T||(1+η)/(1−η||T||). Taking E=E_t gives T=λ^(-1)Q_t and exactly ||Q_t||/|λ|. The strictly singular distance inequality is the universal one-line consequence of factoring an infinite-dimensional identity through D. The source need not literally mention the ideal to imply the advertised bound; title/keyword nonoccurrence is not novelty.
