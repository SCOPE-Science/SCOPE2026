For Janson--Sobolev and Janson--Sobolev--Hardy diagonal operators, every ultrafilter coefficient on a bounded canonical block gives a quantitative obstruction to strict singularity: \(\operatorname{dist}(D,\mathcal{SS})\ge |\lambda_{\mathcal U}^k(D)|/\|Q_{\mathcal K_t}^{\mathcal B}\|\). The same obstruction passes to the essential norm and, for \(I-D\), replaces the coefficient by \(1-\lambda_{\mathcal U}^k(D)\).
