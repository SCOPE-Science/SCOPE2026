For every Euclidean convex polygon, the odd simple-polygon perimeter problem collapses from a continuum to finitely many vertex triples: \(S_m(C)=\max_T\{p(T)+(m-3)d(T)\}\). Hence only finitely many perimeter phases can occur, with eventual diameter control; for the unit-side regular pentagon the exact value is \(1+(m-1)(1+\sqrt5)/2\) for every odd \(m\ge3\).
