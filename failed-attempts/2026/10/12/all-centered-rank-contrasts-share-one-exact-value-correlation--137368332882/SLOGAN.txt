For every iid continuous sample, all nonzero zero-sum linear contrasts have exactly the same correlation with their matching rank contrasts: \(\frac{\sqrt3}{2}(G/\sigma)\sqrt{N/(N+1)}\). The value is at most \(\sqrt{N/(N+1)}\), with equality exactly for a uniform parent, and the two-sample Mann-Whitney/mean-difference correlation is therefore allocation-invariant at fixed total size.
