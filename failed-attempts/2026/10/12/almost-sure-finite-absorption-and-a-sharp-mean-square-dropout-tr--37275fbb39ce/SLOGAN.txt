Classical scalar error-feedback gradient descent with unit step and Bernoulli packet drops reaches the exact minimizer in finitely many steps almost surely at **every** positive transmission probability, yet its expected squared error grows geometrically when \(p<2/3\). At \(p=2/3\) the mean-square error converges to \((2/5)x_0^2\); it decays geometrically only for \(p>2/3\).
