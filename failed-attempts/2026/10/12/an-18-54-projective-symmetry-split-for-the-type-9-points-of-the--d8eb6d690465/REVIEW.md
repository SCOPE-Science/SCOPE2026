The claimed orbit partition is correct over C, conditional on the directly checked published fact that the 72 type-9 points are the 9-torsion points other than 3-torsion. The Hessian of the Fermat cubic is proportional to xyz; any plane automorphism preserves its triangle, is monomial and has group (mu_3)^2 semidirect S3, order 54. ## originality

FAIL

The full primary Artebani–Dolgachev 2008/2009 Hesse-pencil study supplies a stronger equivalent geometric decomposition. Its Proposition 5.2 constructs eight Halphen cubics B_1,...,B_8, each intersecting any smooth Hesse pencil member in a nine-point E[3]-translation orbit, and their union is the 72 non-3 9-torsion points. Its displayed generators act on the indices by B_i->B_{i+4} and a pair of 3-cycles fixing indices 1 and 5; therefore on the Fermat member they give exactly the index orbits {1,5} and {2,3,4,6,7,8}, i.e. 18 and 54 points. The same paper identifies an invariant sextic Phi_6 meeting the Fermat cubic in the 18 exceptional points from B1 and B5. Reducing its formula on F yields 12(x^6+x^3y^3+y^6); similarly B1 B5 yields 3 times that sextic. Thus ALL distinctive components of the proposed result are mathematically implied by a published antecedent, irrespective of absence of identical 18+54 wording. That decisive primary comparison overrides negative keyword searches and the older self-review PASS.

Translating their explicit 2+6 orbit table into 18+54 and using orbit–stabilizer, then simplifying their invariant sextic on F, is an immediate specialization/computation rather than a new substantive mathematical result. The package adds neither new structure, proof method, boundary, nor independent unknown classification surviving antecedent coverage.
