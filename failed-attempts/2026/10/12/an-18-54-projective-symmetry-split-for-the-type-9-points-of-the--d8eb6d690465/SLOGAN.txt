The \(72\) type-\(9\) points of the Fermat cubic are not projectively homogeneous: the full plane automorphism group has exactly two orbits of sizes \(18\) and \(54\). The \(18\)-point orbit is exactly \(x^9=y^9=z^9\), equivalently the intersection with the sextic factor \(x^6+x^3y^3+y^6=0\) already present in the published degree-\(24\) equation.
