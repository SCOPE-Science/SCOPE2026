The induced finite age of the nonhomogeneous set-homogeneous three-hypergraph \(M_3\) has a weighted Catalan generating polynomial \(P_n(q)=\sum_{a=1}^{n-1}q^{a\binom{n-a}{2}}P_a(q)P_{n-a}(q)\). Its five-vertex members have 14 isomorphism types but none with five of the possible ten edges; at every other order every edge count between zero and the maximum is attained; the total number of \(n\)-vertex types is \(C_{n-1}\).
