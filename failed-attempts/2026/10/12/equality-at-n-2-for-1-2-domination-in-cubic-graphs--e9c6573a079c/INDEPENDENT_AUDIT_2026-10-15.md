# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

PASS: In any connected finite cubic G, if D is (1,2)-dominating, each vertex inside D has at most one edge to R=V-D, so |E(D,R)|≤|D|; each vertex outside D has at least one edge back, giving |E(D,R)|≥|R|=n-|D|. Thus |D|≥n/2. Equality makes both inequalities equalities, forcing every vertex of both D and R to have exactly one cross neighbor; that crossing edge set is a perfect matching and both shores induce 2-regular graphs. Conversely any perfect matching cut leaves internal degree two, so either shore is (1,2)-dominating of size n/2. This covers all finite connected simple cubic graphs without experiment and matches the 2021 published argument verbatim in mathematical content.
