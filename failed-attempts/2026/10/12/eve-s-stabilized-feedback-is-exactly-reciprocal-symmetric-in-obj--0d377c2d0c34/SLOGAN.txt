Eve's stabilized global-feedback coefficient is exactly \(\max\{q,q^{-1}\}-1\) for consecutive positive objective-gap ratio \(q\), so reciprocal improvement and worsening traces receive identical global-rate feedback. On geometric traces this yields an exact three-zone clipping law, and with the source default \(c=10\), a ten-fold improvement and a ten-fold worsening both asymptotically use \(\alpha_1/9\).
