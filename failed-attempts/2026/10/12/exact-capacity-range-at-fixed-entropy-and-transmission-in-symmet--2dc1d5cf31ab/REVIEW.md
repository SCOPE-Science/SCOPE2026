Writing u=1/t+d and v=1/t−d, the simultaneous bounds are equivalent to |1/t−1−1/r|+|d|<1. This gives exactly the stated t-domain and Δ, which is positive throughout that domain. The product is t⁻²−d², so its continuous image as d² ranges over [0,Δ²) gives exactly the asserted endpoints, with only d=0 attaining the maximum. For r=1/2 and t=1/3 this gives (2/3,3/4], with 35/48 for d=−1/2. The invariant maximum follows by multiplying by c. No endpoint convention from the earlier, inclusive formulation is imported.
