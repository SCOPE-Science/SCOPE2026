For Crank–Nicolson on the unit-rate complete bipartite chain \(K_{p,q}\), positivity is lost exactly when the backward-Euler resolvent diagonal on the smaller part falls below \(1/2\). For \(1\le p\le q\) and \(p+q>2\), the sharp step is \(h_*=(p+\sqrt{p^2+4q(p+q-2)})/[q(p+q-2)]\), strictly above the generic SSP bound \(2/q\).
