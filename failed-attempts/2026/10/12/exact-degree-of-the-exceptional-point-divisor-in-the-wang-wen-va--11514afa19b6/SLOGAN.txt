For a general principal bundle in Wang--Wen's vanishing theorem, the exceptional points \(x\) with \(H^0(C,E_\rho(P)((g-1)x))\ne0\) form a nonempty divisor of exact total multiplicity \(r\,g\,(g-1)^2\), where \(r=\dim V\). The formula upgrades their qualitative finiteness theorem to an exact generalized-theta intersection count.
