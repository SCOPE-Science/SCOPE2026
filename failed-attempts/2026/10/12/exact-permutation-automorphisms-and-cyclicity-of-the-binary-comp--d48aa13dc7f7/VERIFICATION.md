# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For even k≥6, C_k={(u,u+parity(u)1)}. Interleaving paired coordinates gives weight 2wt(u) for even-weight u, and constant weight k for odd-weight u. Since k≥6, weight-four words are exactly the sums of the two-pair characteristic vectors P_i+P_j. Their incidence signatures determine the k complementary coordinate pairs intrinsically. Thus every permutation automorphism must permute pairs and optionally exchange positions inside each; preserving an odd-weight codeword forces an even number of exchanges. Conversely every even exchange pattern and every pair permutation preserves both cosets. Hence Aut(C_k)≅C2^(k−1)⋊S_k. An element acting as one cycle of length 2k must induce a k-cycle on pairs and an odd total exchange parity, which is excluded, so no cyclic permutation equivalence. The exceptional k=2 cyclic and k=4 noncyclic cases are correctly separated; full k=4 group is larger.

## originality

FAIL

FAIL: Huffman–Pless, Fundamentals of Error-Correcting Codes (2003), §9.7 and Table 9.8, already identify precisely the component binary code d_{2m} generated by weight-four pair sums, glue coset c=(10)(01)^{m−1}, and the action of its automorphisms on glue cosets. The textbook explicitly gives Aut(d_{2m}) order 2^m m!, the subgroup G0 fixing each glue coset of order 2^(m−1)m!, and says the other half exchanges cosets a and c. After interleaving, C_k=d_{2k}∪(c+d_{2k}); its automorphism group is exactly that classical c-stabilizer, with the even pair swaps and S_k pair permutations. The claimed group structure and no-2k-cycle criterion are immediate from this older explicit glue action. García–Martínez–Rúa (2026) only state a self-reciprocal-factor sufficient condition and do not supply this direct argument, but that does not make a textbook consequence original.

## value

FAIL

FAIL as a new mathematical fact. The code family and its precise glue-coset automorphism stabilizer are textbook objects, and the asserted noncyclicity for even k≥6 follows mechanically from the standard even-flip stabilizer: a 2k-cycle would require an odd pair-swap parity. This package is a useful restatement and correction of the scope of a later paper, but does not contribute a genuinely unknown natural invariant beyond the old structural classification; code reproducibility alone is not independent value.
