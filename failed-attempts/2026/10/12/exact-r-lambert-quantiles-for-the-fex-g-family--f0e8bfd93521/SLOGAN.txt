The 2026 FEx-G quantile equation is exactly an \(r\)-Lambert equation with parameter \(r=-1\), and its unique negative branch gives \(Q_F(u)=G^{-1}(1-e^{r(u)})\). For the FEx-Kumaraswamy model this yields a genuine explicit special-function quantile, replacing the published circular display that still contains the unknown \(x\).
