For equal relaxation and excitation rates \(\mu\), identity-versus-generalized-amplitude-damping discrimination has the exact fixed-resolution Stein exponent \(\Gamma_{s_{\min}}=-2s_{\min}^{-1}\log_2((1+e^{-\mu s_{\min}})/2)\). Its unrestricted limit is \(\mu\log_2 e\), and finite temporal resolution loses \((\mu^2s_{\min}/4)\log_2 e+O(s_{\min}^3)\) from that rate.
