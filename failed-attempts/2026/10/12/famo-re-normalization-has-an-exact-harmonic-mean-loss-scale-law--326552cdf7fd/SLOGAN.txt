FAMO's log-gradient direction is exactly invariant to independent positive task-loss rescaling, but its practical convex-combination re-normalization multiplies the model step by the weighted harmonic mean \(H_w(s)=(\sum_i w_i/s_i)^{-1}\). Thus practical FAMO preserves task-balancing orientation while reintroducing a one-dimensional loss-unit dependence in the effective learning rate.
