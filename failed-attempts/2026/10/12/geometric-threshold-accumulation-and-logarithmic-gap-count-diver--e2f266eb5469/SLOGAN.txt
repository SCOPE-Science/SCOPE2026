In the golden-mean rectangular Bethe–Sommerfeld quantum graph, the exact finite-gap thresholds approach the infinite-gap boundary geometrically: \(g_c-A_N\sim C_\phi\phi^{-4N}\). Consequently the exact number of positive gaps diverges as \(N(g)=\log(C_\phi/(g_c-g))/(4\log\phi)+O(1)\) when \(g\uparrow g_c\).
