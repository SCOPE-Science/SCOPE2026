# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

With Cdot=f(C,B), Bdot=g(C,B), zdot=H(C,B) independent of z, vertical translations commute with the flow. An equilibrium of (f,g) with H=0 lifts to a one-dimensional equilibrium set, excluding attractivity of an individual point by perturbing z(0). For a T-periodic (C,B) orbit, integrate z(t+T)-z(t)=integral_t^{t+T} H(C(s),B(s))ds=Delta independent of t. Thus full T-periodicity iff Delta=0, and otherwise drift nDelta. Existence is conditional on cell equilibria and flux balance; no full-state equilibrium is asserted when H not zero. This is a direct rigorous calculus proof and needs no numerical proxy.

The primary Chaiya et al source already explicitly gives a passive z equation and says in Section 3 that Eq(2.3) is isolated from the 2D cell stability analysis. This is mathematical coverage by a known general integrator principle and its previously stated application, not a newly developed nonstandard lemma; source did not claim 3D point stability that would require correction.
