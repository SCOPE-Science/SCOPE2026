For every non-Hamilton noncommutative centrally finite division ring, the splitting-field Amitsur–Small witness can be chosen so that its contracted left ideal has the exact overideal interval of \(K^2\): one maximal overideal for each point of \(\mathbf P^1(K)\). Since \(K\) is infinite, the contraction lies below infinitely many maximal left ideals, and any two distinct ones intersect exactly in the contraction.
