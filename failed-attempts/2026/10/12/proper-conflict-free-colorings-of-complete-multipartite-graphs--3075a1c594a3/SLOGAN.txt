A proper coloring of a connected complete multipartite graph is proper conflict-free exactly when at least two partite classes contain singleton color classes. If \(t\) parts have order one, then \(\chi_{\mathrm{pcf}}=r+\max\{0,2-t\}\), every color count from this minimum through \(N\) occurs, and an explicit Stirling-number formula counts all surjective colorings.
