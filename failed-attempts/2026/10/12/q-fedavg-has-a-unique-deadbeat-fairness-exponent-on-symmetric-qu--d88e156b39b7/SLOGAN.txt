With two symmetric equal-curvature quadratic clients and one exact local gradient step, source-form q-FedAvg is exactly Newton's method on the q-FFL objective. Among all positive fairness exponents, \(q=1/2\) is uniquely deadbeat: every broadcast point strictly between the two client minimizers reaches the global minimizer in one communication round; all other positive \(q\) have a nonzero cubic local error term.
