For a uniform random mapping, the \(r\)-th factorial moment of the number of \(\ell\)-cycles depends on the total cyclic mass only through the feasibility event \(L_n\ge r\ell\). Fixed points are the unique first-order exception: each fixed-point indicator is independent of \(L_n\) and \(\mathbb E[F_n\mid L_n]=1\), even though \(F_n\) and \(L_n\) are not independent.
