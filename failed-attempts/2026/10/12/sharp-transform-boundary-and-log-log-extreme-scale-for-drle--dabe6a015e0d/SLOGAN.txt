The DRLE law has exact linear-geometric survival \(S_n=(1+cn)e^{-\alpha n/\beta}\), so its probabilistic MGF exists exactly for \(t<\alpha/\beta\), while the hazard approaches \(1-e^{-\alpha/\beta}\) with gap asymptotic to \(e^{-\alpha/\beta}/n\). The same linear prefactor produces the extreme-quantile correction \((\beta/\alpha)\log\log(1/u)\) beyond the leading logarithmic scale.
