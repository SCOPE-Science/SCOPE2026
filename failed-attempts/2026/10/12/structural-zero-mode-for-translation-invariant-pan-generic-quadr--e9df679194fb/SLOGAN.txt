For the \(4+4\) quadratic-form splitting used in the recent dense-primes theorem, translation invariance forces the discriminant matrix \(\Delta\) to have the all-ones vector as a zero eigenvector. Under pan-genericity this zero is simple, so the quadratic form has rank exactly \(7\) and kernel exactly the translation direction; this explains why allowing eigenvalue \(0\) is essential for the new application.
