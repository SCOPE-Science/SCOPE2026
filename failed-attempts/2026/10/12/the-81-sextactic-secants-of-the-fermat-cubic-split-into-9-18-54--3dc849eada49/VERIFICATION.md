# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For a flex-origin elliptic Fermat cubic, S=E[6]\E[3]; triples of S lie on a line exactly when their sum is zero. Independent enumeration of ALL 27 S-points and 81 unordered zero-sum triples in (Z/6)^2, acted on by all 54 maps E[3] ⋊ µ6 with order-three CM matrix (0,-1;1,-1), recovered exactly 9,18,54 orbits and stabilizers 6,3,1, with 9 singleton doubling images, 3 fixed-CM-direction affine-line images each of fibre size 6, and 9 other-direction images each of fibre size 6. The full result also has an elementary group-theoretic proof: every double image is a point or affine F3-line; over a point is one lift triple, over each affine line six lifts from the three nonzero F2-points, and the affine-line stabilizer acts transitively on the six bijections via S3; µ6 fixes one F3-direction and cycles the other three. This checks all finite objects and boundaries, not sampling. No positive-characteristic or abstract incidence automorphism claim.

## originality

FAIL

Moe and Toft arXiv:2412.16993v2 (May 2025) had already classified EXACTLY all 81 sextactic three-secants as B-lines (9), M/N-lines (18), and 54 mixed-cluster lines, and presented the full order-54 monomial group. A one-representative stabilizer analysis yields the same 9,18,54 projective orbits. Thus the headline orbit classification is a direct implication of a previously published exhaustive parameterization. The submitted doubling-image classifier is not directly written in that paper, but its 1/6 fibre arithmetic is an elementary equivariant labeling of those preexisting complete families, not enough to establish a substantively new final headline claim. This is decisive primary full-text prior coverage, not lack-of-access reasoning or merely similar titles.

## value

FAIL

The symmetry types of the 81 Fermat sextactic secants are natural and mathematically interesting, but the exact exhaustive 9 B, 18 M/N, and 54 mixed classification and the full monomial group are already available in a 2025 primary source. Deriving the 9+18+54 orbit count and relabeling those already tabulated classes with a routine doubling-of-torsion invariant is not an independently unknown exact fact or a motivated new classification that would warrant new research acceptance. This judgment does not deny the geometric usefulness of the torsion viewpoint or the validity of its proof; it separates expository value from a new original mathematical contribution.
