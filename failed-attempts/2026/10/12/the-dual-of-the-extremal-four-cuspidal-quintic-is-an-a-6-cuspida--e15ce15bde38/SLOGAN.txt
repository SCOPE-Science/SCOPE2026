The unique four-cuspidal quintic has projective dual \((B^2-4AC)^2+128BC^3=0\), an irreducible rational quartic with one \(A_6\) cusp. Its Gauss map is injective and its Wronskian vanishes only at the four cusp parameters, so the quintic has neither a tangent line shared by two distinct normalization points nor a smooth flex.
