# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The displayed map is exactly Eq. (4.30) of Remark 4.16 in arXiv:2206.00269v5; the reference's v1 pin is wrong, but the scientific source attribution is correct with v5. On W≠0 write x=X/W, y=Y/W and z=Z/W. The map sends (x,y,z) to (x,−y,D(1,x)/(C(1,x)z)); this is self-inverse on CDZ≠0 and gives nonidentity. The polynomial representatives are primitive by coprimality, so its degree and inverse degree are four; in P³ these give both interior multidegrees. The base equations reduce set-theoretically to D=0 and either W=X=0 or CZ=0. Coprimality excludes C=0 away from W=X=0, giving the five concurrent lines. The affine Jacobian on this chart is D/(Cz²); homogenizing the Jacobian by Euler's identity for degree-four forms multiplies it by 4(Cz)^4, giving 4Z²C³D as a global polynomial. Off the seven planes the map is an isomorphism onto the same open set. On the q=CZ planes the general image is [0:0:0:1], and on a D-plane the ratio W:X is fixed and Y varies, giving the indicated line as the Zariski closure of the image. This last statement is about the rational image closure (the regular image omits the common point [0:0:1:0]), not surjectivity of a regular restriction. The frozen script was read only: it prints a factorization but does not assert the claimed determinant; the independent chart calculation supplies that check. The full base scheme and degenerate root collisions are not being certified.

The source already displays the exact ambient rational map and describes it as a projective automorphism inducing the Nikulin involution. Its ambient invariants here are a direct specialization of the established type-one de Jonquières normal form, not a distinct extension or new K3 theorem. In Pan–Simis's notation take the supporting linear map G=[W:X:−Y], q=CZ and f=D; the fiber matrix is [[0,D],[C,0]], and the base reflection fixes both coefficients. Squaring gives a scalar, with the same degree-four primitive formula for the inverse. The normal-form base and contraction descriptions then give the exact support and image types by factoring two squarefree binary forms. The numerical counts and Jacobian factor powers are elementary evaluations for those particular monoids; they are not explicitly tabulated in the source, but no additional classification or K3-specific computation is needed. This is an affirmative standard-form comparison, not a failure inferred from the wrong v1 pin or from an index miss.
