# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

On an S1 K3 double plane, exactly six (-2)-curves and the deck pairing (Proposition 8) force each smooth even-contact conic to be among the three pullback pairs. The polynomial fiber uniqueness follows from unique factorization: equal sextic presentations give (f-f′)(f+f′)=(1-ν)q1q2q3; for ν≠1 an irreducible quadratic factor must divide one of two cubics, leaving too little degree for the remaining two quadrics. Thus ν=1 and f′=±f. Any plane projective automorphism lifts to X, whose entire automorphism group is generated by the deck map, hence the projective stabilizer is trivial. These are valid conclusions with the exact Picard-lattice and smoothness hypotheses.

## originality

FAIL

FAIL: primary-text inspection overturns the self-review novelty claim. Roulleau, arXiv:1909.01909v5, page 18 (PDF page index 17), PROVES the exact same presentation-fiber uniqueness in the proof of Theorem 20: he permutes and rescales the qj, obtains q1q2q3(1-nu)=(f3-f3prime)(f3+f3prime), forces nu=1 and f3=plus/minus f3prime. Proposition 8 states the six (-2)-curves, three conics, and Aut(X)=Z/2 generated by deck. Completeness and plane rigidity are direct corollaries. The original full final claim is therefore already covered, even though the individual corollaries may not have been separately displayed as stand-alone theorems.
