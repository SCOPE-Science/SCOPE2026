For odd-prime powers \(n=p^k\), the full generating graph of \(D_{2n}\) is an isolated-vertex block plus one explicit complete multipartite component, giving a three-term zero forcing polynomial and \(Z=2p^k-2\). Its real symmetric minimum rank is \(2\) for \(k=1\) and \(3\) for \(k\ge2\), so the prime-power family crosses from \(M=Z\) to the strict gap \(Z=M+1\).
