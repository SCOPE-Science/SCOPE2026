The intersection graph of all proper subspaces of \(\mathbb F_2^3\) has zero forcing polynomial \(1204u^8+2100u^9+1792u^{10}+980u^{11}+364u^{12}+91u^{13}+14u^{14}+u^{15}\), with \(Z=M=8\) and real minimum rank \(7\). Its \(1204\) minimum forcing sets split into exactly ten \(\mathrm{GL}(3,2)\)-orbits, of sizes \(28\), four copies of \(84\), and five copies of \(168\).
