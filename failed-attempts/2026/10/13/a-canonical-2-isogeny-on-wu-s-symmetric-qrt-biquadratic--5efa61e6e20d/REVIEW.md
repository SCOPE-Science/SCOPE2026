Nonvanishing smoothness discriminant forces αγ≠0, so the action is fixed-point-free over the algebraic closure. Since char k ≠ 2, the quotient is finite etale degree 2 and Riemann-Hurwitz with g(C)=1 gives quotient genus 1. The fixed field is k(x²,xy): k(C)/k(x²,xy) is generated by x with x²=u and has nontrivial deck involution. Setting u=x²,v=xy gives αu²+(v²+βv+γ)u+αv²=0, verified by symbolic polynomial identity (multiply the original F by x² and substitute). The affine equation represents the function field, with projective normalization needed for the quotient. Geometry excludes characteristics 2 and 3 as asserted.
