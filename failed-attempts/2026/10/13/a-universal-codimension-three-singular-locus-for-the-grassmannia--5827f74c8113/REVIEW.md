# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In each standard Plücker chart the quadric is det(I+BB^T) by Cauchy–Binet; its gradient is 2 adj(I+BB^T)B. At corank one, a nonzero kernel vector v cannot satisfy B^T v=0 since (I+BB^T)v would then equal v. Thus exactly the corank-at-least-two points are singular. For radical R of dimension r, the derivative of the isotropy equations at R surjects onto Sym²(R*) because V/R→R* via q is surjective and the field has characteristic zero. This yields dim OGr(r,n)=r(n−r)−r(r+1)/2, also at maximal isotropy. The nondegenerate (k−r)-plane in R^⊥/R gives a nonempty open Grassmannian fiber of dimension (k−r)(n−k−r). Adding, and taking the maximum over exact radical strata, gives both dimension claims for every admissible r, including r=2. The recorded symbolic checks are finite consistency checks, not the proof.

## originality

FAIL

The all-k singular-set description is equivalent to the earlier all-k singular ideal in Friedman–Hoşten–Rosana. The all-r dimension formula is a specialization of earlier exact rank-control orbit dimensions for symmetric matrices, not just of a generic expected-codimension statement. Both parts are covered without relying on a later catalogue duplicate or on inaccessible literature.

## value

FAIL

The exact codimension is relevant in the Euclidean-distance application and the short chart proof is useful exposition. But the singular set translates an existing ideal, and the dimension assertion is a directly computable case of a stronger rank-control theorem. The record gives no distinct new geometric or optimization consequence after crediting these prior results; its own scope explicitly excludes a stronger ED-generality obstruction.

The dated certificate retains the supplied scientific assessment, sources and limitations.
