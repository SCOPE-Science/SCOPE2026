# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For characteristic-zero n-dimensional right binary Zinbiel A, primary Corollary 4.3 proves every nonzero minimal two-sided ideal M has dimension 1 and AM=MA=0, for A and every quotient. Inductively lifting the quotient flag by M produces two-sided ideals I_j with dim j and AI_j+I_jA subset I_{j-1}; the j=2 preimage step still contains M, so the lift is valid. Repeated right multiplication gives A^{n+1}=0 in the source left-normed convention. The primary homogeneous free-algebra spanning lemma decomposes degree-d words into left-normed products with degree-1 and degree-3 generators/defects; if a+3b=d with m=a+b factors then m>=ceil(d/3), so all bracketings vanish for d>=3n+1. This is a universal inductive proof, not finite dimensional empirical extrapolation. n=1 agrees with A²=0. Source hypotheses apply only characteristic 0.

## originality

FAIL

Smadyarov already proves qualitative binary-Zinbiel nilpotency by the same one-dimensional annihilating-minimal-ideal induction and uses the homogeneous defect spanning lemma; thus the flag and 3n+1 estimate are short corollaries of results explicitly contained in that primary source. The source does not state the flag or numerical bound, but mathematical implication, rather than wording, governs originality. The quantitative estimate requires straightforward extraction of at most three original degrees per left-normed factor, not a new structural lemma; under the audit originality standard the submitted independent claim is substantially covered by the 2026 source.

The flags and linear dimension bounds are mathematically natural invariants and may be useful quantitatively, but in the exact submitted proof they are immediate induction/degree-bookkeeping corollaries of the recently published Corollary 4.3 and Lemma 2.2. With no sharpness, improved constant, new extremal examples or genuinely independent bound argument, this submission does not resolve a materially separate gap beyond the source theorem; mathematical correctness does not by itself justify an independent new result.
