For a finite poset \(P\), the idempotents of \(I(P,\mathbb F_q)\) are counted exactly by the cut-size enumerator of its comparability graph: \(|\operatorname{Idem} I(P,\mathbb F_q)|=\sum_{S\subseteq P}q^{c_P(S)}\). Thus the idempotent-count polynomial records \(\operatorname{MaxCut}(G_P)\) and depends only on comparability, not on the orientation of comparable pairs.
