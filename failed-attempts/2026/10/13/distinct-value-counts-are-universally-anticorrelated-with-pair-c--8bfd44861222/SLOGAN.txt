For iid categorical sampling, the number of distinct observed values \(K_n\) and the number of matching pairs \(C_n\) have an exact covariance that is strictly negative for every nondegenerate probability vector, not only for uniform birthdays. In the uniform birthday regime \(n/\sqrt m\to c\), pair matches and repeat losses become \(L^2\)-equivalent, forcing \(\operatorname{Corr}(K_n,C_n)\to-1\).
