# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The last three conditions are precisely the cover requirement. Two omitted leaves on one side give indistinguishable pendant edges, since all other vertices reach both edges via the shared center. Conversely a selected leaf separates same-side pendant edges; a selected center separates opposite-side pendant edges; and the central edge is separated from a pendant edge by the opposite center or, if it is omitted, any of its forced selected leaves. For an independent count, regard the complement of the generator as the omitted vertices. No more than two can be omitted. The valid two-element omissions are a pair of opposite leaves (pq), or one center and a leaf on the opposite side (p+q). All p+q+2 single omissions and the empty omission work. This proves the polynomial and minima for every p,q>=1, including p=q=1. A separate distance-based enumeration tested 3,600 subsets over 1<=p,q<=4 and agreed on both set feasibility and coefficients.

## originality

FAIL

Although no literal double-star enumerator was found, the central all-set claim for arbitrary p,q>=2 follows mechanically from the existing all-but-one-legs necessity and leaf-generator construction for ordinary edge resolution in Kelenc–Tratnik–Yero, Remark 3 and its proof, together with Tavakoli et al.'s cover definition. This covers unbalanced as well as balanced stars in the two-branching-center regime. The polynomial there is just the count of allowed omissions under that criterion, not an independent invariant calculation. The one-leaf boundary is not expressly covered by the leaf-only construction, so this is not a claim that every frozen sentence was previously published; the boundary adds only a routine adjustment and does not salvage the assigned finding as an original contribution.

## value

FAIL

Double stars and generator counts are natural. In this case the main coefficients are obtained mechanically from the earlier ordinary tree-leg characterization plus elementary cover constraints, and the only extra scope is the routine one-leaf substitution and the three-edge path. No motivated new boundary phenomenon or independently useful unknown exact claim survives that comparison in the frozen finding.
