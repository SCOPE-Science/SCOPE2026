Within the Egrot–Hirsch modified-rainbow scheme, the standard depth-\(n\) representable/nonrepresentable witness pair is exactly size-optimal: the larger algebra must have at least \(2^{4^{n+1}+2^{n+1}+5}\) elements, and their chosen parameters attain this bound. The two optimal witnesses differ in cardinality by exactly a factor of \(2\).
