# Euler characteristic of every ladder Pfaffian variety is a surviving-coordinate count
## Finding
Let
\[
X=(x_{ij})
\]
be a generic skew-symmetric
\[
n\times n
\]
matrix. Let
\[
\mathcal Y
=
\mathcal X_1\cup\cdots\cup\mathcal X_s
\]
be a ladder in the sense of De Negri--Gorla, with ladder-variable set
\[
Y
=
\{x_{ij}:i<j,\ (i,j)\in\mathcal Y\}.
\]
For a vector of positive integers
\[
t=(t_1,\ldots,t_s),
\]
let
\[
I_{2t}(Y)
=
I_{2t_1}(X_1)+\cdots+I_{2t_s}(X_s)
\subset
\mathbb C[Y]
\]
be the corresponding ladder Pfaffian ideal, and put
\[
V_{Y,t}
=
\operatorname{Proj}\!\bigl(\mathbb C[Y]/I_{2t}(Y)\bigr).
\]

Define the set of ladder coordinates killed by the size-two Pfaffian blocks:
\[
K
=
\bigcup_{a:t_a=1}
\{x_{ij}:i<j,\ (i,j)\in\mathcal X_a\}.
\]

Then
\[
\boxed{
\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(V_{Y,t})
=
|Y\setminus K|.
}
\]

If
\[
Y\setminus K=\varnothing,
\]
then the projective variety is empty and both sides are zero.

In particular, whenever
\[
t_a\ge2
\qquad
\text{for every }a,
\]
one has
\[
\boxed{
\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(V_{Y,t})
=
|Y|
=
\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(\mathbb P^{|Y|-1}).
}
\]

Thus every genuinely higher Pfaffian condition is invisible to ordinary Euler characteristic. The invariant changes only when a block contributes size-two Pfaffians, which are linear coordinate equations.

The standard Pfaffian rank varieties are the one-block special case. Hence every nonempty projective skew-symmetric rank locus defined by Pfaffians of size at least four has Euler characteristic equal to that of its ambient projective space.

## Assumptions and scope
The base field is
\[
\mathbb C.
\]

The ladder and the ideals
\[
I_{2t_a}(X_a)
\]
are those of the standard ladder-Pfaffian construction: each
\[
I_{2t_a}(X_a)
\]
is generated by the
\[
2t_a
\]
Pfaffians contained in the square subladder
\[
\mathcal X_a.
\]

The projective variety is taken in
\[
\mathbb P^{|Y|-1}
\]
with homogeneous coordinates indexed by the variables in
\[
Y.
\]

No smoothness assumption is made. Ladder Pfaffian varieties are typically singular; the argument uses only torus invariance and constructible Euler characteristic.

## Proof
Choose positive integer weights
\[
w_i=2^i
\qquad
(1\le i\le n),
\]
and let
\[
\mathbb C^*
\]
act diagonally on the underlying \(n\)-dimensional vector space.

The induced action on a skew coordinate
\[
x_{ij}
\]
has weight, up to an irrelevant common sign convention,
\[
w_i+w_j.
\]
Because binary expansions are unique, the sums
\[
2^i+2^j
\qquad
(i<j)
\]
are pairwise distinct.

Therefore the fixed locus of the ambient projective space
\[
\mathbb P^{|Y|-1}
\]
consists exactly of the coordinate points
\[
[x_{ij}],
\qquad
x_{ij}\in Y.
\]

Every Pfaffian generator is a weight vector for this diagonal action, so the ladder Pfaffian ideal and its projective variety are invariant.

We now determine which coordinate fixed points lie on
\[
V_{Y,t}.
\]

Fix
\[
x_{ij}\in Y
\]
and evaluate every generator at the coordinate point where
\[
x_{ij}\ne0
\]
and every other ladder coordinate is zero.

If
\[
t_a\ge2,
\]
then every generator from
\[
I_{2t_a}(X_a)
\]
is a Pfaffian of size at least four. A skew matrix with only one nonzero off-diagonal pair has rank two, so every Pfaffian of size at least four vanishes. Hence such a block never excludes a coordinate point.

If
\[
t_a=1,
\]
then
\[
I_2(X_a)
\]
is generated by the individual coordinates belonging to
\[
\mathcal X_a.
\]
Consequently the coordinate point
\[
[x_{ij}]
\]
fails the equations of this block exactly when
\[
x_{ij}\in\mathcal X_a.
\]

It follows that
\[
(V_{Y,t})^{\mathbb C^*}
=
\{[x_{ij}]:x_{ij}\in Y\setminus K\}.
\]
Thus the fixed locus is finite and has cardinality
\[
|Y\setminus K|.
\]

For any complex algebraic variety \(Z\) with an algebraic
\[
\mathbb C^*
\]
action,
\[
\chi_c(Z)
=
\chi_c(Z^{\mathbb C^*}).
\]
Indeed, after removing the fixed locus, a finite Rosenlicht stratification and refinement by stabilizer type decomposes the complement into locally trivial pieces with fiber a finite quotient of
\[
\mathbb C^*,
\]
whose compactly supported Euler characteristic is zero.

Since
\[
V_{Y,t}
\]
is projective,
\[
\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(V_{Y,t})
=
\chi_c(V_{Y,t})
=
\chi_c((V_{Y,t})^{\mathbb C^*})
=
|Y\setminus K|.
\]

If every
\[
t_a\ge2,
\]
then
\[
K=\varnothing,
\]
so the value is
\[
|Y|,
\]
the Euler characteristic of the ambient
\[
\mathbb P^{|Y|-1}.
\]

## Verification
The bundled checker generates deterministic families of square subladders inside skew-symmetric matrices of sizes
\[
4\le n\le10.
\]

For each test ladder it chooses mixed Pfaffian sizes, including both linear blocks and blocks of size at least four. At every ambient coordinate point it directly evaluates all relevant Pfaffians by an exact recursive Pfaffian routine.

The checker verifies that a coordinate point survives exactly when its coordinate is outside every block with
\[
t_a=1,
\]
and that all higher Pfaffians vanish at every coordinate point.

These finite checks are regression evidence only. The theorem for arbitrary ladders follows from the rank-two coordinate-point observation and torus localization.

## Relationship to prior work
De Negri and Gorla define ladder Pfaffian varieties from mixed-size Pfaffians in subladders of a generic skew-symmetric matrix and prove that these schemes are reduced, irreducible, arithmetically Cohen--Macaulay, and projectively normal. Their later work computes multiplicity, Hilbert functions, \(h\)-vectors, free resolutions, and Castelnuovo--Mumford regularity for Pfaffian ladder ideals.

The result here concerns a different invariant: ordinary topological Euler characteristic. For the entire ladder-Pfaffian class it collapses to a coordinate count, and every Pfaffian constraint of size at least four contributes nothing to that count.

Targeted searches for Euler characteristics of ladder Pfaffian varieties, mixed-size Pfaffian ladder schemes, and torus-fixed descriptions of these varieties did not locate this surviving-coordinate formula.

## Limitations
Euler characteristic is extremely coarse here. The theorem does not imply that two ladder Pfaffian varieties with the same surviving-coordinate count have comparable singularities, Hilbert series, degree, or cohomology.

The formula depends on the standard coordinate ladder presentation. It does not assert an analogous statement for arbitrary linear sections or arbitrary specializations of skew-symmetric matrices.

The localization proof is short once the diagonal torus action is imposed, so an older or unindexed observation may exist even though the inspected ladder-Pfaffian literature does not state it.
