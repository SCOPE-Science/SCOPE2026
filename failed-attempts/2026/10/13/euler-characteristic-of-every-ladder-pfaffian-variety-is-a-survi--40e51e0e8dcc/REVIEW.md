## correctness

PASS

For every De Negri–Gorla mixed-size ladder Pfaffian ideal over C, a one-parameter diagonal torus with pairwise distinct weights 2^i+2^j preserves each Pfaffian block, and its projective fixed points are exactly coordinate points. Each such point is a skew matrix of rank at most 2, so all Pfaffians of size >=4 vanish there, while 2-Pfaffians kill precisely the coordinates K in t_a=1 blocks. The standard singular-projective C* Euler localization identity gives χ_top(Proj(C[Y]/I))=|Y\K|, including empty locus. Verified rank-two coordinate observations independently on small skew matrices and reviewed exact Pfaffian-coordinate verifier. Finite checks corroborate, not prove, the all-ladder formula.

## originality

FAIL

The claimed invariant is already a direct instance of the general torus fixed-point Euler characteristic theorem for singular projective C*-varieties, applied to the known De Negri–Gorla ladder-Pfaffian coordinate ideals. The additional single-coordinate test is tautologically rank-two and eliminates every Pfaffian of degree at least two; size-two blocks are individual variables. Thus prior general theorem + standard construction imply every stated case with no additional nonstandard Pfaffian geometry. The original De Negri–Gorla paper does not need to state the identical Euler formula verbatim for mathematical implication to cover the result.

## value

FAIL

Once the general fixed-point identity is applied, counting surviving variables is an immediate one-coordinate rank observation, not a new difficult invariant, structural classification or motivated boundary. Different ladder parameters merely alter which degree-one generators kill coordinates; the all-higher-Pfaffian invisibility is automatic from homogeneity. An arbitrary direct substitute of a textbook torus formula into a standard coordinate-presentation class provides insufficient independent mathematical value.
