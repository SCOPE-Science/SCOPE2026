# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

By the classical abelian multiplier formula, for an abelian p-primary component with cyclic factor exponents a_i, M(P) consists of C_{p^{min(a_i,a_j)}} over pairs i<j. An elementary rank-k p-multiplier thus forces k=binom(r,2), and C_p^r gives the converse. For every remaining k, Holt §2 constructs G=N⋊C with N elementary abelian and C cyclic, M(G)=C_p^k; the p^k=4 exception uses a fixed-point-free order-five action. These groups are nonabelian metabelian. Thus delta_p(k)=1 for triangular ranks and 2 otherwise. Checked source proof and independent finite triangular obstruction examples; no finite census is substituted for Holt general construction.

## originality

FAIL

The exact optimization is a direct elementary implication of the two results appearing together in the already published October 1 Holt paper: Proposition 1.2(iii) says M(A)=A wedge A for abelian A, and §2 explicitly constructs a metabelian G with M(G)=C_p^k for every k. The one-line decision between derived length 1 and 2 adds no independent structural theorem. A distinct terminology for the minimum does not confer originality.

## value

FAIL

Minimum derived length is a meaningful invariant in general, but THIS elementary-abelian two-level dichotomy is already mechanically determined by the published classical exterior-square formula and Holt’s construction. No new construction, obstruction, or unexplored natural exact boundary survives. This is a science rejection for prior implication and routine synthesis, not a proof defect.
