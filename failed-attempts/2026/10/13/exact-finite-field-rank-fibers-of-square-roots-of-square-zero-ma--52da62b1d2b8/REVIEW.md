Independently verified the block, centralizer, fiber and rank arguments. X^4=0, and the squares of blocks of lengths 1,2,3,4 have types (1),(1,1),(2,1),(2,2), by splitting the Jordan chain into even and odd positions in any characteristic. Thus 2a+b=r, b+2c+d=m, and k=3a+2b+c. In the commuting endomorphism algebra, the (i,j) block has a_i a_j min(i,j) field parameters. On reduction modulo t, maps from shorter to longer blocks vanish, so invertibility is equivalent to invertibility of the independent diagonal reductions, giving q^D times the U factors. Reduction detects invertibility because t is nilpotent. Two roots of the same type over the same N are conjugate by an element commuting with N (square the conjugacy identity); hence each type contributes the stated integral centralizer index. Taking a=floor(r/2) gives b=0 or 1 and all claimed ranks by varying c; feasibility is m>=b. For any admissible type, k=2r-a+c and 2c<=m-r+2a give the same lower and upper bounds. This covers r=0, m=0, n=1, and characteristic two. The centralizer indices also give q(q-1) at n=3,r=1 and 192 and 576+96 at n=6,r=2,q=2.
