# Exact first-relation degree for anticanonical Fano hypersurfaces in products of projective spaces
## Finding
Let \(F=X_{(a_1,\ldots,a_j)}\subset \prod_{i=1}^j\mathbf P^{n_i}\) be a smooth positive-dimensional hypersurface over \(\mathbf C\), where \(a_i\ge0\), not all \(a_i\) vanish, and \(-K_F\) is ample. Define
\[
M=\left\lceil\max_{1\le i\le j}\frac{a_i}{n_i+1-a_i}\right\rceil.
\]
Then
\[
t_1(R(F,-K_F))=\max\{2,M\}.
\]
Here \(t_1\) is the largest degree of a minimal homogeneous generator of the defining ideal of the standard graded anticanonical section ring. In particular, the upper bound in Proposition 7.17 of arXiv:2610.00709v1 is exact throughout this smooth positive-dimensional Fano hypersurface family.

## Assumptions and scope
The ground field is \(\mathbf C\), as in the cited source. The hypersurface is smooth and positive-dimensional. The Fano condition is exactly that every integer \(n_i+1-a_i\) is positive, because by adjunction
\[
-K_F=\mathcal O_F(n_1+1-a_1,\ldots,n_j+1-a_j).
\]
The statement concerns the invariant \(t_1\) of the anticanonical section ring, not the optimality of every downstream automorphism-extension theorem using \(t_1\).

## Proof
Write
\[
L=\mathcal O_{\prod_i\mathbf P^{n_i}(n_1+1-a_1,\ldots,n_j+1-a_j).
\]
Proposition 7.17 of arXiv:2610.00709v1 identifies the anticanonical ring as \(A/J\), where
\[
A=\bigoplus_{m\ge0}H^0\!\left(\prod_i\mathbf P^{n_i},L^{\otimes m}\right),
\]
where \(L\) is normally presented, and where the homogeneous ideal \(J\subset A\) has
\[
J_m=0\quad(m<M),\qquad J_M\ne0,
\]
and is generated in degree \(M\). The same proposition therefore gives
\[
t_1(R(F,-K_F))\le\max\{2,M\}.
\]
We prove the matching lower bound.

Choose the standard polynomial presentation \(S\twoheadrightarrow A\), and let \(I_A\) be its kernel. Normal presentation means that \(I_A\) is generated by quadrics. Let \(I\subset S\) be the inverse image of \(J\), so \(S/I\cong R(F,-K_F)\).

First suppose \(M>2\). Every element of \(I\) of degree less than \(M\) maps to \(J_m=0\), hence already belongs to \(I_A\). But \(J_M\ne0\), so \(I_M\) contains an element whose image modulo \(I_A\) is nonzero. Such an element cannot lie in the ideal generated by the elements of \(I\) of degrees less than \(M\), because those lower-degree elements generate an ideal contained in \(I_A\). Therefore every minimal homogeneous generating set of \(I\) contains a generator of degree \(M\). Hence \(t_1\ge M\), and the published upper bound gives \(t_1=M\).

Now suppose \(M\le2\). The published upper bound gives \(t_1\le2\). The complete anticanonical system is very ample and gives a nondegenerate embedding, so its defining ideal has no linear forms. The ideal is also nonzero: if it vanished, the anticanonical embedding would identify \(F\) with a projective space \(\mathbf P^N\) and identify \(-K_F\) with \(\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^N}(1)\), contradicting \(-K_{\mathbf P^N}=\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^N}(N+1)\) for \(N\ge1\). Thus there is a minimal relation of degree at least \(2\). Consequently \(t_1=2\).

Combining the two cases proves the formula.

## Verification
The proof uses no finite experiment. Its non-elementary input is exactly the structural calculation in Proposition 7.17 of arXiv:2610.00709v1: the ambient Segre--Veronese section ring is normally presented, the quotient ideal first appears in degree \(M\), is nonzero there, and is generated in degree \(M\). Definition 2.10 in the same paper identifies \(t_1\) with the largest degree among minimal homogeneous ideal generators.

The bundled `verify.py` is a regression check for the arithmetic threshold \(M\), the Fano denominator condition, and representative case splits. It is not used as a substitute for the universal proof above.

## Relationship to prior work
Quedo's Proposition 7.17 proves only the upper bound
\[
t_1(R(F,-K_F))\le\max\{2,M\},
\]
although its proof supplies the stronger structural facts \(J_{<M}=0\), \(J_M\ne0\), and generation of \(J\) in degree \(M\). The argument above extracts the missing minimality statement and shows that the displayed upper bound is always attained.

Targeted searches for the exact equality, for a lower bound on \(t_1\) in this family, and for an anticanonical first-relation formula on product-projective Fano hypersurfaces found the initiating preprint but no prior statement implying the equality. The closest indexed results concerned unrelated weighted-projective Fano invariants or general syzygy bounds rather than this exact anticanonical relation degree.

## Limitations
The statement is restricted to smooth positive-dimensional hypersurfaces in products of ordinary complex projective spaces satisfying the Fano inequalities. It does not assert that the strict numerical inequality used in every automorphism-extension corollary is logically necessary; it proves sharpness only of the intrinsic invariant \(t_1\) entering that criterion. Search-based originality cannot exclude an unindexed equivalent observation.
