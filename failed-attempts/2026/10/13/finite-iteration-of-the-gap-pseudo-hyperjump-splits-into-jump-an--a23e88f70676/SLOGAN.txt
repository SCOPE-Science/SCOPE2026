Cintioli's gap pseudo-hyperjump has two forward-invariant finite dynamics: starting from \(\omega_1^X=\omega_1^{\mathrm{CK}}\), its \(n\)-th iterate has the degree of \(X^{(n)}\); starting from \(\omega_1^X>\omega_1^{\mathrm{CK}}\), its \(n\)-th iterate has the degree of the \(n\)-fold relativized hyperjump tower. No finite orbit crosses the switch, and every orbit is strictly increasing in Turing degree.
