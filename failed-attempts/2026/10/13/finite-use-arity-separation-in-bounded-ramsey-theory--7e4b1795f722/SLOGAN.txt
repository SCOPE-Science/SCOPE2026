The bounded Ramsey hierarchy has a genuine finite-use arity gap: no fixed finite number of applications of \(\mathsf{BRT}^{2}_{2}\) can solve \(\mathsf{BRT}^{3}_{2,4}\). Each bounded pair instance is solved by one ordinary \(\mathsf{RT}^{2}_{2}\) call, so such a reduction would contradict Patey--Shafer's finite-use separation from \(\mathsf{RT}^{2}_{2}\).
