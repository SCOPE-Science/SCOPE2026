# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Ore’s exact criterion (as reproduced in Oller-Marcén) identifies non-arithmetic squarefree integers as n=2m with m odd squarefree and every p|m congruent 1 mod 4. Prabhu Theorem 3 for N=4, k=r−1, residue tuple (1,…,1) then directly yields E_r(x)∼x(loglog x)^(r−2)/(2^r (r−2)! log x) for fixed r≥3. For r=2 the prime number theorem mod4 gives E_2(x)∼x/(4log x). Landau fixed-r formula implies relative deficit (r−1)/(2^r loglog x). Independent direct factorization confirmed Ore equivalence for every squarefree n≤20,000, with exceptional layers {1:1,2:609,3:353,4:34}; this is a finite consistency check, not proof of asymptotics.

The exact arithmetic-number wording is new labeling, not an independent mathematical result; absence of the same printed corollary cannot establish originality.
