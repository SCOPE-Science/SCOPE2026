For a nondegenerate real switching quadric of signature \((p,q)\), the complete endpoint-fixed trajectory classification follows from \(\mathcal Q\cong(S^{p-1}\times S^{q-1})/\{\pm1\}\): depending on the signature, \(\pi_1\) is \(0\), \(\mathbb Z\), \(\mathbb Z^2\), or \(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\). In the source survey, the \(n=19\) and \(n=27\) real switch spaces are tori, while \(n=23\) is simply connected.
