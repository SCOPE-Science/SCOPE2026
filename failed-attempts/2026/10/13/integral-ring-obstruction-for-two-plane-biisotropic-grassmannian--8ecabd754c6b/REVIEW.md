# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

On Gr(2,2n+1), restricting an alternating form gives a section of the rank-two tautological determinant dual, the Plücker line bundle. The two independent forms cut the smooth codimension-two section scheme-theoretically. The first hyperplane can be chosen for a form with one-dimensional radical: vanishing of the differential at an isotropic plane would force the entire plane into that radical. Integral weak Lefschetz across both smooth sections therefore preserves the primitive generator of H^2 since the final dimension is at least four. Its top integral is the ambient Plücker degree. On Gr(2,N), Pieri counts paths in a two-row rectangle of row length N-2; reflection gives the Catalan number C_(N-2). The two degrees have ratio (4n-3)/n, greater than 1 for n>=2. An integral graded isomorphism can only change the signs of the primitive degree-two and top generators. The affine pavings identify integral Chow groups with even cohomology via the cycle map, so this is also a Chow-ring obstruction. The additive isomorphism does not identify multiplication. For n=1 both varieties are points.
