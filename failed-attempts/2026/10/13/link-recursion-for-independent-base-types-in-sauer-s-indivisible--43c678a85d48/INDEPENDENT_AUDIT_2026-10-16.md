# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For 3<=k<n and an independent finite I, every one-point extension adds only k-edges incident to x, hence is precisely a cone of its (k-1)-uniform link. For forbidden irreducible n-subsets (Gaifman complete), any forbidden set in such a cone must include x and corresponds to an irreducible link set of cardinality at least n-1; the converse holds for these boundary sizes (>=k). Free amalgamation realizes the legal diagrams and ultrahomogeneity equates types iff the labeled links agree. At k=3 the (k-1)-age is precisely K_{n-1}-free graphs. Fixed-clique-free graph asymptotics are borrowed from Erdős--Kleitman--Rothschild. Direct independent cone-versus-link enumeration corroborated 3072 small cases. This is a correct restriction, not a novel proof of Sauer’s theorem.

## originality

FAIL

Sauer already supplies the complete one-point type encoding over a finite sockel via (k-1)-ary incidence and a largest-irreducible-substructure rank test in the proof of Theorem 2.5. For an independent sockel the old k-ary part is empty; applying Sauer’s existing test yields exactly the claimed A_{k-1,n-1} link criterion. The subsequent k=3 count is immediate enumeration of legal labeled links and its leading entropy exponent is the classical fixed-clique-free graph theorem. The candidate is therefore a direct specialization/corollary of prior stronger coverage, not an original structural recursion. A prior Resultary finite-type theorem independently already states the analogous triangle-free independent-base case for the different K4-minus-free age; the latter is corroborative only and not substituted for the decisive Sauer implication.

## value

FAIL

The only advertised novelty is a restriction of Sauer’s already available arbitrary-sockel type formula to an independent base, followed by the standard K_r-free graph asymptotic. This is a short direct specialization/renaming rather than a motivated missing structural calculation or independent exact invariant; no new parameter-sensitive classification, complexity bound, proof improvement or boundary phenomenon remains. The result can be a useful explanatory corollary pedagogically, but not a substantively valuable independent new scientific finding under the stated criterion.
