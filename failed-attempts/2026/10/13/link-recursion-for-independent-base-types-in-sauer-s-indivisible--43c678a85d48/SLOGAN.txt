For Sauer's indivisible \(k\)-uniform limits, one-point types over an independent parameter set are exactly the lower-uniformity forbidden-irreducible hypergraphs \(\mathcal A_{k-1,n-1}\). At \(k=3\), this turns the type count into the number of labeled \(K_{n-1}\)-free graphs and gives the corresponding Erdős--Kleitman--Rothschild entropy exponent.
