# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Choose a degree-minimal positive-rank divisor D on the metric banana path Γ, of degree d. Corollary 5.3 supplies a smooth proper connected curve over an algebraically closed complete non-Archimedean field, with skeleton Γ and genus g(Γ), and a positive-rank divisor B whose tropicalization is linearly equivalent to D. Degree is preserved both by tropicalization and by linear equivalence, so gon(X)≤d. The Amini–Baker rank inequalities give r_Γ(trop(A))≥r_X(A) for any divisor A, so any positive-rank algebraic divisor has degree at least d. This proves gon(X)=d for the chosen genus-preserving lift. The equality with geometric gonality follows from Theorem 1.1 of Mascuch–Teixidor i Bigas. The argument is existential; it neither assumes nor implies all lifts are optimal.

The full existence statement is already a direct consequence of the stronger prior genus-preserving divisor-lifting result for banana paths plus standard specialization. The wording need not occur as a named theorem: choosing a divisor of minimal positive-rank degree and applying a universal lower bound to the same lifted curve gives the exact gonality without any new lifting step.
