# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The logical derivation is valid on the primary theorem statements as cited: because ACFA is model-complete, every same-language extension R is model-complete. Choose complete R-prime extending consistent R. Theorem 1.2 of Beyarslan--Kowalski excludes a model companion for R-prime_tau, and their cited Corollary 6.2 transfers existence of a companion for R_tau to every completion R-prime. Contradiction. The independent audit checked the inference and all language/consistency/quantifier restrictions; FULL text of Cor.6.2 could not be retrieved, a specifically recorded premise-verification risk, while the main no-companion theorem is confirmed in the published abstract.

## originality

FAIL

The claimed universal strengthening is the IMMEDIATE composite corollary of two results explicitly cited from one primary paper: for every completion of ACFA, adding a commuting automorphism has no model companion (Theorem 1.2); if a model-complete S has an automorphism expansion with companion, every completion S-prime has one (Corollary 6.2). Every same-language R extension of model-complete ACFA is automatically model-complete. Taking any completion R-prime is exactly the published transfer contrapositive, requiring no independent theorem. Nonidentical wording and omission of the corollary in the source abstract are not novelty.

## value

FAIL

Once the two published statements and model-completeness inheritance are given, the submitted extension theorem takes only a completion and the direct contrapositive. It does not isolate a new nonstandard lemma, unknown invariant, significant special-case obstruction or distinct construction. The broader generalization from complete to incomplete R is precisely what the existing completion-transfer theorem exists to perform. Thus the presentation is a routine textbook-level composition of prior metatheorems, not a separately valuable contribution.

The dated certificate retains the supplied scientific assessment, sources and limitations.
