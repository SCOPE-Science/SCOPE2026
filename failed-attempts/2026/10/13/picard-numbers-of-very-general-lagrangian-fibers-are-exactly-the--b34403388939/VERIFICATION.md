# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Varvak's Corollary 1.3 supplies d pairwise non-isogenous Hodge-generic factors of dimension g/d, and Theorem 1.2 gives the joint group with independent symplectic factors. On H²(1) the invariants consist of the d polarization lines: in each exterior square there is one symplectic invariant and the cross terms have none. Equivalently one may take invariants of the Hodge group on H²; the weight torus of the full untwisted Mumford–Tate group must not be used as if it acted trivially there. Lefschetz (1,1) identifies this dimension with the Picard number, unchanged by isogeny. Proposition 2.8 gives the varying symmetric-power examples for every factorization g=hd, including d=1 and h=1. This proves the claim about very general smooth complex fibers of the specified primitive symplectic varieties, not about the generic fiber over the function field or the attainable counts for smooth total spaces.

## originality

FAIL

Varvak supplies the full classified decomposition and realizations with the same hypotheses. The standard Picard-rank calculation on a product of pairwise non-isogenous Hodge-generic abelian varieties already implies every part of the frozen claim. The inspected source passages do not state the result in Picard-number language, but there is no new classification or example after that calculation. Oguiso concerns a different fiber, and is not used to defeat the claim.

## value

FAIL

The rank formulation is a useful explanatory consequence of Varvak's work, but it is a routine invariant calculation from the classified factors and uses exactly the already constructed realization examples. It does not settle any new attainability question, including the smooth-total-space question.
