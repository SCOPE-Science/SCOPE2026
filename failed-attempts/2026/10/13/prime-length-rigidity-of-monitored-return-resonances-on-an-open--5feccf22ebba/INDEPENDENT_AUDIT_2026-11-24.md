## correctness

The unweighted path on p−1 sites has spectrum ±a_i, a_i=2 cos(πi/p)>0 for 1≤i≤(p−1)/2, with nonzero endpoint sine weight at every eigenvalue. Restricting Uτ to the endpoint cyclic subspace C leaves one vector per distinct sampled phase. Q=(I_C−P)Uτ has norm ≤1 and no eigenvalue of modulus 1: equality of norms under projection would give a Uτ eigenvector orthogonal to the endpoint in C, impossible. Thus Q^t→0 and detection is almost sure. The no-detection probability after t steps is ||Q^tψ||²; tracing the telescoping sum generated by QQ†=I_C−P gives its total over t≥0 as dim(C), the mean measurement count. For rational independence, the vanishing polynomial in RESULT.md lines 43–51 has degree ≤p−1 and zero middle coefficient, whereas the minimal polynomial Φ₂ₚ of ζ=e^(πi/p) has degree p−1 and a nonzero middle coefficient. All relation coefficients must vanish. The positive energy gaps have nonproportional distinct coefficient vectors 2e_i, e_i+e_j, e_i−e_j (i<j for the last two); different vectors cannot give rationally related gaps. At any positive sampling time only one gap magnitude may resonate, with one opposite-energy pair for 2a_i and two disjoint pairs for each of the sum and difference types; off-ladder there is no collision. This gives m² disjoint ladders and the stated counts, with the two-pair branch empty at p=3. The frozen floating-point checks (220 ladder representatives and 43 Lyapunov means) were inspected in verify.py and VERIFICATION.md, not run; they do not certify nonoverlap at arbitrary multiples or unbounded sizes.
