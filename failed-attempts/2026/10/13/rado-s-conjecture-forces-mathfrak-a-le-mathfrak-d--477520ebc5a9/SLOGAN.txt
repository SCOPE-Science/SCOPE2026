Rado's Conjecture forces \(\mathfrak a\le\mathfrak d\), so it cannot coexist with \(\mathfrak d<\mathfrak a\). In Honzik's random-algebra model this sharpens the cardinal-invariant pattern to \(\mathfrak a=\mathfrak d=\operatorname{non}(\mathcal N)=\omega_1<\operatorname{cov}(\mathcal N)=\mathfrak c=\omega_2\).
