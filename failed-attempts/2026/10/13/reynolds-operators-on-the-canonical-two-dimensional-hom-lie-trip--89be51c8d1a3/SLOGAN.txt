For the canonical two-dimensional Hom–Lie triple system \([e_1,e_2,e_2]=e_1\) with \(\alpha=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)\), every Reynolds operator in characteristic different from \(2\) lies on one of three diagonal affine lines. Over odd \(\mathbf F_q\) there are exactly \(3q-2\) operators, all fixed by automorphism conjugacy.
