# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For a Polish group G with ample generics, the Kechris–Rosendal automatic-continuity theorem applies to any abstract homomorphism into a separable target. If L is dense in G, continuity implies φ(G)⊂closure φ(L), while the reverse closure inclusion follows from L⊂G. Every finite set in φ(L) lifts to finitely many generators of the locally finite L, hence generates a finite group. If H is torsion-free, every φ(l)=1, and Hausdorffness of H forces φ(G)={1}. Siniora–Solecki supply precisely ample generics plus dense locally finite L for all stated free-homogeneous finite-relational M. Nonseparable targets and homomorphisms to non-Hausdorff torsion-free topological groups are outside scope.

## originality

FAIL

The claimed universal image-closure theorem is immediately implied by the conjunction of the two precisely cited earlier theorems plus elementary density, functoriality of local finiteness, and torsion-freeness. Explicit novelty of wording is not enough to distinguish a covered corollary; no additional nontrivial lemma remains.

## value

FAIL

The result joins automatic continuity with the elementary fact that a dense locally finite subgroup has locally finite image; its only torsion-free application is immediate from definitions. No new motivated structural lemma, quantitative boundary, or independently unknown invariant is isolated. This is a routine corollary of the two credited theorems, rather than a valuable additional mathematical gap.
