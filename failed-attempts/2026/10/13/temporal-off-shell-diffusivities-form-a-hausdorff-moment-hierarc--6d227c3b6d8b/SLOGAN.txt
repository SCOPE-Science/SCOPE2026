At zero wave number, the temporal off-shell diffusivity coefficients are a matrix Hausdorff moment sequence, so their block-Hankel matrices are positive and adjacent scalar ratios give monotone finite-order bounds on the coupled relaxation edge. In the anchor's Fokker-Planck model the coefficients are exactly \(C_{n+1}\mathfrak D^{n+1}\), making the ratio \(4\mathfrak D-6\mathfrak D/(n+3)\) converge explicitly to \(\tau_g=4\mathfrak D\).
