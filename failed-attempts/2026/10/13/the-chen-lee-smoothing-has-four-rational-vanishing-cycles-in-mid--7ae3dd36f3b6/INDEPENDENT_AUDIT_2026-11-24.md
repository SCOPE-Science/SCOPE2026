# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Chen–Lee Theorem 1.1 (4) and Proposition 4.6 give the actual smoothing to the Picard-rank-one prime Fano of genus 10, not just a numerical candidate. Kawasaki's cohomology calculation gives the central fiber rational cohomology of projective three-space. Genus 10 is anticanonical degree 18; the known intermediate Jacobian of X18 is an abelian surface, giving h^(1,2)=2 and b₃=4 by Fano Hodge vanishing. Connectedness, Picard rank one and Poincaré duality give rank one in the even degrees on the smooth fiber. A relatively ample Cartier multiple of the relative anticanonical class, restricted to the fibers, gives compatible nonzero powers in degrees 2,4,6 (top self-intersection is positive). Thus the even specialization maps are isomorphisms, including degree zero by the unit. Proper nearby cycles identify the nearby hypercohomology with cohomology of a nearby smooth fiber. For the stated unshifted triangle, each vanishing group is an extension of the cokernel of specialization in its degree by the kernel in the next degree. Every kernel vanishes and the only nonzero cokernel is H³(Y₁₀,Q), of rank four. This proves concentration, polynomial difference 4u³ and Euler change −4. The polynomial difference alone, outside this specialization argument, would not prove concentration.

The cited paper's local four-dimensional cover cohomology is not this group, nor was a literal prior statement of the global claim found. The reason for FAIL is implication from known results, not a conflation of local with global, a keyword miss, or an unavailable table page.
