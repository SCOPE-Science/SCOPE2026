# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

A many-one degree of the empty or full set consists of one set. All other recursive sets with nonempty complement reduce many-one to one another using computable membership and fixed witnesses; Richter–Stephan–Zhang establish exactly three finite-one degrees for that degree and the global at-most-countable bound. Cintioli proves every nonrecursive many-one degree has one or infinitely many finite-one degrees. Thus the exhaustive recursive/nonrecursive split yields exactly 1,3,aleph_0, with 3 unique to the nontrivial recursive degree; examples of aleph_0 follow from nonrecursive nonirreducible constructions. The inference is logically sound.

## originality

FAIL

The whole conclusion is a direct, routine consequence of two explicitly acknowledged primary theorems: Cintioli Corollary 7 excludes every nonrecursive finite cardinality >1; Richter–Stephan–Zhang identify the sole three-degree recursive case and bound the global number by countability. No new construction, nontrivial lemma, or unknown case is added. Equivalent statement is already logically covered even though the combined boxed spectrum is not identically worded in either paper.

It resolves no additional mathematical uncertainty and does not establish any new structural invariant. An independently unknown theorem would require more than formatting this immediate corollary; correct synthesis alone is insufficient for the required value bar.
