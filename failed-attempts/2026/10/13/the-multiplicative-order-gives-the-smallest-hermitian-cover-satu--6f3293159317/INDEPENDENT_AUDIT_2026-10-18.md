Harashita Lemma 7.7 provides the diagonal Fermat quotient with cover degree e^(2m+1), e=(q+1)/N prime to p, and Proposition 7.9 gives the exact p-local q^m bound and ℓ-adic unimodularity for ℓ∤e. The bound is not asserted sharp for the lattice itself.
