For a supersingular even-dimensional Fermat hypersurface, the least Hermitian cover is controlled by half the multiplicative order \(d=\operatorname{ord}_N(p)\). It gives the canonical \(p\)-local saturation bound \(p^{md/2}\) for the pushforward-generator lattice, and this is the smallest bound supplied by Harashita's diagonal Hermitian-cover construction.
