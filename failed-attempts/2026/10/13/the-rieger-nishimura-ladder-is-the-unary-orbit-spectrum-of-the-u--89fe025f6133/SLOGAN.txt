For the universal homogeneous finite-Heyting Fraïssé limit \(L\), unary automorphism orbits are exactly graded by the finite Rieger–Nishimura algebras: there are two orbits with \(|\langle a\rangle|=2\) and exactly one orbit for every size \(n\ge3\). Thus the unary orbit series is \(2z^2+z^3/(1-z)\).
