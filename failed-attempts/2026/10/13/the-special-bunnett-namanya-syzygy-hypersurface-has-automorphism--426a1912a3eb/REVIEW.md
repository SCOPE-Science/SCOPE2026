The four coefficient quadrics are basepoint free, so the first projection is a projective-plane bundle and the two ambient hyperplane restrictions form a Picard basis. A curve in a fibre of each projection shows that the nef cone is their nonnegative span. Its primitive rays are distinguished by the dimensions of their complete linear systems, 2 and 3; these systems are the two projections (restriction of ambient sections is an isomorphism, by the hypersurface sequence). Thus every automorphism extends to the product. Since the coefficients are linearly independent, the action on the second factor is uniquely determined projectively by the action on their span. Passing to the dual representation, the annihilator pencil has ideal z2(z0,z1) = (z2) ∩ (z0,z1). A pencil stabilizer fixes its reduced line and off-line point individually, and any projectivity fixing that pair preserves the full degree-two space of its ideal. So the group is the block diagonal group modulo common scalars. The map (A,c) ↦ A/c identifies it with GL₂ as an algebraic group. This gives connectedness, tangent dimension 4, and the displayed two-dimensional torus is maximal. Independently expanding the derivatives of z0z2 and z1z2 shows that the four off-block entries and only those entries must vanish. The recorded script's two polynomial-identity assertions do not check the ideal intersection; the global argument does.
