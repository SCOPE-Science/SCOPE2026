# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Correct over every field, with closure understood as geometric Zariski closure of the automorphism locus, not closure of the finite group of rational points. In the basis e=e1−e2, f=e2, z, only [e,f]=z survives. Preservation of the derived line gives a free central row and quotient block A=[[a,b],[c,d]]; the equations eliminate t=ad and leave ac=bc=bd=0. This is the intersection of the coordinate ideals (b,c), (a,b), (c,d); each component with the two central coordinates is A^4. Their pairwise dimensions are 3,3,2 and triple dimension 2. The principal open ad≠0 of the diagonal component is exactly the unit locus. Inclusion–exclusion gives 3L^4−2L^3 and exponentiating extension-field counts gives the asserted zeta function. In the two singular row planes each nonzero block admits q central rows of rank one and q²−q rows of rank two; the zero block and units give the remaining ranks. Each nonzero singular projection has one of the two row-type image lines, q complementary kernels and q choices of lift, so each lift occurs q times. A singular quotient block is nilpotent iff its trace is zero; the two trace-zero lines meet only at zero, and the central row remains free. An independent basis-product enumeration at q=2,3,5 checked rank counts (1,15,12,4), (1,56,96,36), (1,264,960,400), idempotent totals 10,20,52, nilpotent totals 12,45,225 and 2q proper retracts with q idempotents per retract. The field-independent proof, not these tests, establishes all quantifiers. The singular components meet the automorphism closure; only their points outside the diagonal component are outside the closure.

This is demonstrable mathematical coverage, not a conclusion about chronological priority.
