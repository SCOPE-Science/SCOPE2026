# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In the nonpure cases the surviving pure powers cover the source and its closed image. Ordinary vertex charts retain their two linear coordinate ratios except at the tangent vertex. In that chart the omitted u=y/x is the only missing monomial of the full affine semigroup: v,u²,uv,u³ generate every other monomial for d≥3. Thus R=ℂ+I⊂B=ℂ[u,v], I=(v,u²)B. B is finite birational and B/R has length one, generated by the class of u. A nonzero constant term prevents a member of R from multiplying u into R, so the conductor equals I. The four stated generators are independent modulo the square of the maximal ideal I; the localized normalization is the regular ring at (u,v), so the inclusion and length-one cokernel give depth one for R by the depth lemma. For n≥1, I^n as an R-ideal equals I^n as a B-ideal: any B coefficient can be absorbed into the first factor in a product of n elements of I. For v-exponent j=0,…,n−1, the excluded u-exponents run from 0 to 2(n−j)−1; hence ℓ(B/I^n)=n(n+1), and subtracting one gives the asserted Hilbert–Samuel function at n=q+1, including q=0, and multiplicity two. Completion preserves these data. The length-one normalization quotient also subtracts one from the intrinsic projective Euler characteristic. For the pure omission x^d the lone simple base point is [1:0:0]. On its blowup the sections x^(d−1)y and x^(d−1)z cover the exceptional divisor and pull back their target charts to the ordinary two blowup charts. For the first of these the ratios yield both u=y/x and w=z/y, with all other ratios polynomial in u,w; the second chart is symmetric and y^d,z^d cover away from the base point. L=dH−E gives L²=d²−1 and −K·L=3d−1, hence the stated Riemann–Roch formula. The other cases have degree d², and the exceptional exponent patterns have three and six permutations. The frozen finite checker and recorded output were read only; these analytic arguments establish the unbounded degree and length ranges.

## originality

FAIL

Maddox–Pandey already compute the exact graded monomial omissions in each regime for arbitrary variable count and all relevant d. Their semigroup description implies the three projective atlas types and the local and global invariants by degree-zero localization and elementary ideal arithmetic. The distinction between cone and projective singularities is real but does not break this explicit implication. The failure is based on positive prior coverage, not on the unresolved historical access.

## value

FAIL

These are naturally motivated geometric invariants and the exposition usefully distinguishes a singular projective point from the cone vertex. As a new finding, however, it adds only the standard projective interpretation and straightforward exact invariant computations of the already determined rings. The precise local Hilbert–Samuel function is mechanically implied by the known single affine hole, so no standalone unestablished but valuable remainder is validated.

The dated certificate retains the supplied scientific assessment, sources and limitations.
