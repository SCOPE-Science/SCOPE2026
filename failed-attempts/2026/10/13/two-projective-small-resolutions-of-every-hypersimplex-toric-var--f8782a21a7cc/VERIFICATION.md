# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

FAIL

The central construction works, but the frozen Finding asserts without a qualifier that the two exceptional fiber dimensions differ. At the admitted case (k,n)=(2,4) both fibers are P^1; throughout n=2k they have equal dimension and both maps are Goresky–MacPherson small. This also qualifies the 'only for the selected orientation' wording in the Limitations. The proof's explicit dimensions themselves are correct. At S the dual semigroup consists of integer transfers from S to its complement, generated by the e_j-e_i, hence the chart is the rank-one matrix cone, smooth off its origin. Each perturbation has exactly one free coordinate at each vertex, with signed coordinate normals omitting it forming an integral basis of Z^n/Z1. Minimizing a generic functional orders coordinates just as maximizing its negative; the greedy extremizers give the refinement, with no new rays. The incidence charts at a chosen free coordinate yield precisely P^(k-1) or P^(n-k-1) over each fixed point, and there is an isomorphism away from those points. The sole local ray relation requires k=n-k for a canonical support function; then integer values realize it and absence of exceptional divisors gives crepancy. Otherwise X is not Q-Gorenstein, so usual crepancy is unavailable. With r=min(k,n-k), 2(r-1)<n-1; substituting the larger fiber dimension shows that orientation is GM-small exactly in the balanced case. The frozen CHECKER.py and output were inspected, not run: 42 finite rational box-slice checks, 7540 vertices, not proof of the general fan or canonical assertions. Frozen attestation/audit/Lean statuses were not_performed.

## originality

FAIL

The core construction is already covered as the direct adjacent-chamber case of Dolgachev–Hu's VGIT variation theorem, Theorem 4.2.7 / 0.2.5, for the diagonal scaling torus of (P^1)^n. The box model is exactly the ordinary weight slice of that very quotient in each chamber, not a different globalization. The equal-weight tangent representations identify both local models and exceptional fibers, and canonical and GM-smallness thresholds are direct consequences of those dimensions and the standard toric canonical criterion. This verdict is by implication and identification of the actual maps, not a claim that the source states them verbatim under the name hypersimplex.

## value

FAIL

The example is a useful exposition of elementary VGIT for a product of projective lines. As a research finding, the valid package is exactly the two ordinary adjacent variations, with their standard rank-one fibers and immediate canonical and smallness thresholds, rather than an independent unknown invariant or new globalization. The apparent difference of the fiber dimensions also vanishes on the balanced boundary; correcting that qualifier would leave the covered core and not produce a valuable new claim. No repair is submitted.
