# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The square map in the stated column convention is A applied to coordinatewise Frobenius, so it does not kill a nonzero vector. Closure of a line generated by v is exactly v²=λv with λ≠0, and λ⁻¹v is its only nonzero idempotent. With two fixed lines as a basis, Frobenius has diagonal semilinear action; the mixed directions solve one nonzero coordinate ratio equation and give exactly one additional line. Thus r∈{0,1,3}. A fixed line imposes q(q−1)² choices on A; fixing two distinct lines gives (q−1)² choices because their Frobenius images are independent. Summing over lines and unordered line pairs gives first moment N and pair total N/2 for N=|GL₂(F_q)|, hence the three claimed counts. This includes q=2 and counts labeled structure matrices, not isomorphism classes. The polynomial follows because the only remaining subspaces are zero and the whole algebra. Inspected RESULT.md, VERIFICATION.md and checker.py without executing the supplied checker. Fresh direct enumeration of nonzero vector idempotents and projective closure in binary polynomial fields (moduli x+1, x²+x+1, x³+x+1) gave histograms (2,3,1), (60,90,30), (1176,1764,588) at q=2,4,8, agreeing matrix by matrix on lines versus idempotents. This is bounded corroboration, not the proof.
