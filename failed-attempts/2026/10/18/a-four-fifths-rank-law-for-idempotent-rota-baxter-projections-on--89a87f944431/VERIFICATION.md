# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For an idempotent, the identity reduces to closure of image and kernel under the Lie bracket, including in characteristic two. A center-free summand is the graph of a functional on an isotropic subspace of dimension at most m; hence exactly one summand contains the center. For a center-free b-space its graph functional has q^b choices and its projected complement has q^{b(2m−b)} choices. Counting isotropic frames and dividing by the number of ordered bases gives the stated product; the two summand orderings give disjoint symmetric ranks including 0 and 2m+1. Factoring out the leading exponent leaves a product quotient uniformly bounded above and below for fixed q. The exponent is a constant minus (5/2)(b−(8m+3)/10)^2; comparison with a nearest integer and summation give bounded fluctuations with discrete-Gaussian tails. The involution P↦id−P gives the exact equal weights and the asserted limiting rank fractions. Independent arithmetic at (m,q)=(2,2) gives (1,240,960,960,240,1), agreeing with the frozen replay.
