For discounted first-hitting coverage on every unbalanced complete bipartite graph with both parts of size at least two, the minimum source count lies within one of a closed two-mechanism bound: pinning part of the smaller side or balancing source densities across the two sides. The minimizing mechanism changes at a sharp aspect-ratio boundary \(b/a=1/\lambda\) below the fidelity-squared threshold, and the additive-one integrality loss is attained already on \(K_{3,4}\); an exact binomial polynomial counts all optimal placements.
