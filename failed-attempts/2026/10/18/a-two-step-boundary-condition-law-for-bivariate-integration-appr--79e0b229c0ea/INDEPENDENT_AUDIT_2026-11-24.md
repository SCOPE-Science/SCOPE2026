# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

JJ* and BB* have covariance kernels min(x,y) and min(x,y)−xy, the Green kernels for Dirichlet–Neumann and Dirichlet–Dirichlet boundary data. The reciprocal square roots of the differential eigenvalues give the claimed singular ladders, not eigenvalues of J or B. Hilbert-space tensoring and optimal rank truncation give s_k=1/(π²X_k) with ordered products counted with multiplicity; zero singular values from kernels do not intervene. An independent parity reduction verifies the counting constants: for D the ordinary ordered divisor sum, N_BB(X)=D(X), N_JB(X)=D(2X)−D(X), and N_JJ(X)=D(4X)−2D(2X)+D(X). The classical asymptotic gives exactly B_r and O(√X); ties are at most the divisor count of 4X_k and therefore O(√X_k). Hence k=X_k(log X_k+B_r)+O(√X_k) and X_k~k/log k. Setting w=k/X_k gives w+log w=log k+B_r+O(X_k^(−1/2)); the inverse has Lipschitz constant ≤1. This recovers the printed remainder and the elementary Lambert expansion gives both adjacent differences and the two-step limit. The frozen integer/rank checker was inspected, not run, and is not evidence for the infinite asymptotic without the proof.

The mathematical claim is correct but covered by a mechanical reduction to the established ordinary Dirichlet divisor summatory theorem on exact standard tensor spectral ladders. The all-integer, odd-by-integer, and odd-by-odd counts are three fixed inclusion–exclusion combinations of one classical function; its standard two-term formula already fixes every boundary constant. The Lambert remainder is a scalar inverse of that same count with its established error, not an independent new spectral estimate. The failure is demonstrated coverage by implication, not a claim that a paper was found that explicitly prints the triplet in these exact operator terms.
