Conditional on the age and averaged over the fixed IID reset template, the voter dual gives a connected pair product only when the two walks coalesce. Their separation has jump rate one per direction; the bounded Laplace solution and substitution s=(1−v)²/v give the two beta integrals, convergent exactly for ℓ>p.
