# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

On each half the branch lengths are k copies of 1/μ and one of θ/μ, each onto the interval interior with an affine inverse. Inverse images multiply Lebesgue measure by branch length, so summing the lengths gives invariance and composing gives independent branch labels. Shrinking inverse cylinders give a generating coding up to the null branch-boundary preimages and hence the claimed one-sided isomorphism. Reflection pairs the lengths, so writing the branch label as side and index factors its probability as the fair bit law times the stated index law. This proves output-path nonidentifiability at fixed Lebesgue initialization. Shannon entropy of the generating partition gives log μ−(2θ/μ)log θ. The full symbolic shift has entropy log(2k+2); the metric gap is the divergence of the index law from the uniform law. Differentiation gives dh/dθ=k log(1/θ)/(k+θ)² and the two endpoint limits on each strip give the asserted monotonicity and even-parameter jumps. These are symbolic one-sided limits, not assertions about the pointwise discontinuous map at an undefined even parameter. Odd integral parameters are admitted by the mathematical definition, unlike the implementation's stated parameter domain.

## originality

FAIL

The signed generalized Lüroth theorem already gives the full generating Bernoulli coding with exact probabilities equal to interval lengths and the corresponding entropy. The earlier papers give exactly the normalized modulo branches and the side readout. Choosing their branch intervals and their left-increasing/right-decreasing orientations in that theorem directly gives the frozen system. Equal-length pairs immediately factor the exact branch probabilities into a fair bit and the stated independent index; the scalar profiles use just the known length list and alphabet size. No separate substantive new claim survives the implication check. This is not a finding that the exact parameter formulas were printed in the triggering paper, and does not rely on inaccessible 2013 material.

## value

FAIL

It is useful to distinguish empirical randomness tests from an exact measure statement, and the math is a sound explanatory application. But for this already-defined pair of mirrored full-branch partitions the exact bit law, entropy and jump are mechanical readouts of interval probabilities and alphabet size. No independent unknown dynamical obstruction, different observation law, or nontrivial exact invariant remains after the general result is applied. The continuum assumptions also give no new practical finite-precision or security conclusion.
