# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Exponential pathwise contraction and the stationary alternating renewal law give a unique stationary position. The two-run update is Y'=2(1-C)+BCY; the iid product tends to zero and comparison to the telescoping sum of 2(1-BC) gives 0<Y<2. Occupation within an independent positive run gives the equilibrium-age kernel, with the factor 1/2 for sign. Testing stationary spatial current on the whole real line shows its distributional derivative is zero; compact support then forces zero current and p_-/p_+=y/(2-y). For the inverse moment, the stated Laplace bootstrap is sound: C<1/2 yields exp(-t), while C>=1/2 gives an O(t^-m) endpoint factor. Splitting the B integral at order 1/t yields the exponent min(s,r) with arbitrarily small loss from logarithms. Iteration reaches any exponent below r+m, giving all inverse moments of order 0<p<r+m. In the normalized age kernel, for y<Y<2 the log(Y/y)/log(1/y) ratio is uniformly bounded; dominated convergence gives exactly r^m E[Y^-r]/(2m!). Symmetry and integration give the remaining claims, including m=r=1.

The pointwise density is stronger than a bare tail asymptotic, but it follows directly from that indexed entrance-moment theorem and the existing equilibrium-age occupation/current representation (explicit implication below). The m=1 case is previously known. The more extensive inverse-moment bound in the proof is supporting analysis, not a distinct complete sharp moment classification claimed in the Finding. This is a coverage judgment, not an assertion that the later-catalogued record establishes earlier priority.
