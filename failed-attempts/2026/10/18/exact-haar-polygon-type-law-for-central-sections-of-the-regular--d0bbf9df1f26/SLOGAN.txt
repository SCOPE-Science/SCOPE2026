A uniformly oriented central slice of the regular dodecahedron is almost surely a hexagon, octagon or decagon, with exact probabilities \(10-15(\alpha+\beta)/\pi\), \(15(2\alpha+\beta)/\pi-15\) and \(6-15\alpha/\pi\), where \(\alpha=\arccos(1/3)\) and \(\beta=\arccos(\sqrt5/3)\). Its normal sphere has precisely \(20\) hexagonal, \(60\) triangular and \(12\) pentagonal spherical chambers, whose exact areas give the entire distribution.
