# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Reconstructed the stratification for p≥5 over the finite field and its algebraic closure. For a unit coefficient, nilpotence is equivalent to 1 lying in the image (the kernel is one-dimensional), or equivalently f⁻¹ having no t^(p−1) term. For the converse choose the antiderivative with zero constant term; its nonzero linear term then gives a valid change of coordinate in the local algebra. Unit centralizers are scalar lines by the quotient rule. Linear-order coefficients are not nilpotent; order ≥2 raises the degree filtration. At minimal nonlinear order k≤h, the first possible term of fg′−gf′ has coefficient (ℓ−k)f_k g_ℓ, forcing proportionality modulo t^(p−k+1). Each normalized truncated direction has p−2k free coefficients; for each such direction the m entries have k coordinates each and at least one leading scalar must be nonzero. The order ≥h+1 locus is abelian by degree. These disjoint strata give the stated sum. Over the closure the low strata have affine parameters with a nonzero scalar vector, giving dimensions p−2k+mk; the unit stratum has dimension p−2+m and the high one mh. Pivot charts recover the normalized direction regularly. The largest dimension is attained at k=h and exceeds the unit dimension by (m−2)(h−1). The finite checker was inspected, not rerun; its only in-domain exhaustive field is F_5, and it does not check the geometric dimension.
