# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Halving b gives the affine recurrence with constant 1; the displayed Pell formula satisfies it and the two zero initial values. Squaring gives the five bases 1, lambda^(±1), lambda^(±2), constant term 5/64, and unit coefficients for all four nonconstant bases at every odd prime. The monic characteristic polynomial has constant -1, so any five consecutive windows generate the same gcd as the full sequence. For the least period modulo p, the norms of lambda^j-1 for j=1..4 exclude collisions except at 3 and 7; in the split algebra the coordinates of lambda are inverse, so a collision in one coordinate implies a collision in both. A unit Vandermonde then forces lambda^h=1 for any period h, and for p=3,7 the direct square sequences (0,0,1,1) and (0,0,1) give the exact periods instead. Window differences force that period to divide k; the unit geometric-sum denominators then leave 5k/64 modulo p. Lifting lambda^k-1 makes every nonconstant window term vanish one p-power beyond the constant term when p is not 5 and p divides k. At 5, lambda^3=-1 mod 5, its order is 6, and lambda^6-1=19600+13860*sqrt(2) has exact valuation 1. Odd-prime binomial lifting preserves the first nonzero normalized coefficient and gives exact valuation 1+v_5(k). If all windows vanished one level deeper, their differences and the invertible five-mode matrix at 5 would force the lambda mode deeper, a contradiction. The argument excludes every other odd prime case and makes no 2-adic claim.

A later complete factorization independently confirms how much of the statement overlaps the catalog; it is not used as evidence of earlier priority.
