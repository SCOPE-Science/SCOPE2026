For the quintic graph \(\{(t,t^5):t\in\mathbb F_q\}\) in characteristic greater than five, every nonzero-first-coordinate two-sum fiber has multiplicity \(0,1,2,3\), or \(4\) with an explicit quadratic-character census. The resulting exact additive energy is \(4q^2-6q+3-3\chi(-1)(q-1)\): the full fiber distribution sees both \(\chi(-1)\) and \(\chi(-2)\), but the fourth Fourier moment forgets \(\chi(-2)\).
