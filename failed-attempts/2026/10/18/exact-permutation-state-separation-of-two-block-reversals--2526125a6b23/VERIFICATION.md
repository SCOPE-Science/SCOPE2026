Different word permutations differ at some initial point, and an accepting singleton then separates the two endpoints; identical word permutations cannot be separated. Write H_m for the normal subgroup generated by m-th powers. Two sets of powers commute pairwise exactly when their generated subgroups centralize each other. For S_3 the possible H_m are S_3, A_3 and 1; the two nontrivial choices centralize one another only when both are A_3. For S_4 the choices are S_4 for odd m, A_4 for even m not divisible by 3, V_4 for m≡6 mod 12, and 1 otherwise. Conjugation by a 3-cycle is nontrivial on V_4, so only the V_4/V_4 nontrivial pair centralizes. S_1 and S_2 are abelian. For d≥5, E_d is the exponent; if it does not divide m, a cycle witnesses a nontrivial power. Odd m retains all transpositions and even m gives a nontrivial normal subgroup of the simple A_d. Both nontrivial possibilities therefore contain the same nonabelian A_d. This proves the exact lower and upper thresholds, and the LCM increases precisely at a prime power, giving the stated corollary for r=s=E_N, N≥4.
