## correctness

PASS

Conditional on the first k arrows, finishing the permutation pairs the free heads and tails of n−k open path components uniformly; the resulting expected cycle count is H_(n−k) plus the already closed cycles. For a specified r-cycle avoiding k, at least one of the remaining k−r images is at least k; exchangeability then gives conditional record probability 1/(k−r). A nonsingleton cycle through k excludes the record, while the fixed point at k contributes joint probability (k−1)!(n−k)!/n! = 1/(k binom(n,k)). The identity binom(k−1,r)/(k−r)=binom(k,r)/k cancels all terms of length r<k, leaving the claimed kernel, including zero at k=1. At k=n the contribution is 1/n−1/n²; at n−1 it is 1/n²+O(n⁻⁴); at k=2 it is 1/(2n²)+O(n⁻³). Bound the next boundary terms individually and the interior by H_n/binom(n,4). The independent record ranks and cycle-count marginal give common variance H_n−H_n^(2), proving the correlation asymptotic.
