# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The established one-dimensional spectral bands have equal masses and exact p-step returns, with the stated open-lower/closed-upper slope convention. Independence is preserved by the shift and the single coordinate update. The map then advances the product cells by the displayed affine permutation and F^(dp) is exactly the product of the p-step one-dimensional returns. Exactness follows by tensoring on rectangular simple functions and extending by L¹ contraction; a map with an exact positive power is exact. Thus a least cycle of length ℓ is one ergodic component under the natural product probability and gives precisely ℓ mutually singular phase limits in the stated a.c. sense. In a k-step shift the index permutation splits into g=gcd(d,k) cycles; summing carry increments along each index cycle gives k/g. There is one free base label per cycle exactly when p divides that carry. Burnside and divisor inversion give the formulas. The proof's phrase ‘group generated by R, of order dp’ should instead say the action of Z/(dp) by powers of R: for p=1 the permutation is the identity on a singleton, but the formula uses the valid nonfaithful action and still returns one component of period 1. The independent replay confirms the two histograms, the singleton, and the four rational cyclic intervals at β=11/10, and checks the fixed-count formula against 30 integer-encoded orbit histograms.

The frozen measure-theoretic formulation is not quoted as stated in Simpson, but on the given exact bands it follows directly from the published partition and a textbook finite product of exact returns, since F^(dp) acts coordinatewise. Thus the central census and its claimed novelty are covered, and the remaining a.c. interpretation is a direct consequence of the already established structure, not a disproof of the mathematical finding.
