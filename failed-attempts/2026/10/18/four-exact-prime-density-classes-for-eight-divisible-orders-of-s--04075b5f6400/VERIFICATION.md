# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Outside finitely many primes the split model reduces with all four two-torsion points rational and distinct. A finite abelian 2-primary group with exactly four points killed by 2 has order divisible by 8 iff it contains an order-four point, equivalently a rational half of a nonzero 2-torsion point. Applying Bekker–Zarhin gives the three ordered-root tests (ma=mc=1), (a=mb=1), and (b=c=1). The positive gap squareclasses give a totally real multiquadratic field, so adjoining i supplies an independent sign. Chebotarev yields equal frequencies for the permissible patterns. Independently generating all relation subspaces by spans and using the number of successful m-signs (2,2,2,0,1,1,1,1 in negative-sign mask order) reproduces the complete 16-row calculation. The four proposed examples have relation spaces {0,3,4,7}, {0}, {0,1} and the full space. For the D(8) model the nonsquare classes are 238 and 2, independent since 238/2=119 is nonsquare, while the third difference is 1024; the test succeeds in 6 of the 8 allowed patterns. A separate matrix-based census also confirms the spectrum.

## originality

FAIL

The exact spectrum is a short, explicitly verified projection of prior results, not merely work in a similar area. The published Frobenius determinant formula gives the prime density from the exact mod-4 image when mod 2 is trivial: for g=I+2A, det(I−g)≡4det(A) mod 8. Reducing the realized groups in the Rouse–Zureick-Brown companion data to mod 4, retaining precisely the groups trivial mod 2, and counting singular elements gives the four stated values, each already attained among their non-CM representatives. For the CM extension the same published determinant test applies; all possible matrix spaces containing the prescribed complex-conjugation type give no extra value. The gap-character proof is a transparent alternative calculation, but the frozen claim of a distinct exact spectrum is covered as a consequence of stronger prior data and the standard test. No claim is made that the same boxed wording or the specific D(8) density appeared verbatim.

## value

FAIL

The family and invariant are mathematically natural, and the gap proof gives a convenient way to compute the density without looking up an image. But the frozen finding presents the exact spectrum and a single-model evaluation as the new mathematical contribution. The four values and their attainment already come from a short projection of the exact prior images and the standard local test; the example is one elementary specialization of the known halving criterion. No separate structural or general result is proved that survives this overlap as a valuable new claim.
