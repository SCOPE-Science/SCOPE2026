# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

With the stated column convention the element condition is x=M sigma(x) for coefficientwise squaring sigma. Expansion in a cyclic normal basis turns that equation into v_j=M v_(j-1), and a choice of v_0 in ker(M^f−I) determines exactly one solution. This works even for singular M and when f is even. Repeated substitution kills the nilpotent Fitting summand; on the stable summand W the restriction B is invertible and sigma(x)=B^(-1)x. All geometric solutions are defined over F_(2^L) by this identity for L=ord(B); that field has exactly 2^r solutions by the normal-basis formula. The count reaches 2^r exactly when B^f=I, proving minimality. The zero-core case gives one geometric point and L=1. Frobenius orbit lengths divide L, and inversion of N_f=sum_(d|f) d c_d gives the claimed formal zeta product. In the cyclic formula, multiplication by a^f−1 modulo t^n−1 has kernel generated by (t^n−1)/gcd(t^n−1,a^f−1), so the nullity is the gcd degree without a squarefreeness requirement. For C5 the t=1 factor is killed by adjacency and on the remaining fifth-root factor a=t+t^(-1) solves a²+a+1=0. For Petersen M²=M+J and MJ=J give M³=M over F2. Binary elimination gives the respective odd and even nullities 5 and 6; the zeta factors follow. These are idempotent elements including zero, not idempotent multiplication operators or a graph Ihara zeta.
