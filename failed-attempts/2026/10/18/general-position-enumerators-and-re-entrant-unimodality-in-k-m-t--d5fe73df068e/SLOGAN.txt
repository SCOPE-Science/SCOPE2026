For \(m,n\ge3\), all general-position sets and all inclusion-maximal ones in the direct product \(K_m\times K_n\) admit closed counting polynomials, with maximal sizes exactly \(\{4,m,n\}\) and unbounded gaps in the \(K_3\times K_n\) family. Strikingly, the complete general-position polynomial of \(K_3\times K_n\) is nonunimodal precisely for \(11\le n\le17\), regaining unimodality for every \(n\ge18\).
