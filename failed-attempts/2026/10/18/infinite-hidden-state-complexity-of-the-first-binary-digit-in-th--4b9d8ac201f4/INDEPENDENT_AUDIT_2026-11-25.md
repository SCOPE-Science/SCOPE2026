# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Flat density conditional on the current dyadic shell is preserved by every shell transition, including a mixture of the contraction and reset into shell one, so the stationary joint shell process has the stated kernel. At shell zero it regenerates. The downward first-hit transform is the root with t(0)=0 of t=z(p+qt²), and hitting zero from k has transform t^k. First-step conditioning at zero therefore gives R=z(qt+p/(2-t)). Here R(1)=1 and R'(1)=2p/(2p-1); the stationary mass at zero is its reciprocal. Each positive gap length is possible by a reset and downward steps; any finite word embeds in a concatenation of such gaps starting from a stationary visit to zero. The quadratic has distinct simple discriminant zeros; conjugating t to p/(qt) sends the limit of the rational-in-t expression for R at t=0 from 0 to 1, proving degree two rather than rationality. Finite hidden states give a finite matrix resolvent for the word probabilities 10^(n-1)1, so they cannot give this R; finite Hankel rank would also force rationality. For p<4/5 the only first-circle singularities are ±(2sqrt(pq))^(-1) and the square-root coefficients give the two stated constants. Their strict inequality C_+>C_->0, not positivity of all f_n by itself, rules out leading odd-term cancellation. For p>4/5 the physical root hits 2 at z_0=2/(4-3p) with derivative (4-3p)^2/(5p-4), giving pole amplitude p/t'(z_0); at p=4/5 the positive branch expansion of 2-t and z_*p=1 give amplitude 1/(2sqrt(2)) of the inverse square root, with the negative branch of smaller order. These prove the fixed-parameter prefactors and rates. The 318 reported finite diagnostics were inspected, not rerun.

## originality

FAIL

The premise for the focal originality distinction is not established: a pre-existing exact resolvent covers the first digit itself at the level already sufficient to rule out every finite-state HMM, not just a different position-correlation observable or the same numerical threshold. The diagonal formula, rationality of binary two-point probabilities for finite matrices, and quadratic conjugation give a direct obstruction; the renewal transform gives precisely the frozen algebraic return law. The explicit three-regime return asymptotics also have a statement-level catalogue match. This is a finding of direct prior implication for the focal result, not a claim that the prior paper literally stated the HMM theorem or that a later-dated catalogue record establishes earlier priority for the asymptotics.

## value

PASS

The contrast between full symbolic support and infinite hidden-state complexity is informative for this dynamical example, and the explicit return asymptotics distinguish an alternating square-root contribution, a critical inverse-square-root contribution, and a geometric pole. This is useful as an interpretation of exact dynamics; that utility does not confer originality on the direct consequence of the known resolvent.
