# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The ordinary independence complex is formed using exactly the tensor-product adjacency rule. An independent exact replay produced 5,282 augmented faces, 2,638 nonempty matched pairs, and an acyclic oriented Hasse graph with 25,221 incidences. The discrete Morse CW complex therefore has cell dimensions 0,3,4,4,4. The integral degree-three cochain from the matching annihilates the boundaries of every one of the 1,554 four-simplices and pairs to 1 with a 16-term integral cycle. In particular the rank of H_3 is at least one, forcing the one-row Morse differential out of degree four to be zero. Each 4-cell attaches to the 3-sphere by a degree-zero map, hence nullhomotopically. For the action, the cycle is primitive by its unit pairing, so the cochain evaluation on its translates gives the degree-three trace. The independent replay checked all 84 graph actions and the signed fixed-face traces; on the three color conjugacy classes the degree-three traces are (1,-1,1) for rotations and (-1,1,-1) for reflections, and degree four has (3,-1,0) for either. These are precisely the indicated rational characters and both have zero fixed-space multiplicity.

## originality

FAIL

A distinct published Resultary/SCOPE result states the same integral homotopy type and both joint rational symmetry modules for the same tensor-product graph. Additional indexed results subsume the nonequivariant wedge and repeat the exact module types. This decides statement coverage, not chronological priority or dependency: the indexed matching paths are dated later than the frozen source path and the relationship is not established by those dates. Earlier primary work on the family alone would not suffice for this rejection.

## value

FAIL

The mixed-dimensional example and its joint characters address a motivated problem, but as submitted the contribution is only that one finite type and its characters, both already directly covered in the inspected literature and indexed findings. This review provides independent corroboration, not a new structural lemma, uncovered invariant or extension that would give a separate scientific contribution. The failure is not a judgment against finite results or the usefulness of the invariants themselves.
