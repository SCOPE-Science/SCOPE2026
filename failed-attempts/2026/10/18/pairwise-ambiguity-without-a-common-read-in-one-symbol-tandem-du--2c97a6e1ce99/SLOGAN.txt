For exact single-symbol tandem duplication, every set of at least three pairwise-confusable words has one of two uniquely characterized shapes: either all words share one noisy read, or they share none. Both shapes admit exact counts for every clique size; the largest pairwise ambiguity has \(n-1\) words, while any one received word has at most \(\lfloor(n+1)/2\rfloor\) parents.
