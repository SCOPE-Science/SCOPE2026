Continuous cocycles from a prime-adic adding machine into any finite group have a complete classification by prime-regular conjugacy classes: each is cohomologous to a unique such constant up to conjugacy. A representative of order \(r\) produces exactly \(|G|/r\) minimal components, each an \(rp^\infty\)-odometer, linking their classification count to the classical Brauer count of modular irreducibles.
