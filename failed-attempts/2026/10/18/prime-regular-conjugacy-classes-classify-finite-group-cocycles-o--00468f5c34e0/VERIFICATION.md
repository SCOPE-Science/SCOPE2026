## correctness

PASS

The finite cyclic-level gauge lemma is correct: two cyclic label sequences are cohomologous exactly when their ordered products are conjugate, by multiplying the gauge identities and recursively constructing the transfer. A continuous map from compact Z_p to finite G factors through a finite p-power quotient; at higher levels the loop product is P_s^{p^{j-s}}. Removing the p-primary part and applying the inverse of p^s modulo the p-regular order yields the stated constant representative. Uniqueness follows because exponentiation by p^j is invertible on p-regular cyclic orders. For a constant h of order r coprime to p, the skew product splits into |G|/r clopen left cosets of <h>; CRT makes each component an r p^infinity odometer. The local return values are <h> up to conjugacy. The Brauer class count is correctly cited as a standard theorem. The finite verifier is corroboration only.

The component count then follows directly from the order of that constant. Thus the claimed complete class invariant is a short corollary/repackaging of prior coverage rather than an uncovered theorem. The Brauer counting identity is explicitly classical.
