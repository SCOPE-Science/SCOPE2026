# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Every coordinate has x_i²+x_i=S where S is the sum of coordinate squares. In odd characteristic put t_i=2x_i+1. The common-square-zero case has just x_i=-1/2 and occurs exactly for n=-1 in the field. Otherwise t_i=epsilon_i y with y nonzero; substituting in S gives (n-1)y²-2my+(n+1)=0, m=sum epsilon_i, and exactly the two representations (epsilon,y) and (-epsilon,-y). The discriminant is 4(1-4k(n-k)). For n=1 the equation is linear and soluble iff m is nonzero; for n=-1 subtract the zero root per sign vector and then add the one scalar solution. The other cases count 1+chi roots per sign vector, halved. In characteristic two, the coordinate differences are in F_2. Writing x_i=a+eta_i gives (n-1)a²+a+k=0; the representation is again exactly two-to-one by complementing eta and replacing a by a+1. For n odd this gives one root per eta; for n even, the two roots for k=1 require F_4, while k=0 always has two. These equations are nonzero of degree at most two over the prime field; their roots and the scalar point establish quadratic descent and extension parity. For odd p>n² the discriminants are nonzero and the exceptional leading or constant terms do not vanish, so the geometric count is exactly 2^n.
