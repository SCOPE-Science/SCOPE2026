Subtracting one from each positive count and deleting zeros is a bijection to partitions of excess k=N-s with at most s parts. The two Hill evenness indices are strictly decreasing functions of the integer product T and shifted square sum S, respectively. An independent exhaustive replay gave partition counts 1,1,2,3,5,7,11,15 at k=0 through 7 and compared every unordered pair. At k≤5 the orders coincide; at k=6 only the two stated square-sum ties occur, neither a product tie; the two pairs have maximum partition lengths 4 and 3. Restricting by partition length cannot create an inversion. At fixed N for s=2, the binary entropy and inverse concentration both increase strictly as the smaller share increases toward 1/2; at s=1 there is only one multiset. Hence the globally smallest strict reversal is at N=10, s=3.
