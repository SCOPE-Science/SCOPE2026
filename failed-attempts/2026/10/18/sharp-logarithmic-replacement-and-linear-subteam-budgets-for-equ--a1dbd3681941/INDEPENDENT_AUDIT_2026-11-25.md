With t nonempty rows, every column is a word and exactly m of the m^t words are constant. Allocate distinct nonconstant words to the r nonconstant classes, and distinct constant words to the at most m constant classes. At the least feasible t, duplicate rows are impossible: deleting a duplicate position leaves both forms of distinction intact and contradicts minimality. If r=0, one row suffices. For extraction include all m constant functions: the first row has m classes and each additional selected row strictly refines the restrictions of the m+r distinct functions until all are separated, in at most r more rows. In the binary staircase, deleting an endpoint makes a column constant and deleting an interior row identifies adjacent columns; these failures persist under further restrictions. This works inside any m≥2.
