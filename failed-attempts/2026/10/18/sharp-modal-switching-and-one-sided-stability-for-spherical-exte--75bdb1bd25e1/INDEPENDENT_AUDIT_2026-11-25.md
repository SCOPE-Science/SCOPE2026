# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The spherical Fourier–Bessel multiplier has modulus (2π)^{d/2}r^{−ν}|J_{ν+k}|, and polar integration leaves r dr. Differentiating rJ_{ν+k}J_{ν+k+1} gives the adjacent difference; adding two differences and using the recurrence orders each parity chain. Consecutive orders have no common positive zero by uniqueness for Bessel's ODE. This yields a single, double or triple top space as claimed and excludes every higher degree. The gap is the difference from the best nonmaximal entry of either parity chain, with sharpness realized in one excluded mode. At a J_{ν+1}-root z the product has positive derivative zJ_ν(z)²; on the right the quadratic even competitor dominates the minimum, on the left the linear odd-to-even gap does. At a J_ν-root y the product has derivative −yJ_{ν+1}(y)², and the same-parity competitors remain uniformly separated, giving linear onset on both sides. The gap at the collision is positive because the projector there excludes the entire tied space; neither side limit equals this positive value.
