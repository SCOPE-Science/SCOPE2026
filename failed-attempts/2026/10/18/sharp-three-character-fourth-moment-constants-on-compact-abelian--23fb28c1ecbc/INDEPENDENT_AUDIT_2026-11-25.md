# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Haar orthogonality reduces the fourth moment to the squared norm of the autocorrelation of (a,b,c). For order at least five or infinite, the two nontrivial positive-frequency correlations are b·conj(a)+c·conj(b) and c·conj(a); the negative ones are their conjugates. For order four the latter pair instead aliases to 2Re(c·conj(a)). With coefficient squared mass 1, s=|a|²+|c|² and q=|a||c|≤s/2, phase alignment and q=s/2 maximize each envelope. The remaining parabolas 1+4s−(7/2)s² and 1+4s−3s² give the claimed constants and force s=4/7 and 2/3 respectively. Phase alignment forces the geometric phase progression; real c·conj(a) in order four further forces the ratio to be a fourth root. The stated deficit is the termwise loss from these upper bounds and the completed square. At order three, the three-point discrete transform turns equality into support at exactly one point. These are fourth powers of the synthesis norms, and the equality families have the stated normalizations.
