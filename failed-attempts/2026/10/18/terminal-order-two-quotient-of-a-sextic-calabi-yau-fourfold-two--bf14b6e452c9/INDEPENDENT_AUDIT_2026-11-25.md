# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Smoothness follows from the disjoint gradient blocks and Euler's identity. The two projective eigenspaces cut out plane sextics, and the normal rank-three summand on each is anti-invariant and is O_C(1)^3. Equivariant local coordinates give a product with the Veronese cone, with Reid–Tai age 3/2 and determinant character of order two. Lefschetz and general-position avoidance of the fixed curves give a simply connected free covering of the regular quotient; Armstrong's theorem kills the involution in the full quotient. Blowing up the cone vertex is locally the total space of O_{P^2}(-2), obtained as the quotient of the ordinary upstairs blowup in anti-invariant coordinates. The upstairs canonical coefficient 2 minus ramification coefficient 1, divided by the pullback multiplicity 2, is 1/2. The middle residue action is negative exchange, and rational finite-quotient pullback identifies the invariant Hodge structure. The equivariant blowup formula adds two copies each of the curve cohomology in Tate twists 1 and 2. Independent inclusion–exclusion in evidence/5-hodge-counts.js reproduced the primitive dimensions (0,208,866,208,0), both Betti vectors, and (2610,1287,1215) for the Euler characteristics of X, Y and Z. The calculation is for ordinary rational cohomology and does not assert integral or intersection cohomology; the exact arithmetic is not a substitute for the geometric and topological arguments.

Its fundamental-group, reduced blowup and separate Hodge-diamond assertions go beyond what is stated in that source. They do not make the overlapping assertions original; no independently valuable narrowed replacement was established.

The remaining fundamental groups follow directly by general-position and orbit-space arguments, the resolution is the standard blowup of the already identified transverse Veronese cone, and the additional Hodge data come from textbook residue and blowup formulas. The original does not establish a separately motivated, nonroutine invariant or obstruction in that remainder. The computations can be useful as exposition without sustaining this frozen finding as a new result.
