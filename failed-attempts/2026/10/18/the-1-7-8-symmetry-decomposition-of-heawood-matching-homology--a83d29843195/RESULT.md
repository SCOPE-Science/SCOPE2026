# The \(1+7+8\) symmetry decomposition of Heawood matching homology
## Finding
Let \(H\) be the Heawood graph, the point-line incidence graph of the Fano plane. Write its points and lines as the seven nonzero vectors in \(\mathbb F_2^3\), with an edge \((p,\ell)\) precisely when \(p\cdot\ell=0\). Let \(K=\mathcal M(H)\) be the ordinary matching complex of its 21 edges, and \(G=\operatorname{Aut}(H)\) the full group of order 336, including dualities between the two vertex classes.

Let \(\mathfrak F\) be the set of edge one-factorizations of \(H\): unordered partitions of its 21 edges into three perfect matchings. This set contains eight elements, and \(G\) acts on it. There is an isomorphism of rational \(G\)-representations
\[
\widetilde H_4(K;\mathbb Q)\cong\mathbb Q[\mathfrak F]\oplus W,
\]
where \(W\) is an absolutely irreducible rational representation of dimension eight. The eight-point permutation representation \(\mathbb Q[\mathfrak F]\) is the direct sum of the trivial representation and an irreducible seven-dimensional augmentation representation. Consequently, the total decomposition has irreducible dimensions \(1+7+8\); all other reduced rational homology groups of \(K\) vanish.

The following table gives the *full* character \(\chi\) of reduced degree-four homology, the character \(\pi\) of \(\mathbb Q[\mathfrak F]\), and the irreducible residual character \(\chi_W=\chi-\pi\). An element type is described by whether it interchanges points with lines, its order, and the number of elements of that type. Types with the same displayed values need not be individual conjugacy classes.

| Swaps point/line classes? | Element order | Number | \(\chi\) | \(\pi\) | \(\chi_W\) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| No | 1 | 1 | 16 | 8 | 8 |
| No | 2 | 21 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| No | 3 | 56 | 1 | 2 | -1 |
| No | 4 | 42 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| No | 7 | 48 | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| Yes | 2 | 28 | 4 | 2 | 2 |
| Yes | 6 | 56 | 1 | 2 | -1 |
| Yes | 8 | 84 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

In particular, the full symmetry quotient has
\[
\widetilde H_i(|K|/G;\mathbb Q)\cong
\begin{cases}\mathbb Q,&i=4,\\0,&i\ne4.\end{cases}
\]

## Assumptions and scope
The complex is the ordinary matching complex: vertices are edges of \(H\), and faces are all sets of pairwise endpoint-disjoint graph edges, including the empty face in augmented chains. This is not the perfect-matching complex, the graph independence complex, or the complete-graph matching complex. The full group is generated by the 168 invertible linear maps \(A\in\mathrm{GL}(3,2)\), acting as \((p,\ell)\mapsto(Ap,A^{-T}\ell)\), and the polarity \((p,\ell)\mapsto(\ell,p)\). Each preserves the incidence equation, and the resulting 336 maps are all automorphisms of this Fano incidence graph. The claims concern rational homology and its \(G\)-action, not integral equivariant lattices or equivariant homotopy type.

## Proof
**(1) Exact rational homology.** Enumerate the faces by scanning all subsets of the 21 edges and requiring each point and each line to occur at most once. The numbers of faces of cardinalities zero through seven are
\[
(1,21,168,644,1218,1050,336,24).
\]
For the *augmented* chain complex, exact binary elimination on all boundary columns gives the ranks of boundaries from cardinality \(k\) to \(k-1\), for \(k=1,\ldots,7\), as
\[
(1,20,148,496,722,312,24).
\]
It follows by rank-nullity that reduced mod-two homology has dimension sixteen in simplicial degree four and is zero in every other degree. Over \(\mathbb Q\), every boundary rank is at least its rank over \(\mathbb F_2\) because a nonzero determinant modulo two is a nonzero integer determinant. Hence each rational Betti number is at most its corresponding mod-two Betti number. The reduced Euler characteristic computed from the face vector is sixteen. Because rational homology can occur only in degree four and has that Euler characteristic, it is precisely \(\mathbb Q^{16}\) in degree four.

**(2) Full finite group and signed Hopf traces.** Independently enumerate all invertible binary \(3\times3\) matrices and the same maps composed with polarity, producing 336 distinct edge permutations. A second construction enumerates all point permutations preserving the seven Fano lines and gets 168 collineations before polarity. For each graph automorphism \(g\), compute the reduced cellular Lefschetz number by summing \((-1)^{k-1}\operatorname{sgn}(g|_\sigma)\) over its invariant nonempty \(k\)-edge matchings \(\sigma\), and subtract one for the augmented empty face. The sign is the parity of the permutation induced on the increasing ordering of the edges of \(\sigma\). The Hopf trace identity and Step (1) show that this number is \(\operatorname{tr}(g|\widetilde H_4(K;\mathbb Q))\). The resulting exhaustive trace profile is the \(\chi\) column of the table. Each group element is checked, not sampled.

**(3) The eight-factorization action.** Of the 24 seven-edge perfect matchings, exactly eight unordered triples form disjoint covers of all 21 edges. Each factorization is transported by a graph automorphism. Counting fixed factorizations for each of the 336 automorphisms gives the \(\pi\) column, which is an actual permutation character. These counts are verified by a direct exhaustive construction, rather than assuming an isomorphism between permutation actions.

**(4) Rational splitting and irreducibility.** For rational characters use the inner product \(\langle f,h\rangle_G=|G|^{-1}\sum_{g\in G}f(g)h(g)\). The table yields
\[
\langle\chi,\chi\rangle_G=3,\quad
\langle\pi,\pi\rangle_G=2,\quad
\langle\chi-\pi,\chi-\pi\rangle_G=1,\quad
\langle\pi,\chi-\pi\rangle_G=0.
\]
The degree of the virtual character \(\chi-\pi\) is eight. A virtual complex character of norm one is a positive or negative irreducible character; positive degree excludes the negative choice. Since both \(\chi\) and \(\pi\) have rational realizations, subtracting the irreducible constituent in characteristic zero gives the asserted splitting over \(\mathbb Q\). The character has norm one after complexification, so this eight-dimensional rational module is absolutely irreducible. Also \(\langle\pi,\mathbf1\rangle_G=1\); hence the eight-point action is transitive. Since \(\langle\pi,\pi\rangle_G=2\), the seven-dimensional augmentation representation is irreducible. Finally \(\langle\chi,\mathbf1\rangle_G=1\). Rational transfer applied to the barycentric subdivision, where simplex stabilizers act without inversions, identifies reduced rational quotient homology with \(G\)-invariant reduced rational homology. This proves the quotient formula.

## Verification
Execute `python3 verify_complex.py`, `python3 verify_characters.py`, and `python3 verify_matrix_char.py` in the directory containing this document. All programs use Python's standard library only. The first reconstructs every face, exactly computes binary boundary ranks, and checks the reduced Euler characteristic. The second independently reconstructs every collineation by permutations preserving Fano lines, checks the full eight-factorization action, and verifies the character and inner-product identities. The third reconstructs the group using binary matrices and recomputes all 336 signed traces with a different edge order and matching enumerator. All three complete with affirmative printed checks. Recomputing signed traces over every group element and rank checks over every boundary column supplies finite exhaustive evidence; the representation-theoretic deductions in Step (4) are exact mathematical implications of those checked integers.

## Relationship to prior work
The Dong–Wachs combinatorial-Laplacian analysis treats the matching complex of a **complete graph** and its symmetric-group homology, not the sparse 21-edge Heawood incidence graph. Wachs's bounded-degree matching/chessboard survey includes complete-host families under degree constraints, not this particular graph's 336-symmetry representation. A published exact Heawood calculation already gives \(K\simeq\bigvee^{16}S^4\); that nonequivariant sphere-wedge type, including its Betti number, is expressly treated here as known. The existing table of eight Heawood edge one-factorizations is likewise treated as known. Neither fixes the isomorphism type of the full matching-homology representation or relates it to the factorization permutation module. The new claim is precisely the above \(1+7+8\) equivariant decomposition, complete trace table, and its rational quotient consequence, not the individual known ingredients.

## Limitations
No integral torsion, integral \(\mathbb Z[G]\)-lattice classification, or equivariant homotopy type is asserted. The result is specific to the Heawood graph and the ordinary matching complex. The exhaustive computer checks furnish finite proofs of the 336-element character table and complete finite chain ranks, with two separate symmetry enumerations; they do not establish a general symbolic formula for other incidence graphs. Bibliographic searches cannot exclude an unindexed equivalent representation calculation, and priority is not absolute.
