# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The all-rank proof works for finite linear words over exactly the used alphabet. At one-based position i the letter is 1+v2(i). If the middle u of a minimal absent aub is nonempty with maximum h, every occurrence sits at the same offset in an aligned Z_h block (the unique h fixes that offset). If h=n the occurrence is unique; if u is shorter than Z_h, a fixed neighbor on an end interior to the block forces co-occurrence of the two extensions. Hence the only nonempty possible middles are full Z_j, j<n. At an internal Z_j block its two neighbors are determined by the valuations of consecutive positive block indices: exactly one neighbor equals j+1. Both orientations with each larger letter occur using the block indices 2^(letter-j-1) and its predecessor; the one-sided sets include the two ends of the host and in the two-block case contain just n. For the empty middle all letters are available and adjacency is exactly 1 next to a larger letter, in both orders, with no adjacency for n=1. The missing ordered pairs give the stated disjoint union, sizes, lengths and sum. This proof, not the recorded finite checker, establishes completeness.
