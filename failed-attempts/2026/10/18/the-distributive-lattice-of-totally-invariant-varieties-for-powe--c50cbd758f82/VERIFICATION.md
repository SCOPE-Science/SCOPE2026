The cited Linearity Theorem applies over C to irreducible closed sets satisfying first inverse-image equality for an endomorphism of degree d>1. No general linearity theorem is being verified anew. If V=P(W) satisfies that equality, coordinate root-of-unity operators preserve W, and (g_j-1)/(ζ-1) projects onto coordinate j. Hence W is coordinate. The power map preserves each coordinate plane in both directions; the invertible mixing map preserves one exactly when the stated block vanishes. Successor closure and SCC contraction then give unions and intersections of upper sets, including the formal empty bottom. Each fixed-s-plane locus is a nonempty open part of a projective linear space with s(N-s) independent equations, relatively closed in PGL_N. For distinct support sets I,J of size s, choose j in I minus J and i in J minus I: the elementary matrix identity plus E_ij satisfies J's condition and violates I's, proving distinctness at fixed dimension. A strict-poset incidence matrix plus identity is triangular in a suitable order, and an invertible fully supported two-by-two matrix realizes the two-element lattice. This checks all dimensions without relying on the finite support-pattern verifier. The Chang–Luo citation describes a numerical consequence correctly but their bound concerns totally periodic, not only first-iterate, subvarieties.
