For the Heawood graph, the natural \(\mathrm{GL}(3,\mathbf F_2)\)-action on fourth reduced rational matching-complex homology decomposes as \(\mathbf1\oplus U_7\oplus U_8\), with the \(7\)- and \(8\)-dimensional summands absolutely irreducible. The character has values \((16,0,1,0,2)\) on elements of orders \((1,2,3,4,7)\), a symmetry invariant not determined by the known nonequivariant sphere wedge.
