For the Heawood graph, the full 336-automorphism representation on the second rational homology of its perfect-matching complex is the bipartition-exchange sign twist of the known fourth-homology representation of its ordinary matching complex: \(\widetilde H_2(\mathcal M_{\mathrm p}(H);\mathbb Q)\cong\varepsilon\otimes(\mathbf1\oplus T_7\oplus W_8)\). This flips every point-line-exchanging trace and removes all full-group invariant homology, despite the two complexes having equal top Betti number 16.
