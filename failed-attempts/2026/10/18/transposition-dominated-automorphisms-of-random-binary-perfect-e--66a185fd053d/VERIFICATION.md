# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Invertibility makes the squares of the natural basis a basis, so images of distinct natural basis vectors must have disjoint supports. Over F_2 this makes the full automorphism group exactly the simultaneous permutation stabilizer. The rank-one square-zero part of a transposition has invertible centralizer of size 2^(2n-3)|GL_(n-2)(F_2)|, also valid for n=2. The cycle-pair orbit dimension gives exact codimensions 4n-6 for support three and at least 4n-8 for support four. For larger supports m the codimension is at least mn-m^2/2, and the n^m counting majorant gives the stated negligible tail by a geometric sum up to n/2 and a quadratic exponent thereafter. Two distinct transpositions force their product, of support three or four; pair intersections are thus O(2^(-4n)) uniformly, which justifies the Bonferroni error. Nontrivial stabilizers not generated by a single transposition lie in the support-at-least-three event; dividing its bound by the nontrivial probability gives conditional failure O(n^2 2^(-2n)).
