## originality

FAIL

The strongest published overlaps are exact: one Resultary record gives the same Lie target law and another gives the same group target law in all characteristics. Their numerical formulas identify every target fiber across the two maps by addition of I. Consequently even the combined equality of counts is an immediate consequence of those statements taken together. The Lie record is dated before and the group record after the frozen path; this is a finding of demonstrable mathematical coverage, not a determination of chronology of discovery or provenance. No distinct new count or structural theorem survives as stated.

## value

FAIL

The full prescribed-target distribution is a useful invariant of a natural class-three group, not just a commuting probability. But the claimed increment here is comparison of two already exactly enumerated lists; the equality is mechanically implied and the proof is confined to this degree. It supplies no distinct further invariant or general comparison theorem.
