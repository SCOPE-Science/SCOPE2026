# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Equality of individual softmax probabilities is equivalent to equality of the log odds for all two competitors i,k. Using the binary basis states and varying the two positive payoff parameters independently gives the row identity lambda_i P_i - lambda_k P_k = (s-1)(e_i-e_k)^T. For s≠1, a nonadjacent common-neighbor pair violates the i-coordinate, so a connected graph cannot have an induced path of length two and is a clique. A third common neighbor forces equal lambda on the clique, then s=1-lambda/(n-1). For s=1 the row sums give constant lambda on each even-walk class; on a nonbipartite graph identical rows at nonzero lambda contradict the zero diagonal. On a bipartite graph identical supports on a nonzero side and connectedness force the complete bipartite graph. The stated cases satisfy the identity even at leaves. For uniform lambda on a clique the payoff difference is -c-b/(n-1), so the effective constant ratio is R. In the mixed-state birth–death recursion the product of reverse-to-forward ratios to level j is [n-1+j(R-1)]/[(n-1)R^j]. Its reciprocal normalization is (n-1)/(n sum_{j=0}^{n-2} R^{-j}), which gives the stated formula, the neutral limit and the strict sign relative to 1/n. The independent replay checks the full nullspace including the scalar variable on all 4, 38 and 728 connected labeled graphs of orders 3, 4 and 5, and solves 60 exact tridiagonal absorption systems.
