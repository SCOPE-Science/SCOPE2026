# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

A proper prefix–suffix cannot contain all the ones: its suffix occurrence would then have to start at the position of its prefix occurrence to contain the same first one at the same offset. For a zero-ended three-one word, a complete return to a zero-only border requires equal outer gaps strictly longer than both inner gaps. A one-one privileged border has equal flanking zeros and, if it brackets the first and last ones, occurs around the middle one too. A two-one privileged border forces equal outer gaps and equal inner gaps, with the latter at least the former. One-ended words must use the two-one border and have equal inner gaps. All qualifying borders give exactly two occurrences, including overlaps. The split generating functions follow by summing free gaps; the total fraction is proper, so roots-of-unity partial fractions give a quasipolynomial from length zero, not just eventually. Its numerator at primitive third and fourth roots does not vanish, and its order-three pole at one gives n²/24. Independent exhaustive enumeration of all binary strings through length 15 confirmed the predicate, split, and low-weight palindromicity.
