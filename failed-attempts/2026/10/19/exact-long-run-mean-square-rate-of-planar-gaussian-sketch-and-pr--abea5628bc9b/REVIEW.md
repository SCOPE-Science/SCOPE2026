# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In the energy coordinates the step is P=nnᵀ with n proportional to (−b sin θ,a cos θ), where 0<a≤b and θ is uniform. Independently, t=tan θ is standard Cauchy: partial fractions integrate E[n₁²]=b/(a+b), and differentiating this expectation in b gives 2E[n₁²n₂²]=b∂_b E[n₁²]=ab/(a+b)². Subtraction gives the two diagonal fourth moments, and reflection kills the mixed odd moments. Fresh sketches make the resulting positive 2×2 transfer exact at every iteration. Perron positivity of both the trace functional and every nonzero squared-coordinate start gives the same exponent even for an eigenaxis start and a circular Hessian. The expected projector gives the sharp one-step factor; the positive difference of the squared expressions is 8q(1−q²), and the Perron rate has derivative 3/4 at q=1, yielding the 3/2 gap coefficient. Independent integration of the rational Cauchy integrands matched 12 moment values for (a,b)=(1,1),(1,3),(2,5),(1,100) at 65 decimal places. These checks corroborate the exact proof; they are not a proof for all curvatures.

FAIL

A distinct published Resultary record gives materially the same planar iid Gaussian (or uniform-circle) exact-line-search model, full two-state expectation transfer, common Perron exponent for every nonzero initial error, and separation from the sharp one-step rate. The two coordinate conventions give the same formula, not just a similar asymptotic coefficient. This is a finding of existing equivalent coverage, not a determination of who obtained the theorem first: the catalogue counterpart is dated later than the frozen source path.
