# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Laurent units have singleton support, as can be seen from the extremes of a generic integral linear functional on the support of a product. Thus monomial substitutions give exactly integral invertible exponent matrices, with a normal scalar torus and a monomial splitting. Applying D_μ to monomials gives local finiteness, the commuting equation, and the kernel lattice of the surjective homomorphism Z^n→W_μ. The lattice has index p^r, and coset monomials give a basis over its Laurent group algebra. Localizing at the nonzero constants gives a finite-dimensional domain over a field and hence gives the fraction field with the same basis and degree; all p-th powers lie in the lower field, even if K is imperfect. Reduction of integral GL has precisely determinant ±1, by elementary lifting of SL and a sign matrix (with n=1 immediate). For a rank r weight coefficient matrix C with r<n, any two full row frames are related by an invertible change, and its determinant can be corrected on a complement without changing the target frame. In full rank the stabilizer in the image is trivial and the orbit invariant is the determinant coset. Frame counts and Gaussian subspace counts yield all formulas. The zero tuple gives index one and a single class; p=2 has one sign; for F₂₅ in two variables there are six rank-one lines, two full-rank orientations and zero, giving nine. The finite verifier was inspected, not executed; these checks do not replace the general argument.
