## correctness

PASS

Under conjugation by a Weyl operator, the Weyl inputs transform by distinct characters of the translation/modulation indices. Orthogonality over the N² projectors therefore makes the analysis columns orthogonal; their squared norms for inputs W/√N are N|⟨g,Wg⟩|², with N=q². Each normalized-window ambiguity coefficient is a graph-fiber sum divided by q, which cancels √N. The Gold derivative has kernel of size two for nonzero first-coordinate shift by gcd(k,m)=1; half of the characters of a nonempty two-point fiber agree on its difference. A purely vertical nonzero translation has no intersection. At zero translation the quadratic polar radical is given by v^(2^k)r^(2^(2k))+vr=0, by moving Frobenius through the trace. Cyclic-group divisibility gives sizes two for all nonzero v in odd dimension and four on cubes, one otherwise, in even dimension. The squared Walsh sum is q times the sum of the induced character on the radical; exactly q/|R_v| linear terms cancel the restricted quadratic form. This reconstructs the table, rank, indexed Weyl null directions and positive-spectrum condition number. Zero modulation gives the APN converse, and affine conjugation permutes shifts and dual characters up to phase, preserving the full spectrum (not generally the horizontal labels). An independent direct matrix check using 256 actual q=4 measurement projectors gave orthogonal Weyl columns and squared bands {0:198, 4:56, 16:2}. The supplied finite checks are not an all-degree proof; the orthogonality and radical argument is.
