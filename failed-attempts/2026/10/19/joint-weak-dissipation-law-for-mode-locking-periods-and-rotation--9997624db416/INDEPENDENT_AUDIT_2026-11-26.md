# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The joint weak limit and every fixed mixed moment are supported. The rational plateau formula places mass C_h/(e^(hq)-1) at each reduced p/q, C_h=4 sinh²(h/2), including the separate zero plateau of width 1−λ with q=1. In plateau interiors there is a periodic orbit; the strictly increasing degree-one lift preserves cyclic order on its points, so the permutation is a cyclic shift by a number coprime to the number of points, and the least period is the reduced denominator. The exceptional set is Lebesgue null. Lambert series normalization follows from sum_{d|n} φ(d)=n. The summatory totient estimate gives local convergence of h² times the totient point masses to (6/π²)t dt. Möbius inversion of the reduced-numerator sum of a Lipschitz test function gives a Riemann-sum error O_g(τ(q)); on each window a≤hq≤b the summed plateau-weighted error is O(√h). Product test functions then give the joint limit on compact windows. The small-period mass is bounded by O(a+h), and the exponential upper-tail estimate with each fixed polynomial factor gives uniform integrability. Termwise integration against the limiting density gives Γ(k+2)ζ(k+2), with normalization at k=0. The plateau paper is by Laurent and Nogueira, not Laurent, Nogueira and Pollicott; consult Theorems 3(ii) and 4 in the available manuscript. The finite checker was inspected, not run; the recorded VERIFY_OK is not a fresh execution, and the proof does not rely on it.

But the frozen record adds no independently useful result beyond the already indexed joint law and its tail control: continuous weighting gives the apparent mixed-moment extension by truncation, with no new parameter regime, rate or invariant.
