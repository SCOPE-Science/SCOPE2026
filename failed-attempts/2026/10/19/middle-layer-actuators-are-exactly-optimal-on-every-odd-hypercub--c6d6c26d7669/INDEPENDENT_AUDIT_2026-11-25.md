For n=2r+1, the adjoint inclusion and deletion operators obey DU−UD=(n−2k)I at rank k; raising is injective below the middle. The ballot count is binom(n,t)−binom(n,t−1), the primitive dimension. Adjointness gives the orthogonal decomposition of their raised images. The remaining coefficient of z^(j−t) in (1−z²)^(r−t)(1+z) is the nonzero signed binomial in the proof. Thus each primitive sector maps isomorphically to its corresponding target sector for j≤r.
