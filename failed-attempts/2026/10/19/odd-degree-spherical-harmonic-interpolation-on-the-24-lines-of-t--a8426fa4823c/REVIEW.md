The probability-sphere normalization gives n U_ell, with diagonal n^2. The inner-product values on and across the two orbits give the signed coefficients n epsilon and n sqrt(2) eta. One can verify the incidence algebra without a numerical spectrum: form the 12-by-4 matrices A and C of short and long representatives. Both have transpose times themselves 3I_4, by cancellation of mixed terms and the coordinate multiplicities. Then S=2(AA^T-I), T=2(CC^T-I), and B=sqrt(2) AC^T, giving all the stated identities by multiplication. Their complementary eight-dimensional sectors have eigenvalue n(n-2 epsilon) each, and the two four-dimensional frame ranges couple with singular value sqrt(18) for B, giving the two stated eigenvalues of multiplicity four. At n=2,4,6,8,10 the ranks are 4,16,20,24,20; for n>=12 the smallest bracket is at least n-10>0. Equality of the three sectors implies eta=epsilon=0, i.e. 4 and 3 both divide even n. Flipping representatives conjugates by a diagonal sign matrix. The pseudoinverse interpolation statement follows from G=EE*, including its singular cases. An independent exact coordinate reconstruction verified the signed identities and low-degree ranks.
