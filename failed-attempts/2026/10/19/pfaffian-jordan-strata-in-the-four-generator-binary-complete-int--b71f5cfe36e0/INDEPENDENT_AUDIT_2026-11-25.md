## correctness

PASS

In characteristic two every radical element squares to zero, so rank determines the number of size-two Jordan blocks. A nonzero linear part extends to an algebra coordinate system: after a linear change the substitution x₁↦u fixes the associated graded generators and is filtration-invertible. Multiplication has rank eight there. For nonzero quadratic leading term α, the images of 1 and of degree two contribute one dimension each in degrees two and four; modulo the degree-four line the degree-one contribution is the rank of the alternating form (v,w)↦[αvw] in top degree. Thus the quadratic case is rank six or four according to the Pfaffian. Group the six coefficients into complementary triples a,b, so P=a·b. For a≠0 each value has q² preimages in b, giving q²(q³−1)(q−1) nonsingular forms and (q²+1)(q³−1) nonzero singular forms. There are five free higher coefficients. Cubic, quartic and zero cases follow from complementary pairings and give ranks two, one and zero. The Pfaffian is geometrically irreducible (its polar form has rank six, impossible for a product of two linear forms); the punctured cone is dense and all other closure assertions follow from nonempty affine open subsets. The closures refer to algebraic strata, not finite rational point sets.
