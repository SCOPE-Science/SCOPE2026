# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Projection to the symplectic space V identifies each center-avoiding subalgebra with a graph of a functional on a nonzero totally isotropic s-space, so there are q^s lifts. The projection of the generated algebra is the sum of the images. If that sum is V, some cross-bracket is nonzero since the form is nondegenerate and the two images are individually isotropic. This supplies the central line and makes the generated algebra all of H. The sum condition forces complementary Lagrangians, so all lower-dimensional fibers are isolated and active fibers are independent sets. Building an isotropic basis gives q^(2n−j)−q^j possibilities for the next vector; divide by the ordered-basis count and multiply by q^s for the isolates. At dimension n the quotient product simplifies to L_n. In a splitting into two complementary Lagrangians, complements to one are graphs of symmetric matrices, and common complements to both correspond to the invertible ones. The nonsingular symmetric-matrix recurrence is valid in characteristic two: a nonzero first diagonal gives the one-by-one Schur complement; for zero first diagonal and a nonzero first off-diagonal row choose coordinates making it (1,0,…,0). In block form [[0,1,0],[1,b,w],[0,w^t,D]], the determinant is −det(D); b,w give q^(k−1) choices. A zero first row contributes nothing. Substitution proves the two closed products. Multiply the base degree, edge and triangle counts by q^n, q^(2n) and q^(3n), respectively; base triangles are L_n C_n S_n/6. For a clique the projected Lagrangians have disjoint nonzero vectors, giving the bound q^n+1. Lines over K=F_(q^n) in K² attain it under the trace of the K-determinant form, a nondegenerate alternating F_q-form by nondegeneracy of the trace pairing. The first example gives L=135, C=64, S=28 and 126+1260=1386 isolated lifts, with degree 512, 276480 edges and 20643840 triangles as claimed.

## originality

FAIL

The structure of the assigned induced graph is already determined by a distinct indexed theorem for the full Heisenberg comaximal graph, including all fields and ranks in scope. Restrict that theorem to the functional graphs over nonzero isotropic images: exact sum-adjacency gives the stated blow-up and isolates. The induced triangle and clique claims then recover standard polar data, not independent graph invariants not already determined by the stronger description. The earlier rank-two record already uses the same projection proof without a dimension-specific step in that part. This is a statement-level coverage decision, not a claim that the November record predates the assigned October record or evidence of copying.

## value

FAIL

Triangles and maximal clique size are natural invariants, and the self-contained reduction is useful exposition. But as a standalone new finding the exact structure is already specified by the full-graph theorem, and the formulas are routine counts on its induced relation: a lift factor times an intersection number, the elementary spread bound and attainment, and an isotropic subspace sum. There is no surviving unresolved classification or independently needed exact fact beyond these mechanical consequences in the inspected record.
