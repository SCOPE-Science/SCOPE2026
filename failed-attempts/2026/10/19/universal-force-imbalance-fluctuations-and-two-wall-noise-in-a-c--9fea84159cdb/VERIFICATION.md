# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The sticky-boundary identity gives signed contact minus integrated orientation as a bounded displacement, so the telegraph tilted generator gives the full SCGF and the LDP, including rate λ at each endpoint. The variance limit follows separately from the bounded correction and telegraph covariance. A uniform no-flip trip to the right wall gives the finite-box minorization; stationary flux balance gives total wall mass p=1/(1+z). Independently, in centered coordinates u=λx/v−z/2 the dimensionless Poisson solution for total contact minus p is g±=p(u²±u): its generator is p in the bulk and p−1 at the stuck walls. Integrating against the stationary law gives the total variance rate 2p³(z³/6+z²/2)/λ. Reflection makes signed and total contacts uncorrelated at every T; their two rates then recover the displayed matrix. The monotonicity and iterated limits follow from this rate and E[(A_T⁺)²]≤T²/(1+z) at fixed T. The frozen finite lattice experiments are consistency checks, not proof.
