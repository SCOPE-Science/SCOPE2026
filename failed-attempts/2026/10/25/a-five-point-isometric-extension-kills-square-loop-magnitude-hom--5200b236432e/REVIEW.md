# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

All nonzero distinct corner distances are 2 or 2sqrt(2). At total length 4sqrt(2), the only two-chains in X are the four backtracks (A,opposite A,A), all cycles, and no three-chains in X exist because 3*2>4sqrt(2). In Y any two-edge chain through the center is at most 3sqrt(2), so degree-2 chains remain precisely these four. Each is the boundary of (A,O,opposite A,A) since O lies between opposite corners and the other deletion is not geodesic. Four explicit integral boundaries kill all of C2. Independent exact lattice distance and chain computation confirms C2(X)=4,C3(X)=0,C2(Y)=4,C3(Y)=36 and all boundary certificates. No floating point rank or conjectural proof used.

## originality

PASS

Bi–Feng–Li–Wu introduce persistent magnitude homology for finite metric spaces and give other small-scale examples, but their inspected paper does not state this exact square-center degree-two computation. It is a specialization of the general definition of magnitude boundary and its midpoint/geodesic mechanism, so no new structural theory is claimed. The identical death mechanism already works for the simplest two-point space with an inserted midpoint, making any purported novelty of the *mechanism* itself illusory; only the square-specific numeric rank four is potentially unreported.

## value

FAIL

FAIL: Although a finite exact witness is in principle an admissible mathematical contribution, the advertised square/rank-four phenomenon is two independent copies of the elementary 2-to-3 point midpoint construction. For X={a,b}, length 2d, the degree-two backtracks (a,b,a),(b,a,b) generate Z² and inserting one midpoint m makes each the boundary of (a,m,b,a) or (b,m,a,b). The square corners are merely two antipodal pairs meeting at the center; no square-specific interaction, threshold, minimum, obstruction, new classification or independent metric invariant is established. A repeat of this routine chain-differential calculation without motivation for exactly 4 generators/5 points is not a substantive mathematical gap. This judgment does NOT reject finite work in general: it rejects an arbitrary replicated example whose mechanism is already immediate from definitions.
