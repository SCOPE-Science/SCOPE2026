For every \(n\ge4\), the difference between the count of subsets whose gcd divides \(n\) and the count whose gcd is coprime to \(n\) has strict sign \((-1)^n\), resolving Pongsriiam’s alternating-sign question. Its leading magnitude is \(2^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}\), with an explicit correction of size \(2^{\lfloor n/3\rfloor}\).
