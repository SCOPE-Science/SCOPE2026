On every split square-zero local algebra in characteristic two, weight-zero Rota–Baxter operators are controlled by a square-zero linear map and an unrestricted vector; they can have nilpotency index three, unlike their characteristic-not-two counterparts. In radical dimension \(r\ge1\), the complete conjugacy classification has exactly \(2r\) classes for odd \(r\) and \(2r+1\) for even \(r\), with exact finite-field rank and nilpotency counts.
