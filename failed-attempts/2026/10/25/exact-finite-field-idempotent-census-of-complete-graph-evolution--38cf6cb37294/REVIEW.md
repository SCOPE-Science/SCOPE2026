# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Writing x_i²+x_i=S with S the sum of squares forces at most two coordinate values. If the marked r coordinates equal a and the rest equal -1-a, the consistency polynomial is (n-1)a²+(2(n-r)-1)a+(n-r). The involution that swaps value and complement pairs the two descriptions of each nonconstant vector; constants must be added separately. In odd nonsingular characteristic the discriminant is 1-4r(n-r); the potentially repeated descriptions of the constant -1/2 are removed exactly for p|n+1 because the polynomial evaluates to (n+1)/4 there. In the odd singular case the zero linear coefficient forces a nonzero constant term, so precisely the stated residue is excluded. In characteristic two there is one linear root for all proper supports at odd n; at even n the polynomial is a²+a+r, with roots over every field at even r and exactly over even extensions at odd r. The last fact follows also because T²+T+1 defines F4. With two constants in the even-n branch and half of all subsets of each parity, this yields the stated powers of two. The uploaded checker was inspected, not executed: it enumerates labeled vectors and the actual off-diagonal multiplication using small-field tables. Its finite scope does not establish the theorem.

## originality

FAIL

A prior public record states literally the same two odd-characteristic formulas, including singularity and coincident-value corrections, for the same unnormalized K_n algebra, and also gives its extension-field zeta function. The new characteristic-two statement is not in that record's theorem, but direct reduction of its already derived support polynomial gives either a linear equation or a²+a in {0,1}; the roots are in the prime field or F4. No new classification mechanism is needed.
