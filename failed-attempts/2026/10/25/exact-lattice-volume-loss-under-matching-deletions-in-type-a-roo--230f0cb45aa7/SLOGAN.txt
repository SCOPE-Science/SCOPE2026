Deleting a matching of \(r\) graph edges from \(K_n\) removes exactly \(r2^{n-2}\) units of normalized lattice volume from its symmetric edge polytope, yielding \(\binom{2n-2}{n-1}-r2^{n-2}\). In dimension \(n-1\ge4\), just one deletion changes Minkowski decomposability while removing only an exponentially small fraction of the volume.
