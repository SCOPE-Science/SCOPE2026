Every order-reversing, order-decreasing nonempty partial map on a finite chain satisfies \(f^3=f^2\), with its square either zero or a nonzero rank-one idempotent. At width \(w\) and height \(h\), these two cases have the exact counts \(\binom{w-1}{h-1}\binom{n}{w+h}\) and \(\binom{w-1}{h-1}\binom{n}{w+h-1}\), whose totals are \(F_{2n-1}-1\) and \(F_{2n}\), respectively.
