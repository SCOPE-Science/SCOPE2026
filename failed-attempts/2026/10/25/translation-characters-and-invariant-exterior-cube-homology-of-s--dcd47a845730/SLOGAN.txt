In every dimension \(n\ge3\), the complete translation character of third rational homology for the scale-two hypercube Vietoris–Rips complex depends only on whether a nonzero translation has Hamming weight at most two. Its translation-invariant part is exactly the exterior-cube coordinate-permutation representation \(\bigwedge^3\mathbb Q^n\), even though the underlying sphere-wedge homotopy type was already known.
