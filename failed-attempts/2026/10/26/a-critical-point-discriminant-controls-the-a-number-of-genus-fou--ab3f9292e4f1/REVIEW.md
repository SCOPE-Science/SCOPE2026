Riemann–Hurwitz gives genus 4; the valuations of x,y,dx there are -5,-3,6, so dx,x dx,y dx,y^2 dx are a regular basis, with no finite poles. As x is a separating coordinate, Cartier extracts exponents congruent to 4 modulo 5 on forms in x. Substituting y=y^5-f and using inverse Frobenius semilinearity makes the first three basis images zero and the fourth H^(1/5) dx, H=beta^2+2 alpha gamma (the x^4 coefficient of f^2). This proves the ranks, and Cartier squared is zero, giving 5-rank zero. The quadratic discriminant is 4H; the H divisor is smooth since its gamma derivative is 2 alpha. Centering the cubic makes the remaining linear coefficient H/(2 alpha); when zero, an additive translation of y and cubic scaling of x give the stated model.
