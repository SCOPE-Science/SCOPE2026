Rooted-forest ancestor graphs cover all trivially perfect graphs. On disjoint components domination multiplies, and adding a universal root to a nonempty forest adds z(1+z)^(n-1) to its polynomial; the one-vertex start is z. Independently I enumerated forests by integer partitions of their orders and combinations with replacement of the rooted types in each equal-size block. Rooting such a forest once gives exactly the connected tree shapes at the next order. All 1,011,310 positive-order forest shapes through 16 were checked with exact integer coefficients. The only violating forest is the rooted tree with three five-vertex chains attached at its root, and so gives exactly the stated graph isomorphism class, irrespective of whether uniqueness of a forest presentation is assumed. The only failing index is 2; a separate 2^16 subset count agrees with the full witness polynomial and the defect 230-225=5. The general clique-bouquet defect is m(2m^2-9m-3)/2, positive at 5 and for all larger integer m. The order-zero forest has constant polynomial 1.
