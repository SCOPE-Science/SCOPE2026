The five partials force all homogeneous coordinates to vanish, so X is smooth. The two coordinate sections give cones on the smooth cubic u²v+v³+z³=0 with j=0; imposing both equations leaves x₄³=0 in the plane. The odd valuation of −x₂/x₃ and local freeness give an integral degree-two cover. In the affine quadratic field set v=x₃, a=s/x₃, b=x₄/x₃ and Q₀=a⁶−b³−1. The cubic gives x₁²−a²=Q₀v²; c=v/(x₁−a) recovers the parametrization, and changing the sign of a replaces c by 1/(Q₀c). This proves rationality and the stated involution. Being a hypersurface regular in codimension one it is normal and Gorenstein. On a transverse slice away from the vertices the two branch curves meet to order three; completing the square gives UV=z⁶. At either vertex the specified slice has double-plane equation s²=v(v³+z³), after a change of unit and renaming the variable. Its weighted blowup is the degree −2 line bundle over the smooth elliptic double cover of P¹ branched in four points, confirming the simple-elliptic Ẽ₇ type. Adjunction gives K_Y=−π*H and volume 6.
