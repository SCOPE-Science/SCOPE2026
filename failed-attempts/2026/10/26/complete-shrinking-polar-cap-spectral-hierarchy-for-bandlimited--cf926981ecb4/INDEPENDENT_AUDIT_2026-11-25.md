# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The fixed-L ±m blocks are the degree ≤d=L−m polynomials with global Jacobi weight t^m(1−t)^m. After t=δu the k-th exterior product first uses distinct indices 0,...,k−1, so its squared norm is δ^{k(m+k)} det(H_k) det((Q^{-1})_{0:k,0:k})(1+O_L(δ)), with H the local moment matrix and Q the global one. Ratios of consecutive products give the stated exponents and coefficients. The local pivot is the monic shifted-Jacobi norm for weight u^m; the global pivot is the reciprocal Schur complement at degree j, i.e. the degree-(d−j) Jacobi kernel at zero for weight t^{m+2j}(1−t)^m. The claimed kernel expression telescopes from the normalized Jacobi endpoint squares (subtract its n−1 value, taking K_{−1}=0). Orders m for exponent k+1 have parity k and lie from 0 to min(k,2L−k), with signed orders counted separately. This proves the multiplicities and the unique extreme sectors, including L=0. For the least zonal mode the positive definite Hilbert moment matrix gives ∑_i |δ^i c_i|²=O_L(δ^{2L}) for its polynomial coefficients c_i, so the normalized polynomial tends to the stated multiple of t^L after a phase choice.

## originality

FAIL

The full hierarchy of exponents AND determinant-ratio leading constants is covered by the earlier smooth-kernel flat-limit result of Barthelmé–Usevich, Theorem 4.1(1), Theorem 4.2 and Corollary 4.3. A fixed Gaussian quadrature and the weighted form of their principal-minor calculation give the frozen hierarchy directly. The remaining factorial evaluation and extremal/multiplicity consequences do not require an additional spectral result. This is demonstrable coverage by implication, not a claim that the earlier paper explicitly states a spherical-cap theorem.

No independent new spectral phenomenon or surviving nonroutine exact claim was identified in the submitted scope; a convenient explicit restatement is not sufficient here.
