# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For real Pauli coordinates the norm is |sθ|+‖qθ‖, and the lower-bound gap is 2 Eθ‖sθqθ‖−‖2 Eθ sθqθ‖. Equality is automatic for zero trace. Otherwise rotate the phase to make the trace positive real: equality forces u+v tan θ to lie on one nonnegative ray, so v=0, including the all-zero vector case. At zero trace the integral gives the squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm divided by four. For the upper bound equality forces the continuous angular norm to be constant. A nonzero scalar part has an upward cusp at a zero of sθ that the Euclidean term cannot cancel. With zero scalar part constancy means equal lengths and orthogonality of u,v, precisely square zero by the Pauli product; square zero implies trace zero, including the zero matrix. The checker was inspected, not executed; it corroborates equality examples and inequalities, not the necessity directions.
