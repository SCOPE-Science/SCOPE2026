# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Set K=k^p. Entrywise p-th powers send GL_n(k) onto GL_n(K): entrywise roots of a matrix over K have determinant whose p-th power is its nonzero determinant. On projective matrices this gives precisely the embedded PGL_n(K). The embedding is faithful because proportional invertible matrices over K have proportionality scalar in K. Consequently the action in question is exactly the standard action of that subgroup on P^{n−1}(k). Invertible K-coordinate changes preserve the span, and changing the homogeneous representative multiplies it by a scalar in k×. Conversely, rescale so that the spans agree. The two maps from K^n onto that common span sending the standard basis to the coordinates are surjections. Matching respective kernel bases and lifts of a basis of the common target yields a domain automorphism that intertwines the maps; its transpose acts on the coordinate columns. Every nonzero K-subspace of dimension at most n occurs by taking basis coordinates and padding with zeros. For perfect k all spans have dimension one and one homothety class; for imperfect k choose t∉K and compare [1:0:…] with [1:t:0:…], whose ranks one and two are distinct under homothety. This argument applies also to infinite [k:K], requires no numerical certificate, and asserts neither a general parabolic classification nor infinitude of orbits. The frozen proof and verification note are reproducible as elementary algebra without external data.

## originality

FAIL

The full frozen orbit criterion, not just its finite-imperfection-degree restriction, is mechanically implied by Neri–Zullo's earlier rank-metric code/system correspondence. The reduction from potentially infinite [k:k^p] to their finite extensions is decisive: each pair of finite coordinate lists lies in a finite intermediate extension, over the same base k^p, and both equivalence relations descend to it. This is established prior implication, not an inference about novelty from absence of a search result.

## value

FAIL

A complete invariant for the twisted action is a natural question, and the frozen elementary proof is a useful explanation. However, for this particular result the invariant and the perfectness boundary follow directly from the known dimension-one system classification: restricting to the field generated by the coordinates and identifying the image of Frobenius leaves no additional classification problem. The frozen statement does not classify a new parabolic action beyond this reduction or resolve a remaining homothety census. It therefore does not provide an independently valuable new finding under the audit standard.
