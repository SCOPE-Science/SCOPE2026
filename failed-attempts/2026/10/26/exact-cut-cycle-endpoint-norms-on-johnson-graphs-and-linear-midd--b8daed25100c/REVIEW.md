On positive-side orbit j, neighbor counts are j(n−2k+j) down, (k−1−j)² up, one to its negative partner and n−2 to zero. The latter two types yield nv_j in the reduced equation. The density and fixed-rank limits follow by division by n or passage to the fixed-rank limit, respectively.
