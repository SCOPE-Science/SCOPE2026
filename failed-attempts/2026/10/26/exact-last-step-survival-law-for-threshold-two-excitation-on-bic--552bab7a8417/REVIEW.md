# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The readout is first entry into a periodic orbit, not time of last excitation. Within each shore, the initial color classes remain internally synchronized. For any subset of classes the test ‘sum of sizes ≥2’ is unchanged by capping each size at 2. This preserves each active class's entire itinerary: with class membership fixed, equality of class-state tuples is equivalent to equality of full labelled states. I wrote and ran a separate bitset simulator on all 676 admissible signatures; it reproduced the full (preperiod, least period) table and verified the thin–fat predicate on every signature, with 24 signatures of preperiod 5 and all 24 eventually fixed. Direct labelled simulations included K_(3,4), where 144 of 2187 states have preperiod 5 and no periodic orbit is nonconstant. In one orientation the fat part contributes P_a ways and the thin part contributes 2Q_b; the reverse case is disjoint since thin parts have fewer than two excited vertices. The two forbidden excited-count ranges used for the closed form of P_n are disjoint for n≥3, and P_n/3^n→1. Fat needs at least four vertices and thin at least three, yielding the exact size boundary, including smaller positive shores.

## value

FAIL

The last-entry stratum is a sensible quantitative object, and its simple expression is convenient. But the frozen submission extracts an already exactly tabulated shell of the same model using the same finite quotient. The short factorization groups two lists of shore triples in that table without a new dynamical invariant, argument, or boundary beyond the existing census.

The dated certificate retains the supplied scientific assessment, sources and limitations.
