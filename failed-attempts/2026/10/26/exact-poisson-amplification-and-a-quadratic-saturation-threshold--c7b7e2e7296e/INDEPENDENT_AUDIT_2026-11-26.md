# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Tensor projections give the pseudoinverse for the unnormalized Laplacian; the four orbit multiplicities are 1,n−1,m−1,(m−1)(n−1). Symmetry and zero row sums give both induced endpoint norms as twice the positive row mass, attained by the signs of one row on the full input space. For m≥3, R≥0 and summing over the root's row gives D+(n−1)R=(m−1)/m². The remaining candidate positive entry has numerator Cnum=−n²+(m²−m)n−m². Its smaller root is below m, and the larger is the claimed irrational ρ_m for m≥4. As an independent integer boundary check, Cnum is m²−2m−4>0 at n=m²−m−2 and −m−1<0 at n=m²−m−1. For m=3 it is −(n−3)²; direct substitution gives the m=2 exceptions. On the active branch the bound 3(m−1)/m² for m≥4 is less than 5/8, and the other cases establish unique equality at (2,2). Rescaling the branch formula for n=O(m) and the positive-part formula for n/m²→λ gives both claimed limits.

## originality

FAIL

There is a distinct indexed record that gives the exact pointwise kernel of this very pseudoinverse for all positive conductances. With both conductances set to one, only four scalar values and their immediate multiplicities remain. The endpoint norm and support claims in the frozen record are direct consequences of that stronger explicit kernel, not merely consequences of a general existence theorem. This is an overlap decision, not a claim about same-day priority.

The vertex endpoint norm and finite cutoff are naturally motivated, and the cutoff is a useful way to display the sign change. For this particular family, however, exact values and the boundary are mechanically obtained from the already available four-entry kernel by summation and a quadratic inequality. The frozen scaling and extremizer observations add no independently supported nonroutine result beyond that evaluation.
