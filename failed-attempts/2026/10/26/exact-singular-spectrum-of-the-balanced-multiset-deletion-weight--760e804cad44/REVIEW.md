The same-color contribution to DU-UD is 2u_i+1 and cross-color terms cancel, giving 2k+m in degree k. The eigenvalues are strictly decreasing for r<n and positive, so their extreme square roots and their ratio are as claimed. For r=n the one-color matrix is [n]; for r=1 the sole Gram eigenvalue is n.
