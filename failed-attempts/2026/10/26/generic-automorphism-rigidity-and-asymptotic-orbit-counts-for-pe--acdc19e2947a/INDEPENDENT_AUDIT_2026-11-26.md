# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Perfectness makes the target basis squares independent, so every isomorphism is monomial. A monomial M acts by A ↦ M A (M^(2))^(-1), with entrywise squaring multiplicative on monomial matrices. Every nonidentity diagonal tuple forces one distinct entry per column to vanish. If k points move, k(2n−k) ordered matrix positions move; each nontrivial coordinate orbit gives at most one free entry. Conditioning on invertibility costs at most γ_q^(-1); summing fixed-point probabilities gives the expectation bound. Burnside identifies the relative error with E(S−1), and each orbit contributes the same total stabilizer mass, giving the separately claimed class-uniform bound by s≤2(s−1) on s≥2. For k≤n/2 the rank-loss exponent is at least 3kn/4, and above n/2 it is at least 3n²/8, proving the stated fixed-field error. An independent n=2, q=3 replay at /workspace/branches/33f8145a-9743-4cd5-8fc3-e91fcc439adc/check_record4_small.py gives 48 invertible matrices, stabilizer frequencies 40 and 8 for orders 1 and 2, and 7 classes, 2 nonrigid. The uploaded verifier was inspected but not run; the finite check is not the general proof.
