# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For any ideal, W is its projection to A⊕B and D its intersection with Z. For fixed W,D the exact ideal condition is [W,A⊕B]⊆D and the lifts are all graphs of linear maps W→Z/D. Their count is q^(dim W·(ab−dim D)), with no gradedness restriction. Independent bracketing against the two factors gives [W,A⊕B]=A′⊗B+A⊗B′, with intersection A′⊗B′, so its dimension is rb+as−rs. Once the projections A′,B′ are chosen, their two quotient kernels of codimension h and a quotient isomorphism describe W bijectively, giving the Gaussian and GL factors and dim W=r+s−h. Counting D in the remaining central quotient establishes all terms including zero projections and h=0. Alternation and the argument still hold in characteristic two. An independent RREF enumeration checked K₁,₁ and K₁,₂ over F₂ (16 and 374 subspaces, yielding [1,1,3,1] and [1,3,7,7,7,1]); no universal inference is made from these counts.
