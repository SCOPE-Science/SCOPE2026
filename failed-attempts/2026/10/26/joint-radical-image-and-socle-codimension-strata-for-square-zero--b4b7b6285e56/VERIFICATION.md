On the locus of the two exact ranks the maps to W and K are regular: locally a nonzero maximal minor of the horizontal or vertical concatenation gives the corresponding Grassmannian chart. Recovering the flag from the tuple therefore inverts the incidence-bundle parametrization on the reduced stratum. Its fiber is the open subset of Hom(V/K,W)^ell where the horizontal concatenation is onto and the vertical concatenation is injective. The two rank inequalities are necessary and sufficient: for a≥b use an injective first map covering b basis vectors of W and the unused slots in the other ell−1 maps to hit the rest; for b≥a use a surjective first map and assign the remaining input vectors to distinct slots in the other maps. This works over every field and handles equality; a single zero rank forces the other to be zero. The flag dimension a(n−a)+b(n−a−b) plus the affine fiber dimension ell·a·b gives the claimed formula.
