# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For each strictly interior bias the independent binary law is non-atomic and homogeneous self-similar. Conditioning on any finite prefix leaves the same law in affine coordinates, with the limiting biased frequency unchanged. The maps x^2/4 and sqrt(1+x^2)/2 both have positive second derivatives, also after composition with a dyadic affine map. Theorem 3.1 gives a power exponent depending on the original measure only, with map-dependent constants. The inputs and the shifted root therefore have the asserted absolute normality by Fourier normality and translation of the Fourier phase. The unshifted root is half the binary variable; its limiting frequency theta differs from 1/2. Every t in the specified set is non-dyadic, so t and t+1/4 have identical binary tails after position 2 and different root frequencies along the full sequence, which suffices to contradict (TU). The biased-frequency class is Borel and Lebesgue null, and the square map on the relevant compact interval is Lipschitz, proving singularity and nullity. Choose a dyadic cylinder whose closure maps into J. For any 2k sigma>1 the convolution Fourier transform is in L^2, so the convolution is absolutely continuous and gives full mass to the analytic k-fold sumset. The sumset is Lebesgue measurable and positive in measure, so Steinhaus gives a neighborhood in its difference set, not necessarily in the sumset itself.

## originality

FAIL

Stephan already gives almost-sure Isaac counterexamples for the identical shifted-square pair and explicitly extends the construction to biased dyadic Bernoulli digits. For that exact family, the additional assertions are direct consequences of Mosquera–Shmerkin's stronger map-uniform nonlinear Fourier decay theorem and its stated normality consequence. On each cylinder one substitutes the two strictly convex compositions into the same theorem on the same measure, and a sufficiently high convolution power then has an L^2 density by Plancherel. Thus lack of an inspected earlier statement of the full conjunction does not establish a distinct scientific claim under the prior-implication standard. This is a finding of prior coverage of the mechanism and its direct consequences, not an assertion that Stephan printed the full conjunction.
