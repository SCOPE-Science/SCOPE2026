## correctness

The degree-L component of cyclic derivatives can arise only from cyclic monomials of degree L+1=nr; each arrow derivative of (a_0...a_{n-1})^r equals r times the unique compatible path of length L. If r=0 in K, all degree-L derivative terms vanish so a Jacobian ideal cannot generate m^L. If r!=0, these derivatives generate all length-L paths. For hyperpotentials, endpoint compatibility forces L congruent n-1 mod n, and the one-path-per-arrow relations satisfy sum_i[a_i,p_i]=0 by cycle telescoping. Independently checked path-length and occurrence arithmetic for n=3,4,5,7; 2<=L<=27; char 0,2,3,5: 416 parameter-characteristic configurations. Finite arithmetic corroborates the all-parameter proof.
