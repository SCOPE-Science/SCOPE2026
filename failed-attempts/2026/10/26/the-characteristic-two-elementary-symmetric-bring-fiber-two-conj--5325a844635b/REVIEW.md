# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In the hyperplane e₁=0, the e₂ gradient has entries Xᵢ, so it is nowhere projectively proportional to the all-ones vector on that hyperplane. The resulting smooth quadric has exactly five F₂-points, all of weight four, hence is nonsplit. The restriction of e₃ is not identically zero: (1,ω,ω²,0,0) with ω²+ω+1=0 lies on the quadric and has e₃=1. At each weight-four point e₃ vanishes and its differential agrees with that of e₂, so the cubic section has order at least two. Over F₄ the two rulings are interchanged; the semilinear descent has the form (A(v²),B(u²)), and its square over F₄ gives AB^(2)=1, so replacing v by A^(2)(v) puts descent in the stated swapped form. The two Frobenius graphs have types (2,1),(1,2); their Segre parametrizations use all four cubic monomials, and the equations have independent differentials even at infinity. They meet precisely at the five rational fixed points. A (3,3) divisor not containing either graph would have intersection length 9 with that graph, less than the required 10. Both components exhaust the divisor class and are reduced. The node formula gives genus four and the components have total degree six. Frobenius swaps them in odd degrees and fixes them in even degrees, yielding 5 or 2(2ⁿ+1)−5 respectively and the claimed zeta function.

A distinct same-day indexed theorem gives the very same geometric and arithmetic description for the elementary-symmetric integral model, together with flatness and explicit parametrizations. The cited power-sum papers do not supply this coverage.
