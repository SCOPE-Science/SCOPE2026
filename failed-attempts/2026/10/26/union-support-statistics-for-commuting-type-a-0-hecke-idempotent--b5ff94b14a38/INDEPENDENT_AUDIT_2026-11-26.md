# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Demazure multiplication never decreases length and a word's support is invariant under the presentation relations. For an idempotent, multiplying it on the right by its own reduced word must therefore leave it unchanged at each step. Every generator of its support is a right descent, forcing the full reversal on each support block. If two idempotents commute, their product is thus the longest element of the union. On a union interval with a missing J-edge p and missing K-edge q, the leftmost entry cannot reach the right end in the two stages if p≤q, and the rightmost cannot reach the left end if p>q. Conversely each full component reversal absorbs the smaller factor, and distinct components act on disjoint position blocks. The four membership statuses then give exactly 2^{r+1}−1 admissible labels in a nonzero run of r indices. The run/zero generating function is the one stated, including m=0. Specialization yields the exact totals and commutation probability. For z near 1 the analytic dominant root λ_+(z) has λ_+(1)=2+√2, λ_+'(1)=(3+√2)/2 and λ_+''(1)=−1/(4√2). Differentiating log λ_+(e^s) gives the stated mean and variance slopes, with bounded contributions from the analytic prefactor and the exponentially negligible second root. Chebyshev gives the claimed law of large numbers. The frozen script and notes were inspected, not executed; enumeration through m=7 is corroboration, not the proof.
