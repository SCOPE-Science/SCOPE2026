## correctness

PASS

Independently verified for real masses. Write P_i,N_i for the positive and negative mass in part i, and q_i for the diagonal mass of a coupling at part level. Capacity outside i forces q_i >= (P_i+N_i-P)_+ for each i; at most one bound is positive since the U_i sum to 2P. If none is positive, fractional Hall applies: a source set in one part sees demand P-N_i, while one meeting two parts sees all demand. If i alone is overloaded, send all outside demand from i and all outside supply into i, then match the equal residual masses within i. Thus all diagonal lower bounds are attained in one plan. Every plan costs P+sum_i(t_i-1)q_i and the coefficients are nonnegative; this proves simultaneous optimality, the norm formula and affinity, including t_i=1 or 2, singleton parts and the zero measure. The frozen rational enumerator is illustrative evidence rather than a proof and was not executed.
