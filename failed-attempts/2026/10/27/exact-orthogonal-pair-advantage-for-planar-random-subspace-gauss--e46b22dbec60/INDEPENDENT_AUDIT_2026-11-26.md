# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

With r equal to the square root of the condition number, the energy coordinates turn the two exact searches into Euclidean projections. After the first search the residual is (w^T y_0)w, where w=(-sin(theta),r cos(theta))/sqrt(r^2 cos^2(theta)+sin^2(theta)). The fresh normalized search vector has covariance diag(r,1)/(r+1). The rotated second vector instead has squared inner product r^2/(DE) with w, with D and E the two conjugate angular denominators. Mixed coefficients integrate to zero by reflection. Substituting X=tan^2(theta) gives rational functions with poles at -1, -r^2 and -1/r^2, of order at most two. Independently decomposed and integrated the rational terms exactly: the two diagonal iid coefficients are (r^2+r+2)/(2(r+1)^3) and r(2r^2+r+1)/(2(r+1)^3), and each paired coefficient is its iid counterpart times ((r^2-1)/(r^2+1))^2. For r=1 the paired projections kill both coordinates exactly and the iid coefficients are 1/4. Both energies vanish for the zero start; otherwise iid energy is strictly positive and the ratio is less than one for every finite r.
