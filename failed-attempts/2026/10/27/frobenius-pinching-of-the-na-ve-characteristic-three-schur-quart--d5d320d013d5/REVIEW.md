# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In characteristic three the invertible substitution gives xa³−zb³, primitive and linear in x over k[z,a,b], hence irreducible over the algebraic closure. The partials generate (a³,b³), a projective complete intersection of degree nine with transverse local basis a^i b^j, 0≤i,j<3. Failure of regularity in codimension one proves nonnormality. In the projective bundle with quotient convention the four basepoint-free sections give [x:z:a:b]=[λt³:λs³:μs:μt]. Off the line the base and fibre coordinates are recovered regularly on the a≠0 and b≠0 charts. The pullback of (a,b) cuts out μ=0, and on it [s:t] maps to [t³:s³]. This is relative Frobenius up to coordinate exchange, so fibres have one geometric point, and projectivity gives finiteness. The map is finite birational from a normal surface and is radicial and surjective. Twisting both summands by O(−1) identifies the normalization as the Hirzebruch surface F₂. Over F_q, off the singular line each [a:b] gives an affine line, for q(q+1) points, and the line itself contributes q+1; this also gives the zeta function independently of radicial invariance. A fresh enumeration by first-nonzero-coordinate projective charts in F₃ and F₃[u]/(u²+1) returned (surface,line) counts (16,4) and (100,10). The frozen verification script was read, not run.
