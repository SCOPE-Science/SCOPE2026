The partials are x_(j−1)^2, giving smoothness. On a rational subspace the ordered coefficients of the cubic restriction in t_a^2 t_b are precisely B(b_a,b_b); binary point containment alone would not suffice. The orthogonal splitting T⊕E yields six Q-null two-spaces in E, three in each of two disjoint-nonzero-vector partitions, so no Q-null three-space exists (each nonzero vector lies on two, rather than three, null two-spaces). The graphs over the six spaces, and the subspaces meeting T in a line of vector dimension one, give 75 lines and nine planes. For the point degrees, a nonzero t in T lies on nine vertical lines; at a nonzero pure e in E there are three vertical and four graph lines; at t+e with both components nonzero there are one vertical, two rank-one graph, and two isomorphism graph lines. An independent RREF enumeration of 63, 651 and 1395 candidate spaces evaluated the original cubic on every candidate over F4, not just the nine positives. This is exact by interpolation since the restriction has individual degrees ≤3<4. It matched 39,75,9, all four histograms, and a common point for each row triple and a common line for each column triple, with each row and column meeting in exactly one plane.
