# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The special-fibre factorization is correct coefficient by coefficient. The two factors are graphs of squaring maps in opposite directions on the two projective lines. Their tangent lines are complementary, and their intersection number is five; the five computed F2 points therefore exhaust the reduced intersections. On the Segre coordinates Frobenius acts by ((s:v),(t:u)) -> ((u:t),(v:s)), swapping the factors. Modulo 2 the quadric is smooth and integral and the cubic is nonzero on it, so successively lifting those nonzerodivisors shows that 2 is a nonzerodivisor in the local complete intersection. This supplies the flatness step used in the proof. At the representative node, smooth elimination of the quadric gives cubic constant 10 and even linear coefficients; its class modulo the square of the ambient maximal ideal is 2, which proves regularity and hence thickness one. Coordinate symmetry handles all other nodes. Orienting the five graph edges alike gives cycle lattice rank four and sign action on every edge under component exchange. The degree-zero multidegree lattice modulo the intersection lattice is Z/5 with vertex swap acting by inversion. The resulting counts 1 and 5 and toric conductor 4 follow from the semistable Jacobian formulas.
