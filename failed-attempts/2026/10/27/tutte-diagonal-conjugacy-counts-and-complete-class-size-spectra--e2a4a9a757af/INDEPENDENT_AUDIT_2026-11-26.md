# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Multiplying in the square-zero basis gives (d_s-d_t)y_a=(b_s-b_t)x_a for commuting units. Off H(d), this determines y_a uniquely; on H(d), all y_a are free and b must be constant along the support S of x on H. Thus the centralizer has order q^|H| (q-1)^(n-r(S)), and the class size is q^(m-|H|) (q-1)^r(S). The diagonal and the support on equal-diagonal arrows are invariant under conjugation: diagonal units rescale, while radical conjugation changes x only on different-diagonal arrows. Dividing the number of elements of the stratum by its class size gives (q-1)^(|S|-r(S)) classes per diagonal and support. Equality propagates along components of H, so H is a flat; the exact diagonals for a flat are proper (q-1)-colourings of the contraction, counted by (q-1)^c chi(M/H;q-1). This gives (1), with q=2 collapsing all W-degrees for a given U-degree. Independently, each simultaneous equality edge for a pair of diagonals contributes an extra factor q to the count of commuting pairs. Expanding the product and dividing by the group order yields summands (q-1)^(n+|S|-2r(S)), the Tutte normalization c+r(E)-r(S)+|S|-r(S). The proof includes loops, parallel arrows, mixed orientations, no arrows, and isolates; loops are always equality edges and contribute independent central additive factors. The two reported q=3 example multisets also follow from the diagonal/support count.
