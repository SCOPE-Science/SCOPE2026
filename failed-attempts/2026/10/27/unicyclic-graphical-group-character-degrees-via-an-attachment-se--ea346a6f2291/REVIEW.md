# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Leaf-pair elimination gives rank 2ν on forests with nonzero edge weights, over any field. For an attached tree T with bridge endpoint u, restriction to the coordinate hyperplane u=0 has the same rank exactly when some radical vector has nonzero u-coordinate; otherwise that hyperplane maps to a hyperplane of the symplectic quotient and the rank drops by two. This is the frozen matching-essentiality test. Split the tree form into a nondegenerate complement and its radical. Eliminating the nondegenerate block does not change other entries: the bridge meets only one cycle vertex and the self-pairing is zero. A nonessential root leaves a radical vector paired with that cycle vertex v. Eliminating this pair leaves the unchanged forest on the remaining vertices (and zero radical coordinates). The matching number in this case is ν(T)+1+ν(H−(T∪{v})), using the bridge plus maxima in T−u and the remaining forest, and at most one matching edge incident to v. If every root is essential, the cycle survives unchanged and maximum matching sizes add. The odd cycle has maximal possible rank by deleting one vertex; the even cycle loses exactly two ranks on equality of its alternating products (a vertex-deleted path gives the lower bound). Solving for one nonzero cycle weight gives precisely (q−1)^(|S|−1) exceptional assignments. For odd q the class-two coadjoint orbit for a fixed edge functional of rank 2r has size q^(2r) and degree q^r, giving q^(n−2r) characters per such functional. A bounded independent GF(4) enumeration of a square, square with a leaf and square with a two-edge tail gave, in increasing half-rank, (1,75,180), (1,123,900), and (1,135,1800,2160). On full support the exceptional counts were 27, 0 and 243, respectively, as predicted.

## originality

FAIL

Distinct indexed records contain the same full all-field rank enumerator, supportwise rank-drop condition, and odd-field character enumerator. The first uses the equivalent condition that every maximum support matching pairs the entire cycle internally; the second gives an equivalent factorization over rooted trees. This is verified existing coverage of the mathematical claim, not a finding about priority or who discovered it first. The path dates differ, but do not independently establish priority or lineage. The frozen proof is correct, yet no distinct surviving theorem is established by the different presentation.

## value

PASS

As mathematics the full enumerator is useful: it classifies all assignments, distinguishes a leaf from an even-length tail via a precise combinatorial test, and converts the result into degree multiplicities in a natural family of graphical groups. This is a general structural formula rather than an arbitrary small example. That usefulness does not make this presentation a distinct contribution given the verified coverage.
