The independence complex of the ordinary two-subset Kneser graph is \(\mathfrak S_m\)-equivariantly homotopy equivalent to the two-skeleton of an \((m-1)\)-simplex for every \(m\ge4\). Its full integral top-homology representation is \(\bigwedge^3\ker(\mathbb Z^m\to\mathbb Z)\), strengthening the previously known nonequivariant wedge-of-spheres result.
