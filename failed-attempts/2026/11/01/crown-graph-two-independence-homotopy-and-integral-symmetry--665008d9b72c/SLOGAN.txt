Deleting just one perfect matching from \(K_{n,n}\) shifts the first higher-independence complex from a wedge of circles to \(\operatorname{Ind}_2(K_{n,n}-M)\simeq\bigvee^{\binom{n-1}{2}}S^2\) for every \(n\geq3\). Its full integral top-homology representation is \(\bigwedge^2\ker(\mathbb Z^n\xrightarrow{\mathrm{sum}}\mathbb Z)\), twisted by the sign of exchanging the two bipartition classes.
