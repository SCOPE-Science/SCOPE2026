## originality

FAIL

FAIL: Krötz, Kunze, and Stanton (2009), arXiv:0902.4618, full author-uploaded primary text, Sections 5–7, already calculate the Bochner density of zonal spherical functions for split real rank-one groups, INCLUDING complementary-series parameters. Their eqs (6.3),(6.4), Lemma 7.3 and Proposition 7.1 show gamma-factor spectral densities and positivity. For SL2(R) there is no 2-alpha root, so the hypergeometric factor in eq (6.3) equals 1; with their lambda=1-2nu and r=1/4, their complementary gamma arguments (1±lambda±i frequency)/4 equal precisely nu/2 and (1-nu)/2 (up to torus-frequency normalization). Those gamma factors have no zeros and denominators are positive, so strict pointwise positivity follows. Their principal formula is also explicit. Standard spherical Bochner mixture/Tonelli then supplies the present all-mixtures statement; omission of an explicit all-L1-mixtures corollary cannot restore originality. This is decisive prior coverage, not a search failure.
