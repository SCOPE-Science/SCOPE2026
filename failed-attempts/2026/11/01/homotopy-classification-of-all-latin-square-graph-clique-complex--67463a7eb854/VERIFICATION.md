# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

PASS. For any Latin square n≥5, the rook clique subcomplex is the union of full row and column simplices with nerve K_{n,n}; inclusion into the full row/column/symbol clique complex is surjective on π1 because every extra symbol edge is triangulated with a two-edge rook path. All rook loops are normally generated by rectangles. Every rectangle is filled by two triangles if a diagonal is a symbol edge, otherwise by the explicit six triangles on four corners plus the two unique cells with the symbol of one corner. Checked that these triangles form a genuine topological disk: four outside boundary edges once, other edges twice, two interior vertex links circles and four boundary links intervals. This argument is uniform in any Latin square, not a homology-only cancellation. Cioabă–Mim all-field homology Thm3.12 gives H1=0, H_i≥3=0, H2 rank (n-1)^3−I(M); UCT plus simple connectivity and Hurewicz–Whitehead produce the integral wedge. Independently reconstructed every rectangle disk for cyclic Latin n=5,6,7 (100,225,441 rectangles, including 50,144,294 six-triangle disks); all-n proof rests on Latin uniqueness, not enumeration.

Their Theorem3.12 calculates all positive-degree homology over every field, and the standard Hurewicz/Whitehead consequence gives the exact stated wedge count once those already-proved loop contractions are recognized. The new submission reorganizes this published argument via the rook nerve and spells out a consequence not titled as a theorem, but the full final claim is mathematically covered by prior methods and direct implications.
