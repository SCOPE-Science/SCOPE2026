If all four-subsets of a five-set are connected, the set is connected and cannot be a tree (delete a nonleaf), so it induces C5. Six such five-subsets would give 4e=30. In a star the three-leaf triangle has a unique completion, since a second common neighbor creates a square. In a path the triangle comprising an end and the disjoint end edge has a second completion exactly in a pentagon; it has at most two completions globally, by the no-square condition.
