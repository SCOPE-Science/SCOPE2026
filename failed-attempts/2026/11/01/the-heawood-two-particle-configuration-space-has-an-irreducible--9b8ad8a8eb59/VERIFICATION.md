# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The Heawood graph is sufficiently subdivided for two particles: essential vertices have separation at least one and cycles have at least three edges. The equivariant cubical inclusion computes ordinary unordered homology. An independent Fano-incidence computation gives (91,252,168) cells, 28 hexagons, 42 vertex-disjoint pairs, and 42 integral product cycles of zero boundary. Modulo three the two boundaries have ranks 90,154 and the torus span has rank 14. Augmentation bounds the first rational rank by 90; the 14 independent rational torus cycles bound the second by 168−14=154. This proves (1,8,14) and rational spanning. The graph hexagons span H1: replace each edge of the complete graph on Fano points by its two-edge incidence path. This surjects on graph cycles, and the complete-graph triangles map either to hexagons or to zero. Moving a particle on each such cycle with the second fixed elsewhere therefore gives the surjective, equivariant particle-sum map, which is an isomorphism by dimension. Separately, enumerating all permutations of the seven points preserving the cyclic Fano line system, together with an explicit reversing polarity, verified all eight rows by signed graph and cubical trace. A reversed edge contributes a minus and a swap of the two square factors contributes another minus. The weighted pairings of χ1 and χ2 have norms 1 and 2 and cross-pairing 1. Hence χ1 is absolutely irreducible and the rational complement of its unique copy in H2 is absolutely irreducible of dimension six. Both weighted sums vanish. The argument uses only ranks and characters over Q and does not establish integral saturation.
