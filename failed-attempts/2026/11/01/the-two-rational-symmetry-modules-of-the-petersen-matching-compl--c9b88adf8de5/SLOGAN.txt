The ordinary matching complex of the Petersen graph has exactly two nonzero reduced rational homology groups, both five-dimensional: \(\widetilde H_2\) is the sign twist of the permutation module on five symbols, whereas \(\widetilde H_3\) is the irreducible \(S^{(3,2)}\) module. An exact signed cellular action computes these modules for every one of the 120 automorphisms.
