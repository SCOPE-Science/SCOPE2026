For every pair of nonconstant analytic polynomials, a weighted harmonic Bergman Toeplitz commutator has singular values asymptotic to \(4(\alpha+1)L(f,g)^2/j^2\), where \(L(f,g)\) is the circle average of \(\sqrt{|f'g'|}\). The formula gives the exact weak-Schatten endpoint and detects the combined effect of all polynomial monomials rather than only their individual commutators.
