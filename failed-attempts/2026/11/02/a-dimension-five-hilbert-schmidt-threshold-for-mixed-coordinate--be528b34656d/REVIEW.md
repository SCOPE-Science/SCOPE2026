# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The stated bounded coordinate operators have the required monomial action. Each order of composition has a unique term per output shift (p,q); subtraction gives the displayed (p−q) numerator and the first shift on constants gives 1/4. Orthogonality of distinct output monomials for each input suffices for the squared Hilbert–Schmidt energy. The generating function for the input-shell weight sum is p!q!(1−t)^(−d−p−q) divided coefficientwise by (n+1)_(p+q), giving the factorial expression. For s=p+q, each shell summand is bounded by s^2 (n+s+1)^(d−5)/[(n+1)^2(s+1)^(d−1) binom(s,p)]. For d≤5 this yields an O((n+1)^−2) shell bound by summing over shifts. For d≥5, n+s+1≤(n+1)(s+1) gives, after the claimed rescaling, the summable bound s^2/[(s+1)^4 binom(s,p)], since the inverse-binomial sum over each positive s is at most three. The fixed-shift limit gives the claimed factorial constant by dominated convergence; the shifts (1,0),(0,1) make it positive. Summation gives the logarithmic/power laws and the two-sided cutoff. The finite checker was inspected, not executed; no finite extrapolation is needed.
