# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

FAIL

The general automorphism identification and order formulas are correct with U=(ks+kt)^perp as in the proof, but the Finding's assertion for any nondegenerate complement of the hyperbolic plane is false without 'orthogonal'. For q=3,m=6, take s=(1,1,1,1,1,1), t=(2,1,1,1,1,1) and w=(0,1,2,0,0,0). The first two are a hyperbolic pair; w is orthogonal to them and has norm 2. In the orthogonal complement replace its line kw by k(w+s+t), leaving its orthogonal three-space unchanged. This is still a nondegenerate direct complement, but the new line has norm 1, so the determinant changes by the nonsquare 2 and swaps the even-dimensional orthogonal order used in the formula. For the intended orthogonal interpretation: Ann(E^2)=s^perp has product span ka (the form rank there is m-1 or m-2, always positive), and Ann(a)=W. Thus automorphisms are precisely block pairs with multiplier u and g(s)=u^2s. A nonisotropic s forces u^3=1; rescaling by u^-2 gives an independent isometry on s^perp of discriminant 1/m. An isotropic s gives an orthogonal hyperbolic splitting; the t-coordinate of g(t) is u^-1 and its U-coordinate is arbitrary; isotropy and the mixed pairings uniquely force the remaining coefficients. The GO(U) multiplier image is all units in even dimension (decompose into hyperbolic planes and a possible norm plane, whose norm map is surjective) and the squares in odd dimension (determinants and scalar maps). This proves the intended formulas with the correct signs and orders. The ambiguity is repairable in isolation but not enough to rescue originality or value.
