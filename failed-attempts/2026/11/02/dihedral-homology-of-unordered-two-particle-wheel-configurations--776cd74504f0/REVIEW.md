# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The two-particle Abrams hypotheses hold (minimum cycle length 3, minimum path length between essential vertices at least 1) and the inclusion is equivariant. The disjoint-cell model is connected by moving one point to the hub and the other around the vacant rim. In a square cycle, restricting the boundary to rows with a fixed spoke leaves the boundary of a chain on a path (delete the hub and endpoint), so all spoke coefficients vanish. For the remaining rim pairs each such restriction again lands on a path. This works over every field and over Z; universal coefficients exclude H1 torsion, and chi=-k gives rank k+1. Signed fixed cells give the stated traces: the half-turn's exchanged rim-edge pairs cancel its opposite vertex pairs in the alternating sum; reflections have the listed orientation reversals and exchanged-square signs. Rational characters determine the module. For quotient averaging, one can triangulate the product graph equivariantly for particle swap and the rim group with the diagonal a subcomplex, then barycentrically subdivide. The complement retracts equivariantly by renormalizing barycentric coordinates outside the diagonal subcomplex. After admissible subdivision, quotient rational chains are the coinvariants, hence the invariants by averaging. This applies without assuming the group acts freely; a rotation subgroup of order q averages (k+1)+(q-1), and all reflections sum to -2k.
