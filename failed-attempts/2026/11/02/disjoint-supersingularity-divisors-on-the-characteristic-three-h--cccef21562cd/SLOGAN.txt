For the characteristic-\(3\) genus-five classical Humbert family, the five elliptic-quotient supersingularity divisors are smooth, geometrically irreducible, and pairwise disjoint, so every Jacobian has geometric \(3\)-rank \(4\) or \(5\), never lower. Over \(\mathbb F_{3^m}\), their ordered parameter counts are exactly \(5(3^m-3)\) and \((3^m-3)(3^m-7)\), respectively.
