For the modular Witt Lie algebra \(\operatorname{Der}_{\mathbb F_q}(\mathbb F_q[x]/(x^p))\), with \(p\ge5\) and \(q\) a power of \(p\), the centralizer dimension of every nonzero element depends only on the valuation of its coefficient polynomial. Consequently its complete adjoint-rank spectrum is explicit and two uniformly sampled elements commute with probability \(q^{1-p}+\frac{p-1}{2}(q^2-1)q^{-p-2}\).
