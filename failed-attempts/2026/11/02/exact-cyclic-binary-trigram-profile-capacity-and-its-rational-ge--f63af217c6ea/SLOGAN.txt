The number of distinct complete cyclic trigram-count observations of binary words has the exact generating function \[\sum_{n\ge1}T_nz^n=\frac{2z}{1-z}+\frac{z^2}{1-z^2}+\frac{2z^3}{(1-z)(1-z^2)(1-z^3)}+\frac{z^4(1+z^3)}{(1-z)^2(1-z^2)(1-z^3)(1-z^4)}.\] Consequently \(T_n=n^4/288+O(n^3)\), and \(T_n\) is the exact maximum size of a noiseless binary code distinguished only by those cyclic observations.
