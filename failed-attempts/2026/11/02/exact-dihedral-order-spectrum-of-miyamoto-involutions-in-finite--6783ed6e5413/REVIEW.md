# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The idempotent equation and the Peirce decomposition give exactly the norm-one axes and their negative reflections; their one-dimensional fixed spaces identify only the two opposing vectors. With v=tu+w, the perpendicular form has determinant class Delta and w has norm 1−t². When t²≠1 the reflection product acts identically off the nondegenerate plane and on it has trace 4t²−2 and determinant one. At t=0 it is −I on that plane; otherwise the distinct reciprocal eigenvalues give the actual order, not a projective order. For t=±1 replace v by −v if needed. If w≠0, N=T_uT_v−I has image in span(u,w), sends u to −2w and w to zero, hence has pth power zero and yields a nontrivial element of order p. The standard quadratic-shell counts give Z, both C values and S with the stated discriminant signs. The cyclic half-torus count gives phi(d) distinct values of t, including the single t=0 for order two. Each involution has two axes above it; conjugation permutes the family, and the all-pair factor of four and 3-transposition boundary follow. The group generated by two involutions with product of order d is dihedral of order 2d, including d=1.
