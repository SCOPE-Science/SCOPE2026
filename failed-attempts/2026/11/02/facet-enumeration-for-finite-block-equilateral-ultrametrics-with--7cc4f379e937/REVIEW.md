# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The inequalities define a full-dimensional normalized dual polytope; polarity exchanges its vertices with primal facets. Connectedness of the tight-edge graph is necessary by a small perturbation constant on a tight component missing the basepoint, and sufficient since tight differences are forced in both summands of a feasible midpoint and connectivity pins their potentials. A connected tight graph has a cross-cluster edge, so oscillation is precisely one and translation puts all potentials in [0,1]. A tight cross-edge must use endpoints 0 and 1, so every cluster contains one of them, but not both since t_i<1. In a lower cluster, every value is in [0,t_i] and a strict interior value would be isolated in the tight graph. The upper case is symmetric. Conversely opposite-class anchor sets form a connected complete bipartite subgraph, with each nonanchor joined tightly to the anchors of its own cluster. This also works for singleton blocks, all-anchor blocks, and coincidences between intermediate values of different clusters. The cluster signs and anchor sets are recovered after translating the minimum to zero. Thus the strict-region formula and the 98-facet example hold. At the simultaneous endpoint only the nonconstant normalized binary potentials are extreme, giving 2^N-2 (62 in that example). The frozen checker, inspected but not run, tests ten finite quarter-grid instances, whose tight spanning trees ensure quarter-integrality after normalization; it is not the proof for arbitrary scales.

## originality

FAIL

The arbitrary cluster result is a direct specialization of the prior weighted compatible-split zonotope theorem for finite tree-like metrics (Delucchi–Hoessly, Theorem 3.1, explicitly restated by coauthor Hoessly in Theorem 3.6 of On some polytopes in phylogenetics). In this two-level tree, taking the signs of the singleton and cluster sums gives exactly the claimed potentials and product, with no new tree-polytope claim. This strong coverage does not rely on what might be in the inaccessible journal PDF. The earlier shortened-pair record and the classical equilateral endpoint alone are only partial overlaps and would not justify this decision.

The equilateral comparison follows by removing the stem generators, and the numerical example is immediate. The frozen claim supplies no further nonroutine or independently valuable invariant beyond that covered specialization.
