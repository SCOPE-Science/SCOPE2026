# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

FAIL

The frozen proof establishes the generic count of distinct critical points of the trace objective before cut-locus exclusion. The span argument ensures every simple cyclotomic pole persists and infinity is unramified. For generic t each P'(t) block has eigenvalues ±λ_i(t), λ_i(t)≠0, and zero of multiplicity n_i−1 (the rank-two block is diagonalizable off a finite set, since its nonzero eigenvalues at t=0 are ±1). Thus the commuting locus has the stated dimension bound, and the critical incidence is generically reduced: no vertical divisor exists because P'(t) is never zero, as follows by differentiating P_i(t)_{01}/P_i(t)_{00}=t. Generic smoothness gives simple roots, and Riemann–Hurwitz supplies their number. But the consequent assertion about GD degree, as defined in the cited literature, requires excluding rank(P(t)Q)<d, a data-dependent cut locus within the regular projection chart, not the roots of L. The frozen proof and finite checks do not establish that exclusion. The formula itself is not disproved: the earlier all-scroll record supplies a separate argument, using block-rank-one data where a cut event is g_i(t)=0; on that hypersurface another block's trace derivative is nonconstant, so the simultaneous critical/cut condition has codimension at least two at each t, yielding generic avoidance and a nonzero resultant on the full projector variety. This is missing from the frozen implication, not a counterexample to the count.
