## correctness

PASS

Projection terms reduce to a single variable. In the nonprojection case, a noninvertible B=1−T gives the strict intermediate probability |ker B|/|A| for x*y=y. When B and T are invertible their inverses belong to the finite ring R=Z[T]=Z[B] by finite order. On the finite set of realized binary coefficients, the maps f↦Bf and f↦Bf+T are permutations. Composing the second with the inverse of the first and conjugating by powers of the first yields translations by B^jT. Their additive span is TR=R; starting with coefficient zero gives all of R without adding operations to the term language. A nonzero nonunit of R acts nontrivially but nonbijectively (a bijective endomorphism has a positive power equal to the identity), and so gives a two-variable strict intermediate probability. Conversely, if R is a field, any equation with a nonzero difference coefficient defines an onto linear map onto A and has exactly probability 1/|A|. A field R acts on A as a finite vector space and T generates it with scalar distinct from 0 and 1. For B invertible the displacement group acts by all additive translations: conjugating it forces an isomorphism fixing zero to be additive, then to intertwine T. Rational canonical form gives one class per allowed irreducible polynomial and centralizer GL_{m/d}(F_{p^d}); multiplying by the translation factor and using relabeling gives the stated formulas and the unique projection operation.
