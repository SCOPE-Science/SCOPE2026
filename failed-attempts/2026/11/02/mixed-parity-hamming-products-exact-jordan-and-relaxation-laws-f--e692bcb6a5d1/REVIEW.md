# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The Cartesian adjacency is the tensor sum of the coordinate all-ones operators plus (d mod 2)I. Odd-coordinate factors split into a rank-one projector and its kernel; evaluating the multiplicity polynomial at +1 and -1 gives the two parity sector dimensions, with integer rather than field arithmetic. Even-coordinate factors split into two-step rank-one chains and m_i-2 trivial dimensions. On any tensor summand with a chain, reversing that chain gives a contracting homotopy for the total differential because cross terms cancel in characteristic two. The square-zero differential therefore has homology dimension H and (V-H)/2 two-blocks, including when size two makes H zero. Sectorwise scalar shifts give the four stated block counts; they work over the prime field and extend without change. Both two-block counts are positive in the mixed case. On the zero sector the second power vanishes, while on the one sector it is the identity. Counting the nested zero-sector kernels and the one-sector fixed points gives all the claimed populations, including the division by two for primitive two-cycles. For (2,3) the zero and one sector dimensions are 4 and 2, so over F2 the entry populations are 4, 12 and 48 and there are 2 fixed points and 1 two-cycle.
