# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Since t² fixes the image of t, each functional component is a fixed-root star or a two-cycle with direct leaves. The two commutation/absorption relations make e constant on each such component with image among the fixed points of t, and e²=e distinguishes one fixed-root star in each fiber. Thus the fiber series is UPH, with floor(j/2) two-cycle types of size j from unordered splits of j−2 leaves. Taking a multiset of fibers and subtracting the no-cycle case gives exactly the rank-three orbits. In the Gould–Grau–Johnson generation regime n≥5, a nonessential rank-two pair is a square (u²,e) of a rank-three pair. A 2-cycle of u supplies two nondistinguished roots in one e-fiber; conversely swapping any two such roots and sending each of their leaves to the opposite root constructs a permissible u with u²=t. Essential fibers thus have at most one extra star and contribute U(1+U), with coefficient j in degree j. Subtracting the rank-one multiset produces MacMahon minus P. Proposition 4.2 then supplies the generator count, and logarithmic differentiation gives the stated coefficient recurrence. The n≥5 restriction is necessary for this use of Proposition 4.2.
