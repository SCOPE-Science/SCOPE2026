# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The four rows are correct, including the subgroup-scheme rather than pointwise-rational interpretation. The quartic roots form a free transitive orbit under negation and inversion: a collision forces t^4=1 and s=±2. Conversely these exceptional parameters give multiple roots, and any overlap with x²+1 forces s=2. Frobenius commutes with those two symmetries, so its action on the orbit is translation by an element of the Klein four group: identity or a double transposition. The two roots of x²+1 contribute fixed points or a transposition according to q mod 4. The split quartic parameters are the image of the four-to-one map t↦−(t²+t⁻²) on K× minus the gcd(4,q−1) roots of t⁴=1; the nonsplit parameters form its complement among the q−2 allowed parameters. In the order 1^6, 1²2², 1⁴2, 2³ the numbers of stable even subsets are 32, 8, 16, 8, halved for complementation. There is no further fixed class obtained by interchanging a subset with its complement: a fixed branch point rules this out, and for 2³ such a subset would have odd size 3. The 15 nonzero 2-torsion classes are uniquely represented by pairs; the Weil pairing detects overlap, so maximal isotropics are precisely the three mutually disjoint pairs of a perfect matching. With fixed vertices, an invariant matching pairs them among themselves, forcing the others when only one transposition remains and giving the three possibilities on the four moving points of 1²2². For 2³ there are the three internal pairs or the choice of one internal pair and two ways to match across the other two orbits, yielding seven. Squaring Frobenius fixes all points and all kernels, giving the respective 0, 6, 6, 4 degree-two orbits. The counts are independent of c, since branch points and their action are unchanged; the empty rows at q=3 and q=5 are handled by the fiber count.
