## correctness

PASS

The past-path stationary stochastic integral is conditionally centered Gaussian with bounded variance u₂(1+cY). Its construction gives the invariant law, and the decaying initial-position term shows uniqueness for a stationary environment. For the discounted telegraph process, the stationary generator implies E[Y^k]=E[ZY^(k−1)] and E[ZY^k]=(k/(k+γ/a))E[Y^(k−1)] for k>0. This independently gives E[Y²]=1/(1+γ/a), E[Y⁴]=3/[(1+γ/a)(3+γ/a)], and symmetry gives zero odd moments, corroborating the needed beta identities. The resulting A and R give γ/a=(3R−1)/(1−R) and c²=2AR/(1−R); their strict physical inequalities are exactly u₂>0, A>0, 1/3<R<1/(1+2A). Substitution verifies sufficiency; R=1/3 is the zero-switching boundary, and the upper bound at fixed A>0 is zero minimum diffusivity. At fixed u₂ and A, u₆=15u₂³(1+3A) and lower moments are constant along c²=A(1+γ/a) with 0<γ<a(A⁻¹−1). This proves actual nonidentifiability through degree seven and identifies degree eight as the first sufficient one. An independent exact generator replay reconstructed three admissible triples, including irrational contrasts via rational squared contrast, and checked a pair with equal moments through seven but different eighth moments.
