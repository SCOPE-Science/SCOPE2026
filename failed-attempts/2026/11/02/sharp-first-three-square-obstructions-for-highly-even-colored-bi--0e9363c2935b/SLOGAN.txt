For every colored binary-partition count with at least six factors of two in its number of colors, the earliest coefficient that is not a sum of three squares occurs at one of the exact indices \(1,2,3,4,5,6,8,14\), determined solely by the parity of the color valuation and its odd part modulo eight. Both the bound \(14\) and the valuation threshold six are sharp, and the first obstruction is also determined for every color count \(2^k\) with \(k\ge1\).
