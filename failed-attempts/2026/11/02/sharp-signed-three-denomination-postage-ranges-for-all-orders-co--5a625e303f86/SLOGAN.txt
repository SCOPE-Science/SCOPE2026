For every \(h\equiv9\pmod{12}\) with \(h\ge9\), the optimal three-denomination additive basis containing a negative integer has consecutive range exactly \((h^2+6h+1)/4-1\). An explicit extremizer is \(\{-b,b,2b+1\}\) with \(b=(h+3)/6\), and a residue-class argument proves its full interval coverage.
