For the nonlinear Active Flux TVD correction interval, the exact guaranteed Courant ceiling depends on the ratio \(r=a_{\min}/a_{\max}\): it rises from the limiting value \((\sqrt{73}-7)/2\) for extreme contrast to \(6/7\) once \(r\ge1/6\), improving the source's \((\sqrt{97}-7)/4\) sufficient bound. The full local admissibility region also has an exact slope-ratio-dependent formula; sharpness applies to this specified sufficient interval, not to all possible TVD schemes.
