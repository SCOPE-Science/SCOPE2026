The elementary-symmetric model of Bring's genus-four curve collapses in characteristic five to exactly six reduced \(\mathbb F_5\)-lines meeting at one point, a six-branch complete-intersection singularity of delta invariant \(9\). Coordinate permutations realize the exceptional six-point action \(S_5\cong\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbb F_5)\) on the components.
