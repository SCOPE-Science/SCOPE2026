# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The two gradients are dependent only when all coordinate squares agree. The five signs must agree modulo 5, giving just P. With x=s(1,1,1,1,1)+(a,b,c,-a-b-c,0) the cubic is 3(sQ+D), where Q=a^2+b^2+c^2+(a+b+c)^2 and D=-(a+b)(a+c)(b+c). The rank-three quadratic cone gives A1. The six normalized permutations of (1,2,3,4) give six simple intersections B of Q and D by Bézout. The inverse projection is given by [s:a:b:c]=[-D:Qa:Qb:Qc]; its graph is the plane blowup of (Q,D). Over a point of B its exceptional divisor maps to the corresponding line through P, and outside P the first projection has its inverse given by the direction. Only the conic transform contracts, with square -2. Every line missing P projects isomorphically to a plane line M on which D is a linear multiple of Q; the conic intersection with M must therefore be a length-two subscheme of the reduced B. Conversely every secant to two points of B gives exactly one such line. This proves the geometric 6+15 total; the explicit pairing lines exhaust the latter fifteen and all descend. The direction stabilizer acts by relabeling the field values via x↦ux+v, the order-20 normalizer of its Sylow-five subgroup, giving the claimed faithful coset action and the conic realization over F5. Blowing up adds 6q and contracting subtracts q from q^2+q+1. The resulting all-extension counts give the stated zeta function. Independent replay recovered all 21 rational lines (15 pairing, six nodal), 120 permutations on the six and counts 56 and 776 over F5 and F25.
