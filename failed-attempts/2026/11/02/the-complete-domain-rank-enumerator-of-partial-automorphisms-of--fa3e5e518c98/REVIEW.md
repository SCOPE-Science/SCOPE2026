## correctness

PASS

For the induced strict order the comparability graph of a proper subset is a union of paths. A singleton forgets parity; an even path has endpoints at different order levels and no reversal automorphism, while an odd nonsingleton path admits both the identity and endpoint reversal, and its endpoint level distinguishes its type. Matching components gives exactly the factorials and the powers of two. A positive run-gap pair changes the start parity by the total length mod 2; thus the two-state trace tracks rooted run lists. A fixed parity contributes n label choices; each nonempty subset with t runs has t roots, even when its lengths repeat periodically. At least 2t vertices are consumed, giving the cutoff at n and validating the formal log-determinant identity at degree 2n. The empty and full subsets contribute separately; for the full crown the order-level preserving dihedral subgroup has size 2n, including the four-vertex crown. For two deletions, distance 1 and distance 2 contribute 4n² each for n at least 3; thereafter a non-opposite odd distance contributes 4n² and an even distance 8n². These profiles do not mix across distances since the unordered remaining run lengths recover the distance. Opposite deletion gives 2n² for n odd and 4n² for n even; for n=2 the two isolated vertices can be swapped, giving 16+8=24. Single deletions give two odd path types of size n each, yielding 4n². An independent sparse transfer computation and direct induced-subset component census agreed through n=8 and matched all seven totals. Direct strict-relation matching of all partial permutations agreed at n=2,3,4.
