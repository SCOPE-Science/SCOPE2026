# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In characteristic two a diagonal coordinate squaring to 1 is 1, so g=1+X and g^2=1 iff X^2=0. The radical only has the strict blocks A,B,C; the square condition is exactly AB=0, independently of the field. Projection of im X onto the middle space has image im B, and the kernel inside im X is im A+C(ker B), so rank X=alpha+beta+rho as defined in the source. For fixed rank-alpha A, count rank-beta maps B into ker A. Restricting C to ker B and quotienting by im A is a surjective linear map of Hom spaces with fiber q^{ac-(a-alpha)(c-beta)}, the claimed exponent. The Gaussian factor chooses the image subspace and the product counts surjections onto it. The square-zero, nonzero row/column counts and alpha+rho<=a, beta<=c give all upper bounds. For attainment sort the part sizes as d>=e>=f. If d>=e+f match all of the smaller parts to the largest; otherwise in the even case the between-part edge totals (d+e-f)/2, (d+f-e)/2, (e+f-d)/2 are nonnegative integers and admit disjoint endpoints, and in the odd case deleting one vertex from a largest part gives an even triple satisfying the triangle inequalities. Orienting a matching by level gives square-zero and its cardinality equals matrix rank, with submatchings realizing all intermediate ranks. Square-zero Jordan blocks are at most size two, with exactly r blocks of size two; only rank zero is the identity. Substitution at (2,2,2), q=2 gives (1,81,630,216), sum 928 including the identity. The support notes and verifier were inspected but not run; its exact enumeration is only for twelve small prime-field cases, whereas the proof works over every finite field for the radical count.
