# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The degree-81 tame diagonal cover has five inertia groups of order 3, giving genus 55. The index-three quotient projectors split the 2-divisible group: pullback and norm induce the projector to the subgroup invariants since the subgroup order is odd, and the trivial invariant summand is zero. Nontrivial character pairs have support counts 10,10,15,5 for sizes 2,3,4,5, with quotient genus support size minus two. The support-three elliptics are supersingular. For an independent support-four check, normalize the quotient to y³=(t−a)(t−b)(t−c)² with infinity the fourth branch point and put z=y²/(t−c). The regular forms dt/y and dt/z give Cartier maps to √(a+b)·dt/z and dt/y, so both directions are invertible. In support-five normalize to a monic squarefree quartic. Cartier sends dt/y to √s₃·dt/y²+√s₁·t dt/y², kills dt/y² and sends t dt/y² to dt/y. Squarefreeness rules out s₁=s₃=0, so the rank is always two; the stable rank is two for s₁≠0 and zero otherwise. Summing gives a=15 and 2-rank 40−2m. Sending the distinguished point to infinity gives s₁ as the sum of its four finite roots; clearing the nonzero denominators gives exactly the five P_i. For their intersections, write the first four loci as μ=λ+1, λ/(λ+1), 1/λ, λ², and the fifth as μ²=λ. The ten comparisons give λ²+λ+1 up to factors λ and λ+1; conversely each root with μ=λ+1 lies on all five. The gradients are nonzero on the smooth marked locus and at the intersections give slopes 0,1,α,α²,∞. This verifies the complete ordered-marked stratification without extrapolation from finite-field data.
