# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Peirce projections preserve an arbitrary two-sided ideal. A diagonal corner element with nonzero idempotent coefficient is invertible in that corner because its loop part squares to zero; hence the vertex idempotent itself lies in the ideal. After extracting such idempotents and all incident arrows, the independent arrow subspaces on the remaining corners are unconstrained, since every arrow product vanishes. This works with loops, cycles, and parallel arrows, and gives the stated cross-fiber inclusion. Counting codimensions gives the Gaussian product. Each Gaussian coefficient has leading term q^{r(m-r)} of coefficient one, so the surviving subsets with maximal q-degree are exactly those containing U; the other degrees have an integer gap of at least one. Normalizing at z=1 proves both the probability limit and the coefficientwise error; the q=2 example also follows by expanding the eight subset terms. No frozen script was run.
