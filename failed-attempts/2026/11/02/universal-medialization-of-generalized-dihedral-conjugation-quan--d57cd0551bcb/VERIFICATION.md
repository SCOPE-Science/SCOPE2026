# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Direct right conjugation in the semidirect product gives ((epsilon,a)*(delta,b))=(epsilon,(-1)^delta a+2 epsilon b). The proposed reflection relation is compatible with the formula and with inverse right translations; rotations are unchanged as individual classes. Subtracting the index expressions of the two medial sides gives zero for a rotational first operand and a sum of multiples of 4 in A for a reflection. Necessity is witnessed by (F_b,R_a,F_0,R_0), for which the two sides are F_{-b-2a} and F_{-b+2a}; varying a,b forces exactly the required pairs into every medial congruence. Minimality gives the universal property, and counting cosets on each cyclic factor gives the stated finite formula. These arguments apply to arbitrary abelian groups, including the trivial group and exponent-two groups where the two tags still differ.

## originality

FAIL

An earlier distinct indexed result proves the universal medialization of the full conjugation quandle for every group as the conjugation-orbit quotient by its derived subgroup. It also gives the cyclic dihedral instance with the same operation table and count. For an arbitrary abelian kernel, substituting Dih(A) into that exact general theorem yields all of the frozen statement by routine commutator and orbit computations. This is established coverage, not a priority inference from a title or a failure to access literature.

## value

FAIL

The formula and direct proof would make a useful worked example, but as a separate research finding the generalization of the already explicit cyclic example adds only the routine derived-subgroup and orbit calculation inside a theorem already covering every group. No independently useful new invariant or nonmechanical conclusion survives that comparison.
