## correctness

Polar beta integration yields ||z^n||^2=B(n+2,beta+1), thus K=(beta+1)[(1-s)^-(beta+2)-1]/s; s=0 is removable and nonzero. The principal log has |Arg(1-s)|<pi/2; nonzero roots are s=1-exp(2pi i j/(beta+2)) with 0<|j|<(beta+2)/6. A fixed basepoint w admits exactly those with |s|<|w|, yielding ceil((beta+2)arcsin(|w|/2)/pi)-1 pairs; derivative of numerator proves simplicity. Boundary s of modulus 1 excluded. Independently checked beta-moment and finite root counts at multiple nonintegral and boundary parameters; analytic argument covers all beta>-1.
