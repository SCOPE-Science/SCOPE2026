# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For stationary iid alternating runs with finite mean m, equilibrium run age has survivor H(s)/m conditional on the sign. The deficit Z=(B-X)/(2B) decays as Z_entry exp(-kappa A) on a positive run. The preceding negative duration U gives Z_entry >=1-exp(-kappa U); hence any near-edge event with A<=(1-theta)s requires U=O(exp(-kappa theta s)), controlled by the stated lower-tail bound. Negative-current-run contribution is O(exp(-kappa s)). Squeezing H((1-theta)s)/H(s) by regular variation and letting theta down to zero gives P(X>B-eps)~H(kappa^-1 log(2B/eps))/(2m). Karamata gives H(s)~s P(T>s)/(alpha-1). For half-t, H(s)=sqrt(s^2+tau^2)-s and m=tau, yielding kappa tau/[4 log(2B/eps)] with the quoted relative error. This checks both sides, the normalization and the finite-mean boundary; it does not yield a density or arbitrary-force theorem.
