# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For perfect evolution algebras (invertible structure matrix), the independent squares e_i^2 force all derivations diagonal; support edges i→j impose d_j=2d_i. When p=2 all vanish. In odd characteristic, a connected nonzero component admits h=ord_p(2) uniquely determined cyclic vertex colors up to h rotations; invertibility forces equal class sizes and h invertible intercolor blocks. The multivariate exponential formula for connected colored components yields F_q(z)G_{q,h}(z)^{(u-1)/h}, and disjoint h-cycles establish exact 0..floor(n/h) spectrum and first-window count. Direct integer check for GL4(F3) gives 24,261,120=24,253,200+7,872+48; the formal-series checker source has been read, but finite checks do not replace the exponential-formula proof.

## originality

FAIL

Decisively covered by October 25, 2026 published "A cyclic-graded generating function for derivation dimensions of finite-field evolution algebras". It states exactly the same bivariate EGF for marked invertible structure matrices over every finite field, using G_q(x) and F_{q,r}(x^r) for factors renamed F and G here, with r=ord_p(2)=h. It also proves the same dimension bound, first-stratum formula for h<=n<2h, char2 vanishing and identical F3 n4 numerical count. Recasting the exponent, summation index or function names contributes no original claim.
