# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

On the full-side-sampling event, each missing corner is exactly a triangle of area (1/2)sin(theta_i)X_iY_i, and its perimeter replacement is X_i+Y_i-sqrt(X_i²+Y_i²-2cos(theta_i)X_iY_i). Exponentially rare empty or insufficiently populated edges do not change the limit. The 2m directed gaps have joint survival (1-r/n)^n and converge to mutually independent Exp(1), so continuous mapping gives the joint area-perimeter law. Exponential gap tails and exponentially rare exceptional events control all fixed mixed moments. Integrating the two-gap survival function gives P² sin(theta_i)/(2(n+1)(n+2)) plus exponential errors. The area transform is a product of Laplace integrals; the maximal sine coefficient is determined by a stretched-exponential product tail and remaining coefficients by positive Laplace-factor division. Sine rule gives triangle similarity. These arguments are correct for fixed nondegenerate polygon, but much of the content was already published.

Correctness and reproducing a known law do not establish new value.
