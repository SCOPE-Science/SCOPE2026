# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Conditioning on a fixed induced H on k vertices freezes the k internal edges but leaves every exterior edge independent Bernoulli(p). For an internal vertex parity character, conditional mean is (-1)^degree_H(v) q^(n-k), whose sum equals (k-2o(H))q^(n-k); exterior vertices retain unconditional q^(n-1). Summing all k-subsets yields the stated covariance and correct automorphism factor in E[T_H]. For P5, o(H)=2 and q^(n-5)(1-5q^4) with odd exponent gives exactly three simple sign changes for even n>=6. Rational samples p=1/10,2/5,3/5,9/10 independently reproduced the alternating signs; no finite test is the universal proof.
