# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For the labelled GP(10,2) dodecahedron, every binary colouring must frustrate at least six edges because each of 12 pentagons has a monochromatic edge and each edge borders two faces. Recolouring 3-sites by the lesser common-colour neighbor class raises bond penalty by at most one per site, yielding b+k>=6. The cyclic 81-state transfer trace exactly enumerates sectors b+k=6 and proper-colouring coefficients; the inspected independent DFS enumerator is structurally distinct. The sector vector (250,3000,13800,30400,32760,15840,2720) sums to 98770 and independently computed mean/variance are 34836/9877 and 119729580/97555129. The fixed finite energy gap gives compact-uniform x crossover P(exp(-x)), so mathematics is valid.

## originality

FAIL

A published complete dodecahedral three-state Potts field diagram contains a full exact (k,p_k,N_k) table. For k=0,...,6 its N_k values are precisely every coefficient of submitted P(z), with p_k=6-k. Under h_old/J=-1 the earlier Hamiltonian b-h_old k is exactly b+k, hence the earlier table proves the entire new critical polynomial, 98770 degeneracy and both phase-side ground counts. The submitted low-temperature scaling is the elementary finite-sum consequence of this fully known table, so the claimed new quantitative result is covered, not just generically computable.

## value

FAIL

The natural exact dodecahedral polynomial is worthwhile in isolation, but this manuscript recomputes an already published exact 21-sector table and repackages its already-known seven numbers. The low-temperature scaling and derivative are routine finite-model consequences of those known coefficients; no independent mathematical gap is filled.
