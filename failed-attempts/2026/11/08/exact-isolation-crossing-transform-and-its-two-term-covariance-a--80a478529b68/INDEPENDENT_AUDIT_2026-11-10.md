# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Fixing a distinguished isolated chord leaves two independent noncrossing arcs of lengths a and b=n−1−a; the crossing count is the sum of crossings in independent arc matchings. Counting all oriented chords twice yields E[J_n z^{X_n}]=n/(2n−1)!! sum_a Q_a(z)Q_b(z). The expectation E X_n=n(n−1)/6 and conditional E[X|isolation]=(a(a−1)+b(b−1))/6 give exact negative covariance −n/3 sum_a p_{n,a}(n−1+ab), zero at n=1. Ratio tail estimates justify −n/3−1/6+O(n−1). Independent exhaustive pairings through five chords reproduced every polynomial coefficient and small exact covariances. No infinite inference from enumeration.
