# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For a homogeneous simple random walk on C_n, the λ-discounted hitting function on a source-free gap of ℓ edges uniquely solves h_i=(λ/2)(h_{i-1}+h_{i+1}), with both endpoint values one and contraction uniqueness for 0<λ<1. This gives h_i=cosh(θ(i-ℓ/2))/cosh(θℓ/2), cosh θ=1/λ. The least gap value φℓ occurs at the middle vertices, strictly decreases from one to zero and yields the stated even/odd floor threshold. A labelled k-source set is feasible iff every cyclic positive gap ≤L; mark one of k sources to count n/k times the number of bounded compositions. The optimum is ceil(n/min(L,n)), including n=3, singleton source, equality at τ and τ=1. Independent rational tridiagonal equations gave φ1=1, φ2=1/2, φ3=1/3, φ4=1/7 at λ=1/2 and bounded-composition coefficients for n up to ten; finite cases support the symbolic proof.
