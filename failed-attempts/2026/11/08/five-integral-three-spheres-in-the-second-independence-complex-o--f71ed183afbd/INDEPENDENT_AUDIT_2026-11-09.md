# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

A face iff each selected pair is disjoint from at most one other selected pair; exhaustive independent implementation checked 32767 nonempty masks and found 746 faces with f=(15,105,260,255,96,15). The pivot-toggle matching independently yields 370 matched pairs, six critical masks [1,5664,10784,19488,28736,29184], 2580 Hasse incidences and acyclic orientation visiting all 746 faces. Forman Morse theory gives CW model with one 0-cell and five 3-cells attached to a point, hence a wedge of five 3-spheres over Z. This verifies the complete finite case, not by extrapolating ranks.

## originality

FAIL

Published all-n classification for Ind_2(KG(n,2)) gives wedge^{binom(n-1,4)} S^3 for every n>=6; substitution n=6 yields five spheres. The 370-pair matching is an alternative finite certificate for that known result, not a new theorem.

## value

FAIL

The graph is natural and the calculation would have value in isolation, but its exact homotopy type and even its integral S6 module are already given by stronger all-parameter published findings. This package makes no substantively new natural invariant or boundary claim.

The dated certificate retains the supplied scientific assessment, sources and limitations.
