# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For a set of at least five vertices, a part of size at least three gives the pattern A B A C A if there are two outsiders (B and C may coincide); if all counts are at most two, the possible patterns on any chosen five are 2+2+1, 2+1+1+1, and 1+1+1+1+1, all schedulable without adjacent repetitions. Conversely the largest part can supply at most three path vertices. The cover by the complete 3-skeleton and the contractible joins follows. The attaching intersections have dimension at most three, not necessarily exactly three, but are 2-connected by cellular approximation. If q=1,...,4, the number of (q-1)-simplices is C(n_i,q)+(N-n_i)C(n_i,q-1); alternating summation gives the stated rank also for n_i <= 3. Their 3-chain supports are disjoint because each tetrahedron has at least three vertices in its designated part. Each cycle kernel is saturated in its coordinate summand, so the relative connecting map injects primitively into the 3-cycle lattice of the complete skeleton. The relative sequence gives free H3 of the stated rank, no H4, and no other reduced homology. No lower homotopy group can be created by the higher attachments, and Hurewicz and homological Whitehead give the wedge, including the zero-rank case. The advertised sample ranks are 5, 70 and 306. The finite-field evidence was inspected as supplemental evidence; it is not needed to prove the integral result.
