# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

From the complete graph condition, a face uses fewer than t graph parts. The H-fixed affine point has equal coordinates on every H-vertex orbit, hence is the orbit-union complex; an H-vertex orbit meets exactly every graph part in one H-part orbit. All vertex orbits above that part orbit are interchangeable, and collapsing them to one representative is a simplicial map contiguous to the identity because the total occupied graph parts remain unchanged. The resulting scalar threshold complex has weights w_j=number of parts in each part orbit, faces of total weight ≤T=t−1. If its smallest weight exceeds T it has empty realization. Otherwise its minimum-weight-vertex closed star A is contractible. Any face S outside A has weight T−w_min < w(S)≤T, and every proper subface has weight at most T−w_min, so it lies in A. The quotient by the star is a wedge of a sphere S^{|S|−1} for each such S; the quotient map is a homotopy equivalence. A simplex case t>m, dimension-zero wedge, and empty fixed locus are handled. The example weights (1,1,1,2,2), t=5 give two S² and one S¹, and the ambient trivial-action weights (1^7) give C(6,4)=15 spheres S³. The identical proof is corroborated by the independently inspected broader published affine retraction theorem. An independently authored finite diagnostic directly constructs H-orbit faces and mod-2 reduced homology for 305 cases (21,009 such faces) and the seven-part example, which corroborate but do not prove the unrestricted statement.
