# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The Heisenberg normal form D(v)=Av+f(v)z, D(z)=lambda z is equivalent to omega(Au,v)+omega(u,Av)=lambda omega(u,v), A^2=A and fA+lambda f=f. When lambda=1 image and kernel of A form an ordered transverse Lagrangian pair, giving exactly L_n,q maps of rank n+1. When lambda=0 in odd characteristic only A=f=0; in characteristic two A projects orthogonally onto a nondegenerate 2r-subspace, yielding q^(2r)S_n/(S_r S_(n-r)) maps of rank 2r. The branch rarity follows comparing exponents n^2+n versus n^2+2n, and discrete Gaussian conditional limits from r=n/2+j or (n+1)/2+j and bounded Euler product factors; these arguments are mathematically valid. Independent GF2 exhaustive n=2 symplectic matrices gave rank counts {0:1,2:80,3:480,4:16}, consistent with the theorem. Finite tests do not prove asymptotics.
