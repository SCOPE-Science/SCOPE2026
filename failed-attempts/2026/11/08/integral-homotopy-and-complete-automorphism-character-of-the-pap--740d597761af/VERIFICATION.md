# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The 18-vertex cubic Pappus LCF graph is reconstructed with exactly 27 edges. Independent check of all 262144 vertex subsets yields 204492 augmented induced-forest faces and acyclic greedy matching with 102223 pairs on the complete Hasse graph of 1704456 covers. The critical faces consist of 43 ten-cells and 3 eleven-cells; exact signed 3×43 Morse boundary has a unimodular minor diag(-1,1,1) on columns 6,9,11. Cell cancellation gives ∨40 S^10 integrally. Independent 216-automorphism enumeration and signed fixed-subset character yields frequencies χ=40 once, 4 on 32, 1 on 48, 0 on 135, norm 10 and invariant rank 1; two 3^6 conjugacy classes have traces 1 and 4. This proves a fixed-graph classification, not a general family theorem.
