## correctness

PASS

For M=uv^T with M_ab nonzero, rank-one minors give M_ij=M_ib M_aj/M_ab. Zero diagonal annihilates opposing coordinates on active supports and leaves u_iv_i=0 independently on each missing index. Thus the completed local ring is C[[m-1-c smooth variables, x_i,y_i]]/(x_iy_i), c=number of unused indices. Normalization, conductor product, 2^c branches/multiplicity and singular-support count follow. The original source explicitly assumes the determinantal scheme reduced (Jia–Li). Finite Jacobian calculations are auxiliary.

These are mathematical implications of the earlier theorem plus an elementary rank-one orientation reduction, not an independent complete local classification.
