An ordinary cycle is unshatterable, noncrossing spanning trees are shattered via positive-edge component partitions, and crossed spanning-tree edges prevent one-negative realization. All-positive diameters force one block, while any selection of positive diameters is represented by one nonsingleton block, giving inclusion maximality. n=2,3 boundary and even n>=4 are respected. Independent unrestricted proof read, and finite direct trace counts checked to n=5 (55 top faces).
