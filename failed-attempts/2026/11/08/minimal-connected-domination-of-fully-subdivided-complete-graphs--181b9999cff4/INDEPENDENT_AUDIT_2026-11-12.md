# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For n>=3, identifying the selected old vertices proves at most one can be omitted: otherwise an isolated selected subdivision vertex is needed to dominate their connecting subdivision. All-old selections have a dispensable leaf; exactly one omitted vertex v leaves a spanning tree on n-1 old vertices plus precisely one pendant subdivision incident to v. This parametrization is unique and minimal. Cayley yields n*(n-1)^(n-3)*(n-1)=n*(n-1)^(n-2), all size 2n-2; n=3 boundary explicitly checked. Independent exact subset enumeration on S(K_3), S(K_4), S(K_5) returned 6,36,320 minimal sets, all at sizes 4,6,8, respectively. This is support, not infinite proof.
