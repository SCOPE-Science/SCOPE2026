# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Trace on each individual Jordan diagonal block gives e a²=0, including when the same eigenvalue appears in more than one block. Finite-field separability preserves lengths upon splitting a primary block. The derivation t²d/dt descends to every allowed cyclic summand and its commutator with multiplication by t is multiplication by t². Thus each nonempty fiber is a translate of the commuting algebra with the stated dimension (sum of pairwise minima, or squared column lengths); empty and nilpotent cases are included. The fiber weight cancels the q-power of the centralizer-unit order by orbit–stabilizer. Summing independent multiplicities by Euler's identity, then using polynomial unique factorization for sizes divisible by p, gives the normalized product, with constant coefficient 1. At n=p the nonnilpotent types are single length-p blocks with q−1 choices of eigenvalue and weight q/(q−1), giving qG_p(q).
