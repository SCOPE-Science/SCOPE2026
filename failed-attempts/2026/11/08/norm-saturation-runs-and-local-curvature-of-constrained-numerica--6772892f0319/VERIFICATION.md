# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The norm-attaining subspace of any finite nilpotent weighted shift is the span of input coordinates with weight modulus M; compression of T/M is a block sum of zero and Jordan blocks whose largest numerical range disk radius is cos(pi/(L+1)). Equality in norm bound therefore gives precisely the claimed plateau. At unique maximum, the maximizing ray at q=0 is isolated, its transverse Hessian has strict positive gap, and an independent symbolic inverse of the 2×2 neighbor quadratic form yields exactly (M²-l²)(M²-r²)/(2M(2M²-l²-r²)); far coordinates enter at higher order. Complex phase gauges and end-zero conventions are valid. The formula matches the independent historical 2008 calculation, supporting C while negating novelty.

## originality

FAIL

The submitted final finding treats the exact two-neighbor q² coefficient as a new local curvature law. Chien–Nakazato (2008) Theorem 4.1 states the identical formula; their periodic-shift result and the 2007 reduction of periodic q-radii to cyclic matrices cover the finite nilpotent case upon inserting a terminal zero weight and padding the period. The maximal-run plateau part was not found as a general published formula (equal-weight Jordan special case is known) and may retain standalone originality, but the uncorrected two-part final claim prominently includes a covered central formula; it is not acceptable as wholly original without a new, fully checked final claim. No repair is supplied here.
