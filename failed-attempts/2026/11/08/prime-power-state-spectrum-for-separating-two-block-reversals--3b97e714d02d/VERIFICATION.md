All pairs of powers commute iff the two subgroups generated by those powers centralize each other. H_t(m) is normal and is trivial exactly when every cycle length at most m divides t. For m≥5 any nontrivial normal subgroup contains A_m: if its intersection with A_m is trivial it centralizes A_m, and a centralizer element must preserve every support of a three-cycle and so fix every point.
