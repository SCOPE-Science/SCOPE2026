Explicitly, Y_31=Y_32=Y_21=0 and Y_33=−Y_11, giving 2t(n−t) independent scalar conditions in each radical coordinate. This also checks characteristic two and requires no coefficient field. The denominator of the probability tends to the zero-stratum size in relative terms.
