# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Writing a nonzero rank-one map as a⊗B(b,−), the left side of the weight-zero identity vanishes and the right side is the wedge of B(b,−) with B(b,[a,−]), times a. Invariance and nondegeneracy make vanishing equivalent to [a,b] in kb. The centralizer of nonscalar b in M2 is span(I,b), whose trace-zero part is kb in odd characteristic; therefore the commuting family is exactly the self-adjoint diagonal. If [a,b]=lambda b with lambda nonzero then invariance gives lambda B(b,b)=0. For such nilpotent b, conjugate its line to kE using a basis adapted to its common image and kernel; its normalizer is the upper-triangular trace-zero plane b-perp. Now R²=B(a,b)R, so precisely that nilpotent incidence family is square-zero. Each line of k² supplies a unique projective nilpotent line (image and kernel that line), giving q+1; the diagonal has q²+q+1 projective points and the nilpotent incidence has (q+1)². Each projective tensor gives q−1 nonzero maps, and their overlap is the conic. Off the conic each diagonal tensor line has a unique idempotent. These counts establish all the formulas, including in characteristic three. Over the algebraic closure, the diagonal and projectivization of the normalizer bundle over the conic are closed irreducible surfaces, neither containing the other; their affine cones meet in the cone over that conic. This is a reduced-variety argument, not a scheme-theoretic assertion.
