# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For uniform invertible n-by-n matrices over F_2, automorphisms reduce to simultaneous coordinate permutations: invertibility makes supports of images of distinct natural basis vectors disjoint, and F_2 has no nontrivial units. The transposition rank-one square-zero centralizer has exactly 2^(2n-3)|GL_(n-2)(2)| elements. Counting fixed matrix-position orbits for other permutations, bounding transposition intersections by the products, and applying Bonferroni supports the explicit n>=32 bound |p_n-t_n|<=250 n^3 2^(-3n). Independently checked GL_2=6, GL_3=168, GL_4=20160, their transposition-fixed counts 2,8,192, and GL_5 centralizer 21504. These finite checks corroborate, not prove, the n>=32 bound. Normalization is uniform on labeled invertible matrices, not classes.

The published Resultary full theorem sharp rare-symmetry law for perfect binary evolution algebras treats the IDENTICAL F_2, uniformly invertible labeled structure-matrix model; it gives the identical exact transposition probability, asymptotic n(n-1)2^(-2n), conditional unique C2 symmetry and first Burnside correction. The claim as a whole is materially covered by a previous full primary theorem. No within-day publication ordering is inferred, as both dated November 8.
