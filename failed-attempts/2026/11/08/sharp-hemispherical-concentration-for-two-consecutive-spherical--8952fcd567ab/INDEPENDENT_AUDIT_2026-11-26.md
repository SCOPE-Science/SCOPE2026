The normalized Legendre Lagrange identity gives the signed overlap as the boundary Wronskian at 0 divided by 2(ℓ+1); the t=1 term vanishes (m=0 is smooth; for m≥1 the factors have endpoint order m/2). For j+m=2r, j-m=2s the normalized value at zero has modulus √((2j+1)B_r B_s/2). For j+m=2r-1, j-m=2s-1 the normalized derivative has modulus √((2j+1)(2r)(2s)B_r B_s/2). Adjacent degrees have matching signs in the value–derivative product in the even case and opposite signs in the reversed product in the odd case, giving positive overlap in both.
