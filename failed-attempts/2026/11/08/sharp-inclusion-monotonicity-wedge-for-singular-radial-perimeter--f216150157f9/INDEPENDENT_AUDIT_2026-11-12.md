# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For Q(a,b) centered rectangles and -1<p<0, direct boundary integration gives partial_a E_p=4a^p G_p(b/a), partial_b E_p=4b^p G_p(a/b). The derivative G_p'(r)=p(1+r)(1+r²)^{p/2-1}<0, G_p(1)>0, and G_p(infinity)<0 prove a unique R_p>1. On the convex aspect-ratio cone 1/R_p<=a/b<=R_p, both gradient coordinates are nonnegative and at least one is positive, so strict inclusion monotonicity follows by integrating along a comparable segment. Outside the cone one partial is strictly negative and yields arbitrarily close nested counterexamples. Implicit derivative and dominated convergence establish the endpoint laws; p=-1 and p<−1 scale boundaries are treated separately. The proof is valid for common centered/oriented rectangles, not arbitrary convex sets.
