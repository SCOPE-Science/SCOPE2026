# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

On the incidence filtration, A₁ acts on the degree-j quotient by (4−j)(n−4)−j(5−j): for a fixed j-set the number of adjacent four-sets containing it is (4−j)(n−4) if it is already contained, 5−j if one element is missing, and zero otherwise. The adjacency-square counts give A₁²=4(n−4)I+(n−2)A₁+4A₂. These identities and J=0 off the constants recover all four eigenvalues. For 0<p≤1, t=(3^p−2^p)/(3^p−1) belongs to (1/3,1/2]; concavity gives the upper bound, and 2·3^p−3·2^p+1 starts at zero with strictly positive derivative. With L_j=θ_j+tν_j the differences L₁−L₃, L₂−L₃ and L₄−L₃ factor respectively as (n−3)(4+3(n−8)t)/2, (n−4)(2+(n−11)t)/2 and (n−6)(3t−1); all are strictly positive even at n=8. Thus E₃ is the sole maximal nonconstant mode on the asserted range. Its eigenvalue is strictly increasing for all p≥0, by 2 log 3>3 log 2, so from −1 at zero and n−7 at one it crosses exactly once and also directly excludes every larger p. Centered Gram rank gives the two dimension formulas with the stated nullity. At n=8 the equation is 3^p−2=0; Taylor expansion at p=c/n gives −1+c log(9/8)+O_c(1/n), bracketing the rescaled root. The finite numerical verifier was inspected, not run or needed for these conclusions.
