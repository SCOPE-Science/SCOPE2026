# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

A two-sided Brownian motion with an independent stationary Poisson clock gives a stationary solution by summing the Brownian increments on past Poisson intervals, with multipliers a^j. The backward gaps at a fixed time are independent exponentials; mixing the Gaussian increments over their gaps makes the scaled terms independent centered Laplace variables, and the squared scales sum. Synchronous coupling multiplies any initial difference by a^(N_t), so the law is unique. Its positive even Fourier product has integrable polynomial weights of every order (retain sufficiently many factors), which proves smoothness, symmetry and p(0)>0. The rescaling gain term carries the Jacobian 1/rho; differentiating the stationary equation 2n times gives the multiplier lambda²(1-rho^(-(2n+1))) for either sign of a. Its product has sign (-1)^n and exponent -n². The remaining odd-factor products converge to a positive limit, so log derivative magnitudes grow quadratically in n and outgrow every finite Gevrey bound and the Taylor factorial. The signed-contraction exponential series is normal on the open positive complex half-plane for the positive-side density, and reflection proves the negative-side result. At zero Euler's q-binomial identity at q=rho² and argument rho gives exactly the stated height. No assertion needs an extension to zero resetting or the unit-modulus endpoints.
