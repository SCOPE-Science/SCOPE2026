# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For normalized planar area on a<=|x|<=1, covariance is (1+a^2)I/4 and K_a(x,y)=1+4x·y/(1+a²). Its angular |kernel| average and uniform squared radius give the stated divided-difference norm [Φ(B)-Φ(A)]/(B-A); radial strictness puts all norming rows on the outer circle. The chord/derivative proof establishes strict hole monotonicity, and cap integration gives exact endpoints, thin-annulus and small-hole expansions. A separate numerical radial integral verified sample values. All quantifiers are for real affine L² projection and 0<=a<1; the a=1 case is a limit, not normalized area.
