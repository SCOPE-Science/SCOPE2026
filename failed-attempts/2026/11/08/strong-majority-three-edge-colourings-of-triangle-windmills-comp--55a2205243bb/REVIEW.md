# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For every rim its two adjacent spokes must differ. A spoke has 2m adjacent edges, and a rim colour is admissible precisely if absent from at least one spoke pair. Inclusion-exclusion by the image size of missing-spoke colours produces the six-exponential formula. Direct-definition and grouped enumeration independently agree for m=1,2,3, where counts are 6,108,2472. The proof covers all integers m≥2; finite checks alone do not.
