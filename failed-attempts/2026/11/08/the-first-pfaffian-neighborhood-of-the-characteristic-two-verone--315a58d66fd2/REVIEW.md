# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The tangency incidence tests Q(x)=0 and Bx=0. The maximal-Pfaffian vector w satisfies Bw=0 by the paired-matching identity and generates the radical on the rank-2m open set. The exceptional common zero locus of its coordinates has codimension three (the alternating rank strata), so there is no common divisor of the w_i. The polynomial Q(w) is primitive and linear in the diagonal variables over the mixed-coefficient fraction field, hence irreducible; the rank-deficient complement does not add a divisor. Independently reconstructing the first-order variation over k[ε]/(ε²), let Q change by εH and w by εz. The coefficient of ε in F is H(w)+B(w,z)=H(w), identically, even on the rank-deficient locus. Every coefficient direction H gives one of the products w_iw_j. F itself belongs to their ideal; thus the equality is a universal ideal equality before projectivization, not just about the set of singular points. On the corank-three stratum, splitting off a nondegenerate (2m−2)-block gives an alternating 3-by-3 Schur complement with three independent entries. Under that invertible congruence the Pfaffian generators are the complementary-block entries times the unit Pfaffian of the large block. The rank locus is smooth there and the square ideal gives transverse basis 1,u,v,s. Lower-rank points are included in the ideal equality but not in the transverse length claim. The universal proof, not the finite recorded 3,5,7 checks, supplies this conclusion.

## originality

FAIL

The mathematical identity is correct. The exact coefficient polynomial and its Pfaffian radical were already published for the same arbitrary odd dimension, and the asserted Jacobian square and generic length are mechanical consequences. Dolgachev–Duncan §§2.1–2.2 place this in precisely the half-discriminant setting; their §3.3 also uses the relevant first-order cancellation for the ordinary one-singular-point locus. This is a failure of distinct mathematical content by implication, not evidence that they explicitly printed the ideal square or transverse length, and not an inference from inaccessible text.

## value

FAIL

The scheme rather than the support is a natural question. Here the asserted refinement reorganizes the coefficient products already present in the classical half-discriminant using immediate first-order cancellation; the length is just that of the squared ideal of a smooth codimension-three center on its standard chart. The record establishes no additional geometric phenomenon beyond those routine consequences. The all-dimension proof is a useful exposition, not an independent research result under this criterion.
