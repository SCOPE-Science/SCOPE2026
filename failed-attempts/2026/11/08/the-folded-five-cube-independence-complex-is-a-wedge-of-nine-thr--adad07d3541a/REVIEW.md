# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The 16-vertex Cayley graph with difference set {1,2,4,8,15} has 40 edges and strongly regular parameters (16,5,0,2). Independent full 2^16 mask enumeration yielded 392 nonempty independent faces, f-vector (16,80,160,120,16); pivot sequence (6,13,7,8,14,11,4,0,3,10,5,15,12,1,2,9) yielded 191 disjoint pairs with critical cells one 0-simplex and nine 3-simplices. All 1200 oriented cover edges were topologically sorted; discrete Morse gives ∨9S3 over integers. Independently matched published equivalent graph and wedge theorem; this checks correctness, not originality.

## originality

FAIL

The full integral wedge of nine S3 was published earlier for exactly this five-regular folded five-cube, together with the stronger full group rational character; the present final claim solely repeats its homotopy and ordinary homology. Cayley models are linearly isomorphic: five weight-4 generators in even-parity F2^5 have the single relation summing to zero, as do 1,2,4,8,15 in F2^4.

## value

FAIL

A natural graph invariant, but exactly known for the identical graph and strictly extended by the previously published 4⊕5 full-symmetry character; reproducing its non-equivariant wedge theorem is known-result recomputation, not a fresh motivated gap.
