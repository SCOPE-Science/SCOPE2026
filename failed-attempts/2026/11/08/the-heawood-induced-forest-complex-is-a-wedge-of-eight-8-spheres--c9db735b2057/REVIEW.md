# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The frozen RESULT.md defines the vertex-induced forest complex, not an edge forest or matching complex. Independent mask enumeration by induced acyclicity gives 12,792 faces including empty, f-vector (14,91,364,1001,2002,2975,3208,2254,812,70). A fresh stagewise pair generation yields 6,391 nonempty-face pairs, precisely one critical vertex and eight critical 8-faces. The complete 81,634 directed Hasse incidences are acyclic by full topological traversal of all 12,791 nonempty faces. Forman collapses to a CW complex with only a 0-cell and eight 8-cells, so the result is integrally a wedge of eight S^8 rather than merely mod-two Betti data.
