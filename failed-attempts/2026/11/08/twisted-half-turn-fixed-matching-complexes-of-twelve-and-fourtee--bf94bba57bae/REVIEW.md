# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The fixed-point PL model follows exactly: the involution is free on the prism graph vertices and graph edges, each edge orbit is admissible and projects to a rim or opposite chord of M2k; a barycentric fixed point has constant weights along edge orbits, and two orbit supports are disjoint iff their quotient edges are disjoint. This establishes the PL correspondence for every k≥3, not merely sampled cases. In an independently written Python enumerator for k=6,7, all matchings and deterministic toggle acyclic matchings were rebuilt from the edge definitions. For M12: 1,102 augmented faces with f=(18,117,336,420,192,18), 546 pairs, ten unmatched 3-simplices, 4,008 Hasse incidences and complete topological-sort acyclicity. For M14: 3,546 augmented faces with f=(21,168,644,1225,1085,371,31), 1,765 pairs, sixteen unmatched 4-simplices, 15,057 Hasse incidences and complete acyclicity. The empty-cell pair supplies a basepoint in the reduced Morse model. With a single vertex and only critical 3- or 4-cells, all attaching maps are trivial and the stated integral wedges follow. Quotient support-disjointness was independently checked on all 153 and 210 pairs of orbit edges in the two finite cases. Finite verification supports exactly the two claimed widths; the all-width PL part is proved structurally.
