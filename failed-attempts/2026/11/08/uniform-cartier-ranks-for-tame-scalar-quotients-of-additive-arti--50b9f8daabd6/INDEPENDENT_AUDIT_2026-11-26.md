Summing, then subtracting from the genus, verifies all formulas including h=2. BBTZ Proposition 2.1 with r=p and epsilon=2 gives the stated genus, and Theorem 1.3(ii), equation (9), supplies the arithmetic application hypotheses for n≥2. The archived checker was inspected but not executed; it is finite and specializes the coefficients. It is not necessary for the proof.
