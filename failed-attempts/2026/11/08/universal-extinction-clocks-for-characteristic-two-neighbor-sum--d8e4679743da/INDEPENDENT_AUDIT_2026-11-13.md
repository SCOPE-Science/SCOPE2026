The unique even-degree root implies an even number of children at every vertex (nonroots have odd total degree, hence even child counts). Defining childward U and rootward D yields DU=0 over any characteristic-two field; with height h, U^(h+1)=D^(h+1)=0. Therefore A^k=sum_{a+b=k} U^a D^b, A^(2h+1)=0, A^(2h) has rank one with image deepest-level indicator, and A^(2h-1) has rank two for h>=1 with disjoint outputs in levels h,h-1. Applying to a depth-ell impulse proves its last nonzero state at time h+ell and death at h+ell+1. Rank-nullity over F_q gives counts (q-1)q^(N-1), (q-1)q^(N-2), q^(N-2); isolated root h=0 is correctly separate. Independent direct GF(2) matrix-power checks on 21 eligible nonisomorphic trees through order nine corroborated the ranks but are not a universal proof.
