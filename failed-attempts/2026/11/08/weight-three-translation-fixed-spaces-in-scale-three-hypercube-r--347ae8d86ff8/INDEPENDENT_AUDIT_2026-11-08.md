# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For a free weight-three translation, fixed simplex barycenters are indexed by pairs x,x+v; all cross-distances of two orbits equal q+h or q+3-h, giving compatibility only for same lower cube vertex with distinct color or adjacent cube vertices with distinct color. Every cube vertex supports a tetrahedron, and each cube edge supports the join (S0)^*4≅S3; distinct edge blocks meet only in their shared tetrahedron. Contracting contractible vertex tetrahedra and selecting paths across edge spheres reduces to the cube graph with one S3 per edge. For n≥4, β1=m2^(m-1)-2^m+1, β3=m2^(m-1); n=3 is a tetrahedron. A separate independent original-orbit GF(2) boundary calculation reproduced (β1,β3)=(0,1),(1,4),(5,12) for n=4,5,6 and contractibility at n=3. This finite corroboration is not the all-n proof.
