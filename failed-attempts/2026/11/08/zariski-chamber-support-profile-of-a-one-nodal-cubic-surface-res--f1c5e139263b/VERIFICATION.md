# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The exact domain is the blowup over C at six distinct points with just one collinear triple, and no six-point conic. Adjunction and Cauchy bounds enumerate 21 (-1) curves and the unique (-2) curve R=H-E1-E2-E3. Each negative support without R is an independent vertex set of the (-1)-intersection graph; with R, the Schur complement is -2 plus the count of selected curves meeting R, allowing at most one. The reconstructed star/triangle and exhaustive recurrence give coefficients (1,22,156,490,725,447,57), total 1898. A separate comparison of the full 22-vertex weighted intersection graphs against the six-on-conic A1 cubic model found a graph isomorphism preserving self-intersection and all 81 incidence edges. This finite computation supplements, rather than replaces, the curve inventory and chamber-support bijection.

## originality

FAIL

A complete published A1 degree-three cubic-resolution result establishes the identical support polynomial and 1898 total on a birationally different configuration with the same weighted negative-curve intersection graph; the two proofs enumerate the same chamber invariant. An isomorphism of these graphs makes the full support polynomial identical by the negative-definite support correspondence. This is substantive prior coverage; identical publication day does not establish which author or record came first.

## value

FAIL

Although nodal cubic chamber spectra are natural exact invariants, this is already the same enumerated A1 weak-degree-three del Pezzo invariant in a published complete result. A second encoding of that invariant with no new surviving generalization or structural distinction is recomputation of a covered fact, not independent mathematical value.
