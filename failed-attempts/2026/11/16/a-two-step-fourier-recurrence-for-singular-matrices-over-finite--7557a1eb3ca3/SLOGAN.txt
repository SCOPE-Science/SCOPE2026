A two-step Smith-type recursion evaluates every additive Fourier coefficient of the determinant-zero set in \(M_2(R)\) for any finite commutative chain ring, independent of the ring's characteristic. At radical length three it gives exactly four negative Smith classes, two zero classes, and the sharp endpoint Fourier projection norm \(2q^3-2q+1-2q^{-2}+2q^{-3}-2q^{-5}+2q^{-6}\).
