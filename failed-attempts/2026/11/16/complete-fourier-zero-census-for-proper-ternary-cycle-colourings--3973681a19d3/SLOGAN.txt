For proper three-colourings of every cycle \(C_n\), the full additive Fourier transform has extra zeros beyond global-colour-translation symmetry exactly when \(n\equiv1\pmod3\); in that case their exact number is \(n!/(a!^2(a+1)!)\), where \(n=3a+1\). The resulting convolution rank is exactly \(3^{n-1}-n!/(a!^2(a+1)!)\) in that residue class and \(3^{n-1}\) otherwise.
