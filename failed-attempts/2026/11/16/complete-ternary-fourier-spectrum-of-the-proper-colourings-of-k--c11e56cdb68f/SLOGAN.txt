Proper three-colourings of \(K_{2,n}\) have a complete signed additive Fourier spectrum with only two explicit multiplier types per leaf-zero count. A new family of balanced Fourier zeros occurs exactly for even \(n\), with \((n+4)2^{n-1}\) extra zeros and an exact surviving spectral gap of \(9\), versus \(3\) for odd \(n\).
