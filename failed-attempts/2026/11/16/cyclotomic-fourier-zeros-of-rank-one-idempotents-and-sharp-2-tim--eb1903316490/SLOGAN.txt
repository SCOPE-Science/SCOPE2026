For the additive Fourier transform of rank-one idempotents in \(M_n(\mathbb F_q)\), a coefficient vanishes exactly when eigenlines are equidistributed across the \(p\) absolute-trace classes of their eigenvalues. In dimension two, the complete zero counts and the sharp Fourier-projection norm have explicit formulas for every prime-power field, with a discontinuous characteristic-two zero-density law.
