The coordinate Walsh characters sampled on spanning trees of an \(n\)-rim wheel have a fully explicit \(2n\)-point Gram spectrum, exactly one forced null mode, and a sharp least positive eigenvalue \(\frac{8H_n}{\sqrt5}\frac{1-\cos(\pi/n)}{3-2\cos(\pi/n)}\), with \(H_n=(1+\rho^n)/(1-\rho^n)\) and \(\rho=(3-\sqrt5)/2\). Their optimal Gram condition ratio on the nonnull subspace grows as \(\frac{(\sqrt5-2)^2}{2\sqrt5\pi^2}n^3\).
