For two equal, nonoverlapping spherical caps with perpendicular centers, the entire pure degree-two spherical-harmonic concentration spectrum has four explicit polynomial branches. The least-observable space jumps from dimension one to dimension three to dimension two at the exact threshold \(c=\sqrt5/3\), while the unique most-concentrated mode remains \(x^2-z^2\); a second spectral crossing occurs at \(c=\sqrt{11/15}\).
