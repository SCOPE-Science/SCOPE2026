The sharp endpoint Fourier-projection norms onto the finite-field quadratic Veronese frequencies have exact rank-sum formulas for every odd \(q\) and dimension \(n\), with a mod-four field distinction. Their fixed-field growth is \(\frac12C_qq^{n/2}\), and a second term of order \(q^{-n/2}\) has explicit parity-dependent coefficients given by rapidly convergent series.
