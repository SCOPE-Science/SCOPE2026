The \(111\)-avoiding binary cubes have an exact four-periodic Walsh gap: the minimum nonzero multiplier is \(1\), \(2\), or \(4\) according to dimension modulo four, and all multipliers are divisible by \(4\) when the dimension is \(2\pmod4\). This gives exact Hilbert deconvolution norms in every dimension and isolates the nontrivial multivariate rigidity beyond the known tribonacci cardinality recurrence.
