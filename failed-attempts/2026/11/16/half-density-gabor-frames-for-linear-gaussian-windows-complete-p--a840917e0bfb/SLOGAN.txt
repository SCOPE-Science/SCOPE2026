At Gabor lattice product \(ab=1/2\), the degree-one polynomial–Gaussian window \((t-c)e^{-\beta t^2}\) is a frame for every rectangular lattice and every \(\beta>0\) exactly when \(c\ne0\). In the canonical normalization its sharp lower frame bound opens quadratically but anisotropically, with different exact coefficients for real and imaginary Gaussian perturbations.
