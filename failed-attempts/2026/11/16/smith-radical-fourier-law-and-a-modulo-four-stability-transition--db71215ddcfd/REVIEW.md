The mathematical generality is already covered as a direct corollary of Turaev 1998 Lemma 1, which gives the Gauss-sum magnitude/vanishing criterion from the polar radical for arbitrary finite quadratic forms. Williams 2014 Corollary 4.4 supplies exactly the three integer Smith forms for cycle adjacency by n mod 4. Combining these previously proved statements with the standard Fourier projection convolution kernel and translation averaging immediately gives the claimed sharp norm and dyadic specialization: no independent nontrivial theorem remains after subtracting known coverage.
