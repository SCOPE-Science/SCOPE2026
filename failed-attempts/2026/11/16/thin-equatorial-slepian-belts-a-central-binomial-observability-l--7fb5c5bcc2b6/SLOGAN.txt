For spherical harmonics of every degree up to fixed \(L\), the smallest energy fraction observable on a shrinking equatorial belt of area fraction \(a\) is asymptotic to \(\bigl(2^L/\binom{2L}{L}\bigr)^2a^{2L+1}\). The uniquely least-observable mode is eventually zonal, and its scaled belt zeros approach the Gauss–Legendre nodes.
