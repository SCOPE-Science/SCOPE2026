In every type-\(C_n\) root poset, restricting a full ideal to its short-root membership profile is exactly two-to-one; this forces zero-free binary Fourier convolution at every power-of-two rank. Exact spectra through rank five exhibit genuine nonmonotonicity: ranks three and five lose Fourier modes, whereas rank four recovers all \(4096\) modes, and an explicit rank-six cancellation restores singularity.
