The flag complex of the \(25\)-vertex Paley graph is homotopy equivalent to a wedge of \(64\) two-spheres, and the complete action of its \(600\)-element automorphism group on rational second homology has an explicit sixteen-class character. The previously known Betti number is strengthened to an integral homotopy statement, while translation symmetry preserves four rational classes and full symmetry preserves none.
