For the standard Euclidean graphical zonotope of \(K_{2,q}\), every Steiner intrinsic volume has an explicit binomial–radical formula obtained from a sharp two-hub forest classification. Its weighted intrinsic-volume index is within \(O_t(q^{-1/2})\) total variation of \(2+\operatorname{Bin}(q-1,2t/(1+2t))\), and its Wills-type sum has an exact two-term asymptotic expansion.
