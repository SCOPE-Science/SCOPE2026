Nearly maximum-perimeter spherical triangles have a nontrivial conditional geometry: their rescaled signed displacement from the first two vertices' great circle is uniformly distributed within an angle-dependent band, and their angle profile is a three-particle circular Dyson law of exponent \(1/2\). The marked law converges in total variation and yields the exact rare-event constant \(3\pi\Gamma(3/4)/\Gamma(1/4)^3\) in the \(\sqrt\varepsilon\) perimeter tail.
