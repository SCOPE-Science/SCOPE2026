For every finite simple graph \(G\), the geometric layer-swap fixed space of the edge-forest complex of its diagonal-augmented bipartite double cover is naturally PL-homeomorphic to the forest complex of the cone graph \(G^\star\). This transfers the known complete-graph matroid sphere wedges to genuine fixed spaces, giving \(1,6,51,560\) spheres in dimensions \(1,2,3,4\) for the first four nontrivial balanced complete-bipartite cases.
