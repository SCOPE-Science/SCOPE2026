For every finite nonempty triangle-free graph \(H\) and \(s\ge2\), the three-clique-free complex of \(K_s\vee H\) is exactly a wedge of \(\binom{s}{2}\) circles and \(s\) unreduced suspensions of \(\operatorname{Ind}(H)\), up to homotopy. Thus all the topology of the independence complex transfers through a clique join; for instance \(K_3\vee C_6\) yields \(3\) circles and \(6\) two-spheres.
