Conditioned on a random ideal hyperbolic quadrilateral having cross ratio \(Q<\varepsilon\), its two opposing boundary gaps collapse on complementary random logarithmic scales: the split exponent, normalized cross ratio, and macroscopic gap become asymptotically independent, with the macroscopic gap density \(\sin^2(y/2)/\pi\). The joint marked law converges in total variation; the underlying scalar cross-ratio density was already known.
