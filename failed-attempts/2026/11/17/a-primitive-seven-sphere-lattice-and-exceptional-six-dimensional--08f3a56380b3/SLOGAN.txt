The seven shortest-support tetrahedral spheres in the Fano matroid independence complex span a **primitive**, symmetry-stable integral homology lattice of rank \(7\), leaving a torsion-free quotient of rank \(6\). Under the full \(168\)-element Fano collineation group, the rational \(13\)-dimensional homology splits into a geometric irreducible \(7\)-module and the exceptional rationally irreducible \(3\oplus\overline3\) module.
