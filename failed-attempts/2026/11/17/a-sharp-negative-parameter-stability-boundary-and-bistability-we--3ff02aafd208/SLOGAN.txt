For the quadratic ETM-3D flow on its negative-parameter branch, the origin loses global exponential attraction at the exact algebraic fold \(\rho_f=-1.308660544\ldots\), but remains a local sink until the transcritical value \(\rho_t=(5-\sqrt{105})/4\). Between these two thresholds there are exactly two distinct attracting equilibria, the origin and a positive-coordinate branch, separated locally by a one-unstable saddle.
