For the maximal outerplanar fan \(F_n=K_1\vee P_n\), the ordinary matching complex is always a wedge of equal-dimensional spheres: \(\bigvee^{q+1}S^{q-1}\) when \(n=3q\), \(\bigvee^{q}S^{q}\) when \(n=3q+1\), and \(\bigvee^{2q+2}S^{q}\) when \(n=3q+2\), for every \(n\ge3\). The exact three-periodic sphere counts refine the previously known outerplanar sphere-wedge existence theorem.
