For three noncollinear nonzero vectors summing to zero in a real normed space, the sharp opposite-pair separation ratio \(3/2\) is attained exactly when their span has an affine-regular hexagonal unit ball and the vectors point to alternate edge midpoints. This determines the equality geometry of the separation estimate used in Fermat–Torricelli radial rescaling; it is not a claim of a new endpoint characterization.
