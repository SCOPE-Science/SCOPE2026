## correctness

PASS

Let P be the nonempty DAG arc-subsets of a finite loopless simple strongly connected digraph, and Q the nonempty proper vertex subsets. The head-support map φ:P→Q is equivariant and order-preserving: a nonempty F has at least one head and, being acyclic, at least one source, so its heads form a proper subset. For each invariant T in Q^H, the lower fiber comprises invariant acyclic F with heads in T. Strong connectivity provides an invariant nonempty crossing set B_T of arcs V\T→T. No cycle can involve a B_T arc when every F arc has head in T, because there is no F or B_T arc returning to V\T. Thus F→F∪B_T←B_T forms an H-invariant contraction for each subgroup H; the equivariant Quillen fiber lemma gives a genuine Aut(G)-equivariant homotopy equivalence to the proper-subset poset, i.e. to S(U_V). Its H-fixed dimension is o_V(H)−1, orientation determinant is vertex-permutation sign, and quotient rational top homology is the invariant orientation line. This is valid for n≥2, includes opposite-arc 2-cycles, and does not claim a homeomorphism. Small independent DAG-cone tests corroborate rather than establish the universal homotopy theorem.
