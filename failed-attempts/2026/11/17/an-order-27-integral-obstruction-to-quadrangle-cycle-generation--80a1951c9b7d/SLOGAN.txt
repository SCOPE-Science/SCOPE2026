In the rank-three ternary projective-plane matroid, all 234 shortest embedded 2-sphere cycles belong to one projective orbit and are individually primitive, yet together generate a sublattice of \(\widetilde H_2\cong\mathbb Z^{168}\) of index exactly 27. The integral obstruction to generation is precisely \((\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z)^3\), although the homology itself is torsion-free.
