The geometric antipodal fixed space of the graphic-forest complex of \(K_{2m}\) is exactly the forest complex of an apex graph with doubled complete-graph edges, and is therefore a wedge of \(b_m\) spheres of dimension \(m-1\) for every \(m\ge2\). The exact coefficient formula gives \(b_2=2\), \(b_3=24\), \(b_4=396\) and an infinite integral sphere-wedge classification.
