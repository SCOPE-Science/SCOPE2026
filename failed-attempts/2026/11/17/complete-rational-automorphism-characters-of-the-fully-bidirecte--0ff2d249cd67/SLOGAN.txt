For the multipath complex of the fully bidirected \(K_{3,3}\), the full \((\mathfrak S_3\times\mathfrak S_3)\rtimes C_2\) action splits rational third and fourth homology into seven and five multiplicity-one irreducibles, respectively. Exactly one rational third-homology class is invariant under all graph automorphisms, and the geometric quotient has the rational homology of \(S^3\).
