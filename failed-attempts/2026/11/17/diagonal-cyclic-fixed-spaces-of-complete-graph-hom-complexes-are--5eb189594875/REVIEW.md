## correctness

PASS

For a simultaneous source full m-cycle and target permutation pi, fixed points in Hom(K_m,K_n) obey p_i=pi^i p_0 and p_0=pi^m p_0, with pairwise disjoint supports. On a pi-cycle of length ell, pi^m-orbits number gcd(m,ell): disjoint m translates require gcd(m,ell)=m, or m|ell. Each eligible target cycle supplies exactly m distinct choices of one residue class, and their nonnegative total masses are barycentric coordinates; incompatible choices from the same target cycle never coexist. This gives a piecewise-linear homeomorphism to a q-fold join of D_m, including the empty case, and integral reduced Betti rank (m-1)^q in degree q-1. Independent support enumeration for (ell,m)=(6,3),(3,3),(4,3) yielded respectively 3,3,0 eligible choices. Correctness does not confer novelty.
