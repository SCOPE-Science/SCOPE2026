Simultaneously rotate all \(m\) source vertices and permute target colors in \(\operatorname{Hom}(K_m,K_n)\). If exactly \(q\) target-color cycles have lengths divisible by \(m\), the geometric fixed-point space is PL-homeomorphic to a join of \(q\) discrete \(m\)-point sets: it is empty for \(q=0\) and otherwise a wedge of \((m-1)^q\) spheres of dimension \(q-1\), even when no coloring vertex is fixed.
