The permutation complex generated by every cyclic rotation of a word and of its reversal has integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^{(n-1)^2-2}S^2\) for every \(n\ge3\). Each orientation alone has homotopy type \(S^1\); their common complete bidirected graph maps primitively onto the two fundamental circles, making the combined complex simply connected and producing quadratically many two-spheres.
