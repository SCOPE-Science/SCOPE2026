For Everett's farthest-projection dynamics on the three infinite lines of an equilateral triangle, every orbit either reaches one of three fixed midpoints or converges to one of two period-three cycles. The orientation basins on every outward ray alternate in exact dyadic shells, so their one-sided spatial densities oscillate sharply between \(1/3\) and \(2/3\), while capture times have an exact reversed-geometric law.
