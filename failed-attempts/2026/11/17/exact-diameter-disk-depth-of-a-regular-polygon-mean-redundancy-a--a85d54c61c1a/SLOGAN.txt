For the \(n\) disks with diameters from the center to the vertices of a regular \(n\)-gon, the number covering a uniform interior point has exact mean \((\pi/2-\pi/n)/\sin(2\pi/n)+1/2\). Its fraction converges with an explicit uniform CDF error to the nondegenerate law \(F(q)=\sin^2(\pi q)\) on \([0,1/2]\), and a sharp \(1/n\) pointwise discrepancy explains this limit.
