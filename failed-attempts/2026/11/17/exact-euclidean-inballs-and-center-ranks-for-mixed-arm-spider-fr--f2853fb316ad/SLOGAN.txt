For a tree with \(p\ge1\) single-edge arms and \(q\ge2\) two-edge arms, the canonical Euclidean inradius of its real Lipschitz-free ball is exactly \(\bigl(p+5q-d_{p,q}^2/(1+p+2q)\bigr)^{-1/2}\), where \(d_{p,q}\) is a parity-controlled sign discrepancy. Every contacting facet uses full-height two-edge arms, and an exact two-group sign-rank formula determines the affine dimension of all optimal inball centers, including a two-dimensional parity jump at \(p=3\).
