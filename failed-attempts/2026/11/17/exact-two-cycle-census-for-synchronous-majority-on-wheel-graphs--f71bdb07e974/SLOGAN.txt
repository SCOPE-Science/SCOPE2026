Synchronous binary majority on the wheel \(K_1\vee C_n\) has exactly two fixed points, and the complete primitive two-cycle census is a finite coefficient extraction from the explicit no-isolated-ones cyclic-run polynomial. The only two-cycles that keep the hub fixed occur when \(n\) is even, exactly two such cycles; all others are counted by strict global hub-flip inequalities, yielding exponentially rare periodic configurations but exponentially many cycles along infinite subsequences.
