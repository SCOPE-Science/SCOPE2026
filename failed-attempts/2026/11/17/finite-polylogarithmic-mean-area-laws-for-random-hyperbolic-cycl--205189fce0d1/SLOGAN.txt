For every fixed number \(n\ge3\) of arclength-uniform points on a hyperbolic circle, the expected geodesic hull area is an explicit finite combination of even polylogarithms at \(\tanh^2(R/2)\). It strictly determines the circle radius and approaches the ideal \((n-2)\pi\) area with sharp deficit \(4n(n-1)Re^{-R}/\pi\).
