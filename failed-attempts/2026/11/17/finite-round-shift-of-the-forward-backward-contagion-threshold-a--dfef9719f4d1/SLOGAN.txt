In the exact deterministic finite-round two-stage contagion map, the boundary between forward and backward invasion is a unique \(p_*(\nu,\tau)\in(0,1/2)\), rather than the continuous-time value \(1/2\). Above this boundary, critical adopter density decays with the explicit sharp law \(n a_n\to-1/Q\); below it, the invasion threshold has an unstable subcritical endemic branch.
