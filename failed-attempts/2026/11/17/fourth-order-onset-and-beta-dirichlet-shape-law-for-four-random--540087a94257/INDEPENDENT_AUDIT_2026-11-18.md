## correctness

PASS

For four independent Haar-uniform cap centers, support-function containment of the centered t-ball in the inscribed tetrahedron gives the critical radius arccos(1/3). Near each of the two ordered chiral tetrahedra the three independent facet-height deficits are O(delta) and the two disphenoid transverse parameters O(sqrt(delta)); summing facet heights gives 4/3-2Q+o(delta), with Q=2(alpha²+alpha beta+beta²) of determinant 3. For fixed Q, the three slack variables fill a simplex of volume (4delta-2Q)^3/6. Direct integral ∫_0^{2delta}(4delta-2q)^3(pi/(6sqrt3))dq =16pi delta^4/(3sqrt3), times 2*(729/(128pi²)) gives 81sqrt3/(4pi)delta^4; sin(w0)=2sqrt2/3 gives 16sqrt3/pi in angular excess. Under conditioning, uniform rescaled shape volume gives Beta(1,4) for Q/(2delta), while uniformly distributed simplex slacks give independent Dirichlet(1,1,1,1). Boundaries delta=0 and q=1 have zero probability. This reconstructs the statement, without treating Monte Carlo as proof.
