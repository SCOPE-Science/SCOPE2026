For four independent uniform spherical caps, the probability of covering the sphere turns on at the tetrahedral optimal radius \(\omega_0=\arccos(1/3)\) with the sharp fourth-order law \(p(\omega)\sim(16\sqrt3/\pi)(\omega-\omega_0)^4\). Conditioned on this rare coverage, a rescaled disphenoid-shape defect and four normalized facet slacks converge independently to \(\operatorname{Beta}(1,4)\) and \(\operatorname{Dirichlet}(1,1,1,1)\).
