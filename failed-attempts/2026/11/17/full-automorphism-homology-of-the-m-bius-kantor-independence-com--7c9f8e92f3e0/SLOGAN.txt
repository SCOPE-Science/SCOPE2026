The full 96-element automorphism group of the Möbius–Kantor graph splits its independence-complex rational homology into an absolutely irreducible three-dimensional module, a separate linear character in degree three, and the graph-bipartition sign in degree four. The two corresponding index-two quotients have rational cohomology rings of \(S^3\) and \(S^4\), while the full quotient is rationally acyclic.
