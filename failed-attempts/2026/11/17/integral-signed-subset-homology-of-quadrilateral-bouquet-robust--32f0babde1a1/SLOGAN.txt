The known sphere wedges for robust clique complexes of quadrilateral bouquets carry an explicit **integral** signed-subset symmetry lattice: a neighbor swap reverses precisely those top-homology generators indexed by subsets containing that square, while square permutations act without an orientation sign. Consequently the complete automorphism character is a signed cycle-index coefficient, there are no full-group invariant top cycles, and the integral coinvariants are \(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\).
