# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For prime power q≥2, rank-four spherical building is a two-dimensional simply connected wedge of q^6 two-spheres by Solomon–Tits. Projective Singer G=C_{(q+1)(q²+1)} acts without simplex inversions. Its only nonidentity vertex stabilizers are H=F_{q²}^×/F_q^× (order q+1) on q²+1 middle-dimension subspaces; other elements have no fixed geometric point. Armstrong gives π1(Y)=G/H=C_{q²+1}, hence H1 cyclic same order. Burnside fixed-cell Euler gives χ(Y)=(1+q^6+q(q²+1))/((q+1)(q²+1))=q³−q²+1. The two-dimensional quotient cellular C2 is free, so H2 is free of rank χ−1=q²(q−1); UCT gives H²=Z^{q²(q−1)}⊕C_{q²+1}, higher H=0. The argument includes nonprime powers. Finite independently recomputed q=2,3,4,5,7 Euler and group orders corroborate but do not establish all prime powers.

## originality

FAIL

The published broader all-rank/all-Singer-subgroup theorem states π1(Y_t)=H1(Y_t;Z)=C_{t/gcd(t,L)} and gives the exact rational top Betti number for every n≥4 and t|N, with L=N/Φ_n(q). At n=4 and t=N, Φ4(q)=q²+1 and L=q+1, so this entails π1=H1=C_{q²+1}; its rational H2 rank is q²(q−1). Because the quotient rank-four building is 2-dimensional, H2 is a free submodule of cellular C2, so integral H2 rank equals rational rank. UCT supplies H² and higher H vanishing. Thus every new assertion of this paper is a direct corollary of an already-published result with strictly broader rank/subgroup scope. Independent classical Drudge fixed flats and Armstrong isotropy explain the mechanism but are not the decisive coverage source.

## value

FAIL

This rank-four Singer quotient is a natural torsion-bearing topology and would be worth recording absent previous work. Yet the claimed exact H1, H2, π1 and cohomology are immediately recoverable from the stronger all-rank/full-subgroup quotient theorem and elementary 2-dimensional cellular algebra. The package offers no independent previously unknown dimension-four invariant, so specialized recomputation has no original mathematical value.
