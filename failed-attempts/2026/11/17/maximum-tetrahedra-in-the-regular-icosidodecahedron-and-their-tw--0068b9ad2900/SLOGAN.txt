For the unit-edge regular icosidodecahedron, the largest inscribed tetrahedron has volume \((25+11\sqrt5)/24\); every maximizer belongs to exactly one of twenty triangular facial families. Precisely sixty vertex tetrahedra attain this bound, and the next vertex-supported volume is \((11+5\sqrt5)/12\), with multiplicity 240 and an exact gap \((3+\sqrt5)/24\).
