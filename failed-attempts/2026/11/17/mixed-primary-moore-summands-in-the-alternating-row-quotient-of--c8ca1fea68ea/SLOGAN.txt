The ordinary geometric quotient of the first shellable four-row chessboard complex \(\Delta_{4,7}\) by even row permutations is simply connected but has simultaneous \(2\)- and \(3\)-primary second-homotopy torsion. Its exact homotopy type is \(M(\mathbb Z/6,2)\vee\bigvee^{5}M(\mathbb Z/3,2)\vee\bigvee^{15}S^3\), whereas the unquotiented complex is a wedge of \(225\) three-spheres.
