The ordinary matching complex of the Petersen graph has integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^5 S^2\vee\bigvee^5 S^3\). Its full \(S_5\)-action separates the two five-dimensional homology groups into a signed permutation module and an irreducible Specht module, making the full symmetry quotient rationally acyclic while the alternating-group quotient retains the rational cohomology of \(S^2\).
