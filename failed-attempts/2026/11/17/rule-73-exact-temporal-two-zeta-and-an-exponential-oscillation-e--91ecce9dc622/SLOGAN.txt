Elementary cellular automaton Rule 73 has an exact rational spatial zeta function for its configurations of temporal period dividing two. Primitive two-cycles grow as \(\alpha^n/2\), with \(1.490<\alpha<1.491\), whereas its fixed configurations grow only as \(\beta^n\), with \(1.220<\beta<1.221\): the equilibrium share among temporally one- or two-periodic configurations decays exponentially.
