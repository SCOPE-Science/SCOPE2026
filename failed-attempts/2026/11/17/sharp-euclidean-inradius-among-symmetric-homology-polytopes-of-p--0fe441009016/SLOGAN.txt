The oriented boundary-column polytope of any convex-polygon triangulation has inradius \(\bigl[\mathbf1^{\mathsf T}(3I-A_T)^{-1}\mathbf1\bigr]^{-1/2}\), controlled entirely by its triangle-dual tree. Among all triangulations, this inradius is maximized exactly by dual paths, with an explicit radical formula and a full \((n-1)\)-dimensional family of optimal ball centers.
