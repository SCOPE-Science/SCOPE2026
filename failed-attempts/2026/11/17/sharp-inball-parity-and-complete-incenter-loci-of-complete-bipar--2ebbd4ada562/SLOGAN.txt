For the Euclidean symmetric edge polytope of every complete bipartite graph, the largest inscribed ball has radius \(\bigl(\max(p,q)-(\max(p,q)\bmod2)/(p+q)\bigr)^{-1/2}\). Its full incenter locus undergoes a sharp parity transition: unequal parts give affine dimensions \(\min(p,q)\) or \(\min(p,q)-1\), while equal odd parts have a unique center and equal even parts yield an explicit nontrivial segment.
