For the two-dimensional risk-sensitive population-learning map, global one-step invertibility ends at the exact memory-dependent boundary \(a_c=4(1-k)/(1-k(1+cp_1))\) when \(k<1/(1+cp_1)\); faster belief adjustment preserves injectivity at every learning intensity. At the boundary the inverse has sharp cubic-root regularity, and just beyond it a physical state can have three distinct immediate predecessors.
