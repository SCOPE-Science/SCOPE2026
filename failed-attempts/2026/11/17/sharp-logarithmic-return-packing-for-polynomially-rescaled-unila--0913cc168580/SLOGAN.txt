For polynomial scalar multiples \(n^\alpha B^n\) of the ordinary unilateral backward shift, dense mixing universality coexists with a sharp logarithmic ceiling on return visits to one fixed norm ball. The maximal logarithmic return frequency is exactly \(\alpha/\log((1-\varepsilon)/\varepsilon)\) on \(c_0\), and every \(\ell^p\) has the same sharp small-ball coefficient \(\alpha\).
