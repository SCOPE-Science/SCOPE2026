The fifth rational independence homology of the dodecahedral graph is the antipodal-sign twist of the four-dimensional augmentation module on its five inscribed cubes, and it stays absolutely irreducible under icosahedral rotations. Full, rotational, and antipodal symmetry quotients are rationally acyclic, but quotienting by the rotational stabilizer of one cube retains the rational cohomology ring of \(S^5\).
