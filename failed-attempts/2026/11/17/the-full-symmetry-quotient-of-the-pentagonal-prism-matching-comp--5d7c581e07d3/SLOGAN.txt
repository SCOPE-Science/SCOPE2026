The full \(D_5\times C_2\) geometric symmetry quotient of the ordinary matching complex of the pentagonal prism is homotopy equivalent to \(S^3\), even though the unquotiented complex is a wedge of two two-spheres and two three-spheres. Exact integral quotient-chain cancellations and a separate fundamental-group presentation upgrade its previously known rational sphere cohomology to a complete homotopy classification.
