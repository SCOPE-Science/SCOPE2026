For the ternary three-cube at inclusive Hamming scale two, the \(46\)-dimensional third rational homology is multiplicity-free under the full isometry group \(\mathfrak S_3\wr\mathfrak S_3\), splitting into seven irreducible modules of dimensions \(8,12,6,1,12,6,1\). The unique class fixed by independent symbol relabelings changes sign when two coordinate axes are exchanged, so no nonzero third-homology class survives all isometries and the full geometric quotient is rationally acyclic.
