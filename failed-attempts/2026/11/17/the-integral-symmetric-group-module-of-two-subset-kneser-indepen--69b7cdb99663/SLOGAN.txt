For every two-subset Kneser graph on \(n\ge4\) ground points, a canonical relabeling-equivariant simplicial map identifies its independence homology integrally with \(\bigwedge^3\ker(\mathbb Z^n\to\mathbb Z)\), rather than merely determining the known sphere count. Under relabeling within \(r\) blocks, the ordinary quotient retains exactly \(\binom{r-1}{3}\) independent rational two-dimensional homology classes, regardless of block sizes.
