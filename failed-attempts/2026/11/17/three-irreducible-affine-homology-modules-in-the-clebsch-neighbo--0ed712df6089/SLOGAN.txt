The \(31\)-dimensional rational second homology of the five-regular Clebsch graph's open-neighborhood complex splits under its full affine symmetry into three absolutely irreducible modules of dimensions \(6\), \(15\), and \(10\). The translation-fixed six-dimensional part is \(\bigwedge^2\operatorname{Std}_{S_5}\), giving a rational six-sphere-bouquet cohomology ring for the translation quotient while the full symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic.
