For \(m\ge2\) hexagons glued at a common vertex, the independence complex is \(S^m\vee S^{2m-1}\), and its entire integral wreath-product homology action splits into two distinct one-dimensional characters. Individual hexagon reflections reverse the upper class, while permutations of the hexagons act by their sign on the lower class; the full-symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic.
