The seven-dimensional rational third homology of the four-cube independence complex is, under all \(384\) cube symmetries, the direct sum of a three-dimensional sign-twisted standard \(S_4\)-module and an absolutely irreducible four-dimensional signed-coordinate module. Translations preserve three top-homology directions, but the full automorphism group preserves none; the known nonequivariant sphere count is not the new result.
