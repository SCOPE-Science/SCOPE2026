For every pair of deleted vertices in the binary cubes of dimensions three, four and five, inclusive Hamming-scale-two Vietoris–Rips complexes have an explicit integral sphere-wedge homotopy type. In the five-cube, deleting a pair at Hamming distance three, four or five yields exactly \(29\) three-spheres, while the previously covered distance-one and distance-two cases yield \(32\).
