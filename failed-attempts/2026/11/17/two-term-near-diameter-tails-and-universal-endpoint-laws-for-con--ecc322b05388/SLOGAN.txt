For uniformly sampled point pairs in any fixed convex polytope, the probability of a distance within \(\varepsilon\) of its diameter has an explicit positive two-term expansion beginning with \(\varepsilon^{2d}\), governed by diametral vertex tangent cones. Conditional on this rare event, the rescaled endpoint depths obey a universal \(\operatorname{Dirichlet}(d,d,1)\) law; in boxes the coefficients are explicit and the cube uniquely minimizes the leading coefficient at fixed volume.
