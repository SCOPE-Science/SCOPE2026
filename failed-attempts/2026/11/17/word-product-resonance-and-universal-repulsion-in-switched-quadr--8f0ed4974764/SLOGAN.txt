For every finite nontrivial word of two-species quadratic stochastic maps \(q_t(x)=x+t x(1-x)\), a unique interior fixed point exists exactly when both endpoint products \(\prod(1+t)\) and \(\prod(1-t)\) are less than one, and that point is strictly repelling. Consequently every regular nonconstant periodic orbit in any finite piecewise selection of these maps is repelling, regardless of the number of discontinuities.
