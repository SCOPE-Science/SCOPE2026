For every odd prime \(p\), the singular cyclic Coxeter-element quotient of the type-\(B_{2p}\) sphere has an explicit universal cover homeomorphic to a **double suspension of a \(p\)-fold lens space**, giving \(\pi_1\cong C_4\), \(\pi_2=0\) and \(\pi_3\cong C_p\). Its integral homology contains cyclic \(4p\)-torsion in every eligible odd degree, a specialization of existing general weighted-lens homology formulas.
