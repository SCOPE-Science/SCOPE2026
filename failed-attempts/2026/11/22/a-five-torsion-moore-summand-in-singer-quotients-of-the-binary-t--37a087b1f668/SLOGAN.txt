The ordinary quotients of the binary type-\(A_3\) spherical building by all four Singer-cycle subgroups have exact homotopy types: the trivial and order-three quotients are wedges of \(64\) and \(24\) two-spheres, while the order-five and order-fifteen quotients are \(M(\mathbb Z/5,1)\vee\bigvee^{12}S^2\) and \(M(\mathbb Z/5,1)\vee\bigvee^{4}S^2\). This exhibits a degree-five Moore summand even in the full nonfree Singer quotient and is certified by explicit two-dimensional attaching-map cancellations.
