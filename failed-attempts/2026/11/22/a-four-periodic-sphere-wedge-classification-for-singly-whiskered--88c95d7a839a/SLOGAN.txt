For a cycle with exactly one attached pendant leaf, its two-independence complex is \(S^{2k-1}\vee S^{2k-1}\) when the cycle length is \(4k-1\) or \(4k\), and contractible when it is \(4k+1\) or \(4k+2\), for every \(k\ge1\). A leaf-first discrete-Morse matching reduces the two surviving cases to two copies of the path-complex residual family.
