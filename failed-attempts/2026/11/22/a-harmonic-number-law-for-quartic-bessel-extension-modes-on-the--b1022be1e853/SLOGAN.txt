Every positive-integer quartic Bessel moment \(\int_0^\infty J_n(r)^4\,dr/r\) equals \((H_{2n}-H_n/2)/(\pi^2n^2)\), giving the complete four-dimensional quartic spherical Fourier-extension mode profile and its sharp \((\log n)/(2\pi^2n^2)\) tail. The formula evaluates the previously established sharp radial--angular stability coefficient as \(16\pi^3(1-1/\sqrt3)\), without reasserting originality for the known base mode or general stability principle.
