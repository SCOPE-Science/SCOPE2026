For endpoint-normalized Gegenbauer coefficients of unit-variation data, the Chebyshev point has a sharp logarithmic boundary layer: setting \(\lambda_n\log n\to\tau\), the limiting rescaled optimal coefficient bound is \((2/\pi)\max\{1,e^\tau\}\). The change of slope at \(\tau=0\) records a switch of worst jump locations from the outermost to the innermost polynomial zeros.
