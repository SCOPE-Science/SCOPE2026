# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Under the prime cyclic action an invariant partition has either one block or five singleton blocks: a nonempty proper block has an orbit of length five. Chain rank fixes the order of vertices, so there are no nontrivial cell stabilizers. Independent enumeration gives (50,205,180) before quotienting and (10,41,36) afterward. After tree contraction, the boundary word of a triangle is AB·BC·AC^−1. With the packaged ordering and pivot rule, an independent reconstruction gives 31 valid single-occurrence eliminations of the 32 loop generators, ending with (-31)^5 twice and three empty words. Such a pivot disk together with its once-traversed edge can be removed relative to the rest of the graph in the graph plus pivot disk; transporting all other attaching maps across that equivalence gives the substitutions. One degree-five disk gives the Moore summand and the next is null in that summand. This, along with the other three null maps, gives four spheres.

## originality

FAIL

The homotopy type is a direct corollary of Donau's analysis of this exact quotient and the published classification of finite two-complexes with finite cyclic fundamental group. The explicit cell certificate is a new proof route relative to the sources inspected, but it does not supply a new homotopy classification.

## value

FAIL

The quotient is a natural example and the direct cancellation is reproducible, but the asserted complete homotopy invariant is mechanically determined by two established theorems. It does not settle an open attaching-map classification; no independent substantive consequence of the particular choice of pivots is given.
