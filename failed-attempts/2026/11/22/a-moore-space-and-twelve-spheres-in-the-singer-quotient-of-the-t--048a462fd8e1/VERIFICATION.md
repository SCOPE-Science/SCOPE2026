# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The difference set gives the projective plane in the stated field coordinates. Translation has no stabilizers of nonempty faces. The quotient has one vertex, six step loops and eighteen triangle disks. An independent enumeration of noncollinear triples and cyclic orbits reproduces the cancellation sequence: with the five selected triangle words the generators at steps 2,3,4,5,6 are replaced successively by a²,a³,a⁴,a⁵,a⁶. Once a generator is used exactly once in the selected disk boundary, the graph with just that disk retracts up to homotopy onto the graph without the generator, relative to the remaining graph. Pushing the other attaching maps through this equivalence justifies actual CW eliminations, not inference from presentation groups alone. Eight remaining words reduce to identity and five to degree 13. Attaching one degree-13 disk makes all the others null, giving the claimed wedge. The connected 13-sheeted cover is universal since the quotient has π₁=C₁₃; the Moore boundary on lifted cells is multiplication by the norm, whose kernel is the augmentation ideal, and there are twelve free deck orbits of lifted sphere cells.

## originality

FAIL

The published finite-cyclic two-complex classification implies the entire asserted homotopy type once the already known π₁ and H₂ are inserted. It also gives the integral deck module by lifting this classification. The explicit certificate gives a direct calculation, but does not make these classifications new.

## value

FAIL

The canonical quotient and integral lattice are meaningful objects. However, both stated invariants are routine consequences of the established finite-cyclic classification combined with known Singer invariants. The short constructive proof has expository and reproducibility value, but the record claims no additional marked attaching invariant, equivariant splitting or other substantive consequence that the existing theorems leave open.
