# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The (21,5,1) difference set has all twenty ordered nonzero differences and defines the stated plane. Translation is free on vertices and edges, with exactly one orbit of noncollinear triangles with order-three stabilizer. On its closed triangle the stabilizer acts by rotation; the quotient is a disk, and the quotient of the boundary traverses the step-seven edge only once. Giving every other triangle orbit its ordinary characteristic disk yields the actual CW orbit space with (1,10,54) cells. Reconstructing all 54 step-words and implementing the specified greedy reductions independently gives the nine stated substitutions. Each selected pivot word after earlier substitutions uses its pivot edge exactly once, giving a homotopy equivalence relative to the other graph edges in the graph plus pivot disk; the other attachments are transported by that equivalence. There remain 31 empty words, nine seventh powers and five inverse seventh powers on e₁. After the first degree-seven disk, 44 residual maps are null. This proves the homotopy decomposition for the nonfree ordinary quotient.

## originality

FAIL

The first singular orbit is mathematically pertinent, but its presence and its effect on π₁ and homology are already prior coverage. Dyer–Sieradski then gives precisely the claimed Moore–sphere decomposition from those known data. The fact that the original action is nonfree is no exception to their theorem on the resulting two-complex.
