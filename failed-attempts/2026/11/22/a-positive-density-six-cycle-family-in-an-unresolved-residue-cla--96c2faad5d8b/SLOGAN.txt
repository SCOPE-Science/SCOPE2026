A prime congruence class still unresolved in the classification of finite-ring structure graphs contains an explicit positive-density infinite family with primitive six-cycles: among all primes, those congruent to \(19\pmod{24}\) for which a specified sextic has a root have exact density \(5/72\). The same calculation gives exact conditional frequencies \(4/9,4/9,1/9\) for zero, one or two certified six-cycles. The proof combines an order-\(18\) dynatomic Galois group with its quadratic intersection with a cyclotomic field; it does not settle every prime in the unresolved class.
