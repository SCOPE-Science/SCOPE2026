## value

FAIL

The curve is intrinsically motivated, and the geometry is correctly described, but this narrow result is a direct extraction from the already specified product subgroup. Write Γ=K∩Γ₀(3), with K the kernel of the two central local quotients at 8 and 5. The regular intermediate quotient is then just the pullback of X_K→X(1) along X₀(3)→X(1). At 3 this is the standard four-point projective action; at 8 and 5 all nontrivial action is in regular quotient factors. Thus the degree and deck group are the factor orders, the relative cusp inertia is the scalar modulus 8·5 for either base cusp, and the Galois closure replaces the stabilizer of a projective point by the action kernel. The partitions and genera are standard arithmetic consequences of that same coset decomposition. This is not a case where a necessary exact invariant had to be resolved by a new classification, argument about the given curve, or nontrivial exact computation. The record does not give a further geometric or representation-theoretic consequence that would supply a distinct mathematical reason for this routine specialization. Value fails on mechanical implication, not on the size of the genus or on access to a table.
