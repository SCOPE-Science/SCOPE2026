Auffarth's genus-\(1777\) modular curve is quasiplatonic: it has a regular \((3,40,40)\) Belyi map of degree \(5760\), with deck group \((\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb Z/8\mathbb Z)/Z_8)\times A_5\). The canonical \(j\)-map has geometric monodromy of order \(69120\) and a Galois closure of genus \(5473\).
