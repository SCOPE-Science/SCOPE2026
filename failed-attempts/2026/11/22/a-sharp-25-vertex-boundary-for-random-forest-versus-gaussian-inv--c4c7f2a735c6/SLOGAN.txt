Root-count random-forest inverse-trace estimation has no greater one-draw variance than Gaussian trace probing on every connected weighted graph with at most 25 vertices, but the preference can reverse starting at 26 vertices. The exact maximal forest-variance excess in order \(n\) is \((n-25)/12\), attained uniquely by a uniformly weighted complete graph with nonzero Laplacian eigenvalue \(5q\).
