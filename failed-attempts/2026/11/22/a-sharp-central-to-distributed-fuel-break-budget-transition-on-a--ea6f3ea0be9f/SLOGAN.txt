On a four-site forest corridor with independent fire-spread links, a small mitigation budget should go entirely to the middle link, but outer links receive positive investment exactly when \(B>\log(1+p)\). Above that sharp boundary the unique optimal efforts are \((\log z,\log(z+p),\log z)\), where \(z^3+pz^2=e^B\); they approach equal thirds at large budgets.
