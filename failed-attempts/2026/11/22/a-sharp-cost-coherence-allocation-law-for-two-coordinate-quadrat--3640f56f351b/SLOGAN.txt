For exact two-coordinate descent on any positive-definite quadratic, unequal update prices and nonzero coordinate coupling force a unique nonuniform sampler maximizing sharp worst-direction one-step objective decrease per expected work. Its allocation is \(p_\star=(\sqrt{c_1c_2}+\rho c_2)/(2\sqrt{c_1c_2}+\rho(c_1+c_2))\), and it improves the equal-sampling progress-per-work guarantee by a sharp factor approaching two.
