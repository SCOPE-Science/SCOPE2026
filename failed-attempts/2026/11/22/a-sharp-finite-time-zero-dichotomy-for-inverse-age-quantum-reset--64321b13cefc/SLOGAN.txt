For a qubit reset with inverse-age probability \(\gamma/(n+1)\), its finite-time coherence polynomial has **every zero inside** the unit circle when \(\gamma<1/2\), **every zero outside** when \(\gamma>1/2\), and **every zero on** the circle precisely at \(\gamma=1/2\). Consequently a phase gate can erase the ensemble coherence exactly at a finite time only at this balanced reset strength, where the already-known Legendre zero phases occur.
