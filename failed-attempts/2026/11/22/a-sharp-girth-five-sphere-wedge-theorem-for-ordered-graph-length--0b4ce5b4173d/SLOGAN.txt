For every connected graph with no triangles or four-cycles, the ordered metric length nerve at scale two has homotopy type \(|G|\vee\bigvee^{|E(G)|}S^2\). Its integral two-dimensional homology is the **signed** oriented-edge lattice, while exactly one **unsigned** edge-backtrack class dies in first homology at that threshold; the distinction is invisible to the Betti numbers alone.
