For unaugmented Bayesian quadrature with the Matérn-\(3/2\) kernel on \([-1,1]\) at the symmetric nodes \((-a,0,a)\), both outer weights are always positive, and **every weight is strictly positive for all finite inverse lengthscales** exactly when \(a\ge6-\sqrt{30}\). For smaller spacings, the central weight is necessarily negative in the flat-kernel regime but positive again at sufficiently short kernel lengthscales.
