Moving just the central Chebyshev–Lobatto interpolation node by a fixed fraction \(\alpha\in(0,1)\) of the angular mesh leaves the Lebesgue constant logarithmic, but changes its sharp coefficient to \((2/\pi)\max_{0<u<1/2}\sin(\pi u)(1+\alpha/u)\), strictly greater than the unperturbed coefficient. The asymptotically dominant norming hot spot approaches the original central node from the side opposite the displacement.
