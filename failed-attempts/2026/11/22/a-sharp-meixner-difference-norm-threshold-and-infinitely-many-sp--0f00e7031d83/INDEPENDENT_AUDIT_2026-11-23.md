# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The density argument is valid: exponential moments and Cauchy–Schwarz give holomorphy of Σ w_k f(k) z^k for |z|<c^(−1/2); orthogonality to falling factorials makes its entire Taylor jet zero at 1. This yields full density and, by boundedness of the shift, γ_n increasing to the operator norm. The unitary diagonal weight change followed by alternating signs gives the claimed Jacobi matrix, including diagonal 1 at site 0 and 1+a²_(k−1) otherwise. Since the bonds tend to a=c^(−1/2), it is compactly equivalent to constant half-line coefficients, with sine-transform range [(a−1)²,(a+1)²]. For β≥1, all shift weights are ≤a, giving Γ≤1+a, with equality by the essential band. For β<1 the sine-block Rayleigh bound has the exact minimal diagonal gain (1−β)/[c(K+L+β−2)] and a band-edge loss 2a[1−cos(π/(L+1))]. At L=⌊K^(3/4)⌋ the gain dominates uniformly at large K. At least one vacant site separates blocks, annihilating all off-block form entries; min–max yields infinitely many eigenvalues strictly above the band. The first coordinate and the three-term recurrence give simplicity. At every β<1 the top eigenvector may be taken positive: taking absolute values cannot decrease the Rayleigh quotient, and a zero then propagates to neighbors. Strict coefficient comparison yields strict monotonicity below one. The endpoint limits follow from a₀−1≤Γ≤a₀+1 at β<1 and operator-norm convergence to the limiting constant shift as β→1. Density ensures some finite degree exceeds the edge in the strict phase and all larger degrees do too. The finite checker and verification note were inspected, not executed; its finite sections are in the sequence representation, and alone cannot prove the infinite count or the polynomial limit. No result for eigenvalues of Δ rather than Δ*Δ is inferred.

## originality

FAIL

The main measured gap is already covered by the same Meixner problem's bounds plus an established stronger Jacobi edge criterion. In particular, a concrete reduction to the constant-bond Kneser criterion of Luef–Teschl gives infinitely many upper outliers in the whole strict phase; details below. The Resultary Bergman analysis also proves a general discrete Coulomb comparison and transfer method covering even the growth of the number of such outliers, without relying on same-day order. The paper of Dimitrov–Nikolov does not itself print the exact β<1 phase classification. The analytic density bridge and elementary positivity and norm comparisons are correct, but do not supply an independent substantial finding avoiding these implications.

## value

FAIL

The Meixner norms are a natural object and the finite-to-infinite bridge is pedagogically useful. But the asserted narrow exact cutoff and the infinite-outlier part are mechanically implied by the published Meixner bounds and the existing general edge criterion once the elementary coefficients are written down. The record supplies no outlier positions, counts beyond infinitude, new coefficient regime, or nonroutine density result that survives independently as a worthwhile unknown exact fact.
