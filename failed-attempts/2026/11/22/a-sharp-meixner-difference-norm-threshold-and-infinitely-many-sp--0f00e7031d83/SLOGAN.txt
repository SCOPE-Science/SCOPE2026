For the Meixner-weighted forward-difference inequality, the limiting sharp constant equals \(1+c^{-1/2}\) exactly when \(\beta\ge1\), but is strictly larger and strictly decreases in \(\beta\) below that threshold. Every \(0<\beta<1\) produces infinitely many simple singular-spectrum outliers above the universal essential edge, while algebraic polynomials are dense for all allowed weights.
