For every full sliding window of width \(m\ge2\) on a signal with intensity range \([a,b]\) and length at least \(2m\), the sharp two-input adjacent-variation coefficient of population variance is \((b-a)4\lfloor m^2/4\rfloor/m^2\). This gives nonexpansivity on unit-range signals for all widths, with a strict \(1/m^2\) contraction deficit for odd widths and sharp equality for even widths.
