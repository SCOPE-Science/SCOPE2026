# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Centering y=P−C₀ gives G(y)=A E_χ(ky)−y and odd symmetry; |E_χ|<1 implies |y|<A at equilibrium and therefore P>0. The positive equilibria are the intersections R_χ(z)=1/(Ak). Direct differentiation gives E_χ'' with sign given by χ²−2−χ cosh z on z>0, negative throughout for χ≤2 and changing once from positive to negative for χ>2. The derivative numerator J=zE_χ'−E_χ has J'=zE_χ'', J(0)=0 and limit −1. Thus R decreases strictly in the former case and has exactly one strict maximum in the latter, with nonzero slopes off the peak and R''<0 there. Level crossings give the claimed counts, including folds and the one remaining positive intersection at A=A₊ for χ>2. At a nonzero simple root G' has the sign of R_χ'; at the central threshold the sign of R_χ(z)−R_χ(0) on small positive z gives attraction for χ≤2 and repulsion for χ>2 even with G'(0)=0. A tangent nonzero root at A₋ is semistable. The witness uses E₅(log 9)=20/43 and G(log 9)>0 from the six-term exponential Taylor bound, with G'(0)<0 and G(5)<0; independent bisection agrees with both positive displacements. The theorem is correct within the uncalibrated, centered scalar model.

## originality

FAIL

Cassandro, Orlandi, Picco and Vares (2005), §9, analyze the same scalar fixed-point equation for symmetric bimodal random-field Curie–Weiss. They give the concavity threshold, the central stability boundary, the two positive solutions on the five-root side, their slope stability tests, and their coalescence at a unique off-center tangent boundary. These are the same curves in a different coordinate system, not merely a prior example of five roots. The gain equation in the frozen record is the transformed prior tangency condition. Wainwright (2020) independently includes the same subfamily and qualitative stability count, though the inspected passages there do not give the exact frontier. The mathematical coverage, not an access issue, is the reason for failure.

## value

FAIL

The centered ODE is useful as a pedagogical illustration of competing release thresholds. As a scientific contribution, however, its exact phase diagram is a rescaled previously analyzed mean-field one. The phosphorus labels, positivity obtained by a shift, and a numerical witness do not provide a distinct structural result or a calibrated ecological finding.
