For an incoherently pumped, shelved three-level emitter reset independently to its ground state, the long-time photon Fano factor crosses shot noise exactly once at \(r=\sqrt{ps}-u>0\) when \(ps>u^2\), independently of radiative decay rate. Even at that Fano-equality point, a strictly sub-Poisson third count cumulant proves the photon process is not Poisson.
