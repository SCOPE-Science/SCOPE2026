In the equilibrium FK random-cluster gas on an \(m\)-page triangular book, distinct fully open pages are always positively correlated in pairs, but their third connected correlation changes sign exactly once as activity grows if and only if \(q>2\). The unique critical activity satisfies \(\sqrt m\,v_m\to\sqrt{q\log(q-1)}\), exposing a sharp non-Gaussian bridge-fluctuation boundary.
