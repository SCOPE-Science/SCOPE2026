Two equal Gaussian resource blooms produce exactly one optimal center for a fixed-duration foraging window until their half-separation exceeds the unique threshold \(aw/\sigma^2=\operatorname{artanh}(w/a)\); above it, precisely two symmetric optimum dates emerge. The onset has an explicit square-root law, and simply lengthening the foraging window can erase the two-optimum regime without altering the resource peaks.
