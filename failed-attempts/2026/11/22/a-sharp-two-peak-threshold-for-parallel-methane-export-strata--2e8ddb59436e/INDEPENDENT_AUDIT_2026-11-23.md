# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The equilibria give I_i κ_i v/(a_i+v)^2; the triangular stock dynamics have both eigenvalues −(a_i+v). Measuring flow in units of a_1 and dropping a positive common multiplier gives the stated F. Its derivative is positive up to 1 and negative from R onward; inside (1,R) its sign is that of H_R−K. The log derivative of H_R has the sign of −[2v²−(R+1)v+2R], since its denominator and 2(R−1) are positive. The discriminant is R²−14R+1; above its larger zero the roots are in (1,R) (the polynomial is positive at both endpoints and its minimum is negative and interior). The two simple turns give the open odds window by continuity and the endpoint limits ∞ and 0. At either odds boundary a double crossing root has no sign change and the other root gives the sole peak. At the critical separation H_R is still strictly decreasing, with a second-order derivative zero at v_0; at K=H_R(v_0) the first nonconstant local term of F is negative and quartic. For R=20 the endpoints of the window are exactly 2401/54675 and 2187/38416, with 1/19 strictly between. This is an analytic verification of the specified positive-domain classification, not a validation of physical methane dynamics. Inspected the packaged supplement without executing it.

## originality

FAIL

Demonstrated exact prior coverage: Došlá (2009), Proposition 3, gives both the identical separation and identical strict weight interval for the positively rescaled flux after a monotone logarithmic change of the independent variable. The mathematical classification is not distinct from the common-scale logistic-mixture result.

The mass-balance normalization and a witness chosen inside the known open interval do not add a distinct structural fact. The restricted proxy supplies neither an observational test nor a new mechanistic or robustness result.
