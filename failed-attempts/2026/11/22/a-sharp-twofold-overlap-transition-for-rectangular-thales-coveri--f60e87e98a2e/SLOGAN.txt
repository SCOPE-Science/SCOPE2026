For the four diameter disks joining the center of a rectangle to its vertices, the fraction of the rectangle covered twice has a complete analytic shape law: it strictly increases from \(\pi/4-1/2\) at the square toward \(1\) for thin rectangles. The law is \(C^1\) but not \(C^2\) at the sharp aspect-ratio transition \(2\sqrt2\), and its thin-rectangle deficit is asymptotic to \(3/(2\lambda^2)\).
