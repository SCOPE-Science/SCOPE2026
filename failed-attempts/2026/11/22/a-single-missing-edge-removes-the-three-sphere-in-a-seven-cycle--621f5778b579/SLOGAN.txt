Deleting any one edge from \(C_7\times K_3\) removes precisely the unique \(S^3\) summand of its independence complex while preserving all three \(S^4\) summands integrally. The natural inclusion is, up to homotopy, the wedge projection \(S^3\vee\bigvee^3S^4\to\bigvee^3S^4\), with a primitive degree-one connecting map.
