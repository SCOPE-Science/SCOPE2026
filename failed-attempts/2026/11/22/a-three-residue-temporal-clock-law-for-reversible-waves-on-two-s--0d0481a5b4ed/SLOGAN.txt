On two-subset Kneser graphs, a reversible characteristic-three wave has sharp global temporal order \(12\), \(6\), or \(36\) according to the ground-set size modulo three. The entire labelled orbit census follows from an incidence-driven Jordan decomposition; in the order-36 regime, the exact proportion of states attaining that period is \((1-q^{-2})(1-q^{-(n-1)})\).
