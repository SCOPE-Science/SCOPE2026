For the graph of Dyck words sharing two distinct one-deletion reads, the number of pairwise-confusable triangles satisfies the exact recurrence \(T_{n+1}-T_n=2E_n\), where \(E_n\) is the edge count. A canonical positive-height splice bijection gives \(T_n=2\sum_{k=2}^{n-1}(k-1)\binom{2(n-k)+1}{n-k-1}\), so the mean number of triangles incident to an edge tends to two.
