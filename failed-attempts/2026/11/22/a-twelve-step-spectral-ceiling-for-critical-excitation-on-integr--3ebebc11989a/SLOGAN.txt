At the exact half-degree activation threshold, three-state excitation on any finite integral even-regular graph has recurrent periods dividing \(12\), and the bound is attained by \(C_3\sqcup C_4\). If its adjacency spectrum misses the three values \(-r,0,r\) for degree \(2r\), every trajectory dies out, including on the triangle-free Gewirtz and Higman–Sims graphs.
