# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

On sites 0 through m+1, the two joint kernels of the future constraints and of the current-plus-future constraints have dimensions 2g_{m+1} and g_{m+2}. Finite-dimensional subspace inclusion–exclusion, which does not require the bond projectors to commute, gives the survival rank as their difference; the corner denominator is 2^m, including m=0. The invertible site-dependent changes of basis for entangled ψ send its adjacent constraints to antisymmetric constraints, whose common kernel is symmetric; for independent product factors the common change of one-site basis leaves just words avoiding 01. Both give g_L=L+1. In the equal-ray case a common unitary gives words avoiding 00, hence g_L=F_{L+2}. Global phase does not change that locus. Substitution gives both tails; their series are (1−z/2)^(−2) and (1−z/2−z²/4)^(−1), respectively. For each tail series S the mass PGF is 1+(z−1)S; S(1)=4 in both cases and S′(1)=8 and 16, respectively, giving variances 4 and 20. Both tails vanish and yield the displayed point masses. The jump at k=1 for an entangled perturbation of |00⟩ extends to every equal-ray point by uniform on-site unitary conjugation. The proof is for the lattice observable, not repeated detection.

## originality

FAIL

The full law is a mechanical consequence of published statements on the very same open-chain projectors and on the very same lattice-return projections, together with the elementary count of binary words avoiding a doubled letter. In particular, Bravyi–Gosset §2.1 is materially stronger than the generic-tree antecedent discussed in the frozen record: it already proves the dimensions for all nonparallel states, including singular independent-factor products, and singles out exactly the diagonal-product family. The Kac paper already proves equality in trace between the actual tail projections and the corresponding consecutive-join difference. This is coverage by implication, not a claim that either paper prints the two PGFs or that a failed search proves anything.

## value

FAIL

The object is natural and the formulas are a useful illustration of the framework, but this precise invariant is mechanically implied by the known lattice trace identity and exact same-chain dimension classification. The elementary exceptional word count and the normalization, generating functions, moments and discontinuity do not supply an independently worthwhile new exact invariant. No genuine surviving correction within the frozen scope was identified.
