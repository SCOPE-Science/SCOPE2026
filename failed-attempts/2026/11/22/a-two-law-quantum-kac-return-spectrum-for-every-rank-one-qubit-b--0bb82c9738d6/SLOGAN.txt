Every rank-one two-qubit bond projection in the tracial quantum Bernoulli shift has one of exactly two first-return spectral laws: a shifted negative-binomial law of variance \(4\), or a Fibonacci law of variance \(20\) when the forbidden vector is \(u\otimes u\). Both have mean \(4\), but their masses jump discontinuously at the parallel-product locus, even for norm-convergent local projections.
