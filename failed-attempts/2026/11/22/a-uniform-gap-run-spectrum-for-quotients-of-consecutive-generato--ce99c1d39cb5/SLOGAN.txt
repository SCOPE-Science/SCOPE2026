For every quotient \(\langle pk+1,pk+2\rangle/p\) with integers \(p\ge2\), \(k\ge1\), the maximal missing-integer runs have exact length-enumerator \(z^{\lceil p/2\rceil k}+p(z+\cdots+z^{k-1})\): a single initial long run, then exactly \(p\) occurrences of each shorter length. This completely determines the local gap spectrum, genus and Frobenius number in one uniform two-parameter law.
