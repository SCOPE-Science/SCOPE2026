A resonant nearest-neighbor quantum chain damped at one end has an exceptional point of any prescribed order \(m\ge2\) at a unique positive coupling profile: \(\gamma=m\beta\) and \(g_j^2=\beta^2j(2m-j)/([2(m-j)-1][2(m-j)+1])\). This supplies an explicit, physically positive realization for an all-order terminal-loss model whose escape-time law at exceptional points was already known.
