For fixed ambient d≥3, set ε=q−q_c and β=(d−1)/2. The common mean of the cosine- and sine-power Hankel tails gives A_d/ε+O_d(1) in both moments. Their mean-zero difference has a uniformly bounded primitive, so integration by parts and the O_d(r^(−2−βε)) error give a uniform O_d(1/R) difference tail including ε=0. At zero the integrands have order r^(d−1+kq). Xu's Theorem 3.9 proof, (3.62), gives strict cellwise positivity at q_c>2; its infinite-moment consequence (3.52) is not applied at q_c. The convergent partial sums over the zero cells therefore have a positive limit D_d. The mean-value identity applied to the interior exact formula (1.9) gives the factor (d−1)/d, A_d^(−1/d), and ε^(1/d); the varying exponent contributes exp(O_d(ε log ε))→1. All statements are for unnormalized area measure, q approaching from above, and fixed d. The supplied finite quadrature was inspected, not executed, and is not needed for the proof.
