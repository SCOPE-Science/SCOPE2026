For every fixed sphere dimension \(d\ge3\), the exact optimal radial--angular Fourier-extension stability gap vanishes at its lower admissible exponent \(q_c=2d/(d-1)\) at the sharp rate \(\Gamma_{d,q}\sim K_d(q-q_c)^{1/d}\), with an explicit positive coefficient given by a convergent difference of adjacent Bessel moments. The result quantifies a critical boundary rate not specified by the known exact interior gap formula.
