The geometric double-contact restriction is proportional to the square of the contact secant on C, so the admissible pencils are exactly the rank-one perturbations of the quadratic form of C by a nontangent line square. Its quadratic matrix determinant is affine in the perturbation, with nonzero slope by the dual nontangency criterion; excluding the repeated conic and singular parameters leaves q-2. In split contact coordinates the chord of u,v has coefficients (1,-u-v,uv) and dual tangency is (u+v)^2=4auv. Over the quadratic field the nonsplit contact coordinate has Frobenius rule t -> c/t on rational inputs; c lies in the base field by the Frobenius cocycle condition and norm-surjectivity allows scaling to c=1. Scaling/inverting t preserves a, so the normal-form parameter descends. Discriminant 16a(a-1) puts the reciprocal step pair in the split or norm-one torus; r cannot be +/-1. The shared tangents give no edge incident to the split contacts: any chord through a contact is tangent to the second conic only if it is the common tangent, which meets the first conic twice there.
