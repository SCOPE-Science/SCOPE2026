# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The Boolean idempotents recover the underlying points; singleton products recover every partial bijection, so all automorphisms are conjugations. The existence of each two-point bijection recovers equality of positive distances, not their names. On a cycle the image of the distance-one class must itself be a connected cycle; a step k generates the vertex group, and the edge isomorphism is forced by the image of 0 and of 1. The even antipodal class is a disconnected matching and n=3 has just one class. For n>=5 a wheel has two classes; the complement has maximum degree n-3 rather than n, so there is no class swap. The unique hub is fixed and the rim is dihedral. These structural arguments settle all sizes regardless of the supplied finite test bounds.

## originality

FAIL

The exact graph-specific formulas are not printed together in the sources checked, but their abstract monoid conclusions are routine consequences of published stronger descriptions, not merely similarities of titles or groups. In particular the proof of Jajcay et al. Corollary 6.5 recovers the underlying bijection from the monoid isomorphism, identifies rank-two restrictions and recovers the graph up to complement on that same bijection. The wheel metric monoid is PAut(W_n), so the wheel result specializes this proof with the degree obstruction to self-complementarity. The colored-graph monoid framework already has the same rank-two data as the general metric result. For cycles, the older dihedral-restriction lemma and spatiality reduce the answer to the ordinary permutation normalizer of D_{2n}; its rotation subgroup is characteristic and its normalizer is the usual cyclic holomorph. This is positive coverage by implication, not a failure based on retrieval limits.
