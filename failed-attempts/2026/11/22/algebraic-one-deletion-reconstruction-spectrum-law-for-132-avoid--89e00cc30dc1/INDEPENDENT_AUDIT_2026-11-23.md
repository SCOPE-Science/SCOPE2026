# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For deletions at i<j of values x<y, equality of outputs forces the values in the intervening shifted positions to be x,x+1,…,y: comparing the ranks after deleting x and y starts with rank x and uniquely forces the next value x+1, continuing until y at j. Complementing values gives the decreasing case. Conversely deletions in a consecutive monotone run agree, so deletion classes are the components of the bond path and |D|=n−b. In L n R for a 132-avoider, the only possible boundary bond touches n−1, which belongs to L unless L is empty. Starting-maximum and ending-maximum classes have the same weighted recurrence (removing the extreme-position maximum preserves avoidance, with the only added bond requiring the same endpoint condition). This yields E=z(1+A+(u−1)E) and A=E+z(A+(u−1)E)(1+A); elimination proves the stated quadratic and its unique zero-constant solution. At u=1, differentiating gives (1−z)/(2√(1−4z))−(1+z)/2; extraction and division by the Catalan total give the stated mean. In terms of w=u−1 the discriminant is 1−4z−2z(1−z)w+z²(1+z)²w². Its simple root near 1/4 has derivatives −3/32 and 49/512. For complex u near 1 there is exactly one root in a fixed disk extending past 1/4, by root separation, with nonvanishing square-root coefficient. Uniform transfer and quasi-powers with u=e^t therefore give the bond mean slope 3/8 and positive variance slope 17/128 with bounded variance error, and the CLT transfers by reflection to |D|. The singleton deletes to the empty permutation; n=0 is excluded.

## originality

FAIL

Kitaev–Remmel–Tiefenbruck already give the entire distribution under an equivalent marked-mesh statistic; the frozen claim that the related mesh literature covers only a different statistic misses this distributional equivalence. If Q(z,u)=Q_132^(0,0,2,0)(z,u), the earlier theorem gives zuQ²−[1+z(u−1)(1+z)]Q+1=0, Q=1+z+O(z²). Put A=uQ−u+(1−u)z. Substituting uQ=A+u+(u−1)z in the earlier equation multiplied by u yields the frozen quadratic exactly; uniqueness identifies the formal series. Thus b is equidistributed with 1+MMP(0,0,2,0) for each n≥2, giving the exact deletion-sphere law via the classical identity. The printed mean and Gaussian constants are direct calculations from that published generating function. No claim is made that the earlier text explicitly prints the same Gaussian limit.
