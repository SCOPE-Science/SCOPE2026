The retractions of every finite dicyclic group obey a coordinatewise odd-prime-power label law: the exact number of ordered commuting \(t\)-tuples is \(\prod_i[1+(2^t-1)r_i]\) when the trivial map is excluded. Their reduced operator-commutation graph is edgeless for one odd prime factor and has diameter exactly three for at least two, with precisely \(\prod_i r_i\) maximal cliques.
