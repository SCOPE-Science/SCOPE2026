The integer lift has the right colors modulo 3. Neighbor differences stay at most one because, when two heights differ by one, the lower vertex must increment. No vertex overshoots the multiple-of-three ceiling K, which an initial maximizer reaches in K-M steps and transmits to neighbors at speed one. First passage to K-2 at a lower site requires a neighbor already at that threshold. An all-zero word in the lifted dynamics has one common height by the Lipschitz property; it is K by monotonicity and the ceiling. This proves both finite bounds, including singleton and initial all-rest cases. Initial differences and the first color are independent by the modular bijection, so Donsker applies to the interpolated profile. In any uniform limit with a unique maximizer all points within vanishing height error cluster there; consequently the bounds converge to the same folded arcsine law.
