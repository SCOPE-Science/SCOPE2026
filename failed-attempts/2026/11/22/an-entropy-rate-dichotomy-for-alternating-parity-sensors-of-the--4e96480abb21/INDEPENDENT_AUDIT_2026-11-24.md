# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Lebesgue binary coding gives independent fair digits, and a finite binary linear observation block is uniform on a linear image, so its Shannon entropy in bits is exactly the binary row rank. For U the block spans the first N translates by s^2 of p and s q; in the (1,s) basis the two rows are (p_e,p_o) and (z q_o,q_e), z=s^2. The determinant is the even-power part of p q. Its vanishing is equivalent to p q = s r(s)^2, since p and q are nonzero and coefficients are binary. A nonzero determinant gives all 2N independent rows. In the rank-one case the bounded-degree nonzero relation supplies N-O(1) independent shifts and p alone supplies N independent rows. For V the first N translates by s of both sensors have rank between N and N+O(1) by the explicit gcd syzygy, with no use of an independence assumption between the two sensor streams. The two examples reduce to E_k and E_k XOR E_{k+1} for V or E_k and E_{k+1} XOR E_{k+2} for U. The E streams are iid; all output linear forms are distinct and nonzero, so every distinct pair is independent. The first N pairs recover all the underlying E's from 0 to N for V and from 0 to N+1 for U when N>=2, giving the listed entropies and deterministic three-bit relations. The supplied finite checker was read, not executed. An independent mask product count confirmed 72 degenerate and 889 full cases of degree at most four; the unbounded theorem is analytic. The U witness checker starts at N=2; the N=1 pair is immediate and does not satisfy the N+2 formula, consistent with the theorem's stated N>=2.

## originality

FAIL

The headline two-schedule entropy classification, including the finite-block conclusions of the general theorem, is a direct specialization of Giordano Bruno and Salce (2012), Definition 2.4, Lemma 2.9, Proposition 2.10 and Corollary 4.8. The isomorphism is at the level of precisely the trajectory dimensions defining entropy, not an analogy to stable inversion or an inference from correlation. Choose F=span(p,s q), phi=multiplication by s^2 for U, or F=span(p,q), phi=multiplication by s for V, and restrict to the invariant trajectory M generated by F. Every finite-dimensional subspace of M lies in some finite trajectory of F. Thus the supremum in the prior entropy definition equals H(phi,F), which is the limit of the reported block entropy divided by N. Prior Corollary 4.8 makes this the polynomial-module rank; Proposition 2.10 gives the eventual constant increments and O(1) remainder. With full rank two, nonincreasing increments and the upper bound 2 per pair force exact independence at every block length. Rank in the two-dimensional coordinates is the displayed determinant test, and the odd-square clause merely rewrites that determinant's vanishing. Tsuneda supplies the already-defined schedules, not the entropy calculation by autocorrelation. The concrete XOR examples need not appear verbatim in the earlier sources; they illustrate the rank theorem by a two-tap copy of one iid stream and do not establish a distinct new main theorem.

## value

FAIL

Contrasting pairwise correlations with joint uncertainty is a legitimate motivation, and the presentation is a useful worked example. As frozen, however, the classification instantiates the existing general trajectory-rank theorem without a new obstruction or invariant. The remaining examples simply duplicate an iid stream with a two-shift XOR output, and their finite ranks follow directly from that choice. They do not supply a separately needed nonroutine witness or precise new fact sufficient for a narrowed finding. No scientific repair is claimed.
