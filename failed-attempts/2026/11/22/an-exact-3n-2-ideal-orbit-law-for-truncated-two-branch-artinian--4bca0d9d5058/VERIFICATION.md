# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In the radical x kills everything, so an ideal is exactly a subspace stable under multiplication by y. Project onto the chain spanned by y through y^(N-1): the image is zero or the tail generated by y^s. The kernel lies in kx. If the kernel is kx one obtains K_s; if it is zero the graph map is determined by the x coefficient c on y^s and vanishes on the deeper tail. Zero image gives 0 and (x); the whole ring is separate. For any automorphism the y-linear coefficient of the image of x must vanish, since its square would contain a nonzero y^2 term. Cotangent invertibility forces u,a nonzero, and the relation xy=0 forces any y-tail in the image of x into ky^(N-1). The displayed images conversely satisfy the relations and give an invertible cotangent map, hence an automorphism. The K_s are invariant. The s=1 slope is affine with arbitrary translation; middle slopes transform by uc/a^s, separating zero and uniting all nonzero slopes. In the socle the pure y^(N-1) line is invariant, and the lines spanned by x+z y^(N-1) form an affine orbit under the socle shear, including (x). This accounts for the exception to projection-exponent invariance implicit in the proof, and works at N=3 and in characteristic two. The count is 2N-1 fixed ideals as listed before size coincidences, N-3 middle nonzero orbits and two affine orbits, totaling 3N-2. Summing sizes gives (N-1)q+N+2. At q=2 add the q-1 classes to the size-one count. Inspected verify_orbit.py and verify_output.txt without executing; outputs count only proper ideals and one adds the whole ring. These finite tests over small prime fields are not the arbitrary-field proof.
