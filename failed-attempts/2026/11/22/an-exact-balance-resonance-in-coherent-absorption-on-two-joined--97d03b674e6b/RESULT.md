# An exact balance resonance in coherent absorption on two joined cliques

## Finding
Let \(m,n\ge 3\) be integers. Join a unit-weight complete graph on \(m\) sites to one on \(n\) sites by a single positive-weight edge \(ab\) of conductance \(t>0\). Put a positive local irreversible absorber at an ordinary site \(w\ne a\) of the first clique. The coherent Hamiltonian is the weighted combinatorial graph Laplacian \(L\), and the no-capture evolution is generated by \(L-i\kappa |w\rangle\langle w|\), where \(\kappa>0\).

For every normalized initial state \(\psi\), the eventual capture probability is \(\langle\psi,P\psi\rangle\), where the orthogonal bright projector \(P\) has the following exact classification. Put \(X=V(K_m)\setminus\{w,a\}\), \(Y=V(K_n)\setminus\{b\}\), and define \(u_C=|C|^{-1/2}\sum_{v\in C}|v\rangle\) for each of the five classes \(C\in\{\{w\},X,\{a\},\{b\},Y\}\). Set \(P_S=\sum_C|u_C\rangle\langle u_C|\).

* If \(m\ne n\), then \(P=P_S\) and \(\operatorname{rank}P=5\). In particular both bridge endpoints \(a,b\) are captured with probability exactly one.
* If \(m=n\), define
  \[
  d=\frac1{m-2}\sum_{x\in X}|x\rangle-|a\rangle-|b\rangle+\frac1{m-1}\sum_{y\in Y}|y\rangle,
  \qquad Q=\langle d,d\rangle=2+\frac1{m-2}+\frac1{m-1}.
  \]
  Then \(P=P_S-|d\rangle\langle d|/Q\), \(\operatorname{rank}P=4\), and either bridge endpoint is captured with probability \(1-1/Q\), tending to \(1/2\) as \(m\to\infty\).

The entire classification is independent of both positive rates \(t\) and \(\kappa\). Consequently, **adding just one site to one clique changes capture from a bridge-localized excitation from a value approaching one half to exactly one**, for every positive bridge strength.

For completeness, when \(m\ne n\), the sitewise probabilities are \(1\) at \(w,a,b\), \(1/(m-2)\) on \(X\), and \(1/(n-1)\) on \(Y\). When \(m=n\), they are respectively \(1\) at \(w\), \(1/(m-2)-1/((m-2)^2Q)\) on \(X\), \(1-1/Q\) at either bridge endpoint, and \(1/(m-1)-1/((m-1)^2Q)\) on \(Y\). A maximally mixed initial state therefore has captured fraction \(5/(m+n)\) off balance and \(4/(2m)\) at balance.

## Assumptions and scope
Each internal clique edge has hopping conductance one and the only interclique edge has conductance \(t>0\). Graph size parameters are integers \(m,n\ge3\). The trap is strictly off the bridge in the first clique, and the loss rate \(\kappa>0\) is fixed. The quantum walk uses the *combinatorial Laplacian*, not the adjacency Hamiltonian. Eventual absorption refers to infinite time with no separate spontaneous loss or dephasing. This is a finite one-excitation model; the initial state may be any complex normalized vector.

## Proof
**1. The absorbing subspace.** Let \(B=L-i\kappa|w\rangle\langle w|\). Since \(L\) is real symmetric, the norm of \(e^{-iBs}\psi\) is nonincreasing, with derivative \(-2\kappa|\langle w,e^{-iBs}\psi\rangle|^2\). An eigenvector of \(B\) with real eigenvalue must be an eigenvector of \(L\) orthogonal to \(|w\rangle\). Conversely every such vector is loss-free. The orthogonal complement of the span of all those eigenvectors is the cyclic spectral subspace
\[
 \mathcal K=\operatorname{span}\{|w\rangle,L|w\rangle,L^2|w\rangle,\ldots\}.
\]
It is invariant under \(B\) and has no loss-free eigenvector. In finite dimension every eigenvalue of \(-iB\) restricted to it has strictly negative real part, so the norm decays to zero there. The dark complement evolves unitarily. Hence the eventual captured fraction from \(\psi\) is precisely \(\|P_{\mathcal K}\psi\|^2\), independent of \(\kappa>0\).

**2. The complete five-class quotient.** Every vector constant on each of the five listed vertex classes belongs to the invariant subspace \(S\) of dimension five. Acting on the class values in the order \((w,X,a,b,Y)\), the weighted Laplacian has quotient matrix
\[
M=\begin{pmatrix}
m-1 & -(m-2)&-1&0&0\\
-1&2&-1&0&0\\
-1&-(m-2)&m-1+t&-t&0\\
0&0&-t&n-1+t&-(n-1)\\
0&0&0&-1&1
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Let \(e_1=(1,0,0,0,0)^\top\). Direct expansion gives the **exact algebraic identity**
\[
\det[e_1,Me_1,M^2e_1,M^3e_1,M^4e_1]=-mt^3(m-n).
\]
For \(m\ne n\) and \(t>0\), these five Krylov vectors span \(S\). Therefore the bright space is exactly \(S\) and its projector is \(P_S\).

**3. The balanced exceptional mode.** When \(m=n\) the determinant vanishes. Nevertheless the first four Krylov vectors are independent: successive powers first acquire support, respectively, at the trapped vertex, a vertex adjacent to it, the opposite bridge endpoint, and a nonbridge vertex of the far clique (the latter occurs in the third power with a nonzero coefficient proportional to \(t\)). Thus \(\dim\mathcal K=4\). The vector \(d\) displayed in the Finding lies in \(S\), is orthogonal to \(|w\rangle\), and substitution into the quotient matrix shows \(Ld=md\), for **every** \(t>0\). As \(S\ominus\mathcal K\) has dimension one, it is spanned by \(d\). This gives \(P=P_S-|d\rangle\langle d|/Q\).

**4. Absorption formulas.** For a localized vertex \(|v\rangle\), the probability is \(\langle v,Pv\rangle\). Evaluating the class-constant projector and subtracting the rank-one dark projector in the balanced case gives every stated expression. For a maximally mixed initial state the fraction is \(\operatorname{rank}(P)/(m+n)\). All results are exact finite-size identities.

## Verification
The standalone standard-library program `verify_capture.py` constructs each full weighted Laplacian (not the five-class quotient), computes the exact rational Krylov projector by Gaussian elimination, and checks rank and every site's capture fraction against the theorem. Its 100 checked parameter combinations have \(3\le m,n\le7\) and \(t\in\{1/3,1,2,7/2\}\). It reports all tests passed. Separately, symbolic matrix algebra applied directly to the displayed quotient gives \(-mt^3(m-n)\) for the Krylov determinant and verifies \((M-mI)d=0\) when \(n=m\). These finite tests corroborate, but do not replace, the all-size symbolic proof.

## Relationship to prior work
Novo, Chakraborty, Mohseni, Neven and Omar introduce the general Krylov-subspace methodology and trapping projector for coherent graph transport; that methodology is used here, not claimed as new. Razzoli, Paris and Bordone derive four-dimensional transport and explicit sitewise absorption for **equal-size**, unit-bridge joined cliques. In particular, their balanced bridge-efficiency expression agrees with \(1-1/Q\) when \(t=1\). Duda and Wong analyze **equal-size weighted** barbell graphs for *unitary spatial search with an oracle*, not lossy capture of a bridge-localized excitation. The additional result here is the all-integer **unequal-size** classification, the universal five-dimensional cyclic subspace, and the exact balance-only rank-one trapping resonance for all positive bridge weights.

## Limitations
Changing the walk Hamiltonian to adjacency rather than the Laplacian can change the spectrum and these conclusions. At \(t=0\) the graph disconnects and the theorem's positive-bridge conclusion does not apply; the infinite-time capture probability can be discontinuous at zero bridge strength. The theorem does not estimate finite-time transfer or absorption times, which can grow as \(t\downarrow0\). The historical-priority claim is limited to the examined sources and searches; it is not an independent verification of priority.
