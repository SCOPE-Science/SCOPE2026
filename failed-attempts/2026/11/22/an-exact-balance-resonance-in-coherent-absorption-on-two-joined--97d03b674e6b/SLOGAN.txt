For a coherent Laplacian walker on two cliques joined by one positive-weight bridge, absorption at an ordinary vertex captures an excitation initially at either bridge endpoint with probability exactly one whenever the clique sizes differ. When the cliques have equal size \(m\), the same probability is only \(1-[2+1/(m-2)+1/(m-1)]^{-1}\), approaching one half; the sharp resonance is independent of bridge strength and absorber rate.
