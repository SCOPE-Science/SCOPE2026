For a uniform point in a regular \(d\)-simplex, the fraction of centroid-to-vertex Thales balls covering it converges to \(e^{-2}\), and the centered covering count has Gaussian fluctuations of variance \(e^{-2}(1-e^{-2})\) per vertex. The binomial-looking variance arises from an exact cancellation between exponential exceedances and the common quadratic random threshold, not from independence.
