In a payoff-feedback replicator game, every fixed-budget schedule of favorable-contrast regimes maximizes final cooperation by applying regimes in increasing order of the exact index \(\kappa=(R-T-S+P)/(S-P)\); reversing that order minimizes it for every nonfixed interior starting fraction. Two positive-payoff interventions with identical exposure budgets can consequently send the same population toward full cooperation or toward \(1/2\) cooperation solely by reversing their order.
