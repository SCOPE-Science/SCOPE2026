Every \(q\)-ary de Bruijn circle of order \(n\ge2\) produces exactly \((q-1)q^{n-1}\) distinct necklaces under one deletion: deleting from different cyclic runs never gives the same necklace. More strongly, the complete probability spectrum under a uniformly chosen deletion position is independent of which de Bruijn circle is used, with exact counts at probabilities \(\ell/q^n\).
