For four or more periodic commuter departures with independent missed departures and at least one guaranteed departure, the unique timetable minimizing uniform-arrival waiting has each headway proportional to the sum of the adjacent service reliabilities transformed by \(p\mapsto p/(2-p)\). The exact minimum \(T/[2\sum_i p_i/(2-p_i)]\) does not depend on service order, and every deviation carries an explicit positive quadratic waiting-cost penalty.
