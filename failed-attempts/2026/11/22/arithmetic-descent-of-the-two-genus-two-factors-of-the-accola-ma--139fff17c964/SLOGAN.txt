At the first prime split completely in the fifth cyclotomic field, the genus-four Accola–Maclachlan Jacobian over \(\mathbb F_{11}\) splits up to isogeny into two nonisogenous simple ordinary genus-two Jacobians with explicit quartic Frobenius polynomials. Those factors become isomorphic as principally polarized abelian surfaces exactly after the quadratic extension to \(\mathbb F_{121}\).
