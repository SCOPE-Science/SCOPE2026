# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For each fixed exterior degree in A=Lambda(x_i,z_i,w_i), the differential is the two-operator cochain Koszul differential for E12 and E13. The end cohomologies are their common kernel and their joint coinvariants, and the middle dimension is the sum of the end dimensions by Euler characteristic. In a GL3 summand of highest weight (a,b,c) the kernel is stable under the lower-right GL2; each irreducible constituent's GL2 highest line would also be GL3 highest, so there is exactly one constituent of weight (b,c). Coinvariants are dual to the common kernel on the dual representation of weight (-c,-b,-a), hence have dimension a-b+1. The middle dimension is a-c+2. Skew Cauchy gives multiplicity dim S_(a,b,c)'(C^n), which dual Jacobi–Trudi at n ones gives as the displayed determinant, including zero binomial entries and nonnegativity. Degree shifts and scalar extension are valid, and Nomizu with the rational graph structure gives the claimed Betti numbers. The independent finite calculations agreed with the vectors and totals. Standard parabolic Kostant theory also reproduces the exact formula term for term, as detailed under originality.

## originality

FAIL

The exact contribution is covered by the stronger Kostant parabolic nilradical cohomology theorem. The complete bipartite graph Lie algebra is itself the nilradical of the upper triangular three-block parabolic of sizes (2,1,n) in sl_(n+3). Under the usual shuffle indexing, Kostant's full graded character gives the frozen coefficients and determinant term by term, with no further rank or multiplicity problem. No verbatim printed K_{2,n} instance is required to establish this implication.

## value

FAIL

Complete bipartite graph nilmanifolds are natural, and the short independent proof may have pedagogical value. As a research finding, however, the exact rational invariant is mechanically given by standard parabolic Lie algebra cohomology, with the same number and indexing of positive summands and the same multiplicities. The determinant notation and diagnostic checks do not create new cohomological information or a new computational reduction over the published theorem.
