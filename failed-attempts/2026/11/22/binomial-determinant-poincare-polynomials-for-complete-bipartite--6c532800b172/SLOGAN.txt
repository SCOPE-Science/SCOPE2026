For every complete bipartite graph \(K_{2,n}\) with \(n\ge2\), the entire rational Poincare polynomial of its compact graph nilmanifold is given by a positive sum of \(3\times3\) binomial determinants. The formula replaces exponentially sized cochain matrices by \(O(n^3)\) explicit terms and begins with total Betti numbers \(116,728,4792,32568\).
