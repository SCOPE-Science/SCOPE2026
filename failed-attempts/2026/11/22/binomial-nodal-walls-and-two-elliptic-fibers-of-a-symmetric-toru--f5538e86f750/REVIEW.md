# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

At a projective point, dependence of the gradients of G and H forces every nonzero coordinate to be equal, so G cannot vanish there. Thus the smooth degree-(2,3) base complete intersection has local equation u^3+tv^2, including exactly six reduced base points in the plane. Off the base, substituting t=-G^3/H^2 gives Xi(H-GXi)=0; a support of size k therefore gives precisely t=-k. The homogeneous Hessian has radial kernel, eigenvalue -6k^2 on the supported sum-zero tangent space and +6k^2 in the unused coordinates, also when either space is zero. In the plane all singularities are isolated so the sextic is reduced. Different components cannot intersect at a cusp (one branch), and could intersect only transversely at the at most three nodes; a decomposition of degrees d and 6-d would require d(6-d) >= 5 intersections by Bezout. This provides an independent integrality argument. A factorization in higher dimension would restrict to a nonzero positive-degree factorization on the coordinate plane. The genera are 10-6 minus the node count, giving 4,1,1,3. Two genus-one fibers means two distinct parameter values, not a claim of different j-invariants.

## originality

FAIL

Not a verbatim duplicate, but the exact new-content claim is a direct consequence of previously enumerated simple tensor eigenvectors, with standard local and plane-curve consequences. The diagonal tensor of order 3 with unit coefficients has vector map x -> (Xi^2), so its projective eigenvectors directly determine the entire outer discriminant of the pencil, not merely its total number of points. On G=1 the finite nonzero pencil is 1+tH^2: the only possible critical points are the known indicator directions, H^2=1/k there, and the critical scheme is locally the simple eigenvector scheme. There is no eigenvector at G=0, which also gives base transversality; the cusps, integrality and genera are then forced as in the implication check.

## value

FAIL

The symmetric pencil is natural and the short correct calculation can be useful exposition. However, the proposed exact invariant is already mechanically determined by the classified simple diagonal tensor eigenvectors and elementary local and plane-curve facts. No further independent invariant of the exceptional fibers, such as their moduli, is established; naming the two forced genus-one parameters is not a distinct scientific contribution.
