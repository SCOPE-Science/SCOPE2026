In the symmetric torus-type pencil \(V((\sum X_i^2)^3+t(\sum X_i^3)^2)\), the only additional singularities occur at the exact walls \(t=-k\), with \(\binom nk\) ordinary nodes and a persistent smooth cusp locus. The plane sextic members are all integral and have exactly two elliptic normalizations, at \(t=-1\) and \(t=-2\).
