For the six-element semiring of Jiao, scalar free algebras are exactly parametrized by labelled bipartite graphs with a disjoint marked linear-variable set, giving \(f(r)=\sum_{\ell=0}^r\binom r\ell B_{r-\ell}\). Against the already understood full-matrix normal forms, their logarithmic free-spectrum growth has the sharp ratio \(1:4\).
