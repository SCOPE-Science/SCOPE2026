# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

This is a correct fixed-m asymptotic for full idempotents. For a rooted fiber of s blocks, the r image points are fixed, so the r-point fiber rank weight is binom(n,r) r^(sn−r), independent of choices at other roots given the block map. Writing t=r log n/n, the normalized log weight has t-dependent term s log t−t plus the two corrections of (6). The latter are bounded globally by 1/e+log 2 and uniformly vanish on compact positive t-intervals (the binomial term is bounded by binary entropy). Because s log t−t tends to minus infinity at both endpoints and has a nondegenerate unique maximum at s, an integer nearest sn/log n yields the matching lower bound; outside any fixed neighborhood the weights have a strict n-scale gap. At most n summands do not affect this scale. Subtracting the logarithmic normalizer yields (1). For fixed k, strict discrete convexity moves one unit from the smaller of any two nonsingleton parts to the larger and uniquely leaves profile (m−k+1,1,…,1); the finite number of possible maps gives (2) and then (3). Rank concentration holds for each fixed block map and hence for the finite unconditional mixture. Label symmetry gives exact conditional uniformity of the unique root. Formula (1) follows by subtraction of logarithms, not literal division of equations (9) and (10). The supplied checker was inspected, not executed; the finite checks do not justify the asymptotics.
