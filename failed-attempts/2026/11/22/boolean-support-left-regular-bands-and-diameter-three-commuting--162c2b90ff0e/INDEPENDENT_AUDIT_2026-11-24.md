Every abstract endomorphism sends the additive Sylow p-subgroup into itself, inducing an additive map T there and a power map h on the cyclic quotient. From conjugation T(ab)=h(a)T(b), so if T is nonzero then T(1)≠0 and T(a)=h(a)T(1) for a≠0. This gives injectivity on the kernel and quotient, hence an automorphism, of which only the identity is idempotent. If T=0, the complement cocycle satisfies (1−h(a))c(b)=(1−h(b))c(a); when h is nontrivial this forces a unique scalar parameter u, and trivial h gives the zero map by coprimality. Substitution multiplies the two quotient exponents and retains the outer map's parameter; idempotent exponents are exactly CRT zero/one choices. The image of each proper nonzero map is the corresponding cyclic subgroup conjugated by translation by u.
