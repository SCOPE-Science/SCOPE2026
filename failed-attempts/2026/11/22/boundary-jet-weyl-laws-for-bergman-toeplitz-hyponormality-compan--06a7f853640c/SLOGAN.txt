The normalized Jacobi operator controlling Bergman Toeplitz hyponormality has a compact correction whose entire singular-value decay is determined by its first surviving radial boundary jet. For the natural power symbol \(g(t)=t^{s/2}\), a unique resonance at \(s=1/2\) improves the sharp decay from \(N^{-1}\) to \(N^{-2}\) and moves the Schatten threshold from \(p>1\) to \(p>1/2\).
