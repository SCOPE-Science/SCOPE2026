# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Each eligible chord must join opposite parities because its two open arcs must contain even numbers of vertices. Characters of the F_2 chord group diagonalize unnormalized convolution. Reflection pairs all noncentral root-partner classes. The central class exists only for odd n=2m+1 and has C_m^2 elements, so the parity recurrence, with C_1=1, characterizes odd Catalan numbers at the Mersenne orders; an odd sum of signs never vanishes. For even n each reflected pair contributes opposite signs to the proposed root-star frequency. For odd n, the two embedded copies of the smaller frequency are disjoint, reflected and away from the root. The noncentral cancellation is intact, and conditioning on the antipodal chord makes the contribution exactly W_m(S_m)^2, with root sign +1. Non-Mersenne odd orders recurse to a positive even order before reaching 1. At n=1 the usual two-vertex matching convention gives a single edge and two nonzero multipliers.
