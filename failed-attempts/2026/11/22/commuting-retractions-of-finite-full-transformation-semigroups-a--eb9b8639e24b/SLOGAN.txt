The commuting graph of composition-idempotent endomorphisms of the full transformation semigroup \(T_n\), for \(n\ge5\), has a complete rooted-fiber description and diameter four. Its triangle counts have the exact exponential generating function \(\exp(z\exp(z+z^2/2))-\exp(z\exp(z-z^2/2))\), and its maximal commuting families are counted by the same parity-weighted involution structure.
