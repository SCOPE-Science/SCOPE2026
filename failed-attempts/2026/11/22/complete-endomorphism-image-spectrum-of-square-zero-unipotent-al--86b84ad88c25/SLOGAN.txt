For the square-zero unipotent Alexander quandles \((a,b)\triangleright(c,e)=(a+b-e,b)\) on \(\mathbb F_p^d\times\mathbb F_p^d\), every set-endomorphism has an explicit linear-plus-arbitrary-function normal form, yielding the complete Stirling-number polynomial of image-cardinality multiplicities. Every nonautomorphism has image cardinality at most \(p^{2d-1}\), and the exact number attaining this sharp bound is determined.
