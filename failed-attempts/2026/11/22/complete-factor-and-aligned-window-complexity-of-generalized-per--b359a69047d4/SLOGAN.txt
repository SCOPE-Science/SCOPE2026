For every \(q\ge2\), the generalized period-doubling word has exactly \(P_q(n)=qn-\max\{2^{\lfloor\log_2n\rfloor-1},n-2^{\lfloor\log_2n\rfloor}\}\) factors of length \(n\ge2\), while the aligned-window count is \(W_q(n)=P_q(n/2^{\nu_2(n)})\). Thus every factor occurs at an aligned window precisely at odd lengths, but every dyadic window length has only \(q\) distinct windows.
