Every odd-characteristic genus-three hyperelliptic Jacobian has thirty geometrically natural maximal two-isogeny kernels beyond the 105 branch-pairing kernels; their complete Frobenius descent is classified for all twenty-two branch splitting patterns. A curve with one rational and one degree-seven branch orbit has exactly two rational non-pairing kernels, no rational pairing kernel, and no nonzero rational two-torsion; its thirty-point non-pairing kernel scheme has zeta function \(1/((1-T)^2(1-T^7)^4)\).
