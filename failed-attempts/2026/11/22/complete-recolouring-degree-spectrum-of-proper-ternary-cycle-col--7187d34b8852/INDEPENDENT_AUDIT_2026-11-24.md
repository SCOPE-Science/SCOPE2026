# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For three named colours, reading successive differences gives ±1 cyclic words of sum M≡0 (mod 3), with exactly three lifts per indexed word. A vertex admits exactly one move when its two neighbours agree, namely when the adjacent signs differ. Thus degree equals the even number of domain walls. At positive degree 2r, marking a positive run gives n choices of its first index and independent positive compositions of the two sign counts into r parts each, with r markings per word. The two constant words are available exactly for 3|n. Marking one movable vertex fixes two opposite signs in two ways; the closure filter on the other n−2 signs has (2^(n−2)+2(−1)^(n−2))/3 completions. Counting lifts and dividing by two proves the edge formula. Two alternating words attain degree n for even n. For odd n=2m+1, n−1 walls force M=±1; at n−3 the two admissible positive counts are m−1 and m+2, giving n m(m+1) colourings for m≥2. At n=3 all six are isolated. If 3 does not divide n, a positive count between 1 and n−1 satisfying closure exists, giving a degree-two word of two runs. All normalization and small cases agree.

## originality

FAIL

The complete degree census is an immediate congruence marginal of the earlier explicit joint periodic Ising count of magnetization and domain walls (Antal–Droz–Rácz, §IV.A, equation (30); equation (26) for zero walls). This is stronger enumerative coverage of precisely the data used by the claim, not merely an abstract encoding or a search resemblance. The translation to recolouring uses successive signed colour increments and three possible initial colours; it does not require a new counting result.

## value

FAIL

The degree spectrum is a natural mobility statistic and a useful recolouring interpretation. But the submitted exact census is a direct normalization and congruence restriction of a known joint exact count. The first moment and extrema are straightforward consequences of that same count; no independently new exact fact with mathematical utility survives the comparison. This is not a rejection of the proof or of the reconfiguration question.
