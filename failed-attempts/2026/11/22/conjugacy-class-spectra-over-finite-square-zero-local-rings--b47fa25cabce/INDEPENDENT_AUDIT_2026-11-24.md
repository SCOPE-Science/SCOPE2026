# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Invertibility is detected modulo the radical. A commuting residue partner of a nonscalar lift is a polynomial of degree at most one in the residue matrix and lifts by lifting the coefficients; the remaining choices form a 2d-dimensional kernel. For a scalar reduction the simultaneous commutant of the radical coefficients controls all lifts, with fiber size q^{4d}. A nonscalar 2×2 coefficient generates its two-dimensional commutant. Distinct such algebras meet only in scalars; a noncommuting pair has scalar common commutant. This gives the five scalar-lift rows by counting coefficient tuples and the three nonscalar rows by field conjugacy types, also when q=2 or d=1. Weighting by centralizer order divided by |G| gives the class count. The exceptional row contributes D/[q(q+1)] classes; its exact complement is q^{2d+2}−q^d, so dominance is asymptotic at fixed q, not asserted at every d≥2. The argument does not require a coefficient field. Independent bounded arithmetic (python3 /workspace/independent_arithmetic_checks.py) checked totals, weighted orbit counts and the complement for 48 parameter pairs. The frozen checker and its recorded five small-ring runs are corroboration only and were not executed.

The distinct indexed full-matrix-ring result proves the exact lift fibers and the identical tuple census, not just four numerical ring-centralizer sizes. The two records have the same displayed date; this is a coverage judgment, not a claim about order of discovery or publication on that day.
