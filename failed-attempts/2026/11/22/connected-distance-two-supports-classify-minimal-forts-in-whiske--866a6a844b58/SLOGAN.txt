For a tree with one pendant leaf attached at every vertex, inclusion-minimal zero-forcing forts are exactly the sets whose selected core vertices form a connected distance-two link graph. On the fully whiskered path with \(n\ge2\) core vertices, the complete minimal-fort polynomial is explicit and has exactly \(\lfloor(n+1)^2/4\rfloor\) terms counted with multiplicity, although the graph has exponentially many nonminimal forts.
