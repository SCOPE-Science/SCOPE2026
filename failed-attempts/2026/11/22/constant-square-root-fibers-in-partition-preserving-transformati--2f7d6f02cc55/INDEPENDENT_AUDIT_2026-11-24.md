# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Every square root fixes u and its nonroot vertices have depth one or two. Its induced map has the same depth bound with root block 0. For a prescribed admissible map, the k chosen nonroot direct preimages in C_0 are the only targets for remaining points of C_0 and for points mapping into C_0 but not directly to u. In each nonroot first-layer block, the h direct preimages of u are precisely the allowable destinations of all its incoming second-layer blocks; this gives the factor h^N_j with no duplication. Mapping whole first-layer blocks to u shows every admissible map occurs. Splitting by the first-layer indices gives the claimed support count, and the two-block factor follows by the binomial theorem. The uniqueness cases and all their complements follow from existence or impossibility of a block-preserving chain of length two on distinct vertices. An independent exact depth-color enumeration agreed fiber by fiber on ten profiles, including the seven-point four-block profile (2,1,2,2), with 10 fibers and 117 roots.

## originality

FAIL

The strongest distinct indexed result gives a multitype EGF for height-bounded forests with a prescribed block character. Its one-component term at height two gives precisely the claimed fiber, not just a loose superset. This is demonstrable coverage, without inferring historical priority from a shared index date.

FAIL

The prescribed fiber is a natural combinatorial invariant, but the claimed advance is a routine coefficient extraction from an existing generating function that already tracks exactly the requisite character and individual tree components. The support and uniqueness corollaries do not supply a separate substantive result.
