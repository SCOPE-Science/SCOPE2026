The negative Dirichlet Jacobi matrices have infinitely many exterior eigenvalues with a universal Coulomb accumulation law: their number above \(2+\varepsilon\) is asymptotic to \(s/(2\sqrt\varepsilon)\) for coefficient weights \((n+1)^{-s}\). Equivalently, the ordered exterior eigenvalue excess obeys \(\lambda_j-2\sim s^2/(4j^2)\), without determining the unresolved top-eigenvalue extremum.
