In a self-reinforcing finite-speed run–rest walk, the exact diffusive mean-square growth amplitude diverges as the memory exponent \(\theta\) approaches one, and the critical trajectory instead has \(\mathbb E X_t^2\sim C t\log(\lambda t)\), with an explicit positive coefficient \(C\). This identifies the natural marginally superdiffusive boundary left unresolved by the published power-law classification.
