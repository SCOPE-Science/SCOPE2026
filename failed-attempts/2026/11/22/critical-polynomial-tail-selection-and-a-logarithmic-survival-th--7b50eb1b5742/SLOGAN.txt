For an absorbed homogeneous birth–death walk started from a critical geometric–power tail \(m_i\propto\rho^i i^{-\beta}\), every \(\beta\le2\) still selects the minimal quasi-stationary law in total variation. Survival has the exact polynomial correction \(t^{-(\beta+1)/2}\) below \(\beta=2\) and the sharp logarithmic correction \(t^{-3/2}\log t\) at the boundary; these regimes extend the previously established \(\beta>2\) theory.
