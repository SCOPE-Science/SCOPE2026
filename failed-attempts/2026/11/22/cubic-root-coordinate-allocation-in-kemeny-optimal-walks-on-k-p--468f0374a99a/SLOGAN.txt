For every \(p,q\ge2\), the unique globally Kemeny-optimal symmetric walk on \(K_p\square K_q\) has two explicitly determined edge weights and no self-loops, governed by the cubic-root equation \(q t^3(t+2)=p(2t+1)\). When \(p=2\) and \(q\to\infty\), a globally fastest-mixing walk pays a sharp \(9/8\) Kemeny-cost ratio relative to this optimum.
