Two distinct subcritical generalized Cesàro averaging operators have an injective, quasinilpotent commutator whose singular values obey a sharp \(n^{-3}\) law with an explicit two-parameter circle-integral coefficient. This gives the exact Schatten cutoff \(p>1/3\) and a canonical logarithmic singular-trace limit, sharply stronger than the compactness of the individual operators.
