On the coefficient-weighted Hardy space with weights \(\exp(k^\theta)\), \(2/3<\theta<1\), a nontrivial Blaschke automorphism makes normalized monomial norms grow with two distinct, sharp positive stretched-exponential exponents \(n^\theta\) and \(n^{3\theta-2}\). The second coefficient is exactly \(\theta^3U^{3\theta-2}(U^2-1)/96\), where \(U=(1+\lambda)/(1-\lambda)\), and it quantifies a boundary-escape effect invisible in qualitative composition boundedness criteria.
