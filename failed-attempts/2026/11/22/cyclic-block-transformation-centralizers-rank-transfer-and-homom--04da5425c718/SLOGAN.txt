For partition-preserving transformations with cyclic block shifts on two-point blocks, every idempotent has an exact composition-centralizer rank polynomial controlled by cyclic binary autocorrelation, and the global commuting-pair census is a totient-weighted transfer formula. Its unindexed fingerprint first fails to separate conjugacy classes at five blocks, its shift-resolved fingerprint at six blocks, and an established strictly homometric pair produces an indexed non-dihedral collision at eight blocks.
