For the fixed Klein plane quartic in every odd characteristic \(p\ge5\), \(p\ne7\), its \(24\) ordinary flexes have four exact Frobenius cycle types determined solely by \(p\bmod7\), and their common splitting extension has degree \(\operatorname{ord}_7(p)\). Explicit cyclotomic integral coordinates and separation certificates establish the uniform reduction theorem; the characteristic-five case was previously known.
