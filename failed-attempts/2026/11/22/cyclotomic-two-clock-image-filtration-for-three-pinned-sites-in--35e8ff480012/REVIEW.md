## correctness

PASS

For the infinite bilateral map the character pullback is multiplication by P^n on Laurent polynomials and is injective. A compact image subgroup is determined by its annihilator; its index is 2 to the dimension of support-S multiples of P^n, and changing pinned bits only translates the coset. At a primitive cube root alpha, a binomial vanishes exactly for equal residues and the trinomial exactly for three distinct residues. Removing the common dyadic power of the differences leaves a simple root in either case: in the normalized trinomial the derivative is either a monomial or two distinct powers of alpha. Thus each eligible sparse sum has the asserted exact survival clock, including n equal to its threshold. In the equal-residue case the binomials span the even-parity plane until the shared minimum clock and only the unique maximum survives thereafter; all other cases have at most one relation. At time zero a pinned cylinder is proper. A surviving triple of clock h has span at least 2h, while an eligible pair of clock h has gap at least 3h. The scaled triple {0,h,2h} has all residues distinct and its trinomial is P^h, proving both sharpness and lack of any pairwise obstruction. An independent check used the invertible endpoint recurrence for the kernel of F^n: any length-2n seed extends uniquely to a bilateral kernel element, and the image codimension is 3 minus the rank of its evaluation on the pins.
