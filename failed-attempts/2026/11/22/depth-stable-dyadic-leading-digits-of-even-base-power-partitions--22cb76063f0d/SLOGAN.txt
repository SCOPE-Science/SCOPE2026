For every even base \(m\), the exact two-adic valuation of the ordinary \(m\)-ary partition number at index \(m^r-1\) is \(1+(r-1)(\nu_2(m)-1)\), for all \(r\ge2\). More generally, the leading binary digit of every normalized value at \(m^rn-1\) is independent of \(r\) and equals the parity of \(\sum_{j=1}^n j\,b_m(n-j)\).
