A weighted set on the two-sphere gives an exact Parseval combination of cubic-harmonic values and intrinsic gradients if and only if its degree-two and degree-four moments match spherical measure. At the twelve icosahedral vertices the separate value and gradient operators have complementary blind spaces of dimensions four and three, while the uniquely weighted mixture \(3S_0/7+4S_1/7\) is exactly the identity.
