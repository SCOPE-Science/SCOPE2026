For independent point clouds with persistent labels, the number of reciprocal-nearest-neighbor pairs shared across two replicates has an exact, distribution-free conditional law once the two matching sizes are known. On the uniform circle, all pairwise overlaps converge jointly to independent \(\operatorname{Poisson}(2/9)\) variables, independently of the Gaussian size fluctuations, even though pairs of overlaps sharing a replicate have positive finite-size covariance \(8n/[405(n-1)^2]\).
