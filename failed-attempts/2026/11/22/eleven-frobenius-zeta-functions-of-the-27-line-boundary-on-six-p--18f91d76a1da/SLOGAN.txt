The reduced union of the twenty-seven geometric lines on an Eckardt-free cubic surface obtained from a Frobenius-stable six-point blowup has eleven distinct zeta functions, one for each six-point Frobenius cycle type. Exactly the \((3,3)\) and \((6)\) types have no rational point on that union, while the \((2,2,2)\) type has six rational nodes lying on no individually rational component; an exact formula also counts points on the connected Picard torus.
