# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The two signs have identical histograms because P_{u,−1}(x)=P_{u+1,1}(x+1). The parameter discriminant for the positive sign is Δ=(u²+u−3)²−32; its quartic discriminant is −2¹⁴·7³ (from the two quadratic discriminants and their resultant). Thus in every admitted characteristic there is no triple specialization: translating any triple root to zero makes the cubic discriminant vanish to at least second order in the coefficient increments. At a zero of Δ the double and simple roots are distinct and individually rational. At a nonzero Δ, nonsquare discriminant is exactly the one-root case by Frobenius sign. The total incidence is q−2 since each x outside {0,ε} supplies one u. The monic squarefree quartic y²=Δ has two rational points at infinity, and its displayed maps X=2(T+y), Y=X(2u+1) identify its affine part with the stated Weierstrass curve away from X=0. The latter is smooth since its discriminant is −2¹⁸·7³. These facts give the four bins including characteristic three. The points (0,0) and (4,28) witness orders two and three; the nonzero quadratic discriminants 13±16√2 yield the stated even z_q count. Hasse's bound gives the claimed error.

## originality

FAIL

Mattarei–Pizzato (arXiv:2111.04166; Finite Fields Appl. 84 (2022), 102111) already give the exact rational cubic splitting-count procedure for coprime g,h with g monic cubic, including an exact formula in terms of the affine discriminant-curve point count. Take g=x²(x−ε)+1, h=x(x−ε), n=1. Lemmas 5 and 8 and the equality in the proof of Lemma 8 give all four bins after the substitutions A=2, B=z_q, C=0 and N_aff=H_q−2. This is verified stronger prior coverage, not rejection based on an inaccessible possibility. The chosen Weierstrass equation is an elementary coordinate change of that very discriminant curve, not a distinct counting theorem.
