Every endomorphism of the exponent-four core quandle \(Q_r\) has a unique binary-linear-and-fiberwise normal form, giving its complete image-cardinality polynomial. Its idempotent endomorphisms have images of cardinalities only \(4^k\) for \(0\leq k\leq r\), with an exact Gaussian-binomial census of every retraction stratum.
