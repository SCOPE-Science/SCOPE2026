# Every prescribed bracket fiber on odd graphical cycles

## Finding
Fix an odd integer \(n\ge3\) and a finite field \(k=\mathbb F_q\), where \(q\) is any prime power. Let \(L_n=k^V\oplus k^E\) be the two-step graphical Lie algebra of the cycle \(C_n\), with vertices \(V=\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z\) and oriented edges \(e_i=(i,i+1)\). The second summand is central, and the vertex basis brackets are \([v_i,v_{i+1}]=z_i\). For a prescribed central element \(z=\sum_{i\in V}c_i z_i\), put \(S=\{i:c_i\ne0\}\), and let \(V(S)\) be the set of incident vertices.

For each \(Z\subseteq V\setminus V(S)\), let \(U=V\setminus Z\). Among the edges of the induced graph \(C_n[U]\), contract all edges whose indices lie outside \(S\); retain the edges in \(S\) as inequality constraints between contracted vertices. Call the resulting graph \(H_{S,Z}\). Parallel edges may be replaced by single edges. If an edge in \(S\) becomes a loop, put \(P_{H_{S,Z}}(t)=0\); otherwise \(P\) is the ordinary chromatic polynomial of this graph. An empty graph has chromatic polynomial one.

Then the exact number of ordered Lie-algebra pairs with prescribed bracket \(z\) is
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Fib}_{n,q}(z)=\frac{q^{2n}}{(q-1)^{|S|}}\sum_{Z\subseteq V\setminus V(S)}(q-1)^{n-|Z|}P_{H_{S,Z}}(q+1).}
\]
This is a nonnegative integer, is strictly positive for every prescribed \(z\), and depends only on its set \(S\) of nonzero edge coordinates. In particular, on the full-support stratum \(S=E\) **all** \((q-1)^n\) prescribed central targets have exactly the same fiber:
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Fib}_{n,q}(z)=q^{2n}(q^n-q)\quad\text{when all }c_i\ne0.}
\]
The same prescribed-fiber counts hold for the group commutator word map on the class-two graphical group attached to \(C_n\), using its natural edge-coordinate identification. No statement of even-cycle support-only uniformity is made.

## Assumptions and scope
All fields of cardinality \(q=p^f\), including characteristic two and proper extensions, are allowed. The cycle has odd length at least three, with its vertex and central edge spaces each of dimension \(n\). Every bracket value is central. The orientation convention fixes signs but not fiber cardinalities. The graph \(H_{S,Z}\) is defined from the **full zero-edge relation on surviving vertices**, not merely the original subgraph on active edges. Its chromatic polynomial is evaluated at the integer \(q+1\).

For explicit coordinates write \(x=(a,\xi)\), \(y=(b,\eta)\), with \(a,b\in k^n\) and unrestricted central coordinates \(\xi,\eta\in k^n\). Set \(w_i=(a_i,b_i)\in k^2\). Then \([x,y]=z\) means precisely \(\det(w_i,w_{i+1})=c_i\) for every \(i\). This is a statement about a natural algebraic word map, not a result about graph colorings alone.

## Proof
**1. The complete support count.** For any assignment of vectors \((w_i)\), let \(Z=\{i:w_i=0\}\). Every edge whose prescribed determinant is nonzero has two nonzero endpoints, hence \(Z\subseteq V\setminus V(S)\). Every nonzero vector \(w_i\) determines one of the \(q+1\) projective directions in \(\mathbb P^1(k)\), with precisely \(q-1\) nonzero vector representatives for each direction. For an edge with both endpoints in \(U=V\setminus Z\), its determinant is zero exactly when the directions agree; its determinant is nonzero exactly when they differ. Contracting zero-labelled edges therefore leaves a proper coloring of \(H_{S,Z}\) by \(q+1\) colors, with zero choices if a nonzero-labelled edge becomes a loop. Exactly \((q-1)^{|U|}P_{H_{S,Z}}(q+1)\) vector assignments produce this exact zero/nonzero pattern and zero-vertex set. Summing over the mutually disjoint sets \(Z\) gives the numerator \(T(S)\) in the formula before division by \((q-1)^{|S|}\).

**2. Transitivity of fixed support targets except the complete cycle.** Rescaling each \(w_i\) by any \(t_i\in k^\times\) changes the determinant on edge \(i\) by \(t_it_{i+1}\). If \(S\subsetneq E\), then the graph on vertices \(V\) and active edges \(S\) is a forest. For any prescribed ratios \(r_i\in k^\times\), \(i\in S\), choose a root scale on each tree and recursively solve \(t_it_{i+1}=r_i\). All ratios can thus be attained, and all coefficient assignments with support \(S\) have equal fibers.

**3. The full odd-cycle case.** If \(S=E\), consider the edge scaling homomorphism \((k^\times)^n\to(k^\times)^n\), \((t_i)\mapsto(t_it_{i+1})\). Its image contains all tuples whose product around the cycle is a square: choose \(t_0\), solve successively for all \(t_i\), and the final closure equation is \(t_0^2=\prod_{i\text{ even}}r_i/\prod_{i\text{ odd}}r_i\), with indices taken from \(0\) to \(n-1\). For odd \(n\), this is the only obstruction; if \(q\) is even, squaring is bijective on \(k^\times\), so there is no obstruction. If \(q\) is odd, the scaling image has index exactly two. Applying any single \(g\in\mathrm{GL}_2(k)\) to all vectors changes every edge determinant by \(d=\det g\), and arbitrary \(d\in k^\times\) is possible. As \(n\) is odd, multiplying all \(n\) ratios by a nonsquare reverses their product square class. Hence scaling and simultaneous \(\mathrm{GL}_2\) transformations together act transitively on **all** nonzero target vectors, preserving their individual fiber sizes. Thus the \((q-1)^{|S|}\) targets of any fixed support \(S\) have exactly \(T(S)/(q-1)^{|S|}\) preimages in vertex coordinates.

**4. Closed full-support evaluation and positivity.** If every edge is nonzero, each \(w_i\) is nonzero and adjacent projective directions must differ. The number of proper colorings of a cycle of odd length \(n\) by \(q+1\) colors is \(q^n-q\). Each direction choice has \((q-1)^n\) vector lifts. Dividing by the \((q-1)^n\) nonzero edge-target tuples yields exactly \(q^n-q\) vertex-coordinate solutions for any full-support target. This is positive for \(q\ge2\).

For proper support \(S\), choose **all** vertex vectors nonzero. The graph \(H_{S,\varnothing}\) obtained by contracting zero-labelled edges in the cycle has vertices equal to the zero-edge connected components, and distinct components are joined only by active edges. If \(|S|\ge2\), this quotient has at most \(|S|\) vertices, and no active edge can turn into a loop (a zero-edge path connecting its endpoints would force \(|S|=1\)). It is a cycle when the active components remain cyclically arranged, or an edge when \(|S|=2\). Thus it is properly colorable using at most three colors; \(q+1\ge3\) is enough. If \(|S|=1\), the zero-edge subgraph is a spanning path and the active edge would be a loop upon contraction, so the all-nonzero term is zero. However choose one zero vertex outside the endpoints of that unique active edge: the surviving zero-edge paths cannot connect its endpoints, so the quotient consists of at least two components joined by the active edge, and is properly colorable. If \(S=\varnothing\), the all-zero vector assignment is allowed. Thus at least one term of the displayed finite sum is positive in every case. The unrestricted central coordinates of \(x\) and \(y\) supply the factor \(q^{2n}\), proving the formula and surjectivity.
## Verification
The standalone program `verify_odd_cycle_brackets.py` independently enumerates all pairs of vertex coordinate vectors over prime fields, directly computes each determinant on every edge, and compares **every** prescribed target fiber against an independent chromatic contraction sum. The full target checks passed for \((n,q)=(3,2),(3,3),(3,5),(5,2),(5,3),(7,2),(9,2)\). The recorded output is in `odd_cycle_checker_output.txt`. The field sizes and cycle lengths checked are finite; the proof above is characteristic-free and covers arbitrary prime powers.

## Relationship to prior work
Dani and Mainkar's graph-associated two-step nilpotent Lie constructions are classical and are not claimed as new. Mainkar's arXiv:1010.4005 studies isomorphism of these Lie algebras. Rossmann's open 2022 article, Sections 1–4, determines conjugacy and centralizer distributions for graphical groups, hence the zero bracket total, not the complete pointwise nonzero-target fiber law. The existing explicit **forest** theorem on prescribed Lie bracket fibers covers every acyclic graph, and its full proof explicitly excludes cycles. The existing **friendship graph** theorem gives fibers for bouquets of triangles, not the cyclic family of all odd lengths. Existing three-block matrix-radical commutator fiber formulas cover the four-cycle via its complete bipartite structure, not the odd cycles here. The precise novelty claimed is the complete support-only prescribed-target classification for every odd cycle, with the uniform full-support formula; neither chromatic-polynomial counting nor the graph-associated Lie algebra itself is claimed to be new.

## Limitations
Only odd simple cycles are covered. A full-support even cycle has an additional multiplicative edge-ratio invariant, so support alone does not generally determine its prescribed fibers. No claim is made about full-support even-cycle formulas, different graph families, or representation-theoretic consequences. Historical priority of any implicit equivalence in general bilinear-form word-map literature remains open. Independent mathematical review has not been performed.
