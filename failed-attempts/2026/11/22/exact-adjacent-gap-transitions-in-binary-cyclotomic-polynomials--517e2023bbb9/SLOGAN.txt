Every binary cyclotomic polynomial \(\Phi_{5q}\) with prime \(q>5\) has an exact, residue-class-dependent adjacent-gap transition law: all two-gap pairs and their multiplicities are explicitly classified by \(q\bmod5\). In particular, when \(q\equiv\pm2\pmod5\), gaps of lengths \(2\) and \(4\) cannot be neighbors of each other, a rigidity invisible in one-gap histograms.
