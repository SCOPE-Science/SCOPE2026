For every fixed odd squarefree \(m>1\), the *entire* gap-length histogram of \(\Phi_{mp}\) is exactly affine in \(\lfloor p/m\rfloor\) on each reduced residue class of primes \(p>m\), with explicit increments from cyclic coefficient support. For \(m=15\), all eight exact histogram formulas show that the set of possible gap lengths has precisely four residue-controlled shapes, a finer invariant than the known largest gap and total coefficient counts.
