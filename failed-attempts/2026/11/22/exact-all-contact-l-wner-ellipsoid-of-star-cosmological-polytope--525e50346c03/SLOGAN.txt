The star cosmological polytope with \(m\) leaves has an exact anisotropic Löwner ellipsoid touching all \(3m\) vertices, whose three squared-semiaxis classes admit closed formulas. Its volume relative to the minimum enclosing ball is \(\sqrt{m+1}\left(\frac{2\sqrt{m(m+1)}}{3m+1}\right)^m\sim e^{1/6}\sqrt m\left(\frac23\right)^m\), an exponential saving despite universal vertex contact.
