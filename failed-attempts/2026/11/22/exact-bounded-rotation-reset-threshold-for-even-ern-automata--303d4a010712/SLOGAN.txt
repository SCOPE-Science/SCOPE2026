In the standard \(2m\)-state Černý automaton, banning three consecutive cyclic rotations raises the shortest reset length from \((2m-1)^2\) to exactly \(6m^2-8m+3\), for every \(m\ge2\). An explicit alternating-parity block construction attains the sharp bound, giving an exact reset-latency penalty of \(2(m-1)^2\).
