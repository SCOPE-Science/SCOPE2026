Rank-weighted Kullback–Leibler denoising on a strictly positive finite probability simplex forms an exact time-additive semigroup: \(T_s\circ T_t=T_{s+t}\) for every fixed nonconstant descending rank-weight vector. A finite, explicitly computable maximum of centered prefix-sum ratios is the exact first time when every probability becomes \(1/n\), and the entire trajectory is isotonic projection of shifted log-probabilities followed by softmax.
