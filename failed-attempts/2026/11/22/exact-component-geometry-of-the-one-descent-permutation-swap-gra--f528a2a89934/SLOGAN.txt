For every \(n\ge3\), the adjacent-swap graph on permutations with exactly one descent consists of precisely \(n-1\) paths on \(n-1\) vertices, plus \(2^n-n^2+n-2\) isolated vertices. Thus a descent-preserving local-swap sampler has exponentially many absorbing states, although optimal routing within each nontrivial component is an explicit one-dimensional path metric.
