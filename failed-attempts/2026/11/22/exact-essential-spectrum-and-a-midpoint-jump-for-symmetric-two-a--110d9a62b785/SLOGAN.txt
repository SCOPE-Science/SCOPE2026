For the reflection-symmetric two-atom generalized Hilbert matrix on \(H^2\), every nonmidpoint parameter has the entire signed interval \([-2/\sqrt{t(1-t)},2/\sqrt{t(1-t)}]\) as its Fredholm essential spectrum, despite strictly positive entries. At the midpoint its spectrum becomes \([0,4]\), a sharp spectral sign discontinuity even though the operators vary strongly continuously.
