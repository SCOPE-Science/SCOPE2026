Uniform pairwise averaging on a star has an exact two-mode finite-horizon squared-error law: at every positive horizon the hardest inputs are leaf contrasts, and their sharp contraction factors form a strictly log-concave sequence. For stars with \(m\) leaves, the exact long-run mean-square factor is \((5(m-1)+\sqrt{9m^2-2m-7})/(8m)\), giving a sharp \(4/3\) asymptotic acceleration in logarithmic decay relative to the standard one-step spectral bound.
