For binary strings with exactly two ones, a single complete multiset of length-\(h\) substring compositions has exactly \(h^2+h-1\) possible outputs once \(n\ge3h-1\), with an explicit formula for every ambiguity-fiber size. The threshold is sharp, and for fixed \(h\), two independent random source words have identical readouts with probability \(1-12(h-1)/n+O_h(n^{-2})\).
