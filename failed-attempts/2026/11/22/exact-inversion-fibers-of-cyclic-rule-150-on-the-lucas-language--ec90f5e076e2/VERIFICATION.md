# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For indexed rings n≥3, the recurrence h[i+1]=h[i]+h[i−1] over F₂ gives a trivial kernel off multiples of three and exactly the three nonzero three-periodic differences otherwise. With difference (011)^m, each flipped adjacent pair must be unequal and each unflipped bit must be zero. Independent choices of 001 or 010 give 2^m endpoints, or 2^(m−1) unordered pairs. Two distinct differences force the sole triple over 1^n, and its fourth coset word is 1^n itself, inadmissible as a source. The Lucas trace counts source incidences, giving the quoted singleton and doubleton counts. Uniformity makes every posterior uniform on its fiber, so both error and entropy follow. For odd n the square-free quotient polynomial gives identical kernels for every positive time; for 6|n, (101000)^(n/6) maps to (101101)^(n/6), then to zero. This is not an all-time classification on even multiples of three. This is finite corroboration, not the proof. The frozen verifier was inspected, not executed.
