For binary words avoiding `11`, the entire distribution of distinct outputs after exactly one adjacent-pair tandem duplication has a cubic-denominator rational generating function. The sharp maximum is \(1+\lceil 2(n-2)/3\rceil\), with exactly \(1\), \(2\), or \((n+7)/3\) maximizers according to the length modulo three; exactly three sources have a single distinct output.
