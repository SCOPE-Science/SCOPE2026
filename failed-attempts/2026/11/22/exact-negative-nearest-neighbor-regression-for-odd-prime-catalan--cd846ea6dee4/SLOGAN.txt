Across every odd-prime-power block, the exponent of an odd prime in a Catalan number has an explicitly computable, strictly negative covariance with the change in that exponent at the next index, except at the single zero-covariance case \(p=3\), depth one. Two closed formulas quantify the effect for \(p=3\) and \(p\ge5\), with distinct prime-dependent limits.
