The determinant of the full bandlimited spherical-harmonic sampling matrix on a pole-plus-nested-odd-rings grid factors exactly into weighted latitude spacings, independently of all ring rotations. For the first nontrivial nine-sample one/three/five layout, the uniquely determinant-optimal latitude cosines are \((11+\sqrt{2305})/78\) and \((15-\sqrt{2305})/130\), across both possible ring orders.
