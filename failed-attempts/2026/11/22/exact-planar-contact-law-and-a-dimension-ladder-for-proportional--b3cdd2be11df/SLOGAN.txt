Synchronous proportional resetting of an isotropic Brownian particle has an exactly logarithmic stationary density at the origin in two dimensions, \(p_2(0)=\frac{r}{2\pi D}\log(1/|a|)\), and exact central-density ratios between every pair of dimensions separated by two. These identities yield a complete even/odd dimension hierarchy of sharp power-law and logarithmic singularities as the retained fraction tends to zero.
