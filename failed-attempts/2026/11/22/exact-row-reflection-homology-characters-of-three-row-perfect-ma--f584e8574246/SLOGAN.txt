For the perfect-matching complex of every \(3\times2n\) rectangular grid, interchange of the top and bottom rows has a reduced Lefschetz number repeating \(0,1,-1\) as \(n\) runs through residue classes modulo three. Combined with the known Fibonacci sphere-wedge classification, this gives exact rational reflection eigenspaces and shows that the row-reflection quotient first retains reduced homology at \(n=4\).
