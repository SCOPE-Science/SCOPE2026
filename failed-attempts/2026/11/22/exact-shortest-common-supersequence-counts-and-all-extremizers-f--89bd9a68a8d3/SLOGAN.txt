For every pair of opposing binary two-run inputs \(0^a1^b\) and \(1^c0^d\), a four-case binomial formula counts all distinct shortest common supersequences, including exact tie regimes. At fixed combined input length \(N\), equality in the previously known upper bound \(\binom{N-2}{\lfloor(N-2)/2\rfloor}\) occurs within this family exactly when \((a,c)=(1,1)\) with balanced \(b,d\), or \((b,d)=(1,1)\) with balanced \(a,c\).
