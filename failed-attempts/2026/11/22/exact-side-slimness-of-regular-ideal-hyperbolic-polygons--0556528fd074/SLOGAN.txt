The exact side slimness of a regular ideal hyperbolic \(n\)-gon of curvature \(-1\) is \(\operatorname{arsinh}(2\cos(\pi/n))\), attained uniquely at each side midpoint against its adjacent sides. This strictly increases to \(\operatorname{arsinh}2\), showing that growing numbers of sides in highly symmetric ideal polygons need not produce increasing-order polygonal slimness.
