The order-three geometric-mean Zbăganu constant of the real cube \(\ell_\infty^d\) is exactly \(1,2,1+\sqrt6/2,3\) as \(d\) passes through \(1,2,3,4\), and remains \(3\) thereafter. The exceptional three-dimensional value forces the sharp unequal radius ratio at every maximizer \((\sqrt6-2):1:1\), separating geometric from arithmetic signed-sum geometry.
