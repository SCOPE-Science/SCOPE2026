Among binary palindromes containing exactly four ones, every scattered length-three subsequence deck has at most two in-class preimages; the ambiguous pairs are exactly \(P(a,c+5d,c)\leftrightarrow P(a+d,c+d,c+6d)\) for \(a,c\ge0\), \(d\ge1\). Their exact generating series is \(z^{10}/((1-z^2)(1-z^3)(1-z^{10}))\) by zero count, and precisely \(2/15\) of long four-one palindromes are ambiguous in the limit.
