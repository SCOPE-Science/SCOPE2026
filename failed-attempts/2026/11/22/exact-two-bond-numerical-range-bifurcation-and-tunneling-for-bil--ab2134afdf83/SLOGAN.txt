For a bilateral weighted shift with two identical enhanced bonds separated by \(d\) ordinary bonds, the second positive outlier of its real part exists exactly when \(d(c^2-1)>2\), while its numerical range is always a closed disk with a strictly shrinking radius. Once both modes exist, their separation is asymptotic to \((c^2-1)^2/(2c^{d+3})\), giving an exact off-diagonal tunneling constant.
