For every pair of strictly contractive affine symbols on Gaussian Fock space, the products \(C_\varphi C_\psi^*\) and \(C_\psi^*C_\varphi\) have exact geometric singular-value sequences with explicitly computable and generally different leading constants. When the slopes coincide, the reverse order always has the larger Schatten quasi-norm; when they differ, a single indefinite Hermitian displacement form governs both possible strict orderings and equality, simultaneously for every Schatten exponent \(p>0\).
