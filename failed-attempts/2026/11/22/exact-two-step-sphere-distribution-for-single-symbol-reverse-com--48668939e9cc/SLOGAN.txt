Two successive single-letter reverse-complement duplications have exactly \(n(n+3)/2-\lambda(x)\) distinct outputs, where \(\lambda(x)\) weights each constant run whose letter complements its preceding run by that run's length. The complete \(q\)-ary sphere-size spectrum, including every nonmaximal size, has rational generating function \(qz(1-zt)/(1-(q-1)z-2zt+qz^2t)\).
