The product of two independent uniform nonidentity transvections in \(\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb F_q)\) occupies exactly \(q+3\) similarity classes for every \(n\ge4\), with a complete all-characteristic, class-by-class formula. Every nonzero trace-shift class has exactly the same probability, \(q^{2n-3}/|\mathcal T|\), and an explicit centralizer adjustment gives the factorization count of each prescribed matrix.
