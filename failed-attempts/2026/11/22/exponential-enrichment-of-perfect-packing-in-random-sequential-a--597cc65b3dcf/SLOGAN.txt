On an open two-row ladder with nearest-neighbor exclusion, perfect random-sequential-adsorption packings occur with a sharply defined exponentially small probability \(C\lambda^L\), where \(\lambda\) is the Perron eigenvalue of an explicit positivity-improving transfer operator. Against uniform sampling of all jammed configurations, the kinetic probability is nevertheless exponentially enhanced, with enrichment base \(\lambda\varphi>1\).
