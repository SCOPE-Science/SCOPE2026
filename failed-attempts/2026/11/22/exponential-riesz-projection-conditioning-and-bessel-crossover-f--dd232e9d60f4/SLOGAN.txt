For the compact generalized Cesàro matrix on \(\ell^2\), every Riesz-projection norm has an exact binomial formula and grows at a sharp exponential rate for any fixed positive coupling, even though all eigenvalues remain simple and unchanged. At coupling \(t_m\sim\tau/m\), the projection norm instead converges to \(I_0(2\tau)\), exposing a precise spectral-conditioning crossover and proving that the complete eigenvectors cannot form a Schauder basis for fixed \(t>0\).
