The compact generalized Cesàro operators \(C_t\) form a full singular-value **exterior-power order**: increasing \(t\) increases the product of the largest \(k\) singular values for every \(k\), with a strict operator-norm increase. Their exact ordered weak-trace coefficient is \(\chi(t)=2K(2\sqrt t/(1+t))/(\pi(1+t))\), diverging logarithmically as \(t\uparrow1\), even though all nonzero ordinary eigenvalues remain \(1/n\).
