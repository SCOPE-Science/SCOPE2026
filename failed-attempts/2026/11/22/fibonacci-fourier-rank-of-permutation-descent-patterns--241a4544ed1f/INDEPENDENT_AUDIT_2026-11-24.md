## correctness

PASS

Relative ranks of iid continuous labels are uniform. A maximal run of ℓ selected positions uses ℓ+1 labels; two distinct runs use disjoint labels because at least one position is unselected between them. This factors the normalized signed moment as a product of A_{ℓ+1}(−1)/(ℓ+1)!, including the trivial moment at the empty mask. The classical Eulerian exponential generating function at −1 is 1+tanh z, giving zero for positive even degrees and the indicated nonzero signed tangent numbers for odd degrees. The admissible masks are uniquely tiled by 0 and 11, so their count at length m is F_{m+1}, starting with one word at m=0 and m=1. At m=n−1 the real Walsh basis diagonalizes the operator, with the claimed rank and nullity, including n=2. Every singular value is the absolute value of a multiplier, at most the total kernel mass n!, attained at the trivial character. Binet gives the stated fraction. The supplied verifier, inspected but not run, is an exact bounded consistency check, not the proof.
