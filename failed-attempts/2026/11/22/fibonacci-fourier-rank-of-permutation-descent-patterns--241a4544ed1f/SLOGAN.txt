The Walsh transform of the full permutation descent-pattern counting measure factors over maximal runs of selected comparison positions; a coefficient survives exactly when every such run has even length. Consequently its convolution operator on \((\mathbb F_2)^{n-1}\) has exact rank \(F_n\) and nullity \(2^{n-1}-F_n\), with nonzero values given by products of tangent numbers.
