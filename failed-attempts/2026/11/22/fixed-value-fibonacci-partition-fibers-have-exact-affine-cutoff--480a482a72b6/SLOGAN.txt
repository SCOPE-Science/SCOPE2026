For every fixed number \(k\) of distinct-Fibonacci representations, the number of integers below the next Fibonacci cutoff having exactly \(k\) representations is eventually an affine function of the cutoff index, with an explicit finite-state polynomial formula and a positive logarithmic density coefficient. For \(k=12\) that exact count is \(44L-550\) at every cutoff \(0\le n<f_{L+1}\) with \(L\ge24\).
