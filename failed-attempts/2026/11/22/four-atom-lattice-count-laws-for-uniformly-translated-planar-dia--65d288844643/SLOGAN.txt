For a uniformly shifted square lattice, the number of points in a fixed-orientation \(\ell^1\) diamond of radius \(n+s\) has an explicit four-atom law for every integer \(n\ge1\). As the radius grows with fixed fractional phase, its centered and radius-scaled fluctuations converge to a centered sum of two Bernoulli variables, degenerating at integer and half-integer phases.
