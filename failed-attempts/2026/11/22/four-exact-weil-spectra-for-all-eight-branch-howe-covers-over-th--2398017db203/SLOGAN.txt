All 140 coefficient-marked genus-five Howe covers obtained by partitioning \(\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_7)\) into two branch quartets have exactly four Weil numerators, with frequencies \(84,14,14,28\). Exactly 84 become maximal with 120 points over \(\mathbb F_{49}\) and have supersingular, completely elliptic-split Jacobians; the remaining 56 have 88 points and Jacobian \(7\)-rank two.
