# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Bézout with the smooth conic gives anticanonical degree at least d for any other nonexceptional plane curve of degree d, so adjunction leaves just the fifteen pair lines, six exceptional curves and the conic root. Two intersecting (-1)-curves cannot belong to a support; with disjoint (-1)-curves the conic Schur pivot is -2 plus the number of chosen exceptionals. To justify descent precisely, for invariant support Gram M and ample k-divisor A, solve (-M)b=(A·C_i). The positive-definite matrix -M with nonpositive off-diagonal has strictly positive b for this strictly positive right side: taking the negative part would contradict positive definiteness; a zero coordinate would contradict the right side. The resulting P=A+sum b_iC_i is big nef, orthogonal exactly to the support, and adding its rational invariant component sum gives the desired negative part. Realizability requires sufficiently many closed points, not just one in each degree: on P¹ for q>=5 there are q+1>=6 rational points, (q²−q)/2>=3 degree-two and (q³−q)/3>=2 degree-three points, and at least one each of degrees 4,5,6 by irreducible-polynomial counts. Independent exact rational-pivot Gram enumeration confirms all eleven fixed-support and conic-containing profiles, all eleven cycle decompositions in the frozen JSON, the 39 group orbits and their sizes, and the three six-cycle supports (empty, conic only, all six exceptionals).
