A six-point conic blowup has eleven distinct Frobenius cases for its Zariski chambers: the rational-chamber counts are \(1,1,1,1,1,1\): \(1898\), \(2,1,1,1,1\): \(468\), \(2,2,1,1\): \(118\), \(2,2,2\): \(24\), \(3,1,1,1\): \(113\), \(3,3\): \(11\), \(4,1,1\): \(26\), \(4,2\): \(8\), \(5,1\): \(8\), \(3,2,1\): \(33\), \(6\): \(3\). The full label-action on the known \(1898\) geometric chambers has \(39\) orbits, and a six-cycle fixes only the nef, conic, and six-exceptional-curve chambers.
