# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For the stipulated anticanonical embedding, tritangent triples are precisely the distinct line triples whose sum is -K. The degree patterns in the plane blowup basis are (0,1,2), giving E_i+L_ij+Q_j with i≠j, and (1,1,1), giving perfect matchings. This description uses divisor classes, not distinctness of intersection points, and hence is unchanged by Eckardt concurrency. Frobenius just relabels. A fixed first-kind triple has both indices fixed; a fixed flag of the matching kind selects a fixed pair and a fixed matching on its complement. Rational triples cannot have exactly two individually rational lines because the remaining member would also be fixed. With no fixed centres and no two-cycles the only partitions of six are (6) and (3,3). Independent enumeration from sums of divisor classes, without using the supplied tritangent or matching lists, reproduces all orbit columns and all rational-plane compositions; it confirms one and three rational planes in those two cases and no rational flags. The zeta product counts finite configurations, not their geometric unions.

The numerical flagship is covered by exact indexed tables after a canonical equivariant identification: 135 line–plane flags are the 135 unordered adjacent line pairs, via the two residual lines. Published boundary data evaluate the permutations on those adjacent pairs and ordered endpoints for all eleven partitions, and the 25-class graph-Jacobian table gives the full plane and line spectra. Together they also mechanically give the rational-plane composition and the line-free boundary claim. This assessment is about demonstrated coverage, not the order of same-day entries.
