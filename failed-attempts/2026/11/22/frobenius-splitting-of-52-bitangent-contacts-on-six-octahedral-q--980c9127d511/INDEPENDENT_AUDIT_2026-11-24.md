# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For lambda outside F3 the equation is smooth: on XZ≠0 the X and Z derivatives require X^2=Z^2, and the Y derivative and either remaining derivative then force lambda=±1 (or give an impossibility); on XZ=0 no singular point lies on the curve. An independent F81/F9 quadratic-tower calculation parameterized lines as X=uY+vZ, Y=tZ, or Z=0. It used four evaluations to match normalized monic quartic sections with monic quadratic squares, and counted each contact quadratic's roots; this does not use the frozen coefficient/discriminant algorithm. The (hyperflex, split pair, nonsplit pair) counts over F9 and F81 are respectively (4,0,0)/(4,24,0), (4,0,0)/(4,12,12), and (4,0,12)/(4,24,0), for a=0,1,2 and both b. The geometric 28-line count makes this exhaustive. Uniqueness of a tangent ensures that contacts on different lines are disjoint. The numbers of fixed points of first and second Frobenius powers, together with splitting by the fourth, then give exactly the frozen cycles and Euler products.

## originality

FAIL

The earlier all-odd-characteristic Ciani census gives more than the line counts: it gives the complete contact quadratics as square roots of the restricted quartics. The BKS pencil is F3-linearly equivalent to the standard symmetric Ciani form with parameter t=-lambda/(1+lambda). The earlier formulas therefore imply the exact frozen cycles by elementary discriminant and norm computations. This is established prior implication, not a failure based on potential overlap or on the characteristic-at-least-five contact paper.
