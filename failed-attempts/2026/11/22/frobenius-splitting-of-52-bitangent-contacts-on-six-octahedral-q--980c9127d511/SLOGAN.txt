For all six non-Hermitian smooth \(\mathbb F_9\)-members of a \(\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbb F_3)\)-invariant quartic pencil, the 52 geometric bitangent contacts have exactly two arithmetic Frobenius types: \(1^4 2^{24}\) or \(1^4 2^{12}4^6\). All bitangent lines split over \(\mathbb F_{81}\), but some contacts require \(\mathbb F_{6561}\).
