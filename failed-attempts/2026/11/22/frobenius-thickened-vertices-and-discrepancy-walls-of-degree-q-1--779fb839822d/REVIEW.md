# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

FAIL

Finding 2 literally asserts that π is a smooth birational morphism. It is not smooth: the integral source has zero-dimensional fibers off V and fibers of dimension 2r−2>0 over V, whereas relative dimension of a smooth morphism is locally constant on the source. The proof establishes the intended statement that π is a resolution from a smooth source. With that wording the mathematical calculations check: projective reduction gives the alternating presentation; the pure-power derivatives give a saturated complete intersection; the smooth transverse base and Serre's criterion give normality; adjunction in the ambient blow-up gives coefficient 2r−q−2 and the single smooth exceptional divisor gives the stated walls. In the regular ambient local ring every term lies in the p-th Frobenius power of the maximal ideal, so the principal Fedder test fails, including at nonclosed vertex points. The radical plus GL/Sp count and scalar division give 868 and 28,314 in the two five-variable examples. The wording correction is identifiable but would not fix the independent originality and value failures.

## originality

FAIL

The advertised rank count is exactly the existing alternating-rank census divided by the scalar orbit size, and the full Jacobian scheme follows by a change of basis and the known gradient identity, not just a comparison of reduced supports. The remaining wall and purity tests specialize the general blow-up formula and Fedder criterion to this known homogeneous cone by reading off the multiplicity and the p-th powers in the terms. No separate nonroutine claim of originality is supported by putting these consequences together. This is not a finding that a single prior source already states the entire package verbatim, nor is it a rejection based on inaccessible text.

## value

FAIL

The family is natural and the crepant/non-F-pure example is a valid illustration, but the record does not isolate a substantive gap beyond routine consequences. The known gradient gives the scheme and all its listed invariants as pure-power complete-intersection formulas; the count is an existing table rescaled. The wall is just the degree versus transverse dimension rule for a smooth hypersurface cone, and purity failure is a termwise containment in a Frobenius power. The conjunction adds neither a finer local invariant nor a new obstruction requiring more than these direct applications. A wording repair alone would not leave an independently valuable new result.
