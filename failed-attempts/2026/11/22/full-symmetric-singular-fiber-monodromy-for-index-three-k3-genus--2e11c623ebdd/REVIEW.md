## originality

FAIL

The central symmetric-group claim follows from the established characteristic-zero sectional monodromy theorem, not just the general Harris proof technique or an analogy with Weierstrass K3s. In the full (2,3) triple family restrict F,G,H to be frames of a fixed general projective plane Λ in the space of cubics. The section C=Λ∩Δ is geometrically integral by Bertini and has degree 12. Its quadratic Veronese image is a nondegenerate integral curve of degree 24 in P⁵ (no conic contains C). Its generic hyperplane sections are conic sections of C and have S₂₄ monodromy by Kadets, Proposition 1.1. The frames with their fixed standard quadratic parametrization range over all smooth conics in Λ; the frame-to-conic map has geometrically connected fibers, the inverse image in GL₃ of the conic stabilizer PGL₂ in PGL₃. Therefore extension to the frame function field does not shrink the Galois group. Restricting to the open of smooth total surfaces and transverse nodal sections also does not change it, and a subfamily with S₂₄ forces S₂₄ in the full family. The stated lattice and exchanged pencils are prior, their indices follow from the lattice pairings, and the relative involution gives the same root cover for the companion. This is demonstrable implication by prior theorems, without asserting priority between same-day catalogue entries.
