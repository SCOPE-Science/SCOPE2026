# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The classical cycle independence-complex sphere/wedge formula gives simple connectivity already at n=8. Fixed barycentric supports for r^a exist precisely when one congruence-class orbit modulo gcd(a,n) is independent, equivalently gcd(a,n)>1. A proper rotation subgroup has a generator satisfying this; for the full cyclic subgroup the fixed elements generate the index-p subgroup at prime powers and the whole group otherwise. Vertex-axis reflections fix a vertex, and the standard even edge-axis reflection i↦1−i interchanges the independent pair {2,n−1} for n≥8. A subgroup containing any reflection is generated by reflections (a rotation is the product of the chosen reflection with another in the subgroup). The finite action satisfies Armstrong's hypotheses, so the ordinary geometric quotient has the claimed fundamental group. For a prime p≥11 the action is free, the fiber in the Borel model is a homotopy sphere S^(2m−1), and the odd order forces trivial action on its orientation. In the two-row integral spectral sequence, d_(2m) takes the upper generator to a multiple of u^m, necessarily nonzero since otherwise infinitely many bottom powers survive, contradicting finite dimensionality of the quotient. Its multiples remove all positive-filtration top-row terms and all bottom powers of exponent ≥m; the filtration-zero kernel is pZ. The remaining additive groups, multiplicative relations (by degree), and integral homology by the universal coefficient theorem are as stated, without identifying the homotopy type with a lens space. An independent signed simplex-orbit replay checked ∂²=0 and got mod-p homology dimension one in every degree 0 through d and zero higher at p=11,13,17, with mod-101 and mod-103 homology supported only in 0,d. It also checked fixed supports at n=8,9,12,16,18,20,25,27,30. The replay does not certify integral torsion exponents or cup products; those follow from the proof.
