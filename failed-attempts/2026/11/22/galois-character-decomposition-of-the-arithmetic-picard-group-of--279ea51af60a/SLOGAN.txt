The BHK counterexample quartic \(x^3y+xy^3+z^3w+zw^3=0\) has a rational arithmetic Picard Galois module \(\mathbf{1}^{\oplus9}\oplus\chi_i^{\oplus7}\oplus\chi_{\sqrt2}^{\oplus2}\oplus\chi_{\sqrt{-2}}^{\oplus2}\). Its Picard ranks over \(\mathbb Q\), the three quadratic subfields of \(\mathbb Q(\zeta_8)\), and the full cyclotomic field are exactly \(9,16,11,11,20\).
