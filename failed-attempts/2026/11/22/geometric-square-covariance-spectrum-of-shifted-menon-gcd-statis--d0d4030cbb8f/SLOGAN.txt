For the \(\varphi(p^k)\) shifted gcd observables on the units modulo \(p^k\), the covariance spectrum consists exactly of a single zero and explicit prime-power multiplicities of \(\varphi(p^k)j^2\). After scaling, its empirical law is within exactly \(1/\varphi(p^k)\) in total variation of the geometric distribution \(\sum_{j\ge1}(p-1)p^{-j}\delta_{j^2}\).
