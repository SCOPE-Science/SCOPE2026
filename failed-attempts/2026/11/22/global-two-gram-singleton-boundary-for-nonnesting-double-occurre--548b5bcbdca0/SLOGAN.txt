Among labeled nonnesting double-occurrence words on \(n\) letters, global reconstruction from all one- and two-gram multiplicities holds exactly for concatenations \(B_1B_1\cdots B_mB_m\) of doubled distinct-letter blocks, equivalently when the interlacement graph is a disjoint union of cliques. There are exactly \(n!2^{n-1}\) such words, while every other nonnesting word has an explicit distinct competitor with the same observation.
