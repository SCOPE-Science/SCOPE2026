For the quadratic energy \(Q=\int_0^1B(t)^2\,dt\) of a standard Brownian bridge, the unique globally Bayes-risk-optimal pair of noiseless deterministic observation sites is \(\bigl((21-\sqrt{41})/40,(19+\sqrt{41})/40\bigr)\), with exact mean-square risk \(1009/96000-287\sqrt{41}/288000\). The optimal estimator is nonlinear in the observed bridge values, and the theorem covers repeated and boundary sites without claiming adaptive or noisy-measurement optimality.
