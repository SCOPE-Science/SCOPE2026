For every multi-theta graph with \(m\ge3\) internally disjoint pole-to-pole paths, all of edge length at least two, its vertex-induced-forest complex is integrally homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{m-1}S^{N-m}\), where \(N\) is the number of graph vertices. The calculation uses an Alexander dual that collapses onto the \((m-3)\)-skeleton of a simplex and covers arbitrary, unequal arm subdivisions; the unsubdivided complete-bipartite special case was already known.
