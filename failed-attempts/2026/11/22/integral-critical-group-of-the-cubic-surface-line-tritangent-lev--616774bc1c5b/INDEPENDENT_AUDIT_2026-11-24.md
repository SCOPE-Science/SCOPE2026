# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

On the blow-up of six general points, the 27 line classes have plane degrees 0,1,2. Three distinct classes summing to −K have degree patterns (0,1,2) and (1,1,1). The exceptional coefficients in the two cases force, respectively, E_i+L_ij+Q_j for ordered i≠j, and three L_ab for a partition of the six indices into pairs. Each such effective anticanonical sum is the section by a plane. This gives the 45 tritangent triples, independent of the chosen smooth complex surface, including the possibility of concurrency of the lines in a triple. I built the incidence directly from the ordered indices and perfect matchings. On deleting a curve vertex and a plane vertex, independent FLINT integer Smith and determinant computations both gave 1^42,3^10,9^5,27^8,135^6 and 3^62·5^6. The determinant is nonzero, so the graph is connected; 135 edges and 72 vertices give cycle rank 64, and the exponent is 135. This is ordinary graph torsion, not torsion of a divisor group of the surface itself.

## originality

FAIL

The full claimed group is a mechanical consequence of the Hermitian polar-graph critical-group result of Pantangi–Sin, arXiv:1706.08175v3 (2018), after a unit-block Schur reduction and an elementary 5-primary splitting. A separate same-day Resultary result also states the exact group as its identity-fixed case. The disposition is based on the earlier theorem and the demonstrated implication, not on assigning priority from the same-day index.

## value

FAIL

The Levi graph is natural, and the correct decomposition is useful as reference data for chip-firing or Weyl actions. However, the isolated frozen invariant is completely determined by applying an existing polar-graph theorem through prime-local degree-block elimination. The frozen record supplies no further nonroutine structural or equivariant theorem; the mere recomputation of an implied invariant does not meet the value criterion.

The dated certificate retains the supplied scientific assessment, sources and limitations.
