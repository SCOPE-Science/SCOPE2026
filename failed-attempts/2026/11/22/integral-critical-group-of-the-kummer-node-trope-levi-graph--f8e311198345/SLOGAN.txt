The node–trope incidence graph of the classical Kummer quartic has exact graph Jacobian \((\mathbb Z/2)^4\oplus\mathbb Z/4\oplus(\mathbb Z/16)^4\oplus(\mathbb Z/32)^9\oplus\mathbb Z/96\), not determined by its known spectrum alone. Its order is \(3\cdot2^{72}\), exponent \(96\), and cycle rank \(65\).
