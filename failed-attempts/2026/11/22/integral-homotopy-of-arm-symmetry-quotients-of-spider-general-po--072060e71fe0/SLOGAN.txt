The geometric quotient of the geodesic general-position complex of an equal-arm spider by all arm permutations has integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^{\binom{L}{2}+L-1} S^1\vee\bigvee^{\binom{L-1}{r}}S^{r-1}\), for every \(r\ge3\), \(L\ge1\). Sorting and merging repeated-depth barycentric weights gives a strong deformation retraction of its join core to a simplex skeleton, proving torsion-freeness and a free fundamental group beyond the previously determined rational ranks.
