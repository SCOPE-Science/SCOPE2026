For every \(n\ge3\), the geodesic general-position complex of the three-row rook graph \(K_3\square K_n\) is integrally homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{2^n}S^{n-1}\vee\bigvee^n S^2\). A join of three-point sets supplies the exponential family of high spheres, while exactly one nullhomotopic triangle attachment per column supplies the two-spheres.
