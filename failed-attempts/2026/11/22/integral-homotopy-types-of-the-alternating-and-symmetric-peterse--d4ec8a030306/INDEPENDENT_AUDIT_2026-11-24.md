# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The claimed homotopy types are correct. Realization of the barycentric flag quotient is the ordinary orbit space: the rank sets distinguish subflags in each closed simplex, and rank-preserving identifications do not identify distinct points within that simplex. Thus the live unique-cofacet tests prove genuine elementary collapses. Independent enumeration reproduced the quotient f-vectors (14,58,94,60,12) for A5 and (10,32,47,30,6) for S5, with 100 and 62 free-face pairs and the 12 stated A5 triangle words. For A5, after contracting the tree, the claimed elimination order 4,1,6,7,10,3,8,5,9,2,11 successively has a singleton defining relator (using the preceding substitutions); the unused word x11 x2^{-1} becomes empty. A loop occurring once in a defining word can be changed by a free-group basis transformation into the loop filled by that disk, followed by a contraction and substitution into the other attaching maps. Alternatively, the verified words give trivial π1 and the 2-complex has χ=2, so Hurewicz and homological Whitehead give S² without assuming a homotopy version of arbitrary presentation moves. The S5 collapse ends at a point.

## originality

FAIL

Both advertised integral homotopy conclusions appear verbatim as mathematics in distinct earlier catalogued findings on the very same geometric realization quotients. The same-record search hit is excluded. The explicit topological free-face certificate differs from integral chain cancellation, but does not make the two already established types original.

The distinction between integral and rational quotient types is worthwhile in principle, but both integral types were already established. The independent free-face route is a useful corroboration (and exhibits collapsibility for the chosen S5 cell model), not a justified new classification; the exact counts depend on the specified reductions, and no separately motivated, checked new structural conclusion is advanced as the finding.
