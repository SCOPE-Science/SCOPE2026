The ordinary matching complex of the Petersen graph has sharply different quotient homotopy types under its even and full label-permutation symmetries: \(|\mathcal M(P)|/A_5\simeq S^2\) whereas \(|\mathcal M(P)|/S_5\simeq *\). Exact free-face collapses and attaching-word cancellations establish integral homotopy classifications beyond the previously known rational homology contrast.
