For every finite graph regarded as a one-dimensional simplicial complex and every tolerance \(t\ge2\), its tolerance complex is an integral wedge of spheres in degrees \(t\) and \(t+1\). The sphere counts depend only on the graph's component count and its number of independent \((t+2)\)-sets, and the lower integral homology carries the natural exterior-power representation of the graph-component augmentation lattice.
