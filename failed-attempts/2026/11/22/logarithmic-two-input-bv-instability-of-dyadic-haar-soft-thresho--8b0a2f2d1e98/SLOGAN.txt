Independent soft thresholding of all orthonormal Haar details is Euclidean-nonexpansive but has an **unbounded two-input variation modulus**: on \(2^d\) samples its sharp modulus is an explicit all-depth dyadic cut/mask maximum with \(\lfloor d/4\rfloor\le K_d\le2d\). Exact values begin \(1,3/2,2,21/8,3,117/32,4,597/128\) for depths one through eight, and every bound in the finite maximum is attainable by arbitrarily close inputs.
