For every \(n\ge5\), the monoid of identity-preserving endomorphisms of the full transformation monoid on \(n\) points has exact generating rank \(3+\lfloor n/2\rfloor+c_n-p(n)\), where \(c_n\) is the degree-\(n\) coefficient of \(\prod_{j\ge1}(1-z^j)^{-\lceil j/2\rceil}\). Its indispensable singular generators are classified by involution-decorated rooted fibers, with a sharp size-two boundary for essential retractions.
