The eighteen minimum dominating five-sets of the Pappus incidence graph reconstruct every graph edge as a pair of vertices that never co-occur in an optimum. Their vertex-membership concepts shatter exactly the nonedges and singletons, with polynomial \(1+18z+126z^2\), VC dimension \(2\), Littlestone dimension \(3\), and a shattering complex homotopy equivalent to a wedge of \(109\) circles.
