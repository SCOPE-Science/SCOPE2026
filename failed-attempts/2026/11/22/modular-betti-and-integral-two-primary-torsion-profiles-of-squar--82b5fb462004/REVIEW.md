## correctness

PASS

The displayed structure constants define the Lie ring on the free Z-module with the stated basis (not a free Lie algebra on generators). The alternating CE differential sends X_i to −T∧Y_i and is zero on the T-containing summand. Thus H^j splits into the degree-j kernel and the degree-(j−1) cokernel of the derivation. In characteristic two this derivation squares to zero and factors as a tensor differential; each pair contributes homology in exterior degrees 0 and 2, giving H_δ=(1+q²)^r(1+q)^s. The kernel dimensions are half the sum of the exterior and δ-homology dimensions, yielding (1). The torsion of the integral cokernel survives unchanged in CE cohomology, and the difference of rational and F₂ ranks counts exactly the even Smith factors. For the rational kernel one can also group by singly occupied pairs and sum the middle binomial coefficients of their Boolean lattices, giving the characteristic-zero profile cited in the record. Multiplication by (1+q)^s and a one-degree shift give (2), and Vandermonde gives (3). For r≥3 the three-singly-occupied-pair block already has a 3×3 minor of determinant ±2 on its isolated middle map, yielding a rank drop in that block. The r=3 and r=4 examples agree with the profiles. An independent W_{2,3}(5) test gave determinant −48 and ranks 6 and 9 in characteristics 2 and 3, as expected from its diagonal form; this is not a full-complex replay.
