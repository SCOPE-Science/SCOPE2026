For a finite loop-free digraph, the acyclic arc-set complex is naturally equivariantly contractible when an arc connects distinct strongly connected components; otherwise it is equivariantly homotopy equivalent to the unit sphere of the vertex permutation representation minus the component permutation representation. This determines every automorphism subgroup's geometric fixed-space sphere, ordinary quotient model, and integral orientation sign, refining the classical nonequivariant classification.
