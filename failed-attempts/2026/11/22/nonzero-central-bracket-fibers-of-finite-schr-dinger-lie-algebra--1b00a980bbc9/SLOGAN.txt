For every odd finite field, all nonzero central elements of a finite Schrödinger Lie algebra have exactly the same number \(q^{2n+1}[(q^{2n}-1)+(q+1)(q^2-1)(q^n-1)]\) of ordered bracket presentations. The extra presentations beyond the Heisenberg ideal arise precisely from nilpotent projective directions in \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\); the zero central fiber exceeds each nonzero central fiber by exactly \(q^{2n+2}(q^4+q^3-q)\).
