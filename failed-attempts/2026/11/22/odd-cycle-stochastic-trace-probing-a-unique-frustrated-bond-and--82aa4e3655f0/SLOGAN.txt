For every odd unweighted cycle with at least five vertices and every positive screened-Laplacian shift, the unique variance-optimal two-class Rademacher trace-probing partitions (up to class exchange) are the \(n\) almost-alternating signings with exactly one monochromatic edge. Their exact variance has an order-one positive parity-frustration correction \(4c_\infty^2r^2/(1+r^2)^2\) beyond the leading linear-in-size checkerboard density.
