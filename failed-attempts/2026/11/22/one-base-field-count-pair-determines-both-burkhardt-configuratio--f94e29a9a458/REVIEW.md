# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The geometric finite sets are, equivariantly, the lines in the symplectic F₃-space and the unordered pairs of complementary orthogonal nondegenerate 2-spaces. The explicit geometric automorphisms are defined with the primitive cube root already in k. With constant geometric group action, continuous procyclic cocycles are determined by arbitrary Frobenius images up to conjugacy, so all classes occur. Changing the Frobenius convention to the inverse does not affect cycles. Independently enumerated all projective symplectic bases with first column normalized modulo sign (40×27×8×3=25920), computed their faithful action on points, and partitioned by conjugation with every element. The 45 orthogonal decompositions were encoded by their eight projective points; the 45 distinct masks thus distinguish them, and each point belongs to nine. All twenty rows of frozen spectra.csv match. There are 15 fixed-count pairs, each selects one joint spectrum, with only (4,5), (11,12), (13,14), (17,18), (19,20) repeated; (0,0) has all cycles of length five. A d-cycle contributes d points over F_{q^n} iff d divides n, hence factor (1−T^d)^{-1} in the zeta product. Nothing about the full threefold zeta function is inferred.

## originality

FAIL

The decisive prior coverage is not the split zeta function or just a geometric action. In the proof of Hoffman–Weintraub Theorem 4.5 the exact two permutation characters, in ATLAS notation, are explicitly 1+15a+24a for the 40 objects and 1+20a+24a for the 45. These give the two columns of base-field counts by addition in the existing character table. Evaluating the same class functions on powers via the already catalogued power/class data, then applying elementary cycle inversion, gives both full cycle columns. Sorting the twenty rows by their first two counts and comparing the full columns yields exactly the claimed sufficiency and number of collisions. Thus both the exact census and its compression are a direct comparison of existing table data after standard descent, not a distinct exact invariant requiring new permutation-character computation. This finding is true; no claim is made that the same inverse-problem sentence is printed in the older work.

## value

FAIL

The inverse problem on two canonical schemes is naturally motivated, and the table is a convenient summary. But the frozen contribution is limited to the re-evaluation and grouping of already determined permutation-character/power data in this one fixed group. Once the existing two characters are credited, the exact finite compression needs routine table operations, with no genuinely unknown exact invariant or separate structural theorem established by the submitted claim. Reproducibility rediscovering the table is not additional mathematical value under the review standard.
