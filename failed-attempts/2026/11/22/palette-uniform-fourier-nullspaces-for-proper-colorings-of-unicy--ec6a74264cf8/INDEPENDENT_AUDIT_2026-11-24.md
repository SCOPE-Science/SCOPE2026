# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Diagonal translation forces vanishing at every unbalanced frequency. A tree obtained by deleting one cycle edge integrates arbitrary tree differences after choosing one root value, and forces the last difference by the unique cycle relation. On the dual side the free ambiguity is precisely a common cycle-character shift. Character filtering the cycle relation cancels the factor q from the root and leaves one sum of products of B(s), with bridge factors nonzero. In a cancelling q-term sum of powers of 1−q, divide by the smallest power and reduce modulo q−1. The number of terms attaining the minimum must equal q−1 (q≥3), and the remaining power cancels exactly when its exponent is one larger. The case of minimum zero would give c=1 and is excluded. Choosing the exceptional character, then dividing by the free q-shift, leaves Z_{q,c} choices per bridge assignment. The count of balanced characters is q^(N−1); subtracting proves the claimed rank and nullity. For c=qm+1, Z_{q,c} is (qm+1)/(m+1) times (qm)!/(m!)^q, so Stirling gives the stated fixed-q constant after division by q^(c−1). As a separate finite check I matched sixteen coefficients by inclusion–exclusion on edge equalities for resonant decorated cycles with q=6 and noncyclic q=8. The argument, not these examples, proves the universal quantifiers.
