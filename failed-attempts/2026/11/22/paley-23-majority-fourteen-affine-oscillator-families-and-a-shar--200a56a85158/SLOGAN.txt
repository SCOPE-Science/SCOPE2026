Strict-majority voting on the twenty-three-vertex Paley tournament has exactly \(3{,}542\) primitive four-cycle orbits in fourteen full affine symmetry families, with no other nonconstant attractors. Every state becomes periodic within a sharp ten updates, and exactly \(1{,}012\) exceptionally slow inputs enter consensus on that last update; the long-period resonance present at order nineteen does not persist.
