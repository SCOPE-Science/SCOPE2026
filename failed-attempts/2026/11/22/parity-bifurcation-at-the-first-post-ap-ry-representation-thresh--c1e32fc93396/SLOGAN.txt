At the first representation threshold beyond the known consecutive-triple Apéry regime, the entire low-multiplicity gap set has a sharp parity bifurcation: for odd \(a\) it is one interval, whereas for even \(a\) an interval is followed by exactly \(a/2\) isolated gaps. The full description holds for every integer \(a\ge3\) at \(p=\lfloor a/2\rfloor+1\).
