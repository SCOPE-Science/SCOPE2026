# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Length-three incidence walks give A^3=(s+t)A+H, with H all ones across the two shores. If s,t are odd, AH=0 and A^3=H has rank 2, so the zero-primary index is exactly four. In the other parities A^5=A^3 and E=A^4 is an idempotent with A^2E=E and A^3(I+E)=0. In the even-even case E projects onto the two shore-constant directions, and A^2(I+E)=A^2+E is nonzero at a noncollinear pair: a point has s(t+1) other collinear points, leaving s^2t noncollinear points. In mixed parity A^2(I+E)=AH is a nonzero one-shore block. Also E cannot vanish in mixed parity, since then A^2=AH and A^3=0, contrary to A^3=A+H being nonzero on nonincident pairs. Thus outside odd-odd the nilpotent part has index three and the one-primary part is nonempty. For a spatial J_j(a), the temporal characteristic polynomial is (z^2+az+1)^j. At each root lambda, u=lambda v and the eigencondition on v reduces to (A-aI)v=0, of dimension one on that block. This supplies the Jordan-size justification implicit in the proof's separability step: one size-2j block at 1 for a=0, or one size-j block at each primitive cubic root for a=1. Kernel dimensions of T^k-I use root multiplicity 2^nu2(k); scalar extension leaves ranks unchanged. Differences of spatial ranks recover the block counts and Möbius inversion gives the orbit counts. The largest nilpotent block gives temporal 2-power order eight in each branch, and the one-primary part adds the factor three exactly outside odd-odd. These arguments include thin quadrangles.
