Pure-degree spin-two spherical harmonics sampled at a single equatorial regular \((2\ell+1)\)-gon have an exact arithmetic nullspace: one zonal mode is invisible in every odd degree, and two additional signed-azimuth modes vanish precisely at the Pell degrees \(\ell=8,49,288,\ldots\). The resulting exact rank formula exposes infinitely many even-degree failures of otherwise square, alias-free equatorial sampling.
