For a rank-\(r\) complex bundle on \(S^{2k}\) with \(r\ge k\ge2\), a nonzero top Chern class forces the spectral depth of every matrix-amplified endomorphism section algebra to be exactly \(rm-k+1\), rather than the fiberwise maximum \(rm\). Every maximizer has one rank-\(k\) Riesz spectral bundle carrying the full Chern class and only trivial line bundles otherwise.
