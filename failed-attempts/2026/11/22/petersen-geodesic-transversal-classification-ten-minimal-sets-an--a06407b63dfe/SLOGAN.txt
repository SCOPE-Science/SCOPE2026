The Petersen graph has exactly ten inclusion-minimal geodesic transversals: five four-vertex stars and five six-vertex complements of stars in its \(KG(5,2)\) realization. Its complete transversal polynomial is \(5z^4+30z^5+80z^6+90z^7+45z^8+10z^9+z^{10}\), and all five optimal configurations become reconfigurable by single additions and deletions precisely when the capacity reaches seven vertices.
