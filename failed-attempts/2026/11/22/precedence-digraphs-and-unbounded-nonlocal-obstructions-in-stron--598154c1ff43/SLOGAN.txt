For every finite graph regarded as an unfilled one-dimensional complex, faces of its strong discrete-Morse matching complex are exactly the incidence sets whose explicit precedence digraph is acyclic. For forests this gives a concrete flag independence-complex model, while every unfilled cycle of length \(n\ge5\) has an inclusion-minimal forbidden face of size \(\lceil n/2\rceil\), showing arbitrarily nonlocal compatibility obstructions.
