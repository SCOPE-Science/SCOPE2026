Every prescribed nonzero commutator in a rank-one rectangular matrix sandwich semigroup has an explicit fiber count controlled by ordinary rank and the two annihilator tests \(AZ=0\), \(ZA=0\). The rank-two fibers split sharply into \(q^{2(m-1)(n-1)}|\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb F_q)|\) and \(q^{2(m-1)(n-1)}|\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb F_q)|\), with other rank-two target types unattainable.
