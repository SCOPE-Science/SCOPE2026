For a nonassociative three-dimensional pre-Lie algebra with irreducible quadratic left action, the Nijenhuis operators over \(\mathbb F_q\) form two rational affine three-spaces meeting on a line, yet their reduced geometric locus has four components, with two exchanged by Frobenius. Exact rank counts and an alternating extension-field point census yield a rational Hasse--Weil zeta function.
