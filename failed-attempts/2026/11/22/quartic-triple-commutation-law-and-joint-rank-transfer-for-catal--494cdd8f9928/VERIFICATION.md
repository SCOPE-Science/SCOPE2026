Every idempotent is the predecessor projection of its fixed set containing 1. In a gap between common fixed points (also the terminal gap), suppose a fixed point of the second map alone follows one of the first map alone. At that later point the first projection lands on a first-only point; applying the second projection then strictly decreases it. The composites differ. If at most one exclusive color occurs, the two composites both return the preceding common point. Hence commuting pairs and triples have intersection product. Scanning the three pair conditions together yields the stated 13 reachable states and the letter weights for all four ranks. Removing the all-ones reset letter splits each word of product rank k into k unconstrained gap words in the rank-one gap language, with n-k total gap letters, giving the claimed coefficient and G.
