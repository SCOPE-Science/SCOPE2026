The Fourier transform of the indicator of primitive length-\(n\) words over any finite abelian alphabet is nonzero at exactly the characters annihilating the subgroup of words with period dividing \(n/\operatorname{rad}(n)\). Thus primitive-word convolution has rank \(q^{n-n/\operatorname{rad}(n)}\), smallest nonzero singular value \(q^{n/\operatorname{rad}(n)}\), and an exactly characterized translation symmetry.
