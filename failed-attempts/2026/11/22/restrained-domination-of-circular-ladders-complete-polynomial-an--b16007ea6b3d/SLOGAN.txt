The complete restrained-domination polynomial of each labelled circular ladder \(C_n\square K_2\) follows from two explicit small determinant factors. Minimum restrained configurations have counts \(4\), \(2n\), or \(n^2\) (with a three-set correction at \(n=6\)) according to circumference, while restrained configurations form an exponentially vanishing proportion, asymptotic to \((0.854312453992\ldots)^n\), of all ordinary dominating sets.
