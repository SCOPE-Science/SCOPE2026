For the rank-one unipotent Alexander quandle \(Q=\mathbb F_{p^f}^2\), every quandle retraction has square image cardinality \(p^{2r}\), and an exact Gaussian-power formula counts all retractions at each rank. Its embedded retracts are precisely affine-base bundles of affine fibers, with explicit counts of distinct images and retractions per image; unlike arbitrary subquandles, their sizes obey this sharp restriction.
