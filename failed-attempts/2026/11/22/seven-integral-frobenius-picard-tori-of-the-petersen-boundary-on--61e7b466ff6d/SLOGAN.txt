The connected Picard torus of the ten exceptional curves on any smooth degree-five del Pezzo surface over a finite field has exactly seven integral Frobenius types, one for each conjugacy class of \(S_5\). An explicit unimodular decomposition of its Petersen-graph cycle lattice identifies the actual Weil-restriction and norm-one factors, including \(R_6\) for type \((3,2)\) and \(R_4\times N_4\) for type \((4,1)\), not merely their isogeny classes.
