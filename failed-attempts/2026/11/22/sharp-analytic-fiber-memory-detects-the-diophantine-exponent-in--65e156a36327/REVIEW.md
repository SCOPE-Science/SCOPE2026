In v=u−αx mod 1 the cocycle is α⌊2x⌋. The first n digits are independent fair bits independent of uniform v, so the circle character multiplier is ((1+exp(2πikα))/2)^n. Orthogonality and Cauchy–Schwarz give the claimed bilinear norm; each of the two normalized observables contributes a factor exp(−ρ|k|), and a single frequency attains the maximum. At n=0 the multiplier is 1 even at a frequency whose positive-time multiplier is zero. For rational p/q only the multiples of q survive asymptotically; for irrational α every fixed mode decays, and the analytic weight makes the tail uniformly small. If δ_k=dist(kα,Z), then −log|cos(πkα)| is bounded below by a positive constant times δ_k² and above by another such multiple for δ_k≤1/4. For ν=∞ arbitrarily strong subsequences give 0, which is also a lower bound because A≥2ρ.
