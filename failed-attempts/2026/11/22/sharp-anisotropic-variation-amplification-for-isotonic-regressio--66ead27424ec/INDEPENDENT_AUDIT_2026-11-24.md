For an active isotonic face the connected equality pools are precisely the blocks whose coordinates are averaged; this is orthogonal projection onto the face span. The feasible partitions consist of the discrete and one-block partitions, four single-edge pools, the two disjoint opposite-edge pairings, and the triples omitting either the bottom or the top vertex. A proper connected pool containing both bottom and top forces the remaining point into that pool. On a segment the piecewise affine formulas give a telescoping bound by the largest local operator norm, including boundary segments by continuity from generic segments. The layer-cake identity and graph coarea reduce every local norm exactly to nonconstant Boolean cuts. An independent exact replay (python3 -I -B /workspace/branches/diamond_cut_replay.py) generated the partitions as set partitions rather than by edge contraction, used one representative per complementary pair, and found 140 cases, 22 zero numerators and 118 nonzero numerators with nine distinct rational forms. Cross multiplication in powers of r-1 certifies the upper bound for all r>=1. For (1,1,-1,2) and its perturbation by (0,0,1/4,1/4), the residuals are positive multiples of the sole active normal e_0-e_2 and the output variation is (r+1)/4 versus input variation 1/2; hence equality. Swapping vertices 1 and 2 sends V_r to r V_(1/r) and commutes with Q. The frozen verify.py was read, not run; the independent exact finite enumeration certifies the cut calculation and the analytic reduction covers arbitrary input pairs.
