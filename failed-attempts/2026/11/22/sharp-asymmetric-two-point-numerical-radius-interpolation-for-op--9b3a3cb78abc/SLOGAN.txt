For unequal positive endpoint strengths \(a\ne b\), two-point interpolation between \(aE_{12}\) and \(bE_{21}\) has exact optimal uniform numerical radius \(\bigl(a+b+\sqrt{(a+b)^2-4\rho^2ab}\bigr)/(4\rho)\), where \(\rho\) is the nodes' pseudohyperbolic distance. An explicit rational extremizer attains the bound; the previously known equal-strength case is not claimed as new.
