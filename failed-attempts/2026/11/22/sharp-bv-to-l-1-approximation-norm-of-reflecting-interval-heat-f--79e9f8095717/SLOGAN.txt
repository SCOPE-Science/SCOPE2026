On the reflecting unit interval, the exact \(BV\)-to-\(L^1\) approximation norm of heat flow at every time \(t>0\) is \(\frac12-\frac4{\pi^2}\sum_{j\ge0}e^{-(2j+1)^2\pi^2t}/(2j+1)^2\). Only centered single jumps attain it, and the sharp error grows strictly and concavely from \(2\sqrt{t/\pi}\) at short times toward \(1/2\) at long times.
