Under fixed stock, total Ricker recruitment across \(m\) identical spawning habitats undergoes a sharp equal-to-unequal optimum transition: it is continuous at \(s=4\) for \(m=2\), but discontinuous at an explicit variational threshold \(2<s_m<2m\) for every \(m\geq3\). At high stock the optimal allocation retains \((m-1)/e\) recruits in normalized units while the equal-spread recruitment vanishes.
