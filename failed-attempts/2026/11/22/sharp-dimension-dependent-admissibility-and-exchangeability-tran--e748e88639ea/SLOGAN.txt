The generalized ratio-polynomial copula is valid in dimension \(n\ge2\) exactly for \(\alpha\ge-s_n\), where \(s_n(2-s_n)^n=1\) has a unique root in \((0,2/(n+1))\), improving the previously known sufficient interval. Negative parameters have an exact finite-dimensional cutoff within this construction and cannot extend to an infinite exchangeable law, whereas nonnegative parameters admit an explicit two-component conditionally i.i.d. representation in every dimension.
