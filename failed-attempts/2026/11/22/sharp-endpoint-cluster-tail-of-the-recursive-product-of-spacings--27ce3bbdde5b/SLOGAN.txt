Under a fully specified continuous null, the recursive product-of-spacings statistic for \(n\) observations has the sharp fixed-sample upper tail \(\Pr(R_n>t)\sim C_n e^{-2t/(n+1)}\), with a finite explicit simplex integral for \(C_n\) and critical exponential-moment order \(2/(n+1)\). Two endpoint-cluster configurations alone determine the leading constant; the published bounded score therefore has an essential-exponential lower tail, with \(C_2=2B(1/3,1/3)\).
