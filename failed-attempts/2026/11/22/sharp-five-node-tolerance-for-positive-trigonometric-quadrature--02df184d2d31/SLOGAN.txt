Exactly integrating all trigonometric polynomials through degree two from five independently perturbed equispaced circular samples forces nonnegative interpolatory weights for every configuration exactly up to the sharp relative-jitter radius \(\alpha_*=\frac12(\frac5\pi\arccos\sqrt{\cos(\pi/5)/2}-1)\approx0.20145942246\). At the boundary an explicit alternating displacement pattern makes one weight zero, and beyond it a negative weight is unavoidable for some allowed perturbation.
