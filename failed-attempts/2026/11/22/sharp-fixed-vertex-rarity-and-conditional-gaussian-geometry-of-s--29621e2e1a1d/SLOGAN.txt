Among involutions of the hyperoctahedral group acting on binary-cube vertices, the probability of any fixed vertex is asymptotic to \(e^{3/8-\sqrt{n/2}}\); for even-signed involutions the sharp prefactor doubles. Conditional on fixing a cube vertex, the logarithm (base two) of the number of fixed vertices has Gaussian fluctuations on the \(n^{1/4}\) scale around \(n/2+\sqrt{n/2}/2\).
