For the attracting cycle of a symmetric three-dimensional Filippov flow, approaching the infinity boundary by \(\delta=H_*-H\downarrow0\) sends its crossing amplitude to \(A/\delta\) while its dominant Floquet contraction gap is \(K\delta\), with explicit positive constants. Their product converges to a nonzero exact value, and the entire rescaled cycle converges to a closed loop of the homogeneous limiting switched flow.
