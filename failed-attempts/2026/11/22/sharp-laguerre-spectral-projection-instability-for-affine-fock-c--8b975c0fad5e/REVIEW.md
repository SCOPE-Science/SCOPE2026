# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For 0<a<1, the adjoint action on normalized monomials follows by taking coefficients in the reproducing-kernel identity; summing their squared image norms by Tonelli gives the finite Gaussian integral exp(|(1−a)u|²/(1−a²))/(1−a²). Equivalently this defines the bounded compact extension via the adjoint matrix. The Taylor series about u diagonalizes the action coefficient by coefficient, identifies all nonzero eigenvalues by compactness, and rules out Jordan chains by injectivity of the coefficient at the claimed eigenvalue on its eigenline. With v_n=(z−u)^n/√n! and w_n=z^n exp(conj(u)z)/√n!, the bounded rank-one projection is f↦⟨f,w_n⟩v_n, since the normalized jets pair to δ_mn and commute with the operator. Gaussian orthogonality gives ∥v_n∥²=L_n(−|u|²); completion of the Gaussian square gives ∥w_n∥²=exp(|u|²)∥v_n∥². Mityagin–Siegl–Viola independently support the fixed-displacement prefactor by the adapted parameter below. In the frozen proof, the Bessel weights with λ=√n|u| concentrate at j/λ→1 by the consecutive quotient λ²/(j+1)² (geometric tails outside each fixed relative neighborhood); the falling-factorial multiplier tends in probability to exp(−|u|²/2) and lies in [0,1]. The stated Bessel integral gives the prefactor. For n|u_n|²→τ² the finite Laguerre series is dominated termwise by C^j/(j!)² and converges to I_0(2τ), including τ=0. Complete minimality follows from polynomial spans and the bounded jets. All enumerations have these same individual coordinate projections if they are Schauder bases; coordinate projections would then be uniformly bounded as differences of consecutive partial sum projections. Similarity to a normal or scalar-type spectral operator likewise implies bounded spectral projections. At u=0 the normalized monomials are orthonormal.

## originality

FAIL

There is demonstrable prior coverage, not merely a similar title. A published Fock-jet result contains the identical rank-one terms and both norm identities in its proof, for more general complex slopes. In a separate prior formulation, the whole family of projections is unitarily equivalent to the imaginary-shift oscillator projections of Mityagin–Siegl–Viola. Their exact Laguerre formula and sharp growth (with an error term) transfer with |u|=√2 times their shift parameter. The asserted crossover is the classical compact-uniform Mehler–Heine limit; the basis and similarity consequences follow mechanically. There is no distinct substantial remainder in this record.

## value

FAIL

The projections are mathematically relevant and the exposition is correct, but the exact natural invariant and its sharp growth were already available both through the oscillator equivalent and from the earlier same-space jet identities. The Bessel crossover is a direct classical limit of that exact invariant; the other consequences are standard boundedness arguments. This does not leave an independently useful unknown exact fact or a new structural conclusion to validate or repair.
