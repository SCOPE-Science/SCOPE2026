For unweighted affine contractions on the Gaussian Fock space, the nonzero eigenvalues stay fixed at \(a^n\) while the exact \(n\)-th spectral-projection norm is \(e^{|u|^2/2}L_n(-|u|^2)\), growing sharply like \(e^{2|u|\sqrt n}/(2\sqrt{\pi|u|}\,n^{1/4})\) whenever the fixed point \(u\ne0\). The critical shift \(\sqrt n|u|\to\tau\) produces a Bessel crossover \(I_0(2\tau)\), and every noncentral contraction has a complete minimal eigenfamily that is not a Schauder basis in any ordering.
