For mean-preserving Euclidean group soft thresholding of real \(n\)-point paths, every individual signal loses variation, yet the exact two-input variation Lipschitz factor is \(\bigl(1+\sqrt{(4n-6)\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor/n}\bigr)/2\). The factor grows as \(n/2+1/8+O(n^{-1})\) and, beyond two points, is approached only by near-threshold pairs rather than attained by a fixed pair.
