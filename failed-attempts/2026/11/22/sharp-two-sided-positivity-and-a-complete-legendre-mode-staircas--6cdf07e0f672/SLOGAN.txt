On the two-sphere, the signed second-harmonic geodesic covariance \(e^{-a\theta}(1+b\cos2\theta)\) has an exact sharp two-sided positive-definiteness cone whose negative boundary is controlled by just two adjacent Legendre degrees, with every degree appearing in an ordered mode staircase. The undiluted oscillatory factor \(e^{-a\theta}\cos2\theta\) is positive definite exactly when \(a\ge\sqrt3\), and all admissible boundary kernels remain strictly positive definite but are not \(C(\mathbb S^2)\)-universal.
