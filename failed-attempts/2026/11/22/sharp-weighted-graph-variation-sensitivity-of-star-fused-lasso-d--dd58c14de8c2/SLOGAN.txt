On a weighted star graph, the fused-lasso denoising map has an exactly attained two-input edge-variation Lipschitz constant \(\max\{1,\max_i [m_i/(m_0+m_i)](\sum_{j\ne i}w_j)/w_i\}\), independent of every strictly positive regularization strength. With equal vertex masses no positive edge-variation weighting can prevent expansion on a star with four or more leaves, despite the denoiser reducing each input's own regularizing variation.
