For the screened cycle Green kernel \(K=(\mu I+L(C_n))^{-1}\), the exact \(k\)-landmark minimizers of Nyström trace error are **also** exactly the maximizers of selected-covariance determinant: their cyclic gaps differ by at most one, for every \(\mu>0\) and every budget. All optimizers and optimal values are explicit, and when \(n=kq\), \(2\le k<n\), and \(q\ge2\), the sharp second-best determinant ratio is \(1-1/F_{q-1}^2\), where \(F_0=1\), \(F_1=\mu+2\), and \(F_j=(\mu+2)F_{j-1}-F_{j-2}\).
