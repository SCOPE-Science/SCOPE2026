For two nonzero rank-one square-zero matrices, every prescribed pair of opposite products \((XY,YX)\) is classified exactly: besides annihilation and one-sided nilpotent products, the only nonzero pairs are mutually orthogonal rank-one matrices of equal nonzero trace, each with precisely \(q-1\) ordered factorizations over \(\mathbb F_q\). In odd characteristic this yields a complete simultaneous Jordan-product and Lie-bracket fiber law, including explicit reconstruction of the factors from the two outputs.
