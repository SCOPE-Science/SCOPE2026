For real vectors of length \(n\ge2\), the exact two-input weighted-total-variation Lipschitz gain of increasing sorting is a maximum weighted path-cut ratio. Among all positive edge weights, the unique optimal design (up to scale) is \(w_j=\sin(\pi j/n)\), with attained gain \(\cot(\pi/(2n))\) for even \(n\) and \(\csc(\pi/(2n))\) for odd \(n\), improving the uniform-weight gain \(n-1\) to asymptotically \(2n/\pi\).
