# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Adding the antipodal maxima of the two disk support functions gives W=A+B+max(|A-B|,delta|Y|). The inequality W>=2max(A,B)>=sqrt(2(1+Y^2)) gives the four equality directions, and W<=2+delta with equality at the axial poles and no others for delta>0. On folding, y=|Y| is uniform on [0,1] and q=(1-y^2)|cos(2phi)|; its conditional arcsine density integrates to 1 and the joint density tends to 2/pi at (0,0). On the tail event, 2max(A,B) bounds y^2+q by sqrt(2) epsilon+epsilon^2/2. At y=sqrt(epsilon)v, q=epsilon s, the scaled width deficit is uniformly v^2/sqrt(2)+max(s/sqrt(2),lambda v)+O(epsilon) on the localized boxes. For lambda in [0,M], a narrow band about level 1 has vanishing area uniformly: the inequality involving s has slope 1/sqrt(2); in the other inequality, v^2/sqrt(2)+lambda v is increasing with derivative bounded below near 1 uniformly on this compact lambda range. The limiting event is 0<=v<u_lambda and 0<=s<sqrt(2)-v^2. Its area gives the stated profile after multiplication by 2/pi and normalization gives the conditional area law. At lambda=0, u=2^(1/4); at infinity u is asymptotic to 1/lambda. For fixed delta>0, W>=A+B+delta y and A+B>=sqrt(2)-O(epsilon^2) on the localized region give y=O_delta(epsilon). Scaling y and q both by epsilon gives a rectangle of area sqrt(2)/delta and the claimed coefficient and conditional law.

## originality

FAIL

Material distinctness of the claimed crossover package is not established. A separate Resultary record, a276467e90bf, affirmatively states results for the same unit filled-disk hull, random directional width and minimum, both tail regimes, the exact crossover coefficient on the same sqrt(epsilon) separation scale, and an explicit conditional law of near-minimum directions. This is substantive statement-level overlap, not a judgment based on titles or on a missing document. The exact coefficient for the same probability and crossover scaling is necessarily unique (up to reparametrizing the separation), and locally uniform convergence to the continuous profile follows mechanically from pointwise coefficient convergence and monotonicity in separation. The submitted conditional formulas supply no established distinct result in light of the other statement's explicit conditional law. The full statement would be needed to decide whether the precise conditional coordinates, both conditionings, and scope match exactly. This FAIL is for unestablished distinctness against affirmative indexed coverage, not a finding of historical priority, plagiarism, or complete formula-for-formula identity.

## value

PASS

The mathematical phenomenon has intrinsic value: a universal deterministic minimum hides a singular change in the prevalence and angular shape of near-minimizers as separation vanishes. Scaling and conditioning resolve information not given by mean width or by either endpoint exponent alone. This is an assessment of the scientific question and result, not of distinctness from the separate crossover record.
