For two equal perpendicular disks whose centers separate along their common axis, the minimum directional width stays exactly \(\sqrt2\), but the probability of being within \(\varepsilon\) of it changes from order \(\varepsilon^{3/2}\) at coincident centers to \(\varepsilon^2\) at every positive separation. An explicit uniform crossover at separation \(\lambda\sqrt\varepsilon\) and its conditional angular law quantify this singular transition for the oloid and its relatives.
