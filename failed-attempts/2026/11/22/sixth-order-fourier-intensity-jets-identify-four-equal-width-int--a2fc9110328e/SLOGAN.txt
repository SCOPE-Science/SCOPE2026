Four equally wide, strictly separated real intervals are uniquely determined up to translation and reflection by the four Fourier-intensity derivatives \(I(0)\), \(I''(0)\), \(I^{(4)}(0)\), and \(I^{(6)}(0)\). Sixth order is optimal: distinct four-interval configurations have identical lower-order jets, while an exact cubic inverse and a geometric monotonicity inequality establish global uniqueness at order six.
