The conditional Euclidean-acuteness kernel on the two-sphere has a strictly negative, explicit nonconstant spherical-harmonic spectrum: uniform sampling uniquely maximizes the chance of an acute chord triangle when the third vertex is uniform. The same spectrum makes the centered count of acute triples, scaled by \(n^2\), converge to an explicit non-Gaussian chi-square chaos with sharp upper endpoint \(1/4\).
