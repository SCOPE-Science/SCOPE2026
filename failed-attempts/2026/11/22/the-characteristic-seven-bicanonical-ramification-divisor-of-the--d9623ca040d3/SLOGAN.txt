In characteristic seven, the complete bicanonical series of the Fermat quartic has nonclassical Hasse orders \((0,1,2,3,4,7)\), and its full ramification divisor is exactly eighty simple noncoordinate points plus the twelve hyperflexes, each of weight three. The reduced ninety-two-point support has eight rational points over \(\mathbb F_7\) and splits completely over \(\mathbb F_{49}\), where its ninety-two points are exactly the full rational point set of this maximal genus-three curve.
