For every odd prime power \(q\), the left Hopf-adjoint actions of elements of the Sweedler algebra have ranks only \(0,1,2,4\), with exact multiplicities \(q,\ q(q-1),\ 2q^2(q-1),\ q^2(q-1)^2\). The action factors through a three-dimensional triangular-matrix quotient, explaining the missing rank \(3\).
