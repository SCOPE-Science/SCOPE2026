Among all 620 Jacobian isogeny classes represented by genus-four curves over \(\mathbb F_2\), exactly 178 contain curves with different ground-field gonalities, and 21 contain curves of all three gonalities \(2,3,4\). An exhaustive gonality–Weil incidence table classifies all 1,044 curve isomorphism classes into gonalities \(2,3,4,5\) with counts \(264,526,253,1\).
