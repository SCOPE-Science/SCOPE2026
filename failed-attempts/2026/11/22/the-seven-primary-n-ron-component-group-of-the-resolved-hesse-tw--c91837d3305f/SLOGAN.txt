The stable boundary obtained by blowing up the nine fourfold points of the classical Hesse twelve-line arrangement has a 21-vertex dual graph with Laplacian spectrum \(0^1,3^{11},7^9\) and integral critical group \((\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z)^2\oplus(\mathbb Z/21\mathbb Z)^8\). Under regular split unit-thickness semistable smoothing this is the exact geometric Néron component group of its Jacobian, revealing eight independent 7-primary factors absent from the incidence-only Hesse Levi graph.
