For \(q\) named colours and one idempotent unary function, the isomorphism classes having exactly two automorphisms have an explicit polynomial census and exist at every size from \(2\) through \(q2^{q-1}(q+2)+q+2\), but never beyond it. Exactly \(q^2\) classes attain this sharp largest size, separating the first nonrigid stratum from the known rigid cutoff.
