With no cross-cycle identifications, the three proper choices are ‘collapse the first cycle’, ‘collapse the second’, and ‘collapse both’, giving two edges to the last. With a cross identification, a collapsed cycle forces the indiscrete partition, while two discrete cycles allow three equivariant matchings; these vertices are isolated in F. Ranks prevent inversions and every cell outside F has trivial stabilizer. Independently generated partitions by successive pair merges and confirmed the frozen degree-wise cell counts by Burnside (original 201,1865,4245,2700; fixed 6,2,0,0; quotient 71,623,1415,900). The ordinary quotient is the homotopy pushout of X_hG←F×BG→F: this follows cell by cell from the fixed subcomplex and the free cells outside it (on each relative cell the Borel-to-orbit map has contractible EG factor). The homology sequence gives H₂ of the quotient as the kernel of the sum map (Z/3)^4→Z/3 on H₁; H₁ and H₂ of F and H₂(X_hG) vanish. ## originality

FAIL

The complete homotopy type is a direct specialization of Arone–Brantner's earlier equivariant partition-complex decomposition, Theorem 5.10, formula (1.1). For the preserved 3+3 blocks take H=S₃×S₃ and G the diagonal C₃. The d=1,3 terms have three primitive binary necklaces of content (3,3) and one of content (1,1), respectively. The d=1 terms are induced from the trivial subgroup and give 3·(36/3)=36 quotient three-spheres. The d=3 atom is S^W smashed with the unreduced suspension of the three isolated partitions in Π₃, where W is the reduced two-dimensional permutation representation of S₃ (remove the invariant line from the direct sum of three real lines). On H/ΔS₃ ≅ S₃ the diagonal C₃ acts by conjugation, giving three fixed cosets and one free orbit. The free orbit contributes one unquotiented atom S³∨S³. At a fixed coset the atom is the cofiber of the equivariant fold C₃₊∧S^W→S^W; ordinary orbits give the cofiber of S²→S²/C₃≅S², the degree-three rotation quotient. Thus each fixed coset gives M(Z/3,2) and the free sphere count is 36+2. The prior equivalence is genuinely equivariant, so passing to ordinary orbits here preserves the homotopy equivalence. This is full implication, not just an earlier example with nearby parameters or unverifiable priority.
