For full covariant-Hessian observations of pure quadratic spherical harmonics on two antipodal caps in every dimension, the entire spectrum has three explicit branches and exactly three sharp mode crossings. The best-observed harmonic symmetry switches at squared polar cutoff \(2/(d+2)\), whereas the least-observed symmetry switches at \(1/(d+2)\) and \((d+3)/(2(d+2))\).
