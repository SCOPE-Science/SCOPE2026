In width two the opposite parent copy dominates each leaf copy in the specified order; in width one the parent suffices. Distances of surviving vertices are unchanged. ## originality

FAIL

The exact arbitrary-tree wording was not located independently, but its homotopy conclusion follows by direct iteration of the published metric-gluing theorem of Adamaszek et al., Theorem 3.11, with the already established circular-ladder prism result. The gluing applies along an entire rim, not merely a point or short path. Its unique-maximal-common-simplex condition is automatic for a leaf-edge gluing at radius two. The integral conclusion then follows from the cellular product cycle quotient and from filling its squares. The alternative strip-and-nerve proof is correct but does not leave a distinct finding as written.

The finding does not claim a new structural feature beyond those consequences, such as a distinct scale regime or a stronger map or collapse theorem.
