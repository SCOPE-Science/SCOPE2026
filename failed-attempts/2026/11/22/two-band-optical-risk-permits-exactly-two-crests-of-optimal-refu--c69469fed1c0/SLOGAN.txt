Even when one best refuge depth exists at every turbidity level, two differently attenuated light-risk channels can make that depth rise, fall, rise and fall as turbidity increases. The explicit strictly convex two-band model \(e^z+e^{-kz}+e^{-20kz}\) has exactly two depth-response crests, whereas every one-band model \(e^z+a e^{-bkz}\) has just one.
