For the resolution of a geometrically one-nodal cubic surface obtained by blowing up six points on a conic over an odd finite field, two counts of ground-field-realizable Zariski-chamber supports—total and those containing the conic \((-2)\)-curve—distinguish all eleven Frobenius cycle types. One count alone fails sharply: types \((4,2)\) and \((5,1)\) each have eight stable supports but respectively two and three containing the conic.
