# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The Pascal factors reproduce the row-normalized recursion; their row sums are bounded above and below, so the negative moment reduces to an interval-product integral. In a decreasing-coordinate sector, first form w_j = D_pi(j)/D_pi(1); normalization of (1,w_2,...,w_m) has Jacobian D_pi(1)^m and passage from successive ratios v_j to w_j adds the factors v_j^(m-j). Thus the integrability test is theta H(S)<|S| for each nested tail S. The run-of-intervals bound gives equality at theta=2/m only for complements of the endpoint indices. Where these largest-coordinate sectors are outside the two endpoint neighborhoods, v_2 is bounded below, so the remaining strict inequalities give a stronger moment. At either endpoint the radial exponent is K=m(m-1)/2 and the remainder is uniformly O(1-D_endpoint); beta radial mass and an integrable angular bound in dimension m-1 give precisely E[Q_(m-1)^(-2/m)] per corner. For fixed angle the radial equivalent follows by squeezing at small radius, without assuming global radial monotonicity. The sharp moment boundary follows from integrating the tail. The bounded-score substitution is valid; level sets are zeros of a nontrivial polynomial on the open simplex after exponentiation and clearing the positive factors, hence null. In the two-observation case the angular product is u(1-u), giving 2B(1/3,1/3); direct quadrature after u=z^3/(z^3+(1-z)^3) gives 10.5998325017127.
