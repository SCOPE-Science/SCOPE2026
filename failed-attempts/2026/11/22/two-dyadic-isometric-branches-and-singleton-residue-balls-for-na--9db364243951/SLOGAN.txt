For Narayana’s cows, the index progressions \(3+7j\) and \(4+7j\) are exact two-adic isometries after a factor of two; modulo every \(2^k\), they enumerate all odd and even residues, respectively, exactly once. Furthermore, every target \(2\) or \(7\pmod8\) has a unique index in the full least period, giving two infinite balls of singleton fibers.
