The two-matching complexes of the Möbius ladders \(M_6,M_8,M_{10},M_{12}\) have integral homotopy types \(S^3,S^5,S^7,\bigvee^3S^8\), respectively. The first three are single spheres while the twelve-vertex case is a wedge of three, and the Wagner graph differs from the cubical graph in its two-matching sphere multiplicity.
