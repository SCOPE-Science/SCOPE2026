# Two-point collision spectrum for alternating words with a scattered `010` deletion

## Finding

Let \(n\ge3\) and let \(W_n=(01)^n\). Delete any positional (order-preserving, not necessarily contiguous) occurrence of \(U=010\) and retain the other letters in order. For a residual word \(v\), write \(m_n(v)\) for the number of positional `010` embeddings whose deletion leaves exactly \(v\). The following is the **entire nonzero multiplicity spectrum**:

\[
\#\{v:m_n(v)=t\}=\begin{cases}
\binom{n-1}{3},&t=1,\\
n-1,&t=n-1,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]

The two displayed multiplicities are different because \(n\ge3\). In particular, all nonsingleton fibers have the same size \(n-1\), and
\[
\big|C(W_n,010)\big|=\binom{n-1}{3}+n-1.
\]

There is an explicit classification. For \(1\le t\le n-1\), let \(v_t\) be the alternating word \((01)^{n-1}\) with the zero of its \(t\)-th `01` block deleted. These \(n-1\) words are exactly the exceptional residuals, each with multiplicity \(n-1\). Every other residual is uniquely generated by one triple of block indices \((i,j,k)\) satisfying \(1\le i<j<k-1\le n-1\), deleting the zero of block \(i\), the one of block \(j\), and the zero of block \(k\). Conversely, every such triple generates a different singleton residual.

The same multiplicity spectrum holds on deleting `101` from \(W_n\), by reversing the word and complementing both bits. These are positional embedding counts, not probabilities and not ordinary counts of embeddings of the residual word.

## Assumptions and scope

All words are linear. The source has \(n\) two-letter blocks \(0_i1_i\), numbered from \(1\) to \(n\), and length \(2n\). Deletion of a scattered `010` uses one zero from block \(i\), one one from block \(j\), and one zero from block \(k\). The order constraint is precisely \(1\le i\le j<k\le n\). Every different ordered triple is a different physical deletion, even if the residuals agree. Only nonzero fibers are counted; the residual length is \(2n-3\), with exactly \(n-2\) zeros and \(n-1\) ones. The theorem includes the smallest case \(n=3\), when there are two fibers, each of multiplicity two.

## Proof

**1. Exhaustion by block triples.** Each zero and one appears exactly once in its source block, and a zero in block \(i\) precedes a one in block \(j\) exactly when \(i\le j\). A one in block \(j\) precedes a zero in block \(k\) exactly when \(j<k\). Thus each embedding is represented uniquely by \((i,j,k)\) with \(1\le i\le j<k\le n\). There are no other embeddings.

**2. The exceptional class from adjacent deletions.** If \(i=j\), the deleted zero and one form an entire source `01` block. After removing them, the remaining \(n-1\) blocks are exactly \((01)^{n-1}\); the original zero in block \(k\) has become the zero in block \(k-1\). Thus the residual is \(v_{k-1}\), and for fixed \(t=k-1\) there are exactly \(t\) such embeddings, \((i,i,t+1)\) with \(1\le i\le t\).

If \(i<j\) and \(k=j+1\), then the deleted one in block \(j\) and the immediately following zero in block \(j+1\) form a contiguous `10`. Erasing this `10` joins the remaining pieces into \((01)^{n-1}\), without changing the earlier zero's block index \(i\). Hence this embedding leaves \(v_i\). For fixed \(t=i\), its possibilities are \((t,j,j+1)\), with \(t+1\le j\le n-1\), numbering \(n-1-t\).

Consequently each \(v_t\) is produced by at least \(t+(n-1-t)=n-1\) embeddings. The \(v_t\) are pairwise different: \(v_1\) begins with `1`, while for \(t\ge2\) the unique `11` in \(v_t\) begins at output position \(2t-2\). None contains `00`.

**3. Rigidity when the three deleted positions are separated.** The only remaining triples satisfy \(i<j<k-1\). Their residuals have a `00` starting at output position \(2j-2\), obtained by joining the retained zeros \(0_j\) and \(0_{j+1}\). There are no other `00` factors. At the right, deleting \(0_k\) joins retained ones \(1_{k-1}\) and \(1_k\), creating a `11` beginning at output position \(2k-4\). If \(i>1\), deleting \(0_i\) also joins \(1_{i-1}\) to \(1_i\), creating a second `11` at output position \(2i-2\), before the `00`. If \(i=1\), the output instead begins with `1` and has no `11` preceding the `00`. All unspecified adjacencies remain alternating, since the three deleted symbols are from mutually separated source sites apart from the indicated end effects.

These features reconstruct the whole triple: the unique `00` position determines \(j\); the `11` following it determines \(k\); and the position of the `11` before it determines \(i>1\), with its absence and the initial `1` identifying \(i=1\). This reconstruction also works when gaps are minimal, namely \(j=i+1\) and \(k=j+2\); the defects remain distinct and ordered. Hence every separated triple generates a distinct residual with multiplicity exactly one. Moreover, its `00` prevents it from coinciding with any \(v_t\).

**4. Count and mirrored pattern.** Set \(\ell=k-1\). The separated-triple constraints become \(1\le i<j<\ell\le n-1\), so the number is exactly \(\binom{n-1}{3}\). By steps 2 and 3, the remaining \(n-1\) residuals have multiplicity exactly \(n-1\), proving the whole spectrum. As a consistency check, the total number of embeddings counted in the fibers is
\[
\binom{n-1}{3}+(n-1)^2=\binom{n+1}{3},
\]
which also equals \(\sum_{j=1}^{n-1}j(n-j)\), the direct triple count. Reversal followed by swapping zero and one maps \((01)^n\) to itself and `010` to `101`, bijectively preserving residual multiplicities. This proves the second assertion.

## Relationship to prior work

Adamson, Fleischmann and Huch introduced complement scattered factors and gave general algorithms and structural criteria, while identifying closed counts for special families as a direction for study. Their complete March 2026 manuscript was inspected, including definitions, the set algorithm, the structural results and conclusion; it does not present this embedding-to-residual collision classification. Existing results counting the **number** of distinct three-symbol deletion residuals for alternating words already imply the displayed *cardinality* and are not claimed anew. They do not determine the fiber multiset, exact locations of collisions, or the all-or-nothing \(1\)-versus-\(n-1\) multiplicity law proved here.

The previously published binomial-tail description of deleting alternating even-length patterns applies to \((01)^k\), not the odd-length `010`. A general cyclic-permutation subsequence-embedding spectrum counts embeddings of a prescribed **surviving** word, without fixing the content of the deleted complement; it does not imply these conditional `010` fibers. A known fixed-composition deletion-output generating function likewise aggregates over many possible deleted patterns. This statement supplies a conditional, collision-resolved refinement in the shortest alternating three-symbol case.

## Limitations

The result is for exactly three deleted symbols, the two alternating patterns `010` and `101`, and the even-length alternating host. It is not a formula for arbitrary patterns, hosts, longer deletions or any noisy observation of the deletion positions. Aggregate cardinality is already known; the new content is the exact full fiber-size and collision-location classification. An unlocated classical shuffle/deletion-channel identity could have the same conclusion; historical priority has not been independently established.
