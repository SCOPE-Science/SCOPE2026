For the intersection of four equal transverse complex cylinders in \(\mathbb C^5\), the axial Bergman kernel has exactly two, then one, then zero attainable scalar-product zeros as the central exponent crosses \(p=1/4\) and \(p=\sqrt{2/3}\). Both thresholds are sharp simple boundary crossings, giving explicit non-Lu-Qi-Keng domains throughout \(0<p<\sqrt{2/3}\), without asserting global zero-freeness above that range.
