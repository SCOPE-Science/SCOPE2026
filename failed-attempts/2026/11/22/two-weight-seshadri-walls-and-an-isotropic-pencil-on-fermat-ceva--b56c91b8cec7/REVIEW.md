# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For degree d and grid/vertex multiplicity sums A,B, Bézout with all arrangement lines and with all coordinate sides gives 3A+mB≤3md and 2B≤3d for curves other than those lines. The two line families themselves give weighted sums mα+β and 2β. Substituting the inequalities, or interpolating the bounds A≤md, A+mB≤2md, B≤2d, proves the weighted value including strictness off the wall (curves other than arrangement lines with A=md must have B=0, and non-side curves have B≤3d/2). With u=mα, the difference of the squared exact and squared volume denominators is 2β(u−β) below the wall and β²−u² above it. The pencil is the quadratic Cremona transform of the diagonal Fermat pencil: the transformation on the coordinate blow-up inverts the torus and permutes the m² grid points. At those grid points the two linear initial terms are independent; at each vertex the two degree-m initial forms have no common projective zero, so these ordinary blow-ups resolve all base points. The general transformed fiber is the proper transform of a diagonal degree-m plane curve with all three coefficients nonzero; in characteristic zero it is smooth and geometrically integral. This also independently gives its genus (m−1)(m−2)/2. The grid exceptional curves are sections, the vertex exceptional curves have fiber degree m, and the three displayed fibers have the indicated multiplicities. T is defined by the displayed grid equation; for m=3 the distinct vertex stratum also consists of triple points. The finite checker was inspected, not executed; it does not test generic smoothness or classify singular fibers.

## originality

FAIL

Demonstrable coverage, not an access failure. Falk–Yuzvinsky do not merely give a general framework or a different Fermat pencil. In their treatment of the full monomial group G(m,1,3) they give the identical degree-2m pencil, the identical base multiplicities, connectedness and special fibers. Its resolved fiber class gives the wall inequality A+mB≤2md. The value in both chambers is then the immediate positive linear combination of that inequality with A≤md or B≤2d by Bézout. Both candidate line families are already explicit in the monomial pencil. Strictness and unique volume saturation are immediate equality checks on those same inequalities. The weighted expression need not have been tabulated for that implication to cover the asserted mathematical content.

## value

FAIL

The two-stratum interpretation is a useful exposition of the known fibration, but the claimed additional content is only its nef ray combined linearly with the elementary grid and triangle bounds. The wall minimizers beyond the stated line families are not addressed and no more general weighted nef region or new geometry is established. The exact slice as asserted is mechanically implied and does not meet the value requirement for a distinct invariant.
