The two-weight multipoint Seshadri constant of every complex Fermat–Ceva line arrangement is exactly \(1/\max\{m\alpha+\beta,2\beta\}\), with a sharp change from arrangement-line minimizers to coordinate-side minimizers at \(\beta=m\alpha\). Precisely on that wall it saturates the volume bound, and the resulting isotropic nef class is the fiber of an explicit genus-\((m-1)(m-2)/2\) pencil with \(m^2\) sections.
