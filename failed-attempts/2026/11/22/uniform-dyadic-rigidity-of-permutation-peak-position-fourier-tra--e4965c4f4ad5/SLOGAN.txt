For every size \(n\ge3\), every Walsh multiplier of the multiplicity-counting interior peak-position profiles of permutations has exactly the same dyadic valuation as \(n!\), so the associated Boolean convolution is invertible. The universal congruence \(W_n(S)\equiv n!\pmod{2^{v_2(n!)+1}}\) is sharp, already at each one-position frequency.
