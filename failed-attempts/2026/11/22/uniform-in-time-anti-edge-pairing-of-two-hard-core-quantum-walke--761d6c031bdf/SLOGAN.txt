Two hard-core bosons launched on a nonedge of a \(2m\)-site cocktail-party network remain on *some* nonedge of the deleted perfect matching with probability \(1-O(1/m)\) **uniformly over all times**: the sharp all-time leakage coefficient is \(\lim_{m\to\infty}m\sup_t(1-Q_m(t))=4\). At the natural inverse-size observation time, corresponding classical exclusion has pairing probability tending to \(e^{-2\pi}\), exposing coherent pairing without an attractive interaction.
