For every odd multiplier \(k\ge5\), the positive odd arithmetic progressions whose sum of cubes equals \(k\) times the square of their sum are classified exactly by the Lucas indices \(3t+1\), \(t\ge1\). Their shortest length is \(k^3-2k\), and their admissible lengths have logarithmic counting growth; the first new higher-multiplier instance occurs at \((k,n,a)=(5,115,437)\).
