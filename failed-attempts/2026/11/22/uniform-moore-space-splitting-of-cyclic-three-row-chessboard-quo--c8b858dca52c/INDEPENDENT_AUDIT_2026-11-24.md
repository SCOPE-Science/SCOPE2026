# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

A point fixed by a column shift would have a setwise fixed support face, which is impossible because rows are fixed and there is at most one rook in each occupied row. The quotient retains three vertices, 3(n−1) edges and (n−1)(n−2) triangles. Collapsing the two tree edges gives triangle words a_i c_(j−i) b_j^−1. The j=1 and i=1 relators eliminate a_i and b_j by homotopy-preserving substitution and cancellation, not just by a group presentation isomorphism. For the remaining relators, r=1 yields c_s=u^(s−1)t with u=c_(n−1)^−1 and t=c_1. The pair (n−2,1) gives c_2^−1 c_3 c_1^−1=t^−1u, so it eliminates u for every n≥5. Remaining exponents E=e(s−r)−e(−r)−e(s) are divisible by n and lie between −2n+3 and n−2, hence are −n or 0. The distinct pair (2,1) survives and gives −n. The cofiber on one circle is invariant under source wedge Nielsen operations, reducing the degree vector to (n,0,...,0) and giving (n−2)(n−4)−1 spheres. Covering space and standard connectivity give an independent check of pi_1. At n=4 the vertex links are six-cycles; signing each row-ordered triangle by the permutation of the occupied columns followed by the unused column orients the torus. The column four-cycle reverses the sign, so the free quotient is a nonorientable surface of Euler characteristic zero, the Klein bottle.

## originality

FAIL

The homotopy type in the Finding is already a direct corollary of Dyer's 1978 theorem on the essential height of homotopy trees for finite abelian groups, using prior chessboard connectivity and the elementary orbit cell counts. The finding is true; this rejection is verified general-theorem coverage, not a failure of correctness. In addition, the exact n=5 instance has an earlier Resultary record. The constructive calculation is distinct from merely calculating homology, but it is an alternate proof of a type already determined by the stronger theorem.

## value

FAIL

The quotient and its homotopy type are natural, and the explicit word calculation can be pedagogically useful. As frozen, however, the asserted new fact is the exact nonequivariant wedge type, mechanically implied by the established positive-level classification; no distinct new invariant or quantitative property of the explicit simplification is claimed. This does not clear the value bar for a narrow exact invariant.
