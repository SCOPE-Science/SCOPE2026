# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

With H=F_q^*, the identity f(xu)=f(x)u^r for u in H forces gcd(r,q-1)=1 and hence odd r. The lift of the block permutation to the elements has sign sgn(sigma)^(q-1)=1, and the even number q+1 of blocks cancels the signs of the identical within-block power maps. In a cyclic group of even order, translation by c has sign equal to its quadratic character, as seen by writing c as a power of the generator (translation by the generator is one even-length cycle). The product of the within-block multipliers is omega^[q(r-1)/2]*(b-epsilon)^2, where omega generates H, b=a^((q+1)/2) and epsilon=(-1)^((q+1)/2). This follows from the product of the coset representatives and the two-to-one map on the group of (q+1)-st roots of unity. Since the product lies in H, (b-epsilon)^2 lies in F_q^*. A nonsquare nonzero a would give b^q=-b; invariance of (b-epsilon)^2 would then force 4*b*epsilon=0, impossible. For square a and for a=0, b is in F_q, so the second factor is a nonzero square in H. The first factor contributes (-1)^((r-1)/2); zero is fixed. This proof handles odd prime powers and the monomial case without assuming any sufficient criterion for permutation.
