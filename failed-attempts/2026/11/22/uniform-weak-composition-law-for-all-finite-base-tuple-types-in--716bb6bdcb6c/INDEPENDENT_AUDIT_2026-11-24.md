# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Under the frozen no-third-displacement hypothesis the 3n cuts are distinct. A unary relation to a named parameter selects one open one-third arc, and intersections of such short arcs are connected when nonempty. Thus the unary diagram uniquely determines a lift of one of the n projected gaps. Cut the quotient at the projection of a named vertex and consistently label its three lifts. For two coordinates in one gap, their arc/nonedge relation distinguishes the sign of the projected difference at each of the three possible lift differences; across different gaps that sign is predetermined. This makes the gap/color/order encoding bijective on finite diagrams. Any such diagram is realized by choosing small disjoint ordered projected subintervals and using density of D on each indicated lift. Homogeneity then makes the diagrams exactly the complete tuple types over the individually named base. Each fixed occupancy admits r! label assignments and within-gap orders combined, times 3^r independent colors. Stars and bars gives the claimed total, including the unique empty tuple at r=0. Uniform occupancy on these weak compositions is the Dirichlet-multinomial with all parameters one. The proof is correct for the stated pure digraph, named nonempty base and injective nonparameter tuples.

## originality

FAIL

This is a direct counting corollary of the stronger structural description in Nguyen Van Thé, More on the Kechris–Pestov–Todorcevic correspondence: Precompact expansions, §8, proof of Proposition 12. That proof explicitly makes the sector-colored S(3) bi-definable with the dense three-colored linear order Q₃ by folding the three arcs. With a named vertex as the cut and that vertex removed, the same colored expansion is available: each of the three colors is exactly one of the ternary relations to the cut vertex, so the named-vertex stabilizer is precisely the automorphism group of the folded three-colored order. Any additional named vertices are just fixed labeled points of that order. Their n−1 distinct projections create n intervals. The labeled tuple orbits over those points are determined by the interval, color, and within-interval order of each new coordinate. Thus the prior structural result yields all the asserted types, not just the total, by counting colored orders in n intervals. The exact formula need not be printed in that paper to be covered.

## value

FAIL

The exact tuple count is natural and the exposition could be useful, but as a new finding it is mechanically implied by the known folding to Q₃. Naming one actual vertex exposes the three already known colors; fixing the other points only cuts an order into intervals. The constant mass for each occupancy is then precisely the cancellation of the labeling multinomial with within-interval factorials. The Dirichlet-multinomial restatement adds no distinct invariant or new premise. No nonroutine surviving original claim is established.
