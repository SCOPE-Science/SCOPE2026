In the exceptional homogeneous circular digraph \(S(3)\), the ordered-injective tuple types over any \(n\ge1\) individually named vertices have the universal count \(3^r r!\binom{n+r-1}{r}\), independently of the base's finite directed graph. Every composition of \(r\) tuple coordinates among the \(n\) projected circle gaps occurs with exactly the same number of logical types.
