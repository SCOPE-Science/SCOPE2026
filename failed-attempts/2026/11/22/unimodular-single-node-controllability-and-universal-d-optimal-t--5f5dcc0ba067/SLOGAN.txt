On every unweighted path, the Kalman controllability matrix from a single vertex has determinant exactly \(0\), \(1\), or \(-1\); it is nonsingular exactly when \(\gcd(2j-1,n)=1\). All such vertices have the **same positive finite-horizon controllability-Gramian determinant**, for every damping and observation horizon, so every vertex of a dyadic path is equally D-optimal despite different local degrees.
