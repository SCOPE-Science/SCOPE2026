For every positive interior-to-endpoint mass ratio \(t\), independently optimizing all three edges of a four-site symmetric-mass diffusion path has a unique answer up to scale: outer-to-middle-to-outer conductances \(1:(1+t)/2:1\). The exact best positive-mode condition number is \((\sqrt{1+t}+1)/(\sqrt{1+t}-1)\), certified by two trial vectors with identical edge energies for every weighting.
