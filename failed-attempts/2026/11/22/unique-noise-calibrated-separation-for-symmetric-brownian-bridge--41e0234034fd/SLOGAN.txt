For two equally noisy observations of a Brownian bridge at positions reflected about the midpoint, the unique integrated-path-variance-optimal separation is the sole positive root of an explicit sextic transformation, remains strictly positive at every finite noise variance \(\eta>0\), and shrinks as \(1/(8\eta)\) at high noise. This symmetric optimal design strictly improves upon every repeated-location two-observation design; unrestricted asymmetric pairs are not classified.
