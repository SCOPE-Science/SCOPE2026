For every fixed cyclotomic index \(m\ge2\), the **entire nonzero-coefficient gap histogram** of \(\Phi_m(X^t)/\Phi_m(X)\) is exactly affine in \(\lfloor t/m\rfloor\) along each reduced residue class, for all coprime \(t>m\), including composite multipliers. The slope of every gap length is an explicit sum of cyclic support-gap counts of finite coefficient words, and no gap exceeds \(\varphi(m)\).
