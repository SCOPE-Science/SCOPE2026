For a parity-checkerboard half-region defined by the signs of \(2q\) coordinates, every polynomial band of degree below \(q\) captures exactly half its energy, whereas the first responsive band has precisely two noncentral concentration levels \(\tfrac12\pm\tfrac12(2/\pi)^q\), each with multiplicity \(\binom{2q}{q}/2\). This spectrum and its sharp recovery condition number are independent of ambient dimension and radial distribution for rotationally invariant measures.
