For every noncollinear zero-mean Stuart–Landau polygon under strictly positive purely real all-to-all coupling, the exact rotating-frame inertia is \((2,M,M-2)\): precisely two unstable directions, independent of the number and heterogeneity of active oscillators. Collinear polygons have one or two unstable directions, separated by the sharp susceptibility threshold \(c\sum_j(2(\beta_j-Mc))^{-1}=1\).
