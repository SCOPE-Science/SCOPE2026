For every finite real path, the weighted variation of differences contracts under **every** convex edge-energy proximal map and its nonlinear gradient flow exactly when the edge weights obey \((w_j-w_{j-1})/m_j+(w_j-w_{j+1})/m_{j+1}\ge0\), with zero boundary edge weights. This superharmonicity criterion is necessary and sufficient even for asymmetric or nonsmooth convex potentials; one quadratic edge supplies a strict counterexample whenever it fails.
